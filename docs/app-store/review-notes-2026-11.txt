2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Atlas is a bilingual (English / Simplified Chinese) interactive visual textbook for iPad, written for upper-primary pupils in Singapore (about 10 to 12 years old), and for general readers who want to see how things work.
Problem: diagrams and maps in a printed textbook cannot move, so a pupil cannot watch a front line shift, follow refrigerant round an air conditioner, or see why 1/2 equals 2/4.
Value: every topic is a scene the reader controls. History unfolds on a map with a timeline. Machines and anatomy are 3D models that can be turned, cut open and pulled apart. Maths ideas are explained step by step and then done by hand, with a short check per chapter. Facts and figures carry their sources. This build has 61 topics in science, biology, history, geography, maths and computing. Free, no ads.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No setup, sign-in, credentials or sample files are needed. Everything works offline.
- Launch the app. The home page lists all topics. Tap any topic card.
- In a topic, the bar at the bottom is the chapter bar: tap a chapter or a dot to jump. Back and Next move one step.
- 3D topics (e.g. "How an Air Conditioner Works"): drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, choose Orbit, Cutaway or Exploded in the VIEW bar, tap a part.
- History topics (e.g. "Second World War"): the map changes as you move through the chapters.
- Maths topics (e.g. "Fractions"): follow the beats with Next; answer the check at the end of a chapter.
- Top right: EN switches English/Chinese, LOOK switches the theme, HELP opens the guide. PRESENT plays a chapter and can read the captions aloud.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. All text, map data, 3D models and fonts ship inside the app bundle, and the app makes no network requests at run time: no backend, analytics, advertising SDK, authentication service, payment processor or AI service. Read-aloud uses the system speech synthesiser on the device. Preferences (language, theme) are stored on the device only. The app is a WKWebView shell built with Capacitor; no other third-party SDK.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Features and content are the same in every region. The reader chooses English or Chinese inside the app; nothing depends on location or storefront.
