# 3D model realism review: batch 3 (13 SpaceScene topics)

Reviewer: Claude (read-only). Build: main @ dee51e3, fresh `dist/`. Date: 2026-11-02.
Method: `pnpm shoot <slug> --locale en --theme paper` at 1920×1080. aircon was shot with `--beats`. The others used the default chapter, mode, preset and hero-clean set, because `--beats` took more than 10 minutes per topic on the loaded machine. I looked at 3–6 shrunk or centre-cropped frames per topic and grepped `data/parts.json` for primitive kinds. I judged form and silhouette, not palette: ivory, graphite and hairlines are intended.

Grades: A = convincing, keep. B = good, polish details. C = recognisable but crude, rebuild key parts. D = placeholder, rebuild model.
Priority: 1 = the subject and visibly crude, 3 = minor prop.

## Per-part notes

### container-port (D, priority 1)
- `ship-hull` (single `extrude`): a constant-section prism with a chamfered bow. A real hull has sheer, bow flare, bulbous bow and a transom stern. Loft 6–8 stations; the deck should be at sheer height.
- `ship-cargo` (4 `extrude`): three loose blocks on a bare deck do not say "container ship". Build bays of instanced boxes (≥ 10 bays × 8 rows × 4 tiers on deck) with hatch covers and lashing bridges. Instance them: a single InstancedMesh keeps draw calls flat.
- `ship-bridge` (4 box): a white cube on a stick. Make it a deckhouse of 6–8 decks with bridge wings and a funnel.
- `portal`, `crane-boom`, `trolley` (box only): the members have no cross-section, the boom is a flat ladder, there is no A-frame apex, and the stays converge on nothing. Build the STS crane as box-girder legs with a sill beam and portal bracing, A-frame, forestays and backstays as `tube`, boom with a hinge, machinery house and operator cab under the trolley.
- `rail-bogies` (blocks + 4 cylinders): real bogies have 8–16 small wheels per corner in equaliser beams.
- `spreader`: a flat extrude. A telescopic spreader has a main frame, twistlock corners and flippers.
- `yard-gantry`, `yard-trolley`: hoop frames. Build an RTG (4 legs, 8 rubber tyres, trolley with cab, diesel/e-house) or ARMG (rail bogies). The box block should sit under it.
- `agv-body`/`agv-wheels`: a slab on pucks. A real AGV (Tuas or PSA) has a low chassis, rounded ends and guide or alignment posts.
- `water-context`: a flat blue slab. Use a plane with a subtle ripple normal and darker tint near the hull.
- Not factually misleading in count, but the scale of the crane relative to the ship looks wrong in hero. Check the outreach (about 70 m) against beam (about 50 m).

### steam-engine (C, Newcomen D, priority 1)
- `beam`: a single wooden plank. Watt's rotative engines used a cast-iron or trussed timber beam with a centre gudgeon on an entablature carried by columns or a wall. Model it as a fish-belly cast beam with bosses.
- `sun` / `planet`: smooth gold discs. **They must have teeth**; the topic explains sun-and-planet *gearing*, so a toothless disc misleads.
- `flywheel`: a thin `torus` ring with 8 cylinder spokes. Give it a heavy rim of rectangular section, tapered spokes and a hub with keys.
- `boiler`, `furnace`, `boiler-seating`: a bare cylinder over a white box with a black slot. Use a haystack or wagon boiler in a brick setting with a flue and chimney stub.
- `engine-frame`: square timber posts. Use an entablature with columns, or show an engine-house wall in cutaway.
- `governor-*`: reads, but give the collar a turned `lathe` profile and the arms forked ends.
- `cylinder`: a plain grey tube with a flat lid. Add flanged covers with a bolt circle, a steam jacket and the valve chest attached.
- Newcomen (`nc-*`): the T-post pivot with no engine-house wall misreads how the beam was supported. Add the bob-wall, the arch heads as solid arcs with chains, the boiler under the cylinder and pump rods going down the shaft.

### saturn-v (C, priority 1)
- `lm-ascent` (+ `lm-windows`): the box-with-triangles reads as a toy. It is the hero of chapter 05. Build faceted ascent-stage geometry from NASA drawings, or decimate the NASA 3D Resources LM (public domain).
- `lm-descent`: a gold octagonal prism. Use the cruciform octagon, MLI foil crinkle (normal map) and black panels.
- `lm-legs`: the strut layout is OK. Add secondary struts, footpad probes and a deployment truss.
- `cm-couches`: wooden blocks. Use three couch frames (`sweep`).
- `sic-fins`: flat triangles. Use engine fairings (conical `lathe` shrouds) plus fins with thickness.
- `f1-*`: bells only. Add the turbopump, gas generator exhaust and turbine-exhaust manifold torus.
- `sic-shell`, `sivb-shell`: plain white. Add the roll-pattern black panels and "USA" as a decal (DESIGN tag); these are identity features of the vehicle.

### energy-conversion (C, priority 1 for the bicycle)
- `bicycle-frame` (18 tubes): the geometry is not a diamond frame and the dynamo disc fills the frame triangle. **Factually misleading**: a bottle dynamo is driven by its roller on the tyre, not mounted mid-frame.
- `front-wheel` / `rear-wheel`: good; consistent with forces' wheel.
- `crank-pedals`: the crank sits too low and the chainring and chain are missing, so energy transfer from pedal to wheel is not visible.
- `kettle`: lathe body OK; the handle is a squared block, there is no spout, and the base is a plain disc.
- `speaker`: a box with a cone. Add the surround and a grille frame.
- `fan-blades`: flat paddles. Give them twist and a hub cap.
- `solar-panels`, `inverter`, `lift-motor`: acceptable.
- `sun`: a flat yellow sphere, fine as a symbol.

### b200-server (C, priority 2)
- `bezel`: a flat tan slab. Its gold perforated grille is the visual identity of DGX; build it as a cellular grille.
- `scale-figure`: a capsule mannequin. Replace it with a shared human figure.
- `top-cover`: floats as a paper-thin sheet. Give it thickness and a fold-down lip.
- `gpu-modules`/`gpu-dies`: fine for schematic, but add SXM board edge, standoffs and HBM stack bumps.
- `fan-modules`: good. `gpu-heatsinks`/`cpu-heatsinks`: good.

### heat (C, priority 2)
- `retort-stand`, `probe-clamp`, `heater-probe-support`, `cooler-probe-support`: cube boss heads with no clamp. Use the shared lab-kit.
- `bath-beaker`, `heater-beaker`, `cooling-beaker`: no spout, rim or graduations.
- `metal-spoon`/`plastic-spoon`: no visible bowl at preset distance. Use a `sweep` handle and a `lathe`/`scale` bowl.
- `expansion-thermometer`, `probe`: hairline thin. They need a visible stem diameter and bulb.
- `bench`: hero framing has apparatus at about 5 % of frame. Reframe.
- `expansion-ball`/`expansion-ring`: good.

### how-planes-fly (B, priority 2)
- `fuselage-shell`: conical nose. Use an ogive nose and set the windscreen into it.
- `wing-left/right`: add the belly fairing and root fillet, and check dihedral.
- `tailplane-*`, `fin`: uniform slabs. Taper thickness toward the tip.
- `compressor-bank`/`turbine-bank`: identical stage rows. Taper blade height per stage, and add OGVs and spinner detail.

### aircon (B, priority 3)
- `inverter-board`: a grey box. Add heatsink fins, PCB and caps.
- `wall-section`: plain, acceptable.

### circuits, forces, light-and-shadows, magnets, materials (B, priority 3)
- See the table. The common thread is lab hardware built from bare blocks (stands, clamps, cells, cans). The shared kits below fix most of it at once.
