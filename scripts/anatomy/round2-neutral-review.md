## Source method and rejected looks

`round2-voxel.py` voxelizes the original complete source surface at 2 mm, retains the original local joint field, removes only the central extension with a smoothly tapered maximum 25 mm half-width crotch arch, and unites a smooth pelvis cap into the same volume. A cropped 9 mm Gaussian implicit blend joins its seam before marching cubes; subsequent source-space relaxation and recomputed smooth normals operate before compression. No smoothing is applied to already quantized meshes. `round2-compact.mjs` now retains approximately 32k body triangles rather than 24k, with 7.5k per anatomical source hand. The runtime GLBs were rebuilt with 14-bit positions and 8-bit normals; repeated decode/prune/write strips orphan buffers, keeping the native assets below 1.5 MB before fresh masks.
