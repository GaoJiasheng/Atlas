This is a limited cached-coverage result, not proof that all BP3D or Z-Anatomy distributions lack those structures. `source-scan.json` only covers joints/muscles/regions/skeleton; separate visceral/cardio FBX files exist but were not natively reimported here. The local `License.txt` says its file is a BodyParts3D derivative adapted by Z-Anatomy and requires historical BP3D CC-BY-SA2.1 Japan plus Z-Anatomy CC-BY-SA4.0 attribution for derived content. Crucially it separately lists **“Anatomy of the Inner Ear” by University of Dundee School of Medicine as CC-BY-NC-SA4.0**, alongside other mixed-provenance assets. Therefore do not assign blanket BY-SA to every Z-Anatomy organ, and do not silently relabel this cache under current direct-BP3D CC BY4.0.

**Recommended minimum:** original, explicitly labelled schematic geometry based on independently checked structural facts and the inspected references. Existing ossicles could be a future separately licensed realism upgrade, after item provenance/native orientation/attachment/visual audit. This audit does not clear any mesh for import.

## Claims retained, cut and remaining gaps

- Retain the spec's eye path, accommodation, three-bone transfer, cochlear conversion, nerve signals and qualitative care. Retain main care wording “frequent breaks / look into distance”; source-specific minute ranges belong only in cited More blocks.
- Cut clinical eye dimensions, real focal lengths, lens-only refraction, real tuning-frequency maps, ossicle gain/amplitude/latency numbers, hearing tests, dB calibration and noise safety countdowns. Model centimetres, 250/500 Hz examples and fixed visual slow factor are original SIM/DESIGN inputs, not measured biology.
- Cut any diagram-induced claim that air travels into cochlear fluid or nerve, that semicircular canals are the sound receptor, that a pupil is solid, or that a brain literally flips a tiny picture on a second screen.
- No new source support for MOE-official Chinese vocabulary is asserted. UCSF visibly supports 振动; amplitude/refraction/inverted-image/myopia editorial translations are not all Singapore-attested in this pass. Common terms can be independently defined without claiming official status.
