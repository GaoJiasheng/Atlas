## Lighting and two visual iterations

All sheets decode the **actual compressed `muscles-core-gym.glb`**, dequantize it, and rasterize depth with interpolated normals. They reuse `scripts/anatomy/rebuild-evidence/studio.ts` and its topic-local copy of the fossils GGX renderer: actual installed RoomEnvironment sampling, engine paper/cinema key/fill/rim/hemisphere, exposure 1.05 / 1.0, ACES and sRGB output. `verifyRig()` checks the actual executing `Lighting.tsx` source. Crucially the finish is taken from the actual equipment code: neutral token, roughness .68, metalness 0, **material envMapIntensity .5**, multiplied by the engine's environment intensity. This avoids the earlier sentinel-file and flat-lighting mistakes. CPU filtering, shader micrograin and GPU AA remain approximate.

1. **Look 1 — iteration1 sheets/contact plus full dumbbell/bench comparison sheets.** Saw the rack liner hovering over the cradle; changed the formed J-cup inner floor to meet the liner bottom at `hook − .020 m`. Saw that a flush dumbbell cap could be buried by the solid end face; the final photo-angle depth study led to a true recessed annular head profile, so its cap remains relief after compression. Saw overly costly fine piping and conspicuous sleeve rings; converted piping to a rounded annular extrusion and refined scoring. The initial chrome adjustable dumbbell photo was the wrong prototype; obtained two fixed round dumbbell references. Added twin pad mounting flanges to the back spine and retained all original equipment counts.
2. **Look 2 — iteration2 rack detail and cable full comparison, then all final front/3q/profile/detail views.** Found endcaps extended the bar/dumbbell beyond the original lengths; inset them to restore exactly 2.2 / .33 m. Moved the cable tower behind the authored output-sheave plane so its post cannot bury the pulley; added the vertical return cable. The subsequent profile grid also showed an unsupported cable return near the tower top; added a second top sheave, its beam connection and a return ending inside the head-frame anchor. The first full profile grid exposed a gap between the diagonal bench legs and seat; added a connected upper chassis/seat mounting flange and extended the front leg into it. Re-rendered the actual updated GLB after these fixes.
3. **Final check.** Four views per hero plus one cinema finish check per hero. The reference mismatch is disclosed on every sheet rather than hidden by cropping. No GPU, exercise-grip acceptance or exact-angle photo acceptance is claimed.

A separate `preview-gym-photo.ts` pass adds **one perspective photo-angle study per hero**, including cropped post/stack/dumbbell detail and a separately meshopt-encoded/decoded flat-bench setting from the same factory. Cameras follow the observed shaft/long-axis direction, near/far perspective size and elevation instead of assuming arbitrary 45° angles. `photo-manifest.json` records each camera, unwarped source crop and visible residual. The barbell photo has larger, multiple plates; the consumer bench has a leg developer/curved frame; the rack photo is a four-post cage; the cable photo is a shrouded multi-station machine. These remain concrete design differences, and these five approximate studies do **not** fulfil four exact matching reference angles per hero.
