# Singapore land use — research for Atlas

Access date for all external sources: **2026-10-20**. Research only; all generated files are in this directory. No application, content or map files in the Atlas repository were edited.

## Summary

- The P3 syllabus directly connects food, housing and transport with changing land use. Printed pages 23–25 are the core alignment. See section 1 and its evidence ledger.

- Open URA land-use layers exist for all four requested Master Plans. Compare **permitted zoning**, not automatically actual land cover. Current HDB footprints and completion-year records can support a carefully labelled animation. [Master Plan evidence](#fact-master-plan-meaning)

- Existing `basemap.local` coast eras L0–L5 can anchor the topic, but L5 begins in 1961 and remains open-ended. Post 1961 reclamation needs additional dated geometry. [Local-file evidence](#fact-local-map-eras)

- Official land area: **581.5 km² (1960), 617.8 km² (1980), 682.7 km² (2000), 735.7 km² (2024)**. Keep the 2002 measurement change visible. [Series](#land-area)

- Treat **30 by 30 as historical**. The refreshed 2035 goals distinguish defined fibre and protein categories; they are capacity targets rather than evidence that those levels have been achieved. [Food targets](#food-targets)

- Housing coverage includes the Bukit Ho Swee fire, emergency rehousing, earlier living conditions and residents’ adjustments. Kampong communities had differing services and experiences. [Section4](#4-living-conditions-and-resettlement)

- Main gaps: July 2024 syllabus text comparison; a fully open, observed land-use/coast/farm sequence for 1950s–1980s; a complete rail-segment opening register; and dates/old footprints for every reservoir. These are documented gaps, not filled from memory.

**How to read the evidence:** each fact has an `E…` reference. The ledger gives its title, publisher, URL, page/section, access date and a quotation of at most 25 words. `facts.json` normalises the same references; `datasets.json` is an array with the requested `name`, `url`, `licence`, `format`, `years`, `use` fields plus evidence and cautions. Recommendations and map-processing inferences are labelled below.

### Atlas map integration — research recommendations and inferences

1. Retain the existing island-scale `basemap.local` architecture and real-data rule from `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/17` SS P3/topic#12, `docs/24` and `docs/28`. Treat coast, zoning, building stock, farming and rail operation as separate dated layers.

2. Use URA2019 geometry as a modern anchor. Label MP2003/2008 catalogue versions carefully: their listed coverage dates are April 2017 and June 2012. Preserve edition name, amendment/download snapshot and approval metadata; do not relabel catalogue timestamps as observed historical land use.

3. **Inference:** join HDB Property Information to Existing Building polygons by verified block+street/code or postal/address matches. Use OneMap at build time for unresolved addresses, retain the returned evidence, and package static results. A block number alone is not unique. Current stock sorted by completion year is not the complete historical building stock.

4. For each new shoreline/farm trace, retain the actual scan, file rights statement, publication and survey dates, control points, transform, residuals, tracing method and uncertainty. A1966 publication based on1961 surveys is not automatically a1966 coast. Check already-reclaimed, under-construction and proposed areas separately.

5. Use Hesp/Wong and Glaser as scholarly checks with their documented access limits. NLB’s reclamation overview includes possible future land to 2030; those areas must not appear as completed land. Do not turn aggregate land-area increases into invented offshore polygons.

6. The1986 farm annexes and 1975 NUS georeferenced topographic mosaic improve historical coverage, but their reuse rights remain to be resolved. Never shrink arbitrary farm shapes to match total hectares. For parks, present managed-area boundaries are neither historical vegetation nor water-catchment boundaries.

### Remaining evidence gaps

- **july-2024-syllabus:** The current official MOE syllabus page links a PDF whose cover says January 2024. The named July 2024 URL is indexed but returned404 in direct download and a bot challenge in web access. July-specific wording and differences remain unverified.

- **syllabus-verbatim-limit:** Full verbatim outcome lists are not reproduced because quotations from one copyrighted source are limited. The inquiry question and two short syllabus extracts are verbatim; remaining outcomes/content are explicitly paraphrased with exact printed-page and PDF-page locators. The downloaded official PDF is retained in sources/moe-jan2024.pdf.

- **observed-historical-polygons:** No complete licence-cleared observed land-use polygon series for 1950s–1980s was verified. Master Plans are planning snapshots; archive access and academic publication do not establish open rights.

- **reclamation-decades:** Published maps support selected dates/areas, not a complete verified decade-by-decade reclamation dataset. Glaser1991 full map pages were not accessible. Wong1992 is identified via Hesp1995; original chapter not inspected.

- **farm-boundaries:** No open whole-island1960s kampong-farm boundary dataset was verified. The1986 government annexes locate agricultural land and proposed estate uses; their map reuse licence remains unverified.

- **farm-statistics:** Historical farm area figures use rounded decades and different definitions. The2000 FAO value cites unrecovered AVA2004 statistics. Current food-farm licence counts exclude some non-food farming; no fully harmonised annual farm-area series was verified.

- **reservoir-inventory:** Reservoir milestones here are selected verified examples, not a complete17-reservoir commissioning register. Western reservoir dates and earlier extension footprints need further primary-source checks. Current polygons must not be back-projected unchanged.

- **mrt-segment-register:** First services and selected opening stages are verified; a complete station-to-station opening register including every extension to 2026 is not assembled. A first line-opening date cannot activate all later segments.

- **town-completion:** A whole new town does not have one defensible completion year. Use documented planning, first blocks, keys and project-completion events; current HDB stock also omits demolished buildings.
