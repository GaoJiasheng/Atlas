# Movement routes

New sea lines are source-derived schematic sailing corridors, not individual vessel tracks. Recurring-corridor dates are editorial display windows. Company expedition dates come from the landing chronology. Offshore endpoints deliberately avoid implying a modern reclaimed waterfront. No strength is supplied for new corridors.
