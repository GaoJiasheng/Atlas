# Procedural dinosaur skeleton studies

Asset URL: `/models/fossils-and-skeletons.glb`.

Original procedural geometry generated by Atlas from
`scripts/geo/kits/dinosaur-skeleton.ts` and
`scripts/geo/fossils-and-skeletons/bake-museum.ts`.

This generated asset is dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
No third-party scans, meshes, photographs or textures are included.
The same provenance and licence are embedded in `asset.copyright`.

Generic anatomical studies, not scans or inventories of Prince, Apollonia
or Twinky. All three selectable nodes share one geometry resource. The flat
asset URL follows Atlas's existing `/models/<name>.glb` schema.

Polish 2026-11-03: this CC0 asset also includes the twelve independently
selectable theropod meshes. The three diplodocids continue sharing one mesh;
there are 13 resources and 15 named selectable nodes in total. The curved
cortical surfaces are generated by the original `sculpt-museum.ts` code.
No third-party geometry, imagery or textures were added.

Round 3, 2026-11-03: the shared sauropod resource is rebuilt with original
`sauropod-study.ts` and `sculpt-museum.ts` geometry by `build-sauropods.ts`.
`swap-museum-mesh.ts` copies all retained theropod buffers without re-encoding.
The CC0 dedication above also covers these original geometry changes.
Reference publications and the no-redistribution record are listed in the
fossils-and-skeletons `data/SOURCES.md`.
