# public/geo

Basemap data for the TimeScene GeoStage. Generated by `pnpm tsx scripts/build-geo.ts`; do not edit by hand.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `land-50m.json` | GeoJSON FeatureCollection of land polygons (1:50m, ~110 m precision) |
| `countries-50m.json` | GeoJSON FeatureCollection of modern country polygons, `properties: { name, lx, ly, rank }` (label point, rank 1 = largest area); simplified to ~2 km |
| `borders-50m.json` | **TopoJSON** mesh of the modern country frontiers: one object `borders` (a MultiLineString) of the arcs two countries share (`mesh(countries, (a, b) => a !== b)`: inland borders only, no coastline), Douglas–Peucker ~2 km, quantised to 0.01°, ~69 KB. The `borders` layer draws this instead of outlining the country polygons; fetched the first time the layer is shown. |
| `land-10m-sea.json` | **TopoJSON**, Southeast Asia only (bbox 95°E–125°E, 9°S–22°N): Natural Earth 1:10m land clipped to the box, Douglas–Peucker ~90 m, islands under ~0.5 km² dropped, ~234 KB (budget 300 KB). One object `sea` with `kind: 'frame'` (the box), `'land'` and `'coast'` (coastline without the straight clip edges; shares its arcs with `land`). The TimeScene GeoStage fetches it only when the camera is at zoom ≥ 6.5 over the box and draws it from zoom 7, fully at 8: the frame in the water colour hides the 1:50m land, the 10m land and coast sit on top with the same tokens. For close-ups of Singapore and Johor (zoom 9–11). |

Rings that cross the antimeridian are made longitude-continuous at build time (`unwrapRing` in `src/engines/time-scene/lib/geo.ts`): longitudes may run past ±180° (MapLibre draws the overhang in the neighbouring world copy), and Antarctica is closed along the south pole. Without this, a ring jumping from 180 to -180 draws a line across the whole map.

## Source and license

[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) 1:50m Physical (land) and Cultural (Admin 0 countries) vectors, and the 1:10m Physical land vector (for `land-10m-sea.json`), version 4.1.0, via the [`world-atlas`](https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas) 2.0.2 TopoJSON redistribution (`land-10m.json`).

Natural Earth data is in the **public domain** (see https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). No attribution is required; we credit it here anyway.

Country names and boundaries are modern (de facto, as drawn by Natural Earth) and are a reference layer only; historical control areas come from each topic's `data/control.json`.

## `land-sg.json` (island-scale basemap, topic `four-taps`)

Not from Natural Earth. Built by `pnpm tsx scripts/geo/lib/local-land.ts --topic four-taps` (recipe `pipeline.localLand` in `scripts/geo/four-taps/sources.json`, provenance in `scripts/geo/four-taps/SOURCES-GEO.md`): the URA Master Plan 2019 Planning Area Boundary (No Sea) dissolved into one land, with the URA Master Plan 2019 Land Use `WATERBODY` polygons of rivers, canals, bays and estuary reservoirs cut out; a `mask` geometry hides the Natural Earth 1:10m Singapore polygon under it. Contains information from Master Plan 2019 Planning Area Boundary (No Sea) and Master Plan 2019 Land Use layer accessed on 14 October 2026 from data.gov.sg (Urban Redevelopment Authority), made available under the terms of the [Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0](https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence). Which water bodies stay land is chosen with OpenStreetMap reservoir outlines (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0). Drawn only for topics with `basemap: { local: land-sg }` (docs/06).
