<Lang en>

The fifth expedition, dated 1417–1419, carried envoys home across the western Indian Ocean. Accounts name <FlyTo preset="view-arabia">Aden</FlyTo>, Mogadishu and Malindi among its destinations. Different groups of ships could visit different ports, so these names do not prove that Zheng He personally landed at every one. [S1]

<FlyTo preset="view-africa">Mogadishu and Malindi</FlyTo> were East African ports with their own inhabitants, rulers and trading interests. Visiting ships entered a world of existing relationships. Each port had its own history, just as ports in China and India did. The coast was not one country, and its inhabitants made decisions about trading with visitors from overseas. [S1, S26]

Farther south, Kilwa belonged to <Term id="term-swahili">Swahili coastal culture</Term>. Its merchants traded across networks linking Africa, Arabia, India and China. They bought and sold Chinese porcelain and other goods. Their port also connected products from the African interior with overseas markets. Kilwa's trade helps us understand this wider world, although a Ming fleet visit there is unconfirmed. [S6, S26]

Chinese porcelain found in an African trading city shows that goods crossed the ocean. It does not identify which ships or merchants carried them. The people who acquired, used and valued these objects also shaped their history. [S1, S6, S17]

<Lang zh>

1417—1419 年的第五次远航运送使节返乡，驶向印度洋西部。记载列出的目的地包括<FlyTo preset="view-arabia">亚丁</FlyTo>、摩加迪沙与马林迪。船队可以分组前往不同港口，因此这些名称不能证明郑和本人曾在每处上岸。[S1]

<FlyTo preset="view-africa">摩加迪沙与马林迪</FlyTo>都有居民、统治者和自己的贸易利益。外来船只进入的是早已存在的往来网络。就像中国和印度的港口一样，东非各港也有各自的历史，不能把整片海岸当成同一个国家。[S1, S26]

更南边的基尔瓦属于<Term id="term-swahili">斯瓦希里沿海文化</Term>。当地商人通过连接非洲、阿拉伯、印度和中国的网络，买卖中国瓷器等商品。港口也把非洲腹地的物产送往海外市场。这些贸易帮助我们了解当时的印度洋世界，但明朝船队是否到访基尔瓦仍未确定。[S6, S26]

非洲贸易城市里的中国瓷器，说明商品曾经跨海而来，却不能告诉我们是哪艘船、哪位商人运送的。取得、使用和珍视这些器物的当地人，也参与塑造了它们的历史。瓷器不只反映远方的生产，也留下港口居民生活的线索。商人的选择与居民的使用，让跨海运来的物品成为当地历史的一部分。[S1, S6, S17]
