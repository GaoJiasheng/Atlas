<Lang en>

The fifth expedition, dated 1417–1419, carried envoys home and reached destinations across the western Indian Ocean. Accounts associate <FlyTo preset="view-arabia">Aden</FlyTo> with the expedition and name Mogadishu and Malindi on the East African coast. These are expedition destinations. They do not establish that Zheng He personally stepped ashore at every place named in an account. [S1]

On the map, <FlyTo preset="view-africa">Mogadishu and Malindi</FlyTo> deserve the same attention as ports in China or India. They were places with inhabitants, rulers and trading interests, rather than scenery at the edge of someone else's voyage. Ships arriving from abroad entered relationships that already existed. Calling the whole coast a single destination would hide the distinctions between its ports. [S1, S26]

Farther south, Kilwa helps explain <Term id="term-swahili">Swahili coastal culture</Term> and its trading world. Local merchants exchanged goods across the Indian Ocean, including Chinese porcelain, within networks linking Africa, Arabia, India and China. Their commerce connected coastal communities with goods from the African interior as well as overseas. Kilwa appears here as historical context; this topic does not claim a confirmed fleet landing there. [S6, S26]

Objects reveal connections, but each kind of evidence has limits. Chinese porcelain in an African trading city supports a history of exchange. It does not identify every ship or merchant who carried it, and it cannot by itself prove an admiral's presence. The illustration gathers categories of goods for comparison; it is not an excavated cargo from a named vessel. [S1, S6, S17]

1417—1419 年的第五航次运送使节返乡，目的地延伸到印度洋西部。记载涉及<FlyTo preset="view-arabia">亚丁</FlyTo>，也列出东非沿岸的摩加迪沙与马林迪。这些名称属于远航的目的地记录，不能据此断言郑和本人曾在每个地方上岸。[S1]

把镜头移到<FlyTo preset="view-africa">摩加迪沙与马林迪</FlyTo>，也应像看中国、印度港口那样认真。这里有居民、统治者和自己的贸易利益，并非外来船队故事的背景。到港船只进入的是既有关系，沿海城市之间也各有区别，不能把整片东非海岸当成同一个国家。[S1, S26]

更南边的基尔瓦，让人看见<Term id="term-swahili">斯瓦希里沿海文化</Term>的贸易世界。当地商人经营跨海交换，商品包括中国瓷器；港口也把非洲腹地的物产与海外市场联系起来。这些网络连接非洲、阿拉伯、印度和中国。地图保留基尔瓦作为背景，不把它列成已确认的船队停靠点。[S6, S26]

器物能证明交流，却未必能指明运送者。贸易城市里的中国瓷器，不能单独证明某艘明朝船来过，更不能作为郑和登陆的凭据。本章插画把商品类别放在一起方便观察，不表示它们曾同在一个货舱，也不是从某处遗址共同出土的一组文物。[S1, S6, S17]
