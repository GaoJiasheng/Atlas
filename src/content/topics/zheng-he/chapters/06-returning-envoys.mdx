<Lang en>

The sixth expedition, dated 1421–1422, carried envoys back across the Indian Ocean. Their return journeys remind us that the fleets connected courts and ports in different directions. An outward voyage from China was also part of someone else's journey home. Diplomatic exchange did not mean that every ruler understood the relationship alike or that every participant had chosen it freely. [S1, S4, S2]

At staging ports such as <FlyTo preset="view-straits">Melaka</FlyTo>, journeys involved assembling, replenishing supplies and waiting. Historian Wan Ming connects the fleets' use of Melaka with seasonal winds and return schedules. Her explanation addresses historical patterns of travel. It does not give a daily sailing log or establish the same stop on every expedition. Waiting could be part of organising a journey rather than a failure to move. [S12]

A <Term id="term-monsoon">monsoon</Term> is a large seasonal change in wind circulation. Modern meteorology helps explain why sailing schedules depended on the season. It cannot tell us the wind beside a particular ship in 1422. Today's Singapore weather records also describe a local climate; their wind directions cannot be stretched into an unchanging arrow across the entire Indian Ocean. [S16, S15]

The court had to decide how much attention and resources to devote to these expeditions. Columbia's account emphasises imperial prestige, expense and competing priorities; Wade gives greater weight to domination over ports and trade. After the 1421–1422 expedition, the sequence pauses before the final sailing period of 1431–1433. The gap concerns this state fleet programme, while merchant networks followed their own changing histories. [S1, S2, S29, S28]

<More title={{ en: "A historical explanation: waiting at Melaka", zh: "历史解释：在马六甲候风" }}>

Wan Ming uses Melaka's position and seasonal sailing patterns to explain its role as a staging place. This is an argument about the organisation of voyages. It supports discussing waiting and return connections without inventing an exact departure day, a daily speed or an identical itinerary for every fleet. [S12]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Modern mechanism: winds change by season", zh: "现代机制：风随季节变化" }}>

Meteorological Service Singapore explains monsoon circulation and describes Singapore's modern seasons. These sources answer a physical question about seasonal winds. They are not measurements from the Ming expeditions. Local wind directions also vary within a named monsoon season, so the season's name is not a complete set of sailing instructions. [S16, S15]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Several possible futures", zh: "并非只有一种结局" }}>

Later European colonial expansion does not prove that each earlier ocean expedition was bound to become a worldwide empire. Historical explanations need evidence about the choices and constraints of their own period. Comparing interpretations should clarify what each explains, while keeping coercion and diplomatic relationships visible together. [S18, S2, S29]

</More>

<Lang zh>

1421—1422 年的第六航次，载着使节返回印度洋各地。从中国看是出海，从一些使节看却是回乡。海上的关系并非只朝一个方向展开。外交往来也不表示各地统治者对关系有同样理解，更不能推定每个参与者都是自愿的。[S1, S4, S2]

经过<FlyTo preset="view-straits">马六甲</FlyTo>这样的停泊港，船队要集合、补给，也要等待。史家万明把马六甲的仓储和候风作用，与往返航程联系起来。这是对航海规律的历史解释，并非逐日航海日志；不能据此为每个航次填上同样的停靠和出发日期。[S12]

<Term id="term-monsoon">季风</Term>是大范围随季节变化的风循环，能帮助理解船队为何需要安排候风。现代气象知识可以说明机制，却不能告诉人们 1422 年某艘船旁边吹什么风。新加坡今天的气候资料也只描述当地，不能直接变成覆盖整个印度洋、全年方向不变的箭头。[S16, S15]

朝廷还须考虑远航的资源投入与优先事务。哥伦比亚大学资料强调皇帝威望、开支和其他事务，韦德则更重视港口支配。第六航次之后，到 1431—1433 年最后一次远航之前出现间隔。这是国家船队序列的停顿，不能写成商人和沿海居民都停止海上活动。[S1, S2, S29, S28]

<More title={{ en: "A historical explanation: waiting at Melaka", zh: "历史解释：在马六甲候风" }}>

万明结合马六甲的位置与季节性航海规律，解释它作为集合和仓储据点的作用。这支持讨论候风与回程的关系，却不能替每次远航补出确切离港日、日行速度或完全相同的行程。[S12]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Modern mechanism: winds change by season", zh: "现代机制：风随季节变化" }}>

新加坡气象署说明季风循环，也介绍现代新加坡的季节。这些资料回答风为何季节性变化，不是明代船队的观测。即使同属一个季风季节，局地风向仍会变化；季风名称本身并不是完整的航海指令。[S16, S15]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Several possible futures", zh: "并非只有一种结局" }}>

不能用后来欧洲殖民扩张的结果，倒推更早的每次远航都必然走向全球帝国。解释历史，需要考察当时的选择与限制。比较不同史家观点时，既要看外交关系，也要保留强制行为的证据。[S18, S2, S29]

</More>
