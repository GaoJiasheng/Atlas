<Lang en>

The first expedition is usually dated 1405–1407. It reached ports on India's southwest coast before returning through the straits. <FlyTo preset="view-india">Calicut and Kochi</FlyTo> were part of this trading world. Local rulers and armed groups competed for influence over its ports. Overseas trade, diplomatic relationships and military power all shaped what happened when a fleet arrived. [S1]

In 1407 the Ming fleet fought Chen Zuyi's force at <Term id="term-old-port">Old Port</Term>, now associated with Palembang. Ming records reported about <Num s="S2">5,000</Num> people killed. Chen was captured and taken to China for punishment. This death figure comes from the attacking side; there is no independent count to confirm it. [S1, S2]

That year, Ming records also reported about <Num s="S2">170</Num> Ming troops killed in a local conflict in Java. The number of local people killed is unknown. The fighting involved particular groups, rather than all Javanese people. Neither event has a complete count of deaths on each side. [S2]

The second expedition is usually dated 1407–1409. The fleets continued their trade and diplomatic work after the killings. They also carried armed forces and used them to intervene in local conflicts. Columbia's account says Zheng He organised the second expedition but did not lead it in person. Commanding a fleet also does not show that he landed at every destination. [S1, S4]

<Lang zh>

第一次远航通常记作 1405—1407 年。船队抵达印度西南岸港口，再经过海峡回程。<FlyTo preset="view-india">古里与柯枝</FlyTo>都属于这个贸易世界。当地统治者与武装势力竞争，希望扩大对港口的影响。[S1]

1407 年，明朝船队在<Term id="term-old-port">旧港</Term>与陈祖义的势力交战。旧港对应今天的巨港。明方记录报告约 <Num s="S2">5,000</Num> 人被杀，陈祖义被俘送往中国接受惩处。死亡数字来自进攻的一方，没有独立统计可以核实。[S1, S2]

同年，明方记录还报告约 <Num s="S2">170</Num> 名明军在爪哇一场当地冲突中被杀。当地人的死亡人数不详。参与冲突的是当地特定势力，不能把责任归给所有爪哇人。两处事件都缺少各方完整的死亡统计。人数不详，不表示没有人死亡，也不能替缺失的记录填上零。[S2]

第二次远航通常记作 1407—1409 年。杀戮发生后，船队继续贸易和外交往来，也继续带着武装力量干预当地冲突。哥伦比亚大学资料说，郑和组织了第二次远航，但没有亲自率队；统领船队也不等于他在每个目的地登陆。船队的往来把港口联系起来，也卷入各地的权力争夺。对港口居民而言，来访者既可能带来交易机会，也可能带来武力干预。[S1, S4]
