<Lang en>

In 1405 the Yongle emperor sent a fleet into the Indian Ocean. He wanted to show Ming power and build relations with other rulers. An <Term id="term-envoy">envoy</Term> spoke for a ruler. <Term id="term-tribute">Tribute</Term> meant bringing gifts and formally recognising the emperor's authority. Port rulers could seek trade and other benefits while understanding this relationship differently. The fleet combined trade, diplomacy and military power. [S1, S4, S29]

Zheng He was originally named Ma He. He came from a Muslim family in Yunnan. Ming forces captured him as a child, castrated him, removing his testicles, and forced him into court service. He later gained power within the court and commanded its expeditions. [S1, S2]

The fleet needed sailors, soldiers, medical staff, repair workers and diplomatic specialists. An <Term id="term-interpreter">interpreter</Term> helped people who spoke different languages communicate. Navigators used compasses, watched the sky and read sailing instructions. A compass showed direction, but could not tell them their exact position. <FlyTo preset="view-china">Look toward the Chinese coast</FlyTo>, where the court organised these journeys. [S1]

Accounts describe <Num s="S1,S4">hundreds</Num> of vessels carrying <Num s="S1,S4">tens of thousands</Num> of people. Their totals differ, and historians still debate the size of the largest vessels, called <Term id="term-treasure-ship">treasure ships</Term>. The cover is an illustration of a Chinese sailing vessel. Its shape and size are not an exact reconstruction of a particular ship. [S1, S4, S10, S11]

Columbia's account gives <Num s="S1">317</Num> ships for the first expedition. The Mariners' Museum gives about <Num s="S4">208</Num>. We do not know whether they count the same kinds of ships, so taking an average would be misleading. [S1, S4]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "How large was a treasure ship?", zh: "宝船有多大" }}>

Late historical dimensions are often converted to about <Num s="S10">137</Num> metres long and <Num s="S10">56</Num> metres wide. Sally Church examines engineering objections and proposals for lengths nearer <Num s="S10">61–76</Num> metres. Wakeman reports arguments for large vessels based on written capacity references and an excavated rudder post. [S10, S11]

A rudder post gives clues about a ship, but it is not a complete hull. Later descriptions also leave questions about size. Historians have not agreed on a single length. [S10, S11]

</More>

<Lang zh>

1405 年，永乐皇帝派船队出海，希望展示明朝的权力，与各地统治者建立关系。代表统治者出使的人称为<Term id="term-envoy">使节</Term>。<Term id="term-tribute">朝贡</Term>包括进献礼物，并正式承认皇帝的权威。港口统治者也希望争取贸易和其他利益，对双方关系未必与明廷有相同理解。船队同时承担贸易、外交与军事任务。[S1, S4, S29]

郑和原名马和，来自云南穆斯林家庭。他童年被明军俘获，遭到阉割，也就是被切除睾丸，随后被迫进入宫廷服役。后来，他在朝廷取得权力，统领庞大的远航船队。[S1, S2]

船队需要水手、士兵、医护、修船人员和外交人员。<Term id="term-interpreter">通事</Term>帮助使用不同语言的人沟通。航海者使用罗盘、观察星空，也参考文字航海资料。罗盘能指出方向，却不能告诉他们船的准确位置。<FlyTo preset="view-china">看中国沿岸</FlyTo>，朝廷在这里组织远航。统帅的命令要靠许多人的日常工作才能完成：修船、照顾病人、沟通语言，都与航行有关。[S1]

远航规模达到<Num s="S1,S4">数百</Num>艘船、<Num s="S1,S4">数万</Num>人，但各来源的总数不一致。史家对大型船只——<Term id="term-treasure-ship">宝船</Term>——的尺寸也有争论。封面画的是中国帆船示意图，外形和大小并非某艘船的精确复原。[S1, S4, S10, S11]

<More title={{ en: "Why fleet totals differ", zh: "船队到底有多少艘" }}>
