<Lang en>

The command module brings the crew back through Earth's atmosphere. Its compact cone contains <Num s="S21,S25">three</Num> couches. After the landing crew rejoins the orbiting craft, the astronauts and their lunar samples travel home in this cabin. The model leaves out people so you can see the seats.

The service module below supplies propulsion and support during the journey. Its main engine and stored supplies help the crew cabin make the return trip. In the cutaway, look for the crew space above and the supporting equipment below.

Before atmospheric entry, the service module separates, taking its large engine nozzle with it. The returning craft also leaves behind the lunar module and rocket stages. Only the command module enters Earth's atmosphere.

The broad, blunt base is the <Term id="term-heatshield">heat shield</Term>. It faces the airflow and protects the cabin from severe heating. Cutaway keeps this continuous surface visible while opening the cabin shell.

Compare the return view with the launch view. The escape tower and docking probe are gone. The cabin carries different attached equipment at different stages of the mission.

下方的服务舱在旅途中提供推进和支持。主发动机和储存物资帮助乘员舱返航。剖面图上方是乘员区，下方是支持设备。

进入大气层前，服务舱分离，大型发动机喷管也随之离开。登月舱和火箭各级也不随指令舱返回，只有指令舱进入地球大气层。
