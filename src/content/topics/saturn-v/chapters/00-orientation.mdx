<Lang en>

The assembled stack includes the Saturn V <Term id="term-launch-vehicle">launch vehicle</Term> and the Apollo spacecraft above it. The launch vehicle lifts and accelerates the spacecraft. Together they stood about <Num s="S24">111 m</Num> tall. The narrow top holds the crew cabin and escape system.

Saturn V had <Num s="S24">three</Num> main propulsion sections, called <Term id="term-stage">stages</Term>. Read upward from the broad lower body to the narrower upper body. The V in the name does not count the stages. Each stage separated after finishing its work, so the whole stack did not travel to the lunar surface.

The spacecraft sections have different jobs. The <Term id="term-cm">command module</Term> carries the crew. The <Term id="term-sm">service module</Term> supplies propulsion and support. At launch, the <Term id="term-lm">lunar module</Term> sits inside the tapered adapter below the service module. Cutaway opens the shell so you can see it.

Only the cone-shaped command module returns through Earth's atmosphere. Along the way, an engine can stop firing while the craft keeps moving. Gravity still acts in space, including on an orbiting spacecraft.

Click the beats to follow the changing arrangement of the parts. The model follows Apollo 11's main outline. Internal equipment, wall thicknesses, reinforcing rings and display gaps are simplified. The gaps help us inspect the craft; they do not show journey distances.

土星五号有 <Num s="S24">三个</Num>主推进段，叫作<Term id="term-stage">级</Term>。从下往上看，宽阔的下段接着较细的上段。名称中的“五”不是级数。各级完成工作后先后分离，整枚火箭不会一起到达月面。

飞船各舱分工不同。<Term id="term-cm">指令舱</Term>搭载乘员，<Term id="term-sm">服务舱</Term>提供推进和支持设备。发射时，<Term id="term-lm">登月舱</Term>收在服务舱下方渐缩的适配器里。打开剖视，就能看见外壳内的登月舱。

最终只有锥形指令舱穿过地球大气层返回。旅途中，发动机关闭后，飞船仍能继续运动。太空中仍有引力，绕轨道飞行的飞船也受它作用。
