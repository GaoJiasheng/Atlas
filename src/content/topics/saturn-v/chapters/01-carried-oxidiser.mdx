<Lang en>

Look inside the first stage at its separate tanks. One holds kerosene <Term id="term-fuel">fuel</Term>. The other holds liquid oxygen, the <Term id="term-oxidiser">oxidiser</Term> that reacts with the fuel. Together they form the <Term id="term-propellant">propellant</Term>. In this pair, kerosene is the fuel and oxygen supports its burning.

Below the tanks sit <Num s="S25">five</Num> F-1 engines: <Num s="S25">one</Num> in the centre and <Num s="S25">four</Num> around it. Follow the lines from both tanks to the engines. The drawing leaves out much of the real pumps, valves and pipework.

The reaction releases energy and heats the gas. The gas expands through a shaped outlet, the <Term id="term-nozzle">nozzle</Term>. As it leaves in one direction, the rocket gains momentum in the other. The force on the rocket is <Term id="term-thrust">thrust</Term>. The rocket carries its oxidiser, so it can work in a vacuum without pushing against outside air.

Compare the turbofan figure. Its intake draws in air, including oxygen for burning fuel. The rocket carries both fuel and oxidiser in tanks.

Use Try it to compare stage readings. The first and second stages each have <Num s="S25">five</Num> main engines; the third has <Num s="S25">one</Num>. Choosing a stage changes the reference readings while the model stays the same size. RUN shows the first-stage feeds moving slowly so you can follow their paths.

<Lang zh>

打开第一级外壳，可以看见两个分开的储箱。一箱装煤油<Term id="term-fuel">燃料</Term>，另一箱装液氧。液氧是与燃料反应的<Term id="term-oxidiser">氧化剂</Term>，两者合称<Term id="term-propellant">推进剂</Term>。这组物质中，煤油是燃料，氧支持燃烧。

储箱下方有 <Num s="S25">五台</Num> F-1 发动机：中央 <Num s="S25">一台</Num>，外围 <Num s="S25">四台</Num>。沿两条供料线，可以从储箱追到发动机。图中省略了许多真实的泵、阀门和管路。

反应释放能量，让气体变热。气体经过有特定形状的出口——<Term id="term-nozzle">喷管</Term>——膨胀排出。气体向一个方向离开，火箭获得相反方向的动量。作用在火箭上的力叫<Term id="term-thrust">推力</Term>。火箭自带氧化剂，无需推着外界空气，也能在真空中工作。

比较涡扇图：进气口吸入空气，也带入燃烧所需的氧气。火箭则把燃料和氧化剂都装在储箱里。
