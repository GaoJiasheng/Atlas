<Lang en>

Look through the first-stage shell at the separate tanks. One holds kerosene <Term id="term-fuel">fuel</Term>; the other holds liquid oxygen, the <Term id="term-oxidiser">oxidiser</Term> that supports the reaction. Fuel and oxidiser together form the <Term id="term-propellant">propellant</Term> carried for these engines. Oxygen is not the fuel in this pair.

Below the tanks are <Num s="S25">five</Num> F-1 engines: <Num s="S25">one</Num> in the centre and <Num s="S25">four</Num> around it. The repeated outside engine is a group of real engine positions, not a row of extra stages. The diagram follows feeds from both tanks toward the engines; it leaves out much of the actual plumbing and machinery.

Reaction energy heats the gas, which expands through a shaped outlet called a <Term id="term-nozzle">nozzle</Term>. Gas leaves in one direction and the vehicle gains momentum in the other. The force on the vehicle is <Term id="term-thrust">thrust</Term>. This does not require surrounding air as a surface to push against. Carried oxidiser lets a rocket engine work in a vacuum.

Compare the explanatory turbofan figure. Its intake draws in air, including the oxygen used by its combustion system. It is an air-breathing engine, not a drawing of an F-1. The rocket carries both reaction supplies instead.

The Try it card changes the stage reference: first and second stages each use <Num s="S25">five</Num> main engines, while the third uses <Num s="S25">one</Num>. It changes sourced readings, not the rocket's size or a calculated flight. Use RUN only to inspect the slow feed demonstration.

<Lang zh>

穿过第一级外壳，可以看到分开的储箱。一箱装煤油<Term id="term-fuel">燃料</Term>，另一箱装液氧；液氧是支持反应的<Term id="term-oxidiser">氧化剂</Term>。两者合称这套发动机的<Term id="term-propellant">推进剂</Term>。这里氧气不是燃料，也不是从周围空气临时吸进来的。

储箱下方有 <Num s="S25">五台</Num> F-1 发动机，中央 <Num s="S25">一台</Num>，外围 <Num s="S25">四台</Num>。外围重复排列的是发动机，不是多出来的火箭级。沿进料线可追到发动机入口；真实的泵、阀门和管路远比图中复杂，这里只保留容易辨认的关系。

反应释放能量，使气体变热并经过<Term id="term-nozzle">喷管</Term>膨胀排出。气体向后离开，火箭获得相反方向的动量；作用在火箭上的力叫<Term id="term-thrust">推力</Term>。观察储箱与喷管时，要同时区分能量从哪里释放，以及排出的物质来自哪里。这不需要空气当作可推动的墙，因此自带氧化剂的火箭也能在真空中工作。

再对照涡扇示意图：进气口吸入空气，燃烧所需氧气随空气进入。那是吸气式发动机，不是 F-1 剖面。试一试卡只切换各级的资料读数，不改变几何比例，也不计算真实飞行。开启运转时看到的是放慢的进料示范，不能把流点速度读成实际排气速度。
