<Lang en>

Read the stack from the bottom upward. A stage is a propulsion section that can separate from the vehicle. The first stage uses <Num s="S25">five</Num> F-1 engines; the second uses <Num s="S25">five</Num> J-2 engines; the third uses <Num s="S25">one</Num> J-2. These main engines do not all operate together.

The first stage burns kerosene with liquid oxygen. Both upper stages use liquid hydrogen with liquid oxygen. As a lower stage finishes its work, separating it removes structure that no longer needs to be accelerated. Watch the outer shell and its internal equipment disappear together. A shell leaving while its tanks stayed attached would tell the wrong mechanical story.

The third stage first helps establish a <Term id="term-parking-orbit">parking orbit</Term>, an orbit around Earth before departure. Later it restarts for <Term id="term-tli">translunar injection</Term>, the acceleration that sends Apollo toward the Moon. Restart means another operation of the same engine, not the appearance of another engine. The ring-shaped <Term id="term-iu">instrument unit</Term> provides guidance and control; it is not an extra main propulsion stage.

The ideal comparison below uses an imaginary small rocket. Keep payload and propellant unchanged and carry an additional <Num s="S90">100 kg</Num> of inert hardware. Its calculated speed change falls from <Num s="S90">3295.84 m/s</Num> to <Num s="S90">2748.87 m/s</Num>. This isolates the effect of carrying extra structure.

That comparison explains a reason for staging, but it is not a prediction of Saturn V's speed. The picture changes configuration, while the table answers a separate question about mass.

The card fixes payload at <Num s="S90">200 kg</Num> and propellant at <Num s="S90">600 kg</Num>. Only the extra <Num s="S90">100 kg</Num> of inert structure changes. These are chosen teaching inputs, not masses of Saturn stages.

</More>

下级使用煤油与液氧，上面两级使用液氢与液氧。下级完成工作后分离，剩下的飞行器便不用继续带着已无用的结构加速。观察时，外壳与内部设备一起退出画面；若只让壳离开而把储箱留住，就会误解真正的连接关系。

第三级先帮助进入环绕地球的<Term id="term-parking-orbit">停泊轨道</Term>，后来再次启动，完成<Term id="term-tli">地月转移入轨</Term>，把阿波罗送向月球。重启是同一台发动机再次工作，不是多生出一台。环状<Term id="term-iu">仪器舱</Term>负责制导与控制，也不算主推进级。
