The rocket picture now accompanies a separate experiment. Its <Term id="term-delta-v">ideal speed change</Term> is calculated for an imaginary small rocket. It is not Saturn V's measured speed, a launch prediction or the speed at a point in the illustrated mission. The SIM label applies even when the picture shows familiar hardware.

The model fixes effective exhaust speed at <Num s="S90">3000 m/s</Num> and dry hardware at <Num s="S90">100 kg</Num>. Payload is useful carried mass; propellant is the mass available to expel. Final mass includes the hardware and payload that remain. Initial mass adds the propellant. Optional inert hardware stays aboard throughout this ideal comparison.

At the default payload of <Num s="S90">200 kg</Num> and propellant of <Num s="S90">600 kg</Num>, with no extra inert hardware, initial mass is <Num s="S90">900 kg</Num> and final mass is <Num s="S90">300 kg</Num>. The calculated change is <Num s="S90">3295.84 m/s</Num>. Increase payload while keeping propellant fixed: more mass remains, and the calculated change falls.

Predict before revealing the new reading, then record a row. Change only one input for a clear comparison. More propellant at the same payload raises the ideal result here; carrying extra unused structure lowers it. Neither observation means a real rocket can carry unlimited propellant or ignore the structure needed to hold it.

This table ignores air resistance, gravity losses and changing exhaust conditions. It does not integrate an orbit or calculate landing safety. Hardware explains part of a mission; its history also includes other programmes, crews, risks and costs. A technical comparison alone cannot decide that wider history.

<More title={{"en": "The calculation behind the table", "zh": "表格背后的算式"}}>

NASA’s ideal rocket equation gives <Num s="S47">Δv = ve × ln(m0/mf)</Num>: effective exhaust speed times the natural logarithm of initial mass divided by final mass. It sets aside weight and drag. This topic chooses its inputs separately in the design study. For the default case the mass ratio is <Num s="S90">3</Num>, giving <Num s="S90">3000 × ln(3) = 3295.84 m/s</Num>.

</More>

模型固定有效排气速度为 <Num s="S90">3000 米/秒</Num>，干结构为 <Num s="S90">100 千克</Num>。载荷是有用的携带物，推进剂是可供排出的质量。最终质量包括留下的结构和载荷，初始质量还要加上推进剂；额外惰性结构在这项对照中始终带着。
