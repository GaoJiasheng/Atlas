<Lang en>

The lunar module has a lower descent stage and an upper ascent stage. On landing, <Num s="S26">four</Num> legs support the lower stage. A ladder runs down <Num s="S26">one</Num> front leg. The angular cabin works in space and near the Moon, where it does not need an aircraft's streamlined shape.

The descent engine below the lower stage slows the craft for landing. The Moon has only an extremely thin layer of gas, called an exosphere. It cannot provide the air resistance a parachute needs.

On Apollo 11, <Num s="S21">two</Num> astronauts, Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin, descended in the lunar module. Michael Collins stayed in lunar orbit. The command module appears beside the lander here to show both crew roles. This display gap does not represent the distance from the surface to orbit.

When the crew leaves the Moon, the upper cabin lifts off using its own ascent engine. The descent stage, legs and ladder stay on the surface. The docking port travels with the upper cabin.

The ascent craft then meets and docks with the command and service modules. Follow these connections through the beats to see which parts leave and which remain.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "The Moon and parachutes", "zh": "月球与降落伞"}}>

A parachute slows a falling craft by pushing through surrounding gas. The lunar exosphere is far too thin for the braking used on Earth, so the lander uses engine thrust to slow down.

</More>

<Lang zh>

登月舱下部是下降级，上部是上升级。着陆时，<Num s="S26">四支</Num>腿支撑下部，前方 <Num s="S26">一支</Num>腿带着梯子。上方乘员舱有棱有角，在太空和月球附近工作，不需要飞机那样的流线外形。

下降级下方的发动机让飞行器减速着陆。月球只有极稀薄的一层气体，叫外逸层，无法提供降落伞所需的空气阻力。

阿波罗 11 号的阿姆斯特朗和奥尔德林共 <Num s="S21">两人</Num>乘登月舱下降，科林斯留在月球轨道。画面把指令舱放在旁边，方便同时了解两组乘员的任务；这个间距不代表月面到轨道的距离。

离开月球时，上部乘员舱靠自己的上升发动机升空。下降级、支腿和梯子留在月面，顶部对接口则随上部一起离开。

随后，上升级与指令舱、服务舱交会对接。沿节拍追踪连接，就能看清哪些部分离开，哪些留下。

<More title={{"en": "The Moon and parachutes", "zh": "月球与降落伞"}}>

降落伞通过周围气体时受到阻力，让飞行器减速。月球外逸层太稀薄，无法像地球大气那样帮助制动，因此登月舱用发动机推力减速。
