<Lang en>

At launch, the lunar module sits below the service module, inside the spacecraft adapter. Its legs fold to fit. The tapered panels protect and carry it during launch. Open the shell to see the lander inside.

After the third stage sends Apollo towards the Moon, the command and service modules move away together. They stay connected as they turn. The command module's nose now faces the lunar module, ready to dock.

The nose probe meets the docking port on top of the lunar module. Once connected, the spacecraft pulls the lander out of the adapter. The lander moves from its enclosed launch position to a new connection at the command-module nose.

Click through the beats in order, then go back to compare the arrangements. The command and service modules move as a whole, carrying their tanks and engine as they turn. Wider display gaps help us see the connections. They are not measured separation distances.

Later, the Apollo cutaway shows the crew cabin and supporting equipment. Here, follow how the connecting parts change: the adapter carries the lander at launch, then the command-module nose docks to it for extraction.

The model places the probe and port together to show how the craft join. It does not calculate docking speed, contact forces or navigation. The attached parts move together.

</More>

<Lang zh>

发射时，登月舱收在服务舱下方的飞船适配器里，支腿折拢，才能放进内部。渐缩的面板保护和承载登月舱。打开外壳，就能看见里面的着陆飞行器。

第三级把阿波罗送向月球后，指令舱和服务舱一起离开。两舱保持连接，然后转身，让指令舱鼻端朝向登月舱，准备对接。

鼻端探杆接上登月舱顶部的接口。连接完成后，飞船把登月舱从适配器中抽出。登月舱从原来被包住的发射位置，移到指令舱鼻端的新连接位置。

依次点击节拍，再回看前一拍，比较各部分的位置。指令舱和服务舱作为整体移动，储箱和发动机也跟着转。画面把间距拉开，方便看清连接，不表示实测分离距离。

后面的阿波罗剖面图展示乘员舱和支持设备。这里先沿连接关系看：发射时，适配器携带登月舱；抽取时，指令舱鼻端接上登月舱。
