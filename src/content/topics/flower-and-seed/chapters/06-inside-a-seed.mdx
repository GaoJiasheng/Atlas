<Lang en>

The green bean, also called the mung bean, is a seed about <Num s="S71">4.3–5.1 mm</Num> long. On the stage it is drawn <Num s="S1">10 times</Num> life size: whole, split open and sprouting. <FlyTo preset="seed-view">Look at the seed</FlyTo>

Its hard outer coat protects the seed. Inside are two thick <Term id="seed-leaves">seed leaves</Term> and a tiny plant, the embryo, with a root tip and a shoot tip. In a bean the seed's food store is in the seed leaves. The seed is alive: respiration takes place in every living seed.

Germination begins when the seed takes in water. The seed swells, its coat splits, and in beans the root is the first part to come out. The root grows down and the shoot grows up, and a mung bean lifts its seed leaves and stem up out of the soil. Until then the young plant lives on its food store, which gives it the energy to form a root and a stem even without light.

How fast a seed germinates depends on the temperature. In a laboratory study in Victoria, Australia, mung beans of <Num s="S44">32</Num> kinds were kept on damp paper towels at three temperatures. At <Num s="S44">20 °C</Num>, half of the seeds that went on to germinate had done so by about day <Num s="S44">4.3</Num>; at <Num s="S44">17 °C</Num> it took about <Num s="S44">6.5</Num> days, and at <Num s="S44">14 °C</Num> about <Num s="S44">9.4</Num> days. At the coolest temperature fewer seeds germinated in the end, too: about <Num s="S44">61 %</Num> by day <Num s="S44">21</Num>, against about <Num s="S44">94 %</Num> at <Num s="S44">20 °C</Num>. The try-it card lets you step through the days at each temperature.

<More title={{ en: "A seed is alive", zh: "种子是活的" }}>

A dry bean in a packet looks like a store of food, and it is one; but it also holds a living embryo. Respiration takes place in all living seeds, even before they germinate.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Food for the first days", zh: "最初几天的养分" }}>

A germinating seed does not take food from the soil. It uses the food stored in its seed leaves. Once its own leaves are open, the plant makes its own food in the light: see <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="leaves-make-food">A Plant: leaves make food</TopicLink>.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What “half” means", zh: "“一半”是什么意思" }}>

The study's number is the median germination time: the day by which half of the seeds that finally germinated had germinated. It is not half of all the seeds. The study counted a seed as germinated once its root was at least <Num s="S44">3 mm</Num> long, tested only <Num s="S44">14</Num>, <Num s="S44">17</Num> and <Num s="S44">20 °C</Num>, and gave the seeds <Num s="S44">10</Num> hours of light a day. Mung beans are estimated to need a temperature above about <Num s="S44">9–10 °C</Num> to germinate.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "How long in Singapore", zh: "在新加坡要几天" }}>

Singapore is warmer than any of those three temperatures: from 2009 to 2018 its mean temperature was <Num s="S29">27.89 °C</Num>. Gardens by the Bay says mung bean sprouts can be grown in just <Num s="S42">3–4</Num> days, after soaking the beans for <Num s="S42">12</Num> hours and keeping them in a dark cupboard. An extension leaflet from North Carolina gives <Num s="S43">3 to 7</Num> days for full-sized sprouts, depending on the temperature. No source gives the day on which the root first appears, so this topic does not give one.

</More>

<Lang zh>

绿豆，英文叫 green bean 或 mung bean，是一粒长约 <Num s="S71">4.3–5.1 毫米</Num>的种子。舞台上的绿豆放大 <Num s="S1">10 倍</Num>：一粒整的、一粒剖开的、一粒正在发芽的。<FlyTo preset="seed-view">看看这粒种子</FlyTo>

硬硬的种皮保护着种子。里面是两片厚厚的<Term id="seed-leaves">子叶</Term>，还有一个带着根尖和芽尖的小小的胚。豆类种子的养分储存在子叶里。种子是活的：每一粒活的种子都在呼吸。

萌发从种子吸水开始。种子胀大，种皮裂开；豆类种子最先长出来的是根。根向下长，芽向上长，绿豆还会把子叶和茎一起举出土面。在这之前，幼小的植株靠储存的养分生活，没有光也能长出根和茎。

种子萌发的快慢和温度有关。澳大利亚维多利亚州的一项实验室研究，把 <Num s="S44">32</Num> 个品种的绿豆放在湿纸巾上，分别放在三个温度下。<Num s="S44">20 °C</Num> 时，大约到第 <Num s="S44">4.3</Num> 天，最终发芽的种子里有一半已经发芽；<Num s="S44">17 °C</Num> 时约 <Num s="S44">6.5</Num> 天，<Num s="S44">14 °C</Num> 时约 <Num s="S44">9.4</Num> 天。温度最低时，最后发芽的种子也少：到第 <Num s="S44">21</Num> 天约 <Num s="S44">61 %</Num>，而 <Num s="S44">20 °C</Num> 时约 <Num s="S44">94 %</Num>。在试一试卡片里，可以在每个温度下一天天往后看。

袋子里的干绿豆看起来是食物，它也确实储存着养分；但里面还有一个活的胚。所有活的种子都在呼吸，发芽之前也是这样。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Food for the first days", zh: "最初几天的养分" }}>

萌发中的种子不是从土里“吃”东西，它用的是子叶里储存的养分。等自己的叶子张开，植物就在光下自己制造食物，见<TopicLink slug="plant" ch="leaves-make-food">“一株植物”的“叶子造食物”</TopicLink>。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What “half” means", zh: "“一半”是什么意思" }}>

研究给出的是中位萌发时间：最终发芽的种子里有一半已经发芽的那一天。它不是全部种子的一半。这项研究在根长到 <Num s="S44">3 毫米</Num>以上时才算发芽，只测了 <Num s="S44">14</Num>、<Num s="S44">17</Num>、<Num s="S44">20 °C</Num> 三个温度，每天照光 <Num s="S44">10</Num> 小时。据估计，绿豆要在大约 <Num s="S44">9–10 °C</Num> 以上才会萌发。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "How long in Singapore", zh: "在新加坡要几天" }}>

新加坡比这三个温度都暖：2009 到 2018 年，新加坡的平均气温是 <Num s="S29">27.89 °C</Num>。滨海湾花园说，绿豆先泡 <Num s="S42">12</Num> 小时，再放进暗柜，只要 <Num s="S42">3–4</Num> 天就能长成豆芽。美国北卡罗来纳州的一份推广资料说，按温度不同，<Num s="S43">3 到 7</Num> 天长成豆芽。没有来源说根在第几天最先长出来，所以本主题不给这个天数。

</More>
