<Lang en>

Not every plant grows from a seed. Plants reproduce in two ways, by spores and by seeds, and the two plants on the small table use spores.

The bird's nest fern is a large <Term id="fern">fern</Term> that is commonly seen on many trees in Singapore. Its broad, strap-shaped fronds spread from the centre like a nest, and a frond can be up to <Num s="S23">150 cm</Num> long or more; the model is drawn at a quarter of life size. Ferns do not flower. They reproduce by spores on the undersides of their leaves. <FlyTo preset="spores-view">Look at the fern</FlyTo>

Turn a frond over and there are narrow brown lines on every vein of its top half. Each line is a group of tiny spore cases. When the spores are ripe they are released from the spore cases into the air.

A <Term id="moss">moss</Term> is a small plant, typically <Num s="S75">0.2–10 cm</Num> tall, that also has no flowers and no seeds. Its spores form in small capsules on thin stalks. A ring of tiny teeth round each capsule's mouth opens and closes with the moisture in the air, releasing spores from time to time. Mosses and their relatives grow mostly in damp places.

A seed is different. Inside its protective coat are a tiny plant, the embryo, and a store of food. A plant spore is usually a single cell, with no tiny plant inside.

<More title={{ en: "Spore cases", zh: "孢子囊" }}>

On the underside of a mature fern frond the spore cases sit in groups called sori. On the bird's nest fern the sori are narrow and run along every vein of the top half of the frond. NParks describes its spores as light brown.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Fungi make spores too", zh: "真菌也产生孢子" }}>

Mushrooms and moulds release spores as well. Fungal spores are smaller and lighter than plant seeds, and travel floating on the wind or hitching a ride on an animal. But fungi are not plants: the syllabus groups them separately, as you met in P3.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Not every plant has flowers", zh: "不是每种植物都开花" }}>

Plants can be sorted into flowering and non-flowering plants. Ferns and mosses are non-flowering plants. <TopicLink slug="living-things" ch="flowering-or-not">Classifying Living Things</TopicLink> sorts more of them.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Try it", zh: "试一试" }}>

With an adult, look for a bird's nest fern on a tree in a park or the school garden. Without picking it, look under one frond near the top for the brown lines. Look but do not pull: the fern is alive.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

不是每种植物都从种子长出来。植物有两种繁殖方式：靠孢子，或靠种子。小台上的两种植物都靠孢子。

巢蕨是一种大型的<Term id="fern">蕨类植物</Term>，在新加坡的许多树上都能见到。宽带状的叶片从中心向四周张开，像个鸟巢；一片叶可以长到 <Num s="S23">150 厘米</Num>或更长。模型缩小到真实大小的四分之一。蕨类植物不开花，靠叶背上的孢子繁殖。<FlyTo preset="spores-view">看看巢蕨</FlyTo>

把叶片翻过来，叶片上半部沿着每一条叶脉，都有一条窄窄的褐色线。每条线是一群细小的孢子囊。孢子成熟后，从孢子囊里放出来，散到空气中。

<Term id="moss">苔藓</Term>是矮小的植物，一般高 <Num s="S75">0.2–10 厘米</Num>，也不开花、不结种子。它的孢子在细柄顶端的小囊里形成。囊口有一圈细齿，随着空气的干湿张开、合上，不时放出孢子。苔藓这一类植物大多长在潮湿的地方。

种子不一样。种皮里面包着一个小小的胚和储存的养分。植物的孢子通常只是一个细胞，里面没有小小的胚。

<More title={{ en: "Spore cases", zh: "孢子囊" }}>

成熟的蕨叶背面，孢子囊聚成一群群，叫孢子囊群。巢蕨的孢子囊群窄窄的，沿着叶片上半部的每一条叶脉生长。NParks 说巢蕨的孢子是浅褐色的。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Fungi make spores too", zh: "真菌也产生孢子" }}>

蘑菇和霉也会放出孢子。真菌的孢子比植物的种子小而轻，随风飘走，或附在动物身上被带走。不过真菌不是植物：课纲把真菌另列一类，小三已经学过。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Not every plant has flowers", zh: "不是每种植物都开花" }}>

植物可以分成开花植物和不开花植物。蕨和苔藓都是不开花植物。<TopicLink slug="living-things" ch="flowering-or-not">“生物的分类”</TopicLink>里有更多例子。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Try it", zh: "试一试" }}>

和大人一起，在公园或学校园圃的树上找一株巢蕨。不要摘，只翻看靠近顶端的一片叶背，找找褐色的线。只看，不拔：巢蕨是活的。

</More>

</Lang>
