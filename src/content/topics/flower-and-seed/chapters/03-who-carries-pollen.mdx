<Lang en>

Bees are not the only animals that carry pollen. Animals visit flowers to feed on nectar or pollen, and pollen sticks to them. An animal that carries pollen from one flower to another is a <Term id="pollinator">pollinator</Term>. On this station, drawn life size, are three that live in Singapore. <FlyTo preset="visitors-view">Look at the visitors</FlyTo>

Sunbirds feed at flowers by day. They have thin, downward-curving beaks, and because of the way they feed they also pollinate the flowers they visit. There are seven kinds of sunbird in Singapore. When a sunbird feeds from the front of a flower, its beak is dusted with pollen, and some is passed on to the stigma of the next flower.

Durian flowers open at night and give off a strong smell, and bats are their main pollinators. One of them is the cave nectar bat, which feeds on nectar and pollen and is an important pollinator of many forest trees, including durians. As bats feed on nectar, their faces and heads become covered with pollen, which is carried to the next flower.

A fig tree's tiny flowers grow inside the fig. Only fig wasps can pollinate fig trees. The common red-stem fig on this table has its own kind of fig wasp; the fig and the wasp are drawn larger, not to scale.

Not every flower needs an animal. Many plants, such as grasses, are pollinated by the wind. No Singapore example was checked for this topic, so the model has none.

<More title={{ en: "Nectar robbers", zh: "偷蜜的太阳鸟" }}>

Not every sunbird visit pollinates the flower. A sunbird photographed at a scarlet spiral flag sometimes pierced the base of the flower petals to reach the nectar by a short cut. Feeding that way, it does not touch the stigma.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Bird flowers, bat flowers", zh: "鸟访的花，蝙蝠访的花" }}>

Brightly coloured flowers with no smell that are open during the day are often pollinated by birds. Durian flowers are the other way round: they open at night and give off a strong smell, and bats visit them.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Bats in Singapore", zh: "新加坡的蝙蝠" }}>

Cave nectar bats roost in large colonies in caves, old buildings and under concrete bridges. In Singapore they are uncommon; the 1994 Singapore Red Data Book listed the species as Vulnerable. One night in April 2012, a cave nectar bat was photographed at a golden penda tree in Singapore with its flight membranes covered in yellow pollen. Watch bats from a distance and do not disturb their roosts.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A study in Malaysia", zh: "马来西亚的一项研究" }}>

A field study of bats and durians was done on Tioman Island, Malaysia. It found that island flying foxes, a larger bat, raised the number of durian fruits that ripened. Cave nectar bats were recorded at the same trees. It is a study from Malaysia, mostly about a different bat, not a Singapore result.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Which sunbird", zh: "是哪一种太阳鸟" }}>

The model is a female, olive-green above and yellow below, as NParks describes the female olive-backed sunbird, Cinnyris jugularis. NParks's species page now names it the ornate sunbird, Cinnyris ornatus. The male has an iridescent blue forehead, throat and upper breast.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "One tree, two jobs", zh: "一棵树，两件事" }}>

Fig wasps pollinate the common red-stem fig, while birds, squirrels and monkeys eat its ripe figs. Pollination moves pollen from flower to flower; eating the fruit moves the seeds. Chapter 05 comes back to the second job.

</More>

<Lang zh>

搬运花粉的不只是蜜蜂。动物来到花上吃花蜜或花粉，身上就沾了花粉。把花粉从一朵花带到另一朵花的动物，叫<Term id="pollinator">传粉者</Term>。这个工位按实物大小画了三种生活在新加坡的传粉者。<FlyTo preset="visitors-view">看看访花的动物</FlyTo>

太阳鸟白天在花间取食。它的喙细长、向下弯；因为它这样取食，也就给所访的花传了粉。新加坡有七种太阳鸟。太阳鸟从花口取食时，喙上沾了花粉，到下一朵花时，一部分就蹭到柱头上。

榴莲花在夜里开，气味很浓，蝙蝠是它们主要的传粉者。洞穴花蜜蝠就是其中一种，它吃花蜜和花粉，是许多森林树木的重要传粉者，榴莲也在其中。蝙蝠吃花蜜时，脸和头上沾满花粉，又带到下一朵花。

榕树细小的花长在榕果里面。只有榕小蜂能给榕树传粉。台上的青果榕有它自己的那一种榕小蜂；榕果和榕小蜂画得比较大，不按比例。

不是每一朵花都要靠动物。草这类植物靠风传粉。本主题没有核实新加坡的例子，所以模型里没有画。

<More title={{ en: "Nectar robbers", zh: "偷蜜的太阳鸟" }}>

太阳鸟访花，不一定给花传粉。有人拍到一只太阳鸟在 Scarlet Spiral Flag 上，有时直接啄穿花瓣的基部，抄近路吃到花蜜。这样取食，它碰不到柱头。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Bird flowers, bat flowers", zh: "鸟访的花，蝙蝠访的花" }}>

颜色鲜艳、没有气味、白天开的花，常常由鸟传粉。榴莲花正好相反：它在夜里开，气味很浓，来访的是蝙蝠。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Bats in Singapore", zh: "新加坡的蝙蝠" }}>

洞穴花蜜蝠成群栖息在山洞、旧建筑和混凝土桥下。在新加坡，它们并不常见；1994 年的《新加坡红皮书》把这种蝙蝠列为易危。2012 年 4 月的一个晚上，有人在新加坡一棵金蒲桃上拍到一只洞穴花蜜蝠，它的翼膜上沾满了黄色的花粉。看蝙蝠要远远地看，不要打扰它们栖息的地方。“洞穴花蜜蝠”这个中文名，是编辑按英文名直译的。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A study in Malaysia", zh: "马来西亚的一项研究" }}>

有一项蝙蝠与榴莲的野外研究，是在马来西亚的刁曼岛做的。研究发现，另一种更大的蝙蝠——岛狐蝠——让榴莲成熟的果实更多。同一批树上也记录到了洞穴花蜜蝠。这是马来西亚的研究，主要讲的是另一种蝙蝠，不是新加坡的结果。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Which sunbird", zh: "是哪一种太阳鸟" }}>

模型画的是雌鸟：上身橄榄绿，下身黄色，和 NParks 对雌性 Olive-backed Sunbird（Cinnyris jugularis）的描述一致。NParks 的物种页现在把它叫作 Ornate Sunbird（Cinnyris ornatus）。雄鸟的额、喉和上胸有闪亮的蓝色。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "One tree, two jobs", zh: "一棵树，两件事" }}>

榕小蜂给青果榕传粉；鸟、松鼠和猴子吃它成熟的榕果。传粉是把花粉从一朵花带到另一朵花；动物吃果实，把种子带走。第 05 章再讲后一件事。

</More>
