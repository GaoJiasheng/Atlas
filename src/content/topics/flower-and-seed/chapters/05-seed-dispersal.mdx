<Lang en>

Pollination carries pollen from flower to flower. Seed dispersal carries seeds away from the parent plant. Seeds travel in four main ways: by the wind, by water, by animals, or when the fruit bursts or splits open. The shape of a fruit shows which way it travels. The fruits on this station are drawn life size, in front of a strip of sea. <FlyTo preset="fruits-view">Look at the fruits</FlyTo>

Wind: the angsana's fruit is a flat, disc-like pod <Num s="S6">3–5 cm</Num> across, with a thin wing round the seed in the middle, and it is carried by the wind.

Water: the sea almond's fruit has a corky rind full of tiny air cavities, so it floats, and currents and waves carry it to new shores. A coconut has a thick, fibrous husk round a hard shell, and the seed inside has a large cavity filled with water. One paper proposes that the cavity adds to the floating that the husk gives.

Animals: birds eat ripe chillies and pass the seeds out whole, still able to germinate. The hot taste keeps away many animals that would destroy the seeds, but not birds. Birds, squirrels and long-tailed macaques eat ripe figs.

Bursting or splitting: when a rubber tree's woody fruit dries, it explodes with a loud bang and throws its three seeds more than <Num s="S10">20 m</Num> from the tree. A garden balsam pod splits open easily and scatters its small seeds.

Wings, air spaces, juicy flesh and bursting pods are the kinds of features that the P6 syllabus calls structural adaptations: physical characteristics that help fruits and seeds in their dispersal.

<More title={{ en: "One fruit, two ways", zh: "一种果实，两种方式" }}>

Some fruits travel in more than one way. Sea almond fruits float, and they are also eaten by fruit bats and monkeys, which help to spread the seeds. The pod of the saga tree twists and splits open, and then birds eat and carry off its red seeds. That is why this topic does not use them as examples of a single way.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Pollination or dispersal", zh: "传粉还是种子传播" }}>

The same tree, or the same kind of animal, can be part of both stories. Fig wasps pollinate the common red-stem fig; birds, squirrels and monkeys eat its figs and carry the seeds. Bats pollinate durian flowers; fruit bats eat sea almond fruits and spread their seeds.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Find them in Singapore", zh: "在新加坡找找看" }}>

NParks names more native plants for each way: the marsh pulai and keruing for the wind; the sea pong pong and katong laut for water; Ohwia caudata and bur mallow for animals; and plants such as the champaca and twin-seed that disperse their own seeds.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A fair test with paper seeds", zh: "用纸做种子的公平测试" }}>

Make two paper helicopters, one larger than the other. Drop them from the same height and compare how long each takes to land. Change only one thing at a time, the size, and keep everything else the same. Real seeds are harder to test fairly, because there are too many things in nature that change at once.

</More>

<Lang zh>

传粉是把花粉从一朵花带到另一朵花；种子传播是把种子带离母株。种子主要有四种传播方式：靠风、靠水、靠动物，或者靠果实爆开、裂开。看果实的样子，就能看出它怎样传播。这个工位的果实按实物大小画，后面是一条海水。<FlyTo preset="fruits-view">看看这些果实</FlyTo>

风：青龙木的果实是直径 <Num s="S6">3–5 厘米</Num>的扁圆荚，中间的种子四周是一圈薄翅，靠风带走。

水：榄仁树的果实外层像软木，布满细小的空隙，所以能浮在水上，被海流和海浪带到新的岸边。椰子坚硬的壳外包着厚厚的纤维，里面的种子有一个装满水的大腔。有一篇论文提出，这个腔让纤维外皮的浮力更大。

动物：鸟吃成熟的辣椒，种子完好地随粪便排出，还能萌发。辣味挡住了许多会毁掉种子的动物，却挡不住鸟。鸟、松鼠和长尾猕猴吃成熟的榕果。

爆开或裂开：橡胶树的木质果实干了以后，会“啪”地一声爆开，把三粒种子弹到离母树 <Num s="S10">20 米</Num>以外。凤仙花的果荚很容易裂开，把小小的种子撒出去。

翅、空隙、多汁的果肉、会爆开的果荚，都是小六课纲所说的“结构适应”：帮助果实和种子传播的外形特征。

<More title={{ en: "One fruit, two ways", zh: "一种果实，两种方式" }}>

有些果实不只一种传播方式。榄仁树的果实会漂浮，也被果蝠和猴子吃，帮着传播种子。相思豆的豆荚会扭转裂开，红色的种子又被鸟吃掉带走。所以本主题不拿它们当“只有一种方式”的例子。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Pollination or dispersal", zh: "传粉还是种子传播" }}>

同一棵树、同一类动物，可能两件事都参与。榕小蜂给青果榕传粉；鸟、松鼠和猴子吃榕果，把种子带走。蝙蝠给榴莲花传粉；果蝠吃榄仁树的果实，传播它的种子。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Find them in Singapore", zh: "在新加坡找找看" }}>

NParks 为每一种方式都举了新加坡本地植物的例子：靠风的有 Marsh Pulai 和 Keruing；靠水的有 Sea Pong Pong 和 Katong Laut；靠动物的有 Ohwia caudata 和地桃花；自己把种子弹出去的有 Champaca 和 Twin-seed。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A fair test with paper seeds", zh: "用纸做种子的公平测试" }}>

做两个纸螺旋桨，一大一小。从同样的高度放下，比一比哪个先落地。一次只改变一样东西，也就是大小，其余都保持不变。真的种子很难做公平测试，因为大自然里同时变化的东西太多了。

</More>
