<Lang en>

Plants make new plants in two ways. Ferns and mosses release <Term id="spore">spores</Term>; flowering plants make <Term id="seed">seeds</Term>. The science syllabus asks you to investigate both ways, and puts the reason for all reproduction like this: living things reproduce to ensure continuity of their kind, and many characteristics of an organism are passed on from parents to offspring.

A flowering plant's seeds come from four processes, in this order. In <Term id="pollination">pollination</Term>, <Term id="pollen">pollen</Term> moves from the part of a flower that makes it to the part that receives it. In <Term id="fertilisation">fertilisation</Term>, a male reproductive cell fuses with a female reproductive cell, and the flower goes on to form fruit and seeds. In <Term id="seed-dispersal">seed dispersal</Term>, the seeds are carried away from the parent plant. In <Term id="germination">germination</Term>, a seed starts to grow into a young plant, which can grow up to flower in its turn.

The stage is one round specimen table, <Num s="S1">1.9 m</Num> across, laid out as that cycle: a chilli flower and a bee, other animals that carry pollen, two pairs of reproductive cells, fruits and seeds, the inside of a seed and a seed test, and back to the flower. A bird's nest fern and a moss stand on a small table to one side, because they reproduce by spores. <FlyTo preset="spores-view">Start at the fern</FlyTo>

Each station has its own scale bar, because some specimens are drawn larger than life and some smaller; the reproductive cells are diagrams with no scale. The seed-test results come from published classroom and laboratory studies. Where the model has to go beyond them, it says so.

The topic <TopicLink slug="plant">A Plant</TopicLink> follows one whole chilli plant, from roots to fruit. This topic starts at its flower and goes further.

<More title={{ en: "How this model works", zh: "这个模型怎样画" }}>

The flower, the bee and the flower cut in half are drawn <Num s="S1">20 times</Num> life size; the green beans <Num s="S1">10 times</Num>; the fern a quarter of life size; the sunbird, the bat, the durian flowers, the fruits and the cups life size. The moss, the spore lines, the fig and the fig wasp are drawn larger with no scale, because no size was found for them. The seed test has four cups, looked at on day <Num s="S1">0</Num> and on day <Num s="S1">7</Num> only: no source gives what a cup looks like on each day in between.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why living things reproduce", zh: "生物为什么要繁殖" }}>

The syllabus starts this topic with two ideas: a cell is a basic unit of life, and living things reproduce so that their kind carries on, passing many characteristics from parents to offspring. Fertilisation, in chapter 04, is the joining of two cells.

</More>

Animals go round a cycle as well, from egg to adult and back to egg. <TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="round-the-cycle">A Mosquito’s Life Cycle</TopicLink> follows one.

</More>

<Lang zh>

植物有两种繁殖方式：蕨和苔藓放出<Term id="spore">孢子</Term>，开花植物产生<Term id="seed">种子</Term>。科学课纲要求探究这两种方式，并这样说明繁殖的意义：生物繁殖，让同类延续下去；生物的许多特征从亲代传给后代。

开花植物的种子要依次经过四个过程。<Term id="pollination">传粉</Term>：<Term id="pollen">花粉</Term>从花上产生它的部分，落到接受它的部分。<Term id="fertilisation">受精</Term>：雄性生殖细胞与雌性生殖细胞融合，花接着形成果实和种子。<Term id="seed-dispersal">种子传播</Term>：种子被带离母株。<Term id="germination">萌发</Term>：种子开始长成幼苗，幼苗长大后又会开花。

舞台是一张直径 <Num s="S1">1.9 米</Num>的圆形标本台，按这个循环排成一圈：一朵辣椒花和一只蜜蜂、别的搬运花粉的动物、两对生殖细胞、果实与种子、种子的里面和种子测试，再回到花。巢蕨和苔藓放在旁边的小台上，因为它们靠孢子繁殖。<FlyTo preset="spores-view">从蕨看起</FlyTo>

有些标本画得比实物大，有些比实物小，所以每个工位各有自己的比例尺；生殖细胞是示意图，没有比例尺。种子测试的结果取自公开的课堂与实验室研究；模型不得不超出这些研究的地方，都会写明。

<TopicLink slug="plant">“一株植物”</TopicLink>讲一整株辣椒，从根讲到果实。本主题从它的花讲起，再往深处走。

<More title={{ en: "How this model works", zh: "这个模型怎样画" }}>

花、蜜蜂和剖开的花放大 <Num s="S1">20 倍</Num>；绿豆放大 <Num s="S1">10 倍</Num>；巢蕨缩小到四分之一；太阳鸟、蝙蝠、榴莲花、果实和杯子按实物大小画。苔藓、孢子囊群、榕果和榕小蜂画得较大，但没有比例尺，因为找不到它们的尺寸。种子测试有四只杯子，只看第 <Num s="S1">0</Num> 天和第 <Num s="S1">7</Num> 天：没有来源说明中间每一天杯子里是什么样子。

</More>
