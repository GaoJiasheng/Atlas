<Lang en>

A flower is not just for show. It is the part of a flowering plant that makes seeds. A chilli flower is white and about <Num s="S26">1 cm</Num> across, with purple anthers; on the stage it is drawn <Num s="S1">20 times</Num> life size, beside the same flower cut in half. <FlyTo preset="flower-view">Look at the flower</FlyTo>

Round the outside are the white petals and, at their base, the green sepals. Inside stands a ring of <Term id="stamen">stamens</Term>. Each stamen is a thin stalk, the filament, topped by an anther, where pollen forms. In the middle is the <Term id="pistil">pistil</Term>: a stigma at the top that receives pollen, a stalk called the style, and at the bottom the <Term id="ovary">ovary</Term>, which holds one or more <Term id="ovule">ovules</Term>.

Pollination is the moving of pollen from an anther to a stigma. The pollen can come from the same flower or from another flower; both are pollination. A chilli flower can pollinate itself, but in a greenhouse study of sweet pepper, flowers visited by bees gave heavier fruit with more seeds.

Bees often carry it. As a bee visits a flower, the hairs on its body pick up pollen, and some rubs off on the stigma of the next flower it visits. NParks lists bees as the chilli's pollinators and notes that the plant gives the most fruit when bees are around.

<Lang zh>

花不只是好看，它是开花植物产生种子的部分。辣椒花是白色的，宽约 <Num s="S26">1 厘米</Num>，花药紫色；舞台上的花放大 <Num s="S1">20 倍</Num>，旁边是剖开的同一种花。<FlyTo preset="flower-view">看看这朵花</FlyTo>

最外面是白色的花瓣，花瓣基部是绿色的萼片。里面立着一圈<Term id="stamen">雄蕊</Term>。每枚雄蕊是一根细细的花丝，顶端是花药，花粉就在花药里形成。正中间是<Term id="pistil">雌蕊</Term>：顶端是接住花粉的柱头，中间是花柱，底部是<Term id="ovary">子房</Term>，里面有一个或多个<Term id="ovule">胚珠</Term>。

花粉从花药落到柱头上，就是传粉。落到柱头上的花粉，可以来自同一朵花，也可以来自另一朵花，都叫传粉。辣椒花自己就能完成传粉；不过一项温室甜椒研究发现，有蜜蜂来访的花，结的果更重，种子也更多。

搬花粉的常常是蜜蜂。蜜蜂访花时，身上的毛沾上花粉，到下一朵花时，一部分就蹭到柱头上。NParks 写明辣椒靠蜜蜂传粉，还说有蜜蜂的时候辣椒结果最多。
