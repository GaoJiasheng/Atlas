<Lang en>

The model shows a man's hips and legs, built from a real anatomical scan. The leg muscles are drawn in warm colours, the bones in ivory, and the neighbouring muscles of the trunk and shin in grey. Left and right always mean the body's own left and right.

Stand up from a chair and <Num s="M109">three</Num> joints work at once: the hip opens, the knee straightens and the ankle keeps your weight over your feet. On the surface, **gluteus maximus** covers the buttock. The <Term id="quadriceps">quadriceps</Term> cover the front of the thigh, the <Term id="hamstrings">hamstrings</Term> the back and the **adductors** the inner side. **Gastrocnemius** makes the calf bulge, and **tibialis anterior** runs beside the shin. Smaller muscles underneath move and steady the same joints [M109] [M59].

Every muscle has an <Term id="origin">origin</Term>, the end that usually stays still, and an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term>, the end it pulls. A muscle can only pull: it shortens and draws its two ends together. With the foot planted, the "moving" end can swap. When you rise from a squat, the thigh muscles pull the pelvis up over the feet rather than swinging the foot [M109]. Hollow markers show origins, solid markers insertions.

The outer thigh also holds the **iliotibial tract**, a thick band of <Term id="fascia">fascia</Term>. Tensor fasciae latae and most of gluteus maximus pull on it, and it carries their pull past the knee. It is not a muscle and cannot shorten by itself [M109] [M59]. <FlyTo preset="front">See the front</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Where this model comes from", zh: "这个模型从哪里来" }}>

The meshes come from Z-Anatomy, an open atlas built on BodyParts3D, which was made from MRI scans of one adult male volunteer [M4]. Atlas selected the hip and leg structures, simplified them and added fibre directions and attachment points. The model and its geometry are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 [M3]. Real bodies vary; this is one reference body, not a standard everyone should match.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Layers are a viewing order", zh: "分层是观察的顺序" }}>

PEEL lifts the surface, middle and deep groups in turn, the order in which you would uncover them. Real muscles are not flat sheets in three neat layers. Gracilis, for example, lies just under the skin of the inner thigh, but it sits in the middle group here so the inner thigh stays readable.

</More>

<Lang zh>

舞台上是一名男性的髋部和双腿，根据真实的解剖扫描数据建成。腿部肌肉画成暖色，骨骼是象牙色，躯干和小腿的邻近肌肉画成灰色。文中说的左和右，一律指身体自己的左右。

从椅子上站起来，有<Num s="M109">三个</Num>关节同时在动：髋打开，膝伸直，踝让体重稳在脚的上方。最表面，**臀大肌**盖住臀部；大腿前面是<Term id="quadriceps">股四头肌</Term>，后面是<Term id="hamstrings">腘绳肌</Term>，内侧是**内收肌群**；**腓肠肌**形成小腿肚的隆起，**胫骨前肌**沿胫骨旁向下。下面较小的肌肉也在移动和稳住这几个关节 [M109] [M59]。

每块肌肉都有<Term id="origin">起点</Term>，通常是不动的那一端；还有<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>，是它拉动的那一端。肌肉只会拉：它缩短，把两端拉近。脚踩在地上时，“动的那一端”可能对调。从深蹲站起时，大腿肌肉是把骨盆拉回到双脚上方，而不是把脚甩出去 [M109]。空心圆标起点，实心圆标止点。

大腿外侧还有一条**髂胫束**，是增厚的<Term id="fascia">筋膜</Term>。阔筋膜张肌和臀大肌的大部分拉着它，它再把拉力传过膝关节。它不是肌肉，自己不会缩短 [M109] [M59]。<FlyTo preset="front">看正面</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Layers are a viewing order", zh: "分层是观察的顺序" }}>

“剥层”按浅、中、深的顺序把三组肌肉依次移开，就像解剖时一层层揭开。真实的肌肉并不是整整齐齐的三层平板。比如股薄肌就在大腿内侧皮下，但这里把它放在中层组，让大腿内侧看得清楚。

</More>
