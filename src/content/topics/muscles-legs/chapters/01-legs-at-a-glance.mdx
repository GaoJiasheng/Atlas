<Lang en>

Stand up from a chair and your legs do more than straighten. The hip opens, the knee unfolds and the ankle keeps your weight over the foot. This model follows those <Num s="M1">three</Num> main joints from the pelvis down to the toes. Its warm muscles belong to the legs and buttocks; the ivory shapes are bones. Grey muscles are neighbours. Left and right always mean the body's own left and right.

On the surface, **gluteus maximus** covers the buttock. The **quadriceps** cover the front of the thigh, the <Term id="hamstrings">hamstrings</Term> its back, and the **adductors** its inner side. **Gastrocnemius** makes the calf bulge; **tibialis anterior** runs beside the front of the shin. The smaller muscles underneath help move and steady the same joints [M1].

Each muscle has an <Term id="origin">origin</Term> and an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term>, the attachment names used on its card. Contracting fibres pull the ends towards each other. In a squat with the feet planted, the moving end can change: the legs help lift the pelvis and trunk rather than swinging a free foot [M1].

The outer thigh also contains the **iliotibial tract**, a thickened band of <Term id="fascia">fascia</Term>. It receives fibres from gluteus maximus and tensor fasciae latae. It transmits tension; it is not a muscle and cannot actively shorten [M1]. <Lang zh>

从椅子上站起来，双腿做的不只是伸直。髋关节打开，膝关节伸展，踝关节让重心留在脚上方。模型沿着这<Num s="M1">三个</Num>主要关节，从骨盆展示到脚趾。暖色是腿和臀部的肌肉，象牙色是骨骼，灰色是邻区肌肉。文中的左右，一律指身体自己的左右。

浅层的**臀大肌**盖住臀部。**股四头肌**在大腿前面，<Term id="hamstrings">腘绳肌</Term>在后面，**内收肌群**在内侧。**腓肠肌**形成小腿肚的隆起，**胫骨前肌**沿胫骨前外侧向下。下面的小肌肉也参与移动和稳定这些关节，并不因为藏得深就不重要 [M1]。

每块肌肉都有<Term id="origin">起点</Term>和<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>，卡片用这两个名字描述附着。收缩的纤维把两端往一起拉。脚踩地深蹲时，活动的一端可能改变：腿帮助抬起骨盆和躯干，而不是把悬空的脚摆出去 [M1]。

大腿外侧还有**髂胫束**，是<Term id="fascia">筋膜</Term>增厚形成的长带，接受臀大肌和阔筋膜张肌的纤维。它传递张力，却不是肌肉，不会主动缩短 [M1]。模型还显示皮肤轮廓，便于把骨内位置对应到自己身上。切换视角时，颜色始终表示同一组结构。从索引打开结构卡，沿起止点看它连接哪些骨，再比较前面、后面和深层。<FlyTo preset="front">看正面</FlyTo>
