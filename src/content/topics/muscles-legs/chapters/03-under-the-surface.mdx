<Lang en>

Fade the surface muscles and the deeper routes appear. **Gluteus medius** and **gluteus minimus** spread from the outer ilium to the greater trochanter, the large projection near the top of the femur. Their pull carries the thigh sideways; when a foot is planted, they help keep the pelvis level. You cannot reliably identify gluteus minimus by touching the skin because other muscles cover it [M1].

On the front of the femur, **vastus intermedius** hides under rectus femoris and joins the same knee-extending tendon. On the inner thigh, **pectineus**, **adductor longus** and **adductor brevis** run from the pubis towards the femur. **Adductor magnus** spreads broadly down its back and inner side. Its posterior portion also helps extend the hip [M1].

**Gracilis** is different: its long strap continues past the knee to the inner tibia. The <Num s="M1">three</Num> tendons of gracilis, sartorius and semitendinosus form the <Term id="pes-anserinus">pes anserinus</Term>, a shared attachment area named for its spreading shape [M1]. Do not press deeply into the groin or the hollow behind the knee to find these structures.

Behind the thigh, **semimembranosus** reaches the posterior inner tibia; the **short head of biceps femoris** starts on the femur and ends mainly on the fibular head. Below, **soleus** starts on the tibia and fibula and reaches the heel through the Achilles tendon. Unlike gastrocnemius, it does not cross the knee [M1]. Psoas major and iliacus appear in grey. They join near the front of the hip and pull towards the lesser trochanter of the femur. Fibularis longus, another neighbour, passes behind the outer ankle and underneath the foot [M1].

<More title={{ en: "One name, different fibre routes", zh: "一个名字，不同纤维路线" }}>

Adductor magnus has an adductor portion and a posterior, hamstring portion with different attachments. The latter reaches the adductor tubercle of the femur. Despite that name, it ends above the knee and does not act as a knee flexor [M1].

<Lang zh>

把浅层肌肉调淡，深处的路线就露出来。**臀中肌**和**臀小肌**从髂骨外面展开，止于股骨上端的大转子。它们把大腿拉向侧面；脚踩地时，帮助保持骨盆水平。臀小肌上面盖着其他肌肉，不能可靠地隔着皮肤摸出来 [M1]。

**股中间肌**藏在股直肌下面，贴着股骨前方，汇入同一条伸膝肌腱。大腿内侧的**耻骨肌、长收肌、短收肌**从耻骨走向股骨。**大收肌**广泛铺在股骨后内侧，后部也帮助伸髋 [M1]。

**股薄肌**不同，它像长带越过膝，止于胫骨内侧；它和缝匠肌、半腱肌一起构成<Term id="pes-anserinus">鹅足</Term>，名字来自<Num s="M1">三条</Num>肌腱展开的形状 [M1]。认识位置不等于做深部触诊，不要往腹股沟或膝后凹处深按。

大腿后面的**半膜肌**到达胫骨后内侧；**股二头肌短头**起于股骨，主要止于腓骨头。下面的**比目鱼肌**起于胫骨和腓骨，经跟腱到脚跟。它不像腓肠肌那样跨膝 [M1]。这些内侧肌肉大多不跨膝，股薄肌是例外。它的长腱让它也能协助屈膝 [M1]。从索引比较附着点，便能理解为什么外形相近的肌肉会做不同工作。藏得深，只是比较难看见，不代表作用更小。<FlyTo preset="deep-view">看浅层下面</FlyTo>
