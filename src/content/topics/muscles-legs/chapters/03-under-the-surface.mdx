<Lang en>

Fade the surface muscles and the deeper routes appear. **Gluteus medius** and **gluteus minimus** fan out from the outer surface of the pelvis to the greater trochanter, the bony knob at the top of the femur. They swing the thigh out to the side. When you stand on one leg they hold the pelvis level, so it does not drop on the other side [M109] [M59]. Gluteus minimus is covered by other muscles and cannot be felt through the skin.

On the front of the femur, **vastus intermedius** lies under rectus femoris and joins the same knee-straightening tendon. On the inner thigh, **pectineus**, **adductor longus** and **adductor brevis** run from the pubic bone to the back of the femur and pull the thigh in towards the midline. **Adductor magnus** is the largest of them. Its back part starts on the sitting bone, like the hamstrings, and also helps extend the hip [M109] [M59].

**Gracilis** is a thin strap and the only inner-thigh muscle that crosses the knee. Its tendon ends on the inner side of the tibia together with those of sartorius and semitendinosus. The three tendons spread out like a bird's foot, the <Term id="pes-anserinus">pes anserinus</Term> [M109].

Behind the thigh, **semimembranosus** lies under semitendinosus and ends on the back of the inner tibia. The **short head of biceps femoris** starts on the femur, not the pelvis, so it bends the knee but cannot extend the hip. In the calf, **soleus** lies under gastrocnemius. It starts on the tibia and fibula below the knee and shares the Achilles tendon, so it acts on the ankle only [M109] [M59]. <FlyTo preset="side">See the side</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "The grey neighbours", zh: "灰色的邻近肌肉" }}>

Psoas major and iliacus, shown in grey, start on the lumbar spine and inside the pelvis and join to end on the lesser trochanter of the femur. Together, as iliopsoas, they flex the hip. Fibularis longus, also grey, runs down the outer shin; its tendon passes behind the outer ankle and under the sole [M109] [M92].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A hamstring part that does not bend the knee", zh: "不屈膝的“腘绳肌部”" }}>

The back part of adductor magnus starts on the sitting bone like the hamstrings and is sometimes called its hamstring part. It ends on the adductor tubercle of the femur, above the knee, so unlike the true hamstrings it does not bend the knee [M109].

把浅层调淡，深处的路线就露出来了。**臀中肌**和**臀小肌**从骨盆外面呈扇形展开，止于股骨上端的骨突——大转子。它们把大腿向侧面抬。单腿站立时，它们拉住骨盆，不让另一侧往下掉 [M109] [M59]。臀小肌被其他肌肉盖住，隔着皮肤摸不到。

股骨前面，**股中间肌**藏在股直肌下面，和其他三个头汇入同一条伸膝肌腱。大腿内侧，**耻骨肌**、**长收肌**和**短收肌**从耻骨走到股骨后面，把大腿往中线拉。**大收肌**是其中最大的一块，它后面的部分和腘绳肌一样起于坐骨结节，也帮助伸髋 [M109] [M59]。

**股薄肌**是一条细长的带，也是大腿内侧唯一跨过膝关节的肌肉。它的肌腱和缝匠肌、半腱肌的肌腱一起止于胫骨内侧，三条腱散开，像鹅的脚掌，叫<Term id="pes-anserinus">鹅足</Term> [M109]。

大腿后面，**半膜肌**藏在半腱肌下面，止于胫骨内侧的后面。**股二头肌短头**起于股骨而不是骨盆，所以它能屈膝，却不能伸髋。小腿里，**比目鱼肌**在腓肠肌下面，起于膝下方的胫骨和腓骨，和腓肠肌共用跟腱，所以只作用于踝 [M109] [M59]。<FlyTo preset="side">看侧面</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "The grey neighbours", zh: "灰色的邻近肌肉" }}>

灰色的腰大肌和髂肌分别起于腰椎和骨盆里面，汇合后止于股骨小转子，合称髂腰肌，负责屈髋。同样是灰色的腓骨长肌沿小腿外侧向下，肌腱绕过外踝后方，再横过足底 [M109] [M92]。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A hamstring part that does not bend the knee", zh: "不屈膝的“腘绳肌部”" }}>

大收肌后面的部分和腘绳肌一样起于坐骨结节，所以有时被叫作它的“腘绳肌部”。但它止于股骨的收肌结节，在膝关节上方，因此不像真正的腘绳肌那样能屈膝 [M109]。

</More>
