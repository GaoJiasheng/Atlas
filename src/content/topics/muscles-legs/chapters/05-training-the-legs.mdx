The cards follow equipment order: barbell, dumbbell, body weight, cable, machine and band. Each card explains setup, movement, cues, mistakes and cautions, so use the whole card before trying a movement.

The barbell back squat and dumbbell goblet squat combine hip and knee movement. The Romanian deadlift is a <Term id="hip-hinge">hip hinge</Term>: send the hips back with a steady trunk and slightly bent knees. The seated leg curl adds direct knee flexion, including work for biceps femoris short head. Reverse and lateral lunges practise stepping and returning under control. Cable abduction and adduction use opposing sideways hip pulls. Calf raises train pushing through the forefoot; tibialis raises, heel walking and band dorsiflexion train lifting or holding it up [M1].

Choose a variation and load that let you control the whole path. Learn with body weight or a light load and a qualified coach; anyone under <Num s="M25">eighteen</Num> needs adult supervision [M25]. Feelings in a region can help locate the work, but soreness does not prove that one muscle was isolated or that an exercise was successful.

Progress gradually once the movement is dependable, and allow recovery after demanding sessions [M23] [M24]. There is no required checklist to finish and no universal sets-and-repetitions plan here. These cards teach movements, not rehabilitation. This is general information, not medical advice; for pain or injury, consult a doctor or physiotherapist.

<More title={{ en: "A sequence, not a prescription", zh: "顺序示例，不是处方" }}>

An easy goblet squat can rehearse the movement. A familiar squat and hinge can come before lighter knee-flexion, hip-control and ankle work. Choose only the movements that suit your skills and available equipment; the session notes describe an example of ordering, not compulsory homework [M23] [M24].
