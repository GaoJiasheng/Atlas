<Lang en>

The list at the end of this chapter has all thirteen exercises, grouped by equipment: barbell, dumbbell, body weight, cable, machine and band. Press one and the model takes up the exercise, the prime movers turn orange and the helpers blue-grey, and its card opens at the top of this panel with a sketch, the steps, cues, common mistakes and cautions. The first time, start with body weight or a light load and learn the movement from a qualified coach; anyone under <Num s="M25">eighteen</Num> should train with an adult supervising [M25].

The exercises cover the main ways the legs work. **Squats** (barbell back squat, goblet squat) bend hips, knees and ankles together and train the quadriceps and gluteus maximus. The **Romanian deadlift** is a <Term id="hip-hinge">hip hinge</Term>: the trunk tips forward at the hips while the knees stay softly bent, so the hamstrings and gluteus maximus do the work. **Lunges** (reverse and lateral) work one leg at a time and train balance. **Calf raises** train gastrocnemius and soleus; the bent-knee version starts gastrocnemius shorter but leaves soleus unchanged. The **seated leg curl** bends the knee and is the one exercise here that loads the short head of biceps femoris. **Cable abduction and adduction** work the outer hip and inner thigh, and the **tibialis raise**, **heel walk** and **band dorsiflexion** train the front of the shin [M109] [M59].

No single leg exercise suits everyone. Surface <Term id="emg">EMG</Term> measures how active a muscle is, and higher EMG is not proof that a muscle grows more [M9]. Choose exercises you can do with good form and equipment you have.

A few principles hold for all of them. Adults are advised to do muscle-strengthening activities on <Num s="M22">two or more days a week</Num>; Singapore's guidelines say the same [M26]. Lower under control, keep the whole foot planted unless the exercise says otherwise, and add load in small steps only once the movement is easy [M23] [M24]. This is general information, not medical advice: for pain or an injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

<More title={{ en: "A sample session", zh: "一节训练课示例" }}>

One way to arrange a leg session, not a prescription: a light goblet squat to warm up; the back squat as the main lift, in a rack with the safety bars set; Romanian deadlifts while you are still fresh; then lighter work such as the seated leg curl, cable hip abduction, seated calf raise and tibialis raise. Big multi-joint lifts come first and smaller single-joint work later, and a hard leg session is followed by at least a day off for the legs [M23] [M24].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What the model cannot show", zh: "模型演示不了的部分" }}>

The model moves the whole body, keeps the planted feet on the floor and draws the barbell, dumbbells, bench, cable column and band at their real size. A few things are simplified. In the goblet squat and lateral lunge the dumbbell is held across the chest rather than standing on end. The wall in the tibialis raise and the hand support in the standing calf raise are not drawn. The ankle cuff of the cable exercises is shown as a cable running to the ankle, and the leg-curl machine as a seat, a back pad and a thigh pad. Each card's sketch shows the real set-up.

</More>

本章末尾的列表收齐十三个动作，按器械分组：杠铃、哑铃、自重、绳索、器械和弹力带。点一个，模型就摆出这个动作，主动肌变成橙色，协同肌变成蓝灰色；动作卡在这个面板顶部打开，有示意图、步骤、要点、常见错误和注意事项。第一次做，先用自重或轻重量，跟合格的教练学动作；未满<Num s="M25">十八岁</Num>的人应在成人监督下练习 [M25]。

这些动作覆盖了腿部的几种主要用法。**深蹲**（杠铃背蹲、高脚杯深蹲）让髋、膝、踝一起弯曲，练股四头肌和臀大肌。**罗马尼亚硬拉**是<Term id="hip-hinge">髋铰链</Term>：躯干以髋为轴前倾，膝保持微屈，主要靠腘绳肌和臀大肌出力。**弓步**（后撤弓步、侧弓步）一次练一条腿，也练平衡。**提踵**练腓肠肌和比目鱼肌，屈膝的坐姿版本让腓肠肌一开始就变短，比目鱼肌则不受影响。**坐姿腿弯举**屈膝，是这里唯一能练到股二头肌短头的动作。**绳索外展和内收**练髋外侧和大腿内侧；**靠墙提脚**、**脚跟行走**和**弹力带背屈**练胫前的肌肉 [M109] [M59]。

没有哪个腿部动作适合所有人。表面<Term id="emg">肌电</Term>测的是肌肉有多活跃，肌电高不等于肌肉长得更多 [M9]。选你能做标准、手边也有器械的动作。

有几条原则对所有动作都适用。成年人每周至少<Num s="M22">两天</Num>做增强肌肉的练习；新加坡的指南也这样建议 [M26]。有控制地下放；除非动作另有要求，全脚掌踩稳；动作做得轻松了，再小步加重 [M23] [M24]。本页是一般性知识，不是医疗建议；疼痛或受伤，请咨询医生或物理治疗师。

<More title={{ en: "A sample session", zh: "一节训练课示例" }}>

一种腿部训练课的安排，不是处方：先用轻的高脚杯深蹲热身；在深蹲架里、调好保护杠，把杠铃背蹲作为主项；趁精力还足做罗马尼亚硬拉；之后做较轻的动作，比如坐姿腿弯举、绳索髋外展、坐姿提踵和靠墙提脚。先做多关节的大动作，后做单关节的小动作；腿练得很累之后，至少隔一天再练腿 [M23] [M24]。

</More>
