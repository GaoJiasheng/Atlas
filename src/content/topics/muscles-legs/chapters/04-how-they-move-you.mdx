<Lang en>

Watch the hip extend and the thigh move back. Gluteus maximus and the long hamstrings pull behind the joint. At the side, gluteus medius and minimus draw the thigh outward; the inner-thigh adductors draw it inward. With the foot planted, these muscles also help control the pelvis over the leg [M1].

The important distinction is a <Term id="biarticular-muscle">biarticular muscle</Term>: one crossing <Num s="M1">two</Num> joints. Biceps femoris long head, semitendinosus and semimembranosus cross both hip and knee. **Biceps femoris short head crosses only the knee**, so a hip hinge cannot directly train its hip-extension action: it has none. Rectus femoris crosses hip and knee; gastrocnemius crosses knee and ankle [M1].

Hip flexion lengthens the long hamstrings at their upper end; knee flexion shortens them at their lower end. The two changes can partly offset each other. For rectus femoris, hip flexion tends to shorten it while knee flexion tends to lengthen it. Muscle length therefore cannot be read from a knee angle alone [M1].

At the ankle, gastrocnemius and soleus point the foot down, called <Term id="plantarflexion">plantarflexion</Term>. Bending the knee shortens gastrocnemius but does not shorten soleus across that joint. Tibialis anterior lifts the forefoot in <Term id="dorsiflexion">dorsiflexion</Term> [M1]. A working muscle can also lengthen under tension, the <Term id="eccentric-phase">eccentric phase</Term>, as you lower the forefoot slowly. A straight-knee calf raise and a bent-knee calf raise change gastrocnemius length before the ankle moves. Bending the knee does not switch gastrocnemius off, and soleus still works in both positions. Think about the whole attachment route [M1].

<Lang zh>

看髋伸展，大腿向后移动。臀大肌和长腘绳肌从关节后方拉动。臀中肌、臀小肌把大腿拉向外侧，内收肌群把它拉回中线。脚踩地时，这些肌肉也帮助控制骨盆在腿上方的位置 [M1]。

关键是<Term id="biarticular-muscle">双关节肌</Term>：一块肌肉跨过<Num s="M1">两个</Num>关节。股二头肌长头、半腱肌和半膜肌跨髋与膝；**股二头肌短头只跨膝**，没有伸髋作用。股直肌跨髋与膝，腓肠肌跨膝与踝 [M1]。

屈髋把长腘绳肌的上端拉长，屈膝让下端缩短，两种改变能部分抵消。股直肌正好相反：屈髋倾向于让它缩短，屈膝倾向于让它拉长。所以只看膝角度，不能判断整块肌肉的长度，更不能认为每个动作都让它同样短缩 [M1]。

踝部的腓肠肌和比目鱼肌让脚向下指，叫<Term id="plantarflexion">跖屈</Term>。屈膝缩短腓肠肌，却不会从膝端缩短比目鱼肌。胫骨前肌抬起前脚掌，叫<Term id="dorsiflexion">背屈</Term> [M1]。慢慢放下时，肌肉仍用力却变长，这是<Term id="eccentric-phase">离心阶段</Term>。伸膝时，股四头肌经髌骨把拉力传到胫骨；屈膝时，后方肌群反向拉动。跨关节的路线决定作用 [M1]。画面上的条纹表示收缩示意，不是力的读数。<FlyTo preset="calf">比较踝部肌肉</FlyTo>

直膝提踵和屈膝提踵，在踝开始动之前就改变了腓肠肌长度。屈膝不是关闭腓肠肌，比目鱼肌在两种位置也都工作。要沿整条附着路线理解动作 [M1]。
