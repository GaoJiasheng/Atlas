<Lang en>

At the hip, the thigh can swing forward, back, out and in. Iliopsoas, rectus femoris and sartorius cross the front of the hip and bring the thigh forward. Gluteus maximus and the hamstrings cross the back and take it back. Gluteus medius and minimus sit above the hip and swing it out; the adductors pull it in [M109] [M59]. With the foot on the ground, the same muscles move or steady the pelvis over the leg instead.

The key idea in this chapter is the <Term id="biarticular-muscle">biarticular muscle</Term>, one that crosses <Num s="M109">two</Num> joints. **The hamstrings, rectus femoris and gastrocnemius each cross two joints.** The long head of biceps femoris, semitendinosus and semimembranosus cross the back of both hip and knee. Rectus femoris crosses the front of both hip and knee. Gastrocnemius crosses the back of both knee and ankle [M109] [M59]. The short head of biceps femoris, the three vasti and soleus each cross only one joint.

This matters because the length of a two-joint muscle depends on both joints. Bending the hip stretches the hamstrings at the top; bending the knee slackens them at the bottom. In a Romanian deadlift the hip bends while the knee stays almost straight, so the hamstrings end up long; in a seated leg curl the knee bends, so they end up short [M109]. Rectus femoris is the mirror image: bending the knee stretches it, bending the hip slackens it.

At the ankle, gastrocnemius and soleus point the foot down and lift the heel, called <Term id="plantarflexion">plantarflexion</Term>. Bending the knee slackens gastrocnemius at its upper end but not soleus, which starts below the knee. Tibialis anterior lifts the forefoot, called <Term id="dorsiflexion">dorsiflexion</Term> [M109] [M59]. When you lower the forefoot slowly, it keeps working while it lengthens: the <Term id="eccentric-phase">eccentric phase</Term> of the movement. <FlyTo preset="calf">See the ankle muscles</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Same calf, two positions", zh: "同一个小腿，两种姿势" }}>

A calf raise with straight knees and one with bent knees both use gastrocnemius and soleus. Bending the knee shortens gastrocnemius before the ankle even moves, which changes how much it can contribute; it does not switch the muscle off [M109].

<Lang zh>

在髋部，大腿可以向前、向后、向外、向内摆。髂腰肌、股直肌和缝匠肌跨过髋的前面，把大腿往前抬；臀大肌和腘绳肌跨过后面，把它往后带；臀中肌和臀小肌在髋的上方，把它往外摆；内收肌群把它往里拉 [M109] [M59]。脚踩在地上时，同样这些肌肉转而移动或稳住腿上方的骨盆。

本章的关键是<Term id="biarticular-muscle">双关节肌</Term>，也就是跨过<Num s="M109">两个</Num>关节的肌肉。**腘绳肌、股直肌和腓肠肌都各跨两个关节。**股二头肌长头、半腱肌和半膜肌跨过髋和膝的后面；股直肌跨过髋和膝的前面；腓肠肌跨过膝和踝的后面 [M109] [M59]。股二头肌短头、三块股肌和比目鱼肌都只跨一个关节。

这一点很重要，因为双关节肌的长度由两个关节一起决定。屈髋从上端拉长腘绳肌，屈膝从下端放松腘绳肌。罗马尼亚硬拉时髋在弯、膝几乎是直的，腘绳肌最后被拉得很长；坐姿腿弯举时弯的是膝，腘绳肌最后变得很短 [M109]。股直肌正好相反：屈膝拉长它，屈髋放松它。

在踝部，腓肠肌和比目鱼肌让脚尖朝下、脚跟抬起，叫<Term id="plantarflexion">跖屈</Term>。屈膝会从上端放松腓肠肌，却放松不了起于膝下方的比目鱼肌。胫骨前肌抬起前脚掌，叫<Term id="dorsiflexion">背屈</Term> [M109] [M59]。慢慢放下前脚掌时，它一边被拉长一边继续用力，这是动作的<Term id="eccentric-phase">离心阶段</Term>。<FlyTo preset="calf">看踝部肌肉</FlyTo>

直膝提踵和屈膝提踵都会用到腓肠肌和比目鱼肌。屈膝在踝还没动之前就让腓肠肌变短，改变了它能出多少力，但并没有把它“关掉” [M109]。
