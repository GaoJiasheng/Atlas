<Lang en>

**Letting the knees travel past the toes in a squat is not harmful in itself.** Hips, knees and ankles share the movement. In one study, <Num s="M33">seven</Num> trained men squatted with and without a board stopping their knees at the toes. Blocking the knees lowered the estimated load on the knee but raised the load on the hip [M33]. That study measured joint loads, not injuries, so it does not prove every depth or load is safe for everyone. Squat to a depth you can control, and get individual advice if a knee is painful or was injured before.

The long hamstrings cross both hip and knee, so bending the hip with a straight knee stretches them from both ends. Strains happen most often in fast running and in movements that overstretch the muscle; the injury often involves the region where muscle fibres join the <Term id="tendon">tendon</Term> [M34]. A sudden sharp pain at the back of the thigh, bruising or difficulty walking needs a professional assessment, not harder stretching.

Gastrocnemius and soleus pull on the heel through the Achilles tendon. Pain in the middle of the tendon that keeps returning with activity needs an assessment of symptoms and function. Clinical guidelines describe loading the tendon gradually, adjusted to each person's pain and ability, under a clinician's guidance [M35]. A general calf-raise card is not an individual treatment plan.

Posture needs context too. Gluteus medius keeps the pelvis level when you stand on one leg [M109] [M59]. A photo of someone standing, or one uneven step, cannot show that the muscle is weak or that it caused pain. Do not diagnose a problem from the direction a knee points.

This is general information, not medical advice. For pain or an injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist. Learn new movements with light loads and a qualified coach; anyone under eighteen should train with an adult supervising [M25] [M23].

<More title={{ en: "What the knee-travel study measured", zh: "膝前移研究测了什么" }}>

The men squatted to parallel with a barbell as heavy as their own body. With the board in place the estimated knee load fell and the hip load rose. The study did not follow the men to see who was injured, so it says nothing directly about injury risk [M33].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Signs that need a professional", zh: "需要找专业人员的信号" }}>

Sharp or lasting pain, swelling, bruising, sudden weakness or trouble putting weight on the leg are reasons to see a doctor or physiotherapist. What exercises to do while recovering belongs in an individual assessment [M34] [M35].

</More>

**深蹲时膝盖超过脚尖，本身并不有害。**髋、膝、踝一起分担这个动作。一项研究让<Num s="M33">七名</Num>有训练经验的男性分别在有、无木板挡住膝盖的情况下深蹲。挡住膝盖后，估算的膝负荷降低了，髋的负荷却上升了 [M33]。这项研究测的是关节负荷，不是受伤，所以不能证明任何深度、任何重量对每个人都安全。蹲到自己能控制的深度；膝盖疼痛或受过伤，要找专业人员个别评估。

长的腘绳肌同时跨髋和膝，直着膝屈髋会从两端拉长它们。拉伤最常发生在快速奔跑和过度拉伸的动作里，受伤处常常在肌纤维与<Term id="tendon">肌腱</Term>相接的一带 [M34]。大腿后面突然剧痛、出现淤青或走路困难，应找专业人员评估，而不是更用力地拉伸。

腓肠肌和比目鱼肌通过跟腱拉动脚跟。跟腱中段在活动时反复疼痛，需要评估症状和功能。临床指南描述的做法，是在专业人员指导下，按个人的疼痛和能力逐步给跟腱加负荷 [M35]。一张普通的提踵卡片不是个人的治疗方案。

看体态也要看情境。单腿站立时，臀中肌让骨盆保持水平 [M109] [M59]。一张站姿照片或一次步子不对称，都不能说明这块肌肉没力，也不能说明疼痛是它造成的。不要凭膝盖朝向哪里来判断问题。

本页是一般性知识，不是医疗建议。疼痛或受伤，请咨询医生或物理治疗师。学新动作时用轻重量，跟合格的教练学；未满十八岁的人应在成人监督下练习 [M25] [M23]。

<More title={{ en: "What the knee-travel study measured", zh: "膝前移研究测了什么" }}>

受试者用和自己体重一样重的杠铃蹲到大腿与地面平行。放上木板后，估算的膝负荷下降，髋负荷上升。研究没有追踪谁受了伤，所以它并不能直接说明受伤风险 [M33]。

</More>

剧烈或持续的疼痛、肿胀、淤青、突然无力，或者腿不敢承重，都应该去看医生或物理治疗师。恢复期间做哪些练习，应根据个人评估来定 [M34] [M35]。
