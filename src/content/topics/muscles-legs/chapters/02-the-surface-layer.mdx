<Lang en>

Start at the front of the thigh. The <Term id="quadriceps">quadriceps</Term> has <Num s="M1">four</Num> heads: rectus femoris down the middle, vastus lateralis outside, vastus medialis inside and vastus intermedius hidden underneath. They converge on the quadriceps tendon at the **patella**, or kneecap. The <Term id="patellar-ligament">patellar ligament</Term> continues the pull to the tibia. Together they straighten the knee [M1].

Rectus femoris starts on the pelvis; the other heads start on the femur. That distinction will matter when both hip and knee bend. With a relaxed knee, gently tightening the thigh lets you feel the broad front muscles without pressing on the kneecap.

Behind the hip, **gluteus maximus** has broad fibres running down and outward. Most join the iliotibial tract; deeper lower fibres reach the femur. Its pull helps you rise from a bent hip [M1]. Behind the thigh, the long head of biceps femoris ends mainly on the fibular head, whereas semitendinosus sends a long tendon to the inner tibia [M1].

**Sartorius** is the diagonal strap from the front of the pelvis to the inner upper tibia. **Tensor fasciae latae** is the short outer-hip muscle that enters the iliotibial tract. Below the knee, the gastrocnemius heads start on the femur and reach the heel through the Achilles tendon. Tibialis anterior starts beside the shin and reaches the inner midfoot [M1]. Follow those routes on the model: a tendon can carry a muscle's pull far beyond its visible belly. <FlyTo preset="calf">See the calf and shin</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "The diagonal strap", zh: "斜着走的长带" }}>

Sartorius assists hip flexion, abduction and outward rotation, and knee flexion. With the knee bent it can help turn the lower leg inward. This combination resembles bringing the foot towards the opposite thigh, but do not force a cross-legged position [M1].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A muscle belly is only part of the route", zh: "肌腹不是整条路线" }}>

The gastrocnemius heads begin above the knee and their tendon reaches behind the ankle. Tibialis anterior crosses in front of the ankle. Finding the fleshy part tells you where a muscle is; following its tendon explains which joint it can move [M1].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

先看大腿前面。<Term id="quadriceps">股四头肌</Term>有<Num s="M1">四个</Num>头：股直肌在中央，股外侧肌在外侧，股内侧肌在内侧，股中间肌藏在下面。它们汇入股四头肌腱，到达膝盖前方的**髌骨**；<Term id="patellar-ligament">髌韧带</Term>再把拉力传到胫骨，让膝伸直 [M1]。

股直肌起于骨盆，其余头起于股骨。同时屈髋和屈膝时，这个区别就很重要。膝放松后轻轻绷紧大腿，能感觉前方大片肌肉变硬，不必按压髌骨。

髋后面的**臀大肌**纤维向外下行，大部分接入髂胫束，深层下部到达股骨，帮助身体从屈髋位站起。大腿后面的**股二头肌长头**主要止于腓骨头，**半腱肌**则以长腱到达胫骨内侧 [M1]。

**缝匠肌**像斜带，从骨盆前方到胫骨上端内侧。**阔筋膜张肌**在髋外侧，向下进入髂胫束。膝下方，腓肠肌两头从股骨出发，经跟腱到脚跟；胫骨前肌从胫旁走到足内侧 [M1]。空心圆标起点，实心圆标止点。它们只代表宽附着中的位置，不是整块肌肉仅有的小点。沿模型追踪这些路线，就能看到肌腱把拉力带到肌腹以外多远，也能分清肌腹和连接骨头的细长部分。<FlyTo preset="calf">看小腿肚和胫前</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "The diagonal strap", zh: "斜着走的长带" }}>

缝匠肌协助屈髋、外展、外旋和屈膝；屈膝时还能帮助小腿内旋。这种组合像把脚移向对侧大腿，但不要强压盘腿的位置 [M1]。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A muscle belly is only part of the route", zh: "肌腹不是整条路线" }}>

腓肠肌两头从膝上方开始，肌腱经过踝后方；胫骨前肌的腱则经过踝前方。找到有肉的肌腹能认识位置，追踪肌腱才知道它能拉动哪个关节 [M1]。

</More>
