<Lang en>

Start at the front of the thigh. The quadriceps has <Num s="M109">four</Num> heads: **rectus femoris** in the middle, **vastus lateralis** on the outside, **vastus medialis** on the inside and vastus intermedius hidden underneath. They join in one tendon on the **patella**, or kneecap, and the <Term id="patellar-ligament">patellar ligament</Term> carries the pull on to the tibia. Together they straighten the knee [M109] [M59]. Rectus femoris starts on the pelvis, the vasti on the femur, so only rectus femoris also crosses the hip.

Behind the hip, **gluteus maximus** runs from the back of the pelvis and sacrum down and outward. Most of its fibres join the iliotibial tract; the deepest lower fibres reach the femur. It extends the hip, the movement that brings you upright from a bend [M109] [M59]. Put a hand on your buttock and push the leg back gently: the broad muscle firms up.

The back of the thigh holds the **hamstrings**. The long head of **biceps femoris** and **semitendinosus** start together on the sitting bone of the pelvis. Biceps femoris passes the outer side of the knee to the head of the fibula; semitendinosus sends a long, cord-like tendon to the inner side of the tibia [M109]. Bend your knee against resistance and you can feel both tendons behind it.

**Sartorius**, which Gray calls the longest muscle in the body, is a strap from the front of the pelvis to the inner side of the tibia; it helps lift and turn the thigh and bend the knee. **Tensor fasciae latae** is a short muscle at the outer front of the hip that pulls on the iliotibial tract [M109] [M59]. Below the knee, the two heads of **gastrocnemius** start on the femur and reach the heel through the Achilles tendon, and **tibialis anterior** lifts the forefoot [M109] [M59]. <FlyTo preset="calf">See the calf</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Why the long strap", zh: "为什么是一条长带" }}>

Sartorius crosses the front of the hip and the inner side of the knee. Pulling on both at once, it helps flex, abduct and turn the thigh outward at the hip and bend the knee, the combination you use to bring one foot up onto the opposite knee [M109] [M59].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A belly is only part of a muscle", zh: "肌腹只是肌肉的一部分" }}>

The bulge of the calf is high up the leg, but gastrocnemius acts on the ankle through a tendon that runs all the way to the heel. Tibialis anterior's belly lies beside the shin; its tendon crosses the front of the ankle. Finding the belly tells you where a muscle is; following its tendon tells you which joint it moves.

</More>

<Lang zh>

先看大腿前面。股四头肌有<Num s="M109">四个</Num>头：中间是**股直肌**，外侧是**股外侧肌**，内侧是**股内侧肌**，股中间肌藏在它们下面。四个头合成一条肌腱止于**髌骨**，<Term id="patellar-ligament">髌韧带</Term>再把拉力传到胫骨，一起把膝伸直 [M109] [M59]。股直肌起于骨盆，三块股肌起于股骨，所以只有股直肌还跨过髋关节。

髋后面是**臀大肌**，从骨盆和骶骨后面向外下方走。大部分纤维汇入髂胫束，最深的下部纤维止于股骨。它伸髋，也就是把弯着的身体带回直立的那个动作 [M109] [M59]。手放在臀部，轻轻把腿往后推，掌下这一大片肌肉就会变硬。

大腿后面是**腘绳肌**。**股二头肌**长头和**半腱肌**一起起于骨盆的坐骨结节。股二头肌从膝外侧经过，止于腓骨头；半腱肌发出一条细长如绳的肌腱，从膝内侧经过，止于胫骨 [M109]。对着阻力屈膝，能在膝后摸到这两条肌腱。

**缝匠肌**被格雷称为全身最长的肌肉，它是一条长带，从骨盆前面斜跨到胫骨内侧，帮助抬腿、转动大腿和屈膝。**阔筋膜张肌**是髋前外侧的一块短肌，拉着髂胫束 [M109] [M59]。膝以下，**腓肠肌**的两个头起于股骨，经跟腱到达脚跟；**胫骨前肌**负责抬起前脚掌 [M109] [M59]。<FlyTo preset="calf">看小腿肚</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Why the long strap", zh: "为什么是一条长带" }}>

缝匠肌横过髋的前面和膝的内侧，同时拉动两个关节：在髋部帮助屈髋、外展和外旋，在膝部帮助屈膝。把一只脚搭到另一侧膝上，用的就是这一组动作 [M109] [M59]。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A belly is only part of a muscle", zh: "肌腹只是肌肉的一部分" }}>

小腿肚的隆起位置很高，但腓肠肌是通过一条一直通到脚跟的肌腱来拉动踝关节的。胫骨前肌的肌腹在胫骨旁，肌腱则经过踝的前面。找到肌腹，知道肌肉在哪里；顺着肌腱，才知道它拉动哪个关节。

</More>
