<Lang en>

The toy car stands on the glass runway. It is <Term id="stationary">stationary</Term>: it is not moving. A short push from the rod behind it makes it move. A pull would do the same: a cord tied to the car and pulled would start it too. A push or a pull is a force, and a force can make a stationary object move. <FlyTo preset="runway-top">Look down on the runway</FlyTo>

Now let the car roll in at <Num s="S1">0.6 m/s</Num> and push it as it passes. In this model a push from behind makes it speed up to <Num s="S1">0.90 m/s</Num>; a push from the front slows it down to <Num s="S1">0.30 m/s</Num>; a harder push from the front stops it. A push from the side changes the direction of its <Term id="motion">motion</Term>: here the car keeps its speed of <Num s="S1">0.60 m/s</Num> but turns <Num s="S1">15°</Num> to one side. Speeding up, slowing down, stopping and turning are all effects of a force.

After the push, nothing pushes the car, yet it keeps rolling. The push did not go into the car to be slowly used up. The runway's surface gradually brings the car to a stop, as chapter 4 shows.

Some forces need contact and some do not. The rod has to touch the car. A magnet does not: with the north pole of the bar magnet facing the north pole of the car's magnet, the still car is pushed away across the gap. This is <Term id="magnetic-force">magnetic force</Term>, which you met in <TopicLink slug="magnets" ch="attract-and-repel">Like poles repel, unlike poles attract</TopicLink>.

A force can also change an object's shape. The brass weight presses the sponge flatter; lift it off and the sponge springs back. Four kinds of force act on this bench: magnetic force, <Term id="gravitational-force">gravitational force</Term>, <Term id="elastic-spring-force">elastic spring force</Term> and <Term id="frictional-force">frictional force</Term>. Each of the next chapters looks at one of them.

<More title={{"en": "Contact and non-contact forces", "zh": "接触力与非接触力"}}>

The rod pushes only while it touches the car, and friction needs two surfaces that touch. Magnetic force and gravitational force act without contact: a magnet pushes or pulls across a gap of air, and a ball falls to the ground although nothing touches it on the way.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "About this model", "zh": "关于这个模型"}}>

Each push gives the model car a fixed change of speed: <Num s="S1">0.3 m/s</Num> from behind or from the front, and <Num s="S1">0.6 m/s</Num> for the hard push from the front, which stops a car rolling at <Num s="S1">0.6 m/s</Num>. The push from the side is chosen to turn the car without changing its speed. The magnet gives the still car <Num s="S1">0.4 m/s</Num>. After each push the car slows down on the glass and stops before the end of the runway.

A push lasts only <Num s="S1">0.15 s</Num>, so its purple arrow shows only while the push happens: use Run again to see it. In the still picture, the rod shows where the push came from.

再让小车以 <Num s="S1">0.6 m/s</Num> 的速度滚过来，在它经过时推一下。在这个模型里，从后面推，它加快到 <Num s="S1">0.90 m/s</Num>；从前面推，它减慢到 <Num s="S1">0.30 m/s</Num>；从前面用力推，它就停下。从侧面推，会改变它的<Term id="motion">运动</Term>方向：这里小车的快慢不变，仍是 <Num s="S1">0.60 m/s</Num>，却向一边偏了 <Num s="S1">15°</Num>。加快、减慢、停下和改变方向，都是力的作用。

推过以后，再也没有东西推小车，它却还在往前滚。推力并没有钻进车里，被一点一点用完。是跑道的表面让它慢慢停下来，第 4 章会讲到。

有的力要接触才能起作用，有的不用。推杆必须碰到小车，磁铁却不用：条形磁铁的北极对着车尾磁铁的北极，静止的小车隔着空隙就被推开了。这是<Term id="magnetic-force">磁力</Term>，在<TopicLink slug="magnets" ch="attract-and-repel">同极相斥，异极相吸</TopicLink>里已经见过。

力还能改变物体的形状。黄铜砝码把海绵压扁；拿开砝码，海绵又弹了回来。这张实验台上有四种力：磁力、<Term id="gravitational-force">重力</Term>、<Term id="elastic-spring-force">弹簧的弹力</Term>和<Term id="frictional-force">摩擦力</Term>。接下来几章各讲其中一种。

<More title={{"en": "Contact and non-contact forces", "zh": "接触力与非接触力"}}>

推杆只有碰到小车时才在推它；摩擦力也要两个表面互相接触才有。磁力和重力不用接触就能起作用：磁铁隔着一段空气就能推或拉，球往下落时，路上没有任何东西碰它。

</More>
