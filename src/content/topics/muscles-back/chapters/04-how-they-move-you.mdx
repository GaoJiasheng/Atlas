<Lang en>

Watch the attachments before the hand. Shrugging raises the shoulder girdle with descending trapezius and levator scapulae. Drawing the blades towards the spine uses transverse trapezius and the rhomboids. Upper and lower trapezius work with serratus anterior during <Term id="upward-rotation">upward rotation</Term>, turning the shoulder socket upward as the arm rises. Rhomboids and levator help downward rotation [M47] [M2].

Latissimus dorsi and teres major pull the upper arm towards the side and backwards. That backward movement at a joint is <Term id="extension">extension</Term>. Their attachments at the front of the humerus also produce internal rotation. Infraspinatus and teres minor pull in the opposite rotational direction while helping steady the shoulder [M47] [M48].

**In pull-ups and lat pulldowns, latissimus dorsi is a prime mover at the shoulder, not merely a helper to the arms.** The elbow flexors bend the elbow and contribute substantially; the shoulder and elbow are doing different jobs [M47] [M51] [M52]. Feeling the forearms work does not show that the back is inactive.

The spinal erectors can extend the spine, and one side can help lateral bending. They also resist movement [M47] [M49]. **During a deadlift performed with a <Term id="neutral-spine">neutral spine</Term>, they work mainly <Term id="isometric">isometrically</Term> to hold that position.** The lift comes chiefly from the hips and legs, rather than repeatedly bending and straightening the lower back [M55].

The contraction poses show moving actions. The brace pose holds still, with no artificial erector shortening. Muscle effort and visible joint movement are different things.

<More title={{ en: "The model\u2019s movement limits", zh: "模型的动作限制" }}>

The shoulder girdle moves through a simplified clavicle–scapula chain. The trunk pose rotates the body as a rigid assembly; it does not reproduce extension or side bending at the spinal segments. These are illustrations of direction, not measured normal ranges. The back rig has no hip or knee articulation; full deadlift and back-extension sequences remain in the sketches and cards.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The hand can be the fixed end", zh: "手也能成为固定端" }}>

During a pulldown the trunk is relatively fixed and the arm moves. During a pull-up the hands hold a fixed bar and the body rises towards them. Latissimus still generates force between the same trunk and arm attachments [M47]. The small model poses show the shoulder action; the sketch gives the full exercise.

</More>

<Lang zh>

先看附着点，再看手。耸肩时，斜方肌降部与肩胛提肌抬高肩带；把肩胛骨拉向脊柱时，斜方肌横部与菱形肌工作。上、下斜方肌配合前锯肌，使肩胛骨<Term id="upward-rotation">上回旋</Term>，抬臂时让关节盂朝上。菱形肌与肩胛提肌帮助下回旋 [M47] [M2]。

背阔肌和大圆肌把上臂拉向身体侧面、向后。关节向后的动作叫<Term id="extension">伸展</Term>；它们在肱骨前方的附着也产生内旋。冈下肌与小圆肌产生相反方向的旋转，并帮助稳定肩关节 [M47] [M48]。

**引体向上和高位下拉时，背阔肌是肩关节的主动肌，并不是给手臂打下手。**屈肘肌也出力很大，但肩和肘做的是不同工作。感觉前臂疲劳，并不证明背肌没参与 [M51] [M52]。

每拍先看哪块骨头移动，再看高亮肌肉。肩胛骨后缩不等于上臂后伸，肩关节旋转也不等于手腕旋转；把相邻关节的任务分开，才能读懂动作。

竖脊肌能伸展脊柱，单侧能帮助侧弯，也能抵抗移动 [M47] [M49]。**保持<Term id="neutral-spine">脊柱中立位</Term>的硬拉中，它们主要<Term id="isometric">等长</Term>用力稳住脊柱。**举起重量主要靠髋和腿，并不是反复弯曲、伸直腰背 [M55]。收缩姿态展示移动；支撑姿态保持静止，没有人为的缩短。用力与看得见的关节移动，是两回事。

<More title={{ en: "The model\u2019s movement limits", zh: "模型的动作限制" }}>

肩带通过简化的锁骨—肩胛骨关节链移动。躯干姿态把身体作为刚性整体旋转，没有再现各节脊柱的伸展或侧弯。这些是方向示意，不是测得的正常幅度。背部骨架没有髋、膝关节；完整硬拉和背伸展过程由示意图与动作卡说明。

</More>

高位下拉时，躯干相对固定，手臂移动；引体向上时，双手握住固定杆，身体向手靠近。背阔肌仍在相同的躯干、上臂附着点之间产生拉力 [M47]。模型的小姿态展示肩关节作用，示意图给出完整动作。
