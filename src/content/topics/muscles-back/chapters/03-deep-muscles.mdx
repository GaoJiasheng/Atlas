<Lang en>

Fade the surface and a different set of connections appears. **Rhomboid minor** lies above **rhomboid major**; their fibres descend outward from the spinal midline to the inner border of the shoulder blade. They draw the blade inward, called <Term id="retraction">retraction</Term>, and help rotate its socket downward. **Levator scapulae** descends from the upper neck to the blade's upper inner corner and helps lift it [M47].

At the outer edge, **teres major** starts near the lower scapular angle and reaches the medial lip of the humeral groove. It helps latissimus draw the arm back, in and inward. Above it, **infraspinatus** covers the hollow below the scapular spine; **teres minor** runs along the blade's outer border. Their tendons reach the greater tubercle of the humerus. They belong to the <Term id="rotator-cuff">rotator cuff</Term>, which helps hold the humeral head against its socket, and produce <Term id="external-rotation">external rotation</Term> [M48].

**Serratus posterior inferior** is a thin sheet from the lower thoracic and upper lumbar midline to the lower ribs. Its traditional description as a rib depressor is not a settled functional finding [M47] [M50].

Deep alongside the spine, **iliocostalis**, **longissimus** and **spinalis** form the erector-spinae columns, from outside inward. This model includes lumbar and thoracic iliocostalis, thoracic longissimus and thoracic spinalis. Their longitudinal fibres extend or steady the back; one side also helps bend it sideways [M47] [M49]. The section shows their position behind the rib cage. <FlyTo preset="back">Look beneath the sheets</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Teres major is outside the cuff", zh: "大圆肌不属于肩袖" }}>

Despite the similar names, teres major is not a rotator-cuff muscle. The cuff consists of supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis [M48]. This back model includes the posterior pair; it is not a complete shoulder lesson.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A function with uncertain evidence", zh: "证据仍不确定的功能" }}>

Gray described serratus posterior inferior as drawing the lower ribs down and backward [M47]. Vilensky and colleagues questioned a respiratory role and proposed a sensory role in position awareness [M50]. A proposed alternative is also not proof. Its exercise links are context, not evidence that it can be isolated.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deep does not mean directly palpable", zh: "深层不等于能直接摸到" }}>

The rhomboids lie beneath trapezius, and the erector columns have covering fascia and muscles [M47]. A firm area felt through the skin can involve several structures. Do not press deeply around the neck or ribs to identify a single muscle.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

调淡浅层，就能看见另一组连接。**小菱形肌**在**大菱形肌**上方，纤维从脊柱中线向外下方走到肩胛骨内侧缘。它们把肩胛骨向内拉，叫<Term id="retraction">后缩</Term>，也帮助关节盂向下回旋。**肩胛提肌**从上颈部下降到肩胛骨内上角，帮助抬高它 [M47]。

外侧的**大圆肌**起于肩胛骨下角附近，止于肱骨骨沟的内侧唇，帮助背阔肌把上臂向后、向内拉并内旋。上方的**冈下肌**覆盖肩胛冈下的凹面，**小圆肌**沿肩胛骨外侧缘走行。它们的肌腱长到肱骨大结节，属于<Term id="rotator-cuff">肩袖</Term>，帮助肱骨头贴稳关节盂，也让上臂<Term id="external-rotation">外旋</Term> [M48]。

**下后锯肌**是下部胸椎、上部腰椎中线到下位肋骨的一片薄肌。传统上说它降肋，但这并不是已经确定的功能结论 [M47] [M50]。

这些深层结构不容易隔着皮肤单独辨认。先用骨骼上的位置定位，再比较纤维走向，会比只凭触感猜名字更清楚。

最深处，**髂肋肌、最长肌、棘肌**从外向内组成竖脊肌柱。模型包含髂肋肌腰部和胸部、最长肌胸部及棘肌胸部。纵行纤维帮助伸展、稳定背部，单侧也帮助侧弯 [M47] [M49]。切面显示它们在胸廓后方的位置。<FlyTo preset="back">看肌片下面</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Teres major is outside the cuff", zh: "大圆肌不属于肩袖" }}>

名字相似，大圆肌却不是肩袖肌。肩袖由冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌、肩胛下肌组成 [M48]。背部模型展示的是后方两块，不是完整的肩部课程。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A function with uncertain evidence", zh: "证据仍不确定的功能" }}>

格雷描述下后锯肌把下位肋骨向后下方拉 [M47]。Vilensky 等质疑其呼吸作用，并提出它可能参与位置感觉 [M50]。替代假说同样不等于证明；动作链接只是背景联系，不是能孤立训练它的证据。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deep does not mean directly palpable", zh: "深层不等于能直接摸到" }}>

菱形肌藏在斜方肌下，竖脊肌柱也有筋膜和肌肉覆盖 [M47]。隔着皮肤摸到的紧实区域可能包含多个结构，不要为辨认单块肌肉而深压颈部或肋骨周围。

</More>
