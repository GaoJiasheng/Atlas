<Lang en>

Fade the surface sheets and a second set of muscles appears. **Rhomboid minor**, a narrow strap, and **rhomboid major** below it run down and out from the spines of the lowest neck and upper thoracic vertebrae to the inner border of the shoulder blade. They pull the blade toward the spine, a movement called <Term id="retraction">retraction</Term>, and help turn its socket downward. **Levator scapulae** comes down from the top four neck vertebrae to the blade's upper inner corner and lifts it [M47].

Along the outer border of the shoulder blade lie three muscles that reach the arm. **Teres major** runs from the lower corner of the blade to the inner lip of the groove on the front of the humerus; it helps latissimus pull the arm down, back and inward. **Infraspinatus** fills the hollow below the scapular spine, and **teres minor** runs along the blade's outer border. Their tendons end on the back of the greater tubercle of the humerus. Both belong to the <Term id="rotator-cuff">rotator cuff</Term>, which holds the head of the humerus in its shallow socket, and both turn the arm outward, a movement called <Term id="external-rotation">external rotation</Term> [M48].

**Serratus posterior inferior** is a thin sheet from the lowest thoracic and upper lumbar spines to the lowest four ribs. Gray describes it as pulling the lower ribs down and back; whether it really helps breathing is still debated [M47] [M50].

Deepest of all, beside the spine, are the columns of the erector spinae: **iliocostalis** on the outside, **longissimus** in the middle and **spinalis** next to the bony midline. This model shows the lumbar and thoracic iliocostalis, the thoracic longissimus and the thoracic spinalis. Their fibres run up and down the back, so they straighten the spine; one side alone helps bend it sideways [M47] [M49]. <FlyTo preset="back">Look beneath the surface</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Teres major is not part of the cuff", zh: "大圆肌不属于肩袖" }}>

Despite the similar name, teres major is not a rotator-cuff muscle. The cuff is supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis [M48]. This back model shows only the two at the back of the shoulder blade; the shoulder topic has all four.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deep means hard to feel", zh: "深层，就难摸到" }}>

The rhomboids lie under trapezius, and the erector columns under fascia and other muscles [M47]. A firm area you feel through the skin usually involves several muscles at once. There is no need to press hard around the neck or ribs to find a single one.

</More>

<Lang zh>

把浅层的两大片调淡，下面的第二组肌肉就露出来了。窄窄的**小菱形肌**在上，**大菱形肌**在下，都从最下面的颈椎和上位胸椎的棘突，向外下方走到肩胛骨内侧缘。它们把肩胛骨拉向脊柱，这个动作叫<Term id="retraction">后缩</Term>，还帮关节盂向下转。**肩胛提肌**从上面四节颈椎下来，止于肩胛骨内上角，负责把它提起来 [M47]。

沿着肩胛骨外侧缘，有三块肌肉通到上臂。**大圆肌**从肩胛骨下角走到肱骨前面那条沟的内侧唇，帮背阔肌把手臂往下、往后拉，并让它内旋。**冈下肌**填满肩胛冈下方的窝，**小圆肌**沿肩胛骨外侧缘走行，两者的肌腱都止在肱骨大结节的后面。它们属于<Term id="rotator-cuff">肩袖</Term>，负责把肱骨头稳在浅浅的关节盂里，也都让上臂向外转，这个动作叫<Term id="external-rotation">外旋</Term> [M48]。

**下后锯肌**是一片薄肌，从最下面的胸椎和上位腰椎的棘突，连到最下面四根肋骨。格雷说它把下位肋骨往后下方拉；它到底是否参与呼吸，至今仍有争议 [M47] [M50]。

最深处、紧贴脊柱两旁的，是竖脊肌的几条肌柱：外侧是**髂肋肌**，中间是**最长肌**，挨着中线骨性突起的是**棘肌**。这个模型里有腰髂肋肌、胸髂肋肌、胸最长肌和胸棘肌。它们的纤维沿着背部上下纵行，所以能伸直脊柱；只有一侧收缩时，还帮躯干侧屈 [M47] [M49]。<FlyTo preset="back">看浅层下面</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Teres major is not part of the cuff", zh: "大圆肌不属于肩袖" }}>

名字只差一个字，大圆肌却不是肩袖肌。肩袖由冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌和肩胛下肌组成 [M48]。背部模型只画了肩胛骨背面的两块，四块都有的是肩部主题。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deep means hard to feel", zh: "深层，就难摸到" }}>

菱形肌藏在斜方肌下面，竖脊肌柱上面还盖着筋膜和别的肌肉 [M47]。隔着皮肤摸到的一片紧绷，通常是几块肌肉一起在用力。不必在颈部或肋骨周围使劲按，去找某一块肌肉。

</More>
