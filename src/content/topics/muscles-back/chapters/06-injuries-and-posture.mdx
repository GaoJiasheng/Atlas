<Lang en>

An ache beside the spine does not tell you which muscle is responsible. Low back pain means pain between the lower edge of the ribs and the buttocks. The World Health Organization estimates that about <Num s="M57">90 %</Num> of people with low back pain have non-specific low back pain: no particular disease or structural cause can be found [M57]. That does not make the pain imaginary, and a highlighted muscle on a model does not explain it.

Training adds load to the body's tissues, so learn the movement first and make it harder gradually [M23] [M24]. In the deadlift on the cards, the erector spinae mainly stop the back from bending while the hips and legs do the lifting. Keeping the spine neutral is the technique chosen for this lift, not a rule that the spine must never bend [M55]. At the top, stand tall; there is no need to lean back.

Posture needs the same care. A review of <Num s="M58">41</Num> systematic reviews found mixed links between spinal posture, physical work and low back pain, and no agreement that these cause it [M58]. Rounded shoulders, a visible curve in the back or a tired upper trapezius cannot be diagnosed from how they look.

The cuff muscles on the back of the shoulder blade work around a moving joint too. Their cards teach anatomy and technique; they are not rehabilitation programmes. Pain that does not go away, unusual weakness, or symptoms that spread down an arm or a leg need a professional assessment.

**This page is general information, not medical advice. For pain or an injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist.**

<More title={{ en: "Lowering still needs effort", zh: "放下也要用力" }}>

The lowering part of a row is the <Term id="eccentric-phase">eccentric phase</Term>: the pulling muscles keep working while they lengthen, so the weight comes down under control instead of dropping. Choose a load you can lower as smoothly as you lifted it [M23].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A muscle card is not a treatment plan", zh: "肌肉卡不是治疗方案" }}>

Care for low back pain depends on the person and aims to restore function and everyday activity [M57]. A diagnosis and a rehabilitation plan need far more than the name of a muscle or an exercise, and the cues on a card do not replace the advice of the clinician who examines you.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

脊柱旁边酸痛，并不能告诉你是哪块肌肉出了问题。腰痛指的是肋骨下缘到臀部之间的疼痛。世界卫生组织估计，约 <Num s="M57">90 %</Num> 的腰痛患者属于非特异性腰痛：找不到某种具体的疾病或结构上的原因 [M57]。这不等于疼痛是想象出来的，模型上高亮的某块肌肉也解释不了它。

训练会给身体组织加负荷，所以要先学会动作，再一点点加难 [M23] [M24]。动作卡上的硬拉，竖脊肌主要负责不让背弯下去，把重量举起来的是髋和腿。保持脊柱中立是这个动作选用的技术，并不是说脊柱永远不能弯 [M55]。站起来以后站直就好，不需要再往后仰。

看体态也要同样谨慎。一篇汇总了 <Num s="M58">41 篇</Num>系统综述的研究发现，脊柱姿势、体力劳动和腰痛之间的关联有的有、有的没有，也没有共识认为它们会导致腰痛 [M58]。圆肩、背部明显的弯曲、上斜方容易累，都不能只凭外表下诊断。

肩胛骨背面的肩袖肌也在一个活动的关节周围工作。它们的动作卡讲的是解剖和技术，不是康复方案。疼痛一直不消、出现异常的无力，或者症状沿着手臂、腿往下放射，都需要专业人员评估。

**本页是一般性知识，不是医疗建议。有疼痛或受伤，请咨询医生或物理治疗师。**

划船往回放的那一段叫<Term id="eccentric-phase">离心阶段</Term>：负责拉的肌肉一边被拉长一边继续用力，所以重量是被控制着放下来的，而不是掉下来。选一个能像拉起时一样平稳放下的重量 [M23]。
