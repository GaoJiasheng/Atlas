<Lang en>

An ache beside the spine does not tell you which muscle is responsible. Low back pain describes pain between the lower ribs and buttocks. The World Health Organization calls about <Num s="M57">90 %</Num> of cases nonspecific: a particular disease or structural cause cannot be identified [M57]. This does not mean the pain is imaginary, or that one highlighted muscle supplies the answer.

Training adds load to tissues, so learn the movement and increase difficulty gradually [M23] [M24]. During the controlled deadlift shown in the cards, the spinal erectors mainly resist bending while the hips and legs lift. Holding a neutral spinal position is a technique choice for this exercise, not a claim that a spine must never bend [M55]. Do not turn a final upright position into a forceful backward lean.

Posture needs similar care. A review of <Num s="M58">41</Num> systematic reviews found mixed relationships between spinal posture, physical exposures and low back pain, with no consensus that those exposures cause it [M58]. An association cannot identify the cause in an individual. Rounded shoulders, a visible back curve or a tired upper trapezius cannot be diagnosed from appearance alone.

The cuff muscles also work around a moving shoulder. Their exercise cards demonstrate anatomy and technique, not rehabilitation programmes for injured tissue. Persistent pain, unusual weakness or symptoms spreading beyond the exercise area need professional assessment rather than a self-selected muscle fix.

This chapter is general information, not medical advice. For pain or injury, consult a doctor or physiotherapist. A professional can consider the person, symptoms, history and activity together.

<More title={{ en: "Lowering still needs effort", zh: "下放仍然需要用力" }}>

The lowering part of a row is an <Term id="eccentric-phase">eccentric phase</Term>: active pulling muscles lengthen as the resistance returns. It is not a free fall. Use a load and path that can be reversed and lowered under control; the muscle map cannot determine an individual safe limit [M23].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A muscle card is not a treatment plan", zh: "肌肉卡不是治疗计划" }}>

Low back pain management depends on the individual and aims to restore function and participation [M57]. A diagnosis and a rehabilitation plan need more than the name of a muscle or an exercise. The card’s general cues should not replace the advice of the clinician assessing a painful or injured person.

</More>

<Lang zh>

脊柱旁酸痛，并不能告诉你是哪块肌肉负责。腰痛指下位肋骨与臀部之间的疼痛。世界卫生组织把约 <Num s="M57">90 %</Num> 的病例称为非特异性腰痛：不能确定某种疾病或结构原因 [M57]。这不表示疼痛是想象，也不表示高亮某块肌肉就能找到答案。

训练给组织增加负荷，应该先学动作，再逐步增加难度 [M23] [M24]。卡片中的受控硬拉，让竖脊肌抵抗弯曲，髋和腿负责举起重量。保持脊柱中立位是这个练习的技术选择，并不表示脊柱永远不能弯 [M55]。站直后不必再用力后仰。

体态也要谨慎解释。汇总 <Num s="M58">41 篇</Num>系统综述的研究发现，脊柱姿势、身体负荷与腰痛之间的关系并不一致，尚无因果共识 [M58]。关联不能确定个人病因；圆肩、背部曲线或上斜方肌疲劳，都不能只看外形就诊断。

肩袖在活动的肩关节周围工作，动作卡讲解解剖和技术，不是受伤组织的康复计划。持续疼痛、异常无力或症状扩散，应由专业人员评估，而不是自行选一块肌肉来修正。

本章是一般性知识，不是医疗建议。疼痛或受伤请咨询医生或物理治疗师，由专业人员结合症状、经历和活动判断。
