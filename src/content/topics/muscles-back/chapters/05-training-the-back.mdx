<Lang en>

The index collects every exercise in this region, grouped by barbell, dumbbell, body weight, cable and machine. Select a card for its sketch, steps, cues, mistakes and evidence. Orange marks prime movers and blue-grey their helpers. Stabilising effort is recorded separately: a muscle can work hard without producing the visible movement.

**Rows** draw the upper arm backwards and the shoulder blade inward. Barbell rows add a demand on the trunk; a supported single-arm dumbbell row reduces that demand. Inverted rows use body weight with fixed hands. **Vertical pulls**, the pull-up and front lat pulldown, draw the upper arm towards the side. Latissimus dorsi is a shoulder prime mover, while the elbow flexors assist at the elbow [M47] [M51] [M52].

The smaller actions are different: a dumbbell shrug raises the shoulder girdle; a prone Y raise involves scapular control; face pulls combine a backward pull and outward rotation; side-lying dumbbell rotation directly practises the cuff's outward rotation [M53]. Deadlifts and the hip-dominant back extension use the spinal erectors mainly to hold the back steady [M55] [M61].

Choose movements you can control. Begin with body weight or a light load and learn from a qualified coach; children and teenagers need adult supervision [M25]. Increase difficulty gradually rather than making the path faster or less stable [M23] [M24]. The cards give principles, not set-and-repetition prescriptions.

Research uses <Term id="emg">EMG</Term> to record electrical activity. Higher activity during one exercise does not prove greater future muscle growth [M9]. This chapter is general information, not medical advice; consult a doctor or physiotherapist for pain or injury.

<More title={{ en: "Other body-weight cards", zh: "其他徒手动作卡" }}>

Open the index for inverted row, prone Y raise and back extension. Their names do not make them interchangeable: one is a pull, one emphasises shoulder-blade control, and the hip-dominant extension keeps the spinal position relatively steady. The cards and sketches explain the complete setups.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What the studies actually measured", zh: "研究实际测了什么" }}>

A pull-up study examined <Num s="M51">25</Num> adults and recorded latissimus as well as arm and scapular muscle activity [M51]. A pulldown study used <Num s="M52">12</Num> healthy men at <Num s="M52">70 %</Num> of their maximum single-lift load. Pronated grip produced higher latissimus EMG than supinated grip under those conditions; grip width did not change it [M52]. These are specific laboratory comparisons, not universal exercise rankings.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A sample sequence, not a prescription", zh: "示例顺序，不是处方" }}>

One possible learning session is an easy assisted row, a main row or assisted vertical pull, then a light shrug or cuff exercise. A demanding deadlift needs its own coaching and a fresh enough trunk to control it. Adapt the selection to experience and equipment; the training data give principles rather than numerical doses [M23] [M24] [M25].

</More>

本章索引收齐本区动作，按杠铃、哑铃、徒手、绳索和器械分组。点卡片看示意图、步骤、要点、错误和证据。橙色是主动肌，蓝灰色是协同肌；稳定作用另列，因为肌肉能很用力，却不产生看得见的移动。

**划船**把上臂向后拉、肩胛骨向内拉。杠铃划船还要求稳住躯干，支撑式单臂哑铃划船减少这项要求；反向划船用自重，手保持固定。**垂直拉**包括引体向上和胸前高位下拉，背阔肌驱动肩关节，屈肘肌帮助弯肘 [M47] [M51] [M52]。

小动作的作用不同：哑铃耸肩抬肩带，俯卧Ｙ举练肩胛控制，面拉结合后拉与外旋，侧卧哑铃外旋练肩袖外旋 [M53]。硬拉和髋主导的背伸展，主要让竖脊肌稳住背部 [M55] [M61]。

选择能控制的动作。先用自重或轻重量，在合格教练指导下学习，儿童和青少年要有成人监督 [M25]。难度逐步增加，不靠加速或失稳完成 [M23] [M24]。卡片只给原则，不规定组数次数。

详细步骤要配合两格示意图阅读，先核对起始姿势，再确认结束姿势与下放路径，不要只照着某一帧摆造型。

研究用<Term id="emg">肌电</Term>记录电活动；某动作肌电更高，并不证明未来增肌更多 [M9]。本章是一般知识，不是医疗建议；疼痛或受伤请咨询医生或物理治疗师。
