<Lang en>

The list of exercises at the end of this chapter has all eleven, grouped by equipment: barbell, dumbbell, body weight, cable and machine. Press one and the model takes up the exercise, the prime movers turn orange and the helpers blue-grey, and its card opens at the top of this panel with the muscles that drive the movement, the steps, cues, common mistakes and what research shows. The first time, start with body weight or a light load and learn the movement from a qualified coach; children and teenagers should train with an adult supervising [M25].

The eleven exercises load the back in four ways. **Rows** (barbell row, one-arm dumbbell row, inverted row) pull the upper arm back while the shoulder blades draw together. **Vertical pulls** (pull-up, lat pulldown) pull a raised arm down to the side: latissimus dorsi does the main work at the shoulder, and the elbow flexors help at the elbow [M47] [M51] [M52]. **Shoulder-blade and cuff movements** (dumbbell shrug, prone Y raise, face pull, side-lying external rotation) each train one direction. **Hip hinges** (deadlift, back extension) straighten the hips while the erector spinae hold the back steady [M55] [M61].

Is one of them the right choice for everyone? No. Research measures muscle activity with surface <Term id="emg">EMG</Term>, and higher EMG is not proof that a muscle grows more [M9]. Choose exercises you can do with good form and the equipment you have; a row and a vertical pull between them cover the main jobs of the back muscles.

A few principles hold for all of them. Adults are advised to do muscle-strengthening activities on <Num s="M22">two or more days a week</Num>; Singapore's guidelines say the same [M26]. Light and heavy loads both build muscle when the sets are hard, though heavy loads build more maximal strength [M20]. Add load in small steps, only once the movement is easy [M23]. This chapter is general information, not medical advice; for pain or an injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

<More title={{ en: "A sample session", zh: "一节训练课示例" }}>

One common way to arrange a back session, not a prescription: an easy inverted row to warm up; the deadlift as the main lift while you are fresh, built up from a light load; a lat pulldown or an assisted pull-up; a one-arm dumbbell row; and a light prone Y raise or side-lying external rotation to finish. Big multi-joint lifts come first, lighter single-joint work later [M23] [M24].

本章末尾的动作列表收了全部十一个动作，按器械分组：杠铃、哑铃、徒手、绳索和器械。点其中一个，模型就做出这个动作，主动肌变成橙色，协同肌变成蓝灰色；这个动作的卡片会在本面板顶部打开，列出驱动动作的肌肉、步骤、要点、常见错误和研究发现。第一次做时，先用自重或轻重量，在合格教练指导下学动作；儿童和青少年要在成人监督下练习 [M25]。

这十一个动作从四个方向给背部加负荷。**划船**（杠铃划船、单臂哑铃划船、反向划船）把上臂往后拉，同时把两块肩胛骨往中间收。**垂直拉**（引体向上、高位下拉）把举起的手臂拉回身体两侧：主要的力气由背阔肌在肩关节上出，屈肘肌在肘关节上帮忙 [M47] [M51] [M52]。**肩胛骨和肩袖的小动作**（哑铃耸肩、俯卧 Y 字上举、面拉、侧卧外旋）各练一个方向。**髋铰链**（硬拉、背伸展）伸直的是髋，竖脊肌负责把背稳住 [M55] [M61]。

该选哪一个？没有适合所有人的唯一答案。研究用表面<Term id="emg">肌电</Term>测量肌肉活动，而肌电高并不能证明肌肉长得更多 [M9]。选你能做得标准、手边有器械的动作就好；一个划船加一个垂直拉，就涵盖了背部肌肉的主要工作。

有几条原则对所有动作都适用。建议成年人<Num s="M22">每周两天或以上</Num>做肌肉强化活动；新加坡的指南也这样建议 [M26]。只要每组都练得够吃力，轻重量和大重量都能增肌，不过大重量更能提高最大力量 [M20]。等动作变轻松了，再小步加重 [M23]。本章是一般性知识，不是医疗建议；有疼痛或受伤，请咨询医生或物理治疗师。
