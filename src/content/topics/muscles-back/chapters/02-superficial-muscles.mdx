<Lang en>

The surface layer is two broad muscles that end on different bones. **Trapezius** runs from the back of the skull, the neck ligament and the spines of the upper back to the outer third of the collarbone, the acromion and the spine of the shoulder blade; it never reaches the upper-arm bone. **Latissimus dorsi** starts over a wide area of the lower back and ends on the humerus [M47]. On the stage, hollow circles mark origins and solid circles insertions; the dashed line between them shows the direction of pull.

Trapezius has three parts, named after the way their fibres run. The descending part (upper trapezius) slopes down and out, the transverse part (middle trapezius) runs straight across, and the ascending part (lower trapezius) climbs up and out. The upper part lifts the shoulder girdle, the middle part draws the shoulder blade toward the spine and the lower part pulls it down. When the arm rises, the upper and lower parts work together to turn the blade upward [M47] [M2]. Rest a hand beside your neck and shrug: the upper part hardens. Squeeze your shoulder blades together and the middle part tightens.

Latissimus fibres gather from the spines of the lower six thoracic vertebrae, the <Term id="thoracolumbar-fascia">thoracolumbar fascia</Term>, the back of the iliac crest and the lowest ribs. They converge and twist into a flat <Term id="tendon">tendon</Term> that wraps round to the front of the upper arm and ends in the floor of the groove between the two tubercles of the humerus [M47].

So although it covers the lower back, latissimus dorsi is above all an arm muscle: it pulls the arm down to the side and back, and turns it inward. Raise one arm and press it down against your other hand: the back fold of the armpit, which it shares with teres major, goes hard. <FlyTo preset="close-back">Follow it to the arm</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "One trapezius, three pulls", zh: "一块斜方肌，三个方向的拉力" }}>

Upper trapezius is often told to “stay relaxed”, but when the arm goes overhead it has a normal job: helping to turn the shoulder blade upward [M47] [M2]. Which way the shoulder blade actually moves depends on all the muscles pulling on it at the same moment, not on one part alone.

The neck vertebrae and the back of the skull are not in this model, so the upper part's origin has no marker here; its insertion on the collarbone does.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Three tendons side by side", zh: "并排的三条肌腱" }}>

Three tendons end next to each other at the top of the humerus: latissimus dorsi in the floor of the intertubercular groove, teres major on its inner lip and pectoralis major on its outer lip [M47] [M48] [M1]. A back muscle and a chest muscle meet here, a finger's width apart.

<Lang zh>

浅层是两块宽大的肌肉，止在不同的骨头上。**斜方肌**从后脑、项韧带和上背中线的棘突出发，止于锁骨外侧三分之一、肩峰和肩胛冈，根本不到肱骨。**背阔肌**从下背很大一片区域起始，最后止在肱骨上 [M47]。舞台上，空心圆表示起点，实心圆表示止点，两者之间的虚线是拉力的方向。

斜方肌分三部，按纤维走向命名。降部（上斜方）向外下斜，横部（中斜方）横着走，升部（下斜方）向外上斜。上部上提肩带，中部把肩胛骨拉向脊柱，下部把它往下拉。手臂抬起时，上部和下部一起把肩胛骨向上旋转 [M47] [M2]。一只手放在颈旁，轻轻耸肩，上部就会变硬；把两块肩胛骨往中间夹，中部会绷紧。

背阔肌的纤维来自下面六个胸椎的棘突、<Term id="thoracolumbar-fascia">胸腰筋膜</Term>、髂嵴后部和最下面几根肋骨。它们汇聚、扭转，变成一条扁平的<Term id="tendon">肌腱</Term>，绕到上臂前面，止在肱骨两个结节之间那条沟的沟底 [M47]。

所以，背阔肌虽然铺满下背，首先却是一块管手臂的肌肉：它把手臂往身体侧面、往后拉，并让上臂内旋。举起一只手臂，用另一只手挡着往下压：腋窝后面那道肉会变硬，那里由背阔肌和大圆肌一起构成。<FlyTo preset="close-back">顺着它找到上臂</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "One trapezius, three pulls", zh: "一块斜方肌，三个方向的拉力" }}>

常有人说上斜方“要放松”，可手臂举过头顶时，它本来就有活要干：帮肩胛骨向上旋转 [M47] [M2]。肩胛骨最后往哪里动，取决于同一时刻拉着它的所有肌肉，而不是某一部分。

这个模型里没有颈椎和后脑的骨头，所以上部的起点在这里没有标记；它在锁骨上的止点有标记。

</More>
