<Lang en>

Look at the model from behind. The warm-coloured muscles stretch from the neck to the pelvis and around the shoulder blades. Ivory marks the bones, grey the neighbouring muscles, and the faint outline the skin. This is an adult male reference anatomy, not a standard that every body must match. Left and right always mean the body's own sides.

The display uses three layers. **Trapezius**, divided by fibre direction, covers the upper back; **latissimus dorsi** spreads across the lower back and narrows towards the upper arm. Beneath them, **rhomboids** and **levator scapulae** connect the spine and neck to the <Term id="scapula">scapula</Term>, or shoulder blade. **Teres major** pulls on the upper arm. **Infraspinatus** and **teres minor** rotate and steady it. The thin **serratus posterior inferior** attaches to the lower ribs. Deepest are the long **spinal erector** columns beside the spine [M47] [M48].

An <Term id="origin">origin</Term> and an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term> name a muscle's attachments. The origin is usually the more fixed end; the insertion is usually the more mobile end. Those roles can reverse when the hand is fixed, as during a pull-up. Muscles pull between attachments; they can also resist movement without visibly shortening [M49].

Separate the jobs before memorising names: some back muscles move the shoulder blade, others move the arm, and others support or move the spine. Follow a muscle's dots and dashed line to see which bones it connects. <FlyTo preset="back">Start from behind</FlyTo>

<Lang zh>

从背后看模型，暖色肌肉从颈部延伸到骨盆，包围着肩胛骨。象牙色是骨骼，灰色是邻区肌肉，淡轮廓是皮肤。这是成年男性的参考解剖，并不是所有身体都必须符合的标准。文中的左右，一律指身体自己的左右。

展示分三个层次。**斜方肌**按纤维方向分部，覆盖上背；**背阔肌**铺在下背，向上臂收窄。它们下面，**菱形肌**和**肩胛提肌**把脊柱、颈部连到<Term id="scapula">肩胛骨</Term>。**大圆肌**拉动上臂；**冈下肌**和**小圆肌**帮助旋转并稳定上臂。薄薄的**下后锯肌**附着在下位肋骨。最深的是脊柱两侧纵行的**竖脊肌**肌柱 [M47] [M48]。

<Term id="origin">起点</Term>和<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>是肌肉附着端的名称。起点通常较固定，止点通常更容易移动。但手固定时，例如引体向上，活动端可以反过来。肌肉在附着点之间产生拉力，也能不明显缩短而抵抗移动 [M49]。

剥层时骨骼留在原位，方便比较每块肌肉的深浅。恢复完整视图后，再沿骨架追踪拉力。

先分清工作，再记名字：有些背肌移动肩胛骨，有些移动手臂，还有些支撑或移动脊柱。沿着圆点与虚线看，就知道它连着哪些骨头。点选肌肉能打开它的卡片，本章索引也收齐了这些结构。<FlyTo preset="back">从背后看起</FlyTo>
