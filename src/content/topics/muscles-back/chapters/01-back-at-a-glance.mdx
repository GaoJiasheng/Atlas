<Lang en>

The model on the stage is a man's upper body, seen from behind and built from a real anatomical scan. The back muscles are drawn in warm colours, the bones in ivory, the neighbouring muscles of the shoulder, arm, belly and hip in grey, and the skin as a faint outline. Left and right always mean the body's own left and right.

The muscles lie in three layers. On the surface are two big sheets: **trapezius**, a diamond over the upper back and the back of the neck, and **latissimus dorsi**, which spreads across the lower back and narrows into a tendon on the upper arm. The middle layer holds the muscles that move the <Term id="scapula">shoulder blade</Term> (the **rhomboids** and **levator scapulae**), three muscles that run from the shoulder blade to the arm (**teres major**, **infraspinatus** and **teres minor**) and the thin **serratus posterior inferior** over the lowest ribs. The deep layer is the **erector spinae**: long columns on either side of the spine [M47] [M48].

Every muscle has an <Term id="origin">origin</Term>, the end that usually stays still, and an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term>, the end it pulls. A muscle can only pull. Which end moves depends on what is held still: in a pull-up the hands grip a fixed bar, so the body rises towards the arms instead of the arms moving [M91].

One line for each group: trapezius, the rhomboids and levator scapulae move the shoulder blade; latissimus dorsi and teres major pull the arm down and back; infraspinatus and teres minor turn the arm outward and steady the shoulder; the erector spinae straighten the spine and hold it steady [M47] [M48]. <FlyTo preset="back">See the back</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Where this model comes from", zh: "这个模型从哪里来" }}>

The meshes come from Z-Anatomy, an open atlas built on BodyParts3D, which was made from MRI scans of one adult male volunteer <Num s="M4">at 2 mm intervals</Num>. The model stands <Num s="M4">1.71 m</Num> tall. Atlas selected the back structures, simplified them and added fibre directions and attachment points [M3]. The model and its geometry are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The neck vertebrae and the back of the skull are not part of this model. The neck attachments of trapezius, levator scapulae and rhomboid minor are described in the text and on the muscle sheets, but have no markers on the stage.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Peel, not explode", zh: "剥层，而不是拆开" }}>

In this topic the explode slider is called PEEL. It lifts the surface layer first, then the middle, then the deep layer, the order in which an anatomist would uncover them. The layers are a teaching order: the middle layer, for example, groups muscles that do not form one continuous sheet in the body.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

舞台上的模型是一名男性的上半身，从背后看，根据真实的解剖扫描数据建成。背部肌肉画成暖色，骨骼是象牙色，肩、上臂、腹部和臀部的邻近肌肉画成灰色，皮肤是一层淡淡的轮廓。文中说的左和右，一律指身体自己的左右。

这些肌肉分三层。最表面是两大片：**斜方肌**，像一块菱形盖住上背和颈后；**背阔肌**，铺满下背，再收窄成一条肌腱连到上臂。中层有移动<Term id="scapula">肩胛骨</Term>的肌肉（**菱形肌**和**肩胛提肌**），三块从肩胛骨长到上臂的肌肉（**大圆肌**、**冈下肌**和**小圆肌**），还有盖在最下面几根肋骨上的薄薄的**下后锯肌**。深层是**竖脊肌**：脊柱两旁纵行的长肌柱 [M47] [M48]。

每块肌肉都有<Term id="origin">起点</Term>，通常是不动的那一端；还有<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>，是它拉动的那一端。肌肉只能拉，不能推。哪一端动，要看哪一端被固定：做引体向上时，双手抓住固定的单杠，于是不是手臂往下走，而是身体被拉向手臂 [M91]。

每组一句话：斜方肌、菱形肌和肩胛提肌移动肩胛骨；背阔肌和大圆肌把手臂往下、往后拉；冈下肌和小圆肌让上臂外旋，并稳住肩关节；竖脊肌伸直脊柱，并把它稳住 [M47] [M48]。<FlyTo preset="back">看背面</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Where this model comes from", zh: "这个模型从哪里来" }}>

网格来自 Z-Anatomy，一个以 BodyParts3D 为基础的开源解剖图谱；BodyParts3D 则来自一名成年男性志愿者的核磁共振扫描，<Num s="M4">每隔 2 毫米一层</Num>。模型身高 <Num s="M4">1.71 米</Num>。Atlas 选取了背部结构，做了简化，并加上纤维方向和附着点 [M3]。模型及其几何数据按 CC BY-SA 4.0 许可共享。

这个模型里没有颈椎和后脑的骨头。斜方肌、肩胛提肌和小菱形肌在颈部的附着，正文和肌肉卡里都有说明，但舞台上没有标记。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Peel, not explode", zh: "剥层，而不是拆开" }}>

在这个主题里，拆开滑块叫“剥层”。它先抬起浅层，再是中层，最后是深层，就像解剖时一层层揭开的顺序。分层是讲解用的顺序：比如中层放在一起的几块肌肉，在身体里并不是连成一片的。

</More>
