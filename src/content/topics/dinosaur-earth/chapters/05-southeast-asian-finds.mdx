<Lang en>

The large map shows land at <Num s="S29">70</Num> million years ago. The small map now shows selected Mesozoic finds of different ages. Each card keeps its own age, so these animals did not all live together. Singapore is a modern reference point to help you locate the region. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Source S29">S29</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S64" aria-label="Source S64">S64</button></sup>

In northeastern Thailand, Sirindhorna came from the Khok Kruat Formation in Nakhon Ratchasima Province. The original paper places it in the Aptian, part of the Early Cretaceous. The card names the region rather than an exact dig site. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="Source S20">S20</button></sup>

In Laos, Early Cretaceous Ichthyovenator was described from partly connected bones from behind the head. The researchers did not have a complete skeleton. In Pahang, Malaysia, dinosaur teeth offer another kind of clue. Pahang has a region card without a dot or guessed location. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="Source S21">S21</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22" aria-label="Source S22">S22</button></sup>

The museum-origin card describes sauropod fossils displayed in Singapore but discovered in Wyoming, in the United States. Fossils can travel to museums. To understand their story, ask what material was found, which rock layers held it and where it was discovered. The exhibition address answers a different question. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S57" aria-label="Source S57">S57</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Do cards and dots describe the same finds?", zh: "卡片和点是同一发现吗？" }}>

Named discovery cards come from their own research sources. We have not identified any particular PBDB dot as the same specimen. The Thailand and Laos finds each have their own rock-layer evidence; the large map’s age does not date either one. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="Source S20">S20</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="Source S21">S21</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Protecting the Pahang site", zh: "保护彭亨发现地" }}>

The researcher said the location was confidential for protection. For that reason, the public dataset leaves out all Malaysian fossil positions. A guessed centre point would give a misleading location. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22" aria-label="Source S22">S22</button></sup>

</More>

A tooth and connected bones provide different clues. Neither tells us everything about missing body parts. 大图显示 <Num s="S29">7000</Num> 万年前的陆地。小图现在显示不同年代的中生代发现。各张卡保留自己的年代，这些动物并非全都一起生活。新加坡是现代参照点，帮助你找到这个地区。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="来源 S29">S29</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S64" aria-label="来源 S64">S64</button></sup>

泰国东北部的诗琳通龙 Sirindhorna，发现于呵叻府的 Khok Kruat 组。原论文把它放在早白垩世的阿普特期。卡片说明地区，没有标出精确挖掘点。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="来源 S20">S20</button></sup>

老挝早白垩世的鱼猎龙 Ichthyovenator，最初根据部分相连的头部以后的骨骼来描述。研究者并没有完整骨架。马来西亚彭亨的恐龙牙齿则提供另一类线索。彭亨只有地区卡，没有定位点，也没有猜测的位置。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="来源 S21">S21</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22" aria-label="来源 S22">S22</button></sup>

馆藏来源卡介绍新加坡展示的蜥脚类化石，它们发现于美国怀俄明州。化石可以被运到博物馆。要读懂它的故事，可以问：找到什么材料？来自哪层岩石？在哪里发现？展出地址回答的是另一个问题。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S57" aria-label="来源 S57">S57</button></sup>
