<Lang en>

The background map stays at <Num s="S29">70</Num> million years ago while the modern inset shows selected discoveries from across the Mesozoic. These finds did not all live together. Their cards describe different geological evidence and retain their own ages. Singapore provides a modern reference for reading the region; its marker is not a dinosaur excavation site. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Source S29">S29</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S64" aria-label="Source S64">S64</button></sup>

In northeastern Thailand, Sirindhorna was described from the Khok Kruat Formation in Nakhon Ratchasima Province. The original paper places the material in the Early Cretaceous and uses the Aptian stage. The Thailand card names the region without claiming a precise excavation point. Its geological account is independent of the older-land sample displayed behind the inset. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="Source S20">S20</button></sup>

Laos preserves Early Cretaceous Ichthyovenator remains. The original description relied on part of the body: partially associated bones from behind the head. It did not begin with a complete skeleton. In Pahang, Malaysia, dinosaur teeth provide another kind of evidence. The researcher explained that the site was kept confidential for protection, so the Pahang card identifies a region and has no pinpoint location. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="Source S21">S21</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22" aria-label="Source S22">S22</button></sup>

The museum-origin card adds another distinction. Sauropod fossils displayed in Singapore were discovered in Wyoming in the United States. A collection can travel to a museum without turning its exhibition address into the place where it fossilized. Read each regional card for the kind of evidence it describes, and compare its stated precision with the background map rather than treating the cards as a single ancient community. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S57" aria-label="Source S57">S57</button></sup>

<More title={{"en": "Regional cards and database dots", "zh": "区域卡与数据库点"}}>

The named discovery cards are based on their cited research accounts. They do not claim that a particular PBDB dot is the same specimen. The Thailand and Laos descriptions have different geological evidence and must not be dated from the background sample. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20">S20</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21">S21</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Why Pahang has no dot", "zh": "彭亨为何没有点"}}>

The original researcher stated that the find site was confidential for protection. This topic therefore withholds all Malaysian fossil positions from the public dataset, and supplies a region-only Pahang card. A guessed centroid would undo that distinction. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22">S22</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{"en": "What a fragment can establish", "zh": "片段证据能说明什么"}}>

A tooth and a set of associated bones answer different questions. Neither is permission to invent missing body parts. <Lang zh>

背景地图停在 <Num s="S29">7000</Num> 万年前，现代小图则展示跨中生代的所选发现。这些发现并非同时生存，各张卡片保留自己的地层年代和证据。新加坡只是帮助阅读区域的现代参照，标记并不代表恐龙挖掘地点。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="来源 S29">S29</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S64" aria-label="来源 S64">S64</button></sup>

泰国东北部的诗琳通龙 Sirindhorna，描述材料来自呵叻府的 Khok Kruat 组。原论文把它归入早白垩世，采用阿普特期。泰国卡说明地区，不声称给出精确挖掘坐标；其地层依据与大图当前采用的陆地样本分别读取。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="来源 S20">S20</button></sup>

老挝的早白垩世鱼猎龙 Ichthyovenator，原始描述依据部分相连的头后骨骼，并非完整骨架。马来西亚彭亨的恐龙牙齿则是另一类证据。研究者说明地点为保护而保密，因此彭亨只有地区卡，不用州府位置代替，也没有猜测的挖掘点。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="来源 S21">S21</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22" aria-label="来源 S22">S22</button></sup>

馆藏来源卡又提醒我们区分发现与展出：新加坡展示的蜥脚类化石发现于美国怀俄明州。标本可以进入博物馆，展出地址却不会因此成为原地出土证据。读区域资料时，要分别看材料是什么、来自哪一地层，以及地点实际精确到什么程度，不能把这些卡拼成同一群远古动物。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S57" aria-label="来源 S57">S57</button></sup>

<More title={{"en": "Regional cards and database dots", "zh": "区域卡与数据库点"}}>

具名发现卡来自各自引用的研究介绍，没有声称某个 PBDB 点就是同一件标本。泰国与老挝材料各有地层依据，不能从背景地图样本推定它们的准确年代。<sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20">S20</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21">S21</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Why Pahang has no dot", "zh": "彭亨为何没有点"}}>

原研究者说明，发现地点为保护而保密。本题因此不发布任何马来西亚化石精确位置，彭亨只保留地区卡。用猜测的中心点代替，仍会误导读者。<sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22">S22</button></sup>

</More>
