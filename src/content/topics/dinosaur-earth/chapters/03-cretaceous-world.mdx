<Lang en>

The Cretaceous follows the Jurassic. The cited geological chart places its beginning at <Num s="S1">143.1</Num> million years ago and its end at <Num s="S1">66</Num> million years ago. Our samples at <Num s="S29">130</Num> and <Num s="S29">100</Num> million years ago lie within that span. Compare their water and exposed land without mistaking either sample for the whole period. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button></sup>

Each large map comes from the same reconstruction model and the same rule for distinguishing land from non-land. The changing shapes offer a way to compare geographical context. They are not photographs, exact measured coastlines or a continuous animation of continental motion. Keeping the model and method consistent makes the samples useful to compare without making them certain in every detail. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Source S29">S29</button></sup>

Now open the Thailand card in the modern inset. Sirindhorna was described from the Early Cretaceous Khok Kruat Formation in Nakhon Ratchasima Province. The Laos card describes Early Cretaceous Ichthyovenator material. Each account has its own geological evidence. The age of the background map does not become the measured age of these fossils, and the cards do not claim exact excavation coordinates. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="Source S20">S20</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="Source S21">S21</button></sup>

The fossil dots also retain the age bounds published for their records. A broad interval may overlap more than one browsing window. It does not mean that an individual animal lived throughout that interval, or that the middle of the interval is its known death date. Read the map sample, the record's age range and the modern location separately. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="Source S24">S24</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="Source S65">S65</button></sup>

Numerical estimates can be revised; their stated uncertainty does not belong to every fossil record. 白垩纪接在侏罗纪之后。所引年代表把起点列为 <Num s="S1">143.1</Num> 百万年前，终点为 <Num s="S1">66</Num> 百万年前。本章 <Num s="S29">1.3</Num> 亿和 <Num s="S29">1</Num> 亿年前的地图都在这个时期之内。它们是不同年代的样本，不能用其中一张代表整个白垩纪。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button></sup>

大图沿用同一个复原模型，也沿用同一套水陆分类方法，便于比较长期变化。轮廓不是远古照片，也不是逐点测得的古岸线。模型提供研究背景，却不保证每段海岸的细节准确；地图切换也不表示已经算出大陆连续移动的过程。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="来源 S29">S29</button></sup>

现代小图中的泰国卡介绍诗琳通龙 Sirindhorna，材料来自呵叻府早白垩世的 Khok Kruat 组。老挝卡介绍早白垩世的鱼猎龙 Ichthyovenator。各自的地层依据要独立阅读，论文所用的地层名称应当保留，不能简单换成地图标签。背景地图的样本年龄不能成为这些化石的测年结果，区域卡也没有标出精确挖掘坐标。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="来源 S20">S20</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="来源 S21">S21</button></sup>

化石点同样保留文献提供的年龄上下界。较宽的范围可能与不同浏览窗相交，不能理解为一只动物活过了整个区间，更不能把区间中点当作已知死亡日期。地图年龄、记录年龄与今天的发现位置，应当分别读清。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="来源 S24">S24</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="来源 S65">S65</button></sup>
