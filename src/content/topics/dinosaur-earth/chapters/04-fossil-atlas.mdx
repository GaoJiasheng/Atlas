<Lang en>

A fossil dot is an entrance to a record, not a count of animals. The Paleobiology Database, or PBDB, organizes published evidence into a <Term id="term-collection">collection</Term>, a group of geographically related fossil information. A collection may contain several <Term id="term-occurrence">occurrences</Term>: records of particular fossil taxa. The small map draws a symbol for each selected collection while keeping the occurrences separate in its details. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="Source S24">S24</button></sup>

Choose a time and the inset looks for published age ranges that overlap the browsing window. A <Term id="term-age-range">range</Term> preserves an older and a younger bound. It does not supply a precise death date. Separate occurrence intervals remain separate, so a gap between them is not silently filled. The window is a way to browse the evidence, not an extra measurement of uncertainty. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="Source S65">S65</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S67" aria-label="Source S67">S67</button></sup>

These dots come from fixed, selected snapshots. The global query was bounded and sorted by age span; a regional query added evidence from Thailand, Laos and Malaysia. Malaysian fossil locations are suppressed before publication. The resulting public selection contains <Num s="S65,S66">1,274</Num> collections. That number describes this selection, not the number of dinosaurs, species or places where dinosaurs ever lived. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="Source S65">S65</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S66" aria-label="Source S66">S66</button></sup>

Clusters and blank patches need the same care. Burial, preservation, exposure, research and database selection affect which fossils become visible as records. Dense dots cannot rank countries by dinosaur abundance, and empty map space cannot establish an ancient habitat boundary. Open a marker to read its individual ages and references before drawing a conclusion from its position. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Source S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="Source S24">S24</button></sup>

The inset's window extends <Num s="S67">5</Num> Ma on either side of the selected age; this width is an interface choice. The collection's records are visible when an interval overlaps that window, including its endpoints.

化石点是进入资料的入口，不是动物数量。古生物数据库 PBDB 把有地理联系的化石资料组成一个<Term id="term-collection">采集记录组</Term>。一个组可以包含多条<Term id="term-occurrence">化石出现记录</Term>，分别记述某类化石。小图按采集记录组画符号，详情仍逐条保留记录，不能把点数当作恐龙只数。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="来源 S24">S24</button></sup>

选择年代后，小图筛出年龄范围与浏览窗相交的资料。<Term id="term-age-range">年龄范围</Term>保留较老与较年轻的界限，不提供精确死亡日期。同组记录之间若有空档，筛选不会擅自填满。浏览窗只是查看方式，并非新测出的年代误差。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="来源 S65">S65</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S67" aria-label="来源 S67">S67</button></sup>

本题使用固定快照。全球查询有数量上限，并按年龄跨度排序；区域查询补入泰国、老挝与马来西亚资料。发布前抑制所有马来西亚化石精确位置，公开选择留下 <Num s="S65,S66">1274</Num> 个采集记录组。这是本次资料的规模，不是恐龙、物种或全部发现地点的总数。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="来源 S65">S65</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S66" aria-label="来源 S66">S66</button></sup>

密集点群与空白都要谨慎解释。埋藏、保存、露出地表、研究工作及资料选择，都会影响哪些发现进入数据库。不能按点群给国家的恐龙数量排名，也不能把空白当作远古栖息地边界。先打开点，读各条年龄与文献，再解释位置。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="来源 S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="来源 S24">S24</button></sup>
