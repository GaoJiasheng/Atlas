At about 66 Ma, the end-Cretaceous <Term id="term-extinction">extinction</Term> eliminated all <Term id="term-non-avian">non-avian dinosaurs</Term>: dinosaurs other than birds. Some bird lineages survived. That distinction matters when describing what ended and what continued. The selected age now reads 66 Ma, while the ancient main map remains the actual 70 Ma sample. This sequence does not contain an exact impact-age reconstruction. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S11" aria-label="Open source S11">S11</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S8" aria-label="Open source S8">S8</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Open source S29">S29</button></sup>

An <Term id="term-asteroid">asteroid</Term> struck Earth at the site of the <Term id="term-chicxulub">Chicxulub crater</Term>, in what is now Mexico. The impactor was roughly <Num s="S13">10</Num> kilometres across; the crater was roughly <Num s="S13">180</Num> to <Num s="S13">200</Num> kilometres across. The inset's locator marks an approximate modern position. Its dot is not the crater's shape, and it has not been moved onto the reconstructed ancient land. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S61" aria-label="Open source S61">S61</button></sup>

Evidence connects the impact with the extinction. A globally distributed layer of impact ejecta, its timing and the pattern of ecological change support the impact as the trigger. Ejecta are material thrown out by an impact. These linked observations provide more than a crater happening to be near a boundary in time; they connect the event to a widespread disruption. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S12" aria-label="Open source S12">S12</button></sup>

Volcanic activity in the Deccan also crossed this interval. Researchers investigate its timing and the release of gases that affect climate. Those questions should be read alongside the impact evidence, with each kind of evidence identified. A climate model can explore possible consequences under stated conditions; its calculated temperatures are not direct measurements of ancient weather. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S15" aria-label="Open source S15">S15</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S51" aria-label="Open source S51">S51</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S13" aria-label="Open source S13">S13</button></sup>

支持撞击触发灭绝的证据，包括全球相关的喷出物层、相符的年代与生态变化模式。喷出物是撞击抛出的材料。这些证据把事件与广泛影响联系起来，不能只把它理解为“恰好有一个年代相近的坑”。<sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S12" aria-label="打开来源 S12">S12</button></sup>
