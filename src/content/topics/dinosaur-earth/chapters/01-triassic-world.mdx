<Lang en>

Start with the land. The Triassic began about 252 Ma, before our 250 Ma map. By about 230 Ma, in the Late Triassic, there are well-established dinosaur fossils. The first map therefore shows an earlier time than these dinosaur records. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S52" aria-label="Source S52">S52</button></sup>

Much of the connected land belonged to <Term id="term-pangaea">Pangaea</Term>, a supercontinent made of land that is separated today. Geological evidence tells us that these lands were joined and later broke apart. The ground-height model supplies the particular coastlines shown here. Today’s coastlines and national borders do not belong on this ancient map. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S62" aria-label="Source S62">S62</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Source S29">S29</button></sup>

The small map adds selected fossil discoveries whose published ages overlap the time you are browsing. Gaps can have many causes: fossils may not survive, rocks may remain buried, or scientists may not have studied an area. Some records are also outside our selection. The dots cannot draw the edges of dinosaur habitats. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Source S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="Source S24">S24</button></sup>

Rock layers help us put fossils in order. In sedimentary layers that have not been disturbed or turned upside down, a lower layer was deposited first and is older. Layer thickness alone cannot tell us how many years passed. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="Source S5">S5</button></sup>

先看陆地。三叠纪约在 2.52 亿年前开始，比本题 250 Ma 的地图更早。到了约 2.3 亿年前的晚三叠世，已有确立的恐龙化石记录。因此，第一张地图显示的时间，比这些恐龙记录更早。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S52" aria-label="来源 S52">S52</button></sup>

当时大片相连的陆地属于<Term id="term-pangaea">盘古大陆</Term>。今天分开的许多陆块，曾连成这片超级大陆。地质证据告诉我们它们曾相连，后来又分开；本图的具体海岸轮廓则来自地面高度模型。今天的海岸和国界不属于这张古地图。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S62" aria-label="来源 S62">S62</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="来源 S29">S29</button></sup>

小图加入了部分化石发现。记录的年龄范围与浏览年代相交时，便会显示。地图空白有许多原因：化石可能没有保存，岩层可能仍被埋着，某个地区也可能还没被研究。本题还只选取部分记录。因此，点群不能画出恐龙栖息地的边界。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="来源 S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="来源 S24">S24</button></sup>

岩层帮助我们排列化石的先后。在没有受扰动或倒转的沉积岩层中，下层先沉积，年代比上层更老。只看层厚，不能知道经过了多少年。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="来源 S5">S5</button></sup>
