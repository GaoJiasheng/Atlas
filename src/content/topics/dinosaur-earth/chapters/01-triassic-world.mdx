<Lang en>

Begin with the land, before looking for dinosaur dots. Our 250 Ma sample lies within the Triassic. The period had already begun, at about 252 Ma on the cited time chart. By about 230 Ma in the Late Triassic, well-established dinosaurs appear in the fossil record. Those statements concern different moments: the first map is not a picture of the first dinosaur. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Open source S1">S1</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S52" aria-label="Open source S52">S52</button></sup>

Much of the connected land belonged to <Term id="term-pangaea">Pangaea</Term>, a supercontinent joining land that is separated today. The broad idea of connection and later breakup comes from geological evidence; the particular outlines on this map come from the selected elevation model. A familiar modern coastline would give the wrong impression. We also leave today's national borders off the ancient main map. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S62" aria-label="Open source S62">S62</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Open source S29">S29</button></sup>

Connected land is a setting, not a complete map of animal life. The modern inset marks selected discoveries when their published ages overlap the browsing window. A gap may reflect preservation, exposure, research or the selection of records. It cannot establish that an area was empty, and the pattern cannot be turned into a measured boundary of dinosaur habitat. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Open source S2">S2</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="Open source S24">S24</button></sup>

Rocks supply another way to put evidence in order. In an undisturbed sedimentary sequence that has not been overturned, a lower deposited bed is older than the bed above it. This compares order; it does not turn the thickness of a layer into a number of years. Read that evidence beside the map rather than asking the map alone to date a fossil. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="Open source S5">S5</button></sup>

<Lang zh>

先看陆地，再加入恐龙发现证据。250 Ma 是三叠纪内的一张地图样本；按所引年代表，三叠纪约在 2.52 亿年前就开始了。到约 2.3 亿年前的晚三叠世，已有确立的恐龙记录。因此，最早显示的地图不能被当作“第一只恐龙出现时”的画面。<sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="打开来源 S1">S1</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S52" aria-label="打开来源 S52">S52</button></sup>

当时大片相连的陆地属于<Term id="term-pangaea">盘古大陆</Term>，今天分开的许多陆块曾连在一起。相连与后来分裂的概括来自地质研究；眼前具体边线则来自所选高程模型。把今天熟悉的海岸或国界搬上来，会混淆不同年代的地理。<sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S62" aria-label="打开来源 S62">S62</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="打开来源 S29">S29</button></sup>

相连的陆地只提供背景，并不等于恐龙的完整分布图。现代小图根据已发表的年龄区间筛选发现记录。保存、岩层出露、研究工作以及本题选取资料的范围，都会留下空白；不能据此断定某片陆地没有动物，也不能把点群边缘画成测量过的栖息地。<sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="打开来源 S2">S2</button></sup> <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="打开来源 S24">S24</button></sup>

岩层还能帮助排列证据。在未受扰动、没有倒转的沉积序列中，下方先沉积的地层比上方更老。这讲的是先后顺序，不能凭某层较厚，就说它代表较长的时间。古地图与地层证据回答不同问题，需要放在一起理解，不能只看地图替化石定年。<sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="打开来源 S5">S5</button></sup>
