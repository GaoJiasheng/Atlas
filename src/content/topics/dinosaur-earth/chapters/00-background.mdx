<Lang en>

250 <Term id="term-ma">Ma</Term> means 250 million years ago. Bigger numbers take us further into the past. Zero means today. The <Term id="term-mesozoic">Mesozoic</Term> includes three periods: the <Term id="term-triassic">Triassic</Term>, <Term id="term-jurassic">Jurassic</Term> and <Term id="term-cretaceous">Cretaceous</Term>. Each has its own beginning and end. Our first map, at 250 Ma, shows a time within the Triassic. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button></sup>

The large map is a <Term id="term-palaeogeography">palaeogeographic reconstruction</Term>. Scientists use evidence and a model to work out where land and sea may have been. Here, land shapes come from the model’s ground heights. The map is not a photograph of the past. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Source S29">S29</button></sup>

The small map shows where finds were recorded on today’s Earth. The continents have moved, so these modern locations cannot be placed straight onto the ancient map. Singapore helps you find your way around the region; its marker is not a dinosaur dig site. Regional cards can describe finds from different ages, rather than animals that all lived together. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S64" aria-label="Source S64">S64</button></sup>

A <Term id="term-fossil">fossil</Term> is evidence of past life. Bones and teeth are remains of bodies. Footprints are traces of activity. Only some evidence survives and is found, and this map shows only selected published records. A blank area does not prove that dinosaurs never lived there. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Source S2">S2</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "What can a footprint tell us?", zh: "足迹能告诉我们什么？" }}>

A footprint shows that an animal touched a surface. A tooth tells us about part of its body. Each gives different clues. A single body part, or a general drawing of a footprint, cannot tell us every detail of a whole animal. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Source S2">S2</button></sup>
