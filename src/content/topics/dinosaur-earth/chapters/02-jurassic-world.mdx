<Lang en>

The Jurassic sits between the Triassic and the Cretaceous. The chart used here places its boundaries at <Num s="S1">201.4</Num> and <Num s="S1">143.1</Num> million years ago. Our first Jurassic land sample is <Num s="S29">200</Num> million years old. A sample inside a period does not mark the beginning of that period: the map sequence and the geological chart answer different questions. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button></sup>

Compare the samples at <Num s="S29">200</Num>, <Num s="S29">170</Num> and <Num s="S29">150</Num> million years ago. The changing pattern belongs to the long breakup of Pangaea. These are separate reconstructions of land derived from modelled elevation. The screen does not calculate continents sliding continuously between them. Watch the map-sample reading whenever you choose a time between the supplied samples. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Source S29">S29</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S62" aria-label="Source S62">S62</button></sup>

The small map adds a different kind of information. Its dots stay at modern find locations. Their published age ranges decide which records enter the browsing window. The dots have not been carried backwards onto the ancient land. A nearby dot and a coastline on the large map therefore cannot establish an animal's exact ancient position. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="Source S65">S65</button></sup>

Open the museum-origin card. Singapore's natural history museum displays diplodocid sauropod fossils from Wyoming's Jurassic Morrison Formation. Their place of exhibition and place of discovery are different. The reconstruction provides broad geographical context; its selected age cannot assign a precise age to an individual museum specimen. Follow the specimen's own source instead. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S17" aria-label="Source S17">S17</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S57" aria-label="Source S57">S57</button></sup>

侏罗纪位于三叠纪之后、白垩纪之前。这里采用的年代表把两端列为 <Num s="S1">201.4</Num> 和 <Num s="S1">143.1</Num> 百万年前。本章最早的地图样本是 <Num s="S29">2</Num> 亿年前，处于侏罗纪之内，并非时期起点。先分清地图的样本年龄与地质时期界线，再读陆地的变化。现代国家名称也不应变成远古大陆上的国界。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button></sup>

依次对照 <Num s="S29">2</Num> 亿、<Num s="S29">1.7</Num> 亿和 <Num s="S29">1.5</Num> 亿年前的样本，可以观察盘古大陆逐渐分开的格局。陆地轮廓来自模型高程，不是远古照片。样本之间没有计算连续的大陆运动；选择中间年代时，要同时看地图样本读数。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="来源 S29">S29</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S62" aria-label="来源 S62">S62</button></sup>

现代小图的点始终留在今天的发现地点，发表的年龄范围则决定哪些记录进入浏览窗。这些点没有被搬到远古大陆上。不能把大图的一段海岸与小图的一个点直接拼合，便认定某只恐龙当时所在的准确位置。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="来源 S65">S65</button></sup>
