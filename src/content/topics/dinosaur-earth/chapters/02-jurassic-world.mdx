<Lang en>

The Jurassic came after the Triassic and before the Cretaceous. The chart used here puts its beginning at <Num s="S1">201.4</Num> million years ago and its end at <Num s="S1">143.1</Num> million years ago. Our first Jurassic map is <Num s="S29">200</Num> million years old, so it shows a time within the period. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button></sup>

Compare the maps at <Num s="S29">200</Num>, <Num s="S29">170</Num> and <Num s="S29">150</Num> million years ago. They show parts of the long breakup of Pangaea. Each map is a separate reconstruction based on ground heights. Between these samples, the display holds a supplied map rather than calculating continents sliding apart. Check “Map sample” to see which age is shown. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Source S29">S29</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S62" aria-label="Source S62">S62</button></sup>

The small map keeps fossil dots at today’s find locations. Each record’s age range decides when it appears. These locations have not been worked out for the ancient continents, so matching a dot to a coastline on the large map cannot tell you exactly where an animal lived. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="Source S65">S65</button></sup>

Open the museum-origin card. Singapore’s natural history museum displays diplodocid sauropod fossils from Wyoming’s Jurassic Morrison Formation. A fossil’s discovery site and its museum are different places. The large map gives a broad setting; the specimen’s own evidence tells us its age. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S17" aria-label="Source S17">S17</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S57" aria-label="Source S57">S57</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "How are the land shapes made?", zh: "陆地轮廓怎样绘出？" }}>

PALEOMAP PaleoDEM gives modelled ground heights and water depths. To draw these maps, we count heights above zero as exposed land. Zero and lower values are outside the land. This drawing rule does not give us an exactly measured ancient shoreline. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="Source S29">S29</button></sup>

</More>

侏罗纪在三叠纪之后、白垩纪之前。这里采用的年代表把起点列为 <Num s="S1">201.4</Num> 百万年前，终点列为 <Num s="S1">143.1</Num> 百万年前。本题第一张侏罗纪地图是 <Num s="S29">2</Num> 亿年前，显示的是这个纪之内的一个年代。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button></sup>

对照 <Num s="S29">2</Num> 亿、<Num s="S29">1.7</Num> 亿和 <Num s="S29">1.5</Num> 亿年前的地图，可以看到盘古大陆长期分裂过程中的一些陆地格局。每张图都分别根据模型中的地面高度复原。两个样本年代之间，画面会保留已有地图，并没有计算大陆连续分开的过程。看“地图样本”读数，就知道眼前是哪一张图。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S29" aria-label="来源 S29">S29</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S62" aria-label="来源 S62">S62</button></sup>

小图的化石点留在今天的发现位置，记录的年龄范围决定何时显示。这些点在古大陆上的位置没有被推算出来。因此，把小图的点对着大图海岸，不能确定某只动物当时的准确位置。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S65" aria-label="来源 S65">S65</button></sup>

打开馆藏来源卡。新加坡自然历史博物馆展示的梁龙科蜥脚类化石，来自怀俄明州的侏罗纪莫里森组。化石的发现地与博物馆是两个地点。大图提供大致背景，标本的年代则要看它本身的证据。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S17" aria-label="来源 S17">S17</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S57" aria-label="来源 S57">S57</button></sup>
