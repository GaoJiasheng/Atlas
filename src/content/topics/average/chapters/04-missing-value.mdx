<Lang en>

Sometimes you know the average but one of the values is missing. <Beat n={1}>The average of four numbers is 6. Three of them are 5, 7 and 4.</Beat> What is the fourth? The dashed tower with a question mark is the one to find.

Start from the total. <Beat n={2}>Four numbers with an average of 6 must add up to 4 × 6 = 24.</Beat> The three you know make 5 + 7 + 4 = 16. So the fourth is 24 − 16 = 8. Two steps: total, then take away.

You can also <Beat n={3}>see it by evening out</Beat>. For all four towers to stand at 6, the 5 needs 1 more block and the 4 needs 2 more; the 7 has 1 to spare. The fourth tower must make its own 6 and fill the gaps: 6 + 1 + 2 − 1 = 8. Both ways give 8.

The missing value is not the average itself. <Beat n={4}>If the fourth were 6</Beat>, the total would be 5 + 7 + 4 + 6 = 22, not 24, and the average would be 22 ÷ 4 = 5.5. Another slip is to work out the total with the wrong number of items, 3 × 6 instead of 4 × 6. The average is about all four numbers, so the total is 4 × 6.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

有时候知道平均数，可是少了一个数。<Beat n={1}>四个数的平均数是 6，其中三个是 5、7、4。</Beat>第四个是多少？画着问号的虚线塔就是要求的那一个。

从总数开始。<Beat n={2}>平均数是 6 的四个数，合起来一定是 4 × 6 = 24。</Beat>知道的三个是 5 + 7 + 4 = 16。所以第四个是 24 − 16 = 8。两步：先求总数，再减去。

也可以<Beat n={3}>用移多补少来看</Beat>。要让四座塔都停在 6，5 少 1 块，4 少 2 块，7 多 1 块。第四座塔除了自己的 6，还要补上缺的：6 + 1 + 2 − 1 = 8。两种做法都得 8。

缺的那个数不是平均数本身。<Beat n={4}>如果第四个是 6</Beat>，总数是 5 + 7 + 4 + 6 = 22，不是 24，平均数就成了 22 ÷ 4 = 5.5。另一个常见错误是用错个数求总数，算成 3 × 6，而不是 4 × 6。平均数说的是全部四个数，所以总数是 4 × 6。
