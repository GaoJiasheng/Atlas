<Lang en>

The practice has seven questions from the whole lesson, in a fixed order: towers to even out, two averages to work out, a total from an average, a missing quiz score, a new average when one more joins, and a choice.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why. If it is not right, the answer is shown, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time. Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. **Try again** starts the same seven questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

<More title={{ en: "Which step each question checks", zh: "每题对应哪一步" }}>

| Question | Kind | Step to revisit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | even out four towers | 01 Even them out |
| 2 | an average with a 0 in it | 02 Total ÷ number of items |
| 3 | an average that is a decimal | 02 Total ÷ number of items |
| 4 | a total from an average | 03 Average, total and number |
| 5 | a missing quiz score | 04 Finding a missing value |
| 6 | the new average when one joins | 05 When an item joins or leaves |
| 7 | choose the average | 01, 02 |

</More>

<Lang zh>

练习有 7 道题，来自整节课，顺序固定：移平方块塔，求两个平均数，用平均数求总数，求缺的一次小测分数，多一个人以后的新平均数，再选一个答案。

每题只检查一次。按“检查”以后，马上就能看到对不对、为什么。如果还没对，会给出答案，还有一个按钮可以回到教这一点的那一步。检查以后答案就不能再改，所以按之前慢慢想。每题有一条提示，看了提示不扣分。

做完最后一题，会出现一张小表：每题一行，标出 ✓ 或 ○，以及可以回看的那一步。按“再做一次”，同样的 7 道题从头开始。不计时，也不保存。
