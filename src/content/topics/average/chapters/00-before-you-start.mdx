<Lang en>

People say "on average" all the time: on average I read 6 books a month, on average it rains 15 days in December. This lesson is about what an average is and how to find one.

On the stage now are three towers of blocks: 2, 4 and 6. The dashed line across them is at 4. If you moved blocks from the tall tower to the short one until all three were the same height, they would all stop at that line. That height is the average.

There are 5 steps and a practice. Step 01 evens out towers of blocks. Step 02 finds the same number another way: add everything up, then share it equally. Step 03 links the average, the total and the number of items, so that you can find any one from the other two. Step 04 finds a missing value, and step 05 sees what happens to the average when one more item joins or one leaves. The last chapter is a practice of seven questions.

Each chapter explains first. The circles on the chapter axis at the bottom are its beats: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the blocks move while the reading panel says what to look at.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question, drawn from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: move blocks between towers, type a number, or choose an answer. Moving blocks is there to help; only the number you write is checked.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** gives the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, each one closer to the answer.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Nothing is timed and nothing is saved.

<Lang zh>

人们常说“平均”：我平均每个月读 6 本书，十二月平均有 15 天下雨。这节课讲平均数是什么，怎样求平均数。

舞台上现在是三座方块塔：2、4、6 块。横过它们的虚线在 4。如果把高塔的方块移给矮塔，直到三座一样高，它们都会停在这条线上。这个高度就是平均数。

全课有 5 步和一章练习。第 01 步把方块塔移平。第 02 步换一种做法求同一个数：先全部加起来，再平均分。第 03 步把平均数、总数和个数连起来，知道其中两个就能求第三个。第 04 步求缺的那个数，第 05 步看多一个、少一个时平均数怎样变。最后一章是 7 道练习题。

每一章都先讲。屏幕底部章节轴上的小圆点就是这一章的拍：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，方块就会移动，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。

每章最后的小方块是一道小题，从十道左右的题里挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：在塔之间移方块、填一个数，或者选一个答案。移方块是帮你想的，只检查你写的数。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按“看答案”。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，一条比一条接近答案。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

不计时，也不保存。
