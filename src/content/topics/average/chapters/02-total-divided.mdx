<Lang en>

Evening out works well with a few blocks. Here is another way that works with any numbers.

First <Beat n={1}>put all the stickers together in one tower</Beat>. That tower is the total: 2 + 4 + 6 = 12. Then <Beat n={2}>share the total equally</Beat> among the 3 friends: 12 ÷ 3 = 4. It is the same 4 as evening out, because both ways share the same total equally. So

average = total ÷ number of items.

With bigger numbers you do not need blocks at all. <Beat n={3}>Siti saved $3, $5, $4 and $8</Beat> in four weeks. The total is $20, and 20 ÷ 4 = $5. On average she saved $5 a week, even though she never saved exactly $5 in one week.

Be careful with zero. <Beat n={4}>In week 5 Siti saved nothing.</Beat> Week 5 is still one of the weeks, so it is still an <Term id="item">item</Term>: (20 + 0) ÷ 5 = $4. Leaving it out would give 20 ÷ 4 = $5, the average of only four weeks. The <Term id="number-of-items">number of items</Term> counts every value, zeros too.

An average does not have to be a whole number. <Beat n={5}>Two bags of rice weigh 7 kg and 8 kg.</Beat> Their total is 15 kg, and 15 ÷ 2 = 7.5 kg: the last kilogram is shared too, half each. 7.5 kg is not the mass of either bag. An average does not have to be one of the values.

<Lang zh>

方块不多的时候，移多补少很好用。还有一种做法，什么数都能用。

先<Beat n={1}>把贴纸全部叠成一座塔</Beat>。这座塔就是总数：2 + 4 + 6 = 12。再把总数<Beat n={2}>平均分</Beat>给 3 个人：12 ÷ 3 = 4。和移多补少得到的 4 一样，因为两种做法都是把同样的总数平均分。所以

平均数 = 总数 ÷ 个数。

数大了，就完全不用方块。<Beat n={3}>Siti 四周分别存了 3、5、4、8 元。</Beat>总数是 20 元，20 ÷ 4 = 5 元。她平均每周存 5 元，虽然没有哪一周正好存了 5 元。

遇到 0 要小心。<Beat n={4}>第 5 周 Siti 一分钱也没存。</Beat>第 5 周仍然是其中一周，仍然是<Term id="item">一项</Term>：(20 + 0) ÷ 5 = 4 元。把它漏掉，得到 20 ÷ 4 = 5 元，那只是四周的平均数。<Term id="number-of-items">个数</Term>要把每一个数都算上，0 也算。

平均数不一定是整数。<Beat n={5}>两袋米分别重 7 kg 和 8 kg。</Beat>总数是 15 kg，15 ÷ 2 = 7.5 kg：最后那 1 kg 也要分，一人一半。7.5 kg 不是哪一袋的重量。平均数不一定是其中一个数。
