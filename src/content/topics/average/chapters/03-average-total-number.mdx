<Lang en>

An average, a total and a number of items always go together. If you know any two, you can find the third.

Look at <Beat n={1}>4 bags of rice with an average mass of 6 kg</Beat>. The model draws it as if every bag were 6 kg: four equal parts of one bar. The whole bar is the total, 4 × 6 = 24 kg. The real bags may not each be 6 kg, but together they are 24 kg, because the average is the total shared equally.

So <Beat n={2}>the three numbers are linked</Beat>:

- total = average × number of items
- average = total ÷ number of items
- number of items = total ÷ average

The last one answers "how many". <Beat n={3}>Hui Min spent $45 in all, $9 a day on average.</Beat> How many days? Count how many parts of $9 make $45: 45 ÷ 9 = 5 days.

A common slip is to take the average as the total. <Beat n={4}>Writing 6 kg over the whole bar</Beat> says that all four bags together weigh 6 kg, which cannot be right: 6 kg is one equal share. Altogether the bags are 4 × 6 = 24 kg. When a question gives an average and asks for "altogether", multiply.

<Lang zh>

平均数、总数、个数总是连在一起。知道其中两个，就能求第三个。

看<Beat n={1}>平均每袋 6 kg 的 4 袋米</Beat>。模型图把它画成好像每袋都是 6 kg：一条分成四段一样长的条。整条是总数，4 × 6 = 24 kg。真的每袋不一定都是 6 kg，可是合起来一定是 24 kg，因为平均数就是把总数平均分。

所以<Beat n={2}>这三个数连在一起</Beat>：

- 总数 = 平均数 × 个数
- 平均数 = 总数 ÷ 个数
- 个数 = 总数 ÷ 平均数

最后一条回答“有几个”。<Beat n={3}>Hui Min 一共花了 45 元，平均每天 9 元。</Beat>花了几天？数一数 45 元里有几个 9 元：45 ÷ 9 = 5 天。

常见的错误是把平均数当成总数。<Beat n={4}>把 6 kg 写在整条上</Beat>，就是说四袋合起来只有 6 kg，这不可能：6 kg 只是平均分到的一份。四袋一共是 4 × 6 = 24 kg。题目给了平均数、问“一共”，就要乘。
