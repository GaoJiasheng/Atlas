Start with <Beat n={1}>three friends and their stickers</Beat>. Wei Jie has 2, Siti has 4 and Arjun has 6. Each sticker is a block, so their stickers make three towers of different heights.

Now <Beat n={2}>move stickers from the tallest tower to the shortest</Beat>. Arjun gives 2 to Wei Jie. The two blocks that moved are drawn in orange; where they came from is dashed. Now every tower is 4 high.

This is called <Term id="evening-out">evening out</Term>. Look at <Beat n={3}>the level the towers reach</Beat>: each friend has 4. Nothing was added and nothing was taken away, so the <Term id="total">total</Term> is still 2 + 4 + 6 = 12. The level they reach, 4, is the <Term id="average">average</Term> of 2, 4 and 6. The dashed <Term id="mean-line">average line</Term> marks it.

The average is not always the number in the middle. Take <Beat n={4}>1, 2 and 9</Beat>. The middle number is 2, but if you even them out, the 9 gives 3 blocks to the 1 and 2 blocks to the 2, and every tower ends at 4. The average is 4, not 2.

In the question for this chapter you can move the blocks yourself. Tap a tower to lift its top block, then tap a shorter tower to put it down. When all the towers are the same height, write that height. You can also write the average straight away; only the number is checked.

When you are ready, <Task id="evening-out">try one question</Task>.

<Lang zh>

<Figure src="figures/average-uneven-stacks" alt={{ en: "Three wooden towers contain two, four and six blocks before evening out.", zh: "移多补少之前，三座木方块塔分别有两块、四块、六块。" }} caption={{ en: "Before: 2, 4 and 6 blocks. After evening out, each tower has 4.", zh: "移之前：2、4、6 块。移多补少之后，每座 4 块。" }} />

先看<Beat n={1}>三个朋友和他们的贴纸</Beat>。Wei Jie 有 2 张，Siti 有 4 张，Arjun 有 6 张。每张贴纸是一块方块，三个人的贴纸叠成高低不同的三座塔。

现在<Beat n={2}>把最高那座的贴纸移给最矮的</Beat>。Arjun 给 Wei Jie 2 张。移过去的两块画成橙色，原来的位置画成虚线。现在每座塔都是 4。

这就叫<Term id="evening-out">移多补少</Term>：把多的移给少的。看<Beat n={3}>塔一样高时的高度</Beat>：每人 4 张。没有多也没有少，<Term id="total">总数</Term>还是 2 + 4 + 6 = 12。一样高时的 4，就是 2、4、6 的<Term id="average">平均数</Term>。那条虚线叫<Term id="mean-line">平均线</Term>，画在平均数的高度上。

平均数不一定是中间那个数。看<Beat n={4}>1、2、9</Beat>。中间那个数是 2，可是移一移，9 给 1 三块，给 2 两块，每座都停在 4。平均数是 4，不是 2。

这一章的小题里，你可以自己移方块。点一座塔，拿起它顶上的一块；再点一座矮一点的塔，放到那里。塔都一样高了，就写下这个高度。你也可以直接写平均数；只检查你写的数。

准备好了，就<Task id="evening-out">做一道小题</Task>。
