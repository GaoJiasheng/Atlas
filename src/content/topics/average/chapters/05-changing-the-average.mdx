<Lang en>

What happens to an average when one more item joins, or one leaves?

<Beat n={1}>Four friends read 5 books each on average.</Beat> Their total is 4 × 5 = 20 books. Then Daniel joins with 10 books. The new total is 20 + 10 = 30, now shared by 5 friends: 30 ÷ 5 = 6. The average went up from 5 to 6, because Daniel read more than the average.

It is tempting to <Beat n={2}>average the two numbers</Beat>: (5 + 10) ÷ 2 = 7.5. That does not work. The old average 5 stands for four friends, but the 10 stands for only one, so they cannot count the same. Always go through the totals: new total, then new number of items.

An item can leave too. <Beat n={3}>Ravi, who read 2 books, leaves the four.</Beat> The total drops to 20 − 2 = 18, shared by 3: 18 ÷ 3 = 6. The average went up again, because the one who left was below the average. A value above the average pulls it up, a value below pulls it down, and a value equal to the average leaves it where it is.

You can also <Beat n={4}>go backwards</Beat>. Three pupils have 8 stickers each on average; with a fourth pupil the average is 9. The old total is 3 × 8 = 24 and the new total is 4 × 9 = 36, so the fourth pupil brought 36 − 24 = 12.

<Lang zh>

多来一项、或者走掉一项的时候，平均数会怎样？

<Beat n={1}>四个朋友平均每人读 5 本书。</Beat>总数是 4 × 5 = 20 本。Daniel 带着 10 本加入。新总数是 20 + 10 = 30，现在由 5 个人分：30 ÷ 5 = 6。平均数从 5 升到 6，因为 Daniel 读得比平均数多。

很容易想到<Beat n={2}>把两个数平均一下</Beat>：(5 + 10) ÷ 2 = 7.5。这不对。旧平均数 5 代表四个人，10 只代表一个人，不能当成一样重。一定要用总数算：先求新总数，再除以新个数。

也可以走掉一项。<Beat n={3}>读了 2 本的 Ravi 离开那四个人。</Beat>总数变成 20 − 2 = 18，3 个人分：18 ÷ 3 = 6。平均数又升了，因为离开的人比平均数少。比平均大的数让平均升，比平均小的数让它降，和平均数一样大的数不会让它变。

还可以<Beat n={4}>倒过来求</Beat>。三个学生平均每人 8 张贴纸，加上第四个人以后平均 9 张。旧总数是 3 × 8 = 24，新总数是 4 × 9 = 36，所以第四个人带来 36 − 24 = 12 张。
