<Lang en>

The digestive organs are back on the stage. In the small intestine, food that has been digested into simpler substances passes into the blood, as in <TopicLink slug="digestive-system" ch="small-intestine-chapter">The small intestine</TopicLink>. On the way back to the heart, that blood passes through the liver first.

In the lungs, as chapters 2 and 4 showed, oxygen passes into the blood and carbon dioxide leaves it. The circulatory system then carries the digested food and the oxygen together, through the heart, to every part of the body. So three systems, digestive, respiratory and circulatory, work as one: a system is a group of parts working together, and these three systems work together too.

Every part of the body, such as the leg muscles, uses digested food and oxygen to carry out <Term id="life-processes">life processes</Term>, such as moving and growing. It gives out carbon dioxide, which the blood carries back to the lungs to be breathed out. <FlyTo preset="legs">Look at the leg muscles</FlyTo>

When you run, the leg muscles work harder. They use more oxygen and digested food and give out more carbon dioxide. The body detects the extra carbon dioxide and the drop in oxygen in the blood, and you breathe faster and deeper and your heart beats faster. Use the card to compare resting, walking and running in this model: at rest it breathes <Num s="S37">20</Num> times and its heart beats <Num s="S37">90</Num> times a minute; running, <Num s="S37">40</Num> and <Num s="S37">165</Num>.

<More title={{ en: "These numbers are a model", zh: "这些数是模型的" }}>

The resting values sit inside the guideline ranges for children aged 6 to 11: <Num s="S9">18 to 25</Num> breaths and <Num s="S9">75 to 118</Num> heartbeats a minute. We found no source that gives the breathing and heartbeat of 10- to 12-year-olds just after walking or running, so the walking and running values here are chosen to show the change; they are not measurements. The running heartbeat stays below the average highest heartbeat of active children, which is in the <Num s="S10">mid-190s</Num>. Everyone is different.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Count your own", zh: "数一数你自己的" }}>

Sit still and count your breaths for 30 seconds, then multiply by 2. Then jog gently on the spot for a minute and count again. You can also count your heartbeat by feeling the pulse at your wrist or the side of your neck. Write your numbers in a table, like the one on the card. If you feel unwell, do not do the jogging part.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Through the liver first · further", zh: "先经过肝 · 再进一步" }}>

Blood from the stomach and intestines does not go straight back to the heart. It flows first through the liver, then on to the heart. In this model that part of the path is drawn, not traced in the blood vessels.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Not the whole story", zh: "模型没画的" }}>

Blood also flows through the brain, the kidneys and every other organ. This model shows only the main paths to the head, the arms, the gut and the legs.

</More>

<Lang zh>

消化器官又回到了舞台上。在小肠里，被消化成更简单物质的食物进入血液，就像<TopicLink slug="digestive-system" ch="small-intestine-chapter">小肠</TopicLink>里讲的那样。这些血在回心脏的路上，先经过肝。

在肺里，就像第 2 章和第 4 章看到的，氧气进入血液，二氧化碳离开血液。然后，循环系统把消化后的食物和氧气一起，经过心脏，运到身体的每个部分。所以消化、呼吸、循环三个系统像一个整体一样工作：系统是一起工作的一组部分，这三个系统之间也一起工作。

身体的每个部分，比如腿部肌肉，都用消化后的食物和氧气进行<Term id="life-processes">生命过程</Term>，例如运动和生长。它们放出二氧化碳，由血液带回肺，再呼出去。<FlyTo preset="legs">看看腿部肌肉</FlyTo>

跑步时，腿部肌肉更用力，用掉更多氧气和消化后的食物，也放出更多二氧化碳。身体察觉到血里二氧化碳增多、氧气减少，于是呼吸变快、变深，心跳也变快。用卡片比较这个模型里休息、走路和跑步的情况：休息时它每分钟呼吸 <Num s="S37">20</Num> 次、心跳 <Num s="S37">90</Num> 次；跑步时是 <Num s="S37">40</Num> 次和 <Num s="S37">165</Num> 次。

<More title={{ en: "These numbers are a model", zh: "这些数是模型的" }}>

休息时的值落在 6 到 11 岁孩子的指南范围里：每分钟呼吸 <Num s="S9">18 到 25</Num> 次，心跳 <Num s="S9">75 到 118</Num> 次。我们没有找到给出 10 到 12 岁孩子刚走完路或跑完步时呼吸和心跳次数的来源，所以这里走路、跑步的值是为了显示变化而取的，不是测量。跑步时的心跳低于好动的孩子平均最大心跳，那大约是每分钟 <Num s="S10">190 多</Num>次。每个人都不一样。

</More>
