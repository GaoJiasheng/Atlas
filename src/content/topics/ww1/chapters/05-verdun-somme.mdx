<Lang en>

In 1916 the battles of attrition at Verdun and on the Somme killed and wounded soldiers on a scale never seen before while the front barely moved; in the east, Russia's Brusilov offensive and Romania's entry and defeat shifted the balance.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

1916 年，凡尔登和索姆河两场消耗战的伤亡规模前所未有，战线却几乎没有移动；东线则有俄军的布鲁西洛夫攻势，以及罗马尼亚参战后迅速战败。

</Lang>
