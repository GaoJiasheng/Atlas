In 1916 the generals on both sides tried to win by <Term id="attrition">attrition</Term>, killing and wounding the enemy's soldiers faster than he could replace them. On 21 February Germany attacked the French fortress city of Verdun. The battle lasted until 18 December. Two-thirds to three-quarters of the French army fought there in turn. About <Num s="S119">163,000</Num> French and <Num s="S119">143,000</Num> German soldiers were killed or went missing, and the front ended almost where it had begun.

On 1 July British and French armies attacked on the Somme after a week-long bombardment of about <Num s="S122">1.5 million</Num> shells. On that first day the British Army lost <Num s="S119">57,470</Num> men, <Num s="S119">19,240</Num> of them killed, the worst day in its history. Tanks went into battle for the first time on 15 September. By 18 November the Entente had advanced <Num s="S119">8</Num> to <Num s="S119,S122">12</Num> km, and British, French and German casualties together passed <Num s="S119">1 million</Num>.

In the east, the Russian offensive led by Aleksei Brusilov broke the Austro-Hungarian front in June. Austria-Hungary lost about <Num s="S128">1 million</Num> to <Num s="S129">1.5 million</Num> men, many of them as prisoners; Russia about as many. Romania joined the Entente on 27 August, but German, Austro-Hungarian, Bulgarian and Ottoman armies invaded and took Bucharest on 6 December. In Italy an Austro-Hungarian offensive at Asiago was halted, and the Italians took Gorizia in August.

At Kut in Mesopotamia about <Num s="S136">13,000</Num> British and Indian soldiers surrendered on 29 April; a third of them died in captivity. In June the Arab Revolt against Ottoman rule began in Mecca. Civilians were drawn in too. Russia's order to conscript Central Asians for labour set off a revolt whose repression killed at least <Num s="S140">150,000</Num> Kazakhs and Kyrgyz, and from October the German army deported about <Num s="S141">60,000</Num> Belgian workers to Germany.

<More title={{ en: "Artillery and the battle of materiel", zh: "大炮与“物资战”" }}>

German soldiers called Verdun and the Somme a Materialschlacht, a battle of materiel: guns, shells and supplies mattered as much as men. Verdun was fed along one road from Bar-le-Duc, later named the Voie Sacrée (Sacred Way): on an average day it carried <Num s="S121">13,000</Num> soldiers, <Num s="S121">6,400</Num> tonnes of supplies and <Num s="S121">1,500</Num> tonnes of ammunition. Fort Douaumont fell on 25 February and was retaken on 24 October.

Before the Somme attack, from 24 June, British guns fired <Num s="S123">1,732,873</Num> shells, but up to <Num s="S122">30 per cent</Num> did not explode, and the German dugouts and barbed wire largely survived. Somme casualties are given as <Num s="S119">419,654</Num> British and Empire, <Num s="S119">202,559</Num> French and <Num s="S119">437,322</Num> German; the German figure is disputed, and other estimates are higher. Today the ossuary at Douaumont holds the bones of about <Num s="S120">130,000</Num> unidentified soldiers of both sides, and the Thiepval memorial on the Somme lists more than <Num s="S126">72,000</Num> British and South African soldiers who have no known grave.

<More title={{ en: "The Somme on film and in memory", zh: "银幕上和记忆中的索姆河" }}>

British official cameramen filmed the preparations and the first days of the battle. The Battle of the Somme opened in cinemas in August 1916, and some <Num s="S127">20 million</Num> people saw it in its first six weeks. For many families it was the first time they saw the war their men were fighting, including real scenes of the wounded and the dead.

The battle is remembered differently in each country. Newfoundland, then a separate dominion, remembers 1 July for Beaumont-Hamel: of about <Num s="S124">800</Num> men of the Newfoundland Regiment who attacked that morning, only <Num s="S124">68</Num> answered the roll call the next day; <Num s="S123">233</Num> were dead, <Num s="S123">386</Num> wounded and <Num s="S123">91</Num> missing. West African tirailleurs sénégalais, whose recruitment reached <Num s="S156">59,000</Num> men in 1916 alone, fought at Verdun and on the Somme, as did soldiers from Britain's empire in India, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and South Africa.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Brusilov's method, and Romania's defeat", zh: "布鲁西洛夫的打法与罗马尼亚的失败" }}>

Earlier offensives had massed men on a narrow sector, so the defender could see it coming and bring up reserves. Brusilov attacked at many points at once along a front of about <Num s="S129">450 km</Num>. His troops dug their trenches forward at night to within about <Num s="S129">100</Num> paces of the Austro-Hungarian line and brought the assault units up only days before. Lutsk fell on 7 June and Czernowitz (Chernivtsi) on 18 June. Losses on both sides were enormous: for Austria-Hungary about <Num s="S128">1 million</Num> to <Num s="S129">1.5 million</Num>, almost half of them prisoners; for Russia about <Num s="S129">1 million</Num> to <Num s="S128">1.5 million</Num>.

Romania declared war on Austria-Hungary on 27 August, but at Tutrakan on the Danube about <Num s="S132">28,000</Num> Romanian soldiers were captured in early September. Constanța fell on 22 October, a German army broke through the Jiu valley around 11 November, another force crossed the Danube at Svishtov on 23 November, and Bucharest fell on 6 December. Of <Num s="S135">500,000</Num> Romanians mobilised, about <Num s="S135">160,000</Num> were killed and <Num s="S135">140,000</Num> captured.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The Arab Revolt, Kut and Sykes–Picot", zh: "阿拉伯起义、库特与赛克斯—皮科协定" }}>

In letters exchanged in 1915 and 1916, Henry McMahon, the British high commissioner in Egypt, and Sharif Hussein of Mecca discussed British support for an independent Arab state in return for an Arab rising against the Ottoman Empire. Hussein's forces rose in June 1916, took Mecca and, with British naval help, the Red Sea ports; the revolt moved north along the coast and took Aqaba in July 1917.

At the same time, in May 1916, Britain and France, with Russia's agreement, had signed the secret Sykes–Picot agreement, dividing the Ottoman Arab provinces into zones of direct control and influence. The two promises did not fit together, and the conflict between them shaped the Middle East after the war.

In Mesopotamia, Ottoman forces surrounded about <Num s="S136">10,000</Num> British and Indian troops and <Num s="S136">3,500</Num> civilian followers at Kut on 7 December 1915. Relief attempts cost about <Num s="S136">23,000</Num> casualties. The garrison surrendered on 29 April 1916, and about a third of the <Num s="S136">13,000</Num> men taken prisoner died from disease, hunger and ill-treatment.

1916 年，双方将领都想打一场<Term id="attrition">消耗战</Term>：让对方士兵伤亡的速度超过补充的速度。2 月 21 日，德军进攻法国要塞城市凡尔登，战役一直打到 12 月 18 日。法军的三分之二到四分之三先后轮换到过这里。法军约 <Num s="S119">16.3 万</Num>人、德军约 <Num s="S119">14.3 万</Num>人阵亡或失踪，战线最后几乎回到了起点。

7 月 1 日，英法军队在索姆河发起进攻，此前一周的炮击共发射约 <Num s="S122">150 万</Num>发炮弹。仅第一天，英军就损失 <Num s="S119">57,470</Num> 人，其中阵亡 <Num s="S119">19,240</Num> 人，是英军史上最惨重的一天。9 月 15 日，坦克第一次投入战斗。到 11 月 18 日，协约国军队只推进了 <Num s="S119">8</Num> 到 <Num s="S119,S122">12</Num> 公里，英、法、德三国的伤亡合计超过 <Num s="S119">100 万</Num>。

东线上，阿列克谢·布鲁西洛夫指挥的俄军 6 月发起攻势，突破了奥匈防线。奥匈损失约 <Num s="S128">100 万</Num>到 <Num s="S129">150 万</Num>人，其中许多是被俘的；俄军的损失与此相当。8 月 27 日，罗马尼亚加入协约国一方，但德国、奥匈、保加利亚和奥斯曼军队随即攻入，12 月 6 日占领布加勒斯特。在意大利，奥匈军队在阿夏戈的攻势被挡住，意军 8 月攻占戈里齐亚。

在美索不达米亚，约 <Num s="S136">1.3 万</Num>名英印士兵 4 月 29 日在库特投降，其中约三分之一死在战俘营和押解途中。6 月，反抗奥斯曼统治的阿拉伯起义在麦加爆发。平民也被卷入战争：俄国下令征召中亚人服劳役，引发起义，镇压中至少 <Num s="S140">15 万</Num>哈萨克人和吉尔吉斯人死亡；从 10 月起，德军把约 <Num s="S141">6 万</Num>名比利时工人强行押往德国。

英国官方摄影师拍下了战役的准备和最初几天。纪录片《索姆河战役》1916 年 8 月在电影院上映，头六周就有约 <Num s="S127">2,000 万</Num>人观看。对许多家庭来说，这是他们第一次看到亲人所在的战争，包括真实的伤员和死者。

各国对这场战役的记忆各不相同。当时还是独立自治领的纽芬兰，把 7 月 1 日与博蒙阿梅尔连在一起：那天早上发起进攻的约 <Num s="S124">800</Num> 名纽芬兰团士兵，第二天点名时只有 <Num s="S124">68</Num> 人应答；<Num s="S123">233</Num> 人死亡、<Num s="S123">386</Num> 人负伤、<Num s="S123">91</Num> 人失踪。仅 1916 年一年就征召了 <Num s="S156">5.9 万</Num>人的西非塞内加尔步兵，在凡尔登和索姆河作战；来自英帝国的印度、加拿大、澳大利亚、新西兰和南非士兵也在这里作战。

<More title={{ en: "The Arab Revolt, Kut and Sykes–Picot", zh: "阿拉伯起义、库特与赛克斯—皮科协定" }}>

1915 年至 1916 年，英国驻埃及高级专员亨利·麦克马洪与麦加的谢里夫侯赛因通信，讨论英国支持建立独立的阿拉伯国家、阿拉伯人起兵反抗奥斯曼帝国。1916 年 6 月，侯赛因的部队起兵，攻占麦加，又在英国海军帮助下夺取红海沿岸港口；起义军沿海岸北上，1917 年 7 月攻占亚喀巴。

就在同一时期，1916 年 5 月，英法两国在俄国同意下签订秘密的赛克斯—皮科协定，把奥斯曼帝国的阿拉伯省份划分为直接控制区和势力范围。两种承诺彼此冲突，这一矛盾塑造了战后的中东。

在美索不达米亚，奥斯曼军队于 1915 年 12 月 7 日在库特包围了约 <Num s="S136">1 万</Num>名英印军队和 <Num s="S136">3,500</Num> 名随军平民。几次解围共伤亡约 <Num s="S136">2.3 万</Num>人。1916 年 4 月 29 日守军投降，被俘的 <Num s="S136">1.3 万</Num>人中约三分之一死于疾病、饥饿和虐待。
