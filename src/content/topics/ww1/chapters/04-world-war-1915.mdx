<Lang en>

By the end of 1915 the war reached East Asia, the Pacific, Africa and the Middle East; at Gallipoli the Entente failed to force the Dardanelles, and the Ottoman government deported and killed most of its Armenian population.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

到 1915 年底，战争已扩展到东亚、太平洋、非洲和中东：协约国在加里波利未能打通达达尼尔海峡，奥斯曼政府则驱逐并杀害了境内大部分亚美尼亚人。

</Lang>
