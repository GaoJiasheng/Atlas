In Petrograd, protests over bread queues began on 8 March 1917 and turned into revolution when soldiers of the garrison joined the crowds. Workers and soldiers elected a <Term id="soviet">soviet</Term>, and on 15 March Tsar Nicholas II abdicated. The new Provisional Government kept Russia in the war, but its last offensive, in July, collapsed. On 7 November the <Term id="bolsheviks">Bolsheviks</Term> led by Vladimir Lenin seized power and called for peace without annexations; on 15 December they signed an armistice with the Central Powers.

Germany's return to unrestricted submarine warfare, and a telegram in which it offered Mexico an alliance, brought the United States into the war on 6 April; the House of Representatives voted <Num s="S166">373</Num> to <Num s="S166">50</Num>. About <Num s="S192">2 million</Num> American soldiers reached France before the war ended. Siam declared war on 22 July, China on 14 August and Brazil on 26 October.

On the Western Front, the French offensive on the Chemin des Dames put nearly <Num s="S168">140,000</Num> French soldiers out of action in its first week; mutinies followed in <Num s="S169">68</Num> divisions. At Vimy Ridge the Canadian Corps lost <Num s="S170">10,602</Num> men. In Flanders the British offensive known as Passchendaele lasted from 31 July to 10 November; estimates of British and Dominion casualties run from about <Num s="S174">275,000</Num> to <Num s="S175">300,000</Num>, of German casualties from about <Num s="S174">220,000</Num> to <Num s="S175">260,000</Num>.

On 24 October Austro-Hungarian and German troops broke through at Caporetto. Italy lost about <Num s="S178">11,000</Num> dead and <Num s="S178">300,000</Num> prisoners, and its army fell back to the Piave, where the line held. In the Middle East, the British government promised in the Balfour Declaration of 2 November to support "a national home for the Jewish people" in Palestine, and on 9 December Jerusalem surrendered to British forces.

<More title={{ en: "Why the United States entered the war", zh: "美国为什么参战" }}>

On 16 January 1917 the German foreign secretary, Arthur Zimmermann, telegraphed his minister in Mexico: if the United States entered the war, Mexico should be offered an alliance and the chance to "reconquer the lost territory in Texas, New Mexico, and Arizona". British codebreakers in Room 40 read the message and passed it to President Woodrow Wilson on 24 February; American newspapers printed it on 1 March. At the same time German submarines were again sinking ships without warning.

On 2 April Wilson asked Congress for war: "The world must be made safe for democracy." The United States fought as an "associated power", not a formal ally of the Entente, and kept its own war aims, which Wilson set out in his <Term id="fourteen-points">Fourteen Points</Term> of 8 January 1918.

The American army had to be built almost from nothing. In the end <Num s="S192">2,084,000</Num> soldiers reached France, and <Num s="S192">1,390,000</Num> of them served in the front line. The army was segregated: the African-American 369th Infantry Regiment was placed under French command and spent <Num s="S193">191</Num> days in the front-line trenches.

<More title={{ en: "The French mutinies", zh: "法军兵变" }}>

General Robert Nivelle had promised to break through on the Aisne within two days. His offensive began on 16 April and failed at great cost. In May and June, soldiers in <Num s="S169">68</Num> infantry divisions refused to go into the attack or held protests, between <Num s="S169">59,000</Num> and <Num s="S169">88,000</Num> men by one count. The refusals took place behind the lines, among men due to go back up; historians see a crisis of confidence in commanders whose costly attacks had failed.

Philippe Pétain replaced Nivelle on 15 May. He gave up costly offensives and gave the men leave, better food and rest. Courts-martial gave <Num s="S169">3,427</Num> judgments and <Num s="S169">554</Num> death sentences. How many men were shot is disputed: historians count <Num s="S169">23</Num> to <Num s="S169">27</Num> executions of mutineers, while the French ministry of the armed forces gives about <Num s="S168">50</Num> soldiers shot.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "October, and the road to Brest-Litovsk", zh: "十月革命与布列斯特之路" }}>

Russia still used the old Julian calendar, 13 days behind the rest of Europe, so the February and October revolutions took place in March and November by today's calendar. After the tsar fell, the German government let Lenin and other exiled socialists travel home from Switzerland through Germany, hoping they would weaken Russia's war effort. New research shows that Lenin received less than <Num s="S179">$40,000</Num> of German money, too little to decide either the revolution or the war.

The Bolsheviks took power in Petrograd on 7 November. The next day Lenin's Decree on Peace proposed to all the warring peoples and governments a peace "without annexations and contributions". Only Germany answered. Russia signed an armistice at Brest-Litovsk on 15 December, and the peace talks that followed would cost Russia its western borderlands.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "China's road to war", zh: "中国的参战之路" }}>

When Germany declared unrestricted submarine warfare, the United States asked neutral countries to break with Germany. On 17 February 1917 a German submarine sank the French troopship Athos in the Mediterranean; of <Num s="S188">754</Num> dead, <Num s="S188">543</Num> were Chinese workers on their way to France. China broke off relations with Germany in March.

Whether to go further split the government in Beijing. Prime Minister Duan Qirui wanted war, hoping to recover Shandong at the peace conference. President Li Yuanhong and the parliament suspected that Duan had a secret deal with Japan, and Li dismissed him in May. General Zhang Xun then dissolved parliament and tried to restore the Qing emperor; Duan's troops ended the attempt on 14 July. With Duan back in power, China declared war on Germany and Austria-Hungary on 14 August. Sun Yat-sen, who opposed entering the war, set up a rival government in Guangzhou.

Japan supported Duan with the Nishihara loans, no less than <Num s="S191">¥145 million</Num> in 1917 and 1918, some secured on railways in Shandong. China sent workers, not soldiers: about <Num s="S151,S150">140,000</Num> Chinese labourers served in France. Siam, which also hoped to revise its unequal treaties, sent a force of <Num s="S185">1,284</Num> volunteers to France in 1918; <Num s="S186">19</Num> of them died, none in combat.

1917 年 3 月 8 日，彼得格勒市民因排队买面包而上街抗议；卫戍部队士兵倒向人群后，抗议演变成革命。工人和士兵选出<Term id="soviet">苏维埃</Term>，3 月 15 日沙皇尼古拉二世退位。新成立的临时政府继续作战，但 7 月发动的最后一次攻势很快崩溃。11 月 7 日，列宁领导的<Term id="bolsheviks">布尔什维克</Term>夺取政权，呼吁不割地的和平；12 月 15 日，他们与同盟国签订停战协定。

德国恢复无限制潜艇战，又发电报提议与墨西哥结盟，美国因此在 4 月 6 日参战，众议院以 <Num s="S166">373</Num> 票对 <Num s="S166">50</Num> 票通过宣战。到战争结束，约 <Num s="S192">200 万</Num>美军抵达法国。暹罗于 7 月 22 日、中国于 8 月 14 日、巴西于 10 月 26 日先后宣战。

西线上，法军在贵妇小径发起攻势，第一周就有近 <Num s="S168">14 万</Num>人失去战斗力，随后 <Num s="S169">68</Num> 个师发生兵变。加拿大军团在维米岭伤亡 <Num s="S170">10,602</Num> 人。英军在佛兰德的帕斯尚尔攻势从 7 月 31 日打到 11 月 10 日；英国及各自治领军队的伤亡估计在约 <Num s="S174">27.5 万</Num>到 <Num s="S175">30 万</Num>人之间，德军约 <Num s="S174">22 万</Num>到 <Num s="S175">26 万</Num>人。

10 月 24 日，奥匈和德国军队在卡波雷托突破意大利防线。意军阵亡约 <Num s="S178">1.1 万</Num>人，被俘 <Num s="S178">30 万</Num>人，一路退到皮亚韦河才守住。在中东，英国政府 11 月 2 日发表《贝尔福宣言》，表示支持在巴勒斯坦建立“犹太人的民族家园”；12 月 9 日，耶路撒冷向英军投降。

1917 年 1 月 16 日，德国外交大臣阿图尔·齐默尔曼给驻墨西哥公使发电报：如果美国参战，就向墨西哥提议结盟，让它“收复在得克萨斯、新墨西哥和亚利桑那失去的领土”。英国“40 号房间”的密码破译人员读出了电文，2 月 24 日交给威尔逊总统；3 月 1 日美国报纸刊出全文。与此同时，德国潜艇又开始不加警告地击沉船只。

4 月 2 日，威尔逊请求国会宣战：“必须使世界对民主是安全的。”美国以“合作国”而非协约国正式盟国的身份参战，保留自己的战争目标；威尔逊在 1918 年 1 月 8 日的“<Term id="fourteen-points">十四点</Term>”中把它们公之于众。

美国陆军几乎是从零建起来的。最终有 <Num s="S192">208.4 万</Num>名士兵抵达法国，其中 <Num s="S192">139 万</Num>人上过前线。美军实行种族隔离：非裔美国人组成的第 369 步兵团被交给法军指挥，在前线堑壕里度过了 <Num s="S193">191</Num> 天。

罗贝尔·尼韦勒将军曾许诺两天内在埃纳河突破。攻势 4 月 16 日开始，代价惨重却没有成功。5 月和 6 月，<Num s="S169">68</Num> 个步兵师的士兵拒绝进攻或举行抗议，据一种统计有 <Num s="S169">5.9 万</Num>到 <Num s="S169">8.8 万</Num>人。抗命都发生在后方，发生在即将重返前线的部队中；历史学者认为，这是士兵对指挥官的信任危机，因为他们代价高昂的进攻屡屡失败。

5 月 15 日，菲利普·贝当接替尼韦勒。他放弃代价高昂的攻势，给士兵休假、改善伙食、安排休整。军事法庭作出 <Num s="S169">3,427</Num> 次判决、<Num s="S169">554</Num> 个死刑判决。究竟有多少人被枪决，说法不一：历史学者统计被处决的兵变者为 <Num s="S169">23</Num> 到 <Num s="S169">27</Num> 人，法国军队部则说约有 <Num s="S168">50</Num> 名士兵被枪决。

<More title={{ en: "October, and the road to Brest-Litovsk", zh: "十月革命与布列斯特之路" }}>

俄国当时仍用儒略历，比欧洲其他地方晚 13 天，所以“二月革命”和“十月革命”按今天的公历分别发生在 3 月和 11 月。沙皇倒台后，德国政府允许列宁等流亡瑞士的社会主义者经德国回国，希望他们削弱俄国的战争努力。新的研究表明，列宁得到的德国资金不足 <Num s="S179">4 万美元</Num>，既不足以决定革命，也不足以决定战争。

11 月 7 日，布尔什维克在彼得格勒夺取政权。第二天，列宁的《和平法令》向所有交战国的人民和政府提议缔结“不割地、不赔款”的和平，只有德国作出回应。12 月 15 日，俄国在布列斯特-立托夫斯克签订停战协定；随后的和谈将使俄国失去整个西部边疆。

<More title={{ en: "China's road to war", zh: "中国的参战之路" }}>

德国宣布无限制潜艇战后，美国要求各中立国与德国断交。1917 年 2 月 17 日，德国潜艇在地中海击沉法国运兵船“阿托斯号”，<Num s="S188">754</Num> 名死者中有 <Num s="S188">543</Num> 名是前往法国的华工。3 月，中国与德国断交。

是否进一步参战，使北京政府陷入分裂，史称“府院之争”。国务总理段祺瑞主张参战，希望在和会上收回山东。黎元洪总统和国会怀疑段祺瑞与日本有秘密交易，黎元洪在 5 月免去他的职务。张勋随即解散国会，拥清帝复辟；7 月 14 日段祺瑞率军入京，结束了复辟。段祺瑞重掌政权后，中国于 8 月 14 日对德国和奥匈宣战。反对参战的孙中山则在广州另立政府。
