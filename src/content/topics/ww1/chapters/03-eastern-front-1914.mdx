<Lang en>

In the east, Russia attacked sooner than Germany had expected. Two Russian armies entered East Prussia in mid-August. The German Eighth Army, now under Paul von Hindenburg and Erich Ludendorff, used the railways to turn on the southern one. At Tannenberg, from 26 to 30 August, it was surrounded: <Num s="S60">92,000</Num> to <Num s="S61">95,000</Num> Russians were captured, and their commander, Alexander Samsonov, shot himself. In September the other Russian army was driven out at the Masurian Lakes.

Further south the result was reversed. Russian armies defeated Austria-Hungary in Galicia and took Lemberg (Lviv) on 3 September. Austria-Hungary lost <Num s="S62">300,000</Num> to <Num s="S64">360,000</Num> men, and its fortress of Przemyśl was besieged.

Serbia, the war's first target, drove back two invasions. At Cer in August the Serbian army won the Entente's first victory of the war. During that first invasion Austro-Hungarian troops in the Mačva region killed or caused the disappearance of up to <Num s="S70,S71">4,000</Num> Serbian civilians, treating the population as an enemy. A second invasion in November reached deep into Serbia, but a counter-offensive from 3 December on the Kolubara river drove it out, and on 15 December Serbian troops retook Belgrade.

On 29 October Ottoman warships, two of them German cruisers, shelled Russia's Black Sea ports, and the Ottoman Empire entered the war on Germany's side. British-Indian troops landed at the head of the Persian Gulf and took Basra in November. In the Caucasus, Enver Pasha's winter offensive at Sarıkamış, begun on 22 December, cost the Ottoman Third Army <Num s="S77">60,000</Num> to <Num s="S77">90,000</Num> men, many of them frozen to death.

By the end of 1914 the Western Front was a line of trenches about <Num s="S63">700 km</Num> long. The front in the east, from the Baltic to the Carpathians and later to Romania, ran for more than <Num s="S63">1,600 km</Num>, through country with few railways and roads. There were never as many soldiers per kilometre as in the west, so armies could break through, march and be surrounded. Germany could move troops quickly on its own railways, as it did before Tannenberg; Russia's transport was much weaker. Battles in the east captured whole armies: at Tannenberg alone the Russians lost <Num s="S61">about 120,000</Num> to <Num s="S62">125,000</Num> men, most as prisoners, against German losses of <Num s="S62">10,000</Num> to <Num s="S62">15,000</Num>. At the Masurian Lakes the Russian First Army lost some <Num s="S62">100,000</Num> men and inflicted nearly <Num s="S62">70,000</Num> German casualties.

<More title={{ en: "Civilians in the path of the armies", zh: "军队经过之处的平民" }}>

In north-west Serbia in August 1914 Austro-Hungarian commanders, expecting resistance from irregulars, ordered hostages taken and suspects shot. The Swiss criminologist R. A. Reiss, who investigated for the Serbian government, counted about <Num s="S70">3,500</Num> to <Num s="S70">4,000</Num> civilian victims of that first invasion, not counting Šabac; at Lešnica <Num s="S70">109</Num> people were killed. At Šabac on 17 August about <Num s="S72">80</Num> people were shot in a churchyard, and some <Num s="S72">200</Num> civilians died there in all. Austrian historians today describe these killings as war crimes of the Habsburg army.

In East Prussia the Russian army deliberately killed <Num s="S37">1,491</Num> German civilians in executions and individual murders, and many East Prussians fled west. These killings came from the same fear of civilian resistance as in Belgium and Serbia. They are remembered alongside the soldiers because they were the war's first reach beyond the battlefield into villages and homes.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Serbia, 1914: a small state against an empire", zh: "1914 年的塞尔维亚：小国对抗帝国" }}>

Serbia had fought two Balkan Wars in 1912–13 and was short of ammunition, clothing and doctors. It still defeated both Austro-Hungarian invasions. Austria-Hungary's General Staff counted <Num s="S67">275,000</Num> men lost in Serbia in 1914: <Num s="S67">28,276</Num> killed, <Num s="S67">122,122</Num> wounded, <Num s="S67">76,644</Num> missing, <Num s="S67">2,046</Num> captured and <Num s="S67">46,716</Num> sick. Serbian losses were about <Num s="S67">163,500</Num> killed, wounded, captured and missing. Then typhus, spread by lice among soldiers, prisoners and refugees, swept the country: estimates of deaths run from <Num s="S74">120,000</Num> to <Num s="S73">150,000</Num>, among them <Num s="S74">120</Num> doctors.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the Ottoman Empire went to war", zh: "奥斯曼帝国为什么参战" }}>

The Ottoman government signed a secret alliance with Germany on 2 August 1914 but did not fight at once. On 10 August the German cruisers Goeben and Breslau, escaping British ships in the Mediterranean, reached the Dardanelles; they were transferred to the Ottoman navy and renamed Yavuz Sultan Selim and Midilli, keeping their German crews. Britain had just taken over the battleships being built in British yards for the Ottoman navy, which angered many Ottomans. In October Germany agreed to lend <Num s="S78">5 million</Num> gold liras. Enver Pasha and other Young Turk leaders hoped to win back land lost to Russia in the Caucasus. On 29 October the fleet raided Russia's Black Sea ports. Russia declared war on 2 November (some accounts say 1 November), Britain and France on 5 November, and on 14 November the Ottoman religious authority called Muslims to a holy war against the Entente powers, including the many Muslims living under British, French and Russian rule.

在东线，俄国比德国预料的更早发动了进攻。8 月中旬，两个俄国集团军攻入东普鲁士。由保罗·冯·兴登堡和埃里希·鲁登道夫接手指挥的德军第 8 集团军，利用铁路调兵，转而对付南面的那个集团军。8 月 26 日至 30 日，它在坦嫩贝格被包围：<Num s="S60">9.2 万</Num>到 <Num s="S61">9.5 万</Num>名俄军被俘，司令亚历山大·萨姆索诺夫开枪自杀。9 月，另一个俄国集团军在马祖里湖一带被赶出东普鲁士。

再往南，结果正好相反。俄军在加利西亚击败奥匈军队，9 月 3 日攻占伦贝格（今利沃夫）。奥匈损失 <Num s="S62">30 万</Num>到 <Num s="S64">36 万</Num>人，普热梅希尔要塞被围。

塞尔维亚是这场战争的第一个目标，它两次击退了入侵。8 月，塞军在采尔山取得了协约国一方在这场战争中的第一次胜利。在第一次入侵中，奥匈军队把马奇瓦地区的居民当作敌人，杀害或致使失踪的平民多达 <Num s="S70,S71">4,000</Num> 人。11 月，奥匈第二次入侵深入塞尔维亚腹地，但塞军 12 月 3 日起在科卢巴拉河一线反攻，把奥匈军队赶了出去，12 月 15 日收复贝尔格莱德。

10 月 29 日，奥斯曼军舰——其中两艘是德国巡洋舰——炮击俄国黑海港口，奥斯曼帝国站到德国一边参战。11 月，英印军队在波斯湾顶端登陆，攻占巴士拉。在高加索，恩维尔帕夏 12 月 22 日发动冬季攻势，在萨勒卡默什使奥斯曼第 3 集团军损失 <Num s="S77">6 万</Num>到 <Num s="S77">9 万</Num>人，其中许多人是冻死的。

到 1914 年底，西线已是一条约 <Num s="S63">700 公里</Num>长的堑壕线。东线则从波罗的海延伸到喀尔巴阡山，后来又延伸到罗马尼亚，长达 <Num s="S63">1,600 公里</Num>以上，沿途铁路和公路都很少。每公里战线上的兵力从来没有西线那么密，所以军队能够突破、长途行军，也会被包围。德国能用本国的铁路迅速调兵，坦嫩贝格战前就是这样；俄国的交通则差得多。东线的战役常常整个集团军被俘：仅坦嫩贝格一役，俄军就损失约 <Num s="S61">12 万</Num>到 <Num s="S62">12.5 万</Num>人，大多数是被俘的，而德军损失 <Num s="S62">1 万</Num>到 <Num s="S62">1.5 万</Num>人。马祖里湖战役中，俄军第 1 集团军损失约 <Num s="S62">10 万</Num>人，使德军伤亡近 <Num s="S62">7 万</Num>人。

1914 年 8 月，在塞尔维亚西北部，奥匈指挥官认定会遭到非正规武装的抵抗，下令扣押人质、枪决嫌疑人。受塞尔维亚政府委托调查的瑞士犯罪学家 R. A. 赖斯统计，第一次入侵造成约 <Num s="S70">3,500</Num> 到 <Num s="S70">4,000</Num> 名平民遇难，不含沙巴茨；莱什尼察有 <Num s="S70">109</Num> 人被杀。8 月 17 日在沙巴茨，约 <Num s="S72">80</Num> 人在教堂墓地被枪杀，那里遇难的平民共约 <Num s="S72">200</Num> 人。今天奥地利的历史学者把这些杀戮称为哈布斯堡军队的战争罪行。

在东普鲁士，俄军通过处决和个别杀害，蓄意杀死德国平民 <Num s="S37">1,491</Num> 人，许多东普鲁士居民向西逃难。这些杀戮和比利时、塞尔维亚的一样，出自对平民抵抗的恐惧。人们把这些平民和阵亡士兵一同纪念，因为这是战争第一次越过战场，伸进了村庄和家门。

塞尔维亚刚在 1912–1913 年打完两次巴尔干战争，弹药、军服和医生都很缺乏，却还是击退了奥匈的两次入侵。奥匈总参谋部统计，1914 年在塞尔维亚共损失 <Num s="S67">27.5 万</Num>人：阵亡 <Num s="S67">28,276</Num> 人、负伤 <Num s="S67">122,122</Num> 人、失踪 <Num s="S67">76,644</Num> 人、被俘 <Num s="S67">2,046</Num> 人、患病 <Num s="S67">46,716</Num> 人。塞军死伤、被俘和失踪约 <Num s="S67">16.35 万</Num>人。随后，靠虱子传播的斑疹伤寒在士兵、战俘和难民中蔓延全国，死亡人数估计在 <Num s="S74">12 万</Num>到 <Num s="S73">15 万</Num>之间，其中有 <Num s="S74">120</Num> 名医生。

奥斯曼政府 1914 年 8 月 2 日与德国秘密结盟，但没有马上参战。8 月 10 日，在地中海躲过英国军舰追击的德国巡洋舰“戈本号”和“布雷斯劳号”驶抵达达尼尔海峡，随后转给奥斯曼海军，改名“严君苏丹塞利姆号”和“米迪里号”，舰上仍是德国水兵。英国此前刚刚扣下了正在英国船厂为奥斯曼海军建造的战列舰，激起许多奥斯曼人的不满。10 月，德国同意借给奥斯曼 <Num s="S78">500 万</Num>里拉黄金。恩维尔帕夏等青年土耳其党领导人则希望夺回在高加索输给俄国的土地。10 月 29 日，舰队袭击了俄国黑海港口。俄国 11 月 2 日宣战（一说 11 月 1 日），英法 11 月 5 日宣战；11 月 14 日，奥斯曼宗教最高机构号召穆斯林对协约国进行圣战，对象也包括生活在英、法、俄统治下的众多穆斯林。
