Known simplifications in K9 (details in `scripts/geo/ww1/SOURCES-GEO.md` §2): central Europe is drawn on the lines of the treaties signed after 28 June 1919, not on the fighting lines of that day (the Hungarian–Czechoslovak and Hungarian–Romanian wars and the Polish–Ukrainian war in Galicia were still going on); the Ottoman Empire is one de jure unit with its occupation in the label (the British, French and Arab occupation zones and the Greek landing at Smyrna are not drawn); Russia is one unit labelled as in civil war, with Ukraine labelled as contested (no sourced front map for the day); Albania is drawn as one unit (Italian, Serbian and French occupation not drawn).
