<Lang en>

The cutaway starts at the rocky outside of Earth and looks towards its centre. The thin <Term id="crust">crust</Term> surrounds the <Term id="mantle">mantle</Term>, a much thicker region made mostly of solid rock. The labels also mark the outer core and inner core. A cut face lets us compare places that cannot all be seen from the surface.

Each later chapter changes to a local model: plate blocks, a volcano, a fault, wave markers, seawater or a structure. These samples have independent scales. A thin layer or a small movement is enlarged so that its role is visible. Their positions on the stage do not place them together on a real map.

Published dimensions describe Earth; DESIGN values describe choices in this teaching model. SIM readings are calculated model results. Neither a moving marker nor a bending frame is a field measurement. For a reminder about solid and liquid, visit <TopicLink slug="states-of-matter">States of Matter</TopicLink>. This topic extends that vocabulary to Earth processes.

<More title={{"en": "Evidence for the interior", "zh": "内部结构的证据"}}>

Seismic waves carry information about the materials they pass through. BGS explains how changes in their paths and travel times help scientists infer Earth's layers. The cutaway is a drawing of that interpretation, not an interior photograph.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Independent scales", "zh": "独立尺度"}}>

The globe compares approximate radii. Local sections enlarge layers, displacements and the passage of time. Do not use the globe as a ruler for the volcano or wave bench.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Processes and places", "zh": "过程与地点"}}>

Here the task is to follow what moves, what changes shape and what carries a disturbance. Maps of named plates, hazard histories and regional comparisons belong to a separate map topic; no geographical map is implied by these blocks.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

剖面从地球外面的岩石一直画到中心。很薄的<Term id="crust">地壳</Term>包着厚得多的<Term id="mantle">地幔</Term>，地幔大部分是固态岩石。再向里是外核与内核。这个切面让我们比较从地表无法同时看见的内部区域。

后面的章节会换成板块、火山、断层、波动、海水和建筑的小模型。各自采用独立尺度，薄层与微小运动会放大，方便看出作用。它们在台上的位置，不代表真实地图上的相邻地点。

公开资料的尺寸用来描述地球；“设计值”说明模型采用的选择；“模拟”读数是模型计算，不是实测。移动的标记和弯曲的框架都不能当成现场记录。<TopicLink slug="states-of-matter">物质的状态</TopicLink>能帮助你复习固态与液态；这里把这些词用到地球过程中。

<More title={{"en": "Evidence for the interior", "zh": "内部结构的证据"}}>

地震波经过不同材料时，路径和传播时间会改变。英国地质调查局说明，科学家利用这些信息推断内部的分层。剖面表达的是根据证据建立的模型，不是地球内部的照片。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Independent scales", "zh": "独立尺度"}}>

地球模型比较近似的半径比例；局部剖面则放大层厚、位移，压缩过程所需时间。地球上的尺度不能拿来量旁边的火山或波台。

</More>
