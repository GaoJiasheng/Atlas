<Lang en>

Look from Earth's rocky outside towards its centre. The thin <Term id="crust">crust</Term> surrounds the much thicker <Term id="mantle">mantle</Term>. Most mantle rock is solid. Farther in are the outer core and inner core. The cutaway lets us compare these hidden layers.

Later chapters show plates, a volcano, a fault, waves, seawater and a building. Each model has its own scale. Thin layers and small movements are enlarged so we can see them. Their positions on the stage do not show real map locations.

Earth's published dimensions describe the real planet. DESIGN marks sizes chosen for the models. SIM marks calculated results. The moving markers and bending frame are illustrations, rather than field measurements. <TopicLink slug="states-of-matter">States of Matter</TopicLink> explains solid and liquid before we explore them inside Earth.

<More title={{"en": "Evidence for the interior", "zh": "内部结构的证据"}}>

Seismic waves change their paths and travel times as they pass through different materials. Scientists use these changes to work out Earth's layers, as BGS explains. The cutaway shows their interpretation of the evidence.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Different scales", "zh": "不同的比例"}}>

The globe compares approximate radii. The smaller models enlarge layers and movements, and show long processes quickly. Each uses its own scale, so the globe cannot serve as a ruler for the volcano or waves.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Processes and places", "zh": "过程与地点"}}>

Follow what moves, what changes shape and how a disturbance travels. A separate map topic will show named plates, hazard histories and regional comparisons.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

从地球外面的岩石向中心看。薄薄的<Term id="crust">地壳</Term>包着厚得多的<Term id="mantle">地幔</Term>，地幔大部分是固态岩石。再向里是外核和内核。剖面让我们看见并比较这些隐藏的层。

后面会看到板块、火山、断层、波、海水和建筑模型。每个模型各用自己的比例。薄层和微小运动会放大，方便观察。它们在台上的位置不表示真实地图上的地点。

公开资料中的地球尺寸描述真实地球。DESIGN（设计值）表示模型选用的大小，SIM（模拟）表示计算结果。移动标记和弯曲框架都是示意，不是现场实测。可以先到<TopicLink slug="states-of-matter">物质的状态</TopicLink>复习固态与液态，再探索地球内部。

<More title={{"en": "Evidence for the interior", "zh": "内部结构的证据"}}>

地震波经过不同材料时，传播路径和时间会改变。英国地质调查局介绍，科学家用这些变化推断地球分层。剖面显示的是根据证据建立的解释。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Different scales", "zh": "不同的比例"}}>

球体比较近似半径，局部模型则放大岩层和运动，并加快漫长过程。各模型比例独立，不能用地球模型的比例去量火山或波。

</More>
