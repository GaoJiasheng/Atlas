<Lang en>

Turn off seafloor displacement. The water stays still, although the card still shows a theoretical wave speed. Turn displacement on and advance a step. The central seabed rises while neighbouring parts fall, moving the water above. This shows one way a tsunami can start. Not every earthquake produces one.

A <Term id="tsunami">tsunami</Term> is a series of long waves caused by a large, sudden movement of water. The disturbance spreads in both directions. A water column extends from the surface to the seabed. Tsunami motion involves this whole depth. The enlarged markers show that idea, rather than measured paths of individual parcels of water.

Now select the shore sample. In shallower water, tsunami waves slow down. Their wavelength, the distance between crests, shortens, and their height can increase. The sample shows the change in shape without calculating a real coast. The steps are diagram stages, rather than seconds.

Predict the speed change, then compare depths in the card. In this model, a depth of <Num s="S63">4,000 m</Num> gives about <Num s="S63">713 km/h</Num>. At <Num s="S63">100 m</Num>, the speed is about <Num s="S63">113 km/h</Num>. Record different depths with displacement and step unchanged. Clear the table before changing those controls. Each row stores depth, theoretical speed and water state.

<More title={{"en": "The speed approximation", "zh": "速度近似"}}>

For a long wave, approximate speed is c ≈ √(gh). Here g is <Num s="S15,S63">9.8 m/s²</Num> and h is water depth. At <Num s="S63">100, 1,000 and 4,000 m</Num>, the model gives <Num s="S63">31.3, 99.0 and 198.0 m/s</Num>, or <Num s="S63">112.7, 356.4 and 712.8 km/h</Num>. To convert units, multiply the unrounded speed by <Num s="S63">3.6</Num>. The formula gives speed if a wave exists, even with displacement switched off. It cannot give wave height, flooding or real arrival time.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Other ways to move water", "zh": "其他水体位移成因"}}>

Tsunamis are unrelated to tides. Landslides and volcanic processes can also move water suddenly. The switch controls only the seabed source shown here. Changing depth changes the calculated speed; it leaves the diagram at the same step.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "The first arrival", "zh": "最先到达的波"}}>

NOAA explains that the sea need not withdraw before a tsunami rises. The first wave need not be the largest. One diagram cannot show every way a tsunami may reach a coast.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

先关闭海床位移。水体保持静止，卡片仍显示理论波速。打开位移并前进一步，中央海床上升，相邻部分下降，带动上方海水。这是海啸形成的一种方式，但地震不一定产生海啸。

<Term id="tsunami">海啸</Term>是大量水体突然移动产生的一系列长波。扰动向两边传播。从水面到海床的整段水体叫水柱，海啸运动涉及整个水深。放大标记用来说明这一点，不是每一团海水的实测轨迹。

再选择近岸小样。海啸进入浅水后会减速，波长，也就是相邻波峰之间的距离，会缩短，波高可能增加。小样显示形状变化，没有计算真实海岸情况。图解步骤也不表示秒数。

先预测速度怎样变化，再用卡片比较。本模型水深 <Num s="S63">4000 米</Num>时约为<Num s="S63">每小时 713 千米</Num>；<Num s="S63">100 米</Num>时约为<Num s="S63">每小时 113 千米</Num>。记录不同水深时，保持位移开关和步骤相同。改变这些条件前先清表。每行保存水深、理论速度和水体状态。

<More title={{"en": "The speed approximation", "zh": "速度近似"}}>

长波速度近似为 c ≈ √(gh)。这里 g 为 <Num s="S15,S63">9.8 米每平方秒</Num>，h 是水深。<Num s="S63">100、1000、4000 米</Num>对应 <Num s="S63">31.3、99.0、198.0 米每秒</Num>，或<Num s="S63">每小时 112.7、356.4、712.8 千米</Num>。换算时，把未舍入的速度乘 <Num s="S63">3.6</Num>。即使关闭位移，公式仍会给出存在长波时的理论速度；它不计算波高、淹没或真实到岸时间。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Other ways to move water", "zh": "其他水体位移成因"}}>

海啸与潮汐无关。滑坡和火山过程也能突然移开水体。开关只控制图中的海床位移源。改变水深会改变计算速度，图解仍停在同一步。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "The first arrival", "zh": "最先到达的波"}}>

NOAA 说明，海啸到来前，海水不一定先退；第一波也不一定最大。一幅图不能显示海啸到岸的所有情况。

</More>

</Lang>
