<Lang en>

Start at the cut edge. The crust is Earth's thin rocky outside, with different thicknesses in different places. This model uses a uniform land-crust example of <Num s="S59,S63">30 km</Num>. It looks thin beside Earth's approximate mean radius of <Num s="S58">6,371 km</Num>.

Below the crust is the mantle. Most of it is solid rock that can slowly change shape over geological time. Farther in, liquid metal in the <Term id="outer-core">outer core</Term> surrounds the solid-metal <Term id="inner-core">inner core</Term>. Neither core supplies volcanic magma.

A radius runs from the centre to a boundary. The inner-core boundary is about <Num s="S59">1,221 km</Num> from Earth's centre. The outer-core boundary is about <Num s="S58">3,485 km</Num> away. These radii set the approximate proportions of the globe. They are rounded values from NASA's Earth information.

Select the enlarged sample below the globe. The crust and rigid uppermost mantle together form the <Term id="lithosphere">lithosphere</Term>. A moving block of this shell is a <Term id="tectonic-plate">tectonic plate</Term>. The mantle below it also remains mostly solid. A plate can carry both continental and oceanic crust, so a coastline need not be a plate boundary. The lithosphere includes parts of two material layers; it is not an extra layer outside them.

<More title={{"en": "Radius and thickness", "zh": "半径与厚度"}}>

A radius starts at the centre. A layer's thickness is the distance between its inner and outer boundaries. Subtracting the inner radius from the outer radius gives this model's outer-core thickness: <Num s="S63">2,264 km</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Crust thickness", "zh": "地壳厚度"}}>

NASA gives an average land-crust thickness of about <Num s="S59">30 km</Num> and an oceanic example of about <Num s="S59">5 km</Num>. The model uses the land value everywhere to keep the shell simple. Real crust varies in thickness.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A solid that deforms", "zh": "会变形的固体"}}>

BGS explains that solid mantle rock can change shape over very long times. Some local regions can be partly molten while most mantle stays solid. <TopicLink slug="states-of-matter">Compare solid and liquid</TopicLink>.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

先看切口最外边。地壳是很薄的岩石外层，各处厚度不同。模型统一采用 <Num s="S59,S63">30 千米</Num>的陆壳示例。与地球约 <Num s="S58">6371 千米</Num>的平均半径相比，这层很薄。

地壳下面是地幔。地幔大部分是固态岩石，能在漫长的地质时间内缓慢变形。再向里，液态金属的<Term id="outer-core">外核</Term>包围固态金属的<Term id="inner-core">内核</Term>。火山岩浆不来自内核或外核。

半径是中心到边界的距离。内核边界离地心约 <Num s="S59">1221 千米</Num>，外核边界约 <Num s="S58">3485 千米</Num>。球体按这些半径比较近似大小。数值来自 NASA 的地球资料，已经取整。

选择球体下方的放大小样。地壳和刚性的最上部地幔合称<Term id="lithosphere">岩石圈</Term>，其中移动的块体叫<Term id="tectonic-plate">板块</Term>。下方地幔仍大部分是固态。一块板块可以同时带着大陆和海洋地壳，所以海岸线未必是板块边缘。岩石圈包含地壳和部分地幔，并不是套在它们外面的新一层。

<More title={{"en": "Radius and thickness", "zh": "半径与厚度"}}>

半径从中心量起。层厚则是内外边界之间的距离。用外半径减去内半径，可得模型的外核厚度：<Num s="S63">2264 千米</Num>。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Crust thickness", "zh": "地壳厚度"}}>

NASA 给出的陆壳平均厚度约为 <Num s="S59">30 千米</Num>，海洋地壳示例约为 <Num s="S59">5 千米</Num>。为简化形状，模型处处采用陆壳数值；真实地壳厚薄不同。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A solid that deforms", "zh": "会变形的固体"}}>

英国地质调查局介绍，固态地幔岩石能在极长时间内变形。局部可以部分熔融，大部分地幔仍保持固态。可回到<TopicLink slug="states-of-matter">固态与液态</TopicLink>作比较。

</More>

</Lang>
