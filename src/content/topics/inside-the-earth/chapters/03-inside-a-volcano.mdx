<Lang en>

Solid rock surrounds a local storage region containing melt and crystals. Below ground, molten rock material is called <Term id="magma">magma</Term>. It can also contain dissolved gas. The storage region contains material, rather than being an empty cave or a molten layer around the whole Earth.

Follow the narrow passage upwards. This conduit carries magma towards a <Term id="vent">volcanic vent</Term>, an opening where gas, fragments or lava reach the surface or atmosphere. The cone surrounds the opening. The connected route runs from storage through the conduit to the vent; it does not reach the core.

Magma that erupts onto the surface is called <Term id="lava">lava</Term>. Compare the slope sample with the material underground. Moving dots show a possible upward route. Their spacing and speed help us see the path, rather than measure an eruption's flow rate.

Pressure can fall as magma rises. Dissolved gas can then form bubbles, which expand and help drive an eruption. Look at the enlarged bubbles inside the conduit. They show how gas is released; they do not calculate a pressure threshold or predict when a volcano will erupt.

<More title={{"en": "One simplified route", "zh": "一条简化路线"}}>

Real magma systems can have several storage regions and more complicated passages. The diagram uses one storage region and one visible route to make the path clear.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Mostly solid and partly molten", "zh": "主体固态与局部熔融"}}>

Partial melting means some material melts while some stays solid. Melt and crystals can coexist locally, while most surrounding rock and most mantle stay solid.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "No supply from the core", "zh": "供浆不来自地核"}}>

NPS explains that volcanic magma does not come from the core. The storage and conduit lie in a local rock section. The globe and volcano use independent scales.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

固态围岩包围着局部储存区，里面有熔体和晶体。地下的熔融岩石物质叫<Term id="magma">岩浆</Term>，也可能含有溶解气体。储存区里装着物质，不是空洞，也不是环绕整个地球的熔融层。

沿狭窄通道向上看。岩浆沿通道到达<Term id="vent">喷发口</Term>。气体、碎屑或熔岩可通过这个开口到达地表或大气。火山锥围着开口。储存区、通道和喷发口相连，这条路线不通往地核。

This diagram simplifies the route.", "zh": "岩浆从储存区沿通道到达喷发口。图中路线已简化。"}} />

岩浆喷到地表后，叫<Term id="lava">熔岩</Term>。把坡上的样本与地下物质比较。移动的点显示一条可能的上升路线，点的疏密和速度方便我们看清路径，不表示实测喷发流速。

岩浆上升时，压力可能下降。溶解气体可形成气泡，膨胀后推动喷发。观察通道内放大的气泡。它们说明气体怎样释放，不计算喷发压力门槛，也不预测火山何时喷发。

<More title={{"en": "One simplified route", "zh": "一条简化路线"}}>

真实岩浆系统可能有多个储存区和更复杂的通道。图中用一个储存区和一条可见路线，让路径更清楚。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Mostly solid and partly molten", "zh": "主体固态与局部熔融"}}>

部分熔融表示有些物质熔化，有些仍是固态。局部可以同时有熔体和晶体，而周围岩石及大部分地幔仍保持固态。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "No supply from the core", "zh": "供浆不来自地核"}}>

美国国家公园管理局说明，火山岩浆不来自地核。储存区和通道位于局部岩石剖面内，球体和火山采用各自的比例。

</More>
