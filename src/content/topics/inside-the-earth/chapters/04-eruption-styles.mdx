<Lang en>

Compare the lava on the slope with the fragments above the vent. Effusive activity means lava flows out. Explosive activity breaks magma into fragments that expanding gas can throw into the air. These samples show contrasting processes. A real eruption can change between different kinds of activity.

Viscosity means resistance to flowing. More viscous magma flows less easily. Gas content matters too. As magma rises and pressure falls, dissolved gas can form expanding bubbles. Gas escapes more easily from less viscous magma. More viscous, gas-rich magma tends to erupt more explosively, though these properties alone do not determine every eruption.

The way magma rises and the conditions along its route also affect an eruption. Select the storage region, bubbles and conduit to follow the process. Their movement shows the relationship. It does not calculate gas pressure, a threshold for eruption or a flow rate, and cannot forecast an eruption.

Select the ash. Volcanic ash contains fragments of rock, minerals and glass. It differs from the soft ash left by a fire. The particles are enlarged so we can see and select them. Both ash and lava remain visible for comparison as each beat highlights one process.

<Lang zh>

比较坡上的熔岩与喷发口上方的碎屑。溢流式活动是熔岩向外流出。爆炸式活动使岩浆破碎，膨胀气体可把碎屑抛到空中。两种样本显示不同过程。一次真实喷发的活动方式也可以改变。

黏度是抵抗流动的程度。黏度高的岩浆较难流动，含气量也会影响喷发。岩浆上升、压力下降时，溶解气体可形成膨胀气泡。气体较容易从黏度低的岩浆中逸出。黏度高、含气多的岩浆往往更易爆炸式喷发，但这些性质不能单独决定每次喷发。

岩浆怎样上升、沿途通道情况怎样，也会影响喷发。依次选择储存区、气泡和通道，跟着过程看。模型运动显示它们的关系，不计算气压、喷发门槛或流速，也不能预报喷发。

选择火山灰。它含岩石、矿物和玻璃碎屑，与火烧后留下的松软灰烬不同。模型放大颗粒，方便观察和点选。每个节拍突出一种过程，两种产物都留在台上供比较。
