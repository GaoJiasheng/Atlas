<Lang en>

A <Term id="fault">fault</Term> is a fracture surface along which rocks can move past each other. This model contains no magma. A locked contact can hold while surrounding movement bends the rocks and stores elastic energy. Watch the marker band bend before the contact slips.

Sudden slip releases stored energy as seismic waves. The marker becomes less bent, but its sides remain offset. Rupture starts at the <Term id="focus">focus</Term> inside the rock. The <Term id="epicentre">epicentre</Term> lies directly above it on the surface. It is a point, rather than the whole fault, and need not be the place that shakes most strongly.

The rows below compare waves travelling through solid material. In a <Term id="p-wave">P wave</Term>, markers move back and forth along the travel direction. In an <Term id="s-wave">S wave</Term>, they move at right angles to it. P waves travel faster than S waves in solids. Both rows show the same instant: the disturbance travels on while each marker moves near its starting place.

Use the snapshot control to compare a marker's positions. Switch the inspected wave to read the other wavefront; this leaves both rows at the same instant. Movement is enlarged, and speeds use diagram units. Real ground motion also includes surface waves. <TopicLink slug="forces">Forces</TopicLink> and <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion">Energy Conversion</TopicLink> explain deformation and stored energy.

<More title={{"en": "Wave model numbers", "zh": "波台的模型数字"}}>

Amplitude, the greatest distance a marker moves from rest, is <Num s="S63">0.08 scene units</Num>. Frequency is <Num s="S63">1 cycle per model second</Num>. P and S speeds are <Num s="S63">4</Num> and <Num s="S63">2 diagram units per model second</Num>. Each step advances <Num s="S63">0.25 model seconds</Num>. These values are chosen for the diagram. The abbreviation du means diagram unit, rather than a real distance in metres.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A liquid outer core", "zh": "液态外核"}}>

S waves cannot travel directly through the liquid outer core. Scientists use this evidence to help work out the core's state. Most mantle rock remains solid.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Event size and local effects", "zh": "事件大小与当地影响"}}>

Magnitude describes an earthquake's size. Intensity describes its effects at a particular place, as the Hong Kong Observatory explains. The model calculates neither. It also cannot predict the exact time and place of a future earthquake.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

<Term id="fault">断层</Term>是岩体能沿着相对移动的破裂面。这个模型没有岩浆。接触处锁定时，周围仍在运动，岩石会变形并储存弹性能。先看标记带弯曲，再看接触处滑动。

突然滑动把储存的能量以地震波形式释放。标记带变直一些，两边却留下错距。岩石内部破裂开始的位置叫<Term id="focus">震源</Term>，地表正上方的点叫<Term id="epicentre">震中</Term>。震中只是一个点，不代表整条断层，也未必是震得最强的地方。

下方两排比较固体中的地震波。<Term id="p-wave">P 波（纵波）</Term>的标记沿传播方向来回运动；<Term id="s-wave">S 波（横波）</Term>的运动则与传播方向成直角。固体中的 P 波比 S 波快。两排显示同一时刻：扰动向远处传播，各标记只在原位附近往返。

用快照控件比较同一标记的位置。切换观察波型，可以读取另一种波的波前，两排仍保持同一时刻。位移已经放大，速度采用图解单位。真实地面运动还包括表面波。<TopicLink slug="forces">力</TopicLink>和<TopicLink slug="energy-conversion">能量转换</TopicLink>介绍变形与储存能量。

<More title={{"en": "Wave model numbers", "zh": "波台的模型数字"}}>

振幅是标记离开原位的最大距离，这里为 <Num s="S63">0.08 场景单位</Num>。频率为<Num s="S63">每模型秒 1 周</Num>。P、S 波速度分别为<Num s="S63">每模型秒 4</Num>和 <Num s="S63">2 图解单位</Num>，每一步前进 <Num s="S63">0.25 模型秒</Num>。这些数值供画图使用。du 表示图解单位，不是以米计的真实距离。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A liquid outer core", "zh": "液态外核"}}>

S 波不能直接穿过液态外核。科学家利用这项证据帮助判断外核的物态。地幔大部分仍是固态岩石。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Event size and local effects", "zh": "事件大小与当地影响"}}>

香港天文台说明，震级描述地震大小，烈度描述某处受到的影响。模型没有计算这两者，也不能精确预测未来地震的时间和地点。

</More>
