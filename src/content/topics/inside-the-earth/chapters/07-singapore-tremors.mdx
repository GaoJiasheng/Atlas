<Lang en>

Singapore lies far from major active plate boundaries. Even so, distant earthquakes in Sumatra can send seismic waves that cause felt tremors here. The fault can be far away while waves reach the ground beneath a building.

Compare the building leaning in opposite directions. The ground moves and the structure responds. Its foundation supports it at ground level. Beams and columns form the frame above. Floors, frame and structural walls stay connected as the model moves. Good connections and suitable bracing matter in real buildings. The lean is enlarged to make these connections visible.

Ground conditions and the way a structure vibrates both affect its response. Height alone cannot tell us how safe a building is. Material strength and flexibility matter, along with the arrangement and connections. <TopicLink slug="materials">Materials Around Us</TopicLink> explores these properties. Here we compare a connected structure without calculating its engineering performance.

The Building and Construction Authority, BCA, says Singapore buildings are designed to withstand distant tremors. Its indoor advice includes taking cover under a table and not using lifts. Read the full guidance below. The model illustrates movement; it cannot assess a real building or guarantee safety in every earthquake.

<More title={{"en": "Resonance", "zh": "共振"}}>

Every structure has a natural vibration frequency: a rate at which it tends to vibrate. Shaking near that frequency can cause a stronger response, called resonance. BGS explains how this works alongside ground conditions and construction.

</More>

This is a general building model, rather than an HDB drawing. Its floors and enlarged lean help explain connections. It has no assumed isolation system, site measurements or structural safety calculation.

</More>

<Lang zh>

新加坡远离主要活动板块边界。即使如此，苏门答腊远震仍能传来地震波，让本地感到震动。断层可以很远，地震波仍会到达建筑下方的地面。

比较建筑向两边倾斜的画面。地面移动，结构随之运动。基础在地面处支撑建筑，上方梁柱组成框架。模型移动时，楼板、框架和结构墙保持连接。真实建筑也需要正确连接和合适支撑。图中倾斜已放大，方便看清连接关系。

地面条件和结构的振动方式都会影响反应。单凭高矮，不能判断建筑是否安全。材料强度、柔韧性、构件排列和连接都很重要。<TopicLink slug="materials">身边的材料</TopicLink>介绍这些性质。这里观察相连结构，没有计算工程性能。

新加坡建设局 BCA 说明，新加坡建筑按抵御远震震动设计。其室内建议包括到桌下躲避、不用电梯。完整建议见下方链接。模型用来说明运动，不能评估真实建筑，也不能保证任何地震中的安全。

<More title={{"en": "Resonance", "zh": "共振"}}>

结构有自身的振动频率，也就是它倾向采用的振动节奏。地面振动频率接近这个频率时，结构的反应可能增强，这叫共振。英国地质调查局把共振、地面条件和建造方式结合起来解释。

</More>
