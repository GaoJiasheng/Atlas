<Lang en>

Compare the plate models side by side. Each block includes crust and rigid uppermost mantle, which move together. At a boundary, neighbouring plates can separate, move towards each other or slide past each other.

At the spreading boundary, plates move apart. Magma rises into the gap and can form new oceanic crust. At one converging boundary, an oceanic slab bends beneath another plate. This is <Term id="subduction">subduction</Term>. Water carried down by the slab helps some rock above it melt. Follow the slab, then find the small molten region above it.

The folded continental model shows another kind of convergence. Colliding continents can build mountains, without the same volcanic arrangement as the subduction example. At the transform boundary, plates slide mainly sideways. The surface marks become offset. This movement does not steadily create or consume crust.

Real plates typically move a few centimetres relative to each other each year, with rates varying by place. The models speed up the motion and enlarge layers and offsets. Select each sample to compare its structure and direction. These are diagrams of processes, with no real map positions or shared global scale.

<Lang zh>

并排比较这些板块模型。每个块体都包括地壳和刚性的最上部地幔，两部分一起移动。相邻板块可以分开、靠近，也可以沿边缘互相错动。

扩张边界的板块向两旁分开。岩浆上升到空隙，可形成新的海洋地壳。一种汇聚边界中，海洋板片弯向另一板块下方，这叫<Term id="subduction">俯冲</Term>。板片带下去的水，帮助上方部分岩石熔融。先沿着板片看，再找它上方的小片熔融区。

起褶皱的大陆模型显示另一种汇聚。大陆碰撞能形成山脉，火山分布却不一定像俯冲示例。转换边界的板块主要沿水平方向错动，地表标记留下错距。这种运动不会持续产生或消耗地壳。

真实板块之间的典型相对运动每年只有几厘米，各处速度不同。模型加快运动，并放大岩层和错距。逐件选择小样，比较结构和方向。这些过程图没有真实地图位置，也没有统一的全球比例。
