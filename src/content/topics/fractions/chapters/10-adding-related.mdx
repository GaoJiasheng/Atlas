Halves and eighths are <Term id="related-fractions">related fractions</Term>: 8 is a multiple of 2, so every half can be cut into 4 eighths. Parts of different sizes cannot be counted together, so first rename the bigger parts in the smaller ones.

To work out <Frac n={1} d={2} /> + <Frac n={3} d={8} />, cut the half into eighths. <Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={4} d={8} />. Now add eighths to eighths:

<Frac n={1} d={2} /> + <Frac n={3} d={8} /> = <Frac n={4} d={8} /> + <Frac n={3} d={8} /> = <Frac n={7} d={8} />

Two mistakes are common. Adding the tops and the bottoms gives <Frac n={4} d={10} />, which is less than the half we started with. Adding without renaming counts the half as 1 eighth and gives <Frac n={4} d={8} />; but a half is 4 eighths, not 1.

When a question asks for the simplest form, finish by joining parts: <Frac n={1} d={6} /> + <Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={1} d={6} /> + <Frac n={3} d={6} /> = <Frac n={4} d={6} /> = <Frac n={2} d={3} />.

For a word problem, draw a model: one bar for the whole, a piece for each amount, and a bracket with a question mark over the total.

二分之几和八分之几是<Term id="related-fractions">相关分数（分母成倍数）</Term>：8 是 2 的倍数，每个二分之一都能分成 4 个八分之一。大小不同的份不能直接合起来数，所以先把大的份换成小的份。

计算 <Frac n={1} d={2} /> + <Frac n={3} d={8} />，先把一半分成八分之几：<Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={4} d={8} />。再把八分之几加起来：

<Frac n={1} d={2} /> + <Frac n={3} d={8} /> = <Frac n={4} d={8} /> + <Frac n={3} d={8} /> = <Frac n={7} d={8} />

有两个常见的错误。把分子、分母分别相加，得到 <Frac n={4} d={10} />，比原来的一半还少。不换就直接加，把一半当成 1 个八分之一，得到 <Frac n={4} d={8} />；可是一半是 4 个八分之一，不是 1 个。

题目要求最简分数时，最后还要合并：<Frac n={1} d={6} /> + <Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={1} d={6} /> + <Frac n={3} d={6} /> = <Frac n={4} d={6} /> = <Frac n={2} d={3} />。
