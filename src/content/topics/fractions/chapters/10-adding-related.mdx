<Lang en>

Halves and quarters are <Term id="related-fractions">related fractions</Term>: each half is 2 quarters. Rename the half first, then add quarters to quarters: <Frac n={1} d={2} /> + <Frac n={1} d={4} /> = <Frac n={2} d={4} /> + <Frac n={1} d={4} /> = <Frac n={3} d={4} />. A model drawing of the whole helps with word problems.
