<Lang en>

Fractions with different denominators are unlike fractions. Their parts are different sizes, so counting parts does not tell you which is greater. First make the parts the same size.

Compare <Frac n={1} d={2} /> and <Frac n={3} d={5} />. 2 and 5 both go into 10, so cut both bars into tenths: <Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={5} d={10} /> and <Frac n={3} d={5} /> = <Frac n={6} d={10} />. Now the parts are the same size, and 5 tenths are fewer than 6 tenths, so <Frac n={1} d={2} /> &lt; <Frac n={3} d={5} />.

When one denominator goes into the other, rename only one fraction. To compare <Frac n={2} d={5} /> and <Frac n={7} d={10} />, write <Frac n={2} d={5} /> = <Frac n={4} d={10} />, which is less than <Frac n={7} d={10} />.

Two shortcuts give wrong answers. Comparing only the top numbers fails: <Frac n={4} d={10} /> has the bigger top number, yet it is smaller than <Frac n={1} d={2} />. Looking only at the gap to a whole fails too: <Frac n={1} d={2} /> is one part short of a whole and <Frac n={3} d={5} /> is two parts short, but a missing half is bigger than two missing fifths.

To order three fractions, rename them all in the same parts, then order the tops.

<More title={{ en: "Same whole, same length", zh: "同一个整体，一样长" }}>

Every pair of bars on the stage is the same length, because both fractions are parts of the same whole. Lining them up, one under the other, is a quick first check: the shaded part that reaches further to the right is greater.

<Lang zh>

分母不同的分数叫异分母分数。它们的每份大小不同，只数份数看不出哪个大。要先化成同样大的份。

比较 <Frac n={1} d={2} /> 和 <Frac n={3} d={5} />。2 和 5 都能变成 10，所以把两条都分成十分之几：<Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={5} d={10} />，<Frac n={3} d={5} /> = <Frac n={6} d={10} />。现在每份一样大，5 个十分之一比 6 个十分之一少，所以 <Frac n={1} d={2} /> &lt; <Frac n={3} d={5} />。

如果一个分母是另一个的倍数，只要换一个分数。比较 <Frac n={2} d={5} /> 和 <Frac n={7} d={10} />：<Frac n={2} d={5} /> = <Frac n={4} d={10} />，比 <Frac n={7} d={10} /> 小。

有两种捷径会出错。只比分子不行：<Frac n={4} d={10} /> 的分子大，却比 <Frac n={1} d={2} /> 小。只看差几份到一个整体也不行：<Frac n={1} d={2} /> 差一份，<Frac n={3} d={5} /> 差两份，但缺的一个二分之一比缺的两个五分之一还多。

给三个分数排序，先把它们都换成同样大的份，再按分子排。
