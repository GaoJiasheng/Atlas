<Lang en>

One of 4 equal parts is one quarter, written <Frac n={1} d={4} />. This is a <Term id="unit-fraction">unit fraction</Term>. The bottom number, the <Term id="denominator">denominator</Term>, counts the equal parts in the whole. The top number, the <Term id="numerator">numerator</Term>, counts the parts we take.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

4 个相等部分中的一份是四分之一，写作 <Frac n={1} d={4} />。这是一个<Term id="unit-fraction">单位分数</Term>。下面的数叫<Term id="denominator">分母</Term>，数整体平均分成几份；上面的数叫<Term id="numerator">分子</Term>，数取了几份。
