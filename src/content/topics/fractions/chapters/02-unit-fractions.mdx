<Lang en>

Cut a whole into equal parts and take one of them. One of 2 equal parts is one half, written <Frac n={1} d={2} />. One of 5 equal parts is one fifth, <Frac n={1} d={5} />. A fraction with 1 on top is a <Term id="unit-fraction">unit fraction</Term>: it names one part.

A fraction has two numbers. The bottom number, the <Term id="denominator">denominator</Term>, counts all the equal parts in the whole. The top number, the <Term id="numerator">numerator</Term>, counts the parts we take.

Find the bottom number first, and count every part of the whole, shaded and not shaded. A common mistake is to count only the parts that are not shaded. A strip with 1 part shaded and 4 parts not shaded has 5 parts in all, so it shows <Frac n={1} d={5} />, not <Frac n={1} d={4} />. Another mistake is to swap the two numbers. The whole always goes at the bottom.

The parts must still be equal. Three parts of different sizes do not make thirds.

Now try: <Task id="prata-one-quarter">shade one part of the prata</Task>, then write and choose unit fractions.

<More title={{ en: "Saying fractions", zh: "分数怎么读" }}>

In English we say the top number first: one half, one third, one quarter, one fifth. In Chinese the bottom number comes first: 二分之一 means "of 2 parts, 1". The Chinese order follows the counting order of this step: first the parts in the whole, then the parts we take.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

把一个整体平均分，取其中一份。平均分成 2 份，其中一份是二分之一，写作 <Frac n={1} d={2} />；平均分成 5 份，其中一份是五分之一，写作 <Frac n={1} d={5} />。分子是 1 的分数叫<Term id="unit-fraction">单位分数</Term>，它表示其中一份。

一个分数有两个数。下面的数叫<Term id="denominator">分母</Term>，数整体一共平均分成几份；上面的数叫<Term id="numerator">分子</Term>，数取了几份。中间的横线叫分数线。

先找分母，要数整体的每一份，涂了的和没涂的都算。常见的错误是只数没涂的份。一条纸条涂了 1 份、没涂 4 份，一共是 5 份，所以表示 <Frac n={1} d={5} />，不是 <Frac n={1} d={4} />。另一个错误是把两个数写反了：整体的份数总是写在下面。

各份还必须一样大。大小不同的 3 份，不是三分之一。

<More title={{ en: "Saying fractions", zh: "分数怎么读" }}>

英语先读分子：one half、one third、one quarter。中文先读分母：「二分之一」就是「分成 2 份，取 1 份」。中文的读法正好是这一步的数法：先数整体有几份，再数取了几份。
