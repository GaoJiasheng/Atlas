<Lang en>

Every <Term id="fraction">fraction</Term> starts with one <Term id="whole">whole</Term>: a strip of paper, a slice of kaya toast, a piece of kueh. Before we can name a part of it, the whole must be cut into <Term id="equal-parts">equal parts</Term>. Equal means every part is the same amount.

Counting the pieces is not enough. A kueh cut into 3 pieces of different sizes is not cut into thirds, even though there are 3 pieces. Look at the size of every piece.

Equal parts do not have to be the same shape. Cut a square kueh from corner to corner and you get two triangles. Turn one over and it covers the other exactly, so the two triangles are equal halves.

On the stage you will also fold and cut. Each fold in half cuts every part into two, so the number of equal parts doubles. When you cut the toast, use the marks under it: equal parts cover the same number of marks.

Now try: <Task id="kueh-equal-parts">find the kueh with equal parts</Task>, then fold the strip and cut the toast.

<More title={{ en: "Sharing a kueh lapis", zh: "分千层糕" }}>

At a festive open house, a kueh lapis is cut into slices for the guests. If one slice is thin and another thick, everyone has "one slice", but not the same amount. Sharing fairly means equal parts. That is why a fraction always starts by checking that the parts are equal.

</More>

<Lang zh>

每个<Term id="fraction">分数</Term>都从一个<Term id="whole">整体</Term>开始：一条纸条、一片咖椰吐司、一块千层糕。要给其中一部分起名字，先要把整体<Term id="equal-parts">平均分</Term>，也就是分成一样多的几份。

只数块数是不够的。一块千层糕切成大小不同的 3 块，虽然有 3 块，却不是平均分成 3 份，每块也就不是三分之一。每一块都要比一比大小。

平均分的各份不一定形状相同。把一块正方形的千层糕沿对角线切开，得到两个三角形；把一个翻过来，正好盖住另一个，所以这两个三角形一样多，各是一半。

在舞台上你还会折纸、切吐司。纸条每对折一次，每一份都被分成两份，份数就变成原来的 2 倍。切吐司时，用下面的刻度量一量：相等的各份占的格数一样。

现在试试：先<Task id="kueh-equal-parts">找出平均分的千层糕</Task>，再折纸条、切吐司。

<More title={{ en: "Sharing a kueh lapis", zh: "分千层糕" }}>

节日到亲友家拜访时，主人会把千层糕切成片招待客人。如果有的片薄、有的片厚，大家虽然都拿到「一片」，吃到的却不一样多。公平地分，就是平均分。所以讲分数，第一步总是看各份是不是一样多。
