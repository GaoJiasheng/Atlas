<Lang en>

Every fraction starts with one <Term id="whole">whole</Term>: a strip of paper, a slice of toast, a piece of kueh. Before we can name a part, the whole must be cut into <Term id="equal-parts">equal parts</Term>. Equal means the same amount, even when the pieces are different shapes. Try it with <Task id="cut-toast-thirds">the toast</Task>.

</Lang>
