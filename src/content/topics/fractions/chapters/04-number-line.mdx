<Lang en>

On a <Term id="number-line">number line</Term>, the stretch from 0 to 1 is one whole. Cut it into equal jumps and count the jumps, not the marks: <Frac n={3} d={5} /> is three jumps of one fifth from 0. Think of a train ride between two MRT stations split into equal stretches.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

在<Term id="number-line">数轴</Term>上，0 到 1 这一段是一个整体。把它平均分成几段，数的是段，不是刻度线：<Frac n={3} d={5} /> 是从 0 跳 3 段五分之一。可以想象两个地铁站之间平均分成几段的路。

</Lang>
