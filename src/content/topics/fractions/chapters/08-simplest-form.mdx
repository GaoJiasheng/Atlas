<Lang en>

You have seen that cutting each part into smaller parts gives <Term id="equivalent-fractions">equivalent fractions</Term>: different names for the same amount. Now you will go the other way, joining smaller parts to make fewer, bigger parts.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>six shaded parts</Beat> of the strip. The <Term id="whole">whole</Term> strip has eight <Term id="equal-parts">equal parts</Term>, so the shaded amount is <Frac n={6} d={8} />. Keep your eye on where the shading ends. When you join parts, this end must stay in the same place because you are keeping the same amount.

Now <Beat n={2}>join the parts in twos</Beat>. Each pair becomes one bigger part. The eight parts become four, and the six shaded parts become three. You get <Frac n={6} d={8} /> = <Frac n={3} d={4} />. The top number, or <Term id="numerator">numerator</Term>, and the bottom number, or <Term id="denominator">denominator</Term>, are both divided by 2. Both counts must divide evenly, with no parts left over.

Next, <Beat n={3}>look at the three shaded quarters</Beat>. Joining in twos would pair up all four parts, but one of the three shaded parts would be left over. No number greater than 1 divides both 3 and 4 evenly. So <Frac n={3} d={4} /> is in its <Term id="simplest-form">simplest form</Term>: you cannot join the parts evenly again while keeping the shaded amount.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>follow the joins from twelve parts</Beat>: <Frac n={4} d={12} /> = <Frac n={2} d={6} /> = <Frac n={1} d={3} />. The model ends with one of three equal parts shaded. A common mistake is to stop at <Frac n={2} d={6} /> because the numbers are smaller. It names the same amount, but both numbers still divide by 2, so it is not yet in simplest form. You can also divide both 4 and 12 by 4 to reach <Frac n={1} d={3} /> in one go.

<More title={{ en: "Why simplest form?", zh: "为什么要化成最简分数？" }}>

Simplest form names an amount using the fewest equal parts. This can help you picture and compare amounts. When a question says “Give your answer in its simplest form”, an equivalent fraction that can still be simplified is not the full answer. When it does not ask for simplest form, any equivalent fraction names the same amount.

</More>

<Lang zh>

你已经知道，把每份再分成更小的份，会得到<Term id="equivalent-fractions">等值分数</Term>：同一个量的不同名字。现在反过来，把小份合起来，变成更少、更大的份。

看纸条上<Beat n={1}>涂色的 6 份</Beat>。整条纸条是一个<Term id="whole">整体</Term>，<Term id="equal-parts">平均分</Term>成 8 份，所以涂色部分是 <Frac n={6} d={8} />。记住涂色部分到哪里为止。合并的时候，这个位置不能动，因为涂色的多少不变。

现在<Beat n={2}>每 2 份合成 1 份</Beat>。每两小份变成一大份：8 份变成 4 份，涂色的 6 份变成 3 份。得到 <Frac n={6} d={8} /> = <Frac n={3} d={4} />。上面的数（<Term id="numerator">分子</Term>）和下面的数（<Term id="denominator">分母</Term>）都除以 2。两个数都要正好分完，不能剩下零散的份。

接着看<Beat n={3}>涂色的 3 个四分之一</Beat>。如果再每 2 份合成 1 份，4 份能两两配对，可是涂色的 3 份会剩下 1 份。除了 1，没有数能同时整除 3 和 4。所以 <Frac n={3} d={4} /> 已经是<Term id="simplest-form">最简分数</Term>：在涂色多少不变的前提下，份不能再平均合并了。

最后，<Beat n={4}>从 12 份开始一步步合并</Beat>：<Frac n={4} d={12} /> = <Frac n={2} d={6} /> = <Frac n={1} d={3} />。模型最后是 3 份中涂了 1 份。常见的错误是因为数变小了，就停在 <Frac n={2} d={6} />。它和 <Frac n={4} d={12} /> 一样多，可是 2 和 6 还能同时除以 2，所以还不是最简分数。也可以把 4 和 12 同时除以 4，一步得到 <Frac n={1} d={3} />。

<More title={{ en: "Why simplest form?", zh: "为什么要化成最简分数？" }}>

最简分数用最少的平均分的份来说一个量，这样更容易想象和比较。题目写着"答案写成最简分数"时，还能再化简的等值分数不算完整的答案。题目没有这样要求时，任何等值分数说的都是同样多。

</More>
