<Lang en>

You have added and taken away parts of the same size. Now you will keep the shaded amount and change how you name it.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the shaded half of the strip</Beat>. The strip is one <Term id="whole">whole</Term>, divided into 2 <Term id="equal-parts">equal parts</Term>. One part is shaded, so its <Term id="fraction">fraction</Term> is <Frac n={1} d={2} />. Keep your eye on where the shading ends.

<Beat n={2}>Fold it in half again</Beat>. Each half becomes 2 quarters, including the shaded half. You now count 4 parts altogether and 2 shaded parts: <Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={2} d={4} />. The fold makes smaller parts without adding any shading.

When you <Beat n={3}>fold once more</Beat>, each quarter becomes 2 eighths. The 2 shaded quarters become 4 shaded eighths, out of 8 parts altogether. The shading still ends halfway along the strip, so <Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={2} d={4} /> = <Frac n={4} d={8} />.

On <Beat n={4}>the fraction wall</Beat>, look at the ends of these three amounts. They line up because each row shows the same whole, and each amount reaches halfway. Fractions that name the same amount are <Term id="equivalent-fractions">equivalent fractions</Term>.

Next, <Beat n={5}>look at the thirds cut into twelfths</Beat>. Each third becomes 4 smaller parts. The bottom number, or <Term id="denominator">denominator</Term>, becomes 3 × 4 = 12. The top number, or <Term id="numerator">numerator</Term>, becomes 2 × 4 = 8, because the shaded thirds are cut too. Thus <Frac n={2} d={3} /> = <Frac n={8} d={12} />. To find a missing number, use the same multiplier on both numbers. For example, 5 × 2 = 10, so 2 × 2 = 4 gives <Frac n={2} d={5} /> = <Frac n={4} d={10} />.

Changing only the bottom number is a mistake: the shaded parts must be counted again too. Adding the same number to both is also a mistake, because cutting multiplies the counts.

<More title={{ en: "Why multiply, not add?", zh: "为什么是乘，不是加？" }}>

To write <Frac n={1} d={3} /> in ninths, someone adds 6 to the bottom (3 + 6 = 9) and 6 to the top, and gets <Frac n={7} d={9} />. But <Frac n={7} d={9} /> is nearly a whole strip, and <Frac n={1} d={3} /> is less than half. Cutting each part into 3 makes 3 times as many parts, so the top and the bottom are both multiplied by 3: <Frac n={3} d={9} />.

</More>

<Lang zh>

你已经会把一样大的份加起来、拿走。现在涂色的多少保持不变，换一个说法来叫它。

看<Beat n={1}>涂了一半的纸条</Beat>。纸条是一个<Term id="whole">整体</Term>，<Term id="equal-parts">平均分</Term>成 2 份。涂了 1 份，所以它的<Term id="fraction">分数</Term>是 <Frac n={1} d={2} />。记住涂色部分到哪里为止。

<Beat n={2}>再对折一次</Beat>。每个二分之一变成 2 个四分之一，涂色的那一半也一样。现在一共 4 份，涂了 2 份：<Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={2} d={4} />。对折只是把份分得更小，并没有多涂。

<Beat n={3}>再折一次</Beat>，每个四分之一变成 2 个八分之一。涂色的 2 个四分之一变成 4 个八分之一，全部是 8 份。涂色部分还是停在纸条的正中间，所以 <Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={2} d={4} /> = <Frac n={4} d={8} />。

在<Beat n={4}>分数墙</Beat>上，看这三个量的右端。每一行都是同一个整体，三个量都到一半，所以正好对齐。表示同样多的分数叫<Term id="equivalent-fractions">等值分数</Term>。

接着看<Beat n={5}>三分之几分成十二分之几</Beat>。每个三分之一分成 4 小份。下面的数，也就是<Term id="denominator">分母</Term>，变成 3 × 4 = 12；上面的数，也就是<Term id="numerator">分子</Term>，变成 2 × 4 = 8，因为涂色的三分之一也被分了。所以 <Frac n={2} d={3} /> = <Frac n={8} d={12} />。要找缺的数，就让分子和分母乘同一个数。比如分母 5 × 2 = 10，分子也 2 × 2 = 4，得到 <Frac n={2} d={5} /> = <Frac n={4} d={10} />。

只改分母是错的：涂色的份也要重新数。给分子分母加同一个数也是错的，因为把每份再分，份数是成倍增加的。

<More title={{ en: "Why multiply, not add?", zh: "为什么是乘，不是加？" }}>

要把 <Frac n={1} d={3} /> 写成九分之几，有人把分母加 6（3 + 6 = 9），分子也加 6，得到 <Frac n={7} d={9} />。可是 <Frac n={7} d={9} /> 差不多是整条纸条，而 <Frac n={1} d={3} /> 还不到一半。每份分成 3 小份，份数就变成原来的 3 倍，所以分子分母都乘 3：<Frac n={3} d={9} />。

</More>
