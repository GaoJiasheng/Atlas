<Lang en>

Shade <Frac n={1} d={3} /> of a paper strip, then fold it in half. Every third is now cut into 2 sixths, the shaded third too. The strip has 6 parts and 2 of them are shaded, but the shaded amount has not changed: <Frac n={1} d={3} /> = <Frac n={2} d={6} />. Fractions that name the same amount are <Term id="equivalent-fractions">equivalent fractions</Term>.

When every part is cut into the same number of pieces, the number of parts and the number of shaded parts are both multiplied by that number. Cut each third into 3, and <Frac n={1} d={3} /> = <Frac n={3} d={9} />: 1 × 3 on top, 3 × 3 at the bottom.

This also finds a missing number. In <Frac n={2} d={5} /> = <Frac n={4} d={10} />, the bottom went from 5 to 10, which is × 2, so the top goes from 2 to 4.

Two mistakes to watch for. Changing only the bottom number, as in <Frac n={1} d={3} /> = <Frac n={1} d={9} />, forgets that the shaded parts were cut too. Adding instead of multiplying gives a different amount, as the box below shows.

Now try: <Task id="fold-half-into-eighths">fold a shaded strip</Task>, then cut parts and fill in the missing numbers.

<More title={{ en: "Why multiply, not add?", zh: "为什么是乘，不是加？" }}>

To write <Frac n={1} d={3} /> in ninths, someone adds 6 to the bottom (3 + 6 = 9) and 6 to the top, and gets <Frac n={7} d={9} />. But <Frac n={7} d={9} /> is nearly a whole strip, and <Frac n={1} d={3} /> is less than half. Cutting each part into 3 makes 3 times as many parts, so the top and the bottom are both multiplied by 3: <Frac n={3} d={9} />.

<Lang zh>

在纸条上涂 <Frac n={1} d={3} />，再对折一次。每个三分之一都被分成 2 个六分之一，涂色的那份也一样。纸条现在有 6 份，涂了 2 份，可是涂色的多少没有变：<Frac n={1} d={3} /> = <Frac n={2} d={6} />。表示同样多的分数叫<Term id="equivalent-fractions">等值分数</Term>。

把每一份都分成同样多的小份，总份数和涂色的份数就都乘这个数。把每个三分之一分成 3 份，<Frac n={1} d={3} /> = <Frac n={3} d={9} />：分子 1 × 3，分母 3 × 3。

这样也能求出缺的数。在 <Frac n={2} d={5} /> = <Frac n={4} d={10} /> 里，分母从 5 变成 10，是乘 2，所以分子从 2 变成 4。

要当心两个错误。只改分母，比如写成 <Frac n={1} d={3} /> = <Frac n={1} d={9} />，忘了涂色的份也被分了。用加法代替乘法，得到的就不是同样多了，下面的小框里有例子。

要把 <Frac n={1} d={3} /> 写成九分之几，有人把分母加 6（3 + 6 = 9），分子也加 6，得到 <Frac n={7} d={9} />。可是 <Frac n={7} d={9} /> 差不多是整条纸条，而 <Frac n={1} d={3} /> 还不到一半。每份分成 3 小份，份数就变成原来的 3 倍，所以分子分母都乘 3：<Frac n={3} d={9} />。
