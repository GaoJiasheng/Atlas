<Lang en>

We can only compare fractions of the same whole. Cut the same whole into more parts and each part gets smaller, so <Frac n={1} d={3} /> is greater than <Frac n={1} d={5} />. When the parts are the same size, as in <Term id="like-fractions">like fractions</Term>, more parts means more.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

只能在同一个整体下比较分数。同一个整体分得越多，每份越小，所以 <Frac n={1} d={3} /> 比 <Frac n={1} d={5} /> 大。每份一样大时（<Term id="like-fractions">同分母分数</Term>），份数多的就大。

</Lang>
