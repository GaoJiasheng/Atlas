<Lang en>

This lesson teaches fractions in 12 small steps, in the order of the MOE primary mathematics syllabus. The first steps, 01 to 06, are equal parts, naming fractions, the number line, comparing, and adding and taking away fractions with the same denominator. The later steps, 07 to 11, are equivalent fractions, simplest form, comparing unlike fractions, and adding and subtracting related fractions. Step 12 goes a little further, to mixed numbers. The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

Each step explains first. The chapter axis at the bottom of the screen has a small circle for every beat: click one, or press **,** and **.**, and the model on the stage cuts, shades or folds while the reading panel says what to look at. The text below the beat line walks through the same beats.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question on its idea, picked from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: tap, fold, cut, shade or type, on the stage or in the panel.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** draws the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, up to three, each one closer to the answer.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Every question can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved: when you leave the page, your answers are gone.

<Lang zh>

这节课分 12 个小步讲分数，顺序跟教育部小学数学课纲一致。前面的第 01 到 06 步讲平均分、认识分数、数轴、比较大小，以及同分母分数的加减。后面的第 07 到 11 步讲等值分数、最简分数、异分母分数的比较，以及分母成倍数的分数的加减。第 12 步再往前一步，讲带分数。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

每一步先讲。屏幕底部的章节轴上，每一拍有一个小圆点：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上的模型就会切开、涂色或对折，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。节拍下面的正文也按同样的顺序讲一遍。

每章最后的小方块是一道关于这一章的小题，从十道左右的题里随机挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：在舞台上或面板里点、折、切、涂，或者输入。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按「看答案」，舞台会画出答案。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，最多三条，一条比一条接近答案。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

每道题都能只用点按完成，也能只用键盘完成。不计时，也不保存：离开这一页，答案就清空了。
