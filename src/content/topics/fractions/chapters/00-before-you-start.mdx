<Lang en>

Each step has a few short tasks. Watch an example if you like, then do the task on the stage: tap, fold, cut, shade or type. Press **Check** when you are ready. If it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look; after two tries **Show me** draws the answer. **Hint** gives a clue at any time. The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

每一步有几道小题。可以先看一个例子，再在舞台上动手：点、折、切、涂，或者输入。准备好了就按「检查」。还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后可以按「看答案」。随时可以按「提示」。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

</Lang>
