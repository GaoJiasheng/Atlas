<Lang en>

This lesson teaches fractions in 12 small steps, in the order of the MOE primary mathematics syllabus. Steps 01 to 06 are the Primary 2 work: equal parts, naming fractions, the number line, comparing, and adding and taking away fractions with the same denominator. Steps 07 to 11 are the Primary 3 work: equivalent fractions, simplest form, comparing unlike fractions, and adding and subtracting related fractions. Step 12 is a bridge to Primary 4 mixed numbers. The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

Each step has two or three tasks. For each task you can:

- **Watch an example** (W): the stage shows a similar task done with different numbers.
- **Try it**: tap, fold, cut, shade or type, on the stage or in the answer tray below it.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** draws the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, up to three, each one closer to the answer.

Every task can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved: when you leave the page, your answers are gone.

<Lang zh>

这节课分 12 个小步讲分数，顺序跟教育部小学数学课纲一致。第 01 到 06 步是小二的内容：平均分、认识分数、数轴、比较大小，以及同分母分数的加减。第 07 到 11 步是小三的内容：等值分数、最简分数、异分母分数的比较，以及分母成倍数的分数的加减。第 12 步是通往小四带分数的桥。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

每一步有两三道小题。每道小题你可以：

- **看例子**（W）：舞台用另一组数字演示一道类似的题。
- **动手做**：在舞台上或下面的作答托盘里点、折、切、涂，或者输入。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按「看答案」，舞台会画出答案。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，最多三条，一条比一条接近答案。

每道题都能只用点按完成，也能只用键盘完成。不计时，也不保存：离开这一页，答案就清空了。
