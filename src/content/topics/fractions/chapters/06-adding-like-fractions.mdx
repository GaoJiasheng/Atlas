<Lang en>

Like fractions have parts of the same size, so we can add them by counting parts. Take a strip cut into 5 equal parts with 1 part shaded: that is <Frac n={1} d={5} />. Shade 2 more parts. Now 3 parts are shaded, each one a fifth:

<Frac n={1} d={5} /> + <Frac n={2} d={5} /> = <Frac n={3} d={5} />

Fifths plus fifths are still fifths. Only the number of parts changes. The size of each part does not change, so the bottom number stays 5.

A common mistake is to add the bottom numbers too and write <Frac n={3} d={10} />. Look at the strip: nobody cut it into 10 parts. It still has 5 parts, and 3 of them are shaded.

Taking away works the same way. From <Frac n={4} d={5} />, cross out 1 fifth and 3 fifths are left: <Frac n={4} d={5} /> − <Frac n={1} d={5} /> = <Frac n={3} d={5} />. The parts left are still fifths.

In Primary 2 the answer always stays within one whole.

Now try: <Task id="add-sevenths">add parts of a strip</Task>, then take some away.

<More title={{ en: "Saying it in words", zh: "用话来说" }}>

Saying the sum in words makes the mistake easy to see: "1 fifth and 2 fifths make 3 fifths", just as 1 apple and 2 apples make 3 apples. "Fifths" is the name of the parts, like "apples". The name stays the same; only the number in front of it changes.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

同分母分数的每份一样大，所以相加时只要数份数。一条纸条平均分成 5 份，涂了 1 份，就是 <Frac n={1} d={5} />。再涂 2 份，现在一共涂了 3 份，每份是五分之一：

<Frac n={1} d={5} /> + <Frac n={2} d={5} /> = <Frac n={3} d={5} />

五分之几加五分之几，还是五分之几。变的只是份数，每份的大小没有变，所以分母还是 5。

常见的错误是把分母也相加，写成 <Frac n={3} d={10} />。看看纸条：没有人把它分成 10 份。它还是 5 份，其中 3 份涂了色。

减法也一样。从 <Frac n={4} d={5} /> 里划掉 1 个五分之一，还剩 3 个五分之一：<Frac n={4} d={5} /> − <Frac n={1} d={5} /> = <Frac n={3} d={5} />。剩下的份还是五分之一。

小二的加减，结果都在一个整体之内。

<More title={{ en: "Saying it in words", zh: "用话来说" }}>

把算式说出来，错误就容易看出来：「1 个五分之一加 2 个五分之一，是 3 个五分之一」，就像 1 个苹果加 2 个苹果是 3 个苹果。「五分之一」是每份的名字，就像「苹果」；名字不变，变的只是前面的个数。

</More>
