<Lang en>

The practice has eight questions from steps 01 to 11. Mixed numbers from step 12 are not included. The questions come in a fixed order and use the same kinds of tasks as the lesson: shading, choosing, comparing, placing on a number line and writing fractions.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why. If it is not, the stage draws the correct model with a dashed line, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time before pressing it.

The helpers are switched off here: no symbol, equivalent or number line views, and no part labels. Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. "Try again" starts the same eight questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

Question 1 is already on the stage.

<Lang zh>

练习有 8 道题，来自第 01 到 11 步；第 12 步的带分数不在里面。题目顺序固定，题型和课里一样：涂色、选择、比较大小、在数轴上放标记、写分数。

每题只检查一次。按「检查」以后，马上就能看到对不对、为什么。如果不对，舞台会用虚线画出正确的模型，还有一个按钮可以回到教这一点的那一步。检查以后答案就不能再改，所以按之前慢慢想。

这里的辅助功能都关掉了：没有符号、等值、数轴这几种辅助显示，也没有每份的标注。每题有一条提示，看了提示不扣分。

做完最后一题，会出现一张小表：每题一行，标出 ✓ 或 ○，以及可以回看的那一步。按「再做一次」，同样的 8 道题从头开始。不计时，也不保存。

第 1 题已经在舞台上了。
