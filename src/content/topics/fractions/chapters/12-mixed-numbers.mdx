<Lang en>

You have practised taking away parts of one <Term id="whole">whole</Term> by making the parts the same size. Now look beyond one whole in this optional step that goes a little further.

Look at <Beat n={1}>seven shaded quarters</Beat> across two strips of the same size. Each strip has four <Term id="equal-parts">equal parts</Term>. Four quarters fill the first strip, and three more lie on the second. You have more than one strip: <Frac n={7} d={4} />. This <Term id="fraction">fraction</Term> is called an improper fraction. Its top number is greater than or equal to its bottom number.

Now <Beat n={2}>group four quarters into one whole</Beat>. The other three quarters stay on the second strip. You can name the amount with a <Term id="mixed-number">mixed number</Term>: one whole and <Frac n={3} d={4} />, written <Frac w={1} n={3} d={4} />. Read it as "one and three quarters". Both names describe exactly the same shaded amount: <Frac n={7} d={4} /> = <Frac w={1} n={3} d={4} />.

On the <Term id="number-line">number line</Term>, <Beat n={3}>find two wholes and one more third</Beat>. Each gap between whole numbers has three equal jumps. Start at 2 and move one third to the right to reach <Frac w={2} n={1} d={3} />. Counting from 0 gives six thirds to reach 2, then one more: <Frac n={7} d={3} />. Both names belong at the same point.

Next, <Beat n={4}>look at eleven shaded quarters</Beat> across three strips. Make as many full strips as you can. Four quarters fill each strip, so eight quarters make two wholes. Three quarters remain on the third strip. That is why <Frac n={11} d={4} /> = <Frac w={2} n={3} d={4} />: count the full strips first, then the quarters left over.

A common mistake is to treat two strips as one whole and call seven shaded quarters <Frac n={7} d={8} />. Each strip is still one whole with four parts, so the bottom number stays 4.

<More title={{ en: "Halves of prata", zh: "半张 prata" }}>

Order 5 halves of prata and you get 2 whole prata and half of another: <Frac n={5} d={2} /> = <Frac w={2} n={1} d={2} />. Each prata is still cut into 2, so the bottom number stays 2 however many prata there are.

</More>

<Lang zh>

你已经会先把份化成一样大，再从一个<Term id="whole">整体</Term>里拿走一些。这一步是可选的，再往前一步：看看超过一个整体会怎样。

看两条一样大的纸条上<Beat n={1}>涂色的 7 个四分之一</Beat>。每条纸条<Term id="equal-parts">平均分</Term>成 4 份。4 个四分之一涂满第一条，还有 3 个在第二条上。涂的比一条纸条还多：<Frac n={7} d={4} />。分子大于或等于分母的<Term id="fraction">分数</Term>叫假分数。

现在<Beat n={2}>把 4 个四分之一合成 1 个整体</Beat>，另外 3 个四分之一留在第二条上。这个量可以用<Term id="mixed-number">带分数</Term>来说：1 个整体和 <Frac n={3} d={4} />，写作 <Frac w={1} n={3} d={4} />，读作"一又四分之三"。两个名字说的是同样多的涂色部分：<Frac n={7} d={4} /> = <Frac w={1} n={3} d={4} />。

在<Term id="number-line">数轴</Term>上，<Beat n={3}>找到 2 个整体再加 1 个三分之一</Beat>。相邻两个整数之间平均分成 3 段。从 2 往右走一个三分之一，就到 <Frac w={2} n={1} d={3} />。从 0 开始数，6 个三分之一到 2，再走 1 个：<Frac n={7} d={3} />。两个名字指的是同一个点。

接着看三条纸条上<Beat n={4}>涂色的 11 个四分之一</Beat>。先尽量拼成满满的整条。每条要 4 个四分之一，8 个四分之一拼成 2 个整体，第三条上还剩 3 个四分之一。所以 <Frac n={11} d={4} /> = <Frac w={2} n={3} d={4} />：先数满的几条，再数剩下的四分之一。

常见的错误是把两条纸条当成一个整体，把涂色的 7 个四分之一说成 <Frac n={7} d={8} />。每条纸条还是一个整体，分成 4 份，所以分母还是 4。

<More title={{ en: "Halves of prata", zh: "半张 prata" }}>

点 5 个半张 prata，就是 2 张完整的 prata 再加半张：<Frac n={5} d={2} /> = <Frac w={2} n={1} d={2} />。每张 prata 还是分成 2 份，不管有几张，分母都是 2。

</More>
