<Lang en>

Seven quarters fill one whole strip and 3 quarters of the next. Written as a <Term id="mixed-number">mixed number</Term> that is <Frac w={1} n={3} d={4} />; as an improper fraction it is <Frac n={7} d={4} />. Each whole is still cut into 4, so the bottom number stays 4.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

7 个四分之一涂满一整条，再加下一条的 3 个四分之一。写成<Term id="mixed-number">带分数</Term>是 <Frac w={1} n={3} d={4} />，写成假分数是 <Frac n={7} d={4} />。每个整体还是平均分成 4 份，所以分母还是 4。
