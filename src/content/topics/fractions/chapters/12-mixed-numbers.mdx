<Lang en>

This step is Primary 4 work. Try it if you like.

So far every fraction fitted inside one whole. Now take two strips, each one whole cut into thirds, and shade 5 thirds. 3 thirds fill the first strip and 2 more go on the second. That amount has two names.

- As a <Term id="mixed-number">mixed number</Term>: 1 whole and <Frac n={2} d={3} />, written <Frac w={1} n={2} d={3} /> and read "one and two thirds".
- As an improper fraction: 5 thirds, written <Frac n={5} d={3} />. Its top number is bigger than its bottom number (or equal to it).

The bottom number stays 3, because each strip is still one whole cut into 3. A common mistake is to treat both strips as one whole and write <Frac n={5} d={6} />. But <Frac n={5} d={6} /> is less than one strip, and we shaded more than one.

To turn an improper fraction into a mixed number, make as many wholes as you can: 3 thirds make 1 whole, so 5 thirds are 1 whole and 2 thirds left over. On a number line from 0 to 3, <Frac w={1} n={2} d={3} /> is two jumps of a third past 1.

Now try: <Task id="seven-quarters">shade quarters on two strips</Task>, then place and write mixed numbers.

<More title={{ en: "Halves of prata", zh: "半张 prata" }}>

Order 5 halves of prata and you get 2 whole prata and half of another: <Frac n={5} d={2} /> = <Frac w={2} n={1} d={2} />. Each prata is still cut into 2, so the bottom number stays 2 however many prata there are.

<Lang zh>

这一步是小四的内容，想试就试试。

到现在为止，每个分数都在一个整体之内。现在拿两条纸条，每条是一个整体，平均分成 3 份，涂出 5 个三分之一。3 个三分之一涂满第一条，另外 2 个涂在第二条上。这个量有两个名字：

- 写成<Term id="mixed-number">带分数</Term>：1 个整体和 <Frac n={2} d={3} />，写作 <Frac w={1} n={2} d={3} />，读作「一又三分之二」。
- 写成假分数：5 个三分之一，写作 <Frac n={5} d={3} />。假分数的分子比分母大（或者相等）。

分母还是 3，因为每条纸条还是一个整体平均分成 3 份。常见的错误是把两条当成一个整体，写成 <Frac n={5} d={6} />。可是 <Frac n={5} d={6} /> 还不到一条，而我们涂的超过了一条。

把假分数化成带分数，就是尽量凑成整体：3 个三分之一是 1 个整体，所以 5 个三分之一是 1 个整体，还剩 2 个三分之一。在 0 到 3 的数轴上，<Frac w={1} n={2} d={3} /> 在 1 后面再跳两段三分之一的地方。

现在试试：先<Task id="seven-quarters">在两条纸条上涂四分之几</Task>，再在数轴上放一放、写一写带分数。

<More title={{ en: "Halves of prata", zh: "半张 prata" }}>

点 5 个半张 prata，就是 2 整张再加半张：<Frac n={5} d={2} /> = <Frac w={2} n={1} d={2} />。每张 prata 还是分成 2 份，所以不管有几张，分母都还是 2。
