<Lang en>

A unit fraction names one part. Most fractions take several parts. Cut a strip into 5 equal parts, and each part is <Frac n={1} d={5} />. Shade 2 of them and you have two fifths:

<Frac n={1} d={5} /> + <Frac n={1} d={5} /> = <Frac n={2} d={5} />

The bottom number still counts the equal parts in the whole, 5. The top number now counts the parts you take, 2. So a fraction such as <Frac n={2} d={5} /> means 2 parts, each of them one fifth.

The same two mistakes from step 02 come back here. Comparing shaded parts with unshaded parts gives <Frac n={2} d={3} />, because 3 parts are not shaded; but the whole has 5 parts. Writing <Frac n={5} d={2} /> swaps the numbers.

When every part is taken, you have the whole thing. <Frac n={5} d={5} /> is all 5 fifths, the whole strip, so <Frac n={5} d={5} /> = 1. Whenever the top and the bottom numbers are the same, the fraction is 1 whole.

A rainbow kueh lapis is often made of 9 thin layers of the same thickness, so each layer is <Frac n={1} d={9} /> of its height. If 3 of the layers are red, the red part is <Frac n={3} d={9} /> of the height: 3 layers, each one ninth.

<Lang zh>

单位分数表示一份。大多数分数表示好几份。把一条纸条平均分成 5 份，每份是 <Frac n={1} d={5} />。涂其中 2 份，就是五分之二：

<Frac n={1} d={5} /> + <Frac n={1} d={5} /> = <Frac n={2} d={5} />

分母还是数整体平均分成几份，是 5；分子数取了几份，是 2。所以 <Frac n={2} d={5} /> 表示 2 份，每份是五分之一。

第 02 步的两个错误，在这里还会出现。拿涂了的份和没涂的份比，会写成 <Frac n={2} d={3} />，因为没涂的有 3 份；可是整体一共有 5 份。写成 <Frac n={5} d={2} /> 则是把两个数写反了。

所有的份都取了，就是整个整体。<Frac n={5} d={5} /> 是全部 5 个五分之一，也就是整条纸条，所以 <Frac n={5} d={5} /> = 1。分子和分母相同的分数，都等于 1 个整体。
