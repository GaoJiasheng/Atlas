<Lang en>

Subtracting related fractions works like adding them: make the parts the same size, then take away.

To work out <Frac n={3} d={4} /> − <Frac n={1} d={8} />, cut the quarters into eighths. <Frac n={3} d={4} /> = <Frac n={6} d={8} />. Cross out 1 eighth, and 5 eighths are left:

<Frac n={3} d={4} /> − <Frac n={1} d={8} /> = <Frac n={6} d={8} /> − <Frac n={1} d={8} /> = <Frac n={5} d={8} />

The mistakes are the same as in adding. Taking away the tops and the bottoms separately, or treating one big part as if it were one small part, gives a wrong answer. Rename first, then count.

To find how much of a whole is left, write the whole in the same parts. A whole is <Frac n={8} d={8} />, so if <Frac n={3} d={8} /> of a carton of milk is drunk, <Frac n={8} d={8} /> − <Frac n={3} d={8} /> = <Frac n={5} d={8} /> is left.

To find how much more one amount is than another, use a comparison model: two bars of the same length, one under the other. Hui Min drank <Frac n={1} d={2} /> of a carton and Arjun drank <Frac n={3} d={10} /> of a carton the same size. <Frac n={1} d={2} /> = <Frac n={5} d={10} />, and <Frac n={5} d={10} /> − <Frac n={3} d={10} /> = <Frac n={2} d={10} />, so Hui Min drank <Frac n={2} d={10} /> of a carton more.

Now try: <Task id="seven-eighths-take-half">take a half away</Task>, then the cake and the bottles.

<More title={{ en: "Two kinds of model", zh: "两种模型图" }}>

A part–whole model has one bar: the parts sit side by side inside the whole, and the question mark is on a part or on the total. A comparison model has two bars of the same whole, lined up on the left; the question mark is on the extra piece of the longer one. The difference is the piece that sticks out.

</More>

<Lang zh>

分母成倍数的分数相减，和相加一样：先化成同样大的份，再减。

计算 <Frac n={3} d={4} /> − <Frac n={1} d={8} />，先把四分之几分成八分之几：<Frac n={3} d={4} /> = <Frac n={6} d={8} />。划掉 1 个八分之一，还剩 5 个八分之一：

<Frac n={3} d={4} /> − <Frac n={1} d={8} /> = <Frac n={6} d={8} /> − <Frac n={1} d={8} /> = <Frac n={5} d={8} />

错误也和加法一样。把分子、分母分别相减，或者把一大份当成一小份来划，结果都不对。先换成同样大的份，再数。

求一个整体还剩多少，先把整体写成同样的份。一个整体是 <Frac n={8} d={8} />，一盒牛奶喝掉了 <Frac n={3} d={8} />，还剩 <Frac n={8} d={8} /> − <Frac n={3} d={8} /> = <Frac n={5} d={8} />。
