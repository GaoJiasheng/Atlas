<Lang en>

Open a pair of scissors a little, then a lot. The blades make a corner, and the corner gets wider as you open them. That corner is an angle, and this chapter is about what makes it big or small.

Start with <Beat n={1}>two lines that meet at a point</Beat>. Two straight lines that meet make an <Term id="angle">angle</Term>. The point where they meet is the <Term id="vertex">vertex</Term>. The two lines are the <Term id="arm">arms</Term> of the angle.

Now <Beat n={2}>turn one arm away from the other</Beat>. Keep one arm still and turn the other one round the vertex. The arc between the arms grows as the arm turns. The more it turns, the bigger the angle. So an angle tells you how much turn there is between two arms.

Next, <Beat n={3}>look at a pair of angles with different arms</Beat>. The first angle has very long arms, but they have turned only a little. The second has short arms that have turned a lot. The second angle is bigger. The length of the arms does not change the size of an angle. You could make the arms longer or shorter, and the turn would stay the same.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>put one angle on top of the other</Beat> to compare them. Place vertex on vertex, and lay one arm along one arm. Now look at the other arms. The angle whose other arm has turned further round is the bigger one. Here the short arm is further round, so the angle with the short arms is bigger.

A common mix-up is to call an angle big because its arms are long, or because the ends of its arms are far apart. Look at the turn instead: how wide the arms are open at the vertex.

<Lang zh>

把一把剪刀张开一点，再张大一些。两片刀刃形成一个尖角，张得越开，这个角就越大。这个尖角就是一个角。这一章讲：角的大小由什么决定。

先看<Beat n={1}>相交于一点的两条线</Beat>。两条直线相交，就形成一个<Term id="angle">角</Term>。它们相交的那个点叫<Term id="vertex">顶点</Term>，两条线叫角的<Term id="arm">边</Term>。

再<Beat n={2}>让一条边转开</Beat>。一条边不动，另一条边绕着顶点转。边转动的时候，两条边之间的弧也跟着变长。转得越多，角越大。所以，角说的是两条边之间转了多少。

接着<Beat n={3}>看一对边长不一样的角</Beat>。第一个角的边很长，可是只转了一点点；第二个角的边很短，却转了很多。第二个角大。边的长短不改变角的大小：把边画长一些、短一些，转了多少还是一样。

最后，<Beat n={4}>把一个角叠到另一个上</Beat>来比一比。顶点对顶点，一条边贴着一条边，再看另外那两条边。哪个角的另一条边转得更远，哪个角就大。这里短边的那一条转得更远，所以边短的那个角大。

常见的误会是看到边长，或者边的两头隔得远，就说这个角大。要看的是转了多少：两条边在顶点那里张得有多开。

准备好了，就<Task id="what-is-an-angle">做一道小题</Task>：比一比哪个角大，或者数一数有几个角。

</Lang>
