<Lang en>

Now you have three facts: angles on a straight line add up to 180°, angles at a point add up to 360°, and vertically opposite angles are equal. This chapter puts them together.

Start with <Beat n={1}>an unknown angle worked out, not measured</Beat>. The unknown angle and 132° sit on a straight line, so the facts say 180° − 132° = 48°. In a test, a drawing is often marked <Term id="not-to-scale">not drawn to scale</Term>: its angles are not drawn at their real sizes, so a protractor would give the wrong answer. Work the angle out; do not measure it.

Some angles take two steps. <Beat n={2}>Look at two crossing lines with a right angle</Beat> beside them. First find ∠y, the angle opposite 35°: <Term id="vertically-opposite">vertically opposite angles</Term> are equal, so ∠y = 35°. Then ∠y, ∠x and the right angle sit on a straight line, so ∠x = 180° − 35° − 90° = 55°.

Next, <Beat n={3}>look at angles at a point with two equal unknowns</Beat>. Both are marked ∠p, so they are the same size. Together they are 360° − 90° − 120° = 150°, so each one is 150° ÷ 2 = 75°.

Last, <Beat n={4}>write each step with its reason</Beat>. Beside each line, say which fact you used: vertically opposite angles, angles on a straight line, or angles at a point. The reason shows how you know.

A common mistake is to measure the drawing, which gives the size it is drawn at, not the size it is. Other mistakes come from the earlier chapters: 360° on a straight line, a forgotten right angle, or the angle next to a known one used as if it were opposite.

When you are ready, <Task id="finding-unknown-angles">try one question</Task> on finding an unknown angle.

<Lang zh>

现在你有三条规律：直线上的角加起来是 180°，一点周围的角加起来是 360°，对顶角相等。这一章把它们放在一起用。

先看<Beat n={1}>一个要算、不要量的未知角</Beat>。未知角和 132° 在一条直线上，按规律算是 180° − 132° = 48°。考试里的图常常写着<Term id="not-to-scale">未按比例画</Term>：图上的角不是按真实大小画的，用量角器量出来的数不对。所以要算，不要量。

有的角要两步才能求出来。<Beat n={2}>看两条相交的直线，旁边还有一个直角</Beat>。第一步，先求 35° 对面的 ∠y：<Term id="vertically-opposite">对顶角</Term>相等，所以 ∠y = 35°。第二步，∠y、∠x 和直角在一条直线上，所以 ∠x = 180° − 35° − 90° = 55°。

接着<Beat n={3}>看一点周围两个相等的未知角</Beat>。两个角都标着 ∠p，说明一样大。它们合起来是 360° − 90° − 120° = 150°，每个就是 150° ÷ 2 = 75°。

最后，<Beat n={4}>每一步都写上理由</Beat>。在每一行旁边写出用的是哪条规律：对顶角、直线上的角，还是一点周围的角。写上理由，别人就知道你是怎么算出来的。

常见的错误是去量图，量到的只是画出来的大小，不是真实的大小。其他错误前面几章都见过：直线上用了 360°、忘了直角、把挨着的角当成对顶角。
