<Lang en>

Saying "smaller than a right angle" is useful, but it does not tell you how much smaller. To say exactly how big an angle is, you measure it.

<Figure src="figures/angles-protractor-arms" alt={{ en: "A protractor centred on a hinge, with one wooden arm along its baseline and the other rising to the right.", zh: "量角器中心对着铰点，一条木臂沿底边向右，另一条斜向右上。" }} caption={{ en: "A protractor’s centre sits exactly on the point where the two arms meet.", zh: "量角器的中心正好对着两条边相交的点。" }} />

Start with <Beat n={1}>a full turn marked in small steps</Beat>. Angles are measured in <Term id="degree">degrees</Term>. A full turn is 360 degrees, written 360°. A <Term id="right-angle">right angle</Term> is a quarter turn, so it is 360° ÷ 4 = 90°.

To measure, you use a <Term id="protractor">protractor</Term>. It works like a ruler bent into a half circle. <Beat n={2}>Lay the protractor on the angle</Beat>: put its centre on the vertex, then turn it until its 0 line lies along one arm.

Now <Beat n={3}>read the scale</Beat>. A protractor has two rows of numbers, an <Term id="inner-outer-scale">inner scale and an outer scale</Term>, counting in opposite directions. Use the row that starts at 0 on the arm you lined up. Count up along that row to the other arm: this angle is 50°.

Next, <Beat n={4}>look at the other row</Beat>. Where the other arm crosses it, the other row says 130. Check with a right angle: this angle is smaller than a right angle, so it must be less than 90°. It is 50°, not 130°.

Last, <Beat n={5}>measure an angle that opens to the left</Beat>. When the arm you line up points left, its 0 is on the other row. Count from that 0 instead: this angle is 65°.

A common mistake is to read the row that does not start at 0 on your arm. Always find that 0 first, and check your answer against a right angle.

<More title={{ en: "Degrees on a clock", zh: "钟面上的度数" }}>

The minute hand turns a full turn, 360°, in 60 minutes. So it turns 360° ÷ 60 = 6° every minute, and 90° in 15 minutes.

</More>

<Lang zh>

说“比直角小”很有用，可是说不出小多少。要说清楚一个角到底有多大，就要量。

<Figure src="figures/angles-protractor-arms" alt={{ en: "A protractor centred on a hinge, with one wooden arm along its baseline and the other rising to the right.", zh: "量角器中心对着铰点，一条木臂沿底边向右，另一条斜向右上。" }} caption={{ en: "A protractor’s centre sits exactly on the point where the two arms meet.", zh: "量角器的中心正好对着两条边相交的点。" }} />

先看<Beat n={1}>分成很多小格的一整圈</Beat>。角的大小用“<Term id="degree">度</Term>”来量。一整圈是 360 度，写作 360°。<Term id="right-angle">直角</Term>是四分之一圈，所以是 360° ÷ 4 = 90°。

量角要用<Term id="protractor">量角器</Term>。量角器就像一把弯成半圆的尺子。<Beat n={2}>把量角器摆到角上</Beat>：中心对准顶点，再转一转，让 0 线贴着一条边。

接着<Beat n={3}>读刻度</Beat>。量角器上有两圈数，叫<Term id="inner-outer-scale">内圈刻度和外圈刻度</Term>，两圈数的方向相反。用 0 对着你对齐那条边的那一圈，沿着这一圈往上数，一直数到另一条边：这个角是 50°。

再<Beat n={4}>看看另一圈</Beat>。另一条边经过的地方，另一圈写着 130。和直角比一比：这个角比直角小，一定小于 90°。所以它是 50°，不是 130°。

最后<Beat n={5}>量一个开口朝左的角</Beat>。对齐的那条边朝左时，它的 0 在另一圈上。这次就从那一圈的 0 数起：这个角是 65°。

常见的错误是读了另一圈，那一圈的 0 不在你对齐的边上。每次都先找到那个 0，量完再和直角比一比。

<More title={{ en: "Degrees on a clock", zh: "钟面上的度数" }}>

分针 60 分钟转一整圈，就是 360°。所以它每分钟转 360° ÷ 60 = 6°，15 分钟转 90°。

</More>
