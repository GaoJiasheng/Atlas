<Lang en>

The practice has eight questions from the whole lesson, in a fixed order. They use the same kinds of task as the chapters: choosing angles, drawing a line on dot paper, measuring and drawing angles with a protractor, and working out unknown angles.

Question 1 asks which of two angles is bigger; one has very long arms. Question 2 asks you to choose every right angle; one of them is tilted. Question 3 asks you to draw a line through P parallel to AB on dot paper. Question 4 asks you to lay the protractor on ∠PQR, which opens to the left, and write its size. Question 5 asks you to draw ∠XYZ = 125°. Question 6 asks for ∠a on a straight line. Question 7 asks for ∠b where two lines cross. Question 8 asks for ∠c round a point, next to a right angle, in a drawing that is not to scale.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why. If it is not right, the stage shows the answer, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time. Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. **Try again** starts the same eight questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

Question 1 is on the stage, with its answer controls above this text. The squares on the chapter axis are the eight questions.

<Lang zh>

练习有 8 道题，来自整节课，顺序固定。题型和各章的小题一样：选角，在点阵上画线，用量角器量角、画角，还有求未知角。

第 1 题问两个角哪个大，其中一个角的边很长。第 2 题要选出所有的直角，其中有一个是斜着放的。第 3 题要在点阵上过点 P 画一条和 AB 平行的线。第 4 题要把量角器摆到开口朝左的 ∠PQR 上，写出它的度数。第 5 题要画出 ∠XYZ = 125°。第 6 题求直线上的 ∠a。第 7 题求两条线相交处的 ∠b。第 8 题求一点周围的 ∠c，旁边有一个直角，图没有按比例画。

每题只检查一次。按“检查”以后，马上就能看到对不对、为什么。如果还没对，舞台会给出答案，还有一个按钮可以回到教这一点的那一步。检查以后答案就不能再改，所以按之前慢慢想。每题有一条提示，看了提示不扣分。

做完最后一题，会出现一张小表：每题一行，标出 ✓ 或 ○，以及可以回看的那一步。按“再做一次”，同样的 8 道题从头开始。不计时，也不保存。

第 1 题已经在舞台上，作答的地方在这段文字上方。章节轴上的 8 个小方块就是这 8 道题。
