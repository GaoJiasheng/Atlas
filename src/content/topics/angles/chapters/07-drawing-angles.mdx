<Lang en>

Measuring tells you the size of an angle that is already drawn. Now you do it the other way round: you know the size, and you draw the angle.

Say you want to draw ∠ABC = 65°. <Beat n={1}>Begin with one arm</Beat>. Draw a straight line BC with a ruler and mark the <Term id="vertex">vertex</Term>, B, at one end. The new arm will start from B.

Next, <Beat n={2}>lay the protractor on B</Beat>. Put its centre on B and its 0 line along BC. Find the row that starts at 0 on BC. Count along it to 65 and mark a small dot at the edge of the <Term id="protractor">protractor</Term>.

Then <Beat n={3}>join B to the dot</Beat>. Take the protractor away and draw a straight line from B through the dot. Label the new arm BA. The <Term id="angle">angle</Term> between BA and BC is 65°. You can lay the protractor on it again and measure, to see that it reads 65 on the row that starts at BC.

Last, <Beat n={4}>see what the other row would give</Beat>. If you counted on the other row, you would reach 65 on the other side and draw an angle of 115°. That angle is greater than a <Term id="right-angle">right angle</Term>. But 65° is less than 90°, so a 65° angle must be smaller than a right angle. Checking with a right angle catches the mistake.

A common mistake is to count on the wrong row and draw the angle that makes a straight line with the one you wanted. Before you join the dot, ask: should this angle be smaller or greater than a right angle?

<Lang zh>

量角，是知道一个已经画好的角有多大。现在反过来：已经知道角有多大，要把它画出来。

比如要画 ∠ABC = 65°。<Beat n={1}>先画一条边</Beat>：用尺子画一条直线 BC，在一头标出<Term id="vertex">顶点</Term> B。新的那条边就从 B 出发。

接着<Beat n={2}>把量角器摆到 B 上</Beat>。中心对准 B，0 线贴着 BC。找到 0 对着 BC 的那一圈，沿着它数到 65，在<Term id="protractor">量角器</Term>的边上点一个小点。

然后<Beat n={3}>把 B 和这个点连起来</Beat>。拿开量角器，从 B 经过这个点画一条直线，新的边标上 A。BA 和 BC 之间的<Term id="angle">角</Term>就是 65°。画好以后，可以再把量角器摆上去量一量：从 BC 那一圈的 0 数起，正好是 65。

最后<Beat n={4}>看看用另一圈会怎样</Beat>。要是在另一圈上数，65 会在另一边，画出来的角是 115°，比<Term id="right-angle">直角</Term>大。可是 65° 小于 90°，65° 的角一定比直角小。和直角比一比，就能发现画错了。

常见的错误是数错了圈，画出来的角正好和想要的角拼成一条直线。连线以前先问自己：这个角应该比直角小，还是比直角大？想清楚了再点点，就不容易画错。
