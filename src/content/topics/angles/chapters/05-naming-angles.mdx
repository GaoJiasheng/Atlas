<Lang en>

When a drawing has more than one angle, you need a way to say which one you mean. Angles have names, written with letters.

Start with <Beat n={1}>an angle with letters on it</Beat>. Points on the arms are named with capital letters: A on one arm, C on the other, and B at the <Term id="vertex">vertex</Term>. This <Term id="angle">angle</Term> is written ∠ABC and said "angle ABC". The vertex letter, B, goes in the middle.

Now <Beat n={2}>read the name the other way</Beat>. ∠CBA names the same angle. Start at a point on one arm, go through the vertex, and end at a point on the other arm. Whichever arm you start from, B stays in the middle, because every name has to pass through the vertex.

Next, <Beat n={3}>add a third arm, BD</Beat>. Now three angles meet at B: ∠ABD, ∠DBC and the big one, ∠ABC, which is made of the other two. If you write only ∠B, nobody knows which of the three you mean. Three letters, with B in the middle, make it clear.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>look at the angles of a shape</Beat>. An angle can also be named with a small letter written inside it, near the vertex. The angles of this shape are ∠a, ∠b, ∠c and ∠d. A small letter is quick to write when there are many angles, and each letter belongs to one angle only, so the name is still clear.

A common mistake is to put the vertex letter first or last, as in ∠BAC for the angle at B. ∠BAC is a different angle: its vertex is A.

When you are ready, <Task id="naming-angles">try one question</Task> on naming an angle.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

图里不止一个角的时候，就要有办法说清楚指的是哪一个。角是有名字的，用字母来写。

先看<Beat n={1}>标了字母的角</Beat>。边上的点用大写字母表示：一条边上是 A，另一条边上是 C，<Term id="vertex">顶点</Term>是 B。这个<Term id="angle">角</Term>写作 ∠ABC，读作“角 ABC”。顶点的字母 B 写在中间。

再<Beat n={2}>反过来读一遍</Beat>。∠CBA 说的也是这个角。从一条边上的点出发，经过顶点，走到另一条边上的点。不管从哪条边开始，B 都在中间。

接着<Beat n={3}>再加一条边 BD</Beat>。现在有三个角都以 B 为顶点：∠ABD、∠DBC，还有大的 ∠ABC，它由前两个角合成。如果只写 ∠B，别人不知道你说的是三个角里的哪一个。写三个字母、B 在中间，就说清楚了。

最后<Beat n={4}>看一个图形的几个角</Beat>。也可以在角里靠近顶点的地方写一个小写字母来给角命名。这个图形的四个角分别是 ∠a、∠b、∠c 和 ∠d。角很多的时候，写小写字母又快又清楚。

常见的错误是把顶点的字母写在头上或尾巴上，比如把顶点在 B 的角写成 ∠BAC。∠BAC 是另一个角，它的顶点是 A。

准备好了，就<Task id="naming-angles">做一道小题</Task>：说出一个角的名字。

</Lang>
