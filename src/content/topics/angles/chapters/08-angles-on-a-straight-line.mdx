<Lang en>

So far you have measured angles. From here on, you work some angles out without measuring, using a few facts about turns.

Start with <Beat n={1}>a straight line</Beat>. Standing on a straight line and turning from one direction to the other is half a full turn. Half of 360° is 180°, so the angle on a straight line is 180°. The flat edge of a protractor is a straight line too, and its scale runs from 0 to 180.

Now <Beat n={2}>draw a line from a point on the straight line</Beat>. The 180° splits into two angles. Turn the new line: one angle grows and the other shrinks, but together they stay 180°. Here they are 130° and 50°, and 130° + 50° = 180°. These are <Term id="straight-line-angles">angles on a straight line</Term>.

So <Beat n={3}>find an unknown angle on a straight line</Beat> by taking the angles you know from 180°. One angle is 65°, so the other is 180° − 65° = 115°. You do not need a protractor: the facts give the answer.

Last, <Beat n={4}>look at three angles on a straight line</Beat>. They also add up to 180°. Two of them are 40° and 75°, so the third is 180° − 40° − 75° = 65°.

A common mistake is to take the angles away from 360° instead of 180°. 360° is a full turn, for angles all the way round a point. A straight line is only half a turn.

<Lang zh>

到现在为止，角都是量出来的。从这一章开始，有些角不用量，而是用几条关于转动的规律算出来。

先看<Beat n={1}>一条直线</Beat>。站在直线上，从一个方向转到相反的方向，正好转了半圈。360° 的一半是 180°，所以直线上的角是 180°。量角器的直边也是一条直线，它上面的刻度从 0 到 180。

再<Beat n={2}>从直线上一点画一条线</Beat>。180° 就分成了两个角。转动这条新线：一个角变大，另一个变小，可是合起来一直是 180°。这里两个角是 130° 和 50°，130° + 50° = 180°。这样的角叫<Term id="straight-line-angles">直线上的角</Term>。

所以，<Beat n={3}>求直线上的未知角</Beat>，就用 180° 减去已知的角。一个角是 65°，另一个就是 180° − 65° = 115°。不用量角器，按规律就能算出来。

最后<Beat n={4}>看直线上的三个角</Beat>。三个角加起来也是 180°。其中两个是 40° 和 75°，第三个就是 180° − 40° − 75° = 65°。

常见的错误是用 360° 去减，而不是 180°。360° 是一整圈，是绕着一点一整圈的角用的；直线只是半圈。
