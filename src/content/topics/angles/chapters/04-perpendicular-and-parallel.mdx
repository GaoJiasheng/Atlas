<Lang en>

Look at a window grille or the lines on a page. Some lines cross each other, and some run side by side and never meet. Two of these arrangements have names.

<Figure src="figures/angles-window-frame" alt={{ en: "A metal window grille with three parallel upright bars and one horizontal crossbar.", zh: "一块金属窗花有三根互相平行的竖杆和一根横杆。" }} caption={{ en: "In a window grille, the upright bars are parallel and the crossbar is perpendicular to them.", zh: "窗花里，竖杆互相平行，横杆和它们垂直。" }} />

Start with <Beat n={1}>two lines that cross at a right angle</Beat>. Two straight lines that meet at a right angle are <Term id="perpendicular">perpendicular</Term>. Line AB is perpendicular to line CD. The small square at the crossing shows the right angle.

Next, look at <Beat n={2}>two lines on dot paper</Beat> that go the same way. Count the squares between them at the left end: 3. Count again at the right end: 3. Lines that go the same way and stay the same distance apart are <Term id="parallel">parallel</Term>. However far you make them, they never meet.

Now <Beat n={3}>look at some tilted lines</Beat>. On the left, two tilted lines cross at a right angle, so they are perpendicular too. On the right, two lines do not meet on the page. But count the squares between them: 3 at one end and 2 at the other. They come closer as they go, so they would meet if you made them longer. They are not parallel.

Last, <Beat n={4}>draw a parallel line and a perpendicular line</Beat> on dot paper. Line AB goes 3 dots right and 1 dot up. To draw a line through P parallel to AB, copy those steps from P: 3 right, 1 up. A line perpendicular to AB turns the steps round: 1 right and 3 down.

A common mistake is to call two lines parallel just because they do not cross on the page. Count the gap at both ends; parallel lines have the same gap everywhere.

<Lang zh>

看看窗花，看看本子上的线。有的线互相交叉，有的线并排走，永远碰不到。这两种情况各有一个名字。

<Figure src="figures/angles-window-frame" alt={{ en: "A metal window grille with three parallel upright bars and one horizontal crossbar.", zh: "一块金属窗花有三根互相平行的竖杆和一根横杆。" }} caption={{ en: "In a window grille, the upright bars are parallel and the crossbar is perpendicular to them.", zh: "窗花里，竖杆互相平行，横杆和它们垂直。" }} />

先看<Beat n={1}>相交成直角的两条线</Beat>。两条直线相交成直角，就说它们互相<Term id="perpendicular">垂直</Term>。直线 AB 垂直于直线 CD。交点上的小方块表示那里是直角。

再看<Beat n={2}>点阵上方向一样的两条线</Beat>。在左边数一数两条线之间隔几格：3 格。到右边再数：还是 3 格。方向一样、之间的距离处处相等的两条直线，互相<Term id="parallel">平行</Term>。不管把它们延长多远，都不会相交。

接着<Beat n={3}>看几条斜着的线</Beat>。左边两条斜线相交成直角，所以斜着的线也能互相垂直。右边两条线在纸上没有相交，可是数一数它们之间的格子：一头隔 3 格，另一头隔 2 格。它们越走越近，延长下去就会相交，所以不平行。

最后，<Beat n={4}>在点阵上画平行线和垂线</Beat>。线 AB 从 A 向右走 3 格、向上走 1 格。要过点 P 画 AB 的平行线，就从 P 照着同样的走法画：向右 3、向上 1。和 AB 垂直的线，走法要转过来：向右 1、向下 3。

常见的误会是两条线在纸上没有相交，就说它们平行。要在两头都数一数间隔；平行线的间隔处处一样。

准备好了，就<Task id="perpendicular-and-parallel">做一道小题</Task>：画一条线，或者找出平行、垂直的线。

</Lang>
