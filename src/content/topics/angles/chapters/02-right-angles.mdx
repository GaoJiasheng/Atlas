<Lang en>

Some angles are so common that they have their own name. Look at the corner of this page, of a door or of a window. They all have the same square shape.

Begin with <Beat n={1}>a square corner</Beat>. A square corner, like the corner of a page, is a <Term id="right-angle">right angle</Term>. When a drawing shows a right angle, it marks it with a small square at the vertex. The small square means "this corner is square".

Now look at <Beat n={2}>a clock at 3 o'clock</Beat>. The minute hand points to 12 and the hour hand points to 3. The two hands make a right angle. In 15 minutes the minute hand turns from 12 to 3. That is a quarter of a full turn. So a right angle is a quarter turn.

You can make your own right angle. <Beat n={3}>Fold a sheet of paper</Beat> in half, then fold it in half again. The corner where the folds meet is a right angle. This folded corner is a <Term id="right-angle-tester">right-angle tester</Term>, and you can carry it anywhere. A rectangle has four right angles, so its corners fit the tester too.

Last, <Beat n={4}>turn a right angle round</Beat>. The arms are now tilted, but the corner is still square. Put the tester in the corner. Both arms lie along the tester's edges, so it is still a right angle. Turning an angle does not change its size.

A common mistake is to see a right angle only when one arm is flat and the other stands straight up. When the corner is tilted, turn the page in your head, or put the tester in it.

<Lang zh>

有一种角太常见了，所以有自己的名字。看看书页的角、门的角、窗的角，它们的形状都是方方正正的。

先看<Beat n={1}>一个方方正正的角</Beat>。像书页的角那样方方正正的角，叫<Term id="right-angle">直角</Term>。图上的直角，会在顶点画一个小方块。小方块的意思就是“这个角是方的”。

再看<Beat n={2}>3 点整的钟面</Beat>。分针指着 12，时针指着 3，两根针正好成一个直角。分针从 12 走到 3 要 15 分钟，刚好转了一整圈的四分之一。所以，直角就是转四分之一圈。

直角也可以自己做。<Beat n={3}>拿一张纸对折</Beat>，再对折一次。两条折痕相交的那个角就是直角。这个折出来的角叫<Term id="right-angle-tester">直角板</Term>，可以带在身边，随时拿来比。长方形有四个直角，它的每个角都能和直角板贴合。

最后，<Beat n={4}>把直角转一转</Beat>。现在两条边都是斜的，可这个角还是方方正正的。把直角板放进去，两条边都贴着直角板的边，所以它还是直角。角转了方向，大小不变。

常见的误会是只认得一条边平放、一条边竖直的直角。角斜着放的时候，就在脑子里把纸转一转，或者拿直角板去比一比。

准备好了，就<Task id="right-angles">做一道小题</Task>：找出哪些是直角。

</Lang>
