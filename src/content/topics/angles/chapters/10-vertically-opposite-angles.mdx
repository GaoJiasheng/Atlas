<Lang en>

When two straight lines cross, they make four angles at once. Some of them are always equal, and you can see why.

Start with <Beat n={1}>two lines that cross</Beat>. They make four angles round the crossing: ∠a, ∠b, ∠c and ∠d. ∠a and ∠c face each other across the crossing. Angles that face each other like this are <Term id="vertically-opposite">vertically opposite angles</Term>. ∠b and ∠d are vertically opposite too.

Now <Beat n={2}>turn one of the lines</Beat>. All four angles change. But ∠a and ∠c change together and are always equal. So are ∠b and ∠d. Vertically opposite angles are equal, however the lines cross.

Why is that? <Beat n={3}>Look at ∠b</Beat>. ∠a and ∠b sit next to each other on one straight line, so they add up to 180°. ∠c and ∠b also sit on a straight line, so they add up to 180° too. Both ∠a and ∠c make 180° with the same ∠b, so ∠a must equal ∠c.

Now <Beat n={4}>put in a size</Beat>. If ∠a is 70°, the angle opposite it, ∠c, is also 70°. The angle next to ∠a is not equal to it. It makes a straight line with ∠a, so it is 180° − 70° = 110°. Now you know all four angles: 70°, 110°, 70° and 110°, and together they make 360°.

A common mistake is to mix up the angle opposite and the angle next to it. Opposite angles face each other across the crossing and are equal. Angles side by side make a straight line and add up to 180°.

<Lang zh>

两条直线相交，一下子就形成四个角。其中有的角总是相等的，而且可以看出为什么。

先看<Beat n={1}>相交的两条直线</Beat>。交点周围有四个角：∠a、∠b、∠c 和 ∠d。∠a 和 ∠c 隔着交点面对面。这样面对面的两个角叫<Term id="vertically-opposite">对顶角</Term>。∠b 和 ∠d 也是对顶角。

再<Beat n={2}>转动其中一条线</Beat>。四个角都跟着变。可是 ∠a 和 ∠c 一起变，一直相等；∠b 和 ∠d 也一直相等。对顶角相等。

为什么？<Beat n={3}>盯着 ∠b 看</Beat>。∠a 和 ∠b 挨在一起，在同一条直线上，所以加起来是 180°。∠c 和 ∠b 也在一条直线上，加起来也是 180°。∠a 和 ∠c 都和同一个 ∠b 拼成 180°，所以 ∠a 一定等于 ∠c。

接着<Beat n={4}>给一个角写上度数</Beat>。如果 ∠a 是 70°，它对面的 ∠c 也是 70°。挨着 ∠a 的那个角和它不相等：它和 ∠a 拼成一条直线，所以是 180° − 70° = 110°。

常见的错误是把对面的角和旁边的角弄混。对顶角隔着交点面对面，相等；挨着的两个角拼成一条直线，加起来是 180°。
