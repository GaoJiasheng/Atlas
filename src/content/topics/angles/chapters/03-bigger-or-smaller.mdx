<Lang en>

Now that you have a right angle in your hand, you can use it to sort every other angle. Is it smaller than a right angle, or greater?

First, <Beat n={1}>put the tester on an angle</Beat>. Place its corner on the vertex and lay one edge along one arm. Then look at the other arm. Here it lies inside the tester, so this angle is smaller than a right angle.

Now <Beat n={2}>turn the other arm further</Beat>, past the tester's edge. This time the other arm lies outside the tester. The angle is greater than a <Term id="right-angle">right angle</Term>. If the other arm lay exactly along the tester's second edge, the angle would be a right angle.

Next, <Beat n={3}>sort five angles with the tester</Beat>. Two are smaller than a right angle, one is a right angle, and two are greater. Look at the first one: its arms are very long, but it is still smaller than a right angle. Look at the last one: its arms are short, but it is greater. Long arms do not make an angle bigger.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>look at the corners of two shapes</Beat>. A shape can have all three kinds of angle. This triangle has one angle greater than a right angle and two smaller ones. The other shape has two right angles, one smaller angle and one greater angle.

A common mistake is to sort by the length of the arms, or to miss an angle because it is tilted. Lay the tester along one arm each time, wherever the angle points.

<More title={{ en: "Names for these angles", zh: "这些角的名字" }}>

An angle smaller than a right angle is called an acute angle. An angle greater than a right angle but smaller than two right angles is called an obtuse angle. You may meet these names in other books; the questions here do not ask for them.

</More>

<Lang zh>

手里有了直角，就可以拿它去比别的角：这个角比直角小，还是比直角大？

先<Beat n={1}>把直角板放到一个角上</Beat>。直角板的角对准顶点，一条边贴着角的一条边，再看另一条边。这里另一条边在直角板里面，所以这个角比直角小。

再<Beat n={2}>让另一条边继续转</Beat>，转过直角板的边。这一次另一条边在直角板外面，这个角比<Term id="right-angle">直角</Term>大。如果另一条边正好贴着直角板的另一条边，这个角就是直角。所以用直角板一比，每个角都只有三种可能：在里面、贴着边、在外面。

接着<Beat n={3}>用直角板给五个角分一分</Beat>。两个比直角小，一个是直角，两个比直角大。看第一个：它的边很长，可它还是比直角小。看最后一个：它的边很短，却比直角大。边长不会让角变大。

最后<Beat n={4}>看两个图形的角</Beat>。一个图形里，三种角都可能有。这个三角形有一个角比直角大，两个角比直角小。另一个图形有两个直角，还有一个比直角小的角和一个比直角大的角。

常见的错误是按边的长短来分，或者角斜着放就认不出来。每次都把直角板贴着一条边放，不管这个角朝哪边。

<More title={{ en: "Names for these angles", zh: "这些角的名字" }}>

比直角小的角叫锐角。比直角大、又比两个直角合起来小的角叫钝角。你可能会在别的书里见到这两个名字；这里的题目不考它们。

</More>
