<Lang en>

A straight line is half a turn. Now you go all the way round.

Begin with <Beat n={1}>a line that turns a full turn</Beat>. Turn a line round a point until it is back where it started. It has turned one full turn, which is 360°.

Now <Beat n={2}>draw several lines from one point</Beat>. They split the full turn into angles that fit together all the way round, with no gaps and no overlaps. These are <Term id="angles-at-a-point">angles at a point</Term>, and they add up to 360°. Here the angles are 90°, 110°, 70° and 90°, and 90° + 110° + 70° + 90° = 360°. The two right angles are marked with small squares.

So <Beat n={3}>find an unknown angle at a point</Beat> by taking the angles you know away from 360°. Two angles are 140° and 95°, so the third is 360° − 140° − 95° = 125°. Check the sum: 140° + 95° + 125° = 360°.

Last, <Beat n={4}>look out for a right-angle mark</Beat>. One angle here has a small square and no number. The small square means a <Term id="right-angle">right angle</Term>, 90°. The other known angle is 130°. These angles go all the way round, so use 360°, not 180°: 360° − 90° − 130° = 140°.

Two mistakes are common here. One is to forget the right angle because no number is written beside it. The other is to use 180° when the angles go all the way round a point.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

直线是半圈。现在要绕满一整圈。

先看<Beat n={1}>转了一整圈的线</Beat>。让一条线绕着一点转，一直转回原来的位置。它转了一整圈，就是 360°。

再<Beat n={2}>从一点画出几条线</Beat>。这几条线把一整圈分成几个角，这些角绕着这一点拼在一起，没有空隙，也不重叠。这样的角叫<Term id="angles-at-a-point">一点周围的角</Term>，它们加起来是 360°。这里四个角是 90°、110°、70° 和 90°，90° + 110° + 70° + 90° = 360°。两个直角都用小方块标出来了。

所以，<Beat n={3}>求一点周围的未知角</Beat>，就用 360° 减去已知的角。两个角是 140° 和 95°，第三个就是 360° − 140° − 95° = 125°。验算一下：140° + 95° + 125° = 360°。

最后<Beat n={4}>留心直角的标记</Beat>。这里有一个角只画了小方块，没写数字。小方块表示<Term id="right-angle">直角</Term>，是 90°。另一个已知角是 130°。这些角绕了一整圈，所以要用 360°，不是 180°：360° − 90° − 130° = 140°。

这里常见两种错误。一种是旁边没写数字，就把直角忘了；另一种是角明明绕着一点一整圈，却用 180° 去减。
