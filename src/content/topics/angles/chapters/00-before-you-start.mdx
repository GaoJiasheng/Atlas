<Lang en>

Open a door, a pair of scissors or a book, and you make an angle. This lesson is about angles: what an angle is, how to tell a right angle, how to measure and draw an angle with a protractor, and how to work out an angle you cannot measure.

On the stage now is an angle of 50°, with a protractor laid on it. The protractor's centre sits on the point where the two arms meet, and its 0 line lies along one arm. Look at the two rows of numbers: the one being read is darker, and the other one is faded.

There are 11 steps and a practice. Steps 01 to 04 are about turning, right angles, and lines that are perpendicular or parallel; they use no degrees at all. Steps 05 to 07 name angles, then measure and draw them in degrees. Steps 08 to 11 work out unknown angles on a straight line, round a point and where two lines cross. You can stop after chapter 04 or chapter 07 and come back to the rest later.

Each chapter explains first. The circles on the chapter axis at the bottom are its beats: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the stage turns an arm, lays a protractor or adds a line, while the reading panel says what to look at.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question, drawn from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: choose an answer, type a number, or work on the stage. Drag the protractor onto the vertex and turn it with its knob, or use the buttons **Centre on the vertex** and **0 line on an arm**. Turn an arm by dragging its end, or with the arrow keys and the −/+ buttons. On dot paper, tap a dot to end a line.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** draws the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, each one closer to the answer.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Every question can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved.

<Lang zh>

推开一扇门，张开一把剪刀，打开一本书，都会出现一个角。这节课讲角：角是什么，怎样认出直角，怎样用量角器量角、画角，还有量不出来的角怎样算出来。

现在舞台上是一个 50° 的角，上面摆着一个量角器。量角器的中心对着两条边相交的那个点，0 线贴着一条边。看量角器上的两圈数：正在读的那一圈颜色深，另一圈淡一些。

全课有 11 步，最后是练习。第 01 到 04 步讲转动、直角，还有互相垂直或平行的线，这几步完全不用度数。第 05 到 07 步给角起名字，再用度来量角、画角。第 08 到 11 步求未知角：直线上的、一点周围的，还有两条线相交处的。学完第 04 章或第 07 章可以先停下，以后再接着学。

每章先讲解。底部章节轴上的小圆点就是这一章的讲解拍：点一个，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上的边会转动、量角器会摆上去、线会画出来，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。

每章最后的小方块是一道小题，从大约十道题里随机抽一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **作答**：选一个答案，填一个数，或者在舞台上动手。把量角器拖到顶点上，用它上面的小圆钮转动；也可以按“中心对准顶点”“0 线对齐一条边”两个按钮。拖动一条边的末端来转它，也可以用方向键和 −/+ 按钮。在点阵上，点一个点，线就画到那里。
- **检查**（C）：如果还没对，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里。试两次以后，可以按“看答案”。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，一条比一条更接近答案。
- **再来一道**，或者**跳过**：什么都不强制。

每道题都可以只用手指点完成，也可以只用键盘完成。不计时，也不保存。

</Lang>
