<Lang en>

The Sun is our main source of energy: it gives out light and heat. Sunlight falls on the roof of the block and on the plant on the window ledge of the top-floor flat. <FlyTo preset="roof-view">Look at the roof</FlyTo>

A <Term id="solar-panel">solar panel</Term> changes light energy into electrical energy. In this model about a fifth, <Num s="S1">21 %</Num>, of the light that reaches the panels becomes electrical energy; most of the rest warms the panels, and a little is reflected. A change of energy from one form into other forms is an <Term id="energy-conversion">energy conversion</Term>. The panels do not make energy; they only change its form. At night, with no light coming in, they give none.

On HDB blocks, rooftop panels supply shared services such as the lifts, corridor lights and water pumps during the day. Any energy the block does not need goes into the <Term id="grid">grid</Term>, the network of cables that carries electrical energy between power stations and the buildings that use it. In the model the lift motor changes electrical energy into kinetic energy and raises the car, which gains potential energy. The flats themselves take their electrical energy from the grid.

To compare skies fairly, change only the sky and keep the same panels at the same tilt. That is a <Term id="fair-test">fair test</Term>: change one thing, keep everything else the same. Record the panel output for each sky and compare the bars.

Most of our energy resources got their energy from the Sun in some way. Plants take in light energy and use it to make food, and the food we eat comes from plants, or from animals that ate plants. <Term id="natural-gas">Natural gas</Term> is a <Term id="fossil-fuel">fossil fuel</Term>: it formed from the remains of living things buried millions of years ago, and those living things got their energy from the Sun too.

<More title={{"en": "How natural gas holds the Sun's energy", "zh": "天然气里怎么会有太阳的能量"}}>

Millions of years ago, the remains of plants and animals built up on land and on the sea floor and were buried under layers of rock. Heat and pressure slowly turned some of them into natural gas. Those plants got their energy from sunlight, and the animals from eating plants, so the gas holds energy that came from the Sun long ago. Coal formed in a different way, mostly from plants that died in swamps. Burning the gas releases its stored energy.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Plants and the soil", "zh": "植物和泥土"}}>

A plant's roots take in water and mineral nutrients from the soil, but the soil is not where the plant's energy comes from. The leaves use light energy to make food from water and carbon dioxide; see <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="leaves-make-food">Leaves make food</TopicLink>. The Sun also warms the sea and drives the rain that fills our reservoirs: <TopicLink slug="water" ch="the-water-cycle">The Sun drives the water cycle</TopicLink>.

太阳是我们主要的能源，它发出光和热。阳光照在组屋的屋顶上，也照在顶层住家窗台上的盆栽上。<FlyTo preset="roof-view">看屋顶</FlyTo>

<Term id="solar-panel">太阳能板</Term>把光能转换成电能。在这个模型里，照到板上的光大约有五分之一，也就是 <Num s="S1">21 %</Num>，变成电能；其余大部分让板变热，还有一点被反射掉。能量从一种形式变成别的形式，就是<Term id="energy-conversion">能量转换</Term>。太阳能板并不制造能量，只是改变能量的形式。到了夜里，没有光照进来，它就一点电能也不产生。

组屋屋顶的太阳能板，白天为电梯、走廊灯、水泵这些公共设施供电；楼里用不完的能量就送进<Term id="grid">电网</Term>。电网是一张电缆网络，把电能在发电厂和用电的建筑之间输送。在模型里，电梯的电动机把电能转换成动能，把轿厢升上去，轿厢的势能就增加了。住家自己用的电能则来自电网。

要公平地比较不同的天空，就只改变天空，太阳能板和倾角都保持不变。这就是<Term id="fair-test">公平测试</Term>：一次只改一样东西，其余的都不变。把每种天空下板的电功率记下来，比一比图上的条。

我们的大多数能源，能量都以某种方式来自太阳。植物接收光能，用它制造食物；我们吃的食物来自植物，或者来自吃植物的动物。<Term id="natural-gas">天然气</Term>是一种<Term id="fossil-fuel">化石燃料</Term>，是几百万年前埋在地下的生物遗骸变成的，而那些生物的能量也来自太阳。

<More title={{"en": "How natural gas holds the Sun's energy", "zh": "天然气里怎么会有太阳的能量"}}>

几百万年前，动植物的遗骸在陆地上和海底越积越多，被一层层岩石埋在下面。在高温和压力下，其中一部分慢慢变成了天然气。那些植物的能量来自阳光，动物的能量来自吃下的植物，所以天然气里的能量，是很久以前从太阳来的。煤的形成方式不一样，主要是死在沼泽里的植物变成的。燃烧天然气，就把它储存的能量释放出来。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Plants and the soil", "zh": "植物和泥土"}}>

植物的根从泥土里吸收水和矿物质养分，但植物的能量不是从泥土里来的。叶子用光能把水和二氧化碳制成食物，见<TopicLink slug="plant" ch="leaves-make-food">叶子造食物</TopicLink>。太阳还让海水变暖，推动降雨，把雨水送进我们的蓄水池：<TopicLink slug="water" ch="the-water-cycle">太阳推动水循环</TopicLink>。
