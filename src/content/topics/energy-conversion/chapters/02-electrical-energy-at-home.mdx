<Lang en>

Most of the flat's electrical energy comes through the grid from a <Term id="power-station">power station</Term>, a place where the energy of a fuel or another energy resource is changed into electrical energy. In 2024 about <Num s="S5">94 %</Num> of Singapore's electricity was made from natural gas. The rooftop panels in this model power the block's shared services; the flat has its own supply from the grid, and the distribution board shares it out among the flat's circuits. <FlyTo preset="living-room">Look at the living room</FlyTo>

Each appliance changes electrical energy into the form we want from it, and always into some heat as well. The fan's motor changes it into kinetic energy of the blades and the air, and the motor warms up and hums. The lamp changes it into light energy, and some into heat. The speaker's cone moves back and forth and gives out sound energy, but in this model only about one part in a hundred, <Num s="S1">1 %</Num>, becomes sound; the rest becomes heat.

The kettle is different: here heat is the form we want. Its element gets hot, and heat passes into the water. In this model <Num s="S1">85 %</Num> of the electrical energy heats the water, and the kettle would bring one litre from <Num s="S1">30 °C</Num> to the boil in a little over two and a half minutes. <FlyTo preset="kitchen">Look at the kettle</FlyTo>

Read the energy-chain card: the wider a band, the bigger its share of the energy. In the try-it card, keep the same supply and change only the appliance. Record the share that ends in the form we want and the share that becomes heat, then compare the four appliances on the chart.

<More title={{"en": "Where Singapore's electricity comes from", "zh": "新加坡的电从哪里来"}}>

In 2024 natural gas was the fuel for <Num s="S5">94.0 %</Num> of Singapore's electricity, other energy products for <Num s="S5">2.7 %</Num>, solar panels for <Num s="S5">2.1 %</Num>, and coal and oil products for <Num s="S5">1.2 %</Num>. These are national figures for one year. The grid joins many power stations, so the energy reaching a home cannot be traced back to one of them.

</More>

The model's kettle takes <Num s="S1">2,200 W</Num>, like one common kettle sold in Singapore, from the flat's <Num s="S18">230 V</Num> supply. Heating <Num s="S19">1 kg</Num> of water by <Num s="S19">70 °C</Num> takes about <Num s="S19">293 kJ</Num>: <Num s="S19">4,186 J</Num> for each kilogram and each degree. With <Num s="S1">85 %</Num> of <Num s="S1">2,200 W</Num> going into the water, that takes about <Num s="S1">157 s</Num>. The other <Num s="S1">14 %</Num> warms the kettle and the air, and <Num s="S1">1 %</Num> is the hiss; all three shares are illustrative. The kettle body and the steam are hot.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Even the fan's breeze ends as heat", "zh": "连风扇吹的风最后也变成热"}}>

The fan gives the air kinetic energy. As the moving air slows down, that energy becomes heat in the room. So even a fan, whose job is movement, ends up warming the room a little. 家里的电能大多经电网从<Term id="power-station">发电厂</Term>送来。发电厂是把燃料或其他能源里的能量转换成电能的地方。2024 年，新加坡大约 <Num s="S5">94 %</Num> 的电是用天然气发的。模型里屋顶的太阳能板为整座楼的公共设施供电；住家自己从电网取电，再由配电箱分到家里的各个电路。<FlyTo preset="living-room">看客厅</FlyTo>

每件电器都把电能转换成我们想要的形式，同时总有一部分变成热。风扇的电动机把电能转换成扇叶和空气的动能，电动机自己也会变热、嗡嗡地响。灯把电能转换成光能，也有一部分变成热。音箱的纸盆前后振动，发出声能；但在这个模型里，一百份里只有大约一份，也就是 <Num s="S1">1 %</Num>，变成声音，其余都变成热。

电热水壶不一样：我们要的正是热。发热盘变热，热传进水里。在这个模型里，<Num s="S1">85 %</Num> 的电能让水变热，把一升 <Num s="S1">30 °C</Num> 的水烧开，要两分半钟多一点。<FlyTo preset="kitchen">看水壶</FlyTo>
