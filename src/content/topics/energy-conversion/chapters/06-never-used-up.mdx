<Lang en>

Follow energy all the way from natural gas to a lamp in the flat. At the power station, burning the gas releases its stored energy, and part of it is changed into electrical energy; the rest becomes heat. In the cables a little more becomes heat. At the lamp, some becomes light and the rest heat. Even the light, once it falls on the walls and the furniture, ends up as heat. At every step, the energy that goes in equals the energy that comes out: none of it disappears. <FlyTo preset="living-room">Look at the lamp</FlyTo>

So energy is never used up. What changes is how useful it is. Spread out as heat in the air, it is very hard to collect and turn back into electricity or movement. Energy that ends in a form we did not want, and cannot easily use again, is wasted energy.

The lamp you choose makes a difference. A filament lamp changes only about a tenth of its electrical energy into light. An LED lamp gives the same light with at least <Num s="S15">75 %</Num> less electrical energy; in this model it needs <Num s="S1">25 %</Num> of the energy, a quarter, for the same light. In the try-it card, keep the brightness the same, change the lamp, and compare the energy each one needs and the heat each one gives off.

To <Term id="save-energy">save energy</Term> means to waste less of it. Most of Singapore's electricity comes from natural gas, a fuel that will run out one day, and burning it gives off carbon dioxide. Switching off what we are not using, choosing LED lamps and setting the air conditioner at <Num s="S31">25 °C</Num> or higher all mean less gas burnt for the same jobs.

And almost all of this energy started out as light from the Sun: in the panels on the roof today, and in the natural gas long ago.

<More title={{"en": "Saving energy, two meanings", "zh": "节约能源的两层意思"}}>

Scientists say that energy is conserved: the total amount never changes, it only changes form. In everyday life, conserving energy means something else: using less of our energy resources to get a job done, by wasting less along the way. This topic says "save energy" for the everyday meaning.

</More>

跟着能量从天然气一直走到家里的一盏灯。在发电厂，燃烧天然气把它储存的能量释放出来，其中一部分转换成电能，其余的变成热。在电缆里，又有一点变成热。到了灯那里，一部分变成光，其余变成热。就连光，照到墙上和家具上以后，最后也变成了热。每一步，进去的能量都等于出来的能量：一点也没有消失。<FlyTo preset="living-room">看灯</FlyTo>

所以能量不会用完。改变的是它还有多大用处。散在空气里的热，很难再收集起来，变回电或者运动。能量最后变成了我们不想要、又很难再用的形式，这一部分就是浪费掉的能量。

选什么样的灯有差别。白炽灯只把大约十分之一的电能变成光。LED 灯给出同样的光，至少少用 <Num s="S15">75 %</Num> 的电能；在这个模型里，同样亮只要 <Num s="S1">25 %</Num> 的能量，也就是四分之一。在试一试卡片里，保持亮度不变，只换灯，比较每种灯需要的能量和散出的热。

<Term id="save-energy">节约能源</Term>就是少浪费能量。新加坡的电大多用天然气发。天然气是总有一天会用完的燃料，燃烧它还会放出二氧化碳。不用的电器关掉、选用 LED 灯、空调设在 <Num s="S31">25 °C</Num> 或更高，做同样的事就能少烧一些天然气。

而这里几乎所有的能量，最初都是太阳的光：屋顶太阳能板里的是今天的阳光，天然气里的是很久以前的阳光。
