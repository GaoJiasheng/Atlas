<Lang en>

The water starts hotter than its surroundings. It loses energy and cools. Compare the bare and wrapped cups at the same time, with the same starting temperature. The wrapped cup stays warmer because it loses energy more slowly. The wrap supplies no energy.

A <Term id="poor-conductor">poor conductor of heat</Term> transfers heat slowly. Examples include plastics, wood, rubber and still air. Heat still passes through these materials. Move the time control forward: the wrapped cup keeps cooling.

<Term id="insulation">Insulation</Term> slows heat transfer into or out of an object. Start the water colder than its surroundings. Energy now enters the water and warms it. The wrap slows this transfer too, helping the cold water stay cold. Keeping drinks warm and keeping them cold use the same principle.

Change the covering on the card. Keep the water amount and starting temperature the same, then record both readings at matching times. Changing several conditions at once makes it hard to tell what caused a difference. These settings describe the whole cup’s heat transfer, so a real cup may give different results. Waterproofing and insulation are different properties. Explore more in <TopicLink slug="materials" ch="choosing-materials">Choosing materials</TopicLink>.

<More title={{"en":"Still air and moving air","zh":"静止空气与流动空气"}}>

Air conducts heat poorly. Trapping it in small spaces helps insulation. Moving air can carry energy away, so the way air is held matters.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"The cup’s transfer settings","zh":"杯子的传热参数"}}>

The bare and wrapped cups use transfer settings of <Num s="S27">2 W/K</Num> and <Num s="S27">0.5 W/K</Num>. These describe the whole route between water and surroundings, rather than a material alone. The surroundings stay at <Num s="S27">25 °C</Num>.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

水从比环境更高的温度开始，失去能量后便冷却。保持初温和经过时间相同，比较裸杯与包覆杯。包覆杯较暖，因为它失去能量较慢。包覆层没有向水供能。

<Term id="poor-conductor">热的不良导体</Term>传热较慢，例如塑料、木、橡胶和静止空气。热仍然能通过这些材料。继续推进时间，就能看见包覆杯也在冷却。

<Term id="insulation">隔热</Term>减慢能量传入或传出。把水的初温调得比环境低，能量便传入水中，使水变暖。包覆层也会减慢这次传热，帮助冷水保持低温。保温与保冷用的是同一个原理。

用卡片改变包覆条件。保持水量和初温相同，在相同时刻记录两个读数。同时改变几个条件，就难以判断差别由什么造成。这里的参数描述整杯水向外传热的快慢，真实杯子可能有不同结果。防水与隔热是不同性质；到<TopicLink slug="materials" ch="choosing-materials">“选择材料”</TopicLink>继续比较。

<More title={{"en":"Still air and moving air","zh":"静止空气与流动空气"}}>

空气不良导热，把它留在小空隙中，有助于隔热。流动空气却能带走能量，所以空气怎样被留住也很重要。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"The cup’s transfer settings","zh":"杯子的传热参数"}}>

</More>
