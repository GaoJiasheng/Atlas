<Lang en>

The bench holds cups, spoons, a heater and a rod. A <Term id="thermometer">thermometer</Term> measures <Term id="temperature">temperature</Term>: how hot or cold something is. Its sensing end sits in the water, clear of the cup’s bottom and sides. The stand holds it in place.

The heater supplies energy to water. The spoon handles show energy passing along a material. The rod shows how heating can change an object’s size. These experiments share a bench, but their energy transfers stay separate.

SIM labels results calculated by the model. The time control starts at <Num s="S27">0 s</Num>. Move it to see the readings at a later time. Waiting, turning the view or switching on the moving energy symbols does not advance the experiment. Explore the hot apparatus on screen.

<More title={{"en":"Where the energy comes from","zh":"能量从哪里来"}}>

The Sun can transfer energy without touching an object. An electric heater uses an electrical supply. Follow these different routes in <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="energy-from-the-sun">Energy conversion</TopicLink>.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"A model bench","zh":"模型实验台"}}>

The apparatus is a simplified model. Its sizes, starting conditions and responses to heating were chosen for these comparisons. The readings come from calculations, rather than measurements of real spoons or your room.

</More>

</Lang>

实验台上有杯子、勺子、加热器和一根杆。<Term id="thermometer">温度计</Term>测量<Term id="temperature">温度</Term>，告诉我们物体有多热或多冷。感温端浸在水中，不碰杯底或杯壁。支架把它固定在这个位置。

加热器向水供能。勺柄显示能量怎样沿材料传递，杆则显示受热后的尺寸变化。这几组实验共用一个台面，但各自的能量传递互不相连。
