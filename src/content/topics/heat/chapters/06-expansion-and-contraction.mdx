<Lang en>

The rod rests on supports that let it change length. Most substances become larger when warmed under ordinary conditions, if they are free to change size. This is <Term id="expansion">expansion</Term>. The decrease in size on cooling is <Term id="contraction">contraction</Term>. These are usual patterns, with exceptions and conditions; they are not rules that every substance must follow at every temperature.

The model rod has a reference length of <Num s="S27">100 mm</Num> at <Num s="S27">25 °C</Num>. At <Num s="S27">85 °C</Num>, its calculated extension is only <Num s="S27">0.072 mm</Num>. The separate endpoint indicator magnifies the displacement <Num s="S27">500 times</Num> so you can see it. The number reports the calculated extension. The reference rod geometry stays fixed while the separate indicator moves.

<Figure src="figures/heat-expansion" alt={{"en":"An enlarged ball above a ring with an open hole; the ball is wider than the hole and each object has a handle.","zh":"一个放大的球位于有真实孔洞的环上方，球宽于孔，球和环各有手柄。"}} caption={{"en":"Expanded ball above a ring kept at its original temperature; size change exaggerated.","zh":"膨胀球在保持原温的环上方；尺寸变化已夸大。"}} />

The ball and ring show a separate, exaggerated comparison. The ball becomes too wide for the unchanged hole. If the ring itself were heated uniformly and freely, its hole would grow too. Expansion does not mean material fills the hole. Cooling the rod back to its reference temperature restores its model length without adding or removing material.

The liquid column and gas piston show other conditions. Liquid expansion relative to the glass moves a thermometer column along its narrow tube. Warmed gas can occupy more space when a piston is free to move. A rigid sealed container constrains its volume. These qualitative views have no calculated liquid or gas volumes, and are screen demonstrations rather than activities to perform with hot apparatus.

<More title={{"en":"Particles and the water exception","zh":"粒子与水的例外"}}>

An ordinary expanding solid has greater average particle separation; its particles do not balloon. Liquid water expands when cooled from about <Num s="S20">4 °C</Num> toward <Num s="S20">0 °C</Num>. Continue with <TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="solids">States of matter</TopicLink>.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"A movable boundary matters","zh":"边界能否移动很重要"}}>

A fixed amount of ideal gas expands with increasing absolute temperature at fixed pressure. If its volume is constrained, pressure can rise instead. The piston view indicates a movable boundary; it does not solve a gas equation or instruct heating a sealed vessel.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

杆放在支座上，可以自由改变长度。普通条件下，多数物质受热时尺寸增加，称为<Term id="expansion">膨胀</Term>；冷却时尺寸减小，称为<Term id="contraction">收缩</Term>。这些是有条件的一般规律，不能说所有物质在任何温度下都一样。

模型杆在 <Num s="S27">25 °C</Num> 时原长 <Num s="S27">100 mm</Num>。升到 <Num s="S27">85 °C</Num>，计算伸长只有 <Num s="S27">0.072 mm</Num>。独立端点标记把位移放大 <Num s="S27">500 倍</Num>，让微小变化可见；读数仍表示计算伸长。参考杆的几何形状保持不变，独立标记显示放大位移。

<Figure src="figures/heat-expansion" alt={{"en":"An enlarged ball above a ring with an open hole; the ball is wider than the hole and each object has a handle.","zh":"一个放大的球位于有真实孔洞的环上方，球宽于孔，球和环各有手柄。"}} caption={{"en":"Expanded ball above a ring kept at its original temperature; size change exaggerated.","zh":"膨胀球在保持原温的环上方；尺寸变化已夸大。"}} />

球环是另一个夸大尺寸变化的示意：球膨胀后，不能通过保持原温的孔。如果环本身均匀受热、能自由膨胀，孔也会变大。冷却杆到参考温度，模型恢复原长，过程中没有添加或移走材料。

液柱与活塞显示另一些条件。液体相对玻璃膨胀，使温度计液柱沿窄管移动；气体受热时，能推动可动活塞，占更大空间。刚性密闭容器则限制体积。这些画面只作定性说明，没有计算液体或气体体积，也不是热器材实物活动。观察时要区分各个示意：杆的毫米读数只属于杆，不能拿来估计球、液柱或气体改变了多少。球环的夸大图帮助看清能否通过的关系，不表示真实金属会产生同样大的尺寸变化。

<More title={{"en":"Particles and the water exception","zh":"粒子与水的例外"}}>

普通固体膨胀时，粒子平均间距增大，不是粒子本身胀大。液态水从约 <Num s="S20">4 °C</Num> 冷却到 <Num s="S20">0 °C</Num> 时反而膨胀。可到<TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="solids">“物质三态”</TopicLink>继续看。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"A movable boundary matters","zh":"边界能否移动很重要"}}>

定量理想气体在压强固定时，绝对温度增加，体积增大。体积受限制时，压强可能上升。活塞画面说明边界可以移动，没有求解气体方程，也不指导加热密闭器皿。

</More>
