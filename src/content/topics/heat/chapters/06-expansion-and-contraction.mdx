<Lang en>

The rod rests on supports that let it change length. Under ordinary conditions, most substances grow larger when warmed if they have room to expand. This is <Term id="expansion">expansion</Term>. Becoming smaller on cooling is <Term id="contraction">contraction</Term>. Some substances behave differently, as the water example below shows.

In this model, the rod is <Num s="S27">100 mm</Num> long at <Num s="S27">25 °C</Num>. At <Num s="S27">85 °C</Num>, it lengthens by just <Num s="S27">0.072 mm</Num>. A separate pointer shows this movement enlarged <Num s="S27">500 times</Num>. The drawn rod stays fixed; read its calculated extension from the number.

<Figure src="figures/heat-expansion" alt={{"en":"A ball, wider than the ring hole below it. Both ball and ring have handles; the size change is exaggerated.","zh":"球位于环孔上方，比孔更宽。球和环各有手柄，尺寸变化已夸大。"}} caption={{"en":"The warmed ball is too wide for the unchanged hole. Size change exaggerated.","zh":"热球比原来的孔更宽。尺寸变化已夸大。"}} />

The ball-and-ring picture exaggerates another small size change. The warmed ball is too wide for the unchanged hole. Heating the ring evenly, with room to expand, would make its hole larger too. Material does not expand into the hole. Cool the rod to its starting temperature and its length returns to the starting value. No material was added or removed.

The liquid column shows how a thermometer works. Liquid expands more than the glass, so the column rises along the narrow tube. In the gas demonstration, warming lets gas push a movable piston upwards and occupy more space. A rigid sealed container holds the volume fixed. These pictures show the direction of change, without calculating liquid or gas volumes. The rod reading applies only to the rod. Explore these demonstrations on screen.

<More title={{"en":"Particles and the water exception","zh":"粒子与水的例外"}}>

When an ordinary solid expands, its particles become farther apart on average. The particles themselves do not grow. Liquid water is an exception to usual cooling behaviour: it expands as it cools from about <Num s="S20">4 °C</Num> towards <Num s="S20">0 °C</Num>. Explore more in <TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="solids">States of matter</TopicLink>.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"A movable boundary matters","zh":"边界能否移动很重要"}}>

For a fixed amount of ideal gas at constant pressure, volume increases as absolute temperature rises. Absolute temperature is measured from absolute zero. If volume cannot increase, pressure can rise instead. The piston illustrates a movable boundary. It does not calculate a gas equation. Do not heat a sealed vessel.

</More>

<Lang zh>

杆放在支座上，能够自由伸缩。普通条件下，多数物质受热会变大，前提是有空间膨胀。这叫<Term id="expansion">膨胀</Term>。冷却时变小叫<Term id="contraction">收缩</Term>。也有物质不遵循这个通常规律，例如下面提到的水。

模型杆在 <Num s="S27">25 °C</Num> 时长 <Num s="S27">100 mm</Num>。升到 <Num s="S27">85 °C</Num>，只伸长 <Num s="S27">0.072 mm</Num>。独立指示片把位移放大 <Num s="S27">500 倍</Num>。画出的杆保持不动，计算的伸长量要看数字。

球环图把另一个微小的尺寸变化夸大。受热的球变宽，不能通过原来的孔。如果圆环均匀受热，又能自由膨胀，孔也会变大，材料不会向孔内膨胀。把杆冷却回起始温度，它便恢复原长。过程中没有添加或移走材料。

液柱显示温度计的原理。液体比玻璃膨胀得多，所以液柱沿窄管上升。气体示意中，受热气体推动可动活塞向上，占据更大空间。刚性密闭容器则限制体积。这些画面显示变化方向，不计算液体或气体体积。杆的读数只适用于杆。请在屏幕上探索这些演示。

<More title={{"en":"Particles and the water exception","zh":"粒子与水的例外"}}>

普通固体膨胀时，粒子的平均间距增大，不是粒子本身变大。液态水是冷却规律的一个例外：从约 <Num s="S20">4 °C</Num> 冷却到 <Num s="S20">0 °C</Num> 时，它反而膨胀。到<TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="solids">“物质三态”</TopicLink>继续看。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"A movable boundary matters","zh":"边界能否移动很重要"}}>

一定量的理想气体在压强固定时，绝对温度升高，体积就增大。绝对温度从绝对零度算起。如果体积不能增大，压强可能上升。活塞用来说明边界可以移动，没有计算气体方程。不要加热密闭器皿。

</More>
