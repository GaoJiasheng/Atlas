<Lang en>

Compare the spoon tips at the same time. Start your explanation with what you changed and what you kept the same. A <Term id="fair-test">fair test</Term> changes one condition while keeping other relevant conditions the same. For the spoons, keep starting temperatures, bath temperature, immersion depth and spoon shapes alike.

Then describe what you saw: the metal tip warmed faster in the hot bath. Explain why: energy passed from the hotter water towards the cooler tip more readily through metal. If several conditions change at once, you cannot tell which caused the difference.

Try explaining the wrapped cup. At the same starting temperature, water amount, surroundings and time, the wrapped cup stays warmer. It loses energy more slowly; the wrap supplies no energy. Record the conditions alongside each reading, and keep separate experiments in separate comparisons.

Always ask what a temperature describes. Singapore climate records describe outdoor air over a stated period. A hot-spring reading describes water at a particular collection point. Neither gives the temperature of this cup; its room setting is chosen for the simulation. Link your change, observation and explanation in a sentence. Revise it when you have more evidence. Explore these ideas in <TopicLink slug="aircon" ch="switch-on">air-conditioning</TopicLink>, <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="electrical-energy-at-home">energy conversion</TopicLink>, <TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="mass-and-space">states of matter</TopicLink> and <TopicLink slug="materials" ch="choosing-materials">material choices</TopicLink>.

<More title={{"en":"An evidence sentence","zh":"用证据写一句话"}}>

“With the same starting temperature and time, the wrapped cup cooled less because the wrap slowed energy transfer to the surroundings.” Use this pattern to connect your observation to its cause.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Comparing real objects","zh":"比较真实物体"}}>

Real metals and plastics differ. Shape, heat capacity, contact and surroundings also affect heat transfer. A conclusion should say which objects and conditions you compared.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

在相同时刻比较勺子远端。解释先交代改了什么、保持了什么。<Term id="fair-test">公平测试</Term>只改变一个条件，把其他相关条件保持相同。比较勺子时，要保持初温、浴温、浸入深度和勺形相近。

接着说出观察：热浴中的金属勺远端升温较快。再解释原因：能量从较热的水传向较冷远端，经过金属时传得较容易。同时改变几个条件，就无法判断差别由哪一项造成。

再试着解释包覆杯。初温、水量、环境和时间相同时，包覆杯较暖，因为它失去能量较慢，包覆层并没有供能。在每个读数旁记下条件，把不同实验分开比较。

读温度时，先问它描述什么。新加坡气候资料描述一段时期的室外空气，温泉读数描述特定取水处的水。它们都不表示这杯水的温度；模拟的室温由我们设定。把改变、观察和解释连成句，有新证据时再修改。继续看<TopicLink slug="aircon" ch="switch-on">空调</TopicLink>、<TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="electrical-energy-at-home">能量转换</TopicLink>、<TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="mass-and-space">物质三态</TopicLink>和<TopicLink slug="materials" ch="choosing-materials">材料选择</TopicLink>。

<More title={{"en":"An evidence sentence","zh":"用证据写一句话"}}>

“初温和时间相同时，包覆杯降温较少，因为包覆层减慢了能量向环境传递。”用这样的句子，把观察与原因连起来。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Comparing real objects","zh":"比较真实物体"}}>

不同金属和塑料的性质各有差别。形状、热容量、接触和环境也影响传热。写结论时，要说明比较了哪些物体和条件。

</More>
