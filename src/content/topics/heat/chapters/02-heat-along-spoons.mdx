<Lang en>

The two spoon bowls sit in the same bath. Their shapes and immersion depths are alike, and their far ends start at the same temperature. An external source keeps the bath at its chosen temperature as it exchanges energy with the spoons.

<Term id="heat-transfer">Heat transfer</Term> is energy passing from a hotter place to a colder place. The hot bath transfers energy along the handles towards their cooler tips. Metal is generally a <Term id="good-conductor">good conductor of heat</Term>: heat passes through it easily. At the same time, the metal tip is warmer than the plastic tip. Both tips warm, but the plastic tip warms more slowly.

<Figure src="figures/heat-spoons" alt={{"en":"A metal and a plastic spoon, each with a shallow bowl in the same beaker and a handle extending out.","zh":"金属勺和塑料勺的浅勺碗浸在同一杯水中，完整勺柄伸出杯外。"}} caption={{"en":"Two spoons of similar shape share one bath. Temperatures are not measured in this illustration.","zh":"形状相近的两把勺子浸在同一温浴里。图中没有实测温度。"}} />

Now choose a bath colder than the starting tips. Energy flows from the warmer tips towards the colder bath. The metal tip cools faster. A good conductor can warm or cool quickly; the temperature difference sets the direction of transfer.

When the bath and tips start at the same temperature, there is no net heat transfer between them. This is <Term id="thermal-equilibrium">thermal equilibrium</Term>. Compare the tips at the same time and bath temperature. Each change of bath temperature starts a fresh comparison from the same initial tip temperature. The moving symbols show energy transfer along the handles.

<More title={{"en":"Comparing spoon tips","zh":"比较勺子远端"}}>

Each tip has a heat capacity of <Num s="S27">20 J/K</Num> in this model. Heat capacity tells you how much energy is needed for each degree of warming. The two transfer rates were chosen for comparison. Real spoon temperatures vary along the handle and depend on shape, heat capacity and heat lost to air.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"The bath stays fixed","zh":"温浴保持恒温"}}>

The bath receives or loses energy through an external source that keeps its temperature fixed. It is not a closed cup-and-spoons system. At equal temperatures, microscopic energy exchanges still occur, but there is no net heat transfer.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

两个勺碗浸在同一温浴里，形状和浸入深度相近。两根勺柄的远端从相同温度开始。外部来源维持浴温，让水与勺子交换能量时仍保持恒温。

<Term id="heat-transfer">热传递</Term>是能量从较热处传向较冷处。热水把能量沿勺柄传向较冷的远端。金属通常是<Term id="good-conductor">热的良导体</Term>，容易传热。在相同时刻，金属勺远端比塑料勺远端更暖。两个远端都在升温，只是塑料的较慢。

Temperatures are not measured in this illustration.","zh":"形状相近的两把勺子浸在同一温浴里。图中没有实测温度。"}} />

把浴温调得比远端初温更低，能量就从较暖远端传向较冷的水，金属勺远端冷却得较快。良导体可以较快升温，也可以较快降温；传热方向由温差决定。

温浴与远端从相同温度开始时，两者之间没有净传热，这叫<Term id="thermal-equilibrium">热平衡</Term>。比较时，要选相同时间和浴温。改变浴温后，比较会从相同远端初温重新开始。沿勺柄移动的符号表示能量传递。

<More title={{"en":"Comparing spoon tips","zh":"比较勺子远端"}}>

模型中，每个远端的热容量相同，都是 <Num s="S27">20 J/K</Num>。热容量表示升高一度需要多少能量。两条通路的传热快慢是比较用的设定。真实勺子沿柄有温差，变化还受形状、热容量和向空气散热的影响。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"The bath stays fixed","zh":"温浴保持恒温"}}>

温浴通过外部来源获得或失去能量，以保持恒温，所以这不是封闭的“杯水加勺子”系统。同温时，微观能量交换仍在进行，但没有净传热。

</More>
