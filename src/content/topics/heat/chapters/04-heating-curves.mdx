<Lang en>

The probe and graph show the same water temperature. A <Term id="heating-curve">heating curve</Term> shows how temperature changes with time. Time runs along the horizontal axis. Temperature runs up the vertical axis. Move the time control: the graph cursor and probe show the same instant.

Use grams to set the water’s <Term id="mass">mass</Term>, or how much matter it contains. <Term id="power">Power</Term> tells you how much energy is supplied each second. In this simulation, a <Num s="S27">100 W</Num> heater warms <Num s="S27">200 g</Num> of water from <Num s="S27">25 °C</Num>. After <Num s="S27">120 s</Num>, the water reaches about <Num s="S27">39.3 °C</Num>. Constant power gives a straight rising line here because the model leaves out heat loss.

Keep power and time the same, then double the water mass. The same energy heats more water, so the temperature rise is halved. Next, restore the original mass and reduce power. Less energy arrives each second, so the line rises more gently. Record these as separate comparisons. To find the temperature rise, subtract the starting temperature from the final temperature.

Switch off the input. The line becomes horizontal because this model has neither energy input nor heat loss. Real water can still exchange energy with its surroundings after a heater is switched off. Use the card to predict, reveal and record a chosen time. The graph shows calculated temperatures between your recorded observations; it does not add them to your table.

<More title={{"en":"The calculation behind the line","zh":"直线背后的计算"}}>

When there is no change of state and specific heat stays nearly constant, energy change equals mass × specific heat × temperature change. Specific heat is the energy needed to warm a unit mass by one degree. The model uses <Num s="S27">4.2 J/(g·K)</Num> for water and leaves out energy absorbed by the container. The formula explains the graph; you can compare the lines without calculating it.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Water level is illustrative","zh":"液位只是示意"}}>

Read water mass from the control. The drawn water level stays fixed. This model covers heating without changes of state, so it does not show a boiling plateau.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

探头和图线显示同一份水的温度。<Term id="heating-curve">加热曲线</Term>显示温度随时间怎样改变。横轴读时间，纵轴读温度。调节时间，图上的游标和探头便显示同一时刻的水温。

用克设定水的<Term id="mass">质量</Term>，也就是它含有多少物质。<Term id="power">功率</Term>表示每秒供给多少能量。在这个模拟中，<Num s="S27">100 W</Num> 的加热器把 <Num s="S27">200 g</Num> 水从 <Num s="S27">25 °C</Num> 加热。经过 <Num s="S27">120 s</Num>，水温约为 <Num s="S27">39.3 °C</Num>。功率不变，模型又忽略散热，所以图线是上升的直线。

保持功率和时间相同，把水量加倍。相同能量加热更多水，升温幅度便减半。再恢复原水量、降低功率，每秒输入的能量减少，图线就较平缓。把这些比较分别记录。要算升温多少，用末温减去初温。

关闭输入，图线便水平，因为这个模型既没有供能，也没有散热。真实杯水在关掉加热器后，仍会与环境交换能量。用卡片预测、揭示，再记录所选时刻。图线也显示各次记录之间算出的温度，但不会自动把它们加进观察表。

<More title={{"en":"The calculation behind the line","zh":"直线背后的计算"}}>

没有物态变化、比热近似不变时，能量变化等于质量、比热与温度变化的乘积。比热表示单位质量升高一度需要多少能量。模型把水的比热取为 <Num s="S27">4.2 J/(g·K)</Num>，忽略容器吸收的能量。公式用来解释图线；不计算公式，也能比较图线。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Water level is illustrative","zh":"液位只是示意"}}>

从控件读取水的质量，画面的水位保持不变。模型只显示没有物态变化的加热过程，所以没有沸腾时的水平段。

</More>

</Lang>
