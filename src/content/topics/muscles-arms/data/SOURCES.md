## Rig, poses and exercise display

The actual joints are trunk plus paired clavicle, scapula, shoulder, elbow, forearm and hand. Axes come from `arms/rig.json`, limited to movements appropriate to each joint. Radius and hand follow the forearm rotation joint; ulna follows the elbow. The hand joint sits at the wrist; a grip offset of 0.08 m places equipment in the palm. `wrist.l/.r` are anatomical ACTIONS aliases to `joint.hand.l/.r`, declared after `hand.*`. The engine writes a shared node in declaration order, so exercise hand keys also carry equal wrist keys; named wrist poses use the wrist alias. This supplies the existing inspector's wrist demonstrations without modifying the engine.

The overview's `neutral-stand` has comfortably bent elbows and partly pronated forearms; written origins and insertions use anatomical stance. Demos start from the current chapter pose, so elbow extension and supination can return toward straight/palm-forward from that starting pose. Shoulder/elbow demonstration angles are kept within the supplied mesh's conservative working range; all angles, timing, contractions, peel distances, cut levels and camera positions are DESIGN teaching choices, not measured patient ranges, force, EMG, or exercise targets. Contraction strips show activity, not physiological shortening percentages.

There are 18 contract animations, 12 named poses, two named cuts (`transverse-arm`, `sagittal-arm`) and six camera presets. The transverse cut crosses the upper arm; it does not claim to show omitted deep forearm structures. Fingers are not articulated: FDS and extensor digitorum's principal finger actions are explained in text; their available wrist action is explicitly secondary. The clavicle/scapula chain is rotational, not full physiological sliding.

Exercise cards use the supplied 11 sketch stems exactly. Keyframes omit absent hip/knee/ankle joints; forearm rotation no longer incorrectly targets the elbow. The chin-up has no fake moving bar: this rig gives a partial shoulder/elbow direction demonstration; the sketch and steps show the full fixed-bar/body-lift action. The overhead extension is displayed with reduced elevation to avoid unsupported overhead skin deformation; its card says the sketch shows the full position. Cable handles do not constitute a complete cable machine. Supported wrist work and self-resistance use the card/sketch to show support and hand contact; the 3D joint keys do not simulate contact forces. These are explicit display limits, not unresolved anatomical claims.

## Draft fact and technique review

- Preserve the original `triceps-brachii` shared card exactly for chest dependencies; its detailed head cards are added once here.
- Biceps inserts on radius and also supinates; brachialis inserts on ulna and remains a powerful elbow flexor regardless of grip; triceps long head starts below the glenoid and crosses the shoulder [M87, M63]. These are explicit in chapter 04.
- Palmaris longus reaches flexor retinaculum/palmar aponeurosis, not a hand bone; normal absence is stated without an unsupported prevalence number [M87].
- FDS primarily flexes proximal interphalangeal joints; extensor digitorum reaches extensor expansions. Wrist motion does not replace their finger actions [M87].
- Grip-related links for finger/wrist muscles are classified as coordination or stabilisation, without promising isolation or hypertrophy from curls [M72, M9]. Coracobrachialis curl/press links are similarly conditional on upper-arm movement.
- Correct triceps motion contraction maps from exercise identifiers to actual muscle ids; map pronation/supination to the real forearm joints and align grasp orientation with the anatomical rest pose. The mesh rest is palm-forward (supinated); neutral is near a quarter-turn and palm-down near a half-turn. Earlier draft-derived neutral/palm-down angles were too small. The final keys use shortened DESIGN pronation values of 80° for neutral and 150–160° for palm-down; the self-resisted turn runs 80° to 140°.
- Replace the unverified Haff/Triplett textbook attribution with actually read technique sources, including the different NSCA technique manual [M90]. Remove head-only FMA guesses for combined muscles and unsupported fibre-architecture precision. Extensor digitorum is unipennate [M91]; triceps heads use simplified longitudinal/converging descriptions from Gray [M87].
- Self-resisted pronation applies gentle resistance above the wrist, rather than twisting the hand [M71]. Technique guidance and AAOS injury information are general education, with the minor-supervision line and medical disclaimer on cards/chapters [M25, M73, M74].

## Attachment review (parts.json `sites`)

Every left-side candidate for all 18 region mesh muscles was reviewed against Gray 1918 [M87], Myology 7d (arm, pp. 443–446) and 7e (forearm, pp. 446–454), with modern OpenStax §11.5 [M63] as a cross-check. Gray uses "sublimis" for flexor digitorum superficialis. The terminal phalanx footprints were identified on the named individual source bones before projection onto the final merged hand mesh. All bone points use closest point on triangles, not nearest vertices. Native Blender currently crashes before executing Python; geometry was decoded read-only with @gltf-transform and meshopt, with rest-pose skin inverse-bind transforms applied. The cached assimp skeleton GLB was used only to distinguish the named hand bones, converting centimetres to metres and translating by arms/rig.json sourceCentre. This does not change any pipeline asset. Right-side sites are mirrored by the engine; no neighbour sites are fabricated.
