<Lang en>

The long forearm muscles transmit force through tendons near the elbow. “Tennis elbow” usually concerns the wrist-extensor tendons on the outer side, especially extensor carpi radialis brevis. Repeated wrist extension and gripping can be involved; playing tennis is not required. The tendon changes include degeneration, so the familiar name does not mean that inflammation alone explains every case [M73]. <FlyTo preset="close-arm">See the elbow attachments</FlyTo>

A different injury involves the distal biceps tendon on the radius. A sudden load can force a bent elbow straight while the biceps is trying to hold it. A pop, new bruising or weakness when turning the palm upward calls for prompt professional assessment [M74]. Other muscles may still bend the elbow, so being able to bend it does not rule out a biceps tendon injury.

The lowering part of a curl is an <Term id="eccentric-phase">eccentric phase</Term>: the muscle develops force while lengthening. Use a manageable load, lower smoothly and avoid trying to catch an unexpectedly heavy falling object [M23] [M74].

Posture changes which joint positions the muscles work through. Let the shoulder and wrist take comfortable positions, and avoid forcing a fixed grip that hurts. The long triceps head spans the shoulder, so raising the arm changes its length even before the elbow moves [M87]. A pose or an uneven-looking muscle alone cannot diagnose an injury.

This chapter is general information, not medical advice. For pain or an injury, consult a doctor or physiotherapist. Stop the exercise when sudden pain or new weakness appears; do not use the cards as a rehabilitation prescription.

<More title={{ en: "Pain is not a muscle-location test", zh: "疼痛不是定位肌肉的测试" }}>

The outside of the elbow contains several structures. A clinician considers the history and examines strength, movement and other possible causes; the coloured muscle and its attachment marker cannot establish a diagnosis [M73].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Gentle location, not forceful probing", zh: "轻轻定位，不用力按压" }}>

Finding a muscle means noticing where its belly lies during an easy movement. Do not dig into the elbow crease or repeatedly provoke pain to prove a tendon is involved. An acute pop with bruising or lost turning strength deserves medical assessment even if pain later eases [M74].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

前臂长肌经肘旁的肌腱传力。“网球肘”通常涉及肘外侧的腕伸肌腱，尤其桡侧腕短伸肌。反复伸腕和握持可能参与其发生，不打网球也会有。肌腱可出现退变，不能只凭名字把每一例都解释为单纯发炎 [M73]。<FlyTo preset="close-arm">看肘旁附着</FlyTo>

另一种伤在桡骨上的二头肌远端腱。二头肌试图维持屈肘时，突然的负荷可能把肘强行拉直。出现响声、新淤青或转掌向上无力，应尽快请专业人员评估 [M74]。其他肌肉仍可能屈肘，所以能弯手臂并不能排除这条腱受伤。

弯举的下放叫<Term id="eccentric-phase">离心阶段</Term>：肌肉一边用力一边变长。选能控制的重量，平稳下放，不突然接住超出预期的重物 [M23] [M74]。

姿态会改变肌肉工作的关节位置。肩和腕取舒服姿势，不硬扭进会痛的固定握法。三头肌长头跨肩，抬臂在屈伸肘之前就已改变它的长度 [M87]。单凭姿势或两侧外观差别，不能诊断损伤。

本章是一般性知识，不是医疗建议。疼痛或受伤请咨询医生或物理治疗师。突然痛或出现新的无力时应停下，不把动作卡当成康复处方。

练习后若功能发生新的变化，也要把具体经过告诉专业人员。模型只提供结构关系，不能替代病史询问与检查 [M74]。
