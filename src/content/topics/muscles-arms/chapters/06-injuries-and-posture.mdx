<Lang en>

The wrist extensors start together on the bony bump on the outside of the elbow. In "tennis elbow" the tendon at that attachment, most often that of **extensor carpi radialis brevis**, becomes painful. Despite the name, it is not limited to tennis players: repeated gripping and wrist extension at work, at home or in sport can all be involved [M73]. <FlyTo preset="close-arm">See the outer elbow</FlyTo>

A different injury affects the **distal biceps tendon** on the radius. It usually happens when a bent elbow is suddenly forced straight while biceps is pulling against a heavy load. Warning signs are a pop, new bruising at the front of the elbow and weakness when turning the palm up [M74]. Brachialis can still bend the elbow, so being able to bend it does not rule this injury out.

The lowering half of a curl is the <Term id="eccentric-phase">eccentric phase</Term>: the elbow flexors work while they lengthen. Lower under control with a load you can manage, and do not try to catch a falling weight [M23] [M74].

Posture changes the length a muscle works at. The long head of triceps crosses the shoulder, so lifting the arm overhead stretches it before the elbow moves [M87]. Let the shoulders and wrists take comfortable positions, and do not force a grip that hurts. A photograph of someone's arms, or one muscle that looks bigger than the other, cannot diagnose a problem.

This is general information, not medical advice. For pain or an injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist. Stop an exercise if sudden pain or new weakness appears.

<More title={{ en: "Why the model cannot diagnose", zh: "为什么模型不能诊断" }}>

Pain on the outer elbow can come from more than one structure. A clinician uses the history of the pain, an examination of strength and movement, and sometimes imaging. A coloured muscle and its attachment marker show where a tendon runs, not what is wrong with it [M73].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Feel gently", zh: "轻轻地找" }}>

To find a muscle on yourself, move the joint gently and notice where the arm changes shape. Do not dig into the elbow crease or keep pressing a sore spot to prove which tendon it is. A sudden pop with bruising or lost strength deserves a medical check even if the pain fades [M74].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

几块伸腕肌一起起于肘外侧的骨突。所谓“网球肘”，是这个附着处的肌腱疼痛，最常见的是**桡侧腕短伸肌**的肌腱。名字虽然叫网球肘，得的人并不限于打网球的：工作、家务或运动中反复抓握和伸腕，都可能与它有关 [M73]。<FlyTo preset="close-arm">看肘外侧</FlyTo>

另一种伤发生在桡骨上的**肱二头肌远端腱**。常见情形是：肱二头肌正对着重物用力，弯着的肘被突然拉直。警示信号是“啪”的一声、肘前方新出现的淤青，以及转掌朝上时没力 [M74]。肱肌照样能屈肘，所以手臂还能弯，并不能排除这种损伤。

弯举往下放的那一半叫<Term id="eccentric-phase">离心阶段</Term>：屈肘肌一边用力一边被拉长。用自己能控制的重量慢慢放，掉下来的重物不要去硬接 [M23] [M74]。

体态会改变肌肉工作时的长度。肱三头肌长头跨过肩关节，手臂举过头顶时，肘还没动它就已经被拉长 [M87]。肩和腕摆在舒服的位置，会痛的握法不要硬撑。一张手臂照片，或者一侧肌肉看起来比另一侧大，都不能用来诊断问题。

本页是一般性知识，不是医疗建议。疼痛或受伤，请咨询医生或物理治疗师。练习中出现突然的疼痛或新的无力，就停下来。

<More title={{ en: "Why the model cannot diagnose", zh: "为什么模型不能诊断" }}>

肘外侧的疼痛可能来自不止一种结构。医生要结合疼痛的经过、力量和活动的检查，有时还要做影像检查。彩色的肌肉和附着标记只说明肌腱走在哪里，说明不了它出了什么问题 [M73]。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Feel gently", zh: "轻轻地找" }}>

在自己身上找肌肉，轻轻活动关节，留意手臂哪里在变形就行。不要用力按肘窝，也不要反复按压痛点来确认是哪条肌腱。突然“啪”的一声并伴随淤青或无力，即使后来不痛了，也应该去检查 [M74]。

</More>
