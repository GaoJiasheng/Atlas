<Lang en>

Fade the two heads of biceps and **brachialis** appears, lying against the lower half of the front of the humerus. It ends on the ulna, just below the elbow joint [M87]. That one fact decides its job. When you turn your palm up or down, the radius rolls around the ulna while the ulna stays put. So brachialis bends the elbow whichever way your hand faces: **in a palm-up, palm-down or thumb-up grip, brachialis is a main elbow flexor** [M87] [M63]. It is a separate muscle, not a smaller copy of biceps.

High on the inner arm, **coracobrachialis** runs from the coracoid process to the middle of the humerus. It helps raise the arm forward and draw it towards the body. Because it ends on the humerus, it does not act on the elbow [M87]. <FlyTo preset="left-side">See the side of the arm</FlyTo>

Behind the humerus, the **medial head of triceps** is mostly covered by the long and lateral heads; near the elbow it appears beside their shared tendon. **Anconeus** is a small triangle between the outer elbow and the ulna that helps straighten the elbow [M87].

In the forearm, **extensor carpi radialis brevis** lies under the long radial extensor and ends on the third metacarpal. **Flexor digitorum superficialis** sends <Num s="M87">four</Num> tendons to the fingers. Each tendon splits and ends on the middle bone of a finger, so its main job is to bend the middle joints of the fingers [M87].

<More title={{ en: "Tendons that pass through each other", zh: "相互穿过的肌腱" }}>

Each superficialis tendon splits in two, and the tendon of the deeper finger flexor, flexor digitorum profundus, passes through the gap to reach the fingertip bone. The deep muscle is not part of this model [M87].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What is left out", zh: "模型省略了什么" }}>

This model stops at the middle layer. The deep forearm muscles that bend the fingertips and thumb, the supinator that helps biceps turn the palm up and pronator quadratus near the wrist are not drawn. The layers are a viewing order, not a claim that the forearm has only these muscles.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

把肱二头肌的两个头调淡，**肱肌**就露出来了，它贴在肱骨下半段的前面，止于尺骨，紧挨在肘关节下方 [M87]。这一点决定了它的作用：手掌朝上或朝下转动时，是桡骨绕着尺骨转，尺骨本身不动。所以不管手朝哪个方向，肱肌都在屈肘——**掌心朝上、朝下还是拇指朝上，肱肌都是屈肘的主力** [M87] [M63]。它是一块独立的肌肉，不是缩小版的肱二头肌。

上臂内侧高处是**喙肱肌**，从喙突长到肱骨中段，帮助把手臂向前抬、向身体靠。它止在肱骨上，所以管不到肘关节 [M87]。<FlyTo preset="left-side">看手臂侧面</FlyTo>

肱骨后面，**肱三头肌内侧头**大部分被长头和外侧头盖住，到靠近肘的地方才在共同肌腱旁露出来。**肘肌**是肘外侧和尺骨之间的一个小三角，帮助伸肘 [M87]。

前臂里，**桡侧腕短伸肌**藏在桡侧腕长伸肌下面，止于第三掌骨。**指浅屈肌**发出<Num s="M87">四条</Num>肌腱通向手指，每条肌腱分叉后止于手指的中节指骨，所以它主要负责弯曲手指中间那个关节 [M87]。

<More title={{ en: "Tendons that pass through each other", zh: "相互穿过的肌腱" }}>

每条指浅屈肌腱都一分为二，更深的指深屈肌腱从中间的缝里穿过去，一直到指尖的骨。指深屈肌没有画进这个模型 [M87]。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What is left out", zh: "模型省略了什么" }}>

模型只画到中层。前臂深层负责弯指尖和拇指的肌肉、帮助肱二头肌让掌心朝上的旋后肌，以及腕附近的旋前方肌，都没有画出来。分层只是观察的顺序，并不是说前臂只有这些肌肉。

</More>

</Lang>
