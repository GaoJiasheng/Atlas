<Lang en>

The model shows a man's arms, built from a real anatomical scan. The arm muscles are drawn in warm colours, the bones in ivory, and the neighbouring shoulder, chest and back muscles in grey. Left and right always mean the body's own left and right.

The upper arm has two big muscles. In front is **biceps brachii**, with <Num s="M87">two</Num> heads; behind is **triceps brachii**, with <Num s="M87">three</Num>. Under biceps lies **brachialis**, and high on the inner arm **coracobrachialis**. The forearm holds the muscles that bend and straighten the wrist and fingers. Their fleshy parts sit near the elbow; long tendons carry their pull into the hand [M87].

The bones explain the layout. The shoulder blade anchors the muscles that cross the shoulder. The humerus is the upper-arm bone. Below the elbow, the radius lies on the thumb side and the ulna on the little-finger side. When you turn your palm, the radius rolls around the ulna and carries the hand with it [M87].

Every muscle has an <Term id="origin">origin</Term>, the end that usually stays still, and an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term>, the end it pulls. A <Term id="tendon">tendon</Term> joins the fleshy <Term id="muscle-belly">muscle belly</Term> to bone. A muscle can only pull: it shortens and draws its insertion towards its origin. Hollow markers show origins, solid markers insertions. <FlyTo preset="front">See the front</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Where this model comes from", zh: "这个模型从哪里来" }}>

The meshes come from Z-Anatomy, an open atlas built on BodyParts3D, which was made from MRI scans of one adult male volunteer [M4]. Atlas selected the arm structures, simplified them and added fibre directions and attachment points. The model and its geometry are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 [M3].

Only the surface and middle layers are shown. The deep forearm muscles, such as the deep finger flexor, the supinator and pronator quadratus, are left out of this model and only mentioned in the text.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the hands face inward", zh: "为什么手掌朝内" }}>

Anatomy books describe the body standing with the palms facing forward. The model starts with relaxed elbows and the palms turned towards the thighs, the way most people stand. The written origins and insertions still follow the textbook position.

</More>

<Lang zh>

舞台上是一名男性的双臂，根据真实的解剖扫描数据建成。手臂肌肉画成暖色，骨骼是象牙色，肩、胸和背部的邻近肌肉画成灰色。文中说的左和右，一律指身体自己的左右。

上臂有两块大肌肉：前面是**肱二头肌**，有<Num s="M87">两个</Num>头；后面是**肱三头肌**，有<Num s="M87">三个</Num>头。肱二头肌下面藏着**肱肌**，上臂内侧高处还有**喙肱肌**。前臂是屈伸腕和手指的肌肉，它们的肌腹集中在肘附近，再由细长的肌腱把拉力送到手上 [M87]。

骨骼决定了它们的排列。肩胛骨是跨过肩关节的肌肉的起点；上臂里只有一根肱骨。肘以下有两根骨：拇指侧是桡骨，小指侧是尺骨。转动手掌时，是桡骨绕着尺骨转，手跟着一起转 [M87]。

每块肌肉都有<Term id="origin">起点</Term>，通常是不动的那一端；还有<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>，是它拉动的那一端。<Term id="tendon">肌腱</Term>把肉质的<Term id="muscle-belly">肌腹</Term>连到骨上。肌肉只会拉：它缩短，把止点拉向起点。空心圆标起点，实心圆标止点。<FlyTo preset="front">看正面</FlyTo>
