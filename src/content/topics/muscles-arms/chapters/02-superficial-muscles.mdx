<Lang en>

Start with **biceps brachii** on the front of the upper arm. Its long head begins just above the shoulder socket, and its tendon runs through the shoulder joint. The short head begins on the <Term id="coracoid-process">coracoid process</Term>, the small hook at the front of the shoulder blade. The two bellies join above the elbow into one tendon on the radius [M87]. Bend your elbow against your other hand and the front of the arm hardens.

Behind the arm is **triceps brachii**. Its long head begins on the shoulder blade just below the socket, so it crosses the shoulder as well as the elbow. The lateral head begins on the back of the humerus. Both join the deeper medial head in one tendon on the <Term id="olecranon">olecranon</Term>, the point of the elbow [M87]. <FlyTo preset="back">See the back of the arm</FlyTo>

On the thumb side, **brachioradialis** runs from the ridge above the outer elbow to the lower end of the radius. It crosses the elbow but not the wrist, so it bends the elbow. **Pronator teres** runs diagonally from the inner elbow to the middle of the radius and turns the palm down [M87].

Most of the forearm's surface belongs to the wrist and finger muscles. On the palm side, **flexor carpi radialis**, **palmaris longus** and **flexor carpi ulnaris** start together on the inner bump of the elbow and bend the wrist. On the back, **extensor carpi radialis longus**, **extensor carpi ulnaris** and **extensor digitorum** start around the outer bump and straighten the wrist; extensor digitorum also opens the fingers [M87] [M63]. Move your wrist gently and you can feel the forearm change shape near the elbow, where the bellies are.

<More title={{ en: "A muscle not everyone has", zh: "不是人人都有的肌肉" }}>

Palmaris longus is missing in some people, as a normal variation rather than an injury. Its tendon does not reach a bone: it spreads into the flexor retinaculum, the band across the front of the wrist, and the palmar aponeurosis, the tough sheet under the skin of the palm [M87]. Its insertion marker therefore sits in front of the wrist bones, not on them.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where the wrist tendons end", zh: "腕肌腱止在哪里" }}>

Each wrist muscle ends on a particular bone. Flexor carpi radialis reaches the palm side of the second metacarpal. The long and short radial extensors reach the backs of the second and third. Extensor carpi ulnaris ends on the fifth, and flexor carpi ulnaris on the pisiform, a pea-sized wrist bone [M87].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

先看上臂前面的**肱二头肌**。长头起于肩关节盂的正上方，肌腱从肩关节里面穿过；短头起于<Term id="coracoid-process">喙突</Term>，也就是肩胛骨前面那个小钩。两个肌腹在肘上方合成一条肌腱，止于桡骨 [M87]。用另一只手按住前臂，用力屈肘，上臂前面就会变硬。

上臂后面是**肱三头肌**。长头起于肩胛骨、关节盂的下方，所以它既跨肘也跨肩；外侧头起于肱骨后面。它们和较深的内侧头合成一条肌腱，止于<Term id="olecranon">鹰嘴</Term>，也就是肘尖 [M87]。<FlyTo preset="back">看上臂后面</FlyTo>

拇指侧的**肱桡肌**从肘外侧上方的骨嵴一直长到桡骨下端。它跨肘不跨腕，所以作用是屈肘。**旋前圆肌**从肘内侧斜着长到桡骨中段，让掌心转向下 [M87]。

前臂表面大部分是屈伸腕和手指的肌肉。掌侧的**桡侧腕屈肌**、**掌长肌**和**尺侧腕屈肌**一起起于肘内侧的骨突，负责屈腕。背侧的**桡侧腕长伸肌**、**尺侧腕伸肌**和**指伸肌**起于肘外侧骨突附近，负责伸腕；指伸肌还能伸开手指 [M87] [M63]。轻轻活动手腕，能摸到靠近肘的前臂在变形，那里就是肌腹。

<More title={{ en: "A muscle not everyone has", zh: "不是人人都有的肌肉" }}>

有些人天生没有掌长肌，这是正常变异，不是受伤。它的肌腱不止于骨，而是散开到屈肌支持带（横跨腕前方的一条带）和掌腱膜（手掌皮下那层坚韧的膜）[M87]。所以它的止点标记画在腕骨前方，而不是骨面上。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where the wrist tendons end", zh: "腕肌腱止在哪里" }}>

每块腕部肌肉都止在一块确定的骨上：桡侧腕屈肌到第二掌骨底的掌侧；桡侧腕长、短伸肌分别到第二、第三掌骨底的背侧；尺侧腕伸肌到第五掌骨底；尺侧腕屈肌到豌豆骨，一块豌豆大小的腕骨 [M87]。

</More>
