<Lang en>

The index contains the whole arm exercise menu, grouped by equipment: barbell, dumbbell, body weight, then cable. Open a card for its two-panel sketch, setup, detailed steps, cues and common errors. Orange marks prime movers; blue-grey marks helpers. These are anatomical roles rather than measurements of muscle activity.

With a **barbell**, close-grip bench press loads elbow extension alongside the chest's pressing work. Barbell curl bends the elbow with palms up; reverse curl keeps them down. Changing grip changes the contribution of the flexors, but brachialis still bends the elbow [M87].

With **dumbbells**, the supinating curl adds palm turning and the hammer curl keeps the palms facing inward. Overhead triceps extension changes the long head's length at the shoulder while all heads extend the elbow. Supported wrist curl and wrist extension train movement at the wrist. Keep the forearm supported and let the hand move smoothly [M87].

The **body-weight** options are chin-up and self-resisted pronation. A chin-up involves the back and shoulder as well as the elbow flexors; it is not simply a hanging curl. Self-resistance means the other hand supplies gentle opposing force. The **cable pushdown** loads elbow extension with the upper arms kept steady.

Choose movements you can control and increase resistance gradually [M23]. Begin with light loads and learn from a qualified coach; people under <Num s="M25">18</Num> need appropriate adult supervision [M25]. Higher surface <Term id="emg">EMG</Term> measures do not establish superior muscle growth [M9]. This is a menu to learn from, not a requirement to do every exercise.

<More title={{ en: "A learning session", zh: "一节学习课" }}>

One possible sequence is a comfortable compound pull or press, then a light curl and triceps movement, followed by supported wrist work if the grip is still fresh. Choose rather than collect every option. Multi-joint work generally precedes smaller single-joint work; progression depends on control, recovery and previous training [M23] [M25].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Holding a grip is not the same as training a finger action", zh: "握住器械不等于直接练手指动作" }}>

A curl requires the fingers to hold the implement, while wrist muscles help keep it steady. This does not make every gripping muscle a prime mover of the curl or prove that the exercise directly trains finger extension. The finger muscles’ links distinguish a wrist contribution or grip demand from their main finger actions [M87] [M72].

本章索引收齐手臂动作菜单，按器械排列：杠铃、哑铃、徒手，最后是绳索。点开卡片，可看两格示意图、准备、详细步骤、提示和常见错误。橙色是主动肌，蓝灰色是协同肌，表示解剖分工，不是肌肉活动的实测值。

**杠铃**窄握卧推在胸肌推的同时给伸肘加负荷。弯举掌心向上，正握弯举掌心向下。握法改变屈肌的贡献，但肱肌始终负责屈肘 [M87]。

**哑铃**旋后弯举加入转掌，锤式弯举保持掌心朝内。过顶臂屈伸改变长头在肩部的长度，各头仍一起伸肘。支撑前臂做屈腕和伸腕，让手平稳活动 [M87]。

**徒手**选项是反握引体向上与自我抗阻旋前。引体同时用到背部、肩部和屈肘肌，不是悬挂着做弯举；自我抗阻是另一只手施加温和阻力。**绳索下压**保持上臂稳定，让伸肘承担负荷。

选能控制的动作，逐步增加阻力 [M23]。先用轻负荷，在合格教练指导下学习；未满 <Num s="M25">18 岁</Num>需适当成人监督 [M25]。表面<Term id="emg">肌电</Term>更高，不证明肌肉长得更多 [M9]。这是学习菜单，不要求一次做完。

动作卡上的感觉位置用来帮助理解，不能当作必须酸痛的目标。若握不稳、躯干开始借力或下放失去控制，就应调整负荷。器械分类便于查找，不等于每个人都必须照顺序练。

<More title={{ en: "A learning session", zh: "一节学习课" }}>

一种可选顺序：先做能控制的复合拉或推，再做轻负荷弯举与伸肘；握持仍稳时，才加支撑腕练习。选择合适动作，不把所有选项堆起来。通常先多关节、后单关节；进阶要看动作控制、恢复和已有训练 [M23] [M25]。
