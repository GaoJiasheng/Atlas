<Lang en>

The list at the end of this chapter has all eleven exercises, grouped by equipment: barbell, dumbbell, body weight and cable. Press one and the model takes up the exercise, the prime movers turn orange and the helpers blue-grey, and its card opens at the top of this panel with a sketch, the steps, cues, common mistakes and cautions. The first time, start with body weight or a light load and learn the movement from a qualified coach; anyone under <Num s="M25">eighteen</Num> should train with an adult supervising [M25].

The exercises follow the jobs from the last chapter. **Curls** bend the elbow. The palm-up barbell curl shares the work between biceps, brachialis and brachioradialis; the hammer curl, with thumbs up, and the reverse curl, with palms down, lean more on brachialis and brachioradialis. **Brachialis works in all of them** [M87] [M63]. The supinating curl adds biceps' second job, turning the palm up. **Triceps work** straightens the elbow: the close-grip bench press, the cable pushdown and the overhead extension, which starts with the long head stretched because that head also crosses the shoulder. The **wrist curl**, **wrist extension** and **self-resisted pronation** train the forearm muscles directly. The **chin-up** is a back exercise as much as an arm exercise.

No single arm exercise suits everyone. Surface <Term id="emg">EMG</Term> measures how active a muscle is, and higher EMG is not proof that a muscle grows more [M9]. Presses and pulls for the chest and back already work the arms, so choose extra arm exercises you can do with good form and recover from.

A few principles hold for all of them. Adults are advised to do muscle-strengthening activities on <Num s="M22">two or more days a week</Num>; Singapore's guidelines say the same [M26]. Lower the weight under control, keep the wrists in line with the forearms, and add load in small steps only once the movement is easy [M23]. This is general information, not medical advice: for pain or an injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

<More title={{ en: "A sample session", zh: "一节训练课示例" }}>

One way to arrange an arm session, not a prescription: an easy forearm warm-up with self-resisted pronation, assisted chin-ups while fresh, close-grip bench presses with safety bars or a spotter, then lighter curls and pushdowns, and supported wrist work at the end while the grip is still secure. Multi-joint exercises come first and single-joint ones later [M23] [M25].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What the model leaves out", zh: "模型省略了什么" }}>

This model has no legs and no moving trunk, so whole-body movements are shown as the arm part only. In the chin-up the arms move but the body does not rise to the bar; in the overhead extension the arm is raised less than in real life, to keep the shoulder mesh from tearing. The sketch on each card shows the full movement.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the card has no sets and repetitions", zh: "卡片上为什么没有组数和次数" }}>

How much to do depends on the person, their goal and their training so far. These cards give principles only. A coach can turn them into a plan; for children and teenagers that plan should be designed and supervised by a qualified professional [M25].

</More>

</Lang>

本章末尾的列表收齐十一个动作，按器械分组：杠铃、哑铃、自重和绳索。点一个，模型就摆出这个动作，主动肌变成橙色，协同肌变成蓝灰色；动作卡在这个面板顶部打开，有示意图、步骤、要点、常见错误和注意事项。第一次做，先用自重或轻重量，跟合格的教练学动作；未满<Num s="M25">十八岁</Num>的人应在成人监督下练习 [M25]。

这些动作对应上一章讲的分工。**弯举**负责屈肘：掌心朝上的杠铃弯举由肱二头肌、肱肌和肱桡肌分担；拇指朝上的锤式弯举和掌心朝下的正握弯举，更多靠肱肌和肱桡肌。**每一种弯举里都有肱肌** [M87] [M63]。旋后弯举再加上肱二头肌的第二项任务：让掌心朝上。**练肱三头肌**就是伸肘：窄握卧推、绳索下压，以及过顶臂屈伸——长头跨过肩关节，手臂举过头时它一开始就被拉长。**腕弯举**、**伸腕**和**自我抗阻旋前**直接练前臂肌肉。**反握引体向上**练背的分量不比练手臂少。

没有哪个手臂动作适合所有人。表面<Term id="emg">肌电</Term>测的是肌肉有多活跃，肌电高不等于肌肉长得更多 [M9]。练胸和练背时的推和拉已经用到手臂，额外的手臂动作，选你能做标准、也恢复得过来的。

有几条原则对所有动作都适用。成年人每周至少<Num s="M22">两天</Num>做增强肌肉的练习；新加坡的指南也这样建议 [M26]。有控制地放下重量，手腕和前臂保持一条直线；动作做得轻松了，再小步加重 [M23]。本页是一般性知识，不是医疗建议；疼痛或受伤，请咨询医生或物理治疗师。

<More title={{ en: "A sample session", zh: "一节训练课示例" }}>

一种手臂训练课的安排，不是处方：先用自我抗阻旋前轻松热身前臂；精力充沛时做辅助引体向上；接着在保护杠或保护者协助下做窄握卧推；然后做较轻的弯举和下压；握力还稳时，最后做支撑式腕部练习。先多关节动作，后单关节动作 [M23] [M25]。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What the model leaves out", zh: "模型省略了什么" }}>

这个模型没有腿，躯干也不动，所以全身动作只演示手臂那部分。引体向上里手臂在动，身体却不会升到杠前；过顶臂屈伸里手臂举得比实际低，以免肩部网格被拉裂。每张卡上的示意图画的是完整动作。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the card has no sets and repetitions", zh: "卡片上为什么没有组数和次数" }}>

练多少，取决于这个人、他的目标和已有的训练。这些卡片只写原则。教练可以把原则变成计划；儿童和青少年的计划应由合格的专业人员设计和监督 [M25]。

</More>
