<Lang en>

The coast is the edge of the land. A plate has a different edge. The <Term id="term-lithosphere">lithosphere</Term> consists of the crust and the rigid uppermost mantle. A <Term id="term-tectonic-plate">tectonic plate</Term> is a moving part of this outer shell, and may carry both land and ocean floor. A <Term id="term-boundary">plate boundary</Term> is a region where neighbouring plates move relative to one another. It need not follow a continent's outline.

This atlas starts at Tambora on Sumbawa on 10 April 1815. Its historical dates move forward, but the PB2002 plate model and modern coast remain reference geography. They do not reconstruct the islands as they looked on each event day. Slow plate motion does not justify making continents slide visibly across this short historical timeline.

The volcano points come from a modern <Term id="term-holocene-catalogue">Holocene volcano catalogue</Term>. They locate catalogue volcanoes; they do not say that all those volcanoes are erupting. Earthquake points are different again. An <Term id="term-epicentre">epicentre</Term> is the surface point above the place where earthquake rupture begins. It cannot show the full underground rupture.

Use the layer names and their reference notes before comparing points. Historical disaster markers open accounts of particular events. Observation points show where evidence was recorded. Sources explain what was counted, when a record was compiled and what is missing. An empty part of this map is therefore a limit of this selection, rather than proof that nothing happened there.

<More title={{ en: "Why use modern reference lines?", zh: "为什么使用现代参考线？" }}>

Modern datasets provide consistent locations for comparison. PB2002 describes a model of relative plate motion; Natural Earth supplies the coast. Neither supplies the historical outline of Tambora or Krakatau. Switching a layer changes the evidence being displayed, not the shape of the past.

</More>

<Lang zh>

海岸线标出陆地的边缘，板块的边缘却不一定在那里。地壳和上地幔最上部的刚性部分合称<Term id="term-lithosphere">岩石圈</Term>。<Term id="term-tectonic-plate">构造板块</Term>是岩石圈中相对运动的一块，可以同时承载陆地与海底。相邻板块相对运动的地区，就是<Term id="term-boundary">板块边界</Term>；它不等于大陆的轮廓。

这张图从 1815 年 4 月 10 日、松巴哇岛上的坦博拉开始。历史日期向前走，PB2002 板块模型和现代海岸却一直作为参考。它们不是各个年份的岛岸复原，也没有显示大陆在这段公历时间中大幅漂移。读事件之前，先分清背景资料和当时发生的事。

火山点来自现代的<Term id="term-holocene-catalogue">全新世火山目录</Term>，表示收录的位置，不表示每座火山都在喷发。地震点另有含义：<Term id="term-epicentre">震中</Term>是地震破裂开始位置正上方的地表点，不能代表地下整段破裂。历史灾害与相关观测也各有符号。

叠看图层时，要先读名称、版本与范围说明。事件标记连到具体事件，观测点连到记录位置；点开来源，可以知道数字统计了什么、资料何时编成。图上的空白可能只是没有采用相应记录，不能据此说那里没有发生过事件。带着这些限制，再沿年代往下读。

<More title={{ en: "Why use modern reference lines?", zh: "为什么使用现代参考线？" }}>

现代资料让各章有一致的定位依据。PB2002 表达板块相对运动的模型，Natural Earth 提供海岸。它们都不提供坦博拉或喀拉喀托的历史岛形。开关图层，是换一种证据来比较，不是让过去的海岸改变形状。

</More>
