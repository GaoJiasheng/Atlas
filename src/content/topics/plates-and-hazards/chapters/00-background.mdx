<Lang en>

The coast marks the edge of the land. A plate has a different edge. The <Term id="term-lithosphere">lithosphere</Term> is Earth's crust and the rigid rock at the top of the mantle. A <Term id="term-tectonic-plate">tectonic plate</Term> is a moving piece of this outer shell. It can carry both land and ocean floor. Neighbouring plates meet at a <Term id="term-boundary">plate boundary</Term>, which may cross land or sea.

Start at Tambora on Sumbawa on 10 April 1815. Then follow the dates to later events. The PB2002 plate lines and Natural Earth coast stay in place as modern guides. They do not show the islands' historical shapes. Plates move slowly, so continents would not visibly slide across the map during these years.

The volcano points come from a modern <Term id="term-holocene-catalogue">Holocene volcano catalogue</Term>, a collection of volcano records. A point shows a volcano's location, not whether it was erupting on the date shown. Earthquake points mark an <Term id="term-epicentre">epicentre</Term>: the surface point above where the underground break began. The break can extend far beyond that point.

Click a disaster marker to read about an event. Click an observation point to see where evidence was recorded. The layer names explain what each group of points means. Sources tell you what was counted and when the records were collected. Gaps may mean records are missing; they do not prove that nothing happened.

<More title={{ en: "Modern lines as a guide", zh: "用现代地图定位" }}>

PB2002 describes relative plate motion. Natural Earth supplies the coast. These datasets give us consistent locations for comparing events, including Tambora and Krakatau. They do not reconstruct those islands' past coastlines.

</More>

<Lang zh>

海岸线标出陆地的边缘。板块的边缘却不一定在那里。地壳和上地幔最上部的坚硬岩石合称<Term id="term-lithosphere">岩石圈</Term>。<Term id="term-tectonic-plate">构造板块</Term>是岩石圈中移动的一块，可以同时承载陆地与海底。相邻板块相接的地区叫<Term id="term-boundary">板块边界</Term>，可能经过陆地，也可能穿过海洋。

先找到松巴哇岛上的坦博拉，再从 1815 年 4 月 10 日沿年代往下读。日期改变时，PB2002 板块线和 Natural Earth 海岸仍留在原位，作为现代的定位参考。它们没有复原当年的岛岸。板块移动缓慢，在这段历史中，大陆不会在图上明显滑动。

火山点来自现代的<Term id="term-holocene-catalogue">全新世火山目录</Term>，也就是一份火山资料集。一个点标出火山位置，不表示当天正在喷发。地震点标出<Term id="term-epicentre">震中</Term>：地下破裂开始处正上方的地表点。地下破裂可以延伸到远处，范围比一个点大得多。

点开灾害标记，可以读具体事件；点开观测点，可以看证据在哪里留下。先读图层名称，就能分清这些点的用途。来源还会说明数字统计了什么、记录何时编成。图上的空白可能只是缺少记录，不能据此判断那里没有发生过灾害。

<More title={{ en: "Modern lines as a guide", zh: "用现代地图定位" }}>

PB2002 描述板块的相对运动，Natural Earth 提供海岸。它们让坦博拉、喀拉喀托等事件可以在同一套地图上比较，但没有复原这些岛屿过去的海岸。

</More>
