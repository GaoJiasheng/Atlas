The final view contains the complete selected earthquake sample for 2000–2024: <Num s="S604">3,729</Num> reviewed catalogue events with magnitude at least <Num s="S604">6</Num>. These are counts of a frozen selection, collected on 26 October 2026. They are not all earthquakes, a uniform set of moment magnitudes, or a measure of future disaster probability. Older historical examples were selected for a different purpose, so their sparse records cannot establish an increase in earthquake frequency.

Compare the boundary lines, volcano locations and earthquake points. Many patterns can be seen near plate edges, but there are exceptions. Kilauea lies within an oceanic plate. A model boundary is not a precise local fault map, and a space without catalogue points is not a promise of safety. Every layer answers a limited question.

The <Term id="term-ring-of-fire">Ring of Fire</Term> is a popular regional name whose geographical scope varies. MSS broadly includes Indonesia; GVP's stated Pacific-basin definition excludes most Indonesian volcanoes along the Australia–Sunda boundary. This is a difference of definition. The name does not identify one connected volcanic structure or a shared magma reservoir, so no invented ring is drawn around the points.

Singapore belongs in this wider regional view. Being away from active boundaries reduces some exposure without removing distant effects. Return to the 2004 event to see how an impact total keeps its definition and source date even when the map date changes. Useful conclusions come from comparing sources, versions and gaps. Exact earthquake prediction and current public warnings do not come from this historical teaching map.

MSS's broad regional description includes Indonesia. GVP excludes most volcanoes along the Australia–Sunda boundary. Its v5.4.0 FAQ reports <Num s="S11">687</Num> of <Num s="S11">1,214</Num> Holocene catalogue volcanoes, or <Num s="S11">57%</Num>, within its definition, checked for the final specification on 27 October 2026. This is a versioned published statistic, not a spatial count made from an invented polygon on this map.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why catalogue counts can change", zh: "目录数量为什么会变？" }}>

New observations, reviews and changed definitions can alter a catalogue. This atlas freezes its input bytes and dates so its selection can be checked. The modern volcano catalogue does not turn into a list of volcanoes erupting at every date on the timeline.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A gap in evidence", zh: "证据中的空白" }}>

Records without usable coordinates are kept in the data audit but cannot become map points. Historical observation sites are not a complete survey of every coast. Missing values mean “not recorded”, rather than zero. These limits rule out drawing an inundation surface from the dots.

</More>

把板块线、火山与震中叠看，边界附近可以看到许多分布，也有例外。基拉韦厄就在大洋板块内部。模型边界不是本地精细断层图，没有目录点的地方也不等于安全区。每一层都只能回答有范围的问题。

<Term id="term-ring-of-fire">环太平洋火山带</Term>是通俗地区名称，常简称“火环”，范围因资料而异。MSS 广义地包含印尼，GVP 的太平洋盆地定义则排除澳大利亚—巽他边界上的多数印尼火山。这是定义不同，不代表它们共用一个岩浆库，地图也不另外画一个圈来凑成火环。
