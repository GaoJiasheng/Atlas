<Lang en>

Krakatau lies in the Sunda Strait, between Sumatra and Java. On 27 August 1883, a volcanic disaster there generated destructive sea waves. A <Term id="term-tsunami">tsunami</Term> is a series of long waves caused by a sudden displacement of water. Earthquakes often cause tsunamis. Volcanic activity and landslides can also push water aside and start these waves.

The eruption had begun earlier. GVP's episode record starts on 20 May 1883. August's major disaster was one event within that longer period of activity. The modern coast helps us find Krakatau, but the island's shape changed during the disaster. The map does not reconstruct its coast before or after the eruption.

A USGS historical table reports about <Num s="S21">36,000</Num> deaths, mainly from tsunamis. Most people in this estimate died from the waves rather than lava. The more detailed historical estimate appears below. Both figures describe the same disaster; the rounded figure makes its scale easier to read.

Turn on the associated observation layer. Its points show where evidence of the 1883 tsunami was recorded. They do not trace the edge of flooding or show every affected settlement. Gaps may reflect missing records. Nearby Anak Krakatau caused another tsunami in 2018. That event began differently and has its own set of observations, which we will explore later.

<More title={{ en: "The historical death estimate", zh: "历史死亡估计" }}>

The USGS table lists <Num s="S21">36,417</Num> deaths, mainly from tsunamis. This is the detailed estimate behind “about <Num s="S21">36,000</Num>”. The table is undated and was read on 27 October 2026. NCEI also has death counts, but its fields cover different groups and must not be added together.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Reading the observation points", zh: "怎样读观测点？" }}>

The NCEI records use different measurement types. Each point keeps its original position and type code. Heights are not shown because the types have not all been checked. All points appear on the source event's date; this does not mean the waves reached them together.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Krakatau and Anak Krakatau", zh: "喀拉喀托与喀拉喀托之子" }}>

The 1883 disaster and the collapse at Anak Krakatau on 22 December 2018 happened near one another. Their causes, dates and human losses belong to separate events, each with its own sources.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

喀拉喀托（Krakatau）位于苏门答腊和爪哇之间的巽他海峡。1883 年 8 月 27 日，那里发生的火山灾害引起破坏性的海浪。<Term id="term-tsunami">海啸</Term>是海水突然被推移后形成的一系列长波。地震是常见起因；火山活动和滑坡也能推动海水，引发海啸。

喷发其实更早就开始了。GVP 的喷发活动记录从 1883 年 5 月 20 日开始，8 月的重大灾害发生在这段较长的活动期间。现代海岸帮助我们找到喀拉喀托，但岛形在灾害中发生了变化。图上没有复原喷发前后的海岸。

USGS 的历史表记载，灾害造成约 <Num s="S21">36,000</Num> 人死亡，主要由于海啸。这个估计中的多数人死于海浪，并非熔岩。下面的细看列出较细的历史估计。两种写法指同一次灾害，取整后的数字更方便读者理解其规模。

打开相关观测层，可以看到 1883 年海啸留下记录的位置。这些点没有画出淹没边界，也没有列出所有受灾聚落；点与点之间的空白，可能只是缺少记录。附近的喀拉喀托之子在 2018 年也引发了海啸。那一次的起因不同，也有自己的一组观测，后面会继续介绍。喷发推动海水，随后形成海浪，是相连却不同的过程。

<More title={{ en: "The historical death estimate", zh: "历史死亡估计" }}>

USGS 表列出 <Num s="S21">36,417</Num> 人死亡，主要由于海啸，正文取整为约 <Num s="S21">36,000</Num> 人。该表未注明发布日期，读取日为 2026 年 10 月 27 日。NCEI 也有死亡数据，但不同字段统计的范围不同，不能相加。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Reading the observation points", zh: "怎样读观测点？" }}>

NCEI 的记录采用不同测量类型。每个点保留原位置与类型代码。由于类型尚未全部核对，这里不显示高度。所有点都在源事件当天出现，不表示海浪同时到达。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Krakatau and Anak Krakatau", zh: "喀拉喀托与喀拉喀托之子" }}>

1883 年的灾害与 2018 年 12 月 22 日喀拉喀托之子的崩塌发生在相近位置，但起因、日期和伤亡属于两次事件，各有来源。

</More>
