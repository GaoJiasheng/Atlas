<Lang en>

Krakatau lies in the Sunda Strait, between Sumatra and Java. On 27 August 1883 a volcanic disaster generated destructive sea waves. A <Term id="term-tsunami">tsunami</Term> is a series of long waves caused by sudden displacement of water. Earthquakes are a common cause, but volcanic processes and landslides can also start one. An eruption and a tsunami are linked here without being the same process.

The selected disaster date is different from the start of the whole eruption episode. GVP's record begins on 20 May 1883. Modern reference geography places the volcano, but it does not restore the island coastline before or after the disaster. The map point cannot explain all the changes that happened there.

A USGS historical table, read on 27 October 2026, reports about <Num s="S21">36,000</Num> deaths, mainly from tsunamis. This is an estimate of people who died in the disaster, rather than a count of those killed by lava. The more detailed historical estimate is explained below. Neither number should be read as a measure of wave height or used to rank the suffering of communities.

Open the associated observation layer. Its points locate records connected to this tsunami; they are not the outer edge of flooding, or a complete map of affected settlements. Gaps between them may be gaps in surviving or selected evidence. These records belong to 1883. Later, nearby Anak Krakatau produced a separate tsunami in 2018, with a different initiating event and its own observation records.

<More title={{ en: "The historical death estimate", zh: "历史死亡估计" }}>

The same USGS table, accessed on 27 October 2026, lists <Num s="S21">36,417</Num> deaths for Krakatau. This is the attributed historical estimate behind the rounded “about <Num s="S21">36,000</Num>”, with tsunamis identified as the principal cause. Separate NCEI fields have different scopes and are not added together.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What the observation points preserve", zh: "观测点保留了什么？" }}>

NCEI records different measurement types. This atlas preserves the original type codes and positions, without displaying heights: the type dictionary has not been verified category by category. The points appear together on the source event's date. That is an association, not a replay of wave arrival.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Krakatau and Anak Krakatau", zh: "喀拉喀托与喀拉喀托之子" }}>

The 1883 Krakatau disaster and the 22 December 2018 collapse at Anak Krakatau are separate events. A similar location does not make their initiating processes or casualty accounts interchangeable. Each keeps its own source record.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

喀拉喀托（Krakatau）位于苏门答腊和爪哇之间的巽他海峡。1883 年 8 月 27 日，火山灾害引起破坏性的海浪。<Term id="term-tsunami">海啸</Term>是海水突然位移形成的一系列长波；起因常是地震，也可能是火山过程或滑坡。这里的喷发与海啸相连，却不是同一个过程。

历史灾害日也不同于整段喷发活动的起日。GVP 的喷发记录从 1883 年 5 月 20 日开始。现代海岸和火山点提供一致的定位依据，没有复原灾前、灾后的岛形。一个位置标记，不能交代岛上发生过的所有变化。

USGS 的历史表记载，灾害造成约 <Num s="S21">36,000</Num> 人死亡，主要由于海啸；本题于 2026 年 10 月 27 日读取该表。这是死亡人数的历史估计，不是被熔岩夺去生命的人数，也不是海浪高度。较细的历史估计放在细看中，不能拿来排列不同社区的苦难。

打开相关观测层，看到的是与这次海啸相连的记录位置，不是淹没边界，也不是完整的受灾聚落图。点与点之间的空白，可能来自记录的缺失，不能据此判定没有海浪到达。1883 年的记录与附近 2018 年的事件分别保存；位置接近，并不表示起因和影响相同。来源各有范围，比较时也要保留。

<More title={{ en: "The historical death estimate", zh: "历史死亡估计" }}>

同一张 USGS 表列出喀拉喀托灾害的历史死亡估计 <Num s="S21">36,417</Num> 人，主要死因为海啸；表于 2026 年 10 月 27 日读取。正文将它取整写成约 <Num s="S21">36,000</Num> 人。NCEI 的不同死亡字段各有统计范围，不能相加。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What the observation points preserve", zh: "观测点保留了什么？" }}>

NCEI 收录的测量类型不同。本题保留位置与原类型码，不展示高度，因为类型字典还没有逐类核验。观测点按源事件日整体出现，表示与事件相关，不是回放海浪到达的过程。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Krakatau and Anak Krakatau", zh: "喀拉喀托与喀拉喀托之子" }}>

1883 年的喀拉喀托灾害，与 2018 年 12 月 22 日喀拉喀托之子火山的崩塌，是分别记录的事件。相近的位置，不会让两次事件的起因与伤亡统计变成同一回事，各自仍要查回各自的来源。

</More>
