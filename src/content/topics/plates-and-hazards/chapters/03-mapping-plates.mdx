<Lang en>

A modern plate model helps us compare different kinds of evidence. Its boundary lines run across oceans as well as land. Some boundary regions are broad zones of deformation, shown separately by the PB2002 model. These areas describe the model's treatment of motion, rather than districts with a shared level of danger.

At a <Term id="term-divergent">divergent boundary</Term>, plates move apart. At a <Term id="term-transform">transform boundary</Term>, their relative motion is mainly horizontal. <Term id="term-subduction">Subduction</Term> takes oceanic lithosphere beneath another plate. Other convergent segments include collision. The map follows PB2002's segment classifications: the whole edge between neighbouring plates need not have the same motion. A line's shape alone cannot tell us its class.

Earthquakes commonly begin when sudden movement on a <Term id="term-fault">fault</Term> releases stored elastic strain energy. The sample shown here begins in 2000 and includes catalogue events with <Term id="term-magnitude">magnitude</Term> at least <Num s="S604">6</Num>. At this chapter's last date, 25 December 2004, later events are still absent. The point marks an epicentre, not the complete length of a rupture.

Magnitude describes earthquake size. <Term id="term-intensity">Intensity</Term> describes shaking or effects at a particular place. A larger dot is only a display encoding of catalogue magnitude, with the original magnitude type retained. It is not a damage radius. Compare the points, volcanoes and boundaries, including exceptions; their pattern cannot identify the exact place and time of the next earthquake.

These are properties of the dataset checked on 26 October 2026, not competing measurements of how many plates nature must contain.

<Lang zh>

现代板块模型让不同证据能够放在一起比较。边界线经过陆地，也穿过海洋。有些地区的变形分布很宽，PB2002 另以宽边界区表示。它说明模型怎样处理运动，不是把范围内的地方评为同一种危险程度。

在<Term id="term-divergent">张裂型边界</Term>，板块彼此分离；在<Term id="term-transform">转换型边界</Term>，相对运动主要是水平错动。<Term id="term-subduction">俯冲</Term>则是大洋岩石圈沉到另一板块之下。其他汇聚段还包括碰撞。同一对板块之间，各段运动未必相同，不能凭线的弯曲形状猜类型。分类来自原资料的记录，不是把附近灾害点连成线，也不是按人口多少来划分。

地震常由<Term id="term-fault">断层</Term>突然滑动、释放储存的弹性应变能引起。这里选取从 2000 年开始、目录<Term id="term-magnitude">震级</Term>至少为 <Num s="S604">6</Num> 的事件。到本章最后的日期 2004 年 12 月 25 日，后来的地震仍不出现。震中点不能代表整段地下破裂的长度。

震级描述地震规模，<Term id="term-intensity">烈度</Term>描述某个地方的摇晃或影响。点的大小只是目录震级的显示编码，保留原来的震级类别，不表示破坏半径。把震中、火山与边界叠看，可以找到分布和例外，却不能预报下一次地震的确切地点与时刻。
