<Lang en>

Plate boundaries cross oceans as well as land. The PB2002 plate model shows where neighbouring plates move relative to each other. In some places, rock deforms across a broad region. The model marks these regions separately from its boundary lines. They are regions of movement, not zones where every place faces the same danger.

At a <Term id="term-divergent">divergent boundary</Term>, plates move apart. At a <Term id="term-transform">transform boundary</Term>, plates mainly slide sideways past each other. During <Term id="term-subduction">subduction</Term>, oceanic lithosphere sinks beneath another plate. Other convergent boundaries include places where plates collide. Different parts of a plate's edge can move differently. Read the legend to identify each kind.

Earthquakes often begin when rock suddenly slips along a <Term id="term-fault">fault</Term>. This releases energy stored as the rock was stretched or squeezed. The sample starts in 2000 and includes earthquakes with catalogue <Term id="term-magnitude">magnitude</Term> at least <Num s="S604">6</Num>. The last view in this chapter stops at 25 December 2004, so later earthquakes are still hidden.

Magnitude measures earthquake size. <Term id="term-intensity">Intensity</Term> describes shaking or its effects at a particular place. Larger earthquake dots mean larger catalogue magnitudes, not wider circles of damage. The records keep their original magnitude types. Compare the earthquake points, volcanoes and boundaries to look for patterns and exceptions. These patterns cannot tell us exactly where or when the next earthquake will happen.

<Lang zh>

板块边界经过陆地，也穿过海洋。PB2002 板块模型标出相邻板块发生相对运动的地区。有些地方的岩石在很宽的范围内变形，模型便用宽边界区表示，与边界线分开。这些范围描述岩石运动，不表示区内每个地方的危险程度都相同。

在<Term id="term-divergent">张裂型边界</Term>，板块彼此分离；在<Term id="term-transform">转换型边界</Term>，板块主要沿水平方向错动。<Term id="term-subduction">俯冲</Term>时，大洋岩石圈沉到另一板块之下。其他汇聚边界还包括板块碰撞的地方。同一块板块的边缘，各段运动可以不同。读图例，就能认出各类边界。

岩石沿<Term id="term-fault">断层</Term>突然滑动，常会引发地震。岩石先前受到拉伸或挤压，储存的能量便在这时释放。图中的样本从 2000 年开始，收录目录<Term id="term-magnitude">震级</Term>至少为 <Num s="S604">6</Num> 的地震。本章最后的视图停在 2004 年 12 月 25 日，后来的地震还没有出现。

震级衡量地震规模，<Term id="term-intensity">烈度</Term>描述某个地点的摇晃或影响。地震点越大，目录震级越大，并不是破坏范围越宽。各条记录保留原来的震级类型。把震中、火山和板块线叠看，可以找到分布规律和例外，却不能知道下一次地震的确切地点和时刻。
