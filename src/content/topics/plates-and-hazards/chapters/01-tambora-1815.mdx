<Lang en>

Tambora stands on Sumbawa, in present-day Indonesia. On 10 April 1815, a major eruption was taking place there. Find the island and its neighbouring volcanoes on the map. The point locates Tambora; records of ash, settlements and harvests tell us how far the disaster reached.

The eruption affected communities beyond the volcano. Food shortages followed, and people died from hunger. A USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory account published on 29 September 2011 reports more than <Num s="S22">70,000</Num> deaths in surrounding areas. A separate, undated USGS table lists <Num s="S21">92,000</Num> deaths and gives starvation as the main cause. Both are historical estimates that include deaths after the eruption itself.

The Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program, or GVP, records an eruption episode from 1812 to 1815. An episode is a period of volcanic activity. GVP gives this episode a Volcanic Explosivity Index, or VEI, of <Num s="S19,S604">7</Num>. VEI describes an eruption's explosiveness. It is different from earthquake magnitude and does not rate every explosion separately.

Nearby volcanoes may look like a chain of dots. Their closeness does not mean they share one connected underground magma reservoir. The map helps us locate them. To understand each eruption and its effects on people, we also need the written accounts and other evidence.

<More title={{ en: "Why are there two death estimates?", zh: "为什么有两种死亡估计？" }}>

The 2011 USGS account gives more than <Num s="S22">70,000</Num> deaths. The separate USGS table gives <Num s="S21">92,000</Num>. Their counting methods and coverage may differ, so averaging them would not give a better estimate. They are not the lower and upper limits of one calculation.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "When did the eruption begin and end?", zh: "喷发何时开始和结束？" }}>

GVP gives the volcano and the eruption episode different record numbers. Parts of the 1812–1815 episode's dates are uncertain. The 10 April disaster date does not settle its start or end date. Open Tambora's catalogue entry to read the dates and their uncertainty.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A hollow left by collapse", zh: "塌陷留下的洼地" }}>

A <Term id="term-caldera">caldera</Term> is a large hollow formed when part of a volcano collapses. It differs from an ordinary summit crater. This map does not show its historical outline. Explore eruption mechanisms in <TopicLink slug="inside-the-earth">Inside the Earth</TopicLink>.

</More>

<Lang zh>

坦博拉（Tambora）位于今天印度尼西亚的松巴哇岛。1815 年 4 月 10 日，那里正在发生大规模喷发。先在地图上找到岛屿和附近的火山。小点标出位置；火山灰、聚落和收成的记录，才能告诉我们灾害影响了多远。

喷发波及火山以外的居民。灾后粮食短缺，有人因饥饿死亡。美国地质调查局（USGS）夏威夷火山观测站在 2011 年 9 月 29 日发表的说明记载，周边地区超过 <Num s="S22">70,000</Num> 人死亡。另一张未注明发布日期的 USGS 表列出 <Num s="S21">92,000</Num> 人死亡，主要死因为饥饿。这两种历史估计都包含喷发后间接造成的死亡。

史密森尼全球火山计划（GVP）记录了一段从 1812 年延续到 1815 年的喷发活动，火山爆发指数（VEI）为 <Num s="S19,S604">7</Num>。这个指数描述喷发的爆发程度，和地震震级不同。它概括一段活动，并不是给每一次爆炸分别评分。

周围的火山点看起来像一条链。位置靠近，却不表示它们共用一个连通的地下岩浆库。地图帮助我们找位置；要了解各次喷发，以及居民怎样受到影响，还要结合文字记载和其他证据。火山点画不出灰云飘到哪里，也画不出哪些地方的收成受到损害。

<More title={{ en: "Why are there two death estimates?", zh: "为什么有两种死亡估计？" }}>

USGS 的 2011 年说明写超过 <Num s="S22">70,000</Num> 人，另一张表写 <Num s="S21">92,000</Num> 人。两者统计方法和范围可能不同，取平均不会得到更好的答案。这也不是同一次计算的下限和上限。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "When did the eruption begin and end?", zh: "喷发何时开始和结束？" }}>

GVP 的火山和喷发活动各有编号。1812—1815 年这段活动的部分日期仍不确定，4 月 10 日这个灾害日不能补齐起止日。点开坦博拉目录，可以读到日期和误差说明。

</More>
