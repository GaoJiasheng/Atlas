<Lang en>

Tambora stands on Sumbawa, in present-day Indonesia. The event marker takes us to 10 April 1815, during a major eruption. It marks a location and a historical disaster date. The modern coast and the other volcano points around it help us find the place; they do not reconstruct the island, an ash cloud or the communities of that year.

The eruption affected people beyond the immediate volcano. Later shortages of food were part of the loss of life. A USGS Hawaiian Volcano Observatory account published on 29 September 2011 reports more than <Num s="S22">70,000</Num> deaths in surrounding areas. A separate USGS table, read on 27 October 2026, lists <Num s="S21">92,000</Num> deaths and identifies starvation as the principal cause. These are attributed historical estimates, including indirect deaths.

The Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program gives the 1812–1815 eruption episode a Volcanic Explosivity Index, or VEI, of <Num s="S19,S604">7</Num>. That entry covers an episode, rather than a measurement of every separate explosion. VEI describes volcanic eruptions; it is different from the scale used for earthquake size.

Look from the selected event to the nearby catalogue points. Their closeness does not establish a shared underground reservoir. Nor does a point show how far ash spread or where harvests failed. Those questions need other records. Keeping the location, eruption record and human impact distinct helps us read each source with the care it needs.

<Lang zh>

坦博拉（Tambora）在今天印度尼西亚的松巴哇岛上。事件标记把日期带到 1815 年 4 月 10 日，属于那次大规模喷发期间。标记提供历史事件的定位，周围海岸和火山点则帮助认路；这里没有复原当年的岛形、灰云或居民聚落。

喷发的影响并不止于火山附近，后来的粮食短缺也是死亡原因。美国地质调查局（USGS）夏威夷火山观测站 2011 年 9 月 29 日的说明记载，周边地区超过 <Num s="S22">70,000</Num> 人死亡。另一张 USGS 表列 <Num s="S21">92,000</Num> 人死亡，并把饥饿列为主要死因；这张未注明发布日期的表于 2026 年 10 月 27 日读取。两种历史估计各有出处，也包含间接死亡。

全球火山计划（GVP）把 1812—1815 年这段喷发记录的火山爆发指数（VEI）列为 <Num s="S19,S604">7</Num>。它描述一段喷发活动，不是每次爆炸各自的测量，也不是地震规模的指标。一个灾害日期，不能替整段活动补齐不确定的起止日。

再看周围的火山点：位置接近，不等于地下共用一个连通岩浆库。一个点也画不出火山灰或缺粮影响了哪些地方。定位、喷发目录和人的损失，需要分别查证；不能把地图上的小符号，当成灾害全部影响的边界。

<More title={{ en: "Two estimates, not an interval", zh: "两种估计，不是一个区间" }}>

USGS 的 2011 年说明写“超过 <Num s="S22">70,000</Num> 人”；未注明发布日期、于 2026 年 10 月 27 日读取的表写 <Num s="S21">92,000</Num> 人。没有证据表明两者采用完全一致的统计边界，不能平均，也不能把它们拼成统计置信区间。
