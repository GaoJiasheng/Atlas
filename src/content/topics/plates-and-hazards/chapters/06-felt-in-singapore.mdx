<Lang en>

On 25 February 2022, people in Singapore felt an earthquake that began in west Sumatra. The Earth Observatory of Singapore, or EOS, describes their reports on a public-lecture page. The earthquake catalogue tells us about the source. Reports from Singapore tell us what people felt far away. They do not give one measured shaking level for the whole island.

Find the USGS epicentre, then look towards Singapore. The Singapore marker helps you compare the two places. It is an orientation point, not an instrument station or an observer's home. The map does not trace a travelling wave or calculate its speed.

PB2002 places Singapore on the smaller Sunda plate. MSS describes its stable continental setting more broadly as part of the Eurasian plate. The models use different amounts of detail. Singapore lies away from the region's active plate boundaries, but people can still feel distant earthquakes. Ground conditions and the way a building responds both affect shaking. Building height alone cannot tell us how safe it is.

The Building and Construction Authority, or BCA, says Singapore buildings are designed to withstand tremors from distant earthquakes. Its guidance includes taking cover under a table indoors and not using lifts. These precautions come from BCA's guidance read on 27 October 2026. Follow the linked official page for its advice; the map does not assess the safety of individual buildings.

<More title={{ en: "Why do the magnitudes differ?", zh: "为什么震级数字不同？" }}>

EOS's page for a lecture on 17 March 2023 calls the earthquake magnitude <Num s="S601">6.2</Num>. The USGS reviewed record collected on 26 October 2026 gives <Num s="S602">6.1</Num>, type mww, and depth <Num s="S602">4</Num> kilometres. These are different sources' estimates of the same earthquake.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Earthquake size and local shaking", zh: "地震规模与本地摇晃" }}>

Magnitude measures earthquake size. Intensity describes shaking or effects at a particular place. Reports of feeling an earthquake are useful evidence, but they do not form a calibrated intensity map of Singapore.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Shelter and official advice", zh: "屏蔽作用与官方指引" }}>

MSS describes Singapore as relatively sheltered from tsunamis by surrounding land and shallow seas. Some risk remains, and warning arrangements are still in place. Read BCA's <a href="https://www1.bca.gov.sg/guidelines-and-requirements/precautions-to-take-during-earth-tremors/">earth tremor guidance</a> for the indoor precautions described above. BCA's official Chinese name is 建设局.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

2022 年 2 月 25 日，西苏门答腊发生地震，新加坡有人感到震动。新加坡地球观测研究所（EOS）的公开讲座页面记述了这些报告。地震目录告诉我们震源的情况，新加坡的报告则告诉我们远处的人感到了什么；它们没有测出一个适用于全岛的摇晃等级。

先找到 USGS 震中，再看向新加坡。新加坡标记帮助我们比较两个地点，是方位点，不是仪器站，也不是某位报告者的住处。地图没有画出波的传播路线，也没有计算传播速度。

PB2002 把新加坡放在较小的巽他板块上。MSS 用较宽的范围，描述这里稳定的大陆欧亚板块环境。两种模型的细致程度不同。新加坡远离区域内活跃的板块边界，人们仍可能感到远处地震。地面条件和建筑怎样响应，都会影响摇晃；单看楼高，不能判断建筑的安全程度。

建设局（BCA）说明，新加坡建筑按抵御远处地震震动设计。其指引包括在室内到桌下掩护、不使用电梯。这些注意事项来自 2026 年 10 月 27 日读取的建设局指引。具体建议请看所附官方原页，地图并不评定个别建筑的安全。

<More title={{ en: "Why do the magnitudes differ?", zh: "为什么震级数字不同？" }}>

EOS 为 2023 年 3 月 17 日讲座所写的页面，把地震记为 <Num s="S601">6.2</Num> 级。2026 年 10 月 26 日收集的 USGS 已审记录给出 <Num s="S602">6.1</Num>，类型 mww，深度 <Num s="S602">4</Num> 公里。这是不同来源对同一次地震的估计。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Earthquake size and local shaking", zh: "地震规模与本地摇晃" }}>

震级衡量地震规模，烈度描述某个地点的摇晃或影响。有人感到地震，是有用的证据，但这些报告还不是经过校准的新加坡烈度图。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Shelter and official advice", zh: "屏蔽作用与官方指引" }}>

MSS 说明，周围陆地和浅海让新加坡相对受到海啸屏蔽。风险仍然存在，海啸警示安排也继续保留。上文的室内注意事项可查建设局的<a href="https://www1.bca.gov.sg/guidelines-and-requirements/precautions-to-take-during-earth-tremors/">地震震动指引</a>。BCA 的官方中文名是“建设局”。

</More>
