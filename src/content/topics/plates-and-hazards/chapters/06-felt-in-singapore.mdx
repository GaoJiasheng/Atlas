<Lang en>

On 25 February 2022, an earthquake in west Sumatra was felt by people in Singapore. The Earth Observatory of Singapore describes these reports in its public-lecture page. The earthquake's catalogue parameters describe the source. Reports from Singapore describe experience at another place; they do not turn the whole island into one measured shaking level.

The map moves between the USGS epicentre and Singapore without drawing a travelling wave or calculating a speed. Singapore's marker is an orientation point, not a seismic instrument or the home of a particular observer. It helps us compare the source region with a familiar place while keeping the distinction between location and observation.

PB2002 identifies the smaller Sunda plate beneath Singapore. MSS uses the broader description of a stable continental Eurasian plate setting. These descriptions use different levels of model detail. Singapore is away from the active regional boundaries, yet distance does not prevent all effects from earthquakes elsewhere. Ground conditions and a building's response influence the shaking people feel; height alone cannot give a building a safety rating.

The Building and Construction Authority, BCA, says Singapore buildings are designed to withstand tremors caused by distant earthquakes. Its guidance, read on 27 October 2026, includes taking cover under a table indoors and not using lifts. This is an attributed account of official guidance, rather than a promise about every building in every situation. Use the linked official page for the advice itself, alongside this explanation of the regional setting.

<More title={{ en: "The source estimates have versions", zh: "震源估计也有版本" }}>

EOS's page for a lecture on 17 March 2023 calls the 2022 event magnitude <Num s="S601">6.2</Num>. The USGS reviewed solution frozen on 26 October 2026 and rechecked for the specification on 27 October gives <Num s="S602">6.1</Num>, type mww, and depth <Num s="S602">4</Num> kilometres. Each value keeps its source and date; one is not silently substituted for the other.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Magnitude is not local intensity", zh: "震级不是本地烈度" }}>

Magnitude measures earthquake size; intensity describes shaking or effects at a place. Reports of feeling an earthquake are useful evidence, but they are not a calibrated intensity map of Singapore. The atlas assigns no shaking grade to homes or individual buildings.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Relative shelter and official advice", zh: "相对屏蔽与官方指引" }}>

MSS describes Singapore as relatively sheltered from tsunamis by surrounding land and shallow seas, while maintaining warning arrangements. That does not mean zero risk. BCA's <a href="https://www1.bca.gov.sg/guidelines-and-requirements/precautions-to-take-during-earth-tremors/">earth tremor guidance</a> is the source of the indoor precautions paraphrased here, read on 27 October 2026. The agency's official Chinese name is 建设局.

</More>

<Lang zh>

2022 年 2 月 25 日，西苏门答腊发生地震，新加坡有人感到震动。新加坡地球观测研究所（EOS）的公开讲座页面提到这次经验。目录里的参数描述震源，新加坡的报告则描述另一地点的感受，不能把全岛当成一个已经测量过的摇晃等级。

镜头从 USGS 震中移向新加坡，没有画传播路线，也没有计算波速。新加坡标记是帮助认路的方位点，不是仪器站，也不是某位报告者的住处。同一视图能帮助比较远处事件与熟悉地点，但位置和观测仍须分清。

PB2002 在这里区分出较小的巽他板块，MSS 则使用稳定大陆欧亚板块的广义描述，模型的细致程度不同。新加坡远离区域内活跃的边界，仍可能感到远处地震。地面条件与建筑响应会影响人的感受，不能只凭楼高判断安全等级。

建设局（BCA）说明，新加坡建筑按抵御远处地震震动设计。其指引包括在室内到桌下掩护、不使用电梯；本题于 2026 年 10 月 27 日读取并转述。这不是对每幢建筑、每种情况的绝对安全保证。科学解释帮助理解区域背景，具体指引应查所附官方原页，不从教学地图推导自家的建筑状况。

<More title={{ en: "The source estimates have versions", zh: "震源估计也有版本" }}>

EOS 为 2023 年 3 月 17 日讲座所写的页面，把这次地震记为 <Num s="S601">6.2</Num> 级。2026 年 10 月 26 日冻结、定稿规格于 10 月 27 日重核的 USGS 已审解为 <Num s="S602">6.1</Num>，类别 mww，深度 <Num s="S602">4</Num> 公里。数值各留出处与日期，不悄悄互换。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Magnitude is not local intensity", zh: "震级不是本地烈度" }}>

震级衡量地震规模，烈度描述某个地方的摇晃或影响。有人感到地震，是有用的经验记录，却不是经过校准的新加坡烈度图。本题不给住家或个别建筑标摇晃等级。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Relative shelter and official advice", zh: "相对屏蔽与官方指引" }}>

MSS 说明，新加坡受周围陆地与浅海相对屏蔽，仍保留海啸警示安排。“相对”不等于零风险。这里的室内注意事项转述自建设局的<a href="https://www1.bca.gov.sg/guidelines-and-requirements/precautions-to-take-during-earth-tremors/">地震震动指引</a>，读取日为 2026 年 10 月 27 日；机构中文名按官方资料写作“建设局”。

</More>
