<Lang en>

On 26 December 2004 an earthquake began off Sumatra. The selected USGS catalogue solution gives Mw <Num s="S54">9.1</Num> and a depth of <Num s="S54">30</Num> kilometres. The epicentre is a surface location above the start of rupture, not the whole earthquake source. Its separate link to the historical tsunami record is checked explicitly.

Sudden vertical movement of the seabed can displace the water above it, generating long waves. In deep ocean a tsunami may be low and broad; near shallow coasts it can slow and grow higher. The associated observation points spread across the Indian Ocean. They mark recorded evidence rather than a travelling wavefront, and their different measurement types are not reduced to one uniform height.

NOAA/ITIC's October 2024 accounting records <Num s="S17">227,899</Num> people dead or missing and presumed dead in <Num s="S17">15</Num> countries. The definition and source date belong with the total. This compilation cannot fully separate deaths caused by the earthquake from deaths caused by the tsunami. It should not be described as confirmed tsunami deaths alone.

2004 年 12 月 26 日，地震在苏门答腊外海开始。这里采用 USGS 的目录解：Mw <Num s="S54">9.1</Num>，深度 <Num s="S54">30</Num> 公里。震中是破裂开始位置正上方的地表点，不代表整个震源区；它与历史海啸记录的关联另经核对。
