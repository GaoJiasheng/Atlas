<Lang en>

On 26 December 2004, an earthquake began off Sumatra. USGS gives its magnitude as Mw <Num s="S54">9.1</Num> and its depth as <Num s="S54">30</Num> kilometres. The epicentre marks the surface point above where the rupture began. The underground break extended beyond this point. The earthquake is linked to the tsunami through the historical event records.

A seabed that suddenly moves up or down can push the water above it. This produces long waves. In deep ocean, a tsunami may be low and broad. Near shallow coasts, it slows and can grow higher. Observation points across the Indian Ocean show where evidence was recorded. The records measure different things, so their heights cannot all be compared as one kind of wave height.

NOAA/ITIC's October 2024 compilation records <Num s="S17">227,899</Num> people dead or missing and presumed dead in <Num s="S17">15</Num> countries. This total includes losses from both the earthquake and tsunami. The compilation cannot fully separate the two causes of death in its records.

The same table lists Indonesia <Num s="S17">167,540</Num>, Sri Lanka <Num s="S17">35,322</Num>, India <Num s="S17">16,269</Num> and Thailand <Num s="S17">8,212</Num> people dead or missing and presumed dead. These four country estimates form only part of the total. The other countries are not listed here, and their figures are not guessed.

<More title={{ en: "Why official summaries differ", zh: "为什么官方摘要的数字不同？" }}>

NOAA SOS reports more than <Num s="S16">230,000</Num> deaths in <Num s="S16">14</Num> countries. JetStream describes <Num s="S609">17</Num> affected countries. These summaries use different definitions from the October 2024 death table. Their figures cannot be averaged into a single answer.

<Term id="term-runup">Run-up</Term> is the highest level the water reaches onshore above sea level. It differs from a wave's amplitude offshore. Observation details keep the original type codes. Heights and arrival times are not shown because the types have not all been checked. The points appear together on the event date, rather than showing waves arriving. <More title={{ en: "Two magnitude estimates", zh: "两种震级估计" }}>

USGS gives Mw <Num s="S54">9.1</Num>; the Meteorological Service Singapore, or MSS, gives <Num s="S12">9.2</Num>. These are estimates of earthquake size from different sources, not local shaking intensities. The catalogue retains its original magnitude type.

</More>

2004 年 12 月 26 日，苏门答腊外海发生地震。USGS 给出的震级为 Mw <Num s="S54">9.1</Num>，深度 <Num s="S54">30</Num> 公里。震中标出地下破裂开始处正上方的地表点，地下破裂还延伸到更远的地方。历史事件记录把这次地震与海啸连接起来。

海床突然上下移动，可以推动上方海水，形成长波。海啸在深海可能低而宽，进入近岸浅水区后会减速，也可能变高。印度洋各处的观测点标出留下证据的位置。各项记录测量的东西不同，不能把所有高度都当成同一种浪高来比较。
