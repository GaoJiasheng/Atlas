<Lang en>

On 22 December 2018, part of Anak Krakatau collapsed and triggered a tsunami in the Sunda Strait. It lies near the site of the 1883 disaster. This time, volcanic collapse started the tsunami, rather than an earthquake.

The volcano had already been active for months. GVP's eruption episode begins on 18 June 2018, before the December collapse. A period of eruption can include several events with different dates. The map uses the catalogue's Krakatau reference centre to locate the volcano. This point does not mark a measured centre of the material that collapsed.

The observation layer shows records associated with the 2018 tsunami. Each entry keeps its event link and original measurement type. Some points lie near coasts also shown in the 1883 records. Keeping the two groups separate lets us compare events at similar places. Points appear together on the event date; they do not show the hours or order in which waves reached the shores.

A tsunami brings a series of long waves. Calm water after one wave does not mean the series has ended. The sea does not always retreat before a tsunami, and the first wave need not be the largest. The two Krakatau disasters show how different processes can start tsunamis in the same region. Historical records explain these past events; official warnings give current advice.

<Lang zh>

2018 年 12 月 22 日，喀拉喀托之子（Anak Krakatau）火山的一部分崩塌，在巽他海峡引发海啸。它靠近 1883 年灾害发生的位置。这一次，首先推动海水的是火山体崩塌，而不是地震。同一个区域，可以发生起因不同的海啸。

崩塌前，火山已经活动了几个月。GVP 的喷发活动记录从 2018 年 6 月 18 日开始，早于 12 月的崩塌。一段喷发可以包含日期不同的多次事件。地图用目录中的喀拉喀托参考中心标出火山位置，这个点不是实测的崩塌物中心。

观测层列出与 2018 年海啸有关的记录。每条记录保留所属事件和原测量类型。有些点靠近 1883 年记录中的同一段海岸，把两组记录分开，就能比较相近地点发生过的两次灾害。点在事件当天一起出现，没有显示海浪到达各地的小时或先后次序。

海啸会带来一系列长波。一波过去后海面暂时平静，不表示海啸已经结束。海水未必先退，第一波也未必最大。两次喀拉喀托灾害让我们看到，同一区域的海啸可以有不同起因。历史记录帮助解释过去，当前指示则要查看官方警示，不能只看历史观测。
