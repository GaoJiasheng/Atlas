<Lang en>

On 22 December 2018, collapse at Anak Krakatau triggered a tsunami in the Sunda Strait. The location is close to the 1883 disaster, but this is a separate historical event. Its initiating process is volcanic collapse, rather than an earthquake starting the tsunami.

Compare the volcano catalogue with the selected disaster record. GVP's eruption episode begins on 18 June 2018, before the December collapse. An episode can contain activity across a span of dates. Its beginning is not automatically the date of a particular landslide or tsunami. The location used here is the catalogue's Krakatau reference centre, rather than a measured point at the centre of the collapsing material.

The observation layer belongs specifically to the 2018 tsunami. It is kept apart from the 1883 records, even where points lie near the same coast. Each entry retains its event association and original measurement type. Displaying associated observations together tells us which event they describe; it does not show the sequence or hour at which waves reached each shore.

A tsunami is a series of long waves, not necessarily a single wave followed by calm water. The sea does not always retreat first, and the first arriving wave need not be the largest. Neither the reference map nor these historical observations provide current warnings. The value of comparing these events is to recognise different causes and different records, while avoiding a simple story that every tsunami must begin in the same way.

<More title={{ en: "Similar place, different event", zh: "相近的位置，不同的事件" }}>

Krakatau in 1883 and Anak Krakatau in 2018 each has its own disaster marker and sources. A shared regional setting does not supply the same causal sequence, dates or impact statistics.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two records with different dates", zh: "两种记录，各有日期" }}>

The GVP episode starts on 18 June 2018 and ends on 17 April 2020 in the selected record. The NCEI tsunami source date is 22 December 2018. The former describes an eruption episode; the latter identifies this tsunami. They are linked without replacing each other's dates.

</More>

<Lang zh>

2018 年 12 月 22 日，喀拉喀托之子（Anak Krakatau）火山崩塌，在巽他海峡引发海啸。它靠近 1883 年灾害的位置，却是另一次历史事件。这次海啸的起因是火山体崩塌，不是由地震首先触发。位置相近，不能代替对起因的核查。

再对照火山目录和灾害记录：GVP 的喷发活动从 2018 年 6 月 18 日开始，早于年底的崩塌。一段喷发可能跨越很长时间，起日并不自动等于其中一次滑坡或海啸的日期。图上采用的是目录里的喀拉喀托参考中心，也不是实测崩塌物的体积中心。

这层观测属于 2018 年的海啸，与 1883 年的观测分开；即使有些点落在相近海岸，仍各自保留事件关联与测量类型。把相关记录同时显示，是说明它们属于哪一次事件，不是表示海浪同时到达，更不是按小时播放传播过程。

海啸是一系列长波，并非总是大浪一过就平静。海水未必先退，最先到达的一波也未必最大。参考地图和历史观测都不是当前警示。把不同事件并排看，是为了分清原因、日期和证据，不能把一种起因套到所有海啸，也不能从相同地点推定影响完全一样。
