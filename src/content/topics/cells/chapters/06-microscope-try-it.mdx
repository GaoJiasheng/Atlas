<Lang en>

Begin with the lowest objective and the onion specimen. The circular image is a teaching simulation generated on this device, not a photograph or a recording of living cells. It is a place to practise a sequence: find, centre, increase power, refine focus and record. The chosen specimen’s shapes remain the same when you change the optical settings.

Find the target nucleus at low power. Move the slide until the target is near the centre. Remember that the viewed image moves opposite to the slide. Once the target is centred, increase the objective power while keeping the slide position and specimen fixed. The surrounding area decreases, but the same target should remain within the field. If it disappears, return to low power and locate it again.

Now vary fine focus in small steps. A near layer and a far layer can become clear at different settings. A structure coming into focus has not suddenly grown inside the cell. At higher power the useful focus range is narrower in this model, so a small change can matter. The coarse-focus knob remains an identification feature, without a control that could drive the stage into the glass.

Record the observation, then change one condition at a time. Keep the specimen constant while comparing focus; keep focus and position steady when comparing power. Illumination can make the field darker or brighter, but it does not change total power or bring a different depth into focus. The records belong to this exploration and are cleared when the page is refreshed.

<Lang zh>

从最低倍物镜和洋葱标本开始。圆形影像是在本机生成的教学模拟，不是照片，也不是活细胞录像。这里练习的是一组有顺序的动作：找到、居中、换高倍、微调、记录。改变光学设置时，所选标本的形状保持不变。

先低倍找到目标核，再移动玻片，让目标接近中央。记住图像的移动方向与玻片相反。居中以后，保持标本和玻片位置，换高倍物镜。周围范围缩小，同一个目标仍应留在视野中；若找不到，就退回低倍重新定位。

接着小幅改变微调。较近和较远的结构，可能分别在不同设置下变清楚。结构进入焦点，不表示细胞突然长出了新部位。本模型在高倍下的有效调焦范围较窄，小变化也可能明显影响图像。粗调节器只供辨认，不提供大幅推动载物台的控件。

记录后，一次只改一个条件。比较焦点时保持同一标本；比较倍数时先固定焦点和位置。照明能改变明暗，却不会改变总倍数，也不能代替调焦。记录用于当前这次探索，刷新页面会清除，不能把它当作真实显微实验的测量报告。
