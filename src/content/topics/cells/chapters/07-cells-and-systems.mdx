<Lang en>

The tissue patch places the separate cell models back within a larger organisation. Cells contribute to tissues, tissues make up organs, and organs work together in systems. The small intestine is therefore an organ containing tissues and cells, rather than one enormous cell. Each level supports a different explanation of how a living body functions.

The leaf connects this microscopic view to a whole plant. The 2023 syllabus states: “Identify the different parts of plants and state their functions.” This outcome appears on printed page <Num s="S1">56</Num>. Follow a whole plant in <TopicLink slug="plant">A Plant</TopicLink> to see roots, stem and leaves in context. The cell model here explains selected structures within an organ; it does not replace the pathways shown in that topic.

The body outline links the human examples. The heart and lungs belong to different systems that help supply and transport substances around the body. Continue in <TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs">The Heart and Lungs</TopicLink>. The intestine context leads to <TopicLink slug="digestive-system">The Digestive System</TopicLink>, where the pathway of food, digestion and absorption can be followed at the scale of organs.

Some organisms consist of one cell, while others have many. Compare them in <TopicLink slug="living-things">Classifying Living Things</TopicLink>. Keep the two kinds of explanation together: cell structure helps explain what happens within living material, and organs and systems show how those living parts are connected. A microscope field alone does not show the working of an entire plant or body.

<Lang zh>

组织示意把单独放大的细胞放回整体。细胞构成组织，组织组成器官，器官又在系统中共同作用。小肠包含许多组织和细胞，不能把它当作一个巨大的细胞。不同层次解释不同的问题；要认识整个身体，需要把它们联系起来。

叶把微观视角连到整株植物。2023 年课纲在印刷页 <Num s="S1">56</Num>，要求辨认植物部位并说明作用。到<TopicLink slug="plant">《一株植物》</TopicLink>，沿根、茎、叶看整体联系。本主题说明器官内的部分细胞结构，另一个主题则保留完整植物的路径与功能。

身体轮廓连接人体例子。心与肺属于不同系统，共同帮助身体获得并运送所需物质；到<TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs">《心与肺》</TopicLink>继续追踪。小肠示意则连到<TopicLink slug="digestive-system">《消化系统》</TopicLink>，在那里可以按器官尺度看食物经过的路径，以及消化和吸收。

有的生物只有一个细胞，有的由许多细胞组成。到<TopicLink slug="living-things">《生物的分类》</TopicLink>继续比较。细胞结构解释活的材料内部怎样组织，器官和系统解释各部分怎样连接。保留两个尺度，便不会把一个显微视野误当成整株植物或整个人体的完整工作图。

每次跨主题，先确认观察对象和尺度，再沿已有路径理解功能。
