<Lang en>

The plant cell has a supporting outer <Term id="cell-wall">cell wall</Term>. A separate membrane lies just inside it. Follow both outlines: adding a wall does not remove the membrane or take over its role in regulating passage. The wall supports and protects the cell. The cutaway separates these close layers so that their positions and different functions are easier to compare.

The small bodies around the edge are <Term id="chloroplast">chloroplasts</Term>. This is a photosynthetic plant-cell example. Chloroplasts contain <Term id="chlorophyll">chlorophyll</Term>, a pigment that absorbs light energy used in photosynthesis. The process uses light energy to make food; sunlight itself is not food. These bodies sit in the peripheral cytoplasm, rather than floating inside the large central compartment.

That compartment is a <Term id="vacuole">vacuole</Term>. Mature plant cells often have a large central vacuole containing water and dissolved substances, called cell sap. It is not an air-filled hole. Its watery contents contribute to pressure against the cell wall and to support. The nucleus in this model lies beside it, within the cell boundary, rather than occupying the middle of the vacuole.

Look again for the cytoplasm, nucleus and membrane that were present in the animal example. The additional structures do not replace those shared parts. This example brings several structures into one clear view; it is not a promise that every plant cell contains chloroplasts or that every part will be visible together through a microscope.

<More title={{"en": "Counts are drawing choices", "zh": "数量是绘图选择"}}>

This model has <Num s="S20">six</Num> chloroplast shapes and a single large vacuole. The number and arrangement of drawn objects were chosen for legibility, not as a universal count for real cells.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Other cell machinery", "zh": "模型以外的细胞结构"}}>

Plants and animals both have mitochondria. Chloroplasts do not replace the rest of a plant cell’s machinery. This topic leaves that machinery out rather than adding another set of labels and a biochemical explanation.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "The animal comparison", "zh": "与动物细胞比较"}}>

The large central vacuole is characteristic of many mature plant cells. Animal cells can also contain small vacuoles and vesicles. Their omission from the animal model is a simplification, not evidence that animal cells have no vacuoles.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

植物细胞外侧有支持性的<Term id="cell-wall">细胞壁</Term>，里面还有一道独立的膜。沿着两层轮廓看，便能分清壁外、膜内的位置。增加细胞壁，不会让细胞膜消失。壁提供支持和保护，膜调节物质进出；剖开画法把相邻结构分开，便于比较作用。

外围的小结构是<Term id="chloroplast">叶绿体</Term>。这是能进行光合作用的植物细胞例子。叶绿体含有<Term id="chlorophyll">叶绿素</Term>，这种色素吸收光合作用所用的光能。植物利用光能制造食物，不能把阳光本身叫作食物。叶绿体位于周边的细胞质中，没有漂在中央的大腔室里面。

中央腔室叫<Term id="vacuole">液泡</Term>。成熟植物细胞常有一个大液泡，其中的细胞液含水和溶解物质，并不是空气。液泡中的水分有助于维持对细胞壁的压力，参与支持细胞。本模型的核位于液泡旁边，仍在细胞边界以内。

最后再找动物细胞里也有的膜、质和核。植物增加的结构没有代替这些共同部分。模型把多个部位放在同一幅清楚的图里，但不能由此断言每个植物细胞都有叶绿体，或显微镜下总能同时看清所有部位。

比较时先检查共同结构，再看本例多出的部位，避免只记轮廓。

</More>
