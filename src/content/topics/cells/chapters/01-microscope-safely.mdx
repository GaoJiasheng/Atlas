<Lang en>

The complete <Term id="microscope">microscope</Term> replaces the cell models. It uses lenses to make a small specimen easier to observe. At the top is the <Term id="eyepiece">eyepiece</Term>, the lens assembly nearest the observer. An <Term id="objective">objective</Term> is a lens assembly close to the specimen. The rotating nosepiece selects an objective. Light travels upwards from the illuminator, through the condenser and the opening in the stage, towards the specimen and objective.

The <Term id="slide">slide</Term> is the glass support for a thin specimen. A <Term id="coverslip">coverslip</Term> is a thinner piece of glass covering it. Use prepared slides under a teacher’s guidance. Set the microscope on a stable surface. For this kind of school instrument, carry it with one hand at the arm and the other supporting the base; follow the teacher’s carrying method for the actual instrument.

Begin with the lowest-power objective and the stage lowered. Look from the side while checking the gap between the objective and slide. Find the specimen at low power, then centre the area you want to examine before selecting a higher power. Keep the lens from touching the glass. At high power, use gentle fine adjustment rather than large movements with the coarse knob.

The model identifies both focus knobs, but the later Try it card exposes only fine focus. When finished with a real instrument, return to the lowest power, lower the stage and put the slides away as instructed. Avoid touching optical surfaces; use suitable lens paper for cleaning.

<Lang zh>

完整的<Term id="microscope">显微镜</Term>替换了细胞模型。它利用镜片帮助观察微小标本。上方靠近观察者的镜组叫<Term id="eyepiece">目镜</Term>；靠近标本的镜组叫<Term id="objective">物镜</Term>。转动物镜转换器，可以选择不同物镜。光从下方光源出发，经聚光镜和载物台上的通光孔，穿过标本，再进入物镜。

<Term id="slide">载玻片</Term>承载薄标本，较薄的<Term id="coverslip">盖玻片</Term>覆盖在标本上。真实课堂使用预制玻片，并听从教师指导。仪器要放在稳固台面上；搬运这类学校显微镜时，一手扶镜臂，一手托底座。具体仪器的搬法仍以教师说明为准。

开始前退回最低倍，并降低载物台。从侧面检查物镜与玻片之间的距离，避免镜头碰到玻片。先低倍找到标本，把要看的区域移到中央，再提高倍数。高倍下只做温和的微调，不用粗调节器大幅移动载物台。

模型里能辨认粗调和微调两个旋钮，后面的“试一试”只开放微调。用完真实仪器，要退回最低倍、降低载物台，并按指导收好玻片。不要用手触摸镜片；清洁镜头使用合适的擦镜纸。

在画面中依次点选载物台、转换器和旋钮，先确认名称与位置，再把它们放回观察步骤中理解。
