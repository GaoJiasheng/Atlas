<Lang en>

The rib cage protects organs that keep working inside it. The heart pumps blood and the lungs exchange gases; follow their functions in <TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs">The Heart and Lungs</TopicLink>. The chest also moves during breathing, explored further in <TopicLink slug="muscles-chest">Chest muscles</TopicLink>. A protective frame need not be a solid box.

Now move to the separate long-bone specimen. Its opening lets you compare the dense wall with the structure inside. <Term id="compact-bone">Compact bone</Term> forms a hard outer wall along the shaft. <Term id="spongy-bone">Spongy bone</Term> forms a hard lattice, with spaces between its struts. The word “spongy” describes that arrangement: this bone is not a soft washing sponge.

<Term id="marrow">Bone marrow</Term> occupies internal spaces. Red marrow produces blood cells; yellow marrow contains much fat. Marrow is soft tissue, distinct from the hard lattice around it. The cutaway therefore shows different materials doing different jobs, rather than an empty tube. Their distribution changes with the bone and the person’s age.

Blood vessels supply the living tissues within bone. The <Term id="periosteum">periosteum</Term> covers most of its outside and carries vessels and nerves. Hardness does not mean lifelessness: a bone contains living cells, needs a blood supply, and can change its tissue. The enlarged lattice and vessel on this station make those relationships readable. They are a qualitative teaching section, not a scan of this adult’s femur or a microscopic view with measurable strut thickness.

<Lang zh>

胸廓保护着里面持续工作的器官。心脏泵血，肺进行气体交换，功能可接着看<TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs">心与肺</TopicLink>。胸部在呼吸时也会活动，<TopicLink slug="muscles-chest">胸部肌肉</TopicLink>另有说明。能保护器官的框架，不一定是完全封闭的硬盒。

再看旁边的长骨标本。开窗让我们比较外壁与里面的结构：<Term id="compact-bone">骨密质</Term>组成骨干坚硬而密实的外壁；<Term id="spongy-bone">骨松质</Term>则形成硬的网架，骨小梁之间有空隙。“松质”说的是排列方式，不能据此把它想成柔软的洗澡海绵。

<Term id="marrow">骨髓</Term>位于内部空隙。红骨髓制造血细胞，黄骨髓含较多脂肪；它们是软组织，与周围坚硬的骨网架不同。因此，骨内部既不是空管，也不是整块实心材料。不同骨、不同年龄的骨髓分布还会有所变化。

血管为骨里的活组织供血。覆盖多数骨外表面的<Term id="periosteum">骨膜</Term>也带有血管和神经。骨虽然硬，里面仍有活细胞，需要血液，并能更新组织。工位把网架和血管放大，方便看清关系；这是定性教学截面，不是从这副成人股骨扫描出来的内部，也不能拿来测骨小梁的微观厚度。看到空隙时，要同时留意里面占据空间的软组织。
