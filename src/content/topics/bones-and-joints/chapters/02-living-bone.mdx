<Lang en>

The rib cage protects organs that keep working inside it. The heart pumps blood and the lungs exchange gases. Follow these functions in <TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs">The Heart and Lungs</TopicLink>. The chest also moves during breathing, as <TopicLink slug="muscles-chest">Chest muscles</TopicLink> explains. A protective frame can allow movement.

Now look at the separate long-bone specimen. Its window reveals the wall and the structures inside. <Term id="compact-bone">Compact bone</Term> forms the hard, dense outer wall along the shaft. <Term id="spongy-bone">Spongy bone</Term> forms a hard lattice with spaces between its struts. “Spongy” describes this arrangement. The bone itself is hard, unlike a soft washing sponge.

<Term id="marrow">Bone marrow</Term> fills internal spaces. Red marrow makes blood cells. Yellow marrow contains much fat. Both are soft tissues, unlike the hard bone around them. The inside of a bone therefore contains several materials with different jobs. Their distribution depends on the bone and the person’s age.

Blood vessels supply the living tissues inside bone. The <Term id="periosteum">periosteum</Term> covers most of the bone’s outer surface and carries vessels and nerves. A bone is hard, but it contains living cells, needs blood and can renew its tissue. The specimen enlarges the lattice and vessels so you can see how they fit together. It is a simplified section, rather than a scan of this adult’s femur.

胸廓保护着里面持续工作的器官。心脏泵血，肺进行气体交换，详情可看<TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs">心与肺</TopicLink>。胸部在呼吸时也会活动，<TopicLink slug="muscles-chest">胸部肌肉</TopicLink>会进一步说明。保护器官的支架，也能容许身体活动。

再看旁边的长骨标本。开窗显出外壁和内部结构。<Term id="compact-bone">骨密质</Term>组成骨干坚硬而密实的外壁。<Term id="spongy-bone">骨松质</Term>形成硬的网架，骨小梁之间留有空隙。“松质”说的是这种排列方式，骨本身仍是硬的，并不像洗澡海绵那样柔软。

<Term id="marrow">骨髓</Term>占据骨内的空隙。红骨髓制造血细胞，黄骨髓含较多脂肪。它们都是软组织，与周围坚硬的骨不同。因此，骨内部包含几种材料，各有作用。骨髓的分布会随骨的部位和人的年龄而改变。

血管为骨里的活组织供血。<Term id="periosteum">骨膜</Term>覆盖骨的大部分外表面，也带有血管和神经。骨虽然硬，里面仍有活细胞，需要血液，也能更新组织。标本把骨网架和血管放大，方便观察它们的关系。这是简化的截面示意，并不是这副成人股骨的内部扫描图。
