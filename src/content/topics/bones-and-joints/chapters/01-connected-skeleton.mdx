<Lang en>

Follow the frame from the skull down the vertebral column, or spine. The skull surrounds the brain. The spine supports the trunk and surrounds the spinal cord. At the chest, ribs curve towards the breastbone, or sternum. The usual rib cage has <Num s="S8">12 pairs</Num> of ribs. The ribs, sternum and thoracic spine surround and help protect the heart and lungs.

The collarbone and shoulder blade form the shoulder girdle. They link the upper limb to the trunk. Follow the upper arm towards the forearm and hand. Lower down, the hip bones meet the sacrum to form the pelvis. The rounded head of each femur fits into a hip socket. Follow the leg through the knee to the shin and foot. The kneecap lies in front of the knee. The tibia and fibula lie alongside each other as separate bones.

Bones have different shapes. The femur is long, the sternum is fairly flat, and a vertebra has an irregular outline. Even a small finger bone belongs to the long-bone group. “Long” describes the bone’s proportions, rather than a minimum size.

The usual adult count is <Num s="S2">206 bones</Num>. Some selectable parts here contain several bones. A whole hand and a section of spine are examples. One card can therefore stand for many bones. Follow how the frame connects, rather than counting cards to find the total.

<More title={{"en": "Grouped regions", "zh": "按区域合并"}}>

The hands, feet, skull and spinal regions each group several source shapes. The jaw stays separate. A grouped region still contains its separate bones [S30,S34].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Counts and variation", "zh": "骨数与差异"}}>

The usual adult total follows an agreed counting method. Some bones fuse during growth, and small extra bones vary between people [S2,S3].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

沿着骨架，从颅骨向下看到脊柱。颅骨围住脑，脊柱支持躯干，并围住脊髓。胸部的肋骨向前弯，伸向胸骨。通常的胸廓有<Num s="S8">12 对</Num>肋骨。肋骨、胸骨和后方的胸椎一起围住心与肺，帮助保护它们。

锁骨和肩胛骨组成肩带，把上肢连到躯干。沿着上臂，可以继续看到前臂和手。再往下，髋骨与骶骨相接，组成骨盆。每根股骨的圆头嵌入髋臼，也就是髋部的骨窝。下肢经过膝部，连到小腿和足。髌骨在膝的前方。小腿的胫骨与腓骨并排相邻，是两根独立的骨。

骨的形状各不相同。股骨较长，胸骨较扁，椎骨的轮廓不规则。小小的指骨也属于长骨。“长骨”按骨的长宽比例分类，并不要求骨达到某个大小。观察一块骨时，既可以看它的形状，也可以找出它在支架中的位置。

成人骨的常规计数是<Num s="S2">206 块</Num>。这里有些可点选部件包含多块骨，例如一只手或一段脊柱。因此，一张卡片可以代表多块骨。沿着连接关系观察，更容易看懂身体的支架；卡片数量不能当作人体骨的总数。

<More title={{"en": "Grouped regions", "zh": "按区域合并"}}>

手、足、颅骨和各段脊柱分别合并了多个原始形状，下颌仍可独立点选。合并区域里面仍有多块骨[S30,S34]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Counts and variation", "zh": "骨数与差异"}}>

成人总数按通行的方法计算。有些骨会在发育时融合，小型附加骨的数量也因人而异[S2,S3]。

</More>
