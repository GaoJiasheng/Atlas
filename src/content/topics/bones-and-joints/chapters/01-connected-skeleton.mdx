<Lang en>

Follow the frame from the skull down the vertebral column. The skull surrounds the brain, and the spine supports the trunk and surrounds the spinal cord. At the chest, ribs curve round towards the breastbone, or sternum. The usual rib cage has <Num s="S8">12 pairs</Num> of ribs. Together with the sternum and thoracic spine, they surround and help protect the heart and lungs.

The collarbone and shoulder blade form the shoulder girdle linking the upper limb with the trunk. Follow the upper arm towards the forearm and hand. In the pelvis, the hip bones meet the sacrum; the rounded head of each femur fits a hip socket. The leg continues through the knee towards the shin and foot. The kneecap lies at the front of the knee. Notice that the tibia and fibula remain separate bones alongside each other.

Bones differ in shape as well as position. A femur is long; the sternum is comparatively flat; a vertebra has an irregular outline. In anatomical classification, even a small finger bone can be a long bone: “long” describes its proportions, not a minimum size.

The conventional adult count is <Num s="S2">206 bones</Num>, but the selectable pieces here are grouped for reading. A whole hand or a section of spine contains several bones. Selecting one region does not make it a single bone, and counting model cards will not recover the anatomical total. Compare the connections first, then use the names to explain where the support continues.

<More title={{"en": "Grouped regions", "zh": "按区域合并"}}>

The hands, feet, skull and spinal regions combine source meshes. The jaw stays separate. This is a selection convention, not a claim that each combined region contains one bone [S30,S34].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Counts and variation", "zh": "骨数与差异"}}>

The usual adult total is a convention. Some bones fuse during development and small additional bones vary between people. The model does not supply a newborn total or a bone-name memory test [S2,S3].

</More>

沿着骨架，从颅骨向下看到脊柱。颅骨围住脑，脊柱支持躯干，并围住脊髓。到了胸部，肋骨弯向前面的胸骨。通常的胸廓有<Num s="S8">12 对</Num>肋骨，与胸骨及后方的胸椎一起围住心肺，有助保护这些器官。骨架的支持和保护，随所在部位而不同。

锁骨和肩胛骨组成肩带，把上肢连到躯干。顺着上臂继续看前臂和手。骨盆里的髋骨与骶骨相接，股骨的圆头嵌入髋臼；下肢再经过膝、小腿连到足。髌骨在膝前，胫骨与腓骨则并列保留，不能把它们看成同一根骨。

骨的形状也不同。股骨较长，胸骨较扁，椎骨的轮廓不规则。解剖分类里的“长骨”说的是长宽比例，不是规定达到某个大小，因此小小的指骨也能属于长骨。观察形状时，同时想它处在连接链的哪个位置。

成人骨的常规计数是<Num s="S2">206 块</Num>，但舞台为了便于阅读，把一些区域合并点选。一只手或一段脊柱里面都有多块骨；一个部件卡不等于一块骨。这里先沿连接关系看支架怎样延续，再用骨名准确指出位置，不把卡片总数当作人体骨数；区域标签只是阅读工具。

<More title={{"en": "Grouped regions", "zh": "按区域合并"}}>

手、足、颅骨和各脊柱区域合并了源网格，下颌仍独立。这是点选约定，不是说每个合并区域只有一块骨[S30,S34]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Counts and variation", "zh": "骨数与差异"}}>

常规成人总数是一种解剖计数约定；发育时有些骨融合，较小附加骨也有个体差异。这里不设固定新生儿总数，也不做骨名背诵测试[S2,S3]。

</More>
