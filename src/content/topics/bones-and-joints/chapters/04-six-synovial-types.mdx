<Lang en>

Compare the facing surfaces in each prepared view. These are <Num s="S11">6 types</Num> of synovial joint, not a list of every joint in the body. Switch between beats to compare the demonstrated poses. The small hand bones are separate close-up specimens; they have not been pulled out of the whole-body skeleton.

At a <Term id="plane">plane joint</Term>, relatively flat surfaces permit small gliding movements. The wrist-bone example shows a limited glide. At the elbow’s <Term id="hinge">hinge joint</Term>, the ulna mainly bends and straightens relative to the humerus. The nearby <Term id="pivot">pivot joint</Term> has a different role: during forearm rotation, the radius turns relative to the ulna. The forearm bones do not twist together into a single rod.

The finger knuckle is a <Term id="condyloid">condyloid joint</Term>, allowing movement in two main directions. At the thumb base, a <Term id="saddle">saddle joint</Term> has surfaces that are hollow in one direction and rounded in the other. These matching shapes differ from the rounded head and socket of a <Term id="ball-socket">ball-and-socket joint</Term>, shown at the hip.

Several axes of movement do not mean unlimited movement. The capsule, ligaments and muscles also constrain a joint. Compare the hip with the shoulder in <TopicLink slug="muscles-shoulders">Shoulder muscles</TopicLink>, and follow the load-bearing hip and knee in <TopicLink slug="muscles-legs">Leg muscles</TopicLink>. The poses here are design examples for observation. They are not a range that everyone should copy or force their body to reach.

<More title={{"en": "A knee is a modified hinge", "zh": "膝并非纯铰链"}}>

The knee mainly bends and straightens, with rolling and gliding between femur and tibia. When bent it also permits some rotation, so “modified hinge” is more useful than imagining a simple door hinge [S12].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Mobility and stability", "zh": "活动与稳定"}}>

The hip’s deep socket and strong supporting tissues suit weight bearing. The shoulder permits a wider range but depends greatly on surrounding muscles for support. Neither joint is “better”; they have different structural relationships [S12].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Read the movement with the shape", "zh": "把形状与活动对照"}}>

A joint’s shape helps explain its main directions of movement. Compare which surface turns or slides, then read its name. Surrounding tissues still limit movement. Ball-and-socket is also called 杵臼关节 in the Chinese reference [S11,S27].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

逐拍比较相对的骨面。这里介绍<Num s="S11">6 种</Num>滑膜关节，不是把全身所有关节都分成这张名单。切换节拍，比较各幅示范姿态；手的小骨是独立近景标本，并没有从完整骨架里抽走。

<Term id="plane">平面关节</Term>的表面较平，可作小幅滑动，腕骨例子就显示这种受限制的变化。肘部的<Term id="hinge">铰链关节</Term>主要让尺骨相对肱骨屈伸；附近的<Term id="pivot">车轴关节</Term>则参与前臂旋转，使桡骨相对尺骨转动。不能把前臂想成两骨一起扭成的一根棍。

指根的<Term id="condyloid">椭圆关节</Term>可沿两个主要方向活动。拇指根部的<Term id="saddle">鞍状关节</Term>，每个关节面沿一个方向凹、另一个方向凸，彼此配合。髋部的<Term id="ball-socket">球窝关节</Term>又不同：圆形股骨头嵌入窝内，可绕多个轴活动。

能沿多个轴活动，不等于可以无限转动；囊、韧带及肌肉仍会限制关节。可以到<TopicLink slug="muscles-shoulders">肩部肌肉</TopicLink>比较肩，到<TopicLink slug="muscles-legs">腿部肌肉</TopicLink>追踪负重的髋与膝。这里的姿态只是供观察的设计例子，不规定每个人应该达到的幅度，也不需要照着强行扳动身体。观察时先指出哪个骨面相对另一个改变方向，再说明它允许怎样的活动，不只给形状贴名称。

<More title={{"en": "A knee is a modified hinge", "zh": "膝并非纯铰链"}}>

膝主要屈伸，同时存在股骨与胫骨之间的滚动和滑动；屈膝时还可有一定旋转。因此可称修正铰链，不能只想成门上的简单铰链[S12]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Mobility and stability", "zh": "活动与稳定"}}>

髋臼较深，支持组织较强，适合承重；肩的活动较广，也很依赖周围肌肉支持。不能据此评出哪个关节更好，它们的结构关系不同[S12]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Read the movement with the shape", "zh": "把形状与活动对照"}}>

关节形状有助解释主要活动方向。先比较哪个表面转动或滑动，再读名称；周围组织仍会限制活动。球窝关节在中文参考里也称杵臼关节[S11,S27]。

</More>
