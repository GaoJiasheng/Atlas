<Lang en>

Compare the facing bone surfaces in each view. Here are <Num s="S11">6 types</Num> of synovial joint. They do not include every kind of joint in the body. Switch between beats to compare their movements. The small hand bones are separate close-up specimens; the whole-body skeleton still keeps its hand bones.

At a <Term id="plane">plane joint</Term>, fairly flat surfaces allow small gliding movements. Look for this small glide between the wrist bones. At the elbow’s <Term id="hinge">hinge joint</Term>, the ulna mainly bends and straightens relative to the humerus. The nearby <Term id="pivot">pivot joint</Term> allows a different movement. During forearm rotation, the radius turns relative to the ulna. The two bones do not twist together as a single rod.

The finger knuckle is a <Term id="condyloid">condyloid joint</Term>. It moves in two main directions. At the thumb base, a <Term id="saddle">saddle joint</Term> has matching surfaces. Each surface curves inwards in one direction and outwards in the other. At the hip’s <Term id="ball-socket">ball-and-socket joint</Term>, a rounded head fits into a socket and moves around several axes.

The capsule, ligaments and muscles limit how far a joint moves. The poses here help you observe movement; you do not need to copy them or force your body to reach them. Compare the hip with the shoulder in <TopicLink slug="muscles-shoulders">Shoulder muscles</TopicLink>. Follow the weight-bearing hip and knee in <TopicLink slug="muscles-legs">Leg muscles</TopicLink>.

<More title={{"en": "The knee’s movements", "zh": "膝部的活动"}}>

The knee mainly bends and straightens. The femur and tibia also roll and glide against each other. A bent knee allows some rotation. This makes the knee a “modified hinge”, with more movements than a simple door hinge [S12].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Movement and support", "zh": "活动与支持"}}>

The hip’s deep socket and strong supporting tissues help it bear weight. The shoulder moves through a wider range and relies greatly on nearby muscles for support. Their different structures suit different roles [S12].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Shape and movement", "zh": "形状与活动"}}>

A joint’s shape helps explain its main movements. Watch which surface turns or slides, then read its name. Surrounding tissues also limit its movement. The Chinese reference also calls a ball-and-socket joint 杵臼关节 [S11,S27].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

逐拍比较相对的骨面。这里展示<Num s="S11">6 种</Num>滑膜关节，没有包括人体的所有关节种类。切换节拍，可以比较不同的活动。近景中的手部小骨是独立标本，完整骨架里的手骨仍保留在原处。

<Term id="plane">平面关节</Term>的表面较平，容许小幅滑动。先看腕骨之间的小幅滑动。肘部的<Term id="hinge">铰链关节</Term>主要让尺骨相对肱骨屈伸。附近的<Term id="pivot">车轴关节</Term>容许另一种活动：前臂旋转时，桡骨相对尺骨转动，两根骨并不会像一根棍那样一起扭转。

指根的<Term id="condyloid">椭圆关节</Term>可沿两个主要方向活动。拇指根部的<Term id="saddle">鞍状关节</Term>有互相配合的关节面，每个面沿一个方向向内凹，沿另一个方向向外凸。髋部的<Term id="ball-socket">球窝关节</Term>则由圆形骨头嵌入骨窝，可绕多个轴活动。

关节囊、韧带和肌肉会限制关节的活动幅度。图中的姿态帮助观察运动，你不需要模仿，更不应强行扳动身体来达到这些角度。可以到<TopicLink slug="muscles-shoulders">肩部肌肉</TopicLink>比较髋与肩，再到<TopicLink slug="muscles-legs">腿部肌肉</TopicLink>追踪承重的髋与膝。

<More title={{"en": "The knee’s movements", "zh": "膝部的活动"}}>

膝主要屈伸，股骨与胫骨之间也有滚动和滑动。屈膝时还可有一些旋转，因此称为“修正铰链”，活动比门上的简单铰链多[S12]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Movement and support", "zh": "活动与支持"}}>

髋臼较深，周围支持组织强，帮助髋部承重。肩的活动范围较广，很依赖周围肌肉支持。不同结构适合不同作用[S12]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Shape and movement", "zh": "形状与活动"}}>

关节形状有助解释主要活动方向。先看哪个表面转动或滑动，再读名称。周围组织也会限制活动。中文参考里也把球窝关节称为杵臼关节[S11,S27]。

</More>
