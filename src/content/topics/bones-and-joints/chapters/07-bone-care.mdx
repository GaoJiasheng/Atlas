<Lang en>

Return to the whole skeleton. Food, movement and care help keep these living tissues healthy. Calcium forms part of bone mineral. Vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium from food. Bones need several nutrients, so a varied diet matters. A single special food cannot take its place.

Calcium-rich choices include dairy foods, fish eaten with their edible bones, calcium-set tofu and fortified foods. Different foods offer different sources of calcium. Compare their labels to see what they provide. <TopicLink slug="digestive-system">The Digestive System</TopicLink> explains how food breaks down and the body absorbs nutrients.

Activity also supports bone health. Singapore guidance says children above <Num s="S20">6 years</Num> should include muscle- and bone-strengthening activities at least <Num s="S20">3 times a week</Num>. This is one part of broader activity guidance. Suitable activities depend on the person and their circumstances. You can learn by watching this model without copying its poses or comparing your body with its adult frame.

After an injury, tell a trusted adult and seek medical assessment for persistent pain, swelling or difficulty using a limb. Comparing an injury with this picture cannot show whether a growth region is damaged. Follow the frame once more: bones, joints and pulling muscles work together. <TopicLink slug="muscles-back">Back muscles</TopicLink> and <TopicLink slug="muscles-core">Core muscles</TopicLink> explain support around the spine and trunk.

<More title={{"en": "Activities for different ages", "zh": "不同年龄的活动"}}>

HealthHub gives this muscle- and bone-strengthening frequency for children above six. Younger children have different guidance. The frequency is part of general activity advice, rather than a full training plan or strength target [S20].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Read the food label", "zh": "比较食物标签"}}>

Tofu made with calcium salts and calcium-fortified drinks can provide calcium. The amount depends on how they are made. Different tofu and plant drinks may contain different amounts. The calcium source also discusses adults; its supplement doses and sunlight advice are not instructions for children [S21].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "An injured growing bone", "zh": "生长中的骨受伤"}}>

Persistent pain, tenderness, swelling, a changed limb shape or inability to bear weight after injury may need prompt assessment. These signs can have different causes. Tell an adult and seek help: a picture cannot rule out a growth-plate injury [S19].

</More>

回到完整骨架。饮食、活动和照护帮助这些活组织保持健康。钙是骨矿物的一部分，维生素 D 帮助身体从食物吸收钙。骨需要多种营养，多样饮食很重要，不能只靠一种特别食物。

富含钙的选择包括奶类、连可食用骨一起吃的鱼、用钙盐凝固的豆腐，以及钙强化食品。不同食物都能提供钙，可以比较标签，看看含量。<TopicLink slug="digestive-system">消化系统</TopicLink>会解释食物怎样分解、身体怎样吸收营养。

活动也有助骨骼健康。新加坡建议，超过<Num s="S20">6 岁</Num>的儿童每周至少<Num s="S20">3 次</Num>进行强健肌肉与骨骼的活动。这是整体活动建议的一部分，合适的活动还要考虑个人情况。只观察模型也能学习，不必模仿姿势，或拿自己的身体和这副成人骨架比较。

受伤后若持续疼痛、肿胀或难以使用肢体，要告诉可信任的成人并寻求医疗评估。对照图片不能判断生长区域有没有受伤。最后再沿着骨架看一遍：骨、关节和拉动骨的肌肉共同工作。<TopicLink slug="muscles-back">背部肌肉</TopicLink>和<TopicLink slug="muscles-core">腰腹肌肉</TopicLink>继续解释脊柱与躯干周围的支持。

<More title={{"en": "Activities for different ages", "zh": "不同年龄的活动"}}>

HealthHub 把这个强健肌肉与骨骼的活动频率用于超过六岁的儿童。较小儿童另有建议。频率属于一般活动建议，并非完整训练计划或力量目标[S20]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Read the food label", "zh": "比较食物标签"}}>

用钙盐制作的豆腐和钙强化饮品可提供钙，含量取决于制作方式。不同豆腐和植物饮品的含钙量可能不同。钙资料也讨论成人，其中的补充剂剂量和日晒建议不能当作儿童的指引[S21]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "An injured growing bone", "zh": "生长中的骨受伤"}}>

受伤后若持续疼痛、压痛、肿胀、肢体形状改变或不能负重，可能需要及时评估。这些表现可有不同原因。要告诉成人并求助，不能靠图片排除生长板损伤[S19]。

</More>
