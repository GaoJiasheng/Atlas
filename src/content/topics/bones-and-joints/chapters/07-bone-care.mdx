Return to the connected frame. Food, movement and care support the living tissues you have explored; this view does not change bone density in response to a button. Calcium is part of bone mineral, and vitamin D helps the body absorb calcium from food. Bone health involves several nutrients and everyday habits, so a varied diet matters more than finding a single special food.

Calcium-rich choices include dairy foods, fish eaten with their edible bones, calcium-set tofu and fortified foods. Different foods offer different routes, and labels help distinguish them. <TopicLink slug="digestive-system">The Digestive System</TopicLink> explains how food is broken down and nutrients are absorbed. This topic gives no supplement dose or adult nutrition prescription for children.

Activity also supports bone health. Singapore guidance says children above <Num s="S20">6 years</Num> should include muscle- and bone-strengthening activities at least <Num s="S20">3 times a week</Num>. This is part of broader activity guidance, not a complete training plan. Suitable movement depends on the person and their circumstances. You can learn the model’s structure by observation without copying its poses or comparing your body with the reference.

If an injury causes persistent pain, swelling or difficulty using a limb, tell a trusted adult and seek medical assessment. An injured growth region cannot be checked by matching it to this picture. Finally, follow the connections again: bones, joints and pulling muscles work together. <TopicLink slug="muscles-back">Back muscles</TopicLink> and <TopicLink slug="muscles-core">Core muscles</TopicLink> extend that picture to support around the spine and trunk.

<More title={{"en": "Read the food label", "zh": "比较食物标签"}}>

Tofu made with calcium salts and calcium-fortified drinks can contribute calcium; their calcium content depends on how they are made. Do not assume every tofu or plant drink is equivalent. No sunlight schedule or supplement dose is copied from the adult-oriented calcium page [S21].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "An injured growing bone", "zh": "生长中的骨受伤"}}>

Persistent pain, tenderness, swelling, a changed limb shape or inability to bear weight after injury can need prompt assessment. These signs do not identify one diagnosis. Tell an adult; the model cannot rule out a growth-plate injury [S19].

回到完整骨架。饮食、活动和照护支持着刚才看到的活组织；按按钮并不会让图中的骨密度改变。钙是骨矿物的一部分，维生素D帮助身体从食物吸收钙。骨健康需要多种营养与日常习惯，不能只找一种特别食物来代替多样饮食。

含钙选择包括奶类、连可食用骨一起吃的鱼、用钙盐凝固的豆腐，以及强化食品。不同食物提供不同途径，选购时可比较标签。<TopicLink slug="digestive-system">消化系统</TopicLink>说明食物怎样分解、营养怎样吸收。这里不提供补充剂剂量，也不把成人营养处方搬给儿童。

活动也有助骨健康。新加坡建议，<Num s="S20">6 岁</Num>以上儿童每周至少<Num s="S20">3 次</Num>进行强健肌肉与骨骼的活动。这只是整体活动建议的一部分，不是完整训练计划；合适的运动还要考虑个人情况。你可以只观察模型来理解结构，不必模仿姿势，更不用拿自己的身体和成人参考比较。

受伤后若持续疼痛、肿胀或难以使用肢体，应告诉可信任的成人并寻求医疗评估，不能靠对照图片判断生长区域有没有受伤。最后再沿连接看一遍：骨、关节与拉动骨的肌肉共同工作。<TopicLink slug="muscles-back">背部肌肉</TopicLink>和<TopicLink slug="muscles-core">腰腹肌肉</TopicLink>继续讲脊柱与躯干周围的支持。
