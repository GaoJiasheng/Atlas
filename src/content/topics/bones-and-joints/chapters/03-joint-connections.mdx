<Lang en>

Start at the skull. A <Term id="joint">joint</Term> is a place where bones meet. Some joints move freely, but many skull sutures allow little or no movement. Other connections allow only small movements. Joints in the limbs can allow much more. A connection can still be a joint even when you cannot see it bend.

The separate specimen shows the basic structure of a synovial joint. Smooth articular <Term id="cartilage">cartilage</Term> covers the facing bone surfaces. A capsule surrounds the joint cavity. Inside, <Term id="synovial-fluid">synovial fluid</Term> lubricates the surfaces so they move smoothly. The capsule is transparent here to give you a view inside.

A <Term id="ligament">ligament</Term> supports the connection between bones and helps limit excess movement. Ligaments do not actively pull like muscles. The bones’ shapes, the capsule and the surrounding tissues work together to guide and support movement. The bone surfaces alone do not explain every movement a joint allows.

Now look at the elbow. Follow the band that carries a muscle’s pull to bone. This is a <Term id="tendon">tendon</Term>. A tendon and a ligament are both strong connective tissues. A tendon transmits pull from muscle to bone; a ligament supports a connection between bones. To tell them apart, follow each band to its attachments. Its colour or thin shape alone cannot tell you its job. Next, compare joint shapes before following the muscle pull at the elbow.

<More title={{"en": "Structure and movement", "zh": "结构与活动"}}>

Fibrous, cartilaginous and synovial describe how joints are built. Immobile, slightly movable and freely movable describe how much they move. These two ways of grouping joints are related, but describe different features [S10].

先从颅骨看起。<Term id="joint">关节</Term>是骨相接的地方。有些关节能自由活动，许多颅骨缝却几乎不动。另一些骨连接只能小幅活动，四肢的一些关节则能动得更多。即使看不见明显弯曲，骨相接的地方仍可以是关节。

旁边的标本展示滑膜关节的基本结构。相对的骨面覆有光滑的关节<Term id="cartilage">软骨</Term>。关节囊围住关节腔，腔内的<Term id="synovial-fluid">滑液</Term>润滑骨面，让它们活动得更顺畅。图中把关节囊画成透明，方便观察内部各层。

<Term id="ligament">韧带</Term>支持骨与骨之间的连接，并帮助限制过度活动。韧带不会像肌肉那样主动拉动骨。骨的形状、关节囊和周围组织一起引导并支持活动。只看骨面，还不能解释关节的全部运动。

再转到肘部，沿着肌肉通向骨的条带看。这是<Term id="tendon">肌腱</Term>，负责传递肌肉的拉力。肌腱和韧带都是坚韧的结缔组织，但作用不同：肌腱把肌肉的拉力传给骨，韧带支持骨之间的连接。辨认时可以沿条带看两端附在哪里，不能只凭颜色或细长外形。接下来先比较关节形状，再看肘部怎样受肌肉拉动。
