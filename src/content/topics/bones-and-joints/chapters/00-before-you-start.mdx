<Lang en>

Look through the faint body outline to the <Term id="skeleton">skeleton</Term> inside. The skull sits above the trunk; the arm and leg bones extend from it. These parts form a connected frame. Later chapters look inside a bone, compare the places where bones meet, and follow a muscle’s pull across an elbow. Start by looking at how the pieces fit together rather than trying to learn every name.

The model uses adult male reference anatomy. It is the same source body used in this site’s muscle topics. It gives us a consistent way to compare neighbouring structures, but it does not show the proportions of every adult or predict how a pupil’s body will grow. The faint outline is there to locate the bones inside a body, and can be hidden to make the frame easier to see.

Left and right belong to the body being shown. When it faces you, its left arm is on your right. Turn the model and follow that same arm: its name stays the same. Select a bone to read its function, or choose it from the chapter index. Use the beat controls to compare the prepared views at your own pace.

The P4 syllabus introduces the skeletal and muscular systems as parts of the human body. The detailed shapes and mechanisms here go further. You can complete the observations entirely on screen. The poses and model force readings are teaching choices; they are not tests of your body, movement or strength.

先透过淡淡的身体轮廓，观察里面的<Term id="skeleton">骨骼</Term>。颅骨在躯干上方，手臂和腿向两边及下方伸出，各部分连成一副支架。后面会看骨的内部、比较骨相接的地方，再跟着肌肉的拉力经过肘部。开始时先看结构怎样相连，不必急着记住每块骨的名称。

这里采用成人男性参考解剖，和本站肌肉主题取自同一个源身体。统一的参考能帮助我们比较相邻结构，但不能代表所有成人，更不能预测学生长大后的身材。淡显的外轮廓只帮助定位；把它藏起，就能更清楚地看骨架。

左右以模型身体自身为准。它面向你时，它的左臂在画面右边；转到背面后，仍要跟着同一条手臂看，名称不会随着屏幕方向改变。点选一块骨可读它的功能，也能从本章索引找到。用节拍按钮逐幅比较，停在需要的角度观察即可。

小四课纲介绍人体的骨骼系统和肌肉系统，本主题的细部形状与机制属于深化内容。所有观察都能在屏幕上完成，不需要照着摆姿势。图中的动作幅度和后面的模型力值都是教学选择，不拿来评比你的身体、活动能力或力量。
