<Lang en>

Look through the faint body outline to the <Term id="skeleton">skeleton</Term> inside. The skull sits above the trunk. Arm and leg bones extend from the trunk to form a connected frame. Start by following these connections. Later, you will look inside a bone, compare the places where bones meet, and follow a muscle’s pull across an elbow.

This model shows adult male anatomy. The same reference body appears in this site’s muscle topics, so you can compare neighbouring structures. People have different proportions, and children’s proportions change as they grow. The model cannot show how your body will grow. Hide the faint outline to see the bones more clearly.

Left and right belong to the body on screen. When it faces you, its left arm is on your right. Turn the model and follow that arm. It is still the left arm. Select a bone to read what it does, or find it in the chapter index. The beat buttons let you compare views at your own pace.

The P4 syllabus introduces the skeletal and muscular systems. Here you can explore their shapes and workings in more detail. You can make every observation on screen. The poses and force readings help explain the structures; they do not measure your movement or strength. You do not need to copy the poses.

透过淡淡的身体轮廓，看看里面的<Term id="skeleton">骨骼</Term>。颅骨在躯干上方，手臂和腿从躯干伸出，连成一副支架。先沿着这些连接观察。后面会看骨的内部，比较骨相接的地方，再跟着肌肉的拉力看肘部怎样活动。

这里展示成人男性的身体结构，和本站肌肉主题采用同一副参考身体，方便比较相邻部位。每个人的身体比例不同，儿童的比例还会随成长改变。这副骨架不能预测你长大后的身材。把淡淡的身体轮廓藏起来，就能更清楚地看骨。

左右以画面中身体自身为准。它面向你时，它的左臂在你的右边。转动模型，继续跟着同一条手臂看，它仍是左臂。点选一块骨，就能读到它的作用，也能从本章索引找到它。用节拍按钮逐幅比较，按自己的速度观察。

小四课纲介绍骨骼系统和肌肉系统。这里进一步说明它们的形状和工作方式，所有观察都能在屏幕上完成。图中的姿态和力值帮助解释结构，并不测量你的活动能力或力量。你不需要模仿这些姿势。
