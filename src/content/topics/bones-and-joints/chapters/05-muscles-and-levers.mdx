<Lang en>

Begin with the muscle attachments. Predict whether a pull could turn the forearm if both ends stayed on the same bone. Then follow the teaching biceps tendon across the elbow to the radius. The biceps can help bend the elbow because its pull acts across that joint. The triceps tendon reaches the ulna at the elbow’s point and can straighten the elbow. Muscles pull; an opposing muscle supplies the opposite action.

The real arm involves more muscles than this selected pair. <TopicLink slug="muscles-arms">Arm muscles</TopicLink> follows those helpers and forearm rotation. Here the pair makes the attachment pattern readable. The model keeps the attachments anatomically intended even while you make your prediction; it does not display a falsely attached muscle as a real structure.

A bone can act as a <Term id="lever">lever</Term>, a rigid structure turning about a pivot. The separate beam makes a simpler comparison. Hold the external force and angle the same, then move the load farther from the pivot. Predict before revealing: the required equivalent muscle pull increases. Use the Try it card to change distance and record the result.

The <Term id="moment-arm">moment arm</Term> is the perpendicular distance from the pivot to the force’s line of action. It can change when the beam changes angle, even if the load stays at the same point along it. First compare longer and shorter arms, then read the SIM values in PLATE. <TopicLink slug="forces">Forces</TopicLink> introduces pushes and pulls; this ideal turning-force comparison goes further. These readings describe the teaching model, not a person’s measured strength.

先看肌肉的附着。猜一猜：如果两端都在同一块骨上，拉力能不能转动前臂？再跟着示意肌腱看，肱二头肌腱跨过肘部，连到桡骨；拉力跨关节传递，才能帮助屈肘。肱三头肌腱连到尺骨的肘尖，可使肘伸展。肌肉提供拉力，相反动作由另一组拉力完成。

真实手臂还涉及别的肌肉，<TopicLink slug="muscles-arms">手臂肌肉</TopicLink>会继续讲协同与前臂旋转。这里只选取这对肌肉，让附着关系容易看清。预测时模型仍保留正确连接，不会把错误附着画在真实解剖上，再当作正常结构。

骨可以像<Term id="lever">杠杆</Term>那样，受力绕支点转动。旁边的梁把问题简化：固定外加力与角度，把负载移离支点。先猜再揭示，所需等效肌肉拉力会增大。用“试一试”改变距离，并把读数记成表。

<Term id="moment-arm">力臂</Term>是支点到力作用线的垂直距离；即使负载仍在梁的同一点，改变角度也能改变它。先比长短，再到图版读模拟值。<TopicLink slug="forces">力</TopicLink>介绍推与拉，这里的理想转动比较再进一步。读数属于教学模型，不是对某个人肌力的测量，更不是训练目标。比较前先确认固定了哪些量，才能解释读数为什么变化。
