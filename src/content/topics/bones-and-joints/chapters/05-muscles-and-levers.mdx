Begin with the muscle attachments. Could a pull turn the forearm if both ends stayed on the same bone? Follow the biceps tendon across the elbow to the radius. Its pull acts across the joint and helps bend the elbow. The triceps tendon reaches the ulna at the elbow’s point. Its pull straightens the elbow. Muscles pull, and opposing muscles produce opposite actions.

More muscles help move the real arm. Explore these helpers and forearm rotation in <TopicLink slug="muscles-arms">Arm muscles</TopicLink>. Here, the selected pair makes the attachments easier to follow. The model keeps those attachments in their correct places while you make your prediction.

A bone can act as a <Term id="lever">lever</Term>: a rigid structure that turns about a pivot. The separate beam shows a simpler example. Keep the external force and angle the same, then move the load farther from the pivot. Predict what will happen before revealing the result. The model needs a greater muscle pull. Use the Try it card to change the distance and record the readings.

The <Term id="moment-arm">moment arm</Term> is the perpendicular distance from the pivot to the force’s line of action. Changing the beam’s angle can change this distance, even when the load stays at the same point. Compare longer and shorter moment arms, then read the simulation values marked SIM in PLATE. <TopicLink slug="forces">Forces</TopicLink> introduces pushes and pulls. Here, the readings explain an ideal turning-force model, rather than measuring anyone’s strength.

先看肌肉的附着点。如果两端都在同一块骨上，拉力能让前臂转动吗？沿着肱二头肌腱，可以看到它跨过肘部，连到桡骨。拉力跨过关节，帮助屈肘。肱三头肌腱连到尺骨的肘尖，拉动时使肘伸展。肌肉提供拉力，方向相反的肌肉产生相反动作。

真实手臂还有其他肌肉参与运动。<TopicLink slug="muscles-arms">手臂肌肉</TopicLink>介绍这些协同肌肉和前臂旋转。这里选出一对肌肉，方便追踪附着关系。你作预测时，模型仍把附着点保留在正确位置。

骨可以像<Term id="lever">杠杆</Term>一样，受力后绕支点转动。旁边的梁展示一个简化例子。保持外加力与角度相同，把负载移离支点。先预测，再揭示结果：模型所需的肌肉拉力会增大。用“试一试”改变距离，并记录读数。

<Term id="moment-arm">力臂</Term>是支点到力的作用线之间的垂直距离。即使负载留在梁的同一点，改变梁的角度也能改变力臂。先比较长短，再到图版查看标有 SIM 的模拟值。<TopicLink slug="forces">力</TopicLink>介绍推与拉。这里的读数解释理想模型中的转动作用，并不测量个人肌力。
