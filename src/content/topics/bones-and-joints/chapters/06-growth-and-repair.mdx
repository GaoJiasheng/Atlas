<Lang en>

The growing-bone specimen has a band near its end. This <Term id="growth-plate">growth plate</Term> is a region of cartilage involved in lengthening a young long bone. It belongs to the separate growing specimen, not the adult skeleton. Compare the view with the plate and the view after it has closed. The display changes which schematic tissues you see; it does not calculate a child’s height.

When a growth plate closes and is replaced by bone, lengthening stops at that site. This does not mean the bone becomes inactive. <Term id="remodelling">Remodelling</Term> continues: old bone tissue is removed and new tissue forms. The highlighted patch marks the idea of local renewal. It is not a map showing how fast a particular person’s cells work.

The separate repair specimens show a possible sequence after a fracture. A blood clot, or haematoma, forms near the break. A softer callus can bridge it; a bony callus follows and the bone can remodel. These are stages of a biological process, presented as fixed specimens so their differences can be inspected. The row is ordered by process, not divided into equal periods.

Bone’s ability to repair does not make an injury safe to ignore. A growing bone can be injured near its growth plate, and may need prompt medical assessment. The stage cannot diagnose that injury, decide whether a bone is aligned, or tell someone when it is safe to resume an activity. Observe the process without trying to manipulate an injured limb or using the specimens to set a healing deadline.

<More title={{"en": "Closure varies", "zh": "闭合并非同一个年龄"}}>

Growth plates close at different times in different bones and individuals. No single birthday is a universal switch. The source adult frame is not scaled into a child and the specimen does not estimate bone age [S19,S34].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Stages without a clock", "zh": "阶段没有固定时钟"}}>

The repair row has no fitted timeline. Healing depends on the injury and care. A cast or other support can hold a fracture appropriately while living tissues repair; it does not itself manufacture new bone. Assessment and treatment belong to health professionals [S6,S19].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

幼骨标本靠近端部有一条带状区域。这是<Term id="growth-plate">生长板</Term>，属于参与幼年长骨纵向增长的软骨区域。它只出现在独立的生长示意标本，不放进成人骨架。比较有生长板与闭合后的画面，看到的是不同组织状态，不是在计算某个孩子会长多高。

生长板闭合、被骨替代后，骨在该处停止增长，但并没有因此失去活性。<Term id="remodelling">重塑</Term>仍会继续：旧骨组织被去除，新组织形成。显出的更新斑块只是标明局部更新的概念，不是记录某个人细胞工作速度的地图。

旁边的修复标本按过程排列。骨折附近先有血液凝块，也称血肿；之后可出现较软的骨痂，再形成骨性骨痂，继续重塑。各幅固定标本让我们停下来比较组织差别，并不表示每段过程耗时一样，也没有给出从受伤到恢复的倒计时。

骨能修复，不等于受伤可以不理。生长中的骨可能伤到生长板，需要及时医疗评估。舞台不能诊断损伤、判断断骨是否对齐，或决定什么时候能恢复活动。观察过程即可，不要用标本给自己定愈合日期，更不能照着图尝试扳动或复位受伤肢体。

<More title={{"en": "Closure varies", "zh": "闭合并非同一个年龄"}}>

不同骨与不同人的生长板闭合时间不一样，不能把某个生日当作统一开关。源成人骨架没有缩放成儿童，标本也不估算骨龄[S19,S34]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Stages without a clock", "zh": "阶段没有固定时钟"}}>

修复列没有拟合时间轴，愈合与损伤及照护有关。石膏等支持可帮助固定，让活组织修复，但石膏本身不会制造新骨。评估与治疗由医疗人员负责[S6,S19]。

</More>
