<Lang en>

Look for a band near the end of the growing-bone specimen. This <Term id="growth-plate">growth plate</Term> is a region of cartilage where a young long bone lengthens. It appears in this separate specimen, while the whole skeleton shows an adult. Compare the views before and after the growth plate closes. They show changing tissues, rather than calculating a child’s height.

When a growth plate closes, bone replaces it and lengthening stops there. The bone remains active. <Term id="remodelling">Remodelling</Term> continues as old bone tissue is removed and new tissue forms. The highlighted patch shows this local renewal. It does not measure how fast a person’s cells work.

The repair specimens show a possible sequence after a fracture, or broken bone. Blood forms a clot near the break, called a haematoma. A softer callus can bridge the break. A bony callus follows, and the bone can remodel. Compare the tissues in each specimen. The row follows the order of the process, but the stages do not take equal lengths of time.

An injured growing bone may need prompt medical assessment, especially when the growth plate is involved. These specimens cannot show whether an injured bone is aligned or when it is safe to resume an activity. Tell an adult about an injury. Do not try to move an injured limb back into place or use the stages to set a healing deadline.

<More title={{"en": "Growth plates close at different times", "zh": "生长板何时闭合"}}>

Growth plates close at different times in different bones and people. There is no single age when all close. The adult skeleton has not been shrunk into a child, and the separate specimen does not estimate bone age [S19,S34].

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Repair takes time", "zh": "修复需要时间"}}>

Healing time depends on the injury and care. A cast or other support helps hold a fracture in place while living tissues repair it. The cast itself does not make new bone. Health professionals assess the injury and choose treatment [S6,S19].

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

看看幼骨标本靠近端部的带状区域。这是<Term id="growth-plate">生长板</Term>，由软骨组成，幼年长骨在这里增长。它出现在这个独立标本里，完整骨架则展示成人。比较生长板闭合前后的画面，可以看见组织的变化，不能用来计算孩子会长多高。

生长板闭合后，骨取代了原来的软骨，该处不再纵向增长。但骨仍然是活跃的组织。<Term id="remodelling">重塑</Term>会继续：旧骨组织被去除，新骨组织形成。显出的斑块表示局部更新，并不测量某个人细胞工作的速度。

旁边的修复标本展示骨折后可能出现的过程。断裂附近先形成血液凝块，称为血肿。随后，较软的骨痂可连接断处，再形成骨性骨痂，继续重塑。逐个比较，可以看见组织怎样改变。这一列按过程先后排列，各阶段所需时间并不相同。

生长中的骨受伤后可能需要及时医疗评估，尤其是伤到生长板时。标本不能判断受伤的骨是否对齐，也不能决定何时恢复活动。受伤后要告诉成人。不要尝试把受伤肢体扳回原位，也不要按图中的阶段给自己定下愈合日期。

<More title={{"en": "Growth plates close at different times", "zh": "生长板何时闭合"}}>

不同骨、不同人的生长板，闭合时间不一样，没有统一年龄。成人骨架没有缩成儿童，独立标本也不能估算骨龄[S19,S34]。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Repair takes time", "zh": "修复需要时间"}}>

愈合时间与损伤和照护有关。石膏或其他支持物帮助固定断骨，让活组织修复。石膏本身不会制造新骨。医疗人员负责评估损伤和选择治疗[S6,S19]。

</More>
