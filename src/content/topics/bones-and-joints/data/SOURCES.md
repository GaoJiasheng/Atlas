# 骨骼与关节 · Sources

本主题为小四人体系统总览的深化内容[S1]。成人骨架、真实关节表面与肌肉来自既有Z-Anatomy/BodyParts3D源体；教学截面、修复标本、理想杠杆是单独的定性设计。模型部件数不能代替人体骨数；动作与力值不是学生的身体标准。

This is enrichment beyond the P4 systems overview. The adult reference anatomy and the independent teaching specimens serve different purposes. No child body, density, height or healing-date prediction is made.

## Fact and number discipline

正文数量用`<Num s>`，字幕与零件详情使用相同来源事实；字幕为纯文本，保留定稿措辞。常规成人206骨和12对肋骨是TYPICAL；杠杆、动作角度、布局都是DESIGN/SIM。表值24组合均由d=L sinθ、τ=W d、Fm=τ/r定义，不把150牛顿解释为学生应举的负载。

Source-derived quantities are TYPICAL. Model constants and calculations are DESIGN/SIM. All chapter prose quantities use Num citations. Captions remain plain text. The complete formula and omitted effects are in S34; the model does not convert force to a person’s lifting ability.
