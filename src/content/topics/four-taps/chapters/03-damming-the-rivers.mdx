<Lang en>

In 1971 a Water Planning Unit was set up under the Prime Minister's Office to find more water for the newly independent country. Its Water Master Plan of 1972 became the long-term blueprint: collect rain from catchments with towns in them as well as from the forest, and study new sources such as reusing water and desalination.

The first step was to make the old reservoirs bigger. Seletar Reservoir was enlarged in the late 1960s, and in 1975 a higher dam upstream of the Peirce Reservoir created Upper Peirce Reservoir, then Singapore's largest.

The next step was to dam rivers near the sea. Behind a dam across a river mouth the water slowly turns fresh, making an <Term id="estuary-reservoir">estuary reservoir</Term>. Under the Kranji–Pandan Water Scheme, completed in 1975, the Kranji and Pandan rivers in the west were closed this way; Jurong Lake had been formed in 1971 by damming the upper Jurong River. Between 1975 and 1981 the Murai, Poyan, Sarimbun and Tengeh rivers on the west coast were dammed as well, and in 1986 the Sungei Seletar–Bedok Water Scheme added Lower Seletar Reservoir and Bedok Reservoir, the second made from a sand quarry.

These catchments are not forest. Pandan, Bedok and Lower Seletar collect rain from land where homes and industries that do not pollute are allowed, so PUB calls them unprotected catchments. Better water treatment made it possible to collect and treat their water for drinking, with strict pollution control.

<More title={{ en: "Why rivers near the sea?", zh: "为什么在靠海的河上筑坝？" }}>

A river collects rain from all the land upstream of it. Damming it near its mouth catches the water of the whole valley, including towns, instead of only a small hilly area. PUB's estuarine reservoirs of this period include Kranji, Sarimbun, Murai, Poyan and Tengeh. The same idea, on a larger scale, later closed the mouth of the Marina Channel in the city centre.

</More>

<Lang zh>

1971 年，总理公署设立水务规划组，为刚独立的新加坡寻找更多水源。它在 1972 年制定的水务总蓝图成了长远发展的依据：不但收集森林里的雨水，也收集有市镇的集水区里的雨水；还要研究循环用水和海水淡化这样的新水源。

第一步是把旧蓄水池扩大。实里达蓄水池在 1960 年代后期扩建；1975 年，贝雅士蓄水池上游筑起一道更高的坝，形成了当时新加坡最大的蓄水池——贝雅士蓄水池上段。

下一步是在靠海的河上筑坝。在河口筑坝后，坝内的水会慢慢变成淡水，这就是<Term id="estuary-reservoir">河口蓄水池</Term>。1975 年完成的克兰芝—班丹水源计划，就这样封住了西部的克兰芝河和班丹河；更早在 1971 年，裕廊河上游筑坝形成了裕廊湖。1975 年至 1981 年间，西海岸的慕莱河、波扬河、莎琳汶河和登格河也先后筑坝。1986 年，实里达河—勿洛水源计划又增加了实里达蓄水池下段和勿洛蓄水池，后者由采沙场改建而成。

这些集水区不是森林。班丹、勿洛和实里达下段收集的雨水，来自允许建住宅和无污染工业的土地，所以 PUB 称之为非保护集水区。水处理技术进步了，加上严格的污染管制，这些水才能收集起来、处理成饮用水。

<More title={{ en: "Why rivers near the sea?", zh: "为什么在靠海的河上筑坝？" }}>

一条河收集的是它上游所有土地上的雨水。在靠近河口的地方筑坝，就能接住整个河谷的水，连市镇也包括在内，而不只是一小片丘陵。PUB 这一时期的河口蓄水池有克兰芝、莎琳汶、慕莱、波扬和登格。后来，同样的办法在更大的规模上用在市中心，封住了滨海水道的出海口。

</More>
