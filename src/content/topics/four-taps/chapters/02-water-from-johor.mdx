<Lang en>

Johor, across the narrow Strait of Johor, has more land and bigger rivers than Singapore. In 1924 the Causeway joined the two by road and railway, and room was left in it for water pipes; in 1932 pipelines were added. Under a <Term id="water-agreement">water agreement</Term> of 1927, Singapore drew water from Johor. Singapore's sources say the water was free and Singapore paid rent for the land; Malaysian sources describe the water of 1927 to 1961 as free raw water.

Two agreements followed, with the Johor State government on one side and Singapore's water authority on the other; the 1965 separation agreement names it as PUB. The first, dated 1 September 1961, let Singapore draw water from the Tebrau and Scudai rivers and Gunong Pulai for <Num s="S3">50</Num> years. The second, signed on 29 September 1962, lets Singapore draw up to <Num s="S13,S16">250 million gallons</Num> a day from the Johor River for <Num s="S16,S17">99</Num> years, until 2061. The 1962 agreement also provides that Johor may receive treated water from Singapore: up to <Num s="S13">2 %</Num> of the water supplied to Singapore, which Malaysia's ministry gives as <Num s="S16">5 million gallons</Num> a day.

When Singapore separated from Malaysia in 1965, both governments guaranteed in the separation agreement that the two water agreements would be kept. In 1990 PUB and Johor agreed to build Linggiu Reservoir upstream, completed in 1993; Johor owns the dam, and PUB built and runs it.

The 1961 agreement ended on 31 August 2011, and its waterworks and pump houses were handed to Johor. The 1962 agreement runs until 2061, when, Johor's state government records, the Johor River Waterworks and the Linggiu Dam are to be handed to Johor.

<More title={{ en: "One agreement, two sets of words", zh: "同一份协议，两种说法" }}>

On dates and quantities the two sides' official sources agree: 29 September 1962, <Num s="S16,S17">99 years</Num>, up to <Num s="S13,S16">250 million gallons</Num> a day, and an end in 2061. They word some things differently. For Johor's share of treated water, Singapore's foreign ministry writes "<Num s="S13">up to 2 % (or 5 mgd)</Num>" of the water supplied to Singapore; Malaysia's ministry writes <Num s="S16">5 million gallons</Num> a day and quotes the <Num s="S16">2 %</Num> limit in Clause 11. Malaysia's ministry converts 250 million gallons to <Num s="S16">1,094 million litres</Num> a day. Both sides have also published statements on the agreement's price-review clause; this topic gives dates and quantities only and does not cover prices.

<Lang zh>

柔佛与新加坡只隔着狭窄的柔佛海峡，土地更多，河流也更大。1924 年启用的长堤用公路和铁路把两地连起来，堤里还为水管留了位置；1932 年，长堤上加装了输水管。1927 年的<Term id="water-agreement">水供协议</Term>让新加坡从柔佛取水。新加坡方面的来源记载，水是免费的，新加坡为集水区和水厂的土地支付租金；马来西亚方面的来源把 1927 年至 1961 年供应的水称为免费原水。

1961 年和 1962 年又各有一份协议，一方是柔佛州政府，另一方是新加坡的水务机构；1965 年的协议把它写作 PUB。1961 年 9 月 1 日的协议，让新加坡在 <Num s="S3">50</Num> 年里从地不佬河、士姑来河和布莱山取水。1962 年 9 月 29 日的协议，让新加坡在 <Num s="S16,S17">99</Num> 年里、到 2061 年为止，每日从柔佛河取水至多 <Num s="S13,S16">250 百万加仑</Num>。1962 年协议同时规定，柔佛可以获得新加坡供应的处理过的水，数量至多为新加坡供水量的 <Num s="S13">2 %</Num>；马来西亚部门给出的数字是每日 <Num s="S16">5 百万加仑</Num>。

1965 年 8 月 7 日，新加坡脱离马来西亚的协议在第 14 条里写明：新加坡政府保证 PUB、马来西亚政府保证柔佛州政府，都将遵守 1961 年和 1962 年的两份水供协议。1990 年，PUB 与柔佛州政府约定在上游兴建林木水库，1993 年建成；水坝属于柔佛，由 PUB 兴建和运作。

1961 年的协议在 2011 年 8 月 31 日到期，布莱山和士姑来的水厂，以及笨珍和地不佬的抽水站，交给了柔佛州政府。1962 年的协议到 2061 年为止；柔佛州政府的记录是，哥打丁宜的柔佛河水厂和林木水坝届时将交给柔佛。

<More title={{ en: "One agreement, two sets of words", zh: "同一份协议，两种说法" }}>

在日期和数量上，两国的官方来源一致：1962 年 9 月 29 日、<Num s="S16,S17">99 年</Num>、每日至多 <Num s="S13,S16">250 百万加仑</Num>、2061 年到期。有些地方两边措辞不同。说到柔佛可得的处理过的水，新加坡外交部写的是新加坡供水量的“<Num s="S13">至多 2 %（即 5 百万加仑）</Num>”；马来西亚部门写的是每日 <Num s="S16">5 百万加仑</Num>，并引用第 11 条里 <Num s="S16">2 %</Num> 的上限。马来西亚部门把 250 百万加仑换算为每日 <Num s="S16">1,094 百万升</Num>。双方对协议里的价格检讨条款都发表过说明；本主题只写日期和数量，不涉及价格。
