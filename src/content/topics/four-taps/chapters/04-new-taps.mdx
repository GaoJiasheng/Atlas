<Lang en>

Water that has gone down a sink or a toilet is what PUB calls <Term id="used-water">used water</Term>. It flows through sewers, and since 2008 partly through a deep tunnel, to water reclamation plants, where it is cleaned. In 2002 Singapore began to clean part of it again, much more thoroughly, to make <Term id="newater">NEWater</Term>. On 9 August 2002 <Num s="S21">60,000</Num> people at the National Day Parade drank a toast with bottles of it.

NEWater is made in three stages: microfiltration or ultrafiltration, then reverse osmosis, in which water is pushed through a <Term id="membrane">membrane</Term> that holds back salts and other dissolved substances, and finally ultraviolet light. It is very pure, so it is used mainly by factories. In dry spells some is added to the reservoirs, mixed with the rainwater and treated again at a waterworks before it reaches the tap. The first NEWater factories opened in 2003; today <Num s="S21,S25">four</Num> are working, two at Changi and one each at Kranji and Ulu Pandan.

The fourth tap is <Term id="desalination">desalination</Term>. The first plant opened at Tuas in September 2005. Today there are <Num s="S23">five</Num>: three at Tuas, one on Jurong Island and one at Marina East, which can treat either seawater or fresh water from Marina Reservoir. They also use reverse osmosis; pushing seawater through the membranes takes about <Num s="S23">3.5 kWh</Num> of electricity for every cubic metre of water.

PUB expects that by 2065, when Singapore may need almost twice as much water as today, NEWater and desalination will meet most of the demand. Neither depends on rain, so both keep working in a dry spell.

<More title={{ en: "Not straight from the sewer", zh: "不是直接喝污水" }}>

NEWater is not used water poured into a glass. The used water is first treated at a water reclamation plant, then purified in the three stages above. The NEWater that is added to reservoirs in dry periods is mixed with raw water and treated again at a waterworks, like all reservoir water, before it is supplied as tap water. How the plants inside work is shown in the 3D topic <TopicLink slug="water">Water in Singapore</TopicLink>.

</More>

<Lang zh>

从洗手盆和厕所流走的水，PUB 称为<Term id="used-water">用过的水</Term>。它经污水管——2008 年起部分经深隧道——流到再生水厂处理。2002 年起，新加坡把其中一部分再更彻底地净化一次，制成<Term id="newater">新生水</Term>。2002 年 8 月 9 日的国庆庆典上，<Num s="S21">6 万</Num>人举起新生水为国家庆生。

新生水分三步制成：先是微滤或超滤；然后是反渗透，把水压过一层<Term id="membrane">膜</Term>，盐和其他溶解在水里的物质被挡在外面；最后用紫外线消毒。新生水非常纯净，主要供工厂使用。天旱时，一部分新生水加进蓄水池，与雨水混合，再经水厂处理后才进入自来水管。第一批新生水厂在 2003 年启用；今天有 <Num s="S21,S25">4</Num> 座在运作，樟宜 2 座，克兰芝和乌鲁班丹各 1 座。

第四个水喉是<Term id="desalination">海水淡化</Term>。第一座淡化厂 2005 年 9 月在大士启用。今天共有 <Num s="S23">5</Num> 座：大士 3 座、裕廊岛 1 座、滨海东 1 座；滨海东的淡化厂既能处理海水，也能处理滨海蓄水池的淡水。淡化厂同样用反渗透；把海水压过膜，每生产一立方米水约用 <Num s="S23">3.5 千瓦时</Num>电。

PUB 预计，到 2065 年新加坡的用水量可能接近今天的两倍，届时新生水和淡化海水将满足大部分用水需求。这两个水喉不靠下雨，天旱时也能照常供水。

<More title={{ en: "Not straight from the sewer", zh: "不是直接喝污水" }}>

新生水不是把用过的水直接倒进杯子。用过的水先在再生水厂处理，再经上面说的三个步骤净化。天旱时加进蓄水池的新生水，和其他蓄水池的水一样，要与原水混合、在水厂再处理一遍，才作为自来水供应。水厂里面怎样运作，可以看三维主题<TopicLink slug="water">《新加坡的水》</TopicLink>。

</More>
