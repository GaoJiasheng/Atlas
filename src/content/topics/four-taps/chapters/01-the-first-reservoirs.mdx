<Lang en>

Singapore's first reservoir was completed in 1867 in the low hills north of the town: a <Term id="dam">dam</Term> held back a stream, and the water rose behind it. In 1922 it was named MacRitchie Reservoir, after James MacRitchie, the municipal engineer who, in PUB's words, designed and built it.

The town grew, and a second reservoir followed in the valley of the Kallang River, just north of the first. It was first called the Kallang River Reservoir, then Peirce Reservoir from 1922; today it is Lower Peirce Reservoir. By 1920 a third, Seletar Reservoir, had been built further north, "to meet growing water needs", in the words of PUB's history panel.

The three reservoirs have something in common: they lie in the forest that is today the Central Nature Reserve. PUB calls their catchments protected catchments, where major development is not allowed, so the raw water collected there is generally cleaner than water from towns. MacRitchie, Peirce and Seletar are, in PUB's words, Singapore's first <Num s="S4">three</Num> reservoirs.

The map shows today's water surfaces. Seletar was enlarged in 1940 and again in the late 1960s, so in these early years it looks larger here than it really was; the older, smaller outlines are not in any open dataset.

<More title={{ en: "Old names", zh: "旧名字" }}>

The reservoirs changed names several times. The Kallang River Reservoir became Peirce Reservoir in 1922 and Lower Peirce Reservoir in 1975, when a higher dam upstream created Upper Peirce Reservoir. Seletar Reservoir became Upper Seletar Reservoir in 1992, when the newer Sungei Seletar Reservoir downstream was renamed Lower Seletar Reservoir.

<Lang zh>

1867 年，新加坡第一个蓄水池在市镇北面的低丘里建成：一道<Term id="dam">水坝</Term>拦住溪流，坝后的水位升高，形成了蓄水池。

市镇不断扩大，加冷河谷里又建起第二个蓄水池，就在第一个的北边。它起初叫加冷河蓄水池，1922 年改名贝雅士蓄水池，今天叫贝雅士蓄水池下段。也是在 1922 年，第一个蓄水池以市政工程师麦里芝的名字命名为麦里芝蓄水池；按 PUB 的说法，它就是由麦里芝设计和兴建的。到 1920 年，更北的地方又建起第三个——实里达蓄水池。按照 PUB 历史展板的说法，建它是为了“满足不断增长的用水需求”。

这三个蓄水池有一个共同点：它们都在今天的中央自然保护区的森林里。PUB 把它们的集水区称为受保护集水区，区内不允许大型开发，所以那里收集的原水一般比市镇里的干净。按 PUB 的说法，麦里芝、贝雅士和实里达是新加坡最早的 <Num s="S4">3</Num> 个蓄水池。

地图上画的是今天的水面。实里达蓄水池在 1940 年和 1960 年代后期扩建过，所以在这些早年的画面里，它看上去比当时实际的要大；早年较小的轮廓没有任何公开数据记录。

<More title={{ en: "Old names", zh: "旧名字" }}>

这些蓄水池几次改名。加冷河蓄水池 1922 年改名贝雅士蓄水池；1975 年上游筑起更高的坝、形成贝雅士蓄水池上段，原来的就改称贝雅士蓄水池下段。实里达蓄水池在 1992 年改称实里达蓄水池上段，下游较新的实里达河蓄水池则改称实里达蓄水池下段。

</More>
