<Lang en>

The Marina Channel in the city centre is where the Singapore and Kallang rivers meet the sea. Marina Barrage, a dam across its mouth, was opened on 31 October 2008. The barrage keeps the sea out; behind it the water slowly turned fresh, and on 20 November 2010 Marina Reservoir was commissioned as a freshwater reservoir: Singapore's <Num s="S12">15th</Num> reservoir and the first in the heart of the city. Its catchment of about <Num s="S12">10,000 hectares</Num> is the island's largest and most built-up.

In 2011 two more estuary reservoirs were commissioned in the north-east, Punggol and Serangoon. With Marina, they took Singapore's water catchment from about half of the land to about <Num s="S1">two-thirds</Num>, and the number of reservoirs to <Num s="S1,S25">17</Num>. All three have unprotected catchments, where homes and clean industries stand on the land the rain falls on. In a small, crowded country, most of the land is now also a place that collects water, so keeping drains and rivers clean matters for the water people drink.

Rain is still only one of four taps. Since 2011 the 1961 agreement with Johor has ended, while the 1962 agreement runs until 2061; NEWater and desalination keep growing. To follow a raindrop from the sky through a reservoir, a waterworks and a tap and back again, see the 3D topic [Water in Singapore](/en/topics/water/).

<More title={{ en: "The barrage, the city and the sea", zh: "堤坝、城市与大海" }}>

Before 2008 the Marina Channel was open to the sea, and its water was salty. On the earlier maps of this topic the bay is drawn as land and water as they are today, but without the blue tint of a reservoir. The shoreline itself has also changed: parts of the land around Marina Bay were reclaimed from the sea, and this topic's map shows only today's coast.

<Lang zh>

市中心的滨海水道是新加坡河与加冷河入海的地方。2008 年 10 月 31 日，横跨水道出海口的滨海堤坝启用。堤坝把海水挡在外面，坝内的水慢慢变淡，2010 年 11 月 20 日，滨海蓄水池作为淡水蓄水池正式启用，成为新加坡第 <Num s="S12">15</Num> 个蓄水池，也是第一个位于市中心的蓄水池。它的集水区约 <Num s="S12">1 万公顷</Num>，是全岛最大、城市化程度最高的集水区。

2011 年，东北部又有两个河口蓄水池启用：榜鹅和实龙岗。加上滨海，新加坡的集水区从约一半的土地扩大到约<Num s="S1">三分之二</Num>，蓄水池增加到 <Num s="S1,S25">17</Num> 个。这三个蓄水池的集水区都属于非保护集水区：雨水落下的地方就有住宅和无污染的工业。在这个又小又挤的国家，大部分土地如今同时也在收集雨水，所以保持水沟和河流清洁，关系到人们喝的水。

雨水仍然只是四个水喉之一。与柔佛的 1961 年协议已在 2011 年到期，1962 年协议到 2061 年为止；新生水和淡化海水还在增加。想跟着一滴雨，从天空经过蓄水池、水厂和水龙头再回到循环里，请看三维主题[《新加坡的水》](/zh/topics/water/)。
