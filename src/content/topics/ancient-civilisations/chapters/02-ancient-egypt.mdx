<Lang en>

Egypt depended on the yearly flood of the Nile to water the narrow strip of farmland along the river. Around 3000 BCE Upper Egypt, the river valley, and Lower Egypt, the delta, became one kingdom; by tradition King Menes, also called Narmer, joined them and founded the capital, Memphis. Egypt's kings, now called <Term id="pharaoh">pharaohs</Term>, are still grouped into the <Num s="S134">30</Num> dynasties of Manetho, an Egyptian priest.

About 2550 BCE the Great Pyramid was built at Giza for King Khufu, who reigned from about 2551 to 2528 BCE. It stood about <Num s="S123">147</Num> metres high. More than a thousand years later, in the New Kingdom, Egypt reached far up the Nile, and Thebes in the south was its capital, with the great temples of Karnak and Luxor.

Egyptians carved <Term id="hieroglyphs">hieroglyphs</Term> on monuments and wrote everyday texts in a quicker script called hieratic. After Alexander of Macedon took Egypt in 332 BCE, kings named Ptolemy ruled it. In 196 BCE a decree of Ptolemy V, apparently composed by priests at Memphis, was written in <Num s="S133">three</Num> scripts: hieroglyphs, demotic and Greek. A copy carved on stone was found centuries later at Rosetta, and in the 1820s Thomas Young and Jean-François Champollion used it to read hieroglyphs again.

In 30 BCE, after Octavian defeated Mark Antony and Queen Cleopatra, Egypt became a province of Rome. Its pyramids and temples are World Heritage Sites: Memphis with its pyramid fields, and Thebes, both listed in 1979.

<More title={{"en": "Who built the pyramids?", "zh": "谁修了金字塔"}}>

The two oldest accounts were written by Greek authors long afterwards. Herodotus, writing in the fifth century BCE, about two thousand years after Khufu, says the king forced all Egyptians to work for him, in gangs of <Num s="S124">100,000</Num> men, each gang for <Num s="S124">three</Num> months, and that the pyramid took <Num s="S124">20</Num> years. Diodorus, writing in the first century BCE, says <Num s="S125">360,000</Num> men worked for nearly <Num s="S125">20</Num> years, and that the people resented the kings for the hardship. Neither of them calls the builders slaves, and their numbers are their own.

Archaeologists have since dug up a large settlement at Giza, Heit el-Ghurab, which the excavators call the "Lost City of the Pyramids". Labourers, craftsmen, managers and officials lived there, all working for the king's tomb. The excavator Mark Lehner later suggested that the labourers who built the pyramids were probably also sailors. How the work was organised, and how many people did it, is still debated.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Did Egypt have slaves?", "zh": "埃及有没有奴隶"}}>

Egyptian texts mention several kinds of unfree people: foreign captives and slaves who were bought; Egyptians who lost their freedom through debt or famine; and people called up for forced labour on royal projects. In the New Kingdom, the Egyptologist Mark Janzen writes, most slaves were foreigners. Because no Egyptian law code survives, the legal position of slaves "was never fully explained", and scholars disagree about which of these groups should be called slaves at all.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "When did ancient Egypt end?", "zh": "古埃及“结束”于哪一年"}}>

There are two common answers. One is 332 BCE, when Alexander of Macedon conquered Egypt; kings named Ptolemy ruled it after him. The other is 30 BCE, when Egypt became a Roman province. Britannica's account of ancient Egypt uses both. The Rosetta decree shows that hieroglyphs were still being carved under the Ptolemies, so the answer depends on what one counts.

</More>

埃及靠尼罗河每年的泛滥，浇灌河两岸窄窄的一条农田。约公元前 3000 年，上埃及（河谷）和下埃及（三角洲）合成一个王国；按传统说法，美尼斯王（也叫纳尔迈）统一两地，建都孟菲斯。埃及的国王今天称为<Term id="pharaoh">法老</Term>，至今仍按埃及祭司马涅托划分的 <Num s="S134">30</Num> 个王朝排列。

约公元前 2550 年，吉萨建起为胡夫王修的大金字塔。胡夫约在公元前 2551 年至前 2528 年在位，金字塔原高约 <Num s="S123">147</Num> 米。一千多年后的新王国时期，埃及的范围沿尼罗河远远向南伸展，南方的底比斯成为都城，那里有卡纳克和卢克索的大神庙。

埃及人把<Term id="hieroglyphs">古埃及象形文字（学名圣书体）</Term>刻在纪念建筑上，日常文书则用一种写得较快的僧侣体。公元前 332 年马其顿的亚历山大夺取埃及以后，名叫托勒密的国王们统治这里。公元前 196 年，托勒密五世的一道诏令（看来出自孟菲斯的祭司之手）用<Num s="S133">三</Num>种文字写成：圣书体、世俗体和希腊文。刻有诏令的一块石碑后来在罗塞塔出土；19 世纪 20 年代，托马斯·杨和商博良借助它重新读懂了圣书体。

公元前 30 年，屋大维打败马克·安东尼和克娄巴特拉女王，埃及成为罗马的一个行省。孟菲斯及其金字塔群、底比斯，都在 1979 年列为世界遗产。

<More title={{"en": "Who built the pyramids?", "zh": "谁修了金字塔"}}>

最早的两种说法，都出自很久以后的希腊作者。希罗多德生活在公元前 5 世纪，比胡夫晚约两千年，他说国王强迫所有埃及人为他干活，每 <Num s="S124">10 万</Num>人一队，每队干 <Num s="S124">三</Num>个月，金字塔用了 <Num s="S124">20</Num> 年才建成。公元前 1 世纪的狄奥多罗斯则说，有 <Num s="S125">36 万</Num>人干了将近 <Num s="S125">20</Num> 年，百姓因为劳苦而怨恨国王。两人都没有说修金字塔的是奴隶，这些数字也只是他们自己的说法。

后来，考古学家在吉萨挖出一处大型聚落，叫希特·古拉布，发掘者称它为“金字塔失落之城”。那里住着劳工、工匠、管理者和官员，都为国王的陵墓工作。主持发掘的马克·莱纳后来认为，修金字塔的劳工很可能同时也是水手。这项工程怎样组织、到底有多少人参加，至今仍有争论。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Did Egypt have slaves?", "zh": "埃及有没有奴隶"}}>

埃及文献提到好几类不自由的人：外族俘虏和买来的奴隶；因欠债或饥荒失去自由的埃及人；被征召去为王室工程服劳役的人。埃及学家马克·詹森写道，新王国时期的奴隶大多是外族人。由于没有埃及的法典流传下来，奴隶的法律地位“从未说清楚”；这几类人里哪些该算作奴隶，学者意见不一。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "When did ancient Egypt end?", "zh": "古埃及“结束”于哪一年"}}>

常见的答案有两个。一个是公元前 332 年，马其顿的亚历山大征服埃及，此后由名叫托勒密的国王们统治；另一个是公元前 30 年，埃及成为罗马的行省。《不列颠百科全书》讲古埃及时，两种算法都用。罗塞塔石碑上的诏令说明，托勒密王朝时还有人在刻圣书体，所以答案取决于看重什么。

</More>
