<Lang en>

Some ways of writing have lasted far longer than the kingdoms that used them. Cuneiform, Egyptian hieroglyphs, the oracle bones of the Shang and the Old Malay inscriptions of Sumatra were all early ways of writing languages down. The letters used today for English and for modern Malay are the Latin alphabet, which can be traced back through Etruscan and Greek letters to the Phoenician alphabet. Where the very first alphabet came from is still debated; one theory points to Egyptian signs.

Ways of counting lasted too. The <Num s="S501">60</Num> minutes of an hour are usually traced to counting in sixties in Mesopotamia. Some of the earliest dated zeros come from Southeast Asia and India: a Khmer inscription of 683 CE and an inscription at Gwalior of 876 CE. Laws written down for all to see go back to the code named after Ur-Nammu and to Rome's Twelve Tables. Singapore today is a republic, a word from the Latin res publica, “the public thing”.

Several inventions still in use have early histories in Asia. Tradition credits Cai Lun with making paper about 105 CE; a printed Buddhist scroll dated 868 CE survives; sailors in China and Europe used the compass by the 12th century. Some of the earliest known traces of cotton come from the Indus cities, and chess first appeared in India about the 6th century CE.

Many places in this topic are now World Heritage Sites, which the countries they are in have agreed to protect. In Southeast Asia they include Borobudur, Angkor, Sukhothai and Melaka. In Singapore the Botanic Gardens are on the list: a British colonial garden of 1859, not an ancient site.

<More title={{"en": "Zero: who was “first”?", "zh": "零：谁“最早”"}}>

Nobody can say who first wrote a zero, only where the earliest dated examples have been found. A stone found in 1891 near Sambor on the Mekong, in Cambodia, is dated 605 in the Saka era, which is 683 CE; in 1931 the scholar Georges Cœdès read its middle sign as a zero. An inscription in a temple at Gwalior in India, dated 876 CE, also uses zero.

The Bakhshali manuscript may be older. In 2017 the Bodleian Libraries in Oxford announced that carbon dating showed its oldest parts date from as early as the 3rd or 4th century, and that it is made of material from at least <Num s="S502">three</Num> different periods. A group of historians of mathematics replied that the earliest page is unlikely to give the date of the whole text. Both views are still argued.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "How the alphabet travelled", "zh": "字母是怎样传下来的"}}>

The Greeks took their letters from the Phoenicians, who wrote only consonants, and turned some of the signs into vowels between 800 and 700 BCE. A Greek alphabet reached the Etruscans in Italy, and the Romans developed the Latin alphabet from theirs before 600 BCE. It is now the standard script of English. Modern Malay is written in the Latin alphabet and in Jawi; the earliest writing in Malay, Sumatran inscriptions of the late 7th century, used a script from southern India.

</More>

有些书写方式，比使用它们的王国存在得久得多。楔形文字、古埃及象形文字、商朝的甲骨文、苏门答腊的古马来文碑文，都是早期把语言写下来的方法。今天英文和现代马来文用的是拉丁字母，它可以经过伊特鲁里亚字母和希腊字母，追溯到腓尼基字母。最早的字母从哪里来，至今还有争论，其中一种说法认为它源自埃及的符号。

计数的方法也流传下来。一小时 <Num s="S501">60</Num> 分钟，通常追溯到美索不达米亚以 60 为单位的计数。已知最早有日期的零，有几个出自东南亚和印度：公元 683 年的一块高棉碑文，以及公元 876 年瓜廖尔的一篇铭文。把法律写下来让大家看，可以追溯到以乌尔纳姆命名的法典和罗马的十二铜表法。今天的新加坡是共和国，republic 一词来自拉丁文 res publica，意思是“公共的事”。

今天仍在用的好几样发明，早期的历史都在亚洲。按传统说法，蔡伦约在公元 105 年造出了纸；一卷标着公元 868 年的佛经印本保存至今；到 12 世纪，中国和欧洲的水手已经在用指南针。已知最早的棉的痕迹，有一些来自印度河城市；象棋约在公元 6 世纪最早出现在印度。

这个主题里的许多地方，如今都是世界遗产，所在的国家承诺保护它们。东南亚的世界遗产包括婆罗浮屠、吴哥、素可泰和马六甲。新加坡植物园也在名录上，它是 1859 年建立的英国殖民时期植物园，不是古迹。

<More title={{"en": "Zero: who was “first”?", "zh": "零：谁“最早”"}}>

谁最早写出零，没有人说得清；能说的只是，最早有日期的例子在哪里找到。1891 年在柬埔寨湄公河边的三波附近发现的一块石碑，年份是塞种纪元 605 年，也就是公元 683 年；1931 年，学者赛代斯认定其中间的符号是零。印度瓜廖尔一座神庙里的铭文，年代是公元 876 年，也用了零。

巴克沙利手稿可能更早。2017 年，牛津大学博德利图书馆宣布，碳十四测年显示手稿最早的部分可到 3 或 4 世纪，而且手稿由至少<Num s="S502">三</Num>个不同时期的材料组成。几位数学史学者回应说，测得最早的一页，不太可能代表整部文本的年代。两种看法至今仍在争论。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "How the alphabet travelled", "zh": "字母是怎样传下来的"}}>

希腊人的字母学自腓尼基人。腓尼基字母只写辅音，希腊人在公元前 800 至前 700 年间把其中一些符号改成元音字母。一种希腊字母传到意大利的伊特鲁里亚人那里，罗马人又在公元前 600 年以前由伊特鲁里亚字母发展出拉丁字母。今天它是英文的标准文字。现代马来文用拉丁字母书写，也用爪夷文；马来语最早的文字，是 7 世纪后期的苏门答腊碑文，用的是一种源自印度南部的字母。

</More>
