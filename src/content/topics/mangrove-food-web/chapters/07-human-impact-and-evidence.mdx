<Lang en>

Select the tree, fallen leaves and prop roots. Clearing trees changes the supply of new leaves and can remove shelter. The food web helps us trace the leaf-supply change. Old leaves remain, and the loss of hiding places is another effect to consider.

Changes in conditions also affect survival. In an unfavourable environment, organisms may survive with suitable adaptations, move elsewhere or die. A pollutant’s effect depends on the substance, how much reaches the organisms and which organisms are exposed. Evidence about these conditions helps explain the outcome.

People can also protect and restore habitats through conservation and reforestation. Mangrove roots provide shelter and help protect the coast. Protecting the habitat supports more relationships than a single food arrow can show. Observations before and after a restoration can help us judge its success.

Finish by recording two rows: one with the whole web, and one with fiddlers removed. Inspect the mudskipper at the same low tide in both. Explain what you changed and kept the same. Use the food count and the remaining climbing-crab path as evidence. The fish has fewer foods in this model; we need more evidence to predict its future population.

<More title={{"en": "Threats to mangroves", "zh": "红树林面临的威胁"}}>

NParks identifies urbanisation and habitat loss as threats to Singapore mangroves. Measuring a particular development’s effects needs observations of that habitat. A food-path diagram alone cannot compare land uses or measure habitat loss and deaths.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Observing a real habitat", "zh": "观察真实栖息地"}}>

Use the controls to change the diagram. When visiting a mangrove, leave living things in place. Observe from the visitor route and record the conditions and behaviour you can see. Note when animals are hidden.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

选中树、落叶和支柱根。清除树木会改变新叶的供应，也可能失去藏身处。食物网帮助我们追踪叶片供应的变化。旧落叶仍会保留，躲藏处减少则是另一种需要考虑的影响。

环境条件改变也会影响生存。条件不利时，生物可能凭适合的适应特征继续生存、迁到别处或死亡。污染物的影响取决于物质种类、到达生物的量，以及哪些生物接触了它。了解这些条件，才能解释结果。

人们也能通过保育和重新造林来保护、修复栖息地。红树根提供庇护，也帮助保护海岸。保护栖息地所维系的关系，远多于一条食物箭头。比较修复前后的观察，有助于判断成效。

最后记录两行：一行保留完整食物网，另一行移走招潮蟹。两次都在相同低水位查看弹涂鱼。说清改变什么、保持什么。用食物计数和剩余的攀爬蟹类路径支持解释。在这个模型里，鱼的食物减少了；要预测它未来的种群数量，还需要更多证据。

<More title={{"en": "Threats to mangroves", "zh": "红树林面临的威胁"}}>

NParks 把城市化和栖息地损失列为新加坡红树林面临的威胁。要衡量某项开发的影响，需要观察当地栖息地。单靠食物路径图，不能比较土地用途，也不能测量栖息地损失和死亡数量。

</More>
