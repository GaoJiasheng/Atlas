<Lang en>

Look across the mudbank. Trees rise above the water, crabs gather near the roots, a mudskipper rests on the mud, and a heron stands beside the channel. Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve is home to mangroves and many kinds of animals. This model brings some of them together so we can explore how they live.

Select a tree or animal to read about it. Use the chapter index to find it again. The beat marks take you from the whole habitat to roots and food pathways. Close-ups make small structures easier to see.

The food-web card has <Num s="S30">6</Num> nodes and <Num s="S30">6</Num> possible feeding links. A node is a point representing an organism or a group of organisms. Read each arrow from food to the organism that eats it.

Later, predict what will happen when you remove one node. Then try it and record the change. Follow the remaining paths to explain your result.

<More title={{"en": "Reading the model", "zh": "怎样看模型"}}>

The mudbank and animals are arranged for learning. Their positions and numbers do not show a survey of Sungei Buloh. The tide and enlarged microbe and gill diagrams are schematic, with no shared scale. The food web combines possible relationships from different observations; they need not all occur together in one place. It traces food paths, without predicting population sizes.

</More>

Revisit animal features in <TopicLink slug="living-things" ch="animal-groups">Classifying Living Things</TopicLink>. Here we explore how conditions and relationships affect survival.

</More>

<Lang zh>

先看泥岸全景。树伸出水面，蟹在根边活动，弹涂鱼停在泥上，鹭站在潮沟旁。双溪布洛湿地保护区有红树林，也有许多动物。这个模型把其中一些生物放在一起，帮助我们了解它们怎样生活。

点选树或动物，读一读它的结构和作用。想再看一次，可以从本章索引找到它。依次点节拍刻度，便能从栖息地全景看到根部和食物路径。细小的结构会放大显示，方便观察。

食物网卡上有 <Num s="S30">6</Num> 个节点和 <Num s="S30">6</Num> 条可能的取食关系。节点是图上的一个点，代表一种或一组生物。每条箭头从食物指向吃它的生物。

后面可以试着移走一个节点。先预测，再操作并记录变化。沿着剩下的路径，解释你的观察。

<More title={{"en": "Reading the model", "zh": "怎样看模型"}}>

泥岸与动物的位置和数量是教学安排，不是双溪布洛的调查记录。潮位、放大的微生物和鳃腔都是示意，各对象没有共同的实物比例。食物网综合不同观察中的可能关系，它们未必同时出现在同一地方。图能帮助追踪食物路径，但不预测种群数量。

</More>
