<Lang en>

Keep the tide, food links and selected mudskipper the same. Predict what will happen to its food count when you remove the fiddler crabs. Then try it. In this model, the count falls from <Num s="S30">2</Num> to <Num s="S30">1</Num>. The fish can still receive food through the climbing-crab path.

Now remove algae and select the fiddlers. Their only food path in the diagram is lost. The card says “No food path shown”, but the animal stays visible. This tells us about the paths in the diagram, rather than how long a crab can survive.

Keep algae removed and select the mudskipper. Algae loss affects it indirectly: algae feed fiddlers, which can feed the fish. Follow the other route from leaves through climbing crabs to the mudskipper. This route still supplies one food.

Try removing the heron instead. The mudskipper loses one predator shown in the web. Then remove the mudskipper: the heron loses its only shown food path. Real outcomes also depend on other foods, movement, reproduction and time. Fewer predators or food paths alone cannot tell us how a population will change.

<More title={{"en": "Reading the counts", "zh": "怎样读计数"}}>

“Nodes remaining” counts nodes you have not removed. “Nodes reached from producers” counts those connected to an available producer by food paths. “Links remaining” counts the links connecting those nodes. These are diagram counts, not animal counts. An animal with no path here may find other foods in nature.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "What removal means", "zh": "移走表示什么"}}>

The control stops one node’s continuing food supply and closes its links. It leaves out existing litter and dead animals, reproduction, animals moving in and alternative foods. The diagram changes immediately; real biological changes take time.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

保持潮位、食物关系和查看的弹涂鱼相同。先预测：移走招潮蟹后，鱼的食物种类会怎样？再试一试。在这个模型里，计数从 <Num s="S30">2</Num> 种减为 <Num s="S30">1</Num> 种。经过攀爬蟹类的路径仍能向鱼提供食物。

再移走藻类，选中招潮蟹。图中它唯一的食物路径中断了。卡片显示“图内无食物路径”，动物仍留在画面中。这说明图里的路径变化，不能告诉我们蟹还能活多久。

保持藻类被移走，选中弹涂鱼。失去藻类会间接影响鱼：藻类供招潮蟹取食，招潮蟹又能成为鱼的食物。再沿另一条路径看，叶片经过攀爬蟹类，仍能向弹涂鱼提供一种食物。

换成移走鹭，弹涂鱼便少了一个图中的捕食者。再移走弹涂鱼，鹭便失去图中唯一的食物路径。真实结果还受其他食物、迁移、繁殖和时间影响。仅看捕食者或食物路径减少，不能确定种群数量怎样变化。

<More title={{"en": "Reading the counts", "zh": "怎样读计数"}}>

“剩余节点”数没有被移走的节点。“生产者可到达的节点”数能沿食物路径从现有生产者到达的节点。“剩余连线”数连接这些节点的线。这些是图中的计数，不是动物数量。图内没有食物路径的动物，在自然界仍可能找到其他食物。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "What removal means", "zh": "移走表示什么"}}>

控件停止一个节点持续提供食物，并关闭它的连线。计算没有包括原有落叶与动物尸体、繁殖、动物迁入和其他食物。图会立即改变，真实生物的变化则需要时间。

</More>

</Lang>
