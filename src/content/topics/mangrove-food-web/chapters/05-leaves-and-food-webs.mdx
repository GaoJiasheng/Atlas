<Lang en>

Look below the trees at the fallen leaves. Some sesarmid crabs at Sungei Buloh eat leaf material and begin breaking it into smaller pieces. The leaves are dead, but their material still contains food energy. This is a way that material produced by a living tree can enter feeding pathways after a leaf falls.

The microbe close-up shows another part of the process. Bacteria and fungi break down leaf litter. A <Term id="decomposer">decomposer</Term> obtains energy from organic material while breaking it down; it does not make food from sunlight as a plant does. The enlarged shapes stand for organisms involved in decay. They are diagrams beside the habitat, rather than huge microbes on the mudbank.

Fragments of dead organic material are called <Term id="detritus">detritus</Term>. Decomposition can return materials to the environment, where they can enter other processes. Keep material movement separate from energy transfer. Energy moves through the feeding pathways and is dissipated as heat; it does not travel back to the Sun in a recycling loop. A return of mineral nutrients would need a different kind of arrow.

Several chains joined by shared nodes form a <Term id="food-web">food web</Term>. In this model, the climbing-crab functional group receives <Num s="S30">2</Num> selected inputs, leaf material and algae. The group combines different crab kinds and accounts; it does not say one Episesarma species has both diets at this place. Shared nodes give routes to compare, while equal line widths say nothing about equal meals or equal importance.

The NParks illustrated newsletter has indexed text about algae-grazing climbing crabs, including Selatium, and leaf feeding is reported for sesarmids. Only the indexed panel text was available here. The broad functional node preserves those distinctions rather than presenting a single-species diet. Its food links are selected possibilities for a conditional teaching graph.

<Lang zh>

看树下的落叶。双溪布洛有些相手蟹会取食叶片，先把叶弄成较小的碎片。叶已经死亡，其中仍含有食物的能量。这说明活树产生的物质，在叶片掉落后仍能进入取食路径。

微生物近景显示过程的另一部分。细菌和真菌会分解落叶。<Term id="decomposer">分解者</Term>分解有机物时，也从中取得能量；它们不像植物那样利用阳光制造食物。旁边放大的形状表示参与分解的生物，是独立示意图，不是泥岸上巨大的微生物。

死亡有机物的碎片称为<Term id="detritus">碎屑</Term>。分解可使物质回到环境，进入别的过程。要把物质的移动与能量传递分开：能量沿食物路径传递，并以热的形式散失，不会绕一圈回到太阳。若要画矿物质养分的回流，就需要另一类箭头，不能混进取食图。

几条食物链共用节点，连起来形成<Term id="food-web">食物网</Term>。在这个模型里，攀爬蟹类功能组有 <Num s="S30">2</Num> 种选入的食物：叶片与藻类。这个组综合不同蟹类和资料，不表示某一种 Episesarma 在此地同时有这两种食性。共享节点让我们比较路径；线画得一样粗，不表示食量或重要性相同。
