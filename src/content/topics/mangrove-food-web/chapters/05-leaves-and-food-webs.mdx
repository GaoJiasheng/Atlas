<Lang en>

Look at the fallen leaves below the trees. Some sesarmid crabs at Sungei Buloh eat leaf material and break it into smaller pieces. A leaf is dead, but it still contains food energy. Material made by a living tree can therefore feed other organisms after the leaf falls.

Bacteria and fungi also break down leaf litter. A <Term id="decomposer">decomposer</Term> gets energy as it breaks down organic material, such as dead leaves and animal remains. Unlike plants, these decomposers do not use sunlight to make food. The enlarged close-ups show some of the organisms involved.

Fragments of dead organic material are called <Term id="detritus">detritus</Term>. Decay returns materials to the environment, where they can be used again. Energy follows a different path: it passes along food chains and is dissipated as heat. It does not circle back to the Sun. An arrow for returning mineral nutrients would mean something different from a food arrow.

Food chains that share organisms join to form a <Term id="food-web">food web</Term>. In this model, the climbing-crab group has <Num s="S30">2</Num> foods: leaves and algae. It brings together several crab kinds. Compare how these shared food sources link the chains.

<More title={{"en": "Different climbing-crab kinds", "zh": "不同的攀爬蟹类"}}>

Some climbing crabs, including Selatium, graze on algae. Leaf feeding is reported for sesarmid crabs. The group joins these different kinds; it does not mean one Episesarma species eats both foods at the same place. Equal lines do not mean equal meals or equal importance.

</More>

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

看看树下的落叶。双溪布洛有些相手蟹会吃叶片，把叶弄成较小的碎片。叶已经死亡，却仍含有食物中的能量。因此，活树产生的物质在叶片掉落后，还能供其他生物取食。

细菌和真菌也会分解落叶。<Term id="decomposer">分解者</Term>分解有机物时，从中获得能量。有机物包括死叶和动物遗体。这些分解者不像植物那样利用阳光制造食物。放大的近景显示了参与分解的一些生物。

死亡有机物的碎片称为<Term id="detritus">碎屑</Term>。分解使物质回到环境中，再被利用。能量的去向不同：它沿食物链传递，并以热的形式散失，不会绕回太阳。表示矿物质养分回流的箭头，与食物箭头含义不同。

几条食物链共享生物，连起来形成<Term id="food-web">食物网</Term>。在这个模型里，攀爬蟹类有 <Num s="S30">2</Num> 种食物：叶片和藻类。这一组包含几类不同的蟹。看看共同的食物来源怎样把食物链连在一起。

<More title={{"en": "Different climbing-crab kinds", "zh": "不同的攀爬蟹类"}}>

一些攀爬蟹类，包括 Selatium，会刮食藻类；相手蟹则有吃叶片的记录。这一组综合不同蟹类，不表示同一种 Episesarma 在同一地方吃这两种食物。线画得一样粗，也不表示食量或重要性相同。

</More>
