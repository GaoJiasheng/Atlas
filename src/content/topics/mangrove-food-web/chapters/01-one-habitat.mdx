<Lang en>

Select the mudskipper. That individual is an <Term id="organism">organism</Term>, one living thing. A <Term id="population">population</Term> means organisms of the same kind living and reproducing in a place at a time. Looking at a single fish cannot tell you the size of its population. The few animal models here help us name relationships; their number does not represent a count of wild animals.

Different populations living together make a <Term id="community">community</Term>. Trees, algae, crabs, fish, birds, fungi and bacteria can be considered together, even when some are too small or hidden to see. The <Term id="habitat">habitat</Term> is the place where an organism lives. A <Term id="mangrove-forest">mangrove forest</Term> includes the coastal trees and the conditions and other living things around them.

Separate the kinds of evidence. Water, light and temperature are physical factors. Food is material an organism can use for nourishment. Other organisms may provide food, be predators or affect access to shelter. In the scene, mud and water are surroundings, while a fish and a crab are members of the living community. These categories help you explain a change precisely.

Now select the prop roots and the small fish. Roots support the tree, but they also provide hiding places for young animals. That is an interaction a food arrow does not describe. A diagram can be useful while leaving out an important relationship. When an explanation seems incomplete, ask which condition or interaction has not yet been included.

<More title={{"en": "A symbol is not a population count", "zh": "图标不等于种群数量"}}>

The model uses a group of fiddler-crab shapes and one fish shape. Comparing those drawn counts would not compare wild populations. A real count needs a place, time and observation method.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Hidden members of a community", "zh": "群落中看不见的成员"}}>

The close-up later introduces bacteria and fungi. Failing to see them in a wide view does not show that they are absent; the same caution applies to animals inside burrows.

</More>

<Lang zh>

先选弹涂鱼。这一只鱼是一个<Term id="organism">生物</Term>个体。<Term id="population">种群</Term>是在一定地点和时间生活、繁殖的同类生物。只看一只鱼，不能知道整个种群有多大；台上少量动物模型是用来辨认关系的，不代表野外数量。

不同种群生活在一起，组成<Term id="community">群落</Term>。树、藻类、蟹、鱼、鸟、真菌与细菌可以一起考虑，即使其中一些很小，或者躲着看不见。<Term id="habitat">生境</Term>是生物生活的地方。<Term id="mangrove-forest">红树林</Term>不仅有海岸上的树，也有周围的条件和其他生物。

解释时要分清证据的类别。水、光和温度是物理因素；食物是生物可以取用来获得营养的物质。其他生物可能提供食物、成为捕食者，或影响躲藏的条件。图中的泥和水是周围环境，鱼和蟹则是群落成员。分清这些类别，才能具体说明哪一种改变可能影响生存。

再选支柱根与小鱼。根支撑树体，也为幼小动物提供藏身处，这种关系不能用食物箭头表达。图有用，不等于它显示了全部关系；解释还不完整时，要找出尚未纳入的条件或相互作用。同一个对象可能同时参与多种关系，先确定正在解释的是哪一种。
