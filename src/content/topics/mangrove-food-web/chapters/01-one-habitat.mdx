<Lang en>

Select the mudskipper. This fish is an <Term id="organism">organism</Term>: one living thing. A <Term id="population">population</Term> is a group of the same kind of organism living and reproducing in a place at a time.

Different populations living together form a <Term id="community">community</Term>. Trees, algae, crabs, fish, birds, fungi and bacteria all belong to the mangrove community. An organism’s <Term id="habitat">habitat</Term> is the place where it lives. A <Term id="mangrove-forest">mangrove forest</Term> includes coastal trees, other living things and their surroundings.

Water, light and temperature are physical factors that affect survival. Food supplies nourishment. Other organisms may provide food, prey on an animal or affect its access to shelter. The mud and water surround the living community; the fish and crabs are members of it.

Now select the prop roots and small fish. The roots support the tree and provide hiding places for young animals. Shelter is another important relationship, beyond who eats whom.

<More title={{"en": "Counting a population", "zh": "怎样数一个种群"}}>

The drawn animals do not show wild population sizes. To compare real populations, record the place, time and method used to count them.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Hidden members of a community", "zh": "藏起来的群落成员"}}>

Bacteria and fungi can be too small to see in a wide view. Crabs can hide inside burrows. An organism may be present even when you cannot see it.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

选中弹涂鱼。这一条鱼是一个<Term id="organism">生物</Term>个体。<Term id="population">种群</Term>是在一定地点和时间生活、繁殖的同类生物。

不同种群生活在一起，组成<Term id="community">群落</Term>。树、藻类、蟹、鱼、鸟、真菌和细菌都是红树林群落的成员。<Term id="habitat">生境</Term>是生物生活的地方。<Term id="mangrove-forest">红树林</Term>包括海岸上的树、其他生物和周围环境。

水、光和温度是影响生存的物理因素。食物提供营养。其他生物可能提供食物、捕食动物，或影响它们寻找藏身处。泥和水是生物周围的环境；鱼和蟹是群落的成员。

再选支柱根与小鱼。根支撑树体，根之间的空隙也为幼小动物提供藏身处。除了取食关系，庇护也是生物之间的重要联系。
