<Lang en>

Start with the tree and algae. They use sunlight to make food, so they are <Term id="producer">producers</Term>. The living tree supplies leaves, including those that later fall. Dead leaves contain food, but cannot make more. Learn about food-making in <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="leaves-make-food">A Plant: leaves make food</TopicLink>.

An animal gets food from other organisms or their material. It is a <Term id="consumer">consumer</Term>. A <Term id="food-chain">food chain</Term> follows food from one organism to the next. One possible chain here runs from algae to an algae-feeding crab, then to a mudskipper and a heron. Each arrow points from food towards the eater.

A mudskipper can be a <Term id="predator">predator</Term> that catches a crab, and <Term id="prey">prey</Term> caught by a heron. NParks lists crustaceans and smaller fish among giant-mudskipper foods, and herons among its predators. The same animal can have both roles.

Energy passes along food chains, with some dissipated as heat. The arrows show possible routes; their width does not measure energy or the amount eaten. There is no fixed transfer percentage in this model. Explore changes in energy form in <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="never-used-up">Energy Conversion</TopicLink>.

<More title={{"en": "Diets can differ between observations", "zh": "不同观察中的食物差异"}}>

A general guide describes foods an animal can eat. One local sample may contain only some of them. Su and Lim reported no fiddler crabs or fish in their Lim Chu Kang sample. They also reported an observation of a mudskipper taking an Episesarma crab when the opportunity arose, without giving a feeding frequency. These differences are why the crab-to-fish arrows show possible relationships, rather than a fixed diet.

<Lang zh>

从树和藻类开始。它们利用阳光制造食物，是<Term id="producer">生产者</Term>。活树供应叶片，包括后来落下的叶。死叶含有食物，却不能再制造食物。植物怎样造食物，可看<TopicLink slug="plant" ch="leaves-make-food">《一株植物》的“叶子造食物”</TopicLink>。

动物从其他生物或其物质获得食物，是<Term id="consumer">消费者</Term>。<Term id="food-chain">食物链</Term>显示食物怎样从一种生物传给另一种。这里一条可能的链是：藻类、吃藻的蟹、弹涂鱼，再到鹭。每条箭头从食物指向吃它的生物。

弹涂鱼捕捉蟹时，是<Term id="predator">捕食者</Term>；被鹭捕捉时，是<Term id="prey">猎物</Term>。NParks 把甲壳类和小鱼列为弹涂鱼的食物，也把鹭列为它的捕食者。同一动物可以有这两种角色。

能量沿食物链传递，一部分散失为热。箭头显示可能的路径，线的粗细不表示能量多少或吃了多少。模型没有设定固定的能量传递百分比。能量形式怎样改变，可看<TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="never-used-up">《能量的转化》</TopicLink>。

一般指南介绍动物可能吃的食物，一次当地采样可能只找到其中一部分。Su 与 Lim 在林厝港的样本中没有检出招潮蟹或鱼。他们另报告一次弹涂鱼趁机会取食 Episesarma 蟹的观察，但没有给出取食频率。因此，蟹到鱼的箭头表示可能关系，不能当作固定食谱。
