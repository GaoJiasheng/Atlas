<Lang en>

The root view cuts through the mud to show connections. Api-api Putih has shallow lateral roots with upright, pencil-like <Term id="breathing-root">breathing roots</Term>. These take in oxygen above the muddy ground. Explain the relationship in order: muddy soil can be low in oxygen; this connected root system reaches the air; the exposed roots help with gas exchange. The upright pieces are roots, not separate seedlings.

The other tree, Bakau Minyak, has a different pattern. Its <Term id="prop-root">prop roots</Term> reach down from the trunk or branches and anchor it in soft mud. Keep the trees separate when describing them. A feature of one mangrove kind is not automatically a feature of all mangroves. The roots can also provide shelter, linking a plant structure to conditions for young animals.

Move to the mudskipper and its enlarged gill-chamber diagram. This animal is a fish. Retained seawater in modified gill chambers and oxygen uptake through the skin support life out of water; fleshy pectoral fins help it move. Its land activity does not make it a frog. Compare ways of obtaining oxygen in <TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs" ch="plants-fish-humans">Heart and Lungs</TopicLink>.

A male <Term id="fiddler-crab">fiddler crab</Term> has an enlarged claw and a small feeding claw. Claws and roots are structures; climbing or retreating into a burrow are behaviours. An <Term id="adaptation">adaptation</Term> is a structure or behaviour that helps an organism cope, feed, avoid predators or reproduce. Describe the feature and its function without suggesting that an animal chose to evolve it.

NParks records salt glands in Api-api Putih leaves. That is a feature of Avicennia alba; it is not a claim that every mangrove excretes salt through its leaves. Roots taking in water and mineral nutrients are explored in <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="roots-and-water">A Plant</TopicLink>.

<Lang zh>

切开泥层，先看根怎样连接。Api-api Putih 的浅层侧根向上长出铅笔状的<Term id="breathing-root">呼吸根</Term>，在泥面上方吸收氧气。解释时顺着条件、结构、作用来说：泥中可能缺氧，相连的根系伸到空气中，露出的根帮助气体交换。竖起的部分是根，不是独立的小苗。

另一株 Bakau Minyak 的根形不同。<Term id="prop-root">支柱根</Term>从干或枝向下伸入软泥，固定并支撑树体。描述时要把两株树分开：一种红树有的特征，不等于所有红树都有。根还能提供藏身处，把植物结构与幼小动物的生活条件联系起来。

再看弹涂鱼与放大的鳃腔示意。这是鱼。改良的鳃腔保留海水，皮肤也能吸收氧气，帮助它离水活动；肉质胸鳍帮助移动。能够在陆上活动，并不使它变成蛙。不同生物怎样取得氧气，可接着比较<TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs" ch="plants-fish-humans">《心脏与肺》</TopicLink>。

雄<Term id="fiddler-crab">招潮蟹</Term>有一只大螯和一只取食小螯。螯与根是结构，爬高、躲进洞里是行为。<Term id="adaptation">适应</Term>包括帮助生物应对条件、取食、避开捕食者或繁殖的结构与行为。写清特征和作用，不把演化写成动物作出的选择。说明作用时，必须指出对应的环境条件，不能只背一个结构名称。
