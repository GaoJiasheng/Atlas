<Lang en>

Cut through the mud to see the roots. Api-api Putih has shallow side roots with upright, pencil-like <Term id="breathing-root">breathing roots</Term>. Muddy soil can be low in oxygen. The upright roots reach above it and take in oxygen from the air. They join the side roots below the mud.

The neighbouring Bakau Minyak has a different root system. Its <Term id="prop-root">prop roots</Term> reach down from the trunk or branches and anchor the tree in soft mud. The spaces between roots also shelter young animals.

Now look at the mudskipper and the enlarged gill-chamber diagram. The mudskipper is a fish. Its modified gill chambers retain seawater, and its skin can take in oxygen. These features help it breathe out of water. Fleshy pectoral fins help it move across mud. Compare gas exchange in <TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs" ch="plants-fish-humans">Heart and Lungs</TopicLink>.

A male <Term id="fiddler-crab">fiddler crab</Term> has one enlarged claw and a smaller feeding claw. Claws and roots are structures. Climbing and retreating into a burrow are behaviours. An <Term id="adaptation">adaptation</Term> is a structure or behaviour that helps an organism cope with conditions, feed, avoid predators or reproduce.

Api-api Putih (Avicennia alba) has salt glands in its leaves. These help it excrete salt. Mangrove kinds handle salt in different ways. Learn how roots take in water and mineral nutrients in <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="roots-and-water">A Plant</TopicLink>.

<Lang zh>

切开泥层，看看根怎样连接。Api-api Putih 的浅层侧根向上长出铅笔状的<Term id="breathing-root">呼吸根</Term>。泥中可能缺氧，直立的根伸出泥面，从空气中吸收氧气。它们在泥下与侧根相连。

旁边的 Bakau Minyak 有另一种根系。<Term id="prop-root">支柱根</Term>从树干或枝条向下伸入软泥，固定并支撑树体。根之间的空隙也为幼小动物提供藏身处。

再看弹涂鱼和放大的鳃腔示意图。弹涂鱼是鱼。它特化的鳃腔能保留海水，皮肤也能吸收氧气，帮助它离水呼吸。肉质胸鳍帮助它在泥上移动。不同生物怎样交换气体，可看<TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs" ch="plants-fish-humans">《心脏与肺》</TopicLink>。

雄<Term id="fiddler-crab">招潮蟹</Term>有一只大螯和一只取食小螯。螯和根是结构，爬高和躲进洞里是行为。<Term id="adaptation">适应</Term>是帮助生物应对环境条件、取食、避开捕食者或繁殖的结构或行为。
