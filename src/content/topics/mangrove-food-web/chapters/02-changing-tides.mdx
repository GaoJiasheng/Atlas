<Lang en>

At low water, more mud is exposed beside the channel. Switch to high water: water covers more of the same bank. Mangrove shores lie in the <Term id="intertidal">intertidal zone</Term>, where the tide covers and exposes the ground.

Use Try it to change the tide. Keep the removal choice and inspected organism the same. Watch the water rise and the climbing crab move up the trunk. The food links and food counts stay the same: this control changes the view, without calculating how water depth affects feeding.

Find the burrow mouth at high tide. Fiddler crabs can stay in their burrows while the water is high. Their bodies are hidden in this view, but their food-web node remains. A crab that has hidden has not left the community.

Some mangrove crabs climb above incoming water. This may reduce encounters with predators in the water. Climbing is the behaviour we observe; avoiding predators is one possible explanation. Different crab kinds may climb for different reasons.

<More title={{"en": "The water heights in the model", "zh": "模型里的水位高度"}}>

At high water, the water moves up <Num s="S30">0.14</Num> model units and the climbing crabs move up <Num s="S30">0.16</Num> model units. These distances illustrate the change. They do not measure real tide heights, times or water volume, or predict a tide.

<Lang zh>

低水位时，潮沟旁露出较多泥岸。换到高水位，水便盖住更多泥面。红树林海岸属于<Term id="intertidal">潮间带</Term>，潮水涨退时，地面交替被水覆盖和露出。

在“试一试”里改变潮位，保持移走选择和查看对象相同。观察水面升高，树干上的蟹也向上移。食物关系和食物计数保持不变：这个控件只改变画面，没有计算水深怎样影响取食。

高潮时找一找洞口。招潮蟹会留在洞里。画面把它的身体藏起，食物网上的节点仍在。蟹藏起来，并没有离开群落。

有些红树林蟹会爬到涨水水面上方。这可能减少它们遇到水中捕食者的机会。爬高是观察到的行为；避开捕食者是解释原因的一种可能。不同蟹类爬高的原因也可能不同。
