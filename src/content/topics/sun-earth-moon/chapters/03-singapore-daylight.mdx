<Lang en>

The curve follows one fixed Singapore reference point through a year. Move its pointer and the sunrise, solar noon, sunset and daylight readings all change to the chosen date. These are calculated estimates, not measurements or a weather forecast. The nearby globe identifies a place; its fixed lighting does not show the Sun direction for every date on the curve.<Num s="S33,S36" />

Singapore lies close to the equator, so annual daylight changes are small. Near June's longest day in this table, daylight is about <Num s="S33">12 hours 12 minutes</Num>. Near December's shortest day it is about <Num s="S33">12 hours 3 minutes</Num>. Slide between them: similar day lengths still have a small, visible difference.<Num s="S11,S33" />

Day length alone cannot tell us the clock times of sunrise and sunset. Longitude, the time zone and the yearly difference between apparent solar time and mean clock time also matter. <Term id="solar-noon">Solar noon</Term> is when the Sun crosses the local meridian and reaches its highest daily position. It need not coincide with the clock's noon.<Num s="S26,S33" />

Clouds change how much direct sunshine reaches the ground. They do not redefine the astronomical daylight interval used by this calculation. Singapore's monsoons and inter-monsoon periods shape its weather even when daylight stays similar. Follow the role of clouds and rain in <TopicLink slug="water">Water</TopicLink>.<Num s="S11,S33" />

Read every time as an estimate for this reference point and its assumed horizon. The chart uses Singapore time, <Num s="S33">UTC+8</Num>, and displays minutes. Use <TopicLink slug="telling-time">Telling Time</TopicLink> to practise comparing clock readings; retain the separate daylight value when comparing dates.

<More title={{"en": "A standard calculated horizon", "zh": "标准计算地平线"}}>

The calculation uses a flat, unobstructed sea-level horizon and a solar centre threshold of <Num s="S26,S33">−0.833°</Num>. This approximates the solar disc and typical refraction. The earlier geometry chapters instead use the Sun centre at <Num s="S36">0°</Num>. Their results answer slightly different definitions of daylight. Buildings, terrain and actual atmospheric refraction can change a real observed sunrise or sunset.<Num s="S26" />

</More>

<More title={{"en": "One teaching reference point", "zh": "一个教学参考点"}}>

Atlas chose <Num s="S33">1.3342° N, 103.7357° E</Num> as the calculation input. This is a designated teaching point, not a claim about an official observatory coordinate or the skyline at your home. The table does not promise when sunlight will clear a neighbouring building.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A calculated year, not an observing log", "zh": "一年计算表，不是实测记录"}}>

The local table contains <Num s="S33">365 dates</Num> in 2026, calculated with a NOAA/Meeus approximation. NOAA notes atmospheric limits and that its calculator is no longer maintained. Reproducing the table checks the calculation; it does not independently verify a year of observations. Daylight is calculated before rounding the displayed times, so subtracting rounded sunset and sunrise may give a different minute value.<Num s="S26,S33" />

</More>

<Lang zh>

曲线追踪新加坡同一个参考点的全年日长。移动指针，日出、太阳正午、日落和白昼读数就换成所选日期。每个日期都沿用相同方法。曲线中的点来自计算估计，不是实测或天气预报；旁边地球只表示地点，固定的受光摆法不代表每个日期的太阳方向。<Num s="S33,S36" />

新加坡靠近赤道，全年日长变化较小。表中六月最长的一天附近，白昼约 <Num s="S33">12 小时 12 分钟</Num>；十二月最短的一天附近，约 <Num s="S33">12 小时 3 分钟</Num>。来回移动指针，可以看出“接近”仍有小差别。<Num s="S11,S33" />

只知道日长，算不出日出日落的钟点；经度、时区和视太阳时与平均钟表时间的全年差异也有影响。<Term id="solar-noon">太阳正午</Term>是太阳通过当地子午线、到达当天最高位置的时刻，不必正好是钟表十二点。<Num s="S26,S33" />

云会改变地面得到的直接日照，却不改变这份计算所用的天文白昼定义。即使日长相近，新加坡的季风期、季风间期仍有不同天气。云和雨在<TopicLink slug="water">《水》</TopicLink>中继续展开。<Num s="S11,S33" />

读数适用于这个参考点及假定地平线，采用新加坡时间 <Num s="S33">UTC+8</Num>，显示到分钟。比较钟表时刻可看<TopicLink slug="telling-time">《认识时间》</TopicLink>；比较日期的日长，则要保留表中单列的白昼值。

<More title={{"en": "A standard calculated horizon", "zh": "标准计算地平线"}}>

计算假设海平面处平坦无遮挡的地平线，以太阳中心高度 <Num s="S26,S33">−0.833°</Num>为阈值，近似计入太阳圆盘和典型折射。前面几何实验用太阳中心 <Num s="S36">0°</Num>，两种日长定义略有不同。建筑、地形和实际大气折射都会影响真实所见的升落。<Num s="S26" />

</More>
