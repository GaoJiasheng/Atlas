<Lang en>

The curve shows a year of calculated daylight at one Singapore reference point. Move the pointer to choose a date. The sunrise, solar noon, sunset and daylight readings change together. These are estimates. The globe marks the place; its fixed lighting does not follow the dates on the chart.<Num s="S33,S36" />

Singapore is close to the equator, so daylight changes little through the year. Near June's longest day in the table, daylight lasts about <Num s="S33">12 hours 12 minutes</Num>. Near December's shortest day, it lasts about <Num s="S33">12 hours 3 minutes</Num>. Slide between them to see the small difference.<Num s="S11,S33" />

Day length does not give us the clock times of sunrise and sunset. Longitude, the time zone and the yearly difference between sundial time and average clock time also matter. <Term id="solar-noon">Solar noon</Term> is when the Sun crosses the local north–south line in the sky and reaches its highest daily position. It need not happen at clock noon.<Num s="S26,S33" />

Clouds can reduce direct sunshine on the ground while the daylight interval stays much the same. Singapore's monsoons and inter-monsoon periods bring different weather despite similar day lengths. Explore clouds and rain in <TopicLink slug="water">Water</TopicLink>.<Num s="S11,S33" />

The chart uses Singapore time, <Num s="S33">UTC+8</Num>, and displays minutes. Compare the separate daylight readings when choosing dates. Practise reading clock times in <TopicLink slug="telling-time">Telling Time</TopicLink>.

<More title={{"en": "The horizon used in the calculation", "zh": "计算采用的地平线"}}>

The calculation assumes a flat, clear horizon at sea level. It uses a Sun-centre altitude of <Num s="S26,S33">−0.833°</Num> to allow for the Sun's disc and typical atmospheric refraction. Earlier experiments use <Num s="S36">0°</Num>, so their daylight definitions differ slightly. Buildings, terrain and actual refraction can change the sunrise or sunset you see.<Num s="S26" />

</More>

<More title={{"en": "One reference point", "zh": "一个参考点"}}>

The table uses <Num s="S33">1.3342° N, 103.7357° E</Num>, a point chosen for this calculation. It is not an official observatory coordinate. Your home's skyline may differ, and a nearby building may hide the Sun for longer.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A year of estimates", "zh": "一年的估计值"}}>

The table covers <Num s="S33">365 dates</Num> in 2026, using the NOAA/Meeus approximate method. Air conditions limit its accuracy. Daylight is calculated before the clock times are rounded to minutes. Subtracting the displayed sunrise from sunset may therefore differ from the daylight reading by a minute.<Num s="S26,S33" />

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

曲线显示新加坡同一个参考点的全年计算日长。移动指针选择日期，日出、太阳正午、日落和白昼读数会一起改变。这些都是估计值。旁边的地球标示地点，固定的受光画面不会跟着图表日期改变。<Num s="S33,S36" />

新加坡靠近赤道，全年日长变化很小。表中六月最长的一天附近，白昼约 <Num s="S33">12 小时 12 分钟</Num>；十二月最短的一天附近，约 <Num s="S33">12 小时 3 分钟</Num>。来回移动指针，可以看出这个小差别。<Num s="S11,S33" />

只知道日长，还算不出日出日落的钟点。经度、时区，以及日晷所示太阳时与平均钟表时间的全年差异，也有影响。<Term id="solar-noon">太阳正午</Term>是太阳经过当地天空中南北方向的子午线、到达当天最高位置的时刻，不一定是钟表十二点。<Num s="S26,S33" />

云会减少到达地面的直接日照，白昼时长却可以差不多。新加坡的季风期和季风间期，日长相近，天气仍有不同。<TopicLink slug="water">《水》</TopicLink>继续介绍云和雨。<Num s="S11,S33" />

图表采用新加坡时间 <Num s="S33">UTC+8</Num>，显示到分钟。比较不同日期时，可直接看单列的白昼读数。读钟练习见<TopicLink slug="telling-time">《认识时间》</TopicLink>。

<More title={{"en": "The horizon used in the calculation", "zh": "计算采用的地平线"}}>

</More>
