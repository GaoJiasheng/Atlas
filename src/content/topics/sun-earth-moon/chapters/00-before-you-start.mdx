<Lang en>

Start with the three labelled spheres. The Sun is a star that supplies light. Earth is a planet that travels around the Sun; the Moon is Earth's natural satellite and shines mainly by reflecting sunlight. The mechanical instrument on the cover represents these same bodies. Its rods and enlarged globes make their relationships easier to study. They are parts of a teaching instrument, rather than structures in space.<Num s="S34,S4,S7,S5,S36" />

The stage removes the rods and adds a marked observing place. <Term id="rotation">Rotation</Term> means turning about an axis, an imaginary line through a body. An <Term id="orbit">orbit</Term> is a body's path around another body. Follow the marker as Earth turns: its direction towards the Sun changes. Later chapters keep some movements still so that a single cause becomes easier to see.<Num s="S3,S4,S36" />

Sizes and gaps need careful reading. In true scale, an Earth <Num s="S36">10 mm</Num> across puts the Moon about <Num s="S36">302 mm</Num> away, measured between centres. The schematic view enlarges bodies and compresses gaps. Switching views changes the drawing, while physical calculations keep the original radii and distances. The faint light on a dark side also helps us read the model.<Num s="S36" />

SIM marks a calculated result; DESIGN marks a chosen model setting; TYPICAL identifies a reference value. None turns this model into a photograph or a sky forecast. <TopicLink slug="light-and-shadows">Light and Shadows</TopicLink> follows light on Earth. <TopicLink slug="plant">A Plant</TopicLink> and <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion">Energy Conversion</TopicLink> connect sunlight with other processes.

One close view cannot show every body clearly. The Earth–Moon surface gap is about <Num s="S36">295 mm</Num>, smaller than the centre distance because the two radii must be subtracted. 先认舞台上的三个球。太阳是提供光照的恒星，地球是绕太阳运行的行星，月球则是地球的天然卫星，主要反射太阳光而显得明亮。封面三球仪也表示这三个天体，支杆和放大的球方便观察关系，并不表示太空中真的有杆连着它们。<Num s="S34,S4,S7,S5,S36" />

舞台省去支杆，加上一个地表观察点。<Term id="rotation">自转</Term>是天体绕自身的轴转动；轴是穿过天体的一条假想线。<Term id="orbit">公转</Term>是绕另一个天体运行。跟着标记看，地球转动时，这个地点朝向太阳的方向也在改变。后面的实验会暂时固定某些运动，让一个原因单独显现。<Num s="S3,S4,S36" />
