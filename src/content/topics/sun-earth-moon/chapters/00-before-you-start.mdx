<Lang en>

The Sun is a star that gives us light. Earth is a planet that travels around it. The Moon is Earth's natural satellite: it travels around Earth and shines mainly by reflecting sunlight. The instrument on the cover represents these three bodies. Its rods hold the enlarged globes in place to help us study them.<Num s="S34,S4,S7,S5,S36" />

The scene adds a marked place on Earth. <Term id="rotation">Rotation</Term> means turning about an axis, an imaginary line through a body. An <Term id="orbit">orbit</Term> is a body's path around another body. Follow the marker as Earth turns. Its direction towards the Sun changes. In later experiments, some movements stay still so we can study one cause at a time.<Num s="S3,S4,S36" />

In true scale, an Earth <Num s="S36">10 mm</Num> across puts the Moon about <Num s="S36">302 mm</Num> away, measured between centres. The schematic view enlarges the bodies and shortens the gaps so we can see them clearly. The calculations still use their original radii and distances. Faint light on the dark sides helps us see their outlines.<Num s="S36" />

The labels help you read the numbers: SIM means a calculated result, DESIGN a chosen setting, and TYPICAL a reference value. Follow light on the ground in <TopicLink slug="light-and-shadows">Light and Shadows</TopicLink>. Explore other uses of sunlight in <TopicLink slug="plant">A Plant</TopicLink> and <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion">Energy Conversion</TopicLink>.

<More title={{"en": "Sizes and distances at one scale", "zh": "同一比例下的大小和距离"}}>

At the same scale, the Moon is about <Num s="S36">2.73 mm</Num> across. The Sun is about <Num s="S36">1.09 m</Num> across, with its centre about <Num s="S36">117 m</Num> from Earth. A single close view cannot show them all clearly. The gap between Earth's and the Moon's surfaces is about <Num s="S36">295 mm</Num>. To find it, subtract both radii from the centre distance.<Num s="S8,S9,S10,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Using the model", "zh": "使用模型"}}>

Use REFERENCE for a fixed view, ORBIT to look around, and RUN to start the available movements. X-RAY, CUTAWAY and EXPLODED are unavailable because these experiments show positions and light.

The model uses spheres, circular paths, a fixed Earth axis and average periods. It leaves out small changes caused by gravity and details of the Moon's orientation. Its angles have no calendar dates, so it cannot predict real eclipse times or local Moonrise.<Num s="S36" />

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

太阳是发光的恒星。地球是绕太阳运行的行星，月球是绕地球运行的天然卫星，主要靠反射太阳光显得明亮。封面的三球仪表示这三个天体。支杆固定放大的球，方便我们观察它们的位置关系。<Num s="S34,S4,S7,S5,S36" />

画面中还有一个地表标记。<Term id="rotation">自转</Term>是天体绕自身的轴转动，轴是穿过天体的假想线。<Term id="orbit">公转</Term>是绕另一个天体运行。跟着标记看：地球转动时，这个地点朝向太阳的方向也在改变。后面的实验会固定某些运动，让我们一次研究一个原因。<Num s="S3,S4,S36" />

在真尺度中，地球直径画成 <Num s="S36">10 毫米</Num>，地月中心就相距约 <Num s="S36">302 毫米</Num>。示意尺度把球体放大、间距缩短，方便看清，计算仍用原来的半径和距离。暗侧的微弱填光帮助我们看见轮廓。<Num s="S36" />

SIM 表示计算结果，DESIGN 表示选定的模型条件，TYPICAL 表示参考值。<TopicLink slug="light-and-shadows">《光与影》</TopicLink>继续追踪地面的光；<TopicLink slug="plant">《一株植物》</TopicLink>和<TopicLink slug="energy-conversion">《能量的转化》</TopicLink>介绍阳光参与的其他过程。

同一比例下，月球直径约 <Num s="S36">2.73 毫米</Num>，太阳直径约 <Num s="S36">1.09 米</Num>，日地中心距约 <Num s="S36">117 米</Num>。一个近景无法把它们全都显示清楚。地月表面之间相隔约 <Num s="S36">295 毫米</Num>：从中心距减去两个球的半径，就得到表面间隙。<Num s="S8,S9,S10,S36" />

<More title={{"en": "Using the model", "zh": "使用模型"}}>

“参考”提供固定视角，“绕看”让你转动视角，“运行”启动当前可用的运动。这里研究位置和光照，所以不提供透视、剖切和爆炸拆解。

模型采用球形天体、圆轨道、固定地轴和平均周期，省略引力造成的细小变化及月球朝向的细节。模型角度没有对应的历法日期，因此不能预测真实日月食时刻或本地月出。<Num s="S36" />
