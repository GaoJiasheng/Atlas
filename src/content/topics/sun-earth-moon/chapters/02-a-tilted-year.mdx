<Lang en>

Earth's axis leans. Its <Term id="obliquity">axial tilt</Term> is about <Num s="S9,S3">23.4°</Num>, measured from a line perpendicular to its orbital plane. The guides show this reference line. A view from another angle can make the tilt look different, so use the reading rather than measuring the picture.<Num s="S36" />

Follow Earth around its yearly path. Its axis points in nearly the same direction throughout the year. The equator divides Earth into two halves, or <Term id="hemisphere">hemispheres</Term>. As Earth travels around the Sun, the northern and southern hemispheres take turns leaning towards it.<Num s="S3" />

At the model latitude of <Num s="S36">45° north</Num>, both the Sun's midday height and the length of daylight change. When the north leans towards the Sun, daylight lasts about <Num s="S36">15 hours 26 minutes</Num>. Half a model year later, it lasts about <Num s="S36">8 hours 34 minutes</Num>. The circular orbit keeps the distance to the Sun fixed.

<Figure src="figures/sun-earth-moon-parallel-axes" invert={false} alt={{ en: "Earth at two opposite orbital positions, with parallel axes and different sunlit sides.", zh: "地球位于轨道两端，地轴平行，受光方向不同。" }} caption={{ en: "The axes stay parallel at opposite orbital positions. Sizes, gaps and the drawn angle are schematic.", zh: "轨道两端的地轴保持平行。大小、间距和画面角度为示意。" }} />

Move the observer to the matching southern latitude. Long and short days swap. At the same time, the hemispheres receive sunlight at different angles and for different lengths of time. This helps explain their opposite seasons, even though both are almost the same distance from the Sun.<Num s="S3,S36" />

Set the tilt to <Num s="S36">0°</Num>. Seasonal daylight changes disappear in this circular model. In Try it, keep the latitude fixed and record daylight at different yearly positions. The curve shows day length, not temperature.<Num s="S36" />

<More title={{"en": "Nearest the Sun in January", "zh": "一月最接近太阳"}}>

Earth is usually nearest the Sun in January and farthest away in July. Northern winter falls near the nearer part of the orbit. Distance alone cannot explain opposite seasons. The circular model keeps distance fixed so we can study tilt.<Num s="S3,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Sunlight and weather", "zh": "阳光与天气"}}>

Sunlight angle and day length affect the energy a place receives. Clouds also affect weather, but this model does not calculate clouds or temperature. Singapore has a tropical climate with monsoon seasons and inter-monsoon periods.<Num s="S3,S11,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Measuring sunrise and sunset", "zh": "怎样计算日出日落"}}>

Here, daylight lasts while the Sun's centre is above a flat geometric horizon. The Singapore table uses a centre altitude of <Num s="S26,S33">−0.833°</Num>. That standard accounts for the Sun's disc and the usual bending of light in the atmosphere, called refraction. Sunrise to sunset at an equinox therefore need not last exactly <Num s="S26,S36">12 hours</Num>. Actual air conditions and the local skyline also affect what we see.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

地轴是倾斜的。<Term id="obliquity">地轴倾角</Term>从轨道平面的垂线量起，约 <Num s="S9,S3">23.4°</Num>。辅助线标出了这个基准。换个视角，画面中的角度可能看起来不同，所以应看读数，不直接拿插图量角。<Num s="S36" />

跟着地球沿公转轨道走，地轴一年内的方向近似不变。赤道把地球分成南北两个<Term id="hemisphere">半球</Term>。公转时，两半球轮流倾向太阳。<Num s="S3" />

在模型北纬 <Num s="S36">45°</Num>处，正午太阳高度和白昼长短一起变化。北半球倾向太阳时，白昼约 <Num s="S36">15 小时 26 分钟</Num>；模型过了半年，白昼约 <Num s="S36">8 小时 34 分钟</Num>。这段过程中，圆轨道上的日地距离保持不变。

<Figure src="figures/sun-earth-moon-parallel-axes" invert={false} alt={{ en: "Earth at two opposite orbital positions, with parallel axes and different sunlit sides.", zh: "地球位于轨道两端，地轴平行，受光方向不同。" }} caption={{ en: "The axes stay parallel at opposite orbital positions. Sizes, gaps and the drawn angle are schematic.", zh: "轨道两端的地轴保持平行。大小、间距和画面角度为示意。" }} />

把观察点换到相反的南纬，长日和短日就交换了。同一时刻，两半球受到阳光照射的角度和时长不同，有助于解释它们为何季节相反。两个半球到太阳的距离却几乎一样。<Num s="S3,S36" />

把倾角设为 <Num s="S36">0°</Num>，圆轨道模型中的季节性日长变化就消失了。用“试一试”固定纬度，记录不同公转位置的日长。曲线显示白昼时长，不计算气温。<Num s="S36" />

<More title={{"en": "Nearest the Sun in January", "zh": "一月最接近太阳"}}>

地球通常一月最接近太阳，七月最远，北半球冬季却在较近的一段。距离本身解释不了南北半球季节相反。圆轨道模型固定距离，让我们单独研究倾角。<Num s="S3,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Sunlight and weather", "zh": "阳光与天气"}}>

阳光角度和白昼时长会影响当地得到的能量。云也会影响天气，不过模型不计算云量或气温。新加坡属热带气候，有季风期和季风间期。<Num s="S3,S11,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Measuring sunrise and sunset", "zh": "怎样计算日出日落"}}>

这里以太阳中心高于平坦的几何地平线来计白昼。新加坡表格采用太阳中心高度 <Num s="S26,S33">−0.833°</Num>，计入太阳圆盘大小，以及大气通常使光线弯曲的折射作用。因此，春分、秋分的日出至日落不必恰好相隔 <Num s="S26,S36">12 小时</Num>。实际大气状况和当地天际线也会影响所见的升落。

</More>
