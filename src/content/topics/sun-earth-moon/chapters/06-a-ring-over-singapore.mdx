<Lang en>

On **26 December 2019**, an annular solar eclipse was visible from Singapore. The Moon left a bright ring of sunlight around its edge. <Num s="S37" />

The model helps explain that ring. In the disc window, the Moon looks smaller than the Sun and passes near its centre. The shadow diagram uses the same scale for bodies and gaps. Its distances, angles and readings are teaching examples, not a reconstruction of Singapore's sky that day. <Num s="S6,S36" />

Shift the alignment slightly. Now the Moon covers only part of the Sun's disc. The change shows how alignment affects the view. The model does not supply local contact times, eclipse duration or a path across Singapore. <Num s="S36" />

Science Centre's observatory is one place to learn about astronomy locally. Clouds may hide the sky during a planned visit. During an annular eclipse, the bright ring is still strong sunlight. Ordinary sunglasses do not make direct viewing safe. <Num s="S14,S35" />

For this activity, use indirect projection with an adult supervising. Face away from the Sun and watch only its image on paper. Never look through the pinhole at the Sun. Never look at the Sun through unfiltered binoculars or a telescope. Explore how projection works in <TopicLink slug="light-and-shadows">Light and shadows</TopicLink>. <Num s="S35" />

The date above tells us when Singapore saw an annular eclipse. The model lets us explore the positions that produce a ring. Keep the event date separate from your experiment readings. <Num s="S37,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Visiting the observatory", zh: "参观天文台" }}>

Check Science Centre's current official information for opening hours and ticket arrangements before planning a visit. Observing also depends on the weather. <Num s="S14" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Making a projected image", zh: "观察针孔投影" }}>

With an adult, face away from the Sun. Let sunlight pass through a small hole onto paper, then watch the image on the paper. The hole is not an eyepiece. This activity uses a pinhole only; it does not involve setting up binoculars or a telescope. <Num s="S35" />

</More>

<Lang zh>

**2019 年 12 月 26 日**，新加坡可见日环食。月球边缘外露出一圈明亮的太阳光。<Num s="S37" />

模型帮助我们理解光环怎样形成。视盘窗口里，月球看起来比太阳小，又从太阳中央附近经过。影图中的球体与间距采用同一比例。这里的距离、角度和读数是教学示例，没有重现当天的新加坡天空。<Num s="S6,S36" />

稍微改变排列，月球便只遮住太阳圆盘的一部分。可见排列会影响观察结果。模型不提供当地接触时刻、持续时间或经过新加坡的食带。<Num s="S36" />

科学馆天文台是本地学习天文的地点之一，观测时却可能遇到云层遮挡。日环食的亮环仍是强烈的太阳光，普通太阳眼镜不能让直视太阳变得安全。<Num s="S14,S35" />

这里的活动采用成人监督的间接投影。背向太阳，只看纸面上的像。绝不能透过针孔看太阳，也不能用未装专用前置太阳滤镜的双筒望远镜或天文望远镜看太阳。投影原理见<TopicLink slug="light-and-shadows">光与影</TopicLink>。<Num s="S35" />
