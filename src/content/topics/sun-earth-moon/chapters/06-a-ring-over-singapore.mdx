<Lang en>

**Historical event · 26 December 2019.** An annular solar eclipse was visible from Singapore on that date. A local report records the event. This dated fact belongs to the historical account; it does not turn the adjacent model into a reconstruction of the Singapore sky that day. <Num s="S37" />

**Model explanation · no date.** In the disc window, the Moon appears smaller than the Sun and passes near its centre, leaving a bright ring. The shadow diagram uses a common scale. Its distances and angles are teaching inputs, and every simulated reading belongs to this undated model. <Num s="S6,S36" />

Shift the model alignment slightly and the coverage becomes partial. That change helps explain why alignment matters. It does not move an observer across Singapore in 2019. Keep the event date and the model readings in separate notes: the model supplies no local contact times, duration or eclipse track. <Num s="S36" />

Science Centre's observatory is one local place to learn about astronomy. Clouds can prevent a planned observation, so an announced activity does not guarantee a clear sky. A bright annular ring still requires safe solar viewing. Ordinary sunglasses are unsuitable for looking at the Sun, including during an annular eclipse. <Num s="S14,S35" />

For the activity here, use indirect projection with an adult supervising. Face away from the Sun and look only at its image on paper. Never look through the pinhole at the Sun. Never look at it through unfiltered binoculars or a telescope. The paper image connects this topic with the projection principle in <TopicLink slug="light-and-shadows">Light and shadows</TopicLink>. <Num s="S35" />

<More title={{ en: "The evidence does not give a local duration", zh: "这些证据不能给出本地持续时间" }}>

The selected local report supports the event's date, place and annular type. We do not use it to publish a duration for a particular Singapore observing point. The circular teaching model supplies no missing historical timing evidence. <Num s="S37,S36" />

<More title={{ en: "Planning local learning", zh: "安排本地天文学习" }}>

Check Science Centre's current official information when planning an observatory visit. This chapter does not freeze opening hours, ticket arrangements or a promise of successful observing into a science explanation. <Num s="S14" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Reading a projected image safely", zh: "安全观看投影" }}>

With an adult, arrange the pinhole projection while facing away from the Sun, let sunlight pass through the hole onto paper, and watch only the paper. The hole is not an eyepiece. Ordinary sunglasses do not make direct viewing safe; an annular eclipse retains a bright part of the Sun throughout. Do not use this activity to assemble a telescope or binocular solar-viewing setup. <Num s="S35" />

</More>

**历史事件 · 2019 年 12 月 26 日。** 当天新加坡可见日环食，本地报道记录了这个事件。日期属于历史说明，旁边的球体排列属于教学模型。读到事件日期，并不表示模型正在重现当天新加坡天空，更不能从模型读数推回当地的准确时间。<Num s="S37" />

**模型解释 · 没有日期。** 视盘窗口里，月球看起来比太阳小，又从太阳中央附近经过，于是周围留下亮环。局部影图采用同一尺度。距离与角度是教学输入，所有模拟读数都属于这个无日期模型，要与历史事件分开记录。<Num s="S6,S36" />

稍微改变模型的对齐关系，太阳圆盘便只被部分遮住。这一变化说明排列的重要性，并不是把观察点沿着 2019 年的新加坡移动。模型没有提供当日食带、各地接触时刻或持续时间；不能把示意运动补写成真实观测记录。<Num s="S36" />

科学馆天文台是本地学习天文的地点之一，但云层可能挡住天空，计划中的观测未必成功。日环食留下的亮环仍是强烈太阳光，必须安全观察。普通太阳眼镜不适合看太阳，不能因为太阳被遮去一部分就直接抬头观看。<Num s="S14,S35" />

这里的活动只用成人监督的间接投影：背向太阳，只看纸面上的像。不要透过针孔看太阳，也不要用未装专用前置太阳滤镜的望远镜直视太阳。这与<TopicLink slug="light-and-shadows">光与影</TopicLink>里的投影原理相连；活动不包含儿童安装望远镜的步骤。<Num s="S35" />

<More title={{ en: "The evidence does not give a local duration", zh: "这些证据不能给出本地持续时间" }}>

选用的本地报道支持事件日期、地点和日环食类型；本章不据此发布某个新加坡观察点的持续时间。圆轨道教学模型也不能补足缺少的历史计时证据。<Num s="S37,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Planning local learning", zh: "安排本地天文学习" }}>

安排科学馆天文台活动时，应查阅场馆当前的官方信息。本章不固定开放时段、票务安排，也不承诺一定能观测成功，把可能变化的活动信息与科学原理分开。<Num s="S14" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Reading a projected image safely", zh: "安全观看投影" }}>

由成人监督，背向太阳布置针孔投影，让光穿过小孔落到纸上，然后只观察纸面。小孔不是目镜，不得透孔看太阳。普通太阳眼镜不能使直接观看变安全；日环食全过程都有明亮太阳区域露出。不要借这个活动组装望远镜或双筒望远镜观日装置。<Num s="S35" />
