<Lang en>

Move the Moon and watch its bright shape change smoothly. Names such as new Moon and full Moon mark parts of this cycle. A calendar divides time into whole days and gives them names and numbers so people can arrange dates. <Num s="S5,S18,S36" />

A <Term id="synodic-month">synodic month</Term> is the time from one phase to the same phase again. It averages about <Num s="S8">29.53 days</Num>. The Chinese calendar combines lunar months with information about the solar year. This makes it a <Term id="lunisolar-calendar">lunisolar calendar</Term>. Its rules need more than the average length of a Moon cycle. <Num s="S18" />

Mid-Autumn falls on day fifteen of the eighth lunar month. Its name refers to autumn, while Singapore has a tropical climate without a temperate autumn. The festival date tells us which day people mark, rather than measuring the Moon's brightness. <Num s="S38,S11" />

A calendar day lasts a whole day. Exact full Moon happens at a particular moment, which need not fall on the night of the fifteenth. Moving the model Moon explores phases; the real festival date stays the same. <Num s="S39,S36" />

Keep a Moon diary. Record the date, clock time, clouds and a sketch of the Moon's shape. If you cannot see it, leave the sketch blank. Label model sketches so you can tell them apart from sky observations. Practise recording times in <TopicLink slug="telling-time">Telling time</TopicLink>. <Num s="S36" />

<More title={{ en: "An average cycle", zh: "平均月相周期" }}>

The <Num s="S8">29.53 days</Num> is an average. Actual cycles vary in length, and neighbouring named phases need not be equally spaced in time. The model does not convert lunar-calendar dates into Gregorian dates. <Num s="S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The name lunar calendar", zh: "农历与阴阳历" }}>

Families often call the Chinese calendar the “lunar calendar”. It uses lunar months, but its rules also use solar-year information. “Lunisolar” describes both parts. <Num s="S18" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A Moon diary", zh: "月亮日记" }}>

Use this blank format: date ___; clock time ___; clouds ___; Moon sketch ___; observed sky or model ___. You can add a separate space for family traditions or stories. Families mark festivals in different ways. Record those accounts alongside your observations, with clear labels. <Num s="S36" />

</More>

<Lang zh>

移动月球，看亮部怎样连续变化。新月、满月等名称，标出周期中的不同位置。历法则把时间分成完整的一天一天，并给日期编号，方便人们安排活动。<Num s="S5,S18,S36" />

从一个月相回到同一月相的周期叫<Term id="synodic-month">朔望月</Term>，平均约 <Num s="S8">29.53 天</Num>。农历结合月相月份和太阳年的信息，因此属于<Term id="lunisolar-calendar">阴阳历</Term>。要排出历法，还需要其他规则，不能只靠平均月相周期。<Num s="S18" />

中秋节在农历八月十五。节名提到秋天，新加坡却属热带气候，没有温带的秋季。节日日期告诉人们在哪一天过节，不是月亮亮度的测量值。<Num s="S38,S11" />

历法的一天是一整段时间，准确满月却发生在某个时刻，不一定落在十五的晚上。移动模型月球可以研究月相，真实节日日期仍保持不变。<Num s="S39,S36" />

试着做月亮日记，记录日期、钟表时刻、云量和月亮形状。看不见月亮，就把草图栏留白。模型草图另加标签，方便与真实天空的观察比较。记录时刻可练习<TopicLink slug="telling-time">认识时间</TopicLink>。<Num s="S36" />

<More title={{ en: "An average cycle", zh: "平均月相周期" }}>

<Num s="S8">29.53 天</Num>是平均值。真实周期长短会变化，相邻的命名月相之间也不一定相隔同样长的时间。模型不把农历日期换算成公历日期。<Num s="S36" />

</More>
