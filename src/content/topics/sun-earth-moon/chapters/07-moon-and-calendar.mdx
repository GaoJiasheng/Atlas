<Lang en>

Move the Moon through the model positions and watch its bright shape change continuously. The familiar phase names mark parts of a cycle; they do not make the shape jump between separate states. A calendar does something different: it gives names and numbers to whole days so people can arrange dates. <Num s="S5,S18,S36" />

A <Term id="synodic-month">synodic month</Term> is a cycle from one phase back to the same phase, averaging about <Num s="S8">29.53 days</Num>. The Chinese calendar uses lunar months together with solar information about the year. This combination makes it a <Term id="lunisolar-calendar">lunisolar calendar</Term>. A mean cycle length alone is not a complete calendar rule. <Num s="S18" />

Mid-Autumn falls on day fifteen of the eighth lunar month. That is a calendar date, not a measurement of the Moon's brightness. The festival's name also does not give Singapore the temperate autumn of a place farther from the equator. Local climate and a festival name describe different things. <Num s="S38,S11" />

A calendar day covers an interval; the exact full-Moon instant is a particular moment. The fifteenth does not promise that full Moon occurs that night. Keep the calendar example independent of the model's phase reading: moving its Moon cannot change a real festival date or certify an observed sky. <Num s="S39,S36" />

Make an observation diary with spaces for date, time, clouds and a sketch of the Moon's shape. Leave a gap when it cannot be seen. Label a model sketch separately from a sky observation. Compare this way of recording time with <TopicLink slug="telling-time">Telling time</TopicLink>, while keeping the diary's calendar entry and physical observation distinct. <Num s="S36" />

<More title={{ en: "A mean period is not every interval", zh: "平均周期并非每次间隔都相同" }}>

About <Num s="S8">29.53 days</Num> is an average phase cycle. It does not mean every actual cycle lasts exactly that long, or that each pair of neighbouring named phases is separated by the same interval. This teaching model does not convert lunar-calendar dates into Gregorian dates. <Num s="S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A familiar name and a scientific category", zh: "日常名称与科学分类" }}>

Families may call the Chinese calendar the “lunar calendar”. “Lunisolar” identifies its use of lunar months and solar-year information together; the everyday name does not remove the solar part of its rules. <Num s="S18" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A blank record, ready for evidence", zh: "留白记录，等待观察" }}>

Use this blank format: date ___; clock time ___; cloud conditions ___; Moon sketch ___; observed sky or model ___. Families differ in how they mark festivals. Keep a family's account or a traditional story in its own diary field, separate from the geometry used to explain Moon phases. This activity supplies no invented observations. <Num s="S36" />

</More>

<Lang zh>

沿模型位置移动月球，窗口里的亮部连续改变。熟悉的月相名称，标出一个周期中的不同位置，并不会让月球形状在几个状态之间突然跳换。历法做的是另一件事：给完整的一天安排名称和编号，方便人们约定日期。观察过程与编排日子，不能混成同一个读数。<Num s="S5,S18,S36" />

从一个月相回到相同月相的周期，叫<Term id="synodic-month">朔望月</Term>，平均约 <Num s="S8">29.53 天</Num>。农历既使用与月相相关的月份，也使用太阳年的信息，因此属于<Term id="lunisolar-calendar">阴阳历</Term>。只拿一个平均周期不断相加，还不能得到完整历法，更不能自动排出公历节日表。<Num s="S18" />

中秋节在农历八月十五。这是历法日期，不是月球亮度的测量值。“中秋”这个名字，也不表示新加坡有较高纬度地区那样的温带秋季。当地气候与节日名称各有含义，不应由名称推断天气，也不把文化叙述当成月相形成的物理原因。<Num s="S38,S11" />

历法的一天是一段时间，准确满月却是某个时刻。十五这个日号，不保证准确满月一定落在当天晚上。日历示例应与模型亮部读数分开；拖动模型月球，既不会改变真实节日日期，也不能证明当天真实天空恰好就是这个月相。<Num s="S39,S36" />

做月亮日记时，留出日期、钟表时刻、云量和月亮形状的位置；看不见就如实留白。模型草图另作标记，不填成真实观测。可联系<TopicLink slug="telling-time">认识时间</TopicLink>，比较记录时刻的方法，同时把历法条目、模型解释和天空观察分别写清。<Num s="S36" />

<More title={{ en: "A mean period is not every interval", zh: "平均周期并非每次间隔都相同" }}>

约 <Num s="S8">29.53 天</Num>是平均月相周期，不是说每次真实周期都恰好这么长，也不是相邻的命名月相之间总隔着一样长的时间。本模型不把农历日期换算成公历日期。<Num s="S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A familiar name and a scientific category", zh: "日常名称与科学分类" }}>

家庭日常说的“农历”，从科学分类看属于阴阳历，月份与月相相连，年历安排也使用太阳年信息。日常名称与分类角度不同，不能因名字里没有“太阳”就忽略规则里的太阳部分。<Num s="S18" />

</More>
