<Lang en>

At the opening new-Moon position, the Moon misses the alignment needed for an eclipse. Its orbit is tilted by about <Num s="S8">5.1 degrees</Num> relative to Earth's orbital plane. That is a different angle from Earth's axial tilt. New Moon alone therefore does not guarantee a <Term id="solar-eclipse">solar eclipse</Term>, and full Moon alone does not guarantee a <Term id="lunar-eclipse">lunar eclipse</Term>. <Num s="S6,S40" />

The planes intersect along the drawn line. An <Term id="node">orbital node</Term> is where the lunar orbit crosses Earth's orbital plane. Near a crossing, the bodies can line up closely enough. Turn the node control to bring the crossing towards the alignment; it changes the geometry, rather than advancing to a real eclipse date. <Num s="S40,S36" />

A solar eclipse needs the Moon between the Sun and a surface observer. A lunar eclipse instead puts the Moon inside Earth's shadow near full Moon. The umbra receives no direct light from the Sun; the surrounding penumbra receives light from only part of it. The local shadow diagram uses one common scale. <Num s="S6,S36" />

Next compare central alignments at different Moon distances. <Term id="apparent-diameter">Apparent diameter</Term> means angular width in an observer's view, rather than physical width. Bringing the Moon nearer makes its apparent disc larger; moving it farther away can leave a bright solar ring around it: an <Term id="annular-eclipse">annular eclipse</Term>. Its physical radius stays unchanged throughout this comparison. <Num s="S6,S8,S9,S36" />

The disc window belongs to the marked model observer; the phase window uses Earth's centre. Keep those viewpoints separate. These circles and shadows explain conditions for an eclipse. They supply neither a dated forecast nor a real eclipse path across the ground. <Num s="S36" />

<More title={{ en: "Where the observer stands", zh: "观察者站在哪里" }}>

Different places can see different solar coverage during the same real event. This model uses one explicitly marked surface point. Changing the node or Moon distance is a geometry experiment, not a journey along an actual eclipse track. <Num s="S6,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where each shadow falls", zh: "影子落在哪里" }}>

In a solar eclipse, the Moon casts the shadow towards Earth. In a lunar eclipse, Earth casts the shadow towards the Moon. Beyond the tip of the Moon's umbra is the antumbra, where a centrally aligned observer can see an annular eclipse. The diagram computes finite-Sun shadow cones using physical radii and distances, independently of the enlarged stage bodies. <Num s="S6,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A deliberately flat experiment", zh: "刻意设成共面的实验" }}>

The total-versus-annular comparison sets lunar tilt to <Num s="S36">0 degrees</Num>. The planes then coincide, so there is no unique node line. This is a design setting, not the real lunar orbit. Records in Try it describe only the current experiment settings; changing another input replaces the record at the same node value. <Num s="S36" />

</More>

<Lang zh>

开场虽是新月位置，月球却没有排到发生<Term id="solar-eclipse">日食</Term>所需的位置。月轨道相对地球轨道面倾斜约 <Num s="S8">5.1 度</Num>，与地轴倾角是两回事。因此，仅有新月不保证日食，仅有满月也不保证<Term id="lunar-eclipse">月食</Term>。先看轨道在空间怎样偏离，再看是否真正排齐。<Num s="S6,S40" />

两个轨道平面沿画出的直线相交。月球轨道穿过地球轨道面的地方叫<Term id="node">轨道交点</Term>，靠近这里才可能排得足够齐。转动交点方向控件，把交线带向所需方向。这是在改变几何条件，并不是把日期推进到某次真实日食。<Num s="S40,S36" />

日食需要月球来到太阳与地表观察者之间；月食则是满月附近的月球进入地影。本影内完全收不到太阳的直接光；周围的半影，只收得到太阳部分区域的光。局部影图里的球体和距离采用同一尺度，不是直接拿舞台上放大的球体投影。<Num s="S6,S36" />

接着保持中心对齐，只比较不同的地月距离。<Term id="apparent-diameter">视直径</Term>是在观察者眼中占据的角宽，不是天体实际直径。移近月球，月盘看起来较大，能盖住太阳；移远则可能留下亮环，形成<Term id="annular-eclipse">日环食</Term>。月球实体半径在比较中始终没有改变。<Num s="S6,S8,S9,S36" />

视盘窗口属于标出的模型地表观察点，月相窗口却从地心看，两种视角要分清。圆盘和影锥说明食的条件，不提供真实日期、接触时刻或地面食带。记录结果时，也要写明当时改变了哪些输入。<Num s="S36" />

<More title={{ en: "Where the observer stands", zh: "观察者站在哪里" }}>

同一次真实日食，不同地点看见的遮盖程度可能不同。这里固定一个明确标出的模型地表点；改变交点方向或地月距离，是几何实验，并不是沿真实食带移动观察者。<Num s="S6,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where each shadow falls", zh: "影子落在哪里" }}>

日食是月影投向地球，月食是地影投向月球。月球本影尖端以外是伪本影，中心排齐的观察者可在这里看见日环食。影图用物理半径与距离计算有限大小太阳形成的影锥，不使用舞台球体的放大倍率。<Num s="S6,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A deliberately flat experiment", zh: "刻意设成共面的实验" }}>

全食与环食对照把月轨道倾角设为 <Num s="S36">0 度</Num>。此时两平面重合，没有唯一的交线，这是设计实验，不是真实月轨道。“试一试”的记录只对应当前设定；改变其他输入后，同一交点方向下的记录会被覆盖，不能把不同条件的记录当成单变量曲线。<Num s="S36" />

</More>
