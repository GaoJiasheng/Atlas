<Lang en>

Moonlight is reflected sunlight. Except during an eclipse, about half the Moon receives sunlight. From Earth, we see different amounts of that lit half as the Moon moves. The shape of the bright part we see is its <Term id="phase">Moon phase</Term>. <Num s="S5" />

Move the Moon while keeping the Sun's direction fixed. The observation window shows the view from Earth's centre, with north up. It stays that way when you turn the main camera. Near new Moon, the bright side faces mostly away from Earth. As the Moon moves, more of it comes into view. <Num s="S5,S36" />

At first quarter, the right half of the disc is bright in this north-up view. At full Moon, the lit side faces Earth. At last quarter, the left half is bright. “Quarter” names a position in the cycle. The bright shape changes smoothly between these named phases. <Num s="S5" />

Earth's shadow does not cause ordinary Moon phases. At either quarter, the Moon is far from that shadow. In this flat model, Earth, Sun and Moon line up at full Moon, so Earth's shadow can also dim the Moon. That is a lunar eclipse. In reality, the Moon's tilted orbit prevents eclipses at most full Moons. <Num s="S5,S6,S40,S36" />

The view is from Earth's centre, not Singapore's horizon.", zh: "北朝上的新月、上弦、满月和下弦。视角来自地心，不是新加坡地面。" }} />

The surface mark helps show <Term id="synchronous-rotation">synchronous rotation</Term>. The Moon turns once as it orbits Earth once, keeping nearly the same face towards us. Switch synchrony off without moving the Moon to compare the mark's direction. The far side also receives sunlight. <Num s="S5,S21,S36" />

<More title={{ en: "The Moon in daylight", zh: "白天的月亮" }}>

We can sometimes see the Moon during daylight. Full Moon rises roughly around sunset, though the exact time varies. This window has no local horizon and does not predict Moonrise times. <Num s="S5,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two lunar periods", zh: "两个不同的月球周期" }}>

The Moon orbits Earth relative to distant stars in about <Num s="S8">27.32 days</Num>. A phase cycle averages about <Num s="S8">29.53 days</Num>. Earth and Moon also travel around the Sun, so the Moon needs more time to return to the same phase. This experiment holds the Sun's direction fixed to help us compare the shapes. <Num s="S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Turning while orbiting", zh: "一边绕行，一边转身" }}>

The Moon's real motion lets us see slightly different parts of its surface over time. This small change is called libration and is left out of the model. With an adult, walk around a chair while always facing it. Your body turns as you walk. Then walk around while facing the same wall to compare the movements. <Num s="S21,S36" />

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

月光是月球反射的太阳光。除了月食期间，月球表面约有一半受光。月球移动时，我们从地球看见的亮部多少也改变。亮部呈现的形状就是<Term id="phase">月相</Term>。<Num s="S5" />

固定太阳方向，再移动月球。观察窗从地心看，始终保持地球北方朝上，不随主镜头转动。新月附近，亮面主要背向地球；月球继续移动，我们便逐渐看见更多亮部。<Num s="S5,S36" />

上弦时，这个北朝上的视图中，月盘右半边亮着；满月时，受光面朝向地球；下弦时，左半边亮着。英文的“quarter”表示周期中的位置，不表示只有四分之一月盘亮。各种月相之间，亮部连续变化。<Num s="S5" />

普通月相不是地影造成的。上弦和下弦时，月球远离地影。在这个共面模型中，满月时三个天体排齐，地影还会遮暗月球，这才是月食。真实月轨道有倾角，所以大多数满月不会发生月食。<Num s="S5,S6,S40,S36" />

The view is from Earth's centre, not Singapore's horizon.", zh: "北朝上的新月、上弦、满月和下弦。视角来自地心，不是新加坡地面。" }} />

月面标记帮助我们观察<Term id="synchronous-rotation">同步自转</Term>：月球公转一周时，也自转一周，让近似同一面朝向地球。保持月球位置，关掉同步，再比较标记朝向。月球背面也会受到阳光照射。<Num s="S5,S21,S36" />

<More title={{ en: "The Moon in daylight", zh: "白天的月亮" }}>

白天有时也能看见月亮。满月大致在日落前后升起，具体时刻会变化。这个观察窗没有本地地平线，不预测当地月出时刻。<Num s="S5,S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two lunar periods", zh: "两个不同的月球周期" }}>

相对远方恒星，月球公转一周约 <Num s="S8">27.32 天</Num>；月相周期平均约 <Num s="S8">29.53 天</Num>。地月系统也在绕太阳移动，所以回到同一月相还要多一点时间。这里固定太阳方向，方便比较月相形状。<Num s="S36" />

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Turning while orbiting", zh: "一边绕行，一边转身" }}>

真实月球的运动让我们随时间看见略有不同的表面范围，这种小变化叫天平动，模型省略了它。在成人陪同下绕椅子走，始终面向椅子，身体就必须跟着转。再绕一圈，这次始终面向同一面墙，比较两种运动。<Num s="S21,S36" />

</More>
