<Lang en>

Start at the top of the working box. The <Term id="corner-fitting">corner fittings</Term> connect the box to equipment for lifting, stacking and holding it in place. In this top-lift example, the <Term id="spreader">spreader</Term> engages the <Num s="S10">four upper corner fittings</Num>. The connection is at the corners, rather than a hook pulling on the middle of the roof.

The spreader is an open frame above the box. Its <Term id="twistlock">twistlocks</Term> connect it to the fittings. Compare the locked and raised views. In this simulation, the connection must be confirmed before the box rises. Switch that confirmation off in Try it: the model keeps the box at the starting position. This switch shows why the connection matters. Real operators follow a full checking procedure.

The <Term id="hoist">hoist</Term> changes the height of the suspended load. The box rises while the <Term id="trolley">trolley</Term> stays in place. Next, the trolley moves along the boom with the box still raised. These are different motions, even though real equipment can coordinate them.

Finally, the box is lowered onto the carrier. Once it rests on the carrier, the locks release and the empty spreader rises. The box stays on the carrier. Replay the whole sequence, then compare the positions in each view. The ropes follow the trolley and spreader as they move.

先看工作箱的顶部。<Term id="corner-fitting">角件</Term>位于箱角，用来连接搬运、堆放和固定箱子的设备。在本例的顶部起吊方式中，<Term id="spreader">集装箱吊具</Term>接入<Num s="S10">四个上角件</Num>。起吊连接在角部，不是用一个钩子拉住薄箱顶的正中央。

吊具是箱子上方留空的框架，靠<Term id="twistlock">旋锁</Term>与角件连接。比较锁住和抬高的画面。本模拟要先确认连接，才开始抬升；在“试一试”里关闭确认，模型中的箱子就留在起点。这个开关说明连接为什么重要。实际操作员需要完成整套检查步骤。

<Term id="hoist">起升机构</Term>改变悬吊物的高度。箱子抬高时，<Term id="trolley">小车</Term>还没有横移。接着，小车沿臂架行走，让已抬起的箱子移向岸边。升降和小车行走是不同动作。真实设备可以协调两种动作；这里分开演示，方便比较。

最后，箱子落在运输车上。有了承托，旋锁才松开。空吊具升起，箱子仍留在车上。重放完整过程，再回到各幅画面比较位置。绳子的两端跟着小车和吊具移动。
