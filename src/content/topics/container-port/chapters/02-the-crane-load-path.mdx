<Lang en>

Start at the top of the working box. The <Term id="corner-fitting">corner fittings</Term> are connection structures at the corners, used in handling, stacking and securing. For the selected top-lift method, the <Term id="spreader">spreader</Term> engages the <Num s="S10">four upper corner fittings</Num>. The connection is at the corners, rather than a hook pulling on the middle of the roof.

The spreader is an open frame above the box. Its <Term id="twistlock">twistlocks</Term> connect it to the fittings. Look at the locked snapshot before the raised one. This teaching sequence requires a confirmation before lifting. Switch that confirmation off in Try it: the model keeps the box at the starting position. This illustrates the importance of the connection, without teaching an actual operator’s checking procedure.

The <Term id="hoist">hoist</Term> changes the height of the suspended load. In the raised snapshot the box is higher, but the <Term id="trolley">trolley</Term> has not crossed. Next, the trolley moves along the boom with the box still raised. These are different motions, even though real equipment can coordinate them.

Finally the box is lowered onto the carrier. After the box has support, the illustrative locks release and the empty spreader rises. The box stays on the carrier. Use the replay step to watch the whole sequence, then return to the snapshots to compare individual positions. The straight ropes follow both moving ends; they do not model the crane’s complete pulley arrangement.

先看工作箱的顶部。<Term id="corner-fitting">角件</Term>是箱角的连接结构，与搬运、堆放和固定有关。在本例的顶部起吊方式中，<Term id="spreader">集装箱吊具</Term>接入<Num s="S10">四个上角件</Num>。起吊连接在角部，不是用一个钩子拉住薄箱顶的正中央。

吊具是箱子上方留空的框架，靠<Term id="twistlock">旋锁</Term>与角件连接。先看锁住的快照，再看抬高的快照。本教学过程要先确认连接，才开始抬升；在“试一试”里关闭确认，模型中的箱子就留在起点。这说明连接的重要，但不代表实际操作员只需做这个开关动作，也不是现场检查步骤。

<Term id="hoist">起升机构</Term>改变悬吊物的高度。箱子抬高的快照里，<Term id="trolley">小车</Term>还没有横移。接着，小车沿臂架行走，让已抬起的箱子移向岸边。升降和小车行走是不同动作；真实设备可以协调它们，模型把它们分开，方便看清。

最后，箱子落在运输车上，有了承托，示意旋锁才松开。空吊具升起，箱子仍留在车上。在重放那一拍看完整过程，再回到快照比较位置。直绳的两端跟着小车和吊具移动，但这幅画没有复现岸桥完整的滑轮与穿绳系统。
