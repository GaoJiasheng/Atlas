Singapore’s container port has grown in stages. Tanjong Pagar container terminal opened in <Num s="S27">1972</Num>, long before Tuas began operating.

Tuas’s first <Num s="S5">two berths</Num> began operations in December <Num s="S5">2021</Num>. Its official opening followed on <Num s="S5">1 September 2022</Num>. The port began work before its official opening. Construction continued after both milestones.

Automation changes how work is done. Tuas uses automated guided vehicles, electric automated yard cranes and remote management from a control centre. Select the carrier, yard crane and control room to connect those descriptions to the model. People still plan the work, maintain the equipment and supervise operations.

Compare the figures below. <Term id="throughput">Throughput</Term> is how much cargo a port actually handles in a given period. <Term id="capacity">Capacity</Term> is how much it could handle under given conditions. A future capacity for Tuas and a past result for all Singapore describe different things. The planned capacity tells us what Tuas is being built to handle. It does not guarantee how much cargo will arrive.
