<Lang en>

Return to the working box from the ship. In the first view it has reached the carrier. In the next it waits in the yard. Follow the same box as it moves between these places.

Now look towards the onward vessel. The same box joins that ship for another destination. This illustrates one common transhipment journey: arrival by sea, a landside transfer and departure by sea. The port is an intermediate connection in the cargo’s journey. Unloading here therefore does not mean that the cargo’s final destination is here.

The separate local-delivery box represents another destination for cargo. It is a different box from the one we are following. Comparing the two makes the distinction visible: some cargo continues overseas, while other cargo is delivered into the local economy. The model shows both journeys, but does not show how much cargo follows each one.

A yard can provide a place to wait between these stages. The arrival and departure of the vessels need not happen together. Use the index to select the working box and follow each stage. The model shows the order of the journey, rather than real sailing routes or waiting times.

The box is lifted off one ship and onto another. Following those lifts explains its journey. To understand the MPA annual total, use the published counting rules and units; the lifts alone do not tell us how the total is counted.

<Lang zh>

回到刚才从船上卸下的工作箱。先看它到了运输车上，再看它在堆场等待。一路跟着同一只箱子，看看它的位置怎样改变。

接着看接续船。同一只工作箱装上这条船，前往另一个目的地。这说明一种常见的转运过程：海上到达，经过岸上搬运，再从海上离开。港口是旅程中的中间连接点。因此，在这里卸货，不代表这里就是货物最终要去的地方。

另一个本地交付箱代表不同去向。它与我们一直追踪的工作箱是不同的箱子。把两者放在一起比较，就能看清：有的货继续出海，有的货交付本地。模型展示了这两种去向，没有表示各占港口货量的多少。

堆场能在这些环节之间提供暂存位置。前后两条船不一定同时到达，所以箱子可能需要等待。从索引选中工作箱，逐段追踪。模型展示旅程的先后顺序，不表示真实航线或等待时间。

<More title={{ en: "Following a box and counting traffic", zh: "追踪箱子与统计货量" }}>

箱子从一条船卸下，再装上另一条船。跟着这两次起吊，可以了解它的旅程。要读懂海事及港务管理局（MPA）的年度总量，还要看公布的统计规则和单位；只看起吊次数并不足够。

</More>
