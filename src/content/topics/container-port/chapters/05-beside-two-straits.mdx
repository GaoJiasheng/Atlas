<Lang en>

The port model shows how boxes move. The maps below show where Singapore is. Find the Malay Peninsula, Sumatra and Singapore. The land shapes come from Natural Earth geographical data.

Follow the water between the Malay Peninsula and Sumatra towards Singapore. The Strait of Malacca and the Strait of Singapore connect sea passages here. Singapore lies along East–West trade routes, helping it connect with other ports. The dots mark places named on the maps.

<StraitsMap view="regional" />

<StraitsMap view="straits" />

A <Term id="hub">hub</Term> brings transport connections together. A useful location needs working machines, sailing connections and people to organise them. Cranes, carriers, yard operations and planning help cargo reach its next ship. Pilots guide ships, tugboats help move them, and suppliers bring what they need.

Maritime services also include finance, insurance and legal services. Together with the machines and transport links, these services keep the hub working.

</More>

<Term id="hub">枢纽</Term>把多条运输联系接起来。有利的位置还需要机器、班轮联系和人员安排，才能发挥作用。模型里看到的岸桥、运输车、堆场作业和背后的计划，都参与让连接成为实际运输。引航员带领船只航行，拖轮协助移动船只，补给服务提供船上所需的物品。

联系还包括画面以外的活动，例如航运金融、保险和法律服务。这些服务配合机器和运输联系，让枢纽运作起来。
