<Lang en>

Start with the chain of quay crane, carrier and yard crane. A box must pass through these stages, so increasing one stage’s ability does not necessarily increase the whole chain’s ability. Predict what will happen if the model gets an extra quay crane while everything else stays the same. Then use Try it to check and record the result.

The selected SIM rates per resource are <Num s="S1">30, 24 and 18 boxes an hour</Num>, respectively. With one resource at each stage, the yard stage has the smallest capacity. The chain’s modelled capacity is therefore <Num s="S1">18 boxes an hour</Num>. The limiting stage is called the bottleneck. These are teaching assumptions, not measurements of the machines shown or of Tuas Port.

Add the quay crane. Its own stage can now handle <Num s="S1">60 boxes an hour</Num> in the model. Yet the yard stage still limits the chain to <Num s="S1">18 boxes an hour</Num>. Return to the original quay-crane count and increase the yard resources instead. The yard stage can handle more, but the carrier stage now becomes the limit: <Num s="S1">24 boxes an hour</Num>. A bottleneck can change when resources change.

Compare arriving and completed boxes. At arrivals of <Num s="S1">24 boxes an hour</Num>, the original chain leaves <Num s="S1">12 boxes</Num> queued after <Num s="S1">2 hours</Num> in this simulation. The revised chain with the extra yard resource stops building that queue. Read the capacity, completed amount and queued amount with their units; none of these is the Singapore-wide annual TEU statistic.

<More title={{ en: "The arithmetic behind the readings", zh: "读数背后的算式" }}>

Chain capacity is the smallest of the stage capacities. Completed boxes equal the smaller of arrivals and capacity, multiplied by hours. Queued boxes equal the positive excess of arrivals over capacity, multiplied by hours. For the default case: <Num s="S1">(24 − 18) × 2 = 12 boxes</Num>. If arrivals are below capacity, spare capacity does not create extra cargo.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What this queue cannot predict", zh: "这条队列不能预测什么" }}>

The model starts with no queued boxes and treats flow as continuous. It omits startup delays, equipment interference, weather, storage limits and vessel schedules. MOT’s account of a 2024 disruption discusses off-schedule arrivals and onward cargo, illustrating why real congestion needs more information. Our simulated queue is not a live waiting count or an operational forecast.

</More>

</Lang>

本模型选的 SIM 处理率依次为每台<Num s="S1">每小时 30、24、18 只箱</Num>。每段都只有一份设备资源时，堆场一段能力最小，因此全链模拟能力是<Num s="S1">每小时 18 只箱</Num>。限制全链的一段叫瓶颈。这些是教学假设，不是画面中机器或大士港口的实测效率。

再比较到达和完成的数量。到达率为<Num s="S1">每小时 24 只箱</Num>时，原来的链经过 <Num s="S1">2 小时</Num>，在本模拟中积压 <Num s="S1">12 只箱</Num>；增加堆场资源后，这个情景不再继续积压。读数要带单位，分清每小时能力、已完成箱数和排队箱数；它们都不是新加坡全港的年度 TEU 统计。

<More title={{ en: "The arithmetic behind the readings", zh: "读数背后的算式" }}>

全链能力取各段能力的最小值。完成箱数是到达率与能力中较小者乘时间；积压箱数是到达率超过能力的部分乘时间。默认算式为 <Num s="S1">（24－18）×2＝12 只箱</Num>。若到达率更低，空余能力不会凭空制造货物。
