<Lang en>

Start with the chain of quay crane, carrier and yard crane. A box must pass through these stages, so increasing one stage’s ability does not necessarily increase the whole chain’s ability. Predict what will happen if the model gets an extra quay crane while everything else stays the same. Then use Try it to check and record the result.

In this simulation, each quay crane, carrier and yard crane handles <Num s="S1">30, 24 and 18 boxes an hour</Num>, respectively. With one machine at each stage, the yard crane has the smallest capacity. The whole chain can therefore handle <Num s="S1">18 boxes an hour</Num>. The stage that limits the chain is called the bottleneck. These rates are chosen for the simulation.

Add the quay crane. Its own stage can now handle <Num s="S1">60 boxes an hour</Num> in the model. Yet the yard stage still limits the chain to <Num s="S1">18 boxes an hour</Num>. Return to one quay crane and add a yard crane instead. The yard stage can handle more, but the carrier stage now becomes the limit: <Num s="S1">24 boxes an hour</Num>. A bottleneck can change when resources change.

Compare arriving and completed boxes. At arrivals of <Num s="S1">24 boxes an hour</Num>, the original chain leaves <Num s="S1">12 boxes</Num> queued after <Num s="S1">2 hours</Num> in this simulation. With the extra yard crane, the chain keeps up with arrivals. Read each unit carefully: boxes per hour measures a rate, while completed and waiting boxes are counts.

<More title={{ en: "The arithmetic behind the readings", zh: "读数背后的算式" }}>

Chain capacity is the smallest of the stage capacities. Completed boxes equal the smaller of arrivals and capacity, multiplied by hours. If arrivals exceed capacity, multiply the difference by hours to find the queue. For the default case: <Num s="S1">(24 − 18) × 2 = 12 boxes</Num>. If arrivals are below capacity, spare capacity does not create extra cargo.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What this queue cannot predict", zh: "这条队列不能预测什么" }}>

The model starts with an empty queue and assumes a steady flow of boxes. Real ports also face startup delays, machines getting in each other’s way, weather, limited storage space and changing vessel schedules. The Ministry of Transport’s account of a 2024 disruption describes ships arriving off schedule and cargo waiting for onward journeys. Real congestion depends on these conditions too. Those extra conditions mean this simple simulation cannot predict a real port’s queue.

</More>

从岸桥、运输车、场桥这条链开始看。箱子要逐段通过，所以只提高其中一段的能力，不一定提高整条链的能力。先预测：其它条件不变，多一台岸桥会怎样？再用“试一试”核对，并记下一行结果。

在这个模拟中，每台岸桥、运输车和场桥依次能处理<Num s="S1">每小时 30、24、18 只箱</Num>。每段都只有一台机器时，堆场一段能力最小，因此全链模拟能力是<Num s="S1">每小时 18 只箱</Num>。限制全链的一段叫瓶颈。这些处理速度是为模拟选定的。

增加岸桥后，岸桥一段在模型中可处理<Num s="S1">每小时 60 只箱</Num>，但堆场仍把全链限制在<Num s="S1">每小时 18 只箱</Num>。恢复为一台岸桥，改为增加一台场桥。堆场能力提高了，这次却轮到运输环节限制全链，能力变为<Num s="S1">每小时 24 只箱</Num>。改变资源后，瓶颈也会换到别处。

再比较到达和完成的数量。到达率为<Num s="S1">每小时 24 只箱</Num>时，原来的链经过 <Num s="S1">2 小时</Num>，在本模拟中积压 <Num s="S1">12 只箱</Num>；增加一台场桥后，整条链便能跟上到达速度。读数要带单位：每小时箱数表示处理速度，完成箱数和排队箱数表示数量。

<More title={{ en: "The arithmetic behind the readings", zh: "读数背后的算式" }}>

全链能力取各段能力的最小值。完成箱数是到达率与能力中较小者乘时间；积压箱数是到达率超过能力的部分乘时间。默认算式为 <Num s="S1">（24－18）×2＝12 只箱</Num>。若到达率更低，空余能力不会凭空制造货物。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What this queue cannot predict", zh: "这条队列不能预测什么" }}>

模型从空队列开始，假设箱子均匀到达和离开。真实港口还会遇到机器起动延迟、设备互相阻挡、天气变化、存放空间不足和船期改变。交通部在说明 2024 年一次拥堵时，提到船只未按计划到达，以及等待后续转运的货物。真实拥堵也受这些因素影响。有了这些额外因素，这个简单模拟就不能预测真实港口的排队数。

</More>
