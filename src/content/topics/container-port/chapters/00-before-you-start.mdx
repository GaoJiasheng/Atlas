<Lang en>

Start with the box on the ship. A <Term id="container">container</Term> holds cargo as it moves between ships and land transport. The cargo can stay inside when the transport changes. Here it will leave the ship, cross the <Term id="quay">quay</Term> and reach a <Term id="yard">yard</Term>, where boxes can wait between handling jobs. Follow this same box throughout its journey.

Each machine does part of the job. The tall <Term id="quay-crane">quay crane</Term> reaches out over the vessel. Its lifting equipment brings the box up and across to a low carrier. The carrier moves the box across the land. A yard crane can lift the box into or out of a stack. Select these objects in the chapter index to follow the connections.

The journey can continue by sea. A box may wait in the yard and then join another ship for another destination. This is <Term id="transhipment">transhipment</Term>. Other cargo is delivered locally. A box unloaded in Singapore may stay here or continue overseas.

The model places the machines close together so you can see how their jobs connect.

<More title={{ en: "What this model leaves out", zh: "模型省略了什么" }}>

The model leaves out tides, waves, cargo planning, inspections and detailed machinery. SIM marks times and handling rates chosen for the simulation. Chapter 5 uses real coastline data to show the port’s location.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A prediction about floating", zh: "关于浮沉的预测" }}>

Would a large object always sink? What about a heavy one? Size or heaviness alone cannot tell us whether it will float. The port model does not calculate floating and sinking. Explore the different materials in <TopicLink slug="materials">Materials Around Us</TopicLink>.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

先看船上的这只<Term id="container">集装箱</Term>。它装着货物，在船和陆上运输工具之间转移，不必每换一种运输方式就卸出货物重装。在这里，箱子将离开船，经过<Term id="quay">码头岸壁</Term>，再到<Term id="yard">堆场</Term>暂存。接下来，一直跟着这只箱子看。

每种机器负责其中一段。高大的<Term id="quay-crane">岸桥</Term>把臂架伸到船上方，用起吊设备把箱子抬高、移到岸上的低平运输车。运输车负责陆上的水平搬运；到了堆场，场桥再把箱子放入箱堆或从箱堆取出。从本章索引选中这些对象，就能逐段看它们怎样接上。

接下来的旅程也可能仍在海上。箱子可以暂放堆场，再装上另一条船，前往别的目的地。这叫<Term id="transhipment">转运</Term>。另一些货物则在本地交付。所以，看见箱子在新加坡卸下，还不能判断里面的货物是不是送给新加坡的。

模型把机器放得较近，方便看清各段工作怎样连接。

<More title={{ en: "What this model leaves out", zh: "模型省略了什么" }}>

模型省略了潮汐、波浪、配载计划、检查和复杂机械。SIM 表示为模拟选定的时间和处理速度。第 5 章用真实海岸数据说明港口的位置。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A prediction about floating", zh: "关于浮沉的预测" }}>

大的物体一定下沉吗？重的呢？只知道大小或轻重，还不能判断它会不会浮。港口模型不计算浮沉；可到<TopicLink slug="materials">身边的材料</TopicLink>比较不同材料。

</More>
