<Lang en>

You can now read a <Term id="clock-face">clock face</Term> to the nearest <Term id="minute">minute</Term>. The next step is to tell whether that reading belongs to the morning or later in the day.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the clock beside the whole day</Beat>. The <Term id="hour-hand">hour hand</Term> points to 7 and the long hand points to 12, giving 7.00. The short hand takes 12 <Term id="hour">hours</Term> to go round once, so it goes round twice each day. The <Term id="timeline">timeline</Term> shows why 7 o'clock can mean morning or evening.

Find <Beat n={2}>the wake-up mark</Beat> on the first part of the line. The letters <Term id="am-pm">am and pm</Term> tell you which part of the day you mean. From <Term id="noon-midnight">midnight to noon</Term>, use am. Here, 7.00 am is in the morning, before school starts. Its mark comes before the middle of the day.

Now look at <Beat n={3}>the dinner mark farther along</Beat>. From noon until midnight, use pm. This includes the afternoon and evening, so the dinner mark is at 7.00 pm. The hands point to the same numbers as at wake-up time. You need the letters because the hands alone cannot tell these two times apart.

Next, compare <Beat n={4}>the midnight and noon marks</Beat>. Midnight starts a new day, and the times just after it are am. Noon is the middle of the day, and the times just after it are pm. At 12.30 pm, the short hand is halfway between 12 and 1. This is half an hour after noon, so it is already pm; you do not wait until 1 o'clock to change the letters.

A common mistake is to swap am and pm, or to keep am just after noon. Check the part of the day: evening is pm, and pm begins at noon.

<Lang zh>

钟面上只有 1 到 12，可一天有 24 个小时。这一步看看，同一个钟面读数怎样分出早上和晚上。

<Beat n={1}>钟面下面的时间线</Beat>画的是一整天，从半夜 12 点到第二天半夜 12 点。时针一天要转两圈，所以钟面上的 7 点，一天里会出现两次：一次在早上，一次在晚上。光看钟面，分不出是哪一次：早上 7 点，你可能刚起床；晚上 7 点，你可能正在吃晚饭。

于是我们在时刻后面加上 am 或 pm。从半夜 12 点到中午 12 点，写 <Term id="am-pm">am</Term>，也就是上午。<Beat n={2}>早上 7 点起床</Beat>，写作 7.00 am。这时天刚亮，还没上学。

从中午 12 点到半夜 12 点，写 pm，也就是下午和晚上。<Beat n={3}>晚上 7 点吃晚饭</Beat>，写作 7.00 pm。两个钟面一模一样，只有 am 和 pm 能把它们分开。所以写时刻的时候，别忘了 am 或 pm。

最容易弄混的是 12 点。<Beat n={4}>中午 12 点</Beat>是一天的正中，<Term id="noon-midnight">半夜 12 点</Term>是新一天的开始。中午 12 点一过，就是 pm 了：中午 12 点过半小时，写作 12.30 pm，不是 12.30 am。12.30 am 是半夜过了半小时，大家都在睡觉。

常见的错误是 am 和 pm 写反了：晚饭写成 7.00 am，那是早上。写之前先想一想：这件事在中午以前，还是中午以后？在学校里也可以这样想：早会、课间休息多半是 am；放学、CCA、吃晚饭、睡觉多半是 pm。
