<Lang en>

You may have seen a clock at home or at school. You already know how to count in fives; now you can use that counting to read its hands.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the two hands on the clock</Beat>. The <Term id="clock-face">clock face</Term> has the numbers 1 to 12 arranged round its edge. Both hands turn from the centre, but they have different jobs. The short hand is the <Term id="hour-hand">hour hand</Term>. The long hand is the <Term id="minute-hand">minute hand</Term>. Check their lengths so you know which one to read first.

Now look at <Beat n={2}>where each hand points</Beat>. The short hand points at 3, so the <Term id="hour">hour</Term> is 3. The long hand points straight up at 12. Here, 12 marks the start of counting the <Term id="minute">minutes</Term> past the hour, so no minutes have passed yet. Together, the hands show 3 o'clock: 3.00.

Watch as <Beat n={3}>the long hand moves halfway round</Beat> to 6. Half an hour has passed, so this is half past 3: 3.30. Look at the short hand too. It has moved halfway from 3 to 4 because half of the hour has passed. It does not stay on 3 while the long hand moves.

Next, <Beat n={4}>follow the long hand to 8</Beat>. Start at 12 and count five more minutes at each number: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40. The time is 40 minutes past 3: 3.40. Each space between neighbouring numbers stands for five minutes, so 4 would mean 20 minutes. At 3 it is 15 minutes past, which people also call quarter past.

A common mistake is to read the long hand on 8 as eight minutes past. It means eight groups of five minutes, which is 40 minutes. Another is to swap the hands: the short hand gives the hour, the long hand the minutes.

<Lang zh>

钟面每天都看得到：教室的墙上、巴士换乘站里、组屋楼下的小贩中心。读懂钟面，只要分清两件事：哪根针看几时，哪根针看几分。

先看<Beat n={1}>整个钟面</Beat>。<Term id="clock-face">钟面</Term>上有 1 到 12 这些数，边上一圈有 60 个小格，每 5 小格有一条长一点的刻度。中间有两根针：短的叫<Term id="hour-hand">时针</Term>，长的叫<Term id="minute-hand">分针</Term>。

<Beat n={2}>3 点整</Beat>的时候，分针直直地指着 12，时针指着 3。分针指着 12，说明正好是整点，一分钟也没有多；时针指着几，就是几点。这是 3 点，写作 <Time t="3:00" as="face" />。

过了半小时，<Beat n={3}>分针走了半圈</Beat>，从 12 走到 6。可别只看分针：时针也在走，它离开了 3，停在 3 和 4 的正中间。这就是 3 点半，写作 <Time t="3:30" as="face" />。几点半的时候，时针总在两个数中间，不会正好指着哪个数。

分针每走过一个数，就多 <Dur d="5m" />。<Beat n={4}>5 个 5 个地数</Beat>：指着 1 是 5 分，指着 2 是 10 分，指着 3 是 15 分（也叫“一刻”），一直数到 8，是 40 分。所以钟面上是 3 点 40 分，写作 <Time t="3:40" as="face" />。这时时针已经走了大半格，快到 4 了，但还是 3 点多。

常见的错误是把分针指着的数直接当成分钟，分针指着 8，就读成 3 点 8 分。对分针来说，钟面上的数要 5 个 5 个地数：8 个 5 是 40。另一个错误是把两根针读反了。记住：短针读时，长针读分。

准备好了，就<Task id="the-clock-face">做一道小题</Task>：读一读钟面，或者把钟拨到一个时刻。
