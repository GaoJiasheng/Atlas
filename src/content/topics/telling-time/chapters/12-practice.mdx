<Lang en>

The practice has eight questions from the whole lesson. The first three are about the clock face: reading a clock, setting the hands to a time, and choosing the clock that is drawn right. The fourth changes hours and minutes into minutes. Questions 5, 6 and 8 work along a timeline: how long a film lasts, when a swimming lesson ends, and when a bus left. Question 7 writes an evening time in the 24-hour clock. The questions come in a fixed order and use the same kinds of tasks as the lesson.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why. If it is not, the stage shows the answer, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time before pressing it.

Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing. On the timeline questions, the jump chips are there to help you count; only the answer you write is checked.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. "Try again" starts the same eight questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

Question 1 is on the stage, with its answer controls above this text. The squares on the chapter axis are the eight questions.

<Lang zh>

练习有 8 道题，来自整节课。前 3 道是读钟面：读出钟面上的时刻、把钟针拨到一个时刻、找出画对的那个钟。第 4 道把几小时几分换成分钟。第 5、6、8 道在时间线上做：一部电影放了多长时间、游泳课几点结束、巴士几点开出。第 7 道把晚上的一个时刻写成 24 时制。题目顺序固定，题型和课里一样。

每题只检查一次。按「检查」以后，马上就能看到对不对、为什么。如果还没对，舞台会显示答案，还有一个按钮可以回到教这一点的那一步。检查以后答案就不能再改，所以按之前慢慢想。

每题有一条提示，看了提示不扣分。时间线上的题，跳段的按钮是帮你数的，只检查你最后写下的答案。

做完最后一题，会出现一张小表：每题一行，标出 ✓ 或 ○，以及可以回看的那一步。按「再做一次」，同样的 8 道题从头开始。不计时，也不保存。

第 1 题已经在舞台上，作答的地方在这段文字上方。章节轴上的 8 个小方块就是这 8 道题。
