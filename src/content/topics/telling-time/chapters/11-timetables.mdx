<Lang en>

You can now read the <Term id="24-hour-clock">24-hour clock</Term> and change its times into 12-hour time. You can use those skills to follow a journey from a timetable.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the two bus markers</Beat> on the <Term id="timeline">timeline</Term>. In this made-up journey, Siti's bus leaves the interchange at 1350 and arrives at 1435. Each marker shows when something happens. The gap between them shows how long the bus ride lasts. Read the labels before counting, so you use the bus departure and arrival rather than the ends of the timeline.

To find the ride's <Term id="duration">duration</Term>, <Beat n={2}>follow the two jumps</Beat>. First go to 1400, taking 10 min. Then go to 1435, taking another 35 min. Splitting at the full <Term id="hour">hour</Term> helps you count the <Term id="minute">minutes</Term>: 10 + 35 = 45, so the ride lasts 45 min.

Now <Beat n={3}>follow Siti's wait and MRT ride</Beat>. From the bus arrival at 1435, she waits 20 min, until 1455. Her MRT ride lasts 1 h 10 min. Add the whole hour to reach 1555, then the remaining minutes to reach 1605. Move forwards because you are finding when she reaches the end of her trip. The wait belongs in the journey too, because time keeps passing while she waits.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>look across the whole journey</Beat>, from 1350 to 1605. Jump 10 min to 1400, then 2 h to 1600, then 5 min to the end. Together these give 2 h 15 min. Keep the timetable's time form while counting. If asked for 12-hour time, change at the end: 16 − 12 = 4, so she arrives at 4.05 pm.

A common mistake is to subtract timetable readings as ordinary numbers: 1605 − 1350 = 255 looks like 2 h 55 min, but an hour has 60 minutes, not 100. Counting jumps through the hour marks keeps the units clear. Another is to take away 10 instead of 12 when changing back to 12-hour time.

<Lang zh>

学会了 24 小时计时法，就能读时刻表了。这一步把前面学的都用上：跳着数经过的时间，求开始或结束的时刻，再在 12 时制和 24 时制之间换一换。下面的行程都是编的，不是真实的班次。

<Beat n={1}>时刻表用 24 小时计时法</Beat>。这趟巴士 1350 从换乘站开出，1435 到站。时间线上的钟点也写成四位数：1300、1400、1500……

坐巴士用了多长时间？<Beat n={2}>跳法和以前一样</Beat>：先从 1350 跳到整点 1400，是 10 分钟；再从 1400 跳到 1435，是 35 分钟。10 + 35 = 45 分钟。时刻写法变了，跳的办法没有变。

下了巴士，Siti 还要换 MRT。<Beat n={3}>她先等了 20 分钟</Beat>：1435 过 20 分钟，是 1455。然后坐 1 小时 10 分钟的 MRT：1455 过 1 小时是 1555，再过 10 分钟，是 1605。每一段都是“开始时刻 + 经过的时间 = 结束时刻”。

<Beat n={4}>整趟路</Beat>从 1350 到 1605 一共多久？还是跳：到 1400 是 10 分钟，到 1600 是 2 小时，到 1605 是 5 分钟，加起来是 2 小时 15 分钟。如果要用 12 时制说她几点到：16 − 12 = 4，就是下午 4 点 05 分，写作 4.05 pm。

读时刻表常见的错误，是把 1605 − 1350 当成普通的数来减，得到 255，以为是 2 小时 55 分钟。时间是 60 进位的，不能这样减。还有人把 1605 换成 12 时制时减了 10，写成 6.05 pm。一步一步跳，再按规则换写法，就不会弄错。

准备好了，就<Task id="timetables">做一道小题</Task>：读一张小时刻表，算一算时间。
