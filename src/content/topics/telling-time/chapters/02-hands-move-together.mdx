<Lang en>

You already know that the short <Term id="hour-hand">hour hand</Term> shows the <Term id="hour">hour</Term>, while the long <Term id="minute-hand">minute hand</Term> helps you count <Term id="minute">minutes</Term> in fives. At half past, you saw the short hand between two numbers.

Start at 2 o'clock and <Beat n={1}>watch both hands</Beat> on the <Term id="clock-face">clock face</Term>. The long hand points to 12 and the short hand points to 2. Keep both in view as time passes. They move together, but the short hand moves much more slowly.

Follow <Beat n={2}>one full turn of the minute hand</Beat> until it reaches 12 again. The short hand has moved from 2 to 3, just one number along. One full turn measures an hour: 60 min = 1 h. The hour hand moves through the gap little by little as those minutes pass.

Now <Beat n={3}>look closely at the hour hand</Beat> at 3.45. It is between 3 and 4, nearer to 4. You still read the hour as 3 because it has passed 3 but has not reached 4. The minute hand points to 9, showing 45 minutes past that hour.

Next, <Beat n={4}>compare the two clocks</Beat> for half past 4. On the real clock, the short hand is halfway between 4 and 5. The long hand has travelled halfway round to 6, so the short hand has travelled halfway to the next number. The other drawing leaves the short hand on 4, as though it had stayed still.

A common mistake is to read the number the hour hand is nearest to, even before it reaches that number. Another is to leave it exactly on a number at half past; both overlook its movement as minutes pass.

<Lang zh>

上一步看到，过了半小时，时针也离开了原来的数。这一步专门看时针：分针转圈的时候，时针在做什么。

从<Beat n={1}>2 点整</Beat>开始。分针指着 12，时针指着 2。现在让分针转一整圈，两根针都要盯着看。

分针<Beat n={2}>转完一整圈</Beat>，用了 60 分钟，也就是 1 小时。这段时间里，时针只从 2 走到 3，走了一格，钟面上的斜线就是它走过的路。分针走得快，时针走得慢：分针转一圈，时针才走一格。

再看<Beat n={3}>3 点 45 分</Beat>的钟。分针指着 9，是 45 分。放大镜里，时针已经快到 4 了，可是还没碰到 4。读几时，要看时针已经走过的那个数，而不是它快要到的那个数。时针走过了 3，还没到 4，所以是 3 点多，写作 3.45。

<Beat n={4}>左右两个钟</Beat>都想表示 4 点半。左边的时针在 4 和 5 中间，这是真的钟。右边的时针正正地指着 4，分针却指着 6，这个钟是画错的：过了半小时，时针不可能一动不动。所以看到一个钟，先看分针走了多少，再看时针有没有走到相应的地方：分针走了半圈，时针也该走了半格。

常见的错误是按时针快到的数来读：时针离 4 很近，就读成 4 点 45 分，其实还要再过 15 分钟才到 4 点。看时针的时候，先问自己一句：它已经走过了哪个数？

准备好了，就<Task id="hands-move-together">做一道小题</Task>：读一个快到整点的钟，或者找出画对的那个钟。
