<Lang en>

In the last step, you found the <Term id="duration">duration</Term> between two times by adding jumps. Now you know when an activity starts and how long it lasts, and you need to find when it ends.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the swimming lesson's start mark</Beat> on the <Term id="timeline">timeline</Term>. Its <Term id="starting-finishing-time">starting time</Term> is <Time t="14:35" />, and it lasts <Dur d="50m" />. Begin at that mark and move forwards, because the lesson ends later than it starts. Adding the duration to the starting time gives the finishing time.

First, follow <Beat n={2}>the jump to the next full hour</Beat>, <Time t="15:00" />. There are 35 <Term id="minute">minutes</Term> past the <Term id="hour">hour</Term> already, so 60 − 35 = 25 minutes remain before the hour ends. This jump uses <Dur d="25m" /> of the lesson's <Dur d="50m" />; the lesson is still going.

Now follow <Beat n={3}>the second jump</Beat> from <Time t="15:00" />. You have used 25 minutes, so 50 − 25 = 25 minutes are left. Add those remaining minutes to reach <Time t="15:25" />, the end mark. The two jumps together cover <Dur d="50m" />, so the swimming lesson ends at <Time t="15:25" />. You have used the whole duration, counting each part once.

Next, look at <Beat n={4}>the jumps across noon</Beat>. This activity starts at <Time t="11:40" /> and lasts <Dur d="45m" />. Jump <Dur d="20m" /> to <Time t="12:00" />, then the remaining <Dur d="25m" /> to <Time t="12:25" />. The <Term id="am-pm">am and pm</Term> bands change at <Term id="noon-midnight">noon</Term>, so your answer uses pm. Keeping am would place the finish before the activity started.

A common mistake is to add 40 + 45 and leave 85 in the minutes part. An hour has 60 minutes, so 85 minutes must become one hour and 25 minutes.

<Lang zh>

上一步，开始和结束都知道，求经过了多长时间。这一步换一换：知道开始的时刻和用了多长时间，求什么时候结束。用的还是时间线，跳法也一样。

游泳课<Beat n={1}>下午 2 点 35 分开始</Beat>，要上 <Dur d="50m" />。时间线上只标出了开始的时刻。结束一定在开始之后，所以我们从这个标记出发，往右跳。

<Beat n={2}>先跳到下一个整点</Beat>，下午 3 点。从 2 点 35 分到 3 点是 <Dur d="25m" />。<Dur d="50m" />已经用掉了 <Dur d="25m" />，还剩 50 − 25 = <Dur d="25m" />。

<Beat n={3}>再跳剩下的 <Dur d="25m" /></Beat>：下午 3 点过 <Dur d="25m" />，是 3 点 25 分。游泳课在<Time t="15:25" /> 结束。做完可以往回推一遍来检查：3 点 25 分往回 <Dur d="50m" />，正好回到 2 点 35 分。可以写成一句：<Term id="starting-finishing-time">开始时刻</Term> + 经过的时间 = 结束时刻。

有时候一跳会<Beat n={4}>跨过中午 12 点</Beat>。上午 11 点 40 分开始，过 <Dur d="45m" />：先跳 <Dur d="20m" />到中午 12 点，再跳 <Dur d="25m" />，到 12 点 25 分。跨过了中午，am 就变成 pm，所以要写 <Time t="12:25" as="ampm" />。

这一步最常见的错误，是把分钟直接相加：40 + 45 = 85，写成 11.85。可是钟面上没有“85 分”，满 <Dur d="60m" />就是一小时，要进到下一个整点。先跳到整点，就不会出现这样的数。另一个错误是忘了改 am 和 pm：跨过中午以后，要写 pm。

准备好了，就<Task id="finishing-time">做一道小题</Task>：从开始的时刻往后跳，找出结束的时刻。
