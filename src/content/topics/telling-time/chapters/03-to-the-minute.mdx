<Lang en>

You have seen how the <Term id="hour-hand">hour hand</Term> moves between numbers while the <Term id="minute-hand">minute hand</Term> travels round. Now you can use the small marks to read times between the fives.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the long hand pointing at 5</Beat> on the <Term id="clock-face">clock face</Term>. Start at 12 and count in fives: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25. Each gap between neighbouring numbers stands for five <Term id="minute">minutes</Term>. The short hand has passed 7, so this is 25 minutes past 7, written 7.25.

Watch as <Beat n={2}>the minute hand moves three small marks further</Beat>. Each small step is one more minute. Keep the 25 you have already counted, then count 26, 27, 28. You have added three ones to five fives: 25 + 3 = 28. The clock now shows 7.28. You do not need to count every mark from 12.

Now look at <Beat n={3}>the two hands as they light up</Beat>. Use their lengths to tell them apart. The long hand gives the minutes, and the short hand gives the <Term id="hour">hour</Term>. The short hand is between 7 and 8 because part of the hour has passed. Swapping the hands is a mistake because each hand tells you a different part of the time.

Compare <Beat n={4}>the two written times</Beat>: 7.28 and 7.05. Look at the minute hand on each clock. One shows 28 minutes past 7; the other shows five minutes past 7. Write the minutes with two digits. For five minutes, put a zero before the 5 to keep both places filled.

A common counting mistake is to add another five after reaching 25. The hand has moved only three small steps past the 5, so add three ones instead.

When you are ready, <Task id="to-the-minute">try one question</Task> on reading or setting a clock to the minute.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

前面读的都是 5 分钟一跳的时刻。可分针不会只停在数字上，它一格一格地走。这一步学读到每一分钟。

先看<Beat n={1}>7 点 25 分</Beat>。分针正指着 5，5 个 5 个地数：5、10、15、20、25。时针过了 7，还没到 8。所以是 7 点 25 分。

过了一会儿，<Beat n={2}>分针又走了 3 小格</Beat>。钟面边上每一小格是 1 分钟。读法分两步：先 5 个 5 个地数到分针走过的最后一个数，也就是 5，得 25；再一格一格地往下数：26、27、28。所以是 7 点 28 分。

接下来<Beat n={3}>两根针轮流亮起来</Beat>。短的是时针，读几时；长的是分针，读几分。要是读反了，把长针当成时针，读出来的就成了另一个时刻，差得很远。比如 7 点 28 分，要是把长针当时针、短针当分针，就会读成 5 点 37 分左右，差了两个多小时。

最后看看<Beat n={4}>怎样写</Beat>。7 点 28 分写作 7.28：点前面是几时，点后面是几分。分钟总是写成两位数，所以 7 点 5 分不写 7.5，而是写作 7.05，在 5 前面补一个 0。这样一看就知道是 5 分，不会和 50 分弄混。

常见的错误是 5 个 5 个数的时候多数或少数一次。比如 7 点 28 分，有人从 30 往回数，或者数成 33 分。先找准分针走过的最后一个数，再数小格，就不容易出错。

准备好了，就<Task id="to-the-minute">做一道小题</Task>：读出一个到每一分钟的时刻，或者把钟拨准。

</Lang>
