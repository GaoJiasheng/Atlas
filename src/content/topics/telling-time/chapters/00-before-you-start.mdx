<Lang en>

This lesson is about reading the time and working with it. A clock has two hands that turn at different speeds, a day has two halves called am and pm, and an hour has 60 minutes, not 100. Most mistakes with time come from forgetting one of these three things, so the lesson shows each of them on the stage before it asks you to use it.

There are 11 steps and a practice. Steps 01 to 03 read the <Term id="clock-face">clock face</Term>: the two hands, why the hour hand moves too, and reading to the minute. Step 04 is am and pm. Step 05 changes between hours and minutes. Steps 06 to 08 work out how long something takes, when it ends and when it started, by jumping along a <Term id="timeline">timeline</Term>. Step 09 is seconds. Steps 10 and 11 are the 24-hour clock and timetables. The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

Each step explains first. The chapter axis at the bottom of the screen has a small circle for every beat: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the hands on the stage turn, or the jumps appear on the timeline, while the reading panel says what to look at. The text below the beat line walks through the same beats.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question on its idea, drawn from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: read a clock and type the time, set a clock by dragging its hands or tapping its numbers, or jump along a timeline and write the answer.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** shows the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, up to three, each one closer to the answer.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Every question can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone: the clock hands move with the arrow keys. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved: when you leave the page, your answers are gone.

<Lang zh>

这节课讲怎样读时间、算时间。钟有两根针，走得一快一慢；一天分成两半，叫 am 和 pm；<Dur d="1h" />是 <Dur d="60m" />，不是 <Dur d="100m" />。时间上的错误，大多是忘了这三件事中的一件。所以每一件事，课里都先在舞台上讲清楚，再让你去用。

全课有 11 步和一章练习。第 01 到 03 步读<Term id="clock-face">钟面</Term>：两根针，时针为什么也在走，怎样读到每一分钟。第 04 步讲 am 和 pm。第 05 步讲小时和分钟的换算。第 06 到 08 步在<Term id="timeline">时间线</Term>上跳着数，求经过了多长时间、什么时候结束、什么时候开始。第 09 步讲秒。第 10、11 步讲 24 小时计时法和时刻表。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

每一步先讲。屏幕底部的章节轴上，每一拍有一个小圆点：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上的钟针就会转动，或者时间线上会画出一跳一跳的弧，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。节拍下面的正文也按同样的顺序讲一遍。

每章最后的小方块是一道关于这一章的小题，从十道左右的题里挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：读钟面再写出时刻，拖动钟针或点钟面上的数来拨钟，或者在时间线上跳一跳再写答案。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按「看答案」。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，最多三条，一条比一条接近答案。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

每道题都能只用点按完成，也能只用键盘完成：钟针可以用方向键拨动。不计时，也不保存：离开这一页，答案就清空了。
