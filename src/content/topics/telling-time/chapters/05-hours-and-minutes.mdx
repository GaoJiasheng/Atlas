<Lang en>

You have seen how <Term id="am-pm">am and pm</Term> tell you which part of the day a clock reading belongs to. Now you will measure a <Term id="duration">duration</Term>: how long something lasts.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the band halfway round the clock</Beat>, from 4.00 to 4.30. The <Term id="minute-hand">minute hand</Term> has travelled from 12 to 6. You can count six groups of five <Term id="minute">minutes</Term> along that path: 6 × 5 = 30 minutes. This is half an <Term id="hour">hour</Term>. You write minutes as min.

Now <Beat n={2}>follow the band all the way round</Beat> to 5.00. The hand has travelled another half turn, adding another 30 min. Together, the two halves make 60 min, or one whole hour. You write hours as h. Each full turn takes the same amount of time, wherever the hand starts.

On the <Term id="timeline">timeline</Term>, <Beat n={3}>look at the two jumps</Beat> that make 1 h 20 min. The first jump goes from zero to 60 min, using one whole hour. The second adds 20 min. To write the whole duration in minutes, replace the hour with 60 min, then add: 60 + 20 = 80 minutes. The length of time stays the same; only the units change.

You can also <Beat n={4}>split the stretch of 95 min</Beat> into hours and minutes. Take out 60 min for one whole hour. The minutes left are 95 − 60 = 35, so 95 min is 1 h 35 min. Another whole hour would need another 60 min. Only 35 min remains, so keep that part in minutes.

A common mistake is to count an hour as 100 minutes. That would turn 1 h 20 min into 120 min, but the clock shows that each whole hour contains 60 minutes.

<Lang zh>

前几步读的是“现在几点”。这一步换一个问题：一段时间有多长。量时间要用单位，最常用的是小时和分钟。

<Beat n={1}>从 4 点到 4 点半</Beat>，分针走了半圈，钟面上那条环带就是它扫过的地方。这段时间是半小时，也就是 30 <Term id="minute">分钟</Term>。分钟写作 min，30 分钟写作 30 min。生活里常说的“一刻钟”，就是 15 分钟。

接着走到 5 点，<Beat n={2}>分针转了一整圈</Beat>，环带也合成了一整圈。分针转一整圈是 1 <Term id="hour">小时</Term>，小时写作 h。所以 1 h = 60 min：1 小时有 60 分钟。

<Beat n={3}>1 小时 20 分钟有多少分钟</Beat>？在线上先跳一整个小时，是 60 分钟；再跳 20 分钟。合起来是 60 + 20 = 80 分钟。要特别当心：1 小时是 60 分钟，不是 100 分钟。把 1 小时 20 分钟写成 120 分钟，就是把 1 小时当成了 100 分钟。

<Beat n={4}>反过来</Beat>，95 分钟是几小时几分钟？每 60 分钟凑成 1 小时。95 里拿出 60，凑成 1 小时，还剩 95 − 60 = 35 分钟。所以 95 分钟就是 1 小时 35 分钟，写作 1 h 35 min。如果有 150 分钟，就能拿出两个 60，凑成 2 小时，还剩 30 分钟。

常见的错误是把时间当成普通的数，以为满 100 才进一位：把 2 小时 15 分钟写成 215 分钟，或者把 150 分钟写成 1 小时 50 分钟。换算时心里只记一句：一小时，六十分钟。量一节课、一场电影、一段车程有多长，都要用到这个换算。

准备好了，就<Task id="hours-and-minutes">做一道小题</Task>：把小时和分钟换算一下。
