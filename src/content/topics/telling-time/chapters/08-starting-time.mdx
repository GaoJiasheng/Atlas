<Lang en>

In the previous step, you found the <Term id="starting-finishing-time">finishing time</Term> by adding the <Term id="duration">duration</Term> to the starting time. Now you know when a trip ends and how long it takes, and you need to find when it began.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the arrival mark</Beat> on the <Term id="timeline">timeline</Term>. The bus arrives at <Time t="16:10" />, after a trip lasting <Dur d="1h25m" />. The missing starting time belongs to the left of that mark, because the bus must leave before it arrives. The number sentence shows this: starting time + duration = finishing time.

Start at the arrival mark and <Beat n={2}>jump back one whole hour</Beat> to <Time t="15:10" />. A whole <Term id="hour">hour</Term> takes you to the same number of <Term id="minute">minutes</Term> past the hour before. You have taken away the hour part of the journey. There are still <Dur d="25m" /> to take away, so this landing point is not yet the starting time.

Next, <Beat n={3}>jump back to the full hour</Beat>, <Time t="15:00" />. This jump uses <Dur d="10m" /> of the remaining <Dur d="25m" />. Split the minutes here because the full hour gives you a place to count from. Since 25 − 10 = 15, you still need to go back <Dur d="15m" />.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>jump back the last fifteen minutes</Beat> to <Time t="14:45" />. Going back across a full hour means counting back from 60 minutes: 60 − 15 = 45. The bus left at <Time t="14:45" />. Check by adding forwards from there: <Time t="14:45" /> + <Dur d="1h25m" /> = <Time t="16:10" />. You reach the arrival mark again, so the whole journey fits.

A common mistake is to add the duration to the finishing time when finding the start. That gives a time after arrival, but the bus must have started earlier: finishing time − duration = starting time.

<Lang zh>

求结束时刻，是从开始往后跳。如果知道的是结束的时刻和用了多长时间，要求什么时候开始，又该怎么办？

生活里常有这样的问题：知道电影几点开场、坐车要多久，倒推出几点该出门。一趟巴士<Beat n={1}>下午 4 点 10 分到站</Beat>，路上用了 <Dur d="1h25m" />。这一次，时间线上标出的是结束的时刻。开始的时刻还不知道，它一定在 4 点 10 分的左边。

出发一定在到站之前，所以要<Beat n={2}>往回推</Beat>，不是往后加。弧画在线的下面，表示往回跳。先往回跳 <Dur d="1h" />，从下午 4 点 10 分退到 3 点 10 分。

<Dur d="1h25m" />里，还剩 <Dur d="25m" />要退。<Beat n={3}>先退到整点</Beat>：从 3 点 10 分往回 <Dur d="10m" />，是下午 3 点。<Dur d="25m" />退掉了 <Dur d="10m" />，还差 <Dur d="15m" />。

<Beat n={4}>再往回 <Dur d="15m" /></Beat>，从 3 点退到 2 点 45 分。所以巴士是<Time t="14:45" /> 出发的。可以写成：结束时刻 − 经过的时间 = 开始时刻。最后往前验算一遍：2 点 45 分过 <Dur d="1h25m" />，正好是 4 点 10 分。验算对得上，答案就靠得住。

常见的错误是方向弄反了，把时间加到结束的时刻上，算出 5 点 35 分，这比到站还晚，当然不可能是出发时间。另一个错误是借位时把 <Dur d="1h" />当成 <Dur d="100m" />，算出“2 点 85 分”这样的时刻。先退到整点，再退剩下的，就不会出错。

准备好了，就<Task id="starting-time">做一道小题</Task>：从结束的时刻往回推，找出开始的时刻。
