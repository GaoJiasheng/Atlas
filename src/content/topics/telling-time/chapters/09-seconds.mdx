<Lang en>

You have used <Term id="hour">hours</Term> and <Term id="minute">minutes</Term> to work back to when an activity began. Some activities take less than a minute, so you need a smaller unit.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the thin hand</Beat> on the <Term id="clock-face">clock face</Term>. This is the <Term id="second-hand">second hand</Term>. It measures <Term id="second">seconds</Term>, written s. Starting at 12, it takes 60 seconds to go once round and return to 12. That full turn lasts one minute: 1 min = 60 s.

Watch <Beat n={2}>the second hand move from 12 to 3</Beat>. Each small step to the next mark takes one second. There are 15 steps along this part of the circle, so the hand has travelled for 15 s. The marks are the same ones you use for the <Term id="minute-hand">minute hand</Term>, but that hand takes one minute for each step.

Now read <Beat n={3}>the stopwatch</Beat>: 1 min 15 s. A stopwatch measures how long an activity lasts. Change the whole minute into 60 seconds, then add the 15 seconds left over. So 60 + 15 = 75 seconds. The readings 1 min 15 s and 75 s describe the same length of time.

Compare <Beat n={4}>the race and the lesson</Beat>. The race lasts 40 s; the lesson lasts 40 min. Both readings use 40, but the units tell you very different lengths of time. Seconds suit a short run across the school field. Minutes suit a lesson, while hours suit longer activities. Always read the unit beside the number.

A common mistake is to read 1 min 15 s as 115 seconds by joining the numbers. A minute contains 60 seconds, so you add 60 and 15, rather than 100 and 15.

<Lang zh>

有些事很快就完成了，用分钟来量太粗。比如跑过学校操场、跳一轮绳，只要几十秒。这一步认识比分钟更小的单位：秒。

<Beat n={1}>这个钟多了一根针</Beat>，又细又长，叫<Term id="second-hand">秒针</Term>。秒针转一圈是 1 分钟，也就是 60 <Term id="second">秒</Term>。秒写作 s，所以 1 min = 60 s。

<Beat n={2}>秒针走了 15 小格</Beat>。对秒针来说，每一小格是 1 秒；对分针来说，每一小格是 1 分钟。同一个钟面，同样的小格，看的针不一样，单位就不一样。秒针走了 15 小格，就是过了 15 秒。分针几乎不动，因为 15 秒还不到 1 分钟。

量很短的时间，常用<Beat n={3}>秒表</Beat>。这个秒表外圈记秒，上面的小表盘记分钟。小表盘指着 1，外圈指着 15，就是 1 分 15 秒。换成秒：1 分钟是 60 秒，再加 15 秒，60 + 15 = 75 秒。注意，不是 115 秒：1 分钟只有 60 秒，不是 100 秒。

那什么时候用秒，什么时候用分钟和小时？<Beat n={4}>左边的秒表</Beat>记的是一次赛跑，只有 40 秒；右边的钟上，环带表示一节课，有 40 分钟。数一样是 40，长短却差了很多。很短的事用秒，长一些的用分钟，再长的用小时。

常见的错误是把秒和分钟的换算当成满 100 进位，把 1 分 15 秒说成 115 秒。分钟和秒之间，和小时和分钟之间一样，都是 60 进位。

准备好了，就<Task id="seconds">做一道小题</Task>：读一读秒表，或者把分和秒换算一下。
