<Lang en>

You have used <Term id="hour">hours</Term> and <Term id="minute">minutes</Term> to work back to when an activity began. Some activities take less than a minute, so you need a smaller unit.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the thin hand</Beat> on the <Term id="clock-face">clock face</Term>. This is the <Term id="second-hand">second hand</Term>. It measures <Term id="second">seconds</Term>, written s. Starting at 12, it takes 60 seconds to go once round and return to 12. That full turn lasts one minute: <Dur d="1m" /> = <Dur d="60s" />.

Watch <Beat n={2}>the second hand move from 12 to 3</Beat>. Each small step to the next mark takes one second. There are 15 steps along this part of the circle, so the hand has travelled for <Dur d="15s" />. The marks are the same ones you use for the <Term id="minute-hand">minute hand</Term>, but that hand takes one minute for each step.

Now read <Beat n={3}>the stopwatch</Beat>: <Dur d="1m15s" />. A stopwatch measures how long an activity lasts. Change the whole minute into 60 seconds, then add the 15 seconds left over. So 60 + 15 = 75 seconds. The readings <Dur d="1m15s" /> and <Dur d="75s" /> describe the same length of time.

Compare <Beat n={4}>the race and the lesson</Beat>. The race lasts <Dur d="40s" />; the lesson lasts <Dur d="40m" />. Both readings use 40, but the units tell you very different lengths of time. Seconds suit a short run across the school field. Minutes suit a lesson, while hours suit longer activities. Always read the unit beside the number.

A common mistake is to read <Dur d="1m15s" /> as 115 seconds by joining the numbers. A minute contains 60 seconds, so you add 60 and 15, rather than 100 and 15.

<Lang zh>

有些事很快就完成了，用分钟来量太粗。比如跑过学校操场、跳一轮绳，只要几十秒。这一步认识比分钟更小的单位：秒。

<Beat n={1}>这个钟多了一根针</Beat>，又细又长，叫<Term id="second-hand">秒针</Term>。秒针转一圈是 <Dur d="1m" />，也就是 60 <Term id="second">秒</Term>。秒写作 s，所以 1 min = 60 s。

<Beat n={2}>秒针走了 15 小格</Beat>。对秒针来说，每一小格是 <Dur d="1s" />；对分针来说，每一小格是 <Dur d="1m" />。同一个钟面，同样的小格，看的针不一样，单位就不一样。秒针走了 15 小格，就是过了 <Dur d="15s" />。分针几乎不动，因为 <Dur d="15s" />还不到 <Dur d="1m" />。

量很短的时间，常用<Beat n={3}>秒表</Beat>。这个秒表外圈记秒，上面的小表盘记分钟。小表盘指着 1，外圈指着 15，就是 <Dur d="1m15s" />。换成秒：<Dur d="1m" />是 <Dur d="60s" />，再加 <Dur d="15s" />，60 + 15 = <Dur d="75s" />。注意，不是 <Dur d="115s" />：<Dur d="1m" />只有 <Dur d="60s" />，不是 <Dur d="100s" />。

那什么时候用秒，什么时候用分钟和小时？<Beat n={4}>左边的秒表</Beat>记的是一次赛跑，只有 <Dur d="40s" />；右边的钟上，环带表示一节课，有 <Dur d="40m" />。数一样是 40，长短却差了很多。很短的事用秒，长一些的用分钟，再长的用小时。

常见的错误是把秒和分钟的换算当成满 100 进位，把 <Dur d="1m15s" />说成 <Dur d="115s" />。分钟和秒之间，和小时和分钟之间一样，都是 60 进位。

准备好了，就<Task id="seconds">做一道小题</Task>：读一读秒表，或者把分和秒换算一下。
