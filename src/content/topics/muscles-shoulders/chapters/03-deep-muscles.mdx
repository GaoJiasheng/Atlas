<Lang en>

Fade deltoid and look at the back of the scapula. **Infraspinatus** occupies the broad hollow below the scapular spine. Its fibres converge towards the middle facet of the <Term id="greater-tubercle">greater tubercle</Term>, a prominence on the humerus. **Teres minor** follows the upper part of the scapula's lateral border and ends on the lower facet. Both pull behind the joint and help turn the upper arm outward [M1] [M48].

Above the spine, **supraspinatus** narrows to a tendon that passes under the acromion and attaches to the upper facet of the same tubercle. It helps lift the arm sideways and supports the humeral head throughout movement. Do not imagine an abrupt handover in which it stops working as soon as deltoid starts [M1] [M48] [M2].

Turn the scapula around. **Subscapularis** fills its rib-facing hollow, with fibres running towards the <Term id="lesser-tubercle">lesser tubercle</Term> on the front of the humerus. It turns the arm inward. These <Num s="M2">four</Num> muscles form the cuff: their tendons reinforce the joint capsule and help keep the humeral head in the socket [M86] [M1] [M48] [M2].

**Teres major** starts near the scapula's inferior angle, below teres minor. Its fibres run up and outward to the inner lip of the groove between the humeral tubercles. It helps extend, adduct and internally rotate the arm. **It is not a rotator cuff muscle** [M1] [M48] [M2]. <FlyTo preset="back">Look behind the shoulder</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "A sleeve, not a circular muscle", zh: "袖套，不是一块环形肌肉" }}>

The cuff is a group of separate muscles whose tendons blend with the capsule above, in front and behind the joint [M1] [M48]. Their combined pull is important for stability. Saying the head stays in the socket does not mean its geometric centre never moves.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deep muscles are hard to feel separately", zh: "深层肌肉难以单独摸到" }}>

Subscapularis lies between scapula and ribs; supraspinatus sits beneath the shoulder's roof. Even where the posterior muscles are closer to the surface, a feeling of effort cannot distinguish neighbouring muscles reliably. Use their bone attachments to understand their lines of pull.

</More>

<Lang zh>

把三角肌调淡，从后面看肩胛骨。**冈下肌**占据肩胛冈下方的宽窝，纤维汇向肱骨<Term id="greater-tubercle">大结节</Term>的中部附着面。**小圆肌**沿肩胛骨外侧缘上部走，止于大结节的下部附着面。它们从关节后方拉动肱骨，帮助上臂外旋 [M1] [M48]。

肩胛冈上方的**冈上肌**收拢成腱，从肩峰下面经过，连到同一大结节的上部附着面。它帮助侧举，也在活动时支持肱骨头。不要想成三角肌开始用力后，冈上肌就突然停止的“接班”关系 [M1] [M48] [M2]。

再看肩胛骨朝向肋骨的一面。**肩胛下肌**填在这里的窝内，纤维走向肱骨前方的<Term id="lesser-tubercle">小结节</Term>，帮助上臂内旋。这<Num s="M2">四块</Num>肌肉组成肩袖；它们的腱加强关节囊，共同把肱骨头稳在关节盂里 [M86] [M1] [M48] [M2]。

**大圆肌**在小圆肌下方，起于肩胛骨下角附近。纤维向外上方走，止于肱骨两结节之间沟的内侧唇，帮助肩伸、内收和内旋。**它不属于肩袖** [M1] [M48] [M2]。大、小圆肌虽然名字相似，附着位置和旋转方向却不同。看起止点之间的连线，可以追踪肌肉从肩胛骨向外走到肱骨的路线。不要把肩胛下肌的“下”理解成它位于骨头下方，它实际藏在骨头前面、靠近胸廓。<FlyTo preset="back">看肩部后方</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "A sleeve, not a circular muscle", zh: "袖套，不是一块环形肌肉" }}>

肩袖是数块独立肌肉的集合，肌腱在关节上方、前方和后方与关节囊融合 [M1] [M48]。共同拉力对稳定很重要。说肱骨头稳在关节盂里，不表示它的几何中心永远不移动。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deep muscles are hard to feel separately", zh: "深层肌肉难以单独摸到" }}>

肩胛下肌位于肩胛骨与肋骨之间，冈上肌藏在肩部的骨性屋顶下面。即使后方某些肌肉靠近表面，用力的感觉也不能可靠地区分邻近肌肉。看骨上附着位置，更能理解拉力方向。

</More>
