<Lang en>

Fade the deltoid and look at the back of the shoulder blade. **Infraspinatus** fills the broad hollow below the scapular spine; its fibres converge on the middle facet of the <Term id="greater-tubercle">greater tubercle</Term>, a bump on the upper humerus. **Teres minor** follows the upper part of the blade's outer border and ends on the lowest facet of the same tubercle. Both pull from behind the joint and turn the upper arm outward [M1] [M48].

Above the spine, **supraspinatus** narrows into a tendon that passes under the acromion and ends on the top facet of the greater tubercle. It helps lift the arm out to the side and holds the head of the humerus in place as the arm moves. It does not simply start the lift and then switch off when the deltoid takes over [M48] [M2].

Now turn the shoulder blade round. **Subscapularis** fills its rib-facing side, and its fibres run to the <Term id="lesser-tubercle">lesser tubercle</Term> on the front of the humerus; it turns the arm inward. These <Num s="M2">four</Num> muscles form the rotator cuff: their tendons blend with the joint capsule and hold the head of the humerus in its socket from above, in front and behind [M86] [M48] [M2].

**Teres major** starts near the lower corner of the shoulder blade, below teres minor, and runs up and out to the inner lip of the groove between the two tubercles of the humerus. It helps pull the arm back, down to the side and inward. **It is not a rotator-cuff muscle**: its tendon ends lower on the humerus, away from the capsule [M48] [M2]. <FlyTo preset="back">Look behind the shoulder</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "A cuff of tendons", zh: "一圈肌腱组成的“袖口”" }}>

The cuff is not one round muscle but four separate ones whose flat tendons merge with the joint capsule over the top, front and back of the joint [M48] [M86]. Their combined pull keeps the ball of the humerus centred on the shallow socket, much as several hands holding a ball steady on a saucer.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deep muscles are hard to feel", zh: "深层肌肉不好摸" }}>

Subscapularis lies between the shoulder blade and the ribs, and supraspinatus sits under the shoulder's bony roof. Even infraspinatus and teres minor, closer to the surface, cannot be told apart by touch. Their attachments on the stage show their lines of pull more clearly than your fingers can.

</More>

<Lang zh>

把三角肌调淡，从后面看肩胛骨。**冈下肌**填满肩胛冈下方那片宽宽的窝，纤维汇聚到<Term id="greater-tubercle">大结节</Term>中间的小面上，大结节是肱骨上端的一个骨性突起。**小圆肌**沿着肩胛骨外侧缘的上段走，止于同一个大结节最下面的小面。两块肌肉都从关节后方拉，让上臂外旋 [M1] [M48]。

肩胛冈上方的**冈上肌**收成一条腱，从肩峰下面穿过，止于大结节最上面的小面。它帮着把手臂向侧面抬起，也在手臂活动时把肱骨头稳住。并不是它先把手臂抬起来，等三角肌接手后就不干了 [M48] [M2]。

再把肩胛骨翻过来看。**肩胛下肌**铺满肩胛骨贴着肋骨的那一面，纤维走到肱骨前面的<Term id="lesser-tubercle">小结节</Term>，负责让上臂内旋。这<Num s="M2">四块</Num>肌肉组成肩袖：它们的腱和关节囊融合在一起，从上面、前面和后面把肱骨头稳在关节盂里 [M86] [M48] [M2]。

**大圆肌**起于肩胛骨下角附近，在小圆肌下方，向外上方走，止于肱骨两个结节之间那条沟的内侧唇。它帮着把手臂往后、往身体侧面拉，并让它内旋。**它不属于肩袖**：它的腱止在肱骨较低的地方，离关节囊很远 [M48] [M2]。<FlyTo preset="back">看肩后方</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "A cuff of tendons", zh: "一圈肌腱组成的“袖口”" }}>

肩袖不是一块环形的肌肉，而是四块独立的肌肉，扁平的腱在关节的上面、前面和后面和关节囊融合在一起 [M48] [M86]。它们一起拉，让肱骨的球形头保持在浅浅的关节盂中央，就像几只手一起把一个球稳在碟子上。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deep muscles are hard to feel", zh: "深层肌肉不好摸" }}>

肩胛下肌夹在肩胛骨和肋骨之间，冈上肌藏在肩部骨性屋顶的下面。就连离表面近一些的冈下肌和小圆肌，用手摸也分不开。舞台上的附着点，比手指更能看清它们的拉力方向。

</More>
