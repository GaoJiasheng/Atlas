<Lang en>

The list of exercises at the end of this chapter has all eight, grouped by equipment: barbell, dumbbell, body weight and cable. Press one and the model takes up the exercise, the prime movers turn orange and the helpers blue-grey, and its card opens at the top of this panel with the muscles that drive the movement, the steps, cues, common mistakes and what research shows. The first time, start with body weight or a light load and learn the movement from a qualified coach; children and teenagers should train with an adult supervising [M25].

The eight exercises ask different things of the shoulder. **Presses** (barbell overhead press, pike push-up) raise the arms overhead while the elbows straighten. The **lateral raise** lifts the arms out to the side. The **chest-supported row** pulls the upper arms back and draws the shoulder blades together. **Cable rotations** turn the upper arm out or in with the elbow by your side. The **straight-arm pulldown** and **pullover** bring a raised arm back down toward the body [M48] [M2]. The shoulders also work hard in chest presses and back pulls, so count that work too.

Is one of them the right choice for everyone? No. Research measures muscle activity with surface <Term id="emg">EMG</Term>, and higher EMG is not proof that a muscle grows more [M9]. Choose exercises you can do with good form and the equipment you have; between them, a press, a raise and a row cover all three parts of the deltoid.

A few principles hold for all of them. Adults are advised to do muscle-strengthening activities on <Num s="M22">two or more days a week</Num>; Singapore's guidelines say the same [M26]. Light and heavy loads both build muscle when the sets are hard, though heavy loads build more maximal strength [M20]. Add load in small steps, only once the movement is easy, and lift and lower through a range that feels comfortable [M23]. This chapter is general information, not medical advice; for pain or an injury, see a doctor or physiotherapist.

本章末尾的动作列表收了全部八个动作，按器械分组：杠铃、哑铃、徒手和绳索。点其中一个，模型就做出这个动作，主动肌变成橙色，协同肌变成蓝灰色；这个动作的卡片会在本面板顶部打开，列出驱动动作的肌肉、步骤、要点、常见错误和研究发现。第一次做时，先用自重或轻重量，在合格教练指导下学动作；儿童和青少年要在成人监督下练习 [M25]。

这八个动作给肩部的任务各不相同。**推举**（杠铃推举、折髋俯卧撑）把手臂举过头顶，同时伸直手肘。**侧平举**把手臂向侧面抬起。**胸托划船**把上臂往后拉，同时把两块肩胛骨往中间收。**绳索旋转**在手肘贴着身体的姿势下，让上臂向外或向内转。**直臂下拉**和**仰卧上拉**把举起的手臂拉回身体 [M48] [M2]。练胸的推、练背的拉，肩部也出了不少力，这部分也要算进去。

该选哪一个？没有适合所有人的唯一答案。研究用表面<Term id="emg">肌电</Term>测量肌肉活动，而肌电高并不能证明肌肉长得更多 [M9]。选你能做得标准、手边有器械的动作就好；一个推举、一个侧平举、一个划船，就把三角肌的三个部分都练到了。

有几条原则对所有动作都适用。建议成年人<Num s="M22">每周两天或以上</Num>做肌肉强化活动；新加坡的指南也这样建议 [M26]。只要每组都练得够吃力，轻重量和大重量都能增肌，不过大重量更能提高最大力量 [M20]。等动作变轻松了，再小步加重；举起和放下都在舒服的范围里进行 [M23]。本章是一般性知识，不是医疗建议；有疼痛或受伤，请咨询医生或物理治疗师。

一种常见的肩部训练安排，不是处方：先空手或用很轻的哑铃熟悉侧平举的路线；主项做杠铃推举；接着做胸托哑铃划船；再做较轻的侧平举；最后用受控的绳索旋转收尾。多关节的大动作放在前面，较轻的单关节动作放在后面 [M23] [M24]。
