<Lang en>

The index groups the exercises by equipment: barbell, dumbbell, body weight and cable. Open a card to see its sketch, preparation, steps, cues and mistakes. Orange shows the prime movers, blue-grey the helpers. These colours describe roles; they are not measured muscle activity.

The movements ask different questions of the shoulder. **Overhead presses** and **pike push-ups** combine raising the upper arm with straightening the elbow. A **lateral raise** emphasises lifting sideways. The **chest-supported row** combines moving the upper arm backwards with drawing the scapula towards the spine. **Cable rotations** turn the humerus with the elbow near the side. The **pulldown** and **pullover** bring a raised arm back towards the body. Shoulder muscles also stabilise the joint during movements driven by the chest and back [M1] [M2].

Learn the movement with body weight or a light load and a qualified coach. Anyone <Num s="M25">under 18</Num> should practise with adult supervision. Body weight can still be demanding: use an easier variation when you cannot control the lowering phase [M25].

Choose a comfortable range you can reverse smoothly. Increase load gradually once the movement is controlled, and allow recovery between demanding sessions [M23] [M24]. Shoulder training also includes the work done during chest presses and back pulls.

Research may report <Term id="emg">EMG</Term>, an electrical activity measurement. Higher EMG does not establish greater muscle growth or prove that an exercise suits every person [M9]. These cards offer principles, not an individual training plan. They are general information, not medical advice; for pain or injury, consult a doctor or physiotherapist.

<More title={{ en: "A sample session, not a prescription", zh: "训练课示例，不是处方" }}>

A coach might begin with easy movement practice, then a suitable press, a supported row, a light lateral raise and controlled rotation work. Multi-joint work can come before simpler single-joint movements [M23]. The session in the exercise index is an example; it does not require completing every exercise or prescribe sets and repetitions.

索引按杠铃、哑铃、徒手和绳索分组。打开动作卡，可以看示意图、准备、步骤、要点和错误。橙色表示主动肌，蓝灰色表示协同肌；这些颜色说明分工，不是测出的肌肉活动。

各动作给肩部不同任务。**推举**和**折髋俯卧撑**把抬臂与伸肘结合；**侧平举**侧重侧举；**胸托划船**同时让上臂后移、肩胛骨靠向脊柱；**绳索旋转**让肱骨转动，肘靠近身体；**下拉**和**上拉**把举起的手臂拉回。胸背肌肉主导的动作里，肩部也要稳定关节 [M1] [M2]。

第一次用自重或轻重量，在合格教练指导下学。<Num s="M25">未满十八岁</Num>应在成人监督下练习。徒手也可能很吃力；下放不能控制时，选择更简单的变化 [M25]。

选择舒服、能平稳返回的幅度。动作受控后才小步加重，较累的训练之间留出恢复 [M23] [M24]。练胸的推和练背的拉，也计入肩部负荷。
