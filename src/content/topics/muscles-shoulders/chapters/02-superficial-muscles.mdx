<Lang en>

The deltoid wraps the front, side and back of the shoulder. It is one muscle with <Num s="M1">three</Num> parts, which gyms call the front, side and rear delts. Each part starts on a different bone, and that is why each pulls the arm a different way [M1] [M48] [M31]. On the stage, hollow circles mark origins and solid circles insertions.

The **clavicular part** (front deltoid) starts on the front edge and upper surface of the outer <Num s="M1">third</Num> of the collarbone. The **acromial part** (side deltoid) starts on the outer edge and upper surface of the <Term id="acromion">acromion</Term>, the flat roof of bone at the top of the shoulder. The **spinal part** (rear deltoid) starts on the lower lip of the scapular spine, the ridge across the back of the shoulder blade [M1] [M48].

All three meet on the <Term id="deltoid-tuberosity">deltoid tuberosity</Term>, a rough patch halfway down the outer side of the humerus. Inside the middle part, short fibres run at an angle between several tendon sheets, an arrangement called <Term id="multipennate">multipennate</Term>; the front and rear parts are built more simply. The moving stripes on the model show only the general fibre direction, not every bundle [M1] [M48]. <FlyTo preset="back">See the rear part</FlyTo>

The front part raises the arm forward, which is shoulder flexion. The middle part lifts it out to the side. The rear part pulls the arm back and opens a raised arm out and backward [M2] [M31]. Try it on yourself without a weight: lift your arm forward, sideways and back, and feel a different part of the shoulder cap harden each time.

<More title={{ en: "Two sets of names", zh: "两套名字" }}>

Clavicular, acromial and spinal name the bones the parts start from; front, side and rear (anterior, lateral, posterior) describe where they sit. Both name the same three parts of one muscle, not extra muscles. The deltoid's own sheet describes the whole muscle; the stage and the index let you pick each part.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Touch lightly", zh: "轻轻摸就好" }}>

Use the other hand and an easy lift without a weight; you do not need resistance to feel the deltoid. The cuff muscles lie under it and cannot be told apart by pressing through it. Their attachment markers on the stage are a better guide.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

三角肌包住肩部的前面、侧面和后面。它是一块肌肉，分<Num s="M1">三个</Num>部分，健身房里叫前束、中束和后束。三个部分起于不同的骨头，所以各自把手臂往不同方向拉 [M1] [M48] [M31]。舞台上，空心圆表示起点，实心圆表示止点。

**锁骨部**（三角肌前部）起于锁骨外侧<Num s="M1">三分之一</Num>的前缘和上面。**肩峰部**（三角肌中部）起于<Term id="acromion">肩峰</Term>的外侧缘和上面，肩峰就是肩膀顶上那块扁平的“骨头屋顶”。**肩胛冈部**（三角肌后部）起于肩胛冈的下唇，肩胛冈是横过肩胛骨背面的那条骨嵴 [M1] [M48]。

三个部分都止于<Term id="deltoid-tuberosity">三角肌粗隆</Term>，那是肱骨外侧中段一块粗糙的地方。中部肌肉内部有几片腱膜，短短的肌纤维斜着接在它们之间，叫<Term id="multipennate">多羽状排列</Term>；前部和后部的结构简单一些。模型上移动的条纹只表示大致的纤维方向，并没有画出每一束 [M1] [M48]。<FlyTo preset="back">看后部</FlyTo>

前部把手臂向前抬，也就是肩屈。中部把手臂向侧面抬起。后部把手臂往后拉，也能把抬起的手臂向外、向后打开 [M2] [M31]。不拿重量在自己身上试试：手臂分别向前、向侧面、向后抬，每次肩头变硬的地方都不一样。

<More title={{ en: "Two sets of names", zh: "两套名字" }}>

锁骨部、肩峰部、肩胛冈部，是按起点所在的骨头命名的；前部、中部、后部，说的是它们在身上的位置。两套名字说的都是同一块肌肉的三个部分，不是另外几块肌肉。三角肌整体的肌肉卡讲整块肌肉，舞台和索引则可以单独选中每一部分。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Touch lightly", zh: "轻轻摸就好" }}>

用另一只手，在不负重、轻松的抬臂动作中摸。要摸到三角肌，不需要任何阻力。肩袖肌肉在它下面，隔着三角肌按是分不出来的；舞台上的附着点标记更能帮你找到它们。

</More>
