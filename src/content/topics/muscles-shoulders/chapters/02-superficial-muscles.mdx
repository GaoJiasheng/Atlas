<Lang en>

Deltoid wraps the front, side and back of the shoulder. It is one muscle with <Num s="M1">three</Num> anatomical parts, often called the front, side and rear delts in a gym. Their different starting points explain their different pulls [M1] [M48] [M31].

The **clavicular part (anterior deltoid)** begins on the front edge and upper surface of the outer <Num s="M1">third</Num> of the clavicle. Its fibres descend towards the arm. The **acromial part (lateral deltoid)** begins on the outer edge and upper surface of the <Term id="acromion">acromion</Term>, the scapula's roof-like projection. The **scapular spinal part (posterior deltoid)** begins on the lower lip of the scapular spine behind the shoulder [M1] [M48].

All meet on the <Term id="deltoid-tuberosity">deltoid tuberosity</Term>, a rough area on the outer humeral shaft. The middle part has a <Term id="multipennate">multipennate</Term> arrangement: fibres run obliquely between internal tendon slips. The front and rear parts have a different internal arrangement; the model's straight stripe is a teaching simplification, not a drawing of every bundle [M1] [M48]. <FlyTo preset="back">See the rear part</FlyTo>

The front part helps with shoulder flexion, bringing the arm forward. The lateral part is prominent in abduction, lifting it sideways. The rear part helps with extension and horizontal abduction, pulling it back or opening a raised arm backwards [M2] [M31]. Feel the surface gently during unloaded movements. Feeling a muscle firm up tells you that it is working; it cannot measure its force or prove that only that part is active.

<More title={{ en: "Names describe the attachment", zh: "名字描述附着位置" }}>

Clavicular, acromial and scapular spinal name the bones or projections from which the parts arise. Anterior, lateral and posterior describe their positions on the body. They refer to the same parts, not extra muscles. A whole deltoid sheet groups them, while each mesh and index entry selects one part.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Touch lightly", zh: "轻轻摸就好" }}>

Use the opposite hand and a comfortable unloaded lift. You need no hard resistance to recognise the rounded superficial muscle. Deep cuff muscles cannot be identified reliably by poking through deltoid. The diagram and attachment markers are more useful for locating them.

</More>

<Lang zh>

三角肌包住肩部前面、侧面和后面。看模型时，可以从锁骨沿纤维追到上臂，再从肩后方比较另一条拉力线。骨头上的附着标记，比只看肩外形更能说明肌肉走向。它是一块肌肉，分<Num s="M1">三个</Num>解剖部分，健身时常叫前束、中束、后束。各部分的起点不同，所以拉力方向不同 [M1] [M48] [M31]。

**锁骨部（三角肌前部）**起于锁骨外侧<Num s="M1">三分之一</Num>的前缘和上面，纤维向下走向上臂。**肩峰部（三角肌中部）**起于<Term id="acromion">肩峰</Term>的外侧缘和上面，肩峰是肩胛骨向外伸出的屋顶状突起。**肩胛冈部（三角肌后部）**起于肩后方的肩胛冈下唇 [M1] [M48]。

各部分都汇到<Term id="deltoid-tuberosity">三角肌粗隆</Term>，也就是肱骨干外侧的粗糙附着区。中部呈<Term id="multipennate">多羽状排列</Term>：纤维斜接在内部腱束之间。前后部的内部排列有所不同；模型的直条纹只是教学简化，并没有画出每一束 [M1] [M48]。<FlyTo preset="back">看后部</FlyTo>

前部帮助肩屈，把手臂前举；中部在侧举的肩外展中发挥重要作用；后部帮助肩伸与水平外展，把手臂后拉，或把抬起的手臂向后打开 [M2] [M31]。不负重时轻摸表面即可辨认三角肌。摸到变硬只能说明肌肉在工作，不能测出力量，也不能证明只有这一部分在活动。

<More title={{ en: "Names describe the attachment", zh: "名字描述附着位置" }}>

锁骨部、肩峰部和肩胛冈部按起点所在的骨或骨突命名。前部、中部和后部描述身体上的位置。两套名字说的是同样的部分，并不是额外的肌肉。整体三角肌卡把它们归在一起，每个网格和索引条目则选中其中一部。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Touch lightly", zh: "轻轻摸就好" }}>

用另一只手，在舒服、不负重的抬臂动作中轻摸。辨认表面的圆形肌肉，不需要很大的阻力。隔着三角肌用力戳，不能可靠地区分深处的肩袖肌。定位这些肌肉时，图谱和附着点标记更有帮助。

</More>
