<Lang en>

Put a hand over the rounded top of your other arm. The muscle just under the skin is **deltoid**. Beneath it are smaller muscles running from the shoulder blade to the upper arm. This shoulder region lets you reach a shelf, raise an arm sideways and pull something towards you [M1] [M2].

The model separates the region into surface, middle and deep display layers. The surface holds deltoid's anterior, lateral and posterior parts. The middle holds **infraspinatus**, **teres minor** and **teres major** behind the shoulder blade. Deep inside are **supraspinatus**, above the scapular spine, and **subscapularis**, on the blade's rib-facing surface. These are teaching groups, rather than uniform sheets covering the whole shoulder.

The bones explain the arrangement. The collarbone links the shoulder to the chest. The scapula carries the <Term id="glenoid">glenoid</Term>, a shallow socket receiving the head of the humerus. Muscles pull across this joint from an <Term id="origin">origin</Term> to an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term>; the socket and the shoulder blade can move too [M1] [M2].

Remember the central distinction. Deltoid's front part helps raise the arm forward, its lateral part raises it sideways, and its rear part pulls it back. The <Term id="rotator-cuff">rotator cuff</Term> has <Num s="M2">four</Num> muscles: supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis. Their shared task is stabilising the humeral head in the glenoid during movement [M86]. **Teres major is not part of the cuff** [M1] [M2]. <FlyTo preset="front">See the front</FlyTo>

把手放在另一侧上臂顶端圆圆的地方。皮肤下面的肌肉就是**三角肌**。它里面还有从肩胛骨长到肱骨的较小肌肉。这个区域让你够到架子、向侧面抬臂，也能把东西拉向自己 [M1] [M2]。

模型把本区分成浅、中、深层显示。浅层是三角肌的前、中、后三部分。中层有肩胛骨后方的**冈下肌**、**小圆肌**和**大圆肌**。深层有肩胛冈上方的**冈上肌**，以及贴在肩胛骨朝向肋骨那一面的**肩胛下肌**。这些是教学分组，并不是覆盖整个肩部的整齐薄片。

看骨头就能理解这个安排。锁骨把肩部连到胸前。肩胛骨上的<Term id="glenoid">关节盂</Term>是一个浅窝，承接肱骨头。肌肉从<Term id="origin">起点</Term>长到<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>，跨过关节拉动骨头；关节盂所在的肩胛骨也会活动 [M1] [M2]。因此抬臂不只是上臂骨在一个固定窝里转，肩带周围的邻区肌肉也要配合。淡显的骨架与肌肉帮助你看清这种相互关系。

记住关键区别：三角肌前部帮助前举，中部帮助侧举，后部帮助后拉。<Term id="rotator-cuff">肩袖</Term>有<Num s="M2">四块</Num>肌肉：冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌和肩胛下肌。它们共同的重要任务，是在活动时把肱骨头稳在关节盂里 [M86]。**大圆肌不属于肩袖** [M1] [M2]。<FlyTo preset="front">看正面</FlyTo>
