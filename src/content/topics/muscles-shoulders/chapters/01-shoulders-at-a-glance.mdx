<Lang en>

The model on the stage is a man's upper body, built from a real anatomical scan. The shoulder muscles are drawn in warm colours, the bones in ivory, the neighbouring muscles of the chest, back and arm in grey, and the skin as a faint outline. Left and right always mean the body's own left and right.

Put a hand over the rounded top of your other arm: the muscle just under the skin is the **deltoid**, the surface layer, with a front, a middle and a rear part. Under it, on the back of the shoulder blade, the middle layer holds **infraspinatus**, **teres minor** and **teres major**. The deep layer holds **supraspinatus**, above the bony ridge of the shoulder blade, and **subscapularis**, on the side of the blade that faces the ribs [M1] [M48].

The bones explain how they work. The collarbone joins the shoulder to the chest. The shoulder blade carries the <Term id="glenoid">glenoid</Term>, a shallow socket for the ball-shaped head of the humerus. Every muscle has an <Term id="origin">origin</Term>, the end that usually stays still, and an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term>, the end it pulls. Here most of them start on the shoulder blade or the collarbone and pull across the joint on the humerus [M1] [M2].

Remember one distinction. The deltoid moves the arm: its front part raises it forward, its middle part sideways and its rear part pulls it back. The <Term id="rotator-cuff">rotator cuff</Term> is <Num s="M2">four</Num> muscles, supraspinatus, infraspinatus, teres minor and subscapularis, whose main job is to hold the head of the humerus in its socket while the arm moves [M86]. **Teres major is not part of the cuff** [M48] [M2]. <FlyTo preset="front">See the front</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Peel, not explode", zh: "剥层，而不是拆开" }}>

In this topic the explode slider is called PEEL. It lifts the deltoid first, then the middle layer, then the deep layer, the order in which an anatomist would uncover them. The layers are a teaching order: the cuff muscles do not form three neat sheets in the body.

<Lang zh>

舞台上的模型是一名男性的上半身，根据真实的解剖扫描数据建成。肩部肌肉画成暖色，骨骼是象牙色，胸、背和上臂的邻近肌肉画成灰色，皮肤是一层淡淡的轮廓。文中说的左和右，一律指身体自己的左右。

把手放在另一侧上臂顶端圆鼓鼓的地方：皮肤下面那块肌肉就是**三角肌**，也就是浅层，它分前部、中部和后部。三角肌下面、肩胛骨背面是中层：**冈下肌**、**小圆肌**和**大圆肌**。深层有肩胛骨那条骨嵴上方的**冈上肌**，以及铺在肩胛骨贴着肋骨那一面的**肩胛下肌** [M1] [M48]。

看骨头就明白它们怎么工作。锁骨把肩连到胸前。肩胛骨上有<Term id="glenoid">关节盂</Term>，一个浅浅的窝，接住肱骨球形的头。每块肌肉都有<Term id="origin">起点</Term>，通常是不动的那一端；还有<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>，是它拉动的那一端。这里的肌肉大多起于肩胛骨或锁骨，跨过关节去拉肱骨 [M1] [M2]。

记住一个区别。三角肌负责移动手臂：前部把手臂向前抬，中部向侧面抬，后部向后拉。<Term id="rotator-cuff">肩袖</Term>是<Num s="M2">四块</Num>肌肉，冈上肌、冈下肌、小圆肌和肩胛下肌，它们最主要的任务，是在手臂活动时把肱骨头稳在关节盂里 [M86]。**大圆肌不属于肩袖** [M48] [M2]。<FlyTo preset="front">看正面</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Peel, not explode", zh: "剥层，而不是拆开" }}>

在这个主题里，拆开滑块叫“剥层”。它先抬起三角肌，再是中层，最后是深层，就像解剖时一层层揭开的顺序。分层是讲解用的顺序：肩袖的几块肌肉在身体里并不是整整齐齐的三层。
