<Lang en>

Watch the upper arm rather than the hand. A muscle pulls its insertion toward its origin, and which side of the joint its tendon passes decides the direction. Each beat of this chapter moves the arm one way so that you can see which muscles shorten [M1].

The **front deltoid** raises the arm forward, which is shoulder flexion; the clavicular part of pectoralis major helps. The **middle deltoid** lifts the arm out to the side, called <Term id="abduction">abduction</Term>. The **rear deltoid** pulls the arm back and opens a raised arm out and backward, called <Term id="horizontal-abduction">horizontal abduction</Term>. These are the three jobs behind the gym names front, side and rear delts, though the parts also help each other [M2] [M31].

The cuff turns the arm about its own long axis. **Infraspinatus** and **teres minor** turn it outward, which is <Term id="external-rotation">external rotation</Term>; **subscapularis** turns it inward. Bend your elbow to a right angle by your side to see it: the forearm swings like a pointer while the upper arm turns, and the trunk does not need to twist [M1] [M48]. **Supraspinatus** helps lift the arm sideways.

Whatever the arm does, all <Num s="M2">four</Num> cuff muscles keep working to hold the head of the humerus in its socket [M86]. **Teres major**, outside the cuff, helps pull a raised arm back, down and inward [M48] [M2].

Finally, the shoulder blade is a moving base, not a fixed wall. Serratus anterior and trapezius turn it as you reach and lift overhead. Drawing the shoulder blade toward the spine is a different movement from pulling the humerus back, though a row does both [M2] [M29]. <FlyTo preset="left-side">See the moving base</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "What the model can and cannot move", zh: "模型能动什么、不能动什么" }}>

The shoulder and elbow turn on a simplified skeleton, and the shoulder blade only turns a little on the collarbone instead of sliding over the ribs. Raising the arm far overhead stretches the model's skin around the armpit, so the poses stop short of the full range. Holding the joint steady cannot be animated at all; that beat simply shows the four cuff muscles together. The angles are chosen to show direction; they are not measured ranges of movement.

<Lang zh>

看上臂，而不是只看手。肌肉把止点拉向起点，它的腱从关节哪一侧经过，就决定往哪个方向拉。这一章每一拍让手臂朝一个方向动，方便你看清是哪些肌肉在缩短 [M1]。

**三角肌前部**把手臂向前抬，也就是肩屈，胸大肌锁骨部在旁边帮忙。**三角肌中部**把手臂向侧面抬起，叫<Term id="abduction">外展</Term>。**三角肌后部**把手臂往后拉，也能把抬起的手臂向外、向后打开，叫<Term id="horizontal-abduction">水平外展</Term>。健身房里说的前束、中束、后束，对应的就是这三件事，不过三部分也会互相帮忙 [M2] [M31]。

肩袖让手臂绕着自己的长轴转。**冈下肌**和**小圆肌**让上臂向外转，叫<Term id="external-rotation">外旋</Term>；**肩胛下肌**让它向内转。把手肘贴在身体侧面、弯成直角就看得清：上臂一转，前臂就像指针一样摆动，躯干不用跟着扭 [M1] [M48]。**冈上肌**帮着把手臂向侧面抬起。

不管手臂在做什么，四块肩袖肌都一直在工作，把肱骨头稳在关节盂里 [M86]。不属于肩袖的**大圆肌**，帮着把抬起的手臂往后、往下、往里拉 [M48] [M2]。

最后，肩胛骨是一个会动的底座，不是一堵固定的墙。伸手够东西、举手过头时，前锯肌和斜方肌会转动它。把肩胛骨拉向脊柱，和把肱骨往后拉，是两个不同的动作，不过划船时两者会一起发生 [M2] [M29]。<FlyTo preset="left-side">看会动的底座</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "What the model can and cannot move", zh: "模型能动什么、不能动什么" }}>

肩和肘在一副简化的骨架上转动，肩胛骨只能绕着锁骨稍微转一点，不会真的在肋骨上滑动。手臂举得太高，模型腋窝附近的皮肤会被拉变形，所以姿态没有做到最大幅度。“稳住关节”这件事本身没法用动画表现，那一拍只是把四块肩袖肌一起标出来。演示的角度是为了看清方向，不是测量出来的活动范围。

</More>
