<Lang en>

Watch the upper arm rather than the hand. A muscle pulls its insertion towards its origin; where its tendon passes around the joint helps decide the direction. The same muscle can contribute differently as the arm changes position [M1].

**Anterior deltoid** helps with shoulder flexion: the arm rises forward. **Lateral deltoid** helps with <Term id="abduction">abduction</Term>, lifting the arm away from the body's side. **Posterior deltoid** helps with extension, pulling it backwards, and <Term id="horizontal-abduction">horizontal abduction</Term>, opening a raised arm backwards. These are the useful front, side and rear distinctions, with overlapping roles [M2] [M31].

**Supraspinatus** assists abduction and stabilisation. **Infraspinatus** and **teres minor** produce <Term id="external-rotation">external rotation</Term>, turning the humerus outward. **Subscapularis** produces internal rotation. Bend the elbow comfortably near the side to see rotation clearly: the forearm swings as the upper arm turns, without the torso twisting [M1].

Together the cuff's <Num s="M2">four</Num> muscles support the humeral head in the glenoid. That stabilising work accompanies lifting and turning [M86]. **Teres major**, outside the cuff, helps draw the arm back, inward and towards the side [M1] [M2].

Finally, the scapula is not a fixed wall. Serratus anterior and trapezius help move it when you reach or lift overhead. Pulling the shoulder blade towards the spine is different from pulling the humerus backwards; both can happen during a row [M2] [M29]. <FlyTo preset="left-side">See the moving base</FlyTo>

<Lang zh>

观察上臂，而不是只看手。肌肉把止点拉向起点，腱从关节哪一侧经过，有助于决定拉动方向。手臂位置变化时，同一块肌肉也可能以不同方式参与 [M1]。

**三角肌前部**帮助肩屈，让手臂前举；**中部**帮助<Term id="abduction">外展</Term>，让手臂离开身体侧面；**后部**帮助肩伸，把手臂后拉，也帮助<Term id="horizontal-abduction">水平外展</Term>，把抬起的手臂向后打开。前、中、后的区别很有用，但工作仍会重叠 [M2] [M31]。

**冈上肌**协助外展与稳定。**冈下肌**和**小圆肌**让肱骨<Term id="external-rotation">外旋</Term>；**肩胛下肌**让它内旋。把手肘舒服地弯曲、靠近身体，就能看清旋转：上臂转动时前臂摆动，躯干不用跟着扭 [M1]。这种观察方法把肩部旋转与弯肘区分开，也把上臂骨移动与肩胛骨移动区分开。模型里的收缩条纹让拉力方向更容易看清，但颜色深浅不能比较肌肉的实际用力大小。

肩袖的<Num s="M2">四块</Num>肌肉一起支持关节盂里的肱骨头，稳定工作伴随抬臂和旋转 [M86]。袖外的**大圆肌**帮助把手臂往后、往内、往身体侧面拉 [M1] [M2]。

肩胛骨也不是固定墙壁。伸手或高举时，前锯肌和斜方肌帮助移动它。肩胛骨向脊柱靠拢，与肱骨向后移动是不同动作；划船时两者可以同时发生 [M2] [M29]。<FlyTo preset="left-side">看活动的底座</FlyTo>
