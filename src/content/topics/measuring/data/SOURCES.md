# Sources · measuring

The syllabus lines quoted in the lesson come from S1 only (wording and page numbers checked against the PDF on
2026-10-19, docs/32 §1.1). The MOE textbooks (S4) were used for the usual order and the words used in class
("Each small part stands for …", "We write ℓ for litre"), not copied. The misconception research (S5–S12) shapes
which mistakes the beats name and which wrong answers the checks diagnose; it is not quoted in the chapters, and
the lesson does not claim how often any mistake happens. Heights, masses, distances and amounts of juice in the
beats and questions are made-up simple numbers, not measurements or typical values; the path with a post every
100 m is imaginary.

The cover drawing (a wet-market weighing scale with a fish) and the three chapter figures
(`public/illustrations/figures/measuring-{height-board,ball-and-stone,sugarcane}.webp`) are illustrations made for
this lesson (pencil style, `scripts/illustrate.ts`). None of them carries numbers, letters or brand names, and none
is used to read a scale: every scale in the lesson is drawn on the stage, with its parts the same size.
