<Lang en>

A <Term id="balance">balance</Term> compares two sides. A weighing scale shows a <Term id="mass">mass</Term> with a needle instead, and you read it the same way as a measuring tape.

First, <Beat n={1}>check the empty scale</Beat>. With nothing on the pan, the needle should point to 0. If it starts somewhere else, every reading will be out by that much. You can check this at a wet-market stall too.

Watch <Beat n={2}>the dial straighten into a number line</Beat>. The <Term id="scale">marks</Term> and numbers stay in the same order and the same distance apart: 0, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500. A dial is a number line bent into a circle; bending it changes its shape, not the amounts.

Now, with the dial curled back, <Beat n={3}>look between 200 and 300</Beat>. Four short marks make five equal parts. Count the parts, not the marks. The difference is 100, and 100 ÷ 5 = 20. Each <Term id="small-part">small part</Term> stands for <Qty u="20 g" />. Dividing by four would give 25, which is a mistake: four counts only the short marks.

Next, <Beat n={4}>read the needle for Siti's prawns</Beat>. Start at the numbered mark just before it, 200, and count four parts in twenties: 220, 240, 260, 280. The prawns have a mass of <Qty u="280 g" />. Adding just 4 to 200 is a mistake: each part stands for <Qty u="20 g" />, so the answer is not <Qty u="204 g" />.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>read the scale for the watermelon</Beat>. This bigger scale is marked kg, for <Term id="kilogram">kilograms</Term>, not g. Its numbers go up in twos with one short mark between, so each small part stands for <Qty u="1 kg" />. The needle is one part past 2: the watermelon is <Qty u="3 kg" />. Writing <Qty u="3 g" /> would name a mass as light as three paper clips, so always read the unit on the dial too.

<Lang zh>

<Term id="balance">天平</Term>是两边比轻重。指针秤不一样，它用一根指针直接指出东西的<Term id="mass">质量</Term>。读指针秤的办法，和读卷尺是一样的。

第一件事，<Beat n={1}>看空秤</Beat>。盘上什么都没有的时候，指针应该指着 0。要是一开始就没指着 0，后面称出来的数就都差了那么多。在湿巴刹买东西时，你也可以先这样看一看。

再看<Beat n={2}>秤盘拉直成一条数轴</Beat>。<Term id="scale">刻度</Term>和数的顺序不变，间隔也不变：0、100、200、300、400、500。秤盘其实就是弯成圆圈的数轴，弯起来只是样子变了，表示的量没有变。

秤盘卷回去以后，<Beat n={3}>看 200 到 300 这一段</Beat>。中间有 4 条短线，分出 5 小格。数格子，不数刻度线。两数相差 100，100 ÷ 5 = 20，所以<Term id="small-part">每一小格</Term>表示 <Qty u="20 g" />。要是除以 4，就得到 25，那是只数了短线，把格子少算了一格。

接着<Beat n={4}>读 Siti 买的虾</Beat>。从指针前面那个标了数的刻度 200 开始，往后数 4 格，二十二十地数：220、240、260、280。虾的质量是 <Qty u="280 g" />。如果每格只加 1，就会读成 <Qty u="204 g" />，那是把每格当成了 <Qty u="1 g" />。

最后，<Beat n={5}>读称西瓜的秤</Beat>。这台大一点的秤上写的是 kg，就是<Term id="kilogram">千克</Term>，不是 g。它的数一次跳 2，中间一条短线，所以每一小格表示 <Qty u="1 kg" />。指针在 2 后面 1 格，西瓜是 <Qty u="3 kg" />。写成 <Qty u="3 g" /> 就只有三个回形针那么重了。所以读完数，还要看清秤上的单位。

准备好了，就<Task id="weighing-scales">做一道小题</Task>：读指针秤，或者往秤上加东西。

</Lang>
