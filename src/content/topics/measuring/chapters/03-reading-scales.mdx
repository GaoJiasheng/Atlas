<Lang en>

You have counted <Term id="centimetre">centimetre</Term> parts between a thing's ends. On many measuring tools, not every mark has a number, so first you need to find what each part stands for.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the measuring tape with an arrow</Beat>. Its <Term id="scale">scale</Term> has numbers going up in tens, with one short mark halfway between each pair. The marks are equally spaced, but a part here does not stand for <Qty u="1 cm" />.

The magnifier lets you <Beat n={2}>look between 30 and 40</Beat>. These are numbered marks next to each other, and 40 − 30 = 10. The one short mark splits that distance into two equal parts. Share 10 between them: 10 ÷ 2 = 5. Each <Term id="small-part">small part</Term> stands for <Qty u="5 cm" />.

Now <Beat n={3}>count on from 30 to the arrow</Beat>. Start at the numbered mark just before the arrow. There is one small part to cross, so 30 + 5 = 35. The arrow points to <Qty u="35 cm" />. You can count on from 30 instead of counting all the way from 0.

Next, <Beat n={4}>look at Mei Ling's height chart</Beat>. Between 120 and 130 there are four short marks. They make five equal parts. Count the parts, not the marks. The difference is 130 − 120 = 10, and 10 shared between five parts is 2: each small part stands for <Qty u="2 cm" />.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>count up to the head board</Beat>. It is four parts above 120: 122, 124, 126, 128. You can write 120 + 4 × 2 = 128. Mei Ling is <Qty u="128 cm" /> tall.

Taking every part as <Qty u="1 cm" /> is a mistake: it would read the arrow as <Qty u="31 cm" />. Dividing by the four short marks on the height chart is a mistake too, because there are five parts to share between.

<Lang zh>

前两步的尺子，每一格都是 <Qty u="1 cm" />，每条刻度线旁边也都标了数。可是很多量具不是这样：有的刻度线标了数，有的没有。读这样的量具，先要弄清楚每一小格表示多少。

先看<Beat n={1}>这条带箭头的卷尺</Beat>。它的<Term id="scale">刻度</Term>一次跳 10，两个数中间只有一条短线。线和线的间隔都一样，可是这里的一格并不是 <Qty u="1 cm" />。

用放大镜<Beat n={2}>看 30 到 40 这一段</Beat>。30 和 40 是两个相邻的标数，相差 40 − 30 = 10。中间那条短线把这一段平均分成 2 格，10 平均分成 2 份，10 ÷ 2 = 5。所以<Term id="small-part">每一小格</Term>表示 <Qty u="5 cm" />。

再<Beat n={3}>从 30 往后数到箭头</Beat>。从箭头前面那个标了数的刻度开始，往后只有 1 格，30 + 5 = 35。箭头指着 <Qty u="35 cm" />。不必从 0 一格一格数起，从最近的标数往后数就行了。

接着看<Beat n={4}>Mei Ling 的身高尺</Beat>。120 和 130 之间有 4 条短线，它们把这一段分成 5 格。数格子，不数刻度线。两数相差 130 − 120 = 10，10 平均分成 5 格，每格是 2。所以这把尺上，每一小格表示 <Qty u="2 cm" />。

最后<Beat n={5}>往上数到头顶的木板</Beat>。木板在 120 上面 4 格：122、124、126、128。也可以写成 120 + 4 × 2 = 128。Mei Ling 身高 <Qty u="128 cm" />。

这一步有两个容易犯的错误。第一，把每一格都当成 <Qty u="1 cm" />，卷尺上的箭头就会被读成 <Qty u="31 cm" />。第二，用短线的条数去除：身高尺上有 4 条短线，可格子是 5 格，要除以 5，不是除以 4。

准备好了，就<Task id="reading-scales">做一道小题</Task>：先找出每一小格表示多少，再读刻度。
