<Lang en>

The practice has eight questions from the whole lesson, in a fixed order. They use the same kinds of tasks as the chapters: reading a ruler, a weighing scale and a height chart, tapping the small parts of a scale, pouring to a mark, choosing an estimate, putting measurements in order and changing units. The step that goes further into decimals is left out.

Question 1 asks how long a key is when it does not start at 0. Questions 2 and 3 use weighing scales: first find what each small part stands for, then read the mass of some long beans. Question 4 asks you to fill a measuring cup to a mark. Question 5 asks for a sensible estimate of a watermelon's mass. Question 6 puts three bags of rice in order, from the lightest; keep each number with its unit. Questions 7 and 8 use two units: read a height in metres and centimetres, then change a mass into grams.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why. If it is not, the stage draws the answer with a dashed line, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time and look at the unit before you press it.

Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing. The tools for zooming in and counting the parts are there too.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. **Try again** starts the same eight questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

Question 1 is on the stage, with its answer controls above this text. The squares on the chapter axis are the eight questions.

<Lang zh>

练习有 8 道题，来自整节课，顺序固定。题型和各章的小题一样：读尺子、读指针秤、读身高尺，点出刻度上的每一小格，倒水到某条刻度，选一个合理的估计，给几个量排顺序，换单位。“再往前一步”那一章讲的小数刻度不在练习里。

第 1 题问一把不从 0 开始的钥匙有多长。第 2、3 题用指针秤：先找出每一小格表示多少，再读出一些长豆有多重。第 4 题要把量杯倒到指定的刻度。第 5 题要给一个西瓜的质量选一个合理的估计。第 6 题把三袋米从轻到重排，数和单位要一起看。第 7、8 题用两个单位：先用米和厘米读出身高，再把一个质量化成克。

每题只检查一次。按“检查”以后，马上就能看到对不对、为什么。如果还没对，舞台会用虚线画出答案，还有一个按钮可以回到教这一点的那一步。检查以后答案就不能再改，所以按之前慢慢想，也看一看单位。

每题有一条提示，看了提示不扣分。放大和数格子的工具也照样能用。

做完最后一题，会出现一张小表：每题一行，标出 ✓ 或 ○，以及可以回看的那一步。按“再做一次”，同样的 8 道题从头开始。不计时，也不保存。

第 1 题已经在舞台上，作答的地方在这段文字上方。章节轴上的 8 个小方块就是这 8 道题。
