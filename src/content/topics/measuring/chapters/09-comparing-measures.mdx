<Lang en>

You have chosen units and used an <Term id="estimate">estimate</Term> to check a reading. Now use the numbers and their units together to compare measurements and put them in order.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the two ribbons on rulers</Beat>. Both start at 0, so read their other ends: ribbon A is <Qty u="12 cm" /> and ribbon B is <Qty u="9 cm" />. Both are in <Term id="centimetre">centimetres</Term>, so compare 12 and 9: A is longer. Take away to find how much longer: <Qty u="12 cm" /> − <Qty u="9 cm" /> = <Qty u="3 cm" />, the three parts of A that stick out past B.

Next, <Beat n={2}>compare the two bags of rice</Beat>. Bag A has a <Term id="mass">mass</Term> of <Qty u="900 g" /> and bag B has a mass of <Qty u="1 kg" />. 900 is more than 1, but the units are different. A <Term id="kilogram">kilogram</Term> is a thousand <Term id="gram">grams</Term>: <Qty u="1 kg" /> = <Qty u="1000 g" />. On the same scale, bag B takes the needle to 1000, past 900. So <Qty u="900 g" /> &lt; <Qty u="1 kg" />: bag B is heavier.

Now <Beat n={3}>order the three strings</Beat> from the shortest. They are <Qty u="85 cm" />, <Qty u="1 m" /> and <Qty u="120 cm" />. Change <Qty u="1 m" /> to <Qty u="100 cm" /> first, then compare 85, 100 and 120. The order is A, then B, then C. String B is no longer or shorter for being written in centimetres; only the way of writing it has changed.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>read the mango and the guava</Beat>. On these scales each <Term id="small-part">small part</Term> stands for <Qty u="10 g" />. The mango's needle is two parts past 500: <Qty u="520 g" />. The guava's needle is halfway between 400 and 500: <Qty u="450 g" />. How much heavier is the mango? Take away: <Qty u="520 g" /> − <Qty u="450 g" /> = <Qty u="70 g" />.

Comparing only the numbers is a mistake when the units are different. 900 is more than 1, but <Qty u="900 g" /> is less than <Qty u="1 kg" />. Change to the same unit first.

When you are ready, <Task id="comparing-measures">try one question</Task> on comparing, ordering or finding how much more.

<Lang zh>

上一步学会了选单位，也学会了用估计来检查读数。这一步要把数和单位放在一起看，比一比、排一排。

先看<Beat n={1}>两把尺子上的丝带</Beat>。它们都从 0 开始，看另一端就行：丝带 A 长 <Qty u="12 cm" />，丝带 B 长 <Qty u="9 cm" />。单位都是<Term id="centimetre">厘米</Term>，直接比数，12 比 9 大，A 更长。长多少？用减法：<Qty u="12 cm" /> − <Qty u="9 cm" /> = <Qty u="3 cm" />，就是 A 比 B 多出来的那 3 格。

再<Beat n={2}>比一比两袋米</Beat>。A 袋的<Term id="mass">质量</Term>是 <Qty u="900 g" />，B 袋是 <Qty u="1 kg" />。900 比 1 大，可是单位不一样。1 <Term id="kilogram">千克</Term>是 1000 <Term id="gram">克</Term>：<Qty u="1 kg" /> = <Qty u="1000 g" />。放在同一种秤上，B 袋让指针转到 1000，比 900 还多。所以 <Qty u="900 g" /> &lt; <Qty u="1 kg" />，B 袋更重。

接着<Beat n={3}>把三根绳子从短到长排</Beat>。它们是 <Qty u="85 cm" />、<Qty u="1 m" /> 和 <Qty u="120 cm" />。先把 <Qty u="1 m" /> 换成 <Qty u="100 cm" />，再比 85、100、120。顺序是 A、B、C。绳子 B 换成厘米来写，并没有变长或变短，变的只是写法。

最后，<Beat n={4}>读芒果和番石榴</Beat>。这两台秤上，<Term id="small-part">每一小格</Term>表示 <Qty u="10 g" />。芒果的指针在 500 后面 2 格：<Qty u="520 g" />。番石榴的指针在 400 和 500 正中间：<Qty u="450 g" />。芒果比番石榴重多少？用减法：<Qty u="520 g" /> − <Qty u="450 g" /> = <Qty u="70 g" />。

最容易出的错，是单位不一样时只比数。900 比 1 大，可是 <Qty u="900 g" /> 比 <Qty u="1 kg" /> 轻。单位不一样，先换成同一个单位，再比。

准备好了，就<Task id="comparing-measures">做一道小题</Task>：比一比、排一排，或者算出多多少。
