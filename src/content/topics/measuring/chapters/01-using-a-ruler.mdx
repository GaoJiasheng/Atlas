<Lang en>

You can look at two things and say which seems longer. A ruler lets you say how long something is, using equal parts that anyone else can measure too.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the pencil beside the school ruler</Beat>. The ruler measures up to <Qty u="15 cm" />. Start at 0: line one end of the pencil up with the 0 mark, then look at the other end. It meets 9, so the pencil's <Term id="length">length</Term> is <Qty u="9 cm" />. We write cm for <Term id="centimetre">centimetre</Term>.

Now <Beat n={2}>count the parts under the pencil</Beat>. Each <Term id="small-part">small part</Term> between two marks next to each other stands for <Qty u="1 cm" />. You cross nine parts from 0 to 9: nine lots of <Qty u="1 cm" /> make <Qty u="9 cm" />. The mark at 0 shows where you begin; you have not crossed a part yet. Count the parts, not the marks.

Next, <Beat n={3}>find the 0 mark beside the eraser</Beat>. On this ruler a short piece sticks out before 0. Line the eraser up with the 0 mark, not with the edge of the ruler and not with 1. Its other end reaches 4, so it measures <Qty u="4 cm" />.

Look at <Beat n={4}>the crayon ending between 6 and 7</Beat>. Its end is past halfway, so it is nearer 7. To the nearest centimetre, it is about <Qty u="7 cm" /> long. Choose the nearer mark, even when that is the bigger number.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>draw a line from 0 to 5</Beat>. Put your pencil on 0, draw along the straight edge and stop at 5. The line covers five parts, so it is <Qty u="5 cm" /> long.

Counting the 0 mark as a part is a mistake: it adds a part you have not crossed. Starting at the edge of the ruler, or at 1, is a mistake too, because then the thing does not begin at 0.

<Lang zh>

两样东西放在一起，你一眼就能看出哪个长一点。可是要说清楚“到底有多长”，就要用尺子：尺子上的每一格一样长，谁来量都一样。

先看<Beat n={1}>学校尺子旁边的铅笔</Beat>。这把尺子能量到 <Qty u="15 cm" />。从 0 量起：铅笔的一端对准 0 这条刻度线，再看另一端，它对着 9，所以铅笔的<Term id="length">长度</Term>是 <Qty u="9 cm" />。cm 是<Term id="centimetre">厘米</Term>的写法。

再来<Beat n={2}>数一数铅笔下面有几格</Beat>。两条相邻刻度线中间的<Term id="small-part">每一小格</Term>表示 <Qty u="1 cm" />。从 0 到 9，铅笔盖住了 9 格，9 个 <Qty u="1 cm" /> 就是 <Qty u="9 cm" />。0 那条线只是起点，还没有走过任何一格。数格子，不数刻度线。

接着<Beat n={3}>在橡皮旁边找 0</Beat>。这把尺子的 0 不在最边上，前面还空出一小段。橡皮要对准 0 这条线，不是对准尺子的边，也不是对准 1。橡皮的另一端到 4，所以它长 <Qty u="4 cm" />。

再看<Beat n={4}>末端落在 6 和 7 之间的蜡笔</Beat>。它的末端已经过了 6 和 7 的中间，离 7 更近。量到最接近的厘米，蜡笔大约长 <Qty u="7 cm" />。取离得近的那一条，就算那是较大的数也一样。

最后，<Beat n={5}>从 0 画到 5</Beat>。笔尖放在 0，沿着尺子的直边画，到 5 停下。这条线盖住 5 格，所以长 <Qty u="5 cm" />。

常见的错误有两个。一是把 0 那条线也数成一格，这样就多算了一格没有走过的长度。二是把东西对准尺子的边或者对准 1，这时东西就不是从 0 开始的，直接读另一端的数就不对了。
