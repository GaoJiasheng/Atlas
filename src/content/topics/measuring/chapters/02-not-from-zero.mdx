<Lang en>

In the last step, you lined a thing up with 0 and read its <Term id="length">length</Term> at the other end. You can still measure when a thing sits further along the ruler, or when the end of the ruler is missing.

Look at <Beat n={1}>Ravi's key from 3 to 8</Beat>. Its left end meets 3 and its right end meets 8. The number 8 only tells you where the key ends on this ruler. To find its length, you need the distance between its two ends.

Now <Beat n={2}>count across from 3 to 8</Beat>. Each <Term id="small-part">small part</Term> stands for <Qty u="1 cm" />. Cross one part to reach 4, another to reach 5, and carry on to 8: you have crossed five parts. The key is <Qty u="5 cm" /> long. You can also take away the start from the end: 8 − 3 = 5. That removes the <Qty u="3 cm" /> of ruler before the key begins.

Next, <Beat n={3}>look at the six marks</Beat>. They are at 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8, and between them there are only five parts. The first mark is where you start, before you cross any part. There is always one more mark than parts. Count the parts, not the marks.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>measure the ribbon on the broken ruler</Beat>. This ruler now begins at 4, and the ribbon stretches from 6 to 13. Count the seven parts it covers, or work out 13 − 6 = 7. Its length is <Qty u="7 cm" />. You do not need the missing 0: the equal parts between the ribbon's ends are all still there.

Reading just the far end is a mistake, because it counts the ruler before the thing starts as well. Counting the marks is another mistake: it gives 6 for the key, although the key covers only five parts.

When you are ready, <Task id="not-from-zero">try one question</Task> on measuring a thing that does not start at 0.

<Lang zh>

上一步，东西都从 0 开始，看另一端对着几，就知道它有多长。可是东西不一定正好放在 0 上，尺子也可能断了一截。这时照样能量。

先看<Beat n={1}>Ravi 的钥匙</Beat>。它的左端在 3，右端在 8。8 只告诉我们钥匙在哪里结束，并不是它的<Term id="length">长度</Term>。要知道钥匙有多长，得看它两端之间隔着多远。

再<Beat n={2}>从 3 数到 8</Beat>。<Term id="small-part">每一小格</Term>表示 <Qty u="1 cm" />。从 3 走一格到 4，再走一格到 5，一直走到 8，一共走过 5 格，所以钥匙长 <Qty u="5 cm" />。也可以用减法：末端减起点，8 − 3 = 5。减去的 3，正好是钥匙前面那 <Qty u="3 cm" /> 空着的尺子。

接着<Beat n={3}>看这六条刻度线</Beat>：3、4、5、6、7、8。线有 6 条，中间的格子却只有 5 格。第一条线是起点，站在那里还一格也没走。刻度线总比格子多 1 条，所以要数格子，不数刻度线。

最后看<Beat n={4}>断尺上的丝带</Beat>。这把尺子断掉了前面一截，从 4 开始。丝带从 6 一直到 13。数一数它盖住几格，一共 7 格；或者算 13 − 6 = 7。丝带长 <Qty u="7 cm" />。少了 0 也没关系，丝带两端之间一样长的格子一格都没少。

这一步最常见的错误有两种。一种是只看末端的数，把钥匙说成 <Qty u="8 cm" />，这样连钥匙前面那段空尺子也算进去了。另一种是数刻度线，把钥匙说成 <Qty u="6 cm" />，可钥匙只盖住了 5 格。

准备好了，就<Task id="not-from-zero">做一道小题</Task>：量一量不从 0 开始的东西。
