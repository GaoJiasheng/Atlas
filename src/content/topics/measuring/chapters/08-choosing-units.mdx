<Lang en>

You have measured <Term id="volume">volume</Term> in <Term id="litre">litres</Term>. Now put together everything you have measured, to choose a unit and make a guess before you read.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the pencil on the ruler</Beat>. It starts at 0 and reaches 14, so its <Term id="length">length</Term> is <Qty u="14 cm" />. Each kind of measuring has its own units. Length is in <Term id="centimetre">centimetres</Term> (cm) and <Term id="metre">metres</Term> (m). <Term id="mass">Mass</Term> is in <Term id="gram">grams</Term> (g) and <Term id="kilogram">kilograms</Term> (kg). Volume is in litres (ℓ).

Next, <Beat n={2}>weigh a paper clip</Beat>. This small scale is marked in g, and the needle points to 1. A paper clip is about <Qty u="1 g" />, so light things are weighed in grams.

Now <Beat n={3}>weigh a bag of rice</Beat>. This scale is marked in kg, and the needle points to 5. The bag is <Qty u="5 kg" />, so heavy things are weighed in kilograms. A bag of rice of <Qty u="5 g" /> would hold only a spoonful.

Before you read, <Beat n={4}>guess the papaya's mass</Beat>. A guess of about how much is an <Term id="estimate">estimate</Term>. A papaya feels about as heavy as the pineapple from step 05, so about <Qty u="1 kg" />: not <Qty u="1 g" />, and not <Qty u="100 kg" />. Then read the scale: the needle is at 1, <Qty u="1 kg" />.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>look at the pail of water</Beat>. A pail for washing the corridor holds about <Qty u="8 l" />, and the level is at 8. If a reading is far from your guess, check the unit and read again.

Choosing the unit from the number alone is a mistake: <Qty u="5 g" /> and <Qty u="5 kg" /> are very different masses. An estimate a hundred times too big is a mistake too; picture the real thing before you choose.

When you are ready, <Task id="choosing-units">try one question</Task> on choosing a unit or a sensible estimate.

<Lang zh>

上一步用升量了液体。这一步把学过的几种量放在一起：先选对单位，读数之前再先估一估。

先看<Beat n={1}>尺子上的铅笔</Beat>。它从 0 开始，到 14 结束，所以<Term id="length">长度</Term>是 <Qty u="14 cm" />。量不同的东西，用不同的单位：长度用<Term id="centimetre">厘米</Term>（cm）和<Term id="metre">米</Term>（m），<Term id="mass">质量</Term>用<Term id="gram">克</Term>（g）和<Term id="kilogram">千克</Term>（kg），<Term id="volume">容量</Term>用<Term id="litre">升</Term>（ℓ）。

再<Beat n={2}>称一个回形针</Beat>。这台小秤上写的是 g，指针指着 1。一个回形针大约 <Qty u="1 g" />，所以轻的东西用克来称。

接着<Beat n={3}>称一袋米</Beat>。这台秤上写的是 kg，指针指着 5。这袋米是 <Qty u="5 kg" />，所以重的东西用千克来称。要是一袋米只有 <Qty u="5 g" />，那就只有一小勺了。

读秤之前，先<Beat n={4}>估一估木瓜有多重</Beat>。这样先猜一个大约的数，叫作<Term id="estimate">估计</Term>。木瓜拿在手里，和第 05 步的黄梨差不多重，大约 <Qty u="1 kg" />；不会是 <Qty u="1 g" />，也不会是 <Qty u="100 kg" />。估完再读秤：指针指着 1，是 <Qty u="1 kg" />。

最后看<Beat n={5}>一桶水</Beat>。洗走廊用的一桶水，大约 <Qty u="8 l" />，水面正好在 8。如果读出来的数和你估的差得太远，就看看单位，再读一次。

这一步常见的错误是只看数、不管单位：<Qty u="5 g" /> 和 <Qty u="5 kg" /> 差得很远。估计差了一百倍也不行。选之前，先在脑子里想一想真的东西有多大、多重。

准备好了，就<Task id="choosing-units">做一道小题</Task>：选一个合适的单位，或者选一个合理的估计。
