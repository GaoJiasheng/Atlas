<Lang en>

You can read a measurement in <Term id="compound-units">compound units</Term>. Now you will write the same amount all in the smaller unit, and then work back to two units.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the two brackets beside Siti's height</Beat> again. The whole <Term id="metre">metre</Term> is <Qty u="100 cm" />, with <Qty u="32 cm" /> above it. Add them: <Qty u="132 cm" />. Her height has not changed; you are counting all of it in <Term id="centimetre">centimetres</Term>. The chart shows both ways: <Qty u="1 m 32 cm" /> on one side and 132 on the other.

Next, <Beat n={2}>look at the rice and the weights</Beat>. The <Term id="balance">balance</Term> is level, so both sides have the same <Term id="mass">mass</Term>. Three <Term id="kilogram">kilogram</Term> weights make <Qty u="3000 g" />, and two small weights add <Qty u="40 g" />, so the rice is <Qty u="3 kg 40 g" /> = <Qty u="3040 g" />. Writing 3 and 40 side by side is a mistake: <Qty u="340 g" /> is less than one kilogram. Change the big units first, then add.

Now <Beat n={3}>join the two brackets along the path</Beat>. Each <Term id="kilometre">kilometre</Term> is <Qty u="1000 m" />, so multiply the whole kilometres by 1000. The first bracket is <Qty u="2000 m" /> and the second adds <Qty u="350 m" />: altogether <Qty u="2350 m" />. Using 100 would give only <Qty u="550 m" />, shorter than the two whole kilometres already walked.

To go back, <Beat n={4}>split the water in the jug into two parts</Beat>. Each whole <Term id="litre">litre</Term> is <Qty u="1000 ml" />. From <Qty u="1250 ml" /> you can take out one whole litre, leaving <Qty u="250 ml" />. Write <Qty u="1 l 250 ml" />, not <Qty u="12 l 50 ml" />: there is not enough water for twelve litres.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>compare the two tape readings</Beat>. A shows <Qty u="205 cm" />: two whole metres and <Qty u="5 cm" /> left, so <Qty u="2 m 5 cm" />. B shows <Qty u="250 cm" />: <Qty u="2 m 50 cm" />. The 0 in 205 holds the tens place and keeps the 5 in the ones place.

<Lang zh>

上一步学会了读复合单位。这一步要把同一个量全部化成小单位来写，再从小单位化回两个单位。

再看一次<Beat n={1}>Siti 身高旁边的两段括号</Beat>。整整 1 <Term id="metre">米</Term>是 <Qty u="100 cm" />，上面再多 <Qty u="32 cm" />，加起来是 <Qty u="132 cm" />。身高没有变，只是全部用<Term id="centimetre">厘米</Term>来数。身高尺两边各写一种：一边是 <Qty u="1 m 32 cm" />，另一边是 132。

接着看<Beat n={2}>米和砝码</Beat>。<Term id="balance">天平</Term>平了，两边的<Term id="mass">质量</Term>一样。3 个 1 <Term id="kilogram">千克</Term>的砝码是 <Qty u="3000 g" />，两个小砝码再加 <Qty u="40 g" />，所以这袋米是 <Qty u="3 kg 40 g" /> = <Qty u="3040 g" />。如果把 3 和 40 并排写成 <Qty u="340 g" />，那连 1 千克都不到。要先把大单位化成小单位，再相加。

再<Beat n={3}>把路边的两段括号接起来</Beat>。1 <Term id="kilometre">千米</Term>是 <Qty u="1000 m" />，千米化米要乘 1000。第一段是 <Qty u="2000 m" />，第二段再加 <Qty u="350 m" />，一共 <Qty u="2350 m" />。如果乘 100，只得到 <Qty u="550 m" />，比已经走过的 2 千米还短，显然不对。

反过来，<Beat n={4}>把量杯里的水分成两段</Beat>。1 <Term id="litre">升</Term>是 <Qty u="1000 ml" />。<Qty u="1250 ml" /> 里能拿出 1 个整升，还剩 <Qty u="250 ml" />，所以写成 <Qty u="1 l 250 ml" />。不能写成 <Qty u="12 l 50 ml" />，这些水根本不够 12 升。化回来的时候，先拿出尽量多的整个大单位，剩下的还是小单位。

最后，<Beat n={5}>比一比两条卷尺上的读数</Beat>。A 是 <Qty u="205 cm" />：两个整米，还剩 <Qty u="5 cm" />，就是 <Qty u="2 m 5 cm" />。B 是 <Qty u="250 cm" />，就是 <Qty u="2 m 50 cm" />。205 中间的 0 占住了十位，让 5 留在个位上。
