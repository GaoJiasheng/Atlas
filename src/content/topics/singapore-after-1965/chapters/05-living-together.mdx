<Lang en>

In 1964 two waves of riots between Malays and Chinese had shaken Singapore: in July <Num s="S443">23</Num> people died and <Num s="S443">454</Num> were injured, and in September <Num s="S443">13</Num> died and <Num s="S443">106</Num> were injured. The People's Action Party said the July riots had been planned by UMNO activists; Malaysia's Deputy Prime Minister, Tun Abdul Razak, said they began on their own. The riots are told in <TopicLink slug="singapore-1819-1965" ch="separation">Singapore, 1819–1965: riots and separation</TopicLink>. The new country had to answer a question: how could its peoples live together?

Two ministers worked on it in different ways. Othman Wok had joined the PAP in 1954 because he believed in its policy for all races; in 1964, after he attended a PAP meeting with Malay organisations, some UMNO activists called him a traitor. As Minister for Social Affairs he saw through a law on Muslim affairs in 1966, under which a Muslim council was set up in 1968. S. Rajaratnam, the foreign minister, wrote much of the Pledge, with its promise to live together “regardless of race, language or religion”.

Schools were one place to learn together. Singapore has four official languages: Malay, Chinese, Tamil and English. Under the <Term id="bilingual-policy">bilingual policy</Term>, pupils learned English and a mother tongue. A report of 1978 found that over <Num s="S437">60</Num> per cent of pupils sitting the main examinations in 1975–1977 had failed one or both languages, and that <Num s="S437">85</Num> per cent of Chinese pupils spoke dialects at home. In 1979 the <Term id="speak-mandarin-campaign">Speak Mandarin Campaign</Term> began, and by 1987 English was the first language in almost all schools.

Living together is also practised. Since 1997 schools have marked <Term id="racial-harmony-day">Racial Harmony Day</Term> on 21 July, the day the riots of 1964 began.

<More title={{ en: "S. Rajaratnam and Othman Wok", zh: "拉惹勒南与奥斯曼渥" }}>

**S. Rajaratnam** (1915–2006) was Singapore's first foreign minister, until 1980. He signed the ASEAN Declaration for Singapore in 1967, and he largely drafted the Pledge in 1966.

**Othman Wok** (1924–2017) joined the PAP in 1954 because he believed in its multiracial policy. On 19 July 1964 he attended the PAP's meeting with Malay organisations at the Victoria Theatre, and UMNO activists then denounced him as a traitor. He was Minister for Social Affairs from 1963 to 1977. Under the Administration of Muslim Law Act of 1966, the Islamic Religious Council of Singapore (Majlis Ugama Islam Singapura, MUIS) was set up in 1968.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Dialects: what was gained and what was lost", zh: "方言：得到的与失去的" }}>

In 2009 Lee Kuan Yew, who had launched the Speak Mandarin Campaign, explained why: left alone, he said, Chinese Singaporeans “would be speaking an adulterated Hokkien-Teochew dialect”. He also said why English, not Mandarin, was the common language: “Singapore's multi-racial peoples will never be united if we had used Mandarin as our common language.” He described a cost: “we closed down all dialect programs on radio and TV from 1979”, and added, “I understand the strong emotional ties to one's mother tongue.” Homes that mainly spoke a dialect fell from <Num s="S438">76</Num> per cent in 1980 to <Num s="S438">48</Num> per cent in 1990; those that mainly spoke Mandarin rose from <Num s="S438">13</Num> to <Num s="S438">30</Num> per cent. This topic gives the policy in the words of the man who led it, with the numbers, and does not sum up how families felt.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "1969: a short, small outbreak", zh: "1969 年：短暂的冲突" }}>

On 13 May 1969 riots broke out in Kuala Lumpur. From the evening of 31 May there were attacks in Singapore too. The Straits Times of 1–8 June 1969 reported <Num s="S445">17</Num> people injured on the first night, <Num s="S445">two</Num> deaths and <Num s="S445">13</Num> people in hospital in one police statement, five youths charged on 3 June, and calm again by 8 June. The totals usually given, <Num s="S444">four</Num> dead and about <Num s="S444">80</Num> injured over seven days, come from a Singapore government web page; no other source for them was found.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why 21 July?", zh: "为什么是 7 月 21 日？" }}>

Racial Harmony Day falls on 21 July because the first riots of 1964 began on that day, during a procession for the Prophet Muhammad's birthday. It is one of four days that schools mark under National Education, launched on 17 May 1997 (chapter 06). The National Library Board and the Straits Times of 22 July 1997 date the first Racial Harmony Day to 1997. A Ministry of Education press release of 2012 speaks of “its inception in 1998”. The National Library Board adds that community development councils joined in from 1998. Both years are given here.

</More>

1964 年，马来人与华人之间的两波骚乱震动新加坡：7 月，<Num s="S443">23</Num> 人死亡，<Num s="S443">454</Num> 人受伤；9 月，<Num s="S443">13</Num> 人死亡，<Num s="S443">106</Num> 人受伤。人民行动党说，7 月的骚乱是巫统活跃分子策划的；马来西亚副首相敦阿都拉萨则说是自发的。骚乱的经过见<TopicLink slug="singapore-1819-1965" ch="separation">《新加坡 1819–1965》的“骚乱与分家”</TopicLink>。新国家必须回答一个问题：不同族群怎样一起生活？

两位部长用不同的方式回答。奥斯曼渥 1954 年加入行动党，是因为认同党的多元种族政策；1964 年，他出席行动党与马来团体的会议后，一些巫统活跃分子骂他是“叛徒”。他任社会事务部长期间，推动国会在 1966 年通过管理伊斯兰教事务的法令，1968 年依这项法令成立伊斯兰教理事会。外交部长拉惹勒南写下信约的大部分文字，誓愿“不分种族、言语、宗教”，团结一致。

学校是一起学习的地方。新加坡有四种官方语文：马来文、华文、泰米尔文和英文。按照<Term id="bilingual-policy">双语教育政策</Term>，学生学英文，也学母语。1978 年的一份报告发现，1975 至 1977 年参加主要考试的学生，<Num s="S437">60</Num> % 以上一科或两科语文不及格；<Num s="S437">85</Num> % 的华族学生在家讲方言。1979 年开始<Term id="speak-mandarin-campaign">讲华语运动</Term>；到 1987 年，几乎所有学校都以英文为第一语文。

和谐共处也要在生活里练习。1997 年起，学校每年 7 月 21 日举行<Term id="racial-harmony-day">种族和谐日</Term>活动，那一天是 1964 年骚乱开始的日子。

<More title={{ en: "S. Rajaratnam and Othman Wok", zh: "拉惹勒南与奥斯曼渥" }}>

**拉惹勒南**（1915–2006）是新加坡第一任外交部长，任职到 1980 年。1967 年，他代表新加坡签署《东盟宣言》；1966 年，信约主要由他起草。

**奥斯曼渥**（1924–2017）1954 年因为认同行动党的多元种族政策而入党。1964 年 7 月 19 日，他出席行动党在维多利亚剧院与马来团体举行的会议，事后被巫统活跃分子斥为“叛徒”。1963 年至 1977 年，他担任社会事务部长。依 1966 年的《回教法执行法令》，新加坡伊斯兰教理事会（MUIS）在 1968 年成立。

2009 年，发起讲华语运动的李光耀解释过原因。他说，如果放任不管，新加坡华人“会讲一种混杂的福建—潮州话”。他也说明为什么共同语是英文而不是华语：“如果以华语作为共同语，新加坡的多元种族人民永远不会团结。”他讲到代价：“我们从 1979 年起停播电台和电视上所有的方言节目。”又说：“我理解人们对母语的深厚感情。”主要讲方言的家庭，从 1980 年的 <Num s="S438">76</Num> % 降到 1990 年的 <Num s="S438">48</Num> %；主要讲华语的家庭，从 <Num s="S438">13</Num> % 增加到 <Num s="S438">30</Num> %。本主题用推动这项政策的人自己的话，加上数字来写，不替各个家庭总结感受。

种族和谐日定在 7 月 21 日，因为 1964 年的第一波骚乱就是在这一天、在庆祝先知穆罕默德诞辰的游行中开始的。它是学校在国民教育下纪念的四个日子之一（见第 06 章）；国民教育在 1997 年 5 月 17 日推出。新加坡国家图书馆管理局和 1997 年 7 月 22 日的《海峡时报》，都把第一个种族和谐日定在 1997 年。教育部 2012 年的新闻稿则说种族和谐日“1998 年开始”。国家图书馆管理局另外提到，社区发展理事会是 1998 年起参与的。这里两个年份都列出。
