<Lang en>

From 1963 to 1966 Indonesia waged its Confrontation against the new Malaysia. In Singapore a series of bombs killed <Num s="S449">seven</Num> people and injured <Num s="S449">50</Num>; the worst, at MacDonald House in March 1965, is told in <TopicLink slug="singapore-1819-1965" ch="separation">Singapore, 1819–1965: riots and separation</TopicLink>. In August 1966 Malaysia and Indonesia made peace in Jakarta, and on 7 September 1967 Singapore and Indonesia established diplomatic relations.

On 8 August 1967, in Bangkok, five leaders from Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand signed a declaration that founded the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, ASEAN. S. Rajaratnam signed for Singapore. Indonesia and Malaysia, in conflict until the year before, were both among the founders. How ASEAN grew is a story of the whole region, and only its beginning is told here.

On 22 January 1984 the government introduced <Term id="total-defence">Total Defence</Term>. The Ministry of Defence calls it “the national defence concept”. It has <Num s="S454">five</Num> pillars: military, civil, economic, social and psychological defence. In 2019 a sixth, digital defence, was added: good habits online and care with false information.

Total Defence Day is 15 February, the day in 1942 when the British surrendered Singapore to Japan; that defeat and the occupation that followed are told in <TopicLink slug="ww2" ch="fall-of-singapore">World War II: Malaya and Singapore</TopicLink>. Sources differ on when the day was first marked.

<More title={{ en: "Which day did Confrontation end?", zh: "对抗在哪一天结束？" }}>

The National Library Board says that Tun Abdul Razak of Malaysia and Adam Malik of Indonesia ratified the peace agreement in Jakarta on 12 August 1966. A United States intelligence note written on 12 August says that the “Bangkok Accord”, named after earlier talks in Bangkok, was signed in Jakarta on 11 August. So both dates are given, and both are in Jakarta: one for the signing, one for the ratification.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "When did Total Defence Day begin?", zh: "全面防卫日从哪一年开始？" }}>

The Home Team NS magazine and the Promote Mandarin Council's database say the first Total Defence Day was held on 15 February 1984. On 17 May 1997, the day <Term id="national-education">National Education</Term> was launched in schools, The New Paper reported that Total Defence Day would be added to the days that schools commemorate; and on 14 February 1998 the Straits Times reported pupils at a primary school eating sweet potato and drinking water “to mark Total Defence Day”. Both can be true: a day first held in 1984 may have become a school commemoration in 1997–98. The sources do not say so, and this topic does not decide.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "ASEAN's later members", zh: "东盟后来加入的国家" }}>

Brunei joined ASEAN in 1984, Vietnam in 1995, Laos and Myanmar in 1997, and Cambodia in 1999, making ten members. ASEAN and Southeast Asia are a separate part of the primary Social Studies syllabus.

</More>

<Lang zh>

1963 年至 1966 年，印度尼西亚对新成立的马来西亚展开对抗。新加坡发生一连串炸弹袭击，<Num s="S449">7</Num> 人死亡，<Num s="S449">50</Num> 人受伤；其中最严重的 1965 年 3 月麦唐纳大厦爆炸案，见<TopicLink slug="singapore-1819-1965" ch="separation">《新加坡 1819–1965》的“骚乱与分家”</TopicLink>。1966 年 8 月，马来西亚和印度尼西亚在雅加达讲和；1967 年 9 月 7 日，新加坡与印度尼西亚建立外交关系。

1967 年 8 月 8 日，印度尼西亚、马来西亚、菲律宾、新加坡和泰国的五位代表在曼谷签署宣言，成立东南亚国家联盟，简称东盟。拉惹勒南代表新加坡签署。前一年还处于对抗中的印度尼西亚和马来西亚，都是创始国。东盟怎样发展，是整个地区的故事，这里只讲它的开头。

1984 年 1 月 22 日，政府提出<Term id="total-defence">全面防卫</Term>。国防部称之为“国家防卫概念”。它有<Num s="S454">五</Num>大支柱，即军事、民事、经济、社会和心理防卫。2019 年加上第六个支柱数码防卫：养成良好的上网习惯，提防虚假信息。

全面防卫日是 2 月 15 日，也就是 1942 年英军把新加坡交给日本的那一天；那场失败和随后的占领，见<TopicLink slug="ww2" ch="fall-of-singapore">《第二次世界大战》的“马来亚与新加坡”</TopicLink>。这个纪念日从哪一年开始，来源说法不一。

<More title={{ en: "Which day did Confrontation end?", zh: "对抗在哪一天结束？" }}>

新加坡国家图书馆管理局说，马来西亚的敦阿都拉萨和印度尼西亚的亚当·马立克，1966 年 8 月 12 日在雅加达批准和平协议。美国一份 8 月 12 日写成的情报备忘录说，这份因先前在曼谷谈判而得名的《曼谷协议》，8 月 11 日在雅加达签署。所以两个日期都列出，地点都是雅加达：一个是签署，一个是批准。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "When did Total Defence Day begin?", zh: "全面防卫日从哪一年开始？" }}>

内政团队国民服役人员协会的杂志和推广华语理事会的资料库都说，第一个全面防卫日在 1984 年 2 月 15 日举行。1997 年 5 月 17 日，学校推出<Term id="national-education">国民教育</Term>的那一天，《新报》报道全面防卫日将列入学校的纪念日；1998 年 2 月 14 日的《海峡时报》报道，一所小学的学生吃番薯、喝白开水，“纪念全面防卫日”。两种说法可以同时成立：1984 年开始的纪念日，可能在 1997 至 1998 年成为学校的纪念日。不过来源没有这样说，本主题也不下结论。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "ASEAN's later members", zh: "东盟后来加入的国家" }}>

文莱 1984 年加入东盟，越南 1995 年，老挝和缅甸 1997 年，柬埔寨 1999 年，成员国共十个。东盟和东南亚是小学社会研究课纲中另外一部分的内容。

</More>
