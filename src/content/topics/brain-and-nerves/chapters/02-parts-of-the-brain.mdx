<Lang en>

The largest part of the brain is the <Term id="cerebrum">cerebrum</Term>. Its surface is folded into ridges and grooves; spread flat, the outer layer of both halves would cover about <Num s="S28">2,500 square centimetres</Num>, the area of a square 50 centimetres on each side. The colours on the stage mark its four <Term id="lobe">lobes</Term> on each side, and the cerebellum and brainstem below.

The cerebrum has a left and a right half, joined by the <Term id="corpus-callosum">corpus callosum</Term>, a thick band of nerve fibres along which information passes all the time <Num s="S10"></Num>. Each half mainly works with the opposite side of the body: most of the fibres that carry movement commands cross to the other side on their way down to the spinal cord <Num s="S12"></Num>, so the left half moves the right hand.

The frontal lobe takes part in planning, reasoning and solving problems, and its back strip starts voluntary movements <Num s="S8, S11"></Num>. The parietal lobe takes in touch, warmth and pain <Num s="S8"></Num>. The temporal lobe helps with hearing and with forming memories, and the occipital lobe at the back handles what the eyes send <Num s="S8"></Num>. No lobe does only one job, and most tasks use several areas together.

Behind and below the cerebrum is the <Term id="cerebellum">cerebellum</Term>, which coordinates the muscles so movements are smooth and helps keep balance <Num s="S9, S13"></Num>. In front of it, the <Term id="brainstem">brainstem</Term> joins the brain to the spinal cord and keeps vital jobs such as breathing and the heartbeat going <Num s="S9"></Num>. <FlyTo preset="brain">Look at the whole brain</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Most of the neurons are in the small part", zh: "大部分神经元在小的那一块" }}>

An adult brain has about <Num s="S15">86 billion</Num> neurons. Only about <Num s="S15">19 %</Num> of them are in the cerebral cortex. The cerebellum is only about a tenth of the brain's mass, but it holds about <Num s="S15">69 billion</Num>, about four in five.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Hungry for energy", zh: "很耗能量" }}>

In an average adult the brain is about <Num s="S16">2 %</Num> of the body's weight, yet it uses about <Num s="S16">20 %</Num> of the oxygen, and so of the energy, the body takes in.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Deeper parts not shown", zh: "深处没画的部分" }}>

Deep inside the brain there are many more structures, and fibres that join the lobes to each other. They are not drawn here. The corpus callosum is drawn as a simple arch.

</More>

<Lang zh>

脑最大的部分是<Term id="cerebrum">大脑</Term>。它的表面折成一条条隆起和沟；把两半的外层展平，约有 <Num s="S28">2,500 平方厘米</Num>，相当于边长 50 厘米的正方形。台上的颜色标出它每一侧的四个<Term id="lobe">叶</Term>，以及下方的小脑和脑干。

大脑分左右两半，由<Term id="corpus-callosum">胼胝体</Term>连起来；它是一大束神经纤维，信息一直沿它在两边之间传递 <Num s="S10"></Num>。每一半主要和身体的另一侧打交道：传动作命令的纤维大多在下到脊髓的路上交叉到对侧 <Num s="S12"></Num>，所以是左半边让右手动。

额叶参与计划、推理和解决问题，它最后面的一条区域发起随意动作 <Num s="S8, S11"></Num>。顶叶接收触觉、冷热和痛觉 <Num s="S8"></Num>。颞叶参与听和形成记忆，最后面的枕叶处理眼睛送来的信号 <Num s="S8"></Num>。没有哪一叶只做一件事，大多数事要好几个区一起做。

大脑后下方是<Term id="cerebellum">小脑</Term>，它协调肌肉，让动作平稳，也帮助保持平衡 <Num s="S9, S13"></Num>。小脑前面的<Term id="brainstem">脑干</Term>把脑和脊髓连起来，让呼吸、心跳这些维持生命的事一直进行 <Num s="S9"></Num>。<FlyTo preset="brain">看整个脑</FlyTo>
