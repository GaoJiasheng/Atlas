<Lang en>

The <Term id="central-nervous-system">central nervous system</Term> is the brain and the spinal cord <Num s="S7"></Num>. Everything outside them, the nerves and small knots of nerve cells, belongs to the peripheral nervous system <Num s="S4"></Num>. On the stage the brain sits inside the skull, and the <Term id="spinal-cord">spinal cord</Term> carries on below it, down a canal inside the backbone.

The spinal cord is nerve tissue, not bone. It runs from the base of the skull to about the level of the <Num s="S5">first lumbar vertebra</Num>, in the lower back, so it is shorter than the backbone <Num s="S27"></Num>. Below its end, nerve roots carry on down the canal in a bundle that looks like a horse's tail. <FlyTo preset="spine">Look along the backbone</FlyTo>

From the brain and spinal cord, nerves branch out to every part of the body. A <Term id="nerve">nerve</Term> is a bundle of nerve fibres wrapped in connective tissue <Num s="S4"></Num>: like a cable with many wires inside, it carries many separate signals at once. Some fibres bring messages in from the skin and other parts; others take commands out to the muscles. There are <Num s="S4">12 pairs</Num> of cranial nerves, which leave the brain itself, and <Num s="S4">31 pairs</Num> of spinal nerves, which leave the spinal cord.

The nervous system works with the other body systems. The brainstem, at the base of the brain, holds centres that help control breathing and the heart <Num s="S12"></Num>, which is how your breathing and heartbeat speed up when you run, as in <TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs" ch="three-systems">Three systems working together</TopicLink>.

<More title={{ en: "Twelve and thirty-one", zh: "12 和 31" }}>

The 31 pairs of spinal nerves are named after the part of the backbone they leave by: <Num s="S4">8</Num> in the neck, <Num s="S4">12</Num> in the chest, <Num s="S4">5</Num> in the lower back, <Num s="S4">5</Num> from the sacrum and <Num s="S4">1</Num> from the tailbone. The 12 pairs of cranial nerves serve mostly the head and neck; the optic nerves for seeing and the nerves for hearing are among them.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Shorter than the backbone", zh: "比脊柱短" }}>

Because the cord stops in the lower back, the lower spinal nerves have to run down inside the canal before they leave the backbone; that bundle is called the cauda equina, Latin for "horse's tail" <Num s="S5"></Num>. Sources differ a little on where the cord ends: one gives the last thoracic vertebra <Num s="S27"></Num>, another the first lumbar <Num s="S5"></Num>. In this model the drawn cord ends at the first lumbar. The end of the cord does not slowly rise through childhood: scans of children show it reaches the level it keeps as an adult in the first few months of life <Num s="S44"></Num>.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

<Term id="central-nervous-system">中枢神经系统</Term>就是脑和脊髓 <Num s="S7"></Num>。它们以外的一切，神经和一小团一小团的神经细胞，属于周围神经系统 <Num s="S4"></Num>。在台上，脑在颅骨里，<Term id="spinal-cord">脊髓</Term>从脑往下，在脊柱里的一条管道中走。

脊髓是神经组织，不是骨头。它从颅底一直到约<Num s="S5">第一腰椎</Num>的高度，也就是腰部，所以比脊柱短 <Num s="S27"></Num>。脊髓末端以下，神经根在管道里继续往下，聚成一束，看上去像马的尾巴。<FlyTo preset="spine">沿着脊柱看</FlyTo>

神经从脑和脊髓分出去，通到身体各处。一条<Term id="nerve">神经</Term>是一束用结缔组织包着的神经纤维 <Num s="S4"></Num>：像一根电缆里装着很多电线，它能同时传很多不同的信号。有的纤维把皮肤等处的消息送进来，有的把命令送到肌肉。脑神经有 <Num s="S4">12 对</Num>，直接从脑出来；脊神经有 <Num s="S4">31 对</Num>，从脊髓出来。

神经系统和其他系统一起工作。脑底部的脑干里有帮助控制呼吸和心脏的中枢 <Num s="S12"></Num>；跑步时呼吸和心跳会变快，就和它有关，见<TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs" ch="three-systems">三个系统一起工作</TopicLink>。

<More title={{ en: "Twelve and thirty-one", zh: "12 和 31" }}>

31 对脊神经按它们从脊柱哪一段出来命名：颈部 <Num s="S4">8</Num> 对、胸部 <Num s="S4">12</Num> 对、腰部 <Num s="S4">5</Num> 对、骶骨 <Num s="S4">5</Num> 对、尾骨 <Num s="S4">1</Num> 对。12 对脑神经主要分布在头和颈部；管看的视神经和管听的神经都在其中。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Shorter than the backbone", zh: "比脊柱短" }}>

因为脊髓在腰部就结束了，下面的脊神经要先在管道里往下走一段才离开脊柱；这一束叫马尾，拉丁文是“马的尾巴” <Num s="S5"></Num>。脊髓在哪里结束，来源说法有一点不同：一个说到最后一节胸椎 <Num s="S27"></Num>，另一个说到第一腰椎 <Num s="S5"></Num>。这个模型里画的脊髓到第一腰椎为止。脊髓的末端不是在整个童年里慢慢往上移：对孩子做的扫描显示，出生后头几个月里它就到了成年后一直保持的高度 <Num s="S44"></Num>。

</More>
