<Lang en>

A nerve in your arm holds many long fibres, each part of one nerve cell. A nerve cell, or <Term id="neuron">neuron</Term>, is a kind of animal cell, with a cell membrane, cytoplasm and a nucleus, as in <TopicLink slug="cells" ch="animal-cell-parts">The parts of an animal cell</TopicLink>. On the bench is one neuron drawn about <Num s="S1">4,000 times</Num> life size. Its short branches, the <Term id="dendrite">dendrites</Term>, take in signals from other cells; the cell body holds the nucleus; and one long <Term id="axon">axon</Term> carries the signal away <Num s="S6, S23"></Num>. Some axons reach up to about <Num s="S24">1 metre</Num>, from the base of the spine to the big toe; the one on the bench is drawn short. <FlyTo preset="bench">Look at the bench</FlyTo>

The signal is not electricity flowing down a wire. It is a brief electrical change at the cell's outer layer: tiny openings let charged particles move in and out, and the change passes along the membrane bit by bit <Num s="S23"></Num>. Many axons are wrapped in <Term id="myelin">myelin</Term>, in segments with bare gaps between them; the signal seems to jump from gap to gap, which is faster, and thicker axons are faster too <Num s="S21"></Num>.

How fast? Fibres with myelin carry signals at up to about <Num s="S20">150 metres per second</Num>; without myelin they rarely reach <Num s="S20">10 metres per second</Num>. Use the card to compare two model fibres over one metre: <Num s="S1">10 milliseconds</Num> for the thick, wrapped one and <Num s="S1">1,000 milliseconds</Num> for the thin, bare one.

At its end the axon meets the next cell at a <Term id="synapse">synapse</Term>. At most synapses there is a narrow gap. The signal makes the ending release a chemical, which crosses the gap and binds to the next cell, starting a new signal there; the crossing takes about <Num s="S22">0.5 to 1 millisecond</Num>.

<More title={{ en: "Not like electricity in a wire", zh: "不像电线里的电" }}>

A nerve is sometimes compared to a cable of wires, and that is a fair picture of its shape: a bundle of separate fibres. The signal itself is different from the electric current in a wire. In a nerve fibre the change happens at the membrane and is made again at each point as it passes <Num s="S23"></Num>, and even the fastest fibres carry it at only about <Num s="S20">150 metres per second</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "How fast, really", zh: "到底有多快" }}>

The fastest nerve fibres, thick and wrapped in myelin, reach about <Num s="S20">150 metres per second</Num>; thin fibres without myelin are slower than about <Num s="S20">10 metres per second</Num>. The model's two fibres, <Num s="S1">100</Num> and <Num s="S1">1</Num> metre per second, sit inside these ranges. The thick fibre is slowed <Num s="S1">200 times</Num> on the screen, the thin one <Num s="S1">2 times</Num>, so both take two seconds to cross.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The gap", zh: "那道窄缝" }}>

The chemical synapse is the most common kind, with a gap the chemical must cross <Num s="S22"></Num>. The gap is only about <Num s="S23">20 to 40 nanometres</Num> wide, far too narrow to see; on the bench it is drawn much wider.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

手臂里的一条神经装着很多长纤维，每条都是一个神经细胞的一部分。神经细胞，也叫<Term id="neuron">神经元</Term>，是一种动物细胞，有细胞膜、细胞质和细胞核，见<TopicLink slug="cells" ch="animal-cell-parts">动物细胞的结构</TopicLink>。工作台上是一个放大约 <Num s="S1">4,000 倍</Num>的神经元。它短的分支叫<Term id="dendrite">树突</Term>，接收其他细胞的信号；细胞体里有细胞核；一条长的<Term id="axon">轴突</Term>把信号送出去 <Num s="S6, S23"></Num>。有的轴突长达约 <Num s="S24">1 米</Num>，从脊柱下端一直到大脚趾；台上这一条画短了。<FlyTo preset="bench">看工作台</FlyTo>

信号不是沿电线流动的电。它是细胞外层膜上一个短暂的电变化：膜上的小开口让带电的微粒进进出出，这个变化沿着膜一段一段往前传 <Num s="S23"></Num>。很多轴突外面包着一段段的<Term id="myelin">髓鞘</Term>，段与段之间有裸露的缝隙；信号像是从一个缝隙跳到下一个，所以更快，轴突越粗也越快 <Num s="S21"></Num>。

有多快？有髓鞘的纤维最快约每秒 <Num s="S20">150 米</Num>；没有髓鞘的很少达到每秒 <Num s="S20">10 米</Num>。用卡片比较模型里的两条一米长的纤维：粗的、有髓鞘的要 <Num s="S1">10 毫秒</Num>，细的、没有髓鞘的要 <Num s="S1">1,000 毫秒</Num>。

轴突末端和下一个细胞在<Term id="synapse">突触</Term>相接。大多数突触有一道窄缝。信号让末梢放出一种化学物质，它跨过缝，和下一个细胞结合，在那里产生新的信号；跨过去约要 <Num s="S22">0.5 到 1 毫秒</Num>。

<More title={{ en: "Not like electricity in a wire", zh: "不像电线里的电" }}>

人们有时把神经比作一根装着很多电线的电缆。用来形容它的形状没有错：它是一束各自分开的纤维。但信号本身和电线里的电流不同。在神经纤维里，变化发生在膜上，每经过一处都要重新产生一次 <Num s="S23"></Num>；就连最快的纤维，也只有约每秒 <Num s="S20">150 米</Num>。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "How fast, really", zh: "到底有多快" }}>

最快的神经纤维又粗又有髓鞘，约每秒 <Num s="S20">150 米</Num>；没有髓鞘的细纤维比每秒约 <Num s="S20">10 米</Num>还慢。模型里两条纤维每秒 <Num s="S1">100</Num> 米和 <Num s="S1">1</Num> 米，都在这个范围里。画面上粗纤维放慢了 <Num s="S1">200 倍</Num>，细纤维放慢了 <Num s="S1">2 倍</Num>，所以两条都用两秒走完。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The gap", zh: "那道窄缝" }}>

化学突触是最常见的一种，有一道要靠化学物质跨过去的缝 <Num s="S22"></Num>。这道缝只有约 <Num s="S23">20 到 40 纳米</Num>宽，窄得看不见；工作台上把它画宽了很多。

</More>
