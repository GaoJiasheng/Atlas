<Lang en>

Sleep is not the brain switching off. A network of cells running through the brainstem, joining the spinal cord to the brain, strongly shapes how active the outer layer of the brain is through the cycle of sleeping and waking <Num s="S38"></Num>. The signals on the stage keep moving slowly while the body rests.

Sleep helps with learning and with forming long-term memories, and too little sleep can make it hard to focus on tasks and to think clearly <Num s="S33"></Num>. Getting enough sleep regularly goes with better attention, learning and behaviour in children <Num s="S31"></Num>.

How much is enough? Singapore's guidelines for children say that 7- to 13-year-olds should have regular sleep of at least <Num s="S31">9 hours</Num>. A US health institute gives <Num s="S32">9 to 12 hours</Num> a day for children aged 6 to 12. The PE syllabus asks you to know why sleep matters and to use good sleep habits for healthy growth <Num s="S3"></Num>. <FlyTo preset="brain">Look at the brain again</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Two guidelines", zh: "两份指引" }}>

The Singapore guideline gives a lowest amount, at least <Num s="S31">9 hours</Num> for ages 7 to 13 and at least <Num s="S31">8 hours</Num> for ages 14 to 17. The US guidance gives a range, <Num s="S32">9 to 12 hours</Num> for ages 6 to 12. Both say the same thing for a primary-school pupil: at least nine hours.

</More>

<Lang zh>

睡觉不是脑关机。脑干里有一张由细胞组成的网络，连着脊髓和脑；在睡和醒的循环中，它大大影响脑外层的活跃程度 <Num s="S38"></Num>。身体休息时，台上的信号仍在慢慢地走。

睡眠帮助学习、形成长期记忆；睡得太少，会难以专心做事、想不清楚 <Num s="S33"></Num>。孩子规律地睡够，与更好的注意力、学习和行为相关 <Num s="S31"></Num>。

睡多少才够？新加坡的儿童指引说，7 到 13 岁的孩子要规律地睡至少 <Num s="S31">9 小时</Num>。美国的一个卫生研究机构给 6 到 12 岁的孩子的建议是每天 <Num s="S32">9 到 12 小时</Num>。体育课纲要求你知道睡眠为什么重要，并用好的睡眠习惯帮助健康成长 <Num s="S3"></Num>。<FlyTo preset="brain">再看一看脑</FlyTo>
