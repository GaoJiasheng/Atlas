<Lang en>

The brain is soft, and it is well wrapped. Outside it is the skull, a hard case made of bones that fit together; how they join is in <TopicLink slug="bones-and-joints" ch="connected-skeleton">The connected skeleton</TopicLink>. Between the bone and the brain are three coverings, the <Term id="meninges">meninges</Term>: the tough dura mater outside, the arachnoid, which looks like a cobweb, and the thin pia mater, tightly bound to the surface of the brain <Num s="S5"></Num>.

The space under the arachnoid is filled with <Term id="cerebrospinal-fluid">cerebrospinal fluid</Term> <Num s="S5"></Num>, a clear liquid that acts as a buffer between the brain and the skull <Num s="S7"></Num>. It is made mainly in the spaces inside the brain, by the choroid plexus, at about <Num s="S28, S29">500 millilitres a day</Num>, yet there is only about <Num s="S28">125 to 150 millilitres</Num> of it at any time, so it is replaced several times a day. <FlyTo preset="bench">See the layers on the bench</FlyTo>

Lower down, the bones of the backbone stack up so that the holes through them make a canal, and the spinal cord runs inside it with its own tough covering <Num s="S5"></Num>.

A helmet adds another layer outside the head. Its foam lining crushes in a fall or crash and absorbs much of the energy of the knock, so it does not all reach the head <Num s="S36"></Num>. In studies of injured cyclists, wearing a helmet went with far fewer serious head injuries <Num s="S35"></Num>. In Singapore, cyclists must wear a helmet on the road <Num s="S34"></Num>. Wear one, with the strap fastened, whenever you ride a bicycle or a scooter or skate. If you hit your head, tell an adult straight away.

<More title={{ en: "What the numbers say", zh: "数字怎么说" }}>

A review of <Num s="S35">40</Num> studies of more than <Num s="S35">64,000</Num> injured cyclists found that wearing a helmet went with lower odds of head injury (odds ratio <Num s="S35">0.49</Num>), serious head injury (<Num s="S35">0.31</Num>) and fatal head injury (<Num s="S35">0.35</Num>). These are links found in injury records, not an experiment. No helmet design has been shown to prevent concussion <Num s="S36"></Num>.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The rules", zh: "规定" }}>

On the road, cyclists must wear a helmet <Num s="S34"></Num>. Children under <Num s="S34">16</Num> must not ride an e-scooter on cycling paths unless an adult aged at least <Num s="S34">21</Num> is supervising, and must not ride a power-assisted bicycle on cycling paths or roads even with an adult <Num s="S34"></Num>.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the fluid matters", zh: "液体的作用" }}>

The brain floats in this fluid, which limits knocks against the skull <Num s="S7"></Num>. It is made fresh all the time and drains away, so the fluid round the brain is replaced several times a day <Num s="S28, S29"></Num>.

</More>

<Lang zh>

脑很软，但包得很好。最外面是颅骨，由几块拼在一起的骨头围成一个硬壳；骨头怎样连接，见<TopicLink slug="bones-and-joints" ch="connected-skeleton">连成一体的骨骼</TopicLink>。骨头和脑之间有三层被膜，叫<Term id="meninges">脑膜</Term>：外层是坚韧的硬膜，中间是像蛛网一样的蛛网膜，最里面是紧贴在脑表面的薄薄的软膜 <Num s="S5"></Num>。

蛛网膜下面的空间充满<Term id="cerebrospinal-fluid">脑脊液</Term> <Num s="S5"></Num>，它是一种透明的液体，在脑和颅骨之间当缓冲 <Num s="S7"></Num>。它主要在脑里的空腔中由脉络丛产生，每天约 <Num s="S28, S29">500 毫升</Num>，但任何时候都只有约 <Num s="S28">125 到 150 毫升</Num>，所以一天要更换好几次。<FlyTo preset="bench">看工作台上的几层</FlyTo>

往下，脊柱的骨头一节节叠起来，中间的孔连成一条管道，脊髓就在里面，外面还有自己的一层坚韧的膜 <Num s="S5"></Num>。

头盔在头外再加一层。摔倒或碰撞时，它的泡沫内衬被压扁，吸收掉撞击的很多能量，不让它全部落到头上 <Num s="S36"></Num>。对受伤骑车人的研究发现，戴头盔的人头部重伤少得多 <Num s="S35"></Num>。在新加坡，在马路上骑脚踏车必须戴头盔 <Num s="S34"></Num>。骑脚踏车、滑板车或溜冰时，都要戴好头盔、扣好带子。如果撞到了头，要马上告诉大人。

<More title={{ en: "What the numbers say", zh: "数字怎么说" }}>

一项综述汇总了 <Num s="S35">40</Num> 项研究、<Num s="S35">64,000</Num> 多名受伤的骑车人，发现戴头盔与较低的头部受伤几率（比值比 <Num s="S35">0.49</Num>）、重度头伤（<Num s="S35">0.31</Num>）和致命头伤（<Num s="S35">0.35</Num>）相关。这些是受伤记录里找到的关联，不是实验。还没有哪种头盔被证明能防止脑震荡 <Num s="S36"></Num>。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The rules", zh: "规定" }}>

在马路上骑脚踏车必须戴头盔 <Num s="S34"></Num>。未满 <Num s="S34">16</Num> 岁的人不得在脚踏车道上骑电动滑板车，除非有至少 <Num s="S34">21</Num> 岁的成人监督；也不得在脚踏车道和马路上骑电动助力脚踏车，即使有成人陪同 <Num s="S34"></Num>。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the fluid matters", zh: "液体的作用" }}>

脑浮在这种液体里，它减少脑撞到颅骨 <Num s="S7"></Num>。它不停地产生、又不停地流走，所以脑周围的液体一天要更换好几次 <Num s="S28, S29"></Num>。

</More>
