## Claims not used

- "NEWater can meet up to 40 % of demand" and "desalination up to 25–30 %": not stated on any PUB page that could be read; left out (docs/24 F-12, F-14).
- The year Linggiu Reservoir was completed (secondary sources give 1993–1995): not on PUB or MFA pages; left out.
- A heavy-rain class for 30 mm/h: MSS publishes none that could be found; the simulation calls it "a heavy shower" (S1).
- Sea-surface temperature around Singapore in °C: MSS gives only anomalies; the simulated reading uses air temperature (S23) instead.
- Dual-flush 3 L / 4.5 L (SG60 panels, S9): older than PUB's current labelling requirement (2-tick cisterns 4.0 L / 3.0 L); not used.
- Bukit Timah Hill 163.63 m: the primary page found gives 163 m (S31).
- The first NEWater factory's date: S9 gives 2002, S16 January 2003; the topic says NEWater was introduced in 2002 (S5) and the first factories opened in 2003.
