<Lang en>

Singapore uses about <Num s="S11">440 million gallons</Num> of water a day, and demand may almost double by 2065. Households use about <Num s="S32">45 %</Num> of the water today; factories, offices and other non-domestic users take about <Num s="S12">55 %</Num>, a share expected to rise to about <Num s="S12">two-thirds</Num>. <FlyTo preset="home">Look at one block of flats</FlyTo>

In 2025 each person in a household used, on average, <Num s="S12">141 litres</Num> of water a day. The national target is <Num s="S13">130 litres</Num> by 2030. That is where small habits come in, because <Term id="water-conservation">water conservation</Term> starts at the tap. Turning off the tap while you brush your teeth saves about <Num s="S9">23 litres</Num> a day; turning off the shower while you soap yourself saves about <Num s="S9">27 litres</Num>. Across a whole island, those litres add up.

Keeping water clean matters as much as saving it. Litter, oil and chemicals left on streets wash into drains that lead to reservoirs, and what is poured down a sink goes to a water reclamation plant that has to remove it. The cleaner the water that enters each step, the less energy and treatment it needs.

Look at the whole slice running. Rain fills the reservoirs; water arrives from Johor; used water becomes NEWater; seawater becomes fresh water. The water cycle the Sun drives in chapter 02 and the four taps work together as one system, and every drop that is not wasted is a drop that none of them has to supply. The map topic <TopicLink slug="four-taps">Singapore's Four Taps</TopicLink> shows how the system grew across the island. <FlyTo preset="hero">See all four taps running</FlyTo>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Pollution", zh: "水污染" }}>

Polluted water harms plants and animals that live in it and is harder to treat. In Singapore, the National Environment Agency monitors the water in rivers, reservoirs and the sea and controls what factories may discharge, and PUB keeps catchments and drains clean. Anyone can help by not littering and never pouring oil, paint or chemicals into drains.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Try it: a fair test at the sink", zh: "试一试：水槽前的公平测试" }}>

Find out how much water brushing your teeth uses. One day, let the tap run while you brush for two minutes, with a large container in the sink to catch the water, and measure it with a measuring jug. The next day, brush for the same two minutes with the tap turned off, using only one cup of water, and measure that. Keep the tap opened the same amount and the brushing time the same; change only whether the tap runs. How many litres did you save?

</More>

新加坡每天用水约 <Num s="S11">4.4 亿加仑</Num>；到 2065 年用水量可能接近翻倍。今天，家庭用掉约 <Num s="S32">45 %</Num> 的水，工厂、办公室和其他非家庭用户约用掉 <Num s="S12">55 %</Num>，这个比例预计会升到约 <Num s="S12">三分之二</Num>。<FlyTo preset="home">看看一栋组屋</FlyTo>

2025 年，家庭里平均每人每天用水 <Num s="S12">141 升</Num>，国家目标是到 2030 年降到 <Num s="S13">130 升</Num>。这就要靠生活里的小习惯，因为<Term id="water-conservation">节约用水</Term>从水龙头开始。刷牙时关上水龙头，每天约省 <Num s="S9">23 升</Num>；淋浴抹肥皂时关水，约省 <Num s="S9">27 升</Num>。全岛加起来，就是很大的数量。

保持水的清洁和节约用水同样重要。留在街上的垃圾、油污和化学品会被冲进通往蓄水池的排水沟；倒进水槽的东西会流到再生水厂，要在那里去掉。每一步进来的水越干净，需要的能源和处理就越少。

<More title={{ en: "Pollution", zh: "水污染" }}>

被污染的水会伤害生活在水里的动植物，也更难处理。在新加坡，国家环境局监测河流、蓄水池和海水的水质，管制工厂可以排放什么；PUB 保持集水区和排水沟的清洁。每个人都可以出一份力：不乱丢垃圾，也绝不把油、油漆或化学品倒进排水沟。

</More>
