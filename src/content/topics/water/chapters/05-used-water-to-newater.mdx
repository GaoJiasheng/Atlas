<Lang en>

Water does not disappear down the drain. PUB calls it used water, because it is collected to be cleaned and used again. On the stage the front of the ground is cut away so that the pipes and tunnel underneath show. <FlyTo preset="home">Start at the kitchen sink</FlyTo>

From homes, used water runs into sewers and then into the Deep Tunnel Sewerage System, a network of tunnels about <Num s="S14">206 km</Num> long. The newer tunnels lie <Num s="S15">35 to 55 m</Num> below ground and slope gently, so the water flows by gravity to <Num s="S14">three</Num> large water reclamation plants. There, solids are allowed to settle out, and in big aerated tanks microbes feed on the dirt dissolved in the water.

Part of this treated water goes on to a NEWater factory. First it is pushed through a <Term id="membrane">membrane</Term> of hollow fibres with tiny pores that hold back bacteria and fine particles. Then comes <Term id="reverse-osmosis">reverse osmosis</Term>: under pressure, water is forced through a membrane so fine that dissolved salts stay behind. Finally, ultraviolet light disinfects it. NEWater was introduced in 2002, and <Num s="S5">four</Num> factories are in operation today. <FlyTo preset="plants">Look inside the NEWater factory</FlyTo>

Most NEWater goes through its own pipes to industry, especially wafer fabrication plants that make computer chips and need very clean water, and to cool air-conditioning systems. In dry periods, some is added to reservoirs. There it mixes with rain water, and the mixture is treated again at a waterworks before it reaches anyone's tap.

<More title={{ en: "Not straight from the sewer", zh: "不是直接喝下水道的水" }}>

People sometimes say that Singaporeans drink sewage. That is not what happens. Used water is first treated at a water reclamation plant, then purified in three more steps into NEWater, which meets drinking-water standards. Even so, the NEWater that is added to reservoirs is mixed with rain water and treated once more at a waterworks.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A closed loop", zh: "闭合的循环" }}>

Every drop of used water that is reclaimed is a drop that does not have to come from rain, Johor or the sea. Singapore has about <Num s="S9">3,700 km</Num> of public sewers and about <Num s="S9">660 km</Num> of NEWater pipes. Reusing water this way turns a one-way flow from tap to sea into a loop.

</More>

<Lang zh>

水流下排水口，并没有消失。PUB 把它叫做"用过的水"，因为它会被收集起来、净化，再用一次。舞台上把地面的前缘切开了，好让人看见下面的管道和隧道。<FlyTo preset="home">从厨房水槽看起</FlyTo>

住家用过的水流进污水管，再流进深隧道排污系统，这是一张约 <Num s="S14">206 公里</Num>长的隧道网。较新的隧道位于地下 <Num s="S15">35 至 55 米</Num>，缓缓倾斜，水靠重力流到 <Num s="S14">3</Num> 座大型再生水厂。在那里，先让固体沉下去，再在通着空气的大池里让微生物吃掉水里溶解的污物。

处理过的水有一部分再送到新生水厂。首先，水被压过由空心纤维做成的<Term id="membrane">膜</Term>，膜上的小孔挡住细菌和微小颗粒。接着是<Term id="reverse-osmosis">反渗透</Term>：加压把水压过一层极细的膜，溶解的盐被留在另一侧。最后用紫外线消毒。新生水 2002 年推出，今天有 <Num s="S5">4</Num> 座新生水厂在运行。<FlyTo preset="plants">看看新生水厂里面</FlyTo>

大部分新生水经专用管道送到工业用户，特别是制造电脑芯片、需要非常干净的水的晶圆厂，也用来冷却空调系统。干旱时期，一部分新生水加进蓄水池，和雨水混在一起，混合后的水还要在水厂再处理一遍，才送到水龙头。

<More title={{ en: "Not straight from the sewer", zh: "不是直接喝下水道的水" }}>

有人说新加坡人喝的是污水。事实不是这样。用过的水先在再生水厂处理，再经过三道工序提纯成新生水，达到饮用水标准。即使这样，加进蓄水池的新生水还要和雨水混合，在水厂再处理一次。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A closed loop", zh: "闭合的循环" }}>

每回收一滴用过的水，就少一滴要靠下雨、柔佛或大海供应的水。新加坡有约 <Num s="S9">3,700 公里</Num>的公共污水管和约 <Num s="S9">660 公里</Num>的新生水管。这样重复用水，把"从水龙头流进大海"的单程，变成了一个循环。

</More>
