A <Term id="pie-chart">pie chart</Term> shows how one whole is shared out. It does not have a scale; it has a whole.

On <Beat n={1}>this pie chart</Beat> the whole circle stands for all 32 pupils in a class. Each part is the pupils who go to school one way.

<Beat n={2}>The walk part is half the circle.</Beat> Fold the circle along the straight edge and the two halves match. So half of the class walks to school: ½ of 32 is 16 pupils.

<Beat n={3}>The bus part is a quarter of the circle</Beat>, so it is a quarter of the class: ¼ of 32 = 32 ÷ 4 = 8 pupils. This is fraction of a set, the same as in the fractions lesson. A common slip is to answer 4, the bottom number of ¼. The fraction is a part of the whole, not a number of pupils.

Be careful with <Beat n={4}>parts that only look alike</Beat>. This pie is also cut into 4 parts, but they are not the same size, so they are not quarters. Only a part with a right angle at the centre, like part B, is a quarter of the circle.

You can <Beat n={5}>compare parts</Beat> just as you compare bars. Walking is 16 and bus is 8, so 8 more pupils walk than take the bus, and the walk part is twice as big as the bus part. The MRT and driven parts are each ⅛: 32 ÷ 8 = 4 pupils each.

<Term id="pie-chart">饼图</Term>表示一个整体怎样分开。它没有刻度，只有一个整体。

<Beat n={1}>这张饼图</Beat>里，整个圆表示全班 32 个学生。每一份是用一种方式上学的人。

<Beat n={2}>走路那一份是半个圆。</Beat>沿那条直边对折，两半正好重合。所以全班一半的人走路上学：32 的 ½ 是 16 人。

<Beat n={3}>巴士那一份是圆的四分之一</Beat>，就是全班的四分之一：32 的 ¼ = 32 ÷ 4 = 8 人。这就是分数课里学过的“一组的几分之几”。常见的错误是答 4，也就是 ¼ 下面的那个数。分数是整体的一部分，不是人数。

小心<Beat n={4}>只是看起来像的份</Beat>。这个饼也分成 4 块，可是大小不一样，所以不是四分之一。只有在圆心是直角的那一块，比如 B 块，才是圆的四分之一。

<Beat n={5}>比较两份</Beat>和比较两根条一样。走路 16 人，巴士 8 人，走路的比坐巴士的多 8 人，走路那一份是巴士那一份的两倍大。地铁和家长送各是 ⅛：每份 32 ÷ 8 = 4 人。
