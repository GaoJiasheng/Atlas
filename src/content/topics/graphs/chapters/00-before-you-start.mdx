<Lang en>

Charts are everywhere: on a class noticeboard, in a newspaper, on a weather page. Each one holds numbers. This lesson is about getting those numbers out again, correctly.

On the stage now is a bar graph of the fruit a class likes best. A dashed line goes from the top of the mango bar across to the scale, where it meets 8. That is how you read a bar: from its top, straight across to the scale.

There are 7 steps and a practice. Step 01 starts with picture graphs and turns them into a bar graph. Steps 02 and 03 read bar graphs, first on a scale of 1, then on scales of 2, 5, 10 and more. Step 04 is tables and tally marks. Steps 05 and 06 read line graphs and how they go up and down. Step 07 reads pie charts with halves, quarters and eighths. The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

Each chapter explains first. The circles on the chapter axis at the bottom are its beats: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the stage changes the chart while the reading panel says what to look at.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question, drawn from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: type a number, fill in a table, or choose an answer. On a graph, tap a bar or a point to draw a reading line to the scale. The line is there to help; it is not checked.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** gives the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, each one closer to the answer.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Nothing is timed and nothing is saved.

<Lang zh>

图表到处都有：班上的布告栏、报纸、天气预报。每一张图表里都装着数。这节课讲的是怎样把这些数正确地读出来。

舞台上现在是一张条形图，表示一个班最喜欢的水果。一条虚线从芒果那根条的顶端，平平地连到刻度上，碰到 8。读一根条就是这样：从条的顶端，平平地看到刻度上。

全课有 7 步和一章练习。第 01 步从象形图开始，再把它变成条形图。第 02、03 步读条形图，先用每格 1 的刻度，再用每格 2、5、10 等刻度。第 04 步讲表格和划记。第 05、06 步读折线图，看它怎样升、怎样降。第 07 步读饼图，用到二分之一、四分之一和八分之一。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

每一章都先讲。屏幕底部章节轴上的小圆点就是这一章的拍：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上的图表就会变，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。

每章最后的小方块是一道小题，从十道左右的题里挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：填一个数、把表格填完整，或者选一个答案。在图上点一根条或一个点，会画出一条连到刻度的读数线。这条线只是帮你读，不算对错。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按“看答案”。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，一条比一条接近答案。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

不计时，也不保存。
