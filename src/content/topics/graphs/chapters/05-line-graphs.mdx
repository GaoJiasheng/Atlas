A bar graph compares different things. A <Term id="line-graph">line graph</Term> follows one thing as it changes over time.

Start with <Beat n={1}>the points on their own</Beat>. A class measured a bean plant at the end of each week. Each point is one measurement: the week is along the bottom, the height in centimetres up the side. At week 0 the plant was 2 cm tall.

Now <Beat n={2}>join the points in order with straight lines</Beat>. The line shows at a glance when the plant grew fast (a steep part) and when it grew slowly (a gentle part). The line only joins the readings; in this lesson you read values at the points, not between them.

To read a point, <Beat n={3}>go up from the week to the point, then straight across to the scale</Beat>. At week 3 the point is halfway between 4 and 6, so the plant was 5 cm tall. It is the same skill as reading the top of a bar, and the same care with the scale: here each gridline is 2.

Here is <Beat n={4}>a different line graph</Beat>: the money in Siti's savings box at the end of each month. Where the line goes up, the amount increased. Where it is flat, nothing changed. Where it goes down, the amount decreased: Siti took some money out. The coloured parts show one of each.

条形图比较不同的东西。<Term id="line-graph">折线图</Term>跟着一样东西，看它随时间怎么变。

先看<Beat n={1}>单独的点</Beat>。一个班每周末量一次豆苗。每个点是一次测量：底下是第几周，旁边是高度（cm）。第 0 周，豆苗高 2 cm。

现在<Beat n={2}>按顺序用直线把点连起来</Beat>。一眼就看得出豆苗什么时候长得快（陡的一段），什么时候长得慢（平缓的一段）。线只是把读数连起来；这节课只读点上的数，不读两点之间。

读一个点，<Beat n={3}>从那一周往上找到点，再平平地看到刻度</Beat>。第 3 周的点在 4 和 6 正中间，所以豆苗高 5 cm。这和读条顶是同一种本领，也一样要注意刻度：这里每一格是 2。

再看<Beat n={4}>另一张折线图</Beat>：Siti 储蓄罐里每月底有多少钱。线往上，钱多了；线是平的，没有变；线往下，钱少了：Siti 拿出了一些钱。图上有颜色的几段，各是一种。
