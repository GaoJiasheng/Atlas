<Lang en>

A line graph is most useful for questions about change: how much more, how much less, when did it change most?

Look at <Beat n={1}>January to February</Beat> on Siti's savings graph. The line goes up from 10 to 25. The <Term id="increase-decrease">increase</Term> is the difference between the two points: 25 − 10 = $15. As with bars, the answer to "how much more" is a gap, not one of the two numbers.

Now <Beat n={2}>write the change beside every part of the line</Beat>: +15, +5, 0, −5, +10. The biggest increase is the steepest part going up, from January to February. June is the highest point, so it is easy to pick, but from May to June the money went up by only $10. The highest point tells you when there was the most money, not when it grew the most.

Two more kinds of change: <Beat n={3}>from March to April the line is flat</Beat>, so there was no change (30 − 30 = 0). From April to May it goes down: Siti took out 30 − 25 = $5. A decrease is a difference too.

You can also read a line graph <Beat n={4}>the other way round</Beat>. Start at $30 on the scale and go straight across. The line has two points there, March and April. When a question asks "in which months", there may be more than one answer.

<Lang zh>

折线图最适合回答关于变化的问题：多了多少，少了多少，什么时候变化最大？

看 Siti 储蓄图上的<Beat n={1}>1 月到 2 月</Beat>。线从 10 升到 25。<Term id="increase-decrease">增加</Term>了多少，就是两个点的差：25 − 10 = 15 元。和条形图一样，“多了多少”的答案是差，不是两个数中的一个。

现在<Beat n={2}>把每一段的变化写在旁边</Beat>：+15、+5、0、−5、+10。增加最多的是往上最陡的一段：1 月到 2 月。6 月的点最高，很容易被选中，可是 5 月到 6 月只多了 10 元。最高的点告诉你什么时候钱最多，不是什么时候长得最多。

还有两种变化：<Beat n={3}>3 月到 4 月线是平的</Beat>，没有变化（30 − 30 = 0）。4 月到 5 月往下：Siti 拿出了 30 − 25 = 5 元。减少也是一个差。

折线图也可以<Beat n={4}>反过来读</Beat>。从刻度上的 30 平平地看过去，线上有两个点在那里：3 月和 4 月。题目问“哪几个月”的时候，答案可能不止一个。
