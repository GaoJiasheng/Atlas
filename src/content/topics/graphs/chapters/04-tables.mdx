<Lang en>

A <Term id="table">table</Term> puts numbers in rows and columns, each with a name. It is often the first place data is written down.

When you count things as they happen, <Term id="tally">tally marks</Term> help. <Beat n={1}>Ravi tallied the drinks a stall sold</Beat>: each time a cup was sold he drew a stroke, and every fifth stroke goes across the four before it. So each bundle is 5. Count in fives: barley water has 4 bundles, 5, 10, 15, 20. Then he wrote 20 in the table.

<Beat n={2}>A table often ends with a total row.</Beat> The <Term id="total">total</Term> is all the rows above it added together: 35 + 20 + 45 + 30 = 130 cups.

If you know the total, you can find a row that is missing. In <Beat n={3}>this table of favourite sports</Beat> the total is 60, and three of the four rows are known. The missing one is the total take away the others: 60 − 18 − 15 − 12 = 15. Be careful: the total is not one of the rows, so it is never added in.

Some tables have <Beat n={4}>rows and columns</Beat>. This one shows how pupils of two classes go to school. To answer "how many P4 pupils take the bus", go along the P4 row and down the bus column; the cell where they meet holds the answer, 14. The last column and the last row are totals.

When you are ready, <Task id="tables">try one question</Task>: fill in a table or read one.

<Lang zh>

<Term id="table">表格</Term>把数排成行和列，每一行、每一列都有名字。数据常常是先写在表格里的。

边发生边数的时候，用<Term id="tally">划记</Term>很方便。<Beat n={1}>Ravi 在饮料摊上划记卖出的杯数</Beat>：每卖出一杯就画一竖，第五笔斜着划过前面四竖。所以每一捆是 5。五个五个地数：薏米水有 4 捆，5、10、15、20。然后他把 20 写进表格。

<Beat n={2}>表格最后常常有一行总数。</Beat><Term id="total">总数</Term>是上面所有行加起来：35 + 20 + 45 + 30 = 130 杯。

知道总数，就能求出缺的那一行。在<Beat n={3}>这张最喜欢的运动表</Beat>里，总数是 60，四行里知道三行。缺的那一行是总数减去其他几行：60 − 18 − 15 − 12 = 15。要小心：总数本身不是其中一行，不能再加进去。

有的表格<Beat n={4}>有行也有列</Beat>。这一张表示两个班的学生怎样上学。要回答“四年级坐巴士的有几人”，沿四年级那一行看过去，再沿巴士那一列看下来，交叉的那一格就是答案：14。最后一列和最后一行是总数。
