You have counted things with pictures since you started school. A <Term id="picture-graph">picture graph</Term> does the same on paper: each row or column of pictures shows how many of something there are.

Start with <Beat n={1}>a small group of pupils</Beat>. Each picture is one pupil, so you can simply count: 3 walk, 5 take the bus, 2 are driven and 4 take the MRT. Together that is 14 pupils.

With a whole class there is more to count, and one picture can stand for more than one. Look at <Beat n={2}>the fruit graph and its key</Beat>. The <Term id="key">key</Term> in the corner says that one picture stands for 2 pupils. Mango has 4 pictures, so 4 × 2 = 8 pupils chose mango. Always read the key first: the number of pictures is not the number of pupils.

Sometimes a row ends with <Beat n={3}>half a picture</Beat>. Half a picture is half of what a whole picture stands for. Here a whole picture is 2 pupils, so half is 1, and rambutan is 4 × 2 + 1 = 9 pupils. It is not 5 (counting the half as a whole), and not 4 (leaving it out).

Now <Beat n={4}>stack the pictures and draw round them</Beat>. The columns become bars, and a scale on the side tells you how many each bar stands for. This is a <Term id="bar-graph">bar graph</Term>. You no longer count pictures; you read where the top of each bar is on the scale. The numbers have not changed: mango 8, durian 6, rambutan 9, banana 4.

When you are ready, <Task id="picture-graphs">try one question</Task> on reading a picture graph.

从上学开始，你就会用图来数东西了。<Term id="picture-graph">象形图</Term>把这件事画在纸上：每一行或每一列图，表示某样东西有多少。

先看<Beat n={1}>一小组学生</Beat>。每个图是一个学生，所以直接数就行：走路 3 人，坐巴士 5 人，家长送 2 人，坐地铁 4 人。一共 14 人。

换成全班，要数的多了，一个图就可以表示不止一个。看<Beat n={2}>水果图和它的图例</Beat>。角上的<Term id="key">图例</Term>说，一个图表示 2 人。芒果有 4 个图，所以选芒果的是 4 × 2 = 8 人。一定先看图例：图的个数不等于人数。

有时一行的最后是<Beat n={3}>半个图</Beat>。半个图表示一个整图的一半。这里一个整图是 2 人，一半就是 1 人，所以红毛丹是 4 × 2 + 1 = 9 人。不是 5（把半个当成一个），也不是 4（把半个漏掉）。

现在<Beat n={4}>把图叠起来，沿外边画一圈</Beat>。一列一列的图变成了条，旁边的刻度告诉你每根条表示多少。这就是<Term id="bar-graph">条形图</Term>。这时不用再数图，看每根条的顶端在刻度的哪里就行。数没有变：芒果 8，榴梿 6，红毛丹 9，香蕉 4。

准备好了，就<Task id="picture-graphs">做一道小题</Task>：读一张象形图。
