<Lang en>

A bar graph tells you a lot before you read a single bar. Look at <Beat n={1}>its parts</Beat>: the title says what was counted (books four friends read in June), each <Term id="axis">axis</Term> has a label, and one axis has a <Term id="scale">scale</Term>. On this scale each <Term id="gridline">gridline</Term> is worth 1, and every second gridline has a number, so 15 sits between the lines for 14 and 16.

To read a bar, <Beat n={2}>go from its top straight across to the scale</Beat>. Mei Ling's bar ends one line above 14, so she read 15 books. Use a ruler or your finger if it helps; on a question you can tap a bar and a dashed line is drawn for you.

Bar graphs are good for comparing. <Beat n={3}>How many more books did Mei Ling read than Ravi?</Beat> The answer is the gap between the two tops: 15 − 7 = 8. A common slip is to answer 15, the bigger number, or to add the two. "How many more" always asks for a difference.

A bar graph can also <Beat n={4}>lie on its side</Beat>. Then the bars go across, the names are on the left, and the scale is along the bottom. Read it the same way: from the end of a bar straight down to the scale. Adding all four bars gives how many books the friends read together: 12 + 7 + 15 + 9 = 43.

When you are ready, <Task id="reading-bar-graphs">try one question</Task>.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

一张条形图，还没读条，就已经告诉你很多。看<Beat n={1}>它的各个部分</Beat>：标题说数的是什么（四个朋友六月读的书），两条<Term id="axis">轴</Term>各有名字，其中一条轴上有<Term id="scale">刻度</Term>。这个刻度每一<Term id="gridline">格</Term>是 1，每隔一格写一个数，所以 15 在 14 和 16 两条线中间。

读一根条，<Beat n={2}>从它的顶端平平地看到刻度上</Beat>。Mei Ling 的条顶在 14 上面一格，所以她读了 15 本。需要的话可以用尺子或手指比着看；做题时点一下条，就会画出一条虚线帮你。

条形图最适合比较。<Beat n={3}>Mei Ling 比 Ravi 多读了几本？</Beat>答案是两个顶端之间的差：15 − 7 = 8。常见的错误是答 15（大的那个数），或者把两个数加起来。“多多少”问的永远是差。

条形图也可以<Beat n={4}>横着放</Beat>。这时条是横的，名字在左边，刻度在下面。读法一样：从条的末端直直地往下看到刻度。把四根条加起来，就是四个人一共读的书：12 + 7 + 15 + 9 = 43。

</Lang>
