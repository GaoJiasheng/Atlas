<Lang en>

The same numbers can be drawn on different scales. This chapter is about reading the scale before you read the bars.

Here is <Beat n={1}>the fruit graph again</Beat>, with each gridline worth 1. The bars are tall. Now <Beat n={2}>change the scale so each gridline is 2</Beat>. Watch the stage: every gridline stays the same height, the numbers beside them change, and the bars get shorter. The bars still show the same numbers. Rambutan now ends halfway between 8 and 10, and halfway between 8 and 10 is 9.

Bigger numbers need a bigger scale. On <Beat n={3}>the drinks graph</Beat> each gridline is 5. Soya bean milk ends at the 7th gridline, so it is 7 × 5 = 35 cups, not 7. Counting gridlines as 1 each is the most common mistake with scales: always ask, how much is one gridline?

With each gridline worth 10, <Beat n={4}>soya bean milk ends halfway between 30 and 40</Beat>. Halfway is 35, the same number as before. Lime juice ends halfway between 40 and 50: 45.

One more thing to check: <Beat n={5}>where the axis starts</Beat>. On the left graph the axis starts at 20, not 0, so the bar for 28 looks twice as tall as the bar for 24. But 28 is not twice 24. Drawn from 0, on the right, the two bars are nearly the same height. Before you compare heights, look at the bottom of the scale.

<Lang zh>

同样的数，可以画在不同的刻度上。这一章讲的是：读条以前，先读刻度。

还是<Beat n={1}>那张水果图</Beat>，每一格表示 1，条很高。现在<Beat n={2}>换个刻度，让每一格表示 2</Beat>。看舞台：每一格还是一样高，旁边的数换了，条变矮了。可条表示的数没有变。红毛丹现在顶在 8 和 10 正中间，8 和 10 正中间是 9。

数大了，就要用大一点的刻度。在<Beat n={3}>饮料图</Beat>上，每一格是 5。豆奶顶在第 7 格，所以是 7 × 5 = 35 杯，不是 7。把每一格都当成 1，是读刻度最常见的错误：一定先问，每一格表示多少？

每一格是 10 时，<Beat n={4}>豆奶顶在 30 和 40 正中间</Beat>。正中间是 35，和刚才一样。酸柑汁顶在 40 和 50 正中间：45。

还要看一件事：<Beat n={5}>轴从哪里开始</Beat>。左边那张图的轴从 20 开始，不是从 0，所以 28 的条看起来是 24 的两倍高。可 28 并不是 24 的两倍。右边从 0 开始画，两根条差不多高。比高矮以前，先看刻度的最底下。
