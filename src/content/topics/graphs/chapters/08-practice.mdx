<Lang en>

The practice has eight questions from the whole lesson, in a fixed order: a picture graph, two bar graphs, a table to fill in from tally marks, a table with a missing row, two line graphs and a pie chart.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why. If it is not right, the answer is shown, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time. You can still tap a bar or a point to draw a reading line. Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. **Try again** starts the same eight questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

| Question | Kind | Step to revisit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | read a picture graph with half a picture | 01 From pictures to bars |
| 2 | how many more, on a scale of 5 | 02 Reading a bar graph, 03 Scales |
| 3 | a bar halfway between two gridlines | 03 Scales |
| 4 | fill in a table from tally marks | 04 Tables |
| 5 | a missing row from the total | 04 Tables |
| 6 | the biggest increase on a line graph | 06 Going up, going down |
| 7 | a quarter of a pie chart | 07 Pie charts |
| 8 | read a point between two gridlines | 05 Reading a line graph |

<Lang zh>

练习有 8 道题，来自整节课，顺序固定：一张象形图，两张条形图，一张按划记填写的表格，一张缺一行的表格，两张折线图和一张饼图。

每题只检查一次。按“检查”以后，马上就能看到对不对、为什么。如果还没对，会给出答案，还有一个按钮可以回到教这一点的那一步。检查以后答案就不能再改，所以按之前慢慢想。做题时照样可以点条或点，画出读数线。每题有一条提示，看了提示不扣分。

做完最后一题，会出现一张小表：每题一行，标出 ✓ 或 ○，以及可以回看的那一步。按“再做一次”，同样的 8 道题从头开始。不计时，也不保存。
