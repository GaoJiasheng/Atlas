<Lang en>

The practice has eight questions from the whole lesson, in a fixed order. They use the same kinds of task as the chapters: naming a solid from its net, deciding whether a shape folds into a cube, choosing nets, drawing a net, tapping a face and finishing a net.

Question 1 shows a net of rectangles and triangles and asks which solid it folds into. Question 2 asks whether a net that does not look like the cross folds into a cube. Question 3 asks you to choose every net of a cube from five shapes; more than one is right. Question 4 gives you one square on squared paper and asks you to draw a whole net of a cube by tapping five more squares. Question 5 asks for the face opposite a shaded one. Question 6 shows the net of a box with no lid and asks which square is the bottom. Question 7 asks you to choose the rectangle that finishes a cuboid's net. Question 8 asks which net folds into a cone.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why; a net you drew or finished is folded up on the stage so you can see what happens. If it is not right, the stage shows the answer, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time.

Here the FOLD slider stays locked: fold each net in your head before you check. The turn buttons still work. Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. **Try again** starts the same eight questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

Question 1 is on the stage, with its answer controls above this text. The squares on the chapter axis are the eight questions.

<Lang zh>

练习有 8 道题，来自整节课，顺序固定。题型和各章的小题一样：看展开图说出立体，判断一个图形能不能折成正方体，选出展开图，画展开图，在展开图上点一个面，把展开图补完整。

第 1 题给出一个由长方形和三角形组成的展开图，问它能折成什么立体。第 2 题问一个不像十字形的展开图能不能折成正方体。第 3 题要从五个图形里选出所有正方体的展开图，对的不止一个。第 4 题在方格纸上先给你一个正方形，要你再点 5 个格子，画出一个完整的正方体展开图。第 5 题要找出和涂色的面相对的面。第 6 题给出一个没有盖的盒子的展开图，问哪个正方形是底。第 7 题要选出那个能把长方体展开图补完整的长方形。第 8 题问哪个展开图能折成圆锥。

每题只检查一次。按“检查”以后，马上就能看到对不对、为什么；你画的或补的展开图，舞台会把它折起来给你看。如果还没对，舞台会给出答案，还有一个按钮可以回到教这一点的那一步。检查以后答案就不能再改，所以按之前慢慢想。

练习里 FOLD 滑块一直锁着：检查以前，先在脑子里把展开图折一折。转动按钮照样能用。每题有一条提示，看了提示不扣分。

做完最后一题，会出现一张小表：每题一行，标出 ✓ 或 ○，以及可以回看的那一步。按“再做一次”，同样的 8 道题从头开始。不计时，也不保存。

第 1 题已经在舞台上，作答的地方在这段文字上方。章节轴上的 8 个小方块就是这 8 道题。
