<Lang en>

A cube has 6 faces, so it has 3 pairs of faces that face away from each other. This chapter finds those pairs on a net, before it is folded.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the cube with its pairs in colour</Beat>. Opposite faces never touch. The top and the bottom are <Term id="opposite-faces">opposite faces</Term>, so are the front and the back, and so are the left and the right. Each pair has its own colour and its own mark, ●, ▲ or ■, so you can tell the pairs apart even without the colours.

Now <Beat n={2}>unfold the cube, keeping the colours</Beat>. In the row of 4, squares one apart are opposite: the 1st and the 3rd, the 2nd and the 4th. Between the two squares of a pair there is exactly one square. The two squares off the row are a pair too.

Next, look at <Beat n={3}>the shaded square and the squares beside it</Beat>. Squares that share an edge in the net always meet on the cube, so they can never be opposite. The edge they share becomes an edge of the cube, where the two faces meet. To find the face opposite the shaded one, look for a square that does not touch it at all.

Last, <Beat n={4}>a net that folds into a box with no lid</Beat>. It has only 5 squares. The row of 4 wraps round to make the four sides, and the square at the end of the net becomes the bottom. The bottom is the face opposite the open side. A bottom at the end of the net is the hardest to spot: in one test of 155 students in New Zealand, this was the open-box net they found <Num s="S19">hardest</Num> to recognise.

The usual mistake is to choose a square right next to the shaded one. Squares side by side in a net are neighbours on the cube. Look one square further along the row instead.

When you are ready, <Task id="opposite-faces">try one question</Task>: tap the face opposite a shaded one, or tap the bottom of a box.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

正方体有 6 个面，所以有 3 对背对着背的面。这一章要在展开图还没折起来的时候，就把这几对找出来。

先看<Beat n={1}>每一对都涂上颜色的正方体</Beat>。相对的两个面不相挨。上面和下面是<Term id="opposite-faces">相对的面</Term>，前面和后面相对，左面和右面也相对。每一对有自己的颜色，还印着自己的记号 ●、▲ 或 ■，不看颜色也分得开。

再<Beat n={2}>把正方体展开，颜色不变</Beat>。在 4 个一排里，隔一个的两个相对：第 1 个和第 3 个，第 2 个和第 4 个。一对里的两个正方形中间，正好隔着一个正方形。不在这一排上的那两个，也是一对。

接着看<Beat n={3}>涂色的正方形和它旁边的正方形</Beat>。在展开图里共用一条边的两个正方形，折起来一定挨着，不可能相对。它们共用的那条边，折好以后就是两个面相接的那条棱。要找和涂色的面相对的那个，得找一个和它完全不挨着的正方形。

最后看<Beat n={4}>折成没有盖的盒子的展开图</Beat>。它只有 5 个正方形。一排 4 个绕一圈当四周，展开图尽头的那个正方形当底。底就是空着的那一面的对面。底在一端的最难看出来：在新西兰一次有 155 名学生参加的测试里，他们最<Num s="S19">难认出</Num>的开口盒展开图，就是这一种。

常见的错误，是点了紧挨着涂色正方形的那一个。展开图里并排挨着的两个，在正方体上也挨着。应该沿着这一排再隔一个去找。

准备好了，就<Task id="opposite-faces">做一道小题</Task>：点出和涂色的面相对的面，或者点出盒子的底。
