<Lang en>

The cube in the last chapter opened into a cross. Is that the only way it can open?

Look at <Beat n={1}>the cross-shaped net</Beat>. It is the net most people know, and many people think it is the only one. It is not. Cut the cube along different edges and you get a different net.

Here is <Beat n={2}>a net with the top square moved to the end</Beat>. The row of 4 stays put, and the square above it slides to the end. It still folds into a cube. The row of 4 wraps round to make four sides, and the two other squares close its two open ends.

Now see <Beat n={3}>the six nets with a row of 4</Beat>. Each has a row of 4 in the middle and one square on each side of the row. There are 6 different ones. Turning a net round, or flipping it over, does not make a new one.

Then look at <Beat n={4}>the other five nets</Beat>. In these, the longest row has only 3 squares, or only 2. They do not look much like the cross, but every one of them folds into a cube. Altogether a cube has <Num s="S3">11</Num> different nets: 6 with a row of 4, 4 whose longest row has 3, and 1 whose longest row has 2.

Last, watch <Beat n={5}>the zigzag net fold up</Beat>. To test a net in your head, try this. Choose a bottom, fold up the sides, then close the top. The zigzag works too: one square is the bottom, the squares joined to it fold up as sides, the squares hanging on those fold up next, and the last one closes the top.

People who know only the cross often say "no" to a net that looks different. Do not judge a net by how much it looks like the cross. Fold it, in your head or with the FOLD slider, and see whether every face is covered.

<More title={{ en: "Six squares, many shapes", zh: "6 个正方形，很多种拼法" }}>

Join 6 squares edge to edge and you can make <Num s="S6">35</Num> different shapes, counting a shape turned or flipped over as the same one. Only 11 of them are nets of a cube; all the others leave a face open when you fold them. The next chapter shows how to tell.

</More>

<Lang zh>

上一章的正方体打开成了一个十字形。正方体只能这样打开吗？

先看<Beat n={1}>十字形的展开图</Beat>。这是大家最熟悉的展开图，很多人以为它是唯一的一种。其实不是。沿着不同的棱剪开，就会得到不同的展开图。

再看<Beat n={2}>上面那个正方形挪到了一头</Beat>。中间一排 4 个不动，上面那个沿着这一排挪到最边上。它照样能折成正方体：一排 4 个绕一圈，围成四个侧面，另外两个正方形盖住两头的口。

现在看<Beat n={3}>中间一排 4 个的六种展开图</Beat>。它们都是中间一排 4 个，这一排的上面一个、下面一个，一共有 6 种不同的展开图。把展开图转一转、翻过来，不算新的一种，只是同一个展开图换了个方向放。

接着看<Beat n={4}>另外五种展开图</Beat>。这几种，最长的一排只有 3 个，或者只有 2 个。它们看上去不太像十字形，可是每一种都能折成正方体。正方体一共有 <Num s="S3">11</Num> 种不同的展开图：一排 4 个的有 6 种，最长一排 3 个的有 4 种，最长一排 2 个的有 1 种。

最后看<Beat n={5}>Z 字形的展开图折起来</Beat>。在脑子里试一个展开图：先选一个底，把四周折起来，最后盖上顶。Z 字形的也行：选一个正方形当底，和它相连的几个折起来当侧面，挂在侧面上的接着往上折，最后一个盖上顶。

只认得十字形的人，常常对样子不一样的展开图说“不行”。判断展开图，不能看它像不像十字形，要折一折：在脑子里折，或者用 FOLD 滑块折，看每个面是不是都盖住了。

<More title={{ en: "Six squares, many shapes", zh: "6 个正方形，很多种拼法" }}>

把 6 个正方形边挨着边拼起来，一共能拼出 <Num s="S6">35</Num> 种不同的图形（转一转、翻过来算同一种）。其中只有 11 种是正方体的展开图，其余的折起来都会空着一个面。下一章讲怎样分辨。

</More>
