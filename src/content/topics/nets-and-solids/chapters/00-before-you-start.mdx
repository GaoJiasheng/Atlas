<Lang en>

Boxes, tins and dice are all around you. Many of them started as one flat piece of card or metal that was cut out and folded or rolled up. This lesson is about that flat piece: how a solid opens out flat, and how a flat shape folds back into a solid.

On the stage now is a net of a cube, folded half way. Its six squares are still joined along their edges and are turning up towards a cube. In the lower right corner of the stage there is a **FOLD** slider: drag it, or tap it and use the arrow keys, to fold the net all the way up or lay it flat again. The two turn buttons beside it, ↺ and ↻, turn the solid a quarter of the way round, so you can look at the other side.

There are 8 steps and a practice. Step 01 names the solids and their faces. Steps 02 to 05 open a cube: one net, then all 11, how to test a shape by folding it, and which faces end up opposite each other. Steps 06 and 07 do the same for a cuboid, a prism and a pyramid. Step 08 opens a cylinder and a cone; there you only need to recognise their nets. The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

Each chapter explains first. The circles on the chapter axis at the bottom are its beats: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the stage folds, unfolds, turns or changes, while the reading panel says what to look at.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question, drawn from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: choose an answer, tap squares to finish a net, tap a face on a net, or type a number.
- **Check** (C): when you finish a net, the stage folds it up to show you what happens. If it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** folds the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, each one closer to the answer. In a question the FOLD slider is locked at first; it opens after your first check or your second hint.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Every question can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved.

<Lang zh>

盒子、罐头、骰子，身边到处都是。它们很多本来是一整块平平的卡纸或铁皮，剪好以后折起来或者卷起来。这节课讲的就是这一块平的东西：立体怎样摊平，平面图形又怎样折回立体。

舞台上现在是一个正方体的展开图，折到了一半。6 个正方形还沿着边连在一起，正往上翻，快要合成正方体。舞台右下角有一个 **FOLD** 滑块：拖动它，或者点一下再按方向键，可以把展开图一直折到合拢，也可以再摊平。旁边 ↺ 和 ↻ 两个按钮能把图形转四分之一圈，让你看到另一边。

全课有 8 步和一章练习。第 01 步认识各种立体和它们的面。第 02 到 05 步打开正方体：先看一种展开图，再看全部 11 种，学会用“折一折”来检验，再找出哪些面折起来相对。第 06、07 步对长方体、棱柱和棱锥做同样的事。第 08 步打开圆柱和圆锥，这一步只要认得它们的展开图。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

每一章都先讲。屏幕底部章节轴上的小圆点就是这一章的拍：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上的图形就会折起、摊开、转动或者换一个，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。

每章最后的小方块是一道小题，从十道左右的题里挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：选一个答案，点几个格子把展开图补完整，在展开图上点一个面，或者填一个数。
- **检查**（C）：补完展开图以后，舞台会把它折起来，让你看看会怎么样。还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按“看答案”，舞台会把答案折给你看。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，一条比一条接近答案。做题时 FOLD 滑块先锁着，第一次检查以后，或者看了第二条提示以后，才能用。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

每道题都能只用点按完成，也能只用键盘完成。不计时，也不保存。
