<Lang en>

You know that a cube has 6 square faces. Now you will open one out and see what it looks like flat.

Start with <Beat n={1}>a cube made of card</Beat>. The line where two faces meet is an <Term id="edge">edge</Term>. Imagine cutting along some of the cube's edges with scissors, but not all of them. With the right cuts, the card can open out and lie flat.

Watch <Beat n={2}>the cube half open</Beat>. The faces stay joined along the edges you did not cut. Each face turns about its edge, like a door turning on its hinge. The dashed outline on the ground shows where each face will lie when it is flat. The bottom face does not move; the others swing down around it.

Now <Beat n={3}>the cube lies flat on the table</Beat>. It has become 6 squares joined edge to edge. This flat shape is a <Term id="net">net</Term> of the cube. Count the squares: there is one for each face, no more and no fewer. Every square shares at least one edge with another square, so the net is all in one piece.

Last, <Beat n={4}>fold it back up</Beat> along the same edges. The 6 squares close up into a cube again, and each square becomes one face. A net is a flat shape that folds up into a solid. This is also why many paper boxes travel flat and are only folded into boxes when they are needed.

Two mistakes are common when you picture a net. A shape with 5 squares, or with 7, cannot be a net of a cube: a cube has exactly 6 faces, so its net has exactly 6 squares. And a net folds only along edges; the squares themselves do not bend or stretch.

You can use the FOLD slider on the stage to fold and unfold the net yourself, as slowly as you like, and the turn buttons to look at it from another side.

When you are ready, <Task id="opening-a-cube">try one question</Task> on what a net folds into.

<Lang zh>

你已经知道，正方体有 6 个正方形的面。现在把它打开，看看它摊平以后是什么样子。

先看<Beat n={1}>一个卡纸做的正方体</Beat>。两个面相接的那条线叫作<Term id="edge">棱</Term>。想象用剪刀沿着正方体的几条棱剪开，但不是每一条都剪。剪对了，卡纸就能打开，平平地摊在桌上。

再看<Beat n={2}>打开了一半的正方体</Beat>。没剪开的棱还连着。每个面绕着自己那条棱转，像门绕着门轴转。地上的虚线是摊平以后每个面要落到的地方。最下面那个面不动，别的面围着它往下翻。

现在<Beat n={3}>正方体平平地摊在桌上</Beat>，变成了 6 个边挨着边的正方形。这个平面图形叫作正方体的<Term id="net">展开图</Term>。数一数：每个面正好一个正方形，不多也不少。每个正方形都至少和另一个共用一条边，所以整个展开图连成一片，没有掉下来的。

最后<Beat n={4}>沿着同样的棱折回去</Beat>，6 个正方形又合成正方体，每个正方形又变回一个面。展开图是能折成立体的平面图形。很多纸盒做好以后先平放着运走，要用的时候才折成盒子，道理就在这里。

想展开图的时候，常有两个地方出错。5 个或 7 个正方形不可能是正方体的展开图：正方体正好有 6 个面，它的展开图就正好有 6 个正方形。还有，展开图只沿着棱折，正方形本身不会弯，也不会被拉长。

舞台上有 FOLD 滑块，你可以自己慢慢地把展开图折起来、再摊开；旁边的两个按钮能把它转过来，从另一边看。

准备好了，就<Task id="opening-a-cube">做一道小题</Task>：看看一个展开图能折成什么立体。
