<Lang en>

Every net of a cube has 6 squares. But do any 6 squares make a net?

Look at <Beat n={1}>these 6 squares</Beat>. Four of them make a 2 by 2 block, and two more carry on in a row. Will it fold into a cube? Counting is not enough. Fold it and look.

Now see <Beat n={2}>the same squares, folded up</Beat>. Two squares land on the same face: the stage draws them with a double line and the words "2 faces here". One face is left open, drawn dashed with the words "nothing here". So this shape is not a net of a cube. A 2 by 2 block always does this. Only 3 faces of a cube meet at a corner, so four squares round one point cannot all fit.

Next, <Beat n={3}>5 squares in a row</Beat>. Five in a row is too many. The first four wrap once round the cube, and the fifth square lands on top of the first.

Then <Beat n={4}>two squares on the same side of a row of 4</Beat>. The row of 4 wraps round, and the two extra squares both fold over the same end. They close that end twice, and the other end stays open.

Last, <Beat n={5}>four shapes side by side</Beat>. They show the quick signs of a shape that is not a net: a 2 by 2 block, 5 in a row, or two squares on the same side of a row of 4. If you see one of these, it is not a net. But some shapes show no sign and still fail. The fourth shape, marked "?", has none of them, yet when it is folded, two of its faces land on each other. So fold to be sure.

The mistake here is to stop at counting. Six squares is the first thing to check, not the last. Fold it and look: a net covers every face of the cube once, and leaves no face open.

When you are ready, <Task id="fold-to-test">try one question</Task>: decide whether a shape folds into a cube, or add squares to make a net.

<Lang zh>

正方体的展开图都有 6 个正方形。那么，随便拿 6 个正方形，都能拼成展开图吗？

先看<Beat n={1}>这 6 个正方形</Beat>。其中 4 个拼成一个 2×2 的方块，另外 2 个接在旁边排成一行。个数是对的，它能折成正方体吗？光数个数不够。折一折，看一看。

再看<Beat n={2}>把它折起来以后</Beat>。有两个正方形叠在同一个面上，舞台用双线画出来，旁边写着“这里有两个面”；另一个面空着，画成虚线，写着“这里空着”。所以它不是正方体的展开图。有 2×2 方块的图形都会这样：正方体的一个角上只碰着 3 个面，4 个正方形围着同一个点，折起来放不下。

接着看<Beat n={3}>一排 5 个正方形</Beat>。一排 5 个太多了。前 4 个绕正方体一圈，第 5 个正好压在第 1 个上。

然后看<Beat n={4}>4 个一排、同一边有两个</Beat>。4 个一排绕一圈，多出来的两个都折到同一头，那一头被盖了两次，另一头却空着。

最后看<Beat n={5}>并排的四个图形</Beat>。它们是“不是展开图”的快速记号：看到 2×2 的方块、一排 5 个、或者 4 个一排的同一边有两个，就不是展开图。可是，有的图形一个记号也没有，照样折不成。第四个图形打着“?”，三个记号它都没有，折起来却有两个面叠在一起。所以要折一折才确定。

这一步容易犯的错，是数完个数就停下。6 个正方形只是第一关，不是最后一关。折一折，看一看：展开图要把正方体的每个面正好盖住一次，没有一个面空着。

准备好了，就<Task id="fold-to-test">做一道小题</Task>：判断一个图形能不能折成正方体，或者补上几个正方形，拼成展开图。
