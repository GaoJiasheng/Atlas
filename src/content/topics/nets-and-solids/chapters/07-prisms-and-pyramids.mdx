<Lang en>

Cubes and cuboids are not the only solids with flat faces. This chapter opens two more kinds.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the triangular prism</Beat>. A <Term id="prism">prism</Term> has two ends that are the same shape and size, joined by rectangles. This triangular prism has 2 triangles and 3 rectangles: 5 faces. Its two ends have the same colour, because they are a pair. A prism's ends can have more sides: if they are pentagons or hexagons, the sides of the prism are still rectangles, one for each side of an end.

Now <Beat n={2}>unfold the prism</Beat>. Its net is 3 rectangles side by side, with a triangle above and below the middle one. The triangles fold in to close the two ends. Count the faces in the net: 5, the same as on the prism.

Next, <Beat n={3}>a square pyramid</Beat>. A <Term id="pyramid">pyramid</Term> has one <Term id="base">base</Term>, and triangles that all meet at one point at the top. That point is a <Term id="vertex">vertex</Term>. This square pyramid has 1 square and 4 triangles: 5 faces.

Then <Beat n={4}>unfold the pyramid</Beat>. Its net is the square with a triangle on each edge, like a star. Fold the triangles up and their tips meet at the top.

Last, compare <Beat n={5}>the two nets side by side</Beat>: a triangular prism's and a triangular pyramid's. Both nets have triangles, so look at what else is there. Rectangles joining two equal ends make a prism. Triangles meeting at one point make a pyramid.

The common mix-up is to see a triangle and say "pyramid" straight away. A prism can have triangles too, at its two ends. If the net has rectangles joining two equal ends, it is a prism.

A triangular pyramid whose 4 faces are all the same size of triangle has only <Num s="S3">2</Num> different nets: a big triangle cut into 4, and a strip of 4 triangles. <Num s="S27">One kind of paper milk carton</Num> was made in this shape.

</More>

<Lang zh>

有平的面的立体，不只正方体和长方体。这一章再打开两类。

先看<Beat n={1}>三棱柱</Beat>。<Term id="prism">棱柱</Term>有两个形状、大小都一样的底，中间用长方形连起来。这个三棱柱有 2 个三角形、3 个长方形，一共 5 个面。它的两个底颜色一样，因为它们是一对。棱柱的底也可以有更多条边：底是五边形、六边形的时候，侧面还是长方形，底有几条边，就有几个长方形。

再<Beat n={2}>把三棱柱展开</Beat>。它的展开图是 3 个并排的长方形，中间那个的上边、下边各接一个三角形。三角形折进去，盖住两头。数一数展开图里的面：5 个，和三棱柱上的一样多。

接着看<Beat n={3}>四棱锥</Beat>。<Term id="pyramid">棱锥</Term>有一个<Term id="base">底面</Term>，三角形的尖都碰在顶上的一个点。这个点是棱锥的<Term id="vertex">顶点</Term>。这个四棱锥有 1 个正方形、4 个三角形，一共 5 个面。

然后<Beat n={4}>把四棱锥展开</Beat>。它的展开图是正方形每条边上接一个三角形，像一颗星。把三角形折起来，尖就在顶上碰到一起。

最后比一比<Beat n={5}>并排的两个展开图</Beat>：一个是三棱柱的，一个是三棱锥的。两个展开图都有三角形，看看还有什么：用长方形把两个一样的底连起来的是棱柱；三角形的尖碰在一个点上的是棱锥。

常见的错误是一看到三角形就说“棱锥”。棱柱也可以有三角形，就在它的两头。展开图里有长方形把两个一样的底连起来，就是棱柱。

<More title={{ en: "Pyramids made of triangles", zh: "全是三角形的棱锥" }}>

4 个面都是一样大的三角形的三棱锥，只有 <Num s="S3">2</Num> 种不同的展开图：一个大三角形分成 4 个，或者 4 个三角形排成一长条。<Num s="S27">有一种纸质牛奶盒</Num>就是做成这个形状的。

</More>
