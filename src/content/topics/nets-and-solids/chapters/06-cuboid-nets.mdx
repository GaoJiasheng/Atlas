<Lang en>

A cube's net is 6 equal squares, so any square can go anywhere. A cuboid's net is made of rectangles of different sizes, and they have to fit together.

<Figure src="figures/nets-and-solids-open-carton" alt={{ en: "Pencil drawing of a tall paper box with square ends, standing upright. Its square lid is still joined to the box along the back top edge and has been turned up and open, so the inside of the box shows.", zh: "一个两端是正方形的高纸盒的铅笔画，竖着放。正方形的盖子还沿着盒口后面那条棱连在盒子上，已经翻起来打开，看得见盒子里面。" }} caption={{ en: "The lid turns about an edge, like every face of a net. An illustration made for this lesson.", zh: "盖子绕着一条棱转，展开图的每个面都是这样折的。为本课画的示意图。" }} />

Look at <Beat n={1}>the cuboid with its pairs in colour</Beat>. Its 6 faces are rectangles in 3 pairs: top and bottom, front and back, left and right. The two faces in a pair are the same size. This cuboid is 3 squares long, 2 squares wide and 1 square high.

Now <Beat n={2}>open it out on squared paper</Beat>. The colours show the pairs: each rectangle has a twin with the same length and breadth. Count the squares along the sides to check. The top and the bottom are 3 by 2, the front and the back are 3 by 1, and the two ends are 1 by 2.

Next, look at <Beat n={3}>the lengths along the folds</Beat>. Rectangles that fold together must share an edge of the same length. The front, 3 by 1, meets the bottom, 3 by 2, along an edge 3 squares long. Each end meets the bottom along an edge 2 squares long.

Then <Beat n={4}>swap in a rectangle that is too short</Beat>. The right end is now only 1 square long. Folded, it covers only half of its side, and a gap is left. The box cannot close, even though the net still has 6 rectangles.

Last, see <Beat n={5}>three different nets of the same cuboid</Beat>. Like a cube, a cuboid has many nets. Any of them works if the rectangles pair up and nothing overlaps when you fold it. The same test helps. Choose a bottom, fold up the sides, then close the top.

A common mistake is to count the rectangles and stop. Six are needed, but they must make three matching pairs, and every edge must meet an edge of the same length.

<More title={{ en: "How many nets?", zh: "一共有多少种？" }}>

When the three lengths of a cuboid are all different, like 2, 3 and 4 squares, people have counted <Num s="S7,S8">54</Num> different nets. A cube, whose lengths are all the same, has only 11.

</More>

<Lang zh>

正方体的展开图是 6 个一样大的正方形，哪个正方形放在哪里都一样。长方体的展开图是大小不同的长方形，它们得配得上才行。

<Figure src="figures/nets-and-solids-open-carton" alt={{ en: "Pencil drawing of a tall paper box with square ends, standing upright. Its square lid is still joined to the box along the back top edge and has been turned up and open, so the inside of the box shows.", zh: "一个两端是正方形的高纸盒的铅笔画，竖着放。正方形的盖子还沿着盒口后面那条棱连在盒子上，已经翻起来打开，看得见盒子里面。" }} caption={{ en: "The lid turns about an edge, like every face of a net. An illustration made for this lesson.", zh: "盖子绕着一条棱转，展开图的每个面都是这样折的。为本课画的示意图。" }} />

先看<Beat n={1}>每一对涂上颜色的长方体</Beat>。它的 6 个面是 3 对长方形：上和下、前和后、左和右。每一对的两个面一样大。这个长方体长 3 格、宽 2 格、高 1 格。

再<Beat n={2}>把它摊在方格纸上</Beat>。同样的颜色是一对：每个长方形都有一个长、宽都一样的“双胞胎”。沿着边数一数格子就能核对：上面和下面都是 3 × 2，前面和后面都是 3 × 1，两头的两个面都是 1 × 2。

接着看<Beat n={3}>折痕两边的长度</Beat>。折在一起的两个长方形，接着的那条边要一样长。前面 3 × 1，底面 3 × 2，它们接在一条 3 格长的边上。两头的面和底面接在 2 格长的边上。

然后<Beat n={4}>换上一个太短的长方形</Beat>。右边那一头现在只有 1 格长。折起来，它只盖住那一面的一半，留下一条缝。虽然还是 6 个长方形，盒子却合不拢。

最后看<Beat n={5}>同一个长方体的三种展开图</Beat>。和正方体一样，长方体也有很多种展开图。只要长方形配成对、折起来不重叠，就行。检验的办法还是那一个：先选一个底，把四周折起来，最后盖上顶。

常见的错误是数完长方形的个数就停下。6 个是要的，可它们还得配成 3 对一样大的，而且每条边都要接上一条一样长的边。

<More title={{ en: "How many nets?", zh: "一共有多少种？" }}>

三条棱都不一样长的长方体，比如 2 格、3 格、4 格，有人数过，一共有 <Num s="S7,S8">54</Num> 种不同的展开图。三条棱都一样长的正方体，只有 11 种。

</More>
