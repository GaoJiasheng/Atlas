## Movement routes

Each route in `movements.json` is digitised as a polyline of real places (town and junction coordinates along the roads, railways, river or sea lane named), traced against the campaign map listed; no straight-line or freehand paths. Land routes were checked to stay on land and the sea route to stay at sea against the base map (`public/geo/land-50m.json`, Natural Earth), except where a route deliberately crosses water (the Johor Strait at Sarimbun). Times are linear between `from` and `to`, so the arrow head is only approximately where the front was on intermediate dates.
