<Lang en>

On the evening of 31 August 1939 SS men staged a fake Polish "attack" on the German radio station at Gleiwitz, leaving behind murdered prisoners dressed in Polish uniforms. Hitler used such incidents as his pretext. Before dawn on 1 September German forces invaded Poland from the west, from East Prussia in the north and from Slovakia in the south: <Num s="S60">55</Num> divisions, about <Num s="S60">1.5 million</Num> men, against <Num s="S60">about 800,000</Num> Polish soldiers. On 3 September Britain and France declared war on Germany.

German aircraft bombed airfields, railways and towns while armoured divisions drove deep behind the Polish lines. Polish units fought hard but were cut off one by one. On 17 September the Soviet Red Army crossed Poland's eastern border, as the secret protocol had foreseen; Polish sources count <Num s="S61">more than 600,000</Num> Soviet soldiers. Poland's president and commander-in-chief fled to Romania. Warsaw, besieged and bombed, surrendered on 27 September with <Num s="S60">140,000</Num> troops, and the last Polish force laid down its arms at Kock on 6 October.

Germany and the Soviet Union divided Poland along the rivers Narew, Bug and San. By their own count the Germans took <Num s="S60">694,000</Num> prisoners and the Soviets <Num s="S61">about 250,000</Num>; German Army losses were <Num s="S60">about 40,000</Num> killed, wounded and missing. Both occupiers set out to destroy Poland's leading classes. In spring 1940 the Soviet secret police shot <Num s="S62">about 22,000</Num> Polish officers and others at Katyn and other sites. Under German rule the heaviest blow fell on Poland's <Num s="S63">3.35 million</Num> Jews.

In the west almost nothing happened. French and British troops waited behind the Maginot Line through the winter, a period that came to be called the "Phoney War". Further east, the Soviet Union invaded Finland in November 1939; the Finns held out until March 1940 and then gave up territory.

<More title={{ en: "Blitzkrieg: tanks, aircraft and radio", zh: "闪电战：坦克、飞机和无线电" }}>

"Blitzkrieg" means "lightning war". The method was to mass tanks and motorised infantry in armoured divisions, break through on a narrow front and keep driving deep, while aircraft attacked airfields, railways and troops, and radio kept the fast columns and the aircraft in touch. In 1939 Germany had <Num s="S58">102</Num> divisions, <Num s="S58">about 3,000</Num> tanks and <Num s="S58">more than 4,000</Num> aircraft. Poland's air force had <Num s="S58">935</Num> aircraft, many of them obsolete, and was destroyed or driven off in the first days; in the Polish Corridor mounted cavalry charged German armour. The same method was used against France in 1940 and the Soviet Union in 1941.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Katyn", zh: "卡廷" }}>

The Soviet Union took tens of thousands of Polish officers, policemen, officials and professionals prisoner in 1939. On 5 March 1940 Stalin and the Politburo approved Beria's proposal to shoot them. In April and May 1940 the NKVD killed them at Katyn forest near Smolensk, at Kalinin (Tver), at Kharkiv and in prisons in the western Ukraine and Belorussia. A secret KGB report of 1959 gives <Num s="S62">21,857</Num> killed, <Num s="S62">4,421</Num> of them at Katyn. German forces found the Katyn graves in 1943; the Soviet Union blamed Germany for the next half-century and admitted responsibility only on 13 April 1990. For <Num s="S62">fifty years</Num> the victims' families could not say openly who had killed them.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Poland's Jews", zh: "波兰的犹太人" }}>

Before the war Poland had the largest Jewish community in Europe: <Num s="S63">3.35 million</Num> people in 1937. Between <Num s="S63">2.77</Num> and <Num s="S63">3 million</Num> of them were murdered in the Holocaust, more than <Num s="S63">four in five</Num>. Non-Jewish Poles also died in very large numbers: the US Holocaust Memorial Museum estimates <Num s="S64">1.8 to 1.9 million</Num> non-Jewish Polish civilians killed under German occupation and in the war. <Lang zh>

1939 年 8 月 31 日晚，党卫队人员在德国格莱维茨电台制造了一起“波兰人袭击”的假事件，现场留下几名穿着波兰军服、已被杀害的囚犯。希特勒拿这类事件当作开战借口。9 月 1 日拂晓前，德军从西面、北面的东普鲁士和南面的斯洛伐克同时入侵波兰，共 <Num s="S60">55</Num> 个师、约 <Num s="S60">150 万</Num>人，对手是<Num s="S60">约 80 万</Num>波兰军人。9 月 3 日，英国和法国对德宣战。

德国飞机轰炸机场、铁路和城镇，装甲师则向波兰防线后方纵深突进。波兰部队顽强抵抗，但一支接一支被分割包围。9 月 17 日，苏联红军按照秘密议定书的安排，越过波兰东部边界；据波方统计，苏军兵力在 <Num s="S61">60 万以上</Num>。波兰总统和总司令逃往罗马尼亚。华沙在围困和轰炸中坚持到 9 月 27 日投降，守军 <Num s="S60">14 万</Num>人；10 月 6 日，最后一支波兰部队在科克放下武器。

德国和苏联沿纳雷夫河、布格河和桑河瓜分了波兰。按各自的统计，德军俘虏 <Num s="S60">69.4 万</Num>人，苏军俘虏<Num s="S61">约 25 万</Num>人；德国陆军伤亡失踪<Num s="S60">约 4 万</Num>人。两个占领者都着手消灭波兰的社会精英。1940 年春，苏联秘密警察在卡廷等地枪杀了<Num s="S62">约 2.2 万</Num>名波兰军官和其他人员。在德国统治下，受打击最重的是波兰的 <Num s="S63">335 万</Num>犹太人。

西线几乎没有动静。法军和英军整个冬天都守在马奇诺防线后面，这段时间后来被称为“假战”。在更远的东北方，苏联 1939 年 11 月入侵芬兰；芬兰人坚持到 1940 年 3 月，最后割让了领土。

<More title={{ en: "Blitzkrieg: tanks, aircraft and radio", zh: "闪电战：坦克、飞机和无线电" }}>

“闪电战”的意思是像闪电一样快的战争。做法是把坦克和摩托化步兵集中编成装甲师，在狭窄的正面突破，然后不停地向纵深推进；同时飞机攻击机场、铁路和部队，无线电让快速纵队和飞机保持联系。1939 年德国有 <Num s="S58">102</Num> 个师、坦克<Num s="S58">约 3,000</Num> 辆、飞机 <Num s="S58">4,000 多</Num>架。波兰空军只有飞机 <Num s="S58">935</Num> 架，许多已经过时，开战头几天就被摧毁或被迫撤离；在波兰走廊，波兰骑兵骑着马向德军装甲部队冲锋。1940 年对法国、1941 年对苏联，德军用的都是同样的方法。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Katyn", zh: "卡廷" }}>

1939 年，苏联俘虏了数以万计的波兰军官、警察、官员和专业人士。1940 年 3 月 5 日，斯大林和政治局批准了贝利亚枪杀他们的提议。1940 年 4 月和 5 月，内务人民委员部在斯摩棱斯克附近的卡廷森林、加里宁（特维尔）、哈尔科夫以及乌克兰西部和白俄罗斯西部的监狱里处决了这些人。1959 年克格勃的一份秘密报告列出被杀者 <Num s="S62">21,857</Num> 人，其中卡廷 <Num s="S62">4,421</Num> 人。1943 年德军发现了卡廷的坟坑；此后半个世纪苏联一直归咎于德国，直到 1990 年 4 月 13 日才承认是自己所为。<Num s="S62">五十年</Num>间，遇难者家属不能公开说出亲人死在谁的手里。

</More>

战前波兰的犹太人社群是欧洲最大的：1937 年有 <Num s="S63">335 万</Num>人。其中 <Num s="S63">277 万</Num>到 <Num s="S63">300 万</Num>人在大屠杀中遇害，超过<Num s="S63">五分之四</Num>。非犹太裔波兰人也大量死亡：美国大屠杀纪念馆估计，在德国占领下和战争中被杀害的非犹太裔波兰平民有 <Num s="S64">180 万至 190 万</Num>人。第 10 章会再讲到集中营，以及大屠杀是怎样实施的。
