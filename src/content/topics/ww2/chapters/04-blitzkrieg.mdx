<Lang en>

On 9 April 1940 Germany occupied Denmark and landed troops at the main ports of Norway, from Oslo to Narvik, the port for Swedish iron ore; a first wave of <Num s="S65">8,850</Num> soldiers came in warships. British, French and Polish troops landed to help the Norwegians and retook Narvik on 28 May, but were withdrawn in early June, and Norway's army laid down its arms on 10 June.

On 10 May Germany attacked the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg, and the best Allied armies moved north into Belgium to meet it. The main German blow came further south, through the wooded Ardennes, which the French command had judged impassable for tanks. Armoured divisions crossed the river Meuse at Sedan on 13 May and reached the Channel coast at Abbeville on 20 May, cutting the Allied armies in two. The Belgian Army surrendered on 28 May.

Between 26 May and 4 June naval and civilian ships carried <Num s="S66">338,226</Num> British and Allied soldiers from Dunkirk to England, <Num s="S66">nearly 100,000</Num> of them from the open beaches. Italy declared war on 10 June, and German troops entered Paris on 14 June. On 22 June France signed an armistice at Compiègne. Germany occupied the north and the Atlantic coast; the rest was ruled from Vichy by Marshal Philippe Pétain's government, which worked with Germany. From London, General Charles de Gaulle called on the French to fight on.

Britain and its empire now fought on alone. From 10 July the German air force tried to win control of the skies over southern England before an invasion. It failed: by 31 October <Num s="S72">about 1,733</Num> German and <Num s="S72">915</Num> British aircraft had been destroyed, and the invasion was postponed. From September German bombers attacked London and other cities by night, the "Blitz", until May 1941.

<More title={{ en: "Why the Maginot Line did not help", zh: "马奇诺防线为什么没起作用" }}>

France had built the Maginot Line of concrete forts along its border with Germany in the 1930s. Allied planners expected that, faced with it, Germany would go round through Belgium and the Netherlands, so the Allied plan was to advance into Belgium. The Ardennes, between the end of the line and the Belgian plain, was held by second-line divisions because the French command believed tanks could not get through it in strength. When the German armour came that way, the forts were not attacked at all. In June the French still had the equivalent of <Num s="S66">thirteen</Num> fortress divisions in the Maginot Line and on the Swiss border while the front further west collapsed.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Vichy and Free France", zh: "维希与自由法国" }}>

On 18 June 1940, before the armistice was signed, General Charles de Gaulle broadcast on the BBC from London, calling on French soldiers, engineers and technicians to join him in continuing the fight. His movement became Free France. On 10 July the French parliament, meeting at Vichy, gave Marshal Pétain full powers by <Num s="S70">569</Num> votes to <Num s="S70">80</Num>, with <Num s="S70">20</Num> abstentions. The Vichy regime worked with Germany and passed its own laws against Jews. The <Num s="S70">80</Num> who voted no are remembered in France today. Until the liberation, French people chose, or were forced into, very different sides.

</More>

1940 年 4 月 9 日，德国占领丹麦，并在挪威从奥斯陆到纳尔维克的各主要港口登陆；纳尔维克是瑞典铁矿石的出海港。第一批 <Num s="S65">8,850</Num> 名德军是由军舰运去的。英国、法国和波兰部队登陆支援挪威，5 月 28 日夺回纳尔维克，但 6 月初就被撤走；6 月 10 日，挪威军队放下武器。

5 月 10 日，德国进攻荷兰、比利时和卢森堡，英法两国最精锐的部队北上进入比利时迎战。可是德军的主攻方向在更南面：穿过森林密布的阿登山区。法军统帅部原以为坦克无法从那里通过。德军装甲师 5 月 13 日在色当渡过默兹河，5 月 20 日在阿布维尔抵达英吉利海峡沿岸，把盟军一切两半。5 月 28 日，比利时军队投降。

5 月 26 日至 6 月 4 日，海军舰艇和民船把 <Num s="S66">338,226</Num> 名英国和盟国士兵从敦刻尔克运回英国，其中<Num s="S66">近 10 万</Num>人是从开阔的海滩上撤走的。6 月 10 日意大利宣战，6 月 14 日德军开进巴黎。6 月 22 日，法国在贡比涅签订停战协定。德国占领北部和大西洋沿岸，其余地区由设在维希、与德国合作的贝当元帅政府管辖。戴高乐将军则在伦敦号召法国人继续战斗。

此后，英国和它的殖民帝国在欧洲独自作战。从 7 月 10 日起，德国空军企图夺取英格兰南部上空的制空权，为登陆做准备。它没有成功：到 10 月 31 日，德国飞机<Num s="S72">约 1,733</Num> 架、英国飞机 <Num s="S72">915</Num> 架被毁，德国推迟了登陆计划。从 9 月起，德国轰炸机夜袭伦敦等城市，这就是一直持续到 1941 年 5 月的“大轰炸”。

<More title={{ en: "Why the Maginot Line did not help", zh: "马奇诺防线为什么没起作用" }}>

1930 年代，法国沿法德边境修建了由混凝土要塞组成的马奇诺防线。盟军的设想是：德国面对防线，会绕道比利时和荷兰，所以盟军计划进入比利时迎战。防线尽头和比利时平原之间的阿登山区只由二线师防守，因为法军统帅部认为大批坦克过不来。德军装甲部队偏偏从那里通过，防线上的要塞根本没有遭到进攻。到 6 月西面战线崩溃时，法军仍有相当于 <Num s="S66">13</Num> 个要塞师的兵力留在马奇诺防线和瑞士边境。
