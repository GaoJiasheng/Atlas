On 6 June 1944 <Num s="S131">156,115</Num> Allied soldiers landed in Normandy by sea and air, and on 25 August Paris was free. In the east the Red Army's summer offensive, Operation Bagration, destroyed <Num s="S134">28</Num> German divisions and cost Germany <Num s="S134">400,000</Num> men. Germany's last attack in the west, in the Ardennes, cost the US Army <Num s="S137">over 89,000</Num> casualties.

On 27 January 1945 Soviet troops reached Auschwitz and found <Num s="S139">about 7,000</Num> prisoners still alive. <Num s="S138">About 1.1 million</Num> people had been killed there, <Num s="S138">about 1 million</Num> of them Jews. The Allies bombed cities too. At Dresden on 13–15 February British and American bombers killed <Num s="S143">up to 25,000</Num> people. In Tokyo on the night of 9–10 March, American B-29s set the city on fire and <Num s="S144">more than 100,000</Num> people died.

The Allies could not hide that an invasion was coming, so they tried to hide where. Operation Fortitude invented a "First US Army Group" of <Num s="S132">11</Num> divisions, <Num s="S132">150,000</Num> men, in south-east England, opposite the Pas de Calais, and put General Patton, whom the Germans rated highly, in command. It existed as radio traffic, inflatable tanks and dummy landing craft. A Spanish double agent, Juan Pujol, codenamed GARBO, fed the Germans reports from <Num s="S132">24</Num> invented sub-agents and, after 6 June, told them that Normandy was a diversion before the real attack. German divisions waited in the Pas de Calais for weeks.

<More title={{ en: "Why the Holocaust is a crime of its own", zh: "为什么说大屠杀是一桩单独的罪行" }}>

Soldiers and civilians die in every war. The Holocaust was different in kind. Jews were not killed because of anything they had done or because they stood in the way of an army, but because the Nazi state had decided that every Jew, of any age and in any country it controlled, should die. The persecution began in Germany in 1933, years before the war; the killing was organised by government offices, with railways, camps and records. About <Num s="S140">2.7 million</Num> Jews were murdered in killing centres and about <Num s="S140">2 million</Num> in mass shootings. In 1944 the lawyer Raphael Lemkin coined the word "genocide" for such crimes, and the United Nations Genocide Convention of 1948 made it a crime in international law: acts committed "with intent to destroy, in whole or in part, a national, ethnical, racial or religious group". Roma and Sinti were also targeted for destruction, at least <Num s="S140">250,000</Num> of them murdered.

<More title={{ en: "Anne Frank", zh: "安妮·弗兰克" }}>

Anne Frank was born in Frankfurt on 12 June 1929. Her family, German Jews, had moved to Amsterdam, and when the Germans began deporting Dutch Jews they went into hiding in rooms behind her father's business on 6 July 1942. Anne kept a diary there. On 4 August 1944 the eight people in hiding were discovered and arrested. Anne and her sister Margot were sent to Auschwitz and then to Bergen-Belsen in Germany, where both died of disease and hunger in February 1945; Anne was <Num s="S142">15</Num>. Of the eight, only her father, Otto, survived.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Burma, the Indian National Army and Singapore", zh: "缅甸、印度国民军与新加坡" }}>

Indian soldiers fought on both sides in Burma. Most served in the British Indian Army. The Japanese offensive at Imphal in 1944 included one division of the Indian National Army, formed in Singapore in 1942 and later led by Subhas Chandra Bose, which hoped to march into India and end British rule. The offensive failed: Japan's Fifteenth Army lost <Num s="S145">53,000</Num> men dead and missing, and the British and Indian defenders <Num s="S145">16,500</Num> casualties. The Fourteenth Army then pushed south, took Mandalay and Meiktila in March 1945 and Rangoon on 3 May. In Singapore, a monument to the Indian National Army's dead was put up on the Esplanade in August 1945; the British pulled it down soon after they returned.

<More title={{ en: "Why the Holocaust is a crime of its own", zh: "为什么说大屠杀是一桩单独的罪行" }}>

每场战争都有军人和平民死去，大屠杀却是另一回事。犹太人被杀，不是因为他们做了什么，也不是因为他们挡了军队的路，而是因为纳粹国家决定：凡是它控制的地方，所有犹太人，不论老少，都要死。迫害从 1933 年就在德国开始，比战争早了好几年；屠杀由政府机关组织，用的是铁路、营地和档案。约 <Num s="S140">270 万</Num>犹太人在灭绝营被杀，约 <Num s="S140">200 万</Num>人死于集体枪杀。1944 年，律师拉斐尔·莱姆金为这样的罪行造了一个词：“灭绝种族”（genocide）；1948 年联合国《防止及惩治灭绝种族罪公约》把它定为国际法上的罪行：“蓄意全部或局部消灭某一民族、人种、种族或宗教团体”的行为。罗姆人和辛提人也是纳粹要消灭的对象，至少 <Num s="S140">25 万</Num>人被杀害。

</More>
