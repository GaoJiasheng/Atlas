<Lang en>

Japanese forces landed in southern Thailand and northern Malaya on 8 December 1941 and reached Singapore in early February 1942; the British commander surrendered the island on 15 February. The map's next keyframe, 9 March 1942, shows the result across the region: Malaya, Singapore, Hong Kong, most of the Netherlands East Indies, most of Luzon and southern Burma under Japanese control.
