In mid-1942 the Axis powers held more territory than ever. Within eight months they were stopped on three continents.

In the Pacific, Japan sent its carriers to take Midway atoll. On 4 June 1942 American carrier aircraft sank all <Num s="S116">four</Num>; Japan lost <Num s="S116">about 3,057</Num> men, the United States <Num s="S116">about 362</Num>. In August US Marines landed on Guadalcanal in the Solomon Islands. In Egypt, Britain's Eighth Army of <Num s="S118">about 195,000</Num> men from Britain and its empire attacked the German and Italian army at El Alamein on 23 October; by 4 November the Axis forces were in retreat. On 8 November <Num s="S120">more than 100,000</Num> American and British troops landed in Morocco and Algeria. In May 1943 <Num s="S119,S120">250,000–275,000</Num> Axis soldiers surrendered in Tunisia.

The largest battle was at Stalingrad on the Volga. From 23 August 1942 German troops fought their way into most of the city, street by street. In China the war went on: the Japanese took the city of Changde in December 1943, and Chinese troops retook it six days later.

<More title={{ en: "Midway: a victory of codebreakers", zh: "中途岛：密码破译员的胜利" }}>

American Navy cryptanalysts had been reading parts of Japan's naval code since early 1942. They knew an attack was coming on a place the Japanese called "AF", but not where AF was. To test their guess, the base at Midway sent out a false message saying it was short of fresh water. Soon a Japanese signal reported that AF was short of water. The codebreakers of Station Hypo in Hawaii could then give the place, the date and the Japanese order of battle, and Admiral Nimitz placed his <Num s="S116">three</Num> carriers north-east of Midway to wait. Japan lost <Num s="S116">four</Num> of the <Num s="S85">six</Num> carriers that had attacked Pearl Harbor.

<More title={{ en: "Pavlov's House", zh: "巴甫洛夫大楼" }}>

At the end of September 1942 a platoon of the Soviet 42nd Guards Regiment seized a four-storey building overlooking a square near the Volga. Its lieutenant was blinded early in the fighting, and Sergeant Yakov Pavlov took command; the building has carried his name ever since. Several civilians sheltering in the basement stayed throughout, and one of them, Mariya Ulyanova, joined the defence. The soldiers broke through the cellar walls to move between positions and cut firing holes for machine-guns and anti-tank rifles. When German tanks came, they went down to the cellar or up to the top floor, where the tank guns could not reach them. The defence lasted <Num s="S122">fifty-eight days</Num>; Vasily Chuikov, the Soviet commander in the city, wrote "<Num s="S122">more than fifty</Num>".

<More title={{ en: "China, 1942–43: the Hump and Changde", zh: "1942–43 年的中国：驼峰航线与常德" }}>

In 1942 Japan's conquest of Burma cut the Burma Road, China's last land route to its allies. From then until November 1945 supplies were flown from India to Kunming over the eastern Himalayas, a route the pilots called "the Hump"; in all <Num s="S125">about 650,000</Num> tons of fuel, munitions and other goods, and people, were carried. The flights were dangerous, and many aircraft and crews were lost.

On the ground China fought on largely alone. On 2 November 1943 Japanese forces attacked in northern Hunan towards Changde. Inside the city, <Num s="S127">8,529</Num> men of the Chinese 57th Division held out against the Japanese 116th Division for <Num s="S127">eleven</Num> days; by the division's report <Num s="S127">7,869</Num> of them were killed or wounded. Changde fell on 3 December and was retaken <Num s="S127">six</Num> days later. Chinese reports of the time recorded Japanese use of poison gas in the campaign.

从 1942 年初起，美国海军的密码分析员已经能读懂部分日本海军密码。他们知道日军要进攻一个代号“AF”的地方，却不确定 AF 是哪里。为了验证猜测，中途岛基地故意发出一份假电报，说岛上淡水短缺。不久，日方电报报告“AF 缺水”。夏威夷“Hypo 站”的破译员由此确认了地点、日期和日军的兵力部署，尼米兹海军上将把手上的 <Num s="S116">3</Num> 艘航母部署在中途岛东北等候。袭击珍珠港的 <Num s="S85">6</Num> 艘日本航母，在这里沉了 <Num s="S116">4</Num> 艘。

<More title={{ en: "Pavlov's House", zh: "巴甫洛夫大楼" }}>

1942 年 9 月底，苏军近卫第 42 团的一个排占领了伏尔加河附近一座广场边的四层楼房。排长在战斗初期双目失明，雅科夫·巴甫洛夫中士接替指挥，这栋楼从此以他的名字命名。几名躲在地下室的平民一直没有离开，其中一位叫玛丽亚·乌里扬诺娃的妇女也参加了守楼。士兵们打通地下室的墙壁，以便在各个阵地之间往来，又在墙上凿出射孔，架起机枪和反坦克枪。德军坦克开来时，他们就躲进地下室或者爬上顶楼，坦克炮打不到那里。这栋楼守了 <Num s="S122">58 天</Num>；在城中指挥苏军的崔可夫写的是“<Num s="S122">五十多天</Num>”。
