<Lang en>

The First World War ended in 1918. Under the Treaty of Versailles (1919) Germany lost territory, had to pay reparations, and was allowed an army of no more than <Num s="S49">100,000</Num> men. Many Germans resented the treaty, and the depression after 1929 brought mass unemployment. On 30 January 1933 Adolf Hitler, leader of the National Socialist (Nazi) party, became Germany's chancellor. Within months he ruled as a dictator, and Germany rearmed in breach of the treaty.

Germany and Japan now sought more territory by force. Japan had occupied north-east China in 1931 and had been at war with China since 1937. In 1936 Germany and Mussolini's Italy announced a Rome–Berlin "axis", and Germany and Japan signed a pact against international communism; these three became the Axis powers. Britain and France, the main powers of the other side, still ruled large empires in Asia and Africa. In Asia, Britain opened a naval base at Singapore in February 1938 to protect them.

In March 1938 German troops marched into Austria, which became part of Germany. Hitler then demanded the German-speaking border areas of Czechoslovakia. At Munich on 29 September Britain, France, Germany and Italy agreed that Czechoslovakia, which was not represented, must hand them over: areas with about <Num s="S50">3.5 million</Num> people, <Num s="S50">700,000</Num> of them Czechs. In March 1939 Germany occupied most of what was left, and Britain and France promised to defend Poland.

On 23 August 1939 Germany and the Soviet Union, bitter ideological enemies, signed a non-aggression pact in Moscow. A secret protocol divided eastern Europe between them, cutting Poland in two along the rivers Narew, Vistula and San. The Soviet Union denied that the protocol existed until 1989. With its eastern border secure, Germany was ready to attack Poland.

<More title={{ en: "Where the word 'Axis' comes from", zh: "“轴心”这个词从哪来" }}>

On 1 November 1936 Benito Mussolini announced that a new understanding between Rome and Berlin formed an "axis". On 25 November Germany and Japan signed the Anti-Comintern Pact against the Communist International. On 27 September 1940 Germany, Italy and Japan signed the Tripartite Pact, a military alliance; Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria and others joined later. The countries fighting the Axis called themselves the Allies, a name taken from the First World War. In August 1939 the Allies were Britain, France and Poland, with the British dominions and colonies; the Soviet Union and the United States joined only in 1941, and China had been fighting Japan alone since 1937.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The pact of 1939: how it is judged", zh: "1939 年的条约：怎样评价" }}>

The Soviet government argued that it had been left alone. Britain and France had made their deal with Hitler at Munich without it, and their talks with Moscow in the summer of 1939 had produced no agreement; the pact, in this view, bought time and a buffer zone. The 1989 resolution of the Congress of People's Deputies accepted that the treaty itself was signed in a critical international situation. Critics point to the secret protocol: it handed half of Poland, the Baltic states and Bessarabia to the Soviet Union, and freed Germany to start the war in the west. The same 1989 resolution condemned the protocol and declared it invalid from the moment of signing. Atlas gives both arguments and does not choose between them.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Singapore in 1939", zh: "1939 年的新加坡" }}>

In 1939 Singapore was part of the Straits Settlements, a British colony; the town had a peacetime population of <Num s="S48">about 550,000</Num>. Work on the naval base at Sembawang began in 1928, and it opened on 14 February 1938 with the King George VI Dock; Singapore was called the "Gibraltar of the East". But no battle fleet was based there. British plans assumed a fleet could be sent from Europe within <Num s="S56">70 days</Num> of war breaking out; in September 1939, with war in Europe, the Chiefs of Staff extended that period to <Num s="S56">180 days</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The world in numbers, 1939", zh: "1939 年的数字" }}>

The world's population around 1940 was <Num s="S57">about 2.3 billion</Num>. Armies differed greatly in size. In 1939 the German Army had <Num s="S58">about 730,000</Num> active soldiers and <Num s="S58">about 1.1 million</Num> trained reservists, <Num s="S58">102</Num> divisions in all, with <Num s="S58">about 3,000</Num> tanks and <Num s="S58">more than 4,000</Num> aircraft. Poland kept <Num s="S58">280,000</Num> men in peacetime and could call up <Num s="S58">1.5 million</Num> reservists; its air force had <Num s="S58">935</Num> aircraft, many of them obsolete. The active United States Army numbered <Num s="S59">about 174,000</Num> enlisted men, fewer than Poland's peacetime army.

</More>

<Lang zh>

第一次世界大战 1918 年结束。根据《凡尔赛条约》（1919），德国割让领土、支付赔款，陆军不得超过 <Num s="S49">10 万</Num>人。许多德国人对条约心怀不满，1929 年后的大萧条又带来大批失业。1933 年 1 月 30 日，纳粹党（国家社会主义德国工人党）领袖阿道夫·希特勒出任德国总理，几个月内就建立了独裁统治，德国随即违反条约重整军备。

德国和日本这时都想用武力扩张领土。日本 1931 年占领中国东北，1937 年起与中国全面开战。1936 年，德国与墨索里尼统治的意大利宣布结成罗马—柏林“轴心”，德国和日本又签订了反对共产国际的协定，这三国后来被称为轴心国。另一方的主要国家英国和法国，当时在亚洲和非洲仍统治着大片殖民地。在亚洲，英国 1938 年 2 月在新加坡启用了海军基地，用来保卫这些殖民地。

1938 年 3 月，德军开进奥地利，奥地利并入德国。希特勒接着要求捷克斯洛伐克交出讲德语的边境地区。9 月 29 日，英、法、德、意四国在慕尼黑商定，捷克斯洛伐克必须交出这些地区，而捷克斯洛伐克没有代表在场。被割走的地区约有 <Num s="S50">350 万</Num>人，其中 <Num s="S50">70 万</Num>是捷克人。1939 年 3 月，德国又占领了捷克斯洛伐克剩下的大部分土地，英法随后承诺保卫波兰。

1939 年 8 月 23 日，意识形态上势不两立的德国和苏联在莫斯科签订互不侵犯条约。一份秘密议定书把东欧划分为两国的势力范围，沿纳雷夫河、维斯瓦河、桑河把波兰一分为二。直到 1989 年，苏联一直否认有这份议定书。东面没有了后顾之忧，德国已经准备好进攻波兰。

<More title={{ en: "Where the word 'Axis' comes from", zh: "“轴心”这个词从哪来" }}>

1936 年 11 月 1 日，墨索里尼宣布罗马和柏林之间的新谅解构成了一条“轴心”。11 月 25 日，德国和日本签订了针对共产国际的《反共产国际协定》。1940 年 9 月 27 日，德、意、日三国签订军事同盟性质的《三国同盟条约》，匈牙利、罗马尼亚、保加利亚等国后来陆续加入。与轴心国作战的国家自称“同盟国”，这个名称沿用自第一次世界大战。1939 年 8 月，同盟国是英国、法国和波兰，以及英国的自治领和殖民地；苏联和美国到 1941 年才加入，而中国从 1937 年起已经独自对日作战。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The pact of 1939: how it is judged", zh: "1939 年的条约：怎样评价" }}>

苏联政府的说法是，它被孤立了：英法在慕尼黑撇开苏联与希特勒做了交易，1939 年夏天英法与苏联的谈判也没有结果；按这种看法，条约为苏联争取了时间和缓冲地带。1989 年苏联人民代表大会的决议也承认，条约本身是在紧张的国际形势下签订的。批评者则指向秘密议定书：它把半个波兰、波罗的海三国和比萨拉比亚划给苏联，也让德国可以放手在西面开战。同一份 1989 年的决议谴责了这份议定书，宣布它自签字之时起无效。本书列出两种看法，不作取舍。

</More>

1939 年，新加坡属于英国殖民地海峡殖民地；和平时期市区人口<Num s="S48">约 55 万</Num>。三巴旺海军基地 1928 年动工，1938 年 2 月 14 日连同乔治六世船坞一起启用，新加坡因此被称为“东方直布罗陀”。但基地里并没有驻扎战列舰队。英国的计划假定开战后 <Num s="S56">70 天</Num>内可以从欧洲派来舰队；1939 年 9 月欧洲开战后，参谋长委员会把这一期限延长到 <Num s="S56">180 天</Num>。
