<Lang en>

Roads take land, and a small island cannot keep adding them. In 1973 several bus companies were merged into Singapore Bus Service. On 2 June 1975 the government began the Area Licensing Scheme: most cars entering the city centre in the morning rush had to show a paid licence; buses, taxis, goods vehicles and cars carrying <Num s="S157">four</Num> people were let through free. Charging for the busiest streets was a way to share road space without widening every road.

Travel beyond the island needed land too. Changi Airport opened on 1 July 1981 at the eastern tip, partly on land reclaimed from the sea, and took over civilian flights from Paya Lebar. An airport needs far more room than its terminal: runways, taxiways and roads all take space.

On 7 November 1987 the first Mass Rapid Transit trains carried passengers between Toa Payoh and Yio Chu Kang, <Num s="S154">five</Num> stations apart. By 1990 the two first lines reached Boon Lay, Pasir Ris and Marina Bay. In the city the trains run underground and outside it mostly on viaducts, so a railway uses little land at street level.

The MRT grew from <Num s="S156">67</Num> kilometres of line in 1990 to <Num s="S156">242.5</Num> kilometres in 2024. Light rail loops link homes in Bukit Panjang, Sengkang and Punggol with MRT stations. Since May 1990 a person buying a car needs a Certificate of Entitlement, and from 1 April 1998 Electronic Road Pricing began charging drivers for using busy roads at busy times, first on the East Coast Parkway and from September 1998 in the city.

<More title={{ en: "Three ways to manage traffic", zh: "管理交通的三种办法" }}>

The Area Licensing Scheme charged for **entering an area**: a paper licence bought for the day and displayed in the car. The Certificate of Entitlement controls **owning a vehicle**: only a set number of new vehicles can be registered, and buyers bid for the right. Electronic Road Pricing charges for **using a road**: gantries over the road detect a unit fitted in every vehicle and take the charge from a card in it. All three try to keep traffic moving on a fixed amount of road.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Opening day and first passengers", zh: "开幕日与第一批乘客" }}>

The first MRT trains carried passengers on 7 November 1987, but the system's official opening was on 12 March 1988. This map uses the day each stretch first carried passengers, so the first lines are already on the map before the ceremony.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Light rail in the new towns", zh: "新镇里的轻轨" }}>

Light rail trains are small and run on their own tracks above the streets. The first, in Bukit Panjang, opened in 1999; Sengkang followed in 2003 and Punggol in 2005. Later stations were added to loops that were already running, so a loop's first day does not date every stop on it. The light rail lines are not counted in the MRT length above.

</More>

<Lang zh>

道路要占地，小岛不能一直加路。1973 年，几家巴士公司合并成新加坡巴士服务有限公司。1975 年 6 月 2 日，政府推行区域执照计划：早上交通高峰时进入市中心的汽车，大多要出示付费买来的通行证；巴士、德士、货车和载满 <Num s="S157">4</Num> 人的汽车可以免费通过。对最拥挤的街道收费，是不必把每条路都拓宽也能分配路面的办法。

往来岛外也需要土地。1981 年 7 月 1 日，樟宜机场在岛的东端启用，一部分建在填海得来的土地上，民航班机也从巴耶利峇搬到这里。机场需要的地远比航站楼大：跑道、滑行道和道路都要占地。

1987 年 11 月 7 日，第一段地铁载客通车，从大巴窑到杨厝港，共 <Num s="S154">5</Num> 个站。到 1990 年，最早的两条线已经通到文礼、巴西立和滨海湾。地铁在市区走地下，出了市区多半走高架，所以铁路在地面上占的地不多。

地铁线从 1990 年的 <Num s="S156">67</Num> 公里，增加到 2024 年的 <Num s="S156">242.5</Num> 公里。武吉班让、盛港和榜鹅还有轻轨，把住家和地铁站连起来。1990 年 5 月起，买车要先有拥车证；1998 年 4 月 1 日起，电子道路收费在繁忙时段向使用繁忙道路的车辆收费，先在东海岸公园大道实施，同年 9 月扩大到市区。

<More title={{ en: "Three ways to manage traffic", zh: "管理交通的三种办法" }}>

区域执照计划管的是**进入一个地区**：一张当天买来、放在车上让人看见的纸质通行证。拥车证管的是**拥有车辆**：每段时间只能登记一定数量的新车，买车的人要竞标这个权利。电子道路收费管的是**使用道路**：路上的闸门感应每辆车上装的车内单元，从插在里面的卡扣费。三种办法都是为了让有限的道路保持通畅。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Opening day and first passengers", zh: "开幕日与第一批乘客" }}>

地铁在 1987 年 11 月 7 日第一次载客，正式开幕却是 1988 年 3 月 12 日。这张地图用的是每一段第一次载客的日子，所以开幕典礼之前，最早的线路已经出现在地图上。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Light rail in the new towns", zh: "新镇里的轻轨" }}>

轻轨列车比较小，在高出街道的专用轨道上行驶。第一条在武吉班让，1999 年通车；盛港 2003 年、榜鹅 2005 年接着通车。有些车站是后来才加进已经在运行的环线里的，所以一条环线的通车日，不等于线上每个车站的启用日。上面的地铁长度不包括轻轨。

</More>
