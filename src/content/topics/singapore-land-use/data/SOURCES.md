# Sources · Singapore land use

English chapter drafts, numbered evidence and map provenance for a draft topic. Chinese fields marked 【待译】 retain the English draft. Existing reservoir names, summaries and source texts are copied from `four-taps` and retain its bilingual wording.

Every geometry source, licence, processing method, error budget and omission is recorded in `scripts/geo/singapore-land-use/SOURCES-GEO.md`. Dataset receipts include download timestamps and SHA-256 hashes in `raw/fetch-log.json`. Supplemental fact checks and quotations are in `../RESEARCH.md`; evidence identifiers retain the earlier research ledger's identifiers where available. Text-only sources do not grant permission to reproduce their maps or photographs.

## Content cautions

- HDB's historical population chart changes from total-population to resident-population denominators. Chapter 02 keeps the distinction explicit.
- The Land Acquisition Bill passed in October 1966; the Act took effect in June 1967. Chapter 02's first beat was moved to the supported approval date.
- Home ownership began on 12 February 1964, correcting the brief's first-of-month beat.
- Queenstown's early blocks: indexed NHB text supports October 1960 completion and 1961 handover; direct-page bot protection prevented a fresh full-page read.
- The cause of the Bukit Ho Swee fire remains unknown. Loh's original pages on rumours and official responses were not accessible; specific rumours are not asserted.
- Jurong Island opening, reclamation completion and individual project phases are separate milestones.
- Current 90% State-land ownership, precise indoor-farm site coordinates, Jurong West's first development year and some estate dates remain unverified; no unsupported exact claim is used.
- Policy markers use representative city/island coordinates, not invented office or ceremony locations. Site-marker map dates are labelled when a founding date is unknown.
