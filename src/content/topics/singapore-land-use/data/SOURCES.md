# Sources · Singapore land use

Numbered sources, map provenance and the rail register for a draft topic. The historian review of 20 October 2026 (Opus) rewrote the English chapters, wrote all the Chinese and checked every layer; its ledger is [AUDIT.md](../AUDIT.md) and the passages awaiting Gavin are in [REVIEW.md](../REVIEW.md). Reservoir names, summaries and source texts are copied from `four-taps` unchanged.

Every geometry source, licence, processing method, error measure and omission is recorded in `scripts/geo/singapore-land-use/SOURCES-GEO.md`. Dataset receipts (download times, SHA-256 hashes) are in `scripts/geo/singapore-land-use/fetch-receipts.json`. Codex's research register is `../RESEARCH.md`. Text-only sources do not grant permission to reproduce their maps or photographs.

## Movement routes

Rail segments follow the open URA MP2019 line geometry, split at station footprints and dated by first passenger service. Rail line names use the transport entity colour rather than official logos or brand colours. The full dated route register, including endpoints, source URLs and precision limitations, is in `scripts/geo/singapore-land-use/rail-report.md`. Planned alignments alone are not treated as evidence that service had opened.

## Content cautions

- HDB's share-of-population chart counts the whole population for 1960 and 1965 and residents from 1970; chapter 02's More block says so.
- The Bukit Ho Swee fire's cause is unknown (NLB, NHB). The rumours of arson and the government's response are told as accounts, with their sources (S55, S221, S231); Loh Kah Seng's book itself was not read.
- Land acquisition, resettlement, the pig-farm phase-out and sand imports are each told from two sides with sources for both.
- “About 90 per cent State land today” is not used: no official source was found. The text uses CLC's 44 per cent (1960) to over 85 per cent (2007).
- Policy markers (home ownership, COE, Food Story 2, land acquisition) use a city or island point, not an invented office or ceremony site; the event details say so.
- Sembawang and Changi airfields carry the 1959 map date, not an opening date.

## Geographic acceptance status

The 1959 trace is approximate: it was georeferenced on a 1920-pixel preview with 16 constructed graticule points, not independent ground controls. The satellite coasts follow a fixed method; along unchanged shores their median deviation is 19–49 m, but the 95th percentile is 113–268 m, above the spec's targets, and tides were not matched. The display coast is simplified at 50 m.
