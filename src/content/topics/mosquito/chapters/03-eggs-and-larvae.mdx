<Lang en>

Look just above the waterline. The female lays her eggs one by one on the damp wall of the container, not on the water. Each egg is less than <Num s="S22">1 mm</Num> long, and it turns black soon after it is laid. In a laboratory study at <Num s="S21">28 °C</Num>, a larva took about <Num s="S21">62 hours</Num> to form inside a freshly laid egg. After that the egg can wait, dry, for months; it hatches when rising water covers it.

<Figure src="figures/mosquito-water-stages" alt={{ en: "A cut-away pot plate of still water: a band of small dark eggs on the wall above the waterline, larvae of different sizes hanging head down below the surface, and two comma-shaped pupae.", zh: "剖开的花盆底盘里有积水：水面上方的壁上有一圈深色的小卵，水面下倒挂着大小不一的孑孓，还有两只逗号形的蛹。" }} caption={{ en: "In a real container every stage is mixed together: eggs on the wall, larvae of all sizes and pupae in the same water.", zh: "真实的容器里，各个阶段混在一起：壁上有卵，同一处积水里有各种大小的孑孓和蛹。" }} />

The young mosquito that hatches is a larva. It is not a worm: it has a head, a thorax and an abdomen, though no legs. It hangs head down below the surface and breathes through a short tube at its tail end, the <Term id="siphon">siphon</Term>, whose tip reaches the air. The cool-coloured dots are air going in.

<Figure src="figures/mosquito-larva" alt={{ en: "A fourth-stage mosquito larva hanging head down from the water surface: a round head with mouth brushes, a broad thorax with tufts of hair, a slim abdomen of eight segments and a short dark siphon at the surface.", zh: "一只四龄孑孓头朝下倒挂在水面下：圆圆的头上有口刷，宽宽的胸两侧有毛丛，细长的腹部有八节，尾端短而深色的呼吸管伸到水面。" }} caption={{ en: "A larva in its fourth instar: head, thorax and abdomen, with the siphon at the surface.", zh: "一只四龄孑孓：头、胸、腹，呼吸管伸到水面。" }} />

Brushes beside its mouth sweep tiny bits of food and microbes out of the water; the green dots are food. Larvae eat, and that is how they grow.

Its hard head capsule cannot get bigger, so a larva grows in steps. It sheds its old skin in a <Term id="moult">moult</Term>, and the new head capsule is wider; the softer body grows between moults too. A larva passes through <Num s="S13">four</Num> stages, called <Term id="instar">instars</Term>, before it becomes a pupa, and the shed skins float beside it. A grasshopper also grows by moulting: see <TopicLink slug="grasshopper" ch="nerves-and-growing-up">Nerves, senses and growing up</TopicLink>.

In the try-it card, change the day and keep the water at 28 °C. In this model the larva reaches its fourth instar on day <Num s="S1">4</Num> after hatching. Record its head width each day: the graph climbs in steps, one for each moult.

<More title={{ en: "Eggs that wait", zh: "会等待的卵" }}>

A freshly laid egg cannot yet survive drying out; it becomes able to as the larva forms inside it. After that, NEA says, eggs can survive dry for up to about <Num s="S4">nine months</Num>; CDC says up to <Num s="S19">8 months</Num>. So tipping out the water leaves the eggs on the wall: the wall has to be scrubbed as well. Some other mosquitoes, such as Culex, lay their eggs stuck together in a raft that floats on the water instead.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Growing in steps", zh: "一步一步长大" }}>

In one measured sample of larvae, the heads of the four instars were <Num s="S29">0.24 mm</Num>, <Num s="S29">0.39 mm</Num>, <Num s="S29">0.64 mm</Num> and <Num s="S29">0.99 mm</Num> wide on average, and the larvae <Num s="S29">1.7–7.2 mm</Num> long. The larvae in the dish are drawn to these sizes. They were a different sample from the timed larvae the day counts come from, so the try-it puts the two together as a model. A small adult mosquito is not a young one: once it has come out of the pupa, an adult does not grow.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where the days come from", zh: "天数从哪里来" }}>

The days come from one laboratory study of male Aedes aegypti reared one to a well at <Num s="S16">28 °C</Num>: from hatching to adult took <Num s="S16">7.23 days</Num> on average. NEA says that in good conditions the larval stage lasts about <Num s="S4">four days</Num>, without giving a temperature. Real larvae vary: females are a little slower than males, and food and crowding matter too.

</More>

孵出来的小蚊子就是孑孓。它不是蠕虫：它有头、胸、腹，只是没有足。它头朝下倒挂在水面下，用尾端一根短短的<Term id="siphon">呼吸管</Term>吸气，管口伸出水面。冷色的小点就是吸进去的空气。

<Figure src="figures/mosquito-larva" alt={{ en: "A fourth-stage mosquito larva hanging head down from the water surface: a round head with mouth brushes, a broad thorax with tufts of hair, a slim abdomen of eight segments and a short dark siphon at the surface.", zh: "一只四龄孑孓头朝下倒挂在水面下：圆圆的头上有口刷，宽宽的胸两侧有毛丛，细长的腹部有八节，尾端短而深色的呼吸管伸到水面。" }} caption={{ en: "A larva in its fourth instar: head, thorax and abdomen, with the siphon at the surface.", zh: "一只四龄孑孓：头、胸、腹，呼吸管伸到水面。" }} />

嘴边的口刷把水里细小的食物颗粒和微生物扫进嘴里；绿色的小点就是食物。孑孓要吃东西，才能长大。

孑孓坚硬的头壳长不大，所以它一步一步地长。它脱掉旧皮，这叫<Term id="moult">蜕皮</Term>，新的头壳就宽一些；在两次蜕皮之间，柔软的身体也会长。孑孓要经过 <Num s="S13">四</Num>个阶段，叫做四个<Term id="instar">龄期</Term>，才变成蛹；蜕下的皮就漂在它旁边。蝗虫也靠蜕皮长大，见<TopicLink slug="grasshopper" ch="nerves-and-growing-up">神经、感觉与成长</TopicLink>。

在试一试卡片里改变天数，水温保持 28 °C。在这个模型里，孑孓到孵化后第 <Num s="S1">4</Num> 天就是第四龄了。每天记下它的头宽：图上的线一级一级往上走，每蜕一次皮升一级。

<More title={{ en: "Eggs that wait", zh: "会等待的卵" }}>

刚产下的卵还不耐干；里面的幼虫慢慢成形，卵才变得耐干。之后，据环境局说，卵能干着活长达约 <Num s="S4">九个月</Num>；美国疾病控制与预防中心说长达 <Num s="S19">8 个月</Num>。所以只把水倒掉，壁上的卵还在：壁也要刷干净。别的一些蚊子，比如库蚊，会把卵粘成一块，像小筏子一样浮在水面上。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Growing in steps", zh: "一步一步长大" }}>

在一份测量样本里，四个龄期的孑孓头宽平均是 <Num s="S29">0.24 毫米</Num>、<Num s="S29">0.39 毫米</Num>、<Num s="S29">0.64 毫米</Num>和 <Num s="S29">0.99 毫米</Num>，体长 <Num s="S29">1.7–7.2 毫米</Num>。皿里的孑孓就按这些大小画。这批孑孓和用来计算天数的那批不是同一批，所以试一试是把两者放在一起做的模型。小的成蚊并不是“小时候”的蚊子：从蛹里出来以后，成虫就不再长大了。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where the days come from", zh: "天数从哪里来" }}>

天数来自一项实验室研究：雄性埃及伊蚊一只一格分开养，水温 <Num s="S16">28 °C</Num>，从孵化到成虫平均用了 <Num s="S16">7.23 天</Num>。环境局说，条件适宜时幼虫期约 <Num s="S4">四天</Num>，但没有说是什么水温。真实的孑孓有快有慢：雌的比雄的稍慢，食物和拥挤程度也有影响。

</More>
