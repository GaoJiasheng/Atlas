Here the water has been tipped out of the dish. The larvae and pupae are left on the bottom; they can live only in water, so they die before they become adults. But in this model the dish was left for <Num s="S1">14 days</Num>, longer than the <Num s="S1">7.2 days</Num> the young need at <Num s="S1">28 °C</Num>, so some adults had already come out and flown away. The eggs on the wall survive dry, so tipping out the water is not enough: the wall has to be scrubbed as well. Use the try-it card to change how often the water is tipped out, and whether the wall is scrubbed.

Dengue is spread mainly by infected female Aedes mosquitoes. Its signs include a fever, a bad headache, pain behind the eyes, pain in the muscles and joints, feeling sick or vomiting, and a rash. Many people have only a mild illness, but some become very ill. If you feel unwell, tell an adult; a doctor can check whether it is dengue.

Places at home that hold still water are <Term id="breeding-habitat">breeding habitats</Term>. In 2025 the ones NEA found most often in homes were pails, flowerpot plates and vases. NEA's <Term id="mozzie-wipeout">Mozzie Wipeout</Term> has five steps, B-L-O-C-K:

- **B**: Break up hardened soil
- **L**: Lift and empty flowerpot plates
- **O**: Overturn pails and wipe their rims
- **C**: Change water in vases
- **K**: Keep roof gutters clear and place BTI insecticide inside

BTI is an insecticide that acts on mosquito larvae in water. Do these steps with an adult, and tell an adult whenever you see still water; climbing up to a gutter and handling insecticide are jobs for adults. NEA asks for the Mozzie Wipeout at least <Num s="S41">once a week</Num>.

Singapore also releases male Aedes aegypti that carry <Term id="wolbachia">Wolbachia</Term> bacteria. Males do not bite. When they mate with females that do not carry the same Wolbachia, the eggs do not hatch, so fewer mosquitoes grow up. This works alongside clearing still water; it does not replace it.

The signs listed above are the ones both the World Health Organization and Singapore's Ministry of Health give. Only a doctor can tell whether an illness is dengue.

Alongside B-L-O-C-K, NEA asks people to protect themselves with S-A-W: Spray insecticide in dark corners around the house; Apply insect repellent regularly; Wear long-sleeve tops and long pants. Insecticide sprays are for adults to use.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Clean water counts too", zh: "干净的水也会滋生" }}>

NEA says Aedes prefers to breed in clean, still water, so clear water in a vase or a plate needs as much care as dirty water. For flowerpot plates and vases, NEA's checklist asks for the water to be changed <Num s="S42">every other day</Num>, and the inside of the vase scrubbed to remove eggs.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "How Wolbachia works, and why “regularly”", zh: "沃尔巴克氏菌怎样起作用，为什么要“定期”" }}>

In the areas where these males are released, NEA reports <Num s="S11">80–90 %</Num> fewer Aedes aegypti and a dengue risk over <Num s="S11">70 %</Num> lower. A large trial in Singapore found that the releases protected people against dengue by <Num s="S12">71–72 %</Num>. These results are for the release areas, not for every home in Singapore, and the method targets only Aedes aegypti.

Why check often? In this model, at <Num s="S1">28 °C</Num>, a larva becomes an adult in about <Num s="S1">7.2 days</Num>, and at <Num s="S1">32 °C</Num> in <Num s="S1">6.4 days</Num>. Real mosquitoes vary: some grow a little faster than the average, so waiting until just before a week is up is cutting it fine.

骨痛热症主要由受感染的雌性伊蚊传播。它的征兆有：发烧、头痛得厉害、眼睛后面痛、肌肉和关节痛、恶心或呕吐，还有皮疹。许多人病得很轻，但也有人病得很重。觉得不舒服，要告诉大人；医生会检查是不是骨痛热症。

家里会积水的地方，就是<Term id="breeding-habitat">滋生处</Term>。2025 年，环境局在住家里最常发现伊蚊滋生的地方，是水桶、花盆底盘和花瓶。环境局的<Term id="mozzie-wipeout">灭蚊行动</Term>有五个步骤，叫 B-L-O-C-K：

- **B**：铲松干硬的泥土
- **L**：清除花盆底盘的积水
- **O**：倒置存放水桶并擦干水桶边缘
- **C**：更换花瓶里的水
- **K**：疏通阻塞的屋顶檐槽并放置BTI杀蚊药

BTI 是一种对付水里孑孓的杀蚊药。和家里的大人一起做这些步骤，看到积水就告诉大人；爬高清理檐槽、使用杀虫药，都是大人做的事。环境局建议至少 <Num s="S41">每星期一次</Num>进行灭蚊行动。
