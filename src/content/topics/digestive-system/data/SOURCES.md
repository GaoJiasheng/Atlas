# The digestive system — sources

P4 scope follows the MOE Science syllabus [S2]: five body systems and the functions of the mouth, gullet, stomach, small intestine and large intestine. Liver, gallbladder and pancreas are Further study / 再进一步. The chicken-rice story follows the destination and changing state of a meal; it does not rate a dish's nutrition. Physiology follows OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e [S3–S8], checked against NIDDK [S9]; health advice follows Singapore HPB [S13, S14, S19]. Figures supplied with the brief are Atlas teaching illustrations, not copied OpenStax or Gray plates.

Every quantitative statement in chapter prose is wrapped in `<Num s="S#">`; numbers in data descriptions cite `[S#]`. Textbook values describe adult reference anatomy, not measurements of a P4 pupil or this mesh. Demonstration durations, particle counts, rendering scales and contraction phases are design values [S1], not physiological readings. No disease diagnosis, prescribed intake or quiz is included.

## Model limitations and teaching conventions

- The supplied inventory has no separately named ileum, caecum, rectum or anal canal/anus. These names remain part of the textual explanation; the 3D index contains only actual model parts. The sigmoid is not renamed as a rectum. No urinary or reproductive structure fills the missing sections.
- Jejunum is an actual coiled source tube. Its derived centreline is about 2.0191 m, a mesh quantity rather than a physiological estimate. It must not be called the full living small intestine or a separately identified ileum.
- The pipeline reports 13 jejunal flow segments crossing overlapping neighbouring source walls. Flow paths omit neighbourhoods around those stations; gaps remain visible and the source defect is not described as a sealed, unobstructed lumen.
- Other source endpoint gaps are preserved between segments. Appendix is a blind side branch and is excluded from the food route. Source partial wall windows and open surfaces are not repaired or declared watertight by the content.
- The supplied dental meshes contain 28 teeth without third molars, not a complete 32-tooth adult dentition. Left and right salivary-gland nodes each merge three actual major glands.
- Particle routes through the mouth, salivary branches, helper-organ branches and absorption branches are schematic, anchored to the reference meshes. Separate centrelines are not joined through missing anatomy. Directions and colour changes explain movement and changing contents, not measured flow rates.
- Staggered whole-organ contraction pulses provide the supported fallback. They do not reproduce a local travelling constriction. Their visual timing is independent of the sourced transit ranges.
- The blood absorption branch represents nutrients that enter blood directly. Further study explains that most absorbed dietary fat first travels through lymph; the short branch does not represent every nutrient route.
- Surface-area estimates are reported together: OpenStax [S6] gives about 200 m² for the small-intestine surface; Helander and Fändriks [S15] estimate about 32 m² for total adult digestive mucosa, including about 2 m² in the large intestine. They use different scope and estimation methods; neither number is inferred from this model.

## Verification ledger

Verified for the displayed specification rows: adult living mouth-to-anus tube about 7.6 m [S3]; whole transit typically 24–72 hours [S5]; adult stomach can stretch to about 4 L [S5], an upper expansion figure rather than an ordinary serving size. Typical timings vary by person and meal. Stomach emptying 2–4 hours [S5], small-intestine passage 3–5 hours and large-intestine residence 12–24 hours [S6] are separate typical intervals, not exact boundaries to add into a meal clock.

The draft spec's unverified empty-stomach volume, oesophageal wave velocity, gastric-juice daily yield, absolute stomach contraction rate, liver mass, pancreatic-juice range, and meal-dependent gastric-pH readout were not adopted. No unverified number from these proposals is displayed. Gray's archive catalogue was verified, but its scan/OCR did not load in this pass; no exact Gray figure number is asserted. OpenStax A&P 2e's currently stated licence was verified as CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. MedlinePlus is an organ-name index, not a quantitative physiology source. Biology 2e's broad cross-check does not override the more specific A&P sections; its wording about liver enzymes is not repeated.
