<Lang en>

Here the digestive organs are shown on their own inside the body outline. Follow them in order and they make one long tube. Food goes in at the mouth, down the <Term id="gullet">gullet</Term> into the stomach, then through the <Term id="small-intestine">small intestine</Term> and the <Term id="large-intestine">large intestine</Term>. What is left leaves the body through the anus. These five parts, mouth, gullet, stomach, small intestine and large intestine, are the ones the P4 syllabus asks you to know.

In a living adult the whole tube is about <Num s="S3">7.6 m</Num> long, more than four times the height of the body. It fits because most of it is folded and coiled. The small intestine alone is about <Num s="S6">3 m</Num> long and about <Num s="S6">2.5 cm</Num> wide. The large intestine is about three times as wide, about <Num s="S6">7.6 cm</Num>, but only about half as long. So "small" and "large" describe how wide the tubes are, not how long.

The tube has a fixed order and a fixed place. The gullet runs down the chest behind the windpipe and heart. The stomach sits on the body's left, under the ribs. The small intestine fills the middle of the abdomen, and the large intestine runs round it like a frame. On the stage the model faces you, so its left side is on your right. <FlyTo preset="abdomen">Look at the abdomen</FlyTo>


<More title={{ en: "Longer after death", zh: "死后量得更长" }}>

Measured after death, the same tube is about <Num s="S3">10.7 m</Num> long, because its muscles have relaxed. In life the muscles in its wall keep it shorter. The model's coils are about <Num s="S1">2 m</Num> long, shorter than a living small intestine: the model shows where the organs are and what shape they are, not their exact length.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Try it: coil a long tube", zh: "试一试：把长管盘起来" }}>

Measure <Num s="S3">7.6 m</Num> of string along a corridor. Then coil it loosely into a school bag. The string shows how a long tube can fit into a small space by folding; it does not show how thick the gut is.

</More>

<Lang zh>

这里只显示身体轮廓里的消化器官。按顺序看，它们连成一条长长的管子。食物从口进入，沿<Term id="gullet">食道</Term>到达胃，再经过<Term id="small-intestine">小肠</Term>和<Term id="large-intestine">大肠</Term>，剩下的东西从肛门排出体外。口、食道、胃、小肠、大肠，就是小四课纲要求认识的五个部分。

成人活体的整条消化道长约 <Num s="S3">7.6 米</Num>，相当于身高的四倍多。它大部分折叠、盘绕着，所以能装进身体里。单是小肠就长约 <Num s="S6">3 米</Num>，直径约 <Num s="S6">2.5 厘米</Num>。大肠粗得多，直径约 <Num s="S6">7.6 厘米</Num>，长度却只有小肠的一半左右。所以“小”和“大”说的是管子的粗细，不是长短。

这条管子的顺序和位置都是固定的。食道在气管和心脏后面，沿胸腔向下；胃在身体左侧的肋骨下面；小肠占满腹部中间，大肠像一个框，围在小肠外面。舞台上的模型面对着你，所以它的左边在你的右手边。<FlyTo preset="abdomen">看看腹部</FlyTo>
