<Lang en>

The digestive organs form a continuous tube through the body. Food enters at the mouth, passes down the <Term id="gullet">gullet</Term> into the stomach, then enters the <Term id="small-intestine">small intestine</Term> and the <Term id="large-intestine">large intestine</Term>. Undigested material eventually leaves through the outlet. The model shows the route inside a body outline, rather than spreading the organs flat on a page.

The gullet runs down the chest. The stomach lies mainly towards the body's left, below the ribs. The small intestine coils through the abdomen, and the wider large intestine forms a frame around much of it. Here, left and right always mean the person's own left and right. Facing the model reverses them on your screen.

In a living adult, the entire digestive tube is about <Num s="S3">7.6 m</Num> long. It fits inside the body because much of it is folded and coiled. This is a typical textbook length, not a measurement of this particular model or of a child's body. Small intestine means narrower, not shorter.

<More title={{ en: "A continuous body, an incomplete model", zh: "身体连续，模型并不完整" }}>

After death, relaxed muscles can make the tube measure about <Num s="S3">10.7 m</Num>. This model omits some connecting sections. Dots disappear at those gaps; the real digestive tube is continuous. The coiled mesh is not a ruler for physiological length.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Try it: coil a long tube", zh: "试一试：把长管盘起来" }}>

Lay out about <Num s="S3">7.6 m</Num> of string, then loosely coil it inside a bag. Compare the space occupied before and after coiling. The string illustrates packing, not the thickness of the intestine.

</More>

<Lang zh>

消化器官连成穿过身体的长管。食物从口进入，沿<Term id="gullet">食道</Term>到达胃，再进入<Term id="small-intestine">小肠</Term>和<Term id="large-intestine">大肠</Term>，没有被消化吸收的剩余物最后从出口离开。舞台把器官放在身体轮廓里，能看到它们的位置关系；书上的平面图常把管子拉开，方便读者看清顺序。

食道沿胸部向下伸。胃主要在身体左侧、肋骨下面；小肠盘绕在腹部，大肠较粗，围在小肠的外侧。这里的左右都以模型本人的身体为准。面对模型时，身体的左边在屏幕右侧。不要根据画面哪边较亮来判断左右。

成人活体的整条消化道长约 <Num s="S3">7.6 米</Num>。它大部分折叠、盘绕着，所以能容纳在身体里面。这是课本给出的典型长度，不是这个模型的实测值，也不是儿童身体的长度。“小肠”的“小”指管径较小，不是长度较短。沿着管道认顺序，比只记器官的颜色更有用。

<More title={{ en: "A continuous body, an incomplete model", zh: "身体连续，模型并不完整" }}>
