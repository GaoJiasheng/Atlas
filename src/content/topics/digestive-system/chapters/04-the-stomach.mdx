<Lang en>

At the bottom of the gullet the food enters the stomach, a J-shaped bag of muscle under the left ribs. Empty, it is about the size of a fist, with its lining in deep folds. As a meal arrives the folds flatten out and the stomach stretches; it can hold up to about <Num s="S5">4 litres</Num>, though a normal meal is far less.

The stomach wall has three layers of muscle running in different directions. Together they squeeze, twist and churn the food. At the same time the lining pours in gastric juice, a digestive juice that is strongly acidic, at about <Num s="S5">pH 1.5 to 3.5</Num>. The churning breaks the food up further, and the juice starts to digest the protein in it into simpler substances. A thick layer of mucus keeps the acid off the stomach's own lining. <FlyTo preset="stomach-view">Look at the stomach</FlyTo>

The food is not fully digested when it leaves. It has become a thick, soupy mixture, and a ring of muscle at the lower end of the stomach lets it out into the small intestine a little at a time. The stomach is usually empty about <Num s="S5">2 to 4 hours</Num> after a meal; some meals stay longer. On the stage the dots change from olive to ochre as the food is mixed.

<More title={{ en: "Reading the model", zh: "怎样看这个模型" }}>

The source model had a round window cut in the front of the stomach, a way of showing the lining; Atlas closed it so the stomach looks whole. The cut in beat 2 is only a view. The swelling and the gentle pulsing show stretching and churning far faster and smaller than in life.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Try it: churn some bread", zh: "试一试：搅一搅面包" }}>

Put a slice of bread and a few spoonfuls of water in a sealed bag. Squeeze and knead it for a minute and watch it turn into a paste. This copies only the churning: water is not gastric juice, and nothing is chemically digested.

</More>

<Lang zh>

食物到了食道的下端，就进入胃。胃是左侧肋骨下面一个 J 形的肌肉囊。空的时候它只有拳头大小，内壁有很深的褶皱。饭菜进来时，褶皱展平，胃随之撑大，最多能容纳约 <Num s="S5">4 升</Num>，不过平常一顿饭远没有这么多。

胃壁有三层走向不同的肌肉，一起挤压、扭动、搅拌食物。同时，胃的内壁分泌胃液。胃液是一种消化液，酸性很强，pH 约 <Num s="S5">1.5–3.5</Num>。搅拌把食物弄得更碎，胃液则开始把食物里的蛋白质分解成更简单的物质。一层厚厚的黏液保护胃自己的内壁，不被胃酸伤害。<FlyTo preset="stomach-view">看看胃</FlyTo>

食物离开胃的时候，还没有完全消化，而是变成了浓稠的糊。胃下端的一圈肌肉让它一点一点地进入小肠。饭后约 <Num s="S5">2–4 小时</Num>，胃通常就空了；有些饭菜停留得更久。舞台上，食物和胃液混合后，点从橄榄色变成赭黄色。

源模型在胃的前壁开了一个圆窗，用来展示内壁；Atlas 把它补上，让胃看起来是完整的。第 2 拍的剖面只是观察用的切口。胃撑大和轻轻脉动的动画，比真实的变化快得多，幅度也小得多。
