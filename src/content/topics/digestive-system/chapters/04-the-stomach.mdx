<Lang en>

Below the gullet, the stomach curves towards the body's left. It is a muscular organ with a space inside. Its wall stretches as food arrives and contracts to mix food with gastric juice. This digestive juice is acidic. The mixing breaks up food further, while substances in the juice help digest it into simpler substances. Food is not fully digested when it leaves the stomach.

Open the stomach section to see the space within it. The folds belong to the lining; they can flatten as the stomach expands. The enlarged wall specimen separates the lining from muscle layers running in different directions. Together, the muscles squeeze and churn the contents. A protective mucus barrier helps keep the stomach lining from being damaged by its own acidic contents.

Food commonly remains in the stomach for about <Num s="S5">2–4 hours</Num>, although meals differ. Small amounts of the soft mixture pass into the beginning of the small intestine as processing continues. The stomach can expand greatly: a textbook describes capacity up to about <Num s="S5">4 L</Num>. That is an adult anatomical capacity, not a recommended meal size.

<Lang zh>

食道下面的胃向身体左侧弯曲。胃有肌肉构成的壁，里面有容纳食物的空间。食物进入时，胃壁能够伸展；胃壁收缩时，把食物和胃液混合。胃液是酸性的消化液，帮助把食物消化成更简单的物质。搅拌也使食物进一步碎散，但食物离开胃时，还没有完成全部消化。

打开胃的剖面，可以看见内腔与内壁褶皱。胃扩大时，褶皱能够展平。旁边放大的胃壁标本把内层和方向不同的肌肉层分开表示，显示肌肉怎样挤压、搅拌内容物。胃内壁还有保护性的黏液屏障，帮助隔开酸性内容物。标本只是局部结构的示意，不是从模型切下来的真实组织。

食物在胃里通常停留约 <Num s="S5">2–4 小时</Num>，不同饭菜并不完全相同。处理过程中，少量糊状混合物陆续进入小肠开头，并不是整顿饭同时离开。课本记载成人胃能扩张到约 <Num s="S5">4 升</Num>；这是解剖学容量，不是建议吃进的饭量，也不代表儿童的胃有相同大小。
