<Lang en>

The small intestine coils below the stomach. Most digestion and absorption of nutrients happen here. Digestive juices continue breaking food down into simpler substances, which pass across the wall and are absorbed into the blood. Blood then carries them around the body. Food does not pour through an open hole into a vessel.

The lining has finger-like projections called <Term id="villi">villi</Term>. They increase the surface available for absorption, the taking in of substances across a surface. Blood capillaries lie inside them. The enlarged specimen shows these relationships. The short outward branch of ochre-green particles represents digested nutrients entering blood; its speed is schematic.

Passage typically takes about <Num s="S6">3–5 hours</Num>. Digestion and absorption overlap. Most water entering the digestive tract is also absorbed here.

<Lang zh>

胃下面盘绕的管子是小肠。大部分消化和营养物质的吸收在这里发生。消化液继续把食物分解成更简单的物质；它们穿过肠壁，被吸收进血液，再运送到全身。食物不是从肠壁的洞直接倒进血管。

小肠内壁有指状的<Term id="villi">绒毛</Term>，增大了发生吸收的表面积。绒毛里面有细小的血管。放大标本显示这些关系，赭绿色的点向外分支，表示消化后的营养物质进入血液，速度只是示意。

食物通过小肠通常需要约 <Num s="S6">3–5 小时</Num>，消化、混合与吸收同时进行。进入消化道的水，大部分也在小肠被吸收；大肠继续处理余下的水。
