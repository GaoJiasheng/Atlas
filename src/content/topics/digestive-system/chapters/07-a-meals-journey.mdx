<Lang en>

A plate of Hainanese chicken rice gives this journey a familiar starting point. Rice, chicken and cucumber contain different substances, but they enter the same digestive route. The presentation follows the changing contents through the syllabus parts: mouth, gullet, stomach, small intestine and large intestine. It shows the main processes separately so that each can be seen.

In the mouth, teeth break up food and saliva begins digestion. The gullet pushes swallowed food towards the stomach. There, muscular mixing and gastric juice make a soft mixture. In the small intestine, digestive juices continue digestion into simpler substances, and digested nutrients are absorbed into the blood. In the large intestine, water is absorbed from the remaining material before waste leaves the body. Digestion does not make every part of the meal enter the blood.

The whole passage commonly takes about <Num s="S5">24–72 hours</Num>. This is a typical range, not a timetable for this plate of food. Parts of a meal can move at different rates, and processes happen together. While food is still in the digestive tract, blood can already be carrying absorbed nutrients elsewhere. Breathing supplies oxygen, and circulation carries it too: body systems work together throughout the journey.

<Lang zh>

舞台用海南鸡饭作为这趟旅程的起点。米饭、鸡肉和黄瓜含有不同物质，却经过相同的消化路线。演示沿着课纲的口、食道、胃、小肠和大肠前进，把主要过程分开显示，方便观察。饭菜在真实身体里不会保持盘中的形状，也不会整齐排队，等前面的过程全部结束后才进入下一段。

口里的牙齿弄碎食物，唾液开始消化。食道推动吞下的食物进入胃，胃的肌肉与胃液把它混成较软的糊状物。小肠里的消化液继续把食物消化成更简单的物质，消化后的营养物质被吸收进血液。大肠从剩余物里吸收水分，废物最后离开身体。不是饭菜的所有成分都能被消化吸收。

整个过程常需约 <Num s="S5">24–72 小时</Num>。这是典型范围，不是这盘鸡饭的准确时刻表。同顿饭的不同部分可以移动得快慢不同，搅拌、消化与吸收也会重叠进行。消化道里还有内容物时，血液已经能把吸收的营养运往别处。呼吸提供氧，循环系统也运送氧。身体的各个系统在整段过程中共同工作。
