<Lang en>

Take a plate of Hainanese chicken rice: rice cooked in chicken stock, slices of chicken, cucumber and a little chilli sauce. Follow one meal through the five parts of the syllabus. In the mouth the teeth chew it and saliva starts to digest the starch in the rice. Each mouthful is swallowed and squeezed down the gullet in about <Num s="S4">4 to 8 seconds</Num>.

In the stomach the meal is churned with gastric juice, which starts to digest the protein in the chicken. The stomach is usually empty in about <Num s="S5">2 to 4 hours</Num>. In the small intestine, juices from the pancreas and bile from the liver join in, the rice, chicken and oil are digested into simpler substances, and these are absorbed into the blood over about <Num s="S6">3 to 5 hours</Num>. The blood carries them to every part of the body. What is left, including fibre from the cucumber, moves on to the large intestine, where water is taken back over about <Num s="S6">12 to 24 hours</Num>. Finally, faeces leave through the anus.

From first bite to the last of the remains, digestion usually takes about <Num s="S5">24 to 72 hours</Num>. These times are typical ranges from a textbook, not a timetable: they overlap, and they differ from person to person and from meal to meal. All the while, the other systems are working too. The heart pumps the blood that carries digested food away from the intestine, and the lungs take in the oxygen that the muscles of the gut use as they move the meal along.

<More title={{ en: "The times are typical, not exact", zh: "时间是典型范围，不是准确时刻" }}>

The stomach, small intestine and large intestine each work on a meal over a range of hours, and part of one meal can still be in the stomach while another part has reached the small intestine. The dots on the stage cover each part in seconds, far faster than food moves in a real body. For a balanced plate, the Health Promotion Board's My Healthy Plate suggests filling <Num s="S13">½</Num> with fruit and vegetables, <Num s="S13">¼</Num> with wholegrains and <Num s="S13">¼</Num> with protein foods.

<Lang zh>

拿一盘海南鸡饭来说：用鸡汤煮的饭、几片鸡肉、黄瓜和一点辣椒酱。跟着这一顿饭走过课纲的五个部分。在口里，牙齿嚼碎饭菜，唾液开始消化米饭里的淀粉。每一口都被吞下，沿食道被挤送下去，大约 <Num s="S4">4–8 秒</Num>到达胃。

在胃里，饭菜和胃液一起被搅拌，胃液开始消化鸡肉里的蛋白质。大约 <Num s="S5">2–4 小时</Num>后，胃通常就空了。在小肠里，胰液和肝制造的胆汁也加入进来，米饭、鸡肉和油被消化成更简单的物质，在约 <Num s="S6">3–5 小时</Num>里被吸收进血液，再由血液送到身体各处。剩下的东西，包括黄瓜里的纤维，进入大肠；大肠在约 <Num s="S6">12–24 小时</Num>里把其中的水吸收回去。最后，粪便从肛门排出体外。

从第一口到最后排出，整个消化过程通常需要约 <Num s="S5">24–72 小时</Num>。这些时间是课本给出的典型范围，不是时刻表：各段会互相重叠，也因人、因饭菜而不同。在这段时间里，其他系统也一直在工作。心脏推动血液，把消化后的养分从肠运走；肺吸进的氧，供给推动食物前进的肠壁肌肉使用。

<More title={{ en: "The times are typical, not exact", zh: "时间是典型范围，不是准确时刻" }}>

胃、小肠和大肠处理一顿饭，各要几个小时；同一顿饭，一部分可能还在胃里，另一部分已经到了小肠。舞台上的点几秒钟就走完一段，比真实身体里的食物快得多。至于怎样吃得均衡，保健促进局的“我的健康餐盘”建议：<Num s="S13">½</Num> 盘水果和蔬菜，<Num s="S13">¼</Num> 盘全谷物，<Num s="S13">¼</Num> 盘蛋白质食物。
