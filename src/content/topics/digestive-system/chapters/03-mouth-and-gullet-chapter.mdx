<Lang en>

At the top of the model, the upper and lower teeth meet around the tongue. Teeth cut and grind food into smaller pieces. This is a physical change: smaller pieces expose more surface to digestive juices, the liquids that help digest food. <Term id="saliva">Saliva</Term>, made by the salivary glands, moistens food and begins digesting starch into simpler substances. Digestion therefore starts in the mouth, before the food reaches the stomach.

The tongue mixes food with saliva and pushes it towards the throat. During swallowing, structures in the throat help direct food away from the windpipe and into the gullet behind it. The illustration shows this arrangement from the side. The windpipe carries air; it is not another route for food.

The gullet moves the swallowed food towards the stomach. Its wall has muscles that contract in a travelling sequence. This movement is <Term id="peristalsis">peristalsis</Term>. The gullet transports food; it does not add digestive juices to digest it. Gravity is not the only force moving food downwards. The particles on the stage show direction, rather than individual pieces of a real meal.

<Lang zh>

模型头部，上下排牙齿围在舌的外侧。牙齿把食物切开、磨碎，使碎块变小。这是形状和大小的改变，也让更多食物表面接触消化液。唾液腺分泌的<Term id="saliva">唾液</Term>润湿食物，并开始把淀粉分解成更简单的物质。所以，消化在口里已经开始，不是等食物进入胃才发生。

舌把食物和唾液拌匀，再把食物推向咽部。吞咽时，咽部的结构帮助食物避开气管，进入气管后面的食道。插图用侧面剖视显示这段路线。气管输送空气，食道输送食物，不能把它们当成相通的食物通道；模型的咽部显示了食物经过的部分。

食道壁的肌肉按顺序收缩，形成推进食物的波，这种运动叫<Term id="peristalsis">蠕动</Term>。食道把食物送到胃里，不分泌消化液来消化食物。推动食物的并不只有重力。舞台上的点表示内容物前进的方向，并不是按真实大小画出的饭粒，也不能用点移动的速度推算吞咽时间。
