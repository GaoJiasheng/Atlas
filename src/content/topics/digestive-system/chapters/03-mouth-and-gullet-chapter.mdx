<Lang en>

Digestion begins in the mouth. The front teeth cut food, the pointed canines tear it and the broad back teeth grind it into small pieces. This does not yet change what the food is made of, but small pieces are easier to swallow, and digestive juices can reach more of their surface. An adult has <Num s="S4">32</Num> permanent teeth; the model's source has <Num s="S11">28</Num>, without wisdom teeth.

Three pairs of salivary glands pour <Term id="saliva">saliva</Term> into the mouth, about <Num s="S4">1 to 1.5 litres</Num> a day in an adult. Saliva wets and softens the food, and a juice in it starts to break starch down into sugar. The tongue mixes the food with saliva and presses it into a soft lump. On the stage, blue-grey dots stand for digestive juice and olive dots for food.

To swallow, the tongue pushes the lump back into the throat. A small flap, the epiglottis, folds down over the opening of the windpipe, so the food goes into the gullet behind it and not into the lungs. The gullet is a muscular tube about <Num s="S4">25 cm</Num> long. Rings of muscle in its wall squeeze one after another, a movement called <Term id="peristalsis">peristalsis</Term>, and push the food down to the stomach in about <Num s="S4">4 to 8 seconds</Num>. The gullet only moves food; it adds no digestive juice.

<More title={{ en: "What the moving parts show", zh: "动画表示什么" }}>

While the scene runs, the lower teeth rock up and down and the gullet swells and narrows. Real chewing also moves sideways, and in the real gullet a narrow ring of squeezing travels down behind each lump. The model can only make the whole gullet pulse, so the dots show where the food goes; their speed is chosen so you can watch them.

</More>

Chew a plain cracker slowly for a minute without swallowing. Notice whether it starts to taste a little sweet. Crackers are mostly starch; the juice in saliva breaks some of it down into sugar.

<Lang zh>

消化从口开始。门牙把食物切断，尖尖的犬齿把它撕开，宽大的后牙把它磨成小块。这时食物的成分还没有改变，但小块容易吞咽，消化液也能接触到更多表面。成人有 <Num s="S4">32</Num> 颗恒牙；模型的源文件有 <Num s="S11">28</Num> 颗，没有智齿。

三对唾液腺把<Term id="saliva">唾液</Term>送进口腔，成人每天约分泌 <Num s="S4">1–1.5 升</Num>。唾液润湿、软化食物，唾液里的消化液还开始把淀粉分解成糖。舌把食物和唾液拌匀，压成一个软软的食团。舞台上，青灰色的点代表消化液，橄榄色的点代表食物。

吞咽时，舌把食团推向咽。一块叫会厌的小软骨向下盖住气管的入口，食物就进入气管后面的食道，而不会进入肺。食道是一条长约 <Num s="S4">25 厘米</Num>的肌肉管。管壁的肌肉一圈接一圈地收缩，这种运动叫<Term id="peristalsis">蠕动</Term>，大约 <Num s="S4">4–8 秒</Num>就把食物推到胃里。食道只负责输送食物，不分泌消化液。

<More title={{ en: "What the moving parts show", zh: "动画表示什么" }}>

场景运转时，下牙上下摆动，食道一胀一缩。真实的咀嚼还会左右移动；真实的食道里，一圈窄窄的收缩跟在每个食团后面向下传。模型只能让整段食道一起脉动，所以点只表示食物的去向，速度是为了方便观看而设的。

</More>
