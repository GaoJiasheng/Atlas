<Lang en>

On the stage, a body outline surrounds the digestive organs. The model also includes bones, muscles, lungs and a heart. A <Term id="system">system</Term> is a group of parts that work together to carry out a function. An <Term id="organ">organ</Term> is a body part with a particular function. The <Term id="stomach">stomach</Term>, for example, is an organ within the digestive system.

This topic introduces the <Num s="S2">five</Num> systems in the primary science syllabus. The skeletal system supports the body and protects organs. The muscular system moves body parts, often by pulling on bones. The respiratory system takes in oxygen and removes carbon dioxide. The circulatory system carries blood around the body; its heart pumps blood through vessels. These functions continue while food is being processed.

The digestive system breaks food down. During <Term id="digestion">digestion</Term>, food is digested into simpler substances that can be absorbed into the blood. Chewing changes the size of the pieces; <Term id="digestive-juice">digestive juices</Term> help change the substances in them. Digestion changes food; <Term id="absorption">absorption</Term> moves digested substances across the gut wall into the blood.

<Lang zh>

舞台上，身体轮廓里面是消化器官，也能看到骨骼、肌肉、肺和心脏。<Term id="system">系统</Term>是一起完成某项功能的一组部分；<Term id="organ">器官</Term>是身体里有特定功能的部分。例如，<Term id="stomach">胃</Term>属于消化系统，心脏属于循环系统。身体的各个系统同时工作，并不是轮到消化时，其余系统就停下来。

这里介绍小学科学课纲中的 <Num s="S2">五</Num>个系统。骨骼系统支撑身体，保护器官。肌肉系统让身体各部分活动，常常靠拉动骨骼产生动作。呼吸系统吸进氧，排出二氧化碳。循环系统靠心脏推动血液，沿着血管把物质运送到全身。模型只选出部分结构，没有显示全身所有血管和肌肉。

消化系统把食物分解。<Term id="digestion">消化</Term>时，食物被分解成更简单、能被吸收进血液的物质。咀嚼让食物碎块变小，<Term id="digestive-juice">消化液</Term>则帮助改变食物里的物质。消化改变食物，<Term id="absorption">吸收</Term>则让消化后的物质穿过肠壁进入血液。后面的章节沿着食物经过的路线，把这些变化分开来看。
