<Lang en>

The faint outline on the stage is one adult body. Inside it you can see bones, muscles, lungs, a heart and, in the middle, the long tube of the digestive organs. A <Term id="system">system</Term> is a group of parts that work together to do one job. Each part, such as the <Term id="stomach">stomach</Term> or the heart, is an <Term id="organ">organ</Term>: a part of the body with its own work to do.

The P4 science syllabus names <Num s="S2">five</Num> systems. The skeletal system holds the body up and protects soft organs; the skull guards the brain and the ribs guard the heart and lungs. The muscular system moves the body, mostly by pulling on bones. The respiratory system takes in oxygen and gives out carbon dioxide. The circulatory system, the heart and the blood vessels, carries oxygen and other substances round the body in the blood.

The digestive system breaks food down. In <Term id="digestion">digestion</Term>, food is digested into simpler substances that can pass into the blood. Teeth and stomach muscles break food into smaller pieces; <Term id="digestive-juice">digestive juices</Term> change what the food is made of. Taking the simpler substances into the blood is <Term id="absorption">absorption</Term>.

The systems work at the same time and depend on one another. Digested food reaches the rest of the body only because the blood carries it, and the muscles that move food along the gut need oxygen from the lungs. How the lungs, the heart and the blood do their part is in <TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs" ch="three-systems">The Heart and Lungs</TopicLink>. <Lang zh>

舞台上淡淡的轮廓是一个成人的身体。里面能看到骨骼、肌肉、肺、心脏，中间是一条长长的消化器官。<Term id="system">系统</Term>是一起完成一项工作的一组部分。系统里的每个部分，例如<Term id="stomach">胃</Term>或心脏，都是一个<Term id="organ">器官</Term>，也就是身体里负责特定工作的部分。

小四科学课纲列出 <Num s="S2">五</Num>个系统。骨骼系统撑起身体，保护柔软的器官：头骨保护脑，肋骨保护心和肺。肌肉系统让身体活动，大多靠拉动骨骼。呼吸系统吸进氧，排出二氧化碳。循环系统由心脏和血管组成，用血液把氧和其他物质运送到全身。

消化系统负责分解食物。<Term id="digestion">消化</Term>就是把食物分解成更简单的物质，让它们能够进入血液。牙齿和胃的肌肉把食物弄成更小的碎块；<Term id="digestive-juice">消化液</Term>则改变食物本身的成分。更简单的物质进入血液，叫作<Term id="absorption">吸收</Term>。

这些系统同时工作，互相依靠。消化后的养分要靠血液才能送到全身；推动食物在肠里前进的肌肉，也需要肺吸进的氧。肺、心脏和血液怎样完成它们的工作，见<TopicLink slug="heart-and-lungs" ch="three-systems">心与肺</TopicLink>。接下来的几章，沿着消化系统从头走到尾。<FlyTo preset="hero">看看整个身体</FlyTo>
