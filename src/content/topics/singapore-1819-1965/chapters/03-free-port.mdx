<Lang en>

In 1826 Penang, Malacca and Singapore were joined as the <Term id="straits-settlements-colony">Straits Settlements</Term>, a colony run by the East India Company under the government of British India; in 1832 its seat moved to Singapore. Beyond the colony's borders, Britain fought the Malay chiefdom of Naning, near Malacca, in 1831–32 to make it pay tax.

Singapore was a <Term id="free-trade-port">free port</Term>: ships paid no duties on what they landed or took away. Goods were brought in, stored and sold on, an exchange called entrepôt trade. Junks from Amoy and Canton came on the north-east monsoon early in the year and sailed home on the south-west monsoon; Bugis traders came from Makassar; country ships carried Indian cotton and opium in, and tin, pepper and sea produce out to China.

Without duties, the government lived on <Term id="revenue-farm">revenue farms</Term>: it auctioned the sole right to sell opium, spirits and other goods to the highest bidder. Opium was the biggest: in different years it brought in between <Num s="S352">40 and 60 percent</Num> of the government's revenue, and <Num s="S208">over half</Num> at its nineteenth-century peak. The farms made a few merchants rich and paid for the government, while many of the labourers who smoked the opium spent much of their wages on it and fell into debt and ill health.

In 1864 the Tanjong Pagar Dock Company was formed to build deep-water wharves west of the town. On 1 April 1867 the Straits Settlements became a <Term id="crown-colony">Crown Colony</Term>, ruled from London. In 1869 the Suez Canal opened, and steamships from Europe could come by the Red Sea instead of round Africa.

<More title={{"en":"The monsoon timetable","zh":"季风时刻表"}}>

Sailing ships could not sail against the monsoon. Junks left southern China with the north-east monsoon around the turn of the year, traded in Singapore, and went home with the south-west monsoon from about May. Bugis prahus from Sulawesi came later in the year. The arrival of the junks was the busiest time in the port. The routes on the map are drawn from sailing directions of the time; they show the way ships went, not one voyage.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Who paid for the colony","zh":"谁在养这个殖民地"}}>

A revenue farm was not a farm. It was a monopoly: the merchant who won the auction paid the government in advance and then sold opium, through licensed shops, to recover his money. Most buyers were Chinese labourers, who smoked it to bear long hours of heavy work. The government knew the harm, but opium paid for its police, courts and roads. Private opium farms ended in 1909, and from 1910 the government sold opium itself. Smoking it was banned under the Japanese in 1943, and again by the British in 1946.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Gambier, pepper and tigers","zh":"甘蜜、胡椒和老虎"}}>

Chinese planters, many of them from Riau, grew gambier and pepper on the island's hills. Gambier had to be boiled, and the boilers burned wood, so the planters cleared one patch of forest after another and moved on. By the middle of the century much of the island's forest was gone. Tigers swam across from Johor, and attacks on plantation workers were reported for decades; the records are too patchy to give a reliable total.

</More>

<Lang zh>

1826 年，槟城、马六甲和新加坡合成<Term id="straits-settlements-colony">海峡殖民地</Term>，由东印度公司管理，归英属印度政府管辖；1832 年，首府迁到新加坡。殖民地边界之外，英国在 1831 年至 1832 年间与马六甲附近的马来酋邦南宁交战，逼它纳税。

新加坡是<Term id="free-trade-port">自由港</Term>：船只装卸货物都不用交关税。货物运来、存进货栈、再转卖出去，这叫转口贸易。厦门、广州的帆船年初趁东北季风南下，再趁西南季风回航；武吉斯商人从望加锡驶来；港脚商船运来印度的棉布和鸦片，把锡、胡椒和海产运往中国。

不收关税，政府就靠<Term id="revenue-farm">饷码</Term>过日子：把鸦片、酒等货物的专卖权拍卖给出价最高的商人。鸦片饷码最大：不同年份占政府收入的 <Num s="S352">40% 到 60%</Num>，19 世纪最高峰时超过<Num s="S208">一半</Num>。饷码让少数商人致富，也养活了政府；而吸鸦片的许多劳工，把大部分工钱花在烟上，欠债、伤身。

1864 年，丹戎巴葛船坞公司成立，在城区以西的深水处修建码头。1867 年 4 月 1 日，海峡殖民地改为<Term id="crown-colony">直辖殖民地</Term>，由伦敦直接管理。1869 年苏伊士运河通航，欧洲来的蒸汽船可以经红海直达，不必再绕过非洲。

<More title={{"en":"The monsoon timetable","zh":"季风时刻表"}}>

帆船不能逆着季风航行。中国帆船在年底年初趁东北季风离开华南，到新加坡做完买卖，大约 5 月以后再趁西南季风回去。苏拉威西的武吉斯船晚些时候才来。帆船到港的季节，是港口最忙的时候。地图上的航线是按当时的航海指南画的，表示船走的大致方向，不是某一次航行。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Who paid for the colony","zh":"谁在养这个殖民地"}}>

饷码不是农场，而是专卖权：拍得饷码的商人先把钱交给政府，再通过有执照的烟馆卖鸦片，把钱赚回来。买烟的大多是华人劳工，他们靠吸烟熬过长时间的重活。政府知道鸦片的害处，但警察、法院和道路的开支都靠它。私人鸦片饷码到 1909 年结束，1910 年起改由政府自己专卖。日据时期的 1943 年禁止吸鸦片，英国人回来后在 1946 年再度禁止。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Gambier, pepper and tigers","zh":"甘蜜、胡椒和老虎"}}>

华人种植者（许多来自廖内）在岛上的山坡种甘蜜和胡椒。甘蜜要熬煮，熬煮要烧柴，所以种植者砍完一片森林就搬到下一片。到 19 世纪中叶，岛上的森林已经被砍去大半。老虎从柔佛游过海峡，几十年间不断有种植园工人被老虎咬伤咬死的报道，但记录零散，得不出可靠的总数。

</More>
