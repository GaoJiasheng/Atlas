<Lang en>

The town grew in rows of shophouses: a shop or workshop downstairs, rooms upstairs, and in front a covered walkway, the five-foot way, that kept off sun and rain. Merchants and labourers lived a few streets apart. In the labourers' lodgings dozens of men shared a few rooms; cholera, smallpox and malaria spread fast, and opium and debt held many.

Each community built its own places. On South Bridge Road a Hindu temple, Sri Mariamman, began in 1827, and the Chulia Muslims' Masjid Jamae in 1826. Hokkien merchants built Thian Hock Keng on Telok Ayer Street, then on the shore, in 1839–42. The Armenian Church followed in 1835, the Sultan Mosque at Kampong Glam in 1824–26. In 1844 Tan Tock Seng, a merchant from Malacca, paid for a hospital for the poor of every community.

The same networks that helped newcomers could also fight. A <Term id="kongsi">kongsi</Term> might be a clan association or a secret society that controlled jobs, gambling and protection. In May 1854 fighting between Hokkien and Teochew groups lasted about ten days and spread to the plantations; contemporary reports gave <Num s="S178">400 or more</Num> dead, and a later account <Num s="S58">about 500</Num>. In 1876 police fired on crowds protesting a new post office and killed <Num s="S210">three</Num> people.

The town also pushed into the sea. From 1879 the bay in front of Telok Ayer Street was filled in, finished by 1890 by one account and 1897 by another; on the map the shore moves out in 1887. Thian Hock Keng, once on the water's edge, now stands inland.

<More title={{"en":"1854: ten days in May","zh":"1854 年：五月的十天"}}>

The fighting began on 5 May 1854 in the town and spread to the gambier and pepper plantations inland; about <Num s="S178">300</Num> houses were burned. Police, troops and <Num s="S353">200</Num> Malay men sent by the Temenggong took ten days or more to stop it. About <Num s="S353">500</Num> people were arrested <Num s="S353">six</Num> were sentenced to death, and <Num s="S353">two</Num> were executed. The dead were mostly ordinary labourers and planters, and their names were not recorded. The two figures, <Num s="S178">400 or more</Num> from the colonial records Buckley used and <Num s="S58">about 500</Num> from a later account, are estimates, not counts. Secret societies were banned in 1890.

</More>

The four buildings in the drawing stand for kinds of building that rose in the town; the real buildings were rebuilt several times. Sri Mariamman's first temple was of wood and attap, rebuilt in brick in 1843. St Andrew's Cathedral, finished in 1861, was built by Indian convicts. The present Sultan Mosque was rebuilt between 1924 and 1932.

</More>

城镇是一排排店屋长出来的：楼下是店铺或作坊，楼上住人，门前有一道遮阳挡雨的走廊，叫“五脚基”。商人和劳工只隔几条街。劳工住的客馆里，几十个人挤在几个房间；霍乱、天花和疟疾传得很快，许多人又被鸦片和债务缠住。

各个族群都建起自己的地方。桥南路上，兴都庙马里安曼 1827 年起建，注辇穆斯林的詹美回教堂 1826 年起建。福建商人在 1839 年至 1842 年间，在当时还靠海的直落亚逸街建起天福宫。亚米尼亚教堂建于 1835 年，甘榜格南的苏丹回教堂建于 1824 年至 1826 年。1844 年，马六甲出身的商人陈笃生出钱建医院，免费医治各族的穷人。

帮助新客的同一张网，也可能变成打斗的网。<Term id="kongsi">公司</Term>可能是同乡会馆，也可能是控制工作、赌博和“保护费”的私会党。1854 年 5 月，福建帮与潮州帮的冲突持续约十天，蔓延到种植园；当时的记载说死了 <Num s="S178">400 多</Num>人，后来的一份记载说<Num s="S58">约 500</Num> 人。1876 年，警察向抗议新设邮政局的人群开枪，打死 <Num s="S210">3</Num> 人。

城市也向海里扩张。1879 年起，直落亚逸街前面的海湾被填平，一说 1890 年完工，一说 1897 年；地图上 1887 年的海岸向外推出。原本在海边的天福宫，如今远在内陆。
