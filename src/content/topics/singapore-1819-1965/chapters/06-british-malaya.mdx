<Lang en>

“British Malaya” was not one country. Singapore, Penang and Malacca formed the Straits Settlements, a colony. In the Malay states Britain ruled through the sultans. At Pangkor in 1874, a quarrel over the throne of Perak was settled by a treaty that gave the Sultan a British <Term id="resident">Resident</Term>, whose advice he had to follow in all things except Malay religion and custom. When the first Resident, J. W. W. Birch, was killed in 1875, British troops fought Malay chiefs in Perak until 1876.

In 1896 Perak, Selangor, Negeri Sembilan and Pahang were joined as the Federated Malay States, run from Kuala Lumpur. Johor kept its own government under treaties of 1885 and later, and in 1909 Siam handed Kedah, Perlis, Kelantan and Terengganu to British protection. The sultans kept their courts and titles while British officials ran much of the government.

Tin from the mines and rubber from the plantations made Singapore their port: the goods were processed, traded and shipped here. Chinese miners and Tamil rubber tappers did the work for low wages. In 1924 the Causeway linked the island to Johor by road and rail. On 12 March 1927 police fired on a crowd outside the Kreta Ayer police station during a memorial for Sun Yat-sen, killing <Num s="S197,S356">six</Num> men. After 1930 the government cut the number of Chinese men allowed to enter, but not women.

In February 1915 part of the 5th Light Infantry, an Indian regiment, mutinied in Singapore. The mutineers killed British officers, soldiers and civilians, among them Chinese and Malays: <Num s="S57">44</Num> people died. The mutiny was put down within days. <Num s="S360">47</Num> mutineers, or more by another account, were executed, shot before crowds outside Outram Prison.

<More title={{"en":"1915: the mutiny and the executions","zh":"1915 年：兵变与处决"}}>

The 5th Light Infantry was made up of Muslim soldiers from northern India, Ranghars and Pathans, and was due to sail for Hong Kong. Explanations differ: rumours that the men were being sent to fight the Turks, fellow Muslims; German and Ottoman propaganda; the ideas of the Indian revolutionary Ghadar movement; quarrels inside the regiment. The mutineers killed officers, soldiers and civilians; of the <Num s="S57">44</Num> dead, most were British, the others Chinese and Malay. Police, the Singapore Volunteer Corps, sailors from British, Japanese, French and Russian ships and the Sultan of Johor's men put the mutiny down. More than <Num s="S57">200</Num> men were court-martialled. On 25 March <Num s="S355">22</Num> were shot at once by a volunteer firing squad; on one occasion <Num s="S360">15,000</Num> people watched. The mutineers' killings and the public executions are both part of this history.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Sultans and Residents","zh":"苏丹与驻扎官"}}>

British reports described Residents as bringing order and roads. For Malay rulers the treaties meant losing control of taxes, land and appointments to officials they had not chosen; Perak's chiefs who fought in 1875–76 saw the Resident as a foreign ruler. The map shows each state's border as it was drawn on an 1898 map, so it is approximate.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Water across the strait","zh":"跨海峡的水"}}>

Singapore signed its first water agreement with Johor in 1927, and pipes along the Causeway began to carry water to the island. The story of that water is told in <TopicLink slug="four-taps">Four taps</TopicLink>.

<Lang zh>

“英属马来亚”不是一个国家。新加坡、槟城和马六甲组成海峡殖民地，是殖民地；在各马来州属，英国通过苏丹来统治。1874 年在邦咯，霹雳的王位之争以一份条约了结：苏丹身边设一名英国<Term id="resident">驻扎官</Term>，除了伊斯兰教和马来习俗，苏丹凡事都要听他的意见。1875 年第一任驻扎官毕治被杀，英军与霹雳的马来酋长作战，一直打到 1876 年。

1896 年，霹雳、雪兰莪、森美兰和彭亨组成马来联邦，以吉隆坡为中心。柔佛依据 1885 年以后的几份条约保有自己的政府；1909 年，暹罗把吉打、玻璃市、吉兰丹和登嘉楼交给英国保护。苏丹们保留了王廷和名号，政府的大部分事务却由英国官员掌管。

矿场的锡和种植园的橡胶，都经新加坡加工、买卖、装船出口。干活的是领低工资的华人矿工和泰米尔割胶工人。1924 年，长堤用公路和铁路把新加坡岛和柔佛连起来。1927 年 3 月 12 日，在一次纪念孙中山的集会中，警察在牛车水警察局外向人群开枪，打死 <Num s="S197,S356">6</Num> 人。1930 年以后，政府限制华人男子入境，却不限制女性。

1915 年 2 月，驻新加坡的印度第五轻步兵团部分官兵兵变。兵变者杀害英国军官、士兵和平民，其中也有华人和马来人，共 <Num s="S57">44</Num> 人死亡。兵变几天内被镇压。<Num s="S360">47</Num> 名兵变者（另一说更多）被处决，在欧南监狱外当着围观的人群被枪决。

<More title={{"en":"1915: the mutiny and the executions","zh":"1915 年：兵变与处决"}}>

第五轻步兵团由来自印度北部的穆斯林士兵组成，有兰加尔人，也有帕坦人，原定开往香港。兵变的原因有几种说法：传言他们会被派去和同为穆斯林的土耳其人作战；德国和奥斯曼的宣传；印度革命组织“加达尔运动”的思想；部队内部的矛盾。兵变者杀害军官、士兵和平民，<Num s="S57">44</Num> 名死者中大多是英国人，其余是华人和马来人。警察、新加坡志愿军，英、日、法、俄军舰的水兵，以及柔佛苏丹的人马，一起镇压了兵变。<Num s="S57">200</Num> 多人受军事审判。3 月 25 日，志愿军的行刑队一次枪决 <Num s="S355">22</Num> 人；有一次围观的人多达 <Num s="S360">15,000</Num> 人。兵变者的杀戮和公开处决，都是这段历史的一部分。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Sultans and Residents","zh":"苏丹与驻扎官"}}>

英国的报告说，驻扎官带来了秩序和道路。对马来统治者来说，条约意味着税收、土地和官员任免交到了不是他们挑选的人手里；1875 年至 1876 年起兵的霹雳酋长，把驻扎官看作外来的统治者。地图上各州的边界是按 1898 年地图画的，只是近似。

</More>
