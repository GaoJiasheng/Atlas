<Lang en>

Raffles came back to Singapore in October 1822 for the last time and disliked how the town had grown. He set up a Town Committee, and on 4 November gave it instructions. Lieutenant Philip Jackson drew the plan. Singapore Hill, now Fort Canning, and the plain below it were kept for the government; a European town ran along the shore; the Chinese <Term id="kampong">kampong</Term> lay south of the river, the Chulia kampong upriver, and the Bugis and Arab kampongs and the Sultan's compound at Kampong Glam to the north-east.

This was a colonial plan to place each community in its own quarter, under its own headmen. It suited the people who drew it: it made the town easier to police and tax. Some people were left off the plan altogether, such as the Orang Laut and many labourers. The plan was a proposal, and in practice people soon lived side by side.

The land itself was changed. A small hill where Raffles Place now stands was cut down, and its earth was used to fill the swamp on the south bank, where Boat Quay was built. The shore drawn on the map from 1823 is the shore after this first reclamation.

Three treaties changed who ruled. On 7 June 1823 the Sultan and the Temenggong gave up most of their rights in the settlement for monthly allowances of <Num s="S7">$1,500</Num> and <Num s="S7">$800</Num>. On 17 March 1824 Britain and the Netherlands signed a treaty in London that divided the Malay world between them; no Malay ruler took part. On 2 August 1824 John Crawfurd, the Resident, had Hussein and the Temenggong cede the island, and the islands within ten geographical miles of it, to the East India Company for ever.

<More title={{"en":"Reading Jackson's plan","zh":"怎样读杰克逊的规划图"}}>

The original plan is a single-colour drawing: the quarters are named in writing, not coloured. On this map each named quarter is traced from the georeferenced plan and coloured, so its edges are approximate, and it shows what was planned, not who actually lived where. Many of the planned streets are still there: North and South Bridge Roads, and the grid around Chinatown.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"What the cession cost and paid","zh":"割让的代价与价钱"}}>

Under the 1824 treaty the Sultan received <Num s="S7">$33,200</Num> and an allowance of <Num s="S7">$1,300</Num> a month for life, and the Temenggong <Num s="S7">$26,800</Num> and <Num s="S7">$700</Num> a month. In return they gave up the island and their right to make treaties with any other power. Both stayed on the island with their followers, at Kampong Glam and Telok Blangah. British officials called it a fair bargain. But Hussein owed his title to the British, the rival court in Riau did not recognise him, and he had little room to refuse.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"A line through the Malay world","zh":"一条把马来世界分开的线"}}>

The Anglo-Dutch Treaty gave Malacca to Britain and Bencoolen, on Sumatra, to the Netherlands. The Dutch dropped their objection to the British in Singapore, and each side agreed to keep out of the other's half of the straits. The Johor–Riau sultanate was cut in two: Johor and Singapore on the British side, Riau and Lingga on the Dutch side. The line is one of the roots of today's border between Malaysia and Indonesia.

</More>

<Lang zh>

1822 年 10 月，莱佛士最后一次来到新加坡，对城镇的发展很不满意。他成立了城区委员会，11 月 4 日向委员会下达指示。规划图由杰克逊中尉绘制：新加坡山（今福康宁）和山下的平地留给政府，欧洲人市区沿着海边，华人<Term id="kampong">甘榜</Term>在河的南岸，注辇甘榜在上游，武吉士甘榜、阿拉伯甘榜和苏丹在甘榜格南的居地在东北边。

这是殖民政府按族群划分居住区的规划，每个族群由自己的头人管理。这样划分对规划的人有利：治安和收税都方便。有些人根本不在图上，例如海人和许多劳工。规划只是一个方案，实际上各族很快就混居了。

土地本身也被改造。今天莱佛士坊所在的一座小山被削平，泥土用来填河南岸的沼泽，驳船码头就建在这片填出来的地上。地图上 1823 年以后的海岸，就是这第一次填土之后的海岸。

三份条约改变了由谁统治。1823 年 6 月 7 日，苏丹和天猛公放弃在商站里的大部分权利，改领每月 <Num s="S7">1,500</Num> 元和 <Num s="S7">800</Num> 元的月俸。1824 年 3 月 17 日，英国和荷兰在伦敦签订条约，把马来世界分给两国，没有一位马来统治者参加。1824 年 8 月 2 日，驻扎官克劳福让胡先和天猛公把新加坡岛，连同周围 10 地理英里以内的岛屿，永久割让给东印度公司。

<More title={{"en":"Reading Jackson's plan","zh":"怎样读杰克逊的规划图"}}>

规划图原件是单色的，各区用文字标名，没有上色。这张地图上的各个分区，是从配准后的规划图上按文字标注描出来再上色的，边界只是近似；它画的是规划，不是实际住在哪里的人。图上规划的许多街道今天还在，例如桥北路、桥南路和牛车水一带的街道格局。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"What the cession cost and paid","zh":"割让的代价与价钱"}}>

按 1824 年的条约，苏丹一次领得 <Num s="S7">33,200</Num> 元，此后终身每月 <Num s="S7">1,300</Num> 元；天猛公一次领得 <Num s="S7">26,800</Num> 元，每月 <Num s="S7">700</Num> 元。作为交换，他们交出这座岛，也不能再和别的国家订约。两人都带着随从留在岛上，分别住在甘榜格南和直落布兰雅。英国官员说这是公平交易。可是胡先的苏丹名号是英国人给的，廖内的朝廷并不承认他，他几乎没有拒绝的余地。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"A line through the Malay world","zh":"一条把马来世界分开的线"}}>

《英荷条约》把马六甲给了英国，把苏门答腊的明古连给了荷兰。荷兰不再反对英国人留在新加坡，双方同意互不进入对方那一半海峡。柔佛—廖内苏丹国被一分为二：柔佛和新加坡在英国一边，廖内和林加在荷兰一边。这条线是今天马来西亚与印度尼西亚边界的来历之一。

</More>
