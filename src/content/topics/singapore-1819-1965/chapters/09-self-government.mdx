David Marshall's Labour Front government took office in 1955 and went to London in 1956 to ask for self-government. The talks broke down over who would control internal security, and Marshall resigned in June 1956. His successor, Lim Yew Hock, moved against left-wing unions and student groups. In September 1956 his government dissolved the Chinese middle-school students' union. Students occupied the Chinese High School and Chung Cheng High School; when police moved in, rioting spread across the island, and <Num s="S155">13</Num> people were killed and <Num s="S155">123</Num> injured.

The government said communists were using students to overthrow the state. Students and their supporters said they were defending Chinese education and their right to organise. In the week of the riots the police arrested <Num s="S154">290</Num> people for rioting and detained <Num s="S154">912</Num> more under the security laws.

On 31 August 1957 the Federation of Malaya became independent under Tunku Abdul Rahman; Singapore stayed a colony. A citizenship law of 1957 let many people born in China, India and elsewhere become Singapore citizens; more than <Num s="S333">320,000</Num> registered within three months. Voting was made compulsory. The number of voters grew from <Num s="S189">300,299</Num> in 1955 to <Num s="S189">586,098</Num> in 1959.

In the election of 30 May 1959 the People's Action Party won <Num s="S190">43</Num> of the <Num s="S190">51</Num> seats with <Num s="S334">53.4</Num> percent of the votes. Singapore became a self-governing state on 3 June, and Lee Kuan Yew and eight ministers were sworn in on 5 June. Under <Term id="self-rule">self-government</Term> Britain kept defence and foreign affairs, and internal security was shared. On 3 December Yusof Ishak was installed as head of state, and the state flag, crest and anthem, “Majulah Singapura”, were presented.

Lim Yew Hock's government said the students' union and some unions were part of a communist “united front”, and that it had to restore order. It detained student leaders and unionists and closed schools.

Many students and former detainees remember it differently: as a fight for Chinese-language education, which they felt the colonial government neglected, and for the right to protest against colonial rule. Order was restored on 28 October. The heritage board's account gives <Num s="S155">13</Num> dead and <Num s="S155">123</Num> injured; the sources read for this topic do not name the dead.

<More title={{"en":"Who could vote","zh":"谁能投票"}}>

In 1948 only British subjects who registered could vote, and just <Num s="S189">22,395</Num> did, for <Num s="S189">six</Num> elected seats. From 1954 voters were registered automatically, and the 1957 citizenship law added many people born outside Singapore. In 1959 voting was made compulsory. Within eleven years the vote had passed from a small, mostly English-speaking group to most adults.

1955 年，马绍尔领导的劳工阵线政府上台，1956 年到伦敦争取自治。谈判在内部安全由谁掌控的问题上破裂，马绍尔在 1956 年 6 月辞职。继任的林有福政府对左翼工会和学生团体采取行动。1956 年 9 月，政府解散新加坡华文中学学生联合会。学生留守华侨中学和中正中学；警察进校清场时，骚乱蔓延到全岛，<Num s="S155">13</Num> 人死亡，<Num s="S155">123</Num> 人受伤。

政府说，共产党在利用学生颠覆政府。学生和支持者则说，他们是在捍卫华文教育和结社的权利。骚乱那一周，警方以暴乱罪逮捕 <Num s="S154">290</Num> 人，另有 <Num s="S154">912</Num> 人依安全法令被扣押。

1957 年 8 月 31 日，马来亚联合邦在东姑阿都拉曼领导下独立，新加坡仍是殖民地。1957 年的公民权法令让许多在中国、印度等地出生的居民成为新加坡公民，三个月内就有 <Num s="S333">32 万</Num>多人登记。投票也改为强制。选民人数从 1955 年的 <Num s="S189">300,299</Num> 人增加到 1959 年的 <Num s="S189">586,098</Num> 人。

1959 年 5 月 30 日的大选，人民行动党以 <Num s="S334">53.4%</Num> 的得票率，在 <Num s="S190">51</Num> 席中赢得 <Num s="S190">43</Num> 席。6 月 3 日新加坡成为自治邦，6 月 5 日李光耀和八名部长宣誓就职。实行<Term id="self-rule">自治</Term>以后，国防和外交仍归英国，内部安全由双方共管。12 月 3 日，尤索夫·伊萨就任国家元首，州旗、州徽和州歌《前进吧，新加坡》同时亮相。

林有福政府说，华文中学学生联合会和一些工会是共产党“统一战线”的一部分，政府必须恢复秩序。政府扣留了学生领袖和工会人士，关闭学校。

许多当年的学生和后来的被扣押者记得的却是另一回事：他们在争取被殖民政府冷落的华文教育，争取反对殖民统治的权利。10 月 28 日秩序恢复。国家文物局的记载是 <Num s="S155">13</Num> 人死亡、<Num s="S155">123</Num> 人受伤；本主题读到的资料没有列出死者的名字。
