Shortly after midnight on 8 December 1941 Japanese troops began landing at Kota Bharu in north-east Malaya. Hours later bombers struck Singapore: <Num s="S37">61</Num> people were killed and <Num s="S37">133</Num> injured. The Japanese advanced down the peninsula and crossed the Johor Strait on the night of 8 February. The Malay Regiment fought on the Pasir Panjang ridge and at Bukit Chandu, where Second Lieutenant Adnan Saidi was captured and killed on 14 February; Chinese volunteers of the Dalforce also fought.

On 14 February Japanese soldiers killed <Num s="S38">about 200</Num> patients and staff at Alexandra Hospital. The next day Lieutenant-General Arthur Percival surrendered at the Ford factory; the island garrison numbered <Num s="S29">about 85,000</Num>. Singapore was renamed Syonan-to. In the Sook Ching, which began on 18 February and ran to 4 March, Chinese men aged <Num s="S100">18 to 50</Num> were screened, and those picked out were taken away and shot. The Japanese figure at the 1947 trial was <Num s="S96">about 5,000</Num>; most estimates lie between <Num s="S95">25,000 and 50,000</Num>, and the Chinese community's go up to <Num s="S95">100,000</Num>.

The occupation fell on communities differently. Chinese residents were targeted in the screening and made to raise <Num s="S95">$50 million</Num> in “tribute money”. Indian prisoners of war were urged, and pressed, to join the Indian National Army against British rule. Eurasian and Chinese Catholic families were sent to farm at Bahau, and European civilians were interned at Changi. Everyone faced rationing and occupation money that lost almost all its value.

In October 1943 Japan handed four northern Malay states to Thailand. From November 1944 American bombers attacked Singapore's docks. After Japan surrendered, Admiral Mountbatten accepted the surrender of Japanese forces in Southeast Asia at the Municipal Building on 12 September 1945.

<More title={{"en":"The Sook Ching: dates and numbers","zh":"肃清：日期与数字"}}>

The operation began on 18 February 1942. The historian Hayashi Hirofumi shows that the mass screening in the city was carried out mainly on 21–23 February, and in the suburbs at the end of the month; it ran until 4 March. At Changi Beach <Num s="S98">66</Num> men were killed on 20 February; on Punggol beach <Num s="S99">some 400</Num> on 28 February.

| Figure | Who gave it |
|---|---|
| <Num s="S96">about 5,000</Num> | Japanese figure at the 1947 trial |
| <Num s="S97">11,110</Num> “missing as a result of bombing and the purge” | Japanese 25th Army record, May 1942; Hayashi doubts it |
| <Num s="S95">25,000 to 50,000</Num> | most estimates |
| up to <Num s="S95">100,000</Num> | Singapore's Chinese community |

The figures measure different things and cannot be added together. In 1947 a British military court tried seven Japanese officers for the killings and sentenced two to death. Bones found at Siglap and elsewhere in the 1960s are buried under the Civilian War Memorial, where a service is held every 15 February.

<More title={{"en":"One occupation, different lives","zh":"同一场占领，不同的遭遇"}}>

For Chinese families the occupation began with the screening and the killings. Malay fishing communities at Punggol, Pasir Ris and Pasir Panjang kept fishing and helped feed the town, but shared the hunger. Indians lived between the Indian National Army's call to free India and the pressure to join it; Asian labourers from many communities, sent to build the Burma–Thailand Railway, died there in tens of thousands.

At Bahau, in Negeri Sembilan, about <Num s="S177">3,000</Num> Eurasian and Chinese Catholic settlers cleared jungle with little food or medicine; one account counts <Num s="S177">about 500</Num> deaths in 1944–45, another gives between <Num s="S101">300 and 1,500</Num>. More than <Num s="S106">2,500</Num> civilians and prisoners were packed into Changi Prison. After the Double Tenth raid of October 1943 the military police tortured internees and local people, among them Elizabeth Choy.

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Bombs from the other side","zh":"另一方的炸弹"}}>

From 5 November 1944, when <Num s="S120">53</Num> American B-29 bombers attacked the naval base's dry dock, Allied aircraft bombed the docks, Keppel Harbour and oil depots until March 1945. The official American history judged that a raid on 24 February 1945 destroyed <Num s="S120">39 percent</Num> of the warehouse area at the Empire Dock. These are the attackers' own figures. The docks lay beside crowded neighbourhoods and the workers who served them, and civilians were killed and injured, but no reliable count of their deaths has been found.

</More>

1941 年 12 月 8 日午夜过后，日军开始在马来亚东北部的哥打巴鲁登陆。几个小时后，轰炸机空袭新加坡，<Num s="S37">61</Num> 人死亡，<Num s="S37">133</Num> 人受伤。日军沿半岛南下，2 月 8 日夜渡过柔佛海峡。马来军团在巴西班让山脊和鸦片山作战，阿南·赛迪少尉 2 月 14 日被俘后遇害；华人志愿者组成的星华义勇军也参加了战斗。

2 月 14 日，日军在亚历山大医院杀害了<Num s="S38">约 200</Num> 名病人和医护人员。第二天，白思华中将在福特车厂投降，岛上守军<Num s="S29">约 8.5 万</Num>人。新加坡被改名为“昭南岛”。“肃清”从 2 月 18 日开始，持续到 3 月 4 日：<Num s="S100">18 岁至 50 岁</Num>的华人男子被集中检证，被挑出来的人被带走枪杀。1947 年审判时，日方的数字是<Num s="S96">约 5,000</Num> 人；多数估计在 <Num s="S95">2.5 万到 5 万</Num>人之间，华人社会的估计最高到 <Num s="S95">10 万</Num>人。

占领落在各个群体身上，轻重不同。华人首当其冲，先被检证屠杀，又被迫筹集 <Num s="S95">5,000 万元</Num>“奉纳金”。印度籍战俘被劝说、也被施压加入反英的印度国民军。欧亚裔和华人天主教徒家庭被送到马口垦荒，欧洲平民被关进樟宜。所有人都要面对配给，手里的占领当局钞票几乎成了废纸。

1943 年 10 月，日本把北马四州交给泰国。1944 年 11 月起，美国轰炸机空袭新加坡的船坞。日本投降后，1945 年 9 月 12 日，蒙巴顿海军上将在市政大厦接受东南亚日军投降。

<More title={{"en":"One occupation, different lives","zh":"同一场占领，不同的遭遇"}}>

对华人家庭来说，占领是从检证和屠杀开始的。榜鹅、巴西立和巴西班让的马来渔村继续出海捕鱼，为城里供应食物，也一样挨饿。印度人夹在印度国民军“解放印度”的号召和被迫入伍的压力之间；被送去修泰缅铁路的亚洲劳工来自各个族群，在那里死了数以万计。

在森美兰的马口，约 <Num s="S177">3,000</Num> 名欧亚裔和华人天主教徒移民在缺粮缺药的情况下开垦丛林；一份记载说 1944 年至 1945 年间<Num s="S177">约 500</Num> 人死亡，另一份记载说在 <Num s="S101">300 到 1,500</Num> 人之间。樟宜监狱挤进了 <Num s="S106">2,500</Num> 多名平民和战俘。1943 年 10 月“双十事件”之后，宪兵队拷打被拘留者和本地居民，蔡杨素梅也在其中。

</More>

<More title={{"en":"Bombs from the other side","zh":"另一方的炸弹"}}>

1944 年 11 月 5 日，<Num s="S120">53</Num> 架美国 B-29 轰炸机空袭海军基地的干船坞；此后直到 1945 年 3 月，盟军飞机多次轰炸船坞、吉宝港和油库。美国官方战史估计，1945 年 2 月 24 日的空袭毁掉了帝国船坞 <Num s="S120">39%</Num> 的仓库面积。这些是进攻一方自己的数字。船坞紧挨着人口稠密的街区，也有在那里做工的人，平民有死有伤，但至今没有找到可靠的死亡人数。
