Inside Malaysia, Singapore's People's Action Party and the Alliance that governed in Kuala Lumpur were soon at odds. In the 1964 federal election the PAP put up candidates on the peninsula. UMNO leaders and the Malay newspaper Utusan Melayu accused the PAP of neglecting Singapore's Malays. The PAP called for a “Malaysian Malaysia”, which Malay leaders heard as a challenge to the special position of Malays. Speeches and newspapers on both sides raised fear between communities.

On 21 July 1964 a procession of some <Num s="S156">20,000</Num> people marking the Prophet Muhammad's birthday was passing through Kallang when fighting broke out between Malays and Chinese. By early August <Num s="S156">23</Num> people had died and <Num s="S156">454</Num> were injured. On 2 September a Malay trishaw rider was killed in Geylang Serai, and new riots killed <Num s="S156">13</Num> people and injured <Num s="S156">106</Num>. Curfews closed the streets. Goodwill committees of community leaders went from district to district to calm people.

Indonesia's Confrontation reached Singapore too. On 10 March 1965 two Indonesian marines set off a bomb at MacDonald House on Orchard Road, killing <Num s="S157">three</Num> people and injuring <Num s="S157">33</Num>.

In mid-1965 the Tunku decided that Singapore must leave. On 7 August the two governments signed the separation agreement, and on 9 August Malaysia's Parliament voted <Num s="S219">126</Num> to none to let Singapore go. That day Singapore became an independent republic. At a press conference Lee Kuan Yew called it “a moment of anguish”. On 22 December Yusof Ishak became the first President. How the new country built an army, chose its national symbols and learned to live together is told in <TopicLink slug="singapore-after-1965">Singapore after 1965: Building a Nation</TopicLink>.

The two governments did not agree on the causes. The PAP said the July riots had been planned by UMNO activists, after weeks of a campaign in Utusan Melayu led by the UMNO politician Syed Ja'afar Albar. UMNO leaders, among them the Deputy Prime Minister Abdul Razak, said the clashes were spontaneous, and blamed the PAP's treatment of Malays. For the September riots, both governments blamed Indonesian agents. In July <Num s="S156">3,568</Num> people were arrested and in September <Num s="S156">1,439</Num>. The dead were Malays and Chinese, most of them ordinary people caught in the streets. Since 1997 Singapore's schools have marked 21 July as Racial Harmony Day.

The bomb went off at 3.07 p.m. in the building, which housed a bank and offices. Elizabeth Suzie Choo Kway Hoi and Juliet Goh Hwee Kuang, who worked in the bank, were killed; the driver Mohammed Yasin bin Kesit died after three days in a coma. The two marines, Osman bin Haji Mohamed Ali and Harun bin Said, were caught, sentenced to death in October 1965 and hanged at Changi Prison on 17 October 1968. In Indonesia they were honoured as heroes; when an Indonesian warship was named after them in 2014, Singapore protested. The two countries remember the same men in opposite ways.

<More title={{"en":"Separation: two capitals, two accounts","zh":"分家：两地的两种说法"}}>

In Parliament the Tunku said he had only two choices: to take “repressive measures” against Singapore's government, which he called repulsive, or to cut Singapore loose; he chose the second to avoid communal violence. Singapore's leaders presented independence as forced on them. Later records tell a more mixed story: Goh Keng Swee's papers, the “Albatross File”, show that he and Abdul Razak worked out the terms of separation in secret in July 1965, with Lee's agreement. Most of Singapore's ministers signed the agreement only after it was settled. One member of Malaysia's Parliament abstained in the vote; none voted against.

加入马来西亚以后，新加坡的人民行动党和在吉隆坡执政的联盟很快就闹翻了。1964 年的联邦大选，行动党在半岛派出候选人。巫统领袖和马来文报章《马来前锋报》指责行动党冷落新加坡的马来人；行动党则提出“马来西亚人的马来西亚”，马来领袖认为这是在挑战马来人的特殊地位。双方的演讲和报章，加深了族群之间的恐惧。

1964 年 7 月 21 日，约 <Num s="S156">2 万</Num>人参加的庆祝先知穆罕默德诞辰游行经过加冷时，马来人与华人发生冲突。到 8 月初，<Num s="S156">23</Num> 人死亡，<Num s="S156">454</Num> 人受伤。9 月 2 日，一名马来三轮车夫在芽笼士乃被杀，新一轮骚乱又造成 <Num s="S156">13</Num> 人死亡、<Num s="S156">106</Num> 人受伤。宵禁让街道空无一人。由各族领袖组成的亲善委员会，逐区安抚民心。

印度尼西亚的“对抗”也来到新加坡。1965 年 3 月 10 日，两名印尼海军陆战队员在乌节路麦唐纳大厦引爆炸弹，<Num s="S157">3</Num> 人死亡，<Num s="S157">33</Num> 人受伤。

1965 年年中，东姑决定让新加坡退出。8 月 7 日，两国政府签署分家协议；8 月 9 日，马来西亚国会以 <Num s="S219">126</Num> 票对 0 票通过，让新加坡退出联邦。当天，新加坡成为独立的共和国。李光耀在记者会上说，这是“痛苦的时刻”。12 月 22 日，尤索夫·伊萨成为第一任总统。新国家怎样建立军队、确立国家象征、学习和谐共处，见<TopicLink slug="singapore-after-1965">《1965 年后的新加坡：建设一个国家》</TopicLink>。
