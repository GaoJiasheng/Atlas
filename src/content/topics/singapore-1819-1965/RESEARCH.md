# Research register: Singapore, 1819–1965

Checked 17 October 2026. Research draft; unresolved entries are not publication clearance. Source keys below are research keys; topic sources.json assigns Atlas S numbers. The machine-readable companions are `scripts/geo/singapore-1819-1965/research/{evidence,tables,ww2-inherited-sources}.json`.

Evidence was read from accessible original texts, institutional articles, archival catalogs and explicitly identified indexed excerpts. A catalog record is not an original document; a search excerpt is not a page read. Figures were never completed from memory. Each source has only one short quotation below (at most 25 words total); tables and findings paraphrase it. Historical census categories and scopes are preserved. A missing quotation means no original passage was obtained, not an invented excerpt.
