<Lang en>

The tray holds objects made from different materials. Move a magnet close to each in turn. The iron nail, steel clips and steel can are attracted. Iron and steel are <Term id="magnetic-material">magnetic materials</Term>: they are attracted by a magnet. A steel clip need not already be a magnet to respond.

The aluminium can tab and plain copper disc are metals too, yet they stay on the tray. The wooden block and plastic ruler also stay put. These are <Term id="non-magnetic-material">non-magnetic materials</Term>. A shiny surface is not enough evidence. The stainless steel spoon needs care: the particular kind represented here is hardly attracted. Different stainless steels can give different results, so test the object rather than assuming from its name.

Place paper cards between a magnet and the clips. The magnet still attracts through the paper. Plastic, wood and aluminium also allow this attraction across them. Adding cards increases the distance from magnet to clips; it does not create an on-or-off shield. <FlyTo preset="tray-view">Look at the card test</FlyTo>

This is a <Term id="fair-test">fair test</Term> when only the number of cards changes. Keep the same magnet, the same clips and the same way of lifting. In this model, <Num s="S1">3 cards</Num> leave <Num s="S1">2 clips</Num> hanging; <Num s="S1">6 cards</Num> leave <Num s="S1">0 clips</Num>. Record the settings in the table and compare the rows. The numbers are illustrative, but the pattern shows attraction weakening as the gap grows.

测试盘里有不同材料制成的物品。把磁铁依次靠近它们，铁钉、钢回形针和钢罐会被吸引。铁和钢是<Term id="magnetic-material">磁性材料</Term>，能被磁铁吸引。钢回形针即使本来不是磁铁，也会有这样的反应。

铝拉环和没有货币图案的铜片也是金属，却留在盘里。木块和塑料尺也不动，它们都是<Term id="non-magnetic-material">非磁性材料</Term>。表面发亮并不足以判断。不锈钢勺要特别留意：这里选用的种类几乎不被吸引；不同种类的不锈钢可能有不同反应，应该测试物品，不能只凭名称判断。
