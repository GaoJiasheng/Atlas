<Lang en>

At the far end of the bench, a fridge magnet holds against a steel door. Its pull can also hold a paper note between magnet and door. Inside the flexible door seal is another magnet: a strip that attracts the steel cabinet. The soft seal fits against the cabinet edge while the magnetic attraction helps keep it there. <FlyTo preset="uses-bay">Look inside the door seal</FlyTo>

A cabinet catch uses the same basic attraction. A magnet fixed to the frame pulls a steel plate on the door. The door does not need a second magnet. Compare this with the aluminium tab in the test tray: a magnet does not stick just because something is metal. Choose a material that is attracted for the plate.

The compass is another use. Its needle turns freely, giving a direction when other magnets are kept away. A nearby magnet can make it point elsewhere. Useful devices depend on the surroundings as well as on the magnet itself.

For lifting scrap, control matters. An electromagnet can pick up steel while current flows and release it when the current is switched off. Use the bench switch to explore that idea with clips. Keep the cells unchanged and observe the pickup. This bench has a steel core that keeps some magnetism; a crane needs a core suited to releasing the load. Electric bells also use electromagnets to move a striker. Maglev systems use magnetic attraction or repulsion to support a train above its guideway. The small teaching models suggest these uses without reproducing full machines.

<More title={{"en": "A bell that repeats", "zh": "反复敲响的电铃"}}>

In a repeating electric bell, an electromagnet pulls a spring-mounted striker. Its movement opens a contact, interrupting current. The spring returns, the contact closes and the action repeats. The moving part and the contact work together; a continuously energised magnet alone would not keep ringing.

</More>

实验台右端，冰箱贴吸在钢制门板上，隔着便条也能把纸压住。柔软的门封里面还有磁铁：磁条吸住钢制箱体。门封贴合箱体边缘，磁力帮助它保持贴紧；这里展示的只是门和箱体的一小段，不是整台冰箱。<FlyTo preset="uses-bay">看门封里面</FlyTo>

橱柜磁吸门扣用同样的吸引。固定在门框里的磁铁吸住门上的钢片，不必在门上再装磁铁。和测试盘中的铝拉环比较，就能看出不是金属都能吸住；做吸片必须选会被磁铁吸引的材料。

指南针也是用途之一。磁针能自由转动，远离其他磁铁时可用来辨别方向；附近有磁铁，就可能改指别处。器具能否正常工作，除了看磁铁本身，也要看周围环境。

吊运废钢时，需要控制什么时候吸、什么时候放。电磁铁通电吸起钢材，断电释放。用台上的开关和回形针观察这种控制，保持电池不变，只看开关造成的差别。台上的钢芯会留一点磁性，起重机则需要容易释放负载的芯材。电铃用电磁铁带动敲铃部件；磁浮系统用磁吸引或排斥支撑列车。旁边的小模型帮助联系用途，没有重现整台机器。

连续敲响的电铃里，电磁铁拉动弹性支撑上的敲铃部件，运动使触点分开，电流中断。弹性部件返回后触点重新接通，动作便能重复。运动部件和触点共同起作用；只让磁铁一直通电，并不会自动反复敲铃。
