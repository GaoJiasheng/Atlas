## Every 待核 item and disposition

- F-4 / §4.6, “Singapore about 40 µT”: not verified with a dated WMM calculation. Omit the local TYPICAL claim. Keep 40 µT only as the explicitly chosen horizontal SIM background (S1). S16 verifies the global scale of about 30–60 µT; total intensity and horizontal intensity must not be conflated.
- F-9, stainless spoon: verified by BSSA chapter 9 (S9). Model a selected austenitic spoon, hardly attracted. Omit the unsupported claim that most Singapore cutlery is austenitic; other stainless structures can be attracted.
- F-11, force table: resolved by design arithmetic. With 8 mm pole inset and 100 mm bar length, f(g)=1/(g+0.016)²−2/(g+0.100)²+1/(g+0.184)² gives 12.224/41.120/100 % at gaps 6/3/1.5 cm after normalisation. Display rounds to 12/41/100. This verifies internal SIM consistency, not a measured magnet law for every specimen.
- §4.2, “magnetic length is about 0.85 of geometric length”: not independently verified. The 0.84 effective length is a declared design assumption, not an empirical universal constant. S6 does not establish it.
- F-12, “about 20 strokes”: no universal experimentally supported count was obtained. Omit a recommended fixed count. Repeated same-direction strokes are supported by S19; the 0/10/20/30 → 0/2/4/6 table is explicitly illustrative S1. The return stroke is lifted clear in this model.
- F-15, Curie point and blows: qualitative demagnetisation and iron’s approximately 770 °C Curie point verified in S4. Optional beyond-syllabus background; no pupil heating or striking activity is prescribed.
- F-16, materials: ceramic permanent magnets and retained magnetisation in iron alloys verified in S22. Specific material assignments for the generic bar/ring/fridge models are DESIGN. Do not claim measured remanence ranges, a particular school product composition or that every fridge magnet has one construction. Material recipes and comparisons outside the syllabus are omitted from pupil text.
- F-17 / P16 / P24, one-sided flexible-sheet magnetisation, Mallinson paper and exact fridge-seal patent construction: not verified. Omit the one-sided-flux and alternating-stripe explanation and do not attribute a patent. Refrigerator attraction is supported by S15; the visible magnetic strip and generic seal cross-section are an authored design representation.
- F-18 / P19, school bar magnets typically 75–150 mm: supplier range not verified. Omit the market-range claim; 100 mm is DESIGN only. Needle, cell and bench dimensions are likewise authored choices, not supplier quotations.
- F-19, Shen Kuo priority and approximately 1088 date: not verified from a primary historical source and omitted. S11 is used for a museum instrument, not for compass-invention priority.
- F-20 / P15, Shanghai line length, opening date and changing operating speeds: operator claims not verified and omitted. Generic attraction-based EMS and the difference from repulsive EDS are verified by the primary US DOT report (S21); no current route claim is made.
- F-21 / P20, electric bell: verified in the University of Kentucky’s own demonstration (S24): attract, open contact, strike, spring back, reconnect. S17 is a motor reference and is not used as evidence for a bell.
- F-22 / P21, Singapore-specific steel/aluminium recycling practice: NEA or local recycler primary detail not verified and omitted. Generic scrap-lifting electromagnets are supported by S4/S8; contrast with aluminium is a material comparison, not a claim about a named recycling facility.
- F-23 / P22, Singapore circulating coins and attraction: MAS claim not verified and omitted. The copper coin-shaped test blank is an unmarked piece of copper, not a reproduction of Singapore currency, and does not inherit a currency composition claim.
- P12 / P25, misconception research: primary Smolleck and Hershberger (2011) full text verified (S13). Hickey and Schibeci (1999) original was inaccessible; Driver’s book was not read. Neither is cited as if read; no pupil prevalence percentage is asserted.

## Data contract note: docs/22 §4.3 versus the circuit-era schema

The authoring instruction explicitly requires §4.3 names when they conflict with the existing sim schema. Keep `gap`, `facing`, `compass-at`, `cards`, `strokes`, `em-switch`, `em-cells`, `top-ring`; keep outputs `force`, `needle-bearing`, `clips-cards`, `clips-stroke`, `clips-em`, `field-state` . Keep `bind`, `overlays`, the `field-lines` and `iron-filings` state bindings, compass `at: compass-ring` and `rotate.settle`, and the paper-clip tables exactly to that contract. Do not rename controls to the older model’s shorthand to make validation pass.

At authoring time the circuit-era schema expects ascending step values; §4.3 explicitly gives gap values `[6,3,1.5]`. It accepts count options shaped as an object, whereas §4.3 uses `count: true`. It requires a rotation scale where §4.3 gives `rotate.axis` and `rotate.settle`. It lacks compass `at`, part/flow `state`, `overlays` force arrows, and `magnet.earth` / `magnet.rings`; its state-id validator rejects required decimal-gap keys such as `ns-1.5`. Existing numeric outputs retain the circuit-era `from` mapping wherever §4.3 does not override it. These are engine-integration differences, not licence to change the documented lesson data. The final `pnpm validate` report is the authority for which remain after the concurrent engine work.

Clip-table entries in S1 must agree with sim data, chapter text and captions: cards 10/5/3/2/1/1/0; strokes 0/2/4/6; cells 3/6 and off 1. The changing scene results stay SIM when RUN is paused. Field and filings states are model representations; no P3 main text introduces formal “magnetic field” terminology, shielding or induction.

## Authoring and engine handoff details

- The topic ID `magnets` already occupies the global ID namespace. Its magnet-object group is therefore `magnet-set`; the visible bilingual group name remains Magnets / 磁铁. All sim controls and outputs keep the §4.3 names.
- Requested object substitutions preserve the spec binding IDs: `aluminium-can` is a can tab, `copper-wire` is a plain solid-copper coin-shaped blank, `plastic-button` is a plastic ruler, and `steel-rod` is the steel needle. The eighth steel-can comparator is retained. The crane and maglev teaching miniatures are geometry within the selectable cabinet-latch display, keeping exactly 35 selectable parts and 3 context objects.
- The numerical source/spec table leaves horseshoe, ring, clip, can-capacity and gasket catalogue dimensions unverified. Every chosen size here is DESIGN; no No. 1 clip standard, 330 ml can capacity, supplier size range or exact commercial seal cross-section is claimed.
- Field-line state payloads extend the existing flow path shape: `states[state]` is an array of `{id, path}`. Filings use `primitive.kind: filings`, `size: [0.002,0.0003,0.0003]`, and `states[state]` arrays of scene-space `{at, rotation}` instances; these follow the established placement-vector convention, because §4.3 names state bindings but does not define the instance buffer representation. Engine integration must confirm this payload shape rather than silently discard it.
- Twelve states contain 24 lines and 2,400 filings each. Lines were integrated offline from Gilbert pole-pair directions using RK4 with 1 mm steps, sampled every seventh step and quantised to 0.1 mm, stopping near a south pole or at the display boundary. Filings use deterministic seed 82, planar projected directions and square-root-strength rejection sampling. They are precomputed data, with no external requests. The final engine generator can replace these buffers using the same named states.
- The §4.3 illustrative B-only flip mapping would label both N–N and S–S while keeping A unchanged. The authored two-pole data instead turns B for N–N and A for S–S (`a-flipped`), giving actual like poles in both cases. This is a mapping correction, not a renamed control or output.
- The barrier setup is raised so the hanging chain clears the bench. Cropped fridge furniture and the non-operating use miniatures are schematic teaching displays. Neither the furniture nor the miniatures claim commercial dimensions.
- See [VALIDATION.md](../VALIDATION.md) for the actual residual errors, masked integration issues, the launcher workaround and the separate established-schema audit. In particular the spec requires a count of stroking clip extras while the old engine counts whole repeated parts; this remains an explicit engine handoff item.
