<Lang en>

A green patch on a regional map does not tell the whole story of its plants. Begin with Singapore as a country-scale location reference, then compare the named cases in the reading panel. NParks describes primary rainforest at Bukit Timah Nature Reserve. At Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve, it describes wetlands and mangroves, including habitat important for migratory birds. These are particular places, rather than a vegetation map of all Southeast Asia.

A <Term id="term-mangrove">mangrove forest</Term> is a coastal plant community. The drawing focuses on an arching stilt-root growth form. NParks records such roots for Rhizophora apiculata. Follow the visible roots from the trunk towards the ground, then look at the leaves above. The drawing helps us notice a plant's structure; it does not identify every tree in a wetland or imply that every mangrove has this root form.

Select Bukit Timah and then Sungei Buloh. The case descriptions change, while Singapore remains the location reference. The map does not place a park point or draw a park boundary. To compare these habitats, use the named descriptions and the figure instead of treating the country's entire land area as rainforest or mangrove.

Both reserves are ASEAN Heritage Parks. NParks also describes regional work involving biodiversity knowledge-sharing and protected areas. Conservation can therefore connect a local habitat with neighbours elsewhere. Looking carefully at plants, their surroundings and the limits of the map is a starting point for understanding what that <Term id="term-cooperation">cooperation</Term>, or working together, is about.

区域图上的绿色，不能把植物的情况说完整。先以新加坡作国家尺度的位置参考，再比较阅读面板中的具名案例。国家公园局介绍，武吉知马自然保护区保留了原始雨林；双溪布洛湿地保留区则有湿地和红树林，也是候鸟的重要栖息地。这些是具体地方，不能据此画出整个东南亚的植被分布。

<Term id="term-mangrove">红树林</Term>是一类海岸植物群落。图中重点是弯拱的支柱根形态，国家公园局在 Rhizophora apiculata 的介绍中有相应记录。沿着树干往下看根，再往上看叶，能帮助观察植物结构；这幅图却不能鉴定湿地里的每一棵树，也不表示所有红树都长同一种根。

依次选武吉知马和双溪布洛，案例文字改变，新加坡仍只作位置参考。地图没有新增公园点，也没有描画公园边界。比较栖息地要读名称与说明，不能把整片新加坡国土都看成雨林或红树林。

这些保护区同属亚细安遗产公园。国家公园局也介绍区域间的生物多样性知识分享和保护区合作。本地植物与远方邻国因此有了联系；先仔细观察植物、周围环境和地图的限度，才能进一步理解<Term id="term-cooperation">合作</Term>的内容，也就是在共同事务上携手行动。
