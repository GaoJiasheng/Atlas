<Lang en>

Singapore has different kinds of green places. NParks describes primary rainforest at Bukit Timah Nature Reserve. Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve has wetlands and mangroves, and provides important habitat for migratory birds. Read the two descriptions to compare these environments.

A <Term id="term-mangrove">mangrove forest</Term> is a coastal plant community. Look at the drawing's arching stilt roots. NParks describes this root form for Rhizophora apiculata. Follow the roots from the trunk towards the ground, then look up at the leaves. Mangroves can have different root forms; this drawing shows one of them.

Select Bukit Timah, then Sungei Buloh. Each has its own description, while both highlight Singapore. The map locates their country, without drawing park points or boundaries. To learn about the habitats, compare the descriptions and the plant drawing.

Both reserves are ASEAN Heritage Parks. NParks describes countries sharing knowledge about biodiversity—the variety of living things—and working together on protected areas. This <Term id="term-cooperation">cooperation</Term> connects local habitats with neighbours elsewhere. Countries can learn from one another while caring for different environments.

<Lang zh>

新加坡有不同的绿色环境。国家公园局介绍，武吉知马自然保护区保留了原始雨林。双溪布洛湿地保留区有湿地和红树林，也是候鸟的重要栖息地。读一读两个地方的介绍，比较它们的环境。

<Term id="term-mangrove">红树林</Term>是一类海岸植物群落。看看插图里弯拱的支柱根，国家公园局也在 Rhizophora apiculata 的介绍中记录了这种根形。沿着树干往下看根，再往上看叶。不同红树的根形可以不同，这幅图展示其中一种。

依次选择武吉知马和双溪布洛。两个地方的介绍不同，地图上高亮的都是新加坡。地图标示它们所在的国家，没有画出公园点或边界。要认识栖息地，可以比较文字介绍和植物图。

这两个保护区都是亚细安遗产公园。国家公园局介绍，各国会分享生物多样性知识，也就是认识各种生物的知识，并在保护区工作上合作。这种<Term id="term-cooperation">合作</Term>把本地栖息地与邻国联系起来。各国照顾的环境不同，也能互相学习。
