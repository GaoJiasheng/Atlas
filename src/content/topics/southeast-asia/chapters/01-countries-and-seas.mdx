<Lang en>

Does every country in Southeast Asia meet the sea? Find Lao PDR, known in Chinese as 寮国 or 老挝人民民主共和国. It is <Term id="term-landlocked">landlocked</Term>, which means it has no coastline. Follow its outline and find the countries around it. Compare it with neighbours that have beaches and sea ports.

Malaysia has land on the peninsula and on part of Borneo, with sea between them. These separate pieces belong to the same country. A country can include both mainland and island areas. An <Term id="term-archipelago">archipelago</Term> is a group of islands. The word describes land, while an organisation's member list describes countries.

Find the Strait of Malacca between the surrounding land areas. A <Term id="term-strait">strait</Term> is a narrow sea passage. Compare it with the wider waters labelled South China Sea, Java Sea and Andaman Sea. Read each sea name together with the land around it.

The index lists the region's <Num s="S3">11</Num> countries, including Singapore and Timor-Leste. Use it to select a small country. Selecting a country highlights all its mapped land, including separate islands. Sea labels name water areas; they do not show ship routes or decide who owns the sea.

<More title={{ en: "Finding a small country", zh: "怎样找到小国" }}>

Tap a marker or choose a country from the index. A button or dot can be wider than the land it marks. Compare land area using the country outlines.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Reading sea names", zh: "怎样读海名" }}>

The labels sit within approximate sea areas from the marine map source. Those areas are not drawn as maritime boundaries. The Chinese names 南中国海 and 马六甲海峡 follow Singapore usage.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

东南亚每个国家都靠海吗？找到寮国，也就是老挝人民民主共和国。它是<Term id="term-landlocked">内陆的</Term>，意思是没有海岸。沿着它的轮廓找找周围的国家，再与有海滩和海港的邻国比较。

马来西亚的国土分布在半岛和婆罗洲的一部分，中间隔着海。分开的陆地仍属于同一个国家。一个国家可以同时包括大陆和岛屿。<Term id="term-archipelago">群岛</Term>是一组岛屿，这个词描述陆地；组织的成员名单则列出国家。

在周围陆地之间找到马六甲海峡。<Term id="term-strait">海峡</Term>是较窄的海上通道。把它与南中国海、爪哇海和安达曼海比较，看看各片水域周围有哪些陆地。

索引列出区域的 <Num s="S3">11</Num> 国，包括新加坡和东帝汶。小国不容易点到时，可以从名单选取。选中国家后，它在图上的全部陆地都会高亮，包括分开的岛屿。海名帮助辨认水域，不显示航线，也不判定海域的归属。

<More title={{ en: "Finding a small country", zh: "怎样找到小国" }}>

点击标记，或从索引选择国家。按钮和圆点可能比它标出的陆地更宽。比较国土大小时，要看国家轮廓。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Reading sea names", zh: "怎样读海名" }}>

海名标签放在海洋地图资料给出的大致范围内。这些范围没有画成海上国界。中文的“南中国海”和“马六甲海峡”采用新加坡用词。

</More>
