<Lang en>

A border around a country does not tell us whether it has a coast. Select Lao PDR, called 寮国 in this topic's Chinese text. It is <Term id="term-landlocked">landlocked</Term>, meaning that it has no coastline. Compare its outline with the neighbouring country shapes. You can find a place's position without assuming that every country in Southeast Asia has a beach or a sea port.

Look next at Malaysia. Its land appears on the peninsula and on part of Borneo, with sea between them. The outline belongs to the same country in both places. Mainland and island are useful words for describing land, but they cannot always divide countries into separate boxes. An <Term id="term-archipelago">archipelago</Term> is a group of islands, not a list of states belonging to an organisation.

The Strait of Malacca lies between land areas. A <Term id="term-strait">strait</Term> is a narrow sea passage. Its name label helps you read that passage on the map; the label is not the route taken by a particular ship. Compare it with the broader positions marked South China Sea, Java Sea and Andaman Sea. Read the sea names alongside the land around them.

The country index contains the region's <Num s="S3">11</Num> countries, including Singapore and Timor-Leste. Use it when a shape is too small to tap easily. Selecting a country identifies its complete mapped geometry, including separate islands. Sea labels serve a different purpose: they name water areas and make no claim about ownership of them.

<More title={{"en": "Finding a small shape", "zh": "怎样找到小国形"}}>

A marker or index entry is a way into the map. Its clickable area can be larger than the land beneath it. Judge land size from the sourced country outline, rather than from the size of a button or dot.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Names are not maritime boundaries", "zh": "海名不是海上国界"}}>

The marine source provides approximate reference areas for labels. This topic uses interior label positions and does not display those areas as sea boundaries. “South China Sea” and “Strait of Malacca” follow the Singapore Chinese wording 南中国海 and 马六甲海峡.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

国家有边界，不等于一定有海岸。先选寮国，也就是老挝人民民主共和国。它是<Term id="term-landlocked">内陆的</Term>，意思是没有海岸。沿着它的轮廓看看邻国，就能比较哪些地方相接、海在什么方向，不必先假定东南亚每个国家都有海滩或海港。

再看马来西亚：它的国土分布在半岛和婆罗洲的一部分，中间隔着海。分开的陆地仍属于同一个国家。大陆和岛屿是描述地形的词，不能把所有国家硬分为互不相交的两类。<Term id="term-archipelago">群岛</Term>指一组岛屿，也不是某个组织的成员名单。

马六甲海峡位于陆地之间。<Term id="term-strait">海峡</Term>是较窄的海上通道，图上的名称帮助辨认位置，并不是某艘船走过的路线。把它与南中国海、爪哇海和安达曼海的标签比较，再观察周围陆地，海陆关系会更清楚。

区域的 <Num s="S3">11</Num> 国都列在索引中，包括新加坡和东帝汶。小国形不易点到时，可以从名单选取；选中的是该国整套地图几何，包括分开的岛屿。海名则只帮助识别水域，不能当成海上所有权的说明。读图时要分清国名、陆地与水域名称，才不会把不同符号的意思混在一起。
