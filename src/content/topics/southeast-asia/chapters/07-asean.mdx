<Lang en>

The countries on a regional map did not all join ASEAN at once. The organisation was founded in Bangkok on 8 August 1967 by Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand: <Num s="S6">5</Num> founding members. The founding declaration addressed economic and social cooperation as well as regional peace and stability. Joining an organisation is a dated event, different from a country's shape on a map.

Brunei Darussalam joined on 7 January 1984. Viet Nam followed on 28 July 1995. Lao PDR and Myanmar joined on 23 July 1997, and Cambodia on 30 April 1999. Read these dates alongside the membership control. The change from Viet Nam's admission to the next admission adds both Lao PDR and Myanmar together, because their dates are the same.

On 26 October 2025, Timor-Leste became ASEAN's eleventh member, according to the ASEAN Secretariat's admission announcement. The organisation therefore has <Num s="S5">11</Num> members at this topic's 27 October 2026 reference date. Selecting an earlier membership date changes which countries are highlighted. All the country outlines remain today's shapes, including countries that had not yet joined at that date.

Cooperation means working together on shared matters. The conservation examples make this concrete: NParks describes regional biodiversity knowledge-sharing and protected-area work. ASEAN membership does not make its countries' habitats, occupations or cultures identical. After following the dates, return to the earlier examples and consider what neighbours can study and care for together.

区域地图上的国家，并非一起加入亚细安。组织于 1967 年 8 月 8 日在曼谷成立，创始成员有印度尼西亚、马来西亚、菲律宾、新加坡和泰国，共 <Num s="S6">5</Num> 国。成立宣言提出经济与社会合作，以及区域和平与稳定。加入组织有特定日期，与国形怎样画是不同的事情。

文莱于 1984 年 1 月 7 日加入，越南于 1995 年 7 月 28 日加入。寮国和缅甸在 1997 年 7 月 23 日同日入会，柬埔寨则在 1999 年 4 月 30 日加入。可把这些日期与成员控件对照，留意寮国和缅甸同时改变状态，不能在同一天中间虚构一个先后阶段。

亚细安秘书处的入会公告说明，东帝汶于 2025 年 10 月 26 日成为第十一个成员。因此，截至本题参考日 2026 年 10 月 27 日，成员共有 <Num s="S5">11</Num> 国。选择较早日期只会改变成员颜色，所有国形仍采用今天的地图，当时尚未入会的国家也继续显示。

合作就是在共同事务上携手行动。前面的保育案例使它更具体：国家公园局介绍区域间分享生物多样性知识和开展保护区工作。成员身份没有让各国的环境、工作或文化变成一样；邻国可以在差异中一起研究和照顾共同关心的事。
