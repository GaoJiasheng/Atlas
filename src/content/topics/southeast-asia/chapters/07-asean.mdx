<Lang en>

ASEAN began in Bangkok on 8 August 1967. Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand were its <Num s="S6">5</Num> founding members. Their declaration called for economic and social cooperation, and regional peace and stability. Other countries joined later.

Brunei Darussalam joined on 7 January 1984. Viet Nam followed on 28 July 1995. Lao PDR and Myanmar joined together on 23 July 1997. Cambodia joined on 30 April 1999. Choose these dates with the membership control to follow the organisation's growth.

The ASEAN Secretariat announced that Timor-Leste became the eleventh member on 26 October 2025. As of 27 October 2026, ASEAN therefore had <Num s="S5">11</Num> members. Choosing an earlier date changes the membership colours. The map uses present-day country outlines throughout, including for countries that had not yet joined.

Cooperation means working together on shared matters. The earlier conservation examples show what this can involve. NParks describes countries sharing biodiversity knowledge and working on protected areas. ASEAN's members have different habitats, jobs and cultures, and can learn from one another as they care for shared concerns.

<More title={{ en: "Two countries join together", zh: "两国同日加入" }}>

Before 23 July 1997, ASEAN had <Num s="S4,S6">7</Num> members. Lao PDR and Myanmar joined that day, bringing the number straight to <Num s="S4,S6">9</Num>. The map counts each country as a member from its admission day.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Steps towards membership", zh: "成为成员的步骤" }}>

Timor-Leste was an observer and received agreement in principle before it formally joined. The Secretariat's admission announcement gives 26 October 2025 as its membership date. Attending a particular meeting, or choosing a political representative for it, is a separate matter from joining the organisation.

</More>

亚细安于 1967 年 8 月 8 日在曼谷成立。印度尼西亚、马来西亚、菲律宾、新加坡和泰国是 <Num s="S6">5</Num> 个创始成员。成立宣言提出经济与社会合作，以及区域和平与稳定。其他国家后来陆续加入。

文莱于 1984 年 1 月 7 日加入，越南于 1995 年 7 月 28 日加入。寮国和缅甸在 1997 年 7 月 23 日同日入会，柬埔寨于 1999 年 4 月 30 日加入。用成员控件选择这些日期，看看组织怎样逐步扩大。

亚细安秘书处宣布，东帝汶于 2025 年 10 月 26 日成为第十一个成员。因此，截至 2026 年 10 月 27 日，亚细安有 <Num s="S5">11</Num> 个成员。选择较早日期，成员颜色就会改变。地图始终采用今天的国家轮廓，当时尚未加入的国家也会显示。

合作就是在共同事务上携手行动。前面的保育例子让我们看到合作的内容。国家公园局介绍，各国会分享生物多样性知识，一起开展保护区工作。成员国的栖息地、工作和文化各有不同，也能互相学习，照顾共同关心的事。

<More title={{ en: "Two countries join together", zh: "两国同日加入" }}>

1997 年 7 月 23 日以前，亚细安有 <Num s="S4,S6">7</Num> 个成员。寮国和缅甸当天一起加入，成员直接增至 <Num s="S4,S6">9</Num> 个。地图从入会当天开始，把该国计为成员。

</More>

东帝汶先成为观察员，并获得原则接纳，后来才正式加入。秘书处的入会公告给出的成员日期是 2025 年 10 月 26 日。出席某场会议，或为会议安排政治代表，与加入组织是不同的事情。
