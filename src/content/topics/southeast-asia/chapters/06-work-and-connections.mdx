<Lang en>

Rice farming connects land, water and knowledge. People in Southeast Asia also work in crafts, technology and tourism. A country can have all these activities and many others. Compare the skills and materials people use, while remembering that one example cannot describe everyone's life.

In Malaysia, songket provides a craft example. UNESCO describes it as a hand-weaving tradition. Weavers pass knowledge between people, just as terrace farmers do in a different setting. Songket's heritage record recognises a tradition in Malaysia; it does not mean the craft belongs only within its borders or that every weaver works at home.

Singapore's semiconductor industry offers a technology example. The Economic Development Board describes the design of integrated circuits—tiny electronic circuits—along with manufacturing facilities and related skills. Compare this work with farming and weaving. The materials, tools and workplaces differ. Selecting a case highlights its country rather than a farm, workshop or factory site.

Tourism connects visitors with places and communities. The terraces are a heritage landscape, and ASEAN has a roadmap for sustainable tourism. The raised-house drawing offers another way to explore how people live. A house, like an occupation, is one example among the many ways of life in a country.

<Lang zh>

种稻把土地、水和知识联系起来。东南亚的人们也从事手工艺、科技产业和旅游。一个国家可以同时有这些活动，还有许多其他工作。比较人们使用的技能和材料，也记住一个例子无法说尽所有人的生活。

在马来西亚，songket 是一个手工艺例子。教科文组织把它介绍为手工织造传统。织工在人与人之间传承知识，梯田农民也在不同环境中这样做。遗产记录承认马来西亚的这项传统，并不表示工艺只属于国界以内，或每位织工都在家中生产。

新加坡的半导体产业提供科技方面的例子。经济发展局介绍其中的集成电路设计，也就是设计微小的电子电路，以及制造设施和相关技能。把它与种稻和织造比较，看看材料、工具和工作环境有何不同。选择案例时，地图高亮所在国家，并非某个农场、织坊或工厂的位置。

旅游把来访者、地方和社区联系起来。梯田是文化遗产地景，亚细安也有可持续旅游路线图。高脚屋图提供另一个观察生活的角度。一种房屋或职业，都只是一个国家里多样生活的一例。
