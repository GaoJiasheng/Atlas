<Lang en>

The terrace case shows agricultural work as a relationship between land, water and knowledge. Farming is one way people make a living in Southeast Asia. Craft production, technology-based industry and tourism provide other ways to investigate work. These headings help compare activities; they do not divide the region into farming countries, craft countries and factory countries. A country can contain many activities and many different lives.

For a craft example, select Malaysia and read about songket. UNESCO describes it as a Malaysian hand-weaving tradition. Handwork involves knowledge passed between people, as the terrace case does in a different setting. This particular heritage example does not establish that every weaver works at home. Nor does recognising a tradition in Malaysia establish that culture stops at a country boundary.

Singapore's semiconductor activities offer a technology example. The Economic Development Board describes integrated-circuit design alongside manufacturing-related infrastructure and skills. Compare that description with the terrace and weaving cases. Each calls attention to different materials, skills and work settings. The country reference only locates the example; it is not a map of farms, weaving workshops or semiconductor factories.

Tourism brings another set of connections. The terrace landscape is a heritage example, while ASEAN has a roadmap for sustainable tourism. Thinking about visitors also raises questions about the environment and communities of a place. Housing deserves the same care: the raised-house drawing is a general form study, with no named building, community or date. Neither an occupation nor a house picture can represent everyone in a country.

<Lang zh>

梯田让我们看到，农业劳动把土地、水和知识联系起来。在东南亚研究工作，还可以看手工艺、科技产业与旅游。这些分类方便比较活动，不是给国家贴标签。一个国家可以有不同职业和生活，不能把人简单分成“种田国家的人”或“工厂国家的人”。

选马来西亚，可以读到 songket 织造的例子。教科文组织把它介绍为马来西亚的一项手工织造传统。织工也运用传承的知识，但环境和材料与梯田不同。这项遗产记录并没有说明所有织工都在家中生产，也不能用一个国家的记录断定文化只存在于国界之内。

新加坡的半导体活动提供科技产业案例。经济发展局介绍其中的集成电路设计，以及与制造有关的设施和技能。把它与梯田、织造比较，可以留意不同的材料、知识和工作环境。图上的国家只帮助定位，没有把农场、织坊或工厂的分布画出来。

旅游又把来访者与地方联系起来。梯田是文化地景案例，亚细安也有可持续旅游路线图，提醒人讨论环境与社区。住宅同样需要谨慎理解：高脚屋图只是一般形式研究，没有具体建筑、族群或年代。职业和房屋图片都不能代替整个国家里各种人的生活。
