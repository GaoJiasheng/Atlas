<Lang en>

The Mekong River Commission describes a river beginning on the Tibetan Plateau and reaching the sea through its delta. Between those places, the Mekong passes through or along China, Myanmar, Lao PDR, Thailand, Cambodia and Viet Nam. Read that description beside the map. A <Term id="term-river-system">river system</Term> brings connected reaches into the same explanation, even when country boundaries divide the land around them.

The mapped upper and lower reaches come from Natural Earth's drainage data. Its Lancang and Mekong features are selected together here. Their lines help locate the system, but the northern endpoint on this map is not a marked spring. Gaps in the acquired lines have been left as gaps. There is no moving water model to show which way a parcel of water travels.

Compare the Ayeyarwady, Chao Phraya and Red River with the Mekong. Each is a separately named system in the source. Follow the displayed line with your eyes and notice the country outlines nearby. A river may cross a border or run along one; its name should not make you assume it stays inside a single country. A map line is a reading aid, rather than a complete channel survey.

Mountain points introduce another kind of symbol. Select Kinabalu on Borneo, then use the index to find the other named peaks. A point locates a mountain without showing the shape or height of all the land around it. To explain how a mountain formed, we would need geological evidence as well as its position.

<More title={{"en": "A line is not a river-length measurement", "zh": "河线不能直接当河长尺"}}>

The Mekong River Commission gives an approximate length of <Num s="S8">4,900</Num> km. Natural Earth smooths drainage lines for display and can include connections through lakes. Measuring the line on this screen would measure that cartographic representation, rather than establish a new river length.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A mountain shape is only a beginning", "zh": "山的外形只是起点"}}>

A familiar-looking peak does not by itself establish how it formed. This map has no mountain-height ranking or relief model. The <TopicLink slug="inside-the-earth">Inside the Earth</TopicLink> topic provides a separate explanation of Earth materials and plate motion. Use that evidence rather than guessing from a silhouette.

</More>

</Lang>

湄公河委员会说明，湄公河发源于青藏高原，经三角洲流向海洋，途中穿过或沿着中国、缅甸、寮国、泰国、柬埔寨和越南。读这段说明时，要与地图互相核对。<Term id="term-river-system">水系</Term>把相连河段放在一起理解，周围陆地的国界却可能把它们分开。

图中的上、下游河线取自 Natural Earth。澜沧江与湄公河是来源里分开的要素，在这里作为同一水系选取。它们能帮助找位置，但图线北端并没有标明真实泉源，取得的河线若有缺口也照样保留。地图没有模拟水团运动，不能把线的端点顺序当作流向证据。

再比较伊洛瓦底江、湄南河和红河。这些都是来源中另有名称的水系。沿图线观察邻近国形，能看出河流位置与国家位置是不同层面的事情。湄公河有些河段穿过国境，有些沿着国境；一个河名并不表示整条河都在同一国。

山峰用另一种符号表示。先选婆罗洲的京那巴鲁，再从索引找其他具名山峰。峰点告诉人一座山大致在哪里，却没有展示周围土地的完整高低起伏，也不能单凭位置判断山怎样形成。若要解释成因，还需要地质证据。

<More title={{"en": "A line is not a river-length measurement", "zh": "河线不能直接当河长尺"}}>

湄公河委员会给出的河长约为 <Num s="S8">4900</Num> 公里。Natural Earth 为了制图而概括河线，也可能包含穿过湖泊的连接。量屏幕上的线，得到的是这套画法的长度，不能据此重定真实河长。

</More>
