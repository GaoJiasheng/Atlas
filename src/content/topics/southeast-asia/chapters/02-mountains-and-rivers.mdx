<Lang en>

The Mekong begins on the Tibetan Plateau and reaches the sea through its delta, according to the Mekong River Commission. Along the way, it passes through or along China, Myanmar, Lao PDR, Thailand, Cambodia and Viet Nam. A <Term id="term-river-system">river system</Term> connects river reaches across the land, even where national borders divide that land.

Select the Mekong to highlight its upper and lower reaches together. Natural Earth's drainage map names these parts Lancang and Mekong. The simplified lines have gaps, and their northern endpoint does not mark the river's spring. Use them to follow the system's position; they do not simulate flowing water.

Now compare the Ayeyarwady, Chao Phraya and Red River with the Mekong. These are separate river systems. Follow each line with your eyes and find the countries nearby. Some rivers cross borders, while others run along them. One river can connect several countries.

Find Kinabalu on Borneo, then choose the other peaks from the index. A point marks a mountain's location. It does not show the height or shape of all the surrounding land. Understanding how the mountain formed also takes evidence about its rocks and Earth's movements.

<More title={{ en: "How long is the Mekong?", zh: "湄公河有多长" }}>

The Mekong River Commission gives an approximate length of <Num s="S8">4,900</Num> km. Natural Earth simplifies river bends and may include links through lakes. Measuring this screen line gives the length of the simplified drawing, rather than a new measurement of the river.

</More>

湄公河委员会介绍，湄公河发源于青藏高原，经过三角洲流向海洋。途中，它穿过或沿着中国、缅甸、寮国、泰国、柬埔寨和越南。<Term id="term-river-system">水系</Term>把相连河段连在一起，即使周围的陆地被国界分开。

选中湄公河，上、下游河段会一起高亮。Natural Earth 水系图把这些部分分别称为澜沧江和湄公河。简化的河线有些地方留有缺口，北端也没有标出真实泉源。沿线可以观察水系的位置，但图线没有模拟水的流动。

再把伊洛瓦底江、湄南河和红河与湄公河比较。它们是不同的水系。沿着每条线看看附近的国家。有些河流穿过国界，有些沿着国界流。一条河可以把几个国家联系起来。

找到婆罗洲的京那巴鲁山，再从索引选其他山峰。点标出山的位置，没有显示周围土地的完整高低起伏。要了解山怎样形成，还要研究岩石和地球的运动。
