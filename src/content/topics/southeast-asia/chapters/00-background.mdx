<Lang en>

Southeast Asia is a <Term id="term-region">region</Term>: a group of places we study together. The United Nations lists <Num s="S3">11</Num> countries in its statistical grouping. They stretch from Myanmar in the west to the Philippines farther east and Timor-Leste in the south. Find Asia on the world map, then move closer to see the mainland, islands and surrounding seas. The chapter index lists all the countries.

The country outlines come from Natural Earth, a map dataset. They show present-day shapes. Singapore is small on this regional map, so a marker helps you find it. The outline shows its land area; the city point marks a named place.

The map offers three ways to explore. The geography view shows countries and landscapes. The climate view compares monthly averages for 1991–2020. The <Term id="term-asean">ASEAN</Term> view shows which countries had joined the organisation by a chosen date. It keeps the same present-day outlines as the colours change.

Later chapters introduce parks and rice terraces. Select a case to highlight its country, then read about the place and study its drawing. The highlight locates the country; it does not mark the park or field boundary.

<More title={{ en: "How a regional map shows detail", zh: "区域地图怎样呈现细节" }}>

Natural Earth's map data are in the public domain. Its mapmakers simplify small bends, islands and other details so that the whole region is easier to read. Use this map for an overview. A survey or legal boundary document gives different information.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A region and an organisation", zh: "区域与组织" }}>

The United Nations groups places for geographical statistics. ASEAN is an organisation that countries join. The last chapter follows its membership dates. Water also connects places across borders: explore the <TopicLink slug="water">water cycle</TopicLink> alongside this map.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

东南亚是一个<Term id="term-region">区域</Term>，也就是放在一起研究的一组地方。联合国的统计分组列出 <Num s="S3">11</Num> 个国家，从西边的缅甸，延伸到较东的菲律宾和南边的东帝汶。先在世界地图上找到亚洲，再移近看看大陆、岛屿和周围的海洋。本章索引列出了全部国家。

国家轮廓取自 Natural Earth 制图资料，显示今天的国土形状。新加坡在区域地图上很小，标记让你容易找到它。轮廓表示陆地面积，城市点则标出一个具名地点。

地图有三种读法。地理图显示国家和自然环境；气候图比较 1991–2020 年的月平均；<Term id="term-asean">亚细安</Term>（也称东盟）图显示各国在所选日期是否已经加入组织。成员颜色改变时，国家轮廓仍采用今天的形状。

后面的章节会介绍公园和水稻梯田。选一个案例，地图就会高亮它所在的国家。再读地方介绍，观察插图。高亮范围只帮助找到国家，并没有标出公园或田块的边界。

<More title={{ en: "How a regional map shows detail", zh: "区域地图怎样呈现细节" }}>

Natural Earth 的地图资料属于公有领域。制图者把细小弯曲、岛屿等细节简化，让整个区域更容易阅读。这张图适合看全局；实地测量和法律边界文件另有用途。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A region and an organisation", zh: "区域与组织" }}>

联合国把地方分组，用于地理统计。亚细安则是国家加入的组织。最后一章会依次介绍各国的入会日期。水也把国界两边的地方联系起来，可以配合<TopicLink slug="water">水循环</TopicLink>一起读。
