<Lang en>

Choose January and look across Southeast Asia. The colours show a <Term id="term-climatology">monthly climate mean</Term>: an average for the same calendar month over 1991–2020. Rainfall differs from place to place. Change to July and compare the colours. The rain legend stays the same, so each colour keeps the same meaning.

The rain estimates come from GPCP and use millimetres per day. The wind comes from NCEP reanalysis, which combines observations with a computer weather model. It describes the <Num s="S203">850</Num> hPa pressure level above the ground. The unit hPa measures air pressure. Rain fills grid cells; wind is shown at its own grid points. Inspect the example near the equator to read both values for the same location.

A <Term id="term-monsoon">monsoon</Term> is a large-scale movement of air that changes with the seasons. Singapore's Meteorological Service places the Northeast Monsoon from December to early March and the Southwest Monsoon from June to September. Singapore has rain all year, but the later Northeast Monsoon is relatively dry. <TopicLink slug="monsoon-and-weather">Monsoons and weather</TopicLink> follows these changes month by month.

Return to Singapore. During the Southwest Monsoon, its surface winds commonly come from the southeast to south. These near-ground winds can differ from the pressure-level arrows on the regional map. Use the map to compare climate patterns. For tomorrow's weather at school, check a weather forecast.

<More title={{ en: "Two grids at one location", zh: "同一个地点，两套格网" }}>

Both grids have <Num s="S203,S204">2.5</Num> degrees of spacing, but they start in different places. The inspector finds a wind point and a rain cell separately for the coordinates you choose. Beyond the data coverage, it shows unavailable. That means there is no reading, rather than no rain or no wind.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Which way is the wind blowing?", zh: "风往哪边吹" }}>

An arrow points where the average wind goes. A wind's name tells you where it comes from. January and July arrows need not point in exactly opposite directions at every place. They average many years; the wind on individual days varies.

</More>

<Lang zh>

选一月，看看整个东南亚的颜色。图上显示<Term id="term-climatology">月气候平均</Term>，也就是把 1991–2020 年中同一个月份的资料求平均。各地雨量并不一样。再换成七月，比较颜色。降水图例保持相同，所以每种颜色仍代表相同的读数。

雨量估计来自 GPCP，单位是毫米／日。风来自 NCEP 再分析，也就是结合观测和电脑天气模式得到的资料，表示地面以上 <Num s="S203">850</Num> hPa 气压层的风。hPa 是气压单位。雨按格子显示，风则用自己的一套格点。查看赤道附近的例子，可以读到同一位置的风和雨。

<Term id="term-monsoon">季风</Term>是随季节改变的大范围空气环流。新加坡气象署把东北季风期列为十二月至三月初，西南季风期为六月至九月。新加坡全年有雨，但东北季风后期较干。<TopicLink slug="monsoon-and-weather">《季风与天气》</TopicLink>按月份介绍这些变化。

回到新加坡。西南季风期，本地地面风常从东南至南方吹来。地面附近的风，可以与区域地图上气压层的风不同。地图帮助比较气候规律；要知道学校明天的天气，则要看天气预报。
