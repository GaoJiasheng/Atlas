<Lang en>

Start with January and look across the region. The colours show a <Term id="term-climatology">monthly climate mean</Term>, calculated for the same calendar month over 1991–2020. They do not show a storm travelling across the map today. Different places can have different rainfall in the same month. Change to July and compare them using the unchanged rain legend, so that the colours keep the same meaning.

The rain product is GPCP precipitation, displayed in millimetres per day. The wind product uses NCEP reanalysis at a pressure level of <Num s="S203">850</Num> hPa. These are different kinds of information. Rain is shown in cells, while wind is sampled at nodes on its own grid. Inspect the shared example near the equator: the location connects the readings, but it does not make the wind node and rain cell the same object.

A <Term id="term-monsoon">monsoon</Term> is a large-scale circulation that changes with the seasons. Singapore's Meteorological Service describes a Northeast Monsoon from December to early March and a Southwest Monsoon from June to September. Singapore has rain throughout the year, and the later Northeast Monsoon is relatively dry. The word monsoon therefore does not mean constant heavy rain. The topic <TopicLink slug="monsoon-and-weather">Monsoons and weather</TopicLink> follows the winds and rain month by month.

Return to the Singapore location view. Its typical Southwest Monsoon surface winds come from the southeast to south, according to the Meteorological Service. The regional pressure-level arrows are a different field from those surface descriptions. Keep the source, level, period and location in view before making a comparison; a regional average cannot forecast the weather at your school tomorrow.

<More title={{"en": "A grid cell is not a weather station", "zh": "格子不是学校气象站"}}>

The wind and rain grids each use <Num s="S203,S204">2.5</Num> degrees of spacing, but they have different origins and spatial support. A sample therefore looks up a wind node and the rain cell containing the same coordinates separately. Missing coverage is shown as unavailable, not as no rain or no wind.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Read arrow direction and wind names separately", "zh": "箭头方向与风名分开读"}}>

An arrow on this map points in the direction the average wind vector goes. A meteorological wind name describes where wind comes from. Neither convention requires the January and July arrows to be exact opposites at every place. The climate view averages many years; individual days vary.

</More>

<Lang zh>

先选一月，看看整个区域的颜色。图上是<Term id="term-climatology">月气候平均</Term>：把 1991–2020 年中同一个月份的资料合起来统计，不是今天正在移动的暴雨。各地同在一月，降水仍可不同。切到七月时，用不变的图例比较，才能保证同一种颜色仍代表同一种读数。

雨采用 GPCP 降水资料，单位是毫米每天；风采用 NCEP 再分析的 <Num s="S203">850</Num> hPa 气压层资料。雨按格子显示，风则在自己的格点上取值。查看赤道附近的共同示例时，坐标把读数联系起来，却没有把风节点和雨格变成同一个对象。

<Term id="term-monsoon">季风</Term>是随季节改变的大范围环流。新加坡气象署把东北季风期列为十二月至三月初，西南季风期为六月至九月。新加坡全年有雨，东北季风后期却较干，所以“季风”不能简单理解为不停下大雨。主题<TopicLink slug="monsoon-and-weather">《季风与天气》</TopicLink>按月份讲风和雨的变化。

回到新加坡的位置图，再分清地面和高空。气象署说，本地西南季风期常见的地面风来自东南至南方；区域气压层的箭头是另一种资料。比较前先读来源、层次、时期和地点，不能把区域多年平均当成学校明天的天气预报；如果要知道某一天是否下雨，还需要另外的天气资料。

<More title={{"en": "A grid cell is not a weather station", "zh": "格子不是学校气象站"}}>

风与雨的格距各为 <Num s="S203,S204">2.5</Num> 度，但起点和代表的空间范围不同。取样会分别寻找风节点与包含同一坐标的雨格。没有覆盖的地方显示无资料，不能补成无雨或无风。

</More>
