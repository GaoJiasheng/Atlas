<Lang en>

The light ruler keeps a distance fixed while changing the unit used to describe it. A <Term id="light-year">light-year</Term> is the distance light travels in vacuum in a year, about <Num s="S29">9.46 trillion kilometres</Num>. Despite the word year, it measures distance. A travel time and a distance are related by the speed of light, but they answer different questions. <Num s="S29,S37" />

Start with an AU. At the speed of light, crossing this defined length takes about <Num s="S37">eight minutes nineteen seconds</Num>. This calculation is useful for comparing scales; the display is not a real-time journey of a photon. The planetary ruler already covers enormous distances by everyday standards, yet the gap to another star is much greater. <Num s="S7,S37" />

Select Proxima Centauri, the closest star beyond the Sun. It is about <Num s="S17">4.24 light-years</Num> away. That is the distance its light crosses in about <Num s="S17">4.24 years</Num>, rather than a promise about how long a spacecraft journey would take. The Sun remains the nearest star to Earth. The selected marker labels a distance beyond the planetary ruler, rather than enlarging a real neighbouring star to that width. <Num s="S17,S3" />

Finally pull back to the <Term id="milky-way">Milky Way</Term>, the <Term id="galaxy">galaxy</Term> containing our Sun and solar system. A galaxy is a large system of stars and other matter held together by gravity. The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy, with a stellar disc roughly <Num s="S16">100,000 light-years</Num> across. The outside view reconstructs that broad structure. We live inside it; no photographer has travelled outside our galaxy to take this portrait. The solar system is a small part of a much larger stellar setting. <Num s="S16,S24,S35" />

<More title={{ en: "The light ruler's arithmetic", zh: "光尺的计算" }}>

The design calculation uses light speed <Num s="S7">299,792.458 kilometres per second</Num> and a Julian year of <Num s="S7">365.25 days</Num>. Speed multiplied by the year's duration gives the light-year; AU divided by speed gives light-travel time. Rounded reading-panel values describe those calculations without implying equally precise galaxy dimensions.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Inside a galaxy", zh: "从星系内部看" }}>

The disc's quoted width is a rough estimate, not a sharp outer wall or the full extent of surrounding matter. The night-sky band called the Milky Way is our view through the galaxy from inside its disc. A structural model can show the overall arrangement from outside while an actual observer sees along directions from within. <Num s="S16,S35" />

</More>

<Lang zh>

光尺保留同一段距离，只改变描述它的单位。<Term id="light-year">光年</Term>是光在真空中一年走过的距离，约 <Num s="S29">9.46 万亿千米</Num>。名称里虽然有“年”，它量的却是长度。光行时间与距离能用光速联系起来，但前者回答多久，后者回答多远。 <Num s="S29,S37" />

先选一天文单位。光走过这段规定长度，约需 <Num s="S37">八分十九秒</Num>。这项计算用于比较尺度，屏幕并没有让光子按真实速度飞完旅程。行星距离尺对日常生活已经很大，换到另一颗恒星，间隔又远远超过这个尺度。 <Num s="S7,S37" />

太阳以外最近的恒星是比邻星，距离约 <Num s="S17">4.24 光年</Num>。也就是说，它的光要走约 <Num s="S17">4.24 年</Num>，不是说飞船一定用这么久。离地球最近的恒星仍是太阳。比邻星标记表示行星距离尺之外的一段距离，标记的宽度不是恒星实际大小。 <Num s="S17,S3" />

最后拉远到<Term id="milky-way">银河系</Term>，也就是包含太阳与太阳系的<Term id="galaxy">星系</Term>。星系是引力维系的大型系统，含大量恒星及其他物质。银河系属于棒旋星系，恒星盘直径粗略约 <Num s="S16">10 万光年</Num>。外部视图重建的是整体结构，我们实际住在它内部；这不是摄影者飞到银河系外拍下的肖像。太阳系只是这片恒星世界中的一小部分。 <Num s="S16,S24,S35" />

<More title={{ en: "The light ruler's arithmetic", zh: "光尺的计算" }}>

设计计算采用光速每秒 <Num s="S7">299,792.458 千米</Num>，儒略年为 <Num s="S7">365.25 日</Num>。光速乘一年的秒数得到光年；天文单位除以光速得到光行时间。阅读面板用取整数字，不表示银河系尺寸也有同样精度。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Inside a galaxy", zh: "从星系内部看" }}>

星系盘宽度是粗略估计，不是一堵界限分明的外墙，也不包括周围物质的全部范围。夜空中称作银河的亮带，是我们从盘内向外看的结果。结构模型能从外侧展示整体，真实观察者则沿着内部位置的视线观看。 <Num s="S16,S35" />

</More>
