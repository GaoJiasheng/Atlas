<Lang en>

The driver and receiver stay in place while the material between them changes. First select air, then water, then the chosen solid. Each provides matter through which a mechanical sound disturbance can travel. A solid does not have to flow like water to transmit sound: local movement can be passed through a connected material. [S3]

The last choice is an ideal <Term id="vacuum">vacuum</Term>, a region with no matter. The model keeps the source active but removes the medium across the gap. There is then no sound-wave path to the receiver. “No transmission” is the result of missing matter, not an exceptionally long travel time or an instantaneous arrival. [S1, S3]

Use the card to compare the media while keeping the distance fixed. The travel-time reading comes from the stated distance divided by the selected teaching speed. The model uses a different speed for each material example. These choices illustrate that sound speed depends on the medium and its conditions; they do not establish a universal ranking of every gas, liquid and solid. [S1, S5, S16]

Only the air setting shows the row of oscillating markers. The water surface and solid bar identify alternative paths; their plain appearance does not mean there is no transmission. The same air-particle diagram would not explain every kind of sound motion in those materials. [S1, S3]

For a separate observation, leave air selected and use the source switch: its speed remains a material value, but there is no travel event. Record the medium alongside its result, including the vacuum case. That preserves the distinction between a numerical travel time and no propagation. Compare the materials themselves in <TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="air-is-matter">States of Matter</TopicLink> and <TopicLink slug="materials" ch="before-you-start">Materials Around Us</TopicLink>.

<Lang zh>

驱动面与接收处保持原位，中间的材料依次改变。先选空气，再选水和本例的固体。它们都能为机械声音扰动提供传播路径。固体不必像水一样流动，也能把局部运动传开；不能因为材料看起来不流动，就断定它不能传声。[S3]

最后的选项是理想<Term id="vacuum">真空</Term>，也就是没有物质的区域。模型仍启用声源，却移除了间隙中的介质，因此没有声波到达接收处的路径。“无传播”不是等待特别久，也不是瞬间到达，而是缺少传递扰动的物质。[S1, S3]

在实验卡上保持距离相同，只换介质。时间读数由距离除以注明的教学声速得到。本例为不同材料选用不同声速，用来说明速度与介质及其条件有关；不能把这些选定值推广为所有气体、液体、固体的统一排名，更不能只凭密度判断。[S1, S5, S16]

只有空气选项显示一排往复运动的点。水面和固体横杆只标明另外的传播路径，外观静止不等于没有传播。这里没有把空气点列直接换个颜色，当成水或固体的完整运动模型。[S1, S3]

另作一次观察时，先保持空气不变，再关闭声源：速度仍是材料常数，却没有传播事件。记录时把介质与结果一起保留，也记录真空的“无传播”。这样才能分清有数值的传播时间和没有传播事件。材料本身的性质可接着看<TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="air-is-matter">物质的三态</TopicLink>与<TopicLink slug="materials" ch="before-you-start">身边的材料</TopicLink>。
