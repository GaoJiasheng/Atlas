<Lang en>

Watch the highlighted air marker beside its fixed tick. Count how often it repeats the same motion, rather than how far it moves. <Term id="frequency">Frequency</Term> is the number of cycles in a second. Its unit is the <Term id="hertz">hertz</Term>: a frequency of <Num s="S4">one hertz</Num> means <Num s="S4">one cycle each second</Num>. [S4]

For a simple tone, a higher frequency corresponds to a higher <Term id="pitch">pitch</Term>, the highness or lowness we hear. The card changes the input from <Num s="S1">250 Hz</Num> to <Num s="S1">500 Hz</Num> while keeping the extent of motion fixed. This is a silent comparison of the model, so you follow its pace visually rather than judging a played note. [S6]

Both settings use the same slowdown. After slowing by <Num s="S1">250 times</Num>, they display <Num s="S1">1</Num> and <Num s="S1">2 cycles per second</Num>. Raising the frequency makes the marker repeat more often; it does not make the chosen excursion wider. Keep watching the same tick so that speed of repetition is easy to separate from distance moved. [S1]

This air-sound study is a <Term id="longitudinal">longitudinal wave</Term>: the local back-and-forth motion lies along the direction of propagation. The highlighted marker stays near its own tick while the compressed and spread-out regions move along the strip. The pattern travels without carrying that marker to the receiver. [S3]

The <Term id="wavelength">wavelength</Term> is the spacing between neighbouring positions at the same stage of the pattern. At the higher frequency it becomes shorter in unchanged air. More cycles fit into a given length; the sound does not therefore travel faster. Record each setting before comparing the readings. [S5]

<More title={{"en": "Scene length is schematic", "zh": "场景长度是示意"}}>

The strip uses <Num s="S1">41</Num> local markers. Its model wavelength changes from <Num s="S1">1</Num> to <Num s="S1">0.5 scene units</Num>. Scene units are drawing distances, not metres of measured air. The fixed relation between the settings preserves the intended comparison.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "The fixed-medium relation", "zh": "介质不变时的关系"}}>

Wave speed equals frequency multiplied by wavelength. When speed stays fixed, doubling frequency halves wavelength. The card compares that relation; it does not calculate temperature changes or a new material. [S5]

</More>

<Lang zh>

观察静止刻度旁被标出的空气点。先数它重复相同运动有多快，不要先看它离刻度多远。<Term id="frequency">频率</Term>是每秒重复振动的次数，单位是<Term id="hertz">赫兹</Term>；<Num s="S4">每秒完成一次循环</Num>，就相当于 <Num s="S4">一赫兹</Num>。[S4]

对于简单音，频率较高通常对应较高的<Term id="pitch">音高</Term>，也称音调，即听起来高或低的感觉。卡片把输入从 <Num s="S1">250 赫兹</Num> 改为 <Num s="S1">500 赫兹</Num>，同时保持往复范围不变。本课静音，我们用显示节律比较模型，不要求听辨实际音符。[S6]

两档采用相同慢放比例。都慢放 <Num s="S1">250 倍</Num> 后，画面分别每秒显示 <Num s="S1">1 次</Num> 与 <Num s="S1">2 次</Num> 循环。提高频率会使重复更快，并不扩大设定的往复范围。一直盯着同一个刻度，便容易把重复次数与运动距离分开。[S1]

空气中的这种声音模型是<Term id="longitudinal">纵波</Term>：局部来回运动与扰动传播方向相同。被标出的点留在自己的刻度附近，疏密区域却沿条带传开。传到接收端的是图样，不是这个点一路迁移过去。[S3]

相邻且处于相同振动阶段的位置之间的距离叫<Term id="wavelength">波长</Term>。空气条件不变时，提高频率会缩短波长，同一段距离容下更多循环，不表示声音传播得更快。先记录各档设置，再比较读数。[S5]

</More>
