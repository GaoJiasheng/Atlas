<Lang en>

Find the stretched string on its box, the drumhead over its shell and the open tube. Each specimen directs attention to a vibrating system. Similar-looking objects need not make the same sound, so start by identifying what moves and what supports that movement. These drawings show construction, not the measured motion of a particular instrument. [S1, S5, S7]

On the string specimen, the bridges support the section that can vibrate freely. The short-string preset moves the support and pairs the shorter active length with the higher frequency setting. For the same string under otherwise unchanged conditions, shortening the vibrating length raises pitch. This paired preset does not independently calculate the string’s tension or mass per length. [S1, S5]

The hollow body has a different role from the string. It helps couple string vibration to the surrounding air. A stronger response near a system’s natural frequency is <Term id="resonance">resonance</Term>; the instrument’s resonating system helps shape its response. Naming the string alone does not explain the complete path to a listener. The static illustration shows where the string is supported and how it relates to the box. [S7]

At the drum, a stretched membrane supplies another vibrating surface. At the tube, the air column is central to the sound. Wind instruments can change pitch by changing the effective length of the resonating air column. The simple open tube is a structural reference; it does not display calculated positions of stationary and strongly moving air. [S1, S7]

The Singapore Symphony Orchestra includes strings, winds and percussion. Use those families to compare vibrating parts rather than sorting instruments by appearance alone. The same ideas connect to <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="before-you-start">Energy Conversion</TopicLink>: motion and vibrating air belong in the explanation of the sound. [S18]

Resonance is a stronger response when a driving frequency is near a system’s natural frequency. Instrument bodies and air columns help shape the response. This bench illustrates their arrangement; it does not calculate a spectrum or membrane modes. [S7]

<Lang zh>

找到木箱上绷紧的弦、鼓壳上的鼓面，以及开口管。这些标本分别突出一种振动系统。物件外形相似，不一定发出相同声音，所以应先指出什么在动、什么提供支撑。这里展示结构，不是某件实物乐器运动的测量结果。[S1, S5, S7]

弦的支撑点之间有能够振动的有效段。短弦预设移动支撑位置，并把较短的有效长度与较高频率配在一起。同一根弦的其他条件保持不变时，缩短振动长度会提高音高。这组预设没有独立计算张力或单位长度质量，不能同时随意改变这些条件。[S1, S5]

空心箱体与弦承担不同作用。箱体帮助把弦的振动传给周围空气。驱动频率接近系统固有频率时，响应较强，叫<Term id="resonance">共振</Term>；共鸣系统也会影响响应。只说“弦在振动”，还没有交代通向听者的完整路径。静态插图帮助辨认弦的支撑、锚定位置及它与箱体的联系。[S7]

鼓的振动表面是绷紧的薄膜，管乐器的重要部分则是空气柱。改变有效共振空气柱的长度，可以改变音高。图中的开口管只作结构参考，没有画出经过计算的空气静止点或运动最强的位置，不能把装饰线当成振动结果。[S1, S7]

新加坡交响乐团包含弦乐、管乐和打击乐。比较这些类别时，可把注意力放在振动部分，而不只看外形。与<TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="before-you-start">能量的转化</TopicLink>联系起来，运动与空气振动才能进入发声的解释。[S18]
