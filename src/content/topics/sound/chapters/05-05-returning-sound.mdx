<Lang en>

At this station the emitting and receiving symbols share a starting place. A reflector stands away from them. An <Term id="echo">echo</Term> is sound that returns after reflection. Follow the outward part of the route first, then the return. The source-to-reflector distance is only part of the journey needed to receive the echo. [S17]

In the default model the reflector is <Num s="S1">34 m</Num> away. Sound must travel that distance to the reflector and the same distance back, giving a total path of <Num s="S1">68 m</Num>. At the chosen air speed of <Num s="S16">340 m/s</Num>, the calculated return time is <Num s="S1">0.2 seconds</Num>. The calculation follows the whole route, even though the symbols are drawn close together on a small bench. [S1]

The moving mark represents a pulse’s position. It is not a parcel of air that leaves the source, strikes the wall and comes back. At the other station, air markers oscillate locally; here the envelope mark tracks where a short disturbance has reached. The two diagrams answer different questions. [S1, S3]

Use the distance card to compare the reflector presets. Keep the medium and source setting fixed, record each return time, and then compare the entries. A longer out-and-back route takes longer at the same speed. The moving mark holds after one completed trip, so the return can be inspected. [S1, S17]

This is an ideal reflection model. It calculates travel time, without predicting the reflected sound’s strength or whether a listener could distinguish it. The display is slowed separately from the continuous wave strip. Read the physical time rather than timing the animation with a watch. [S1]

The default physical return of <Num s="S1">0.2 s</Num> takes <Num s="S1">2 s</Num> to display because the echo is slowed <Num s="S1">10 times</Num>. Reflector presets of <Num s="S1">17</Num>, <Num s="S1">34</Num> and <Num s="S1">68 m</Num> give physical returns of <Num s="S1">0.1</Num>, <Num s="S1">0.2</Num> and <Num s="S1">0.4 s</Num>. The drawn wall positions are schematic, without a shared distance scale.

<More title={{"en": "An active-sonar connection", "zh": "与主动声呐的连接"}}>

Active sonar emits sound and detects its return. When the outward and return paths are equal, reflector distance equals sound speed multiplied by return time, divided by <Num s="S1">2</Num>. Our bench isolates this timing principle; it does not solve underwater refraction, absorption or complex reflected paths. [S17]

这个工位把发射处与接收处放在同一位置，反射面则在远处。声音经过反射再返回，便可能形成<Term id="echo">回声</Term>。先沿去程看到反射面，再沿回程返回起点。声源到反射面的距离只是旅程的一部分，接收到回声还需要返回这一段。[S17]

默认模型里，反射面离起点 <Num s="S1">34 米</Num>。声音去、回各走同样距离，总路程为 <Num s="S1">68 米</Num>。采用空气声速约 <Num s="S16">340 米每秒</Num>，计算返回时间为 <Num s="S1">0.2 秒</Num>。桌面符号画得很近，不改变这个按完整来回路径进行的计算。[S1]

移动标记表示脉冲的位置，不是一团空气离开声源、撞墙后再折返。另一个工位的空气点在局部往复；这里的包络标记则帮助观察短暂扰动到达哪里。两种图回答的问题不同，不能把回声标记当成真实空气粒子。[S1, S3]

在距离卡上比较反射面的不同预设。保持介质与声源设定不变，记录每次返回时间，再比较各行。同样速度下，来回路程较长就需要更多时间。标记完成一次往返后停留，方便检查返回位置。[S1, S17]

这是理想反射模型，只计算传播时间，没有预测返回声音有多强，也不保证听者能够分辨这次回声。显示慢放与连续波条带不同。比较时读取物理时间，不要用手表给动画计时。[S1]
