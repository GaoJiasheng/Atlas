<Lang en>

Start at the fork on the left of the bench. A <Term id="source">sound source</Term> is an object whose motion starts a sound disturbance. Its repeated movement near a rest position is <Term id="vibration">vibration</Term>. The small marker makes that local motion visible; the fork itself stays still as a reference shape. Real movement can be much smaller and faster than this display. [S1, S3]

Next follow the route towards the receiver. A <Term id="medium">medium</Term> is the matter through which sound travels. Air, water and a solid can provide such a path. The travelling disturbance is a <Term id="sound-wave">sound wave</Term>. It carries energy through the medium, while the material does not have to travel all the way from the source to the receiver. [S3, S5]

This topic is silent. Use the moving marks, fixed ticks and written readings to compare sources and paths. Pausing holds a visible instant; the chapter beats let you return to a particular comparison. You can make a prediction on an experiment card, or skip it and continue reading. No listening test is needed. [S1]

Begin with what moves, then compare the medium, the repetition of motion and its extent. Finally follow a reflected disturbance back to its starting place. Each experiment changes a stated factor while holding the others fixed. The diagrams are classroom models, with chosen scales and speeds; their readings are calculated rather than measured. [S1, S2]

The receiver ends this route. Continue inside the receiving system in <TopicLink slug="eyes-and-ears" ch="before-you-start">Eyes and Ears</TopicLink>. The energy connection starts in <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="before-you-start">Energy Conversion</TopicLink>.

<Lang zh>

从实验台左侧的音叉看起。发声的物体叫<Term id="source">声源</Term>，它的运动能在周围物质中引起声音扰动。物体在静止位置附近反复运动，就是<Term id="vibration">振动</Term>。叉臂旁的小点把局部运动放大，音叉本体则保持静止，便于认清结构。真实运动可能比画面更小、更快。[S1, S3]

沿着实验台看向接收处。传播声音的物质叫<Term id="medium">介质</Term>，空气、水和固体都能提供传播路径。在介质中传开的扰动叫<Term id="sound-wave">声波</Term>。它能传递能量，并不要求同一团物质从声源一路走到接收端。后面的静止刻度会帮助我们分清这两种运动。[S3, S5]

本课始终静音。只看运动标记、刻度与文字读数，就能比较声源和传播路径。暂停可以保留一个瞬间，章节节拍则能带你回到指定的比较状态。实验卡可以先作预测，也可以跳过预测继续阅读，不需要听音或调高设备音量。[S1]

接下来先认清哪里在动，再分别改变传播介质、运动节律与往复范围，最后观察反射后返回的扰动。每次比较都要留意哪些条件保持不变。这是课堂模型：尺度和速度有明确的设计选择，读数由方程计算，不是现场测量。[S1, S2]

接收处是这里的终点。想接着了解听觉，可看<TopicLink slug="eyes-and-ears" ch="before-you-start">眼睛与耳朵</TopicLink>；能量关系见<TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="before-you-start">能量的转化</TopicLink>。
