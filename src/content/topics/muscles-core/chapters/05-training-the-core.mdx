<Lang en>

Begin with a task you can control, not a demand to complete every card. The equipment order is barbell, dumbbell, cable, then bodyweight. A Romanian deadlift trains a <Term id="hip-hinge">hip hinge</Term>: hips move while the spinal muscles mainly hold the back shape. Gluteal and thigh muscles drive the lift; the erectors are not hip prime movers. Learn this with a qualified coach before adding load [M39] [M23].

A suitcase carry puts a dumbbell in one hand and asks the trunk to resist leaning. A Pallof press uses a sideways cable pull to challenge resistance to twisting. Move slowly enough to keep the intended position and continue breathing. Neither task isolates one abdominal layer. Stop the carry before grip or upright control deteriorates [M103] [M106].

Bodyweight options cover different demands. Curl-up practises a small trunk flexion; dead-bug and forearm plank oppose arching; side plank opposes side bending and rolling. Bird-dog reaches opposite limbs while keeping the pelvis steady. Standing knee raise provides a supported hip-flexion option; hanging knee raise adds grip, overhead support and a small pelvic curl. Every exercise is available in the chapter index, including those outside the selected beats [M99] [M105].

Choose an easier version before losing position. Increase challenge gradually when control improves, and allow recovery between demanding sessions [M23] [M24]. First learn with bodyweight or a light load under a qualified coach; anyone under <Num s="M25">eighteen</Num> should practise with adult supervision. This is general information, not medical advice; pain or injury needs professional assessment.

<Lang zh>

先选能控制的任务，不必把全部卡片做完。清单按杠铃、哑铃、绳索、徒手排列。罗马尼亚硬拉练<Term id="hip-hinge">髋铰链</Term>：主要动髋，脊柱肌尽量维持背部形状。驱动站起的是臀部和大腿肌肉，不应把竖脊肌称为伸髋主动肌；加重前先请合格教练指导 [M39] [M23]。

单手提哑铃行走要求躯干抵抗侧歪；抗旋转推用侧向绳索拉力挑战转动控制。动作要慢到能守住原定位置，并持续呼吸。它们不能孤立训练某层腹肌；握力或直立姿势开始变差，就结束提重 [M103] [M106]。

徒手任务各不相同：卷腹练小幅前屈；死虫式和前平板抵抗后仰；侧平板抵抗侧弯和翻转；鸟狗式伸对侧肢体，骨盆保持稳定。站姿抬膝可扶稳支撑，悬垂抬膝还要求握持、头顶支撑和小幅卷骨盆。没有进入选定节拍的动作，也都能从本章索引打开 [M99] [M105]。

姿势守不住时先退回容易版本，控制改善后再逐步加难，吃力训练之间留出恢复 [M23] [M24]。第一次先用自重或轻负荷，在合格教练指导下学；未满 <Num s="M25">18 岁</Num>在成人监督下练习。本页是一般知识，不是医疗建议；疼痛或受伤请寻求专业评估。
