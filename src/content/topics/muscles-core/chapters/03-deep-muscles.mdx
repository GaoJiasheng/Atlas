<Lang en>

Fade the surface and **internal oblique** appears beneath external oblique. Its fibres fan in several directions: many upper fibres run up and inward, while lower bundles arch towards the pubis. Through tendon sheets it reaches the lower rib margin, midline and pubic region. **Transversus abdominis** lies deeper, with mostly transverse fibres that compress the abdominal wall [M92].

These muscles attach partly through the <Term id="thoracolumbar-fascia">thoracolumbar fascia</Term>, connective tissue linking the back and abdominal wall. The visible meshes show muscle tissue; broad fascial connections are described on the cards. No surface touch can reliably identify one deep layer independently of its neighbours.

Behind the abdominal cavity, **quadratus lumborum** runs from the iliac crest and iliolumbar ligament to the last rib and upper lumbar transverse processes. It contributes to side bending and rib fixation. The **lumbar erector** display merges iliocostalis lumborum with longissimus thoracis; their long columns help extend or steady the back. Beneath them, **multifidus** has overlapping bundles reaching spinous processes above their origins [M92] [M97].

At the front of the hip, **psoas major** descends from the spine and **iliacus** gathers from the pelvic bowl. Together they form iliopsoas. Their tendon reaches the <Term id="lesser-trochanter">lesser trochanter</Term> of the femur; some iliacus fibres continue just below it. They cross the hip, so lifting the thigh is their main teaching action. Do not try to find them by pressing deeply into the groin. <FlyTo preset="back">Look behind the waist</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "What lies beyond this model", zh: "模型之外的结构" }}>

The diaphragm above and pelvic floor below cooperate with the abdominal wall. They are not exported here, so no contraction or treatment claim is made from the missing geometry. Breathing comfortably during a brace is a learning cue, not proof that one deep muscle has been isolated [M92].

<Lang zh>

把浅层调淡，**腹内斜肌**露在腹外斜肌下面。它的肌束朝不同方向展开：上部多向内上，下部呈弓形走向耻骨，并通过腱性薄片连到下位肋缘、白线与耻骨区。更深的**腹横肌**主要横向走行，牵拉时压缩腹壁 [M92]。

这些肌肉部分经<Term id="thoracolumbar-fascia">胸腰筋膜</Term>联系起来。筋膜是连接背部和腹壁的结缔组织；网格主要显示肌肉，宽阔筋膜连接在卡片说明。轻触体表，不能把某一深层肌从邻近结构中独立认出。

腹腔后方的**腰方肌**从髂嵴、髂腰韧带伸向末肋与上位腰椎横突，参与侧屈并固定末肋。**竖脊肌腰段**零件合并腰髂肋肌和胸最长肌，长肌柱让背部后伸或稳定。其下方的**多裂肌**短束层叠，连到起点上方的棘突 [M92] [M97]。

髋前方，**腰大肌**沿脊柱向下，**髂肌**从骨盆内面汇聚，两者合称髂腰肌。共同腱到股骨<Term id="lesser-trochanter">小转子</Term>，部分髂肌纤维延到其下方。因为跨过髋关节，抬大腿是本章重点作用；不要深按腹股沟寻找它们。<FlyTo preset="back">看腰部后方</FlyTo>
