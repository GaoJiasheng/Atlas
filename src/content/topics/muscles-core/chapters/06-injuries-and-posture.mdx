<Lang en>

A low-back ache sits in a region containing muscles, joints, nerves and other tissues. It is not a label for one damaged muscle. **Non-specific low back pain** means no specific disease or structural cause can confidently explain the symptoms. WHO reports that this describes about <Num s="M57">nine in ten</Num> cases. The anatomical model cannot determine which category a reader belongs to [M57].

An <Term id="non-specific-pain">unidentified pain source</Term> does not mean the pain is imaginary. It means that location and appearance alone are insufficient explanations. Sudden pain during a loaded movement should not be treated as a cue to push harder. Stop the painful task and seek assessment rather than choosing a muscle on the model and calling it strained. A strain is an injury label, not something this illustration can diagnose.

Posture also needs context. A photograph showing a curved back or tilted pelvis cannot measure a muscle's length, strength or contribution to pain. A review of systematic reviews found no consensus that spinal posture or physical exposure causes low back pain [M58]. This does not make every load or prolonged position equally comfortable; it prevents turning one posture into a diagnosis.

For learning, choose a manageable load, comfortable range and an easier support before fatigue changes the task [M23] [M25]. Movement and recovery are parts of general training, while persistent symptoms need an individual assessment. Do not deeply press the abdomen or groin to test iliopsoas, and do not assume isolated deep-muscle exercise is a universal cure. This page is general information, not medical advice; for pain or injury, consult a doctor or physiotherapist.

<More title={{ en: "Appearance cannot identify the cause", zh: "外观不能确定原因" }}>

A segmented abdominal outline, side-wall tension or a pelvic angle is not a clinical test. Anatomy helps describe the area; it does not replace a clinician's history and examination. The same caution applies to calling a deep muscle weak from a surface sensation [M57].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What the evidence supports", zh: "证据支持到哪里" }}>

WHO discusses rehabilitation as a way to support return to valued activities. The appropriate approach depends on the person and pain presentation [M57]. The cards teach general movements, not a rehabilitation programme or a guarantee against injury.

</More>

<Lang zh>

下背痛所在区域包含肌肉、关节、神经等多种组织，不是某块肌肉受损的直接标签。**非特异性腰痛**指不能明确用特定疾病或结构原因解释症状；世界卫生组织报告，这类约占 <Num s="M57">九成</Num>。解剖模型不能判断读者属于哪一类 [M57]。

<Term id="non-specific-pain">原因未能明确的疼痛</Term>不代表痛是想象出来的，只说明位置和外观不足以解释。负重动作突然引起疼痛，不应当作继续加力的信号。先停下疼痛任务并寻求评估，不要在模型点一块肌肉，就称它拉伤；本图不能作损伤诊断。

体态也要结合情况。照片里弯曲的背或倾斜的骨盆，不能测出肌肉长度、力量或疼痛贡献。一篇系统综述的综述发现，脊柱姿势或身体负荷是否导致腰痛，目前没有共识 [M58]。这并不是说所有负荷和久坐姿势都一样舒适，只是不能把一次看到的姿态当成诊断。

学习时采用可控制的重量、舒适幅度和较容易支撑，在疲劳改变任务前结束 [M23] [M25]。活动与恢复都是一般训练的一部分；症状持续不退，需要个人评估。不要深按腹部或腹股沟测试髂腰肌，也不把单练深肌当成通用疗法。本页是一般性知识，不是医疗建议；疼痛或受伤请咨询医生或物理治疗师。
