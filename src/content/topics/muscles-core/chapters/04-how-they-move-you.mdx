<Lang en>

Watch the directions before thinking about exercises. In trunk flexion, the lower chest moves towards the pelvis. Rectus and the obliques contribute to this pull. In extension, the spinal muscles help bring the trunk back. Quadratus and the side-wall muscles can bring the rib cage towards the pelvic rim during a side bend [M92]. These are tasks shared by several muscles.

Rotation uses a diagonal partnership. With the pelvis fixed, **left internal oblique** helps turn the chest left, together with **right external oblique**. The opposite pair assists a right turn. The words describe the body's own sides, even when the front view looks reversed to the reader. The deep multifidus also contributes to spinal control [M92].

During <Term id="hip-flexion">hip flexion</Term>, psoas and iliacus bring the thigh towards the trunk. This differs from curling the rib cage towards the pelvis. A knee raise can contain both actions; calling it an isolated lower-abdominal exercise misses the hip-flexor work. Watch the femur attachment as the thigh rises, rather than treating every forward movement as a spinal bend [M92].

A core muscle need not visibly shorten to work. An <Term id="isometric">isometric</Term> effort develops tension with little change in length. In <Term id="anti-extension">anti-extension</Term> work, the abdominal wall opposes a backward arch; in <Term id="anti-rotation">anti-rotation</Term> work, it opposes twisting. Side holds and carries also resist lateral bending [S2]. The still brace at the end is therefore a working position, not a rest. Highlight shows participation, not measured force or a ranking of muscles [S1].

<More title={{ en: "A schematic trunk pivot", zh: "躯干支点是示意" }}>

This rig uses a single trunk pivot, so a flexion or turn is a schematic direction cue. It does not reproduce individual lumbar joints or fixed-foot mechanics. The illustrative angles and shortening signals are design values; the anatomical attachments explain the actual pulling relationships [S1].

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Quiet does not mean relaxed", zh: "不动不等于放松" }}>

In a deadlift the hips and knees perform the large motion while the erector spinae work mainly isometrically: they hold the spine in a neutral shape instead of bending and straightening it [M55]. A plank similarly asks the abdominal wall to resist extension [S2]. The hold is highlighted without repeated shortening.

</More>

<Lang zh>

先看运动方向，再想训练动作。躯干前屈把下胸部带向骨盆，腹直肌与腹斜肌参与牵拉；后伸时，脊柱旁肌肉帮助躯干返回。侧屈把胸廓带向同侧骨盆上缘，腰方肌与侧腹肌共同参与，不是哪一块独自完成 [M92]。

旋转采用斜向组合。骨盆固定时，**左腹内斜肌**帮助胸口向左转，配合**右腹外斜肌**；向右转则换另一对。左右始终指身体自己的左右，正面看时可能与读者眼前方向相反。深处的多裂肌也参与脊柱控制 [M92]。

<Term id="hip-flexion">屈髋</Term>时，腰大肌与髂肌把大腿带向躯干；这与把胸廓卷向骨盆不同。抬膝可以同时包含两种运动，不能笼统叫作孤立练下腹。观察大腿抬起时的股骨附着，不要把所有向前运动都当成腰椎弯曲 [M92]。

肌肉不必明显缩短才算工作。<Term id="isometric">等长</Term>用力让长度基本不变、张力仍存在。<Term id="anti-extension">抗伸展</Term>抵抗后仰，<Term id="anti-rotation">抗旋转</Term>抵抗扭转；侧撑和提重还抵抗侧弯 [S2]。最后的稳定姿态仍在用力，高亮只表示参与，不是力量测量或肌肉排名 [S1]。

<More title={{ en: "A schematic trunk pivot", zh: "躯干支点是示意" }}>

本模型用单一躯干支点，前屈和旋转只提示方向，不逐节模拟腰椎或解算双脚固定时的运动。关节角和缩短信号是设计值；实际牵拉关系要结合解剖附着阅读 [S1]。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Quiet does not mean relaxed", zh: "不动不等于放松" }}>

硬拉时大幅度的运动发生在髋和膝，竖脊肌主要做等长收缩：保持脊柱中立，而不是让它来回弯伸 [M55]。平板支撑同样要求腹壁抵抗后伸 [S2]。保持姿态用高亮表示，不画反复缩短。
