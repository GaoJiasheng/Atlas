<Lang en>

A curl-up, a quiet breath and carrying a bag all involve the waist, but they ask different things of it. The **core** includes the abdominal wall, muscles beside the spine and the deep hip flexors. The ivory ribs, vertebrae and pelvis are their frame. Warm colours mark the region; grey marks neighbouring muscles. Left and right always mean the body's own sides.

The abdominal wall is built from <Num s="M93">four</Num> muscles in layers. **Rectus abdominis** (the gym's "six-pack") is a paired vertical strap near the midline. Along the sides, **external oblique**, **internal oblique** and **transversus abdominis** lie one under another, outermost to deepest. Rectus sits in a tendon sheath at the front; it is not a fourth sheet covering the whole flank. The model's shallow, middle and deep display groups help you uncover these relationships [M92].

At the back, the **lumbar erectors** ("lower-back muscles" in the gym) extend or steady the spine. **Multifidus** provides overlapping deep bundles, while **quadratus lumborum** connects the pelvic rim, lumbar processes and last rib. **Psoas major** and **iliacus** pass in front of the hip and lift the thigh. Gluteus maximus, tensor fasciae latae, latissimus and serratus are neighbours, rather than extra abdominal layers [M92].

An <Term id="origin">origin</Term> and an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term> name a muscle's attachments. Which end moves depends on the task. Muscles pull; they can also develop tension while holding almost the same length. One of the core's main jobs is to resist bending, arching and twisting of the trunk: in many everyday tasks these muscles stiffen the torso rather than move it [S2]. <FlyTo preset="front">Find the abdominal wall</FlyTo>

<Lang zh>

卷腹、平缓呼吸和提包都用到腰腹，却要求它完成不同任务。核心包括腹壁、脊柱旁的背肌以及深处的髋屈肌。象牙色肋骨、椎骨与骨盆构成支架；暖色是本区重点，灰色是邻近肌肉。文中的左右一律指身体自己的左右。

腹壁由<Num s="M93">四</Num>块肌肉分层构成：**腹直肌**（俗称六块腹肌）在正中线两旁纵行；侧腹由浅到深依次是**腹外斜肌、腹内斜肌、腹横肌**。腹直肌包在前方的腱性鞘里，并不是盖住整个侧腹的第四张薄片。模型把结构归入浅、中、深三个展示组，方便逐层看清位置 [M92]。

后方的**竖脊肌腰段**（俗称下背肌）让脊柱后伸或保持稳定；**多裂肌**的短肌束层层重叠；**腰方肌**连接骨盆上缘、腰椎横突和末肋。**腰大肌、髂肌**（合称髂腰肌，俗称髋屈肌）经过髋前方，帮助抬起大腿。臀大肌、阔筋膜张肌、背阔肌和前锯肌提供邻区背景，不属于额外的腹肌层 [M92]。

<Term id="origin">起点</Term>与<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>是附着端的名称，哪端移动取决于任务。肌肉只能牵拉，也能在长度基本不变时产生张力。核心的主要任务之一，是抵抗躯干的弯曲、后仰和扭转：日常许多动作里，这些肌肉让躯干变得稳固，而不是让它动起来 [S2]。<FlyTo preset="front">找到腹壁</FlyTo>
