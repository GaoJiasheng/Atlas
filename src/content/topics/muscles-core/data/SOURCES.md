## Rig, poses and action semantics

The supplied `core/rig.json` provides `trunk` and `hip.l/.r` with exact `joint.*` node names and anatomical axes. Only relevant axes are carried over; opposite actions use signed angles, not duplicate axes. The original positive Z lateral-flexion axis moves the upper trunk towards body-right, so the explicitly named left-bend pose uses −12 degrees. Positive Y rotation turns chest towards body-left. Left internal oblique pairs with right external oblique for a left turn; right internal pairs with left external for a right turn. Unilateral contraction maps explicitly give the opposite side zero, preventing automatic bilateral expansion from highlighting both sides.

`rig.demo` has an angle for every angular (joint, movement) pair in the linked muscle sheets, including neighbour actions. Stabilisation is not a rotational axis or angle: the schema explicitly forbids it. Its demonstration is the neutral, non-shortening brace pose and the highlighted holds. Shoulder/scapula actions of distant neighbour cards have demo values but no fake shoulder joints in this regional model. Ten named poses, thirteen muscle contraction animations, two named cuts and six presets follow the chest data conventions.

### Lower-limb extension for the shared RDL

The existing `barbell-romanian-deadlift` card in `exercises/legs.json` requires knee and ankle references. The canonical card is reused unchanged; there is no duplicate in `exercises/core.json`. Four genuine same-body joint locations are taken from `scripts/anatomy/legs/rig.json`, converted into the core frame, and appended as descendants of the appropriate hips. Body-outline weights below the thighs are smoothly distributed between hip, knee and ankle. Anatomical muscle/bone mesh weights and the three original joint locations remain. Joint locations are geometric pipeline estimates, not a clinical measurement. No engine or other-region file was edited.

After encoding, static numerical inspection decodes every mesh and evaluates its skin in the rest pose. Before/after triangle count is unchanged, all original mesh names remain, all coordinates and fibre attributes are finite. The largest symmetric nearest-vertex difference is 0.0904 mm (body-outline reweighting plus requantisation); region/bone geometry stays within 0.030 mm of the input rest mesh. This is a numerical check, not visual runtime acceptance.

Rebuild the content extension with `node scripts/anatomy/core/extend-content-rig.mjs` after the base pipeline export; `scripts/anatomy/core/content-rig.json` records coordinates and the numerical audit.

The single trunk pivot remains a schematic reference, not a multi-segment lumbar/hip-hinge solver. Core-owned exercise motions retain only trunk and hip relationships in an upright anatomical view; the complete support posture and equipment are in the supplied sketches and detailed steps. Holds use zero shortening, and the carry has no pseudo-gait or false pair of dumbbells. The RDL beat holds the start key rather than showing an unsolved foot-planted hinge. Upper-limb support, a hanging bar, cable anchoring, unilateral hand-held equipment, fixed-foot mechanics and runtime visual QA remain outside this content pass. These limitations are explicit in chapter 04/05 and the new exercise setups.

## Attachment review

Every candidate was read by muscle, kind, named target, source marker and coordinates. Region candidates were compared with the anatomical attachment text, then projected onto the actual named source surfaces in the core coordinate frame. Final sites use **both** reviewed sides (54 records); the engine therefore does not replace anatomical asymmetry with a mirrored guess. Named ribs/cartilages and vertebrae were inspected separately before choosing representative points. Broad soft-tissue attachments that have no exported surface stay in text; markers do not stand in for every tendon fibre.

Sources: Gray IV Myology §6a/§6d/§8a [M92]; abdominal-layer description [M93]; internal oblique [M94], transversus [M95], quadratus [M96], multifidus [M97]. OpenStax Table 11.6 contains imprecise or inconsistent entries (including trunk “supination”, transversus ribs 5–10 and quadratus ribs 5–10 in the origin cell); those cells were not copied into cards or sites. Physiopedia’s quadratus introduction and its more detailed table also differ in lumbar levels; L1–L4 representatives follow Gray and the standard detailed anatomy table, not the abbreviated L1–L5 sentence; the Elsevier attachment table independently confirms this range [M108].

## Draft review resolutions

- Four abdominal groups are distinguished from three stacked lateral sheets and three model display groups. Rectus is medial within its sheath, not a uniform fourth lateral sheet.
- “Upper/lower abs” remain regions of one rectus. Spot-reduction statement is explicit; the Vispute result is described with its programme, sample and observation period, not attributed to this curl alone.
- Internal-oblique rib-count/aponeurosis descriptions differ across texts. The card uses lower rib margin plus linea alba and pubic destinations, without importing conflicting numerical counts or inguinal fractions.
- Transversus iliac-crest fractions differ between Gray and modern summaries. The card keeps anterior inner iliac crest and lateral inguinal attachment without a fabricated precise fraction; compression is described in plain language, not schema language.
- Quadratus is a posterior abdominal-wall muscle, not an erector. Side bending and last-rib fixation are taught; conflicting unconditional flexion/extension descriptions are not used as an isolated primary action.
- Multifidus bundles vary in span. No universal adjacent-vertebra or two-to-four-level rule is asserted. Lateral-flexion ranking is removed; overlapping bundles, extension assistance, contralateral rotation and stabilisation remain.
- Iliopsoas is a group card with no duplicate mesh; psoas/iliacus canonical entries already owned by legs are referenced unchanged. Detailed shared origins and lesser-trochanter/direct-iliacus insertion are kept. Knee raising is separated from trunk/pelvic curling.
- Diaphragm is optional and absent from the manifest, so its draft card is omitted. There is no artificial diaphragm-to-rib joint action or invented central-tendon mesh. Breathing is discussed without a respiratory-strengthening promise.
- Fibre arrangement is a coarse teaching description, not a measurement. Unverified optional Latin/FMA fields are omitted from new entries. Chinese standard muscle names are preserved; “腰段/腰部” is a display qualifier, not a new official muscle headword.
- Deep muscles cannot be identified independently by sensation. Self-location uses gentle surface touch or effort cues; deep abdominal/groin palpation, isolation claims and diagnostic inference are avoided.
- Bird-dog and loaded suitcase carry are marked intermediate, matching the checked ACE catalogue. Simpler variants remain in steps. Side-plank prose describes resistance to lateral bending and rolling; no product text exposes enum limitations.
- All ten exercise technique checks have source entries. RDL is the existing shared card; curl-up is a controlled learning variant of the checked crunch, hanging-knee raise the checked bent-knee/partial hanging-leg-raise variant. Rack/support arrangements are editorial learning choices, not study outcomes.
- Prime/assist/stabilise roles vary with task. No comparative EMG, hypertrophy ranking, personal dose, deep-muscle rehabilitation claim or universal injury-prevention promise is made.
- Existing core-owned entries, if present, are preserved. Canonical neighbour/core muscle ids owned by chest/back/legs/shoulders and RDL from legs are referenced, never duplicated or edited. The new core muscle file supplies seven new entries and the new exercise file nine.
- Illustration ids use the existing assets verbatim; no new cover illustration is assigned. The topic remains draft.
