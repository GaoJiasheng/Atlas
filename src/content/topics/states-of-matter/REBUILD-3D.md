### Remaining limitations and acceptance

- Tissue is self-graded B: the compact wad now fits and contacts the unchanged cup roof, but the final side-by-side still lacks the thin free folded edges and dense creases in the real crumpled tissue. The current closed radial sculpt cannot represent those folds faithfully; it needs a sheet surface with self-contact, rather than another higher-resolution radial pass.
- Sponge is self-graded B: the real open cells survive close-up, but the available triangle/file budget still limits the fine interconnected fibrous walls visible in the cellulose macrograph. It is improved substantially over the 588-triangle block, but I do not claim museum-level microstructure.
- Ice is self-graded B: separate material batches correct the actual renderer path, but the internal frost remains stylised, and clear ice uses the engine’s existing alpha-glass family without physical refraction or volume scattering.
- Exact-angle evidence is incomplete. The sheets label render orientations; their adjacent unwarped photos are the closest available references. Pump profile/end views, long-balloon end-on, and bench side/joint views are not a registered same-angle photographic comparison. A rear-wheel dish drawing is used only to check flange-plane construction, not to copy rear-wheel asymmetry into this front wheel. No synthetic or reprojected photograph is presented as real evidence.

It reads decoded shipping GLB/source material factors and engine partPieces/material slots. It asserts rig values against Lighting.tsx. Static opaque pieces were baked per existing selectable part into ten single-draw meshes: balance-a, balance-b, beam-balance, syringe-rack, three plungers, sand-heap, sugar-heap and balance-stand. Their nondegenerate triangle surfaces are preserved within 50 µm after quantisation; zero-area lathe pole faces are excluded from that surface comparison.
