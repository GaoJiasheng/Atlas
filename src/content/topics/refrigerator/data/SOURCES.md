Source rules for this topic:

- Every source was read on 15 Nov 2026. Manufacturer documents (S3, S4, S21) and retailer listings (S36, S37) are cited only as typical of the class; the content never names a brand, model or shop. Patents (S38–S44) are cited for how a part is built, not for how common it is.
- Typical (published range), design (S1, inside the range) and simulated (S1, worked out) numbers are kept apart. The yearly 317 kWh is a model estimate for a kitchen held at 28 °C all year, not a label value; the label test (S13) runs at 16 °C and 32 °C.
