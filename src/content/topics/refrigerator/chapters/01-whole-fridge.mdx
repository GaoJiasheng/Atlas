<Lang en>

A refrigerator does not make cold. It moves heat. The heat in the food, the shelves and the air inside is carried out of the box and given to the kitchen, and the machine has to keep doing this because heat keeps leaking back in. The carrier is a <Term id="refrigerant">refrigerant</Term>, a substance sealed in a loop of pipes that boils and condenses over and over again.

This fridge has two compartments. The freezer on top is kept at about <Num s="S1">−18 °C</Num>, cold enough to keep food frozen hard. The fridge below is kept at about <Num s="S1">4 °C</Num>, just above freezing. Singapore's food agency advises <Num s="S17">4 °C</Num> or below for fridges and <Num s="S17">−18 °C</Num> or below for freezers, because bacteria grow fastest between <Num s="S17">5 and 60 °C</Num>.

The model is a generic design study, not a real brand or model. It is <Num s="S1">600 mm</Num> wide, <Num s="S1">680 mm</Num> deep with its doors and <Num s="S1">1,700 mm</Num> high, and it holds <Num s="S1">350 litres</Num>: <Num s="S1">95 L</Num> in the freezer and <Num s="S1">255 L</Num> in the fridge. Open the doors and you find shelves, a crisper drawer and bins on the door. The working parts are hidden: the cold coil sits behind the freezer's back panel, and the <Term id="compressor">compressor</Term> sits in a bay at the bottom of the back.

A fridge runs day and night. In Singapore homes, fridges use about <Num s="S14">one-sixth</Num> of all the electricity.

<More title={{ en: "Why 4 °C and −18 °C", zh: "为什么是 4℃ 和 −18℃" }}>

The Singapore Food Agency's storage advice is <Num s="S17">4 °C</Num> or below in a fridge and <Num s="S17">−18 °C</Num> or below in a freezer. It calls the range from <Num s="S17">5 to 60 °C</Num>, where bacteria grow most rapidly, the temperature danger zone. A fridge at 4 °C keeps food just below that zone. Food agencies elsewhere give the same figures: the United States Food and Drug Administration advises a fridge at or below <Num s="S18">4 °C</Num> and a freezer at <Num s="S18">−18 °C</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What is not in this model", zh: "模型里没有的东西" }}>

This model has no ice maker, no water dispenser and no display. Some larger fridges have two cold coils, one for each compartment; one reason is to stop the air in the fridge compartment from becoming too dry. This one has a single coil, behind the freezer, and fans its cold air to both compartments.

A few thin pipes are drawn thicker than they really are, and the pipes in the side walls are drawn with fewer bends, so that they can be seen. Their real sizes are in each part's description.

<More title={{ en: "What is not in this model", zh: "模型里没有的东西" }}>

这台模型没有制冰机、饮水机，也没有显示屏。有些较大的冰箱有两个冷盘管，两个室各用一个，原因之一是不让冷藏室的空气变得太干。这台只有一个盘管，装在冷冻室后面，靠风把冷空气送到两个室。

几根细管画得比真实的粗，侧壁里的管也少画了几道弯，好让人看清。它们真实的尺寸写在各个零件的说明里。
