<Lang en>

On 27 January 1967, Gus Grissom, Ed White and Roger Chaffee died in a fire inside the Apollo 1 cabin during a ground test. All <Num s="S15">three</Num> crew members were still on Earth. The fire led to an investigation and changes to the spacecraft's design.

On 24 April, Vladimir Komarov died during Soyuz 1's return to Earth when its parachute system failed. He was its <Num s="S16">one</Num> crew member. Apollo 1's fire and Soyuz 1's parachute failure showed that deadly hazards could arise at different stages of a mission, including before launch and during return.

Testing continued along different paths. In September 1968, the uncrewed Zond 5 travelled around the Moon and returned biological specimens to Earth. It did not enter a continuing orbit around the Moon. In December, Apollo 8 carried the first people into lunar orbit, without a lunar lander aboard. Passing around the Moon and being captured into lunar orbit were distinct operations.

Further tests came before anyone tried to land on the surface. The first uncrewed N1 launch test failed in 1969. Apollo 9 tested its lander in Earth orbit. Apollo 10 rehearsed near the Moon without landing. These flights tested particular operations; a successful rehearsal could not guarantee that the next flight would be safe.

<More title={{ en: "A ground test is part of the history", zh: "地面测试也属于历史" }}>

Apollo 1's cabin caught fire during a ground test. Grissom, White and Chaffee died before launch. Investigators studied the accident, and NASA redesigned the spacecraft.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Other deaths on the ground", zh: "地面上的其他死亡" }}>

People also died on the ground in both countries, away from any spacecraft flight. On 24 October 1960, an R-16 military missile exploded on its launch pad at Baikonur while it was being prepared for a test. Soviet records counted <Num s="S48">74</Num> people killed at the pad, among them Marshal Mitrofan Nedelin, and more died of their injuries later. Russia's space agency has said the full number is hard to know and has cited <Num s="S49">126</Num>. On 23 March 1961, Valentin Bondarenko, a trainee cosmonaut aged <Num s="S50">24</Num>, died after a fire in an oxygen-rich test chamber in Moscow. The Soviet government did not make these deaths public at the time. Bondarenko's death was first reported in the Soviet Union in 1986, and the explosion was first described in 1989.

In the United States, <Num s="S51,S52">four</Num> astronauts died when their T-38 training jets crashed: Theodore Freeman in 1964, Elliot See and Charles Bassett in 1966, and Clifton Williams in 1967. The count of seven crew deaths later in this topic covers only the three spacecraft accidents, so it does not include any of these deaths.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A flight around the Moon", zh: "绕月飞行的不同含义" }}>

Zond 5 carried biological specimens around the Moon and back without a human crew. Apollo 8 carried people into lunar orbit. Neither flight landed on the surface.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Tests before the landing", zh: "登月前的试验" }}>

By May 1969, the first N1 test had failed. Apollo 9 and 10 had tested lander operations in Earth orbit and near the Moon. A human landing was still ahead.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

1967 年 1 月 27 日，格斯·格里森、埃德·怀特与罗杰·查菲在阿波罗1号座舱火灾中遇难，共 <Num s="S15">3</Num> 人。当时正在地面测试，飞船尚未发射。火灾发生后，调查人员研究事故，NASA 随后修改了飞船设计。

4 月 24 日，联盟1号返回地球时，降落伞系统失效，弗拉基米尔·科马罗夫遇难。他是该任务的 <Num s="S16">1</Num> 名乘员。阿波罗1号火灾与联盟1号降落伞故障发生在不同阶段，说明发射之前和返回途中都可能出现致命危险。

后续验证沿不同路径继续。1968 年 9 月，无人驾驶的探测器5号绕过月球，把生物标本带回地球，并未进入持续环绕月球的轨道。12 月，阿波罗8号首次把人送入月球轨道，没有携带月面着陆器。经过月球附近与进入月球轨道，是不同操作。前者可以沿返回路径回到地球，后者则让飞船留在绕月路径上。载人登月还需要从轨道下降，再把人安全送回。

真正尝试载人着陆前，还要完成更多试验。1969 年，N1 首次无人发射测试失败。阿波罗9号在地球轨道测试登月舱，10号则在月球附近演练，没有着陆。这些任务验证各项操作，即使演练成功，也不能保证下一次飞行安全。

<More title={{ en: "A ground test is part of the history", zh: "地面测试也属于历史" }}>

阿波罗1号座舱在地面测试时起火，格里森、怀特和查菲在发射前遇难。调查人员研究事故后，NASA 重新设计了飞船。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Other deaths on the ground", zh: "地面上的其他死亡" }}>

两国都有人在地面上遇难，并不在飞船飞行途中。1960 年 10 月 24 日，一枚 R-16 军用导弹在拜科努尔准备试射时，在发射台上爆炸。苏联的记录是发射台上 <Num s="S48">74</Num> 人死亡，其中有米特罗凡·涅杰林元帅，之后还有伤者不治。俄罗斯航天机构说确切人数难以查清，引用的数字是 <Num s="S49">126</Num> 人。1961 年 3 月 23 日，<Num s="S50">24</Num> 岁的受训宇航员瓦连京·邦达连科在莫斯科一间富氧的试验舱里遭遇火灾，伤重去世。苏联政府当时没有公开这些死亡：邦达连科的死 1986 年才在苏联见报，导弹爆炸 1989 年才第一次公开讲述。

在美国，有 <Num s="S51,S52">4</Num> 名宇航员在 T-38 教练机坠毁中遇难：1964 年的西奥多·弗里曼，1966 年的埃利奥特·西和查尔斯·巴西特，1967 年的克利夫顿·威廉斯。本主题后面统计的 7 名乘员死亡只算三次飞船事故，不包括这些死亡。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A flight around the Moon", zh: "绕月飞行的不同含义" }}>

探测器5号没有载人，它带着生物标本绕过月球，再返回地球。阿波罗8号则载人进入月球轨道。两次任务都没有在月面着陆。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Tests before the landing", zh: "登月前的试验" }}>

到 1969 年 5 月，N1 首次测试已经失败。阿波罗9号和10号分别在地球轨道、月球附近测试登月舱操作。载人着陆仍未尝试。

</More>
