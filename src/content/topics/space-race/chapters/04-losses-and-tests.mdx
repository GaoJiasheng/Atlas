<Lang en>

On 27 January 1967, Gus Grissom, Ed White and Roger Chaffee died in a fire inside the Apollo 1 cabin during a ground test. The <Num s="S15">three</Num> crew members had not launched. Naming the test setting matters: a spacecraft can present deadly hazards while it is still on Earth. NASA’s account includes the redesign that followed the fire.

On 24 April, Vladimir Komarov died during Soyuz 1’s return when its parachute system failed. He was the flight’s <Num s="S16">one</Num> crew member. This was a return accident, different from the Apollo ground fire. Both accounts must retain the people’s names, the stage of the mission and the outcome. Their deaths were not a necessary payment for later achievements.

Testing continued along different paths. In September 1968, the uncrewed Zond 5 travelled around the Moon and returned biological specimens to Earth. It did not enter a continuing orbit around the Moon. In December, Apollo 8 carried the first people into lunar orbit, without a lunar lander aboard. Passing around the Moon and being captured into lunar orbit were distinct operations.

Further tests remained before anyone attempted a surface landing. The first uncrewed N1 launch test failed in 1969. Apollo 9 tested its lander in Earth orbit; Apollo 10 rehearsed near the Moon without landing. These results describe what particular tests demonstrated, not a guarantee of safety on the next flight. Later N1 results belong later on the timeline.

<More title={{ en: "A ground test is part of the history", zh: "地面测试也属于历史" }}>

Apollo 1 was a cabin fire during a test, not a rocket explosion after liftoff. Describing only launch risks would omit this accident. The investigation and redesign are evidence to examine alongside the names of Grissom, White and Chaffee.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A flight around the Moon", zh: "绕月飞行的不同含义" }}>

Zond 5 carried biological specimens and no human crew. Its journey around the Moon and back must not become a claim that Soviet crews had entered lunar orbit. Apollo 8’s lunar orbit was also not a landing.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Keeping the test sequence in order", zh: "保留试验的先后次序" }}>

The May 1969 comparison concerns the first N1 test and Apollo 9 and 10. The later total of N1 tests cannot appear as an already completed record here. A lander rehearsal can supply evidence about operations while leaving the landing itself unattempted.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

1967 年 1 月 27 日，格斯·格里森、埃德·怀特与罗杰·查菲在阿波罗1号座舱火灾中遇难，共 <Num s="S15">3</Num> 人。当时正在地面测试，尚未发射。地点与阶段十分重要：飞船仍在地球上，也可能有致命危险。NASA 的事故记述也包括火灾后的重新设计。

4 月 24 日，弗拉基米尔·科马罗夫乘联盟1号返回时，因降落伞系统失效遇难；该任务载有 <Num s="S16">1</Num> 名乘员。这是返回事故，与阿波罗的地面火灾不同。两边都要保留姓名、任务阶段与结局，不能把死亡说成后来成就必须付出的代价。

后续验证沿不同路径继续。1968 年 9 月，无人驾驶的探测器5号绕过月球，把生物标本带回地球，并未进入持续环绕月球的轨道。12 月，阿波罗8号首次把人送入月球轨道，没有携带月面着陆器。经过月球附近与进入月球轨道，是不同操作。

真正尝试载人着陆前，还要完成试验。1969 年，N1 首次无人发射测试失败；阿波罗9号在地球轨道验证着陆器，10号则在月球附近演练而没有着陆。每项结果只说明该次测试证明了什么，不能保证下一次飞行安全；N1 后来的结果也须留在后面的时间线上。

<More title={{ en: "A ground test is part of the history", zh: "地面测试也属于历史" }}>

阿波罗1号发生的是测试中的座舱火灾，不是升空后的火箭爆炸。只描述发射风险，就会漏掉这起事故。调查与改进的记录，应与格里森、怀特、查菲的姓名一起保留。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A flight around the Moon", zh: "绕月飞行的不同含义" }}>

探测器5号载有生物标本，没有载人乘组。它绕月返回，不证明苏联乘员已进入月球轨道；阿波罗8号进入月球轨道，也不等于完成了月面着陆。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Keeping the test sequence in order", zh: "保留试验的先后次序" }}>

1969 年 5 月的比较，涉及 N1 首次测试与阿波罗9号、10号。不能提前把后来才完成的 N1 测试总数搬到这里。着陆器演练能验证操作，但着陆本身尚未尝试。

</More>
