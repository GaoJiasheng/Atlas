<Lang en>

On 4 October 1957, the Soviet Union launched Sputnik 1 from Baikonur using an R-7 rocket. It became the first <Term id="term-satellite">artificial satellite</Term> of Earth: a human-made object circling a natural body. Its radio signals announced that a working instrument had reached space. The small metal sphere was an object with a particular task, not a miniature spacecraft for people.

Sputnik 2 followed on 3 November carrying the dog Laika. It had no system to bring her back, and she did not survive. US programmes also used animals in space experiments. Remembering these flights includes stating what happened to the animals, rather than treating them as cheerful symbols of technological success.

Explorer 1 became the first US satellite early in 1958. Its detector contributed to the discovery of Earth’s radiation belts, showing that satellites could collect scientific evidence as well as send signals. Its launch was on 31 January in Florida, but already 1 February in UTC. NASA began operations on 1 October, after Explorer had flown.

A <Term id="term-probe">space probe</Term> is an uncrewed craft used to investigate space. The Soviet Luna missions produced different results in 1959. Luna 1 made a <Term id="term-flyby">flyby</Term>, passing near the Moon; Luna 2 struck its surface; Luna 3 returned the first photographs of its far side. “Reached the Moon” is too vague to distinguish passing, impact and photography. Each result needs its own description and evidence.

<More title={{ en: "A date needs a time standard", zh: "日期要配合时间标准" }}>

Explorer 1’s Florida launch date and UTC date describe the same launch. The timeline uses 1 February 1958. NASA’s later opening must not be moved backwards: the first US satellite was not launched by an already operating NASA.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Different lunar results", zh: "不同的探月结果" }}>

A flyby does not mean the craft became a satellite of the Moon. An impact does not mean it survived on the surface. The Luna evidence entry gathers the 1959 sequence for comparison; its final date does not mean every flight happened that day.

</More>

<Lang zh>

1957 年 10 月 4 日，苏联用 R-7 火箭从拜科努尔发射斯普特尼克1号。它成为地球第一颗<Term id="term-satellite">人造卫星</Term>，也就是人制造、围绕天体运行的物体。无线电信号说明工作中的仪器已经进入太空；这个金属球执行特定任务，并不是给人乘坐的小飞船。

11 月 3 日，斯普特尼克2号带着狗莱卡升空。任务没有把她送回地球的系统，她未能生还。美国项目也使用动物进行太空实验。记述这些飞行，要讲清动物的结局，不能只把它们当成技术成功的欢庆符号。

1958 年，探险者1号成为美国第一颗卫星，探测仪帮助发现地球辐射带。卫星不只发送信号，也能取得科学证据。发射时佛罗里达还是 1 月 31 日，UTC 已是 2 月 1 日。NASA 到 10 月 1 日才开始运作，晚于这次飞行。这类仪器让研究者把天空中的现象变成可记录、可比较的数据，任务目的也不只剩下抢先发射。

<Term id="term-probe">空间探测器</Term>是不载人、用于调查太空的航天器。1959 年，苏联月球1号<Term id="term-flyby">飞越</Term>月球附近，月球2号撞击月面，月球3号传回首批月球背面照片。笼统说“到达月球”，会混淆经过、撞击与摄影；各自结果必须分别说明。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A date needs a time standard", zh: "日期要配合时间标准" }}>

探险者1号的佛罗里达日期与 UTC 日期指向同一次发射；时间轴采用 1958 年 2 月 1 日。NASA 后来的成立时间不能往前挪，不能说这颗卫星由已经运作的 NASA 发射。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Different lunar results", zh: "不同的探月结果" }}>

飞越不等于成为月球的卫星，撞击也不等于抵达后仍能在表面工作。证据条目汇集 1959 年月球号的不同结果，末项日期不表示所有飞行都发生在同一天。

</More>
