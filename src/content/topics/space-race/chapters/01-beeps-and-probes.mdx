<Lang en>

On 4 October 1957, the Soviet Union launched Sputnik 1 from Baikonur on an R-7 rocket. It became Earth's first <Term id="term-satellite">artificial satellite</Term>: a human-made object circling a natural body. Radio signals from the small metal sphere showed that its instruments were working in space. It carried radio equipment rather than a crew.

Sputnik 2 followed on 3 November carrying the dog Laika. It had no system to bring her back, and she died during the flight. US programmes also used animals in space experiments. These early experiments used living animals to investigate space before the first human flights.

Explorer 1 became the first US satellite early in 1958. Its detector contributed to the discovery of Earth’s radiation belts, showing that satellites could collect scientific evidence as well as send signals. Its launch was on 31 January in Florida, but already 1 February in UTC. NASA began operations on 1 October, after Explorer had flown.

A <Term id="term-probe">space probe</Term> is a spacecraft that investigates space without a crew. The Soviet Luna missions explored the Moon in 1959. Luna 1 made a <Term id="term-flyby">flyby</Term>, passing near the Moon. Luna 2 struck the surface, and Luna 3 returned the first photographs of its far side. Passing, hitting the surface and taking photographs were different results.

Explorer 1 launched on 31 January 1958 in Florida. In UTC, the world time standard, it was already 1 February. The timeline uses the UTC date. NASA began operations later that year, after the satellite had flown.

A flyby takes a craft past the Moon without keeping it in lunar orbit. An impact ends in a collision with the surface. The Luna missions achieved these different results over the course of 1959.

<Lang zh>

1957 年 10 月 4 日，苏联用 R-7 火箭从拜科努尔发射斯普特尼克1号。它成为地球第一颗<Term id="term-satellite">人造卫星</Term>，也就是人制造、围绕天体运行的物体。这个小金属球发回无线电信号，说明仪器正在太空中运作。它搭载无线电设备，没有载人。

11 月 3 日，斯普特尼克2号带着狗莱卡升空。任务没有把她送回地球的系统，她在飞行中死去。美国项目也使用动物进行太空实验。动物先于人类进入太空，成为早期研究的一部分。

1958 年，探险者1号成为美国第一颗卫星。它的探测仪帮助发现地球辐射带，说明卫星不只能够发送信号，还能收集科学数据。发射时佛罗里达仍是 1 月 31 日，世界时间标准 UTC 已是 2 月 1 日。NASA 到 10 月 1 日才开始运作，晚于这次飞行。

<Term id="term-probe">空间探测器</Term>是不载人、用于调查太空的航天器。1959 年，苏联月球1号从月球附近<Term id="term-flyby">飞越</Term>，月球2号撞击月面，月球3号传回首批月球背面照片。经过月球、撞到表面和拍摄照片，是不同的任务结果。飞越没有让飞船留在月球轨道，撞击也没有让仪器继续在表面工作。照片则让人们首次看见月球背面的景象。

<More title={{ en: "A sphere and its antennae", zh: "球体与天线" }}>

斯普特尼克1号的球体直径约 <Num s="S5">58</Num> 厘米，<Num s="S5">4</Num> 根长鞭状天线用来发送无线电信号。插图画的是这颗无线电卫星，它没有载人座舱或让人返回地球的设备。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A date needs a time standard", zh: "日期要配合时间标准" }}>

探险者1号在佛罗里达发射时是 1958 年 1 月 31 日，UTC 已是 2 月 1 日。时间轴采用 UTC 日期。NASA 在同年稍后才开始运作，当时这颗卫星已经飞行。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Different lunar results", zh: "不同的探月结果" }}>

飞越让探测器从月球附近经过，不会让它留在月球轨道。撞击则以碰到表面告终。月球号的这些成果发生在 1959 年的不同时候。

</More>
