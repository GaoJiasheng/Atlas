<Lang en>

Apollo 11 launched on 16 July 1969 with Neil Armstrong, Michael Collins and Buzz Aldrin aboard. Its <Term id="term-cm">command module</Term> carried the <Num s="S21">three</Num> astronauts; the service module supplied propulsion and support. After departure towards the Moon, these joined modules turned around, docked with the separate <Term id="term-lm">lunar module</Term> and extracted it from its launch adapter. The spacecraft’s arrangement changed as its tasks changed.

Armstrong and Aldrin descended in the lunar module Eagle. Collins remained in lunar orbit. Eagle landed in the Sea of Tranquility at about 20:17 UTC on 20 July. Armstrong first stepped onto the surface at about 02:56 UTC on 21 July. Landing the vehicle and stepping outside were different events on different UTC dates. The Earth map marks their associated launch centre, not the landing site.

Collecting material for study was part of the work. Apollo 11’s <Term id="term-sample-return">sample return</Term> brought about <Num s="S20">22</Num> kg of lunar material to Earth. Another sample mission was nearby: the Soviet Luna 15 attempted a robotic return but crashed on 21 July. The programmes exchanged flight-path information to avoid interference, even while pursuing different missions.

After the surface work, the lunar module’s ascent section carried Armstrong and Aldrin back to lunar orbit to rejoin Collins. All returned to Earth on 24 July in the command module; the service module was discarded before re-entry. Describing the journey requires the departure, separate jobs and return, as well as the moment someone first stood on the Moon.

<More title={{ en: "The same event in Singapore", zh: "新加坡当年的时间" }}>

In 1969 Singapore used UTC+07:30. Eagle’s landing was about 03:47 on 21 July locally; Armstrong’s first step was about 10:26 that morning. Both were on the same Singapore date. Using today’s UTC+08 would give the wrong historical local times.

</More>

<Lang zh>

1969 年 7 月 16 日，阿波罗11号载着尼尔·阿姆斯特朗、迈克尔·柯林斯与巴兹·奥尔德林出发。<Term id="term-cm">指令舱</Term>搭载 <Num s="S21">3</Num> 名乘员，服务舱提供推进与支援。飞船向月球出发后，这组舱段转身，对接并从适配器里抽出独立的<Term id="term-lm">登月舱</Term>；任务改变，组合方式也随之改变。不同乘员的任务相互衔接，任何一段都不能拿另一段代替。

阿姆斯特朗与奥尔德林乘鹰号下降，柯林斯留在月球轨道。鹰号于 UTC 7 月 20 日约 20:17 在静海着陆；阿姆斯特朗首次踏足月面，则是 UTC 7 月 21 日约 02:56。飞船着陆与人走到舱外，是发生在不同 UTC 日期的事件。地球地图上的点只是关联发射中心，并非月面着陆点。

乘员采集材料供研究，完成<Term id="term-sample-return">采样返回</Term>，带回约 <Num s="S20">22</Num> 千克月球材料。苏联月球15号也尝试无人采样返回，却于 7 月 21 日坠毁。双方追求不同任务，仍交换飞行路径信息以避免干扰。

月面工作后，登月舱上升部分把阿姆斯特朗与奥尔德林送回月球轨道，与柯林斯会合。全体乘员于 7 月 24 日乘指令舱返回地球，服务舱在再入大气层前抛弃。完整记述这趟旅程，要保留出发、分工和返回，不能只剩首次踏足的一刻。

1969 年新加坡采用 UTC+07:30。鹰号着陆是本地 7 月 21 日约 03:47，阿姆斯特朗首次踏足是当天约 10:26；在新加坡日历上，两件事发生在同一天。套用今天的 UTC+08，会算错历史本地时刻。

</More>
