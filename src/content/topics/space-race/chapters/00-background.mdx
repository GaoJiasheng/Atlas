<Lang en>

The <Term id="term-space-race">space race</Term> was a competition in space technology during the Cold War, a period of rivalry between the United States and Soviet Union. Both countries wanted scientific knowledge, new technical skills and national prestige. Rockets also had military uses. A mission could gather useful measurements while helping a government show its power.

From early satellites to the meeting of spacecraft in 1975, both programmes sent machines into space. Some carried people. Others worked without a crew, investigating places that people had not yet reached. The first human Moon landing was one important step in this longer history of exploration and research.

The engineers had lived through war and persecution. During the Second World War, Wernher von Braun worked on Germany's V-2 rocket. Its production at Mittelwerk used forced labour. Sergei Korolev was imprisoned during Stalin's purges and worked within the Soviet prison system. He later led Soviet space development. These histories include suffering as well as engineering achievements.

Plans, flight records and memoirs help us understand what happened. A plan tells us what people intended to do. A flight record describes the mission's results. A memoir gives a participant's memories. Comparing them helps us understand both the decisions made on Earth and the work done in space.

<More title={{ en: "People and institutions", zh: "人物与制度" }}>

Von Braun's biography describes V-2 work and forced labour at Mittelwerk. Korolev's biography describes imprisonment in the Soviet system. Later achievements did not justify the harm done to people in either system.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Different questions need different evidence", zh: "不同问题要读不同证据" }}>

A press kit describes planned actions. Mission summaries record results, while financial histories describe spending. The related Sun–Earth–Moon topic explains the physical setting for these journeys.

</More>

<Lang zh>

<Term id="term-space-race">太空竞赛</Term>是冷战时期的太空技术竞争。冷战期间，美国与苏联相互竞争，两国都希望取得科学知识、发展技术，并提高国家声望。火箭也有军事用途。一项任务可以收集有用数据，同时让政府展示实力。

从早期卫星到 1975 年飞船会合，两国都向太空发射了机器。有些搭载乘员，有些由无人设备执行任务，调查人类尚未抵达的地方。首次载人登月是其中的重要一步，探索与研究在此前已经开始，此后也继续进行。

工程师也经历过战争和迫害。第二次世界大战期间，韦恩赫尔·冯·布劳恩参与德国 V-2 火箭工作，米特尔工厂的生产使用强迫劳动。谢尔盖·科罗廖夫在斯大林清洗中被囚，曾在苏联监狱体系内工作，后来才领导航天研发。这些历史既有技术成果，也有人的苦难。

计划书、飞行记录和回忆录，都帮助人们了解过去。计划书说明原本打算做什么，飞行记录说明任务取得什么结果，回忆录则留下当事人的记忆。把它们相互比较，有助于了解地面上的决定，以及太空中的工作。

<More title={{ en: "People and institutions", zh: "人物与制度" }}>

冯·布劳恩的传记记述 V-2 工作与米特尔工厂的强迫劳动；科罗廖夫的传记则记述他在苏联被囚的经历。后来的技术成就，都不能成为伤害他人的理由。

</More>
