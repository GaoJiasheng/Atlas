<Lang en>

A <Term id="term-programme-cost">programme cost</Term> tells us how much was spent on a project. To understand it, we need the currency, period and what the account includes. Historian Asif Siddiqi reports N1-L3 spending of <Num s="S18">3.6 billion</Num> rubles by 1 January 1973. This paid for a particular lunar-landing project, rather than all Soviet space activity.

In 1973, NASA reported Apollo's cost as US$<Num s="S42">25.4 billion</Num>. Later reconstructions give a slightly different total. Rubles and dollars are different currencies, and the two project accounts cover different work. These figures alone cannot show which country got more research for its money.

Research continued in orbit. Salyut had begun space-station work, and Skylab launched in May 1973. Earlier missions had also brought human losses. Apollo 1's ground fire killed Grissom, White and Chaffee. Soyuz 1's return killed Komarov. Soyuz 11's loss of pressure killed Dobrovolski, Volkov and Patsayev. These accidents killed <Num s="S15,S16,S17">seven</Num> crew members in all. This subtotal covers only those accidents.

On 17 July 1975, Apollo and Soyuz docked in Earth orbit. A connecting module joined their different spacecraft systems. Apollo had launched on a Saturn IB, rather than a Saturn V. Programmes that had competed now completed a joint task. Their earlier goals, failed tests and human losses remained part of their history.

<Term id="term-programme-cost">项目支出</Term>说明一个项目花了多少钱。理解这笔账，还要知道币种、年代，以及包括哪些费用。历史学家阿西夫·西迪基记载，截至 1973 年 1 月 1 日，N1-L3 支出为 <Num s="S18">36 亿</Num> 卢布。这笔钱用于特定的载人登月项目，并非苏联全部航天活动。

NASA 于 1973 年报告阿波罗支出为 <Num s="S42">254 亿</Num> 美元，后来重建的账目略有不同。卢布和美元是不同币种，两个项目的账目也包括不同工作。单凭这些数字，无法判断哪国花钱取得了更多研究成果。

轨道研究继续进行。礼炮号开拓空间站工作，天空实验室于 1973 年 5 月发射。早期任务也带来人员损失：阿波罗1号地面火灾夺去格里森、怀特、查菲的生命，联盟1号返回事故造成科马罗夫死亡，联盟11号失压造成多布罗沃尔斯基、沃尔科夫、帕察耶夫死亡。这些事故合计 <Num s="S15,S16,S17">7</Num> 人遇难，只包括上述事故。

1975 年 7 月 17 日，阿波罗与联盟号在地球轨道对接，用连接舱衔接不同飞船系统。阿波罗由土星 IB 火箭发射，并非土星五号。曾经竞争的项目完成了共同任务，此前的不同目标、失败测试与人员损失也仍属于这段历史。
