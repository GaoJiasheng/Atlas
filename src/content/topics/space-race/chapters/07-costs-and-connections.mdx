<Lang en>

A <Term id="term-programme-cost">programme cost</Term> is meaningful only when its currency, period and accounting scope stay beside it. Historian Asif Siddiqi reports N1-L3 costs of <Num s="S18">3.6 billion</Num> rubles by 1 January 1973. That is an account for a particular lunar-landing project, not a bill for all Soviet activity in space. Later rough totals use a different endpoint.

NASA reported Apollo’s cost as US$<Num s="S42">25.4 billion</Num> in 1973. Later reconstructions give a somewhat different account. Rubles and dollars cannot be placed on an equal spending scale merely because both numbers are large. Different project boundaries also prevent these figures from deciding which country bought more research for its money.

Research continued in orbit. Salyut had opened a path for station work, while Skylab launched in May 1973 after the Apollo lunar landings. Continuing work also requires an honest account of harm. Apollo 1’s ground fire killed Grissom, White and Chaffee; Soyuz 1’s return killed Komarov; Soyuz 11’s depressurisation killed Dobrovolski, Volkov and Patsayev. Those specified accidents account for <Num s="S15,S16,S17">seven</Num> crew deaths. The subtotal is not a count of everyone who died in the space age.

On 17 July 1975, Apollo and Soyuz docked in Earth orbit. A connecting module joined spacecraft with different systems. Apollo had launched on a Saturn IB, not a Saturn V. The meeting demonstrated a practical connection between programmes that had also competed. Its importance lies in the particular cooperation achieved, without erasing the different goals, failed tests and human losses in the preceding history.

<Lang zh>

<Term id="term-programme-cost">项目支出</Term>必须伴随币种、年代与账目范围。历史学家阿西夫·西迪基记载，截至 1973 年 1 月 1 日，N1-L3 支出为 <Num s="S18">36 亿</Num> 卢布。这是特定载人登月项目的账，不是苏联全部航天活动的账单；后来的粗略总额采用不同截止点。

NASA 于 1973 年报告阿波罗支出为 <Num s="S42">254 亿</Num> 美元，后来的重建账目略有不同。不能只因数字都大，就把卢布与美元画在相同支出尺度上。项目范围也不同，因此这些数值不能直接判断哪国花钱更有效率。

轨道研究仍在继续。礼炮号开拓空间站工作，天空实验室则在阿波罗载人登月后，于 1973 年 5 月发射。继续研究，也要如实记住伤害：阿波罗1号地面火灾中的格里森、怀特、查菲，联盟1号返回时的科马罗夫，以及联盟11号失压时的多布罗沃尔斯基、沃尔科夫、帕察耶夫。这些指定事故合计 <Num s="S15,S16,S17">7</Num> 人死亡，并非整个太空时代的总数。

1975 年 7 月 17 日，阿波罗与联盟号在地球轨道对接，由连接舱衔接不同飞船系统。阿波罗采用土星 IB 发射，并非土星五号。曾经竞争的项目完成了具体合作；这项连接的意义，不需要靠抹去此前不同目标、失败测试与人员损失来说明。
