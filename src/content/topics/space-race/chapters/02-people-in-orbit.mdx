<Lang en>

On 12 April 1961, Yuri Gagarin flew in Vostok 1, becoming the first human in space and the first to circle Earth. An <Term id="term-orbit">orbit</Term> is a path around a body under gravity. Reaching a great height is not enough: a spacecraft also needs the appropriate sideways motion. Gravity continues to act while it travels around Earth.

On 5 May, Alan Shepard's Mercury flight reached space without circling Earth. This is called <Term id="term-suborbital">suborbital</Term> flight. John Glenn completed the first US human orbital mission on 20 February 1962. Both men reached space, but their spacecraft followed different kinds of paths.

On 25 May 1961, President John F. Kennedy proposed landing a person on the Moon and returning that person safely before the decade ended. Engineers still had to develop the spacecraft needed for this goal. Soviet human flights also continued. On 16 June 1963, Valentina Tereshkova flew in Vostok 6 and became the first woman in space.

Voskhod 1 carried the first multi-person crew in October 1964. On 18 March 1965, Aleksei Leonov made the first <Term id="term-spacewalk">spacewalk</Term> from Voskhod 2. He worked outside his spacecraft. Human spaceflight was expanding: crews were learning how to live and work in space as well as how to get there.

<More title={{ en: "Gravity remains in space", zh: "太空中仍有引力" }}>

Gravity keeps accelerating an orbiting spacecraft towards Earth. People can float inside while gravity continues to act. The related Sun–Earth–Moon topic explains the physical setting of orbital and suborbital flights.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A goal and its evidence", zh: "目标与证据" }}>

Kennedy announced a goal in 1961. Later flight records show how and when it was achieved. His proposal included bringing the traveller safely home as well as landing on the Moon.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Women and work outside the cabin", zh: "女性与舱外工作" }}>

Glenn orbited Earth in 1962, Tereshkova flew in 1963 and Leonov made his spacewalk in 1965. Each mission added a different experience to human spaceflight.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

1961 年 4 月 12 日，尤里·加加林乘东方1号飞行，成为首位进入太空并环绕地球的人。<Term id="term-orbit">轨道</Term>是在引力作用下绕天体运动的路径。飞得高还不够，飞船也要有合适的横向运动；围绕地球飞行时，引力仍在作用。引力不断改变飞船运动的方向，让它沿着绕地球的路径前进。飞船抵达多高，以及怎样运动，都是理解这些任务的关键。

5 月 5 日，艾伦·谢泼德乘水星计划飞船进入太空，但没有环绕地球。这称为<Term id="term-suborbital">亚轨道</Term>飞行。约翰·格伦则在 1962 年 2 月 20 日完成美国首次载人轨道飞行。两人都抵达太空，飞船走的却是不同的运动路径。

1961 年 5 月 25 日，肯尼迪总统提出在这个十年结束前，让人登月并安全返回。工程师仍须研制完成这个目标所需的飞船。苏联也继续载人飞行。1963 年 6 月 16 日，瓦莲京娜·捷列什科娃乘东方6号，成为首位进入太空的女性。

1964 年 10 月，上升1号首次搭载多人乘组。1965 年 3 月 18 日，阿列克谢·列昂诺夫从上升2号进行首次<Term id="term-spacewalk">太空行走</Term>，在飞船外工作。载人任务逐渐扩展：除了把人送入太空，也学习怎样在那里生活和工作。
