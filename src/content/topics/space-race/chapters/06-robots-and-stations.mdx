<Lang en>

The first human landing did not end lunar exploration or remove its risks. Apollo 13 did not land on the Moon, but its crew returned alive on 17 April 1970. A mission’s result must distinguish the planned task from the actual outcome. Returning safely and completing a landing were different achievements, even within the same programme.

In September, Luna 16 achieved the first robotic return of lunar samples. Luna 17 delivered Lunokhod 1 in November. Controlled from Earth, the rover could investigate the surface without a human crew beside it. Its tub-like body had a hinged lid carrying solar cells and <Num s="S35">eight</Num> independently driven wheels. The machines extended Soviet lunar investigation beyond earlier flybys, photographs and landings.

Other missions examined living and working around Earth. Salyut 1 was the first <Term id="term-space-station">space station</Term>, a facility where people could stay and conduct research in orbit. Georgi Dobrovolski, Vladislav Volkov and Viktor Patsayev worked aboard it in 1971. On their return in Soyuz 11, the cabin lost pressure and all <Num s="S17">three</Num> died. The station work and the fatal return are both parts of their mission’s history.

Apollo 17 made the final Apollo lunar landing in December 1972. Altogether, <Num s="S12">six</Num> Apollo missions landed people on the Moon. By then, the N1 programme had completed <Num s="S18">four</Num> uncrewed launch tests without a successful flight. That result concerns the N1-L3 landing project; it cannot describe the whole Soviet space programme, whose robots and stations followed other paths.

Dobrovolski, Volkov and Patsayev died following cabin depressurisation on 30 June 1971. Their work on Salyut 1 must not disappear from the story, and its completion must not hide the fatal return. This accident accounts for <Num s="S17">three</Num> deaths in the later specified subtotal.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What the totals include", zh: "总数包括什么" }}>

Apollo’s <Num s="S12">six</Num> landings exclude Apollo 13. The <Num s="S18">four</Num> N1 tests ran from 1969 to 1972 and carried no crews. The December 1972 comparison is a retrospective account, not a claim that every test or landing happened that month.

<Lang zh>

首次载人登月没有结束月球探索，也没有消除危险。阿波罗13号未能着陆月球，乘组却于 1970 年 4 月 17 日生还返回。读任务结果，要分清计划与实际过程；安全回来和完成着陆，即使属于同一项目，也是不同成果。

9 月，月球16号首次由机器人把月球标本带回地球。11 月，月球17号送达月球车1号，由地球遥控，在没有乘员陪同的月面调查。它有盆状车体，铰接舱盖承载太阳能板，配备 <Num s="S35">8</Num> 个独立驱动车轮。苏联探月工作从较早的飞越、摄影与着陆，继续扩展。

其他任务研究人在地球周围怎样生活与工作。礼炮1号是首座<Term id="term-space-station">空间站</Term>，供人留在轨道开展研究。1971 年，格奥尔基·多布罗沃尔斯基、弗拉季斯拉夫·沃尔科夫与维克托·帕察耶夫在站上工作。乘联盟11号返回时，座舱失压，<Num s="S17">3</Num> 人全部遇难；工作成果与致命返航都不能省略。

1972 年 12 月，阿波罗17号完成阿波罗最后一次登月；该计划共有 <Num s="S12">6</Num> 次载人着陆。到这时，N1 的 <Num s="S18">4</Num> 次无人发射测试均未成功。这描述的是 N1-L3 登月项目，不能概括仍在发展机器人与空间站的整个苏联航天项目。失败结果须注明项目范围，不能移作其他任务的结论。
