<Lang en>

Lunar exploration continued after the first human landing, and journeys remained dangerous. Apollo 13 did not land on the Moon. Its crew returned alive on 17 April 1970. They reached home safely, although they had been unable to complete the planned landing.

In September, Luna 16 achieved the first robotic return of lunar samples. Luna 17 delivered Lunokhod 1 in November. Controlled from Earth, the rover could investigate the surface without a human crew beside it. Its tub-like body had a hinged lid carrying solar cells and <Num s="S35">eight</Num> independently driven wheels. The machines extended Soviet lunar investigation beyond earlier flybys, photographs and landings.

Other missions studied living and working around Earth. Salyut 1 was the first <Term id="term-space-station">space station</Term>, where people could stay and conduct research in orbit. Georgi Dobrovolski, Vladislav Volkov and Viktor Patsayev worked aboard it in 1971. During their return in Soyuz 11, the cabin lost pressure. All <Num s="S17">three</Num> died.

Apollo 17 made the final Apollo lunar landing in December 1972. Altogether, <Num s="S12">six</Num> Apollo missions landed people on the Moon. By then, N1 had made <Num s="S18">four</Num> uncrewed launch tests, all unsuccessful. N1-L3 was the Soviet human lunar-landing project. Other Soviet programmes continued their work with robots and space stations.

Dobrovolski, Volkov and Patsayev lived and worked on Salyut 1. On 30 June 1971, their Soyuz 11 cabin lost pressure during return, killing all <Num s="S17">three</Num>. Their mission included research on the station and a fatal journey home.

<Lang zh>

首次载人登月后，月球探索仍在继续，旅程也仍有危险。阿波罗13号没有在月球着陆，乘组于 1970 年 4 月 17 日生还返回。他们没能完成原定登月任务，但安全回到了地球。

9 月，月球16号首次由机器人把月球标本带回地球。11 月，月球17号送达月球车1号，由地球遥控，在没有乘员陪同的月面调查。它有盆状车体，铰接舱盖承载太阳能板，配备 <Num s="S35">8</Num> 个独立驱动车轮。苏联探月工作从较早的飞越、摄影与着陆，继续扩展。采样返回让研究者在地球上检查月球材料，月球车则在月面移动调查。这是探索月球的不同方法。

其他任务研究人在地球周围怎样生活与工作。礼炮1号是首座<Term id="term-space-station">空间站</Term>，供人在轨道上停留和研究。1971 年，格奥尔基·多布罗沃尔斯基、弗拉季斯拉夫·沃尔科夫与维克托·帕察耶夫在站上工作。他们乘联盟11号返回时，座舱失压，<Num s="S17">3</Num> 人全部遇难。

1972 年 12 月，阿波罗17号完成阿波罗最后一次载人登月。整个计划共有 <Num s="S12">6</Num> 次载人着陆。到这时，N1 已进行 <Num s="S18">4</Num> 次无人发射测试，均未成功。N1-L3 是苏联载人登月项目；其他苏联项目仍继续发展机器人与空间站。
