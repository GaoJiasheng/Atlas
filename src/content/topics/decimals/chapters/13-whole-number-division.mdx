<Lang en>

In the previous step, you divided a <Term id="decimal">decimal</Term> by trading what was left into the next place. You also know division with a <Term id="remainder">remainder</Term>; now you can keep sharing instead of stopping there.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>four plates and the dollars left over</Beat>. Aisha shares $7 among 4 children. Each plate has $1, with $3 still to share: 7 ÷ 4 = 1 r 3. The r tells you that 3 whole dollars remain outside the equal shares.

Now <Beat n={2}>follow the discs into the smaller places</Beat>. Trade $3 for 30 ten-cent discs, which represent <Term id="tenth">tenths</Term> of a dollar. Give each plate 7, leaving 2 discs. Trade those for 20 one-cent discs, or <Term id="hundredth">hundredths</Term> of a dollar: 5 each. Trading keeps the total unchanged. Each plate now has $1.75, so 7 ÷ 4 = 1.75. Nothing remains; check that 1.75 × 4 = 7.

On the number line, <Beat n={3}>compare 1.3 with 1.75</Beat>. The mark at 1.3 is to the left, so it is smaller. A common mistake is writing 1.3 for 1 r 3: the remainder is 3 whole dollars still to share, whereas 1.3 means $1.30 for each child. Four shares of $1.30 make only $5.20, leaving some money unshared.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>look at the repeated shares and the enlarged number line</Beat> for 2 ÷ 3. Trade 2 ones for 20 tenths: 6 each, with 2 left. This repeats in every smaller place, so 2 ÷ 3 = 0.666…, where the dots mean the sixes go on for ever. To round to two decimal places, divide one place further and look at the thousandths digit, 6. Since it is at least 5, round up: 2 ÷ 3 ≈ 0.67, the nearer end of the line. Writing 0.66 is a mistake because it cuts off digits without rounding.

When you are ready, <Task id="whole-number-division">try one question</Task> on continuing past a remainder and rounding when asked.

<Lang zh>

整数除以整数，以前常常算出一个<Term id="remainder">余数</Term>就停下来了。学了小数，就可以接着往下分。

先看<Beat n={1}>Aisha 的 $7</Beat>。她把 $7 平均分给 4 个小朋友，每人 $1，还剩 $3。用以前的写法，7 ÷ 4 = 1 r 3。可是剩下的 $3 也是钱，还可以分。

<Beat n={2}>越过小数点接着分</Beat>。把 $3 换成 30 个 10 分，每人 7 个，还剩 2 个 10 分。再把这 2 个换成 20 个 1 分，每人 5 个。每人一共得到 $1、7 个 10 分和 5 分，也就是 $1.75。竖式里，在 7 后面补上小数点和 0，就能一直算下去：7 ÷ 4 = 1.75。

一个常见的错误是<Beat n={3}>把余数写在小数点后面</Beat>，把 1 r 3 写成 1.3。在数轴上看，1.3 和 1.75 差得很远。余数 3 表示还有 3 个整元没分，并不是 3 个十分之一。如果每人只拿 $1.30，4 个人才分了 $5.20，钱还剩着。

有的除法永远分不完。<Beat n={4}>2 ÷ 3</Beat>：每一位都剩下一点，换开以后又剩下一点，商是 0.666……，6 一直写下去。这时题目会告诉你取几位。四舍五入到两位小数，看千分位：是 6，满 5 进一，所以 2 ÷ 3 ≈ 0.67。只截到 0.66、不看下一位，就不是四舍五入了。

准备好了，就<Task id="whole-number-division">做一道小题</Task>：把整数除法接着分下去，写出小数的商。
