<Lang en>

You already know that each place is worth ten times the place on its right. This chapter takes you further than the main lesson: you will use that pattern to multiply and divide by 10, 100 and 1000.

Look at <Beat n={1}>2.35 in the chart</Beat>. The <Term id="place-value-chart">place value chart</Term> shows 2 ones, 3 <Term id="tenth">tenths</Term> and 5 <Term id="hundredth">hundredths</Term>. Moving a digit one column left makes its value ten times as much.

Watch <Beat n={2}>every digit move one place left</Beat>. The <Term id="decimal-point">decimal point</Term> stays still: 2 ones become 2 tens, 3 tenths become 3 ones, and 5 hundredths become 5 tenths. So 2.35 × 10 = 23.5. Appending a zero to write 2.350 is a mistake because 2.350 still equals 2.35; none of the digits has changed place.

Now <Beat n={3}>follow the digits three places left</Beat>. Multiplying by 100 means multiplying by 10 twice, so 2.35 × 100 = 235. For 1000, move three places: 2.35 × 1000 = 2350. The zero holds the empty ones place. Moving only one place for × 100 is a counting mistake: it gives 23.5, only ten times the starting number.

For division, <Beat n={4}>follow 4.8 two places right</Beat>. Each move makes each digit worth one tenth as much. The 4 reaches hundredths and the 8 reaches thousandths: 4.8 ÷ 100 = 0.048. Write zeros before the point and in the empty tenths place. Moving left for division is a direction mistake: it makes this number larger. For ÷ 10, ÷ 100 and ÷ 1000, move right one, two and three places.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>watch 1.8 become 18</Beat>. Since 30 = 3 × 10, first work out 0.6 × 3 = 1.8. Then move both digits one place left for 1.8 × 10 = 18. You have multiplied by all of 30.

When you are ready, <Task id="times-ten">try one question</Task> on moving digits while keeping the point still.

<Lang zh>

在位值表里，每一位都是右边一位的十倍。这一章用这一点，算乘或除以 10、100、1000。

先看<Beat n={1}>位值表里的 2.35</Beat>：2 在个位，3 在十分位，5 在百分位。

<Beat n={2}>乘 10</Beat>，每个数字的值都变成原来的十倍，也就是每个数字往左移一位：2 移到十位，3 移到个位，5 移到十分位。小数点不动。2.35 × 10 = 23.5。有人说“乘 10 就在后面添一个 0”，写成 2.350，可是小数<Term id="trailing-zero">末尾的 0</Term> 不改变大小，2.350 还是 2.35。整数可以添 0，小数要移位。

<Beat n={3}>乘 100</Beat>，往左移两位，得 235；乘 1000，往左移三位，2 到了千位，个位空了出来，要补一个 0 占位：2.35 × 1000 = 2350。乘 10、100、1000，分别移 1、2、3 位，0 的个数就是移的位数。

<Beat n={4}>除法</Beat>反过来，往右移。4.8 ÷ 100：每个数字往右移两位，4 到了百分位，8 到了千分位。十分位空了，补 0；小数点前面也写 0：0.048。移错方向，或者移的位数不对，结果会差上十倍、一百倍。

乘 30、乘 400 这样的数呢？<Beat n={5}>拆成两步</Beat>：乘 30 就是先乘 3，再乘 10。0.6 × 3 = 1.8，1.8 × 10 = 18，所以 0.6 × 30 = 18。

准备好了，就<Task id="times-ten">做一道小题</Task>：在位值表里把数字移一移。

</Lang>
