<Lang en>

In step 09, you added equal amounts and used <Term id="regroup">trading</Term> to combine them. You also know how to multiply whole numbers in columns; the same trades work with parts of a whole.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>three groups of strips</Beat>. Each group has 4 <Term id="tenth">tenths</Term>, so 0.4 × 3 means 0.4 + 0.4 + 0.4. Multiplication counts equal groups, whether each group holds whole ones or parts of one.

Now <Beat n={2}>trade ten strips for a whole</Beat>. The 12 tenths fill one whole and leave 2 tenths, so 0.4 × 3 = 1.2. Writing 0.4 × 3 = 0.12 treats the <Term id="decimal-point">decimal point</Term> as a separator between two whole numbers: 0 and 4, multiplied separately. But 4 × 3 gives 12 tenths here, while 0.12 means 12 <Term id="hundredth">hundredths</Term>.

Next, <Beat n={3}>look along the three rows of discs</Beat>. Each row shows 2.35: 2 ones, 3 tenths and 5 hundredths. You need three copies of every place, so collect discs from matching columns before making any trades.

Follow the <Beat n={4}>trades beside the column multiplication</Beat>, starting on the right. The hundredths give 5 × 3 = 15: keep 5 hundredths and carry 1 tenth. The tenths give 3 × 3 + 1 = 10: keep 0 tenths and carry 1 one. The ones give 2 × 3 + 1 = 7, so 2.35 × 3 = 7.05. Getting 6.95 means leaving out the carried tenth; that tenth must join the 9 tenths and trigger another trade.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>compare 6 and 7.05 on the number line</Beat>. Since 2.35 is about 2, estimate with 2 × 3 = 6. Misplacing the point gives 70.5 or 0.705; one is far too large and the other far too small for three groups of about 2.

<Lang zh>

乘法是几个相同的数连加。小数乘一位数也是这样，只是加的是十分之一、百分之一。

先看<Beat n={1}>0.4 × 3</Beat>：3 组，每组 4 个十分之一。在正方形上，一组涂 4 条，三组用不同的颜色接着涂。

一共涂了 12 条，也就是<Beat n={2}>12 个十分之一</Beat>。其中 10 个十分之一正好涂满一个整体，换成 1 个一，还剩 2 个十分之一。所以 0.4 × 3 = 1.2。有人会写 0.12，这是把 4 × 3 = 12 直接写在了小数点后面，可是 12 个十分之一已经超过一个整体了，不可能比 1 小。

再看一个大一些的数：<Beat n={3}>2.35 × 3</Beat>。位值表里摆 3 行，每行都是 2 个一、3 个十分之一、5 个百分之一。

把三行<Beat n={4}>推到一起</Beat>，从最右边一位开始算。百分之一：5 × 3 = 15 个，10 个换成 1 个十分之一，剩 5 个。十分之一：3 × 3 = 9 个，加上换来的 1 个，正好 10 个，又换成 1 个一，十分位剩 0。一：2 × 3 = 6 个，加上换来的 1 个，是 7。所以 2.35 × 3 = 7.05。忘了把换来的数加上，就会得出 6.95。

算完以后，<Beat n={5}>估一估</Beat>，检查小数点的位置。2.35 大约是 2，2 × 3 = 6，所以积应该在 6 附近。7.05 在 6 附近，是合理的；70.5 太大，0.705 太小，都是小数点点错了位置。
