<Lang en>

You have seen <Term id="hundredth">hundredths</Term> on the grid and in the <Term id="place-value-chart">place value chart</Term>. You also know how to add whole numbers in columns; the same idea works here.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>two shaded amounts</Beat>: 45 small squares, then 38 more. Each square is one hundredth of the same whole. To find 0.45 + 0.38, you count all the shaded squares together.

Now <Beat n={2}>add the hundredths discs</Beat>. There are 5 + 8 = 13 hundredths. Trade 10 of them for 1 <Term id="tenth">tenth</Term>, leaving 3 hundredths. The total stays the same because ten small squares fill one strip. Ten tenths also trade for 1 one.

Follow the <Beat n={3}>addition in columns</Beat>, with the <Term id="decimal-point">decimal points</Term> lined up. The small 1 records your traded tenth, so the tenths give 4 + 3 + 1 = 8. You have 0.45 + 0.38 = 0.83. Leaving out the carried tenth gives 0.73, a mistake because that tenth still belongs in the total.

For 3.4 + 1.25, <Beat n={4}>match the points</Beat> so each column holds the same-sized parts. Write 3.4 as 3.40: the extra 0 changes no value. Add hundredths, tenths, then ones to get 3.40 + 1.25 = 4.65. Lining up the last digits gives 1.59 because it treats 3.4 as 0.34.

Use the same idea with the <Beat n={5}>noodles and drink amounts</Beat>. The cents give 35 + 80 = 115 cents: 1 dollar 15 cents. Include that dollar with the other 3 dollars, giving $2.35 + $1.80 = $4.15. Treating the point as a separator between two whole numbers can give $3.43 by adding 35 + 8. But 8 tenths of a dollar is 80 cents, so you must add 35 + 80.

<Lang zh>

整数加法要把个位对个位、十位对十位。小数加法也是同样的道理，只是多了十分位和百分位。

先在百格上看<Beat n={1}>0.45 + 0.38</Beat>：先涂 45 格，再涂 38 格。一共涂了多少格？

把两个数放进位值表，<Beat n={2}>先加百分之一</Beat>：5 + 8 = 13 个百分之一。13 个比 10 个多，把其中 10 个换成 1 个十分之一，还剩 3 个百分之一。这和整数加法里的满十进一完全一样，叫做<Term id="regroup">换</Term>。

<Beat n={3}>再加十分之一</Beat>：4 + 3 = 7，再加上换来的 1 个，是 8 个十分之一。所以 0.45 + 0.38 = 0.83。竖式里写在上面的小 1，就是那个换来的十分之一。漏加它，就会得到 0.73。

两个数位数不一样时，要特别小心。<Beat n={4}>3.4 + 1.25</Beat>，竖式要按小数点对齐，不能按最后一位对齐。3.4 的 4 在十分位，要对着 1.25 的 2。可以把 3.4 写成 3.40，两个数位数一样，就不容易对错了：3.40 + 1.25 = 4.65。如果按最后一位对齐，4 个十分之一会和 5 个百分之一加在一起，得出 1.59，比两个数里的 3.4 还小，明显不对。

<Beat n={5}>钱的加法</Beat>也一样。$2.35 + $1.80：35 分加 80 分是 115 分，满了 100 分就是 $1.15，一共 $4.15。如果把 35 和 8 当成两个整数相加，就会写出 $3.43，这是把小数点后面的数当成了另一个整数。
