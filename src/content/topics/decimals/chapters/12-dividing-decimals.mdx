<Lang en>

In the previous step, you multiplied numbers such as 2.35 by a whole number. You also know how to share whole numbers equally, dividing in columns.

<Figure src="figures/decimals-four-cups" alt={{ en: "Pencil drawing of an unlabelled plastic water bottle tilted over the last of four identical glasses in a row; the first three are filled to the same level and water is pouring into the fourth.", zh: "一个没有标签的塑料水瓶的铅笔画：水瓶斜着往一排四个一样的杯子里的最后一个倒水，前三个杯子的水一样高，水正流进第四个杯子。" }} caption={{ en: "One bottle shared equally among four cups: every cup ends at the same level. An illustration made for this lesson.", zh: "一瓶水平均倒进四个杯子，每杯一样多。为本课画的示意图。" }} />

Look at the <Beat n={1}>discs for 8.4 and the four plates</Beat>. There are 8 ones and 4 <Term id="tenth">tenths</Term> to share. Keep the two kinds of discs in their own columns so you can see what each is worth.

Now <Beat n={2}>share each column among the plates</Beat>. The 8 ones give 2 ones each; the 4 tenths give 1 tenth each. So 8.4 ÷ 4 = 2.1. In the column working, put the answer's <Term id="decimal-point">decimal point</Term> directly above the point in 8.4 to keep each digit in its place.

For <Beat n={3}>5.6 shared among four plates</Beat>, give each plate 1 one. There is 1 one left. Trade it for 10 tenths without changing the total. Add the 6 tenths already there: 16 tenths give 4 tenths each. So 5.6 ÷ 4 = 1.4.

Next, <Beat n={4}>look at the empty tenths place</Beat> when sharing 6.12 among six plates. Each gets 1 one, but there are too few tenths to give each plate a whole tenth. Write 0 tenths, then trade the tenth for 10 <Term id="hundredth">hundredths</Term>. With the 2 already there, 12 hundredths give 2 each: 6.12 ÷ 6 = 1.02.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>follow the columns for the bottle of water</Beat>: 1.5 ℓ shared among four cups. Writing 1.5 = 1.500 keeps the amount unchanged. Each cup gets 0 ones. Trade to get 15 tenths: 3 each, with 3 left. Those become 30 hundredths: 7 each, with 2 left. Trade those for 20 <Term id="thousandth">thousandths</Term>: 5 each. Each cup holds 0.375 ℓ. Check by multiplication: 0.375 × 4 = 1.5.

A common mistake is leaving out the zero in the answer, writing 6.12 ÷ 6 = 1.2. That makes the 2 mean tenths instead of hundredths.

<Lang zh>

除法是平均分。小数除以一位数，就是把一位一位的圆片平均分到几个盘子里。

<Figure src="figures/decimals-four-cups" alt={{ en: "Pencil drawing of an unlabelled plastic water bottle tilted over the last of four identical glasses in a row; the first three are filled to the same level and water is pouring into the fourth.", zh: "一个没有标签的塑料水瓶的铅笔画：水瓶斜着往一排四个一样的杯子里的最后一个倒水，前三个杯子的水一样高，水正流进第四个杯子。" }} caption={{ en: "One bottle shared equally among four cups: every cup ends at the same level. An illustration made for this lesson.", zh: "一瓶水平均倒进四个杯子，每杯一样多。为本课画的示意图。" }} />

先看<Beat n={1}>8.4 ÷ 4</Beat>：位值表里有 8 个一和 4 个十分之一，下面是 4 个盘子。

<Beat n={2}>从最高位分起</Beat>：8 个一，每盘 2 个；4 个十分之一，每盘 1 个。每盘有 2 个一和 1 个十分之一，所以 8.4 ÷ 4 = 2.1。竖式里，<Term id="quotient">商</Term>的小数点要和 8.4 的小数点上下对齐。

不是每一位都能正好分完。<Beat n={3}>5.6 ÷ 4</Beat>：5 个一，每盘分 1 个，还剩 1 个。剩下的 1 个一不能扔掉，把它换成 10 个十分之一，和原来的 6 个合在一起，一共 16 个十分之一，每盘 4 个。所以 5.6 ÷ 4 = 1.4。

有时候某一位每盘一个也分不到。<Beat n={4}>6.12 ÷ 6</Beat>：6 个一，每盘 1 个。只有 1 个十分之一，不够分给 6 个盘子，每盘 0 个，商的十分位就写 0，再把这 1 个十分之一换成 10 个百分之一。12 个百分之一，每盘 2 个。商是 1.02。漏写十分位的 0，就成了 1.2，那比 1.02 大了很多。

如果分到最后一位还有剩余，可以在末尾补 0 接着分。<Beat n={5}>一瓶 1.5 ℓ 的水</Beat>平均倒进 4 个杯子：把 1.5 写成 1.500，大小不变，却多出了百分位和千分位可以接着分，每杯 0.375 ℓ。验算一下：0.375 × 4 = 1.5。

准备好了，就<Task id="dividing-decimals">做一道小题</Task>：一位一位地分，剩下的就换开。
