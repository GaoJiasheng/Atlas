<Lang en>

You already know that 10 cents is one <Term id="tenth">tenth</Term> of a dollar. You also know fractions such as <Frac n={3} d={10} />; now you will write tenths another way.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>strip with 3 shaded parts</Beat>. The whole strip is cut into 10 equal parts, so each part is one tenth. The 3 shaded parts together are three tenths, written <Frac n={3} d={10} />. The parts must be equal: counting 3 pieces only tells you the fraction when you know how the whole was divided.

Now look at the <Beat n={2}>square cut into 10 equal strips</Beat>. The shape of the whole has changed, but 3 of its 10 equal parts are still shaded. You can write this fraction as a <Term id="decimal">decimal</Term>: <Frac n={3} d={10} /> = 0.3, read "zero point three". The <Term id="decimal-point">decimal point</Term> comes after the ones place. The 0 shows no whole ones, and the 3 in the first place after the point shows 3 tenths. One tenth is <Frac n={1} d={10} />, written 0.1.

Follow the <Beat n={3}>number line below the square</Beat>. From 0 to 1 there are 10 equal jumps, each worth 0.1. Three jumps from 0 reach 0.3, matching the 3 shaded strips. A common mistake is counting marks instead of jumps. Counting the starting mark uses up a count before you move, so count the gaps you cross.

Next, <Beat n={4}>look past one whole</Beat>. Nine tenths and one more tenth fill a whole: 0.9 + 0.1 = 1.0. The first square is full, and 4 strips in the next square are shaded, showing 1.4: 1 whole and 4 tenths. Writing 0.10 for 0.9 + 0.1 is a mistake because it treats the digits after the point as a separate whole number. Ten tenths make 1 whole, so you write 1.0.

When you are ready, <Task id="tenths">try one question</Task> on showing or reading tenths.

<Lang zh>

上一章你看到，10 分是 1 元的十分之一。这一章离开钱，看看任何一个整体的十分之一怎么写。

先看<Beat n={1}>一张纸条</Beat>。把它平均分成 10 份，涂其中 3 份，就是 <Frac n={3} d={10} />。这是你学过的分数。

再把整体<Beat n={2}>画成一个正方形，切成 10 条</Beat>。每一条是一个<Term id="tenth">十分之一</Term>，写作 0.1。涂 3 条，就是 3 个十分之一，可以写成分数 <Frac n={3} d={10} />，也可以写成小数 0.3，读作“零点三”。小数点前面的 0 表示一个整体也不到，小数点后面的 3 表示 3 个十分之一。

把同样的十分之一<Beat n={3}>放到数轴上</Beat>。0 到 1 之间平均分成 10 段，每段是 0.1。0.3 在离 0 三段远的地方。数的时候要数段，不要数刻度线：如果把 0 那条线也数成“1”，就会少走一段，停在 0.2。

接着往后数：0.7、0.8、0.9，再往后是什么？<Beat n={4}>10 个十分之一</Beat>正好是一个整体，所以 0.9 后面是 1.0，也就是 1。正方形涂满了，就换一个新的正方形接着涂。1 个整体再加 4 个十分之一，写作 1.4：整数部分写在小数点前面，十分之几写在小数点后面。

这里有一个常见的错误：0.9 + 0.1 写成 0.10。这是把小数点后面的数当成了整数，以为 9 加 1 得 10，就写在小数点后面。其实 10 个十分之一已经满了一个整体，要向个位进一，写成 1.0。

准备好了，就<Task id="tenths">做一道小题</Task>：涂一涂十分之几，或者把一位小数放到数轴上。
