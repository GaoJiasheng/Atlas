<Lang en>

You have multiplied and divided by 10, 100 and 1000, and used <Term id="compound-unit">compound units</Term> such as 2 km 45 m. Going further, you can use these skills to write measurements in <Term id="decimal">decimal</Term> form.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the two scales for the run</Beat>. The mark at 1.6 km also shows 1600 m. Since 1 km = 1000 m, each <Term id="tenth">tenth</Term> of a kilometre is 100 m. Six tenths add 600 m to 1000 m. The distance stays the same; smaller units need a larger number. Dividing when changing to a smaller unit is a mistake because it makes the number smaller instead.

Now <Beat n={2}>match litres to millilitres</Beat> for the bottle. Since 1 ℓ = 1000 ml, five tenths of a litre is 500 ml. So 1.5 ℓ = 1500 ml. Reading it as 1 ℓ 5 ml is a mistake: the 5 counts tenths of a litre, not millilitres.

Watch <Beat n={3}>the digits move three places left</Beat> in the <Term id="place-value-chart">place value chart</Term>. Since 1 kg = 1000 g, calculate 0.75 × 1000 = 750: 0.75 kg = 750 g. Multiplying by 100 would give 75 g; that uses the factor for metres to centimetres instead of kilograms to grams.

Next, <Beat n={4}>follow the digits two places right</Beat>. Here 100 cm = 1 m, so 125 ÷ 100 = 1.25. Thus 125 cm = 1.25 m. A metre is larger than a centimetre, so you need fewer metres to describe the same length.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>zoom in on the stopping point</Beat> for Daniel's sister. Her 2 km 45 m is 2045 m. Divide by 1000 to get 2.045 km. On the closest scale, each gap is 1 m. The 0 holds the empty tenths place; 2.45 km would mean 2 km 450 m.

When you are ready, <Task id="measures-in-decimals">try one question</Task> on expressing the same measurement in another unit.

<Lang zh>

你已经会把 2 km 45 m 这样的<Term id="compound-unit">复合单位</Term>化成 2045 m。现在用小数，可以只用一个单位写出同一个量。

先看<Beat n={1}>双刻度的数轴</Beat>：上面是千米，下面是米。小学生 NAPFA 的跑步是 1.6 km。1 km = 1000 m，所以每 0.1 km 是 100 m，1.6 km 正好对着 1600 m。

再看<Beat n={2}>一瓶 1.5 ℓ 的水</Beat>。它装的是 1500 ml，不是 1 ℓ 5 ml。小数点后面的 5 表示 5 个十分之一升，而 1000 ml 的十分之一是 100 ml，5 个就是 500 ml。小数部分是大单位的几分之几，不是小单位的个数。

<Beat n={3}>0.75 kg 的米</Beat>是多少克？千克化克，大单位化小单位，数要变大，乘进率 1000：0.75 × 1000 = 750，所以 0.75 kg = 750 g。在位值表里，就是每个数字往左移三位。如果只乘 100，得到 75 g，太少了：要先想清楚两个单位之间的进率。

<Beat n={4}>反过来</Beat>，小单位化大单位，数要变小，就除以进率：125 cm ÷ 100 = 1.25 m。单位变大了，数就变小。如果这时还去乘，结果会大得离谱。

最后看<Beat n={5}>Daniel 的姐姐</Beat>。她在练 2.4 km 跑，今天跑到 2 km 45 m 停了下来。这是 2045 m，也就是 2.045 km。45 m 是 0.045 km，所以十分位要写 0，让 4 和 5 落在百分位和千分位。数轴放大两次，就能看到 2.045 的位置。

准备好了，就<Task id="measures-in-decimals">做一道小题</Task>：在两个单位之间换算。
