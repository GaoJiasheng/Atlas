<Lang en>

You have used a <Term id="place-value-chart">place value chart</Term> and seen that the <Term id="decimal-point">decimal point</Term> stays between the ones and tenths. Now use those same columns to decide which number is greater.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>two hundred grids</Beat> showing 0.3 and 0.25. Both squares represent the same whole. Each small square is one <Term id="hundredth">hundredth</Term>. You can count 30 shaded squares for 0.3 and 25 for 0.25. Comparing equal-sized parts shows that 0.3 is greater.

Next, <Beat n={2}>line up the points</Beat> in the chart. Write 0.3 as 0.30: the added zero means no extra hundredths, so the amount stays the same. Start at the left and compare matching columns. The ones match, so compare the <Term id="tenth">tenths</Term>. Three tenths is more than two tenths, so 0.30 > 0.25. The first place that differs decides which number is greater.

Now compare the <Beat n={3}>grids for 0.4 and 0.45</Beat>. Both have four full strips shaded. The second has five more small squares. You can write 0.4 as 0.40 to see the matching tenths and the extra hundredths: 0.40 &lt; 0.45.

To order several numbers, look at the <Beat n={4}>four marks on the number line</Beat>. Numbers grow greater as you move right. The mark for 0.07 comes before 0.4; 0.45 sits between 0.4 and 0.7. Reading from left to right gives 0.07 &lt; 0.4 &lt; 0.45 &lt; 0.7, from smallest to greatest.

The longer-is-larger mistake is thinking 0.25 is greater than 0.3 because 25 is greater than 3; the grids show 25 hundredths compared with 30 hundredths. The shorter-is-larger mistake is thinking 0.4 is greater than 0.45 because it has fewer digits; 0.45 includes the same four tenths and five hundredths more.

<Lang zh>

有了位值表，就可以比较两个小数谁大谁小了。不过小数比大小，有两个很容易掉进去的“坑”。

先看<Beat n={1}>0.3 和 0.25</Beat>。很多人会说 0.25 大，因为 25 比 3 大。可是看两个百格：0.3 涂了 30 格，0.25 只涂了 25 格。0.3 更大。小数点后面的数字不能当成整数来比。

怎样不看图也能比？<Beat n={2}>按小数点对齐</Beat>，再把位数补成一样多：0.3 写成 0.30。然后从左往右一位一位地比。个位都是 0，十分位一个是 3、一个是 2，3 个十分之一比 2 个十分之一多，所以 0.30 > 0.25。高位一旦比出大小，后面的位就不用再看了。

反过来也有坑。<Beat n={3}>0.4 和 0.45</Beat>，有人想：“十分之一比百分之一大，0.4 只有十分位，所以 0.4 大。”可是 0.45 也有 4 个十分之一，还多出 5 个百分之一。对齐写成 0.40 和 0.45，就看得很清楚：0.4 &lt; 0.45。

几个小数排顺序，可以<Beat n={4}>放到数轴上</Beat>：越往右的数越大。也可以把它们都对齐、补成一样多的位，再一位一位地比。从小到大是 0.07、0.4、0.45、0.7。注意 0.07 和 0.7：十分位上的 0 让 0.07 比 0.1 还小。

记住两句话：位数多的不一定大，位数少的也不一定大。真正决定大小的是从左往右、同一位上的数字。
