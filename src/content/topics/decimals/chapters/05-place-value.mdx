<Lang en>

You have seen <Term id="tenth">tenths</Term>, <Term id="hundredth">hundredths</Term> and <Term id="thousandth">thousandths</Term> on grids and zoomed number lines. Now you can give each of these parts its own column in a <Term id="place-value-chart">place value chart</Term>.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>discs for 0.235</Beat>. You can count 2 tenths, 3 hundredths and 5 thousandths in separate columns. There are no whole ones, so the ones digit is 0. The point sits between the ones and tenths, showing where parts smaller than one begin.

Next, <Beat n={2}>look across 34.506</Beat>. The 3 tens are worth 30, and the 4 ones are worth 4. The 5 tenths are worth 0.5, and the 6 thousandths are worth 0.006: 34.506 = 30 + 4 + 0.5 + 0.006. Each place is ten times the place on its right, because ten of the smaller parts make one of the larger parts.

Notice the <Beat n={3}>empty hundredths column</Beat>. Its 0 keeps the 6 in the thousandths place. Leaving out this placeholder zero is a mistake: writing 34.56 moves the 6 into hundredths, making it worth 0.06 instead of 0.006. So 34.506 ≠ 34.56.

Now <Beat n={4}>put the named parts into columns</Beat>: 4 ones, 7 hundredths and 2 tenths. Match each part to its column to get 4 + 0.07 + 0.2 = 4.27. Writing 4.72 is a place-order mistake: it follows the order you hear, putting the 7 in tenths and the 2 in hundredths.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>read the digits of 34.506</Beat>: "thirty-four point five zero six". After the point, say each digit separately, including zero. Reading "thirty-four point five hundred and six" is a mistake because it treats the point as a separator between two whole numbers. Here, 506 means 5 tenths, 0 hundredths and 6 thousandths.

When you are ready, <Task id="place-value">try one question</Task> on reading or writing a number using its places.

<Lang zh>

十分之一、百分之一、千分之一都认识了。现在把它们放进同一张表里，看清每个数字的<Term id="place-value">位值</Term>。

先把<Beat n={1}>0.235 放进位值表</Beat>。这张<Term id="place-value-chart">位值表</Term>在个位右边接着排十分位、百分位、千分位。十分位放 2 个 0.1 圆片，百分位放 3 个 0.01，千分位放 5 个 0.001。小数点就在个位和十分位之间，它的位置永远不变。

数大了，<Beat n={2}>左边多几位</Beat>：34.506 里，3 在十位，4 在个位，5 表示 5 个十分之一，6 表示 6 个千分之一：34.506 = 30 + 4 + 0.5 + 0.006。每一位都是它右边一位的十倍。

再看<Beat n={3}>百分位</Beat>：这里一个圆片也没有，所以写 0。漏掉这个 0，6 就跑到了百分位，成了 34.56，那是另一个数。

有时候题目说的顺序是乱的：<Beat n={4}>4 个一、7 个百分之一、2 个十分之一</Beat>。不要照着说的顺序写成 4.72，而要把每个数字放进自己的列：2 放十分位，7 放百分位，写成 4.27。

最后看<Beat n={5}>怎么读</Beat>。34.506 读作“三十四点五零六”。小数点前面按整数读，小数点后面一个数字一个数字地读，0 也要读出来。小数点后面的 506 不是“五百零六”，而是 5 个十分之一、0 个百分之一、6 个千分之一。

漏掉占位的 0、按说的顺序摆数字、把小数点后面当成一个整数来读，这几种错误都出在“位”上。看清每个数字在哪一列，就能避开。

准备好了，就<Task id="place-value">做一道小题</Task>：说出一个数字表示多少，或者按位写出一个小数。
