<Lang en>

In the last step, you saw that 0.3 means 3 <Term id="tenth">tenths</Term>, or 3 strips of the square. Now you will split those strips into smaller equal parts.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>3 shaded strips</Beat>. The whole square has 10 equal strips, so each strip is one tenth. You have shaded 3 of those 10 parts. Keep this shaded area in mind: cutting it into smaller pieces will change how you count it, but not how much is shaded.

Now <Beat n={2}>cut every strip into 10 small squares</Beat>. There are 100 equal squares in the whole. Each small square is one <Term id="hundredth">hundredth</Term>: <Frac n={1} d={100} />, written 0.01. The 3 shaded strips contain 30 small squares altogether. So 0.3 = 0.30 = <Frac n={30} d={100} />. You are counting the same area in hundredths instead of tenths.

Next, <Beat n={3}>shade 5 more small squares</Beat>. Keep the 30 squares already shaded and count the extra 5: there are now 35. Write 0.3 + 0.05 = 0.35. This <Term id="decimal">decimal</Term> has 3 tenths and 5 hundredths, or <Frac n={35} d={100} />. Read it "zero point three five", saying each digit after the point separately.

Finally, <Beat n={4}>compare 7 small squares with 7 strips</Beat>. Seven small squares do not fill even one strip. Write 0.07: the first digit after the <Term id="decimal-point">decimal point</Term> is 0 because there are no complete tenths. The second digit, 7, counts hundredths. In 0.7, the 7 counts tenths: 7 whole strips, or 70 small squares. So 0.07 ≠ 0.7.

Thinking 0.30 is bigger than 0.3 is a mistake: the extra zero adds no shaded squares. Writing 7 hundredths as 0.7 leaves out the zero that holds the tenths place, turning 7 small squares into 7 strips.

<Lang zh>

十分之一已经会写了。可是钱里还有“分”，有的量比一条还小，这就要用到更小的单位。

先看<Beat n={1}>正方形里的 3 条</Beat>，这是 0.3。

把每一条都<Beat n={2}>切成 10 小格</Beat>。现在整个正方形有 100 小格，每一小格是一个<Term id="hundredth">百分之一</Term>，写作 0.01。原来的 3 条变成了 30 小格，所以 0.3 也可以写成 0.30，也就是 <Frac n={30} d={100} />。涂的部分一点没变，只是数的单位变小了。这告诉我们：小数<Term id="trailing-zero">末尾的 0</Term> 添上或去掉，大小不变。

在 3 条旁边<Beat n={3}>再涂 5 小格</Beat>。现在有 3 个十分之一和 5 个百分之一，写作 0.35，读作“零点三五”。小数点后面的数字要一个一个地读，不读“零点三十五”。把 3 条也换成小格来数，一共是 35 小格，所以 0.35 也是 35 个百分之一，等于 <Frac n={35} d={100} />。

如果<Beat n={4}>只涂 7 小格</Beat>呢？这还不到一整条，没有十分之一，所以十分位要写 0，百分位写 7：0.07。旁边淡淡的那幅图是 0.7，是 7 整条，比 0.07 多得多。十分位上的 0 就是用来占位的，它告诉你“一条也没有”。

这一章有两个常见的错误。一是以为 0.30 比 0.3 大，因为它多了一个数字；可是 30 小格和 3 条涂的是同样多。二是把 7 个百分之一写成 0.7，漏掉了占位的 0。
