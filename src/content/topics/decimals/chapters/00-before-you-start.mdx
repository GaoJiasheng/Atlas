<Lang en>

This lesson is about <Term id="decimal">decimals</Term>: numbers such as 0.35 and 2.4, where the digits after the <Term id="decimal-point">decimal point</Term> count tenths, hundredths and thousandths. It starts with something you use every day, money, and ends with working out with decimals.

There are 15 steps and a practice. Step 01 starts from dollars and cents. Steps 02 to 05 build tenths, hundredths, thousandths and their places. Steps 06 to 08 compare decimals, change them into fractions and back, and round them. Steps 09 to 13 add, subtract, multiply and divide. Steps 14 and 15 go further: multiplying and dividing by 10, 100 and 1000, and writing measurements as decimals. Their chapters are marked "Going further". The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

Each step explains first. The stage uses four pictures: coins sorted into dollars, ten cents and cents; a hundred grid, where one square is a hundredth and a magnifier shows thousandths; a number line that zooms in; and a place value chart with discs and the column method. The chapter axis at the bottom has a small circle for every beat: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the stage shades, zooms, trades, shares or slides while the reading panel says what to look at.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question, drawn from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: shade a grid, place a number on a zooming line, line up two numbers on the point, share discs, slide digits, or type a decimal.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** draws the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, up to three, each one closer to the answer.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Every question can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved.

<Lang zh>

这节课讲<Term id="decimal">小数</Term>：像 0.35、2.4 这样的数，<Term id="decimal-point">小数点</Term>后面的数字表示十分之几、百分之几、千分之几。课从每天都会用到的钱讲起，一直讲到小数的计算。

全课有 15 步和一章练习。第 01 步从元和分讲起。第 02 到 05 步讲十分之一、百分之一、千分之一和它们所在的位。第 06 到 08 步讲小数比大小、小数和分数互化、四舍五入。第 09 到 13 步讲小数的加、减、乘、除。第 14、15 步再往前一步：乘除以 10、100、1000，以及用小数表示量，这两章的章头标着“再往前一步”。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

每一步先讲。舞台上用四种图：按元、10 分、分排好的硬币；百格，一小格是百分之一，放大镜里看千分之一；可以放大的数轴；带圆片和竖式的位值表。屏幕底部的章节轴上，每一拍有一个小圆点：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上就会涂色、放大、换开、平均分或者移动数字，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。

每章最后的小方块是一道小题，从十道左右的题里挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：在百格上涂色，在放大的数轴上放一个数，按小数点对齐两个数，平均分圆片，移动数字，或者输入一个小数。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按“看答案”，舞台会画出答案。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，最多三条，一条比一条接近答案。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

每道题都能只用点按完成，也能只用键盘完成。不计时，也不保存。
