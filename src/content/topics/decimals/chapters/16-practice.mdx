<Lang en>

The practice has eight questions from the whole lesson, in a fixed order. They use the same kinds of tasks as the chapters: shading a hundred grid, placing a number on a number line that zooms in, putting decimals in order, writing a fraction, rounding on a number line, lining up two numbers on the point, and typing a decimal. Questions 1 to 5 come from place value, comparing, fractions and rounding. Questions 6 to 8 are stories about ribbon, string and money. Question 5 asks you to tap the nearer end first, then to write the rounded number. Question 6 asks you to line up the points first, then to write the difference.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why. If it is not, the stage draws the answer with a dashed line, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time before pressing it.

Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. "Try again" starts the same eight questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

Question 1 is on the stage, with its answer controls above this text. The squares on the chapter axis are the eight questions.

<Lang zh>

练习有 8 道题，来自整节课，顺序固定。题型和各章的小题一样：在百格上涂色，在可以放大的数轴上放一个数，给小数排顺序，写一个分数，在数轴上四舍五入，按小数点对齐两个数，输入一个小数。第 1 到 5 题来自位值、比大小、分数和四舍五入；第 6 到 8 题是关于丝带、绳子和钱的故事。第 5 题要先点较近的一端，再写出近似值；第 6 题要先对齐小数点，再写出差。

每题只检查一次。按“检查”以后，马上就能看到对不对、为什么。如果还没对，舞台会用虚线画出答案，还有一个按钮可以回到教这一点的那一步。检查以后答案就不能再改，所以按之前慢慢想。

每题有一条提示，看了提示不扣分。

做完最后一题，会出现一张小表：每题一行，标出 ✓ 或 ○，以及可以回看的那一步。按“再做一次”，同样的 8 道题从头开始。不计时，也不保存。

第 1 题已经在舞台上，作答的地方在这段文字上方。章节轴上的 8 个小方块就是这 8 道题。
