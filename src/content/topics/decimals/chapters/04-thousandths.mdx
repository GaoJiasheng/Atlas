<Lang en>

In the previous step, you showed 0.23 as 23 <Term id="hundredth">hundredths</Term> on the hundred grid. Now you will look inside one small square to show a smaller part.

Look again at <Beat n={1}>the 2 strips and 3 squares</Beat>. Each full strip is one <Term id="tenth">tenth</Term> of the whole, and each small square is one hundredth. The strips show 0.2 and the extra squares show 0.03, making 0.23 altogether.

Use the magnifier to <Beat n={2}>look inside the next square</Beat>. It is cut into 10 equal thin slivers. Each sliver is one <Term id="thousandth">thousandth</Term> of the whole, written 0.001 or <Frac n={1} d={1000} />. There are 100 squares in the whole, so cutting each into 10 slivers would give 1000 equal parts. Five slivers make 0.005. Together with the shaded strips and squares, they give 0.235 = 0.2 + 0.03 + 0.005.

On the number line, <Beat n={3}>zoom into the gap from 0.2 to 0.3</Beat>. You know 0.235 lies inside this gap because it is more than 0.2 but less than 0.3. The gap has a value of 0.1. Splitting it into 10 equal jumps makes each new jump 0.01, so you can now find 0.23 and 0.24.

Next, <Beat n={4}>zoom from 0.23 to 0.24</Beat>. This gap has a value of 0.01. Split it into 10 equal jumps again: each jump is now 0.001. Start at 0.23 and count 5 jumps to reach 0.235. Read this number as "zero point two three five". Each new jump is a tenth of the jump on the line above.

A common mistake is to keep using the jump size from the line above after zooming in. Check the end labels: from 0.23 to 0.24, 0.01 is the whole gap, so each of its 10 jumps is 0.001.

<Lang zh>

从十分之一到百分之一，我们把每一份又切成了 10 份。这样的事还能再做一次。

先在百格上看<Beat n={1}>0.23</Beat>：2 整条，再加 3 小格。

接下来的一小格太小了，要<Beat n={2}>用放大镜看</Beat>。把它切成 10 条细条，每一细条是一个<Term id="thousandth">千分之一</Term>，写作 0.001。涂其中 5 条，就是 0.005。整幅图一共是 2 个十分之一、3 个百分之一和 5 个千分之一：0.235 = 0.2 + 0.03 + 0.005。千分之一是百分之一的十分之一，小得几乎看不见，所以要放大来看。

数轴上也可以放大。0.235 在 0.2 和 0.3 之间，<Beat n={3}>把这一段放大</Beat>，下面就出现一条新的数轴，从 0.2 到 0.3，又平均分成 10 段，每段是 0.01。

0.235 在 0.23 和 0.24 之间，<Beat n={4}>再放大一次</Beat>：最下面这条数轴从 0.23 到 0.24，每段是 0.001。从 0.23 往后数 5 段，就是 0.235。三条数轴，一条比一条细：每往下一层，一段就是上一层一段的十分之一。

读放大的数轴时，容易犯的错误是忘了换单位。比如在 0.2 到 0.3 那条线上，箭头指在第 3 段，有人读成 0.5，好像每段还是 0.1；也有人读成 0.203，好像每段已经是 0.001。先看这条线两端的数，算出一段有多大，再数段。
