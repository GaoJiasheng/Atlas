<Lang en>

In step 09, you added <Term id="decimal">decimals</Term> and traded 10 smaller units for 1 larger unit. For subtraction, you can reverse that trade.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>72 shaded squares with 35 crossed out</Beat>. Each square is 1 <Term id="hundredth">hundredth</Term>, so removing 35 squares takes away 0.35. The squares left show how much remains.

On the chart, <Beat n={2}>trade from the tenths column</Beat>. You have only 2 hundredths but need to remove 5. Trade 1 <Term id="tenth">tenth</Term> for 10 hundredths, leaving 6 tenths and 12 hundredths. You <Term id="regroup">regroup</Term> without changing the value: 0.72 = 0.6 + 0.12.

Now <Beat n={3}>match the discs to the column subtraction</Beat>. Line up the points so equal places meet. Subtract from the right: 12 − 5 = 7 hundredths, then 6 − 3 = 3 tenths. So 0.72 − 0.35 = 0.37. Taking the smaller digit from the larger in every column gives the mistaken answer 0.43: it reverses the subtraction in the hundredths instead of trading.

Next, <Beat n={4}>write 5 as 5.00</Beat> above 1.47. The zeros show empty places without changing 5. Trade 1 one for 10 tenths, then 1 tenth for 10 hundredths. You now have 4 ones, 9 tenths and 10 hundredths. Subtract to get 5.00 − 1.47 = 3.53. Bringing the decimal digits down unchanged gives the mistaken answer 4.47; those digits describe what you take away, not what remains.

Finally, <Beat n={5}>look at Ravi's $10 note and the subtraction</Beat>. His noodles and drink total $6.85, so his change is $10.00 − $6.85 = $3.15. Check by adding: $6.85 + $3.15 = $10.00. The amount spent and the change must make the amount paid.

<Lang zh>

加法会了，减法就是反过来：从一个数里拿走另一个数。

先在百格上看<Beat n={1}>0.72 − 0.35</Beat>：从涂好的 72 格里，划掉 35 格。

在位值表里减，先看百分位。只有 2 个百分之一，不够拿走 5 个。这时要<Beat n={2}>从十分位退一</Beat>：拿 1 个十分之一换成 10 个百分之一。现在是 6 个十分之一和 12 个百分之一，总数还是 0.72，只是换了一种摆法。

现在可以<Beat n={3}>一位一位地减</Beat>了：12 − 5 = 7 个百分之一，6 − 3 = 3 个十分之一，差是 0.37。常见的错误是每一位都用大的数减小的数：百分位算 5 − 2 = 3，十分位算 7 − 3 = 4，得出 0.43。减法要用上面的数减下面的数，不够减就退位，不能把顺序倒过来。

被减数的位数少时更要小心。<Beat n={4}>5 − 1.47</Beat>：5 后面看不到十分位和百分位，其实它就是 5.00。先把两个 0 补上，按小数点对齐，再从百分位起退位：5.00 − 1.47 = 3.53。如果把 .47 直接落下来，就会得出 4.47，可是 5 减去比 1 还多的数，怎么会只少了 0.53 呢？

最后用一个找零的例子。<Beat n={5}>Ravi 用一张 $10 纸币</Beat>买面和饮料，一共 $6.85。找回 $10.00 − $6.85 = $3.15。减完以后用加法验算：$6.85 + $3.15 = $10.00，对得上，说明差算对了。
