<Lang en>

You have split an <Term id="array">array</Term> into parts you know, and you have seen that a multiplication can be turned round. Those two ideas are enough to build the 6, 7, 8 and 9 <Term id="times-table">multiplication tables</Term> without learning every fact from scratch.

Look at <Beat n={1}>6 rows of 7</Beat>. This is 6 × 7, a new fact. You could count all the dots, but there is a part you already know: 5 rows of 7.

So <Beat n={2}>split off the 5 rows</Beat>. The top part is 5 × 7 = 35. The bottom part is one more row of 7. Add the parts: 6 × 7 = 5 × 7 + 1 × 7 = 35 + 7 = 42. Every 6 × fact is a 5 × fact and one more group.

Now <Beat n={3}>turn the array round</Beat>: 7 rows of 6. Turning adds and takes away nothing, so 7 × 6 = 6 × 7 = 42. Many facts in a new table are ones you have already met the other way round.

For 9 × 6, <Beat n={4}>start from 10 rows</Beat> and cross one out. Ten rows of 6 make 60. Take away the whole last row of 6: 9 × 6 = 10 × 6 − 1 × 6 = 60 − 6 = 54. Every 9 × fact is a 10 × fact and one group less.

For 8 × 7, <Beat n={5}>split the array into two equal halves</Beat>. Each half is 4 rows of 7, which is 28. Two halves make 28 + 28 = 56. Every 8 × fact is double a 4 × fact.

The common mistake is to add or take away 1 instead of a whole row. In 6 × 7 the extra row holds 7 dots, so it is 35 + 7, not 35 + 1. In 9 × 6 the crossed-out row holds 6 dots, so it is 60 − 6, not 60 − 1.

When you are ready, <Task id="tables-6-to-9">try one question</Task> on working out a fact from the 6, 7, 8 or 9 tables.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

你已经会把<Term id="array">阵列</Term>切成会算的几块，也知道乘法可以换个顺序。有了这两样本领，6、7、8、9 的<Term id="times-table">乘法表</Term>就不必一道一道从头死记了。

先看<Beat n={1}>6 行、每行 7 个</Beat>。这是 6 × 7，一道新的乘法。可以一个一个地数，可是这里面有你会的部分：5 行 7 个。

那就<Beat n={2}>先切出 5 行</Beat>。上面一块是 5 × 7 = 35。下面一块是再多一行，7 个。两块加起来：6 × 7 = 5 × 7 + 1 × 7 = 35 + 7 = 42。6 的乘法表里每一道，都是 5 × 再多一组。

再<Beat n={3}>把阵列转过来</Beat>：7 行，每行 6 个。转一下，一个没多、一个没少，所以 7 × 6 = 6 × 7 = 42。新乘法表里的很多算式，你其实已经会了，只是顺序反过来。

算 9 × 6 时，<Beat n={4}>从 10 行算起</Beat>，再划掉一行。10 行 6 个是 60，拿走最后一整行 6 个：9 × 6 = 10 × 6 − 1 × 6 = 60 − 6 = 54。9 的乘法表里每一道，都是 10 × 再少一组。

算 8 × 7 时，<Beat n={5}>把阵列切成一样大的两半</Beat>。每一半是 4 行 7 个，等于 28。两半合起来：28 + 28 = 56。8 的乘法表里每一道，都是 4 × 的两倍。7 的乘法表也一样能推出来：7 × 是 5 × 再多两组。

常见的错误是只加 1 或只减 1，而不是加上或减去一整行。算 6 × 7，多出的一行有 7 个，所以是 35 + 7，不是 35 + 1；算 9 × 6，划掉的一行有 6 个，所以是 60 − 6，不是 60 − 1。

准备好了，就<Task id="tables-6-to-9">做一道小题</Task>：用会的乘法，推出 6、7、8、9 的乘法表里的一道。
