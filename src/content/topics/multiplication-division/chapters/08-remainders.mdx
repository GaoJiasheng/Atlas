<Lang en>

In step 05 every division came out exactly: the things filled the groups with nothing left. Often there are a few left over that cannot make another full group.

<Figure src="figures/multiplication-division-curry-puff-boxes" alt={{ en: "Pencil drawing of four small cardboard boxes on a table: three closed, one open with four curry puffs inside, and two loose curry puffs on a sheet of paper in front.", zh: "桌上四个小纸盒的铅笔画：三个盖着，一个打开，里面装着 4 个咖喱角；前面一张纸上放着 2 个没装进盒子的咖喱角。" }} caption={{ en: "Four boxes of 4 are packed and 2 curry puffs are left over: 18 ÷ 4 = 4 r 2. An illustration made for this lesson.", zh: "装好 4 盒、每盒 4 个，还剩 2 个咖喱角：18 ÷ 4 = 4 r 2。为本课画的示意图。" }} />

Look at the <Beat n={1}>14 curry puffs to be packed</Beat> in boxes of 4. How many boxes can you fill? That is 14 ÷ 4 = ?.

Fill the boxes and you get <Beat n={2}>3 full boxes and 2 left over</Beat>. The 3 boxes hold 12. The other 2 are not enough for another box of 4. The amount left over is the <Term id="remainder">remainder</Term>. Write 14 ÷ 4 = 3 r 2 and read it "14 divided by 4 is 3, remainder 2". The 3 is the <Term id="quotient">quotient</Term>.

Now imagine you stopped at <Beat n={3}>2 boxes with 6 left over</Beat>. Inside those 6 there is still a full box of 4, drawn with a dashed line. When the leftovers can fill another group, you have not finished. So the remainder is always less than the <Term id="dividend-divisor">divisor</Term>, the number in each group. Here it must be less than 4.

To check, <Beat n={4}>line the boxes up as rows</Beat>: 3 rows of 4 is 12, and the 2 left over make 14. Quotient × divisor + remainder gives back the number you started with: 3 × 4 + 2 = 14.

The last beat is a different story: <Beat n={5}>14 children, 4 in each car</Beat>. The division is still 14 ÷ 4 = 3 r 2. But the 2 children left over cannot stay behind, so you need 4 cars. If the question asked how many full boxes you can pack, the answer would be 3. Read the question before you decide what to do with the remainder.

So there are three mistakes to avoid: leaving a remainder big enough for another group, leaving the remainder out of the answer, and forgetting what the story needs.

When you are ready, <Task id="remainders">try one question</Task> on a division with something left over.

<Lang zh>

第 05 步里的除法都正好分完，一个也不剩。可是很多时候，分到最后会剩下几个，不够再分一组。

<Figure src="figures/multiplication-division-curry-puff-boxes" alt={{ en: "Pencil drawing of four small cardboard boxes on a table: three closed, one open with four curry puffs inside, and two loose curry puffs on a sheet of paper in front.", zh: "桌上四个小纸盒的铅笔画：三个盖着，一个打开，里面装着 4 个咖喱角；前面一张纸上放着 2 个没装进盒子的咖喱角。" }} caption={{ en: "Four boxes of 4 are packed and 2 curry puffs are left over: 18 ÷ 4 = 4 r 2. An illustration made for this lesson.", zh: "装好 4 盒、每盒 4 个，还剩 2 个咖喱角：18 ÷ 4 = 4 r 2。为本课画的示意图。" }} />

先看<Beat n={1}>14 个咖喱角</Beat>，每 4 个装一盒。能装满几盒？这就是 14 ÷ 4 = ?。

一盒一盒地装，结果是<Beat n={2}>装满 3 盒，还剩 2 个</Beat>。3 盒装了 12 个，剩下的 2 个不够再装一盒。分剩下的数叫<Term id="remainder">余数</Term>。写作 14 ÷ 4 = 3 r 2，读作"14 除以 4 等于 3 余 2"。这里的 3 是<Term id="quotient">商</Term>。

假如只装了<Beat n={3}>2 盒，剩下 6 个</Beat>，会怎样？这 6 个里面还能装满一盒，图上用虚线圈了出来。剩下的还够再分一组，就说明还没分完。所以余数一定比<Term id="dividend-divisor">除数</Term>小，这里除数是每盒的 4 个，余数只能比 4 小。

想验算，就<Beat n={4}>把盒子排成几行</Beat>：3 行、每行 4 个是 12 个，加上剩下的 2 个正好是 14 个。商 × 除数 + 余数，就回到原来的数：3 × 4 + 2 = 14。

最后一拍换了一个故事：<Beat n={5}>14 个小朋友坐车，每辆坐 4 人</Beat>。算式还是 14 ÷ 4 = 3 r 2，可是剩下的 2 个小朋友不能留在原地，还要再来一辆车，所以一共要 4 辆。如果题目问的是"能装满几盒"，答案就是 3。余数要不要再算一组，先看清题目问什么。

总结起来，要避开三个错误：余数大到还能再分一组；答案里漏了余数；忘了题目里的故事需要什么。
