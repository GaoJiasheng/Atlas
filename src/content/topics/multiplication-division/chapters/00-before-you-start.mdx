<Lang en>

This lesson is about <Term id="multiplication">multiplication</Term> and <Term id="division">division</Term> of whole numbers. It starts with the idea underneath both, <Term id="equal-groups">equal groups</Term>, and ends with the column method for bigger numbers.

There are 12 steps and a practice. Steps 01 to 04 are about multiplying: equal groups, counting in 2s, 5s and 10s, arrays, and new facts from facts you know. Steps 05 and 06 are about dividing: the two kinds of division, and how × and ÷ belong together. Steps 07 and 08 add the 6, 7, 8 and 9 tables and division with a remainder. Steps 09 to 11 work with bigger numbers: tens and hundreds, then multiplying and dividing in columns. Step 12 goes further, to two-digit by two-digit, and its chapter is marked "Going further". The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

Each step explains first. The chapter axis at the bottom of the screen has a small circle for every beat: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the stage groups, deals, lines up, splits, covers, trades or shares while the reading panel says what to look at. The text below the beat line walks through the same beats.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question on its idea, drawn from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: put things into groups, make an array, jump along a number line, cut an array, trade or share place-value blocks, or type a number.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** draws the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, up to three, each one closer to the answer.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Every question can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved: when you leave the page, your answers are gone.

这节课讲整数的<Term id="multiplication">乘法</Term>和<Term id="division">除法</Term>。课从两者共同的基础<Term id="equal-groups">相同的几组</Term>讲起，一直讲到大一些的数怎样用竖式来算。

全课有 12 步和一章练习。第 01 到 04 步讲乘法：相同的几组，2 个 2 个、5 个 5 个、10 个 10 个地数，阵列，还有用会的乘法算新的。第 05、06 步讲除法：除法的两种问法，以及乘法和除法怎样是一家。第 07、08 步加上 6、7、8、9 的乘法表和有余数的除法。第 09 到 11 步换成大一些的数：整十、整百数乘一位数，再到乘法竖式和除法竖式。第 12 步再往前一步，讲两位数乘两位数，这一章的章头标着「再往前一步」。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

每一步先讲。屏幕底部的章节轴上，每一拍有一个小圆点：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上的东西就会分组、发下去、排成行、切开、盖住、换开或者平均分，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。节拍下面的正文也按同样的顺序讲一遍。

每章最后的小方块是一道关于这一章的小题，从十道左右的题里挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：把东西分成几组，排一个阵列，在数轴上跳，把阵列切开，换开或平均分数位块，或者输入一个数。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按「看答案」，舞台会画出答案。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，最多三条，一条比一条接近答案。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

每道题都能只用点按完成，也能只用键盘完成。不计时，也不保存：离开这一页，答案就清空了。
