<Lang en>

You know the <Term id="times-table">multiplication tables</Term>. This step uses them to multiply tens and hundreds. From here on, the number being multiplied is written first, as in the column method: 30 × 4 means 4 rows of 30. Turning an array showed you that the order does not change the answer.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>4 rows of 3 ones</Beat>. Each small block is 1, so there are 12 ones: 3 × 4 = 12.

Now each row is <Beat n={2}>3 tens instead of 3 ones</Beat>. Each long block is a ten, so each row is 30. The picture is the same as before, 4 rows of 3 blocks, so there are 12 blocks again. But now the blocks are tens: 3 tens × 4 = 12 tens. This is <Term id="place-value">place value</Term> at work: the same 3 × 4, counted in tens.

<Beat n={3}>Push the tens together</Beat> into the tens column. There are 12 tens. Ten of them are lit up, because 10 tens can make a hundred.

So <Beat n={4}>trade 10 tens for 1 hundred</Beat>. To <Term id="regroup">regroup</Term> like this changes how the blocks are grouped, not how much they are worth. Now there is 1 hundred and 2 tens: 100 + 20 = 120. So 30 × 4 = 120.

Hundreds work the same way. In <Beat n={5}>3 rows of 2 hundreds</Beat> there are 6 hundred-squares, because 2 × 3 = 6. So 200 × 3 = 6 hundreds = 600. Work out the small fact, then say what you were counting.

Two mistakes are common. Treating tens as ones gives 30 × 4 = 12, but the blocks are 12 tens, which is 120. Adding an extra zero gives 1200, which would be 12 hundreds. Saying the unit out loud, "12 tens", keeps the zeros right.

When you are ready, <Task id="multiplying-tens">try one question</Task> on multiplying tens or hundreds by a number.

<Lang zh>

<Term id="times-table">乘法表</Term>你已经会了。这一步用它来算整十、整百数乘一位数。从这一步起，算式先写被乘的数，和竖式的写法一样：30 × 4 表示 4 行、每行 30。阵列转一转就知道，两个数换个顺序，得数不变。

先看<Beat n={1}>4 行、每行 3 个一</Beat>。每个小方块表示 1，一共 12 个一：3 × 4 = 12。

再看每行<Beat n={2}>换成 3 个十</Beat>。每根长条是一个十，所以每行是 30。图的样子和刚才一样，还是 4 行、每行 3 块，一共 12 块。可是现在每块是十：3 个十 × 4 = 12 个十。这就是<Term id="place-value">位值</Term>：同样是 3 × 4，只是数的单位变成了十。

<Beat n={3}>把十都推到十位那一列</Beat>，一共 12 个十。其中 10 个十亮了起来，因为 10 个十可以换成 1 个百。

于是<Beat n={4}>拿 10 个十换 1 个百</Beat>。这样<Term id="regroup">换</Term>一换，块的分法变了，值没有变。现在有 1 个百和 2 个十：100 + 20 = 120。所以 30 × 4 = 120。12 个十写成数，就是 120。

百也一样。<Beat n={5}>3 行、每行 2 个百</Beat>，一共 6 块百，因为 2 × 3 = 6。所以 200 × 3 = 6 个百 = 600。先算小的那道乘法，再说清楚数的是什么。

常见的错误有两种。一种是把十当成一：30 × 4 写成 12。其实这里是 12 个十，就是 120。另一种是多写了一个 0：写成 1200，那就成了 12 个百。算的时候把单位说出来，“12 个十”，0 的个数就不会多写或少写。
