<Lang en>

So far you have used <Term id="multiplication">multiplication</Term> to find the total of <Term id="equal-groups">equal groups</Term>. Now you start with the total and find something about the groups.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>12 curry puffs and 3 plates</Beat>. Every plate must get the same number, and all 12 must be used. You know how many plates there are, but not how many go on each. This is <Term id="division">division</Term>, written 12 ÷ 3 = ?.

To share them fairly, <Beat n={2}>deal them out one at a time</Beat>, round and round: one to each plate, then another round. Each round keeps the plates equal. After 4 rounds the curry puffs are all gone and each plate has 4. Write 12 ÷ 3 = 4 and read it "12 divided by 3 equals 4". Here the 4 counts curry puffs on each plate.

Now a different question: <Beat n={3}>pack the 12 into boxes of 3</Beat>. Fill one box before starting the next. This time you know how many go in a box, and you are finding how many boxes. There are 4 full boxes and none left over. Again 12 ÷ 3 = 4, but now the 4 counts boxes.

Look at the <Beat n={4}>plates and boxes side by side</Beat>. Sharing into 3 groups gives 4 in each group. Packing in 3s gives 4 groups. Both start with the same 12 and both make equal groups, so both are 12 ÷ 3. The story tells you what the 3 means and what the answer counts.

Two mistakes are common. One is to think division only means sharing; packing in equal groups is division too. The other is to write the numbers the wrong way round, 3 ÷ 12. The number you start with, 12, always comes first.

When you are ready, <Task id="sharing-and-grouping">try one question</Task> on sharing equally or packing in equal groups.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

前面几步都是知道有几组、每组几个，用<Term id="multiplication">乘法</Term>求一共有多少。这一步反过来：先知道一共有多少，再去找组里的数。

先看<Beat n={1}>12 个咖喱角和 3 个盘子</Beat>。每盘要一样多，12 个都要放完。盘子有几个，你知道；每盘放几个，还不知道。这就要用<Term id="division">除法</Term>，写作 12 ÷ 3 = ?。

想分得公平，就<Beat n={2}>一轮一个地发</Beat>：每个盘子先放一个，再来一轮，一轮一轮地发下去。每发完一轮，各盘还是一样多。发了 4 轮，咖喱角发完了，每盘 4 个。写作 12 ÷ 3 = 4，读作"12 除以 3 等于 4"。这里的 4，数的是每盘有几个咖喱角。

再换一个问题：<Beat n={3}>每 3 个装一盒</Beat>，能装几盒？装满一盒，再装下一盒。这一次，每盒装几个是知道的，要找的是盒数。一共装满 4 盒，一个也不剩。还是 12 ÷ 3 = 4，可是这里的 4 数的是盒子。

把<Beat n={4}>盘子和盒子放在一起</Beat>比一比。平均分成 3 份，每份 4 个；每 3 个一份，能分 4 份。两个问题都从同样的 12 个开始，都是分成一样多的几组，所以都写成 12 ÷ 3。算式一样，问的东西不一样：3 是份数还是每份几个，4 是每份几个还是份数，要看题目怎么说。

常见的错误有两个。一是以为除法只有"平均分给几份"这一种，其实"每几个一份，能分几份"也是除法。二是把算式写反，写成 3 ÷ 12。除法要先写原来有多少，这里是 12，所以写 12 ÷ 3。

准备好了，就<Task id="sharing-and-grouping">做一道小题</Task>：平均分一分，或者几个几个地装一装。
