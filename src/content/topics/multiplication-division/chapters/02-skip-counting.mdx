<Lang en>

In the last step you read 4 × 3 as 4 groups of 3. Now you will count equal groups a whole group at a time, with jumps along a number line.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>6 pairs of chopsticks</Beat>. A pair is a group of 2, so there are 6 groups of 2: 6 × 2. You could count every chopstick, but each pair is the same size, so you can count a pair at a time.

Follow the <Beat n={2}>jumps of 2</Beat> under the pairs. Start at 0, before anything is counted. The first jump lands on 2 and counts the first pair. Keep going: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. This is <Term id="skip-counting">skip counting</Term>: you add the same number each time. One jump counts one pair, so 6 jumps count all 6 pairs: 6 × 2 = 12.

Now look at the <Beat n={3}>boxes of 5 curry puffs</Beat>. Each box holds 5, so each jump is 5 long: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. There are 6 boxes, so 6 jumps, and the last one lands on 30. So 6 × 5 = 30.

In the last beat, <Beat n={4}>the jumps are numbered</Beat> 1, 2, 3, 4 under the arcs. Each jump of 10 lands on 10, 20, 30, 40, so 4 × 10 = 40. The numbers you land on when you count in 2s, 5s or 10s are the <Term id="times-table">multiplication tables</Term> of 2, 5 and 10.

The common slip is one jump too many or too few. That adds or misses a whole group: 7 × 5 is 35, and one jump too many gives 40. Counting the 0 at the start as a jump is how the extra jump often sneaks in. Count the jumps, not the marks you pass.

When you are ready, <Task id="skip-counting">try one question</Task> on counting equal groups with jumps of 2, 5 or 10.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

上一步说过，4 × 3 是 4 组、每组 3 个。这一步还是数相同的几组，不过一次数一整组，在数轴上一跳一跳地数。

先看<Beat n={1}>6 双筷子</Beat>。一双是 2 根，就是一组 2 个，6 双就是 6 组、每组 2 个：6 × 2。当然可以一根一根地数，可是每双都一样多，一次数一双更快。

看筷子下面<Beat n={2}>2 个 2 个的跳数</Beat>。从 0 起跳，0 表示还没开始数。第一跳落在 2，数的是第一双；接着跳：2、4、6、8、10、12。每次都加上同样多，这样数叫<Term id="skip-counting">几个几个地数</Term>。一跳数一双，跳 6 次就数完 6 双：6 × 2 = 12。

再看<Beat n={3}>每盒 5 个咖喱角</Beat>。一盒 5 个，所以每跳一次加 5：5、10、15、20、25、30。有 6 盒就跳 6 次，第几盒就是第几跳。最后一跳落在 30，所以 6 × 5 = 30。每跳多远，看的是每组有几个；跳几次，看的是有几组。

最后一拍，弧线下面<Beat n={4}>给每一跳编了号</Beat>：1、2、3、4。每跳 10，依次落在 10、20、30、40，所以 4 × 10 = 40。2 个 2 个、5 个 5 个、10 个 10 个地数，落到的那些数，就是 2、5、10 的<Term id="times-table">乘法表</Term>。

最常见的失误是多跳一次或少跳一次。多跳一次就多算了一整组，少跳一次就漏了一整组：7 × 5 是 35，多跳一次就成了 40。多出来的那一跳，常常是把起点 0 也算成了一跳。要数跳了几次，不是数经过几条刻度。

准备好了，就<Task id="skip-counting">做一道小题</Task>：2 个 2 个、5 个 5 个或 10 个 10 个地跳，数出相同的几组。

</Lang>
