<Lang en>

In the last step you multiplied tens and hundreds and traded 10 tens for a hundred. Now you multiply a number with several digits, and write each step in columns.

Look at <Beat n={1}>3 rows of 2 tens and 6 ones</Beat>. Each row is 26, so the blocks show 26 × 3. The <Term id="column-method">column method</Term> on the right keeps the ones under the ones and the tens under the tens, just like the columns of the mat.

Start with <Beat n={2}>the ones</Beat>. The 6 ones in each row make 18 ones: 6 × 3 = 18. That is 10 or more, so 10 of those ones will be traded for 1 ten. Watch the ones column on the mat and the ones place in the working light up together.

Now look at <Beat n={3}>the 8 ones and the traded ten</Beat>. After the trade, 8 ones stay in the ones column, and the new ten joins the tens. In the working, 8 is written in the ones place and a small 1 above the tens: that 1 is the traded ten. A place holds only one digit, so 18 cannot be written in the ones place.

Next, <Beat n={4}>the tens</Beat>. The 2 tens in each row make 6 tens, and the traded ten makes 7 tens. Write 7 in the tens place: 26 × 3 = 78. Forgetting the small 1 would give 68.

Three digits work the same way. In <Beat n={5}>213 × 4</Beat>: 3 ones × 4 = 12, so write 2 and carry 1; 1 ten × 4 = 4 tens, plus the carried ten makes 5 tens; 2 hundreds × 4 = 8 hundreds. So 213 × 4 = 852.

Three mistakes are common. Writing 618 for 26 × 3 puts 6 and 18 side by side instead of trading. Writing 68 forgets the traded ten. Leaving a place out, such as the hundreds of 213, misses part of the number. Multiply every place and add every carry.

<Lang zh>

上一步算了整十、整百数乘一位数，还把 10 个十换成了 1 个百。这一步算的是几位数乘一位数，每一步都写在竖式里。

先看<Beat n={1}>3 行、每行 2 个十和 6 个一</Beat>。每行是 26，这些块表示 26 × 3。右边的<Term id="column-method">竖式</Term>把个位对着个位、十位对着十位，和数位垫上的列一一对应。

先算<Beat n={2}>个位</Beat>。每行 6 个一，3 行就是 18 个一：6 × 3 = 18。满 10 了，要拿其中 10 个一换成 1 个十。注意看：数位垫上的个位那一列，和竖式里的个位，是一起亮起来的。

再看<Beat n={3}>剩下的 8 个一和换来的十</Beat>。换完以后，个位那一列留下 8 个一，新换来的十到了十位那一列。竖式里，个位写 8，十位上方写一个小小的 1，这个 1 就是换上来的十，也叫进位。一个数位只能写一个数字，所以个位上写 18 是放不下的。

接着算<Beat n={4}>十位</Beat>。每行 2 个十，3 行是 6 个十，加上换上来的 1 个十，一共 7 个十。十位写 7：26 × 3 = 78。要是忘了那个小 1，就会得 68。

三位数也是一样。看<Beat n={5}>213 × 4</Beat>：个位 3 × 4 = 12，写 2 进 1；十位 1 × 4 = 4，加上进上来的 1，是 5 个十；百位 2 × 4 = 8 个百。所以 213 × 4 = 852。

常见的错误有三种。把 26 × 3 写成 618，是把 6 和 18 并排写了，没有换；写成 68，是忘了加进位；漏乘了某一位，比如 213 的百位，就少算了一部分。每一位都要乘，每个进位都要加上。
