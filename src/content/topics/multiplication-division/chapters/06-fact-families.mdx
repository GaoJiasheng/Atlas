<Lang en>

In the last step you met two kinds of <Term id="division">division</Term>. This step shows that you can answer both with a <Term id="multiplication">multiplication</Term> you already know.

Look at the oranges in <Beat n={1}>3 rows of 5</Beat>. Each row holds 5, so there are 3 groups of 5: 3 × 5 = 15. The <Term id="array">array</Term> shows the rows, the number in each row and the total all at once.

Read the same oranges <Beat n={2}>by columns</Beat>. There are 5 columns with 3 oranges in each, so 5 × 3 = 15. Nothing moved. You counted the same 15 another way.

Now <Beat n={3}>cover the number of rows</Beat>. You still know there are 15 oranges and 5 in each row. How many rows? That is 15 ÷ 5 = ?. Think of a multiplication: what times 5 makes 15? You know 3 × 5 = 15, so there are 3 rows, and 15 ÷ 5 = 3.

Next, <Beat n={4}>cover the other side</Beat>. Now you know there are 15 oranges in 3 rows. How many are in each row? Think: 3 times what makes 15? It is 5, so 15 ÷ 3 = 5. This time the division finds the size of each group.

Uncover <Beat n={5}>the whole array</Beat>. The numbers 3, 5 and 15 make a family of four number sentences: 3 × 5 = 15, 5 × 3 = 15, 15 ÷ 5 = 3 and 15 ÷ 3 = 5. Multiplication finds the total. Division starts from the total and finds one of the other two numbers.

A common mistake is to multiply when the question divides: answering 15 ÷ 3 with 15 × 3 = 45. In a division the 15 is already the total, so the answer must be smaller. Ask which number times 3 makes 15.

When you are ready, <Task id="fact-families">try one question</Task> on finding a missing number with a multiplication you know.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

上一步讲了<Term id="division">除法</Term>的两种问法。这一步你会看到：两种问法，都可以用已经会的<Term id="multiplication">乘法</Term>来回答。

先看排成<Beat n={1}>3 行、每行 5 个</Beat>的橙子。每行 5 个，就是 3 组、每组 5 个：3 × 5 = 15。这个<Term id="array">阵列</Term>把行数、每行几个和总数一下子都摆在眼前。

同样这些橙子，<Beat n={2}>按列来看</Beat>：一共 5 列，每列 3 个，所以 5 × 3 = 15。一个都没有动，只是换了一种数法，数出来还是 15。所以 3 × 5 和 5 × 3，是同一个阵列的两种读法。

现在<Beat n={3}>把行数盖住</Beat>。你还是知道一共 15 个、每行 5 个。有几行呢？这就是 15 ÷ 5 = ?。想一想乘法：几乘 5 得 15？3 × 5 = 15，所以有 3 行，15 ÷ 5 = 3。

接着<Beat n={4}>换一边盖住</Beat>。这回知道一共 15 个、排成 3 行，每行几个？想一想：3 乘几得 15？是 5，所以 15 ÷ 3 = 5。这一次，除法求的是每组有几个。

最后<Beat n={5}>把阵列全部揭开</Beat>。3、5、15 这三个数是一家，能写出四个算式：3 × 5 = 15，5 × 3 = 15，15 ÷ 5 = 3，15 ÷ 3 = 5。乘法是求总数；除法是从总数出发，求另外两个数中的一个。所以做除法时，可以在心里问一句："几乘几得这个总数？"

常见的错误是该除的时候去乘：把 15 ÷ 3 算成 15 × 3 = 45。除法里的 15 已经是总数了，得数一定比它小。想一想：几乘 3 得 15？做完除法，还可以把商乘回去验一验：5 × 3 是不是等于 15？

准备好了，就<Task id="fact-families">做一道小题</Task>：用会的乘法，找出缺的那个数。

</Lang>
