<Lang en>

You multiplied in columns starting with the ones. Division in columns goes the other way: it starts with the biggest place. It uses the trading of step 10 and the <Term id="remainder">remainder</Term> of step 08.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the blocks for 72</Beat>: 7 tens and 2 ones, to be shared equally among 3 plates. The long-division sign on the right shows the same thing: 72 ÷ 3.

Share the tens first. <Beat n={2}>Each plate gets 2 tens</Beat>, which uses 6 tens, and 1 ten is left on the mat. In the working, 2 goes above the tens, 6 is written under the 7, and 1 ten is what remains.

That ten cannot go to just one plate, because the shares must stay equal. So <Beat n={3}>trade the ten for 10 ones</Beat>. To <Term id="regroup">regroup</Term> keeps the value: 1 ten and 2 ones make 12 ones. In the working, the 2 ones are brought down next to the 1, which reads 12.

Now <Beat n={4}>share the 12 ones</Beat>: 4 on each plate, with none left. Each plate holds 2 tens and 4 ones, so 72 ÷ 3 = 24. The <Term id="quotient">quotient</Term> 24 is what one plate gets. The mat and the working light up the same place at every step.

Last, <Beat n={5}>412 ÷ 4</Beat>. Each plate gets 1 hundred. Then there is 1 ten: it cannot be shared among 4 plates, so each plate gets 0 tens. Write 0 in the tens place, then trade the ten for 10 ones. With the 2 ones that makes 12 ones, and each plate gets 3. So 412 ÷ 4 = 103.

Two mistakes are common. Leaving a ten on the mat without trading it loses part of the number. Leaving out the 0 turns 103 into 13, which would mean 1 ten and 3 ones on each plate, not 1 hundred.

<Lang zh>

乘法竖式从个位乘起。除法竖式正好反过来，要从最高位分起。这一步要用到第 10 步的"换"，还有第 08 步的<Term id="remainder">余数</Term>。

先看<Beat n={1}>表示 72 的块</Beat>：7 个十和 2 个一，要平均分给 3 个盘子。右边的除法竖式写的是同一件事：72 ÷ 3。

先分十。<Beat n={2}>每盘分到 2 个十</Beat>，一共用了 6 个十，垫子上还剩 1 个十。竖式里，商的十位写 2，7 下面写 6，相减剩下 1，就是剩下的 1 个十。

这 1 个十不能只给一个盘子，否则就不平均了。所以<Beat n={3}>把这个十换成 10 个一</Beat>。这样<Term id="regroup">换</Term>一换，数的大小不变：1 个十和 2 个一合起来是 12 个一。竖式里，把个位的 2 移下来，写在 1 的旁边，就成了 12。

接着<Beat n={4}>把 12 个一分掉</Beat>：每盘 4 个，正好分完。每盘有 2 个十和 4 个一，所以 72 ÷ 3 = 24。<Term id="quotient">商</Term> 24 就是一个盘子分到的数。每一步，数位垫上亮起的那一列，和竖式里亮起的那一位，都是同一位。

最后看<Beat n={5}>412 ÷ 4</Beat>。先分百，每盘 1 个百。再分十：只有 1 个十，不够分给 4 个盘子，每盘分到 0 个十，所以商的十位写 0，再把这个十换成 10 个一。加上原来的 2 个一，一共 12 个一，每盘 3 个。所以 412 ÷ 4 = 103。

常见的错误有两种。一是剩下的十没有换开，就丢了一部分数。二是漏写商中间的 0：103 写成 13，就变成每盘 1 个十和 3 个一了，可每盘明明有 1 个百。
