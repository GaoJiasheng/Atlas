<Lang en>

Start with a far object and far focus. The light paths meet on the receiver, which stays fixed throughout the comparison. Move the object nearer without changing focus. The light now meets beyond the receiver. It lands on the receiver as a spread-out spot.

Keep the object near and the opening unchanged. Predict where the light will meet after you select near focus. Reveal the result: the paths meet on the receiver again. Record both states and compare the focus descriptions. The receiver stayed fixed; the focusing power changed.

In an eye, the lens changes shape to focus on different distances. This is called accommodation. For near objects, the lens becomes rounder. For distant objects, it is flatter. The ring of ciliary muscle changes the pull of the fibres holding the lens. Compare these shapes in the eye, then watch their focusing effect on the bench.

Focusing on a near object differs from <Term id="myopia">myopia</Term>, or short-sightedness. In myopia, light from distant objects focuses in front of the retina, so they can look blurred. An eye-care professional can prescribe lenses to help. 眼睛通过改变晶状体形状，看清不同距离的物体，这叫调节。看近时，晶状体较圆鼓；看远时，晶状体较扁。周围的睫状肌改变牵住晶状体的纤维张力。先在眼球中比较形状，再到实验台看聚焦效果。
