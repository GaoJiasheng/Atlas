<Lang en>

Look inside the opened cochlea. Movement at the oval window disturbs the fluid. A wave travels along the basilar membrane, a flexible layer supporting the sensory structures. Fluid movement carries the disturbance inside the cochlea; outside air does not flow through to the nerve.

The nearby strip shows part of the basilar membrane enlarged and unrolled. It is drawn beside the cochlea so you can see local movement. Follow it towards the enlarged cells. The strip belongs inside the cochlea; its separate position is just a way to show it clearly.

A <Term id="hair-cell">hair cell</Term> is a sensory cell with a bundle of microscopic hair-like projections on top. These are different from ordinary ear hairs. Movement bends the bundles and helps start electrical signals. The enlarged patch shows a few examples, rather than the total cell count. It has its own scale, without a specified magnification.

In the final beats, follow the signal marker from the sensory cells towards the auditory nerve. Mechanical movement has led to electrical signalling. Signals travel along the nerve to the brain, which processes them to recognise and understand sounds. The model follows this route without showing all the connections inside the brain. <FlyTo preset="cochlea">Examine the sensory region</FlyTo>

Higher pitches cause the strongest response near the base of the cochlea. Lower pitches cause it nearer the tip. These are broad locations; the model does not calculate an exact distance for each frequency. [S7, S8]

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Balance beside hearing", "zh": "听觉旁边的平衡结构"}}>

The vestibular region and semicircular canals beside the cochlea help with balance. They have a different role from the hearing pathway. [S8]

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Marks show signals", "zh": "用标记表示信号"}}>

Moving markers represent nerve signals. The enlarged strip rises and falls locally; it does not show the full wave travelling along a cochlea. Cell bodies stay on the strip while their enlarged bundles tilt together. Individual projections are not simulated separately. [S31]

</More>

旁边的长条把部分基底膜放大、展开，方便看清局部运动。沿条带找到放大的细胞。条带本来位于耳蜗内部，分开摆放只是为了展示清楚。

<Term id="hair-cell">毛细胞</Term>是一种感受细胞，顶部有成束的微小毛发状突起，和普通耳毛不同。运动使小束弯曲，帮助启动电信号传递。放大区只显示少量例子，不表示细胞总数。它另用自己的比例，没有指定放大倍数。
