<Lang en>

Return to the whole view with motion switched off. Daily habits can help protect eyes and ears. The model shows their structures; ask an adult for help if you have concerns about seeing or hearing.

Spend time outdoors. Take frequent breaks from reading, screens and other near work, and look into the distance. Singapore health services encourage both outdoor activity and good near-work habits. Outdoor time does not cure myopia. Use suitable sun protection, and never look directly at the Sun or use it to test your eyes.

Keep listening levels down. Spend less time in noisy places and move farther from loud sources when possible. Ask an adult to help you use suitable hearing protection where needed. Loud or prolonged noise can damage the inner ear. Explore the silent model on screen without testing these ideas with loud sounds.

Keep cotton buds and other objects out of the ear canal. They can push wax deeper or injure delicate structures. If you have trouble seeing the board, following conversation or hearing everyday sounds, tell an adult and seek professional advice. Different causes need different checks. Compare the routes once more: light reaches retinal cells, while vibration reaches sensory cells in the cochlea. Both send signals towards the brain.

NUHS suggests <Num s="S14">20–30 minutes</Num>. These services give different intervals, but all encourage regular breaks. Remember sun protection outdoors.

The WHO–ITU personal-audio standard gives a child-mode reference of <Num s="S17">75 decibels for 40 hours per week</Num>. This helps guide device design. It is not a listening target or a guarantee of personal safety. The model cannot measure your sound exposure or count down a safe time in noise. WHO advises reducing volume, time and closeness to loud sources. [S16]

<Lang zh>

关掉运动，回到总览。日常习惯能帮助保护眼睛和耳朵。模型展示它们的结构；看或听有困难时，要请大人帮助。

安排户外活动。阅读、看屏幕等近距离用眼时，经常休息，看看远处。新加坡医疗机构鼓励把户外活动与良好用眼习惯结合。户外活动不能治愈近视。记得防晒遮阳，不要直视太阳，也不用太阳测试眼睛。

降低聆听音量，减少在嘈杂环境里的时间，并尽可能远离很响的声源。需要时请大人帮助使用合适的护耳用品。很响或持续很久的噪声可能损伤内耳。用静音模型观察，不用强声来试验。

不要把棉花棒或其他物品伸进耳道。它们可能把耳垢推得更深，也可能造成损伤。如果看白板、跟上交谈或听日常声音有困难，要告诉大人并寻求专业意见。不同原因需要不同检查。再比较两条路线：光到达视网膜细胞，振动到达耳蜗感受细胞，之后都把信号传向大脑。
