Return to the whole view with the studies and motion switched off. The eye and ear receive different inputs, but daily habits can help protect both. A model can make structures visible; it cannot assess how well a particular person sees or hears. Use the following ideas as everyday care, and ask an adult for help with concerns.

Spend time outdoors and take frequent breaks from near work such as reading or using a screen. During a break, look into the distance. Singapore health services encourage outdoor activity for children alongside good near-work habits. Outdoor time does not cure myopia, and it should go together with suitable sun protection. Never look directly at the Sun or use it to test your eyes.

For listening, keep levels down, limit time in loud surroundings and move farther from loud sources when possible. Use suitable hearing protection in noisy places with an adult's help. Loud or prolonged noise can damage structures in the inner ear. The silent frequency and displacement controls in this topic are teaching examples, not instructions for an experiment with loud sound.

Leave the ear canal free of cotton buds and other inserted objects. They can push wax deeper and injure delicate structures. If seeing the board, following conversation or hearing everyday sounds becomes difficult, tell an adult and seek professional advice. Different causes need different assessment; this topic offers no diagnosis. Its final comparison is about the routes: light reaches retinal cells, while vibration reaches sensory cells in the cochlea, and both routes send signals towards the brain.

回到总览，实验示意和运动都关掉。眼与耳接收不同输入，日常习惯却都能帮助保护它们。模型能够把结构显示出来，但不能评估某个人看得或听得怎样。把下面的做法作为日常照护；有疑问时请大人帮助，不用屏幕活动自行作出诊断。

安排户外活动，阅读或使用屏幕等近距离用眼过程中也要经常休息，看看远处。新加坡医疗机构鼓励儿童把户外活动与良好用眼习惯结合。户外时间不能治愈近视，也要配合合适的防晒与遮阳。不要直视太阳，更不用太阳来测试自己的眼睛。

聆听时降低音量，减少停留在嘈杂环境里的时间，并在可能时远离很响的声源。需要时请大人帮助使用合适的护耳用品。很响或持续很久的噪声能够损伤内耳结构。本主题静音的频率和位移控件只是教学例子，不是进行强声实验的操作说明。

不要把棉花棒或其他物品伸入耳道，它们可能把耳垢推得更深，也可能伤到脆弱结构。如果看白板、跟上交谈或听日常声音变得困难，要告诉大人并寻求专业意见。不同原因需要不同评估。最后再比较路线：光到达视网膜细胞，振动到达耳蜗里的感受细胞，之后都通过神经向大脑传递信号。
