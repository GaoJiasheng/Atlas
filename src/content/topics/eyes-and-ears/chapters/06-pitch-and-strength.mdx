<Lang en>

Watch the highlighted particle beside its rest mark. It moves back and forth while neighbouring particles do the same around their own positions. The pattern travels along the row, but the particles do not travel with it from source to ear. This is a longitudinal-wave study: local movement is along the direction of the travelling disturbance.

<Term id="frequency">Frequency</Term> describes how many cycles occur in a given time. The input examples in this simulation are <Num s="S31">250 hertz</Num> and <Num s="S31">500 hertz</Num>, displayed <Num s="S31">250 times</Num> slower so that movement can be followed. Doubling the input frequency doubles the displayed pace. <Term id="pitch">Pitch</Term> is the highness or lowness of a sound, related to frequency. The study emits no sound.

On the card, keep the relative displacement setting unchanged and predict what happens to its reading when frequency increases. Reveal the change and record both settings. A faster oscillation need not have a larger <Term id="amplitude">amplitude</Term>, the greatest displacement from rest. Compare how often the reference particle repeats its motion with how far it moves from its mark.

Now return to the lower frequency and increase relative displacement. The movement becomes wider while its repetition rate stays the same. Amplitude and pitch describe different features. A screen displacement setting is not a calibrated measure of sound intensity or perceived loudness, and it cannot establish a safe listening level. <More title={{"en": "Amplitude is not loudness calibration", "zh": "振幅不是响度校准"}}>

Amplitude relates to the strength of a sound, but perceived loudness also depends on frequency and listening conditions. The relative slider describes chosen particle displacement. It does not provide a decibel reading or justify a claim of equal sound intensity at different frequencies. [S10, S25, S31]

</More>

Air particles oscillate locally as a sound disturbance travels. The rest ticks make that distinction visible. The diagram’s particle spacing and wave speed are design choices, not measurements of air or an ear. Sound propagation can be explored as a separate topic. [S9, S31]

看看标出粒子旁边的静止位置。它来回运动，相邻粒子也在各自位置附近往复。波的形态沿整排传播，粒子却没有跟着波从声源一路走到耳朵。这是纵波示意：局部运动方向与扰动传播方向相同，不能把一排移动点误读成单向气流。

<Term id="frequency">频率</Term>描述一定时间内重复振动的次数。这个模拟的输入例子为 <Num s="S31">250 赫兹</Num> 与 <Num s="S31">500 赫兹</Num>，都以 <Num s="S31">250 倍</Num> 慢放，让运动容易跟随。输入频率加倍，显示节律也加倍。<Term id="pitch">音高</Term>是声音听起来高或低的感觉，与频率有关；本示意不发出声音。

在卡片上保持相对位移设置不变，先猜提高频率后，这个读数会怎样，再揭示变化并记录前后设置。振动更快，不一定有更大的<Term id="amplitude">振幅</Term>；振幅是离开静止位置的最大偏移。分别比较重复得多快，以及离开标记有多远，便能看清它们不是同一件事。

现在回到较低频率，再增大相对位移。画面中往复范围变宽，重复节律却不变。振幅与音高描述不同特征，屏幕位移设置不是校准过的声强或主观响度测量，也不能判断聆听是否安全。只用画面继续比较，不调高设备音量。<FlyTo preset="wave">跟随参照粒子</FlyTo>
