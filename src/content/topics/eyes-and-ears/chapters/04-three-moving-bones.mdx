<Lang en>

At the ear station, follow the outer folds towards the canal. The canal ends at the eardrum. The membrane lies at an angle in this cutaway view, making its connection to the middle-ear bones easier to follow. Incoming sound sets the membrane vibrating; it does not need a hole through the eardrum to continue the process.

Beyond the membrane is a connected chain of <Num s="S7">three</Num> small bones, together called the <Term id="ossicles">ossicles</Term>. The <Term id="malleus">malleus</Term> has a handle connected to the drum. Its head meets the <Term id="incus">incus</Term>, which links it to the <Term id="stapes">stapes</Term>. Select the names in order and follow their contacts, rather than treating the bones as separate objects floating in an empty space.

The stapes resembles a stirrup. Its footplate moves at the oval window, the interface towards the fluid-filled inner ear. This transfer couples vibration from the air-driven membrane and bone chain into movement of cochlear fluid. The structures transfer mechanical energy; they do not create energy or send a stream of air into the nerve.

Watch the stage's slow rocking and back-and-forth motions. Their size and timing are chosen to make the contacts visible, not to show a measured displacement or delay in an ear. At the final beat, follow the movement towards the cochlea. <More title={{"en": "The pressure connection", "zh": "与调压有关的通道"}}>

The descending Eustachian tube connects the middle ear with the pharynx and helps equalise pressure across the eardrum. It is not the route from the outer ear to the auditory nerve. The eardrum can be described as the canal–middle-ear boundary without making its textbook classification a quiz. [S8]

</More>

在耳朵工位，沿外部的褶皱走向外耳道，尽头便是鼓膜。剖面里的鼓膜斜着放，方便跟随它与中耳听骨的联系。传来的声音让这片薄膜振动，并不需要先在鼓膜上开洞，后面的过程才能继续。

膜后有相连的 <Num s="S7">三块</Num> 小骨，合称<Term id="ossicles">听小骨</Term>。<Term id="malleus">锤骨</Term>的柄连着鼓膜，头部与<Term id="incus">砧骨</Term>相接；砧骨再接到<Term id="stapes">镫骨</Term>。依次点选它们，跟随接触位置，比把它们当作悬浮在空处的装饰更容易理解传振过程。

镫骨的外形像马镫，底板在卵圆窗处活动，把鼓膜和骨链的运动传向充满液体的内耳。比较时还可以留意每个接触点前后的结构，按顺序跟随，而不只盯着摆动幅度。这些结构把机械能传过去，不会凭空创造能量，也不会把一股空气送进神经。读图时要把骨链运动与下一阶段的神经信号分开。
