<Lang en>

The optical bench shows light from a lit object. Its upper and lower markers stand for two places on that object. Predict where light from the upper marker will land. The lines show a few paths. Real light spreads from each visible point along many paths, and the eye receives only some of it.

In the eye, light passes through the curved cornea and the lens behind the iris. Both help focus it. On the bench, one lens represents their combined action. This lets you compare the object, the bending of light and the receiving surface.

Follow light from the upper marker. It lands on the lower part of the receiver. Light from the lower marker lands higher. The image is inverted: top and bottom have swapped places. The receiver represents where light lands on the retina. Select the thin retinal lining inside the eye to see where light-sensitive cells receive it.

In the final beats, follow the change from light to electrical signals. Select the optic nerve and watch the signal markers move towards the brain. Light stops at the retina; signals continue along the nerve. Seeing depends on both the eye receiving light and the brain processing signals. <FlyTo preset="focus-bench">Follow the light paths</FlyTo>

<More title={{"en": "An inverted image", "zh": "倒立的像"}}>

The image on the retina is inverted. The brain processes nerve signals to give us our view of the surroundings. It does not need a person inside it watching a picture or a machine turning a picture over. The bench shows only the optical step. [S4, S31]

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Light-sensitive cells", "zh": "感光细胞"}}>

Rods are light-sensitive cells that help us see in dim light and towards the edges of our view. Cones help us see colour and fine detail near the centre. Their microscopic arrangement is left out of the model. The markers do not count cells. [S24]

</More>

<Lang zh>

光学实验台显示被照亮物体反射的光。上、下标记代表物体上的两个位置。先猜上方标记来的光会落在哪里。图线只选画了部分路径，真实光会从每个可见位置向许多方向传播，眼睛只接收其中一部分。

在眼球中，光先穿过弯曲的角膜，再经过虹膜后方的晶状体，两者共同聚焦光线。实验台用一个透镜代表它们的共同作用，方便比较物体、折光位置和接收面。

跟随上方标记来的光，它落到接收面的下部。下方标记来的光则落到较高处。这样就形成倒立的像：上下位置交换了。接收面代表光落到视网膜的位置。点选眼球内壁的薄薄视网膜，就能找到感光细胞接收光的地方。

最后几拍，跟随光转成电信号的过程。点选视神经，看信号标记朝大脑移动。光到视网膜为止，信号继续沿神经传递。看见东西既需要眼睛接收光，也需要大脑处理信号。<FlyTo preset="focus-bench">跟随光的路径</FlyTo>

<More title={{"en": "An inverted image", "zh": "倒立的像"}}>

视网膜上的像是倒立的。大脑处理神经信号，让我们看见周围环境，不需要脑内有小人看图，也不需要机器把图翻面。实验台只显示其中的光学步骤。[S4, S31]

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Light-sensitive cells", "zh": "感光细胞"}}>

视杆细胞帮助我们在暗处看东西，也支持许多周边视觉。视锥细胞帮助分辨颜色和视野中心的精细部分。模型省略了它们的显微排列，标记数量也不表示细胞总数。[S24]
