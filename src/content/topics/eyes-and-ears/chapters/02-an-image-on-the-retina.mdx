<Lang en>

The separate optical study shows light from an illuminated object. Its upper and lower markers stand for different places on that object. Before following a ray, predict where light from the upper marker will reach the receiver. The lines show selected paths; real light from each visible point spreads along many paths, and the eye admits only some of it.

In the organ, light passes through the curved cornea and then through the lens behind the iris. Cornea and lens work together to focus it. In the study beside the organ, their combined action is represented by a single effective lens. This simplification keeps the object, bending point and receiving plane easy to compare. It is not the outline of a second eye.

Follow the paths from the upper marker. They reach the lower part of the receiver, while paths from the lower marker reach higher. The resulting image is inverted. The model receiver represents where light lands; it is separate from the thin retinal lining shown inside the anatomical eye. The retina contains cells that respond to incoming light.

At the final beats, follow the change from light to electrical signalling and then select the optic nerve. Its moving marks represent signals travelling towards the brain. They do not represent light continuing through a hollow tube. Seeing depends on both the eye's receiving structures and the brain's processing of their signals. <FlyTo preset="focus-bench">Follow the light paths</FlyTo>

<More title={{"en": "An inverted image", "zh": "倒立的像"}}>

The optical image on the retina is inverted. This does not require a small person or a picture-turning machine inside the brain: neural processing supports our perception of the surroundings. The study only calculates the optical part. [S4, S31]

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Light-sensitive cells", "zh": "感光细胞"}}>

Rods support seeing in dim light and much peripheral vision. Cones support colour and fine central detail. Their microscopic arrangement is not modelled here, and the displayed marks do not represent a cell count. [S24]

</More>

<Lang zh>

旁边的光学示意从一个被照亮的物体开始。上、下标记代表物体上的不同位置。沿光路看以前，先猜上方标记来的光会到接收面的哪里。图中只选画了部分路径；真实物体上可见位置的光会朝许多方向传播，眼睛只接收其中一部分。

在眼球中，光先穿过弯曲的角膜，再经过虹膜后面的晶状体。这些结构共同把光聚焦。旁边的实验台用一个有效透镜合并表示角膜与晶状体的作用，让物体、折光位置和接收面容易比较。它是简化的光学研究，不是又一只眼睛的外形。

跟着上方标记发出的光路，看它们到达接收面的下部；下方标记来的光则到达较高处，因此形成倒立的像。模型接收面表示光落到的位置，与眼球内壁上薄薄的视网膜分开展示。真实视网膜中含有响应入眼光线的细胞，并非一块普通投影幕。

最后几拍把光变为电信号的阶段分开，再选中视神经。沿神经移动的标记表示传向大脑的信号，不是光继续穿过空心管。看见东西既依赖眼内的接收结构，也依赖大脑处理传来的信号；不能只用几条光路解释整个视觉过程。<FlyTo preset="focus-bench">跟随光的路径</FlyTo>
