<Lang en>

The eye and ear sit at separate study stations. Both are enlarged and turned towards you so you can see inside. Their sizes and positions differ from those in a head. Cut surfaces and transparent layers reveal the inner parts. Start with the whole view, then select a part to learn what it does.

Imagine watching someone clap. Light reflects from the hands into your eyes. The <Term id="retina">retina</Term> is a thin layer inside each eye. Its light-sensitive cells begin turning light into electrical signals. The <Term id="optic-nerve">optic nerve</Term> carries these signals towards the brain. Seeing starts with light entering your eyes.

The clap also disturbs the surrounding air. Follow the ear canal to the <Term id="eardrum">eardrum</Term>, a thin membrane that vibrates. Movement passes through the middle ear to the <Term id="cochlea">cochlea</Term>, a curled structure filled with fluid. Sensory cells there help turn movement into electrical signals. The <Term id="auditory-nerve">auditory nerve</Term> carries them towards the brain.

The eye receives light. The ear responds to vibration. Both send nerve signals towards the brain, but they use different receiving structures. Follow each route before comparing them. The model makes no sound and does not test your sight or hearing. <FlyTo preset="overview">Compare the study stations</FlyTo>

眼睛和耳朵分放在两个工位，都放大并转向你，方便看清内部。它们在头部里的大小和位置并不是这样。剖面和透明层让内层显露出来。先看全貌，再点选各部分，了解它们的作用。

想象你正在看别人拍手。手反射的光进入眼睛。眼内薄薄的<Term id="retina">视网膜</Term>含有感光细胞，开始把光转成电信号。<Term id="optic-nerve">视神经</Term>把这些信号传向大脑。看见东西从光进入眼睛开始。

拍手也扰动周围的空气。沿外耳道找到薄薄的<Term id="eardrum">鼓膜</Term>，它会随声音振动。运动经过中耳，传到内耳中卷曲、充满液体的<Term id="cochlea">耳蜗</Term>。其中的感受细胞帮助把运动转成电信号，再由<Term id="auditory-nerve">听神经</Term>传向大脑。

眼接收光，耳响应振动。两者都把神经信号传向大脑，但接收结构不同。先分别跟随两条路线，再作比较。模型不发声，也不测试你的视力或听力。<FlyTo preset="overview">比较两个工位</FlyTo>
