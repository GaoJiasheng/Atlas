<More title={{"en": "A straight line and a curve", "zh": "一条直线和一条弯线"}}>

With the torch fixed, equal steps of the screen give equal steps of the shadow: the graph is a straight line. With the screen fixed, equal steps of the torch do not give equal steps of the shadow: the graph is a curve, steepest where the torch is nearest the card. When you compare steps on a graph, check how far apart the distances are; on the torch graph they are not evenly spaced.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "An unfair test", "zh": "一个不公平的测试"}}>

Suppose the torch and the screen stay where they are and you slide the card towards the torch. The shadow grows, but two distances changed at once: the card is nearer the torch and farther from the screen. This test cannot tell you which change made the shadow bigger. It is a fair test of something else, the card's place between a fixed torch and screen, but not of either distance on its own.

</More>
