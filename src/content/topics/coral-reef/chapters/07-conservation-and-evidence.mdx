<Lang en>

The nursery and observation frame represent different parts of conservation work. A nursery supports cultivation; an observation frame marks a place to examine. The displayed tools are general teaching examples. Their size and the number of coral fragments do not describe a particular Singapore project, its planting total or the proportion of corals that survived.

Sisters’ Islands Marine Park protects local coral reefs and supports research. Its <Num s="S12">40 ha</Num> includes Sisters’ Islands and the western reefs of St John’s Island and Pulau Tekukor. Pulau Hantu is a separate reef research location, outside this park. A nursery off Small Sister’s Island supports local corals. These places give context; the scene is not their map.

<Term id="restoration">Restoration</Term> assists damaged habitat to recover. On 10 December 2024, NParks announced the 100k Corals Initiative, aiming to plant <Num s="S14">100,000</Num> corals over the next <Num s="S14">10 years</Num> and beyond. That is a goal. The 2025/2026 annual report describes the cultivation facility opening on 7 April 2025 and the first reared corals being transplanted. Completed work and a planting goal are different evidence.

To judge outcomes, researchers need observations after planting, not just a count placed in the sea. Museum records also help identify corals and support inventories and conservation assessment. At the marine park, observe without collecting animals, plants or shells. Protection, careful restoration and research can help reefs while ocean warming remains a wider challenge that local planting alone cannot remove.

The Lee Kong Chian Natural History Museum describes its coral collection as a foundation for Singapore’s stony-coral inventory and conservation assessment. A stored specimen records identity and collection history; it is not a count of animals now living in the sea. [S16]

<Lang zh>

苗圃与观察框表示保护工作的不同环节：苗圃供培育，观察框标出检查范围。这里的工具都是一般教学示例，支架尺寸、珊瑚碎枝数量都不代表某个新加坡工程，更不表示种植总量或成活比例。

姐妹岛海洋生态园保护本地珊瑚礁并支持研究，面积为 <Num s="S12">40 ha</Num>，包括姐妹岛及圣约翰岛与 Pulau Tekukor 的西侧礁。韩都岛是另一个礁区研究地点，不在园内；小姐妹岛外也有支持本地珊瑚的苗圃。地点用于理解背景，模型不是它们的地图。

<Term id="restoration">生态修复</Term>协助受损生境恢复。国家公园局于 2024 年 12 月 10 日宣布 100k 计划，目标是在之后 <Num s="S14">10 年</Num>及更长时期种植 <Num s="S14">100,000</Num> 株珊瑚。2025/2026 年报另记培育设施于 2025 年 4 月 7 日开放，并已移植首批培育珊瑚；工作进展不等于目标完成。

判断结果还需种植后的观察，不能只数放进海里的数量。博物馆记录也帮助鉴定、编名录与评估保护状况。在生态园观察时，不采集动物、植物或贝壳。保护、修复与研究有帮助，海洋升温却仍是更大范围的挑战，不能只靠本地种植消除。
