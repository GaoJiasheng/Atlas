<Lang en>

Follow the model from the shoreward side towards the sea. The <Term id="reef-flat">reef flat</Term> is a shallow, relatively level area. Some parts can be exposed during low tide. Farther out, the <Term id="reef-crest">reef crest</Term> faces incoming waves. Its structure can reduce wave energy. Beyond it, the <Term id="reef-slope">reef slope</Term> descends into deeper water. These zones join together rather than forming separate islands.

Depth affects the light reaching photosynthetic organisms. Communities can differ between zones as conditions change. Here a <Term id="community">community</Term> means populations of different kinds of organisms living together. A connected coral colony is not automatically the whole population of its species, and one coral species is not the whole reef community.

Try the light comparison. Choose a water case, keep it fixed, then increase the illustrative depth. The marker moves as the relative light index falls. Return to the same depth before comparing the other water case. That way you can describe which input changed. The readout is labelled SIM because it comes from a designed calculation, not an underwater light measurement.

Singapore supports living reefs in turbid water. Suspended particles reduce light, while particles that settle can cover surfaces. These effects interact with local conditions; clearer water alone is not a complete measure of reef health. The scene shows the order of the zones and a comparison rule. It does not set a depth beyond which every coral dies.

<Lang zh>

沿模型由岸边向海看，<Term id="reef-flat">礁坪</Term>较浅而平，部分地方在低潮时会露出水面。再向外是迎向海浪的<Term id="reef-crest">礁脊</Term>，结构可削弱波浪能量；接着，<Term id="reef-slope">礁坡</Term>向深水下降。三个区相连，并不是三座分开的岛。

深度会影响光合生物能得到的光，各区的生物组合也可不同。<Term id="community">群落</Term>指在同一地方共同生活的多个种群；一个相连的珊瑚群体，不自动等于该物种的整个种群，一个珊瑚物种也不是整片礁的全部群落。

试光比较时，先选定水体并保持不变，再增加示意深度，观察相对光指数怎样下降。比较另一种水体前，先回到相同深度。这样才说得清改变了哪项输入。读数标有 SIM，表示来自设计计算，不是海中实测照度。

新加坡浑浊海水中也有活珊瑚礁。悬浮颗粒减少透光，沉下来的颗粒则可覆盖表面，影响还与当地条件相互作用。因此不能只凭水清不清判断整片礁是否健康。模型显示的是分区顺序与比较规则，没有规定所有珊瑚必死的深度界线。
