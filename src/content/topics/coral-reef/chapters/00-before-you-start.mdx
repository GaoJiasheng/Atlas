<Lang en>

Look at the rounded coral colony on the reef. Its surface looks hard, and the whole colony stays attached. Before opening the close-up, decide which visible features might help you identify it. Appearance is a starting observation; it is not enough to decide whether something is an animal, a plant or a rock.

Singapore has coral reefs, mainly around the Southern Islands. NParks reports more than <Num s="S2">250</Num> species of hard corals in Singapore’s waters. That figure describes many local reefs together, not the species on this little model. A reef provides a <Term id="habitat">habitat</Term>, the environment where organisms live. Corals share it with other living things.

Select the colony, then open the enlarged polyp. The close-up separates living tissue from its hard support. These displays use different scales: a tiny structure is enlarged to make its position clear. Their arrangement is a teaching choice. The scene does not reproduce a measured island profile, and its drawn organisms are not a population count.

Use the beat marks to compare views, then return to objects through the chapter index. Later chapters trace selected food pathways and compare tissue evidence. Keep an observation separate from an explanation: a pale surface is visible, but the presence of living tissue needs closer evidence. When a model leaves out a relationship, that does not show that the relationship is absent from nature.

<Lang zh>

先看礁上圆丘状的珊瑚群体：表面像坚硬的石头，整体也附着不动。打开近景之前，可以先判断哪些外形特征值得观察。外形只是线索，还不足以确定眼前是动物、植物还是岩石；后面要检查组织和摄食证据。

新加坡的珊瑚礁主要分布在南部岛屿周围。国家公园局介绍，本地海域有超过 <Num s="S2">250</Num> 种硬珊瑚；这不是模型这一小块地方的种数。<Term id="habitat">生境</Term>是生物生活的环境，同一片礁中，珊瑚还与其他生物共同生活。

点选群体，再看放大的珊瑚虫，把活组织与坚硬支撑分开。两个展示台没有共同的实物比例，细小部分特意放大，位置也是教学安排。这不是某座岛的测绘剖面，画出的生物数量也不代表种群调查结果。

依次点节拍刻度比较视角，再从本章索引选回对象。后面会追踪部分食物路径，并比较组织证据。解释时，要把看见什么和据此推论什么分开：颜色浅可以看见，组织是否还活着却需要进一步证据。模型省略某条关系，也不表示自然界没有那条关系。

<More title={{"en": "What the model leaves out", "zh": "模型省略的内容"}}>

生境剖面、放大标本和选入的食物路径都是原创教学安排。食物网卡采用功能组和一般关系，不代表本地摄食观察，也不给存活率。[S50]
