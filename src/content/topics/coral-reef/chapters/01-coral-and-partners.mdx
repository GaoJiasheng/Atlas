<Lang en>

The enlarged <Term id="polyp">coral polyp</Term> is an animal with a soft body. Its central mouth is surrounded by tentacles. Food enters through this opening; undigested material also leaves through it. The model separates these structures so that their positions can be examined. It represents a general hard coral rather than an identified species or collected specimen.

Many connected polyps form a <Term id="colony">colony</Term>. A thin layer of living tissue links neighbouring polyps over a shared <Term id="skeleton">skeleton</Term>. Hard corals build this support from calcium carbonate. The support is around and beneath the tissue: the hard structure alone is not the complete living animal. Not all corals live in colonies.

Inside the tissue are microscopic <Term id="algal-partner">algal partners</Term>. Most shallow reef-building corals have these photosynthetic partners. They use light to make food and supply products of that process to the animal. The coral provides a protected place and materials used by the algae. This close relationship is a <Term id="symbiosis">symbiosis</Term>; in this example both partners benefit.

The coral remains an animal even though its partners photosynthesise. Its tentacles can also catch small prey. Receiving food products from algae and capturing prey are different food pathways, and neither turns the algae into an organ of the animal. Select tissue, partners and skeleton in turn. Their different positions help explain how separate living organisms can cooperate within one small space.

<Lang zh>

放大的<Term id="polyp">珊瑚虫</Term>是柔软的动物。一圈触手围着中央的口，食物由这里进入，未消化的物质也由同一个开口排出。模型把这些结构分开显示，便于检查相互位置；它代表一般硬珊瑚，没有冒称某个物种或采集标本。

许多相连的珊瑚虫组成一个<Term id="colony">珊瑚群体</Term>。薄薄的活组织连接邻近个体，铺在共有的<Term id="skeleton">骨骼</Term>上。硬珊瑚制造碳酸钙支撑，位于组织周围及下方；坚硬结构本身并不等于完整的活珊瑚。也有独居的珊瑚，并非全部都成群生活。

组织内还有微小的<Term id="algal-partner">共生藻</Term>，多数浅水造礁珊瑚都有这样的光合伙伴。藻类利用光制造食物，向动物提供产物；珊瑚提供受保护的住处和所需物质。这是<Term id="symbiosis">共生</Term>，本例中双方都获益。

伙伴能光合作用，不会改变珊瑚的动物身份。珊瑚虫也能用触手捕捉微小猎物。接收藻类食物产物与捕食是不同路径，共生藻也不是动物的器官。依次点选组织、伙伴和骨骼，再用它们的位置解释这段关系。
