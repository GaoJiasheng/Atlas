<Lang en>

The specimen display separates organisms that are difficult to compare at their real sizes. <Term id="phytoplankton">Phytoplankton</Term> drift in water and make food using light. Algal partners inside coral tissue also photosynthesise. The reef-surface algae shown separately have another location. These are <Term id="producer">producers</Term>: they make organic food, using light in these examples.

Follow the plankton pathway. The selected <Term id="zooplankton">zooplankton</Term> group obtains food from phytoplankton, and coral animals can capture zooplankton. Such animals are <Term id="consumer">consumers</Term>, obtaining food from other organisms. This is a selected general pathway. It does not state that every zooplankton species eats the same food or that these meals were observed at one island.

The arrow from algal partners to the coral has a special meaning. It shows transfer of food products made by the partners, not the animal swallowing them. On another part of the card, parrotfishes graze reef-surface food with strong, beak-like jaws. Those algae are separate from the partners living inside coral tissue. The model keeps them as different nodes.

Read the <Term id="food-web">food web</Term> from food towards consumer. Its lines trace selected pathways by which food and energy reach organisms. Diagram positions and specimen sizes do not give body length or population size. Some pathways are unconnected in this excerpt because many real relationships are left out. No extra feeding link is needed just to make the drawing look connected.

<Lang zh>

标本台把实际大小难以直接比较的生物分开。<Term id="phytoplankton">浮游植物</Term>漂在水中，利用光制造食物；珊瑚组织内的共生藻也能光合作用。另一处礁面藻则长在礁的表面。这些都是<Term id="producer">生产者</Term>，本例利用光制造有机食物，但所在位置与关系不同。

沿浮游生物路径看，这里选取的<Term id="zooplankton">浮游动物</Term>从浮游植物取得食物，珊瑚动物又可捕捉浮游动物。它们是从其他生物取得食物的<Term id="consumer">消费者</Term>。这是一条一般路径，不表示每种浮游动物吃的相同，也不是某座岛上实际观察到的一组餐食。

共生藻到珊瑚的箭头含义特别：转移的是伙伴制造的食物产物，并非珊瑚吞食共生藻。卡片另一处，鹦嘴鱼用强壮的喙状颌在礁面摄食。礁面藻与组织内的伙伴不是同一个节点，不能因为都叫藻类就合在一起。

读<Term id="food-web">食物网</Term>，从食物沿箭头走向消费者。线条表示选入的食物与能量路径，节点位置和标本大小不表示体长或种群数量。本节选中有些路径没有相连，是因为省略了许多真实关系，不能为了画面连通而添一条没有依据的摄食线。
