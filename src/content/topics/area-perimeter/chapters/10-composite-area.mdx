<Lang en>

So far each figure has been one rectangle or square. Many floors are not. A <Term id="composite-figure">composite figure</Term> is made of rectangles and squares joined together, and its area comes from rules you already know.

Look at <Beat n={1}>the floor plan of an L-shaped room</Beat>. It is marked 8 m along the bottom, 6 m up the left side, 5 m across the top and 4 m up the right side. It is not a <Term id="rectangle">rectangle</Term>, so you cannot multiply one length by one breadth straight away: 8 × 6 would also cover the empty corner.

One way is to <Beat n={2}>cut it into two rectangles</Beat> with a cut straight up from the inside corner. The left piece is 5 m by 6 m, so it covers 5 × 6 = 30 <Term id="square-metre">square metres</Term>. The right piece is 3 m by 4 m; its 3 m comes from 8 − 5. It covers 3 × 4 = 12 m². Together: 30 + 12 = 42 m².

You can also <Beat n={3}>cut it the other way</Beat>, straight across from the inside corner. Now the pieces are 8 m by 4 m, or 32 m², and 5 m by 2 m, or 10 m²; the 2 m comes from 6 − 4. The pieces are different, but 32 + 10 = 42 m² again. The floor did not change, so its area did not change.

A third way is to <Beat n={4}>start from the whole 8 m by 6 m rectangle</Beat> and take away the corner that is missing. The big rectangle covers 8 × 6 = 48 m². The missing corner is 3 m by 2 m, which is 6 m². 48 − 6 = 42 m².

A common mistake is to let two pieces overlap. Take the whole 5 m by 6 m piece and the whole 8 m by 4 m piece, and the part where they cross is counted twice. Another is to work out 8 × 6 and forget to take away the missing corner. Every square metre of the floor is counted once, and only once.

When you are ready, <Task id="composite-area">try one question</Task>: cut a figure into rectangles and find its area.

<Lang zh>

到现在为止，每个图形都是一个长方形或一个正方形。可是很多房间的地面不是这样。<Term id="composite-figure">组合图形</Term>是由几个长方形、正方形拼成的图形。求它的面积，用的还是你已经会的办法。

看<Beat n={1}>一个 L 形房间的平面图</Beat>。底边标着 8 m，左边 6 m，上边 5 m，右边 4 m。它不是<Term id="rectangle">长方形</Term>，不能直接拿一条长乘一条宽：算 8 × 6，会把空着的那个角也铺进去。图上标的是米，所以面积的单位是平方米。

第一种办法：<Beat n={2}>切成两个长方形</Beat>，从拐角处竖着切一刀。切的时候，要让切出来的每一块都是长方形。左边一块是 5 m × 6 m，铺满要 5 × 6 = 30 <Term id="square-metre">平方米</Term>。右边一块宽 3 m，这 3 m 是 8 − 5 得来的；它是 3 m × 4 m，面积是 3 × 4 = 12 m²。合起来：30 + 12 = 42 m²。

也可以<Beat n={3}>换一种切法</Beat>，从拐角处横着切。下面一块是 8 m × 4 m，面积 32 m²；上面一块是 5 m × 2 m，面积 10 m²，这 2 m 是 6 − 4 得来的。两块都变了，可 32 + 10 还是 42 m²。地面本身没有变，面积也就不会变。哪种切法都可以，挑你算起来顺手的那一种。

第三种办法：<Beat n={4}>先看 8 m × 6 m 的大长方形</Beat>，再减去缺的那个角。大长方形要铺 8 × 6 = 48 m²，缺的角是 3 m × 2 m，也就是 6 m²。48 − 6 = 42 m²。三种办法，答案都是 42 m²。

常见的错误是切出来的两块重叠了。比如左边取了整块 5 m × 6 m，下面又取了整块 8 m × 4 m，交叉的那一部分就算了两次，总数会太大。还有一种是算了 8 × 6，却忘了减去缺的角。地面上的每 1 平方米，都要算一次，也只能算一次。

准备好了，就<Task id="composite-area">做一道小题</Task>：把图形切成长方形，再求出面积。
