<Lang en>

This lesson is about two ways to measure a flat shape. <Term id="area">Area</Term> is how much surface the shape covers: you cover it with squares of one size and count them. <Term id="perimeter">Perimeter</Term> is the distance round its edge: you walk round it and add up the lengths. The lesson keeps the two apart all the way through, because they are often mixed up.

There are 11 steps and a practice. Steps 01 to 07 are the main lesson: what area is, square units (cm² and m²), what perimeter is, the perimeter of rectangles and squares, the perimeter of rectilinear figures, area as length × breadth, and shapes with the same area but different perimeters. Steps 08 to 11 go further: a missing side of a rectangle, the side of a square, and the area and perimeter of composite figures. Their chapters are marked "Going further". The last chapter is a practice of eight questions.

Each step explains first. The chapter axis at the bottom of the screen has a small circle for every beat: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the grid on the stage covers, walks, cuts or redraws while the reading panel says what to look at. The text below the beat line walks through the same beats.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question on its idea, drawn from a set of about ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: cover a shape with squares, trace round its edge, draw a rectangle, cut a figure, or type a number and pick its unit.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** draws the answer.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, up to three, each one closer to the answer.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Every question can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved: when you leave the page, your answers are gone.

<Lang zh>

这节课讲量一个平面图形的两种办法。<Term id="area">面积</Term>是图形盖住的面有多大：用一样大的正方形把它铺满，再数一数有几块。<Term id="perimeter">周长</Term>是沿着图形的边绕一圈有多长：沿边走一圈，把走过的长度加起来。这两样常常被混在一起，所以全课都把它们分开来讲。

全课有 11 步和一章练习。第 01 到 07 步是主课：什么是面积，平方单位 cm² 和 m²，什么是周长，长方形和正方形的周长，直线图形的周长，面积 = 长 × 宽，还有面积相同、周长不同的图形。第 08 到 11 步再往前一步：求长方形缺的一条边，求正方形的边长，求组合图形的面积和周长。这几章的章头标着「再往前一步」。最后一章是 8 道练习题。

每一步先讲。屏幕底部的章节轴上，每一拍有一个小圆点：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上的方格就会铺、走、切开或重画，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。节拍下面的正文也按同样的顺序讲一遍。

每章最后的小方块是一道关于这一章的小题，从十道左右的题里挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：用正方形铺满图形，沿边描一圈，画一个长方形，把图形切开，或者输入一个数再选单位。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按「看答案」，舞台会画出答案。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，最多三条，一条比一条接近答案。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

每道题都能只用点按完成，也能只用键盘完成。不计时，也不保存：离开这一页，答案就清空了。
