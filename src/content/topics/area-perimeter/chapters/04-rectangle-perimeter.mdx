<Lang en>

In the last step you found a perimeter by walking round a grid, one square side at a time. Often there is no grid, only a shape with some of its side lengths written on it.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>rectangle with two sides marked</Beat>: 5 cm along the top and 3 cm down the left. The note "<Term id="not-to-scale">Not drawn to scale</Term>" in the corner means the picture is only a guide: work with the numbers, not with a ruler. To go all the way round, add all four sides: 5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 cm.

How do you know the two sides that are not marked? A <Term id="rectangle">rectangle</Term> has <Beat n={2}>opposite sides that are equal</Beat>. The top and the bottom are both 5 cm. The left and the right are both 3 cm. The longer side is called the <Term id="length-breadth">length</Term>, and the shorter side is the breadth. So the perimeter is two lengths and two breadths: 2 × 5 + 2 × 3 = 16 cm.

Watch the next walk closely. It <Beat n={3}>stops at the opposite corner</Beat>, after 5 + 3 = 8 cm. That is only half the way round. You still have to walk along the bottom and back up the other side, which is another 8 cm. 8 + 8 = 16 cm.

Last, a <Term id="square">square</Term>. It has <Beat n={4}>four equal sides</Beat>, so one marked <Term id="side">side</Term> tells you all four. This square has sides of 4 cm. Its perimeter is 4 × side: 4 × 4 = 16 cm. It has the same perimeter as the 5 cm by 3 cm rectangle, even though the two shapes are different.

A common mistake is to add only the two marked sides, 5 + 3 = 8 cm. The picture shows two numbers, but the shape still has four sides, and the walk is not over until you are back at the start.

<Lang zh>

上一步，你在方格上一小段一小段地走，求出了周长。很多时候没有方格，只有一个图形，边上写着长度。这时就不能一格一格地数了，要用写在边上的数来算。

看这个<Beat n={1}>标了两条边的长方形</Beat>：上面是 5 cm，左边是 3 cm。角上写着「<Term id="not-to-scale">未按比例画</Term>」，意思是图只是示意，要用标出的数来算，不要拿尺去量。要走一整圈，就把四条边都加起来：5 + 3 + 5 + 3 = 16 cm。

没标的两条边，怎么知道多长？<Term id="rectangle">长方形</Term>的<Beat n={2}>对边一样长</Beat>。上面和下面都是 5 cm，左边和右边都是 3 cm。所以只要知道一条长、一条宽，另外两条也就知道了。长方形较长的边叫<Term id="length-breadth">长</Term>，较短的边叫宽。周长就是两条长加两条宽：2 × 5 + 2 × 3 = 16 cm。

下面这一圈要看仔细。图上标出的正好是上面和右边两条，很多人走到这里就以为走完了。它<Beat n={3}>走到对角就停了</Beat>，只走了 5 + 3 = 8 cm。这才走了半圈。还要沿着下面走过去，再沿着另一边走上来，又是 8 cm。8 + 8 = 16 cm，这才是一整圈。

最后看<Term id="square">正方形</Term>。它的<Beat n={4}>四条边一样长</Beat>，所以只要标出一条<Term id="side">边长</Term>，四条边就都知道了。这个正方形的边长是 4 cm，周长 = 4 × 边长：4 × 4 = 16 cm。它和长 5 cm、宽 3 cm 的长方形形状不同，周长却一样，都是 16 cm。

常见的错误是只把标出的两条边相加：5 + 3 = 8 cm。图上只写了两个数，可是长方形有四条边，一条也不能少；要回到起点，这一圈才算走完。

准备好了，就<Task id="rectangle-perimeter">做一道小题</Task>：求一个长方形或正方形的周长。
