<Lang en>

You can now find the perimeter of a rectangle from its sides. Many shapes have straight sides that turn at square corners but are not rectangles. This step shows how to walk round them.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>L shape</Beat>. All its sides are straight, and every corner is a square corner, like the corner of a book. A shape like this is a <Term id="rectilinear-figure">rectilinear figure</Term>. Four of its sides are marked: 7 cm along the bottom, 5 cm up the left, 4 cm along the top and 3 cm up the right.

Now <Beat n={2}>walk round it</Beat> and count the sides. Start at the bottom left corner. Go along the bottom, up the right, across the step, up the step, back along the top and down the left. There are 6 sides, not 4. Two of them, the two sides of the step, have no length marked yet.

You can <Beat n={3}>find each missing side</Beat> from the side opposite it. Across: the bottom is 7 cm, so the top and the step across must also come to 7 cm. 4 + ? = 7, so the step across is 3 cm. Up: the left is 5 cm, so the right and the step up must also come to 5 cm. 3 + ? = 5, so the step up is 2 cm.

Now <Beat n={4}>add all six sides</Beat>: 7 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 4 + 5 = 24 cm. Look at the faint rectangle drawn around the L. Its perimeter is 2 × (7 + 5) = 24 cm too. Cutting a step from the corner moves two sides inwards, but they are just as long as before.

A common mistake is to add only the four marked sides: 7 + 5 + 4 + 3 = 19 cm. That leaves out the two sides of the step. Walk all the way round and count every side once. The shortcut with the rectangle around it works only when the step is cut from a corner, so check the shape before you use it.

When you are ready, <Task id="rectilinear-perimeter">try one question</Task> on the perimeter of a rectilinear figure.

<Lang zh>

你已经会用边长求长方形的周长了。可是很多图形的边都是直的，在直角处拐弯，却不是长方形，比如学校里一块 L 形的花圃，或者组屋里拐了个弯的走廊。这一步看看怎样绕它们走一圈。

看这个<Beat n={1}>L 形</Beat>。它的边都是直线，每个角都是直角，就像书本的角。这样的图形叫<Term id="rectilinear-figure">直线图形</Term>。图上标了四条边：下面 7 cm，左边 5 cm，上面 4 cm，右边 3 cm。

现在<Beat n={2}>沿着边走一圈</Beat>，数一数有几条边。从左下角出发：沿下面走过去，沿右边往上，横着走过台阶，再往上走一小段，沿上面走回来，最后沿左边往下。每拐一个弯，就换一条边。一共 6 条边，不是 4 条。台阶的两条边，还没有标长度。

没标的边，可以<Beat n={3}>用对面的边求出来</Beat>。横着看：下面是 7 cm，上面的 4 cm 加上台阶横的那一段，也要一样长。4 + ? = 7，所以横的一段是 3 cm。竖着看：左边是 5 cm，右边的 3 cm 加上台阶竖的那一段，也要一样高。3 + ? = 5，所以竖的一段是 2 cm。

现在<Beat n={4}>把六条边都加起来</Beat>：7 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 4 + 5 = 24 cm。再看 L 形外面淡淡画出的那个长方形，它的周长是 2 × (7 + 5) = 24 cm，也是一样。从角上切掉一个台阶，只是把两条边往里挪了，长度并没有变。

常见的错误是只加标出的四条边：7 + 5 + 4 + 3 = 19 cm，漏掉了台阶的两条边。要走完一整圈，每条边都算一次。用外面长方形来算的捷径，只有台阶切在角上时才行，用之前先看清楚图形。

准备好了，就<Task id="rectilinear-perimeter">做一道小题</Task>：求一个直线图形的周长。
