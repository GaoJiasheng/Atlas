<Lang en>

In the last step you found the area of a <Term id="composite-figure">composite figure</Term> by cutting it into pieces. The <Term id="perimeter">perimeter</Term> works differently. It only goes round the outside, so joining shapes can hide some of their sides.

Start with <Beat n={1}>two squares with sides of 4 cm</Beat>. Go round one of them: 4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 16 cm. The other <Term id="square">square</Term> is the same size, so its perimeter is also 16 cm.

Now <Beat n={2}>push them together</Beat> into one figure. The right side of one square now touches the left side of the other. Those two sides are inside the new figure, along the dashed line, and the way round no longer passes them. The new figure is a <Term id="rectangle">rectangle</Term> with a length of 8 cm and a breadth of 4 cm.

Then <Beat n={3}>walk round the outside only</Beat>: 8 + 4 + 8 + 4 = 24 cm. It is not 16 + 16 = 32 cm. The difference, 8 cm, is the two touching sides of 4 cm each. They are no longer on the edge.

Last, go back to <Beat n={4}>the L-shaped room</Beat> from the last step. Four sides are marked: 8 m, 6 m, 5 m and 4 m. Two sides have no label yet. Find them from the sides opposite: across, 8 − 5 = 3 m; up and down, 6 − 4 = 2 m. Now walk round all six sides: 8 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 5 + 6 = 28 m. The two pieces you cut in the last step do not matter here, because the cut is inside the room.

Two mistakes are common here. One is to add the perimeters of the pieces, which counts the inside sides as well. The other is to add only the sides that have labels, which leaves out part of the way round. Walk the whole outside once, and only the outside.

When you are ready, <Task id="composite-perimeter">try one question</Task> on the way round a composite figure.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

上一步，你把<Term id="composite-figure">组合图形</Term>切开来求面积。<Term id="perimeter">周长</Term>不一样：它只绕外面一圈。几个图形拼在一起，有些边就被藏到里面去了。

先看<Beat n={1}>两个边长 4 cm 的正方形</Beat>。绕其中一个走一圈：4 + 4 + 4 + 4 = 16 cm。另一个<Term id="square">正方形</Term>一样大，周长也是 16 cm。两个加起来是 16 + 16 = 32 cm。

现在<Beat n={2}>把它们推到一起</Beat>，拼成一个图形。一个正方形的右边贴住了另一个的左边。这两条边到了新图形的里面，就是那条虚线，绕外面走一圈，已经走不到它们了。拼成的是一个长 8 cm、宽 4 cm 的<Term id="rectangle">长方形</Term>。

然后<Beat n={3}>只走外面一圈</Beat>：上面 8 cm，右边 4 cm，下面 8 cm，左边 4 cm，8 + 4 + 8 + 4 = 24 cm，不是 16 + 16 = 32 cm。少了的 8 cm，正好是贴在一起的两条边，每条 4 cm。它们已经不在外面了。

最后回到上一步的<Beat n={4}>L 形房间</Beat>。标出长度的边有四条：8 m、6 m、5 m、4 m，还有两条没标。用对面的边把它们求出来：横着看，8 − 5 = 3 m；竖着看，6 − 4 = 2 m。求出来的这两条边，就是 L 形缺角那里的两条边。再把六条边走一圈：8 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 5 + 6 = 28 m。上一步切的那一刀在房间里面，求周长的时候不用管它。

这里常见的错误有两种。一种是把每一块的周长加起来，连里面的边也算进去了，答案会太大。另一种是只加标了数的边，漏掉了一段路，答案会太小。走的时候，可以从一个角出发，沿着外边一条一条走，直到回到这个角。外面一整圈要走完，里面的边一条也不走。

准备好了，就<Task id="composite-perimeter">做一道小题</Task>：绕组合图形走一圈，求出它的周长。

</Lang>
