<Lang en>

In the last step you measured area by counting squares of one size. To tell someone else an area, you both need to use the same square. That shared square is a square unit.

Start with <Beat n={1}>one small square</Beat> with sides of 1 cm. A square whose sides are one unit long is a <Term id="unit-square">unit square</Term>. The area of this one is 1 <Term id="square-centimetre">square centimetre</Term>, written 1 cm². Look at the marks on two of its sides: each says 1 cm. It covers a small patch, about the size of a fingernail.

Now <Beat n={2}>cover a shape with these squares</Beat>. It takes 8 of them, in 2 rows of 4, so the area is 8 cm². Look at the small 2 after cm. It tells you the unit is a square, not a line. If you write 8 cm, you are writing a length, like the length of a pencil. An area always needs the small 2.

Some shapes are not made of whole squares only. This one has <Beat n={3}>two half squares</Beat>, one at each end, each a square cut from corner to corner. Two halves fit together to make one whole square. So count the 6 whole squares, then add 1 for the two halves: 6 + 1 = 7 cm².

A bedroom floor would need far too many centimetre squares to count. For <Beat n={4}>bigger surfaces like a floor</Beat>, use a bigger unit square, 1 m by 1 m. Its area is 1 <Term id="square-metre">square metre</Term>, written 1 m². This floor takes 4 rows of 3 such squares, so its area is 12 m². On a grid with no unit named, you just say <Term id="square-unit">square units</Term>.

A common mistake is to write cm for an area, as in 8 cm. Another is to count each half square as a whole one, which gives 8 instead of 7. A half square covers only half as much, so it takes two of them to make one.

<Lang zh>

上一步，你用一样大的正方形数出了面积。要把面积告诉别人，大家得用同一种正方形。这个大家都用的正方形，就是平方单位。

先看<Beat n={1}>一个小正方形</Beat>，它的边长是 1 cm。边长是 1 个单位的正方形，叫<Term id="unit-square">单位正方形</Term>。这一个的面积是 1 <Term id="square-centimetre">平方厘米</Term>，写作 1 cm²。看它两条边上的标注，都是 1 cm。它盖住的地方很小，差不多和指甲一样大。

接着<Beat n={2}>用这样的正方形铺一个图形</Beat>。一共铺了 8 个，2 行，每行 4 个，所以面积是 8 cm²。注意 cm 右上角的小 2，它说明单位是一个正方形，不是一条线。要是写成 8 cm，那就成了一段长度，像一支铅笔有多长。写面积，一定要带上这个小 2。

有的图形不是刚好由整格组成。这个图形两头各有<Beat n={3}>一个半格</Beat>，都是把正方形沿对角线切开的一半。两个半格拼起来，正好是一个整格。所以先数 6 个整格，再把两个半格算成 1 个：6 + 1 = 7 cm²。

卧室的地面要是用 1 cm² 的小正方形去铺，多得数不完。量<Beat n={4}>地面这样大的面</Beat>，要用大一些的单位正方形，它的边长是 1 m。它的面积是 1 <Term id="square-metre">平方米</Term>，写作 1 m²。这块地面铺了 4 行，每行 3 个，面积是 12 m²。方格上没有说是什么单位时，就说有几个<Term id="square-unit">平方单位</Term>。

常见的错误有两个：一是面积写成 cm，比如写 8 cm；二是把半格当成整格来数，结果数成 8，而不是 7。一个半格只盖住半个正方形，要两个合起来才是一个。

准备好了，就<Task id="square-units">做一道小题</Task>：数一数有几个平方单位，再写上单位。
