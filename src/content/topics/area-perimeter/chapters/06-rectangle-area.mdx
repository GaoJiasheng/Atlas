<Lang en>

In the first steps you found an area by covering a shape with squares and counting them one by one. For a rectangle there is a quicker way, and this step shows where it comes from.

Cover a 4 cm by 3 cm <Term id="rectangle">rectangle</Term> with 1 cm squares, <Beat n={1}>one row at a time</Beat>. Each row holds 4 squares, because the rectangle is 4 cm along. There is room for 3 rows, because it is 3 cm from top to bottom. Counting row by row gives 4 + 4 + 4 = 12.

Squares laid out in equal rows like this make <Beat n={2}>an array</Beat>. You know arrays from multiplication: 3 rows of 4 is 3 × 4 = 12. So there are 12 squares, and the area is 12 <Term id="square-centimetre">cm²</Term>.

Now <Beat n={3}>take the squares away</Beat> and keep only the two side lengths. You can still picture the squares. The <Term id="length-breadth">length</Term>, 4 cm, tells you how many squares fit in each row. The breadth, 3 cm, tells you how many rows there are. So area = length × breadth.

Here is a slip to watch for. With only <Beat n={4}>the two numbers 4 and 3</Beat> on the page, you may want to add them. 4 + 3 = 7, but 7 counts no squares at all: it is a length, half the way round. Adding all four sides gives 14, and that is the perimeter. To find the area, multiply: 4 × 3 = 12 cm².

A <Term id="square">square</Term> works the same way. Its length and breadth are equal, so its area is <Beat n={5}>side × side</Beat>. A square with sides of 5 cm has 5 rows of 5 squares: 5 × 5 = 25 cm².

A common mistake is to add the sides when the question asks for area, or to write the answer in cm. Area is a number of squares, so it comes from multiplying, and its unit is cm² or m².

<Lang zh>

前几步，你把正方形铺满图形，再一个一个地数，求出面积。可是长方形一大，一个一个地数就太慢了。长方形有更快的办法。这一步看看这个办法是怎么来的。

用 1 cm 的正方形铺一个 4 cm × 3 cm 的<Term id="rectangle">长方形</Term>，<Beat n={1}>一行一行地铺</Beat>。长方形横着有 4 cm，所以每行放得下 4 个；从上到下有 3 cm，所以能铺 3 行。一行一行地数：4 + 4 + 4 = 12。

像这样排成一样多的几行，就是<Beat n={2}>阵列</Beat>。学乘法时你见过阵列：3 行，每行 4 个，就是 3 × 4 = 12。一共 12 个正方形，面积是 12 <Term id="square-centimetre">cm²</Term>。

现在<Beat n={3}>把方格拿走</Beat>，只留下两条边的长度。正方形不画出来，你也能在脑子里看到它们。<Term id="length-breadth">长</Term>是 4 cm，说明每行有 4 个；宽是 3 cm，说明一共有 3 行。所以，面积 = 长 × 宽。这样，不用画方格，只要知道长和宽，就能算出面积。

这里要当心。纸上只剩<Beat n={4}>4 和 3 两个数</Beat>，有人一看到就把它们加起来。4 + 3 = 7，可是 7 一个正方形也没数到，它是一段长度，只走了半圈。四条边都加起来是 14，那是周长。求面积要相乘：4 × 3 = 12 cm²。

<Term id="square">正方形</Term>也是这样算。它的长和宽一样，所以面积是<Beat n={5}>边长 × 边长</Beat>。它也是一个阵列，只是行数和每行的个数一样多。边长 5 cm 的正方形，可以铺 5 行，每行 5 个：5 × 5 = 25 cm²。

常见的错误是求面积时把边长相加，或者把单位写成 cm。面积是正方形的个数，要用乘法来算，单位是 cm² 或 m²。
