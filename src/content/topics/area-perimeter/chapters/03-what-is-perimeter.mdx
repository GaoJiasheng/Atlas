<Lang en>

So far you have covered shapes with squares to find their area. Now you will do something else with a shape: walk round its edge.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>rectangle on the grid</Beat>. Its <Term id="perimeter">perimeter</Term> is the distance all the way round its edge. Think of an ant that starts at one corner, walks along the edge and stops when it is back where it started. The distance it has walked is the perimeter.

Now <Beat n={2}>walk round the edge</Beat>, one square side at a time. Each side of a grid square is 1 cm long. Count as you go: 1, 2, 3 … There are 4 along the top, 3 down the side, 4 along the bottom and 3 back up. After 14 steps you are back at the start. The perimeter is 14 cm.

Here is a trap. Look at the <Beat n={3}>squares along the edge</Beat>. There are 10 of them, but the perimeter is not 10. A corner square has two of its sides on the edge, one on each side of the corner. Each of the 4 corner squares gives 2 steps of the walk, so counting squares misses 4 steps. Count the sides, not the squares.

Finally, see <Beat n={4}>both measures on one shape</Beat>. The squares inside are its area: 12 cm². The walk round the edge is its perimeter: 14 cm. Area counts squares, so it is in cm². Perimeter is a length, so it is in cm, like a ribbon laid round the edge.

You can see both measures in a garden. A fence goes round the bed: its length is a perimeter. The soil inside, covered with vegetables, is an area.

A common mistake is to mix the two: to count the squares inside when the question asks for the distance round, or to count the squares along the edge. Area covers a shape; perimeter goes round it.

<Lang zh>

前面你用正方形把图形铺满，求出了面积。现在换一种做法：沿着图形的边走一圈。

看<Beat n={1}>方格上的长方形</Beat>。沿着它的边走一圈，走过的长度就是它的<Term id="perimeter">周长</Term>。想象一只蚂蚁从一个角出发，沿着边一直走，回到出发的地方才停下来。它走了多长的路，周长就是多少。

现在<Beat n={2}>沿着边走一圈</Beat>，每次走一个小格的一条边。方格里每个小正方形的边长是 1 cm。边走边数：1、2、3……上面 4 段，右边 3 段，下面 4 段，左边 3 段。走到第 14 段，正好回到起点。周长是 14 cm。

这里有个陷阱。看<Beat n={3}>靠边的那些格子</Beat>：一共 10 个，可是周长不是 10。角上的格子有两条边在外面，拐角两边各一条。4 个角格各走 2 段，只数格子，就少算了 4 段。所以要数边，不数格子。

最后，看<Beat n={4}>同一个图形的两种量法</Beat>。里面铺了 12 个正方形，面积是 12 cm²；沿边走一圈是 14 cm，这是周长。面积数的是正方形，单位是 cm²；周长是一段长度，单位是 cm，就像一条绕着边放一圈的丝带。

菜园里也看得到这两种量。围栏绕着菜圃一圈，它有多长，就是周长；里面的泥土铺满了青菜，那是面积。

常见的错误是把两种量混在一起：题目问绕一圈有多长，却去数里面的格子；或者去数靠边的格子。记住：面积是把图形铺满，周长是绕图形走一圈。

准备好了，就<Task id="what-is-perimeter">做一道小题</Task>：沿着图形的边走一圈，数一数一共走了几段。
