<Lang en>

You already measure lengths with a ruler, in centimetres and metres. A ruler tells you how long a line is. This step is about something different: how much surface a flat shape covers.

Look at the <Beat n={1}>two placemats on the table</Beat>. One is long and narrow. The other is shorter but taller. Which one covers more of the table? One wins on length, the other on height, so your eyes cannot decide. You need a way to measure.

Now <Beat n={2}>cover the first mat with squares</Beat>. Every square is the same size. They sit edge to edge, with no gaps and no overlaps. The first mat takes 12 squares: 3 rows of 4. The number of squares that cover a shape is its <Term id="area">area</Term>. The lines of the <Term id="grid">square grid</Term> help you lay the squares out neatly.

Then <Beat n={3}>cover the second mat with the same squares</Beat>. It is a different shape, 2 rows of 6, but it also takes 12 squares. So the two mats have the same area. A long, narrow shape and a short, tall one can cover exactly the same amount of table.

The last beat shows why the squares must match. Here the second mat is <Beat n={4}>covered with 3 big squares</Beat>, each as big as 4 small ones. Now one mat counts 12 and the other counts 3. That does not mean the first mat is four times as big. 3 big squares cover just as much as 12 small ones. Counts can be compared only when every square is the same size.

A common mistake is to decide which shape is bigger by how long it looks, or to compare counts of squares of different sizes. Length alone does not tell you how much surface is covered, and a count of big squares is not the same kind of number as a count of small ones.

When you are ready, <Task id="what-is-area">try one question</Task> on covering a shape with squares and counting them.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

你已经会用尺量长度，单位是厘米和米。尺告诉你一条线有多长。这一步要量的是另一样东西：一个平面图形盖住的面有多大。

看桌上的<Beat n={1}>两张餐垫</Beat>。一张又长又窄，另一张短一些，可是高一些。哪一张盖住的桌面多？一张比较长，一张比较高，光用眼睛看，说不准。我们需要一个量的办法。

先<Beat n={2}>用正方形把第一张铺满</Beat>。每个正方形都一样大，一个挨着一个，不留空隙，也不重叠。第一张一共铺了 12 块：3 行，每行 4 块。铺满一个图形要用几块这样的正方形，这个数就是它的<Term id="area">面积</Term>。<Term id="grid">方格</Term>上的线，能帮你把正方形排得整整齐齐。

再<Beat n={3}>用同样的正方形铺第二张</Beat>。它的形状不一样，2 行，每行 6 块，可是也要 12 块。所以两张餐垫的面积相同。一个又长又窄的图形，和一个短一些、高一些的图形，可以盖住一样多的桌面。

最后一拍说明为什么正方形要一样大。这一回，第二张<Beat n={4}>改铺 3 块大方块</Beat>，每块有 4 个小正方形那么大。一张数到 12，一张只数到 3，这并不表示第一张大了四倍。3 块大方块盖住的面，正好和 12 块小正方形一样多。只有正方形一样大，数出来的块数才能拿来比。

常见的错误有两种：一是看哪一张比较长，就说它比较大；二是用大小不同的方块去数，再拿块数来比。长短说明不了盖住了多少面；大方块的块数和小方块的块数，也不是同一种数，不能直接比。

准备好了，就<Task id="what-is-area">做一道小题</Task>：用正方形铺满一个图形，再数一数一共有几块。
