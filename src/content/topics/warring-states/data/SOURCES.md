# Spring and Autumn and Warring States: sources

**English and simplified-Chinese drafts; human historical review pending.** Map data are CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution and upstream licences below. All borders are approximate; registration error does not establish ancient territorial accuracy. The complete geographic control-point ledger is scripts/geo/warring-states/SOURCES-GEO.md. Known factual/site verification items are in ../AUDIT.md.

## Geographic source and inference limits

G9 is a point-distribution map, not a polygon map: direct visual inspection disproved the specification’s proposed raster-category method. G10, already on the approved list, supplies the early period polygons instead. G9 is only a capital/location cross-check. **K1–K3 reuse undated period geography and are not year-exact reconstructions.** G10 has no bounded Chen, Cai, Tan, Sui, Shen, Xi, Deng, Yu, Guo, Huo, Geng, Liang, Rui, Yong and other minor annexation units; those changes cannot be digitized as whole-state transfers and are not invented. K2/K3 do not show fabricated territorial expansion.

Registration budgets are 5 km for G1, 25 km for primary regional state maps and 40 km for small/period maps (maximum in-sample residual; leave-one-out statistics are also reported). G11 fails the 25 km budget (maximum 66.657 km, leave-one-out maximum 86.927 km) and has been excluded from K9–K11. Those frames now explicitly carry forward G8’s 280 BCE geography, with only sourced whole-state transfers in K10 and a schematic union in K11. They are not accurate later-year boundary reconstructions. G9 exceeds its check-only 40 km budget slightly. G15 exceeds 25 km, so K10 uses whole-state reassignments and does not trace speculative Qin commandery gains. G13/G17 have only 5/8 secure controls and G16 has no twelve independently identifiable anchors: diagnostic/reference-only, excluded from all frame and route registration dependencies. Thus the request for twelve trustworthy controls on every one of the eighteen maps is **not met**; no synthetic control points or artificially low RMS are claimed.

Earlier source surveys in the approved spec found no usable dated boundary relations in OHM for this region/period. OHM is generally CC0, subject to feature exceptions; no OHM geometry is used here. CHGIS V6 starts at 221 BCE and its redistribution EULA excludes downloadable reuse without written agreement: not downloaded or redistributed. The © Tan Qixiang atlas is not traced locally; Commons authors’ own declared references to it are recorded in their metadata, without claiming independent permission from the atlas publisher.

K6 borrows 350 BCE Han/Zhao/Wei divisions within the fifth-century Jin outline; the remaining source area is labelled residual Jin, not a fabricated city-centred enclave. K9 carries forward 280 BCE geography to 260 BCE without asserting the intervening changes; Zhou has no bounded G8 face and is omitted. K10 reassigns only the whole Han and Lu source components. K11 is the schematic union of these G8-derived ranges, not a reconstruction of the Qin imperial boundary; no Lingnan, steppe, or unbounded extra Yue land is added. Wey’s dependent ruling house survived until 209 BCE; the imperial fill does not date its abolition. No optional K12 is supplied.

The shared coastline buffer-gap step dropped Qin in K10 even though it existed in composed inputs. This topic therefore disables that optional buffer step, runs the unchanged shared simplifier, then intersects each frame with real NE land in shore-clip.ts. Every holder is checked for retention, all frames are re-exported into one shared topology and rings rechecked. No gap-ownership buffer is used. Area-retention diagnostics in area-audit.json distinguish dissolved internal seams from missing territory: the largest coastline/region clipping changes are early Ju (about 22%) and G8 Yan (about 25%, including parts outside the regional plate); these remain source-alignment limits, not inferred annexations. G3 vector network is taken directly from the published `Borders 5 BC states` SVG paths, then polygonized. The source’s open coastal ends are closed against real Natural Earth coastline, with 1 km snapping and a 40 km gap tolerance. Bounded G10 Ba/Ju/Yue components supplement omissions. These source mixes and the unquantified historical error remain visible in approximate labels and feature certainty. G7/G8 textured relief required flat masks traced in original source pixels, whose overlays are in check/raster-*-trace-overlay.png. G11’s classified mask and original registration reports are retained only as diagnostic records; no G11 edge enters the released control geometry. G13/G16/G17 do not supply geometry or frame checks. No synthetic controls have been added to force a registration pass.

The coastline clipping method, retained areas, control-point residuals and per-frame derivation are recorded in scripts/geo/warring-states/SOURCES-GEO.md. Historical accuracy remains unquantified even where map registration passes. G11/G13/G15/G16/G17 contribute no keyframe geometry in this revision.

## Movement routes

All 26 itineraries have been compared with the named primary passages. Unsupported stations have been removed or corrected. **They remain schematic campaign or travel connections, not recovered ancient roads.** Exact rivers, passes and uncertain identifications are stated per route; straight links do not assert a traversable path through every intervening landform.

The strict movement schema has no `note` field. Each route therefore has its own bilingual `note` in S170–S195, mirrored in `scripts/geo/warring-states/verification-routes.json` and below. No engine extension or unsupported JSON field is used.
