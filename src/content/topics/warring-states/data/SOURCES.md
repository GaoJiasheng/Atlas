# Spring and Autumn and Warring States: sources

**Draft English text; Chinese translation and human review pending.** Map data are CC BY-SA 4.0, with attribution and upstream licences below. All borders are approximate; registration error does not establish ancient territorial accuracy. The complete geographic control-point ledger is scripts/geo/warring-states/SOURCES-GEO.md. Known factual/site verification items are in ../AUDIT.md.

## Geographic source and inference limits

G9 is a point-distribution map, not a polygon map: direct visual inspection disproved the specification’s proposed raster-category method. G10, already on the approved list, supplies the early period polygons instead. G9 is only a capital/location cross-check. **K1–K3 reuse undated period geography and are not year-exact reconstructions.** G10 has no bounded Chen, Cai, Tan, Sui, Shen, Xi, Deng, Yu, Guo, Huo, Geng, Liang, Rui, Yong and other minor annexation units; those changes cannot be digitized as whole-state transfers and are not invented. K2/K3 do not show fabricated territorial expansion.

Registration budgets are 5 km for G1, 25 km for primary regional state maps and 40 km for small/period maps (maximum in-sample residual; leave-one-out statistics are also reported). G11 exceeds the 25 km budget and remains a draft geographic limitation in K9–K11. G9 exceeds its check-only 40 km budget slightly. G15 exceeds 25 km, so K10 uses whole-state reassignments and does not trace speculative Qin commandery gains. G13/G17 have only 5/8 secure controls and G16 has no twelve independently identifiable anchors: diagnostic/reference-only, never used for polygon tracing. Thus the request for twelve trustworthy controls on every one of the eighteen maps is **not met**; no synthetic control points or artificially low RMS are claimed.

Earlier source surveys in the approved spec found no usable dated boundary relations in OHM for this region/period. OHM is generally CC0, subject to feature exceptions; no OHM geometry is used here. CHGIS V6 starts at 221 BCE and its redistribution EULA excludes downloadable reuse without written agreement: not downloaded or redistributed. The © Tan Qixiang atlas is not traced locally; Commons authors’ own declared references to it are recorded in their metadata, without claiming independent permission from the atlas publisher.

## Map downloads

All eighteen Commons maps have a local file, API author/licence metadata and SHA-256 in raw/fetch-log.json (raw is gitignored). Full canonical originals were recovered where available; remaining published renders are clearly identified. Raw pixels are not shipped to the application. Derivative flat masks copy visible published source edges in source-pixel coordinates: this is tracing, not invented state geometry.

Inference-based frames: **K1, K2, K3, K4’s G10 supplements, K5, K6, K9’s G8 supplements, K10, K11**. K6 borrows 350 BCE Han/Zhao/Wei divisions within the fifth-century Jin outline; the remaining source area is labelled residual Jin, not a fabricated city-centred enclave. K11 is only the union of represented conquered-state ranges; no Lingnan, steppe, or unbounded extra Yue land is added. No optional K12 is supplied.

The shared coastline buffer-gap step dropped Qin in K10 even though it existed in composed inputs. This topic therefore disables that optional buffer step, runs the unchanged shared simplifier, then intersects each frame with real NE land in shore-clip.ts. Every holder is checked for retention, all frames are re-exported into one shared topology and rings rechecked. No gap-ownership buffer is used. Area-retention diagnostics in area-audit.json distinguish dissolved internal seams from missing territory: the largest coastline/region clipping changes are early Ju (about 22%) and G8 Yan (about 25%, including parts outside the regional plate); these remain source-alignment limits, not inferred annexations. G3 vector network is taken directly from the published `Borders 5 BC states` SVG paths, then polygonized. The source’s open coastal ends are closed against real Natural Earth coastline, with 1 km snapping and a 40 km gap tolerance. Bounded G10 Ba/Ju/Yue components supplement omissions. These source mixes and the unquantified historical error remain visible in approximate labels and feature certainty. G7/G8 textured relief required flat masks traced in original source pixels, whose overlays are in check/raster-*-trace-overlay.png. G11’s source-colour classes and tiny Zhou region are retained only where bounded. G11 does not bound Lu/Wey separately: their bounded G8 components are carried forward from 280 BCE and clearly labelled, not invented around capital dots. This is an additional inference in K9.
