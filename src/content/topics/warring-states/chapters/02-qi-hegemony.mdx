<Lang en>

Duke Huan of Qi, Qí Huán Gōng 齐桓公, ruled from 685 to 643 BCE. His chief minister was Guan Zhong, Guǎn Zhòng 管仲, who had once served Huan's brother and rival and, according to the Shiji, had shot an arrow at Huan himself. Qi held the rich coast of present-day Shandong, with its salt and fish, and its capital, Linzi, would grow into one of the largest cities of the age.

Huan led the other lords in the name of the Zhou king. Later writers summed up his policy as <Term id="zunwang-rangyi">"honour the king, repel the Yi"</Term>. In 663 BCE Qi marched north to help Yan against the Shanrong. When Di forces attacked Xing and Wey, Qi helped both states rebuild their capitals further south. Confucius later said that without Guan Zhong "we would be wearing our hair loose and fastening our robes on the left", like the outsiders. No account by the Shanrong or the Di themselves survives.

In 656 BCE Qi led the armies of eight states to the edge of Chu. Guan Zhong accused Chu of failing to send tribute to the king; Chu's envoy answered that its armies were ready. Neither side attacked, and they swore a covenant at Shaoling. In 651 BCE, at Kuiqiu, the king's envoy brought Huan meat from the royal sacrifice. A <Term id="hegemon">hegemon</Term> was the leader of the lords: he called meetings, settled quarrels and led joint campaigns, but did not rule the other states.

Huan's leadership did not outlive him. When he died in 643 BCE, five of his sons fought for the throne. According to the Shiji, his body lay unburied for <Num s="S167">67</Num> days.

<More title={{"en": "What a covenant promised", "zh": "盟约里写了什么"}}>

At a <Term id="covenant">covenant meeting</Term> the rulers or their ministers killed an animal, smeared its blood on their lips and swore before the spirits. The Mencius lists the oaths of Kuiqiu: punish unfilial sons, do not change the heir, do not make a concubine a wife, do not dam rivers to harm neighbours, and do not stop the sale of grain in a famine. The text was written down three centuries later and records an ideal, not proof that every lord obeyed.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Which five hegemons?", "zh": "“五霸”是哪五位"}}>

"The Five Hegemons" is a later grouping, and the lists disagree. The Xunzi names Duke Huan of Qi, Duke Wen of Jin, King Zhuang of Chu, King Helü of Wu and King Goujian of Yue. One list in the Baihu tong replaces Goujian with Duke Mu of Qin. Zhao Qi's commentary on the Mencius has Huan, Wen, Mu, Duke Xiang of Song and Zhuang. Only Duke Huan and Duke Wen appear in every list.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "The fall of Wey, seen from Chaoge", "zh": "从朝歌看卫国的覆亡"}}>

In the winter of 660 BCE Di forces defeated the army of Wey at Yingze and killed Duke Yi of Wey. The Zuo Tradition says that <Num s="S2">730</Num> men and women of the capital survived to cross the Yellow River; with people from two nearby towns they numbered <Num s="S2">about 5,000</Num>. They lived in temporary shelters at Cao until Qi built them a new walled town at Chuqiu in 658 BCE. The texts call the attackers simply "Di", a label for several northern communities with their own rulers.

</More>

<Lang zh>

齐桓公在位四十三年，从前685年到前643年。他的相是管仲。管仲原本辅佐桓公的兄弟兼对手公子纠，据《史记》，他还曾一箭射中桓公的衣带钩。齐国据有今山东沿海，盛产鱼盐，都城临淄后来成为当时最大的城市之一。

齐桓公以周王的名义号令诸侯，后人把他的方针概括为<Term id="zunwang-rangyi">尊王攘夷</Term>。前663年，齐军北上援燕，攻打山戎。狄人进攻邢、卫两国以后，齐又帮助两国在南方重建都城。孔子后来说：“微管仲，吾其被发左衽矣”，意思是没有管仲，我们恐怕都要披散头发、衣襟向左开了，像周人眼中的外族一样。山戎和狄人自己的说法，没有流传下来。

前656年，齐桓公率领八国军队到达楚国边境。管仲责问楚国不向周王进贡，楚国使者回答，楚军随时可以迎战。双方都没有动手，最后在召陵订立盟约。前651年葵丘之会，周王派使者把祭祀用的肉赐给齐桓公。所谓<Term id="hegemon">霸主</Term>，就是诸侯之长：召集会盟、调解纠纷、率领联军，但并不统治其他国家。

霸业没有延续到桓公身后。前643年桓公去世，五个儿子争夺君位。据《史记》，他的遗体在床上停放了<Num s="S167">67</Num>天无人收殓。

<More title={{"en": "What a covenant promised", "zh": "盟约里写了什么"}}>

<Term id="covenant">会盟</Term>时，诸侯或卿大夫杀牲取血，涂在嘴上，对着神灵发誓。《孟子》记载了葵丘之盟的誓词：惩处不孝之人，不得改换太子，不得以妾为妻，不得修堤坝损害邻国，不得在饥荒时禁止粮食出售。这段文字是三百年后写下的，记录的是一种理想，并不能证明每个诸侯都遵守了。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Which five hegemons?", "zh": "“五霸”是哪五位"}}>

“春秋五霸”是后人的说法，各家名单并不一致。《荀子》列齐桓公、晋文公、楚庄王、吴王阖闾、越王勾践；《白虎通》的一种说法以秦穆公代替勾践；东汉赵岐注《孟子》，列齐桓公、晋文公、秦穆公、宋襄公、楚庄王。每份名单都有的，只有齐桓公和晋文公。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "The fall of Wey, seen from Chaoge", "zh": "从朝歌看卫国的覆亡"}}>

前660年冬，狄人在荧泽击败卫军，杀死卫懿公。据《左传》，都城朝歌的男女只有<Num s="S2">730</Num>人活着渡过黄河，加上附近共、滕两邑的人，一共<Num s="S2">约5000</Num>人。他们在曹邑搭起临时住处，直到前658年齐国在楚丘为他们筑起新城。史书只称进攻者为“狄”，这是对北方好几个各有首领的人群的统称。

</More>
