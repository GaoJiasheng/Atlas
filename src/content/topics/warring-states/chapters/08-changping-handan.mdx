<Lang en>

In 279 BCE Qin's general Bai Qi invaded Chu. He flooded the city of Yan, and in 278 BCE took Ying, the Chu capital, and burned the royal tombs at Yiling. The Chu court fled north-east to Chen. Qin did not always win: in 269 BCE the Zhao general Zhao She broke a Qin army at Yuyu.

In 262 BCE Qin cut off Han's Shangdang commandery, and its governor, Feng Ting, Féng Tíng 冯亭, gave it to Zhao. Qin and Zhao armies faced each other at Changping for two years, with Zhao's general Lian Po, Lián Pō 廉颇, holding fortified lines. In 260 BCE Zhao replaced him with the young Zhao Kuo, Zhào Kuò 赵括, and Qin secretly sent Bai Qi; the Qin king himself went to Henei and sent every man there aged fifteen and over to the front. Bai Qi cut the Zhao army off. According to the Shiji, after <Num s="S152">46</Num> days without food its soldiers were killing and eating one another. Zhao Kuo was killed trying to break out, and the rest surrendered.

The Shiji says <Num s="S152">400,000</Num> men surrendered and that Bai Qi, fearing they would rebel, had them all killed and buried, sending only <Num s="S152">240</Num> of the youngest back to Zhao. Qin's annals say "more than <Num s="S151">400,000</Num>, all killed". These figures come from Qin's war records and are disputed. Bai Qi himself admitted that more than half of Qin's own soldiers had died. Almost none of the dead have names; they were farmers conscripted from Zhao's villages.

Qin then besieged Handan, the Zhao capital, from 259 BCE. In 257 BCE relief armies from Wei and Chu drove Qin off. Bai Qi, who had refused to lead the siege, was ordered to kill himself. In 256 BCE Qin took the last royal domain of Zhou, and King Nan, Zhōu Nǎn Wáng 周赧王, died.

<More title={{"en": "Changping: how many, and how do we know?", "zh": "长平：死了多少人，从哪里知道"}}>

Two numbers survive, both in the Shiji: about <Num s="S152">400,000</Num> surrendered and killed, and <Num s="S152">450,000</Num> "beheaded or captured" over the whole campaign. They come from Qin's merit records, which rewarded large counts. Modern studies, such as Song Yu's in 1983 and Zhang Guangzhi's in 2016, argue that Zhao could hardly have raised and fed so large an army, and that the totals may include earlier fighting. No one has produced a reliable smaller figure.

In 1995 archaeologists excavated a pit at Yonglu village near Gaoping, after farmers found bones there. According to published accounts of the excavation, it held the jumbled remains of about <Num s="S26,S241">130</Num> people, all men, most around thirty. Many skulls lay apart from their bodies; bones bore cutting wounds, and arrowheads were still lodged in some. The excavators concluded that most had been killed and then thrown into the pit, not buried alive. The Shiji's word, keng, can mean "pit" as well as "bury". One pit proves a mass killing at Changping; it cannot tell us how many died. Other pits found nearby since 2011 have not been excavated.

A tiger tally was a bronze tiger split in two: the ruler kept one half, the commander the other, and troops moved only when the halves matched. A Zhao official told Lord Pingyuan that the people of Handan were "burning bones for fuel and exchanging children to eat". In Wei, Lord Xinling, Xìnlíng Jūn 信陵君, had the king's concubine Ru Ji steal the tally; at the Wei camp the strongman Zhu Hai killed the commander Jin Bi with an iron mallet, and Xinling led his army to Handan. Lord Chunshen brought troops from Chu.

<Lang zh>

前279年，秦将白起攻楚，水灌鄢城；前278年攻占楚都郢，焚烧夷陵的楚王陵墓，楚国朝廷向东北逃到陈。秦军也不是每战必胜：前269年，赵将赵奢在阏与大破秦军。

前262年，秦军切断了韩国上党郡与本土的联系，郡守冯亭把上党献给赵国。秦、赵两军在长平相持两年，赵将廉颇筑垒坚守。前260年，赵国改派年轻的赵括为将，秦国则秘密起用白起，秦王还亲自到河内，把十五岁以上的男子全部征发到前线。白起截断了赵军的退路和粮道。据《史记》，赵军断粮<Num s="S152">46</Num>天，士兵们自相残杀，以人为食。赵括突围时中箭身亡，余下的赵军投降。

《史记》说，投降的赵卒有<Num s="S152">40万</Num>人，白起担心他们作乱，把他们全部坑杀，只放回<Num s="S152">240</Num>名年少的人。《秦本纪》则记作“<Num s="S151">四十余万</Num>尽杀之”。这些数字出自秦国的战功记录，学界有争议。白起自己也承认，秦军“死者过半”。死者几乎没有留下姓名，他们大多是从赵国乡村征来的农民。

此后秦军从前259年起围攻赵都邯郸，城中百姓烧骨头做饭。前257年，魏、楚援军赶到，秦军败退。不肯领兵攻邯郸的白起被赐死。前256年，秦攻取周王室最后的领地，周赧王去世。

<More title={{"en": "Changping: how many, and how do we know?", "zh": "长平：死了多少人，从哪里知道"}}>

流传下来的两个数字都出自《史记》：投降后被杀的约<Num s="S152">40万</Num>人；整个战役“前后斩首虏<Num s="S152">四十五万</Num>”。它们来自鼓励多报的秦国军功记录。现代学者，如宋裕（1983年）、张广志（2016年），认为以赵国的人口和粮食，很难组织和供养这样一支大军，总数也可能包括了此前各阶段的伤亡。不过，没有人能给出一个可靠的较小数字。

1995年，村民在高平永录村平整土地时发现人骨，考古人员随后进行了发掘。据有关这次发掘的报道和研究，这个坑里杂乱地堆着约<Num s="S26,S241">130</Num>具遗骸，全是男性，大多三十岁左右。许多头骨与躯干分离，骨头上有砍伤，有的体内还留着箭头。发掘者认为，死者大多是被杀后扔进坑里的，并非活埋。《史记》用的“坑”字，既可以指土坑，也可以指掩埋。一个尸骨坑证明长平确实发生过大规模屠杀，却无法说明一共死了多少人。2011年以来附近又发现了几处尸骨坑，尚未发掘。

虎符是一只剖成两半的铜虎，君主和将领各执一半，两半相合才能调动军队。邯郸被围时，有人对平原君说，城中百姓已经“炊骨易子而食”。魏国的信陵君请魏王的宠妃如姬盗出虎符，到了魏军营中，力士朱亥用铁锤打死主将晋鄙，信陵君率领这支军队救援邯郸。楚国的春申君也带兵赶来。
