<Lang en>

Ying Zheng, Yíng Zhèng 嬴政, became king of Qin in 247 BCE at the age of thirteen and took power himself in 238 BCE. Qin now held the Wei valley, the Sichuan basin and the old Chu heartland. A canal designed by Zheng Guo, an engineer sent by Han, irrigated the plain north of the Wei from 246 BCE onwards. Qin's advance was not smooth: a last coalition reached its borders in 241 BCE, and in 233 BCE the Zhao general Li Mu, Lǐ Mù 李牧, routed a Qin army at Fei.

The conquest itself took nine years. Qin took Han in 230 BCE. In 229 BCE the king of Zhao, believing rumours that the Shiji says Qin had paid for, executed Li Mu; the next year Handan fell. In 227 BCE Jing Ke, Jīng Kē 荆轲, sent by the crown prince of Yan, tried to stab the Qin king and was killed. In 225 BCE Wang Ben flooded Daliang and Wei surrendered.

Chu nearly stopped the advance. In about 225 BCE a Qin army of <Num s="S152">200,000</Num> under Li Xin, Lǐ Xìn 李信, was chased for three days and nights by the Chu general Xiang Yan, Xiàng Yān 项燕, and lost seven commanders. Wang Jian, Wáng Jiǎn 王翦, then led <Num s="S152">600,000</Num> men against Chu; by 223 BCE its king was a prisoner and Xiang Yan was dead.

In 222 BCE Qin captured the last kings of Yan, in Liaodong, and of Zhao, in Dai, and took the lands of the Yue lords south of the Yangtze. In 221 BCE Qi surrendered without a fight. For the first time, one ruler governed all the lands of the Zhou states.

<More title={{"en": "Chu: 224 or 223 BCE?", "zh": "楚亡：前224年还是前223年"}}>

The Shiji gives two orders of events. In the Chu annals and the Table of the Six States, Xiang Yan is killed at Qi in 224 BCE and King Fuchu is captured in 223 BCE. In the annals of the First Emperor, the king is captured in 224 BCE; Xiang Yan then makes Lord Changping, a Chu prince who had served Qin, king south of the Huai, and both die in 223 BCE. Li Xin's defeat is undated in the text and placed in 225 BCE because it falls between other dated events. The place where he was defeated, Chengfu, is traditionally put near present-day Bozhou in Anhui; some scholars place it in Henan instead.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Why Qin?", "zh": "为什么是秦"}}>

Historians give several answers, and most combine them. Geography: the Wei valley was easy to defend behind its passes, and Sichuan added grain. Institutions: Qin's registers and ranks could turn farmers into soldiers on a scale others struggled to match. Diplomacy: the other states rarely stayed united. Chance: able generals died, rulers trusted the wrong ministers. The political scientist Victoria Tin-bor Hui compares Warring States China with early modern Europe, where rival states also fought for centuries but none conquered the rest.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "What the conquered remembered", "zh": "被兼并者的记忆"}}>

When Qin's king entered Handan in 228 BCE, the Shiji says, he had everyone there who had wronged his mother's family during his childhood killed and buried in pits. The siege of Daliang, the townspeople told Sima Qian a century later, lasted <Num s="S212">three</Num> months before the walls fell. In Chu people said, the Shiji records, that "even if only three households are left, it will be Chu that destroys Qin". Within fifteen years of the conquest, rebels from the old Chu lands, among them Xiang Yan's grandson Xiang Yu, did.

<Lang zh>

嬴政在前247年十三岁时即位为秦王，前238年亲政。这时秦已经拥有渭水谷地、四川盆地和楚国旧都一带。韩国派来的水工郑国，从前246年起为秦修建水渠，灌溉渭水以北的平原，关中从此成为沃野。秦的推进并不顺利：前241年各国最后一次合纵，联军打到秦境；前233年，赵将李牧在肥大败秦军。

灭六国本身用了九年。前230年秦灭韩。前229年，赵王听信谣言，处死李牧，据《史记》，这些谣言是秦国花钱散布的；第二年邯郸陷落。前227年，燕太子丹派荆轲刺杀秦王，失败被杀。前225年，王贲水灌大梁，魏国投降。

楚国差一点挡住了秦军。大约在前225年，李信率<Num s="S152">20万</Num>秦军攻楚，被楚将项燕追击三天三夜，营垒被攻破，七名都尉战死。秦王只好亲自赶到王翦的家乡，请这位已经告老的将军重新出山。王翦率<Num s="S152">60万</Num>大军伐楚，到前223年，楚王被俘，项燕也死了。

前222年，秦军在辽东俘虏燕王喜，在代地俘虏赵公子嘉，又收服了长江以南的越人首领。前221年，王贲从燕地南下，齐国不战而降，齐王建被迁往共地。周代诸侯的全部土地，第一次归于一个君主统治。

<More title={{"en": "Chu: 224 or 223 BCE?", "zh": "楚亡：前224年还是前223年"}}>

《史记》对楚亡的记载有两种次序。《楚世家》和《六国年表》说，前224年项燕在蕲被杀，前223年楚王负刍被俘。《秦始皇本纪》却说，前224年秦军先俘虏楚王，项燕又在淮南拥立曾在秦做官的楚公子昌平君为王，前223年昌平君死，项燕自杀。李信兵败一事原文没有年份，因前后事件推定在前225年。他兵败的城父，传统上认为在今安徽亳州附近，也有学者认为在河南。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Why Qin?", "zh": "为什么是秦"}}>

史家给出过好几种解释，大多是几种原因合起来讲。地理：渭水谷地有关隘可守，四川又提供了粮食。制度：秦的户籍和爵位，能把大批农民变成士兵，别国很难做到。外交：其他国家很少长期团结。偶然：良将早死，君主信错了人。政治学者许田波把战国时代的中国与近代早期的欧洲相比较：欧洲各国同样连年争战，却没有一个国家吞并其余各国。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "What the conquered remembered", "zh": "被兼并者的记忆"}}>

据《史记》，前228年秦王进入邯郸，把他幼年在赵国时与他母家有仇怨的人全部坑杀。大梁城被水围困了<Num s="S212">三</Num>个月才塌，这是一百多年后大梁废墟附近的居民告诉司马迁的。楚地流传着一句话：“楚虽三户，亡秦必楚。”不到十五年，起兵推翻秦朝的，大多是楚地人，其中就有项燕的孙子项羽。

</More>
