<Lang en>

Before 770 BCE, Zhou kings ruled from the Wei valley and maintained an eastern centre near present-day Luoyang. Through <Term id="fengjian">enfeoffment</Term>, kings granted lands and people to relatives and supporters. Their descendants became <Term id="zhuhou">regional lords</Term>, owing ritual and military service to the king while governing their own communities. This arrangement differed from the institutions usually called feudalism in European history.

A state grew around walled settlements, fields and the routes connecting them. Its authority did not necessarily reach every valley between its towns. Communities whom Zhou writers called Rong, Di, Yi or Yue lived beside and within these networks. These names express the writers' classifications; they do not describe a single people with a shared government. Blank space on this map means that control is unknown or is not represented, rather than that nobody lived there.

The Yellow, Wei, Han, Huai, Ji and Yangtze rivers carried travellers, grain and armies. Their channels and surrounding coastlines have changed. The reconstructed lower Yellow River shown here ran north through present-day Hebei before 602 BCE. Today's coast is used for orientation, although the shores around Bohai, the Yangtze mouth and Lake Tai were different.

In 771 BCE, King You of Zhou, Zhōu Yōu Wáng 周幽王, was killed during an attack involving the lord of Shen and the Quanrong. The royal court's subsequent move east traditionally marks the beginning of Eastern Zhou. Continuous conventional historical chronology begins in 841 BCE; eclipse records help check later dates. Year labels throughout this topic use BCE: larger numbers refer to earlier years.

<More title={{"en": "All under Heaven", "zh": "天下"}}>

<Term id="tianxia">All under Heaven</Term>, or tianxia, named a political and moral world centred on kingship. Early uses of Zhongguo referred to central royal or regional centres, rather than a country with today's boundaries. <Term id="huaxia">Huaxia</Term> was another changing category used by writers within the Zhou political world. People could change alliances, practices and identities; the categories should not be read as fixed modern nationalities.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A river that moved", "zh": "改道的河流"}}>

The Hanshu, compiled long after these events, quotes a record of the Yellow River changing course in 602 BCE. The map's reconstructed channels come from Kanguole's published map, which cites historical geography and archaeological research. The present lower course through Shandong dates from 1855 CE and is excluded. The Ji, formerly an important independent river system, also survives here as a reconstruction. Dashed river lines signal that the ancient courses are inferred.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Who left the records?", "zh": "谁留下了记录"}}>

The Spring and Autumn Annals were kept in Lu. The Zuo Tradition explains their events through stories, speeches and judgments. Sima Qian compiled the Shiji under the Han dynasty, long after Zhou rule ended. Excavated bamboo and silk texts sometimes preserve different sequences or names. These sources illuminate elite politics more clearly than the lives of farmers, captives or neighbouring communities without surviving written histories.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

前770年以前，周王以渭水流域为统治中心，并在今洛阳附近经营东方据点。王室通过<Term id="fengjian">分封</Term>，把土地与人口授予亲属和支持者。他们的后代成为<Term id="zhuhou">诸侯</Term>，向周王承担礼仪和军事义务，同时治理自己的居民。这套制度与欧洲史中通常所说的封建制度并不相同。

诸侯国以城邑、田地和相连的道路为基础，未必能控制城邑之间的每处山谷。周人文献所称的戎、狄、夷、越，生活在聚落之间及周边。这些名称是记述者的分类，不能视作各有统一政权的固定族群。地图留白表示控制不明或没有绘出，并非无人居住。

黄河、渭水、汉水、淮水、济水和长江运送旅人、粮食与军队，河道和海岸却不断变化。图中复原的黄河下游在前602年以前北流，经今河北入海；底图采用今日海岸，渤海西岸、长江口与太湖附近当时有所不同。

前771年，周幽王在申侯与犬戎参与的进攻中被杀。此后王室东迁，传统上以此为东周之始。连续纪年通常从前841年算起，日食记录可帮助校验后来的年代。本主题使用公元前年份，数字越大，年代越早。

<More title={{"en": "All under Heaven", "zh": "天下"}}>

<Term id="tianxia">天下</Term>指围绕王权形成的政治与道德世界。早期的“中国”常指王室或诸侯的中心地区，与今日国家疆界不同。<Term id="huaxia">华夏</Term>也是周代政治世界中不断变化的分类。人群可以改变盟友、习俗和身份，不能把这些称呼直接当作固定的现代民族。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A river that moved", "zh": "改道的河流"}}>

成书远晚于这些事件的《汉书》引用黄河在前602年改道的记载。本图复原河道取自Kanguole发表的地图，其依据包括历史地理与考古研究。今日流经山东的黄河下游形成于公元1855年，本图不画这段河道。曾经独立而重要的济水也按复原结果显示。河流虚线表示推断的古河道。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Who left the records?", "zh": "谁留下了记录"}}>

《春秋》由鲁国史官记录；《左传》通过叙事、言辞和评判解释其中的事件。司马迁在汉代编写《史记》，距离周代结束已久。出土简帛有时保存了不同的人名和事件次序。这些材料对上层政治的照明较多，对农民、俘虏及没有文字传世的邻近人群则较少。

</More>
