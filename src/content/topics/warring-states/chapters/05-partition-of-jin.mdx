<Lang en>

Jin's ruling house increasingly depended on powerful hereditary ministerial families. Rivalry among them changed the state's government before its name disappeared. In 455 BCE, Zhi Bo, Zhì Bó 智伯, led Han and Wei against the Zhao family's stronghold at Jinyang. The besiegers diverted water against the city, threatening its defenders and the residents sheltering inside.

Zhao Xiangzi, Zhào Xiāngzǐ 赵襄子, persuaded Han and Wei to change sides. In 453 BCE, the coalition destroyed the Zhi family and divided its lands. The episode shows that alliances depended on expectations about the next conflict: partners feared becoming the next targets of a powerful leader. A shared enemy did not remove competition among the survivors.

In 403 BCE, the Zhou king recognised Han, Zhao and Wei as regional lords. This is the opening year of the later chronicle Zizhi tongjian. The date marks formal recognition rather than the beginning of their independent power. The remaining lands of Jin's ruler were divided in 376 BCE; accounts differ over the final ending of his lineage. In Qi, Tian Chang, Tián Cháng 田常, killed the ruler in 481 BCE. The Tian family's replacement of the old ruling house received royal recognition in 386 BCE.

The period's familiar leading states were Qin, Chu, Qi, Yan, Han, Zhao and Wei. Smaller states, including Song, Lu, Wey, Yue and Zhongshan, continued alongside them. Mozi, Mòzǐ 墨子, argued against aggressive warfare and taught techniques of defence. His writings preserve a perspective in which strengthening a ruler's army was not the only answer to insecurity: protecting communities from attack could itself be a political purpose.

<More title={{"en": "When did the Warring States begin?", "zh": "战国从何时开始"}}>

This topic uses 475 BCE for the period's beginning, after a Spring and Autumn period ending in 476 BCE. Other divisions use 481 BCE, when Lu's surviving annals end; 453 BCE, when the Zhi family was defeated; or 403 BCE, when the Zhou king recognised the new lords. These markers identify different kinds of change. None makes the previous day's society fundamentally different from the next day's.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A small state’s material world", "zh": "小国的物质世界"}}>

The tomb of Marquis Yi of Zeng, Zēng Hóu Yǐ 曾侯乙, near present-day Suizhou dates to about 433 BCE. Excavated in 1978 CE, it contained a set of <Num s="S27">65</Num> bronze bells, alongside other instruments and objects. Zeng is widely identified with the Sui of transmitted texts. Its material culture reveals courtly resources and regional connections that disappear in a story limited to the largest military powers.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Mozi and the defence of Song", "zh": "墨子与守宋"}}>

The Gongshu chapter of the Mozi describes its teacher preventing a Chu attack on Song by demonstrating defensive methods and explaining that his followers were ready to help defend it. It is an argumentative story rather than a securely dated campaign report, so this topic does not assign the episode an invented year or a moving route. The contrast between condemning offensive war and teaching defence is deliberate.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Water against a city", "zh": "以水攻城"}}>

At Jinyang, water served as a weapon against a fortified community. Later accounts make the reversal of the flood central to the attackers' defeat. The event connects this chapter with Qin's later attacks at Yan and Daliang. Conditions differ in each account, but flooding threatened inhabitants as well as military positions. No reliable count of deaths at Jinyang survives. The map for 403 BCE borrows the internal Han–Zhao–Wei divisions from a map of about 350 BCE; these are inferred, not contemporary surveyed boundaries.

</More>

<Lang zh>

晋君越来越依赖世袭的卿大夫家族。国名尚未消失，家族间的争夺已改变其治理。前455年，智伯率韩、魏进攻赵氏据点晋阳，引水灌城，威胁守军和避难居民。城墙能够阻挡军队，却不能使城内的人免于水攻与断粮。

赵襄子劝韩、魏转向。前453年，韩赵魏联手灭智氏，分其土地。原先同盟者担心自己会成为下一个目标，因此改变选择。合作依靠对将来利益和威胁的判断，共同对手消失后，幸存各家仍继续竞争。

前403年，周王正式承认韩、赵、魏为诸侯，后世《资治通鉴》也从这一年起笔。这个日期表示名分确认，并非独立权力刚刚产生。前376年，三家瓜分晋君余地，晋君世系最终结束的年代另有异说。齐也发生类似变化：前481年田常杀君，田氏取代旧王室，到前386年获得周王承认。

这一时期熟悉的大国是秦、楚、齐、燕、韩、赵、魏，宋、鲁、卫、越、中山等较小国家仍与它们并存。墨子反对攻伐，同时传授守城方法。他的著作提出另一种回应不安的方式：政治目标不仅可以是增强君主的军队，也可以是保护居民免遭进攻。

<More title={{"en": "When did the Warring States begin?", "zh": "战国从何时开始"}}>

本主题以前475年为战国起点，春秋截至前476年。其他分法取鲁国今存《春秋》结束的前481年、智氏被灭的前453年，或周王承认三家的前403年。不同日期标记不同层面的变化，没有一种意味着前后两天的社会突然完全不同。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A small state’s material world", "zh": "小国的物质世界"}}>

今随州附近的曾侯乙墓约属前433年，公元1978年发掘，出土成套青铜编钟<Num s="S27">65</Num>件，以及其他乐器和物品。学界多将曾与传世文献中的随相联系。这些遗物展示了较小王廷的资源和地区交往，只讲军事大国就容易忽略它们。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Mozi and the defence of Song", "zh": "墨子与守宋"}}>

《墨子·公输》叙述墨子演示守城方法，并说明弟子已准备助守，从而阻止楚攻宋。这是阐明主张的故事，并非定年可靠的战役报告，本主题不为它编造年份或移动路线。反对进攻战争与教授防御并存，是这篇论述有意保留的区别。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Water against a city", "zh": "以水攻城"}}>

晋阳之围中，水成为攻击城内人群的武器。后世叙事把反向放水写作围攻者失利的关键。这与秦后来对鄢、大梁的进攻可以相互参照；各次记载的条件不同，洪水却都威胁居民与军事据点。晋阳没有可靠死亡统计。前403年地图借用约前350年的韩赵魏内部界线，它们是推断，并非当时实测边界。

</More>
