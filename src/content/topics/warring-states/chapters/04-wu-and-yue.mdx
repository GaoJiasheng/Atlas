<Lang en>

Under King Helü of Wu, Hélǘ 阖闾, the lower Yangtze state became a major military power. Wu Zixu, Wǔ Zǐxū 伍子胥, had fled Chu after his father and brother were killed. The Shiji also places Sun Wu, Sūn Wǔ 孙武, at Helü's court, although his biography cannot be independently confirmed from the Zuo Tradition.

In 506 BCE, Wu defeated Chu at Boju and entered its capital, Ying. King Zhao of Chu, Chǔ Zhāo Wáng 楚昭王, fled. The Zuo Tradition records Wu commanders taking over Chu’s royal palaces. The royal household’s losses are more visible in this account than the experience of ordinary residents. Shen Baoxu, Shēn Bāoxū 申包胥, appealed to Qin; Qin intervened, and Wu withdrew in 505 BCE. A military success had exposed Wu to a distant campaign while Yue threatened its homeland.

Helü died after fighting Yue at Zuili in 496 BCE. His successor Fuchai, Fūchāi 夫差, defeated Goujian of Yue, Gōujiàn 勾践, at Fujiao in 494 BCE. Goujian sought peace and later rebuilt his state's strength. The familiar story of enduring hardship mixes an account in the Shiji with details added much later.

Wu pursued influence northwards. The Hangou canal, opened in 486 BCE, linked the Yangtze and Huai systems. Wu defeated Qi at Ailing in 484 BCE and challenged Jin's precedence at Huangchi in 482 BCE. Yue attacked while Wu's ruler was away. In 473 BCE, Yue conquered Wu and Fuchai died. Confucius, Kǒngzǐ 孔子, lived through these wars; his travels among courts reveal how advisers sought employment and argued about order in a world without a ruler able to stop the fighting.

<More title={{"en": "Occupation and vengeance at Ying", "zh": "郢的占领与复仇叙事"}}>

The Zuo Tradition describes Wu's occupation through the distribution and use of Chu's palaces. The Shiji adds the story that Wu Zixu opened the tomb of King Ping of Chu, Chǔ Píng Wáng 楚平王, and struck the corpse. The earlier text does not contain that episode. Both accounts must be identified as texts with different emphases, rather than combined into an independently confirmed eyewitness report. No reliable total for deaths during the occupation is available.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Goujian and later storytelling", "zh": "勾践与后世故事"}}>

The Shiji describes Goujian tasting gall to remind himself of humiliation. Sleeping on brushwood, the other half of the later idiom, developed in subsequent storytelling. Tales about Xi Shi and a perfectly planned sequence of revenge also require attribution. Yue's conquest is a recorded political change; every detail of the moral story attached to it is not thereby established.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Confucius and the military manuals", "zh": "孔子与兵书"}}>

Confucius travelled outside Lu, conventionally dated from 497 BCE to 484 BCE, seeking rulers who would accept his proposals. His itinerary's precise sequence is disputed. The label <Term id="hundred-schools">Hundred Schools of Thought</Term> describes later groupings of varied thinkers, not a formal association. At Yinqueshan in 1972 CE, excavators found texts of both the Sunzi and Sun Bin's military work, showing that they circulated separately. This discovery does not by itself establish every episode in Sun Wu's biography.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Canals as military infrastructure", "zh": "运河与军事运输"}}>

Hangou joined river systems around present-day Yangzhou. Transporting troops and grain by water could connect Wu's homeland with northern campaigns, but building and maintaining channels required labour. The map route to Huangchi represents the sequence of waterways described by historical accounts. Later changes to the canal system prevent it from being read as a survey of a surviving ancient channel.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

吴王阖闾时期，位于长江下游的吴成为军事强国。伍子胥因父兄被楚杀害而出逃至吴。《史记》也把孙武写为阖闾的臣下，但《左传》没有相应记载，他的生平不能据此独立证实。

前506年，吴在柏举击败楚，进入楚都郢，楚昭王出逃。《左传》记吴将分占楚王宫室，显示占领对王室的直接影响；普通居民的处境则较少见于记载。申包胥向秦求援，秦出兵后，吴于前505年撤退。远征使吴远离本土，而越正从另一方向威胁它。

前496年，阖闾在槜李与越交战后死去。继位的夫差在前494年于夫椒击败越王勾践。勾践求和，此后重建国力。后世关于忍苦复仇的熟悉故事，把《史记》中的记载与晚出的情节合在了一起，不能一概当作当时事实。

吴继续向北争取影响。前486年开邗沟，连接江淮；前484年在艾陵击败齐，前482年又在黄池与晋争会盟次序，越趁吴王外出进攻。前473年，越兼并吴，夫差死去。孔子生活于这些战争之间；他周游列国，反映游士如何寻求任用，并在无人能停止战争的局势中讨论秩序。这些经历既见于国家兴亡的记录，也成为后世反复改写的故事。

<More title={{"en": "Occupation and vengeance at Ying", "zh": "郢的占领与复仇叙事"}}>

《左传》通过楚国宫室如何被分配与使用记述吴的占领。《史记》另有伍子胥掘楚平王墓、鞭尸的故事，较早的《左传》没有这一情节。两种文本的重点不同，应分别归属，不能拼成经过独立证实的目击记录。占领期间没有可靠的死亡总数。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Goujian and later storytelling", "zh": "勾践与后世故事"}}>

《史记》写勾践尝胆以提醒自己受辱；成语中“卧薪”的另一半，是后世叙事逐渐加入的。西施以及按完整计划复仇的故事，也须注明出处。越兼并吴是有记载的政治变化，不意味着附着其上的每个道德故事细节都得到证明。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Confucius and the military manuals", "zh": "孔子与兵书"}}>

孔子离开鲁国游历的时期，传统上定为前497年至前484年，目的是寻找愿意采纳其主张的君主；具体行程次序有争议。<Term id="hundred-schools">百家争鸣</Term>是后世对不同思想传统的概括，并非正式组织。公元1972年银雀山出土《孙子兵法》和《孙膑兵法》，证明两书分别流传，不能单凭此项发现证实孙武传记的全部细节。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Canals as military infrastructure", "zh": "运河与军事运输"}}>

邗沟连接今扬州附近的水系，水运可以把吴的本土与北方战事联系起来，运兵运粮都依赖这种通道；开凿和维护河道则需要劳动。图上通往黄池的路线表示史料所述水道的先后关系。运河后来多次变化，这条线不能当作现存古河道的测绘。

</More>
