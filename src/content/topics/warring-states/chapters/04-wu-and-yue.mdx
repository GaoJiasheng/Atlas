<Lang en>

Wu, on the lower Yangtze, rose quickly under King Helü, Hélǘ 阖闾, who seized the throne in 515 BCE. His adviser Wu Zixu, Wǔ Zǐxū 伍子胥, had fled Chu after King Ping of Chu killed his father and brother. The Shiji also places the strategist Sun Wu, Sūn Wǔ 孙武, at Helü's court, but the Zuo Tradition never mentions him.

In 506 BCE Wu, with the small states of Cai and Tang, invaded Chu, won at Boju and, in the Zuo Tradition's words, "fought five battles and reached Ying". King Zhao of Chu fled. Wu's officers moved into the Chu palaces by rank. A Chu official, Shen Baoxu, went to Qin and, the story goes, wept in its court for seven days until Qin sent <Num s="S2">500</Num> chariots. In 505 BCE Wu withdrew.

Yue, to the south, struck back. At Zuili in 496 BCE Helü was wounded and died. His son Fuchai, Fūchāi 夫差, crushed Yue at Fujiao in 494 BCE, and its king, Goujian, Gōujiàn 勾践, retreated to Mount Kuaiji with <Num s="S2">5,000</Num> armoured men and made peace.

Fuchai then turned north. In 486 BCE Wu dug the Han canal from the Yangtze to the Huai. In 484 BCE Wu and Lu defeated Qi at Ailing, capturing <Num s="S2">800</Num> chariots. The same year Fuchai ordered Wu Zixu, who had warned him against Yue, to kill himself. In 482 BCE, while Fuchai argued with Jin at Huangchi over who should lead, Yue attacked his capital. In 473 BCE Yue destroyed Wu, and Fuchai killed himself. Confucius, Kǒngzǐ 孔子, who died in 479 BCE, lived through all these wars.

<More title={{"en": "What happened at Ying", "zh": "吴军在郢都做了什么"}}>

The Zuo Tradition says only that Wu's officers "lodged in the palaces according to rank". The Guliang Tradition adds that they also took the Chu ruler's and ministers' wives. The Shiji says Wu Zixu opened the tomb of King Ping of Chu and whipped the corpse <Num s="S216">300</Num> times; the earlier Zuo Tradition has no such scene. No source records how many people of Ying were killed or displaced.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Goujian: what the Shiji says", "zh": "勾践：《史记》怎么写"}}>

The Shiji says Goujian hung a gall bladder over his seat and tasted it before meals, to remember his humiliation. "Sleeping on brushwood", the other half of the famous saying, was added in much later retellings; so were most stories of the beauty Xi Shi. At Zuili in 496 BCE, the Zuo Tradition says, Yue sent three rows of condemned criminals to stand before the Wu lines and cut their own throats, a sign of how cheaply rulers held some lives.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Confucius and the military writers", "zh": "孔子与兵家"}}>

Confucius left Lu in 497 BCE and spent some thirteen years travelling between courts, looking for a ruler who would take his advice. He is the first of the thinkers later grouped as the <Term id="hundred-schools">Hundred Schools of Thought</Term>. In 1972 a tomb at Yinqueshan in Shandong yielded bamboo copies of both the Sunzi and a separate work by Sun Bin, a century later, showing that two different military books existed. Whether Sun Wu himself lived is still debated.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

位于长江下游的吴国，在阖闾手中迅速强大起来。阖闾在前515年夺得王位，他的谋臣伍子胥原是楚国人，父亲和哥哥被楚平王杀害，于是逃到吴国。《史记》说兵家孙武也在阖闾麾下，但《左传》从未提到此人。

前506年，吴联合蔡、唐两个小国攻楚，在柏举获胜，按《左传》的说法，“五战及郢”。楚昭王出逃，吴军将领按官位高低住进楚王的宫室。楚国大夫申包胥到秦国求救，相传在秦廷哭了七天七夜，秦国终于派出战车<Num s="S2">500</Num>乘。前505年，吴军撤回。楚国百姓在吴军占领期间的遭遇，史书几乎没有留下记载。

南边的越国趁机反击。前496年檇李之战，阖闾受伤而死。他的儿子夫差在前494年于夫椒大败越军，越王勾践带着<Num s="S2">5000</Num>甲兵退守会稽山，向吴求和。

夫差随即挥师北上。前486年开凿邗沟，沟通长江和淮河；前484年与鲁国在艾陵打败齐军，缴获战车<Num s="S2">800</Num>乘。同年，曾劝他提防越国的伍子胥被逼自杀。前482年，夫差在黄池与晋国争当盟主，越军乘虚攻入吴都。前473年越灭吴，夫差自杀，吴国的霸业前后不过二十多年。孔子卒于前479年，一生经历了这些战争。

<More title={{"en": "What happened at Ying", "zh": "吴军在郢都做了什么"}}>

《左传》只说吴军将领“以班处宫”，即按官位高低住进楚王宫。《穀梁传》说他们还占有了楚王和大臣的妻妾。《史记》又说伍子胥掘开楚平王的墓，鞭尸<Num s="S216">300</Num>下，较早的《左传》没有这个情节。郢都有多少百姓被杀、逃散，没有任何记载。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Goujian: what the Shiji says", "zh": "勾践：《史记》怎么写"}}>

《史记》说勾践把苦胆挂在座位上方，吃饭前先尝一尝，提醒自己不忘耻辱。成语里的另一半“卧薪”，是很久以后才加进去的；西施的故事大多也是后人所写。据《左传》，前496年檇李之战时，越国让三排死刑犯走到吴军阵前，当众自刎，可见当时的君主是如何轻贱一部分人的性命。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Confucius and the military writers", "zh": "孔子与兵家"}}>

孔子在前497年离开鲁国，此后大约十三年间往来于各国，寻找愿意采纳他主张的君主。后人所说的<Term id="hundred-schools">百家争鸣</Term>，就从他这一代开始。1972年，山东临沂银雀山汉墓出土了《孙子兵法》和晚一百多年的《孙膑兵法》的竹简，证明这是两部不同的兵书。至于孙武其人是否真实存在，学界仍有争论。

</More>

</Lang>
