<Lang en>

Larger armies required grain, taxes and recruits. Rulers sought officials who could make those resources easier to obtain and reduce dependence on hereditary families. <Term id="bianfa">Reform</Term> therefore involved both agricultural production and control over people. Its benefits for a state did not mean that every household benefited from its rules.

Marquis Wen of Wei, Wèi Wén Hóu 魏文侯, employed Li Kui, Lǐ Kuī 李悝, and other advisers. Later texts connect Li Kui with measures to increase production and with a law code whose authorship remains disputed. Wu Qi, Wú Qǐ 吴起, served Wei and then Chu. His changes in Chu challenged aristocratic privileges. When King Dao of Chu, Chǔ Dào Wáng 楚悼王, died in 381 BCE, opponents killed Wu Qi at the funeral. In Han, Shen Buhai, Shēn Bùhài 申不害, emphasised methods for supervising officials.

Duke Xiao of Qin, Qín Xiào Gōng 秦孝公, supported Shang Yang, Shāng Yāng 商鞅. The conventional chronology places major measures in 356 BCE and 350 BCE. Households were grouped for mutual responsibility; agricultural production and military achievement received rewards. <Term id="rank-by-merit">Rank by military merit</Term> made the taking of enemy heads a route to status. The system helps explain why later accounts repeatedly record large headcounts.

Qin moved its capital to Xianyang in 350 BCE and expanded administration through <Term id="junxian">counties</Term> governed by appointed officials. These changes increased the ruler's reach into local society. After Duke Xiao died in 338 BCE, Shang Yang was executed and his family killed, according to the Shiji. Many institutions associated with his reforms survived. A minister could lose political protection even when his patron's successors retained his policies.

<More title={{"en": "A measure and excavated laws", "zh": "量器与出土法律"}}>

The bronze measure associated with Shang Yang bears an inscription recording a standard of capacity. An imperial inscription was later added to it. Excavated legal manuscripts from Shuihudi reveal how later Qin officials handled matters including labour, theft and responsibility. They are evidence for working administration, not a complete transcript of Shang Yang's original legislation. Objects and manuscripts help test broad claims made in retrospective biographies.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Reward and collective responsibility", "zh": "奖赏与连带责任"}}>

<Term id="legalism">Legalism</Term> is a later label grouping thinkers who disagreed in important ways. Shang Yang's policies combined opportunities for advancement with severe penalties and compulsory reporting. Mutual responsibility could make neighbours liable for failing to report offences. The same administrative reach that allowed predictable rewards could also spread punishment beyond an individual offender. Military headcounts measure what the state rewarded, rather than providing an automatically reliable total of deaths.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Different judgments of Shang Yang", "zh": "对商鞅的不同评价"}}>

Sima Qian criticised Shang Yang's character while acknowledging the effects of his policies. Modern studies variously emphasise administrative capacity, opportunities beyond hereditary rank, coercion and the burdens of mobilisation. These interpretations examine different aspects of the same changes. The Book of Lord Shang contains material from different periods; it should not be treated as a diary written entirely by the historical minister.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "How many counties?", "zh": "究竟有多少县"}}>

The Shiji's biography of Shang Yang gives <Num s="S1">31</Num> counties; its Qin annals give <Num s="S151">41</Num>. Both passages describe administrative consolidation around the time of the move to Xianyang. The discrepancy is retained rather than silently resolved. The conventional division into reform packages in 356 BCE and 350 BCE is useful for orientation, but surviving accounts do not document every measure with equal precision.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

军队扩大，需要更多粮食、税赋与兵员。各国君主寻找能组织这些资源的官吏，并试图减少对世袭家族的依赖。<Term id="bianfa">变法</Term>既涉及农业生产，也涉及对人的管理。国家得到更多资源，不意味着每户居民都从新规定中获益。

魏文侯任用李悝等人。后世文献把提高生产的措施及一部法典与李悝相联系，但法典作者仍有争议。吴起先仕魏，后入楚，改革触及贵族特权。前381年楚悼王去世，反对者在葬礼上杀死吴起。韩的申不害则着重讨论监督官吏的方法。

秦孝公支持商鞅，传统纪年把主要改革定在前356年与前350年。户籍编组并承担相互责任，耕作与军功受到奖赏。<Term id="rank-by-merit">军功爵制</Term>把取得对方首级作为获得地位的途径，也说明为何后来的记载反复列出斩首数。奖赏制度影响了国家记录战争的方式。

前350年，秦迁都咸阳，扩大由任命官吏治理的<Term id="junxian">郡县</Term>行政体系，君主更能深入地方社会。据《史记》，前338年孝公死后，商鞅被处死，家族也遭杀害，但与其改革相联系的许多制度保留下来。臣下可能失去政治庇护，而继任者仍采用他的政策。

<More title={{"en": "A measure and excavated laws", "zh": "量器与出土法律"}}>

与商鞅有关的青铜方升刻有容量标准，后来又加刻秦帝国时期的诏文。睡虎地出土法律文书显示后来的秦吏如何处理劳役、盗窃与责任等事务，是行政运作的证据，不能当作商鞅原始法令的完整抄本。器物与简牍有助于检验后世传记的概括。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Reward and collective responsibility", "zh": "奖赏与连带责任"}}>

<Term id="legalism">法家</Term>是后世的归类，其中思想家有重要分歧。商鞅政策把晋升机会与严刑、强制告发结合起来；不报告违法行为，邻人也可能连带获罪。可使奖赏按规则兑现的行政能力，同样能把惩罚扩大到个人以外。斩首数首先反映国家奖赏什么，不能自动等同可靠的死亡统计。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Different judgments of Shang Yang", "zh": "对商鞅的不同评价"}}>

司马迁批评商鞅为人，同时承认其政策的影响。现代研究分别重视行政能力、世袭地位之外的晋升机会、强制手段与动员负担。这些解释考察同一变化的不同方面。《商君书》包含不同时期的材料，不能视作历史人物商鞅独自写成的日记。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "How many counties?", "zh": "究竟有多少县"}}>

《史记·商君列传》记<Num s="S1">31</Num>县，《秦本纪》则记<Num s="S151">41</Num>县，两处都涉及迁都咸阳前后的行政整合。本主题保留差异，不暗中替史料择一。把改革分为前356年、前350年的两批便于理解，但现存记载对各项措施的年代并非同样明确。

</More>
