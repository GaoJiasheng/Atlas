<Lang en>

Armies were growing from thousands of men to tens of thousands, and rulers needed more grain, more taxes and more soldiers than the old noble families could deliver. Their answer was <Term id="bianfa">reform</Term>: new laws on land, tax and military service, carried out by officials whom the ruler appointed and could dismiss.

Wei led the way. Marquis Wen of Wei, Wèi Wén Hóu 魏文侯, who came to power in 445 BCE, employed Li Kui, Lǐ Kuī 李悝, to raise farm output; later writers credit Li Kui with an early law code, though that is uncertain. The general Wu Qi, Wú Qǐ 吴起, took Qin's lands west of the Yellow River for Wei, then moved to Chu, where he cut the privileges of distant royal relatives. When his patron King Dao died in 381 BCE, nobles shot Wu Qi at the funeral; the new king executed more than <Num s="S165">70</Num> of their families. In Han, Shen Buhai, Shēn Bùhài 申不害, taught rulers how to check on their officials.

Qin went furthest. Duke Xiao of Qin, Qín Xiào Gōng 秦孝公, gave power to Shang Yang, Shāng Yāng 商鞅, from 356 BCE. Households were registered and grouped in fives and tens, each answerable for the others' crimes. Men won land and rank by farming or by taking heads in battle, the system of <Term id="rank-by-merit">rank by military merit</Term>. In 350 BCE Qin moved its capital to Xianyang and merged its villages into <Term id="junxian">counties</Term> under appointed magistrates.

In 338 BCE Duke Xiao died. Shang Yang's enemies accused him of rebellion; he was killed, his body was torn apart by chariots, and his whole family was put to death. His laws remained.

The reforms made each family a unit that the state could count, tax and conscript. Later historians called the result <Term id="bianhu-qimin">registered commoners</Term>. A bronze measure made under Shang Yang in 344 BCE, now in the Shanghai Museum, fixed the official volume of grain; after 221 BCE a decree of the First Emperor was engraved on it. Qin legal texts on bamboo found at Shuihudi in 1975 show officials counting households, cattle and seed, and fining clerks for mistakes.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Heads for rank", "zh": "用首级换爵位"}}>

Under rank by military merit, a soldier who brought back an opposing soldier's head could be promoted one grade and given land and servants. Qin therefore recorded heads taken, and those records lie behind the huge numbers in the Shiji: <Num s="S151">60,000</Num> heads at Shimen in 364 BCE, for example. Reports written to earn rewards had every reason to be generous, and the same law punished those who failed to denounce a neighbour's crime.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Thirty-one counties or forty-one?", "zh": "三十一县还是四十一县"}}>

The Shiji contradicts itself: its biography of Shang Yang gives Qin <Num s="S221">31</Num> counties after 350 BCE, its annals of Qin <Num s="S151">41</Num>. Sima Qian admired the results of the reforms but called Shang Yang "naturally harsh". Modern historians still disagree on how much of the Book of Lord Shang, the work under his name, he wrote himself.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

军队的规模从几千人扩大到几万人，君主需要更多的粮食、赋税和兵员，旧贵族已经提供不了。于是各国纷纷<Term id="bianfa">变法</Term>：在土地、赋税、兵役上订立新法，并交给国君任命、可以随时撤换的官吏去推行。

魏国最早动手。前445年即位的魏文侯任用李悝，鼓励农民“尽地力”，增加产量；后世说李悝编过一部《法经》，但是否属实难以确定。将军吴起为魏国夺取了秦国在黄河以西的土地，后来到楚国，削减王室远亲的特权。前381年，支持他的楚悼王去世，贵族们在葬礼上射杀了吴起；新即位的楚王又因此诛灭了<Num s="S165">70</Num>多家贵族。韩国的申不害，则专门研究君主怎样考核和驾驭官吏。

走得最远的是秦国。前356年起，秦孝公重用商鞅。全国人家登记入册，五家、十家编为一组，一家犯法，其他人家连坐。男子靠耕种或者在战场上斩获首级，就能得到田宅和爵位，这就是<Term id="rank-by-merit">军功爵</Term>制度。前350年，秦迁都咸阳，把分散的乡邑合并为<Term id="junxian">县</Term>，由国君委派县令治理。

前338年，秦孝公去世。商鞅的政敌告他谋反，商鞅被杀，尸体被车裂示众，全家也被处死。但他的新法保留了下来。

变法把每一户人家变成国家可以清点、征税和征兵的单位，后来的史家把这种状况称为<Term id="bianhu-qimin">编户齐民</Term>。前344年商鞅监制的一件铜量器，今藏上海博物馆，规定了官方的容量标准；前221年以后，器身又加刻了秦始皇的诏书。1975年湖北云梦睡虎地出土的秦律竹简显示，官吏要清点户口、耕牛和种子，记错了还要受罚。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Heads for rank", "zh": "用首级换爵位"}}>

按照军功爵制，士兵斩获对方一个首级，就可以升爵一级，并得到田地和仆役。所以秦国会记录斩首数，《史记》里那些惊人的数字就是从这里来的，例如《秦本纪》记前364年石门之战“斩首<Num s="S151">六万</Num>”。为了领赏而上报的数字，自然有夸大的动机；同一套法律，也惩罚知情不报的邻居。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Thirty-one counties or forty-one?", "zh": "三十一县还是四十一县"}}>

《史记》自相矛盾：《商君列传》说前350年后秦国有<Num s="S221">31</Num>个县，《秦本纪》却说<Num s="S151">41</Num>个县。司马迁肯定变法的成效，却说商鞅“天资刻薄”。至于以商鞅命名的《商君书》有多少出自他本人之手，今天的学者仍有不同看法。

</More>
