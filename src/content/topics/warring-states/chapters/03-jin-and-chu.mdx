<Lang en>

In 638 BCE Chu invaded Song, and Duke Xiang of Song, Sòng Xiāng Gōng 宋襄公, met it at the Hong River. According to the Zuo Tradition he refused to attack while the Chu army was crossing, saying that a gentleman does not strike an enemy before it has formed its ranks. Song was routed, and the duke died of his wound the next year.

The real contest was between Jin and Chu. Chong'er, Chóng'ěr 重耳, spent <Num s="S157">19</Num> years in exile before returning in 636 BCE as Duke Wen of Jin, Jìn Wén Gōng 晋文公. In 632 BCE Jin, Qi, Qin and Song defeated Chu at Chengpu. At Jiantu, Jin presented the king with <Num s="S2">100</Num> captured war chariots and <Num s="S2">1,000</Num> prisoners, and was named hegemon.

Qin tried to push east and failed. In 627 BCE Jin ambushed a Qin army in the Xiao mountains and captured its three commanders. Duke Mu of Qin, Qín Mù Gōng 秦穆公, turned west instead; the Shiji says he took twelve Rong states and "became hegemon of the Western Rong".

Chu kept advancing. In 606 BCE King Zhuang of Chu, Chǔ Zhuāng Wáng 楚庄王, camped on the Zhou border and asked the royal envoy how heavy the king's bronze cauldrons were, a question about who should rule. In 597 BCE Chu defeated Jin at Bi; in 575 BCE Jin defeated Chu at Yanling. Zheng, between the two, changed sides so often that people spoke of "serving Jin in the morning and Chu in the evening". In 546 BCE a Song minister, Xiang Xu, Xiàng Xū 向戌, brought the envoys of more than ten states to agree a truce. Jin and Chu would now share the tribute of the smaller states.

In <Term id="chariot-warfare">chariot warfare</Term> each vehicle carried a driver, an archer and a man with a halberd, and foot soldiers ran beside it. A large state could field several hundred chariots. They needed open, dry ground; river crossings and hills broke them up. Every chariot also needed trained horses, carpenters, bronze-smiths and farmers to feed them all, most of whom never appear in the texts.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Killed for the dead: human sacrifice", "zh": "为死者而死：人殉与人牲"}}>

Several states killed people to accompany a ruler in death or as offerings. The Shiji records that <Num s="S151">66</Num> people were made to die with Duke Wu of Qin in 678 BCE and <Num s="S151">177</Num> with Duke Mu in 621 BCE, among them three respected ministers mourned in the poem "Yellow Bird". In 641 BCE Song had the ruler of Zeng 鄫, a small state in present-day Shandong, killed as an offering at an altar; in 531 BCE Chu sacrificed the heir of Cai after conquering it. Excavated tombs, such as that of Marquis Yi of Zeng 曾 in Hubei, show the same practice elsewhere. Qin ended accompanying death by decree in 384 BCE.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Between two powers", "zh": "夹在两强之间"}}>

Zheng lay on the road from Jin to Chu. Each time it joined one side, the other invaded: in 597 BCE Chu besieged its capital for three months before the battle of Bi. The Zuo Tradition records Zheng's ministers arguing over whether to submit, and the gifts and grain they had to send to whichever power was stronger that year. The truce of 546 BCE did not end this burden; it fixed it, by making the small states pay both.

</More>

<Lang zh>

前638年，楚军进攻宋国，宋襄公在泓水迎战。据《左传》，楚军正在渡河时，宋襄公不肯出击，说君子不攻击尚未列好阵的对手。结果宋军大败，襄公受伤，第二年就死了。

真正争夺霸主地位的，是晋和楚。重耳在外流亡<Num s="S157">19</Num>年，前636年回国即位，就是晋文公。前632年，晋联合齐、秦、宋，在城濮打败楚军。战前，晋文公兑现流亡时对楚王许下的诺言，主动“退避三舍”；楚军主帅子玉战败后自杀。随后在践土，晋文公向周王献上楚国战车<Num s="S2">100</Num>乘、步兵俘虏<Num s="S2">1000</Num>人，被周王册命为诸侯之长。

秦想向东发展，没有成功。前627年，晋军在崤山伏击秦军，俘虏了三名主将。秦穆公转而向西，据《史记》，他兼并了十二个戎人国家，“遂霸西戎”。

楚继续北进。前606年，楚庄王陈兵周境，向周王的使者打听王室九鼎的大小轻重，言下之意是谁该拥有天下。前597年楚在邲打败晋，前575年晋又在鄢陵打败楚。夹在中间的郑国反复改换立场，有“朝晋暮楚”的说法。前546年，宋国大夫向戌召集十几个国家的使臣在宋都会盟，约定停战，此后小国要同时向晋、楚两国朝贡，中原的大战暂时平息下来。

<Term id="chariot-warfare">车战</Term>时，每辆车上站着驾车的御者、射箭的射手和持戈的武士，车旁还有步兵跟随奔跑。大国可以出动几百辆战车。战车需要平坦干燥的地面，遇到河流和山地就难以展开。每辆车背后，还有养马人、木匠、铜匠，以及供养他们的农民，这些人在史书里几乎都没有出现。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Killed for the dead: human sacrifice", "zh": "为死者而死：人殉与人牲"}}>

好几个国家都有为君主殉葬或以人祭祀的做法。《史记》记载，前678年秦武公下葬，从死者<Num s="S151">66</Num>人；前621年秦穆公下葬，从死者<Num s="S151">177</Num>人，其中三位贤臣，《诗经·黄鸟》为他们哀悼。前641年，宋襄公让人把鄫国国君杀死在祭坛上作为祭品；前531年，楚灭蔡后，把蔡国太子杀了祭山。湖北随州曾侯乙墓等出土墓葬里，也发现了殉葬的人。秦国到前384年才下令废止从死。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Between two powers", "zh": "夹在两强之间"}}>

郑国正处在晋楚往来的要道上。它投向一方，另一方就来攻打：前597年邲之战以前，楚军围困郑都三个月。《左传》记下了郑国大臣关于是否屈服的争论，也记下了他们每年要给强国送去的财物和粮食。前546年的弭兵之会并没有减轻这种负担，反而把它固定下来：小国从此要两边进贡。

</More>
