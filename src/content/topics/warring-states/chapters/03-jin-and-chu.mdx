<Lang en>

In 638 BCE, Chu defeated Song at the Hong River. The Zuo Tradition presents Duke Xiang of Song, Sòng Xiāng Gōng 宋襄公, refusing to attack before Chu's troops formed ranks. The account became an argument about appropriate conduct in war. It also records the consequences of a ruler's decision for the soldiers required to fight.

Chong'er, Chóng'ěr 重耳, returned from exile to become Duke Wen of Jin, Jìn Wén Gōng 晋文公, in 636 BCE. In 632 BCE, Jin defeated Chu at Chengpu and gathered rulers at Jiantu. Jin's coalition included states with their own reasons to resist Chu's influence. Its success did not create permanent borders or settle every local dispute.

Qin also tried to extend its influence east. In 627 BCE, Jin ambushed a Qin force returning from an unsuccessful attempt against Zheng. Qin's leading commanders were captured at Xiao. Duke Mu of Qin, Qín Mù Gōng 秦穆公, subsequently expanded westward among communities described as Rong. The Shiji's language of western hegemony represents Qin's view of these unequal encounters.

Chu remained a powerful rival. King Zhuang of Chu, Chǔ Zhuāng Wáng 楚庄王, questioned the royal representative about Zhou's ritual bronze cauldrons in 606 BCE, challenging the relationship between rank and power. Chu defeated Jin at Bi in 597 BCE; Jin won at Yanling in 575 BCE. Zheng and other smaller states repeatedly adjusted their loyalties. In 546 BCE, a meeting promoted by Xiang Xu, Xiàng Xū 向戌, at Song arranged shared leadership between Jin and Chu. Negotiation reduced some conflicts without ending warfare elsewhere.

<More title={{"en": "Chariots and the people around them", "zh": "战车与周围的人"}}>

<Term id="chariot-warfare">Chariot warfare</Term> relied on horses, vehicles, crews and supporting infantry. Excavated vehicle pits and bronze fittings reveal equipment that literary speeches can leave out. Terrain, river crossings and supplies constrained chariot armies. Later narratives of honourable single encounters should not hide the larger labour needed to raise animals, build equipment and feed the people accompanying them.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "An exile remembered as a ruler", "zh": "流亡者如何被记作君主"}}>

The Shiji traces Chong'er's travels through Di communities, Wey, Qi, Cao, Song, Zheng, Chu and Qin. His reception varied with each court's interests. The famous promise to withdraw before Chu and its fulfilment before Chengpu belong to that narrative. The map joins the places named in the texts; it cannot recover the travellers' exact road or the duration of every stay.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Human sacrifice in Qin", "zh": "秦的人殉"}}>

The Shiji records <Num s="S1">177</Num> people put to death to accompany Duke Mu's burial in 621 BCE. The poem “Yellow Bird” mourns the death of the men remembered as the Sanliang. Sacrificial killing was practised in several states, and victims should not disappear behind a ruler's reputation. The Qin annals record Duke Xian, Qín Xiàn Gōng 秦献公, ending this form of accompanying death in 384 BCE. That decree concerns a practice, rather than proof that every kind of coercive burial immediately ceased.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Between stronger neighbours", "zh": "处于强邻之间"}}>

Zheng lay between Jin's northern network and Chu's routes into the central plain. Accepting an alliance could bring protection while exposing it to the other power's retaliation. Court histories call these reversals disloyalty or prudence depending on the speaker. For local communities, repeated demands for supplies and armies crossing the countryside could matter more than which ruler presided at a meeting.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

前638年，楚在泓水击败宋。《左传》写宋襄公不愿在楚军尚未列阵时进攻，后人借此讨论战争中应有的行为。这段叙事也记录了君主的决定如何影响被征召作战的士卒。

前636年，重耳结束流亡，成为晋文公。前632年，晋在城濮击败楚，继而在践土会诸侯。参与联盟的小国各有抗衡楚的理由。这次结果既未建立永久边界，也未解决各地争端，诸侯仍须不断选择立场。

秦也试图向东扩大影响。前627年，一支袭郑未成的秦军回程中，在崤山遭晋伏击，主要将领被俘。此后秦穆公向西扩张，与文献所称的戎发生冲突。《史记》称其“霸西戎”，体现了秦对这些不对等关系的记述立场。

楚仍能与晋抗衡。前606年，楚庄王向周王使者问鼎的大小轻重，触及礼仪名分与实际力量的关系。前597年楚胜于邲，前575年晋胜于鄢陵。郑等小国多次调整盟属。前546年，向戌促成在宋的弭兵之会，安排晋楚共同领导；协商减少了部分冲突，却没有终止其他地区的战争。大国的会盟与交战不断改变地方负担，史书保存的君主言辞无法涵盖所有人的处境。

<More title={{"en": "Chariots and the people around them", "zh": "战车与周围的人"}}>

<Term id="chariot-warfare">车战</Term>依靠马匹、车辆、乘员及配合的步卒。出土车马坑和青铜配件展示了言辞叙事容易省略的装备。地形、渡河和补给约束着军队。后世关于礼让与单独交锋的故事，不应遮蔽养马、造车以及供养随军人员所需的劳动。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "An exile remembered as a ruler", "zh": "流亡者如何被记作君主"}}>

《史记》写重耳经过狄人的聚落以及卫、齐、曹、宋、郑、楚、秦，各处接待随王廷利益而异。向楚承诺退避、城濮战前履约的著名情节，也出自这套叙事。地图连接文献所列地点，无法复原旅人走过的确切道路或每处停留的时间。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Human sacrifice in Qin", "zh": "秦的人殉"}}>

《史记》记前621年秦穆公下葬，以<Num s="S1">177</Num>人从死。《诗经·黄鸟》哀悼其中被称为“三良”的死者。多国都实行过人殉，受害者不应因君主的声誉而被略去。《秦本纪》记秦献公在前384年“止从死”。法令针对这一习俗，不能据此断定一切强制随葬都立即消失。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Between stronger neighbours", "zh": "处于强邻之间"}}>

郑处于晋在北方的关系网络与楚通往中原的路线之间。结盟可以得到保护，也可能招来另一方报复。史书中的不同发言者把改盟称作背信或审慎。对地方居民来说，反复征粮、军队穿越田野，可能比由谁主持会盟更直接地影响生活。

</More>
