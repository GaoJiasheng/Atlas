<Lang en>

A reconstructed skeleton begins with work on evidence. Preparators remove surrounding rock, conserve fragile material and record what is present. Anatomy and comparison help researchers arrange separated parts. The skull and jaw connect with the neck and trunk; the pelvis links the hindlimbs and tail. The separated view clarifies these relationships, rather <Num s="S7,S56">than recording an excavation layout</Num>.

The Reconstruction evidence card filters an invented inventory of <Num s="S90">twelve teaching modules</Num>. Each module may contain many bones. Direct records supply <Num s="S90">seven modules</Num>, <Term id="comparison-evidence">comparative evidence</Term> supplies <Num s="S90">three</Num>, and inference supplies <Num s="S90">two</Num>. Hiding inferred and then comparison-based modules leaves visible gaps, while the underlying inventory stays unchanged. These counts are <Num s="S90">not a museum completeness calculation</Num>.

The skull represents a <Term id="cast">museum cast</Term> made from a documented original. Its display material is a replica, but its shape still has direct evidence. By contrast, the neck represents a copy based on another specimen, and the ribs and corresponding forelimb use comparative restoration. The table in the detail below keeps the basis of each shape <Num s="S55,S56,S90">separate from its display material</Num>.

This is a generic Tyrannosaurus rex teaching reconstruction. Each hand has <Num s="S9">two functional fingers</Num>, and each foot has <Num s="S48">three main weight-bearing toes</Num>; a raised smaller toe is outside that count. These constraints describe this animal's anatomy, rather than every dinosaur. A complete outline combines evidence without proving that a complete individual was discovered together. It alone cannot establish precise <Num s="S7,S90">skin colour or a call</Num>.

重建骨架从处理证据开始。工作人员清理围岩、保存易损材料，并记录已经发现的部分，再利用解剖与比较来安排分散骨件。头骨和下颌接向颈部与躯干，骨盆连接后肢和尾部。拆开视图只帮助看清结构关系，<Num s="S7,S56">不是发掘现场的出土位置图</Num>。

复原依据卡筛选一套虚构的 <Num s="S90">十二个教学模块</Num>，模块可能包含许多骨头。其中 <Num s="S90">七个</Num>有直接记录，<Num s="S90">三个</Num>来自<Term id="comparison-evidence">比较依据</Term>，<Num s="S90">两个</Num>是推定。逐步隐藏会留下缺口，库存分类却没有改变；<Num s="S90">这些数量不能换算成馆藏完整率</Num>。

头骨代表已记录原件的<Term id="cast">博物馆复制件</Term>。展示材料是复制品，形状仍有直接依据。颈部则参照其他标本复制；肋部和对应前肢属于有比较依据的补塑。细看表把形状依据与展示材料分别列出，<Num s="S55,S56,S90">避免把复制一律理解为猜测</Num>。

这是通用霸王龙教学复原，每手有 <Num s="S9">两个功能性手指</Num>，每足有 <Num s="S48">三个主要承重趾</Num>，小抬趾不计在内。这些约束不能推广到所有恐龙。完整轮廓结合了不同依据，不证明曾一起发现完整个体；<Num s="S7,S90">骨架本身也不能单独确定精确皮色或叫声</Num>。
