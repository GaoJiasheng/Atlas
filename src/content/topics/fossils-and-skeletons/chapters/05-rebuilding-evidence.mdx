<Lang en>

Rebuilding a skeleton starts with fossils. Preparators remove surrounding rock, strengthen fragile material and record what they find. Researchers compare body structures to arrange scattered parts. The skull and jaw join the neck and trunk. The pelvis connects the hindlimbs and tail. The separated view shows these connections, <Num s="S7,S56">rather than the bones' positions in an excavation</Num>.

A complete outline can combine several kinds of evidence.", zh: "霸王龙教学复原：完整轮廓可以结合多种证据。" }} />

The Reconstruction evidence card filters <Num s="S90">twelve teaching modules</Num>, or sections, in an invented skeleton. Each may contain many bones. Direct records supply <Num s="S90">seven modules</Num>. <Term id="comparison-evidence">Comparative evidence</Term> from other bones or matching body parts supplies <Num s="S90">three</Num>. Inference supplies <Num s="S90">two</Num>. Hide the inferred parts, then the comparison parts, to see the gaps. The counts stay fixed; <Num s="S90">they do not measure museum completeness</Num>.

The skull is a <Term id="cast">museum cast</Term>, a copy of a recorded original. Its material is a replica, but its shape comes from direct evidence. The neck is copied from another specimen. The ribs and matching forelimb are rebuilt using comparisons. The table below separates <Num s="S55,S56,S90">the evidence for a shape from the display material</Num>.

This teaching skeleton represents Tyrannosaurus rex. Each hand has <Num s="S9">two functional fingers</Num>. Each foot has <Num s="S48">three main weight-bearing toes</Num>; a smaller raised toe is outside that count. Other dinosaurs have different anatomy. A complete outline can combine several kinds of evidence. It does not prove that all parts were found together, or reveal <Num s="S7,S90">an exact skin colour or call</Num>.

<More title={{"en": "Cleaning and conserving", "zh": "清理与保存"}}>

Preparators clean, strengthen and record fragile fossils. Replicas can protect originals and let other museums share the evidence. One display may combine <Num s="S56">original fossils, replicas and sculpted restorations</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "What the skeleton does not tell us", "zh": "骨架没有单独告诉我们的事"}}>

重建骨架从处理化石开始。工作人员清除围岩、加固脆弱部分，并记录发现。研究人员比较身体结构，把散落的部分排列起来。头骨和下颌接上颈与躯干，骨盆连接后肢和尾。拆开视图显示这些连接，<Num s="S7,S56">不是骨头出土时的位置</Num>。

复原依据卡可以筛选虚构骨架的 <Num s="S90">十二个教学模块</Num>，也就是分区。每个模块可包含多块骨。其中 <Num s="S90">七个模块</Num>有直接记录；<Term id="comparison-evidence">比较依据</Term>来自其他骨件或对应部位，共 <Num s="S90">三个</Num>；另有 <Num s="S90">两个</Num>靠推定。先隐藏推定部分，再隐藏比较部分，就能看到空缺。各类总数不变，<Num s="S90">也不表示馆藏完整率</Num>。

头骨是<Term id="cast">博物馆复制件</Term>，复制自有记录的原件。材料虽是复制品，形状仍有直接依据。颈部复制自另一标本，肋部和对应前肢则参照其他结构补出。下表分别列出<Num s="S55,S56,S90">形状依据和展示材料</Num>。

这个教学骨架代表霸王龙（Tyrannosaurus rex）。每手有 <Num s="S9">两个功能性手指</Num>，每足有 <Num s="S48">三个主要承重趾</Num>；较小的抬起脚趾不算在内。其他恐龙的结构可以不同。完整轮廓可以结合多种证据，却不能证明全部骨头一起出土，也不能单独确定<Num s="S7,S90">准确肤色或叫声</Num>。
