<Lang en>

Locate the fossil-bearing bed between the ash layers. Suitable minerals within volcanic ash can record an event in their history through radioactive changes. A <Term id="radiometric-age">radiometric age</Term> is a numerical estimate from such a clock, with assumptions and uncertainty. It does not mean that every dinosaur bone can be dated <Num s="S5,S6,S58">directly with the same method</Num>.

In this invented example, the lower ash has a central age of <Num s="S90">150.00 Ma</Num> and the upper ash <Num s="S90">148.00 Ma</Num>. The resulting <Term id="age-bracket">age bracket</Term> runs from the younger upper limit to the older lower limit. The fossil lies within that range under the model's conditions; averaging the dates <Num s="S58,S90">would not measure its birthday</Num>.

Use the Ash age bracket card to compare the closer pair with the wider pair. The closer pair narrows the interval between central ages. Choosing smaller errors keeps the original central ages but reduces the outer envelope. The table below supplies the selected layers' central ages and set errors for every choice. All are SIM values, with no connection to <Num s="S90">the named Singapore museum fossils</Num>.

Keep the geological assumptions visible: <Num s="S58,S59,S90">upright, undisturbed layers, primary ash and a fossil that has not been reworked</Num>. The model treats the relevant crystallisation time as a constraint on deposition. Its bounded error intervals illustrate limits; they are not confidence intervals or a probability distribution. Dinosaur Earth will place discovery-site age ranges in their wider setting.

Radioactive changes in suitable minerals can date an event such as crystallisation. Interpreting that mineral age as a constraint on deposition requires the ash and its history to fit the method. The example assumes <Num s="S5,S58,S90">primary ash, without reworked fossils</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Carbon fourteen has a different reach", "zh": "碳十四的适用范围"}}>

Carbon fourteen is useful for comparatively recent organic remains, on the scale of <Num s="S6">tens of thousands of years</Num>. It is not the clock used for Mesozoic dinosaur bones. Materials and methods have different limits; this lesson <Num s="S6">gives no universal cut-off age</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "An envelope is not a confidence interval", "zh": "外包范围不是置信区间"}}>

The model defines each set error as a bounded half-width. The younger outer limit is the upper ash age minus its set error, bounded below by <Num s="S90">zero</Num>; the older outer limit is the lower ash age plus its set error. This arithmetic does not assign probabilities or claim that real dating <Num s="S90">errors have strict finite bounds</Num>.

</More>

这个虚构算例中，下灰层中心年龄为 <Num s="S90">150.00 Ma</Num>，上灰层为 <Num s="S90">148.00 Ma</Num>。它们提供<Term id="age-bracket">年龄夹定范围</Term>，从较年轻的上层界限到较古老的下层界限。在模型前提下，化石位于范围之内；<Num s="S58,S90">把中心值取平均并不等于测出它的生日</Num>。
