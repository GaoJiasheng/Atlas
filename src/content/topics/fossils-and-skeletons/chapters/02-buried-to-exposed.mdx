<Lang en>

Begin with the hard body part resting above the lower bed. Most remains never become fossils. Decay, scavenging and physical damage can remove evidence before it is preserved. Hard tissues and rapid burial can improve the opportunity for preservation, but neither guarantees a fossil. The exposed bone in the opening view represents a <Num s="S2">possibility, not a promised outcome</Num>.

As <Term id="sediment">sediment</Term> covers the bone, the middle bed surrounds it and the covering bed lies above. The transparent view helps us locate the buried object. It does not mean that real rock is transparent, or that every body covered by <Num s="S2,S90">sediment follows this particular path</Num>.

The enlarged cutaway then replaces the whole-bone view. Spaces remain within the bone wall. In <Term id="permineralisation">permineralisation</Term>, minerals deposited from groundwater fill natural pores. The added blocks represent that filling; they do not replace the whole wall. This magnification has no stated microscopic scale, and the blocks do not <Num s="S4,S90">identify a particular mineral species</Num>.

It is not a dated quarry section.", zh: "骨的示意剖面显示沉积层中的孔隙充填，不是已经测年的采石场剖面。" }} />

<Term id="replacement">Mineral replacement</Term> is a different change: original material dissolves and minerals take its place. Filling and replacement can occur together, while other fossils follow different preservation routes. Finally, removing the covering bed represents exposure through weathering and erosion, with uplift contributing to the wider process. Material above the fossil has been removed; the bone has not moved upwards through solid rock. The illustration records a possible pathway, not a clock measuring <Num s="S3,S4,S90">how long each stage took</Num>.

<More title={{"en": "Filling and replacing", "zh": "充填与置换"}}>

Pore filling adds minerals within existing spaces. Replacement substitutes minerals for dissolved original material. They describe different changes and may occur together; neither is a name for <Num s="S2,S4">every form of fossil preservation</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "One path, many missing records", "zh": "一条路径与许多空缺"}}>

Rapid burial and hard parts improve opportunities, but preservation is selective. The missing record includes remains destroyed before burial and material lost later. This diagram <Num s="S2,S90">gives no numerical success rate</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Why this is not a clock", "zh": "这不是计时动画"}}>

Chapter beats select observation states. They do not simulate groundwater flow, mineral replacement rates or elapsed geological time. The enlarged pores and mineral blocks have no physical <Num s="S90">scale or named crystal type</Num>.

</More>

<Lang zh>

先看下部沉积上方尚未被盖住的坚硬身体部分。大多数遗存不会形成化石；分解、动物取食和物理破坏都可能在保存之前消除记录。硬组织与较快埋藏能提高保存机会，却不能保证结果。开头露出的骨只表示一种可能，<Num s="S2">并不是已经注定成为化石的物件</Num>。

<Term id="sediment">沉积物</Term>盖住骨后，中层围着它，上方再有覆盖层。透视视图帮助我们定位埋藏的身体部分，不表示真实岩石透明，也不表示所有被埋的遗存都会经历同样的保存过程。<Num s="S2,S90">这里各阶段只是供观察的示意状态</Num>。

随后放大的剖块代替整骨视图，显示骨壁内原有的空隙。<Term id="permineralisation">矿物充填作用</Term>是地下水中的矿物沉积到这些孔隙里。加入的小块代表填充物，骨壁并未整块被换掉。这个放大件没有真实显微比例，<Num s="S4,S90">小块也不指定是哪种矿物</Num>。

It is not a dated quarry section.", zh: "骨的示意剖面显示沉积层中的孔隙充填，不是已经测年的采石场剖面。" }} />

<Term id="replacement">矿物置换作用</Term>则是原来物质溶解后，由矿物替代；充填与置换可以同时发生。并非所有化石都经过石化。最后移开覆盖层，表示抬升、风化和侵蚀使化石出露的过程。削去的是上方物质，不是骨穿过岩石往上移动；<Num s="S3,S4,S90">这条可能路径也没有给各阶段计时</Num>。

<More title={{"en": "Filling and replacing", "zh": "充填与置换"}}>

充填是在原有孔隙中加入矿物；置换是原来物质溶解后由矿物替代。这些是不同变化，也可以并存，<Num s="S2,S4">不能用其中任何一项概括所有化石保存方式</Num>。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "One path, many missing records", "zh": "一条路径与许多空缺"}}>

快速埋藏与坚硬部分有利于保存，但记录仍会缺失：有些遗存在埋藏之前被破坏，有些物质后来才流失。<Num s="S2,S90">本图不提供保存成功率</Num>。

</More>
