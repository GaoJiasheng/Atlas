<Lang en>

Start with the hard body part above the lower bed. Most remains never become fossils. Decay, scavenging animals and physical damage can destroy them. Hard tissues and rapid burial improve the chance of preservation, but <Num s="S2">do not guarantee a fossil</Num>.

<Term id="sediment">Sediment</Term> is material deposited in layers. As it covers the bone, the middle bed surrounds it and another bed covers it. The transparent view lets us see <Num s="S2,S90">the buried bone inside the rock</Num>.

The enlarged cutaway shows spaces within the bone wall. During <Term id="permineralisation">permineralisation</Term>, minerals from groundwater fill these natural pores. The small blocks show the filling; the surrounding wall remains. The pores are enlarged without a microscopic scale, and the blocks stand for <Num s="S4,S90">minerals in general</Num>.

<Figure src="figures/fossils-and-skeletons-fossilisation" alt={{ en: "A bone in the middle of three sediment layers, with a cutaway showing pores and mineral filling.", zh: "一根骨位于连续三层沉积的中层，剖面露出孔隙和填入的矿物。" }} caption={{ en: "Minerals fill spaces in a bone buried within sediment. Schematic view.", zh: "示意图：矿物填入埋藏骨的孔隙。" }} />

<Term id="replacement">Mineral replacement</Term> happens when original material dissolves and minerals take its place. Filling and replacement can happen together. Other fossils are preserved in other ways. Later, uplift, weathering and erosion can expose a fossil. The last view removes the covering rock to show this: the bone stays in place. The views show a possible sequence, <Num s="S3,S4,S90">without timing each stage</Num>.

<More title={{"en": "Filling and replacing", "zh": "充填与置换"}}>

Filling adds minerals to existing spaces. Replacement puts minerals in place of dissolved material. Both can happen in one fossil. Other fossils <Num s="S2,S4">form through different processes</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "One path, many missing records", "zh": "一条路径与许多空缺"}}>

The fossil record has gaps. Some remains are destroyed before burial; others are lost later. Hard parts and rapid burial help, but the diagram gives <Num s="S2,S90">no numerical chance of success</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Comparing stages", "zh": "比较不同阶段"}}>

Use the beats to compare stages. They do not calculate groundwater flow, replacement rates or elapsed time. The enlarged pores show general shapes, <Num s="S90">without a physical scale or named crystal type</Num>.

</More>

<Lang zh>

先看下层上方还没有被盖住的坚硬身体部分。大多数遗存不会形成化石。分解、动物取食和物理破坏都可能使它们消失。硬组织和快速埋藏能增加保存机会，<Num s="S2">却不能保证形成化石</Num>。

<Term id="sediment">沉积物</Term>是逐层沉积下来的物质。它盖住骨后，中层围着骨，上面再形成覆盖层。透视画面让我们看见<Num s="S2,S90">岩层里的埋藏骨</Num>。

放大的剖面显示骨壁内原有的空隙。<Term id="permineralisation">矿物充填作用</Term>是地下水中的矿物沉积到这些孔隙里。小块表示填入的矿物，周围骨壁仍然保留。孔隙没有按显微比例绘制，小块也只代表<Num s="S4,S90">一般的矿物</Num>。

<Figure src="figures/fossils-and-skeletons-fossilisation" alt={{ en: "A bone in the middle of three sediment layers, with a cutaway showing pores and mineral filling.", zh: "一根骨位于连续三层沉积的中层，剖面露出孔隙和填入的矿物。" }} caption={{ en: "Minerals fill spaces in a bone buried within sediment. Schematic view.", zh: "示意图：矿物填入埋藏骨的孔隙。" }} />

<Term id="replacement">矿物置换作用</Term>是原来物质溶解后，由矿物取代。充填与置换可以同时发生，其他化石也能通过不同方式保存。后来，抬升、风化和侵蚀可使化石露出来。最后一幅画面移走覆盖岩层，骨仍留在原处。这些画面表示一种可能的过程，<Num s="S3,S4,S90">没有给每个阶段计时</Num>。

<More title={{"en": "Filling and replacing", "zh": "充填与置换"}}>

充填是在原有空隙中加入矿物。置换是用矿物取代已溶解的物质。两者可以发生在同一化石中。其他化石也有<Num s="S2,S4">不同的保存方式</Num>。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "One path, many missing records", "zh": "一条路径与许多空缺"}}>

化石记录存在空缺。有的遗存在埋藏前被破坏，有的后来才流失。坚硬部分和快速埋藏有帮助，但图中<Num s="S2,S90">没有给出保存成功率</Num>。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Comparing stages", "zh": "比较不同阶段"}}>

节拍用来比较不同阶段，不计算地下水流量、置换速率或经过的时间。放大孔隙展示一般形状，<Num s="S90">没有实长比例，也不指定晶体种类</Num>。

</More>
