<Lang en>

A <Term id="fossil">fossil</Term> is evidence of past life preserved in a geological setting. Look at the bone, the hollow footprint and the leaf shape. They record different things: a body part, an activity and the form of a plant part. The objects on this stage are teaching models, rather than specimens <Num s="S2,S54,S90">collected from a particular site</Num>.

A complete-looking skeleton is a <Term id="reconstruction">reconstruction</Term>: a model assembled from preserved evidence and stated comparisons or inferences. Its outline can contain gaps filled using other evidence. Here the colours separate direct records, comparison and inference in an invented example. They describe the basis for a shape, not the material from which <Num s="S7,S55,S90">a display piece is made</Num>.

The layer study separates order from measured age. A position in the rock sequence can show relative order under checked conditions; suitable minerals can add numerical limits. Ages marked SIM are invented examples. Ma means millions of years before the present, with larger values referring to older times. The chapter axis changes our view; it does not make geological time <Num s="S5,S58,S59,S90">run at a realistic speed</Num>.

Living birds and museum displays give different connections with Singapore. <Num s="S8,S23">Birds belong within the dinosaur branch</Num>, while the museum's named skeletons were <Num s="S17,S57">discovered elsewhere</Num>. Keep asking what is preserved, what was measured and what has been reconstructed. The companion topic Dinosaur Earth will place these studies in a wider geological history.

A teaching reconstruction can combine preserved records and stated additions. In this invented example, modules are not bones and are not a museum specimen inventory. Neither the complete outline nor the grey material establishes <Num s="S7,S55,S90">what a particular excavation recovered</Num>.

<Lang zh>

<Term id="fossil">化石</Term>是在地质环境中保存的过去生命证据。先看骨、凹下的足迹和叶形：它们分别记录身体部分、活动和植物身体的形状。舞台上的物件都是教学模型，不是从某个地点采集的具体标本。观察时，先辨认保存了什么，<Num s="S2,S54,S90">再判断这些记录能回答哪些问题</Num>。

看似完整的骨架是一种<Term id="reconstruction">复原</Term>，根据保存的证据，加上说明过的比较或推定来重建。完整轮廓背后可能仍有缺失。本课用不同颜色区分直接记录、比较依据和推定；颜色表示形状的依据，<Num s="S7,S55,S90">并不表示展品本身是用什么材料做的</Num>。

地层研究把先后与年龄分开。核对条件后，岩层位置能约束顺序；合适矿物的测年结果可以再给出数值界限。标有 SIM 的年代属于虚构算例。Ma 表示百万年前，数值越大越古老。章轴只改变观察，<Num s="S5,S58,S59,S90">不让地质时间在屏幕上按真实速度流逝</Num>。

新加坡的活鸟与展馆提供不同入口。<Num s="S8,S23">鸟属于恐龙分支</Num>；馆内具名骨架却<Num s="S17,S57">发现于别处</Num>。阅读时持续分清保存、测量和复原。姊妹主题《恐龙时代的地球》将把这些研究放进更广的地球历史中。
