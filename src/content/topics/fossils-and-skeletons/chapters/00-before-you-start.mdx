<Lang en>

A <Term id="fossil">fossil</Term> is evidence of past life preserved in rocks or sediment. A bone records part of an animal's body. A footprint records an activity. A leaf imprint records the shape of a plant part. These objects are <Num s="S2,S54,S90">teaching models</Num>.

A complete-looking skeleton may include missing parts rebuilt from other evidence. This is a <Term id="reconstruction">reconstruction</Term>. Here, colours show which shapes come from direct records, comparisons or inference: using evidence to work out a missing part. The colours show <Num s="S7,S55,S90">the evidence behind each shape</Num>, rather than its display material.

Rock layers can show which deposits came first. Measurements from suitable minerals can add an age range. SIM marks invented ages used for practice. Ma means millions of years before the present; larger values mean older ages. Use the chapter axis to change views, <Num s="S5,S58,S59,S90">rather than to measure passing time</Num>.

There are connections to Singapore too. <Num s="S8,S23">Living birds belong to the dinosaur branch</Num>. The named skeletons in the museum were <Num s="S17,S57">discovered elsewhere</Num>. As you explore, look for what was preserved, what was measured and what was rebuilt. Dinosaur Earth will connect these examples with Earth's wider history.

<More title={{"en": "Evidence and a model", "zh": "证据与模型"}}>

A reconstruction combines preserved evidence with parts added through comparison or inference. This invented skeleton is divided into modules, or sections. A module can include several bones. Its complete outline does not show <Num s="S7,S55,S90">how much was found in one excavation</Num>.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Millions of years", "zh": "百万年"}}>

Ma helps us describe very old events. The <Num s="S90">invented ages here</Num> help us compare ranges. They do not give dates for the museum skeletons. Dinosaur Earth will explain the wider geological setting.

</More>

<Lang zh>

<Term id="fossil">化石</Term>是保存在岩石或沉积物中的过去生命证据。骨记录动物身体的一部分，足迹记录活动，叶印痕记录植物身体的形状。这里看到的都是<Num s="S2,S54,S90">教学模型</Num>。

看似完整的骨架，可能包含根据其他证据补出的部分。这样重建形体，叫作<Term id="reconstruction">复原</Term>。模型用颜色区分直接记录、比较依据和推定。推定就是根据证据推想缺失部分。颜色表示<Num s="S7,S55,S90">形状的依据</Num>，并不表示展示材料。

岩层能帮助判断沉积的先后。测量合适的矿物，还能得到年龄范围。SIM 表示供练习的虚构年龄。Ma 表示百万年前，数字越大，年代越古老。章轴用来切换观察画面，<Num s="S5,S58,S59,S90">不表示时间按真实速度流逝</Num>。

新加坡也有与恐龙有关的例子。<Num s="S8,S23">活鸟属于恐龙分支</Num>，博物馆里的具名骨架则<Num s="S17,S57">发现于别处</Num>。观察时，分清哪些是保存的遗存、哪些经过测量、哪些经过复原。《恐龙时代的地球》会把这些例子放进更广的地球历史中。

<More title={{"en": "Evidence and a model", "zh": "证据与模型"}}>

复原结合保存下来的证据，以及根据比较或推定补出的部分。这个虚构骨架分成若干模块，也就是分区；一个模块可以包含多块骨。完整轮廓不能告诉我们<Num s="S7,S55,S90">一次发掘找到了多少部分</Num>。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Millions of years", "zh": "百万年"}}>

Ma 帮助我们描述遥远的过去。这里的<Num s="S90">虚构年龄</Num>用来比较范围，不用来给馆内骨架定年。《恐龙时代的地球》会进一步介绍地质背景。

</More>
