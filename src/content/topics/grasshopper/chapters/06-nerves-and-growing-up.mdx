<Lang en>

The nervous system is drawn in pale yellow. The brain sits in the head above the gullet, joined by a ring of nerves to a second centre below it. From there a paired nerve cord runs back along the belly, linking a chain of knots of nerve cells, each a <Term id="ganglion">ganglion</Term>. Each one largely controls its own part of the body: the thoracic ganglia work the legs and wings. Because so much control is local, an insect whose head has been removed can still walk for a time. <FlyTo preset="systems">See the nervous system</FlyTo>

Behind the hind legs, on each side of the first segment of the abdomen, is a <Term id="tympanum">tympanum</Term>, a thin eardrum. In locusts about <Num s="S30">80</Num> sense cells attached to it turn its vibrations into nerve signals.

The female's two ovaries lie above the gut. When she lays, the <Term id="ovipositor">ovipositor</Term> at the tip of her abdomen pushes the soil apart and the abdomen stretches down into the hole. The eggs are laid in a pod sealed with hardened froth; in *Valanga* a pod holds about <Num s="S5">70–100</Num> eggs.

A grasshopper hatches as a nymph, like a small adult without wings. It cannot grow inside its skeleton, so it moults, and each stage between moults is an <Term id="instar">instar</Term>. In the laboratory *Valanga* males pass through <Num s="S3">six</Num> instars and females <Num s="S3">seven</Num>; the nymph stages last about <Num s="S5">80 days</Num>. Wing pads grow at each moult, and at the last one the adult climbs out with full wings. There is no pupa: egg, nymph and adult make an <Term id="incomplete-metamorphosis">incomplete metamorphosis</Term>.

<More title={{ en: "Three stages or four", zh: "三个阶段还是四个" }}>

Grasshoppers, cockroaches and dragonflies grow in three stages. The young look broadly like their parents and change a little at each moult. Butterflies, beetles, flies and mosquitoes have four: egg, larva, pupa and adult. Their larva looks and lives quite differently from the adult, and the body is rebuilt inside the pupa. Singapore pupils compare these life cycles, including the grasshopper's and the butterfly's, in Primary 3.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The egg pod", zh: "卵荚" }}>

The female pushes her abdomen into moist soil, lays a batch of eggs at the bottom and covers them with froth that hardens into a spongy plug, with air spaces for the eggs and the hatching young. In Java a female's first pod holds about <Num s="S4">90</Num> eggs and later ones fewer, the last only about <Num s="S4">10</Num>, in holes <Num s="S4">5–8 cm</Num> deep. In the laboratory females laid an average of <Num s="S3">158</Num> eggs each.

How long the eggs take depends on the place. In a handbook of crop pests in tropical Asia the egg stage is given as about <Num s="S5">two months</Num>; in Java the eggs rest through the dry season, so there is one generation a year. From egg to egg, the whole cycle took <Num s="S3">6½–8½ months</Num> in the laboratory at about <Num s="S3">28 °C</Num>. Adults in Java live for some months: females about <Num s="S4">3–4 months</Num>, males about <Num s="S4">4–5 months</Num>. Singapore has no long dry season; no study of its pattern here was found.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Nymph and adult", zh: "若虫和成虫" }}>

Each moult changes more than size. The antennae gain rings, from <Num s="S10">13</Num> to <Num s="S10">25</Num> in one grasshopper studied. Each compound eye gains facets, from about <Num s="S11">2,500</Num> to <Num s="S11">9,000–9,500</Num> in the desert locust, and new Malpighian tubules are added at every stage. The wing pads grow from the back plates of the thorax and, at the third-last moult, twist up onto the back. The springs in the knees are shed with the old skin and laid down again.

*Valanga* nymphs are usually green and brown, sometimes pink, red or yellow. In the life-cycle tray the stages are drawn at the same enlargement as the adult, but their sizes are schematic, because no published measurements of each stage were found.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What eats grasshoppers", zh: "谁吃蚱蜢" }}>

Many birds, spiders and some wasps catch grasshoppers. Flesh flies and tachinid flies lay their young on or in them; on the Canadian prairies they can parasitise up to <Num s="S38">60 %</Num> of nymphs and adults; fungi can infect them too. The egg pods are attacked as well: in Java by ants and by the larvae of blister beetles, and in both Asia and North America by tiny *Scelio* wasps, which lay their own eggs inside grasshopper eggs.

</More>

神经系统画成淡黄色。脑在头里、食道的上方，通过一圈神经和下方的第二个神经中心相连。从那里，一对神经索沿着腹面向后延伸，串起一个个由神经细胞聚成的小结，叫<Term id="ganglion">神经节</Term>。每个神经节在很大程度上控制它自己那一段身体：胸部的神经节控制足和翅。因为控制大多在局部完成，昆虫失去头以后，还能走动一段时间。<FlyTo preset="systems">看看神经系统</FlyTo>

在后足后面、腹部第一节的两侧，各有一片薄薄的<Term id="tympanum">鼓膜</Term>。蝗虫的鼓膜内侧连着约 <Num s="S30">80</Num> 个感觉细胞，把鼓膜的振动变成神经信号。

雌虫的两个卵巢位于消化道上方。产卵时，腹末的<Term id="ovipositor">产卵器</Term>把泥土撑开，腹部伸长钻进洞里。卵产在一个用变硬的泡沫封口的卵荚里；黑角瓦兰蝗每个卵荚约有 <Num s="S5">70–100</Num> 粒卵。

蚱蜢孵出时是若虫，像一只没有翅的小成虫。它不能在外骨骼里长大，只能蜕皮；两次蜕皮之间的阶段叫一个<Term id="instar">龄</Term>。在实验室里，黑角瓦兰蝗雄虫经历 <Num s="S3">六</Num>龄，雌虫 <Num s="S3">七</Num>龄；若虫期总共约 <Num s="S5">80 天</Num>。翅芽每蜕一次皮就长大一些，最后一次蜕皮时，成虫带着完整的翅爬出来。它没有蛹这个阶段：卵、若虫、成虫，这叫<Term id="incomplete-metamorphosis">不完全变态</Term>。

<More title={{ en: "Three stages or four", zh: "三个阶段还是四个" }}>

蚱蜢、蟑螂和蜻蜓的一生有三个阶段，幼体大体上像父母，每蜕一次皮就变化一点。蝴蝶、甲虫、苍蝇和蚊子则有四个阶段：卵、幼虫、蛹、成虫。它们的幼虫在样子和生活方式上都和成虫很不一样，身体要在蛹里重新改造。新加坡小学三年级的科学课会比较这些动物的生活史，其中就有蚱蜢和蝴蝶。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The egg pod", zh: "卵荚" }}>

雌虫把腹部插进湿土里，在洞底产下一批卵，再盖上泡沫；泡沫变硬后成了海绵状的塞子，给卵和孵出的若虫留下空气。在爪哇，雌虫的第一个卵荚约有 <Num s="S4">90</Num> 粒卵，后面的越来越少，最后一个只有约 <Num s="S4">10</Num> 粒，洞深 <Num s="S4">5–8 厘米</Num>。在实验室里，雌虫平均每只产卵 <Num s="S3">158</Num> 粒。

卵要多久才孵化，因地而异。一本热带亚洲作物害虫手册给出的卵期约 <Num s="S5">两个月</Num>；在爪哇，卵在旱季里休眠，所以一年只有一代。在实验室约 <Num s="S3">28℃</Num> 的条件下，从卵到下一代产卵，整个生活史用了 <Num s="S3">6½–8½ 个月</Num>。在爪哇，成虫能活几个月：雌虫约 <Num s="S4">3–4 个月</Num>，雄虫约 <Num s="S4">4–5 个月</Num>。新加坡没有漫长的旱季；本主题没有找到关于这里情况的研究。

<More title={{ en: "Nymph and adult", zh: "若虫和成虫" }}>

每次蜕皮，变化的不只是大小。触角增加环节，在研究过的一种蝗虫里从 <Num s="S10">13</Num> 节增加到 <Num s="S10">25</Num> 节。每只复眼增加小眼，沙漠蝗从约 <Num s="S11">2,500</Num> 个增加到 <Num s="S11">9,000–9,500</Num> 个；每一龄还会长出新的马氏管。翅芽从胸部的背板长出来，在倒数第三次蜕皮时扭转、翻到背上。膝部的“弹簧”随旧皮一起脱掉，再重新沉积。

黑角瓦兰蝗的若虫通常是绿色和褐色，有时是粉红、红或黄色。标本盘里各龄与成虫按同样的倍数放大，但大小是示意，因为没有找到各龄的公开测量。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What eats grasshoppers", zh: "谁吃蚱蜢" }}>

许多鸟、蜘蛛和一些胡蜂会捕食蚱蜢。麻蝇和寄蝇把后代产在蚱蜢身上或体内；在加拿大草原，它们可寄生多达 <Num s="S38">60 %</Num> 的若虫和成虫；真菌也会感染它们。卵荚同样会受到攻击：在爪哇有蚂蚁和芫菁的幼虫；在亚洲和北美都有细小的黑卵蜂，它们把自己的卵产在蚱蜢的卵里。

</More>
