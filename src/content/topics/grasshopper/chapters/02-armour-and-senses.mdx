<Lang en>

The exoskeleton is armour, but it is also the frame that carries the grasshopper's senses and tools. Its plates are made of cuticle, a tough material that cannot stretch, so a young grasshopper grows by shedding it: in a <Term id="moult">moult</Term> it climbs out of the old cuticle in a new, soft and larger one, which then hardens.

On the head are two large <Term id="compound-eye">compound eyes</Term>. In the desert locust, a close relative, each has about <Num s="S11">9,000–9,500</Num> facets, every one a tiny eye with its own lens. Between them are <Num s="S7">three</Num> small simple eyes, the <Term id="ocellus">ocelli</Term>, which sense changes in brightness. The antennae, with about <Num s="S10">25</Num> rings in adult grasshoppers, are covered with tiny sense organs for touch and smell. <FlyTo preset="head">Look at the head</FlyTo>

The mouthparts are four pieces that work together. The labrum closes the mouth from the front. A pair of hard <Term id="mandible">mandibles</Term> swing sideways to cut and grind leaves. Behind them the maxillae hold the food and push it in, while their palps tap it to taste it first. The labium closes the mouth from behind.

Each leg has five main parts. The third part, the <Term id="femur">femur</Term>, and the fourth, the tibia, work like a thigh and a shin, and the last, the tarsus, ends in a pair of claws; in the hind legs the femur is thick with jumping muscle. The narrow, leathery forewings, the tegmina, cover the thin hind wings. When the grasshopper flies, the hind wings open like a fan and show their red base. <FlyTo preset="dorsal">See the wings from above</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Why insects are not bigger", zh: "昆虫为什么长不大" }}>

The exoskeleton is often blamed for keeping insects small, but the evidence is mixed. One idea is that a bigger shell would have to be thicker and heavier, yet in living insects the skeleton's share of body weight stays about the same as size increases, which does not support it. A second is that a large animal would sag while its new skeleton is still soft after a moult. A third, with the most evidence behind it, is that air tubes become too long and take up too much space in a large body: in beetles the tubes grow faster than the body itself. None of these has been settled.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "How compound eyes see", zh: "复眼怎样看东西" }}>

Each facet of a compound eye points in a slightly different direction and collects light from one small patch of the surroundings. Side by side, the patches make a picture like a mosaic of tiles. The picture is coarse compared with ours, but every facet reacts on its own, so a movement that crosses from one facet to the next is noticed quickly.

The eye grows with the insect. A newly hatched desert-locust nymph has about <Num s="S11">2,500</Num> facets in each eye; new ones are added at every moult until the adult has <Num s="S11">9,000–9,500</Num>. No count has been published for *Valanga*.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Mouthparts as tools", zh: "口器像一套工具" }}>

| Part | Like | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Labrum | a lid | closes the mouth in front; carries a few sense organs |
| Mandibles | shears and a mill | toothed tips cut, the base grinds; they close sideways |
| Maxillae | hands and a tongue | hold and push food; the palps taste it |
| Labium | a lid | closes the mouth from behind; carries short palps |

Before biting, a locust that touches possible food quickly taps it with its palps, which carry taste receptors; whether it then bites depends on what they detect. Our teeth sit in bone and move up and down; a grasshopper's jaws are hardened cuticle and close from the sides.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Folding the wings", zh: "翅怎样折起" }}>

The hind wings are wider than the forewings. To fit under them they fold lengthwise into pleats, like a paper fan, pulled back by a small muscle at the wing base. The leathery forewings then lie on top like a roof and protect them.

In *Valanga nigricornis* the forewings are yellowish brown with a few faint dark spots, and the hind wings dark brown with a red base. The shins of the hind legs carry two rows of spines, <Num s="S1">10</Num> on the inner side and <Num s="S1">8</Num> on the outer.

外骨骼是盔甲，也是一副“框架”，蚱蜢的感觉器官和各种“工具”都装在上面。外骨骼的硬板由表皮构成，表皮坚韧却不能撑大，所以小蚱蜢要靠换皮长大：<Term id="moult">蜕皮</Term>时，它从旧表皮里爬出来，身上已经是一层新的、柔软而更大的表皮，过一段时间才变硬。

头上有两只大大的<Term id="compound-eye">复眼</Term>。在近亲沙漠蝗身上，每只复眼约有 <Num s="S11">9,000–9,500</Num> 个小眼，每个小眼都是一只有自己晶体的小眼睛。两只复眼之间有 <Num s="S7">三</Num>只小小的<Term id="ocellus">单眼</Term>，感受明暗的变化。蝗虫成虫的触角约有 <Num s="S10">25</Num> 节，上面布满感受触碰和气味的小感觉器。<FlyTo preset="head">看看头部</FlyTo>

口器由四个部分配合工作。上唇从前面封住口。一对坚硬的<Term id="mandible">上颚</Term>左右开合，切断、磨碎叶片。上颚后面的下颚抓住食物往里送，下颚须先点触食物、尝尝味道。下唇从后面封住口。

每条足分五大节。第三节<Term id="femur">股节</Term>和第四节胫节，相当于大腿和小腿，最后的跗节末端有一对爪；后足的股节里塞满了跳跃用的肌肉，所以特别粗。狭长、革质的前翅叫覆翅，盖住薄薄的后翅。飞行时，后翅像扇子一样展开，露出红色的基部。<FlyTo preset="dorsal">从背面看翅</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Why insects are not bigger", zh: "昆虫为什么长不大" }}>

人们常说是外骨骼让昆虫长不大，但证据并不一致。一种说法是：体型越大，外壳就得越厚、越重；可是现存昆虫的骨骼在体重中所占的比例并不随体型增大，这个说法得不到支持。另一种说法是：刚蜕皮时新骨骼还软，大动物会被自己的重量压塌。第三种说法证据最多：体型大了，气管就太长、占的空间太多；甲虫的气管比身体长得更快。这些说法都还没有定论。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "How compound eyes see", zh: "复眼怎样看东西" }}>

复眼的每个小眼朝向略有不同，各自接收周围一小块地方的光。这些小块拼在一起，就成了一幅马赛克似的图像。和我们看到的相比，这幅图像很粗糙；但每个小眼各自反应，东西从一个小眼的视野移到下一个，马上就会被察觉。

复眼随着身体长大。刚孵出的沙漠蝗若虫每只复眼约有 <Num s="S11">2,500</Num> 个小眼，以后每蜕一次皮就增加，到成虫有 <Num s="S11">9,000–9,500</Num> 个。黑角瓦兰蝗没有公开的计数。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Folding the wings", zh: "翅怎样折起" }}>

后翅比前翅宽。为了收进前翅下面，它像纸扇一样纵向折成一道道褶，由翅基的一块小肌肉拉向后方。革质的前翅再像屋顶一样盖在上面，把它护住。

黑角瓦兰蝗的前翅黄褐色，有少数不明显的暗斑；后翅暗褐色，基部红色。后足的胫节上有两排刺，内侧 <Num s="S1">10</Num> 根，外侧 <Num s="S1">8</Num> 根。
