### Grids

- Wind: 12 frames × 399 nodes (21 × 19), `lon0 = 90`, `lat0 = −15`, step 2.5°, nodes span [90, −15, 140, 30]; 850 hPa; m/s; index `j·21 + i`, `j` south to north.
- Rain: 12 frames × 360 cells (20 × 18), centres from `lon0 = 91.25`, `lat0 = −13.75`, step 2.5°, footprints span [90, −15, 140, 30]; mm/day; index `j·20 + i`.
- The two grids are offset by half a cell and are sampled independently (wind: nearest node; rain: the cell whose footprint contains the point).
- Valid-year count is 30 for every node, cell and month in both files.

### Monthly frames M01–M12

M01…M12 are `frames[0…11]`, `month = 1…12`, in both files. Every frame has the same provenance: raw inputs `wind-u-subset.ascii` (`dee58174…6e38`), `wind-v-subset.ascii` (`c67a52d4…6f8c`) and `rain-subset.ascii` (`be7b59d1…cd9c`); period 1991–2020; native 2.5° spacing; south-to-north row order; method "native grid, no spatial resampling"; licence PD-derived; residual "not a georeference; no measurement precision added". Months M05 and M11 have no beat but are reachable from the month control (chapter 08 asks readers to look at them).

### Excluded

`rain-subset-rejected-wrong-lat.ascii` (wrong latitude index in an early request) is never read. ERA5 and IMERG (login or registration gates), Singapore Open Data Licence datasets, MSS maps, radar and haze graphics (S31), satellite fire points and any drawn storm or smoke tracks are not used.

## Display rules

Rain colour steps, arrow scale, the calm threshold and the rounding of readouts are Atlas display choices, recorded as the design source S199 and shown as the KEY's source (`climatology.keySources`).
