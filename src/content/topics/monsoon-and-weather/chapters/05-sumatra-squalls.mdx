<Lang en>

A <Term id="term-sumatra-squall">Sumatra squall</Term> is an organised line of thunderstorms. It forms over Sumatra or the Strait of Malacca, to the west of Singapore, and usually moves east. <FlyTo preset="view-straits">Straits</FlyTo>

Squalls are common in the Southwest Monsoon and in the inter-monsoon months. They often reach Singapore during the night or in the early morning, bringing sudden strong gusts and heavy rain.

Three rain systems in this topic work at three different sizes. An afternoon storm cell is built by local heating and sea breezes over the island. A squall line is a long row of storms that travels in from the west. A monsoon surge spreads rain across the region from the South China Sea. <FlyTo preset="view-north">South China Sea</FlyTo>

No squall is drawn on this map. Radar pictures of real squalls belong to the weather service and are not copied here, and the monthly mean blends many nights, with squalls and without, into one average. The map shows where Sumatra and the strait lie, not any one squall.

<More title={{ en: "Why an average hides a squall", zh: "平均图为什么看不到飑线" }}>

A squall is a single event. The July map averages every July day and night from 1991 to 2020, so one storm line, or even many, is spread into the same squares as all the calm nights. Switching the monthly layers back on cannot bring a particular squall back.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "One cell and a line of storms", zh: "一个雨胞与一列雷雨" }}>

Every thunderstorm is built from cells like the three clouds in chapter 03. A squall line is a long row of thunderstorms, so the three drawings show the life of one cell, not the life of the whole line.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

<Term id="term-sumatra-squall">苏门答腊飑线</Term>是一列有组织的雷雨。它在新加坡以西的苏门答腊或马六甲海峡上空形成，通常向东移动。<FlyTo preset="view-straits">海峡</FlyTo>

西南季风和季风过渡期里，飑线很常见。它常在夜里或清晨到达新加坡，带来突然的强阵风和大雨。

本主题讲到的三种雨，大小各不相同。午后的雨胞，由岛上局部受热和海风造成；飑线是从西边移过来的一长列雷雨；季风涌则从南中国海把雨带到整个区域。<FlyTo preset="view-north">南中国海</FlyTo>

这张地图上没有画任何一条飑线。真实飑线的雷达图属于气象部门，本主题不复制；而月平均图把许多个夜晚，不论有没有飑线，都合成一个平均。地图只标出苏门答腊和海峡在哪里，不显示任何一次飑线。

<More title={{ en: "Why an average hides a squall", zh: "平均图为什么看不到飑线" }}>

一条飑线是一次单独的天气事件。7 月的地图把 1991–2020 年所有 7 月的日日夜夜平均起来，一条甚至许多条雷雨线，都被摊进和所有平静夜晚相同的方块里。重新打开月平均图层，也找不回某一次飑线。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "One cell and a line of storms", zh: "一个雨胞与一列雷雨" }}>

每一场雷雨都由雨胞组成，就像第 03 章那三朵云。飑线是一长列雷雨，所以那三幅图画的是一个雨胞的一生，而不是整条飑线的一生。

</More>
