<Lang en>

<Term id="term-weather">Weather</Term> is what the air is doing now: a sudden shower, a breezy afternoon, a hot and still morning. <Term id="term-climate">Climate</Term> is the pattern you find when you look at the weather of a place over many years. Across Southeast Asia, winds and rain follow patterns that return at about the same time each year.

Each map is a <Term id="term-climatology">monthly climatology</Term>. For every calendar month it shows the average of the <Num s="S101,S102">30</Num> Januaries, or the 30 Februaries, from 1991 to 2020. The arrows are wind from a <Term id="term-reanalysis">reanalysis</Term>, at the <Num s="S101">850 hPa</Num> level of the air above the ground. The unit hPa measures air pressure. The shaded squares are rain, estimated from satellites and rain gauges, in millimetres per day. Each rain square covers a grid cell <Num s="S102">2.5°</Num> across. Its straight edges mark the grid. These maps show long-term averages, rather than today’s weather at your school.

Find the city points: Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta, Hanoi, Bangkok and Manila. The points mark cities to help you find your way around the map. They do not mark weather stations. Step through the months with the month control, and switch the wind and the rain on and off in the layers panel. <FlyTo preset="view-region">Southeast Asia</FlyTo>

Rain starts with water vapour that rises, cools and turns back into tiny droplets of water: <Term id="term-condensation">condensation</Term>. The water cycle chapter of <TopicLink slug="water" ch="the-water-cycle">Water in Singapore</TopicLink> shows how that works. <FlyTo preset="view-singapore">Singapore</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "How the twelve maps were made", zh: "十二幅月平均图是怎样做成的" }}>

The wind comes from NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis 1 and the rain from GPCP Version 2.3. NOAA, the United States National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, provides both sets of data. Each regional cell includes all <Num s="S101,S102">30</Num> years from 1991–2020. The map keeps the original values without smoothing them. Wind points and rain squares sit half a cell apart, so the inspector reads each grid separately. Tap "Inspect climate cell" to explore one place and month.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where the wind comes from, where the arrow points", zh: "风从哪里来，箭头指向哪里" }}>

A wind is named for the direction it comes from: a northeast wind blows from the northeast. The arrows on these maps point the other way, to where the air is going, so a northeast wind is drawn as an arrow pointing south-west. Each arrow is a <Term id="term-wind-vector">wind vector</Term>: one part says how fast the air moves east, the other how fast it moves north.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A grid cell is not Changi", zh: "格点不是樟宜站" }}>

Changi's rain gauge measures one spot and adds up the rain in a month, in millimetres. A rain square here is <Num s="S102">2.5°</Num> across and gives an average rate in millimetres per day, so the two kinds of number cannot be compared directly. Over high ground the 850 hPa values may be extrapolated estimates. The 850 hPa layer is above the ground, so its wind can differ from the wind you feel outside.

</More>

<Lang zh>

<Term id="term-weather">天气</Term>是空气此刻的样子：一阵骤雨、一个有风的下午、一个闷热无风的早晨。把一个地方许多年的天气放在一起看，找出其中的规律，就是<Term id="term-climate">气候</Term>。东南亚的风和雨，也有每年差不多同一时候出现的规律。

每幅地图都是一幅<Term id="term-climatology">月气候平均</Term>图：每个月份，把 1991 年到 2020 年的 <Num s="S101,S102">30</Num> 个 1 月（或 30 个 2 月……）平均起来。箭头是风，取自<Term id="term-reanalysis">再分析</Term>资料，位于地面以上的 <Num s="S101">850 hPa</Num> 气压层。hPa 是气压单位。色块是雨，由卫星和雨量计合成估算，单位是毫米／日。每个雨量方块是一个边长 <Num s="S102">2.5°</Num> 的格子，直边标出格网。这些地图显示长期平均，而非今天学校附近的天气。

在图上找一找这些城市点：新加坡、吉隆坡、雅加达、河内、曼谷和马尼拉。这些点标出城市，帮助你认位置，并不标示气象站。用月份控件一个月一个月地看，在图层面板里分别开关风和雨。<FlyTo preset="view-region">东南亚</FlyTo>

雨的来历，要从水蒸气说起：它上升、冷却，又变回极小的水滴，这叫<Term id="term-condensation">凝结</Term>。具体怎样发生，可以看<TopicLink slug="water" ch="the-water-cycle">《新加坡的水》</TopicLink>里讲水循环的一章。<FlyTo preset="view-singapore">新加坡</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "How the twelve maps were made", zh: "十二幅月平均图是怎样做成的" }}>

风来自 NCEP/NCAR 再分析 1，雨来自 GPCP 第 2.3 版，两套资料都由美国国家海洋和大气管理局（NOAA）提供。区域内每个格子都包括 1991–2020 年全部 <Num s="S101,S102">30</Num> 年的资料。地图保留原来的数值，没有做平滑。风格点与雨方块相差半格，所以查看时要分别读数。点“查看气候格点”，就能探索一个地方、一个月份的风和雨。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where the wind comes from, where the arrow points", zh: "风从哪里来，箭头指向哪里" }}>

风按它吹来的方向命名：东北风是从东北吹来的风。图上的箭头却指向空气去的地方，所以东北风画成一支指向西南的箭头。每支箭头都是一个<Term id="term-wind-vector">风矢量</Term>：一个分量说空气向东移动多快，另一个分量说它向北移动多快。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A grid cell is not Changi", zh: "格点不是樟宜站" }}>

樟宜站的雨量计只量一个点，把一个月的雨加起来，单位是毫米。这里的雨量方块边长 <Num s="S102">2.5°</Num>，给的是平均每日降水率，单位是毫米／日，所以两种数字不能直接相比。在高地上，850 hPa 的数值可能是外推出来的估计值。850 hPa 气压层在地面以上，那里的风可以与身边感觉到的风不同。

</More>
