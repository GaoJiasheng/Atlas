<Lang en>

Forest and land fires in the region produce smoke. The wind carries it, and dry weather with little rain makes episodes of <Term id="term-haze">smoke haze</Term> worse. The wind moves the smoke; it does not start the fires. <FlyTo preset="view-region">Southeast Asia</FlyTo>

In Indonesia, the weather and climate agency BMKG describes its work against fires and smoke: early warnings, working with other government agencies, and helping to keep peatland wet so that it is less likely to burn. <FlyTo preset="view-indonesia">Indonesia</FlyTo>

In Malaysia, the meteorological department runs long-term atmospheric monitoring that supports research on regional problems, including haze that crosses borders. In Singapore, the Ministry of Health explains how forest and land fires, dry weather and the direction of the wind affect haze in the region. <FlyTo preset="view-malaysia">Malaysia</FlyTo>

Haze is a problem the countries of the region share, and they have worked on it together. The ASEAN Agreement on Transboundary Haze Pollution was signed in 2002. The August map shows average winds only: it cannot show where a particular fire was or decide who was responsible for it. <FlyTo preset="view-straits">Straits</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Prevention in Indonesia", zh: "印尼的预防工作" }}>

BMKG's account, published in Indonesian in August 2025, is titled “Learning from the past”. It describes science and technology as the key: early warnings, agencies working together, and managing the water in peatland so that it stays wet. It is the agency's own description of its work.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Watching the air in Malaysia", zh: "马来西亚的大气监测" }}>

METMalaysia's Global Atmosphere Watch station measures the air over many years. Its purpose includes research on transboundary haze, acid deposition and climate change. The Kuala Lumpur point on this map is the city, not the station.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Explaining haze in Singapore, and working together", zh: "新加坡的说明与区域合作" }}>

Singapore's Ministry of Health page on haze begins with where it comes from: fires in the region, made worse by dry weather and little rain, with smoke carried by the wind. ASEAN's haze portal lists the regional agreement, signed on 10 June 2002 and in force from 25 November 2003, as part of the region's shared work on the problem.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

区域里的森林和土地火灾会产生烟。风把烟带走，干燥少雨的天气会让<Term id="term-haze">烟霾</Term>更严重。风只是搬运烟，并不会点起火。<FlyTo preset="view-region">东南亚</FlyTo>

在印尼，气象、气候和地球物理局（BMKG）介绍了它应对火灾和烟雾的工作：发出预警、与其他政府机构合作，以及帮助泥炭地保持湿润，让它不容易烧起来。<FlyTo preset="view-indonesia">印尼</FlyTo>

在马来西亚，气象局长期监测大气，支持跨境烟霾等区域问题的研究。在新加坡，卫生部说明了森林和土地火灾、干燥天气和风向怎样影响区域的烟霾。<FlyTo preset="view-malaysia">马来西亚</FlyTo>

烟霾是区域各国共同面对的问题，大家也一起处理过。《东盟跨境烟霾污染协定》于 2002 年签署。8 月的地图只显示平均风：它看不出某一场火在哪里，也不能判定谁该负责。<FlyTo preset="view-straits">海峡</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Prevention in Indonesia", zh: "印尼的预防工作" }}>

BMKG 2025 年 8 月以印尼文发表的文章，题为《从过去学习》，把科学与技术当作关键：发出预警、各机构合作，并管理泥炭地里的水，让它保持湿润。这是该机构对自己工作的介绍。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Watching the air in Malaysia", zh: "马来西亚的大气监测" }}>

马来西亚气象局的全球大气监测站，多年来一直测量大气。它的目的包括研究跨境烟霾、酸沉降和气候变化。地图上吉隆坡的点代表城市，不是这个监测站。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Explaining haze in Singapore, and working together", zh: "新加坡的说明与区域合作" }}>

新加坡卫生部的烟霾网页一开头就说明烟霾从哪里来：区域里的火灾，干燥少雨时更严重，烟随风扩散。东盟烟霾门户网站把 2002 年 6 月 10 日签署、2003 年 11 月 25 日生效的区域协定，列为区域共同处理这个问题的一部分。

</More>
