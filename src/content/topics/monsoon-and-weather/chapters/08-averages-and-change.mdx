<Lang en>

A monthly mean shows what a month is usually like over 1991–2020. It does not show how one year differed from another, and it is not a forecast for any day. Moving the month control steps through the calendar, not through history: every January on the map is the same average January.

Some years are different for reasons that come and go. In El Niño years Singapore generally gets less rain, and in La Niña years generally more. These are tendencies, not rules: other things affect each year's weather too. <FlyTo preset="view-singapore">Singapore</FlyTo>

Scientists have found that human-caused climate change is already affecting many weather and climate extremes in every region of the world. For Singapore's own rainfall, the weather service says that the research available now cannot say for certain how much of the changes seen here come from global warming, how much from natural variation, and how much from other causes. Both statements can be true at once. And an average map like this one cannot pin a single rainstorm on any cause. <FlyTo preset="view-region">Southeast Asia</FlyTo>

Where does the rain go once it falls on Singapore? <TopicLink slug="water" ch="catching-the-rain">Water in Singapore</TopicLink> follows it into drains and reservoirs, and its last chapter, <TopicLink slug="water" ch="every-drop">Every drop counts</TopicLink>, is about using it carefully.

<More title={{ en: "One year and the average", zh: "某一年与平均" }}>

An average of <Num s="S101,S102">30</Num> Julys smooths out the wet ones and the dry ones. A real July in an El Niño year may be drier than the map, and one in a La Niña year wetter. The map shows the average, not a promise.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What we know, and how we know it", zh: "我们知道什么，又是怎样知道的" }}>

The global finding comes from the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change, which assesses research from around the world. The local caution comes from Singapore's weather service, looking at Singapore's own records. One is about the world as a whole and many extremes; the other is about one island's rainfall. Neither cancels the other.

</More>

月平均图显示 1991–2020 年间某个月通常是什么样子。它看不出这一年和那一年有什么不同，也不是哪一天的预报。拖动月份控件，走的是一年里的月份，而不是历史：地图上的每一个 1 月，都是同一个平均的 1 月。

有些年份会不一样，原因时有时无。厄尔尼诺年份，新加坡一般较少雨；拉尼娜年份，一般较多雨。这是倾向，不是规律，每一年的天气还受其他因素影响。<FlyTo preset="view-singapore">新加坡</FlyTo>

科学家发现，人为的气候变化已经在影响世界各地区的许多极端天气和气候。至于新加坡本地的雨量，气象局说，现有研究还不能确定这里观测到的变化，有多少来自全球变暖，多少来自自然变化，又有多少来自其他原因。这两句话可以同时成立。而像这样的平均图，也不能把某一场暴雨归到任何一个原因上。<FlyTo preset="view-region">东南亚</FlyTo>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What we know, and how we know it", zh: "我们知道什么，又是怎样知道的" }}>

全球的结论来自政府间气候变化专门委员会（IPCC），它评估世界各地的研究。本地的保留意见来自新加坡气象局，根据的是新加坡自己的记录。一个讲的是整个世界和许多种极端天气，另一个讲的是一个岛的雨量。两者并不互相抵消。

</More>
