<Lang en>

A monthly mean shows what a month is usually like over 1991–2020. It does not show how one year differed from another, and it is not a forecast for any day. Moving the month control steps through the calendar, not through history: every January on the map is the same average January.

Some years are different for reasons that come and go. In El Niño years Singapore generally gets less rain, and in La Niña years generally more. Other factors also affect each year’s weather, so these patterns are not certain. <FlyTo preset="view-singapore">Singapore</FlyTo>

Human-caused climate change already affects many weather and climate extremes in every region of the world. Singapore’s weather service also studies changes in local rainfall. It says current research cannot yet separate how much comes from global warming, natural variation or other causes. A monthly average map cannot explain the cause of one rainstorm. <FlyTo preset="view-region">Southeast Asia</FlyTo>

Where does the rain go once it falls on Singapore? <TopicLink slug="water" ch="catching-the-rain">Water in Singapore</TopicLink> follows it into drains and reservoirs, and its last chapter, <TopicLink slug="water" ch="every-drop">Every drop counts</TopicLink>, is about using it carefully.

<More title={{ en: "One year and the average", zh: "某一年与平均" }}>

An average of <Num s="S101,S102">30</Num> Julys smooths out the wet ones and the dry ones. A real July in an El Niño year may be drier than the map, and one in a La Niña year wetter. The map shows the average, not a promise.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What we know, and how we know it", zh: "我们知道什么，又是怎样知道的" }}>

The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change assesses research from around the world. Its finding covers many kinds of extremes worldwide. Singapore’s weather service examines the island’s own rainfall records. These studies answer questions at different scales.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Try it: compare May and November", zh: "试一试：比较五月和十一月" }}>

Use the month control to choose May, then November. Both are inter-monsoon months. Look at the rain squares near Singapore and over the South China Sea: how are the two months alike, and how are they different?

</More>

月平均图显示 1991–2020 年间某个月通常是什么样子。它看不出这一年和那一年有什么不同，也不是哪一天的预报。拖动月份控件，走的是一年里的月份，而不是历史：地图上的每一个 1 月，都是同一个平均的 1 月。

有些年份会不一样，原因时有时无。厄尔尼诺年份，新加坡一般较少雨；拉尼娜年份，一般较多雨。每一年的天气还受其他因素影响，所以这些变化并非必然。<FlyTo preset="view-singapore">新加坡</FlyTo>

人为的气候变化已经影响世界各地区的许多极端天气和气候。新加坡气象局也研究本地雨量的变化。现有研究还不能分清，有多少变化来自全球变暖、自然变化或其他原因。月平均地图也无法解释某一场暴雨的成因。<FlyTo preset="view-region">东南亚</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "One year and the average", zh: "某一年与平均" }}>

<More title={{ en: "What we know, and how we know it", zh: "我们知道什么，又是怎样知道的" }}>
