<Lang en>

From about June to September, Singapore is in the Southwest Monsoon. Like the Northeast Monsoon, it is a usual season, not a fixed date.

The name describes the large-scale season. Down at the surface in Singapore, though, the wind in these months commonly blows from the south-east or the south. The arrows on the map are real data, and they are not turned to match the season's name. <FlyTo preset="view-singapore">Singapore</FlyTo>

Three things are easy to mix up. The map's arrow is an average for one pressure level, 850 hPa, over the area around a grid point. A surface observation, like the wind measured at Changi, is taken at one spot near the ground. And the season's name belongs to the region as a whole. On the July map the arrow at the grid point nearest Singapore comes from the south-south-west: a different level and a different scale from Singapore's surface wind. <FlyTo preset="view-equator">Near the equator</FlyTo>

The monsoon months are not all equally wet, and no month is the same every year. Compare June with September: the rain colours are the same as January's, so the squares can be read side by side. <FlyTo preset="view-region">Southeast Asia</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "From and towards", zh: "来向与去向" }}>

The readout gives two directions for every arrow. "From" is the direction the wind blows from, the way winds are named; "towards" is where the arrow points. They always differ by <Num s="S199">180°</Num>. A south-west wind blows from the south-west, so its arrow points north-east.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Same month, different places", zh: "同一个月，不同的地方" }}>

In July, most arrows north of the equator come from between the south and the west, while those south of the equator, over Java, come from the east. A season's name cannot be stamped on every place in the region in the same way.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Not a forecast", zh: "不是预报" }}>

An average arrow is not a forecast for sailing, flying or playing outdoors. For what the weather will do today, a weather forecast is the place to look.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

大约从 6 月到 9 月，新加坡处在西南季风里。和东北季风一样，这是“通常”的季节，不是固定日期。

这个名称描述的是大范围的季节。可是在新加坡的地面上，这几个月的风常常从东南或南方吹来。地图上的箭头是真实数据，没有为了配合季风名称而转向。<FlyTo preset="view-singapore">新加坡</FlyTo>

有三样东西容易混在一起。地图上的箭头，是某个格点周围一片地区、一个气压层（850 hPa）的平均。地面观测，例如樟宜站测到的风，是在地面附近一个点上量的。季风名称则属于整个区域。7 月的地图上，离新加坡最近的格点，箭头从南南西方向来：层次不同，尺度也不同，和新加坡的地面风并不一样。<FlyTo preset="view-equator">赤道附近</FlyTo>

季风里的月份不是一样湿，同一个月每年也不一样。比较 6 月和 9 月：雨量颜色和 1 月用的是同一套，方块可以并排对照。<FlyTo preset="view-region">东南亚</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "From and towards", zh: "来向与去向" }}>

格点读数给每支箭头两个方向。“来向”是风吹来的方向，风就是按它命名的；“去向”是箭头所指的方向。两者总是相差 <Num s="S199">180°</Num>。西南风从西南吹来，所以它的箭头指向东北。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Same month, different places", zh: "同一个月，不同的地方" }}>

7 月，赤道以北的箭头大多从南方到西方之间吹来，赤道以南、爪哇上空的箭头却从东方吹来。一个季风名称，不能原样套在区域里的每一个地方。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Not a forecast", zh: "不是预报" }}>

平均箭头不是航海、飞行或户外活动用的预报。想知道今天的天气会怎样，要看天气预报。

</More>

</Lang>
