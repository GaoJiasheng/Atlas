<Lang en>

**If you hear thunder, tell a responsible adult and go indoors at once**: into a proper, solid building, or into an enclosed vehicle with a metal roof and the windows up. Stay there for at least <Num s="S8">30 minutes</Num> after the last thunder you hear.

A cloud is made of tiny water droplets and, in its colder upper parts, ice. They form when rising air cools and its water vapour condenses. A cumulonimbus is so deep that its icy top can spread out into a flat anvil. The drawing below cuts a cloud open to show what is inside; a real cloud has no surface or shell.

The cut edge is a drawing device; a cloud has no solid shell. No charge-layer measurement is shown.", zh: "为解释而放大了颗粒；切面只是画法，云没有硬壳。图中不表示实测电荷分层。" }} />

Inside a thunderstorm, ice crystals and small soft pellets of ice called graupel bump into each other, and rising and sinking air carries them apart. That separates electric charge; scientists are still studying exactly how. Lightning heats the air along its path so fast that the air expands suddenly, and that makes the sound of thunder. Clouds do not have to crash into each other.

Lightning can strike away from the rain, so no rain where you are standing does not mean you are safe. A tree standing on its own is not a shelter. Indoors, keep away from taps and pipes, and from electrical equipment with a cord, until the storm has passed. <FlyTo preset="view-singapore">Singapore</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "The drawing is enlarged", zh: "图是放大过的" }}>

The ice and the drops in the drawing are drawn far bigger than real ones so that you can see them, and they are not counted or measured. The cut is a way of drawing the inside. A real cloud has no wall to cut through.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What counts as shelter", zh: "怎样才算安全的地方" }}>

A safe building is a solid one with electricity or plumbing, such as a home or a school. Inside, stay away from windows and doors as well. A vehicle counts only if it is enclosed, has a metal roof and has its windows up.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Do not count your way outside", zh: "不要靠数秒决定留在外面" }}>

If you can hear thunder, lightning is close enough to strike you. Counting the seconds between a flash and its thunder does not tell you how long you can stay out; hearing thunder at all means it is time to go in.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

**听到雷声，就告诉负责照顾你的成人，立刻进入室内**：进入合适的坚固建筑，或关好车窗、有金属车顶的封闭车辆。最后一次雷声之后，仍要在里面留至少 <Num s="S8">30 分钟</Num>。

云由极小的水滴组成，较冷的上部还有冰。它们是上升的空气冷却、其中的水蒸气凝结而成的。积雨云非常深厚，冰质的云顶可以向四周铺开，像一块平平的铁砧。下面的图把一朵云剖开，让你看见里面；真实的云没有表面，也没有外壳。

The cut edge is a drawing device; a cloud has no solid shell. No charge-layer measurement is shown.", zh: "为解释而放大了颗粒；切面只是画法，云没有硬壳。图中不表示实测电荷分层。" }} />

在雷雨云里，冰晶和一种松软的小冰粒（叫做霰）互相碰撞，上升和下沉的空气又把它们分开，电荷就这样分离开来；具体怎样发生，科学家还在研究。闪电让它经过的空气在极短时间里变得极热，空气猛然膨胀，就发出了雷声。雷声并不需要两片云相撞。

闪电可以打在下雨区域以外，所以你站的地方没下雨，不等于安全。孤零零的一棵树不是避雷的地方。在室内，雷雨过去以前，要远离水龙头、水管和插着电线的电器。<FlyTo preset="view-singapore">新加坡</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "The drawing is enlarged", zh: "图是放大过的" }}>

图里的冰粒和水滴，画得比真实的大得多，好让你看得见；它们的数目和大小都不是测量出来的。剖开只是一种画法，真实的云并没有可以切开的外壁。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "What counts as shelter", zh: "怎样才算安全的地方" }}>

安全的建筑要坚固，并且通电或装有水管，例如住家或学校。在里面还要远离门窗。车辆要是封闭的、有金属车顶、车窗关好，才算安全。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Do not count your way outside", zh: "不要靠数秒决定留在外面" }}>

听得见雷声，就说明闪电近得可能打到你。数闪电到雷声之间隔了几秒，并不能告诉你还能在外面待多久；只要听见雷声，就该进屋了。

</More>
