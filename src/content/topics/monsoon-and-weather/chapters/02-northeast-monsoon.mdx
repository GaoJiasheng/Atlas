<Lang en>

In Singapore the Northeast Monsoon usually lasts from December to early March. The weather service gives these months as the usual season, not as fixed dates: the change does not happen on the same day every year.

The season has two halves. The first, usually December to early January, is the wet phase. <Term id="term-monsoon-surge">Monsoon surges</Term>, bursts of stronger north-east wind over the South China Sea, gather moisture as they cross it and can bring long spells of widespread rain. The later part, from late January to early March, is windy and relatively dry. So one season's name does not tell you how wet each of its months will be. <FlyTo preset="view-north">South China Sea</FlyTo>

Rain also follows the <Term id="term-rain-belt">tropical rain belt</Term>. It lies near Singapore in the wetter months, November to January, and has moved farther south, towards Java, by February. On the February map the cell around Jakarta averages about <Num s="S102">13</Num> mm a day; the cell around Singapore, about <Num s="S102">4.4</Num>. <FlyTo preset="view-java">Java</FlyTo>

A rain gauge at one place tells the same story. At Changi the average rainfall for 1991–2020 is <Num s="S2">105.1 mm</Num> in February and <Num s="S2">331.9 mm</Num> in December. Those are monthly totals at one station, while the map gives daily rates over large squares.

<More title={{ en: "Changi's two months, and the map's two units", zh: "樟宜的两个月，地图的两种单位" }}>

Changi's <Num s="S2">105.1 mm</Num> and <Num s="S2">331.9 mm</Num> are the mean totals for the whole of February and the whole of December at one rain gauge, 1991–2020. The map's squares give mean millimetres per day over a cell <Num s="S102">2.5°</Num> across. Multiplying a cell's rate by the days in a month does not give Changi's total, because the cell is not the gauge.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why wind from the north can bring rain", zh: "从北方吹来的风为什么会带来雨" }}>

A monsoon surge travels a long way over the South China Sea before it reaches Singapore. On the way it gathers moisture from the sea, which is why it can bring long spells of steady rain instead of one short afternoon shower.

</More>

<Lang zh>

在新加坡，东北季风通常从 12 月持续到 3 月初。气象局说的是“通常”的季节，并不是固定日期：季风不是每年同一天开始的。

这个季节分前后两段。前段通常是 12 月到 1 月初，是多雨的阶段。<Term id="term-monsoon-surge">季风涌</Term>，也就是南中国海上空一阵阵增强的东北风，越过海面时带上水汽，可以造成大范围、长时间的降雨。后段从 1 月下旬到 3 月初，多风而相对干燥。所以，同一个季风名称，说明不了其中每个月有多湿。<FlyTo preset="view-north">南中国海</FlyTo>

雨也跟着<Term id="term-rain-belt">热带雨带</Term>走。11 月到 1 月比较湿，这时雨带就在新加坡附近；到了 2 月，它已经移得更偏南，靠近爪哇。2 月的地图上，雅加达所在的格子平均每天约 <Num s="S102">13</Num> 毫米，新加坡所在的格子约 <Num s="S102">4.4</Num> 毫米。<FlyTo preset="view-java">爪哇</FlyTo>

一个地方的雨量计也说明了同样的情况。1991–2020 年，樟宜站 2 月的平均雨量是 <Num s="S2">105.1 毫米</Num>，12 月是 <Num s="S2">331.9 毫米</Num>。这是一个测站整个月的总量；地图给的却是大方块里每天的平均降水率。

<More title={{ en: "Changi's two months, and the map's two units", zh: "樟宜的两个月，地图的两种单位" }}>

樟宜的 <Num s="S2">105.1 毫米</Num>和 <Num s="S2">331.9 毫米</Num>，是 1991–2020 年间一个雨量计在整个 2 月和整个 12 月的平均总雨量。地图上的方块给的是边长 <Num s="S102">2.5°</Num> 的格子里平均每天的毫米数。把格子的日降水率乘以这个月的天数，也得不到樟宜的总量，因为格子不是那个雨量计。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why wind from the north can bring rain", zh: "从北方吹来的风为什么会带来雨" }}>

季风涌要越过南中国海，走很长一段海路才到新加坡，一路上从海面带上水汽。所以它可以带来持续不断的雨，而不是午后一场短暂的阵雨。

</More>
