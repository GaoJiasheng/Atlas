<Lang en>

Between the two monsoons come two <Term id="term-inter-monsoon">inter-monsoon</Term> periods: from late March to May, and in October and November. March is a mixed month. Its first part can still belong to the Northeast Monsoon; its later part is the start of the transition.

In these months Singapore's surface winds are light and variable, and they mix with land and sea breezes. The 850 hPa arrows on the map average a whole month over a wide area, so they cannot show how the wind behaved on any one afternoon. <FlyTo preset="view-straits">Straits</FlyTo>

On many afternoons the land heats up, sea breezes push in from different coasts and meet, and warm, moist air is lifted. That can help a thunderstorm grow in a tall <Term id="term-cumulonimbus">cumulonimbus</Term> cloud.

An ordinary storm cell has three stages. While it is developing, <Term id="term-updraft">updrafts</Term> feed it and there is little or no rain. When it is mature, rain falls and drags cool air down with it, while updrafts still rise. In the end that rain-cooled air spreads out along the ground and cuts off the warm, moist air feeding the cell, and the cell dies away.

<More title={{ en: "Why March has no single label", zh: "为什么 3 月不能只贴一个标签" }}>

The weather service puts the Northeast Monsoon from December to early March and the first inter-monsoon from late March. The boundary falls inside March and moves from year to year, so a map for the whole of March mixes the end of one season with the start of the next.

</More>

The right cloud is smaller and ragged, with weak rain: the cool air flowing out at its base has cut off its supply.

<Lang zh>

两段季风之间，有两段<Term id="term-inter-monsoon">季风过渡期</Term>：3 月下旬到 5 月，以及 10 月和 11 月。3 月是个混合的月份：月初可能还在东北季风里，月底才开始过渡。

这几个月，新加坡的地面风轻而多变，还和陆风、海风混在一起。地图上 850 hPa 的箭头是一大片地区整整一个月的平均，看不出某一个下午的风是怎样吹的。<FlyTo preset="view-straits">海峡</FlyTo>

许多下午，陆地被晒热，海风从不同的海岸吹进来、相遇，把温暖潮湿的空气往上推。这可以帮助雷雨在高大的<Term id="term-cumulonimbus">积雨云</Term>里发展起来。

一个普通雨胞要经过三个阶段。成长时，<Term id="term-updraft">上升气流</Term>不断供给它，几乎不下雨。成熟时，雨落下来，把冷空气一起往下带，同时上升气流仍在往上。最后，被雨冷却的空气沿地面散开，切断供给雨胞的温暖潮湿空气，雨胞就慢慢消散。

<More title={{ en: "Why March has no single label", zh: "为什么 3 月不能只贴一个标签" }}>

气象局把东北季风定在 12 月到 3 月初，第一段季风过渡期从 3 月下旬开始。分界落在 3 月里面，而且每年不同，所以整个 3 月的平均图，混合了上一季的结尾和下一季的开头。
