# Industrial Revolution — sources

This topic remains draft. Numbered textual sources are in `sources.json`; geographic sources below retain their own rights. Full fingerprint, transformation, error, size and visual-review records: `scripts/geo/industrial-revolution/SOURCES-GEO.md` and `build-report.json`.

## Map data

- **[G1] Natural Earth 50m land** — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_land.geojson. **Public domain**, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/. Physical reference and whole-feature GB/Ireland main-island masks; the existing bundled Natural Earth basemap is reused. No historical shoreline dates.
- **[G2] Natural Earth 10m populated places** — https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_populated_places.geojson. **Public domain.** 21 modern city reference points, original NE_ID preserved; name/current-country matching disambiguates cities without asserting historic countries.
- **[G3] GeoNames cities15000** — https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip; https://www.geonames.org/. **CC BY 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Six modern city reference points with original GeoNames ids. © GeoNames. Coordinate attribution applies to sites and milestone events using those coordinates; authored prose remains Atlas CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- **[G4] RShapes 1.0**, Müller-Crepon, Girardin / ETH Zürich — https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/; https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/RShapes.geojson. **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/. Required reference: Cederman, Lars-Erik, Luc Girardin, Carl Müller-Crepon, Yannick Pengl (2025), *Nationalism and the Transformation of the State: Border Change and Political Violence in the Modern World*, Cambridge University Press. Changes: whole-feature removal using public-domain main-island masks; year≤1914 selection; DP 750 m, quantisation 100,000 (a grid of about 50 m × 40 m at 50° N); original loop lid 17463 restored after collapse; lines of the same construction year that share a source endpoint merged into one connected run. `public/geo/industrial-rail-rshapes.json` keeps all 18,804 selected lids in 8,906 runs (properties: year `f`, lid count `n`), with no CC BY-SA or OHM input. Construction chronology does not establish traction or continued operation.
- **[G5] British Geological Survey, Coal resources for new technologies** — https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/; https://www.bgs.ac.uk/?wpdmdl=41597. **OGL 3.0**, https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/; BGS attribution: https://www.bgs.ac.uk/bgs-intellectual-property-rights/open-government-licence/. **Contains British Geological Survey materials © UKRI 2026.** Source-context notice retained: **Contains OS data © Crown copyright 2020.** Changes: GB strata table only, EPSG:27700→WGS84 using mproj OSGB36 seven-parameter transform (no OSTN15 grid), DP 500 m with ring preservation and topology checks, original polygons coal-gb-273/529/583/599 restored where simplification displaced holes across/outside an exterior. 622 features/624 components/1,017 rings remain in `public/geo/industrial-coal-strata.json`; modern geology is not mine-operating dates. NI and workings/resource-exploitation tables are excluded.

The 27-source-point artifact is retained in `scripts/geo/industrial-revolution/industrial-places.geojson`; runtime coordinates are in `events.json`. Dateline/coordinate sanity, closures, winding, zero-area and ring crossings are checked by the pipeline. No raster georeferencing was performed, so control-point residuals are not applicable. Measured simplification changes and absent historical geometry are recorded in the full pipeline provenance.

## Geometry review and budgets

Offline source/production comparisons are regenerated locally by `scripts/geo/industrial-revolution/compare.ts` (QA records, not in git); they check broad geometry distribution, not geodetic accuracy. Budgets (docs/02, docs/51 §3.3-7): rail 1,304,627 raw bytes (300,369 gzip) ≤ 1.5 MB; coal 266,680 (75,990 gzip) ≤ 350 KB; total geometry 1,571,307 ≤ 2 MB. The first build kept one feature per lid with seven properties each (3,942,408 bytes, 2.05 MB of it properties); the rail was brought within budget by dropping per-feature copies of source/licence fields (recorded once here and in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES) and merging same-year connected lines, as docs/51 §3.3-7 allows. No feature, date or lid was discarded.
