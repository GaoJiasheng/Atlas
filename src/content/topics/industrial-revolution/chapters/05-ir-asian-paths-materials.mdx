<Lang en>

The Shimbashi–Yokohama railway opened in 1872. The railway connected Tokyo’s Shimbashi district with Yokohama. UNESCO describes industrial change across a longer period, involving imported expertise and later adaptation by Japanese engineers. Bringing in knowledge and developing it locally were connected activities. [S78](https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/imagebank/theme/tetsudoukiyoe) [S24](https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/6364)

Singapore’s rubber seedlings arrived in 1877. Ridley became director of the Botanic Gardens in 1888. The seedlings were already there before he took up the post. This rubber history is also distinct from the gutta-percha used to insulate cables in the previous chapter. [S30](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/sbg/research/publications/-/media/sbg/gardenwise/2005-jul-gardenwise-vol-25.ashx) [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/)

In north China, the Tangshan–Xugezhuang railway was built in 1881. Shanghai’s earlier Jiangnan Arsenal supplies another case, involving machines, ships and translation. Railway building, machine-making, shipbuilding and translation were different branches of change. Their development varied across China’s regions and industries. [S79](https://www.dlyj.ac.cn/EN/abstract/article/1000-0585/14696) [S25](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/gmww/202104/t20210406_249554.shtml)

Straits Trading was founded in 1887 and built its Pulau Brani tin smelter in 1890. Smelting ore in Singapore, handling exports and mining tin in Malaya were different activities. These activities connected different places within the region. [S32](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/chapters-of-asia/chapters-on-asia-2015-on-the-waterfront-the-building-of-singapore-s-first-high-rise-skyline-1918-1928/)

Labour also crossed these connections. Indian migration to Malaya and Singapore included <Term id="term-indenture">indentured labour</Term>, <Term id="term-kangany">kangany recruitment</Term> through overseers, and free migration. Recruitment abuses, contracts and prolonged debt could restrict workers' choices. Workers could face serious restrictions without being legally owned as property, as enslaved people were. Different migrant communities also faced different recruitment systems. [S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/)

<Lang zh>

1872 年，新桥—横滨铁路开通。这条铁路连起东京的新桥与横滨。联合国教科文组织描述了更长时期的变化：引入外国专业知识，再由日本工程师改造运用。输入与本地发展是相连的工作。[S78](https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/imagebank/theme/tetsudoukiyoe) [S24](https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/6364)

橡胶苗在 1877 年抵达新加坡；Ridley 到 1888 年才任植物园主任，上任时这些植物已经在园中生长。橡胶与上一章所讲的古塔胶，是两种不同的材料；古塔胶用于电缆绝缘。[S30](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/sbg/research/publications/-/media/sbg/gardenwise/2005-jul-gardenwise-vol-25.ashx) [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/)

中国北方于 1881 年建设唐山—胥各庄铁路，上海则已有江南制造局。铁路建设、机器制造、造船与翻译，是不同方面的变化；各地、各行业的发展速度也不同。[S79](https://www.dlyj.ac.cn/EN/abstract/article/1000-0585/14696) [S25](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/gmww/202104/t20210406_249554.shtml)

海峡贸易公司在 1887 年成立，1890 年建成布拉尼岛炼锡厂。在新加坡冶炼、处理出口，与在马来亚采锡，是不同工作。这些活动把区域内不同地点联系起来。[S32](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/chapters-of-asia/chapters-on-asia-2015-on-the-waterfront-the-building-of-singapore-s-first-high-rise-skyline-1918-1928/)

印度劳工迁往马来亚和新加坡，有<Term id="term-indenture">契约劳工</Term>、通过工头进行的<Term id="term-kangany">康加尼招工制</Term>（kangany recruitment），也有自由移民。招工滥用、契约与长期债务可能限制选择。劳工可能受到严重限制，却没有像奴隶制下的人那样成为别人的财产。不同移民群体的招工经历也各不相同。[S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/)
