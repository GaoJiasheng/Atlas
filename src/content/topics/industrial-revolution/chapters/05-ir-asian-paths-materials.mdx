<Lang en>

The Shimbashi–Yokohama railway opened in 1872. Tokyo and Yokohama locate this particular connection, rather than an overnight transformation of Japan. UNESCO describes industrial change across a longer period, involving imported expertise and later adaptation by Japanese engineers. Bringing in knowledge and developing it locally were connected activities. [S78](https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/imagebank/theme/tetsudoukiyoe) [S24](https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/6364)

Singapore’s rubber seedlings arrived in 1877. Ridley became director of the Botanic Gardens in 1888. These are separate stages: the later director’s work must not be merged with the arrival of the plants. This rubber history is also distinct from the gutta-percha used to insulate cables in the previous chapter. [S30](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/sbg/research/publications/-/media/sbg/gardenwise/2005-jul-gardenwise-vol-25.ashx) [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/)

In north China, the Tangshan–Xugezhuang railway was built in 1881. Shanghai’s earlier Jiangnan Arsenal supplies another case, involving machines, ships and translation. Neither institution nor railway opening can stand for every region or branch of Chinese production. The map uses modern city locations to anchor the cases, without drawing unverified industrial districts. [S79](https://www.dlyj.ac.cn/EN/abstract/article/1000-0585/14696) [S25](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/gmww/202104/t20210406_249554.shtml)

Straits Trading was founded in 1887 and built its Pulau Brani tin smelter in 1890. Smelting ore in Singapore, handling exports and mining tin in Malaya were different activities. The wider regional view helps keep them apart, instead of treating Singapore’s city point as a mine. [S32](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/chapters-of-asia/chapters-on-asia-2015-on-the-waterfront-the-building-of-singapore-s-first-high-rise-skyline-1918-1928/)

Labour also crossed these connections. Indian migration to Malaya and Singapore included <Term id="term-indenture">indentured labour</Term>, <Term id="term-kangany">kangany recruitment</Term> through overseers, and free migration. Recruitment abuses, contracts and prolonged debt could restrict workers' choices. These constraints require attention in their own right. They were not identical to legal ownership under slavery, and this Indian migration account cannot describe every Chinese worker’s experience. [S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/)

It does not attribute the original arrival to the later director. [S30](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/sbg/research/publications/-/media/sbg/gardenwise/2005-jul-gardenwise-vol-25.ashx)

No dated mine or plantation outlines have been established for this map, so none are drawn. [S68](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) [S72](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Different constraints on workers", "zh": "劳工面对的不同约束" }}>

Periasamy’s NLB account describes poor living conditions and recruitment abuses under indenture, prolonged indebtedness, and criticism of kangany recruitment as exploitative. The account concerns Indian migration. Specific Chinese tin-mining contracts need their own sources; one community’s documented experience cannot substitute for another’s. [S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/)

<Lang zh>

1872 年，新桥—横滨铁路开通。东京与横滨帮助定位这条联系，不能表示日本一夜之间完成工业化。联合国教科文组织描述了更长时期的变化：引入外国专业知识，再由日本工程师改造运用。输入与本地发展是相连的工作。[S78](https://www.ndl.go.jp/en/imagebank/theme/tetsudoukiyoe) [S24](https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/6364)

橡胶苗在 1877 年抵达新加坡；Ridley 到 1888 年才任植物园主任，不能把后来的工作并入苗木抵达事件。橡胶也不是上一章所讲的电缆绝缘材料古塔胶，两段材料史要分清。[S30](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/sbg/research/publications/-/media/sbg/gardenwise/2005-jul-gardenwise-vol-25.ashx) [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/)

中国北方于 1881 年建设唐山—胥各庄铁路，上海则已有江南制造局。线路、机器、造船与翻译，各是具体案例，不等于中国所有地区、所有行业同时转变。现代城市点也不能代替历史工业区边界。[S79](https://www.dlyj.ac.cn/EN/abstract/article/1000-0585/14696) [S25](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/gmww/202104/t20210406_249554.shtml)

海峡贸易公司在 1887 年成立，1890 年建成布拉尼岛炼锡厂。在新加坡冶炼、处理出口，与在马来亚采锡，是不同工作；地图上的新加坡点不是矿坑。[S32](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/chapters-of-asia/chapters-on-asia-2015-on-the-waterfront-the-building-of-singapore-s-first-high-rise-skyline-1918-1928/)

印度劳工迁往马来亚和新加坡，有<Term id="term-indenture">契约劳工</Term>、通过工头进行的<Term id="term-kangany">康加尼招工制</Term>（kangany recruitment），也有自由移民。招工滥用、契约与长期债务可能限制选择。这些约束不能忽略，也不能全等同于奴隶制；印度劳工的资料不代表所有华工经历。[S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/)
