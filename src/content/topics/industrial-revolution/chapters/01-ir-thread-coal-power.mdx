<Lang en>

A shuttle belongs to weaving. It carries the weft yarn across the lengthwise warp yarns; a bobbin holds its yarn. The jenny, by contrast, was a spinning machine. Hargreaves patented it in 1770. Arkwright’s water frame and Crompton’s mule were other spinning technologies. Their histories overlapped: a new machine did not instantly replace all older work. [S75](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/glossary-of-weaving-terms.htm) [S13](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co44544) [S14](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ehr.13082)

A steam engine used steam and pressure differences to move machinery. Near Dudley, Newcomen’s practical pumping engine was erected in 1712. Inside its cylinder, <Term id="term-condensation">condensation</Term> changed steam back into liquid water and lowered the pressure. Atmospheric pressure then pushed the piston down. The stronger pressure of the outside air pushed against the lower pressure inside the cylinder. [S4](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50900/newcomen-atmospheric-engine)

Watt developed a working separate-condenser model by 1765. His patent followed on 5 January 1769. The separate condenser let steam cool in a cold vessel while the working cylinder stayed hot. Commercial engines were operating in 1776. The idea developed through a working model, legal protection for the invention, and engines used in businesses. [S6](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/people/ap24508/watt-james) [S7](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/)

The 1788 Lap engine used a centrifugal governor and drove metal-polishing machinery. Steam power could therefore drive metalworking as well as textile machinery. Water power and other ways of working continued alongside steam. Glasgow, Dudley, Birmingham and Manchester bring these different branches of production into view. [S9](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50948/rotative-steam-engine-by-boulton-and-watt-1788) [S68](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) [S72](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/)

Hargreaves patented the jenny in 1770. The jenny, water frame and mule were different spinning machines, each with its own design and history. [S13](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co44544) [S14](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ehr.13082)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Why separate the condenser?", "zh": "冷凝为何有用" }}>

Newcomen’s engine cooled steam inside the working cylinder. Watt separated the place that had to stay hot from the vessel that had to be cold. Earlier steam engines, including Newcomen’s, had already been used for pumping. [S4](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50900/newcomen-atmospheric-engine) [S7](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/)

<Lang zh>

梭子用于织布：它带着纬纱（weft）穿过纵向排列的经纱（warp），纱管装着纬纱。珍妮纺纱机做的是纺纱，哈格里夫斯在 1770 年取得专利。阿克莱特的水力纺纱机与克朗普顿的骡机也是纺纱技术。新旧机器并存，并非新机器一出现，旧方法就全部消失。[S75](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/glossary-of-weaving-terms.htm) [S13](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co44544) [S14](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ehr.13082)

蒸汽机借蒸汽与压力差带动机械。1712 年，纽科门的实用抽水机建在达德利附近。汽缸（cylinder）里的蒸汽<Term id="term-condensation">凝结（冷凝）</Term>成水，缸内压力降低，大气压力便把活塞向下推。汽缸外的大气压力较大，推动活塞朝低压的一侧运动。[S4](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50900/newcomen-atmospheric-engine)

瓦特到 1765 年已做出分离式冷凝器（separate condenser）的工作模型，1769 年 1 月 5 日取得专利。蒸汽在另一个冷容器里凝结，工作汽缸可以保持热。1776 年已有商业用机器运行。从工作模型、专利保护到商业运行，这项发明经历了不同阶段。[S6](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/people/ap24508/watt-james) [S7](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/)

1788 年的 Lap 机装有离心调速器，带动金属抛光机械，说明蒸汽也用于纺织以外的生产。水力与其他生产方式仍继续存在。格拉斯哥、达德利、伯明翰与曼彻斯特，串起了这些不同的生产活动。[S9](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50948/rotative-steam-engine-by-boulton-and-watt-1788) [S68](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) [S72](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/)

<More title={{ "en": "A patent is a particular milestone", "zh": "专利是一个特定环节" }}>

哈格里夫斯在 1770 年为珍妮纺纱机取得专利。珍妮纺纱机、水力纺纱机与骡机各有设计，也各有发展历史。[S13](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co44544) [S14](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ehr.13082)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Why separate the condenser?", "zh": "冷凝为何有用" }}>

纽科门机在工作汽缸内冷凝蒸汽；瓦特把需要保持热的汽缸，与需要冷却的容器分开。在此以前，纽科门等人的蒸汽机已经用于抽水。[S4](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50900/newcomen-atmospheric-engine) [S7](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/)
