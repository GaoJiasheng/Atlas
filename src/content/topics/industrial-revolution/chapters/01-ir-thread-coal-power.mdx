<Lang en>

A shuttle belongs to weaving. It carries the weft yarn across the lengthwise warp yarns; a bobbin holds its yarn. The jenny, by contrast, was a spinning machine. Hargreaves patented it in 1770. Arkwright’s water frame and Crompton’s mule were other spinning technologies. Their histories overlapped: a new machine did not instantly replace all older work. [S75](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/glossary-of-weaving-terms.htm) [S13](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co44544) [S14](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ehr.13082)

A steam engine used steam and pressure differences to move machinery. Near Dudley, Newcomen’s practical pumping engine was erected in 1712. Inside its cylinder, <Term id="term-condensation">condensation</Term> changed steam back into liquid water and lowered the pressure. Atmospheric pressure then pushed the piston down. The difference in pressure did the work; a vacuum was not a substance pulling the piston. [S4](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50900/newcomen-atmospheric-engine)

Watt developed a working separate-condenser model by 1765. His patent followed on 5 January 1769. The separate condenser let steam cool in a cold vessel while the working cylinder stayed hot. Commercial engines were operating in 1776. A working model, a patent and commercial operation show different stages, rather than rival dates for the same moment. [S6](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/people/ap24508/watt-james) [S7](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/)

The 1788 Lap engine used a centrifugal governor and drove metal-polishing machinery. That particular example connects steam power to workshop production beyond textile spinning. It does not show every British factory changing power in that year. On the map, Glasgow, Dudley, Birmingham and Manchester are regional anchors for these connected stories, not precise addresses of every machine discussed here. [S9](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50948/rotative-steam-engine-by-boulton-and-watt-1788) [S68](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) [S72](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/)

<More title={{ "en": "Why separate the condenser?", "zh": "冷凝为何有用" }}>

Newcomen’s engine cooled steam inside the working cylinder. Watt separated the place that had to stay hot from the vessel that had to be cold. This explains the change without making the patent the beginning of all steam power. [S4](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50900/newcomen-atmospheric-engine) [S7](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/)

</More>

The map locates the historical case, rather than presenting a working mechanical model. <Lang zh>

梭子用于织布：它带着纬纱（weft）穿过纵向排列的经纱（warp），纱管装着纬纱。珍妮纺纱机做的是纺纱，哈格里夫斯在 1770 年取得专利。阿克莱特的水力纺纱机与克朗普顿的骡机也是纺纱技术。新旧机器并存，并非新机器一出现，旧方法就全部消失。[S75](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/glossary-of-weaving-terms.htm) [S13](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co44544) [S14](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ehr.13082)

蒸汽机借蒸汽与压力差带动机械。1712 年，纽科门的实用抽水机建在达德利附近。汽缸（cylinder）里的蒸汽<Term id="term-condensation">凝结（冷凝）</Term>成水，缸内压力降低，大气压力便把活塞向下推。起作用的是压力差，不是一种叫“真空”的东西把活塞拉下来。[S4](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50900/newcomen-atmospheric-engine)

瓦特到 1765 年已做出分离式冷凝器（separate condenser）的工作模型，1769 年 1 月 5 日取得专利。蒸汽在另一个冷容器里凝结，工作汽缸可以保持热。1776 年已有商业用机器运行。模型、专利和商业运行，是不同环节，不能合成同一天的发明。[S6](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/people/ap24508/watt-james) [S7](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/)

1788 年的 Lap 机装有离心调速器，带动金属抛光机械，说明蒸汽也用于纺织以外的生产。这不表示英国工厂同年全部改用蒸汽。图上的城市只是地区锚点，不是每台机器的精确地址。可以分别点开模型、专利与商业运行事件，再读各自的文献说明。[S9](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50948/rotative-steam-engine-by-boulton-and-watt-1788) [S68](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/) [S72](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/)

<More title={{ "en": "A patent is a particular milestone", "zh": "专利是一个特定环节" }}>

年表采用珍妮纺纱机的 1770 年专利，不把它写成所有纺纱机器共同的发明年。水力纺纱机、骡机各有自己的历史。[S13](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co44544) [S14](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/ehr.13082)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Why separate the condenser?", "zh": "冷凝为何有用" }}>

纽科门机在工作汽缸内冷凝蒸汽；瓦特把需要保持热的汽缸，与需要冷却的容器分开。这个改变有它的意义，却不等于瓦特的专利是所有蒸汽动力的起点。[S4](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50900/newcomen-atmospheric-engine) [S7](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/james-watt-and-the-separate-condenser/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "When rotation needs regulation", "zh": "转得太快怎么办" }}>

科学博物馆集团指出，1788 年的 Lap 机带有调速器。调节运转速度，与把冷凝器分开，是不同的机械问题。地图帮助定位历史案例，本页没有把插图当作可运行的机器模型。[S9](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co50948/rotative-steam-engine-by-boulton-and-watt-1788)
