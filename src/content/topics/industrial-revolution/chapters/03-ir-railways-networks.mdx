<Lang en>

This chapter steps back from the 1860 census to 1830. That year, a railway between Liverpool and Manchester carried passengers and freight in trains pulled by steam locomotives. The Stockton and Darlington Railway had opened earlier, in 1825. These are different milestones. Rocket, built in 1829, won the Rainhill trials during these early years of railway development. [S76](https://historicengland.org.uk/whats-new/research/early-railways-in-england/) [S77](https://blog.scienceandindustrymuseum.org.uk/seg-if-these-walls-could-talk/) [S65](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co26704)

Railways did not all begin with steam traction. The Saint-Étienne coal railway in France used horse-drawn wagons in 1827. Horses and steam engines were different ways to move wagons along rails. The continental map follows when railway lines were built; the power used and the services run on each line could change over time. [S17](https://www.groupe-sncf.com/en/group/history-archives/two-centuries-railway-history) [S63](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/)

The Brussels–Mechelen service opened on 5 May 1835. Belgium-built Le Belge followed that December. The Nuremberg–Fürth railway also opened in 1835, in the German states. Railway building and locomotive manufacturing developed in different places, under different institutions. [S18](https://trainworld.be/en/collections/history-of-the-belgian-railways/2-belgium-on-track-19th-century/) [S19](https://dbmuseum.de/en/nuremberg/exhibitions/the-history-of-the-railway-in-germany)

In 1837 the Tsarskoye Selo public railway opened near Saint Petersburg. In India, passenger service between Bori Bunder and Thane began on 16 April 1853. The railway reached a region with much older manufacturing traditions. India already had many kinds of production, whose later changes differed by industry and place. [S21](https://cmzt.ru/events/news-events/otkrytie-czarskoselskoj-zheleznoj-do/) [S22](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/cAVh7RwiKiTtKg) [S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

<More title={{ "en": "Different industrial paths", "zh": "不同的工业道路" }}>

Jeff Horn interprets French industrialisation as a distinctive policy path, rather than a failed British imitation. Richard Tilly and Michael Kopsidis emphasise regional origins, institutional reforms, railways and heavy industry in the German case. Their interpretations compare processes, not just opening dates. [S60](https://academic.oup.com/mit-press-scholarship-online/book/14881) [S61](https://academic.oup.com/chicago-scholarship-online/book/37929)

The map’s railway lines come from RShapes. They cover parts of continental Europe from 1834 to 1914; Great Britain and Ireland are outside this layer. The dates describe construction. Railway power, later service, traffic and people’s incomes are separate parts of railway history. [S63](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Public services and calendar dates", "zh": "客运与历法" }}>

The Russian museum gives the opening as 30 October 1837 Old Style, or 11 November New Style. Both describe the same event. The Indian passenger service ran <Num s="S22">34</Num> km between Bori Bunder and Thane. Its distance describes that line, not the extent of Indian industry. [S21](https://cmzt.ru/events/news-events/otkrytie-czarskoselskoj-zheleznoj-do/)

This figure interprets its early form; it is not a precise contemporary construction drawing or a photograph of an unchanged 1829 machine. 上一章最后看到 1860 年的普查，这里回到 1830 年。利物浦—曼彻斯特铁路以蒸汽机车运送旅客与货物；斯托克顿—达灵顿铁路则在 1825 年开通。Rocket 建于 1829 年，在雷恩希尔试验中胜出，是早期铁路发展中的一种机车。[S76](https://historicengland.org.uk/whats-new/research/early-railways-in-england/) [S77](https://blog.scienceandindustrymuseum.org.uk/seg-if-these-walls-could-talk/) [S65](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co26704)

铁路不一定由蒸汽机车牵引。法国圣艾蒂安（Saint-Étienne）1827 年的运煤线使用马匹。马匹与蒸汽机车都曾沿铁轨牵引车辆。欧洲大陆铁路层按建造年份显示线路；一条线路后来使用什么动力、提供哪些服务，还会随时间改变。[S17](https://www.groupe-sncf.com/en/group/history-archives/two-centuries-railway-history) [S63](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/)

1835 年 5 月 5 日，布鲁塞尔—梅赫伦（Mechelen）铁路开通，当年 12 月又有比利时制造的 Le Belge 机车。德意志诸邦的纽伦堡—菲尔特铁路也于 1835 年开通。各地的铁路建设与机车制造，在不同制度下发展起来。[S18](https://trainworld.be/en/collections/history-of-the-belgian-railways/2-belgium-on-track-19th-century/) [S19](https://dbmuseum.de/en/nuremberg/exhibitions/the-history-of-the-railway-in-germany)

1837 年，圣彼得堡附近的皇村铁路（Tsarskoye Selo railway）开通。1853 年 4 月 16 日，印度博里本德（Bori Bunder）至塔纳（Thane）开始客运。铁路连接的地区早已有生产与贸易。印度制造业有多种传统，后来各行业、各地区的发展也各不相同。[S21](https://cmzt.ru/events/news-events/otkrytie-czarskoselskoj-zheleznoj-do/) [S22](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/cAVh7RwiKiTtKg) [S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

<More title={{ "en": "Different industrial paths", "zh": "不同的工业道路" }}>
