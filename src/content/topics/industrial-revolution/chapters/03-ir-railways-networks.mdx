<Lang en>

Returning from the previous chapter’s 1860 census to 1830, Liverpool and Manchester mark a railway carrying steam-hauled passengers and freight. The Stockton and Darlington Railway had opened in 1825. These are different milestones. Rocket, built in 1829 and successful at the Rainhill trials, should not be called the first train ever. [S76](https://historicengland.org.uk/whats-new/research/early-railways-in-england/) [S77](https://blog.scienceandindustrymuseum.org.uk/seg-if-these-walls-could-talk/) [S65](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co26704)

Railways did not all begin with steam traction. The Saint-Étienne coal railway in France used horse-drawn wagons in 1827. A line on a map therefore cannot by itself identify the power that moved its wagons. This matters when reading the continental railway layer: its dated segments are evidence of mapped construction, without a field that tells us each train’s power source. [S17](https://www.groupe-sncf.com/en/group/history-archives/two-centuries-railway-history) [S63](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/)

The Brussels–Mechelen service opened on 5 May 1835. Belgium-built Le Belge followed that December. The Nuremberg–Fürth railway also opened in 1835, in the German states. These events show distinct places, institutions and manufacturing histories; they do not assign a single industrial starting date to a later national territory. [S18](https://trainworld.be/en/collections/history-of-the-belgian-railways/2-belgium-on-track-19th-century/) [S19](https://dbmuseum.de/en/nuremberg/exhibitions/the-history-of-the-railway-in-germany)

In 1837 the Tsarskoye Selo public railway opened near Saint Petersburg. In India, passenger service between Bori Bunder and Thane began on 16 April 1853. Mumbai’s city point helps locate that story, without claiming to mark the exact station. Neither public passenger service nor the first line discussed here dates the beginning of all production in those regions. India already had varied manufacturing traditions, whose later changes differed by industry and place. [S21](https://cmzt.ru/events/news-events/otkrytie-czarskoselskoj-zheleznoj-do/) [S22](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/cAVh7RwiKiTtKg) [S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

<More title={{ "en": "Different industrial paths", "zh": "不同的工业道路" }}>

Jeff Horn interprets French industrialisation as a distinctive policy path, rather than a failed British imitation. Richard Tilly and Michael Kopsidis emphasise regional origins, institutional reforms, railways and heavy industry in the German case. Their interpretations compare processes, not just opening dates. [S60](https://academic.oup.com/mit-press-scholarship-online/book/14881) [S61](https://academic.oup.com/chicago-scholarship-online/book/37929)

Missing lines are a coverage limitation. The layer does not establish traction, continuing operation, traffic or prosperity. [S63](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/)

<Lang zh>

上一章最后看到 1860 年的普查，这里回到 1830 年。利物浦—曼彻斯特铁路以蒸汽机车运送旅客与货物；斯托克顿—达灵顿铁路则在 1825 年开通。Rocket 建于 1829 年，在雷恩希尔试验中胜出，却不能因此叫作世界上第一辆火车。[S76](https://historicengland.org.uk/whats-new/research/early-railways-in-england/) [S77](https://blog.scienceandindustrymuseum.org.uk/seg-if-these-walls-could-talk/) [S65](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co26704)

铁路不一定由蒸汽机车牵引。法国圣艾蒂安（Saint-Étienne）1827 年的运煤线使用马匹。因此，地图上有铁轨，不能单独证明列车用什么动力。欧洲大陆铁路层记录有年份的线段，却没有逐列火车的动力字段；建造记录与实际运营也不是同一回事。[S17](https://www.groupe-sncf.com/en/group/history-archives/two-centuries-railway-history) [S63](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/)

1835 年 5 月 5 日，布鲁塞尔—梅赫伦（Mechelen）铁路开通，当年 12 月又有比利时制造的 Le Belge 机车。德意志诸邦的纽伦堡—菲尔特铁路也于 1835 年开通。这些是不同地区的里程碑，不能合成后来整个国家的工业化起点。[S18](https://trainworld.be/en/collections/history-of-the-belgian-railways/2-belgium-on-track-19th-century/) [S19](https://dbmuseum.de/en/nuremberg/exhibitions/the-history-of-the-railway-in-germany)

1837 年，圣彼得堡附近的皇村铁路（Tsarskoye Selo railway）开通。1853 年 4 月 16 日，印度博里本德（Bori Bunder）至塔纳（Thane）开始客运。孟买点只是地区参照，不是精确车站坐标；印度制造业早已有多种传统，后来各行业的变化也不同。铁路层只记录取得的资料，没有线段的地区也可能有生产与贸易，不能用留白给人们的技能排名。[S21](https://cmzt.ru/events/news-events/otkrytie-czarskoselskoj-zheleznoj-do/) [S22](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/cAVh7RwiKiTtKg) [S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

<More title={{ "en": "Different industrial paths", "zh": "不同的工业道路" }}>

Jeff Horn 把法国工业化解释为有自身政策道路的发展，而不只是模仿英国失败。Richard Tilly 与 Michael Kopsidis 强调德意志地区起源、制度改革、铁路与重工业。这些解释比较过程，不只比较开通年份。[S60](https://academic.oup.com/mit-press-scholarship-online/book/14881) [S61](https://academic.oup.com/chicago-scholarship-online/book/37929)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "What the railway layer records", "zh": "铁路层记录什么" }}>

下载的 RShapes 记录从 1834 年开始。本题取到 1914 年的欧洲大陆子集，排除大不列颠与爱尔兰。缺线是覆盖范围的限制；它不能证明动力种类、持续营业、运量或富裕程度。[S63](https://icr.ethz.ch/data/rshapes/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Public services and calendar dates", "zh": "客运与历法" }}>

俄国铁路博物馆给出的日期是俄历 1837 年 10 月 30 日、公历 11 月 11 日，指同一事件。印度博里本德至塔纳的客运线长<Num s="S22">34</Num>公里；这只是该线的距离，不能衡量印度工业的范围。[S21](https://cmzt.ru/events/news-events/otkrytie-czarskoselskoj-zheleznoj-do/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Why the Rocket drawing needs a limit", "zh": "Rocket 图为什么要加限定" }}>

现存 Rocket 经过较大改建，而且并不完整。铁路博物馆还提醒，后来的绘图可能带入后加的部件。本图只是早期形态的解释性复原，不是当年的精密工程图，也不是保持 1829 年原状的实物照片。[S65](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co26704) [S66](https://blog.railwaymuseum.org.uk/rocket-railtrack-and-a-history-of-design/) [S67](https://tringlocalhistorymuseum.org.uk/morehistory/Railway/c12_locomotive_(II).htm)
