<Lang en>

Shanghai’s Jiangnan Arsenal was established in 1865. It made weapons, machinery and ships and supported technical translation. This late-Qing institution brings manufacturing and the movement of knowledge into the story together. China already had much older traditions of making goods. [S25](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/gmww/202104/t20210406_249554.shtml)

The Suez Canal opened on 17 November 1869, linking the Mediterranean and Red seas. Port Said and Suez mark the canal’s two ends. Around Singapore, <Term id="term-steamship">steamships</Term> and deeper berths helped shift port activity towards New Harbour near Tanjong Pagar. [S28](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=15fcae20-2e3d-4eec-b5ab-2384f1aac2b9)

NLB records <Num s="S28">99</Num> steamers and <Num s="S28">65</Num> sailing vessels visiting New Harbour’s wharves in 1869. For 1879 it gives <Num s="S28">541</Num> steamers and <Num s="S28">91</Num> sailing vessels. These are annual wharf records, not the size of a global fleet or all Singapore shipping. They show a change in recorded activity without measuring the canal’s effect on its own. [S28](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=15fcae20-2e3d-4eec-b5ab-2384f1aac2b9)

The <Term id="term-telegraph">telegraph</Term> transmitted messages with electrical signals. Cooke and Wheatstone patented and tested an electric telegraph in 1837; their plans included distinct uses for railways and personal communication. The Madras–Singapore cable was completed on 31 December 1870. An advertisement announcing the service followed on 6 January 1871. [S27](https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/cooke-and-wheatstones/) [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/)

Regional <Term id="term-gutta-percha">gutta-percha</Term> insulated submarine cables. Gutta-percha and plantation rubber were different materials with different uses. The cable carried electrical signals between Madras, now Chennai, and Singapore. [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/) [S83](https://ingenium.ca/en/learn/teachers-zone/resources/telegraph-key/)

<More title={{ "en": "What the harbour numbers count", "zh": "码头数字的口径" }}>

The records refer to vessels visiting New Harbour wharves in each stated year. The comparison keeps steamers and sailing vessels separate. It cannot identify each unique ship, reconstruct voyages, or turn wharf visits into the size of a fleet. [S28](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=15fcae20-2e3d-4eec-b5ab-2384f1aac2b9)

</More>

The generic key in the figure makes and breaks a circuit with a hand-operated lever. It is a transmitter control, not a steam valve or the receiving instrument. It does not reproduce a specific Cooke–Wheatstone device or Singapore terminal. [S83](https://ingenium.ca/en/learn/teachers-zone/resources/telegraph-key/) [S27](https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/cooke-and-wheatstones/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Ships, signals and materials", "zh": "船、信号与材料" }}>

A steamship uses an engine for propulsion. An electric telegraph sends signals through a circuit, while gutta-percha provides insulation. These technologies could connect the same ports while doing different work. A port could be part of shipping and electrical communication networks at the same time. [S27](https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/cooke-and-wheatstones/) [S28](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=15fcae20-2e3d-4eec-b5ab-2384f1aac2b9) [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/)

The <TopicLink slug="steam-engine" ch="follow-the-energy">engine’s energy path</TopicLink> follows heat and mechanical work. Its stationary model introduces steam power rather than a particular ship’s engine.

<Lang zh>

1865 年，江南制造局在上海建立，制造武器、机器与船舶，也开展技术翻译。这所晚清机构把制造与知识传播连在一起；在它之前，中国早已有悠久的生产传统。[S25](https://www.chnmuseum.cn/zp/zpml/gmww/202104/t20210406_249554.shtml)

1869 年 11 月 17 日，苏伊士运河开通，连接地中海与红海。塞得港和苏伊士分别位于运河两端。新加坡方面，<Term id="term-steamship">蒸汽船</Term>与更深的泊位，推动港口活动向丹戎巴葛附近的新港转移。[S28](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=15fcae20-2e3d-4eec-b5ab-2384f1aac2b9)

国家图书馆管理局记载，1869 年到访新港码头的蒸汽船为 <Num s="S28">99</Num> 艘，帆船 <Num s="S28">65</Num> 艘；1879 年分别为 <Num s="S28">541</Num> 艘与 <Num s="S28">91</Num> 艘。这是全年码头记录，不是全球船队或新加坡全部航运，也不能单凭这些记录算出运河的独立作用。[S28](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=15fcae20-2e3d-4eec-b5ab-2384f1aac2b9)

<Term id="term-telegraph">电报</Term>靠电信号传讯。库克与惠斯通在 1837 年取得专利并试验电报，铁路与私人通信是不同用途。马德拉斯—新加坡电缆在 1870 年 12 月 31 日完成，服务广告则见于 1871 年 1 月 6 日，在电缆完成后向公众介绍服务。[S27](https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/cooke-and-wheatstones/) [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/)

区域出产的<Term id="term-gutta-percha">古塔胶</Term>（gutta-percha）用于海底电缆绝缘，与种植园橡胶不同。电缆靠电信号传讯，连起马德拉斯（今钦奈）与新加坡。船舶运人运货，电缆传送消息，两种网络把港口联系起来。[S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/) [S83](https://ingenium.ca/en/learn/teachers-zone/resources/telegraph-key/)

<More title={{ "en": "What the harbour numbers count", "zh": "码头数字的口径" }}>

记录说的是所列年份到访新港码头的船，蒸汽船与帆船分开比较。它不能辨认每艘独立船舶、重建航程，或把到访记录改成船队规模。[S28](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=15fcae20-2e3d-4eec-b5ab-2384f1aac2b9)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "How an electrical key works", "zh": "电键怎样工作" }}>

图中的通用电键用手控杠杆接通、断开电路，是发送端的控制器，不是蒸汽阀或接收仪。它不复原某一件库克—惠斯通装置，也不冒充新加坡终端实物。[S83](https://ingenium.ca/en/learn/teachers-zone/resources/telegraph-key/) [S27](https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/cooke-and-wheatstones/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Ships, signals and materials", "zh": "船、信号与材料" }}>

蒸汽船以机器推动船身，电报靠电路传信，古塔胶提供绝缘。它们可能连到同样的港口，作用却不同。同一个港口，可以同时加入航运与电讯网络。[S27](https://journal.sciencemuseum.ac.uk/article/cooke-and-wheatstones/) [S28](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/article-detail?cmsuuid=15fcae20-2e3d-4eec-b5ab-2384f1aac2b9) [S29](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue2-jul-2007-tapping-history/)
