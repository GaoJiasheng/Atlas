<Lang en>

From a viewpoint in 1900, Saint Petersburg introduces a later Russian story. Malik describes foreign capital entering Russia around the turn of the century. This is distinct from the public railway opening in 1837. A dense network cannot reveal on its own who financed industry or how its benefits were shared. [S82](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv301gmn)

Workers' conditions changed through laws, organisation and public infrastructure as well as machinery. British legislation in 1844 required dangerous machinery to be guarded; the 1847 Act limited hours for women and young people in textile factories. The TUC describes the 1871 Trade Union Act as securing unions' legal status and protecting their funds. Public Health Acts in 1848 and 1875 developed responsibility for sanitation and water infrastructure. These earlier dates are being recalled, not moved to 1900. [S35](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/livinglearning/19thcentury/overview/laterfactoryleg/) [S37](https://www.tuc.org.uk/research-analysis/reports/section-tucs-first-victories) [S38](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/towncountry/towns/keydates/)

Coal smoke added another cost. Hanlon’s historical study links industrial coal pollution with mortality. It does not establish a fixed loss of life for every resident, and the coal shading here is geology, not a measured pollution field. Machine output, health and earnings answer different questions. [S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf) [S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/)

In India, <Term id="term-deindustrialisation">deindustrialisation</Term> describes declining manufacturing activity in a defined sector or region. Gupta and Roy discuss artisanal decline alongside the rise of modern cotton, jute and tea industries. Hand spinning, weaving and other crafts did not all follow the same path. No single national curve can preserve these differences. [S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

The chapter returns to Malaya and Singapore to keep labour within the picture. Contracts, recruitment and debt could constrain migrant workers, while slavery involved treating people as property. Better transport or greater production alone cannot tell us how free, safe or well paid a worker was. [S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/) [S44](https://www.wcfia.harvard.edu/publications/empire-cotton-0) [S50](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/exehis/v46y2009i4p418-435.html)

Hanlon reconstructs a historical connection between industrial coal use and mortality. This topic uses the qualitative finding. It does not display a lifespan estimate, a universal individual effect or a total “Industrial Revolution death toll”. The geological layer cannot substitute for historical exposure measurements. [S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf) [S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/)

Allen’s “Engels’ pause” interpretation describes wages lagging productivity in early nineteenth-century Britain, with a different pattern later. It is an attributed account, not a rule for every worker or country. Railway density measures neither wages nor health. [S50](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/exehis/v46y2009i4p418-435.html)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Indian industries followed different paths", "zh": "印度各行业的不同变化" }}>

Gupta and Roy compare changing crafts with growing modern industries. Their discussion of Clingingsmith and Williamson brings in political and ecological crises and changing international prices. This chapter follows Gupta and Roy’s discussion; it does not present a separate reading of the inaccessible original study. [S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

</More>

<Lang zh>

从 1900 年回望，圣彼得堡引出较晚的俄国故事。Malik 讨论世纪之交外国资本进入俄国，这与 1837 年公共铁路开通是不同问题。路网密集，不能单独说明谁出资、谁分享收益。[S82](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv301gmn)

机器以外，法律、工人组织与公共设施也改变生活。英国 1844 年立法要求危险机器加防护；1847 年法案限制纺织厂妇女与年轻人的工时。工会大会把 1871 年《工会法》描述为确立工会法律地位、保护基金的法律。1848 与 1875 年公共卫生法发展了卫生和供水责任。这些是回顾的旧日期，不是把立法改写到 1900 年。[S35](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/livinglearning/19thcentury/overview/laterfactoryleg/) [S37](https://www.tuc.org.uk/research-analysis/reports/section-tucs-first-victories) [S38](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/towncountry/towns/keydates/)

煤烟带来另一种代价。Hanlon 的历史研究把工业煤烟与死亡风险联系起来，并未证明每人都固定少活多少年。图中煤层是地质背景，不是污染实测；产量、健康与收入也不能合成同一个指标。[S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf) [S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/)

印度的<Term id="term-deindustrialisation">去工业化</Term>（deindustrialisation）须指明行业或地区。Gupta 与 Roy 并列讨论手工业衰退，以及现代棉纺、黄麻和茶业的发展。手纺、织布与其他行业并非同进同退。[S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

回到马来亚与新加坡，契约、招工和债务可能限制移民劳工的选择；奴隶制则把人当作财产。运输更快、产量更多，并不能单独证明劳工更自由、更安全或工资更高。[S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/) [S44](https://www.wcfia.harvard.edu/publications/empire-cotton-0) [S50](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/exehis/v46y2009i4p418-435.html)
