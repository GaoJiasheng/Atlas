<Lang en>

Around 1900, foreign investors were putting money into Russia. Malik describes these flows of capital at the turn of the century, long after the public railway opening of 1837. Financing industry and sharing its benefits were important parts of industrial change. [S82](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv301gmn)

Workers' conditions changed through laws, organisation and public infrastructure as well as machinery. British legislation in 1844 required dangerous machinery to be guarded; the 1847 Act limited hours for women and young people in textile factories. The TUC describes the 1871 Trade Union Act as securing unions' legal status and protecting their funds. Public Health Acts in 1848 and 1875 developed responsibility for sanitation and water infrastructure. By 1900, these laws formed part of a longer history of changing work and urban life. [S35](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/livinglearning/19thcentury/overview/laterfactoryleg/) [S37](https://www.tuc.org.uk/research-analysis/reports/section-tucs-first-victories) [S38](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/towncountry/towns/keydates/)

Coal smoke added another cost. Hanlon’s historical study links industrial coal pollution with mortality. The risks depended on people’s exposure to pollution. Greater machine output alone did not bring better health or higher earnings. [S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf) [S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/)

In India, <Term id="term-deindustrialisation">deindustrialisation</Term> describes declining manufacturing activity in a defined sector or region. Gupta and Roy discuss artisanal decline alongside the rise of modern cotton, jute and tea industries. Hand spinning, weaving and other crafts did not all follow the same path. A country could have expanding industries alongside declining crafts. [S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

In Malaya and Singapore, migrant labour helped link production and trade. Contracts, recruitment and debt could constrain migrant workers, while slavery involved treating people as property. Better transport or greater production alone cannot tell us how free, safe or well paid a worker was. [S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/) [S44](https://www.wcfia.harvard.edu/publications/empire-cotton-0) [S50](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/exehis/v46y2009i4p418-435.html)

<More title={{ "en": "What the smoke study can tell us", "zh": "煤烟研究能够说明什么" }}>

Hanlon’s research links historical industrial coal use with a higher risk of death. People’s exposure differed, so the study does not give every resident the same loss of life. Studying that risk requires evidence of pollution as well as the location of coal-bearing rocks. [S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf) [S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Output and wages need different evidence", "zh": "产量与工资须分别找证据" }}>

Allen’s “Engels’ pause” interpretation describes wages lagging productivity in early nineteenth-century Britain, with a different pattern later. Production and workers’ earnings could grow at different speeds. More railway lines alone do not show how much workers earned or how healthy they were. [S50](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/exehis/v46y2009i4p418-435.html)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Indian industries followed different paths", "zh": "印度各行业的不同变化" }}>

Gupta and Roy compare changing crafts with growing modern industries. Their discussion of Clingingsmith and Williamson brings in political and ecological crises and changing international prices. Gupta and Roy bring these explanations together when comparing India’s industries. [S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Coal underground and smoke in the air", "zh": "地下的煤与空气中的烟" }}>

A coal-bearing rock layer answers a geological question. It does not tell us the smoke concentration in Manchester in 1900. Coal-bearing rocks lie underground; smoke reaches people through the air. To study health, historians must also ask where and how much coal was burned, and who breathed the polluted air. [S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/) [S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf)

<Lang zh>

1900 年前后，外国投资者把资金投入俄国。Malik 研究世纪之交的外国资本流动；此时距离 1837 年公共铁路开通已过去多年。谁提供资金、谁分享收益，也是工业变化的一部分。[S82](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv301gmn)

机器以外，法律、工人组织与公共设施也改变生活。英国 1844 年立法要求危险机器加防护；1847 年法案限制纺织厂妇女与年轻人的工时。工会大会把 1871 年《工会法》描述为确立工会法律地位、保护基金的法律。1848 与 1875 年公共卫生法发展了卫生和供水责任。到 1900 年，这些法律已经成为工作与城市生活变化的历史。[S35](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/livinglearning/19thcentury/overview/laterfactoryleg/) [S37](https://www.tuc.org.uk/research-analysis/reports/section-tucs-first-victories) [S38](https://www.parliament.uk/about/living-heritage/transformingsociety/towncountry/towns/keydates/)

煤烟带来另一种代价。Hanlon 的历史研究把工业煤烟与死亡风险联系起来。人们接触污染的程度不同，承受的风险也不同；机器产量提高，并不自动带来更好的健康或更高的收入。[S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf) [S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/)

印度的<Term id="term-deindustrialisation">去工业化</Term>（deindustrialisation）须指明行业或地区。Gupta 与 Roy 并列讨论手工业衰退，以及现代棉纺、黄麻和茶业的发展。手纺、织布与其他行业并非同进同退。[S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

回到马来亚与新加坡，契约、招工和债务可能限制移民劳工的选择；奴隶制则把人当作财产。运输更快、产量更多，并不能单独证明劳工更自由、更安全或工资更高。[S59](https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-3-issue3-oct-2007-indian-migration-british-malaya-singapore/) [S44](https://www.wcfia.harvard.edu/publications/empire-cotton-0) [S50](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/exehis/v46y2009i4p418-435.html)

<More title={{ "en": "What the smoke study can tell us", "zh": "煤烟研究能够说明什么" }}>

Hanlon 的研究把历史上的工业用煤与较高的死亡风险联系起来。人们接触污染的程度不同，因此研究并不是说每个人都少活相同的年数。研究健康风险，既要知道哪里有煤，也要知道污染怎样影响人。[S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf) [S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Output and wages need different evidence", "zh": "产量与工资须分别找证据" }}>

Allen 的“恩格斯停顿”解释描述英国十九世纪早期工资落后于生产率、后来又有变化。生产与工人的收入可能以不同速度增长。铁路更多，也不一定说明工人工资更高、身体更健康。[S50](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/exehis/v46y2009i4p418-435.html)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Indian industries followed different paths", "zh": "印度各行业的不同变化" }}>

Gupta 与 Roy 比较手工业变化和现代产业增长；他们讨论 Clingingsmith 与 Williamson 时，还涉及政治、生态危机及国际价格变化。Gupta 与 Roy 把这些解释放在一起，比较印度不同行业的变化。[S49](https://academic.oup.com/book/7358/chapter/152152209)

</More>

<More title={{ "en": "Coal underground and smoke in the air", "zh": "地下的煤与空气中的烟" }}>

含煤地层回答地质问题，不能给出 1900 年曼彻斯特的煤烟浓度。煤藏在地下，烟则进入空气。研究健康影响，还要了解哪里烧了多少煤，以及哪些人呼吸了受污染的空气。[S64](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/datasets/coal-resources-for-new-technologies/) [S81](https://www.anderson.ucla.edu/sites/default/files/documents/areas/ctr/ziman/2015-18WP.pdf)
