<Lang en>

The list of exercises at the end of this chapter has all eight, grouped by equipment: barbell, dumbbell, body weight, cable and machine. Press one and the model takes up the exercise, the prime movers turn orange and the helpers blue-grey, and its card opens at the top of this panel with the muscles that drive the movement, the steps, cues, common mistakes and what research shows. The chips on a muscle sheet and the ticks of chapter 5 on the axis at the bottom lead to the same cards. The first time, start with body weight or a light load and learn the movement from a qualified coach; children and teenagers should train with an adult supervising [M25].

The eight exercises cover the three ways of loading the chest. **Presses** (bench press, incline dumbbell press, push-up, machine press) move the upper arm forward while the elbow straightens. **Flies** (dumbbell fly, cable fly) swing the arm in an arc with the elbow almost still, which is pure horizontal adduction. The **chest dip** pulls the arm down from behind the body. The **push-up plus** trains serratus anterior.

Is one of them the right choice for everyone? No. Research measures muscle activity with surface <Term id="emg">EMG</Term>, and higher EMG is not proof that a muscle grows more [M9]. In one study push-ups made as hard as a heavy bench press raised strength just as much [M17]. Choose exercises you can do with good form and equipment you have.

A few principles hold for all of them. Adults are advised to do muscle-strengthening activities on <Num s="M22">two or more days a week</Num>; Singapore's guidelines say the same [M26]. Light and heavy loads both build muscle when the sets are hard, though heavy loads build more maximal strength [M20]. Add load in small steps, only once the movement is easy [M23].

<More title={{ en: "A sample session", zh: "一节训练课示例" }}>

One common way to arrange a chest session, not a prescription: an easy push-up warm-up; the bench press as the main lift with safety bars or a spotter; a low-incline dumbbell press; a light cable fly; and push-up plus to finish. Big multi-joint lifts come first, lighter single-joint work later, and a hard chest session is followed by at least a day off for the chest [M21] [M23] [M24].

这八个动作涵盖了给胸部加负荷的三种方式。**推**（卧推、上斜哑铃卧推、俯卧撑、器械推胸）让上臂向前，同时伸直手肘。**夹**（哑铃飞鸟、绳索夹胸）让手臂沿弧线摆动，手肘几乎不动，是纯粹的水平内收。**双杠臂屈伸**把手臂从身体后方往下拉。**俯卧撑加**练的是前锯肌。

该选哪一个？没有适合所有人的唯一答案。研究用表面<Term id="emg">肌电</Term>测量肌肉活动，而肌电高并不能证明肌肉长得更多 [M9]。一项研究里，难度加到和大重量卧推相当的俯卧撑，提高的力量与卧推一样多 [M17]。选你能做得标准、手边有器械的动作就好。

有几条原则对所有动作都适用。建议成年人<Num s="M22">每周两天或以上</Num>做肌肉强化活动；新加坡的指南也这样建议 [M26]。只要每组都练得够吃力，轻重量和大重量都能增肌，不过大重量更能提高最大力量 [M20]。等动作变轻松了，再小步加重 [M23]。

一种常见的胸部训练安排，不是处方：先用简单的俯卧撑热身；主项是卧推，用保护杠或请人保护；接着做低角度上斜哑铃卧推；再做较轻的绳索夹胸；最后用俯卧撑加收尾。多关节的大动作放在前面，较轻的单关节动作放在后面；胸部练得很累之后，至少休息一天再练胸 [M21] [M23] [M24]。
