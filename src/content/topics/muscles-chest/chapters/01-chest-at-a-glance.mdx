<Lang en>

The model on the stage is a man's upper body, built from a real anatomical scan. The chest muscles are drawn in warm colours, the bones in ivory and the neighbouring muscles of the shoulder, arm, back and belly in grey. Left and right always mean the body's own left and right.

The muscles lie in three layers. On the surface is **pectoralis major**, a fan in three parts that covers most of the front of the chest. Under it, in the middle layer, are **pectoralis minor**, a thin triangle running up to the shoulder blade, and **serratus anterior**, the saw-toothed sheet on the side of the ribs. The deep layer holds **subclavius**, a small muscle tucked under the collarbone, and the two **intercostal** layers between the ribs.

Every muscle has an <Term id="origin">origin</Term>, the end that usually stays still, and an <Term id="insertion">insertion</Term>, the end it pulls. When a muscle contracts it shortens and draws the insertion towards the origin. That is all a muscle can do: pull, never push. Pressing a bar away from your chest is the chest muscles pulling your upper arms forward and across.

One line for each: pectoralis major moves the arm across the chest; pectoralis minor and subclavius steady the shoulder girdle; serratus anterior slides the shoulder blade forward around the ribs; the intercostals help you breathe [M1] [M2]. <FlyTo preset="front">See the front</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Where this model comes from", zh: "这个模型从哪里来" }}>

The meshes come from Z-Anatomy, an open atlas built on BodyParts3D, which was made from MRI scans of one adult male volunteer <Num s="M4">at 2 mm intervals</Num>. The model stands <Num s="M4">1.71 m</Num> tall. Atlas selected the chest structures, simplified them and added fibre directions and attachment points. The model and its geometry are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

No open model of the full female muscle system exists yet, so this series uses this one body. Real bodies vary: rib counts and attachment details differ between people.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Peel, not explode", zh: "剥层，而不是拆开" }}>

In this topic the explode slider is called PEEL. Instead of pulling every part outwards at once, it lifts the surface layer first, then the middle, then the deep layer, the order in which an anatomist would uncover them.

<Lang zh>

舞台上的模型是一名男性的上半身，根据真实的解剖扫描数据建成。胸部肌肉画成暖色，骨骼是象牙色，肩、上臂、背部和腹部的邻近肌肉画成灰色。文中说的左和右，一律指身体自己的左右。

这些肌肉分三层。最表面的是**胸大肌**，像一把分成三部分的扇子，盖住胸前的大部分。它下面的中层有**胸小肌**，一块向上长到肩胛骨的薄三角形；还有**前锯肌**，贴在胸廓侧面、边缘像锯齿的一片肌肉。深层有**锁骨下肌**，一块藏在锁骨下面的小肌肉；以及肋骨之间的两层**肋间肌**。

每块肌肉都有<Term id="origin">起点</Term>，通常是不动的那一端；还有<Term id="insertion">止点</Term>，是它拉动的那一端。肌肉收缩时变短，把止点拉向起点。肌肉能做的只有这一件事：拉，而不能推。把杠铃从胸口推开，其实是胸部肌肉把上臂向前、向里拉。

每块一句话：胸大肌把手臂拉向胸前；胸小肌和锁骨下肌稳住肩带；前锯肌让肩胛骨沿着肋骨往前滑；肋间肌帮你呼吸 [M1] [M2]。<FlyTo preset="front">看正面</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Where this model comes from", zh: "这个模型从哪里来" }}>

网格来自 Z-Anatomy，一个以 BodyParts3D 为基础的开源解剖图谱；BodyParts3D 则来自一名成年男性志愿者的核磁共振扫描，<Num s="M4">每隔 2 毫米一层</Num>。模型身高 <Num s="M4">1.71 米</Num>。Atlas 选取了胸部结构，做了简化，并加上纤维方向和附着点。模型及其几何数据按 CC BY-SA 4.0 许可共享。

目前还没有完整女性肌肉系统的开放模型，所以本系列都用这一具身体。真实的人各不相同：肋骨数目和附着细节因人而异。

</More>
