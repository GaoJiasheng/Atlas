<Lang en>

A muscle can only pull its insertion towards its origin. So the direction of its fibres, and the position the arm starts from, decide what a muscle does. Pectoralis major is the clearest example, because its fan of fibres pulls in many directions at once.

All three parts together bring the arm forward and across the chest, the movement of a hug, a fly or the top of a press. Anatomists call this <Term id="horizontal-adduction">horizontal adduction</Term> [M5]. The same pull also draws the arm into the side and turns it inward, because the tendon wraps the front of the humerus [M1].

The parts then divide the work. The **clavicular part**, running down from the collarbone, helps raise the arm forward up to about shoulder height, together with the front of the deltoid [M5] [M6]. The **sternocostal** and **abdominal** fibres, running up from below, do the opposite: when the arm starts high in front, they pull it back down towards the side [M1] [M5]. That is why the lower fibres work hard in a dip and the upper fibres in an incline press.

Below pectoralis major, **serratus anterior** and **pectoralis minor** move the shoulder blade forward around the ribs when you reach or push [M29] [M30], and the **external intercostals** lift the ribs when you breathe in [M2]. <FlyTo preset="left-side">See the side of the chest</FlyTo>

<More title={{ en: "Same muscle, different jobs", zh: "同一块肌肉，不同的工作" }}>

Whether a fibre helps a movement depends on which side of the joint it passes. Raise your arm overhead and the lower fibres of pectoralis major sit above the line they pull along, so they pull the arm down. Let the arm hang and the same fibres mostly squeeze it against the side. Anatomy references describe exactly this split: the clavicular part raises the arm, the sternocostal part pulls a raised arm back down [M5].

<Lang zh>

肌肉只能把止点拉向起点。所以纤维朝哪个方向走、手臂从什么位置开始，决定了一块肌肉起什么作用。胸大肌是最清楚的例子，因为它扇形的纤维同时朝很多方向拉。

三部分一起收缩，把手臂向前、向胸口方向拉过来，这就是拥抱、飞鸟、推到顶时的动作。解剖学上叫<Term id="horizontal-adduction">水平内收</Term> [M5]。同样的拉力还会把手臂拉向身体侧面，并让它向内旋，因为止腱是绕在肱骨前面的 [M1]。

接着，各部分分工。**锁骨部**从锁骨向下斜行，和三角肌前部一起，帮助手臂向前抬到大约肩膀高度 [M5] [M6]。**胸肋部**和**腹部**的纤维从下方向上斜行，作用正好相反：手臂在前方举高时，它们把手臂拉回身体两侧 [M1] [M5]。所以双杠臂屈伸时下部纤维出力多，上斜卧推时上部纤维出力多。

胸大肌下面，**前锯肌**和**胸小肌**在你伸手、推东西时，把肩胛骨沿着肋骨往前拉 [M29] [M30]；**肋间外肌**在吸气时把肋骨提起 [M2]。<FlyTo preset="left-side">看胸部侧面</FlyTo>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Same muscle, different jobs", zh: "同一块肌肉，不同的工作" }}>

一根纤维能不能帮上某个动作，要看它从关节的哪一侧经过。把手臂举过头顶，胸大肌的下部纤维就到了它拉力线的上方，于是把手臂往下拉；手臂自然下垂时，同样的纤维主要是把手臂夹向身体侧面。解剖学资料正是这样分工描述的：锁骨部把手臂抬起，胸肋部把抬起的手臂拉回 [M5]。

</More>
