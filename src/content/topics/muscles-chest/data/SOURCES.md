## Rig (parts.json `rig`)

Joints used: `shoulder.l/.r`, `elbow.l/.r` and `hand.l/.r`. The glb has no hand bone, so `hand.*` points at the wrist joint (`joint.wrist.*`): held equipment hangs at the wrist, about a hand's breadth short of the real grip. Axes are anatomical: shoulder horizontal adduction about the vertical axis (listed first so it acts in the trunk's frame), flexion about −X, abduction about +Z (left) / −Z (right), internal rotation about the humeral long axis from the pipeline's `rotation` axis, with the right side mirrored as an axial vector (x, −y, −z). The pipeline's scapula joint pivots at the glenoid, so turning it swings the medial border and the clavicle (which is skinned to it) about the shoulder rather than gliding the blade over the ribs; the rig therefore leaves the scapula joint out, and protraction is shown by contraction only (chapter 04 says so).

## Attachment review (parts.json `sites`)

Candidates from `scripts/anatomy/chest/attachments.json` were checked against Gray 1918 [M1] and OpenStax [M2]: each point was projected onto the named bone mesh (point-to-triangle distance), and rib-level attachments were identified by splitting the merged rib-cage mesh into its connected pieces (ribs 1–12, costal cartilages, manubrium, sternal body, xiphoid).
