<Lang en>

A <Term id="flowchart">flowchart</Term> sorts things with one question at a time, and each question has only two answers: yes or no. Singapore practice questions often show a flowchart, or the same questions set out as a table of ticks.

This flowchart asks two questions, so it makes three groups. Animals that answer yes to the first question go to group A. Of the rest, those that answer yes to the second go to group B. Everything else stays in group C.

The order matters. A good first question picks out something only one group has: feathers, hair or six legs. A poor first question picks out something several groups share. “Can it fly?” puts a bird, a bat and insects together. “Has scales?” mixes fish, reptiles and the pangolin. “Has fins?” puts the dolphin with the fish.

Here are the same questions as a table of ticks for ten of the animals. Each column is one question of a flowchart: read down a column to see which animals answer yes.

| Animal | Six legs | Feathers | Hair or fur | Fins | Scales | Moist skin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grasshopper | ✓ | – | – | – | – | – |
| Spider | – | – | – | – | – | – |
| Javan myna | – | ✓ | – | – | – | – |
| Penguin | – | ✓ | – | – | – | – |
| Plantain squirrel | – | – | ✓ | – | – | – |
| Fruit bat | – | – | ✓ | – | – | – |
| Dolphin | – | – | – | ✓ | – | – |
| Snakehead | – | – | – | ✓ | ✓ | – |
| Tree frog | – | – | – | – | – | ✓ |
| House gecko | – | – | – | – | ✓ | – |

The last step of classifying is to give each group a heading. The flowchart card does this for you: each group is named by the question that sent animals there. Now make a flowchart of your own in the Try it card, and try it on someone else.

<More title={{"en": "Tables and flowcharts", "zh": "打勾表和流程图"}}>

Practice questions in Singapore show both forms. One kind asks which animal goes to a group in a flowchart; another gives a table of ticks and asks you to choose suitable headings for two groups. The questions are the same; only the layout differs.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "What a key is", "zh": "什么是检索表"}}>

In England, pupils in Year 4 learn to use classification keys, and a key is the same idea as a flowchart: questions with only two answers, often yes or no, set out as a branching diagram or a table. In Chinese a key is called 检索表: it splits a group of living things into two again and again, using features that are easy to see.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Make one for someone else", "zh": "给别人做一张"}}>

Choose four to six animals you can see near home or school. Write yes-or-no questions about what you can see, starting with one that only one group answers yes to. Draw the flowchart and let a friend use it to name each animal. When you look for animals outside, watch them, draw them or photograph them, and leave them where they are.

</More>

<Term id="flowchart">分类流程图</Term>一次问一个问题来分组，每个问题只有两个答案：是，或不是。新加坡的练习题常常给一张流程图，或者把同样的问题写成一张打勾表。

这张流程图问两个问题，所以分成三组。对第一个问题答“是”的动物去 A 组。剩下的，对第二个问题答“是”的去 B 组。其余的都留在 C 组。

问题的顺序很要紧。好的第一个问题，挑出的是只有一类才有的特征：羽毛、毛发、六条腿。不好的第一个问题，挑出的是好几类共有的特征。“会飞吗”会把鸟、蝙蝠和昆虫放在一起；“有鳞片吗”会把鱼、爬行动物和穿山甲混在一起；“有鳍吗”会把海豚和鱼分在一起。

下面把同样的问题写成打勾表，列出其中十只动物。每一列就是流程图里的一个问题：顺着一列往下看，就知道哪些动物答“是”。

| 动物 | 六条腿 | 羽毛 | 毛发 | 鳍 | 鳞片 | 皮肤湿润 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 蝗虫 | ✓ | – | – | – | – | – |
| 蜘蛛 | – | – | – | – | – | – |
| 林八哥 | – | ✓ | – | – | – | – |
| 企鹅 | – | ✓ | – | – | – | – |
| 大蕉松鼠 | – | – | ✓ | – | – | – |
| 果蝠 | – | – | ✓ | – | – | – |
| 海豚 | – | – | – | ✓ | – | – |
| 斑鱼 | – | – | – | ✓ | ✓ | – |
| 树蛙 | – | – | – | – | – | ✓ |
| 壁虎 | – | – | – | – | ✓ | – |

分类的最后一步，是给每一组起一个标题。流程图卡已经替你做了：每一组的名字，就是把动物送到那里的那个问题。现在在“试一试”卡片里做一张你自己的流程图，再请别人来用一用。

<More title={{"en": "Tables and flowcharts", "zh": "打勾表和流程图"}}>

新加坡的练习题两种形式都有。有一种问：按流程图，某只动物会进哪一组；另一种给一张打勾表，要你给两组选出合适的标题。问题是一样的，只是排法不同。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "What a key is", "zh": "什么是检索表"}}>

英格兰四年级的学生学用“检索表”（classification key），它和流程图是同一个想法：每个问题只有两个答案，常常是“是”或“不是”，可以画成分叉的图，也可以写成表。中文里叫“检索表”：用容易看到的特征，把一群生物一次又一次地一分为二。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Make one for someone else", "zh": "给别人做一张"}}>

挑四到六种你在家附近或学校里看得到的动物。针对看得见的特征写几个“是 / 不是”的问题，第一个问题要选只有一类才答“是”的。画出流程图，请朋友用它说出每种动物的名字。到户外找动物时，看一看、画下来、拍张照，然后让它们留在原处。

</More>
