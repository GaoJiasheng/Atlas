<Lang en>

The practice has seven questions from the whole lesson, in a fixed order. They use the same kinds of task as the chapters: deciding whether a line is a line of symmetry, choosing symmetric figures and lines, and completing figures on square grid paper.

Question 1 asks whether a rectangle's diagonal is a line of symmetry. Question 2 asks you to choose every symmetric figure from four; one of them lies on a slant. Question 3 asks which lines are lines of symmetry of a square; more than one is right. Question 4 shows a trapezium lying on its side and asks which line is its line of symmetry. Questions 5 and 6 ask you to complete figures made of squares, one with half squares and a line across. Question 7 asks you to complete a figure drawn with lines by putting down the partner of each corner.

Each question is checked once. When you press **Check**, you see straight away whether the answer is right and why. If it is not right, the stage shows the answer, and a button takes you back to the step that teaches it. The answer cannot be changed after the check, so take your time.

Here **Fold it** is not there: fold each figure in your head before you check. Each question has one hint, and using it costs nothing.

After the last question, a short table lists each question with a tick or a circle and the step to look at again. **Try again** starts the same seven questions from the beginning. Nothing is timed, and nothing is saved.

Question 1 is on the stage, with its answer controls above this text. The squares on the chapter axis are the seven questions.

<Lang zh>

练习有 7 道题，覆盖全课，顺序固定。题型和各章一样：判断一条线是不是对称轴，选出对称图形和对称轴，在方格纸上补全图形。

第 1 题问长方形的对角线是不是对称轴。第 2 题要你从四个图形里选出所有对称图形，其中一个是斜放的。第 3 题问哪几条线是正方形的对称轴，答案不止一个。第 4 题是一个侧放的梯形，问哪条线是它的对称轴。第 5、6 题补全用格子拼的图形，其中一道有半格，对称轴是横的。第 7 题补全一个用线画的图形：放下每个顶点的对应点。

每题只检查一次。按**检查**，马上就知道对不对、为什么。如果没对，舞台会画出答案，还有一个按钮带你回到讲这一点的那一步。检查以后答案不能再改，所以慢慢来。

这里没有“折一折”：检查之前，先在脑子里把图形折一折。每题有一条提示，看了不扣什么。

做完最后一题，有一张小表列出每题的 ✓ 或 ○，以及要回看的步。**再做一次**从头再做这 7 道题。不计时，也不保存。

第 1 题已经在舞台上，答题的按钮在这段文字上方。章节轴上的小方块就是这 7 道题。
