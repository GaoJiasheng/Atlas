<Lang en>

In step 04 you found the partners of squares and points. Now you put that together to complete whole figures, including figures drawn with slanted lines and figures made with half squares.

Some figures are drawn with lines. To complete one, <Beat n={1}>work corner by corner</Beat>. Find the partner of each corner first, counting from the line on the dot grid. Then join the partners in the same order as the corners on the given half. The new half is drawn in signal, and its corners meet the given half on the line.

Figures made of squares can have <Beat n={2}>half squares</Beat> too. A <Term id="half-square">half square</Term> has a slanted side. When it flips across the line, its slant turns the other way: a half square that leans right leans left in its partner. On the left the half square has been flipped. On the right its slant has not been turned over, so that half does not match.

Some parts of a figure <Beat n={3}>lie on the line</Beat> itself. Here a column of squares sits across the line of symmetry. Those squares are their own partners: folding leaves them where they are, so you do not copy them. Only the squares off the line need partners.

When you have finished, <Beat n={4}>check by folding</Beat>. Fold along the line in your head, or press Fold it in a question. If the half you made lands exactly on the given half, the figure is complete. If something sticks out, look for the part that was slid instead of flipped, a partner that is too near or too far, or a half square whose slant was not turned.

When you are ready, <Task id="completing-figures">complete a figure</Task> on the grid.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

第 04 步里你找了格和点的对应部分。现在把这些合起来，补全整个图形，包括用斜线画的图形和有半格的图形。

有的图形是用线画的。补全这样的图形，<Beat n={1}>一个顶点一个顶点地来</Beat>。先在点阵上从对称轴数格，找出每个顶点的对应点；再按给的那一半上顶点的顺序，把对应点连起来。新的一半用信号色画出，它的两头在对称轴上和给的那一半接上。

用格子拼的图形也可能有<Beat n={2}>半格</Beat>。<Term id="half-square">半格</Term>有一条斜边。它翻过对称轴的时候，斜的方向也反过来：朝右斜的半格，它的对应部分朝左斜。左边的半格翻过来了；右边那个半格的斜边没有翻过来，所以这一半不重合。

图形有的部分<Beat n={3}>正好在对称轴上</Beat>。这里有一列格子横跨在对称轴上。这些格子自己就是自己的对应部分：对折时它们原地不动，所以不用再画一遍。只有不在轴上的格子才要找对应格。

画完以后，<Beat n={4}>折一折来检查</Beat>。在脑子里沿对称轴折，或者做题时按“折一折”。你补的一半正好落在给的一半上，图形就补全了。如果有露出来的部分，就找一找：是不是有一块挪过去了而没有翻，有没有对应点离轴太近或太远，有没有半格的斜边没有翻过来。

准备好了，就<Task id="completing-figures">补全一个图形</Task>。

</Lang>
