<Lang en>

So far you have tested figures that were already drawn. Now you start drawing: you are given one half of a figure and its line of symmetry, and you find the other half. On square grid paper, the squares let you count.

Start with <Beat n={1}>one shaded square</Beat>. Count from the square to the dashed line: it is 2 squares away. When the paper is folded, the square lands straight across the line, 2 squares from it on the other side. That square is its <Term id="partner-point">partner</Term>.

A bigger piece works the same way, <Beat n={2}>square by square</Beat>. For each square, count how far it is from the line, then count the same number on the other side, straight across. Each square of this step shape finds its own partner, and together the partners make the other half.

Now <Beat n={3}>compare two answers</Beat>. On the left, the piece has been flipped across the line: the part nearest the line stays nearest the line, and the step faces the other way. On the right, the piece has been slid across without turning over. It has the right squares, but the step faces the wrong way, so the right-hand figure does not fold onto itself. Flip it across the line; do not slide it.

When the line goes <Beat n={4}>across instead of up</Beat>, count up and down instead. This point is 3 squares above the line, so its partner is 3 squares below it. A common slip is to put the partner right next to the line, shown here with a dashed circle. Count every time.

So a point and its partner are always the same distance from the line, on opposite sides, and the line joining them runs straight across it.

When you are ready, <Task id="mirror-on-grid">find some partners</Task> and complete a figure on the grid.

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

到现在为止，你检验的都是已经画好的图形。现在开始动手画：给你图形的一半和它的对称轴，你来找出另一半。在方格纸上，可以数格子。

先看<Beat n={1}>一个涂色的格</Beat>。从这个格数到虚线，离了 2 格。把纸对折，这个格正对着翻过对称轴，落在另一边，也离轴 2 格。那个格就是它的<Term id="partner-point">对应格</Term>。

大一点的图形也一样，<Beat n={2}>一格一格地找</Beat>。每个格先数它离轴几格，再在另一边正对着数同样的格数。这个台阶形的每个格都找到自己的对应格，合起来就是另一半。

再<Beat n={3}>比一比两种答案</Beat>。左边是把这一块翻过对称轴：离轴最近的部分还是离轴最近，台阶朝向反了过来。右边是把这一块挪过去，没有翻：格子数对了，可是台阶朝错了方向，所以右边的图形不能折得重合。要翻过去，不是挪过去。

对称轴<Beat n={4}>是横着的</Beat>时候，就改成上下数。这个点在轴上面 3 格，它的对应点就在轴下面 3 格。常见的失误是把对应点画在紧挨着轴的地方，这里用虚线圆圈标了出来。每次都要数，而且要从对称轴开始数，不要从图形的边上数。

所以，一个点和它的对应点，总在对称轴两边，离轴一样远；连接它们的线正对着横穿对称轴。

准备好了，就<Task id="mirror-on-grid">找一找对应格</Task>，在方格上补全一个图形。

</Lang>
