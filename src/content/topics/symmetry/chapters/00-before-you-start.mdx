<Lang en>

Fold a sheet of paper in half and cut a shape along the fold. When you open it, the two sides are the same, turned over. This lesson is about that idea: a figure that folds onto itself.

On the stage now is a house shape on square grid paper. Its left half is being folded over the dashed line in the middle, part of the way. In the chapters, folds like this run all the way: the half turns over and lands on the other half. Where the two halves do not match, the stage hatches the part that sticks out.

There are 5 steps and a practice. Step 01 folds figures to see whether their halves match. Step 02 tests a given line: is it a line of symmetry or not? Step 03 looks at many symmetric figures, some with more than one line, some on a slant. Steps 04 and 05 complete a figure on square grid paper when you are given one half and the line. The last chapter is a practice of seven questions.

Each chapter explains first. The circles on the chapter axis at the bottom are its beats: tap one, or press **,** and **.**, and the stage folds or changes, while the reading panel says what to look at.

The square at the end of each chapter is one short question, drawn from a set of nine or ten. In the reading panel you can:

- **Try it**: choose an answer, or tap squares, half squares and grid points to finish a figure.
- **Check** (C): if it is not right yet, the feedback says why and where to look. After two tries, **Show me** draws the answer dashed.
- **Hint** (I): a clue at any time, each one closer to the answer. After your first check or your second hint, **Fold it** folds the figure over so you can look.
- **Another one**, or **Skip**: nothing is forced.

Every question can be done by tapping alone, or with the keyboard alone. Nothing is timed and nothing is saved.

<Lang zh>

把一张纸对折，沿着折痕剪一个形状，打开以后，两边一模一样，只是翻了个面。这节课讲的就是这个：一个图形能折得和自己重合。

舞台上现在是方格纸上的一个小屋图形。它的左半边正沿着中间那条虚线往右翻，翻到了一部分。在各章里，这样的对折会一直翻到底：一半翻过去，落在另一半上。两半不重合的地方，舞台会用斜线标出露出来的部分。

全课有 5 步和一章练习。第 01 步把图形折一折，看两半是否重合。第 02 步检验给出的一条线：它是不是对称轴？第 03 步看很多对称图形，有的不止一条对称轴，有的斜放着。第 04、05 步给出一半图形和对称轴，在方格纸上把图形补全。最后一章是 7 道练习题。

每一章都先讲。屏幕底部章节轴上的小圆点就是这一章的拍：点一下，或者按 **,** 和 **.**，舞台上的图形就会折起或换一个，阅读面板告诉你该看哪里。

每章最后的小方块是一道小题，从九、十道题里挑一道。在阅读面板里你可以：

- **动手做**：选一个答案，或者点格子、半格和格点，把图形补完整。
- **检查**（C）：还没对时，反馈会说明原因和该看哪里；试两次以后，可以按“看答案”，答案用虚线画出来。
- **提示**（I）：随时可以看，一条比一条接近答案。第一次检查以后，或者看了第二条提示以后，可以按“折一折”，把图形折过来看一看。
- **再来一道**或**跳过**：做不做都由你。

每道题都能只用点按完成，也能只用键盘完成。不计时，也不保存。
