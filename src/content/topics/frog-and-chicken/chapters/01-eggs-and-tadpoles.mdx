<Lang en>

Look closely at the small group of frog eggs. Each jelly coat surrounds an <Term id="embryo">embryo</Term>, a young animal developing inside the egg. Only a small part of the egg group, or clutch, is shown here. Different frogs do not all lay the same number of eggs.

The young frog comes out as a <Term id="tadpole">tadpole</Term>. Coming out of an egg is called <Term id="hatching">hatching</Term>. Look at the swimming tadpole beside the eggs. It has a rounded body and a long tail, with no visible legs. The tail moves it through the water. <FlyTo preset="frog-overview">Follow the frog's growth</FlyTo>

Now compare the tadpole with the adult frog. Their shapes are very different, yet they are stages in the life of the same kind of animal. A tadpole is a young frog. It is not a fish that later turns into a frog. Following the sequence from the egg helps us understand these changes.

Choose the egg and tadpole in turn with the frog control. Look for the body and tail, then compare them with the adult's limbs. The control selects a stage to inspect. Development time depends on the frog species and its surroundings, so there is no single number of days that fits all frogs.

<More title={{"en": "Frogs that hatch as froglets", "zh": "孵出时已是幼蛙的蛙"}}>

Many frogs have a tadpole stage that lives freely outside the egg. Some frogs develop inside the egg and hatch as froglets instead. The pond-frog sequence on this tray is one common pathway.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "A stage and an age", "zh": "阶段与年龄"}}>

A stage name describes the animal's form. It does not tell us its exact age. Two frogs at a similar stage may have taken different amounts of time to get there.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

近看这一小团蛙卵。每个胶质包被都围着一个<Term id="embryo">胚胎</Term>，也就是在卵内发育的幼体。这里仅画了卵团的一小部分。不同的蛙产卵数量不一样，不能靠数模型里的卵，判断每只蛙会产多少卵。

幼体从卵里出来时是<Term id="tadpole">蝌蚪</Term>。从卵中出来叫<Term id="hatching">孵出</Term>。看看卵旁边游泳的蝌蚪：身体圆圆的，后面连着长尾巴，还看不到腿。尾巴摆动，带着身体在水中前进。<FlyTo preset="frog-overview">观察青蛙的成长</FlyTo>

再把蝌蚪和成蛙比较。它们的外形很不一样，却是同一种动物一生中的不同阶段。蝌蚪是青蛙的幼体，不是一条后来变成青蛙的鱼。沿着卵、蝌蚪和后面的阶段观察，就能看清身体怎样逐渐改变。

用青蛙控件轮流选中卵和蝌蚪。找一找蝌蚪的身体与尾巴，再和成蛙的四肢比较。控件让我们选择要看的阶段。发育所需的时间会随蛙种和周围条件而改变，没有一个适用于所有青蛙的天数。
