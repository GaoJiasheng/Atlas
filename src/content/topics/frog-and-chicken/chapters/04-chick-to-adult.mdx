<Lang en>

At hatching, the chick uses its beak and a temporary egg tooth to open the shell. It then comes out, and its soft down dries. The young bird has already developed inside the egg before this moment. The model's opened shell helps us look at this point in the life cycle.

Move the chicken pointer to the chick. Look for its short beak, wings and legs. Although it already has a bird's body shape, it is still young. The chick continues to grow into an adult chicken. It does not pass through a pupa.

Compare the chick with the adult hen. Both have the body form of a bird, but their proportions and feathers differ. The chick is not simply a small copy of the hen. A hen is a female chicken. A cock is a male chicken, and it too can be an adult.

Follow the last link towards the egg. The adult hen remains an adult while reproduction starts a new generation. Choose the egg, chick and adult in turn to compare these stages. Returning the pointer to the egg lets you look again; it does not turn the adult back into an egg.

<More title={{"en": "Comparing body shapes", "zh": "比较身体形态"}}>

The egg, chick and hen stay on the tray so that you can compare them. Each has been enlarged separately. Look at their shapes and features rather than measuring their lengths.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Fowl in Singapore", "zh": "新加坡的鸡"}}>

NParks describes Singapore's roaming flocks as a mixture of domestic fowl and Red Junglefowl. The hen shown here represents a domestic chicken. Use a field guide to identify birds outdoors; this simplified model cannot show an individual bird's ancestry or conservation status.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

孵出时，雏鸡用喙和暂时具有的卵齿打开蛋壳，再从卵中出来。出来以后，柔软的绒羽逐渐干燥。在这以前，幼鸟已经在卵内发育。模型打开的蛋壳，帮助我们观察生活史中的这个时刻。

把鸡的指示珠移到雏鸡。找一找它短短的喙、翅和足。它已经具有鸟类的身体形态，却仍然是一只幼鸟，还要继续生长。雏鸡逐渐长成成鸡，中间不经过蛹期。

再把雏鸡和成年母鸡比较。它们都有鸟类的身体形态，但身体比例和羽毛不同。雏鸡并不是把母鸡简单缩小得到的样子。母鸡是雌性的鸡，公鸡是雄性的鸡；公鸡也可以是成鸡。

沿着最后一段连接，回到卵的位置。母鸡仍然是成体，繁殖让下一代开始。用控件轮流选中卵、雏鸡和成鸡，看看每个阶段的特点。把指示珠移回卵，只是重新观察，不表示成鸡又变回了卵。

<More title={{"en": "Comparing body shapes", "zh": "比较身体形态"}}>

卵、雏鸡和母鸡都留在展台上，方便来回比较。各模型分别放大。观察时，比较它们的形态和特征，不要用模型量实际身长。

</More>
