<Lang en>

An unbroken shell hides the changes inside a chicken egg. The chick begins developing before it hatches. Open the model's shell window to look inside. The window is a cut made for this display, and the embryo is a simple outline without an exact age.

Find the embryo and the <Term id="yolk">yolk</Term> beside it. The yolk supplies food for the developing chick. It is a different structure from the embryo. Egg white surrounds the yolk. Select each part to see where it lies. <FlyTo preset="egg-close">Look inside the egg</FlyTo>

A chick can develop only from a fertilised egg. <Term id="fertilisation">Fertilisation</Term> happens when male and female reproductive cells join. In a chicken, this happens before the shell forms and the egg is laid. Warming an unfertilised egg cannot make it fertilised.

Keep the window open. Predict whether you will see a developing embryo when you choose the unfertilised example, then try it. A fertilised egg also needs suitable incubation conditions to develop successfully. Chicken eggs normally hatch after about <Num s="S8">21 days</Num> of suitable incubation. Count from the start of incubation, rather than the day an egg is bought. Even a fertilised egg may fail to hatch.

<More title={{"en": "Gases pass through the shell", "zh": "气体能穿过蛋壳"}}>

Tiny pores in the shell let oxygen enter and carbon dioxide leave as the chick develops. These pores are too small to see on this model.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Seeing the embryo", "zh": "查看胚胎"}}>

Open the window and choose the fertilised example to see the embryo. Closing the window covers it. In the unfertilised example, there is no developing chick to reveal. Read both the egg condition and the window setting when comparing the views.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

蛋壳还完整时，我们看不到鸡卵内部的变化。雏鸡在孵出前，就已经在卵内开始发育。打开模型的壳窗，看看里面。这个切口方便我们观察；里面的胚胎是简化的轮廓，没有表示确切的日龄。

找出胚胎和旁边的<Term id="yolk">卵黄</Term>。卵黄为发育中的小鸡提供营养，与胚胎是不同的结构。卵白围在卵黄外面。分别选中这些部分，看看它们的位置，便能分清卵黄、卵白和小鸡的胚胎。<FlyTo preset="egg-close">观察卵的内部</FlyTo>

只有受精卵才可能发育出雏鸡。<Term id="fertilisation">受精</Term>是雄性和雌性生殖细胞结合。在鸡的体内，受精发生在蛋壳形成、卵产出之前。给未受精的卵保温，并不能使它受精。

保持壳窗打开。先猜一猜，选中未受精的例子后，会不会看见发育中的胚胎，再动手查看。受精卵还需要适宜的孵化条件，才能顺利发育。鸡卵通常经过约 <Num s="S8">21 天</Num>的适宜孵化后孵出。这个时间从孵化开始算，不是从买到卵的那天算。即使已经受精，卵也可能无法孵出。

<More title={{"en": "Gases pass through the shell", "zh": "气体能穿过蛋壳"}}>

雏鸡发育时，氧气从蛋壳的细小气孔进入，二氧化碳从气孔排出。这些气孔很小，模型没有画出。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Seeing the embryo", "zh": "查看胚胎"}}>

打开壳窗、选择受精的例子，就能看见胚胎。关窗会把它遮住。未受精的例子则没有发育中的小鸡可看。比较视图时，要一起查看卵是否受精、壳窗是否打开。

</More>

</Lang>
