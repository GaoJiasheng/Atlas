<Lang en>

The frog and hen on this display are mature animals. An <Term id="adult">adult</Term> has reached the stage when it can reproduce. Compare them with the younger animals around them. A young frog looks very different from an adult frog. A young chicken already has the body shape of a bird.

A <Term id="life-cycle">life cycle</Term> follows how living things develop and produce the next generation. Start with an <Term id="egg">egg</Term>, then follow the young animal as it grows towards adulthood. On the chicken tray, the young bird is a <Term id="chick">chick</Term>. It must grow before it can reproduce.

Follow each ring to its last link. This link shows <Term id="reproduction">reproduction</Term>: producing offspring. The eggs that begin the next round belong to a new generation. The adult does not become an egg again. Growth changes one animal; reproduction begins the lives of new animals.

Use the controls to choose an animal to inspect. The dark bead marks your choice. You can move it forwards or backwards to compare the stages. The animals stay in place, and real animals develop only forwards.

<More title={{"en": "Size and time", "zh": "大小与时间"}}>

Each stage has been enlarged separately to make its shape clear. The gaps between stages do not show how long development takes. The models show a few points in a continuous process, rather than every change.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Across generations", "zh": "一代接一代"}}>

A life-cycle ring connects one generation with the next. It does not mean that one animal lives for ever. Look for the links marked “next generation” on both trays.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

展台上的成蛙和母鸡都已成熟。<Term id="adult">成体</Term>是已经成熟、能够繁殖的动物。再看看它们周围的幼小动物。青蛙的幼体和成体差别很大；幼小的鸡已经具有鸟类的身体形态。先分别观察两个环，再比较它们怎样成长。

<Term id="life-cycle">生命周期</Term>（也叫生活史）说明生物怎样发育，并产生下一代。从<Term id="egg">卵</Term>开始，沿着环观察幼体怎样长成成体。鸡的幼体叫<Term id="chick">雏鸡</Term>。它还要继续生长，成年后才能繁殖。

沿着环找到最后一段连接。它表示<Term id="reproduction">繁殖</Term>，也就是产生后代。下一轮的卵属于新一代，并不是成体又变回卵。生长改变的是同一只动物；繁殖则让新的生命开始。

用控件选择要细看的阶段，深色小珠会标出你选的位置。你可以向前、向后移动小珠，反复比较不同阶段。展台上的动物留在原处。真实动物的发育只会向前，不会因为小珠往回移动就倒着生长。

<More title={{"en": "Size and time", "zh": "大小与时间"}}>

各阶段分别放大，让我们看清形状。它们之间的距离不表示发育所需的时间。真实发育是连续的，模型只选出了其中几个便于观察的样子。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Across generations", "zh": "一代接一代"}}>

生命周期的环形图把上一代和下一代连起来，不表示同一只动物会永远活下去。找一找两个展台上标着“下一代”的连接。

</More>
