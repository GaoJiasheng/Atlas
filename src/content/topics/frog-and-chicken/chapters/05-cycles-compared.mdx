<Lang en>

Use the comparison tray to put the sequences side by side. In this frog example, the young is a free-swimming tadpole before the body changes into frog form. The chicken hatches as a chick with a bird body form, then grows into an adult. Both sequences include development and reproduction, but their visible changes differ.

Select the mosquito comparison. Its sequence includes egg, larva, pupa and adult. The larva and pupa develop in water, and the adult emerges after the pupa. The frog does not need an extra pupa added to its sequence. One animal's stage names cannot simply be copied onto another. <TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="round-the-cycle">Follow the mosquito's full cycle</TopicLink>, or inspect <TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="pupa-to-adult">the change from pupa to adult</TopicLink>.

The flowering plant comparison begins with a seed, followed by a young plant and an adult plant. A <Term id="seedling">seedling</Term> is a young plant that has developed from a seed. Do not rename the seed an animal egg. <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="seed-to-plant">From seed to plant</TopicLink> follows that growth, while <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="fruits-and-seeds">Fruits and seeds</TopicLink> continues the plant's story.

You can choose a different starting point when reading a cycle. The order of development and the link to a new generation still matter. Real life also needs suitable environments. Singapore conservation work moved Cinnamon Bush Frog tadpoles to suitable Botanic Gardens sites, where breeding was later observed. That is a local species example, not the identity of the generic frog model. Observe animals where they live; this display is not a plan for collecting or moving them.

The eggs in the figure are mosquito eggs on a wall above the water, with a larva and a pupa at the surface. They are not the jelly-coated frog eggs seen earlier. <TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="eggs-and-larvae">Eggs and larvae</TopicLink> explains the mosquito example in more detail.

<More title={{"en": "What a cycle leaves out", "zh": "环图没有画全的部分"}}>

A simple ring leaves out much variation. Not every egg hatches, and animals can die at different stages. The specimens show an ordered pathway without assigning survival chances or promising that every individual completes it. More detailed plant reproduction belongs in <TopicLink slug="flower-and-seed">Flowers and Seeds</TopicLink>.

</More>

<Lang zh>

在比较台上，把不同的顺序放到一起看。这个青蛙例子先有自由游泳的蝌蚪，身体再改变成蛙形；鸡孵出时是具有鸟类身体形态的雏鸡，然后继续长成成鸡。两个顺序都包括发育与繁殖，但看得见的变化并不一样。

选中蚊子的对照。蚊子的顺序包括卵、幼虫、蛹、成虫，幼虫和蛹在水中发育，之后成虫出现。不能因此给青蛙也加上蛹期。一种动物的阶段名称，不能直接搬到另一种动物身上。可以继续看<TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="round-the-cycle">蚊子的完整生活史</TopicLink>，或细看<TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="pupa-to-adult">从蛹到成虫</TopicLink>。

开花植物的比较用种子、幼株和成株。由种子发育出来的幼小植物叫<Term id="seedling">幼苗</Term>，不能把种子改叫动物的卵。<TopicLink slug="plant" ch="seed-to-plant">从种子到植物</TopicLink>继续讲它的生长，<TopicLink slug="plant" ch="fruits-and-seeds">果实与种子</TopicLink>接着讲植物的故事。

读生活史可以换一个起点，发育顺序和通向下一代的连接仍然重要。真实生命还需要适宜环境。新加坡的保育工作曾把 Cinnamon Bush Frog 的蝌蚪迁到植物园的适宜地点，后来观察到繁殖。这是本地蛙种的例子，不是说通用模型画的就是它。观察动物原来的生活环境；这个展示不是自行收集或搬移动物的方案。
