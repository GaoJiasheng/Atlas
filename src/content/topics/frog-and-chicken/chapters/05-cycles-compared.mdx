<Lang en>

Look across the comparison tray. In this example, the frog hatches as a swimming tadpole, then changes into frog form. The chicken hatches as a chick, already shaped like a bird. Both develop and later reproduce, but their bodies change in different ways.

Choose the mosquito. Its sequence is egg, larva, pupa and adult. The larva and pupa develop in water. The adult emerges after the pupa. Frogs have no pupa stage, so we cannot copy all the mosquito's stage names onto a frog. <TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="round-the-cycle">Follow the mosquito's full cycle</TopicLink>, or look at <TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="pupa-to-adult">the change from pupa to adult</TopicLink>.

The flowering plant row shows a seed, a young plant and an adult plant. A <Term id="seedling">seedling</Term> is a young plant that grows from a seed. A seed is different from an animal egg. <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="seed-to-plant">From seed to plant</TopicLink> follows its growth. <TopicLink slug="plant" ch="fruits-and-seeds">Fruits and seeds</TopicLink> explains how the cycle continues.

You can read a cycle from any starting point, as long as you follow the order of growth and the link to a new generation. Real animals also need suitable places to live. Singapore conservation workers moved Cinnamon Bush Frog tadpoles to suitable Botanic Gardens sites, where breeding was later observed. This is a local species example. The tray shows a general pond frog, without naming a species. Observe wild animals in their habitats and leave them there.

<Lang zh>

看看比较台上的几种生活史。这里的青蛙孵出后是会游泳的蝌蚪，身体再逐渐变成蛙形。鸡孵出时已经是具有鸟类形态的雏鸡。它们都会发育、繁殖，但身体的变化方式不同。

选中蚊子。它的顺序是卵、幼虫、蛹、成虫。幼虫和蛹在水中发育，成虫在蛹之后出现。青蛙没有蛹期，所以不能把蚊子的阶段名称全部搬到青蛙身上。继续看<TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="round-the-cycle">蚊子的完整生活史</TopicLink>，或细看<TopicLink slug="mosquito" ch="pupa-to-adult">从蛹到成虫</TopicLink>。

开花植物这一排展示种子、幼株和成株。由种子长出的幼小植物叫<Term id="seedling">幼苗</Term>。种子与动物的卵不同。<TopicLink slug="plant" ch="seed-to-plant">从种子到植物</TopicLink>介绍它的生长，<TopicLink slug="plant" ch="fruits-and-seeds">果实与种子</TopicLink>继续说明下一代怎样开始。

读生活史可以从任何一点开始，但要按发育顺序，并分清哪一段连接到下一代。真实动物还需要适宜的生活环境。新加坡保育人员曾把 Cinnamon Bush Frog 的蝌蚪迁到植物园的适宜地点，后来在那里观察到繁殖。这是本地蛙种的例子；展台画的是没有指定物种的通用池塘型青蛙。观察野生动物时，让它们留在原来的栖息地。
