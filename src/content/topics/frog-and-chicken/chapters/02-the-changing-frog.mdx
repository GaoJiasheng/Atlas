<Lang en>

Begin with the tadpole that has hind legs. A pair of back legs is visible beside the tail. The front legs have not yet appeared. Compare this tadpole with the earlier swimming stage. The body is changing as the same young frog develops.

Next, inspect the <Term id="froglet">froglet</Term>, a young frog with a frog-shaped body. It has front and hind limbs, but a short tail is still attached. The large change from tadpole to frog is called <Term id="metamorphosis">metamorphosis</Term>. Several changes happen together, so the animal can have both limbs and a tail for a time.

Before the close view, predict what will happen to the tail. Then look at the third beat. The body gradually absorbs the tail tissues and the tail becomes shorter. It does not break off or fall behind the frog. The adult has no tadpole tail. A <Term id="pupa">pupa</Term> is a stage in the mosquito's life cycle; the frog does not pass through one.

Look across all five frog views. They show points along a continuous process, from egg to adult. A simpler diagram might group them as egg, young animal and adult. Learn the order and the changes in shape. Different groupings can describe the same growth.

<More title={{"en": "Limbs and tail together", "zh": "四肢与尾巴同时出现"}}>

The hind-leg tadpole and the tailed froglet help us see how changes overlap. The froglet's tail is still attached while its limbs are already visible.

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Changes inside the body", "zh": "身体内部的变化"}}>

Metamorphosis changes tissues inside the body as well as its outer shape. The animal also changes from swimming with its tail to moving with its limbs.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

先看长出后腿的蝌蚪。尾巴两旁各有一条后腿，前肢还没有显现。再看看较早的游泳蝌蚪：身体已经发生变化，但它们仍是同一种动物的幼体。长腿是青蛙逐渐发育的一部分。

接着看<Term id="froglet">幼蛙</Term>。它已经有蛙形的身体，前肢和后肢都出现了，身后还连着一小段尾巴。从蝌蚪变成蛙的明显变化，叫<Term id="metamorphosis">变态发育</Term>。几种变化可以同时发生，所以幼蛙会有四肢，也暂时保留尾巴。

近看以前，先猜一猜尾巴以后会怎样，再看第三拍。身体逐渐吸收尾部组织，尾巴便慢慢缩短。它不会断掉，也不会落在青蛙身后。到了成体阶段，蝌蚪的尾巴已经消失。蚊子的生命周期中有一个阶段叫<Term id="pupa">蛹</Term>，青蛙不经过这个阶段。

回看青蛙的五个视图。它们选出了从卵到成体的几个样子，真实发育却是连续的。较简单的图也可以把过程分成卵、幼体和成体。观察时，要记住先后顺序和身体怎样变化。分组多少不同，也可以描述同一个成长过程。

<More title={{"en": "Limbs and tail together", "zh": "四肢与尾巴同时出现"}}>

长出后腿的蝌蚪、还带尾巴的幼蛙，让我们看见几种变化怎样交叠。幼蛙的四肢已经显现时，尾巴仍然连在身体上。

</More>

<More title={{"en": "Changes inside the body", "zh": "身体内部的变化"}}>

变态发育不只改变外形，也会改变体内组织。动物的运动方式也会改变，从靠尾巴游动，逐渐转为用四肢运动。

</More>
