<Lang en>

Bulbs A and B stand side by side in the middle of the board, and three small link switches, X, Y and Z, decide how they are joined. With only X closed the two bulbs are in series: there is one path, and the current flows through bulb A and then through bulb B. Both glow, each much dimmer than a single bulb on the same two batteries. <FlyTo preset="bulbs-view">Look at the two bulbs</FlyTo>

Unscrew one bulb from the series circuit and its empty holder leaves a gap in the only path. Both bulbs go dark, the one still in place as well.

Now open X and close Y and Z. The bulbs are in <Term id="parallel">parallel</Term>: each has its own path from one end of the batteries to the other. Each glows about as brightly as a single bulb. Unscrew one and the other stays lit, because its own path is still complete.

Parallel has a cost. Each bulb takes about the current a single bulb would, so the batteries supply about twice as much in total and run down sooner.

Other combinations of the switches make other paths. With X and Z closed the current can go round bulb B through Z, an easier path, so only bulb A lights; with X and Y closed only bulb B lights. The circuit diagram at the top right highlights every wire that carries current. Use the Try it card to switch between one bulb, series and parallel, record a row for each, then try the switches one by one.

Decorative lights show the difference outside the classroom. If every bulb on a string is joined in series, one loose or broken bulb leaves a gap in the only path and the whole string goes dark.

<More title={{ en: "The same current all along a series path", zh: "串联时处处电流相同" }}>

In this model, with two batteries and the bulbs in series, the reading for bulb A and the reading for bulb B are the same, <Num s="S1">0.118 A</Num>, and so is the current from the batteries. The second bulb gets just as much current as the first; what the two bulbs share is the batteries' push. Each glows with a brightness of <Num s="S1">26</Num>, against <Num s="S1">100</Num> for one bulb alone. With a single battery the series pair drops to <Num s="S1">0.060 A</Num> and a brightness of <Num s="S1">7</Num> each.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Parallel in numbers", zh: "并联的数字" }}>

In this model the two batteries supply <Num s="S1">0.454 A</Num> to the parallel pair, <Num s="S1">0.227 A</Num> through each bulb, against <Num s="S1">0.233 A</Num> for a single bulb. Each bulb's brightness is <Num s="S1">95</Num>, a little below <Num s="S1">100</Num> because the larger current loses a little more push inside the batteries.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Lights at home are in parallel", zh: "家里的电灯是并联的" }}>

The lights and sockets in a home are wired in parallel, each with its own path, so that each can be switched on and off without the others.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The switch puzzle", zh: "开关谜题" }}>

With the main switch closed, X, Y and Z can be set in eight ways. None closed: nothing lights, because there is no path back to the batteries. X only: A and B in series, both dim. Y only: B alone. Z only: A alone. X and Y: B alone, because A is bypassed. X and Z: A alone, because B is bypassed. Y and Z: both in parallel, both bright. All three closed is the subject of chapter 06. <Lang zh>

电路板中间并排立着灯泡 A 和 B，三个小小的连接开关 X、Y、Z 决定它们怎样连接。只合上 X，两只灯泡就是串联：只有一条路径，电流先流过灯泡 A，再流过灯泡 B。两只都亮，但都比同样两节电池点一只灯时暗得多。<FlyTo preset="bulbs-view">看两只灯泡</FlyTo>

在串联电路里拧下一只灯泡，空灯座在唯一的路径上留下缺口。两只灯都熄了，还在灯座上的那只也一样。

现在断开 X，合上 Y 和 Z。两只灯泡变成了<Term id="parallel">并联</Term>：每只都有自己的一条路径，从电池的一极通到另一极。每只都和单独一只灯时差不多亮。拧下其中一只，另一只照样亮，因为它自己的路径仍然完整。

并联是有代价的。每只灯泡取的电流和单独一只时差不多，电池总共要供出约两倍的电流，用得也更快。并联的灯越多，电池要供出的电流就越大。

开关的其他组合会接出别的路径。合上 X 和 Z，电流可以经 Z 绕过灯泡 B，走更顺畅的路，所以只有灯泡 A 亮；合上 X 和 Y，就只有灯泡 B 亮。右上角的电路图会把有电流的导线都标亮。用「试一试」卡片在单灯、串联、并联之间切换，每种记一行，再一个一个地拨开关试试。

装饰彩灯能让人在课堂外看到这个区别。一串彩灯如果每只灯泡都是串联的，只要一只松了或坏了，唯一的路径就断了，整串都不亮。

<More title={{ en: "The same current all along a series path", zh: "串联时处处电流相同" }}>

在这个模型里，用两节电池、两只灯串联时，灯泡 A 和灯泡 B 的读数一样，都是 <Num s="S1">0.118 A</Num>，电池输出的电流也是这么多。第二只灯得到的电流和第一只一样多；两只灯分摊的是电池的推动力。每只的亮度是 <Num s="S1">26</Num>，单独一只灯时是 <Num s="S1">100</Num>。只用一节电池时，串联的两只灯电流降到 <Num s="S1">0.060 A</Num>，各自亮度只有 <Num s="S1">7</Num>。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Parallel in numbers", zh: "并联的数字" }}>

在这个模型里，两节电池给并联的两只灯共供出 <Num s="S1">0.454 A</Num>，每只灯 <Num s="S1">0.227 A</Num>；单独一只灯时是 <Num s="S1">0.233 A</Num>。每只灯的亮度是 <Num s="S1">95</Num>，比 <Num s="S1">100</Num> 稍低，因为电流大了，电池内部损失的推动力也多一点。

<More title={{ en: "Lights at home are in parallel", zh: "家里的电灯是并联的" }}>

家里的电灯和插座都是并联的，各有各的路径，所以每一个都能单独开关，不影响别的。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The switch puzzle", zh: "开关谜题" }}>

主开关合上时，X、Y、Z 共有八种拨法。全断开：哪只都不亮，因为没有回到电池的路径。只合 X：A、B 串联，都暗。只合 Y：只有 B 亮。只合 Z：只有 A 亮。合 X 和 Y：A 被绕过，只有 B 亮。合 X 和 Z：B 被绕过，只有 A 亮。合 Y 和 Z：两只并联，都很亮。三个全合上，留到第 06 章讲。每拨一种，右上角的电路图都会显示答案。

</More>
