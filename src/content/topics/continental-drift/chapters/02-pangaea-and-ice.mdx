<Lang en>

From about 320 million years ago most of the world’s land joined into one <Term id="term-supercontinent">supercontinent</Term>, <Term id="term-pangaea">Pangaea</Term>. By 250 million years ago it covered about <Num s="S4">30%</Num> of Earth’s surface. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

At the same time Earth was in a long ice age. It began about 370 million years ago, its main phase started about 330 million years ago, and it ended about 260 million years ago. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22" aria-label="Source S22">S22</button></sup>

Rocks left by glaciers from about 300 million years ago are found today on five continents: the Dwyka Group in South Africa, the Itararé Group in Brazil, the Talchir Formation in India, the Wynyard Formation in Tasmania and the Pagoda Formation in Antarctica. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S6" aria-label="Source S6">S6</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="Source S21">S21</button></sup>

Wegener pointed out that, with the continents fitted back together, these glacial rocks gather in one region in the far south. One ice cap there explains them better than separate ice sheets on five far-apart continents, some of which now lie close to the Equator. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "How do you recognise rocks left by ice?", zh: "怎样认出冰川留下的岩石？" }}>

A glacier drags stones along its base. They cut <Term id="term-glacial-striations">glacial striations</Term>, long parallel scratches, into the bedrock. When the ice melts it drops a jumble of mud and stones of every size. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S6" aria-label="Source S6">S6</button></sup>

<Lang zh>

约从 3.2 亿年前起，世界上大部分陆地拼成了一块<Term id="term-supercontinent">超大陆</Term>——<Term id="term-pangaea">盘古大陆</Term>。到 2.5 亿年前，它约占地球表面的 <Num s="S4">30%</Num>。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

同一时期，地球处在一场漫长的冰期里：约 3.7 亿年前开始，约 3.3 亿年前进入主要阶段，约 2.6 亿年前结束。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S22" aria-label="来源 S22">S22</button></sup>

约 3 亿年前冰川留下的岩石，今天分布在五块大陆上：南非的德维卡群、巴西的伊塔拉雷群、印度的塔尔切尔组、塔斯马尼亚的温亚德组和南极洲的帕戈达组。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S6" aria-label="来源 S6">S6</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S21" aria-label="来源 S21">S21</button></sup>

魏格纳指出，把大陆拼回去以后，这些冰川岩石聚在遥远南方的同一片地区。用那里的一片冰盖来解释，比说五块相隔很远的大陆上各有冰盖更合理——何况其中一些大陆今天已靠近赤道。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "How do you recognise rocks left by ice?", zh: "怎样认出冰川留下的岩石？" }}>
