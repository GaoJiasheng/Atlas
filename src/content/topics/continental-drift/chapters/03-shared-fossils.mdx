<Lang en>

Wegener also used fossils. Mesosaurus was a freshwater reptile of the early Permian. Glossopteris was a seed-bearing shrub or tree with tongue-shaped leaves, common through the Permian. Lystrosaurus was a land animal of the early Triassic that probably ate plants. Cynognathus was a mammal-like reptile of the early to middle Triassic. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S6" aria-label="Source S6">S6</button></sup>

<Figure src="figures/continental-drift-four-fossils" alt={{ en: "Fossil slabs showing Mesosaurus, Glossopteris, Lystrosaurus and Cynognathus, not to scale.", zh: "中龙、舌羊齿、水龙兽和犬颌兽的化石岩板，不按比例。" }} caption={{ en: "Schematic fossil evidence from museum specimens; not to scale.", zh: "依据博物馆标本绘制的化石示意，不按比例。" }} />

In the Paleobiology Database snapshot used here, Mesosaurus records come from Brazil, Uruguay, Namibia and South Africa; Cynognathus records from southern Africa, Argentina and Antarctica. Glossopteris records come mostly from India, Australia, South America, Antarctica and southern Africa. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S18" aria-label="Source S18">S18</button></sup>

Lystrosaurus is recorded not only in South Africa, India and Antarctica but also in China and Russia, so it was not limited to the southern continents. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S18" aria-label="Source S18">S18</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S19" aria-label="Source S19">S19</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="Source S20">S20</button></sup>

Fitted back together, the southern finds of each animal or plant form one connected area instead of patches across wide oceans. Wegener argued that the land itself had once been joined. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button></sup>

<Lang zh>

魏格纳也用到了化石。中龙是早二叠世的淡水爬行动物；舌羊齿是一种结种子的灌木或乔木，叶子像舌头，在整个二叠纪都很常见；水龙兽是早三叠世的陆地动物，很可能吃植物；犬颌兽是早至中三叠世的似哺乳爬行动物。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S6" aria-label="来源 S6">S6</button></sup>

<Figure src="figures/continental-drift-four-fossils" alt={{ en: "Fossil slabs showing Mesosaurus, Glossopteris, Lystrosaurus and Cynognathus, not to scale.", zh: "中龙、舌羊齿、水龙兽和犬颌兽的化石岩板，不按比例。" }} caption={{ en: "Schematic fossil evidence from museum specimens; not to scale.", zh: "依据博物馆标本绘制的化石示意，不按比例。" }} />

在本题所用的古生物学数据库快照里，中龙的记录来自巴西、乌拉圭、纳米比亚和南非；犬颌兽的记录来自非洲南部、阿根廷和南极洲。舌羊齿的记录主要来自印度、澳大利亚、南美洲、南极洲和非洲南部。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S18" aria-label="来源 S18">S18</button></sup>

水龙兽不只记录于南非、印度和南极洲，也见于中国和俄罗斯，所以它并不限于南方大陆。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S18" aria-label="来源 S18">S18</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S19" aria-label="来源 S19">S19</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S20" aria-label="来源 S20">S20</button></sup>

把大陆拼回去，每种动物或植物在南方的发现地连成一片，而不是散在辽阔大洋两边的几小块。魏格纳认为，是陆地本身曾经连在一起。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button></sup>
