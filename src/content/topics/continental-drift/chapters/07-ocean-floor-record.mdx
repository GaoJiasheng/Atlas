<Lang en>

India reached Asia; the two collided between about 50 and 40 million years ago, and the Himalayas began to rise. The proof that plates move, though, came from the ocean floor. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="Source S5">S5</button></sup>

In 1952 Marie Tharp drew six depth profiles across the North Atlantic and found a valley running along the middle of the underwater ridge. Her colleague Bruce Heezen at first doubted it, until plotted earthquakes fell along the same valley. The discovery was announced in 1956 by Maurice Ewing and Heezen. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S7" aria-label="Source S7">S7</button></sup>

In 1962 Harry Hess described ocean floor forming at <Term id="term-mid-ocean-ridge">mid-ocean ridges</Term> and moving away on both sides; Robert Dietz had named this <Term id="term-seafloor-spreading">seafloor spreading</Term> in 1961. Rocks record Earth’s magnetic field as they cool, and in 1929 Motonori Matuyama found basalts magnetised in the opposite direction to today’s field; the field has flipped many times. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="Source S5">S5</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S10" aria-label="Source S10">S10</button></sup>

In 1963 Fred Vine and Drummond Matthews, and independently Lawrence Morley, proposed that spreading should leave <Term id="term-magnetic-stripes">magnetic stripes</Term> mirrored on both sides of a ridge. A survey of the Reykjanes Ridge, published in 1966, showed exactly that, and in 1968 drilling in the South Atlantic tested whether sediment gets older away from the ridge. From the mid-1950s onwards, discoveries in rock magnetism and the oceans led most scientists to accept that continents move. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S9" aria-label="Source S9">S9</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S6" aria-label="Source S6">S6</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S8" aria-label="Source S8">S8</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S13" aria-label="Source S13">S13</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Why the stripes are symmetrical", zh: "条带为什么对称" }}>

New rock forms at the ridge, records the field of its time, and is pushed away on both sides. When the field flips, the next rock records the opposite direction. Each <Term id="term-magnetic-reversal">magnetic reversal</Term> therefore leaves a matching pair of stripes; the most recent was about 773,000 years ago. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S8" aria-label="Source S8">S8</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S11" aria-label="Source S11">S11</button></sup>

<Figure src="continental-drift" alt={{ en: "A schematic ocean-floor block with a central rift and paired parallel stripes.", zh: "示意洋底块体：中央裂谷两侧是成对平行条带。" }} caption={{ en: "Schematic ocean crust: irregular bands mirror one another across the ridge.", zh: "洋壳示意：不规则条带在洋脊两侧互为镜像。" }} />

The stripes on the map are measured magnetism today, split only into areas above and below the average field. The ocean-floor colours come from a separate age grid and are shown only at 0 Ma. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S16" aria-label="Source S16">S16</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S14" aria-label="Source S14">S14</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Tharp, Heezen and Morley", zh: "撒普、希曾与莫利" }}>

Tharp drew the profiles in 1952; the public announcement in 1956 was made by Ewing and Heezen. For many years her part was little recognised; in 1998 the Library of Congress honoured her, and in 2001 her institute, Lamont-Doherty, gave her its Heritage Award. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S7" aria-label="Source S7">S7</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S39" aria-label="Source S39">S39</button></sup>

Morley sent his version of the stripe idea to Nature before Vine and Matthews, and then to the Journal of Geophysical Research. Both turned it down, and for a long time his part was not acknowledged. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S12" aria-label="Source S12">S12</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S40" aria-label="Source S40">S40</button></sup>

</More>

<Lang zh>

印度到达亚洲，两者在约 5000 万至 4000 万年前相撞，喜马拉雅山开始抬升。不过，证明板块会移动的证据来自洋底。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="来源 S5">S5</button></sup>

1952 年，玛丽·撒普画出横跨北大西洋的六条水深剖面，发现海底山脊中央有一道谷。她的同事布鲁斯·希曾起初不信，直到标出的地震震中也落在这道谷里。这一发现在 1956 年由莫里斯·尤因和希曾宣布。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S7" aria-label="来源 S7">S7</button></sup>

1962 年，哈里·赫斯描述洋底在<Term id="term-mid-ocean-ridge">洋中脊</Term>生成、向两侧移开；罗伯特·迪茨在 1961 年把它称为<Term id="term-seafloor-spreading">海底扩张</Term>。岩石冷却时会记录地磁场。1929 年，松山基范发现一些玄武岩的磁化方向与今天的地磁场相反；地磁场翻转过很多次。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="来源 S5">S5</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S10" aria-label="来源 S10">S10</button></sup>

1963 年，弗雷德·瓦因和德拉蒙德·马修斯，以及独立提出同样想法的劳伦斯·莫利，推断海底扩张会在洋脊两侧留下互为镜像的<Term id="term-magnetic-stripes">磁条带</Term>。1966 年发表的雷恰内斯洋脊测量正好显示了这一点；1968 年，南大西洋的钻探又检验了沉积物是否离洋脊越远越老。从 1950 年代中期起，岩石磁性和海洋方面的发现，让多数科学家接受了大陆会移动。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S9" aria-label="来源 S9">S9</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S6" aria-label="来源 S6">S6</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S8" aria-label="来源 S8">S8</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S13" aria-label="来源 S13">S13</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Why the stripes are symmetrical", zh: "条带为什么对称" }}>

新岩石在洋脊生成，记下当时的地磁场，再被推向两侧。地磁场翻转后，接下来的岩石记下相反的方向。所以每一次<Term id="term-magnetic-reversal">地磁倒转</Term>都会留下一对对称的条带；最近一次约在 77.3 万年前。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S8" aria-label="来源 S8">S8</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S11" aria-label="来源 S11">S11</button></sup>

<Figure src="continental-drift" alt={{ en: "A schematic ocean-floor block with a central rift and paired parallel stripes.", zh: "示意洋底块体：中央裂谷两侧是成对平行条带。" }} caption={{ en: "Schematic ocean crust: irregular bands mirror one another across the ridge.", zh: "洋壳示意：不规则条带在洋脊两侧互为镜像。" }} />

地图上的条带是今天测得的磁性，只分成高于和低于平均磁场两类。洋底颜色来自另一套年龄格网，只在 0 Ma 显示。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S16" aria-label="来源 S16">S16</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S14" aria-label="来源 S14">S14</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Tharp, Heezen and Morley", zh: "撒普、希曾与莫利" }}>

剖面是撒普在 1952 年画出的；1956 年公开宣布的是尤因和希曾。她的贡献多年没有得到多少承认；1998 年美国国会图书馆表彰了她，2001 年她所在的拉蒙特–多尔蒂地球观测所授予她传承奖。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S7" aria-label="来源 S7">S7</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S39" aria-label="来源 S39">S39</button></sup>

莫利比瓦因和马修斯更早把条带的想法投给《自然》，之后又投给《地球物理研究杂志》，都被退稿；他的贡献很长时间没有得到承认。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S12" aria-label="来源 S12">S12</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S40" aria-label="来源 S40">S40</button></sup>
