<Lang en>

Pangaea began to break apart about 200 million years ago. Its break-up came during and after a huge outpouring of lava along the future central Atlantic, and the central Atlantic began to open about 195 million years ago. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="Source S5">S5</button></sup>

Next, Gondwana split into a western part (Africa and South America) and an eastern part (Madagascar, India, Antarctica and Australia). The split may have begun about 180 million years ago; the oldest sea floor between the two parts that can be identified is about 155 million years old. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

The South Atlantic opened at about 130 million years ago, starting in the south, and by 100 million years ago it was joined to the central Atlantic. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

India broke away from Antarctica and Australia about 130 million years ago and from Madagascar about 85 million years ago, then moved north quickly, at times about <Num s="S4">18</Num> centimetres a year. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

<Lang zh>

盘古大陆约在 2 亿年前开始分裂。分裂发生在未来中大西洋一带大规模熔岩喷发期间及其之后；中大西洋约在 1.95 亿年前开始张开。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S5" aria-label="来源 S5">S5</button></sup>

接着，冈瓦纳分成西部（非洲和南美洲）和东部（马达加斯加、印度、南极洲和澳大利亚）。分裂可能早在约 1.8 亿年前开始；两部分之间能辨认出的最老洋底，年龄约 1.55 亿年。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

南大西洋约在 1.3 亿年前从南端开始张开，到 1 亿年前已和中大西洋连通。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

印度约在 1.3 亿年前离开南极洲和澳大利亚，约 8500 万年前又离开马达加斯加，然后快速北上，有时每年约 <Num s="S4">18</Num> 厘米。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>
