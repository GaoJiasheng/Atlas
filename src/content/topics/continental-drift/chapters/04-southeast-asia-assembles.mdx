<Lang en>

Southeast Asia was built from several blocks of crust that came together over hundreds of millions of years. One of them, Sibumasu, takes its name from Sino or Siam, Burma, Malaya and Sumatra; it left Gondwana in the Early Permian. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S26" aria-label="Source S26">S26</button></sup>

Singapore lies on the other side, at the southern end of a chain of volcanoes called the Sukhothai Arc. As an ocean plate sank beneath it, molten rock rose and cooled underground. Five bodies of granite and related rock in Singapore formed this way, one after another, between about 285 and 230 million years ago; together they are called the Bukit Timah Centre. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="Source S24">S24</button></sup>

<Figure src="figures/continental-drift-granite" invert={false} alt={{ en: "Generic granite with interlocking feldspar, quartz and dark mica on a polished face.", zh: "通用花岗岩：抛光面可见互相嵌合的长石、石英和深色云母。" }} caption={{ en: "Generic granite specimen, with a polished face exposing interlocking minerals.", zh: "通用花岗岩标本，抛光面露出互相嵌合的矿物。" }} />

When the ocean between the blocks closed, they collided. Studies of Singapore put the collision in the latest Triassic to earliest Jurassic, essentially complete by about 200 to 195 million years ago; a regional review puts Sibumasu’s collision in the Middle to Late Triassic. The joining zone, the Bentong–Raub suture, runs about <Num s="S25">50</Num> to <Num s="S25">100</Num> kilometres west of Singapore. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S25" aria-label="Source S25">S25</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S26" aria-label="Source S26">S26</button></sup>

Today Singapore lies on the Sunda plate in one plate model. The line on the map is the model’s path for the ground under Singapore; its east–west position is a convention. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S37" aria-label="Source S37">S37</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Sundaland was about sea level", zh: "巽他古陆与海平面有关" }}>

During the last ice age, sea level was much lower; at its coldest, about 21,000 years ago, it was about <Num s="S31">116</Num> to <Num s="S31">120</Num> metres below today’s. The shallow Sunda Shelf became dry land, called <Term id="term-sundaland">Sundaland</Term>. That was a change in sea level, not plate movement. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S31" aria-label="Source S31">S31</button></sup>

<Lang zh>

东南亚由多块地壳拼合而成，拼合过程长达几亿年。其中一块叫滇缅马苏（Sibumasu），名称取自中国或暹罗、缅甸、马来亚和苏门答腊；它在早二叠世离开冈瓦纳。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S26" aria-label="来源 S26">S26</button></sup>

新加坡在另一侧，位于一串火山——苏可泰岛弧——的南端。洋板块在它下面下沉时，岩浆上升并在地下冷却。新加坡的五个花岗岩及相关岩体就是这样，在约 2.85 亿至 2.3 亿年前先后形成，合称武吉知马中心。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S24" aria-label="来源 S24">S24</button></sup>

<Figure src="figures/continental-drift-granite" invert={false} alt={{ en: "Generic granite with interlocking feldspar, quartz and dark mica on a polished face.", zh: "通用花岗岩：抛光面可见互相嵌合的长石、石英和深色云母。" }} caption={{ en: "Generic granite specimen, with a polished face exposing interlocking minerals.", zh: "通用花岗岩标本，抛光面露出互相嵌合的矿物。" }} />

地块之间的大洋合拢后，它们相撞。研究新加坡的学者把碰撞放在晚三叠世末至早侏罗世初，约 2 亿至 1.95 亿年前基本结束；一篇区域综述则把滇缅马苏的碰撞放在中至晚三叠世。拼合带——文冬–劳勿缝合带——在新加坡以西约 <Num s="S25">50</Num> 至 <Num s="S25">100</Num> 公里处经过。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S25" aria-label="来源 S25">S25</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S26" aria-label="来源 S26">S26</button></sup>

在一个板块模型里，新加坡今天位于巽他板块上。地图上的线是模型给出的新加坡脚下地面的路径；它的东西位置只是约定。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S37" aria-label="来源 S37">S37</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Sundaland was about sea level", zh: "巽他古陆与海平面有关" }}>

末次冰期时海平面低得多；最冷的时候，约 2.1 万年前，比今天低约 <Num s="S31">116</Num> 至 <Num s="S31">120</Num> 米。浅浅的巽他陆架露出水面，成为<Term id="term-sundaland">巽他古陆</Term>。这是海平面变化，不是板块移动。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S31" aria-label="来源 S31">S31</button></sup>
