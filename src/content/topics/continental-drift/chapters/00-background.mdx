<Lang en>

In January 1912 the German scientist Alfred Wegener gave his first public talk on a question: could the continents move? This topic follows the evidence in the order the rocks formed, from 400 million years ago to today. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="Source S1">S1</button></sup>

The map is a <Term id="term-plate-model">plate reconstruction model</Term>, not a photograph. It takes today’s coastlines and turns them back in time with the Merdith et al. 2021 model. The outlines are today’s coasts carried back: ancient shorelines, sea levels and land added later are not shown. For ancient land and sea, open <TopicLink slug="dinosaur-earth" ch="triassic-world">the Triassic world</TopicLink>. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Source S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S3" aria-label="Source S3">S3</button></sup>

Each plate moves as one rigid piece. Where real continents pushed together they were squeezed, so outlines can overlap; ocean floor that has since sunk back into the Earth is left as a gap. Rock magnetism fixes how far north or south a plate was, but not its east–west position, so compare plates with each other rather than with the grid. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Source S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

Ages are in <Term id="term-ma">Ma</Term>, millions of years ago. Drag the timeline or step through the beats: every frame is recalculated from the model’s rotation poles, the way the GPlates software does it. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S34" aria-label="Source S34">S34</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Where does the model come from?", zh: "模型从哪里来？" }}>

This model joins four earlier plate models into one, from 1 billion years ago to today, and places them in a reference frame based on rock magnetism. Its authors advise against using it for spans shorter than 5 million years, so this topic uses GPS, not the model, for today’s movement. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Source S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S3" aria-label="Source S3">S3</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why does each plate turn about a pole?", zh: "为什么每块板块绕一个极转动？" }}>

Any movement of a rigid piece over a sphere can be described as a turn about an axis through Earth’s centre. Where the axis meets the surface is the <Term id="term-euler-pole">rotation pole</Term>. Each plate has its own pole at each age, so neighbouring plates can move in different directions. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S34" aria-label="Source S34">S34</button></sup>

</More>

<Lang zh>

1912 年 1 月，德国科学家阿尔弗雷德·魏格纳第一次公开讲了一个问题：大陆会不会移动？本题按岩石形成的先后，从 4 亿年前一路讲到今天，跟着证据走。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S1" aria-label="来源 S1">S1</button></sup>

这张地图是<Term id="term-plate-model">板块重建模型</Term>，不是照片。它用 Merdith 等 2021 年的模型，把今天的海岸线沿时间往回转。轮廓是被带回去的今天的海岸：古代的海岸线、海平面和后来才增生的陆地都没有画出来。想看古代的陆地和海洋，请打开<TopicLink slug="dinosaur-earth" ch="triassic-world">三叠纪的世界</TopicLink>。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="来源 S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S3" aria-label="来源 S3">S3</button></sup>

每块板块当作一整块刚体移动。真实的大陆相撞时会被挤压，所以地图上的轮廓可能互相重叠；后来沉回地球内部的洋底，则留成空白。岩石磁性能确定板块当时偏南还是偏北，却不能确定东西位置，所以请比较板块彼此的位置，不要对照经纬网。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="来源 S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

年代用 <Term id="term-ma">Ma</Term> 表示，即百万年前。拖动时间轴或逐拍前进：每一帧都按 GPlates 软件的方法，从模型的旋转极重新计算。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S34" aria-label="来源 S34">S34</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Where does the model come from?", zh: "模型从哪里来？" }}>

这个模型把四个较早的板块模型连成一个，从 10 亿年前一直到今天，并放在以岩石磁性为基础的参考系里。作者说明它不适合用于短于 500 万年的时间尺度，所以本题讲今天的移动时用 GPS，不用模型。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="来源 S2">S2</button><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S3" aria-label="来源 S3">S3</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why does each plate turn about a pole?", zh: "为什么每块板块绕一个极转动？" }}>

刚体在球面上的任何移动，都可以看作绕一根穿过地心的轴转动。轴与地表的交点就是<Term id="term-euler-pole">旋转极</Term>。每块板块在每个年代都有自己的极，所以相邻的板块可以朝不同方向移动。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S34" aria-label="来源 S34">S34</button></sup>
