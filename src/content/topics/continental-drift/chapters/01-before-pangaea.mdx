<Lang en>

Four hundred million years ago, a large southern continent, <Term id="term-gondwana">Gondwana</Term>, had already been together for about 150 million years. It held the land that is now South America, Africa, Madagascar, India, Antarctica and Australia. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

The northern continents were separate. Laurussia had formed when North America and northern Europe collided, between about 430 and 420 million years ago; Siberia, Kazakh blocks and North and South China were still apart from it. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

Between Gondwana and Laurussia lay an ocean that was about <Num s="S4">5,000</Num> kilometres wide in the Early Devonian and almost closed by the Early Carboniferous. Its floor has since sunk back into the Earth, so the model leaves the space empty. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

As the continents met, mountains rose. This collision, called the Variscan, was at its height about 340 million years ago. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Real continents bend; model plates do not", zh: "真实大陆会变形，模型板块不会" }}>

The model moves each plate as one rigid piece. Where continents crashed together, real rock was folded and thickened, so outlines on the map may overlap. To see how the layers beneath the plates behave, read <TopicLink slug="inside-the-earth" ch="layers-of-earth">the layers of the Earth</TopicLink> and <TopicLink slug="inside-the-earth" ch="three-boundary-motions">three ways plates meet</TopicLink>. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="Source S2">S2</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why east and west are a convention", zh: "为什么东西位置只是约定" }}>

Rocks that cooled long ago keep a record of Earth’s magnetic field. It shows how far a plate was from the pole and which way it faced, but not its ancient longitude. So the whole map could slide east or west without breaking the evidence; what matters is where plates sit relative to each other. <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="Source S4">S4</button></sup>

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

4 亿年前，南方的大陆<Term id="term-gondwana">冈瓦纳</Term>已经拼在一起约 1.5 亿年。它包括今天的南美洲、非洲、马达加斯加、印度、南极洲和澳大利亚。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

北方的大陆彼此分开。北美和北欧约在 4.3 亿至 4.2 亿年前相撞，形成劳俄大陆；西伯利亚、哈萨克的几个地块以及华北、华南，当时还与它分开。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

冈瓦纳和劳俄之间隔着一片大洋：早泥盆世时宽约 <Num s="S4">5000</Num> 公里，到早石炭世已几乎合拢。这片洋底后来沉回了地球内部，所以模型在那里留空。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

大陆相遇时隆起了山脉。这次称为海西（华力西）造山的碰撞，约在 3.4 亿年前达到高峰。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>

<More title={{ en: "Real continents bend; model plates do not", zh: "真实大陆会变形，模型板块不会" }}>

模型把每块板块当作一整块刚体移动。大陆相撞的地方，真实的岩石会被褶皱、增厚，所以地图上的轮廓可能重叠。板块下面的各层怎样活动，请读<TopicLink slug="inside-the-earth" ch="layers-of-earth">地球的分层</TopicLink>和<TopicLink slug="inside-the-earth" ch="three-boundary-motions">板块相遇的三种方式</TopicLink>。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S2" aria-label="来源 S2">S2</button></sup>

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why east and west are a convention", zh: "为什么东西位置只是约定" }}>

很久以前冷却的岩石，保存着当时地磁场的记录。它能说明板块离极点多远、朝哪个方向，却说明不了古经度。所以整张图向东或向西挪动，并不违背证据；要看的是板块彼此的相对位置。 <sup><button type="button" className="atlas-src" data-source="S4" aria-label="来源 S4">S4</button></sup>
