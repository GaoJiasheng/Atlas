<Lang en>

On the stage is an AI server, the kind of computer used for <Term id="training">training</Term> and running large artificial-intelligence models. Most of its work is done by eight <Term id="gpu">GPUs</Term>, graphics processing units: chips that can do an enormous number of simple calculations at the same time. The model shows one real machine, the NVIDIA DGX B200, announced in March 2024, because its maker publishes its size, weight, power and layout. <FlyTo preset="hero">Start with the whole machine</FlyTo>

It is built to slide into a rack, a tall steel frame that holds many machines one above another. Heights in a rack are counted in <Term id="rack-unit">rack units</Term>, and this server takes <Num s="S3">10</Num> of them: it is <Num s="S3">444 mm</Num> tall and about <Num s="S3">897 mm</Num> deep. Fully loaded it weighs up to <Num s="S3">142.4 kg</Num>, a little more than two average adults of about <Num s="S27">62 kg</Num> each.

Air goes in at the front and comes out at the back. Behind the champagne-coloured bezel are <Num s="S3">20</Num> fan modules in four rows of five, with <Num s="S3">eight</Num> data drives along the bottom. <FlyTo preset="rear-view">Turn to the back</FlyTo> and there are three levels: the exhaust grille of the GPU tray at the top, the ports of the motherboard tray in the middle, and <Num s="S3">six</Num> power supplies at the bottom.

Almost everything inside exists to feed the eight GPUs with electricity and data, and to carry their heat away. The next chapters open the box in that order.

The eight GPUs sit on a board that NVIDIA sells to other server makers as HGX B200. Those makers build their own boxes around it, and they differ: one air-cooled design reviewed in 2025 is <Num s="S15">8U</Num> tall with a GPU tray of <Num s="S15">6U</Num>, and some put the motherboard under the GPU board, others above it. Every number in this topic is for the DGX B200 alone.

</More>

A rack unit is <Num s="S25">1.75 inches</Num>, or <Num s="S25">44.45 mm</Num>, and racks are <Num s="S25">19 inches</Num> wide between their mounting posts. Ten units come to <Num s="S25">444.5 mm</Num>; the DGX B200 is listed at <Num s="S3">444 mm</Num>, leaving a little play. Its front, rack ears included, is given as <Num s="S3">482.3 mm</Num> in the user guide and <Num s="S2">482.2 mm</Num> on the product page; the two sources differ by a tenth of a millimetre.

它是装进机柜用的。机柜是一个高高的钢架，一台台机器上下叠着放。机柜里的高度用<Term id="rack-unit">机架单位</Term>来计，这台服务器占 <Num s="S3">10</Num> 个：高 <Num s="S3">444 mm</Num>，深约 <Num s="S3">897 mm</Num>。满配时最重 <Num s="S3">142.4 kg</Num>，比两个平均体重约 <Num s="S27">62 kg</Num> 的成年人还重一点。

空气从前面进，从后面出。香槟金色的面板后面是 <Num s="S3">20</Num> 个风扇模块，4 排、每排 5 个，最下面一排是 <Num s="S3">8</Num> 块数据盘。<FlyTo preset="rear-view">转到背面</FlyTo>，可以看到三层：最上面是 GPU 托盘的排风面，中间是主板托盘的各种接口，最下面是 <Num s="S3">6</Num> 个电源。

机箱里几乎所有东西，都是为了给这 8 块 GPU 送电、送数据，再把它们产生的热带走。后面几章就按这个顺序把机箱打开。
