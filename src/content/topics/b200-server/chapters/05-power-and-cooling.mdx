Electricity comes in at the back through <Num s="S3">six</Num> power supplies. Each takes mains power at <Num s="S3">200–240 V</Num> and can deliver up to <Num s="S3">3.3 kW</Num> of direct current to the machine. Any five can carry the full load, so the sixth is <Term id="redundancy">redundancy</Term>: one can fail, or be pulled out and replaced, while the server keeps running. The whole server can draw up to about <Num s="S2,S3">14.3 kW</Num>, and each GPU up to <Num s="S3">1,000 W</Num>.

Every watt the chips use ends up as heat. The calculations do not store the energy; it warms the chips, so <Num s="S3">14.3 kW</Num> of electricity in means <Num s="S3">14.3 kW</Num> of heat out, which NVIDIA lists as <Num s="S3">48,794 BTU/h</Num>. That is about as much heat as <Num s="S3,S1">seven</Num> two-kilowatt kettles left boiling.

The fan wall carries that heat away. Its <Num s="S3">20</Num> modules pull up to <Num s="S3">1,550</Num> cubic feet of room air a minute, about <Num s="S3">2,630 m³</Num> an hour, from front to back. The room must keep feeding it air between <Num s="S3">10</Num> and <Num s="S3">35 °C</Num>, so the data centre spends more electricity cooling that air again.

<More title={{ en: "Every watt becomes heat", zh: "每一瓦电都变成热" }}>

Energy is never used up, only changed from one form to another. In a server almost none of it leaves as light, sound or movement, so nearly all the electrical energy becomes heat in the chips, the power supplies and the fans. The maximum heat load NVIDIA gives, <Num s="S3">48,794 BTU/h</Num>, is the maximum power, <Num s="S3">14.3 kW</Num>, written in the British thermal units that cooling engineers often use: <Num s="S3">14.3 × 3,412 ≈ 48,800</Num>.

<More title={{ en: "Five plus one", zh: "五加一" }}>

With five or six supplies working, each GPU may draw up to <Num s="S3">1,000 W</Num>. With three or four the GPUs are held to <Num s="S3">800 W</Num> each, and with fewer than three only the management controller can be switched on. </More>

<More title={{ en: "Why not liquid", zh: "为什么不用液冷" }}>

The DGX B200 is cooled by air alone, which is why its fans, heat sinks and air paths are so large. Denser designs pipe liquid through cold plates on the chips instead: NVIDIA's GB200 NVL72 rack holds <Num s="S18">72</Num> GPUs and is cooled with liquid. Chapter 6 compares the two.

<More title={{ en: "Every watt becomes heat", zh: "每一瓦电都变成热" }}>

能量不会用掉，只会从一种形式变成另一种。服务器里几乎没有能量以光、声音或运动的形式离开，所以电能几乎全部在芯片、电源和风扇里变成热。NVIDIA 给的最大发热量 <Num s="S3">48,794 BTU/h</Num>，就是最大功率 <Num s="S3">14.3 kW</Num> 换成制冷工程师常用的英热单位：<Num s="S3">14.3 × 3,412 ≈ 48,800</Num>。
