<Lang en>

Look at the cylinder’s sealed top. The piston rod passes through a gland, a seal around the moving rod. Steam can push on either face of the piston. In a <Term id="double-acting">double-acting</Term> engine, the piston does work as it moves in both directions. Its connection to the beam must both push and pull. [S2, S7] <FlyTo preset="cylinder-view">Look inside the working cylinder</FlyTo>

To move down, steam enters through the upper inlet. The lower outlet lets exhaust steam leave for the condenser. To move up, the other pair opens: steam enters below and leaves above. Choose each valve to trace these paired routes. [S7]

Pressure pushes on both faces of the piston. Subtract the opposing forces to find the net force. The rod takes up part of the upper face, so steam pushes on a smaller area there. Outside air also pushes on the exposed rod. These differences make the working forces slightly different in the two directions. [S1, S8]

A force does <Term id="work">mechanical work</Term> when it transfers energy as an object moves. For a constant force along the movement, work depends on force and distance. Power tells us how quickly the work is done. In the try-it card, keep the bore, rod and hot-side pressure fixed. Raise the cold-side pressure. Both working forces become smaller, so less work is done in each cycle. [S1, S15]

Read <TopicLink slug="industrial-revolution" ch="ir-railways-networks">Railways, different beginnings</TopicLink> for Rocket and early railways. Rocket had a different structure from this stationary engine.

<More title={{ en: "Calculating the forces", zh: "计算两面的力" }}>

Absolute pressure is measured from a vacuum; gauge pressure is measured relative to the outside air. All readings here use absolute pressure. Let A be the full piston area and Ar the rod area. The downward force is top pressure × (A − Ar) + outside pressure × Ar − bottom pressure × A. A negative answer means the force acts upwards. At the starting model pressures, the working forces are about <Num s="S1">16.022 kN</Num> down and <Num s="S1">15.930 kN</Num> up. [S1, S8]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Work and power in the model", zh: "模型中的功与功率" }}>

The model gives about <Num s="S1">38.96 kJ</Num> of indicated work per cycle: work done by pressure on the piston. At <Num s="S1">10 cycles per minute</Num>, its model indicated power is <Num s="S1">6.493 kW</Num>. These calculations leave out friction, inertia, leakage, steam expansion and pump work, so they do not give the power delivered at the output shaft. The valves show a simplified switching pattern. [S1, S7]

</More>

<Lang zh>

汽缸上端有密封缸盖，活塞杆穿过填料密封口。蒸汽可以推动活塞的两面。<Term id="double-acting">双作用</Term>表示活塞向上、向下移动时都能做功。因此，连接横梁的机构既要能推，也要能拉。[S2, S7]<FlyTo preset="cylinder-view">看看工作汽缸里面</FlyTo>

向下运动时，蒸汽从上方进汽口进入，下方排汽口让蒸汽排向冷凝器。向上运动时，另一对阀口打开：从下面进汽，从上面排汽。逐个点击阀口，就能追踪这两条配对的路径。[S7]

压力作用在活塞的两面。把相反方向的力相减，就得到合力。活塞杆占去上表面的一部分，使蒸汽推动的面积变小；外界空气也推动外露的杆。这些差别让两个方向的做功推力略有不同。[S1, S8]

力使物体移动并传递能量，就是做<Term id="work">机械功</Term>。当力恒定且方向与运动相同时，功取决于力和路程；功率表示做功的快慢。在试一试卡片中，保持内径、杆径与热侧压力不变，再提高冷侧压力。两个方向的推力都会减小，每周期做的功也随之减少。[S1, S15]

<More title={{ en: "Calculating the forces", zh: "计算两面的力" }}>

绝压以真空为零点，表压则以外界空气的压力为起点。这里的读数都采用绝压。设完整活塞面积为 A，杆面积为 Ar，向下合力为：上侧压强 × (A − Ar) + 外界压强 × Ar − 下侧压强 × A。算出负数表示力向上。采用起始模型压力时，向下做功推力约 <Num s="S1">16.022 千牛</Num>，向上约 <Num s="S1">15.930 千牛</Num>。[S1, S8]

</More>
