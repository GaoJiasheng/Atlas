<Lang en>

The piston and beam move back and forth. The machine drive needs rotation. Follow the connecting rod from the beam to the small gears beside the large wheel. Watt’s <Term id="sun-and-planet">sun-and-planet gear</Term> turns the shaft. Its names describe the gears’ positions. [S2, S6] <FlyTo preset="hero">Trace the drive from beam to wheel</FlyTo>

The planet gear is fixed to the connecting rod. It rocks as the rod changes angle. A separate centre link keeps the gear centres the same distance apart. The sun gear is fixed to the output shaft, so they turn together. [S6, S7]

With equal gears, a complete piston cycle gives the sun and shaft <Num s="S6">two net turns</Num>. The rod rocks during the cycle, so the turning ratio changes from moment to moment. Start the running beat to watch the links, gears and valves move together through the same cycle. [S1, S6]

The large <Term id="flywheel">flywheel</Term> stores energy as it turns, then gives energy back as the driving effort varies. Mechanical energy passes to the machine drive. The flywheel stores energy; the governor has a different job, adjusting the steam supply. Pause and choose still positions to see how the gear teeth mesh. [S7]

<More title={{ en: "Rod angle and gear angle", zh: "杆角与齿轮角" }}>

For these equal external gears, sun-angle change + planet-angle change = twice the orbital-angle change. The orbital angle tracks the line joining the gear centres. The planet’s angle changes as its rod rocks. After a full cycle, the rod returns to its starting angle. The sun keeps the net rotation described above. [S1, S6]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two different sets of teeth", zh: "两处不同的轮齿" }}>

ASME and Hulse give different counts for teeth on the flywheel’s outer rim. The model leaves out a countable rim drive. Those outer teeth are separate from the small sun-and-planet pair. The small gears use equal tooth shapes to show how they mesh; the shapes are reconstructed. [S1, S3, S5]

</More>

<Lang zh>

活塞和横梁来回运动，机器取力端却需要转动。沿着横梁下方的传动杆，找到大轮旁的小齿轮。瓦特的<Term id="sun-and-planet">太阳与行星齿轮机构</Term>带动输出轴旋转，名字描述了齿轮的相对位置。[S2, S6]<FlyTo preset="hero">沿横梁找到取力端</FlyTo>

行星轮固定在传动杆上，随杆的角度变化而摆动。另一根独立的定距连杆让两个齿轮的中心距保持不变。太阳轮固定在输出轴上，两者一起转动。[S6, S7]

两个齿轮相等时，活塞一个完整周期让太阳轮和输出轴净转 <Num s="S6">两圈</Num>。周期内传动杆也在摆动，所以转动比例会不断变化。启动运行节拍，就能看到连杆、齿轮和阀门一起完成同一个周期。[S1, S6]

大<Term id="flywheel">飞轮</Term>储存转动的能量，并在驱动力变化时交还能量。机械能从机器取力端传出。飞轮负责储能，调速器则负责调整供汽。暂停后再切换静止画面，可以看清齿轮怎样啮合。[S7]

<More title={{ en: "Rod angle and gear angle", zh: "杆角与齿轮角" }}>

这里相等的外齿轮满足：太阳轮角度变化 + 行星轮角度变化 = 轨道角变化的两倍。轨道角表示两个齿轮中心连线的角度。行星轮随传动杆摆动，角度也跟着改变。完整周期后，杆回到原来的角度，太阳轮则保留上面所说的净转动。[S1, S6]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two different sets of teeth", zh: "两处不同的轮齿" }}>

ASME 与 Hulse 给出的飞轮外缘齿数不同，因此模型省略了可数齿的外缘取力齿圈。外缘轮齿与旁边的太阳轮、行星轮是不同结构。模型的小齿轮采用重建的相等齿形，展示轮齿怎样啮合。[S1, S3, S5]

</More>
