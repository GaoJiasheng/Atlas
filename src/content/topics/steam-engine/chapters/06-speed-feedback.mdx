<Lang en>

The <Term id="centrifugal-governor">centrifugal governor</Term> links rotation to the steam supply. Its spindle carries <Num s="S1">two</Num> flyballs. Upper arms and lower links join them to a sliding collar, which moves along the spindle. Each rod keeps the same length as the balls and collar change position. [S1, S7] <FlyTo preset="governor-view">Look at the governor linkage</FlyTo>

At a higher steady speed in this model, the balls spread farther out and the collar rises. A fork around the collar carries this up-and-down movement without rotating with the spindle. A rod and crank pass it on to the valve disc in the steam pipe. Trace the route from balls to valve. [S1, S7]

The <Term id="throttle">throttle valve</Term> controls the opening in the steam pipe. In the faster position, it closes farther and limits the incoming steam. At lower speed, the movement reverses. This is <Term id="feedback">feedback</Term>: the output, rotation, changes the input, steam. The throttle controls supply. The paired inlet and exhaust valves direct steam into and out of the cylinder. [S7]

Use the card to compare three steady positions. Keep the links, balls and gravity unchanged, then watch the collar rise and the throttle opening change. These still views show how the linkage acts. They do not show how engine speed changes after a load changes, or promise a perfectly constant speed. [S1]

<Term id="centrifugal-governor">离心调速器</Term>把转动与供汽联系起来。转轴旁有 <Num s="S1">两个</Num> 飞球，上摆臂和下连杆把它们接到同一个滑套。滑套可以沿轴升降。飞球和滑套位置改变时，每根杆的长度保持不变。[S1, S7]<FlyTo preset="governor-view">看看调速器的联动</FlyTo>

在这个模型中，稳定转速越高，飞球张得越开，滑套升得越高。套在滑套外的叉环传递上下运动，却不跟着转轴旋转。连杆和曲柄再把运动传给蒸汽管内的阀片。沿着连接处，就能从飞球一路找到阀片。[S1, S7]

<Term id="throttle">节流阀</Term>控制蒸汽管道的开口。较快的姿态把阀关小，减少进汽；较慢时则相反。输出的转动改变了输入的供汽，这叫<Term id="feedback">反馈</Term>。节流阀负责控制供汽量；配对的进汽阀和排汽阀则引导蒸汽进出汽缸。[S7]

试一试卡片比较三个稳定位置。保持杆长、飞球和重力不变，观察滑套升程与阀开度。这些静止画面展示连杆怎样传递运动，没有计算负载改变后转速如何变化，也不表示机器能完全恒速运转。[S1]
