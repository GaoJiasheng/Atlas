<Lang en>

The <Term id="centrifugal-governor">centrifugal governor</Term> connects rotation to the steam supply. Its spindle supports <Num s="S1">two</Num> flyballs, with upper arms and lower links meeting a single sliding collar. The collar can move along the spindle. This chapter compares complete stationary arrangements, so each rod retains its length while the ball and collar positions change together. [S1, S7] <FlyTo preset="governor-view">Look at the governor linkage</FlyTo>

In this ideal static model, a higher steady speed corresponds to balls farther from the spindle and a higher collar. A fork around the collar carries its vertical movement without revolving with the spindle. The fork connects to a throttle rod and crank, which turn the valve disc in the steam passage. Trace that complete route from the balls to the valve. [S1, S7]

The <Term id="throttle">throttle valve</Term> restricts the supply passage. In the faster model position it closes farther, limiting steam admission. At lower speed, the arrangement reverses. This is <Term id="feedback">feedback</Term>: an output, rotation, affects the input supplying it. The throttle does not act as a brake on the flywheel, and it has a different job from the paired inlet and exhaust valves. [S7]

Use the card to compare the steady model positions while keeping the links, balls and gravity unchanged. Observe collar rise and throttle opening. These snapshots explain the direction of the linkage’s action. They do not calculate a machine’s changing speed after a load change, the actual amount of steam passing the opening or perfectly constant running speed. [S1]

<Term id="centrifugal-governor">离心调速器</Term>把转动与供汽联系起来。转轴旁有 <Num s="S1">两个</Num> 飞球，上摆臂和下连杆接到同一个滑套，滑套可以沿轴升降。本章比较完整的静态姿态，球、杆与滑套的位置一起改变，刚杆长度保持不变。[S1, S7]<FlyTo preset="governor-view">看看调速器的联动</FlyTo>

在这个理想静态模型中，较高的稳定转速对应更张开的飞球和更高的滑套。套在滑套外的叉环传递上下运动，却不跟着轴公转。叉环再连接节流连杆和曲柄，转动供汽通道中的阀片。沿着这些连接，可以从飞球一路追踪到阀片。[S1, S7]

<Term id="throttle">节流阀</Term>限制供汽通道的开度。较快的模型姿态把阀关小，限制进汽；较慢时则相反。输出的转动反过来影响供汽输入，这条路径叫<Term id="feedback">反馈</Term>。节流阀不是给飞轮加刹车，也不同于交替开启的进汽阀和排汽阀。[S7]

试一试卡保持杆长、飞球和重力不变，比较稳定姿态下的滑套升程与阀开度。静帧说明联动的方向，却没有计算改变负载后的转速过程，也没有求出实际进汽流量，更不能据此承诺机器完全恒速。每次切换都显示完整静态姿态，而不是播放调速器转轴的旋转。[S1]
