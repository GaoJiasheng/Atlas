<Lang en>

Look at the smaller engine. Its cylinder is open above the piston, so air presses on the top face. Steam enters below. Across the beam, the heavy mine-pump rod moves down and helps raise the engine piston. Choose each still position to follow the steps. Later, compare this open cylinder with Watt’s sealed one. [S11] <FlyTo preset="newcomen-view">Look at the open cylinder</FlyTo>

Cold water sprays into the cylinder. Steam changes into liquid water: this is <Term id="condensation">condensation</Term>. The pressure below the piston falls. Air above still pushes down, while the gas below pushes up less strongly. This <Term id="pressure-difference">pressure difference</Term> gives a downward force. A vacuum does not pull the piston. [S8, S9, S11]

As the engine piston falls, the other end of the beam rises. It lifts the mine-pump rod. Steam then enters again, and the heavy pump rod falls to help raise the engine piston. The flexible chains can pull on the beam’s curved ends. They cannot push like a rigid rod. [S6, S11]

The cold spray also cools the cylinder. Incoming steam has to heat it again. In the next engine, steam works in a hot cylinder and condenses in a separate cold vessel. Follow the cold water, then find where water and air leave each system. [S7, S12]

Read <TopicLink slug="states-of-matter" ch="air-is-matter">States of Matter: air is matter</TopicLink> for more about air. In this calculated example, we use the model’s piston area. Air above is at <Num s="S1">101.325 kPa abs</Num>, and the gas below is at <Num s="S1">20 kPa abs</Num>. The downward force is about <Num s="S1">14.487 kN</Num>. The units mean kilopascals of absolute pressure and kilonewtons of force. Absolute pressure is measured from a vacuum. Unlike the later sealed engine, this piston has no rod passing through a top cover, so no rod area needs to be subtracted. [S1, S8]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Newcomen’s engine", zh: "纽科门蒸汽机" }}>

Newcomen’s engine near Dudley dates to <Num s="S11">1712</Num>. The later engine preserved in the museum was built afterwards. This smaller model shows how an atmospheric engine pumps. Its chains pull; a double-acting engine needs links that can both push and pull. [S6, S11]

<Lang zh>

先看旁边的小蒸汽机。活塞上方的汽缸敞开，空气直接压在活塞上。蒸汽从下方进入。横梁另一侧沉重的矿井泵杆下降，帮助蒸汽机的活塞升起。逐个切换静止画面，就能看清步骤。后面还可以把它与瓦特上端密封的汽缸比较。[S11]<FlyTo preset="newcomen-view">看看开口汽缸</FlyTo>

冷水喷进汽缸，蒸汽变成液态水，这叫<Term id="condensation">凝结</Term>。活塞下方的压力降低。上方空气仍向下推，下方气体向上推的力却减小了。两侧的<Term id="pressure-difference">压强差</Term>产生向下的合力，并不是“真空在拉”活塞。[S8, S9, S11]

活塞下降时，横梁另一端升起，把矿井泵杆提起来。蒸汽再次进入后，沉重的泵杆下降，帮助活塞回到高处。连接横梁弧形端部的链条只能拉，不能像刚杆那样推。[S6, S11]

冷水也冷却了汽缸。下一次通入的蒸汽，又得把汽缸加热。接下来的机器让蒸汽在热汽缸里做功，再到独立的冷容器里凝结。比较时，先找冷水流向哪里，再看水和空气怎样离开。[S7, S12]
