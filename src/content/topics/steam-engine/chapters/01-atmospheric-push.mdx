<Lang en>

At the comparison station, the cylinder is open above the piston. Its upper face is exposed to air. Steam enters the space below it, while the heavy mine-pump rod on the other side of the beam helps raise the engine piston. The model shows distinct end positions so that this sequence can be followed without confusing it with the later sealed cylinder. [S11] <FlyTo preset="newcomen-view">Look at the open cylinder</FlyTo>

Next, cold water sprays inside the cylinder. The steam changes to liquid water: this is <Term id="condensation">condensation</Term>. The pressure below the piston falls. Air still presses on the upper face, while the contents below press upwards less strongly. The resulting <Term id="pressure-difference">pressure difference</Term> produces a downward force. A vacuum is not a substance that pulls the piston. [S8, S9, S11]

As the engine piston descends, the other end of the beam rises and lifts the mine-pump rod. Steam can then enter again. The weighted pump side descends and helps return the engine piston to its high position. The flexible chain segments transmit tension; they cannot push a rod upwards as a rigid connection could. [S6, S11]

This arrangement cools the working cylinder itself. When steam enters again, that cylinder has to warm again. The next chapter separates the place where steam works from the place where it condenses. Follow the cold-water route in both models to see the change; a cold container beside an engine is not enough to explain the complete pumping system. [S7, S12]

Chains can pull on the beam’s curved ends, but a double-acting engine needs a connection that can transmit forces in both directions. [S6, S11]

<Lang zh>

比较工位上的汽缸，活塞上方敞开，直接接触空气。蒸汽从下面进入；横梁另一侧沉重的矿井泵杆下降，帮助蒸汽机活塞升起来。这里用分开的端态展示过程，便于与后面上端密封的汽缸比较。[S11]<FlyTo preset="newcomen-view">看看开口汽缸</FlyTo>

接着，冷水喷入缸内，蒸汽变成液态水，这叫<Term id="condensation">凝结</Term>。活塞下面的压力随之降低。上方的空气仍然向下推，下方的内容物向上推得较弱，于是两侧的<Term id="pressure-difference">压强差</Term>产生向下的合力。并不是有一种叫“真空”的东西在拉活塞。[S8, S9, S11]

蒸汽机活塞下降时，横梁另一端升起，把矿井泵杆提起来。随后重新通入蒸汽，较重的泵杆一侧下降，帮助机构回程。连接横梁弧形端部的链段只能承受拉力，不能像刚杆那样把力朝两个方向传递。[S6, S11]

这种做法把工作汽缸本身也冷却了，下一次通汽时又得重新加热。下一章把蒸汽做功的地方与蒸汽冷凝的地方分开。比较两套模型时，先找冷水流向哪里，再看水和空气怎样离开；沿拍轴回看时，还能把喷冷水、向下做功和回程分别停住观察。只在机器旁边放一个冷罐，还没有解释完整的抽水系统。[S7, S12]
