<Lang en>

The working cylinder and the cold vessel now stand apart. Exhaust steam travels to a <Term id="separate-condenser">separate condenser</Term>, where it becomes liquid. Cold water no longer has to be sprayed into the cylinder that receives the working steam. Keeping these roles separate avoids repeatedly cooling that cylinder directly during condensation. [S12] <FlyTo preset="condenser-view">Follow the exhaust to the condenser</FlyTo>

This model uses a jet condenser. The injection pipe carries cooling water into the vessel, where it meets exhaust steam. Condensation releases heat to the cooling water. The water leaving is a mixture of condensed steam and injected water; the arrangement is not a sealed loop carrying only pure boiler water. The outside cistern and its pipes are part of the cooling-water route. [S7, S9]

The <Term id="air-pump">air pump</Term> removes water and air from the condenser. Its non-return valves allow the mixture to move towards the hot well, a collecting tank for warm water. A feed pump sends some water from there back to the boiler. Excess water leaves through an overflow, while fresh cooling water enters the system. These connections matter as much as the cold vessel itself. [S7]

In the try-it card, keep the piston and hot-side pressure unchanged, then raise the cold-side pressure. The calculated downward force becomes smaller. This compares pressure forces; it does not calculate water temperature, the rate of condensation or fuel savings. The dots in the static pumping views mark connected routes while the mechanism stays at its selected phase. [S1, S8]

<Lang zh>

现在，工作汽缸与冷容器分开了。排出的蒸汽沿管道进入<Term id="separate-condenser">分离冷凝器</Term>，在那里变成水。冷凝时不必再把冷水直接喷进接收工作蒸汽的汽缸，从而避免一次次直接冷却它。[S12]<FlyTo preset="condenser-view">沿排汽管找到冷凝器</FlyTo>

主模型采用喷射式示意：注水管把冷水送进冷凝器，在里面接触排汽。蒸汽凝结时，把热量传给冷却水。离开的水混合了凝结水与喷入的冷水，并不是只有纯净锅炉水的密闭循环。外面的冷水箱和管路也属于冷却水系统。[S7, S9]

<Term id="air-pump">抽气泵</Term>把水和空气移出冷凝器。止回阀让混合物朝热井前进；热井就是收集温水的水槽。给水泵把其中一部分送回锅炉，多余的水从溢流口离开，新的冷却水则不断补进来。解释冷凝系统时，这些接头和流向都不能省略。找到注水口之后，还要沿着混合排水管继续看到热井。[S7]

在试一试卡片中，保持活塞与热侧压力不变，再提高冷侧压力，计算出的向下合力会减小。这是压力的比较，没有根据水温计算凝结速率，也没有推算节煤比例。静帧里的机构保持选定相位，小点只用来显示管道路径。[S1, S8]
