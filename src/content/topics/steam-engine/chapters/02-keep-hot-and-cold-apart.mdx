<Lang en>

The working cylinder and cold vessel now stand apart. Exhaust steam, or steam leaving the cylinder, travels to a <Term id="separate-condenser">separate condenser</Term> and becomes liquid. The cylinder can stay hot because cold water is no longer sprayed into it each time steam condenses. [S12] <FlyTo preset="condenser-view">Follow the exhaust to the condenser</FlyTo>

This is a jet condenser. A pipe brings in cold water from the outside cistern. Inside the vessel, the water meets the exhaust steam. As the steam condenses, it gives heat to the cooling water. The water leaving contains both condensed steam and injected cooling water. [S7, S9]

The <Term id="air-pump">air pump</Term> removes water and air from the condenser. Non-return valves let them travel one way towards the hot well, a tank that collects warm water. A feed pump returns some water to the boiler. Extra water leaves through an overflow, and fresh cooling water enters. [S7]

Use the try-it card to keep the piston and hot-side pressure the same, then raise the cold-side pressure. The downward force becomes smaller. This card compares pressure forces only. While the engine is still, moving dots let you trace the connected pipes. [S1, S8]

<More title={{ en: "Pressure and the pump", zh: "压力与抽气泵" }}>

In this example, the hot side stays at <Num s="S1">110 kPa abs</Num>. Raising the cold side from <Num s="S1">20</Num> to <Num s="S1">50 kPa abs</Num> reduces the downward force from about <Num s="S1">16.022</Num> to <Num s="S1">10.677 kN</Num>. The beam moves the pump rod, so the engine also supplies work to the air pump. That pump work is left out of this simple calculation. Water temperature, condensation rate and coal savings are not calculated here. [S1, S7]

<Lang zh>

工作汽缸与冷容器现在分开了。排汽就是离开汽缸的蒸汽，它沿管道进入<Term id="separate-condenser">分离冷凝器</Term>，在那里变成水。冷凝时不必再把冷水喷进工作汽缸，汽缸就能保持热。[S12]<FlyTo preset="condenser-view">沿排汽管找到冷凝器</FlyTo>

这是喷射式冷凝器。冷水从外面的水箱经过管道，进入容器，与排出的蒸汽接触。蒸汽凝结时，把热量传给冷却水。排出的水既有凝结成的水，也有喷入的冷却水。[S7, S9]

<Term id="air-pump">抽气泵</Term>把水和空气移出冷凝器。止回阀只让它们朝一个方向流向热井；热井是收集温水的水槽。给水泵把一部分水送回锅炉，多余的水从溢流口排出，新的冷却水则继续补进来。[S7]

在试一试卡片中，保持活塞与热侧压力不变，再提高冷侧压力，向下合力就会减小。卡片只比较压力产生的力。机构静止时，移动的小点帮助你追踪相连的管路。[S1, S8]
