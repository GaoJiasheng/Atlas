<Lang en>

The beam turns about a fixed support. Its end follows an arc, while the piston rod must pass through the cylinder’s seal along a nearly straight path. Simply fastening the rod to the beam end would force these paths together. The linked bars above the cylinder provide a different connection, with joints that allow the members to turn relative to one another. [S6] <FlyTo preset="linkage-view">Look at the links above the cylinder</FlyTo>

Watt’s <Term id="parallel-motion">parallel motion</Term> combines an approximate straight-line linkage with a parallelogram. A short inner link joins the beam to a radius rod. The beam has its own fixed <Term id="pivot">pivot</Term>, and the radius rod turns about a different fixed pivot. These supports must stay separate: joining them would create a different mechanism. The moving joints trace paths determined by the lengths of the connected bars. [S6]

The added parallelogram carries the nearly straight motion to the piston rod. Trace its outline around the inner link, the beam section, the hanging outer link and the bottom link. Some bars appear to cross when viewed from the front. Rotate the model to see that they occupy different depths. There is no hinge at their crossing, so they pass without becoming another joint. [S6]

Choose the successive still phases on the beat rail and watch the output connection. Its path is close to straight, rather than mathematically exact. How close depends on the chosen lengths. This reconstructed geometry preserves the linked motion and allows a small sideways movement; the displayed gaps are enlarged so that the difference remains visible. The illustration explains the connections, not their manufacturing dimensions. [S1, S6, S14]

横梁绕固定支座摆动，端点走的是弧线；活塞杆穿过汽缸密封口时，却需要近似直线的路径。若把杆简单固定在横梁端点，两种路径就会冲突。汽缸上方的连杆用可转动的连接点，把这两种运动接起来。[S6]<FlyTo preset="linkage-view">看看汽缸上方的连杆</FlyTo>

瓦特的<Term id="parallel-motion">平行运动机构</Term>，把近似直线机构与平行四边形组合起来。内侧短连杆连接横梁和摇杆。横梁有自己的固定<Term id="pivot">支点或枢轴</Term>，摇杆则绕另一个固定点转动，两个固定点不能合并。活动连接点的路径，由相连杆件的长度共同决定。[S6]

加上的平行四边形，把近似直线运动传到活塞杆。沿内侧短杆、横梁的一段、外侧吊杆和底杆，可以找到它的轮廓。正面看似交叉的杆，实际上处于不同深度；旋转模型便能看清。交叉处没有铰链，两根杆没有在那里接成新的关节。[S6]

依次点击拍轴上的静态相位，观察输出连接点。它走得接近直线，却不是数学上的精确直线，接近程度还取决于杆长。本研究保留重建几何产生的微小横移，并放大显示间隙。配图只用来解释哪根杆接在哪里，不能当作制造尺寸图。[S1, S6, S14]
