<Lang en>

The beam rocks about a fixed support. Its end follows an arc, but the piston rod needs a nearly straight path through the cylinder seal. Fixing the rod directly to the beam end would force it sideways. The jointed bars above the cylinder connect these different paths. [S6] <FlyTo preset="linkage-view">Look at the links above the cylinder</FlyTo>

Watt’s <Term id="parallel-motion">parallel motion</Term> combines a nearly straight-line linkage with a parallelogram. A short inner link joins the beam to a radius rod. A <Term id="pivot">pivot</Term> is a joint that lets a part turn. The beam and radius rod each turn about their own fixed pivot. The bar lengths determine where the moving joints travel. [S6]

The parallelogram carries the nearly straight motion to the piston rod. Trace its four sides: the inner link, part of the beam, the outer hanging link and the bottom link. Rotate the model to look at the crossing bars. They pass at different depths, with no hinge at the crossing. [S6]

Choose each still position on the beat rail and watch the piston-rod connection. Its path is nearly straight. How closely it follows a straight line depends on the link lengths. The enlarged gaps let you see the small sideways movement in this reconstruction. [S1, S6, S14]

<Lang zh>

横梁绕固定支座摆动，端点走弧线。活塞杆穿过汽缸密封口时，却需要近似直线的路径。若把杆直接固定在横梁端点，横梁就会迫使它向旁边移动。汽缸上方的连杆用可转动的连接点，把这两种路径接起来。[S6]<FlyTo preset="linkage-view">看看汽缸上方的连杆</FlyTo>

瓦特的<Term id="parallel-motion">平行运动机构</Term>结合了近似直线机构和平行四边形。内侧短连杆连接横梁与摇杆。<Term id="pivot">支点或枢轴</Term>是让零件绕着转动的连接点。横梁和摇杆各有一个独立的固定支点。活动连接点的路径由杆长决定。[S6]

平行四边形把近似直线运动传给活塞杆。沿着内侧短杆、横梁的一段、外侧吊杆和底杆，找出它的四条边。旋转模型，看看交叉的杆件：它们一前一后地通过，交叉处没有铰链。[S6]

逐个点击节拍刻度，观察活塞杆连接点。它的路径接近直线，但仍有微小横移，横移大小取决于杆长。这个重建模型放大了间隙，方便看清这点差别。[S1, S6, S14]
