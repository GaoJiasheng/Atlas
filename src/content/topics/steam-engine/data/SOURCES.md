## S1 · Reproducible design study

### Reference dimensions and disagreement

S3 Lap paragraph (PDF page 7 of 8, after the Whitbread dimensions): bore 18.75 in, stroke 4 ft. S4 catalogue: bore 18 in, stroke 4 ft. S5 author-researcher Hulse: flywheel 16 ft. Exact S16 conversions, 1 in = 0.0254 m and 1 ft = 0.3048 m, give 0.47625 / 0.4572 / 1.2192 / 4.8768 m. Display rounds the selected reference dimensions to about 0.476 / 1.219 / 4.88 m. The catalogue disagreement is retained without inventing a cause. ASME gives 296 outer rim teeth; Hulse gives 304. The production model omits a countable outer tooth ring; its equal 48/48 sun-and-planet teeth are a different DESIGN parameter.

### Geometry and rigid closure

Metres, +Y up, +Z towards viewer. Main-mechanism origin `[0,5.6,0]`. Local fixed points A=(0,0), O=(2.5,−3), F=(−2.5,−0.8). L=2.5 (beam half length), l=3 (drive rod), r=0.6092838281609874 (orbit), a=0.8 (short links), b=1.25 (radius/bottom links), s=2.4 (piston rod), cylinderX=−2.5. Pump beam fraction −0.4, sliderX=−1, rod=4.5. All reconstructed except the recorded cylinder stroke. The independent Newcomen module and energy ledger stand at separate teaching stations; no claim of historical floor layout.

For circle centres c0/c1, radii r0/r1 and fixed branch q=±1: d=|c1−c0|, k=(r0²−r1²+d²)/(2d), h²=r0²−k², m=c0+k(c1−c0)/d; result=m+q√h²·perp(c1−c0)/d. Invalid/nonintersecting circles are rejected rather than switching branch. Both main branches are +1.

At phase θ: P=O+r(cosθ,sinθ); K=circle(A,L,P,l,+1); C=A−(K−A)/2; B=A−(K−A); E=circle(C,a,F,b,+1); D=B+E−C; piston S=D−(0,s). A/F are fixed distinct pivots; C-B-D-E is a parallelogram, EF crosses BD at another depth with no joint. D.x is allowed to vary: no false exact-straight-line constraint. Sampled reconstructed stroke≈1.219200015 m and full lateral motion≈1.795 mm; this is numerical model output, not a measurement or tolerance guarantee. Radial display clearance=3 mm, enlarged for readability; piston radius=0.235125 m, thickness=0.05 m, working cylinder length≈1.5 m. Pump Q=A−0.4(K−A); W.x=−1; W.y=Q.y−√(4.5²−(Q.x+1)²).

Let α=atan2(K.y−P.y,K.x−P.x), δp=wrapped(α−α0), δs=2θ−δp. Planet is rigid with the drive rod; sun, shaft and flywheel share δs. Planet net angle over a complete cycle is zero, output net angle 4π. Instantaneous output ratio is not constant. Equal 48-tooth involutes use pitch radius r/2, module r/48, pressure angle20°, addendum m, dedendum1.25m, total backlash0.0003m. Four90° outline sectors per gear meet existing primitive limits. These are display-design teeth, not recovered historical tooth geometry.

Valve logic uses the actual piston derivative dD.y/dθ (central difference ε=1e−5 rad). Negative opens upperIn/lowerOut; positive opens lowerIn/upperOut. Magnitude≤1e−7 m/rad is switch, all shut. This ideal rule is S1, not recorded Lap timing and not fixed to nominal90°/270°. Valve lift0.015m is enlarged display geometry. Boiler radius0.6m/length2.2m, main-pipe radius0.05m and all supports/pipe paths/cameras are reconstruction. Flow dots are routes, not molecules, mass/volume rates or temperatures; display speed0.12m/s, spread0.004m and particle counts/sizes are visual settings.
