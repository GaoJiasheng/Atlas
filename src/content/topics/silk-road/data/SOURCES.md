# The Silk Roads — sources

**Draft (docs/73).** Numbered facts are `[S#]`, generated below from `sources.json` by `pnpm tsx scripts/sources-md.ts silk-road`; map geometry is `[G#]`. Survey, Cliopatria inventory, per-keyframe records, ring checks, route statistics and rebuild commands: `scripts/geo/silk-road/SOURCES-GEO.md` and `INVENTORY.md`. Every number in the text links to one of the sources below. Facts that could not be read in a primary or scholarly source were cut (docs/73 §12).

Map data licences: control areas are derived from Cliopatria (CC BY 4.0); rivers, coasts and the route raster from Natural Earth (public domain); every point from Wikidata (CC0). Route lines are Atlas's own, built by rule from those points and the stop lists of the written sources. The published files are under the content licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) with attribution. No CC BY-SA or ODbL geometry is used. All coloured areas and all route lines are approximate.

## Map data

- **[G1]** Cliopatria v0.2.1, Seshat Global History Databank — <https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria/releases/tag/v0.2.1> (Zenodo record 13363121; data paper doi:10.1038/s41597-025-04516-9). **CC BY 4.0.** The 14 control keyframes (`data/control.json`). Changes: clipped to the Natural Earth coast, simplified, records of one state merged, overlaps resolved by paint order (SOURCES-GEO §5). The Srivijaya and Majapahit sphere layers are reused unchanged from `ancient-civilisations` (`public/geo/ac-srivijaya.json`, `ac-majapahit.json`).
- **[G2]** Natural Earth 1:10m land, via `public/geo/ancient-world-plate.json` (built for `ancient-civilisations`) — <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/>. **Public domain.** The plate's land and coast; also rasterised (0.025°) to compute the sea legs of the routes.
- **[G3]** Natural Earth 1:10m rivers and lake centerlines, lakes, v5.0.0 — <https://www.naturalearthdata.com/>. **Public domain.** Today's rivers and the outlines of the Aral Sea and Issyk-Kul (`public/geo/silk-road-rivers.json`).

Not used: al-Thurayya route geometry (its DATA-LICENSE.md excludes "source-derived material"; the routes are georeferenced from Cornu's 1983 atlas, so the 9th-century routes are drawn from Ibn Khurradadhbih's stops instead), Pleiades (no coordinate adopted), Commons maps (none traced), Itiner-e, ORBIS, CHGIS, OWTRAD.

## Same-place events

Events that share one point with another event or with a site fan out around it on screen (`displayOffset`): clockwise every 40° from north at 72 px, in date order, the site keeping the point (the zheng-he rule). Shared points: Chang’an (3 events), Khanbaliq (3), Baghdad (2), Guangzhou (2), and one event each beside the sites of Ferghana, Xuwen, Kashgar, Luoyang, the Yumen Pass, Constantinople, Dunhuang, Mogao, Aden, Karakorum and Caffa.
