In the Chüy Valley near Lake Issyk-Kul, in today's Kyrgyzstan, gravestones from 1338 and 1339 say that the dead died of “pestilence”. In 2022 scientists recovered ancient DNA from <Num s="S79">three</Num> people buried there and found *Yersinia pestis*, the bacterium that causes <Term id="term-plague">plague</Term>. They argue that the great pandemic began in this region; how it then travelled west is not known.

In 1346 plague broke out in the Golden Horde army besieging Caffa, the Genoese port on the Black Sea. In October 1347 ships from Caffa reached Messina in Sicily, and in the same year a ship full of corpses drifted into Alexandria. Within about <Num s="S81">five</Num> years the disease had spread over Europe, North Africa and the Middle East. People at the time called it the great pestilence or the general mortality; “Black Death” is a later name.

Nobody knows exactly how many died. For Europe, estimates run from about <Num s="S81">39</Num> to <Num s="S81">60</Num> per cent of the population. In Egypt, the historian al-Maqrizi reports about <Num s="S82">7,000</Num> deaths a day in Cairo at the peak, around December 1348.

Fear turned into murder. In 1348 and 1349, in town after town, Jews were falsely accused of poisoning wells and killed. On 14 February 1349 the Jews of Strasbourg were burned in their own cemetery; the chronicler Jacob von Königshofen counts about <Num s="S83">2,000</Num> people, and writes that their money was the reason they were killed.

In 1368 the Yuan court left Khanbaliq and the Ming dynasty took power. The next chapter of the sea routes is told in <TopicLink slug="zheng-he" ch="setting-sail">Zheng He</TopicLink>.

<More title={{ en: "How ancient DNA found the cause", zh: "古 DNA 怎样找到病原" }}>

The team tested <Num s="S79">seven</Num> people from the Kara-Djigach and Burana cemeteries. <Num s="S79">Three</Num> from Kara-Djigach carried plague DNA; one gravestone reads “This is the tomb of the believer Sanmaq. He died of pestilence.” That this local outbreak led to the great pandemic is the scientists' interpretation of the evidence, not a written record. [S79]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the estimates differ", zh: "为什么估计差这么多" }}>

No one counted the dead across a whole country, so historians estimate. Ole Benedictow argues for very high losses, around <Num s="S81">60</Num> per cent in several countries; a study of <Num s="S81">274</Num> towns and villages gives an average of about <Num s="S81">39</Num> per cent. For Egypt, Michael Dols estimated that about <Num s="S82">a third</Num> of the people died, and Stuart Borsch argues the losses in the countryside were higher. Whether the pandemic of the 1340s also reached China is still debated. [S81, S82, S85]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Caffa: the corpse story", zh: "卡法：抛尸的说法" }}>

Gabriele de' Mussi, an Italian writer, wrote that the dying besiegers threw corpses into Caffa. Mark Wheelis, who studied the account, thinks this may well have spread the disease inside the town, but that it mattered little for the spread to Europe: plague travelled on many ships and caravans from many places. [S80]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why remember Strasbourg", zh: "为什么要记住斯特拉斯堡" }}>

The people killed in Strasbourg were not killed by the plague: they were killed by their neighbours, who blamed them for it. The chronicle says plainly that their money was the reason. Strasbourg shows where blaming a disease on a group of people can lead. [S83]

</More>

<Lang zh>

在今天吉尔吉斯斯坦伊塞克湖附近的楚河谷，1338 年和 1339 年的墓碑上写着，死者死于“瘟疫”。2022 年，科学家从葬在那里的<Num s="S79">三</Num>个人身上取得古 DNA，找到了引起<Term id="term-plague">鼠疫</Term>的鼠疫杆菌。他们认为，这场大流行是从这一带开始的；它此后怎样传到西方，仍不清楚。

1346 年，围攻黑海边热那亚港口卡法的金帐汗国军中暴发瘟疫。1347 年 10 月，从卡法来的船到了西西里的墨西拿；同一年，一艘满载尸体的船漂进了亚历山大港。大约<Num s="S81">五</Num>年之内，疫病传遍了欧洲、北非和中东。当时的人把它叫作“大瘟疫”“大死亡”；“黑死病”是后来的名字。

究竟死了多少人，没有人确切知道。对欧洲的估计，从人口的约<Num s="S81">39</Num>%到<Num s="S81">60</Num>%不等。在埃及，史家马克里齐记载，约 1348 年 12 月疫情最严重时，开罗每天约有<Num s="S82">7000</Num>人死去。

恐惧变成了杀戮。1348 年和 1349 年，一座又一座城镇的犹太人被诬陷在井里投毒，遭到杀害。1349 年 2 月 14 日，斯特拉斯堡的犹太人在他们自己的墓地里被烧死；编年史家雅各布·冯·柯尼希斯霍芬记为约<Num s="S83">两千</Num>人，并写道，他们被杀是因为他们的钱。

1368 年，元廷撤出大都，明朝取而代之。海路的下一段故事，见<TopicLink slug="zheng-he" ch="setting-sail">郑和下西洋</TopicLink>。

研究团队检测了卡拉-吉加奇和布拉纳两处墓地的<Num s="S79">七</Num>个人，其中卡拉-吉加奇的<Num s="S79">三</Num>人带有鼠疫 DNA。一块墓碑写着：“这是信士桑马克之墓，他死于瘟疫。”说这次本地疫情引发了后来的大流行，是科学家对证据的解释，不是文字记载。[S79]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the estimates differ", zh: "为什么估计差这么多" }}>

没有人清点过一个国家的全部死者，史家只能估算。Ole Benedictow 认为损失极大，几个国家都在<Num s="S81">60</Num>%左右；一项对<Num s="S81">274</Num>个城镇和村庄的研究，得出的平均值约为<Num s="S81">39</Num>%。对埃及，Michael Dols 估计约<Num s="S82">三分之一</Num>的人死去，Stuart Borsch 认为农村的损失更大。14 世纪 40 年代的这场大流行是否也到了中国，至今仍有争论。[S81, S82, S85]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Caffa: the corpse story", zh: "卡法：抛尸的说法" }}>

意大利作者德穆西写道，垂死的围城者把尸体抛进卡法城。研究过这份记载的 Mark Wheelis 认为，这很可能把病带进了城里，但对瘟疫传到欧洲没有多大作用：鼠疫是随着从许多地方出发的船和商队传开的。[S80]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why remember Strasbourg", zh: "为什么要记住斯特拉斯堡" }}>

斯特拉斯堡的这些人不是死于瘟疫，而是被把瘟疫归罪于他们的邻居杀死的。编年史直截了当地说，原因是他们的钱。斯特拉斯堡让我们看到，把疫病归罪于某一群人，会走到哪一步。[S83]

</More>
