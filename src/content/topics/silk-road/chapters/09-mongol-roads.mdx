<Lang en>

When the Great Khan Möngke died in 1259, a civil war over the succession followed, and Khubilai won it in 1264. In 1271 he named his dynasty Yuan; his capital, Khanbaliq or Dadu, was built where Beijing is today. Other Mongol rulers held Iran (the Ilkhanate), Central Asia (the Chagatai <Term id="term-khanate">khanate</Term>) and the western steppe (the Golden Horde, or Ulus of Jochi). On the map they appear as separate realms from the 1330s, as Cliopatria draws them.

People now travelled the whole width of Asia in both directions. Marco Polo, by his own account, left Acre with his father and uncle in 1271 and reached Khubilai about 1275. Rabban Sauma went the other way: a monk of the <Term id="term-church-of-the-east">Church of the East</Term> from Khanbaliq, he was sent west in 1287 by the Ilkhan Arghun, and met the Byzantine emperor in Constantinople, King Philip IV in Paris, King Edward I of England in Gascony and Pope Nicholas IV in Rome.

The Black Sea was a slave market. From about 1260 Genoese merchants at Caffa and Venetians at Tana bought captives from the lands around the sea, and sold many of them to the Mamluk Sultanate of Egypt and Syria, whose army was made up of slave soldiers; others went to Italy.

About 1340 Francesco Pegolotti, a merchant of Florence, wrote a handbook for traders. The road from Tana to Cathay, he says, is “perfectly safe, whether by day or by night”, according to merchants who had used it, and he lists the stops: Astrakhan, Sarai, Urgench, Otrar, Almaliq, Ganzhou, Hangzhou and Khanbaliq.

<More title={{ en: "Did Marco Polo go to China?", zh: "马可·波罗到过中国吗" }}>

In 1995 the historian Frances Wood questioned whether he did. In 2013 Hans Ulrich Vogel argued, from what Polo says about paper money, salt production and government revenues, that he was there. Most dates in the book are uncertain; even Yule, who edited it, warned that they were “corrupt”. [S72, S73, S74]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Rabban Sauma's journey", zh: "拉班·扫马的旅程" }}>

He went by sea from the Black Sea to Constantinople, then to Naples, where he watched a sea battle, Rome, Genoa and Paris; he met Edward I in Gascony, probably at Bordeaux, spent the winter in Genoa and was received by the new pope in Rome at Easter 1288. How he travelled home is not known. [S75]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The Black Sea slave trade", zh: "黑海的奴隶贸易" }}>

Who profited? Genoese and Venetian merchants bought and shipped the captives; Tana, where the Venetians lived, was a Mongol port; the Mamluk sultans and amirs bought the slaves and owned them by the tens of thousands. In Genoa itself, slaves made up a small but growing share of the population. Most of those sold were at first Tatars, later Circassians. [S71]

</More>

<Lang zh>

1259 年大汗蒙哥去世，随后爆发了一场争夺汗位的内战，忽必烈在 1264 年获胜。1271 年他定国号为“大元”，都城大都（汗八里）建在今天北京的位置。其他蒙古统治者分据伊朗（伊利汗国）、中亚（察合台<Term id="term-khanate">汗国</Term>）和西部草原（金帐汗国，即术赤兀鲁思）。按 Cliopatria 的画法，地图上从 14 世纪 30 年代起才把它们分开画。

这时有人在亚洲的东西两头之间走完全程。按马可·波罗自己的说法，他在 1271 年随父亲和叔叔离开阿卡，约 1275 年见到忽必烈。拉班·扫马走的是反方向：他是来自大都的<Term id="term-church-of-the-east">东方教会</Term>僧人，1287 年受伊利汗阿鲁浑派遣西行，在君士坦丁堡见到东罗马皇帝，在巴黎见到法王腓力四世，在加斯科涅见到英王爱德华一世，在罗马见到教宗尼古拉四世。

黑海是奴隶市场。约 1260 年起，卡法的热那亚商人和塔纳的威尼斯商人从黑海四周买入俘虏，许多人被卖到埃及和叙利亚的马穆鲁克苏丹国，那里的军队由奴隶兵组成；也有人被运到意大利。

约 1340 年，佛罗伦萨商人裴哥罗梯为商人写了一本手册。他说，据走过的商人讲，从塔纳到契丹的路“日夜都十分安全”，并列出沿途站点：阿斯特拉罕、萨莱、乌尔根奇、讹答剌、阿力麻里、甘州、行在（杭州）和汗八里。

<More title={{ en: "Did Marco Polo go to China?", zh: "马可·波罗到过中国吗" }}>

1995 年，史学家吴芳思对此提出怀疑。2013 年，傅汉思根据书中关于纸币、盐业和税收的记载，认为他确实到过中国。书中的年代大多不确定，连整理此书的玉尔也提醒，书里的日期多有讹误。[S72, S73, S74]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Rabban Sauma's journey", zh: "拉班·扫马的旅程" }}>

他从黑海坐船到君士坦丁堡，再到那不勒斯，在那里看到一场海战，然后到罗马、热那亚和巴黎；他在加斯科涅见到爱德华一世，地点可能是波尔多；他在热那亚过冬，1288 年复活节在罗马受到新教宗接见。他是怎样回去的，没有记载。[S75]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The Black Sea slave trade", zh: "黑海的奴隶贸易" }}>

谁从中得利？热那亚和威尼斯商人买入并运送俘虏；威尼斯人居住的塔纳是蒙古人的港口；马穆鲁克的苏丹和埃米尔买下奴隶，拥有的人数以万计。在热那亚本城，奴隶在人口中所占的比例虽小，却在增长。被卖的人起初多是鞑靼人，后来多是切尔克斯人。[S71]

</More>
