<Lang en>

In the 2nd century BCE the Xiongnu ruled the steppe north of China. Under treaties of “peace and kinship”, the early Han emperors sent the Xiongnu ruler a princess as a wife and fixed yearly gifts of silk, wine and grain. Xiongnu who came over to the Han told the court that the Xiongnu had killed the king of a people called the Yuezhi and driven them away, and that the Yuezhi hated them. The court wanted to destroy the Xiongnu, and about 138 BCE it sent an officer, Zhang Qian, to find the Yuezhi and ask them to fight together.

Zhang Qian left with more than <Num s="S4">100</Num> men. The Xiongnu captured him and held him for more than <Num s="S4">ten</Num> years; he married there and had a son. He escaped west to Dayuan in the Ferghana Valley, which guided him on to Kangju and the Yuezhi, and from there he went to Daxia, or Bactria. But the Yuezhi would not agree to an alliance. On the way home the Xiongnu caught him again. When their ruler died a year later he escaped, and about 126 BCE he reached Chang'an: of everyone who set out, only <Num s="S4">two</Num> came back. The historian Sima Qian wrote that Zhang Qian had “bored through” the way to the west.

The Han then pushed west. They set up commanderies along the Hexi Corridor, though the Han History gives different years for them in different chapters. Between 104 and 101 BCE two Han armies marched to Dayuan to take its famous horses; Sima Qian blames the heavy losses on officers who cheated their own soldiers. In 60 BCE Zheng Ji became the first <Term id="term-protector-general">Protector-General</Term> of the <Term id="term-western-regions">Western Regions</Term>, with his seat at Wulei.

<More title={{ en: "Only one side wrote it down", zh: "只有一方留下了文字" }}>

The Xiongnu left no written history. Everything here comes from Han historians, who wrote as officials of the Xiongnu's enemy. They tell us what the Han court wanted, not what Xiongnu leaders thought about trade, the frontier or the Han. [S4]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The cost of the Dayuan war, in Sima Qian's figures", zh: "大宛之战的代价：司马迁的数字" }}>

The first army had <Num s="S4">6,000</Num> horsemen from the dependent peoples and “tens of thousands” of young men; when it got back to Dunhuang, “not more than one or two in ten” were left. The second army of <Num s="S4">60,000</Num> left Dunhuang. It took “several dozen” fine horses and more than <Num s="S4">3,000</Num> others; a little more than <Num s="S4">10,000</Num> men and about <Num s="S4">1,000</Num> horses came back through the Jade Gate. Sima Qian says the army did not lack food and few died in battle: many died because their officers were greedy and did not care for them. How many people of Dayuan died is not recorded. [S4]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Horses, grapes and alfalfa", zh: "马、葡萄和苜蓿" }}>

Sima Qian describes Dayuan as a land of settled farmers who made wine from grapes and their horses ate alfalfa. Han envoys brought the seeds back, and the emperor had grapes and alfalfa planted; later both grew beside his country palaces “as far as the eye could see”. Later stories said Zhang Qian brought them himself; Sima Qian says it was the envoys. [S4]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Whose word is “Western Regions”?", zh: "“西域”是谁的词" }}>

“Western Regions” was the Han name for the lands beyond the Yumen and Yang passes. The Han History lists dozens of separate states there, each with its own king, towns and population. Two roads ran past them, south and north of the great desert; on this map they are the dashed lines. [S10]

</More>

<Lang zh>

公元前 2 世纪，中国北方的草原由匈奴统治。汉初按“和亲”之约，把宗室女子嫁给匈奴单于，每年送去定量的丝絮、缯帛、酒和粮食。投降汉朝的匈奴人说，匈奴杀了月氏王，赶走了月氏，月氏一直怨恨匈奴。汉廷正想消灭匈奴，约公元前 138 年派官员张骞去寻找月氏，约他们一起作战。

张骞出发时带了<Num s="S4">一百</Num>多人。他被匈奴抓住，扣留了<Num s="S4">十</Num>多年，在那里娶妻生子。后来他向西逃到费尔干纳盆地的大宛，大宛派人带路，送他到康居和月氏，他又从月氏到了大夏，也就是巴克特里亚。可是月氏不肯结盟。回程时他又被匈奴抓住，一年多后匈奴单于去世，他趁乱逃脱，约在公元前 126 年回到长安：出发的人里只有<Num s="S4">两</Num>个回来。史学家司马迁说，张骞这一行是“凿空”，也就是打通了通往西方的道路。

此后汉朝向西推进，在河西走廊设郡；不过《汉书》不同篇章记下的设郡年份并不一致。公元前 104 年到前 101 年，汉朝两次出兵远征大宛，夺取那里有名的马；司马迁把巨大的伤亡归咎于克扣士兵的将吏。公元前 60 年，郑吉成为第一任<Term id="term-western-regions">西域</Term><Term id="term-protector-general">都护</Term>，治所在乌垒。

<More title={{ en: "Only one side wrote it down", zh: "只有一方留下了文字" }}>

匈奴没有留下自己的史书。这里讲的一切都来自汉朝史家，他们是以匈奴对手的官员身份来写的。他们告诉我们汉廷想要什么，却说不出匈奴首领怎样看待贸易、边境和汉朝。[S4]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The cost of the Dayuan war, in Sima Qian's figures", zh: "大宛之战的代价：司马迁的数字" }}>

第一次出征有属国骑兵<Num s="S4">六千</Num>人，加上各郡国“恶少年数万人”；回到敦煌时，“士不过什一二”。第二次有<Num s="S4">六万</Num>人从敦煌出发，夺得“善马数十匹”和其他马<Num s="S4">三千</Num>多匹；回到玉门关的只有<Num s="S4">一万</Num>多人、军马<Num s="S4">一千</Num>多匹。司马迁说，军队并不缺粮，战死的也不多，许多人是因为将吏贪婪、不爱惜士兵才死去的。大宛有多少人死去，没有记载。[S4]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Horses, grapes and alfalfa", zh: "马、葡萄和苜蓿" }}>

司马迁笔下的大宛人定居耕田，用葡萄酿酒，马吃苜蓿。“汉使取其实来”，天子开始在肥沃的土地上种苜蓿和葡萄，后来离宫别馆旁边种得“极望”。后世说是张骞亲自带回来的，司马迁记的是汉使。[S4]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Whose word is “Western Regions”?", zh: "“西域”是谁的词" }}>

“西域”是汉朝人对玉门关、阳关以西地方的称呼。《汉书》在那里列出几十个国家，各有自己的国王、城镇和人口。两条路分别从大沙漠的南北两边经过这些国家，就是地图上的虚线。[S10]

</More>
