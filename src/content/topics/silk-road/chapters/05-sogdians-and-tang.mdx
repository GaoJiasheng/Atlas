<Lang en>

In 629 the monk Xuanzang asked permission to travel to India to study Buddhism. The court refused, and he went anyway; an order went out for his arrest. He crossed the oasis kingdoms, the Tian Shan and Central Asia to India, and in 645 came back to Chang'an with <Num s="S31">657</Num> texts carried by <Num s="S31">20</Num> horses.

Other faiths travelled east. In 635 Aluoben, a missionary of the <Term id="term-church-of-the-east">Church of the East</Term>, reached Chang'an; a stone stele of 781, in Chinese and Syriac, tells the story of his church in China.

Many of the merchants were <Term id="term-sogdians">Sogdians</Term>. Some of what they traded was people. A Sogdian contract of 639, found in the Turfan oasis, records the sale of a girl from Samarkand to a Chinese Buddhist monk named Zhang for <Num s="S52">120</Num> silver coins, and gives the buyer the right to beat her, tie her up, sell her or give her away.

In 755 An Lushan, a Tang general whose father was Sogdian and whose mother was Turkic, rebelled and took the provinces north of the Yellow River. The fighting lasted until 763. The number of people on the government's registers fell from about <Num s="S18">52.9 million</Num> in 755 to about <Num s="S19">16.9 million</Num> in 764; these are names on registers, not counts of the living or the dead. In 786, after a long siege, the Tibetan Empire took Dunhuang.

<More title={{ en: "One contract", zh: "一纸契约" }}>

The contract lists what the buyer may do: “beat his slave, maltreat her, tie her up, sell her, hold her hostage, give her as a gift”. The girl's own name is not given in the sources read for this topic. The contract is written in Sogdian and was drawn up in the Turfan oasis. It shows that the people traded on these routes included enslaved women and children. [S52]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A census that collapsed", zh: "崩溃的户籍" }}>

The registered population of 755 was <Num s="S18">52,919,309</Num>. Nine years later the Ministry of Revenue reported “more than <Num s="S19">16.9 million</Num>”. Both are counts of people on the government's registers, not of everyone alive. The difference between them is not a death toll, and no reliable count of the war's dead was found for this topic. [S18, S19]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "A stone in two scripts", zh: "两种文字的石碑" }}>

The stele set up in 781 is inscribed in Chinese and Syriac and begins with Aluoben's arrival in 635. Its Chinese title calls the faith Jingjiao. [S53]

</More>

<Lang zh>

629 年，僧人玄奘请求去印度求法，朝廷“有诏不许”，他还是走了，官府随即下令捉拿他。他穿过绿洲诸国、天山和中亚到了印度，645 年带着<Num s="S31">657</Num>部经书回到长安，用<Num s="S31">二十</Num>匹马驮运。

别的信仰也向东传来。635 年，<Term id="term-church-of-the-east">东方教会</Term>的传教士阿罗本到达长安；781 年立的一块石碑，用中文和叙利亚文讲述这个教会在中国的经过。唐代称这个教会为“景教”。

商人里有许多<Term id="term-sogdians">粟特人</Term>。他们买卖的货物里，也有人。吐鲁番绿洲出土的一份 639 年粟特文契约，记下一个撒马尔罕女孩被卖给一位姓张的汉人僧人，价钱是<Num s="S52">120</Num>枚银币；契约写明买主可以打她、捆她、转卖她或把她送人。

755 年，父亲是粟特人、母亲是突厥人的唐朝将领安禄山起兵，攻陷河北诸郡。战乱持续到 763 年。朝廷登记的人口从 755 年的约<Num s="S18">5290 万</Num>降到 764 年的约<Num s="S19">1690 万</Num>；这是登记在册的人数，不是活着或死去的人数。786 年，吐蕃经过长期围困攻下敦煌。

契约列出买主可以做的事：“打她、虐待她、捆绑她、转卖她、拿她作抵押、把她送人”。本主题读到的资料没有写出这个女孩自己的名字。契约用粟特文写成，订立于吐鲁番绿洲。它说明，在这些路上被买卖的，也有被奴役的妇女和孩子。[S52]

</More>
