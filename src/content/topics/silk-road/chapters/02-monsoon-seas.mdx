<Lang en>

At sea, the routes were set by the <Term id="term-monsoon">monsoon</Term>: in one half of the year the wind blew one way, and ships waited for it to turn. The peoples of island Southeast Asia were long known as skilled sailors, and Chinese records of the 3rd century CE describe their large ships in Chinese harbours. Indian, Persian, Arab and Greek ships used the same seas. Modern writers call these sea lanes the <Term id="term-maritime-silk-road">Maritime Silk Road</Term>. The <TopicLink slug="ancient-civilisations" ch="southeast-asia-seas">Southeast Asia chapter of Ancient Civilisations</TopicLink> tells the story of the ports between India and China.

In 111 BCE Han armies conquered Nanyue, a kingdom on the southern coast, and the Han made its lands into commanderies. The Han History then describes envoys who sailed for months from Xuwen and Hepu to far countries, with gold and silk to buy pearls, glass and rare stones; “foreign merchants' ships passed them on”. Where those countries were is not known, so the map shows no route for this voyage.

At the western end, a Greek merchant's guide of the 1st century CE, the *Periplus of the Erythraean Sea*, tells ships to leave Egypt in July and sail to Muziris in south India. It says silk from a land it calls Thinae came overland to India's ports. In 412 the Chinese monk Faxian sailed home from Sri Lanka. Around 830 a ship, most likely Arab, sank off Belitung, south-east of Singapore, with close to <Num s="S89">60,000</Num> pieces of Chinese ceramics.

In 879 the rebel army of Huang Chao took Guangzhou. Abu Zayd of Siraf, writing in Arabic, says <Num s="S37">120,000</Num> Muslims, Jews, Christians and Zoroastrians died there; al-Mas'udi gives <Num s="S38">200,000</Num>.

<More title={{ en: "One ship, many origins", zh: "一艘船，几种来历" }}>

The Belitung ship is thought to be Arab: the earliest Arab vessel found in Southeast Asian waters. Its cargo was Chinese, probably loaded at Yangzhou or Guangzhou, and it sank off an island of today's Indonesia. A bowl on board is thought to carry a date equal to 826. Fishermen found the wreck in 1998, and its cargo has been on permanent display at the Asian Civilisations Museum since 2018. [S89]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Guangzhou, 879: only one kind of record", zh: "广州 879 年：只有一种记载" }}>

The killing is known only from Arabic writers. Abu Zayd says the number was known because the Chinese kept registers of foreign residents, and he dates the event to the Islamic year 264 (877–878). The Chinese official history records that Huang Chao attacked Guangzhou and seized its governor, and that disease killed <Num s="S39">four in ten</Num> of his own men, but it says nothing about foreigners being killed. The two Arabic figures differ by <Num s="S37,S38">80,000</Num>. No modern study of these numbers was read for this topic, so the map gives neither as a count. [S37, S38, S39]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "People sold by sea", zh: "海路上被买卖的人" }}>

The *Periplus* lists people among the goods of several ports: “slaves of the better sort” shipped from Opone on the Horn of Africa to Egypt, slaves exported from Ommana in the Gulf to India and Arabia, and young singers and girls brought to the king at Barygaza in India. [S34]

</More>

海上的路由<Term id="term-monsoon">季风</Term>决定：半年里风朝一个方向吹，船要等风转向才能回航。海岛东南亚的人长期被称为娴熟的水手，3 世纪的中国文献已经写到停在中国港口的东南亚大船；印度、波斯、阿拉伯和希腊的船也在这片海上航行。今天的人把这些海路称为<Term id="term-maritime-silk-road">海上丝绸之路</Term>。印度和中国之间那些港口的故事，见<TopicLink slug="ancient-civilisations" ch="southeast-asia-seas">古文明的东南亚一章</TopicLink>。

公元前 111 年，汉军灭了南方沿海的南越国，在那里设郡。《汉书》接着记下汉使从徐闻、合浦出海，航行几个月到远方的国家，带着黄金和丝帛去买珍珠、琉璃和奇石，“蛮夷贾船，转送致之”，也就是靠外国商船一程一程转送。这些国家在哪里，今天说不清，所以地图上不画这条航线。

在西边，公元 1 世纪一位希腊商人写的《厄立特里亚海航行记》，教船在 7 月离开埃及，驶往印度南部的穆济里斯；书中说，丝从一个叫“提奈”的地方经陆路运到印度的港口。412 年，中国僧人法显从斯里兰卡乘船回国。约 830 年，一艘很可能是阿拉伯的船，载着近<Num s="S89">六万</Num>件中国瓷器，在新加坡东南方的勿里洞附近沉没。

879 年，黄巢的起事军队攻占广州。用阿拉伯文写作的尸罗夫人阿布·扎伊德说，那里死了<Num s="S37">十二万</Num>穆斯林、犹太人、基督徒和琐罗亚斯德教徒；马苏第说是<Num s="S38">二十万</Num>。

<More title={{ en: "One ship, many origins", zh: "一艘船，几种来历" }}>

勿里洞沉船被认为是阿拉伯船，是在东南亚海域发现的最早的阿拉伯船。船货来自中国，很可能在扬州或广州装船，最后沉在今天印度尼西亚的一个海岛附近。船上一只碗据信写有相当于 826 年的日期。1998 年渔民发现了这艘沉船，2018 年起船货在亚洲文明博物馆常设展出。[S89]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Guangzhou, 879: only one kind of record", zh: "广州 879 年：只有一种记载" }}>

这场杀戮只见于阿拉伯作者的记载。阿布·扎伊德说，人数之所以知道，是因为中国人登记外国居民；他把这件事记在回历 264 年（877—878 年）。中国正史记下黄巢“急攻广州，执李迢”，也记下他的军中“大疫，众死什四”，即<Num s="S39">十分之四</Num>，却没有提到外国人被杀。两个阿拉伯数字相差<Num s="S37,S38">八万</Num>。本主题没有读到评估这些数字的现代研究，所以地图上不把任何一个当作死亡人数。[S37, S38, S39]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "People sold by sea", zh: "海路上被买卖的人" }}>

《航行记》把人列在好几个港口的货物里：从非洲之角的奥波内运往埃及的“上等奴隶”，从波斯湾的奥马纳运往印度和阿拉伯的奴隶，还有送给印度婆卢羯车国王的少年歌手和少女。[S34]
