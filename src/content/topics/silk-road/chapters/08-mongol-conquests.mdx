<Lang en>

In 1206 the Mongol and other steppe chiefs gathered at the source of the Onon River and gave Temüjin the title Chinggis Khan. By the 1250s the Cliopatria map shows a Mongol empire reaching from northern China across Central Asia and Iran to the steppe beyond the Volga.

The war with Khwarazm began with a trade <Term id="term-caravan">caravan</Term>. In 1218 the governor of Otrar seized a caravan of about <Num s="S66">450</Num> Muslim merchants sent by Chinggis Khan and had them killed; the sources disagree on whether the shah ordered it. In 1219 the Mongols invaded. The Persian historian Juvaini says that after Merv fell in 1221 a count over <Num s="S66">13</Num> days reached more than <Num s="S66">1.3 million</Num> dead; another writer gives <Num s="S68">1,747,000</Num> for Nishapur. Historians say such figures cannot be taken literally, but the massacres were real. At Nishapur, Juvaini says, <Num s="S66">400</Num> craftsmen were spared and carried off.

The Mongol court drew people from everywhere. William of Rubruck, a Franciscan friar, reached Karakorum in 1254; he found a Muslim quarter, a quarter of Chinese craftsmen, temples and a church, and watched Christians, Muslims and Buddhists debate before the Great Khan Möngke. He also met William Boucher, a goldsmith from Paris captured in Hungary.

In 1258 Mongol forces under Hülegü took Baghdad. In 1260 a Mamluk army from Egypt defeated a Mongol army of the Ilkhanate at Ain Jalut.

<More title={{ en: "Why the numbers are so large", zh: "数字为什么这么大" }}>

Sayfi, a historian of Herat, gave <Num s="S68">1,747,000</Num> dead for Nishapur and <Num s="S68">1,600,000</Num> for Herat. The historian David Morgan calls such figures “staggeringly high” and says they should not be read literally. He also describes the shock the conquest caused across the Islamic world. Both things are true. [S66, S68]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "People carried off", zh: "被掳走的人" }}>

The Mongols moved skilled people across the empire by force. At Nishapur craftsmen were spared and taken away; at Karakorum Rubruck met a goldsmith from Paris who had been captured years before at Belgrade, in Hungary, and a quarter of Chinese craftsmen. This was not an exchange of talent: these people did not choose to go. [S66, S69]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Who were the Mamluks?", zh: "马穆鲁克是谁" }}>

“Mamluk” means a slave soldier. The Mamluk rulers of Egypt and Syria were themselves soldiers who had been bought as slaves, many of them Kipchak Turks from the steppe north of the Black Sea, and trained for war. The Black Sea slave trade that supplied them is in the next chapter. [S70, S71]

</More>

1206 年，蒙古和草原各部的首领聚集在斡难河源头，尊铁木真为成吉思汗。到 13 世纪 50 年代，Cliopatria 的地图上，蒙古帝国已从中国北方经中亚、伊朗，一直延伸到伏尔加河以西的草原。

与花剌子模的战争，从一支<Term id="term-caravan">商队</Term>开始。1218 年，讹答剌守将扣下成吉思汗派来的这支商队，约<Num s="S66">450</Num>名穆斯林商人，把他们杀了；这是不是苏丹下的令，史料说法不一。1219 年蒙古军进攻花剌子模。波斯史家志费尼说，1221 年梅尔夫陷落后，<Num s="S66">十三</Num>天里清点出的死者超过<Num s="S66">一百三十万</Num>；另一位作者说内沙布尔死了<Num s="S68">1,747,000</Num>人。史家认为这样的数字不能照字面理解，但屠杀确实发生过。志费尼说，内沙布尔只留下<Num s="S66">400</Num>名工匠，把他们带走。

蒙古宫廷汇集了各地的人。方济各会修士鲁布鲁克 1254 年到达哈剌和林。他看到那里有穆斯林居住区、汉人工匠居住区、几座庙宇和一座教堂，还看到基督徒、穆斯林和佛教徒在大汗蒙哥面前辩论。他也见到了在匈牙利被俘的巴黎金匠威廉·布歇。

1258 年，旭烈兀率蒙古军攻下巴格达。1260 年，来自埃及的马穆鲁克军在艾因扎鲁特击败了伊利汗国的一支蒙古军。

<More title={{ en: "Why the numbers are so large", zh: "数字为什么这么大" }}>

赫拉特的史家赛菲说内沙布尔死了<Num s="S68">1,747,000</Num>人、赫拉特死了<Num s="S68">1,600,000</Num>人。史学家 David Morgan 说这些数字“高得惊人”，不应照字面理解；他也写到这场征服给整个伊斯兰世界带来的震撼。两件事都是真的。[S66, S68]

</More>

<More title={{ en: "People carried off", zh: "被掳走的人" }}>

蒙古人强迫有手艺的人在帝国各处迁移。内沙布尔的工匠被留下性命、带走；鲁布鲁克在哈剌和林见到一个多年前在匈牙利的贝尔格莱德被俘的巴黎金匠，还有一片汉人工匠的居住区。这不是什么“人才交流”：这些人并没有选择离开家乡。[S66, S69]

</More>
