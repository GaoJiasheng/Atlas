<Lang en>

On the stage is a chilli plant, *Capsicum annuum*, growing in a pot. Above the soil it stands about <Num s="S1">42 cm</Num> tall; chilli plants are usually <Num s="S10">20–80 cm</Num>. It carries flowers, green fruit and ripe red fruit at the same time, so one plant shows almost every part of its life. <FlyTo preset="hero">Start with the whole plant</FlyTo>

A plant is a system: its parts work together, and each part has its own job. The roots hold the plant in the soil and take in water. The stem holds up the leaves and flowers, and carries water and food between them and the roots. The leaves make the plant's food. The flowers make seeds, and the seeds grow inside the fruit.

Look at the shape. A single stem rises from the soil, then splits in two, and each half splits again; chilli stems branch in pairs like this, again and again. The leaves are smooth-edged ovals up to <Num s="S10">13 cm</Num> long. The small white flowers hang below the forks, one at a time.

Some parts are too small to see on the whole plant. They stand beside it as close-ups: a block cut from a leaf, a slice of stem, a root tip, a flower cut in half. Each one's name says how much larger it is drawn, such as ×250 for the leaf block. The scale bar in the plate is true only for the plant itself.

<More title={{ en: "Why a chilli plant", zh: "为什么选辣椒" }}>

Chilli is grown in pots on balconies, in community gardens and in school gardens in Singapore, and NParks publishes a guide to growing it. Its flowers are simple and regular, with every part easy to find, and one plant can carry buds, flowers, green fruit and ripe fruit at once. Chapter 7 uses a green bean seed as well, because its large seed leaves are easier to see than a chilli seed's.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Plants are living things", zh: "植物是有生命的" }}>

Like animals, plants need water, air and energy, grow, respond to light, and reproduce. Plants make their own food using light, and they respire to release energy from that food, by day and by night.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Try it", zh: "试一试" }}>

Find a plant at home, in a park or under your block. Point out its roots (if you cannot see them, where are they?), its stem, its leaves, and any flowers or fruit. Is its stem soft and green, or hard and woody?

</More>

<Lang zh>

舞台上是一盆辣椒（*Capsicum annuum*）。它在土面以上高约 <Num s="S1">42 厘米</Num>；辣椒一般高 <Num s="S10">20–80 厘米</Num>。这一株同时开着花、挂着青果和红果，所以一株植物就能看到它一生中几乎所有的部分。<FlyTo preset="hero">先看整株</FlyTo>

植物是一个系统：各个部分一起工作，每个部分各有各的功能。根把植物固定在土里，并吸收水分。茎撑起叶和花，在它们和根之间运送水和食物。叶子为植物制造食物。花产生种子，种子长在果实里。

看看它的样子。一根茎从土里长出来，然后一分为二，每一半再一分为二；辣椒的茎就是这样一次次成对分叉的。叶子是边缘光滑的椭圆形，长可达 <Num s="S10">13 厘米</Num>。白色的小花一朵一朵地垂在分叉下面。

有些部分太小，在整株上看不清楚。它们作为放大标本放在植株旁边：从叶子上切下的一小块、一片茎的横切、一个根尖、一朵剖开的花。每个标本的名字都写着它放大了多少倍，例如叶片切块是 ×250。图版里的比例尺只对植株本身有效。

<More title={{ en: "Why a chilli plant", zh: "为什么选辣椒" }}>

在新加坡，人们在阳台、社区花园和学校园圃里用盆种辣椒，NParks 还出版了种辣椒的指南。辣椒花结构简单、整齐，每个部分都容易找到；同一株上还能同时看到花蕾、花、青果和红果。第 7 章另外用了一粒绿豆，因为它的子叶大，比辣椒籽更容易看清。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Plants are living things", zh: "植物是有生命的" }}>

和动物一样，植物需要水、空气和能量，会生长、会对光作出反应，也会繁殖。植物利用光自己制造食物，再通过呼吸作用从食物中释放能量，白天和夜里都在呼吸。

</More>
