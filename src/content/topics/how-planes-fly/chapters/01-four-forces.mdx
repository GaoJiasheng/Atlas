<Lang en>

Four forces act on an aircraft in flight. <Term id="weight">Weight</Term> pulls it towards the centre of the Earth. It depends on the aircraft itself, the fuel it carries and its passengers and cargo, so it changes as fuel is used. Lift comes from the air flowing past the wings, and thrust from the engines. <Term id="drag">Drag</Term> is the push of the air that opposes the aircraft’s movement through it.

The arrows on the stage show one particular case: steady, level flight, with the wings not tilted and the thrust pointing straight ahead. In that case lift balances weight and thrust balances drag. Balanced does not mean stopped. The aircraft keeps moving at a steady speed, just as a falling sheet of paper settles at a steady speed once the air’s push balances its weight in <TopicLink slug="forces" ch="gravity-and-weight">Gravity and weight</TopicLink>.

The engines and the wings do different jobs. A glider has no engine at all, yet it still has lift and drag as it moves through the air. On an airliner, the wings provide most of the lift, while the engines supply the thrust that keeps the aircraft moving against drag. The stage hides the engines for a moment to make that comparison; it does not claim this airliner could glide like a glider. <FlyTo preset="aircraft-overview">Look at the wings and engines</FlyTo>

What matters to the wing is how the air moves relative to it. Lift is measured across the direction of the incoming air, and drag along it. In level flight that makes lift point straight up, but in a climb, a descent or a turn it does not. The last beat shows the incoming air beside the wing experiment for that reason.

<More title={{ en: "When the four arrows apply", zh: "四力图在什么条件下成立" }}>

The balanced picture needs all of its conditions: level flight, a steady speed, no bank and thrust pointing along the flight path. It does not explain a climb or a turn; chapter 04 shows what changes in a turn. The arrows show which forces balance. Their lengths are scaled for the drawing, so the lift and weight pair cannot be compared with the thrust and drag pair, and this topic does not give the real weight or thrust of any airliner.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Speed through the air", zh: "相对空气的速度" }}>

The speed that counts for lift and drag is the speed of the aircraft relative to the air around it. Speed over the ground is a different measurement. That is why the wing experiment in chapters 02, 03 and 06 is set up with air flowing past a section that stays in place: what matters is the movement of the two relative to each other.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why balanced forces still move", zh: "力平衡为什么还在动" }}>

When the forces on an object balance, its motion does not change. If it was still, it stays still; if it was moving, it keeps moving at the same speed in the same direction. An aircraft in steady level flight is the second case. To speed up, slow down, climb or turn, one of the forces has to be bigger than its partner, or change direction.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

飞行中的飞机受到四种力。<Term id="weight">重力</Term>把飞机拉向地心，它的大小取决于飞机本身、所带的燃油以及乘客和货物，所以燃油用掉以后重力会变小。升力来自流过机翼的空气，推力来自发动机。<Term id="drag">阻力</Term>是空气对飞机的推，阻碍飞机在空气中运动。

舞台上的箭头只表示一种特定情形：稳定的水平飞行，机翼没有倾斜，推力正对前方。这时升力与重力平衡，推力与阻力平衡。平衡不等于停住，飞机仍然以不变的速度向前飞。<TopicLink slug="forces" ch="gravity-and-weight">“重力与重量”</TopicLink>里那张下落的纸，等空气的推与它的重量平衡以后，也是以不变的速度往下落。

发动机和机翼各做各的事。滑翔机根本没有发动机，在空气中运动时照样有升力和阻力。客机的大部分升力来自机翼，发动机则提供推力，让飞机克服阻力继续前进。舞台上暂时把发动机藏起来，只是为了做这个对照，并不是说这架客机可以像滑翔机那样滑翔。<FlyTo preset="aircraft-overview">看看机翼和发动机</FlyTo>

对机翼来说，要紧的是空气相对它怎样运动。升力垂直于来流方向，阻力沿着来流方向。水平飞行时升力正好竖直向上；爬升、下降或转弯时就不是这样。所以最后一拍把来流方向画在机翼实验旁边。

<More title={{ en: "When the four arrows apply", zh: "四力图在什么条件下成立" }}>

这幅平衡图要满足全部条件：水平飞行、速度不变、没有倾斜、推力沿着航迹方向。它不能用来解释爬升或转弯，转弯时有什么变化见第 04 章。箭头只说明哪些力彼此平衡；箭头的长度为了作图做过缩放，所以升力和重力这一对不能拿来和推力、阻力那一对比长短。本主题也不给出任何客机的真实重量或推力。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Speed through the air", zh: "相对空气的速度" }}>

对升力和阻力起作用的，是飞机相对周围空气的速度；相对地面的速度是另一种量。所以第 02、03、06 章的机翼实验让翼段固定不动、让空气流过它：要紧的是两者之间的相对运动。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why balanced forces still move", zh: "力平衡为什么还在动" }}>

物体受到的力彼此平衡时，它的运动状态不变：原来静止就保持静止，原来在动就以同样的速度、朝同样的方向继续动。稳定水平飞行的飞机属于后一种。要加速、减速、爬升或转弯，就得有一个力大于和它成对的力，或者改变方向。

</More>

</Lang>
