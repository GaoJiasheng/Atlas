<Lang en>

Air gets thinner as height increases. In NASA’s average-atmosphere model, pressure and <Term id="air-density">air density</Term> both fall with height. Density is the mass of air in a given volume. In this simulation, it is about <Num s="S58">1.23 kg/m³</Num> near sea level, <Num s="S58">0.74 kg/m³</Num> at <Num s="S58">5,000 m</Num> and <Num s="S58">0.41 kg/m³</Num> at <Num s="S58">10,000 m</Num>. <FlyTo preset="section-view">Go back to the experiment</FlyTo>

Keep the speed, angle of attack and reference area fixed. Change only the height. At <Num s="S58">40 m/s</Num> and <Num s="S58">4°</Num>, simulated lift falls from about <Num s="S58">924 N</Num> at sea level to <Num s="S58">556 N</Num> at <Num s="S58">5,000 m</Num> and <Num s="S58">312 N</Num> at <Num s="S58">10,000 m</Num>. Lift falls in the same proportion as density. The streamlines keep their shape: in ideal flow, density changes the force without changing the airflow pattern.

A real aircraft still needs enough lift to support its weight. In thinner air, it can fly faster relative to the air or change its angle of attack to maintain lift. Its engines also need enough air to work. The final beat returns to the whole airliner and its weight arrow.

Choosing a cruising height means balancing several needs. Thinner air can reduce drag under fixed conditions, but the aircraft must still fly within its limits. Performance, available thrust, fuel, weather and air traffic all affect the choice. Flying higher is not always better.

<More title={{ en: "The average-atmosphere formula", zh: "平均大气公式" }}>

For the lowest atmospheric layer, NASA’s model gives temperature as <Num s="S11">T = 15.04 − 0.00649 h</Num>, in °C with h in metres. Pressure is <Num s="S11">p = 101.29 × [(T + 273.1) / 288.08]^5.256</Num>, in kilopascals. Density is <Num s="S11">ρ = p / [0.2869 × (T + 273.1)]</Num>. These formulas fit average measurements. Actual weather, including today’s weather over Singapore, can differ.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Drag in thinner air", zh: "稀薄空气中的阻力" }}>

The drag equation is <Num s="S14">D = ½ ρ V² S Cd</Num>. Cd is the drag coefficient. Keep Cd, V and S fixed, and lower density gives less drag. A real aircraft can change these quantities as it climbs, so this comparison alone cannot give its cruising drag. The wing experiment calculates lift only.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "From experiment to flight", zh: "从实验到飞行" }}>

Changing one input at a time reveals the effect of density. A flight crew considers speed, height and angle of attack together, within the aircraft’s limits and air traffic requirements. The experiment’s speeds and heights are chosen for comparison, rather than for flying a real aircraft.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

越往高处，空气越稀薄。在 NASA 的平均大气模型中，气压和<Term id="air-density">空气密度</Term>都随高度减小。密度表示一定体积内空气的质量。在这个模拟里，接近海平面时约为 <Num s="S58">1.23 kg/m³</Num>；在 <Num s="S58">5,000 m</Num> 高处约为 <Num s="S58">0.74 kg/m³</Num>；在 <Num s="S58">10,000 m</Num> 高处约为 <Num s="S58">0.41 kg/m³</Num>。<FlyTo preset="section-view">回到实验</FlyTo>

保持速度、迎角和参考面积不变，只改变高度。在速度 <Num s="S58">40 m/s</Num>、迎角 <Num s="S58">4°</Num> 的模拟中，升力从海平面的约 <Num s="S58">924 N</Num>，降到 <Num s="S58">5,000 m</Num> 高处的约 <Num s="S58">556 N</Num>，再降到 <Num s="S58">10,000 m</Num> 高处的约 <Num s="S58">312 N</Num>。升力和密度按同样的比例减小。流线形状保持不变：在理想流中，密度改变力的大小，却不改变气流的路线。

真实飞机仍然需要足够的升力来支撑重量。在稀薄空气中，它可以提高相对空气的速度，或改变迎角来维持升力。发动机也需要足够的空气。最后一拍回到整架客机，并画出重力箭头。真实飞机爬高时，不会把所有条件都固定。机组需要同时考虑高度、速度和迎角，让这些量互相配合。

选择巡航高度，要兼顾几种需要。固定条件下，稀薄空气可以减小阻力，但飞机仍要在性能限制内飞行。飞机性能、可用推力、燃油、天气和空中交通都影响这个选择，所以并非飞得越高越好。

<More title={{ en: "The average-atmosphere formula", zh: "平均大气公式" }}>

在大气最低的一层，NASA 模型给出的温度是 <Num s="S11">T = 15.04 − 0.00649 h</Num>，单位是 ℃，h 以米计。气压是 <Num s="S11">p = 101.29 × [(T + 273.1) / 288.08]^5.256</Num>，单位是千帕。密度是 <Num s="S11">ρ = p / [0.2869 × (T + 273.1)]</Num>。这些公式根据平均测量值拟合而成。真实天气，包括新加坡今天的天气，都可能与平均值不同。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Drag in thinner air", zh: "稀薄空气中的阻力" }}>

阻力方程是 <Num s="S14">D = ½ ρ V² S Cd</Num>，Cd 是阻力系数。保持 Cd、V、S 不变，密度越小，阻力越小。真实飞机爬高时，这些量也可能改变，所以单靠这个比较得不出巡航时的总阻力。机翼实验只计算升力。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "From experiment to flight", zh: "从实验到飞行" }}>

一次只改一个输入值，方便看清密度的影响。机组需要把速度、高度和迎角一起考虑，同时遵守飞机的限制和空中交通要求。实验中的速度和高度用于比较，不能直接用来驾驶真实飞机。

</More>
