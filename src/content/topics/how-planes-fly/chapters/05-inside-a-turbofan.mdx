<Lang en>

The airliner’s engines are <Term id="turbofan">turbofans</Term>. Look inside the separate engine cutaway. The near half of its casing has been removed. A large fan at the front draws air in. Behind the fan, the air splits into two streams. The outer stream follows a bypass duct around the core. The central stream enters the core. <FlyTo preset="engine-section-view">Open the engine</FlyTo>

<Figure src="figures/how-planes-fly-turbofan-cutaway" alt={{ en: "A turbofan cut open to show the fan, outer bypass duct and central core.", zh: "剖开的涡扇发动机，可见风扇、外涵道和中央核心。" }} caption={{ en: "The front fan feeds two air routes: around the core and through it. This diagram is not to scale; blade rows and rotation speeds are simplified.", zh: "前方风扇把空气送往核心内外两路。图不按比例，叶排数和转速经过简化。" }} />

Follow the <Term id="core-machinery">core machinery</Term>. Rotating compressor blades squeeze the air. Fixed blades between the rotating rows guide it onwards. Fuel is added and burned in the combustor. Hot gas then passes through the turbines and leaves through the core nozzle. Bypass air travels around these parts.

The turbines take energy from the hot gas to turn shafts. In this two-spool engine, the core turbine drives the compressor through a hollow core shaft. The fan turbine drives the fan through a shaft inside the core shaft. Each connected set turns as one spool. The shafts transmit turning energy along the engine. Other engines can have more spools.

Both air streams provide thrust: hot gas leaves the core nozzle, and the fan pushes cooler bypass air out around it. Fuel releases stored energy as heat. The turbines turn some of that energy into shaft rotation. Explore how batteries and food also store energy in <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="stored-energy">Stored energy</TopicLink>.

<More title={{ en: "Bypass ratio", zh: "涵道比" }}>

The bypass ratio compares the two air streams. Divide the mass of air passing around the core each second by the mass passing through it. The moving dots show routes, so their number does not give this ratio. The cutaway shows structure and movement, without calculating engine performance.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two shafts, two speeds", zh: "两根轴，两种转速" }}>

The fan, fan shaft and fan turbine turn together. The compressor rotor, core shaft and core turbine form the other spool. They turn at <Num s="S58">30 rpm</Num> and <Num s="S58">45 rpm</Num> on screen. Both are slowed down for viewing; real engines turn much faster. Fixed stator blades and casings stay still. Blade and row counts are simplified.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Follow the parts from front to back", zh: "从前往后看零件" }}>

Start with the fan. Behind it, the air splits between the bypass duct and the core. In the core, follow the compressor, combustor, core turbine and fan turbine. At the back are two exits: the central core nozzle and the surrounding bypass outlet. Core air is compressed first, then heated by burning fuel, then passed through turbines.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

客机的发动机是<Term id="turbofan">涡扇发动机</Term>。看旁边独立的发动机剖面：靠近你的半边外壳已拿掉，露出里面的结构。前方的大风扇吸入空气。风扇后面，空气分成两路：外圈沿外涵道绕过核心，中央的一股进入核心。<FlyTo preset="engine-section-view">打开发动机</FlyTo>

<Figure src="figures/how-planes-fly-turbofan-cutaway" alt={{ en: "A turbofan cut open to show the fan, outer bypass duct and central core.", zh: "剖开的涡扇发动机，可见风扇、外涵道和中央核心。" }} caption={{ en: "The front fan feeds two air routes: around the core and through it. This diagram is not to scale; blade rows and rotation speeds are simplified.", zh: "前方风扇把空气送往核心内外两路。图不按比例，叶排数和转速经过简化。" }} />

沿着<Term id="core-machinery">核心机械</Term>往后看。压气机的转动叶片压缩空气，夹在转动叶排之间的固定叶片引导气流。燃料在燃烧室加入并燃烧。热气接着流过涡轮，从核心喷口排出。外涵空气则绕过燃烧室和涡轮。

涡轮从热气中取得能量，带动轴转动。这个例子有两个转子：核心涡轮通过空心核心轴带动压气机，风扇涡轮通过内侧的风扇轴带动风扇。风扇轴穿过核心轴的内部。每组相连的零件一起转，轴把转动的能量传到发动机另一端。其他发动机也可能有更多转子。

两路气流都产生推力：热气从核心喷口排出，风扇把较冷的外涵空气从外圈推出。燃料把储存的能量以热的形式释放，涡轮再把部分能量变成轴的转动。电池和食物也能储存能量，可以在<TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="stored-energy">“储存的能量”</TopicLink>中进一步了解。

<More title={{ en: "Bypass ratio", zh: "涵道比" }}>

涵道比用来比较两股气流。把每秒绕过核心的空气质量，除以每秒穿过核心的空气质量，就得到涵道比。移动的点表示路线，数点得不出这个比值。这个剖面展示结构和运动，没有计算发动机性能。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two shafts, two speeds", zh: "两根轴，两种转速" }}>

风扇、风扇轴和风扇涡轮一起转。压气机转子、核心轴和核心涡轮组成另一组。画面中两组分别以 <Num s="S58">30 rpm</Num> 和 <Num s="S58">45 rpm</Num> 转动，都已放慢，方便观察。真实发动机转得快得多。固定的静子叶片和机匣保持不动，叶片数和叶排数经过简化。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Follow the parts from front to back", zh: "从前往后看零件" }}>

先看风扇，后面分成外涵道和核心两路。核心里依次是压气机、燃烧室、核心涡轮和风扇涡轮。最后有两个出口：中间的核心喷口和外圈的外涵出口。核心气流先被压缩，再由燃料燃烧加热，接着流过涡轮。

</More>
