<Lang en>

The engines on the airliner are <Term id="turbofan">turbofans</Term>. This cutaway is a separate model, cut along its length with the half nearest you taken away. At the front, a large fan draws air in. Just behind it the air divides: an outer bypass stream flows around the core through the bypass duct, and a central stream enters the core. <FlyTo preset="engine-section-view">Open the engine</FlyTo>

<Figure src="figures/how-planes-fly-turbofan-cutaway" alt={{ en: "A generic turbofan cutaway with a fan, outer bypass and central core.", zh: "通用涡扇剖面，可见风扇、外涵道与中央核心。" }} caption={{ en: "A generic turbofan cut open: the fan at the front, the bypass duct around the outside and the core in the middle. An illustration made for this topic; not to scale, and it does not show real stage counts or shaft speeds.", zh: "剖开的通用涡扇：前方是风扇，外圈是外涵道，中间是核心。为本主题绘制的示意图，不按比例，也不表示真实的级数或转速。" }} />

Inside the core is the <Term id="core-machinery">core machinery</Term>. The compressor’s rotating blades squeeze the air, with rows of fixed stator blades between them to guide it. In the combustor, fuel is added and burned. The hot gas then flows through the turbines and out of the core nozzle. The bypass air never passes through the combustor or the turbines.

The turbines take energy from the hot gas and use it to turn shafts. In this two-spool example the core turbine drives the compressor through a hollow core shaft, and the fan turbine drives the fan through a fan shaft that runs inside the core shaft. The shafts carry turning work from one end of the engine to the other; they are not air pipes. Other engines can have more spools.

Both streams help to produce thrust: the hot gas leaving the core nozzle and the cooler bypass air pushed out by the fan. Burning the fuel releases its stored energy as heat, and the turbines change part of it into the turning of the shafts. Energy that is stored and then changed into other forms, in a battery or in food, is the subject of <TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="stored-energy">Stored energy</TopicLink>.

<More title={{ en: "Bypass ratio", zh: "涵道比" }}>

Engineers compare the two streams with the bypass ratio: the mass of air going around the core each second divided by the mass going through it. The moving dots on the stage show where the air goes, not how much of it; counting them does not give the bypass ratio, and this model has no engine performance numbers.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two shafts, two speeds", zh: "两根轴，两种转速" }}>

The fan, the fan shaft and the fan turbine turn together as one spool; the compressor rotor, the core shaft and the core turbine turn together as the other. On the stage they turn at <Num s="S58">30 rpm</Num> and <Num s="S58">45 rpm</Num>, slowed far down so that you can follow them; real engines turn much faster, and this topic gives no real speeds. The stator blades and the casings stay still. Blade numbers and the number of rows are drawing choices.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where the stages are", zh: "各段在哪里" }}>

Follow the engine from front to back: fan, then the split into bypass duct and core, then compressor, combustor, core turbine and fan turbine, and finally two exits, the core nozzle in the middle and the bypass outlet around it. The order inside the core does not change: compress first, then burn, then the turbines.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

客机上的发动机是<Term id="turbofan">涡扇发动机</Term>。这个剖面是另一个独立的模型，沿长度方向剖开，靠近你的那一半拿掉了。前方的大风扇把空气吸进来。空气一过风扇就分成两路：外圈的外涵气流经过外涵道绕着核心流过，中央的一股进入核心。<FlyTo preset="engine-section-view">打开发动机</FlyTo>

核心里装的是<Term id="core-machinery">核心机械</Term>。压气机的转动叶片压缩空气，叶排之间有一排排固定的静子叶片引导气流。燃料在燃烧室里加入并燃烧。热气随后流过涡轮，从核心喷口排出。外涵空气始终不经过燃烧室和涡轮。

涡轮从热气中取出能量，用来带动轴转动。在这个两转子的例子里，核心涡轮经空心的核心轴带动压气机，风扇涡轮经风扇轴带动风扇，而风扇轴从核心轴的内部穿过。轴把转动的功从发动机的一头传到另一头，它不是输送空气的管子。其他发动机可能有更多转子。

两股气流都帮助产生推力：一股是从核心喷口排出的热气，一股是风扇推出去的较冷的外涵空气。燃料燃烧，把储存的能量以热的形式放出来，涡轮再把其中一部分变成轴的转动。能量怎样储存在电池或食物里、再转换成别的形式，在<TopicLink slug="energy-conversion" ch="stored-energy">“储存的能量”</TopicLink>里讲过。

<More title={{ en: "Bypass ratio", zh: "涵道比" }}>

工程师用涵道比来比较两股气流：每秒绕过核心的空气质量，除以每秒穿过核心的空气质量。舞台上移动的点只表示空气往哪里走，不表示有多少；数这些点得不出涵道比，这个模型也没有任何发动机性能数据。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Two shafts, two speeds", zh: "两根轴，两种转速" }}>

风扇、风扇轴和风扇涡轮作为一个转子一起转；压气机转子、核心轴和核心涡轮作为另一个转子一起转。舞台上它们的转速是 <Num s="S58">30 rpm</Num> 和 <Num s="S58">45 rpm</Num>，为了看得清放慢了很多；真实发动机转得快得多，本主题不给出真实转速。静子叶片和机匣都不动。叶片数和叶排数都是作图的选择。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Where the stages are", zh: "各段在哪里" }}>

从前往后看：风扇，然后分成外涵道和核心两路，接着是压气机、燃烧室、核心涡轮、风扇涡轮，最后是两个出口——中间的核心喷口和外圈的外涵出口。核心里的顺序不会变：先压缩，再燃烧，然后经过涡轮。

</More>
