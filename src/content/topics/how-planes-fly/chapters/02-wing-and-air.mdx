<Lang en>

Open the wing box. Beneath the skin, <Term id="wing-structure">spars and ribs</Term> form a frame. Spars run along the span and carry bending loads. Ribs run from front to back and hold the wing’s cross-sectional shape. Two spars, the skin and the ribs form a closed box. This example has three ribs; other wings have different structures. <FlyTo preset="wing-structure-view">Open the wing box</FlyTo>

<Figure src="figures/how-planes-fly-wing-cutaway" alt={{ en: "Two spars and crosswise ribs beneath a wing’s outer skin.", zh: "机翼蒙皮下面的两根翼梁和横向翼肋。" }} caption={{ en: "Spars, ribs and skin form a wing box. This drawing is not to scale; its curved section differs from the symmetric experiment.", zh: "翼梁、翼肋和蒙皮组成翼盒。图不按比例；弯曲剖面与对称实验翼型不同。" }} />

The wing experiment has a symmetric section: the top and bottom have the same shape. <Term id="streamline">Streamlines</Term> follow the direction of the air around it. Watch how air bends around both surfaces. Both sides turn the flow and contribute to lift. <FlyTo preset="section-view">Look at the airflow</FlyTo>

Air passing over a wing does not have to meet the air passing underneath at the back edge. Release marked dots together and watch: the upper dots arrive first. The familiar story that air must hurry over the longer top to meet air from below is wrong.

Switch to the <Term id="pressure">pressure</Term> view. It shows how hard the air pushes on different parts of the same surface. Pressure differences and the turning of the air describe the same aerodynamic force. They do not supply two separate amounts of lift.

Speed has a large effect. In this simulation, the section is tilted at <Num s="S58">4°</Num> in sea-level air. At <Num s="S58">20 m/s</Num>, lift is about <Num s="S58">231 N</Num>. Double the speed to <Num s="S58">40 m/s</Num> and lift reaches about <Num s="S58">924 N</Num>: four times as much.

<More title={{ en: "The lift equation", zh: "升力方程" }}>

Lift is <Num s="S4">L = ½ ρ V² S Cl</Num>. Air density, ρ, is the mass of air in each cubic metre. V is speed relative to the air. S is a reference area. Cl, the lift coefficient, includes the effects of shape, tilt and flow conditions. Hold ρ, S and Cl fixed: doubling V makes V², and therefore lift, <Num s="S4">four times</Num> as large. The experiment uses a reference area of <Num s="S58">2 m²</Num>. The visible slice is only a short piece of it.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Real air and ideal flow", zh: "真实空气与理想流" }}>

Real air has some stickiness. A thin layer near the wing surface moves more slowly. The ideal model leaves this layer out and treats the wing as having no ends. Adding its surface pressures gives the calculated lift, with almost no force along the airflow. A real wing also has drag, which this ideal calculation cannot show.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Follow the marked air", zh: "跟着标记点看气流" }}>

In beat 3, ringed dots leave the upstream edge together, one on each line. Dots passing above the section travel faster and reach the back first. Dots below arrive later. Each follows the local air speed. The dots move <Num s="S58">80 times</Num> more slowly on screen so you can follow them.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

打开翼盒，蒙皮下面就是<Term id="wing-structure">翼梁与翼肋</Term>搭成的骨架。翼梁沿翼展延伸，承受使机翼弯曲的力。翼肋从前缘伸向后缘，保持机翼的剖面形状。两根翼梁、蒙皮和翼肋围成一个封闭的盒子。这个例子有三道翼肋，其他机翼的结构会有所不同。<FlyTo preset="wing-structure-view">打开翼盒</FlyTo>

<Figure src="figures/how-planes-fly-wing-cutaway" alt={{ en: "Two spars and crosswise ribs beneath a wing’s outer skin.", zh: "机翼蒙皮下面的两根翼梁和横向翼肋。" }} caption={{ en: "Spars, ribs and skin form a wing box. This drawing is not to scale; its curved section differs from the symmetric experiment.", zh: "翼梁、翼肋和蒙皮组成翼盒。图不按比例；弯曲剖面与对称实验翼型不同。" }} />

旁边的实验件采用对称翼型，上下形状相同。周围的<Term id="streamline">流线</Term>顺着各处空气流动的方向。仔细看，空气绕着上下两个表面弯曲。两面都让气流转向，都影响升力。空气并非只撞在下表面，两侧的流动都要一起考虑。<FlyTo preset="section-view">看看气流</FlyTo>

从上面和下面经过的空气，不必在机翼后缘同时会合。让标记点一起出发，上方的点先到后面。有种常见说法认为，上方路程较长，所以空气必须赶快走，才能和下方空气碰头。这种解释是错的。

切到<Term id="pressure">气压</Term>视图，看看空气在翼面各处推得多大力。表面气压差和空气转向，描述的是同一个空气动力，不能把它们算成两份升力。

速度的影响很大。在这个模拟里，翼段在海平面空气中倾斜 <Num s="S58">4°</Num>。速度 <Num s="S58">20 m/s</Num> 时，升力约 <Num s="S58">231 N</Num>。速度加倍到 <Num s="S58">40 m/s</Num>，升力约 <Num s="S58">924 N</Num>，变成原来的四倍。

<More title={{ en: "The lift equation", zh: "升力方程" }}>

升力是 <Num s="S4">L = ½ ρ V² S Cl</Num>。ρ 是空气密度，表示每立方米空气的质量。V 是相对空气的速度，S 是参考面积。Cl 叫升力系数，包含形状、倾斜程度和流动条件的影响。保持 ρ、S、Cl 不变，V 加倍，V² 和升力都变成 <Num s="S4">四倍</Num>。实验采用的参考面积是 <Num s="S58">2 m²</Num>，眼前的翼段只是其中一小段。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Real air and ideal flow", zh: "真实空气与理想流" }}>

真实空气有一些黏性，贴近机翼表面的一薄层空气流得较慢。理想模型省略了这一层，还把机翼看成没有两端。把模型中各处表面的气压作用合起来，就得到计算出的升力，沿来流方向的力几乎为零。真实机翼还有阻力，这个理想计算显示不出来。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Follow the marked air", zh: "跟着标记点看气流" }}>

第 3 拍中，带圈的点从画面上游一起出发，每条线上一个。上方的点流得快，先到后面；下方的点后到。每个点都按当地气流的速度前进。画面把点的移动放慢 <Num s="S58">80 倍</Num>，方便跟着看。

</More>

</Lang>
