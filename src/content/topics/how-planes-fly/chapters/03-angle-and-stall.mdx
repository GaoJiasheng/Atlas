<Lang en>

The <Term id="chord">chord</Term> is a straight line from the front edge of an airfoil to its back edge. The <Term id="angle-of-attack">angle of attack</Term> lies between the chord and the incoming air. Watch the fixed incoming-air line as the wing section turns. Its chord turns with it, changing the angle. The aircraft’s nose angle above the horizon is a different measurement. <FlyTo preset="section-view">Look at the angle</FlyTo>

At zero angle, this symmetric section has zero lift in ideal flow. Tilt it to <Num s="S58">4°</Num> and lift appears, although the upper and lower shapes are the same. Turning the air matters; a rounded top alone cannot explain lift. In this simulation, the lift coefficient rises from 0 to about <Num s="S58">0.47</Num> at <Num s="S58">4°</Num>, then about <Num s="S58">1.40</Num> at <Num s="S58">12°</Num>.

Increasing the angle helps only up to a point. At a large angle, air can break away from the upper surface and lift falls. This is a <Term id="stall">stall</Term>. Real wings stall at different angles depending on their shape and conditions. This simulation uses <Num s="S58">12°</Num> as its boundary. Beyond it, a simple rule gives a dashed curve. At <Num s="S58">16°</Num>, lift falls to <Num s="S58">0.85</Num> of its peak. Dashed lines around the wing show separated air schematically.

A stalled wing still produces some lift. The engine can also keep running, as the turning fan beside the experiment shows. Stall describes what happens to the airflow and lift at the wing.

<More title={{ en: "Wing angle and nose angle", zh: "迎角与机头角度" }}>

The angle between the nose and the horizon is called attitude. Angle of attack is measured from the incoming air. Change the air’s direction and the same aircraft attitude can give a different angle of attack.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Reading the dashed curve", zh: "怎样看虚线曲线" }}>

A real wing’s stall depends on its shape, surface, size and speed. Engineers test it in matching conditions. The ideal calculation leaves out air’s stickiness, so it cannot calculate separation. Instead, the simulation sets a peak at <Num s="S58">12°</Num>, then uses <Num s="S58">0.85</Num> of that peak at <Num s="S58">16°</Num> and <Num s="S58">0.60</Num> at <Num s="S58">20°</Num>. These are teaching values. NASA’s student program also combines ideal flow with a separate stall correction.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Negative angles", zh: "负迎角" }}>

Tilt the section’s front edge down and the angle becomes negative. This symmetric model mirrors the result: lift points downwards. Beyond <Num s="S58">−12°</Num>, the dashed separation diagram moves to the lower surface.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Lift is a push of the air", zh: "升力是空气的推" }}>

Lift and drag are pushes of air on the wing. Explore these forces in <TopicLink slug="forces" ch="pushes-and-pulls">Pushes and pulls</TopicLink>. During a stall, the size and direction of the air’s push change.

</More>

</Lang>

<Lang zh>

<Term id="chord">翼弦</Term>是连接翼型前缘和后缘的直线。<Term id="angle-of-attack">迎角（攻角）</Term>是翼弦与来流之间的角。看着那条固定的来流线，再转动翼段：翼弦跟着转，迎角就改变了。机头与地平线之间的角，则是另一种量。<FlyTo preset="section-view">看看迎角</FlyTo>

在理想流中，这块对称翼型零迎角时没有升力。转到 <Num s="S58">4°</Num>，上下形状虽然一样，却产生了升力。关键是让空气转向，单靠上表面鼓起解释不了升力。在这个模拟里，升力系数从 0 增加到约 <Num s="S58">0.47</Num>，对应迎角 <Num s="S58">4°</Num>；再增加到约 <Num s="S58">1.40</Num>，对应迎角 <Num s="S58">12°</Num>。

迎角增加到一定程度，空气会离开机翼上表面，升力随之下降。这叫<Term id="stall">失速</Term>。真实机翼的失速角度随形状和流动条件而变。工程师需要在相似条件下进行试验，才能确定真实机翼的失速角度。这个模拟以 <Num s="S58">12°</Num> 为分界，之后用简单规则画出虚线曲线。在 <Num s="S58">16°</Num> 时，升力降到峰值的 <Num s="S58">0.85</Num>。翼段周围的虚线示意分离的空气。

失速时，机翼仍有一部分升力。发动机也可以继续运转，就像实验旁边一直转动的风扇。失速描述的是机翼周围的气流和升力变化，并不表示发动机停机，也不表示升力一下子全部消失。

<More title={{ en: "Wing angle and nose angle", zh: "迎角与机头角度" }}>

机头与地平线之间的角叫姿态角。迎角则从来流方向量起。来流方向一变，同样的姿态角就可能对应不同的迎角。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Reading the dashed curve", zh: "怎样看虚线曲线" }}>

真实机翼的失速角度取决于形状、表面、尺寸和速度，工程师要在相似条件下做试验。理想计算省略了空气的黏性，算不出气流分离。模拟于是把峰值设在 <Num s="S58">12°</Num>，在 <Num s="S58">16°</Num> 时取峰值的 <Num s="S58">0.85</Num>，在 <Num s="S58">20°</Num> 时取 <Num s="S58">0.60</Num>。这些是教学用的数值。NASA 的学生程序也把理想流和另外加入的失速修正结合起来。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Negative angles", zh: "负迎角" }}>

把翼段前缘向下转，迎角就变成负值。这个对称模型的结果也随之翻转：升力朝下。超过 <Num s="S58">−12°</Num> 后，虚线分离示意移到下表面。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Lift is a push of the air", zh: "升力是空气的推" }}>

升力和阻力都是空气对机翼的推，可以在<TopicLink slug="forces" ch="pushes-and-pulls">“推和拉”</TopicLink>中进一步了解。失速会改变这个推力的大小和方向。

</More>

</Lang>
