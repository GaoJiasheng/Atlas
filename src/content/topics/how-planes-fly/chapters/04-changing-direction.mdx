<Lang en>

An aircraft can rotate in three ways. In a roll, one wing goes down and the other goes up. In pitch, the nose turns up or down. In yaw, the nose turns left or right. Hinged <Term id="flight-controls">control surfaces</Term> change these movements. Near the outer back edges of the wings, ailerons move in opposite directions. One goes up, the other down, rolling the aircraft into a bank. <FlyTo preset="controls-view">Look at the tail and the wing edges</FlyTo>

Elevators sit on the back edge of the horizontal tail. They change pitch. The rudder sits on the back edge of the upright fin and controls yaw. During a coordinated turn, it helps the aircraft line up with its curving path. Watch each movement separately in the model.

Banking tilts the lift with the wings. Part of the lift then points sideways, towards the inside of the turn. This sideways force curves the flight path. The fourth beat holds the airliner in a bank so you can compare the arrows: weight points straight down while lift leans sideways.

For slower flight, the wing can change shape. <Term id="high-lift-devices">Flaps and slats</Term> extend from its edges. Flaps slide back and down from the trailing edge; slats move forwards from the leading edge. They help the wing provide enough lift at a lower speed. Flaps also add drag. On top of the wing, spoilers rise to reduce lift and add drag.

<More title={{ en: "Movements in the model", zh: "模型里的偏转" }}>

These movements are chosen to be easy to see. The ailerons turn <Num s="S58">12°</Num> in opposite directions. The elevators turn <Num s="S58">10°</Num> up and the rudder turns <Num s="S58">10°</Num>. The whole airliner banks <Num s="S58">20°</Num>. Flaps slide back <Num s="S58">0.04 m</Num> and turn down <Num s="S58">20°</Num>; slats move forwards <Num s="S58">0.025 m</Num>. These distances apply to the model, which is ten times smaller than the aircraft it represents.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The spoiler", zh: "扰流板" }}>

Look just ahead of the flap on the left wing. One spoiler panel rises in the final pose while the flap lowers. A raised spoiler reduces lift and adds drag. An extended flap helps the wing provide lift at low speed. The model shows one spoiler for clarity; real wings can have different numbers.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Lift during a turn", zh: "转弯时的升力" }}>

A real turn changes the aircraft’s direction. Its speed and height may also change, and the forces adjust accordingly. The model holds one banked position to show lift’s sideways part; it does not calculate a flight path.

</More>

<Lang zh>

飞机可以有三种转动。滚转时，一侧机翼向下，另一侧向上；俯仰时，机头向上或向下；偏航时，机头向左或向右。可以偏转的<Term id="flight-controls">操纵面</Term>改变这些转动。副翼装在两翼外侧的后缘，一边向上、一边向下，让飞机滚转，进入倾斜姿态。<FlyTo preset="controls-view">看看尾翼和机翼后缘</FlyTo>

升降舵位于水平尾翼的后缘，改变俯仰。方向舵位于竖直尾翼的后缘，控制偏航。协调转弯时，方向舵帮助飞机对准弯曲的航迹。模型把每一种转动分开演示，可以逐个观察机翼和尾翼的变化。

飞机倾斜时，升力随机翼一起倾斜。升力有一部分指向侧面，朝向转弯内侧，让飞行路线弯曲。第四拍让客机保持倾斜，方便比较两根箭头：重力仍然竖直向下，升力则斜向一边。

需要慢速飞行时，机翼可以改变形状。<Term id="high-lift-devices">襟翼与缝翼</Term>从机翼边缘伸出：襟翼从后缘向后、向下移动，缝翼从前缘向前移动。它们帮助机翼在较低速度下仍有足够的升力，襟翼也会增加阻力。机翼上方还有扰流板，抬起时减少升力、增加阻力。仔细看最后一拍，就能分清这些活动翼面的不同作用。

<More title={{ en: "Movements in the model", zh: "模型里的偏转" }}>

这里选用的动作幅度方便观察。副翼各向相反方向偏转 <Num s="S58">12°</Num>，升降舵上偏 <Num s="S58">10°</Num>，方向舵偏转 <Num s="S58">10°</Num>。整架客机侧倾 <Num s="S58">20°</Num>。襟翼后移 <Num s="S58">0.04 m</Num>，下转 <Num s="S58">20°</Num>；缝翼前移 <Num s="S58">0.025 m</Num>。这些距离是缩小十倍的模型上的距离。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The spoiler", zh: "扰流板" }}>

看左翼襟翼前方。最后一个姿态中，一块扰流板抬起，襟翼则向下放。扰流板抬起会减少升力、增加阻力；襟翼伸出则帮助机翼在低速时产生足够的升力。模型只画一块扰流板，方便看清；真实机翼上的数量各有不同。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Lift during a turn", zh: "转弯时的升力" }}>

真实转弯会改变飞机的方向，速度和高度也可能变化，各种力随之调整。模型固定在一个倾斜姿态，展示升力的侧向部分，没有计算飞行路线。

</More>
