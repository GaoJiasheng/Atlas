<Lang en>

An aircraft can turn in three ways about its centre. In a roll one wing goes down and the other up; in pitch the nose moves up or down; in yaw the nose moves left or right. Hinged <Term id="flight-controls">control surfaces</Term> change each of them. The ailerons, near the outer back edges of the wings, move in opposite directions, one up and one down. That rolls the aircraft into a bank. <FlyTo preset="controls-view">Look at the tail and the wing edges</FlyTo>

The elevators, on the back edge of the horizontal tail or tailplane, change pitch. The rudder, on the back edge of the vertical fin, controls yaw; in a coordinated turn it helps keep the aircraft lined up with its curving path. The stage shows each movement on its own, with angles chosen so that you can see them, not taken from any aircraft.

Banking tilts the lift. Part of it now points sideways, towards the inside of the turn, and that sideways part curves the flight path. In the fourth beat the airliner is held at one fixed bank: weight still points straight down, while lift leans with the wings. The picture explains the idea; it is not a calculated flight.

For slower flight the wing itself can change shape. <Term id="high-lift-devices">Flaps and slats</Term> do this: flaps slide back and down from the trailing edge, and slats move forward from the leading edge. They let the wing give enough lift at a lower speed, and flaps also add drag. Spoilers are something else: panels on top of the wing that rise to reduce lift and add drag.

The model moves each control by a fixed design angle: the ailerons <Num s="S58">12°</Num> in opposite directions, the elevators <Num s="S58">10°</Num> up and the rudder <Num s="S58">10°</Num>. The banked pose tilts the whole airliner by <Num s="S58">20°</Num>. The flaps slide back <Num s="S58">0.04 m</Num> and turn down <Num s="S58">20°</Num> on the model, and the slats move forward <Num s="S58">0.025 m</Num>. These are drawing choices on a model ten times smaller than the aircraft it represents, not instructions for flying.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The spoiler", zh: "扰流板" }}>

The model draws one spoiler, a panel on top of the left wing just ahead of the flap, and keeps it lying flat. Only one is drawn to keep the picture clear, which says nothing about how many a real wing has. Raised, a spoiler reduces lift and adds drag. Unlike a flap, it is not there to help the wing at low speed.

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the bank is a still picture", zh: "倾斜为什么只画静止的一幅" }}>

A real turn changes the aircraft’s speed, height and direction from moment to moment, and the forces change with them. This topic does not calculate a flight path. It shows a single moment in a bank, which is enough to see where the sideways part of the lift comes from.

</More>

<Lang zh>

飞机绕着自身中心可以有三种转动。滚转是一侧机翼向下、另一侧向上；俯仰是机头向上或向下；偏航是机头向左或向右。可以偏转的<Term id="flight-controls">操纵面</Term>分别改变这三种转动。副翼装在两翼外侧的后缘，偏转方向相反：一边向上，一边向下，使飞机滚转、倾斜。<FlyTo preset="controls-view">看看尾翼和机翼后缘</FlyTo>

升降舵在水平尾翼的后缘，改变俯仰。方向舵在垂直尾翼的后缘，控制偏航；协调转弯时，它帮助飞机对准弯曲的航迹。舞台上每种转动分开演示，偏转角度是为了看得清而选的，不取自任何一种飞机。

飞机倾斜，升力也跟着倾斜。这时升力有一部分指向侧面，朝着转弯的内侧，正是这个侧向的部分让航迹弯曲。第四拍里，客机停在一个固定的倾斜姿态：重力仍然竖直向下，升力随机翼倾斜。这幅画只用来说明道理，不是计算出来的飞行。

需要飞得慢一些时，机翼本身可以改变形状。<Term id="high-lift-devices">襟翼与缝翼</Term>就是做这件事的：襟翼从后缘向后、向下伸出，缝翼从前缘向前伸出。它们让机翼在较低速度下仍有足够的升力；襟翼放下时也会增加阻力。扰流板是另一回事：它是机翼上表面的板，抬起时减少升力、增加阻力。

<More title={{ en: "Roll, pitch and yaw in the model", zh: "模型里的滚转、俯仰和偏航" }}>

模型里每个操纵面按固定的设计角度偏转：副翼反向各偏 <Num s="S58">12°</Num>，升降舵向上 <Num s="S58">10°</Num>，方向舵 <Num s="S58">10°</Num>。倾斜姿态让整架客机侧倾 <Num s="S58">20°</Num>。模型上的襟翼向后移 <Num s="S58">0.04 m</Num>，再向下转 <Num s="S58">20°</Num>；缝翼向前移 <Num s="S58">0.025 m</Num>。这些都是在缩小了十倍的模型上作图的选择，不是驾驶飞机的操作数据。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "The spoiler", zh: "扰流板" }}>

模型只画了一块扰流板，在左翼上表面、襟翼前方，一直收平。只画一块是为了让画面清楚，并不说明真实机翼上有几块。扰流板抬起时，升力减少，阻力增加。它和襟翼不同，作用不是帮助机翼在低速时产生升力。

</More>

<More title={{ en: "Why the bank is a still picture", zh: "倾斜为什么只画静止的一幅" }}>

真实的转弯中，飞机的速度、高度和方向时刻在变，受到的力也跟着变。本主题不计算飞行轨迹，只画倾斜中的一个瞬间，这已足以看出升力的侧向部分从哪里来。

</More>
